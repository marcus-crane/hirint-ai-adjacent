Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

Tenstorrent is hiring a Senior Application Security Engineer to lead AppSec enablement, policy automation, software supply-chain integrity, and vulnerability triage across more than 3,100 GitHub and GitLab repositories. Reporting to the Head of Information Security, you will move our program from audit-only visibility to low-friction inline prevention, partnering with engineering teams, departmental AppSec champions, DevInfra, and our Open Source Program Office. You will scale your impact through tooling, policy-as-code, and AI, and serve as technical lead for Bug Bounty and external vulnerability triage.

This role is remote, based out of the United States or Canada.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* A hands-on AppSec builder who measures success in real risk reduction and developer velocity.
* AI-native: you use Cursor, Claude Code, and MCP tools to triage findings at scale and write reusable security skills for the engineering org.
* Broadly grounded in how web/API apps, microservices, containers, CI/CD pipelines, and AI agent systems work and fail.
* A sharp triage lead who separates real exploitability from scanner noise and drives fixes to closure with engineering owners.

**What We Need**

* Deep experience running ASPM and developer security tooling (such as Cycode, Snyk, Semgrep, or GitHub Advanced Security) across SAST, SCA, secrets, IaC, and container scanning.
* A track record moving large engineering orgs from report-only to PR/MR merge-blocking in GitHub Actions and GitLab CI without false-positive gridlock.
* Programming skills (Python, TypeScript, or Go) and experience using AI tools and MCP servers to automate triage and reporting.
* Ability to reproduce and guide fixes for vulnerabilities such as SSRF, BOLA, auth bypass, injection, and dependency compromises.
* Experience with software supply-chain security (SBOMs, dependency hygiene, container registries) and leading Bug Bounty triage.

**What You Will Learn**

* How to own enterprise-wide AppSec tooling across 3,100+ repositories alongside embedded champions in AI Software, Cloud, DevInfra, and RISC-V.
* How to launch and run the technical triage engine for Tenstorrent's Bug Bounty Program.
* How to build centralized security skills and guardrails so engineers and AI coding agents write secure code from the first commit.
* How to mature automated SBOM exports and cryptographic release signing across our software ecosystem.
