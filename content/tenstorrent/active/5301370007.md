Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

Tenstorrent is hiring a Senior Cloud Security Engineer to own the security of our multi-account AWS and GCP environments, our public internet perimeter, and our internal AI Gateway. As part of the Operations Security (OpSec) team reporting to the Head of Information Security, you will build continuous cloud security posture management, eliminate static credentials, feed cloud telemetry into our SIEM, and run our DAST and external penetration testing programs. This is a hands-on role for a builder who lives in Terraform and writes production code.

This role is remote, based out of the United States or Canada.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* A cloud security builder with deep AWS and GCP experience who fixes misconfigurations by shipping clean Terraform.
* AI-native: you use coding agents to build tools and posture reports, and you are excited to co-own and harden our LiteLLM AI Gateway.
* Offensively informed: comfortable running DAST and attack surface management, coordinating pen tests, and fixing flaws like SSRF, BOLA, and IAM privilege escalation.
* A collaborative partner to OSPO, AI Software, and the TT-Console team as they ship public services.

**What We Need**

* Deep experience securing multi-account AWS (Organizations, IAM Identity Center, GuardDuty, Security Hub, CloudTrail, KMS) and GCP, managed through Terraform.
* Experience running CSPM and migrating workloads to short-lived OIDC / workload identity federation and HashiCorp Vault.
* Strong perimeter security skills across Cloudflare, WAFs, reverse proxies, TLS/PKI, and zero-trust ingress.
* Programming skills (Python, TypeScript, or Go, plus Terraform and Docker) and practical experience using AI tools to build security tooling.
* Experience running DAST and pen test cycles and building cloud log pipelines into a SIEM such as CrowdStrike Next-Gen SIEM / LogScale.

**What You Will Learn**

* How to co-own and scale an enterprise AI Gateway, including authentication, rate limiting, audit logging, and supply-chain pinning.
* How to secure a commercial AI cloud platform (TT-Console) and high-profile open-source GitHub ecosystems.
* How to build Terraform paved roads that make least privilege the default for engineers and their AI coding agents.
