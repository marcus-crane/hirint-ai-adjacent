Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

We are seeking an exceptional Senior Staff or Principal High-Speed Communication Architect to define the next generation of high-bandwidth communication architecture for rack-scale AI accelerator systems. This role owns the end-to-end communication path between AI chips, GPUs, CPUs, switches, memory systems, and network interfaces, spanning 224G+ electrical links, flyover cables, near-package and co-packaged optics, and rack-scale fabrics. You will make system-level tradeoffs across architecture, ASIC and package design, PCB, connectors and cables, signal integrity, optical technology, and rack mechanics.

This role ishybrid, based out of North America.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* You hold a BS, MS, or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field, and have 15+ years of experience in high-speed electrical or optical communications.
* You have architected systems using 112G, 224G, or higher-rate SerDes and bring deep knowledge of PAM4 signaling, channel behavior, link budgets, equalization, jitter, BER, and FEC.
* You have hands-on experience with 224G-class electrical channels, flyover cable technology, and near-package optics, optical engines, or similarly dense optical interconnects.
* You are an expert in signal integrity and high-speed package, PCB, connector, cable, and optical link design, with experience modeling channels and correlating simulation results with lab measurements.
* You can lead architecture decisions across ASIC, package, PCB, optical, mechanical, and system teams, balancing bandwidth, reach, power, latency, cost, thermal impact, manufacturability, and reliability.

**What We Need**

* Define scalable rack-scale communication architecture for AI accelerators, GPUs, CPUs, switches, network adapters, memory, and I/O, including topology, lane counts, link budgets, redundancy, and 224G, 448G, and future-rate interfaces.
* Own end-to-end electrical and optical channel architecture, from die and package through PCB, connectors, flyover cables, optical engines, and fiber, and determine where to use on-package, board-level, flyover, near-package, or co-packaged interconnects.
* Lead 224G+ SerDes and signal-integrity architecture: set channel reach and loss limits; model insertion/return loss, crosstalk, reflections, vias, jitter, and equalization; and establish electrical and optical power, latency, BER, FEC, and thermal budgets.
* Co-design package, PCB, connector, cable, and optical interfaces with internal teams and suppliers; guide low-loss materials, routing, breakout, cable and fiber attach, optical-engine placement, and integration into dense rack and high-power accelerator systems.
* Drive simulation and validation from architecture through production readiness, including reusable modeling methods, SI simulation correlation, 224G+ lab test plans, measurement and failure analysis, supplier requirements, technology roadmaps, and cross-functional design reviews.

**What You Will Learn**

* How to define the complete communication fabric for next-generation rack-scale AI systems as bandwidth scales from 224G to 448G and beyond.
* How to make system-level choices between electrical reach, flyover cables, near-package optics, and co-packaged optics while balancing power, latency, cost, volume, and reliability.
* How package, PCB, connector, cable, optical-engine, and fiber decisions combine to determine end-to-end channel performance and manufacturability.
* How to shape the interconnect technology roadmap through collaboration with ASIC, package, PCB, optical, mechanical, and system teams, as well as semiconductor and component suppliers.
* How to evaluate and bring emerging technologies, including silicon photonics, 800G/1.6T+ optics, advanced packaging, and next-generation SerDes, toward production AI infrastructure.
