Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

Tenstorrent is building next-generation high-performance RISC-V server CPUs and chiplet-based AI compute platforms. We’re looking for a Senior SoC System Architect to own the system architecture and external interfaces of the CPU SoC, connecting the silicon to the broader server platform. You’ll define end-to-end SoC-to-system architecture across BMC and external component interfaces, reset and boot flows, system-level reliability, availability, and serviceability (RAS), and power management. You’ll work closely with CPU, SoC, firmware, and platform teams, as well as board, thermal, power delivery, and mechanical engineers, to align SoC specifications with server, chassis, cooling, and rack requirements. This is a senior technical leadership role for an architect who has driven complex server SoCs from specification through tape-out, bring-up, and production deployment.

This role ishybrid, based out of Santa Clara, CA or Austin, Tx.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* You’re a senior SoC or server CPU architect with deep experience defining system-level architecture and SoC-to-platform interfaces.
* You’ve owned complex SoC architecture across multiple tape-outs and understand the requirements of production server platforms.
* You have strong technical depth in reset and boot flows, system RAS, power management, and external platform connectivity.
* You can work across silicon, firmware, board, and server-platform disciplines to resolve architectural tradeoffs and align teams.
* You’re a clear technical leader who can author specifications, guide senior engineers, and drive decisions across global teams.

**What We Need**

* Bachelor’s, Master’s, or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field, with 10+ years of server CPU or SoC architecture experience.
* Proven senior technical leadership and end-to-end SoC-to-system interface ownership across multiple tape-outs and production server deployments.
* Deep experience with BMC and board-management interfaces and protocols, such as I3C/I2C, MCTP/PLDM, UART, SPI, and PCIe sideband.
* Extensive knowledge of reset sequencing, secure and BMC-assisted boot, early DRAM and PCIe initialization, server-grade RAS, and system-wide power management.
* Strong architectural communication and cross-functional leadership skills; familiarity with RISC-V server standards, chiplet/SiP interconnects, or server firmware and OS stacks is a plus.

**What You Will Learn**

* How Tenstorrent defines and integrates SoC architecture for next-generation RISC-V server CPUs and chiplet-based compute platforms.
* How SoC-level decisions shape server boot, management, RAS, power behavior, and platform integration.
* How to balance silicon requirements with chassis, thermal, power delivery, and rack-level constraints.
* How CPU, fabric, memory, I/O, firmware, and platform teams collaborate through architecture, bring-up, and validation.
* How emerging RISC-V server standards and chiplet technologies influence future server SoC and platform design.
