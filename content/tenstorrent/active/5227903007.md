Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

We’re looking for a Senior Accounts Payable Analyst to drive accurate, efficient AP operations and solve complex finance challenges. You’ll own AP quality, reconciliations, payments, and month-end activities while partnering across Finance and Procurement. Help us build smarter, scalable AP processes through automation, ERP, and AI-enabled workflows.

This role is hybrid, based out of Bangalore, India.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* 4+ years of Accounts Payable experience, ideally in a shared services/GCC environment, with strong exposure to PO/non-PO invoices, vendor reconciliations, AP aging and payment support.
* B.Com/BBA/BBM Finance or equivalent; M.Com, MBA Finance, CA Inter or CMA Inter is a plus.
* Strong attention to detail, ownership, communication skills and a solid understanding of AP controls, GST/TDS, payment terms and audit requirements.

**What We Need**

* Own day-to-day AP quality control, review invoices and coding, resolve complex exceptions, blocked invoices, PO mismatches and vendor account discrepancies.
* Support payment proposals, AP aging, vendor reconciliations and month-end close while maintaining strong maker-checker and segregation-of-duties controls.
* Hands-on experience with QBO and SAP is preferred, along with exposure to ERP implementation/migration, UAT or process improvement.
* Experience with AI-enabled/Agentic AI workflow tools to improve AP efficiency and exception handling is a strong plus.

**What You Will Learn**

* Gain exposure to a global Finance Centre of Excellence and end-to-end AP operations across QBO and SAP.
* Work closely with Finance, Procurement, GL, Treasury, business teams and vendors while building expertise in AP controls, automation and process optimization.
* Help shape scalable, technology-driven finance processes in a fast-growing organization.
