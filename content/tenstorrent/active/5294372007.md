Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

You will join the AI Software Operators team, which designs, develops, and optimizes the key operations used by every AI model running on Tenstorrent hardware. You will work closely with the models, runtime, and hardware teams to bring up and accelerate the latest text, image, and video generation models.

This role ishybrid, based out of Gdańsk or Warsaw, Poland.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* A software engineer with a strong background in low-level software development in C++ or C, a focus on performance optimization, and an understanding of CPU, GPU, or accelerator architecture.
* A creative problem solver who uses agentic AI to automate software development as well as to research, evaluate, and productize their ideas.
* Comfortable with numerical analysis, floating-point arithmetic, and accuracy across different data formats.
* A person motivated by curiosity who wants to keep learning and is interested in how the latest LLMs, Video and ImageGen models work from a computational perspective.
* A collaborative teammate who enjoys working with innovative people and sharing what they learn.

**What We Need**

* Engineers who can design, implement, and optimize high-performance, numerically accurate operations for the latest LLMs and other text, image, and video models on Tenstorrent hardware.
* Ownership of debugging precision issues and building the tests and tooling that catch them early.
* Engineers who can design agentic workflows based on cutting-edge models to generate, test, and tune our operations, and who can define metrics and processes for evaluating agentic code generation.
* Engineers who deliver measurable performance improvements through benchmarking and profiling, creating value for our customers, for example, through the models we serve on OpenRouter.
* Close collaboration with the models, runtime, and hardware teams, including software/hardware co-design on future hardware generations.

**What You Will Learn**

* How to make the latest text, image, and video AI models efficient and fast on Tenstorrent hardware through algorithmic and architecture-specific optimizations.
* How to use agents in real engineering work, and how to help invent what AI-native engineering looks like.
* How software and hardware co-evolve, through software/hardware co-design on current and future Tenstorrent hardware.
* How to do creative, high-impact work in the open: everything is open source, so you can always show what you are working on.
