Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

Tenstorrent is seeking an EDA CAD Infrastructure Engineer to manage and optimize the high-performance compute, storage, and licensing systems that power large-scale semiconductor design workloads. This engineer will maintain and scale batch-scheduling clusters, build automation and monitoring tools, and partner with physical design, verification, IT, and EDA vendors to improve infrastructure efficiency, reliability, and availability.

This role is **hybrid**, based out of **Santa Clara, CA** or **Austin, TX** or **Fort Collins, CO.**

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who you are**

* You have at least five years of experience in EDA CAD infrastructure or HPC management for semiconductor teams.
* You bring strong Linux, scripting, and infrastructure-management skills.
* You take initiative and can independently troubleshoot compute-farm or license outages.
* You communicate clearly and collaborate with design, verification, IT, and vendor teams.

**What we need**

* Manage Slurm, LSF, or similar schedulers and optimize compute, memory, storage, and EDA flows for large-scale workloads.
* Operate EDA license systems and vendor relationships, including provisioning, utilization reporting, and issue resolution.
* Build Python, Bash, or Tcl automation and monitoring; track system health, KPIs, capacity, and upcoming bottlenecks.
* Maintain secure, highly available Linux and storage infrastructure, including NFS/NAS, backups, access controls, and tool-version regression environments; bring a relevant BS or equivalent experience.

**What you will learn**

* How compute, storage, and licensing affect productivity across semiconductor EDA workflows.
* How physical design and verification teams use shared infrastructure and where optimization has the greatest impact.
* How to forecast infrastructure needs using operational metrics, engineering demand, and capacity trends.
* How to improve enterprise infrastructure reliability, security, and readiness for new EDA tools.
