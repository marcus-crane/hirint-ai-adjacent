Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

We are looking for a Principal SoC Platform Architect to own the architecture that ties our RISC-V CPUs, System IP, and SoCs together into platforms we and our customers can ship. Our CPU and System IP architects own the architecture of their blocks, and our engineering teams own microarchitecture, RTL, and verification. You’ll define the CPU subsystems built from those blocks, set what the platform needs from each of them, and own how it all comes together. This role is ideal for senior architects who have taken an SoC from definition through production silicon and who thrive at the intersection of subsystem definition, system-level requirements, IP sign-off, and customer-facing reference designs.

This role is based in Austin, TX, or Santa Clara, CA.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* A senior SoC architect who defined the top-level architecture of at least one SoC that reached production silicon and stayed with it through bring-up.
* Experienced (10+ years) in memory systems, coherent and non-coherent interconnects (CHI, AXI), interrupts, IOMMU, debug, clock/reset/power, boot, and security, and deep in at least two of: coherent interconnect and QoS, IOMMU and interrupts, power management, security and boot, debug and trace, or functional safety.
* Practiced at setting requirements for IP teams you don’t manage and holding them to those requirements at sign-off.
* A clear spec writer who makes data-driven trade-offs and leads through influence across multi-site, multi-timezone teams.
* Experience defining CPU subsystems, RISC-V platform specs (AIA, IOMMU, RPMI, server platform) or Arm equivalents (SBSA, GIC, SMMU, SCMI), ISO 26262, or chiplets and UCIe is a plus.

**What We Need**

* Own the platform architecture: the system-level contracts (address map, coherency, QoS, interrupts and IOMMU, debug, power, boot, security, RAS, safety) that every CPU subsystem, System IP block, and SoC must honor.
* Set platform requirements for each System IP block and sign off that IP specs meet them at interface freeze, driving fixes at the source when pieces don’t fit.
* Define our CPU subsystems: what goes in each, their boundaries and interfaces, and their supported configurations, from compact, functionally safe systems to high-core-count ones. Finalize the specs with the subsystem engineering team that builds them.
* Own the SoC spec for test chips, reference platforms, and production SoCs through tapeout, bring-up, and production, ensuring RISC-V platform standards conformance.
* Produce customer-facing reference architectures and integration guides, and work directly with customers’ SoC architects.

**What You Will Learn**

* How a full platform comes together, from CPU subsystem to System IP to silicon, when the CPU, System IP, and subsystems are all built in-house.
* How your architecture lands in silicon, from fast-turn test chips to production SoCs.
* How customers adopt licensable IP and subsystems, and how your reference designs become the starting point for their RISC-V SoCs.
* How next-generation heterogeneous SoCs with RISC-V CPUs take shape.
* How a senior, deeply technical team makes architecture decisions quickly across design, DV, physical, firmware, and software.
