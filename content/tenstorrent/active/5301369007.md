Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

Tenstorrent is hiring a Senior Data Security Engineer to lead data security posture management (DSPM), data loss prevention (DLP), AI data governance, and technical export-control enforcement across our SaaS estate, cloud, AI pipelines, and silicon engineering filesystems. As a senior member of the Operations Security (OpSec) team reporting to the Head of Information Security, you will protect our RISC-V and AI silicon IP, customer-confidential programs, and export-controlled data without slowing engineering down. This is a hands-on role for a data security engineer who builds their own tooling.

This role is remote, based out of the United States or Canada.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* A DSPM/DLP specialist across Google Workspace, Slack, Atlassian, GitHub/GitLab, and AWS who writes code to fill the gaps off-the-shelf tools leave.
* AI-native: you use coding agents and LLMs to build tools and reports, and you design the guardrails that keep credentials and IP out of unauthorized models.
* Able to turn export-control rules (EAR, CMMC) and customer confidentiality agreements into enforceable identity groups and access controls.
* Pragmatic and high-signal, favoring high-confidence controls over noisy rules that frustrate engineers.

**What We Need**

* Deep experience designing and running DSPM and DLP controls across Google Workspace, Slack, Atlassian, and AWS to stop unauthorized sharing and exfiltration.
* Strong grasp of AI security posture management: governing what data flows into AI tools, inference providers, and autonomous agents.
* Programming and automation skills (Python, TypeScript, or Go, plus Terraform and Docker) to build classification and permission-reconciliation pipelines.
* Experience with networked filesystems (NFS, SMB, AD/POSIX ACLs) and translating export-control boundaries into identity pipelines through Entra ID and Active Directory.
* Experience securing backups (immutability, customer-managed encryption) and running live disaster recovery drills with SRE.

**What You Will Learn**

* How to architect export-control data boundaries and confidential project enclaves for RISC-V processors and AI accelerators.
* How to build AI-native DLP and data classification pipelines across agent workflows and LLM gateways.
* How to lead end-to-end disaster recovery validation across source control, PKI, virtualization clusters, and endpoint fleets.
* How to turn complex permission and data-posture audits into repeatable automation using Tenstorrent's internal AI tooling.

*Compensation for all engineers at Tenstorrent ranges from $100k - $500k including base and variable compensation targets. Experience, skills, education, background and location all impact the actual offer made.*
