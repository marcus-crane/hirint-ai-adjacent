Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

Help bring the next generation of silicon to life. On the Silicon Enablement Team, you’ll work across architecture, design, verification, firmware, systems, and product to turn ambitious silicon designs into successful products - from early planning and tapeout through board and system bring-up and ongoing support. You’ll build firmware, enablement tests, and reusable engineering infrastructure; investigate real-world failures with automation, instrumentation, and system-level debugging; and translate what you learn into actionable production solutions. If you thrive at the intersection of hardware and software and want to see your work shape silicon into real products, this is your opportunity to take ownership of complex, hands-on challenges with impact across the full silicon lifecycle.

This role ishybrid, based out of Vancouver, Canada or Toronto, Canada.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* You have 3+ years of relevant experience in embedded systems, firmware, systems software, hardware/software integration, or a related field, with a degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or equivalent practical experience.
* You develop and debug embedded software or firmware in environments such as bare-metal systems, RTOS, Linux, or device drivers, and are proficient in C/C++ and Python, with working knowledge of shell scripting.
* You understand computer and SoC architecture - including buses, memory systems, clocks, resets, and hardware interfaces - and can connect those fundamentals to observed system behavior.
* You have hands-on hardware bring-up and system-debugging experience using tools such as JTAG, UART, oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, or protocol analyzers; you also build tests, automation, and regression coverage.
* You work methodically through unfamiliar problems, use data to find root causes, and communicate findings while collaborating across hardware and software teams. Experience with pre-silicon platforms, interfaces such as PCIe or DDR, chiplets, RISC-V firmware, or shared test infrastructure is a plus.

**What We Need**

* Develop and execute silicon enablement tasks for IP, subsystems, SoCs, and SiPs, covering feature behavior, system integration, and product-level workloads.
* Design and maintain firmware, test software, scripts, utilities, and reusable enablement infrastructure using C/C++ and Python.
* Define and evolve enablement plans from feature requirements, architecture documents, and product use cases; identify coverage gaps and build tests to address them.
* Develop firmware and host software on simulation, emulation, or FPGA platforms and carry it forward into post-silicon bring-up and system operation.
* Bring up and debug hardware, investigate failures to actionable root causes, and work across design, verification, firmware, systems, and infrastructure teams to resolve issues and document results.

**What You Will Learn**

* Work across the silicon lifecycle, from pre-silicon planning and tapeout through board and system bring-up and ongoing product support.
* Build a broad understanding of how architecture, design, verification, firmware, systems, and product engineering come together to deliver first-silicon success.
* Use firmware, automation, instrumentation, and system-level debugging to understand silicon behavior in real products and turn findings into better designs.
* Contribute to reusable tools and capabilities that enable high-performance computing and support Tenstorrent’s vision for more open computing.
