Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

This role sits at the heart of Tenstorrent’s Systems Engineering team, supporting board bring-up and lab activities across our Toronto office and Markham Data Center. Working alongside experienced engineers, you’ll bring up, test, debug, and characterize our cutting-edge machine learning (ML) processors, boards, and systems. You’ll be hands-on with system assembly, test setup validation, PCB-level troubleshooting, precision soldering and component rework, inventory management, and day-to-day lab operations that keep our hardware moving from prototype to production.

This role ison-site, based out of Toronto, Canada.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* A hands-on electronics professional who enjoys bringing up, testing, debugging, and characterizing complex boards and systems.
* Skilled in precision soldering, desoldering, and PCB rework, including fine-pitch ICs, surface-mount capacitors, resistors, and other small components.
* Knowledgeable in computer hardware, electronic components, system assembly, and Linux, with strong analytical and troubleshooting skills in both hardware and software environments.
* Highly organized and detail-oriented, able to manage multiple tasks, track inventory, and maintain accurate records.
* A clear communicator who collaborates effectively with engineers and documents work thoroughly.

**What We Need**

* Collaborate with senior engineers to bring up, debug, and characterize ML processors, boards, and systems, and execute test plans for chip and board qualification.
* Assemble, validate, and maintain test systems and setups, using electronics lab equipment to support board bring-up, diagnostics, and hardware validation.
* Perform precision soldering, desoldering, and PCB rework on fine-pitch ICs and surface-mount components, along with component-level troubleshooting, inspection, and repair of boards and assemblies.
* Install, configure, and maintain operating systems and test software, including Linux installation, configuration, and troubleshooting.
* Support lab and data center operations, including system deployment, hardware installation, structured cabling, and equipment maintenance, with regular travel between the Toronto office and Markham Data Center.
* Manage lab inventory, maintain technical documentation and test procedures, uphold safety standards, and perform physical tasks including lifting up to 50 pounds.

**What You Will Learn**

* Gain hands-on experience bringing up and characterizing cutting-edge ML processors, boards, and systems from prototype development through production.
* Deepen your expertise in PCB-level debugging, precision rework, and hardware validation using professional electronics lab equipment and test tools.
* Build data center and infrastructure skills, from system deployment and structured cabling to Linux and test software configuration.
* Work alongside cross-functional engineering teams and gain exposure to the technologies powering next-generation AI computing systems.
