Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

We are looking for a Technical Program Manager to lead the integrated plan for taking new AI silicon from pre-silicon readiness through post-silicon bring-up and validation, while coordinating firmware, low-level drivers, and system software delivered on Tenstorrent products. This role connects silicon enablement and system software teams with engineering, manufacturing, operations, and external partners to ensure readiness is measurable, risks are surfaced early, and critical dependencies stay on schedule.

This role ishybrid, based out of Toronto, Canada or Vancouver, Canada.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* Bachelor’s degree or higher in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field, with 4+ years of hands-on technical experience in silicon validation, firmware, embedded software, high-speed interfaces (e.g., PCIe, Ethernet, or memory), or test infrastructure, and 3+ years leading technical programs.
* Strong understanding of the full silicon enablement lifecycle—from emulation and pre-silicon test development through first power-on, interface bring-up, and validation sign-off.
* Comfort working across hardware and software boundaries, spanning SoC and IP validation, embedded firmware, kernel drivers, host tools, lab/CI infrastructure, ATE screening, and manufacturing test flows.
* Proactive communicator who makes program health and risks transparent, driving alignment and influencing cross-functional teams without direct authority while keeping structure amidst shifting schedules.
* Curious and adaptable mindset with practical experience in areas such as multi-die/chiplet bring-up, FPGA/emulation, Linux/RTOS firmware, Python automation, CI pipelines, or hardware debug interfaces (JTAG/MDIO).

**What We Need**

* Own the integrated bring-up and validation plan across silicon enablement and system software teams, AI Systems, other Tenstorrent teams, and external partners; define clear dependencies, owners, readiness gates, and dates.
* Track pre-silicon readiness and post-silicon execution against targets, publish evidence at each gate, and reconcile planned test coverage with what runs on silicon.
* Coordinate lab software baselines and shipping release trains across system platforms—aligning firmware, BMC/DMC images, low-level APIs, kernel drivers, and host tools into validated, compatible bundles, while routing field issues back into release scope.
* Plan lab capacity and hardware allocation, track inbound boards, systems, samples, and debug equipment, and partner with manufacturing and operations to move validation tools into production test and provisioning flows.
* Lead bring-up cadence, live-debug triage, cross-functional issue resolution, and vendor dependencies; maintain program plans and dashboards, and turn postmortems and test/yield findings into reusable improvements.

**What You Will Learn**

* How AI silicon moves from emulation to working, validated hardware and what it takes to make first silicon succeed.
* How validation content, firmware, drivers, tools, lab infrastructure, hardware, and manufacturing tests fit together on one critical path.
* How to measure readiness with concrete evidence, manage changing dependencies, and carry lessons from one chip program into the next.
* How to lead through ambiguity and build trust across hardware and software teams to help engineers solve complex problems faster.
