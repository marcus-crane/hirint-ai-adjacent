Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, and our Legal team supports that work at every turn: commercial deals, global partnerships, financings and IP. We are looking for a Senior Manager, Legal Operations to build the engine behind it. You will own the tools, workflows and data that let a lean legal team operate like a much larger one, so our attorneys can focus on the judgment calls that matter most. This is a builder's role with real room to shape how Legal works as we grow.

This role is hybrid, based out of Santa Clara, CA, Austin, TX, Boston, MA or Toronto, ON.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* 8+ years of experience in operations, including hands-on legal operations, with a track record of building, implementing, improving and scaling legal processes and systems in a fast-paced environment.
* You’ve owned implementing, migrating, and managing legal technology platforms, including contract lifecycle management (CLM), matter management, workflow automation, and AI-enabled tools.
* Hands on experience with AI implementation within a in-house legal department.
* Strong understanding of commercial contract management, including NDAs, MSAs, amendments, and the development of templates, playbooks, and standardized workflows that support efficient contract execution.
* You are skilled in developing KPIs and tracking how legal teams spend their time.

**What We Need**

* The ability to build and Scale Legal Operations by developing and continuously improving the systems, workflows, and processes that enable Tenstorrent's Legal team to operate efficiently as the company grows.
* Lead the implementation, and ongoing management of CLM, matter management, and AI-enabled tools, including building automated workflows and deploying multi-agent systems to improve legal efficiency.
* Oversee the full contract lifecycle, from intake and review through execution and renewal, while developing scalable templates, playbooks, and self-service processes.
* Oversee outside counsel and legal vendors, and establish KPIs and dashboards to measure efficiency, spending, and operational performance.
* Collaborate with Finance, IT, Procurement, Sales, and other teams to integrate legal processes into business operations, drive adoption of legal technology, and support company-wide initiatives.

**What You Will Learn**

* How to build and scale legal operations within a high-growth semiconductor and AI technology company.
* How legal teams partner with Finance, Procurement, Sales, Security, IT, People, Engineering, and Operations across a global organization.
* How to use data, automation, and technology to improve legal team performance, prioritization, and stakeholder experience.
* How to create operating models that enable the legal team to support business growth with greater consistency, speed, and scalability.
