Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

Tenstorrent is leading the industry in cutting-edge AI technology, rethinking performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency across the full stack—from software models and compilers to networking and semiconductors. Our team is growing, and we are looking for a supply chain transformation leader to own the business processes and workflows that run our supply chain.

As **Senior Manager, Supply Chain Business Transformation**, you will lead the business systems team within Supply Chain and own the business processes and change management for new supply chain initiatives, such as new warehouses, manufacturing lines, 3PLs, contract manufacturers, and TMS providers. You will also own governance of the supply chain business applications, including Arena PLM and NetSuite ERP, and partner with IT, which owns system implementation. This is a highly cross-functional role that blends supply chain domain expertise, process design, and change management across procurement, planning, logistics, and returns. We welcome candidates across a range of seniorities; leveling will be determined during the interview process.

This role is hybrid, based out of Toronto, ON.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* 8+ years in supply chain operations or business transformation, with 3+ years leading teams or major initiatives; experienced implementing enterprise systems and partnering with IT on supply chain roadmaps and process solutions.
* Deep knowledge of procurement, MRP, planning, inventory, logistics, transfers, and RMA, with the ability to translate business needs into system requirements and workflows.
* Strategic and analytical leader who prioritizes cross-functional initiatives, builds multi-quarter roadmaps, drives KPI-based improvements, and uses data to guide decisions.
* Skilled in project leadership and change management, with clear communication across Finance, Engineering, IT, suppliers, and executives; comfortable in fast-paced, ambiguous environments.
* Curious and forward-thinking, with the ability to define and execute AI strategies and use agentic workflows to transform supply chain operations.

**What We Need**

* Experience leading or developing a small team of business process or business systems analysts.
* Hands-on experience governing and configuring supply chain applications such as ERP, SCM, TMS, or WMS platforms; familiarity with NetSuite, Arena PLM, BOMs, routings, and change management is valuable.
* Proven ability to partner with Finance and IT on system implementations, from requirements and process design through testing, cutover, and adoption.
* Strong understanding of supply chain workflows across procurement, MRP, planning, logistics, transfers, inventory, and RMA, including related systems and reporting needs.
* Experience prioritizing systems roadmaps and backlogs, managing stakeholders, and identifying opportunities for AI or automation.

**What You Will Learn**

* How to build and scale a resilient supply chain systems function across ERP, PLM, planning, TMS, WMS, and BI solutions.
* How to design scalable, cross-functional processes and systems for a fast-growing AI hardware company.
* How AI and agentic workflows can transform procurement, planning, logistics, and returns.
* How clean data and well-designed workflows can influence Engineering, Finance, and Operations strategy.
* How to support multiple product lines and global contract manufacturers as Tenstorrent scales.
