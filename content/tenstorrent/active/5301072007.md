Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

We are seeking an experienced and detail-driven Payroll Specialist to join our growing global payroll team. In this role, you will own the end-to-end payroll process for our Canadian subsidiary, supporting approximately 250 employees across the organization.

You will be the subject matter expert for Canadian payroll compliance, working collaboratively with global payroll colleagues, HR, Finance, and external vendors to ensure employees are paid accurately and on time, every cycle. This is a high-ownership role with the opportunity to shape and improve our Canadian payroll function as the company scales.

This role ishybrid, based out of Toronto, Canada.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* Experienced payroll professional with strong knowledge of Canadian payroll and multi-provincial requirements.
* Detail-oriented and highly organized, with a focus on accuracy and compliance.
* Trusted partner who handles sensitive information with discretion and professionalism.
* Comfortable working both independently and cross-functionally in a fast-paced environment.

**What We Need**

* Ownership of full-cycle Canadian payroll processing, including variable pay, employee lifecycle changes, and off-cycle runs.
* Management of statutory deductions, CRA remittances, year-end filings, and regulatory compliance.
* Administration of benefits, garnishments, and payroll-related deductions in partnership with HR and vendors.
* Execution of payroll reconciliations, reporting, and support for month-end and year-end close activities.

**What You Will Learn**

* How payroll operates within a global, high-growth technology company.
* How to build scalable payroll processes across multiple provinces and systems.
* How payroll data connects to financial reporting, compliance, and broader business operations.
* How to partner across HR, Finance, and global teams to drive consistency and best practices.
