Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

Tenstorrent is building next-generation high-performance RISC-V server CPUs and chiplet-based AI compute platforms. We’re looking for a Server CPU Platform Architect to own the server platform and system architecture surrounding our silicon, defining how our CPUs and heterogeneous AI compute nodes operate at datacenter scale. You’ll drive platform hardware specifications across power delivery, thermal management, memory and high-speed I/O topologies, manageability, and security. Working closely with CPU SoC, silicon, packaging, firmware, and board teams—as well as external OEM, ODM, and cloud partners. You’ll help bring next-generation compute platforms from architecture definition through hardware bring-up, validation, and deployment.

This role ishybrid, based out of Santa Clara, CA or Austin, TX

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* You’re a senior server platform architect with 10+ years of experience designing and delivering x86, Arm, or comparable server systems.
* You have end-to-end experience architecting server CPU platforms, from system requirements and hardware specifications through bring-up and validation.
* You understand how power delivery, thermal design, memory, high-speed I/O, and platform management interact to shape system performance and reliability.
* You’re comfortable working across silicon, firmware, board design, SI/PI, thermal, mechanical, and external partner teams.
* You’re a strong technical leader who can define platform direction, communicate complex tradeoffs, and guide internal teams and external partners toward decisions.

**What We Need**

* Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field; PhD is a plus.
* 10+ years of experience in server system architecture, motherboard design, or datacenter hardware engineering, with a track record of delivering server CPU platforms.
* Deep understanding of server and SoC architectures, AI compute systems, multi-socket or multi-node topologies, and enterprise-grade hardware design.
* Expert knowledge of datacenter interfaces and memory technologies, including PCIe, CXL, Ethernet, DDR, and LPDDR, plus experience guiding SI/PI teams and understanding PCB/backplane constraints.
* Strong knowledge of server management, security, and RAS- including BMC/OpenBMC, IPMI, Redfish, MCTP/PLDM, NC-SI, secure boot, and system-level error handling. Experience with OCP specifications, hyperscaler power/cooling architectures, or chiplet-based SiP integration is a plus.

**What You Will Learn**

* How Tenstorrent’s RISC-V server CPUs and heterogeneous AI compute platforms are integrated into complete datacenter systems.
* How platform-level decisions across power, cooling, memory, and I/O affect performance, efficiency, reliability, and scalability.
* How to co-design server platforms with silicon, packaging, firmware, and board-level architecture teams.
* How to work with leading OEMs, ODMs, and cloud service providers to move reference platforms from architecture through deployment.
* How emerging OCP designs, hyperscaler infrastructure, and chiplet-based systems are shaping next-generation server platforms.
