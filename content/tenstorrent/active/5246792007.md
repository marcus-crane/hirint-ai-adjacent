Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

As a Datacenter Liquid Cooling Architect, you will define, design, and architect next-generation liquid cooling infrastructure for Tenstorrent’s large-scale AI training and inference clusters. You will partner with systems engineering, mechanical engineering, software, and cross-functional design teams to develop chassis-, rack-, and cluster-scale cooling solutions, including CDU integration, telemetry and control, leak detection, and resilient operating strategies. This role will help shape reliable AI datacenter architectures and deployments for both internal and external customers.

This role ison-site, based out of Toronto, Canada, Austin, Texas or Santa Clara, California.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* A datacenter and system thermal design professional with 10+ years of experience architecting cooling infrastructure for complex computing environments.
* An experienced liquid cooling architect who can design chassis- and rack-scale solutions for large AI training and inference clusters.
* A systems thinker who understands how mechanical, electrical, software, facility, and systems engineering decisions come together in mission-critical datacenter environments.
* A hands-on problem solver with experience in fluid compatibility, pressure testing, leak detection, sensor integration, and cooling-system controls.
* A collaborative technical leader who can work effectively with cross-functional teams and internal and external customers.

**What We Need**

* Strong knowledge of ASHRAE thermal guidelines, liquid-cooled IT environments, facility HVAC integration, and industry best practices for datacenter cooling.
* Proven experience designing and operating single-phase liquid cooling systems, including fluid selection, glycol-water mixtures, dielectric fluids, corrosion inhibitors, material compatibility, and dry-break couplings.
* Expertise in pressure-testing protocols, including proof testing, burst testing, and leak testing, as well as leak detection, mitigation, filtration, redundancy, and resilient cooling architectures.
* Practical experience integrating flow, temperature, and pressure sensors with monitoring and control systems, including defining operational rules and failure-response procedures for commercial CDUs. Experience with pumped two-phase liquid cooling is a plus.

**What You Will Learn**

* Hands-on exposure to architecting next-generation liquid cooling infrastructure for Tenstorrent’s AI training and inference systems at chassis, rack, and cluster scale.
* A deeper understanding of how commercial coolant distribution units are integrated, monitored, and controlled within high-performance AI datacenter deployments.
* The opportunity to establish safe operating rules, telemetry requirements, leak-response strategies, and resilient failure modes for mission-critical cooling systems.
* Experience shaping end-to-end AI cluster architectures and deployment solutions for both internal and external customers.
* The ability to influence future datacenter thermal systems through close partnership with systems engineering, mechanical engineering, software, and other cross-functional teams.
