Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

We are seeking a Technical Program Manager to lead programs that bring AI compute systems from engineering into reliable production and deployment. In this role, you will support the AI Systems organization, the teams responsible for system validation, qualification, reliability, performance and power, debug, certification, lab infrastructure, and customer deployment of Tenstorrent's PCIe cards and multi-chip Galaxy server systems. You will own the integrated plan that turns engineering readiness into production readiness: aligning priorities, milestones, and dependencies across AI Systems, hardware, firmware and software, supply chain, manufacturing operations, contract manufacturers, and data center and customer teams. Working closely with engineers and leaders across these groups, you will help deliver systems that are qualified, manufacturable, and stable in the field at scale.

This role ishybrid, based out of Toronto, Canada.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* You’re a technically fluent program leader with experience driving complex hardware or systems programs in fast-moving engineering environments. You bring a bachelor’s degree or higher in electrical, computer, mechanical, or a related engineering field, at least four years of experience in hardware design, validation, test, or manufacturing, and three or more years leading technical projects or programs.
* You’re comfortable working with engineers across system-level hardware, including validation, reliability and qualification testing, power and thermal, firmware, and failure analysis. Experience with contract manufacturers, ODMs, NPI, production builds, and release processes is important.
* You build alignment across distributed teams and organizational boundaries, including external partners, and can balance competing needs across engineering, manufacturing, supply chain, operations, and customer-facing teams.
* You’re adaptable and curious, balancing urgent fleet and customer issues with longer-term reliability and roadmap investments. You use AI tools responsibly to accelerate planning, analysis, status reporting, and documentation. Mandarin proficiency is a plus, but not required.

**What We Need**

* A program leader to define, maintain, and drive the AI Systems roadmap with engineering and product leaders, aligning system validation, qualification, and reliability plans with hardware, firmware, software, manufacturing, and product roadmaps.
* The ability to coordinate production release readiness with contract manufacturers and manufacturing operations, including firmware and configuration baselines, engineering changes, build readiness, and clear-to-build statements.
* Strong execution across multiple programs: identifying and mitigating technical and schedule risks, resolving dependencies, adapting to changing priorities, and influencing teams without direct authority.
* A leader who can run cross-functional tiger teams on fleet stability and reliability, establishing workstreams, owners, measurable targets, timely decisions, and clear closure.
* Clear operating rhythms and communication: program reviews, dependency tracking, decision forums, issue triage, and concise updates that help distributed teams stay aligned. You use test results, failure data, and fleet signals to prioritize issues and measure progress.

**What You Will Learn**

* How to own AI Systems programs end to end, from setting priorities and sequencing work to aligning milestones and communicating progress across the organization.
* How validation, qualification, reliability, certification, firmware, and documentation gates come together to support engineering changes and production release readiness.
* How to lead fleet and customer issue resolution with debug, data center operations, firmware, customer, engineering, and manufacturing teams—and improve the processes and tools for tracking issues across labs, manufacturing, and deployed systems.
* How high-performance AI systems move from engineering builds into manufacturable, supportable products, and how engineering decisions affect manufacturing, supply chain, and field reliability.
