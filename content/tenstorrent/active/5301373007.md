Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

Tenstorrent is hiring a Senior Identity Management Engineer to architect, build, and run the identity, secrets, and privileged access systems that secure our global workforce, engineering infrastructure, cloud services, and autonomous AI agents. As part of the Operations Security (OpSec) team, you will replace static credentials, over-scoped tokens, and manual ticket queues with automated, self-service paths, partnering with IT, DevInfra, Cloud, and Software Engineering. This is a hands-on role for an identity engineer who also writes software.

This role is remote, based out of the United States or Canada.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* Equal parts enterprise identity architect and software builder, comfortable administering Entra ID, Active Directory, and Vault and writing the CLIs, APIs, and IaC that automate them.
* AI-native: you use coding agents to build internal tools, MCP servers, and audit reports.
* A pragmatic partner who replaces manual gatekeeping with self-service and light-touch approval workflows.
* Ownership-minded and analytical, able to anticipate cross-team impact and respond decisively to identity threats.

**What We Need**

* Deep experience running Microsoft Entra ID and on-premises Active Directory, including SAML/OIDC, SCIM, Conditional Access, and phishing-resistant MFA.
* Production experience with HashiCorp Vault (or equivalent), including workload federation, dynamic short-lived credentials, and removing static secrets from CI/CD.
* Experience rolling out privileged access management and just-in-time elevation across cloud admin roles and Linux SSH/sudo.
* Programming skills (Python, TypeScript, Go, or Rust, plus Terraform and Docker) with a track record of building working tools instead of manual ClickOps.
* Experience with access reviews, identity threat detection and response (such as CrowdStrike Falcon Identity Protection), and auditing service accounts and tokens.

**What You Will Learn**

* How to build an agentic identity platform that governs credentials and lifecycle for autonomous AI agents.
* How to design host-level Linux PAM and just-in-time access across workstations, cloud, and high-performance EDA clusters.
* How to run code-backed, AI-assisted access audits that produce SOC 2 Type 2 evidence.
* How to publish security skills so engineering AI agents adopt Vault, OIDC, and least-privilege standards by default.
