Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

Tenstorrent builds open, scalable AI compute, and Europe is a priority market for revenue growth. As BD Executive, Europe, you will own business development across the UK, Germany, France and other EU countries, deepening existing engagements and opening new ones with enterprise and government clients and system integrators. You will work with the International GTM team, partners and customers to turn technical interest in our hardware into commercial opportunities. This is a senior role on a team that may grow as the region does.

This role is remote, based out of London, Frankfurt, or another major EU city with required travel.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* Sales or business development leader with 10+ years of experience in solution selling for AI hardware, HPC or similar technology.
* Trusted by government and enterprise clients and system integrators, with strong relationships you can bring into new conversations.
* Proven in building pipeline and closing commercial opportunities, from first meeting through signed deal.
* Fluent in English, with German, French or another EU language as a plus.

**What We Need**

* Build and manage pipeline across the UK, Germany, France and other EU countries, growing existing engagements and opening new ones.
* Own client relationships with enterprise, government and system integrator accounts, coordinating several stakeholders per initiative.
* Work across borders with GTM, partner and technical teams so each opportunity has the right support to close.
* Report on region pipeline and market trends so the team can focus on the deals most likely to land.

**What You Will Learn**

* How Tenstorrent's open, scalable AI hardware fits enterprise, government and sovereign compute needs in Europe.
* Ways to build a regional business at a fast-moving AI compute company, with room for the team to grow.
* Technical depth on AI systems and software, with training from our GTM and field engineers.
* Where AI infrastructure demand is heading across the European market, from data center to cloud.
