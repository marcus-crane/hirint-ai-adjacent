Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

As a Senior Specialist, Global Sourcing (Indirect), you will own day-to-day procurement for the indirect categories that keep Tenstorrent's engineering, data center, and operations teams running, from RFQ through delivery. You'll source everything from data center networking and storage to AI compute systems, prototype hardware, and enterprise software, freeing our Global Sourcing lead to focus on strategic sourcing as our Galaxy deployments scale.

This role is hybrid, based out of Toronto, Canada.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* 5+ years in procurement or strategic sourcing for AI compute, semiconductor, or data center/IT infrastructure, owning purchases from RFQ through delivery.
* Hands-on with data center networking and storage, AI compute systems, engineering hardware, and enterprise software licensing.
* A confident negotiator who drives savings and holds suppliers accountable on performance and escalations.
* Fluent in ERP systems such as NetSuite, SAP, or Oracle, plus spreadsheets and reporting tools like Tableau.
* A clear, self-directed communicator who prioritizes well and influences Engineering, Planning, Finance, and suppliers.

**What We Need**

* Source data center infrastructure, from servers, storage, and switches to optics, cabling, and cabinets, while managing qualification, lead times, and supply risk.
* Run RFQs, weigh quotes on total cost, lead time, and terms, negotiate pricing, and issue POs in ERP per policy.
* Buy AI compute, prototype components, and enterprise software and licenses, including supplier onboarding, renewals, and RMAs.
* Track open orders, expedite deliveries, and own urgent escalations and unplanned purchases through resolution.
* Back up direct component procurement for our Galaxy systems, support ISO audit prep, and improve procurement processes.

**What You Will Learn**

* How AI compute hardware moves from engineering prototype to rack-scale deployment.
* How a fast-scaling AI hardware company builds and runs its indirect sourcing function.
* Larger contract negotiation and cost-reduction strategy, working alongside the Global Sourcing lead.
* How Engineering, Planning, Finance, and IT align demand behind major product milestones.
