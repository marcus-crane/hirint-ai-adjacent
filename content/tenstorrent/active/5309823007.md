Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

At Tenstorrent, we are pushing the boundaries of AI/ML hardware platforms and blazing trails with next-gen high-speed networking that powers our performance and scalability. We are looking for a passionate engineer to join our Ethernet enablement team, helping bring up and validate our Ethernet PHY, MAC and controller IP.

This role ishybrid, based out of Vancouver, Canada.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* Passionate about low-level software that brings hardware to life, seeing it run on real systems never gets old.
* Drawn to complex, cross-disciplinary problems at the intersection of hardware and software.
* Curious about IEEE Ethernet standards and high-speed networking challenges like Serdes tuning and link training.
* Have pride in hands-on debugging and working across teams to make systems fast, reliable, and ready for the real world.

**What We Need**

* Solid fundamentals in embedded or low-level C/C++, such as bare-metal firmware, device drivers, or hardware register programming.
* A strong grasp of computer architecture basics, including buses, memory-mapped registers, clocks and resets.
* A methodical approach to debugging, the ability to read logs and register states, form a hypothesis, and communicate findings clearly.
* Hands-on exposure to lab equipment (oscilloscopes, BERTs, or protocol analyzers) or to emulation or FPGA platforms, is a plus.

**What You Will Learn**

* What it means to shape AI acceleration hardware from the ground up, starting at first principles.
* How to enable complex Ethernet IP on silicon from emulation and initial bring-up through validation and production deployment.
* What it takes to partner across world-class silicon, systems, and software teams on frontier engineering challenges.
* How to build and scale AI datacenter networking fabrics that power frontier AI acceleration platforms.
