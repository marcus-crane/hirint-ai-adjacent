Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

At Tenstorrent, we’re pushing the boundaries of AI/ML hardware platforms and blazing trails with next-gen high-speed memory technology that powers our performance and scalability. We’re looking for passionate engineers to take charge of validating and enabling our cutting-edge GDDR7 PHY and controller IP.

This role ishybrid, based out of Toronto, Canada, Vancouver, Canada, Santa Clara, California or Austin, Texas.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* You’re interested in high-speed memory interfaces such as GDDR7, DDR5, or LPDDR6, and enjoy working close to both firmware and hardware.
* You’re comfortable developing firmware and writing low-level C/C++, with Python and shell scripting experience for test automation.
* You’re curious about JEDEC memory standards, PHY training flows, and pre-silicon verification and enjoy solving complex problems across silicon and system platforms.

**What We Need**

* Experience using lab equipment such as oscilloscopes, BERTs, and protocol analyzers to characterize and debug memory systems.
* The ability to independently debug hardware and firmware issues, and to collaborate effectively with internal teams and external vendors.
* Familiarity with Verilog or SystemVerilog and waveform debugging tools such as Verdi.
* An interest in pre-silicon design verification, including using DV environments to verify RTL and develop firmware before tapeout.

**What You Will Learn**

* How to enable GDDR7 IP on silicon, from initial bring-up through coverage closure and production deployment in Tenstorrent servers.
* How to build and execute enablement plans grounded in JEDEC standards and system requirements.
* How to perform process, voltage, and temperature (PVT), signal-integrity, and power-integrity margin analysis.
* How to develop firmware and automation tools while partnering with vendors and internal teams to resolve system-level issues.
