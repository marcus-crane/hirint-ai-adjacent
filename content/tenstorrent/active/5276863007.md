Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

Tenstorrent is building the next generation of AI and RISC-V compute. We are developing the technology, products, and systems needed to make high-performance AI computing more accessible, efficient, and scalable.

The Chief of Staff, Go-to-Market, will be a trusted partner to GTM leadership, helping turn business priorities into coordinated execution. Working across strategy, operations, and delivery, you will bring clarity to complex initiatives, strengthen alignment across teams, and help the go-to-market organization operate effectively as Tenstorrent grows.

This is a high-trust, high-ownership role for an operator who can move between executive priorities and hands-on execution. You will work across Sales, Business Development, Product, Engineering, and corporate functions to clarify decisions, surface risks, manage dependencies, and keep important work moving.

This role is **hybrid**, based out of Singapore

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* A high-agency operator who takes ownership, creates structure in ambiguity, and drives work to completion.
* A clear, organized communicator who can synthesize complex information into decisions and practical next steps.
* Skilled at influencing without formal authority and building trust with senior leaders and cross-functional partners.
* Pragmatic and adaptable, comfortable shifting between strategic questions and detailed execution.

**What We Need**

* 4+ years of experience in chief of staff, strategy and operations, business operations, consulting, program management, or a related field.
* A track record of leading complex, cross-functional initiatives and supporting senior executives or organizational leaders.
* Experience developing operating cadences, planning processes, business reviews, leadership materials, or executive reporting.
* Strong project management, prioritization, analytical, and follow-through skills, including the ability to make sound recommendations with incomplete information.
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills. Experience in technology, semiconductors, AI, hardware, or another complex operating environment is preferred. A bachelor's degree in business, engineering, economics, or a related field is preferred.

**What You Will Learn**

* How a growing AI and compute company translates GTM strategy into operating priorities and measurable execution.
* How Sales, Business Development, Product, Engineering, and corporate teams work together to support commercial outcomes.
* How to shape leadership rhythms, planning, and information flow as a high-growth organization scales.
* How to help senior leaders make decisions and move consequential cross-functional initiatives forward.
