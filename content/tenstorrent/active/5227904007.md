Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

We’re looking for a GL Accounting Analyst to drive accurate month-end close, reconciliations, journal entries, and variance analysis. Partner with Finance and AP teams to resolve issues and improve processes through ERP, automation, and AI-enabled workflows.

This role ishybrid, based out of Bengaluru, India.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* 2+ years of experience in GL accounting, month-end close, reconciliations, accruals, prepaids or financial reporting, preferably in a shared services, CoE, GCC or multi-entity environment.
* B.Com/BBA/BBM Finance or equivalent; M.Com, MBA Finance, CA Inter, CMA Inter or ACCA part-qualified is a plus.
* Strong understanding of journal entries, accruals, prepaids, reclasses, variance analysis and financial controls.
* Detail-oriented with strong analytical, communication and stakeholder-management skills, and the ability to work within close calendars and deadlines.

**What We Need**

* Support month-end close by preparing accrual, prepaid, amortization, reclassification and correction journal entries with complete supporting documentation.
* Perform AP-to-GL reconciliations, investigate variances and reconciling items, and work with AP and Finance teams to drive timely resolution.
* Review expenses and account fluctuations, identify unusual trends, coding issues or missing accruals, and provide clear variance explanations.
* Hands-on experience with QBO and/or ERP GL modules is preferred; exposure to AI-enabled finance workflows, ERP implementation, UAT or process improvements is a plus.

**What You Will Learn**

* Build strong expertise in month-end close, accrual accounting, prepaids, reconciliations and financial reporting.
* Gain hands-on exposure to QBO/ERP systems and learn how Finance teams manage global close processes and internal controls.
* Work closely with AP, Finance and business stakeholders while contributing to automation, process improvement and scalable Finance operations.
