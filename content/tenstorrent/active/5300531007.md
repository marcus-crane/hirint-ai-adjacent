Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

CPU Load/Store Verification Engineer will focus on Load Store verification for high-performance CPUs. The person coming into this role will help execute the load store verification strategy and the DV plan.

This role ishybrid, based out of Austin, TX or Santa Clara, CA.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who you are**

* A CPU verification engineer with 7+ years of experience (BS/MS/PhD in EE/ECE/CE/CS) who has worked on an x86, ARM or RISC-V core
* Deeply familiar with high-performance out-of-order CPU microarchitecture, especially load/store, caches, address translation, memory ordering and the memory subsystem
* Eager to adopt AI tools and agents in day-to-day verification work, from test and checker development to debug and coverage closure.
* A strong debugger who can trace issues across RTL and DV at every level of the design hierarchy
* Someone who likes owning a block end to end, working closely with architecture, RTL and post-silicon teams

**What we need**

* Execute functional and performance verification of the load store unit for a from-scratch, high-performance CPU
* Write block-level verification plans covering both architectural (RISC-V ISA) and microarchitectural behavior
* Build reusable testbench components (microarchitectural models, monitors, checkers) in SV, UVM and C++
* Develop random and directed stimulus that carries across pre-silicon, emulation and post-silicon
* Build DV infrastructure and evaluate open-source toolchains for integration into the flow

**What you will learn**

* How a from-scratch, high-performance RISC-V CPU is built and verified, from first RTL through silicon
* How load/store, memory ordering and coherence decisions shape performance in a modern OOO core
* How to design verification environments that scale from simulation to emulation to post-silicon
* How to work with architecture, RTL and post-silicon teams to shape design decisions early
* How open-source toolchains can be adopted and extended in a leading-edge DV flow
