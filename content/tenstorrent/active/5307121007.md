Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

Tenstorrent is hiring a hands-on engineer who enjoys working at the intersection of advanced packaging, EDA software, routing algorithms, and applied AI. In this role they will translate logical design intent—such as structural Verilog and chiplet bump maps—into practical physical package designs, and build reliable automation for system-in-package (SiP) routing.

This role is **hybrid, b**ased out of **Toronto, ON.**

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**You bring:**

* A bachelor’s or master’s degree in electrical engineering, computer engineering, software engineering, computer science, or a related discipline.
* Experience with Cadence Allegro, Advanced Package Designer, Innovus, or comparable EDA tools.
* Experience with autorouting, multilayer routing, differential pairs, high-speed interfaces, or power-delivery optimization.
* Experience with advanced 2.5D/3D packaging, chiplet integration, or OSAT and substrate-vendor design constraints.

**What You Will Do**

* Support package physical design by defining routing strategies, layer usage, escape routing, and connectivity for chiplet-to-chiplet and chiplet-to-package interfaces.
* Create and maintain BGA pin maps and assignments for signals, power, and ground.
* Evaluate how pin-map and assignment choices affect escape routing, signal and power integrity (SI/PI), and board connectivity.
* Develop and evaluate constraint-aware autorouting workflows for package and interposer connectivity.
* Connect logical design intent—from structural Verilog and chiplet bump maps—to physical package design.

**What You Will Learn**

* How to develop reliable, constraint-aware automation for complex SiP routing.
* How package and interposer routing decisions interact with chiplet connectivity, escape routing, SI/PI, and board-level connectivity.
* How advanced packaging, EDA workflows, routing algorithms, and applied AI come together in practical engineering solutions.
* How OSAT and substrate-vendor constraints shape package design and implementation.
