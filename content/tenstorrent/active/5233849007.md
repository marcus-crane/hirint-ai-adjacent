Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

The Corporate Controller builds a trusted global accounting function that scales with the company. Success means predictable closes, trusted balance sheets, documented accounting positions, well-managed audits, embedded controls, managed risks, clear ownership, strong talent, and less manual work. The mission for this role is to build an accounting organization that the company can trust and that will not need to be rebuilt every time the company scales.

This role is remote, based out of the United States.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* Senior accounting leader with deep technical judgment and strong operational discipline.
* 15+ years leading accounting or controllership teams in complex organizations.
* CPA or equivalent, with deep US GAAP and financial reporting expertise.
* Experienced in technology, semiconductor, hardware, manufacturing, or similar environments.
* Skilled at scaling teams, controls, systems, and processes through rapid growth.

**What We Need**

* Lead a global accounting organization with clear ownership and high standards.
* Own monthly, quarterly, and annual close quality, speed, and predictability.
* Set accounting policy across revenue, inventory, leases, equity, debt, and foreign currency.
* Partner early with Commercial, Legal, Tax, Treasury, Operations, and Strategic Finance.
* Build controls into workflows while protecting execution speed, replace manual accounting work with ERP, automation, data, and AI-enabled tools.

**What You Will Learn**

* How product development, suppliers, manufacturing, contracts, revenue, and cash connect.
* How to design accounting across entities, jurisdictions, currencies, and business models.
* How to build an accounting system with AI integrated into every workflow.
