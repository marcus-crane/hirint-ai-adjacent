Tenstorrent is leading the industry on cutting-edge AI technology, revolutionizing performance expectations, ease of use, and cost efficiency. With AI redefining the computing paradigm, solutions must evolve to unify innovations in software models, compilers, platforms, networking, and semiconductors. Our diverse team of technologists have developed a high performance RISC-V CPU from scratch, and share a passion for AI and a deep desire to build the best AI platform possible. We value collaboration, curiosity, and a commitment to solving hard problems. We are growing our team and looking for contributors of all seniorities.

Tenstorrent is hiring an IT Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) Analyst to run the day-to-day engine of our continuous compliance, third-party risk, security policy, and risk assessment programs. Reporting to the Head of Information Security, you will keep Tenstorrent audit-ready year-round as we scale our commercial AI cloud (TT-Console), federal compliance (CMMC), and global silicon operations. We run compliance as an automated, AI-enabled discipline built on Vanta, Git-managed questionnaires, and AI agents, not spreadsheets and screenshots.

This role is remote, based out of the United States or Canada.

We welcome candidates at various experience levels for this role. During the interview process, candidates will be assessed for the appropriate level, and offers will align with that level, which may differ from the one in this posting.

**Who You Are**

* A tech-savvy GRC operator who uses AI tools (Claude, Cursor, Glean) daily to analyze SOC 2 reports and DPAs, draft risk memos, and maintain policies in Markdown/YAML.
* Relentless about continuous compliance: controls stay green, access reviews land on time, and risk registers stay current.
* A clear translator between Legal, Procurement, and Engineering on privacy and compliance requirements (GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA).
* Technically grounded in cloud infrastructure, SSO/MFA, EDR, encryption, and AI providers, enough to spot red flags in vendor and product reviews.
* A concise, direct communicator with a bachelor's degree in a related field or equivalent practical experience.

**What We Need**

* Hands-on experience running SOC 2 Type 1 and Type 2 programs on a GRC automation platform such as Vanta or Drata, including control tests, evidence collection, and auditor coordination.
* Third-party risk management experience: tiering vendors, running security questionnaires, reviewing SOC 2 and ISO 27001 reports, and partnering with Legal on DPAs and AI data-use terms.
* Experience maintaining risk registers and running security intake reviews for new products, internal tools, and data centers.
* Comfort using AI tools to analyze documents, build reports, and automate workflows in a Git-backed Markdown/YAML workflow.
* Familiarity with CMMC / NIST SP 800-171, ISO 27001, and global privacy regulations.

**What You Will Learn**

* How to run an AI-augmented GRC and vendor risk program that completes reviews in hours instead of weeks.
* How to drive SOC 2 Type 2 continuous assurance and CMMC compliance across a commercial AI cloud and a global silicon engineering organization.
* How to evaluate AI inference providers, agent architectures, and colocation data centers alongside Security, Legal, and Trade Compliance leaders.
* How to grow from executing GRC workflows into shaping cross-functional risk governance and new certifications.
