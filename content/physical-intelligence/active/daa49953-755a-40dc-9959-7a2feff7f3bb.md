## About the Role

Physical Intelligence builds AI for robots. Our operations teams keep hands-on work running on schedule for research and production. As a Set + Props Specialist, you keep workspaces organized and supplied on your shift so work runs without interruption.

## The Team

The Set + Props team supports research and production operations at Physical Intelligence. The team builds and maintains physical workspaces and keeps them supplied so work runs on schedule across every shift. It includes coordinators, shift specialists, and contractors who flex with demand.

## In This Role You Will

* Audits: Check workspaces and supplies against a checklist.
* Prep and staging: Prepare, organize, and stage supplies for upcoming work.
* Inventory: Log inventory accurately in our tools.
* Delivery: Deliver supplies to teams during the shift.
* Flagging issues: Flag anything missing or out of spec.

## What We Hope You'll Bring

* 1+ year in retail merchandising, warehouse, stagehand, hospitality, kitchen, or facilities work
* Reliability and strong attention to detail
* Comfort with basic inventory or tracking tools
* Availability for a morning, swing, or overnight shift
* Ability to stand, lift up to 40 lbs, and move around all shift

## Bonus Points

* Experience with film, TV, or theater props or sets
* Experience with kitting, pick and pack, or cycle counts
