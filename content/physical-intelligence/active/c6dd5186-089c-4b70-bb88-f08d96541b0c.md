**Learning & Development Lead — Operations**

**Role Overview**

The Learning & Development Lead owns training and readiness for Physical Intelligence's robot operator workforce.

This person is responsible for designing and running the systems that take a new operator from candidate work trial through their first 30–60 days, with the goal of getting people productive quickly while maintaining a high quality bar.

They also own ongoing training as the work changes. When researchers introduce new robot workflows, tasks, tools, or operating procedures, the Learning & Development Lead translates those changes into training that can be rolled out quickly and consistently across the operation.

This is a highly operational training role. We are looking for someone who has built or owned training programs for hourly or frontline employees and knows how to turn complex work into a simple, repeatable learning process.

**Responsibilities**

**Operator Trials & Readiness**

* Own the operator work-trial program and ensure candidates are evaluated consistently.
* Maintain clear trial standards, evaluator guidance, and scoring criteria.
* Partner with recruiting and operations to continuously improve what the trial predicts about future operator success.

**New-Hire Onboarding**

* Build and run onboarding from day one through the first 30–60 days.
* Define what an operator should know and be able to do at each stage of ramp.
* Create training plans, checklists, certification steps, and readiness milestones.
* Identify struggling operators early and create clear paths for remediation.
* Continuously improve time-to-productivity for new hires.

**New Workflow Training**

* Partner with researchers and operations teams when new robot tasks, systems, or processes are introduced.
* Learn new workflows quickly and translate them into clear training materials and operating instructions.
* Design the rollout plan needed to train a large operator population quickly.
* Track who has been trained and verify that the training actually resulted in correct execution.
* Maintain consistency as training expands across shifts, teams, and locations.

**Training Operations**

* Maintain a clear training calendar and ensure training happens when required.
* Coordinate trainers, operators, shift leads, and researchers.
* Keep training materials current as workflows change.
* Build repeatable processes so training does not depend on tribal knowledge.
* Track training completion, readiness, and performance metrics.

**Continuous Improvement**

* Use operator performance data and feedback to identify gaps in training.
* Work with operations leaders to understand recurring quality or productivity issues.
* Determine whether problems are caused by training, instructions, process, tooling, or something else.
* Continuously simplify and improve how operators learn.

**What We're Looking For**

* Experience owning training, onboarding, or workforce development programs for hourly or frontline employees.
* Demonstrated ability to get new employees productive quickly.
* Experience building structured ramp programs with clear milestones and standards.
* Strong operational organization and follow-through.
* Able to turn ambiguous or technical workflows into simple, teachable processes.
* Comfortable managing trainers, who are responsible for training large groups of people on new procedures under tight timelines.
* Strong written and verbal communication.
* Able to work effectively with both highly technical stakeholders, trainers, and frontline operators.
* Comfortable in an environment where processes and requirements change frequently.
* Uses data and observation to improve training rather than relying only on subjective feedback.

Experience in robotics is not required.
