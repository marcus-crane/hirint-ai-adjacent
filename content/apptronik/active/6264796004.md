Apptronik is a human-centered robotics company developing AI-powered robots to support humanity in every facet of life. Our flagship humanoid robot, Apollo, is built to collaborate thoughtfully with people, starting with critical industries such as manufacturing and logistics, with future applications in healthcare, the home, and beyond.  
  
We operate at the cutting edge of Applied AI, applying our expertise across the full robotics stack to solve some of society's most important problems. You will join a team dedicated to bringing Apollo to market at scale, tackling the complex challenges like safety, commercialization, and mass production to change the world for the better.

#### **JOB SUMMARY**

We are looking for a highly technical Staff or Principal Technical Program Manager to lead the programs that train Apollo's AI. This role owns the full physical AI training lifecycle: the compute infrastructure we train on, the data engine that feeds it, the pretraining, mid-training, and post-training runs that produce our models, and the path that takes those models onto robots in the field.

You will operate as a technical peer to research and infrastructure leadership, not a schedule keeper. You will set the strategy for how training programs are planned and resourced, own the build-out and utilization of our cloud and on-premises GPU capacity, and turn research goals into executable plans with clear gates and decisions. As the function grows, you will define the program management practices that let Apptronik train and ship models faster than anyone else in physical AI.

#### **ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES or KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES**

* **Compute Strategy & Infrastructure:** Own the strategy and execution for Apptronik's training compute across cloud and on-premises GPUs. Drive capacity forecasting, build-vs-buy decisions, vendor and cloud partnerships, cluster bring-up, and the utilization, cost, and allocation of compute across research teams.
* **Training Program Execution:** Run pretraining, mid-training, fine-tuning, and distillation efforts as disciplined programs. Define compute budgets, data freeze dates, checkpoint and evaluation gates, and go/no-go criteria, and lead the response when a run diverges or infrastructure fails.
* **Data Engine:** Own the program that turns model needs into training data, spanning teleoperation, human demonstration, and simulation. Translate research requirements into collection plans, quality standards, and SOPs, and scale data throughput with the pipelines and storage behind it.
* **Evaluation & Model Release:** Establish a rigorous evaluation and release process across simulation and real robots. Drive statistically sound benchmarks, regression gates, and clear promotion criteria so release decisions rest on evidence, not demos.
* **Deployment & Fleet Learning:** Own the path from trained checkpoint to on-robot inference, including model optimization for edge hardware and integration with software, controls, and firmware. Build the loop that brings fleet data back into post-training and safely rolls out improved policies.
* **Ownership:** Own program execution end to end. Set strategic direction, identify resource requirements, and be accountable for delivering on company goals and roadmap. Challenge assumptions at the org level and foster a results-driven culture.
* **Communication:** Proactively communicate program status, risks, trade-offs, and decisions to executive stakeholders. Make compute, data, and model trade-offs legible to leadership and clearly articulate requirements, goals, and milestones across teams.
* **Leadership & Mentorship:** Mentor TPMs and engineers, and define the frameworks, tooling, and operating rhythms that let research move fast without losing rigor. Raise the technical bar of the program management function.

#### **SKILLS AND REQUIREMENTS**

* **Large-Scale Training Programs** — Deep experience leading programs for large model training, including pretraining, mid-training, fine-tuning, and distillation. Fluency in how runs are planned, resourced, monitored, and recovered when they fail.
* **GPU Compute & ML Infrastructure** — Hands-on understanding of GPU clusters across cloud and on-premises environments, including schedulers, distributed training, high-speed interconnects, and storage. Experience driving capacity planning, procurement, and cluster bring-up.
* **Hands-On Technical Depth** — Ability to read training code, interpret loss curves and evaluation results, and diagnose data and infrastructure issues alongside the team. Comfortable with Python and a modern framework such as PyTorch or JAX.
* **Robot Learning Data** — Experience with the data that drives physical AI: teleoperation, demonstration capture, simulation and synthetic data, annotation, and quality control at scale.
* **Evaluation Rigor** — Experience building evaluation frameworks and release criteria for ML models, ideally spanning simulation and real-world testing.
* **Edge Deployment** — Familiarity with taking models from training to on-device inference, including optimization for embedded and edge AI hardware.
* **Vendor & Partner Management** — Proven success managing cloud providers, hardware vendors, and data partners, including negotiating capacity and resolving cross-org dependencies.
* **Research Program Leadership** — Ability to plan around research uncertainty using experiments, decision points, and gates rather than fixed deliverables, while holding infrastructure and data work to firm commitments.
* **Influence in Resource-Constrained Environments** — Proven ability to allocate scarce compute and data across competing teams, align research, engineering, and executive leadership, and make hard prioritization calls.
* **Strategic Thinking** — Ability to translate company strategy into compute, data, and model roadmaps, and influence those roadmaps through data-driven insight.

#### **EDUCATION and/or EXPERIENCE**

* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical or Computer Engineering, or a related technical field required
* 10+ years of experience in technical program management, ML engineering, or ML infrastructure, including leading large-scale training or compute programs at a senior, staff, or principal level
* Preferred: Experience at a frontier AI lab, hyperscaler, or robotics foundation model company
* Preferred: Experience standing up or scaling GPU clusters or data center capacity
* Preferred: Direct experience with robot learning, vision-language-action models, or large-scale robot data programs
* Preferred: Master's or PhD in Computer Science, Machine Learning, or a related field
