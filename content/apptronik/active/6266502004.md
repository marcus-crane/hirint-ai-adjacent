**Job summary:**

We are seeking a Senior Manufacturing Test Engineer – Robotic Hands to own the design, deployment, and maintenance of automated test systems on Apptronik’s production line. This is a hands-on, multi-disciplinary role spanning mechanical, electrical, firmware, and software, requiring someone who can independently develop test methods, build the fixtures and automation that run them, and sustain those systems as programs scale into production.

You will design and build automated end-of-line and in-process test stations, develop robust calibration and verification workflows, and diagnose hardware issues across the full electromechanical stack. You will not run tests day to day; you will deliver validated, well-documented test systems ready for operators and technicians to run, and use production data to drive yield improvements and design feedback. You will work closely with mechanical, electrical, firmware, controls, and manufacturing teams.

**Responsibilities:**

* Test Strategy & Design: Define and design the end-of-line and in-process test strategy for robotic hand production, including test coverage, pass/fail criteria, and cycle-time targets.
* Automated Test Development: Develop and deploy automated test stations and software (Python, data logging, traceability) that verify finger and hand function, force output, calibration, and electrical performance on the line.
* Fixture & Equipment Ownership: Design, build, and maintain test fixtures, sensor rigs, and station hardware, including calibration schedules, uptime, and upgrades across hand variants and revisions.
* Handoff & Sustaining Support: Transfer validated test processes to line operators and technicians with documentation and training, own station maintenance and troubleshooting, and leave test execution to production.

**Required qualifications**

* Hands-on experience designing, deploying, and maintaining automated test systems for a manufacturing or production line, including end-of-line test stations for electromechanical products.
* Proficiency writing production-grade Python for test automation, data logging, analysis, and traceability.
* Experience designing test fixtures and test methods, including pass/fail criteria, gauge repeatability and reproducibility (GR&R), and handoff documentation, not just executing pre-written scripts.
* Experience with motor, actuator, or sensor testing and calibration, with enough electrical and firmware fluency to diagnose drive and calibration faults independently.
* Strong cross-disciplinary debugging ability across mechanical, electrical, and firmware domains, using data-driven root-cause analysis.
* Ability to read and interpret electrical schematics, mechanical drawings, and component datasheets.

**Education:**

* BS in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Robotics, Computer Science, or related field + 6 years of relevant experience, OR MS in related field + 4 years of relevant experience, OR PhD in related field + 2 years of relevant experience.
