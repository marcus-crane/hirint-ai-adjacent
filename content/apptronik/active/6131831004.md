Apptronik is a human-centered robotics company developing AI-powered robots to support humanity in every facet of life. Our flagship humanoid robot, Apollo, is built to collaborate thoughtfully with people, starting with critical industries such as manufacturing and logistics, with future applications in healthcare, the home, and beyond.  
  
We operate at the cutting edge of Applied AI, applying our expertise across the full robotics stack to solve some of society's most important problems. You will join a team dedicated to bringing Apollo to market at scale, tackling the complex challenges like safety, commercialization, and mass production to change the world for the better.

### JOB SUMMARY:

We're looking for a Senior Electrical Engineer to join our team at Apptronik, focusing on our Apollo 2 humanoid robot platform. In this role, you'll be responsible for driving design, process, and performance improvements across the entire robotic system, from individual components to the full-stack assembly. This position is ideal for a hands-on engineer with a passion for robotics and a proven track record of optimizing complex electrical systems.

### ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

* **PCBA Design for Excellence**
  + Provide feedback and develop processes for design for manufacturing and test (DFM, DFT) for PCBAs and wiring harnesses.
  + Work with supply chain to manage bills of materials (BOM), component sourcing, and component substitutions.
  + Work with vendors and manufacturing leading up to design release, at design release, and during initial builds.
  + Support electrical engineering team on new designs as needed.
* **Debug Specialist**
  + Conduct root cause analysis on system failures, performance issues, and production inefficiencies to implement robust solutions.
  + Coordinate and implement root cause corrective actions.
  + Develop design documentation to support system bring-up and system field deployment.
  + Define and support compliance, EMI, EMC, safety, and reliability testing as needed.
* **Leader for Electrical Engineering Development Processes**
  + Lead continuous improvement initiatives for the Apollo humanoid robot, identifying and addressing opportunities for improvement in design, manufacturing, and field support.
  + Maintain processes for design release, product lifecycle management, manufacturing team support, engineering change orders.
  + Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including mechanical, software, and manufacturing engineers, to integrate improvements seamlessly.
  + Develop and implement best practices for electrical assembly, testing, and quality control.
  + Document all design changes and improvements thoroughly, ensuring clear communication and knowledge transfer.
  + Mentor and provide technical guidance to junior engineers

### SKILLS AND REQUIREMENTS

* Experience with product lifecycle management and PLM systems such as Arena
* Competent in CAD tools including Altium, or similar tools
* Experience with developing and maintaining CAD design rules
* Proven experience designing, prototyping, and debugging PCB/PCBAs as well as interfacing with manufacturing groups during release/build
* Familiarity/experience with electronics lab tools and simulation tools (ex: LTSpice, PSpice)
* Excellent communication (verbal & written); will be expected to develop detailed build assembly instructions and diagrams
* Excellent problem-solving and hardware debugging skills
* Hands-on experience with prototyping and testing, including the use of lab equipment.
* Travel up to 10% within US and internationally

### EDUCATION and/or EXPERIENCE:

* Bachelors, Masters, or PhD degree (or equivalent) in Electrical Engineering or related field with at least 4 years of experience as an Electrical Engineer

### PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
* Must be able to lift 15 pounds at times
* Vision to read printed materials and a computer screen
* Hearing and speech to communicate
