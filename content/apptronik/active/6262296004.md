Apptronik is a human-centered robotics company developing AI-powered robots to support humanity in every facet of life. Our flagship humanoid robot, Apollo, is built to collaborate thoughtfully with people, starting with critical industries such as manufacturing and logistics, with future applications in healthcare, the home, and beyond.  
  
We operate at the cutting edge of Applied AI, applying our expertise across the full robotics stack to solve some of society's most important problems. You will join a team dedicated to bringing Apollo to market at scale, tackling the complex challenges like safety, commercialization, and mass production to change the world for the better.

Apptronik builds general-purpose humanoid robots designed to work alongside people. We're preparing for commercial deployment, and Strategic Finance sits at the center of the decisions that get us there.

### **Strategic Finance at Apptronik has three core responsibilities**

1. **Special Projects.** High-priority problems that don't fit neatly in anyone's job description come to us first. If it's messy and it matters, it's probably ours.
2. **Commercial Strategy.**We're the financial partner to the commercial team, from go-to-market planning through deal structure and customer economics. Humanoids don't have a commercial playbook yet, and we're building it.
3. **Strategic Planning & Investment.**We own the company's long-range strategic model and use it to guide the biggest decisions: where to invest, how to scale manufacturing, when to raise capital, and which partnerships make sense. We bring financial rigor to those calls and keep leadership's view of the future grounded in numbers.

### **What you'll do**

* Own projects end-to-end, from defining the problem to implementing the solution, often with little direction on how to get there
* Drive alignment across many stakeholders. If you don't like leading large meetings or wrangling a group toward a decision, this probably isn't the role for you
* Learn the business top to bottom, working with Finance, Commercial, Engineering, Supply Chain, Manufacturing, Corporate Development, and more
* Own detailed models end-to-end: gathering data on the ground, building the analysis, and presenting it to stakeholders
* Build the planning and reporting systems a growing finance team needs, from forecasting processes to the tools and models that connect them
* Build P&L models for products, business lines, and partnerships
* Build new frameworks for operational decisions across facilities, manufacturing, and supply chain – e.g. Build a 10-year model for a new product we're considering
  + e.g. How should we approach capital allocation as we scale production?
* Model the economics of major deals and show the impact on both Apptronik and the customer
* Explore different ways to monetize our robots and technology
* Embed with engineering, sales, and business development on major new opportunities – e.g. How should we structure a multi-year fleet deployment versus a pilot?
  + e.g. What does a robots-as-a-service offering look like for us?

### **What you bring**

* 5+ years in strategic finance, investment banking, management consulting, or similar
* Strong command of financial and accounting concepts
* Solid FP&A fundamentals. You understand how budgeting, forecasting, and finance systems fit together, and you can stand them up at a startup without hand-holding
* Strong analytical instincts. You think in numbers and turn them into clear, actionable recommendations
* Fast and fluent in Excel
* Self-driven and relentlessly proactive. We'd rather tell you to slow down than speed up
* You step into gaps and solve problems instead of waiting on others
* You're comfortable being uncomfortable, and you can quantify complex, ambiguous problems
* Comfortable communicating broadly, with a lot of senior eyes on your work
* You like the flexibility of a startup and don't mind sprinting when something urgent comes up
