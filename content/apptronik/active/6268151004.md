Apptronik is a human-centered robotics company developing AI-powered robots to support humanity in every facet of life. Our flagship humanoid robot, Apollo, is built to collaborate thoughtfully with people, starting with critical industries such as manufacturing and logistics, with future applications in healthcare, the home, and beyond.  
  
We operate at the cutting edge of Applied AI, applying our expertise across the full robotics stack to solve some of society's most important problems. You will join a team dedicated to bringing Apollo to market at scale, tackling the complex challenges like safety, commercialization, and mass production to change the world for the better.

### **JOB SUMMARY**

The Senior Autonomy Software Engineer is a key, hands-on role. You will build the on-robot autonomy that lets Apollo complete full shifts of real work at customer sites. You will build the mission and behavior framework that turns a job from fleet software into robot actions. You will also build the runtime that runs learned policies safely on the robot, together with navigation, perception, and control. The main focus of this role is to move learned behaviors, including learned and language-conditioned navigation, from simulation to reliable use on physical robots. As a senior member of the team, you will also mentor engineers and raise the team's engineering standards.

### **ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES or KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES**

* Design, build, and own the on-robot mission and behavior framework. This framework coordinates navigation, perception, manipulation, and control to complete customer missions.
* Integrate learned policies on the robot: imitation learning, reinforcement learning, and large multimodal robot models. Make sure that on-device inference meets real-time latency and compute budgets.
* Develop supervisors, safety wrappers, and fallback behaviors for learned autonomy. Work with the safety team to keep learned behaviors within safe limits near people.
* Build failure detection and recovery. The robot must retry, re-plan, or resume a mission at the failed step before it asks a person for help.
* Extend the autonomy stack to learned navigation, including language-conditioned navigation policies. Keep the classical planner as the safe fallback.
* Build tools for telemetry, logging, replay, and evaluation. Run regression tests in simulation (for example Isaac Sim) and on hardware to measure and improve mission success and intervention rates.
* Work closely with the navigation, perception, controls, reinforcement learning, and platform teams. Define clear interfaces, and find the root cause of system-level problems on physical robots.
* Mentor engineers through technical guidance, design reviews, and code reviews. Improve code quality, testing, and CI/CD practices.

### **SKILLS AND REQUIREMENTS**

* Strong modern C++ for fast, real-time robot software, and strong Python for prototypes, tools, and ML workflows, on Linux.
* Hands-on experience with learned policies (imitation learning, reinforcement learning, or robot foundation models) in real robot systems. You integrated these policies and ran them closed loop on hardware.
* Experience with mission or task execution that uses behavior trees or state machines, including failure handling and recovery.
* Good knowledge of robotics systems: state estimation interfaces, and how autonomy, perception, navigation, and control work together.
* Experience with ROS 2 or a similar message-passing framework, and with real-time, multi-threaded, or distributed software.
* Strong plus: you deployed neural networks on embedded GPUs, with model optimization, quantization, and latency profiling.
* Strong plus: knowledge of learned navigation, vision-language models that direct robot tasks, or runtime safety for learned systems (safety filters, monitors, fallback).
* A results-oriented mindset. You want to see autonomy work reliably on real hardware.

### **EDUCATION and/or EXPERIENCE**

* A BS in Computer Science, Robotics, Computer Engineering, or a related field and 5+ years of industry experience, or an MS or PhD and 3+ years.
* A proven record of deploying autonomy, behavior, or navigation software on physical robots. Experience with humanoid, legged, or mobile manipulation robots is a plus.
* Experience as a mentor or technical guide to other engineers on a team.
* Plus: publications at relevant conferences (for example CoRL, RSS, ICRA, or IROS) or contributions to open-source robotics software.

### **PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS**

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
* Must be able to lift 15 pounds at times
* Vision to read printed materials and a computer screen
* Hearing and speech to communicate
