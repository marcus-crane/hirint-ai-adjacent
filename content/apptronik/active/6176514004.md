## **JOB SUMMARY**

As a Global Fleet Operations Specialist, you are the first responder and operational backbone for our global fleet. Global Fleet Operations is the eyes and ears of the Global Services & Support (GSS) organization, our version of Mission Control, actively monitoring fleet performance, managing incoming case queues, diagnosing and triaging Level 1 technical challenges, and coordinating support for our service and repair teams. If you love solving complex puzzles, possess a knack for meticulous organization, are a cross-functional collaborator, and want to play a critical role on the front lines of the humanoid robotics industry, this role is your launching pad.

## **ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES**

### **Fleet Monitoring, Level 1 Triage & Service Coordination**

* **First Line of Defense & Triage:** Actively monitor live fleet alerts and incoming case queues via Salesforce. Perform initial Level 1 diagnostic analysis on reported anomalies using internal software tools to resolve issues directly, whenever possible.
* **Service Administration & Case Management:** Own end-to-end case tracking, ensuring every ticket is properly categorized, populated with accurate metadata, and managed through its complete lifecycle to maintain data integrity and improve fleet MTTR (Mean Time to Repair/Resolve).
* **Work Order & Dispatch Coordination:** Coordinate service requests and dispatch workflows for service teams, ensuring clear handoffs, accurate customer/fleet details, and timely execution against operational SLAs.
* **Actionable Escalations & Package Context:** For complex hardware or software faults exceeding Level 1 scope, compile diagnostic context, logs, and system states into standardized escalation packages before routing to Tier 2/3 service leads or engineering teams.

### **Cross-Functional Communication & Operational Continuity**

* **Operational Alignment:** Act as “Mission Control” for GSS. You are the entry point for all **customers**, both internal and external. You are responsible **for driving** rapid issue resolution, **maintaining** fleet availability, and **ensuring** alignment across teams—keeping all stakeholders and customers informed as we progress towards resolution, escalating as needed.
* **Shift Readiness & Handoffs:** Drive seamless shift handovers and status reports to guarantee continuous, uninterrupted coverage and zero drop-off in active incident tracking as global operations scale.
