Apptronik is a human-centered robotics company developing AI-powered robots to support humanity in every facet of life. Our flagship humanoid robot, Apollo, is built to collaborate thoughtfully with people, starting with critical industries such as manufacturing and logistics, with future applications in healthcare, the home, and beyond.  
  
We operate at the cutting edge of Applied AI, applying our expertise across the full robotics stack to solve some of society's most important problems. You will join a team dedicated to bringing Apollo to market at scale, tackling the complex challenges like safety, commercialization, and mass production to change the world for the better.

**JOB SUMMARY**

The Software Engineer - Humanoid Sim-to-Real is a core contributor to how our humanoid robots carry behavior from simulation onto hardware. This role is responsible for closing the gap between simulated and real robot behavior through simulation development, hardware experimentation, and system identification.

This role will bridge the gap between simulation and physical hardware. Working closely with the simulation, controls, learning, and hardware teams, this engineer will turn measured robot behavior into accurate models of dynamics, kinematics, contact, and actuation that enable high-performance locomotion and manipulation.

**ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES or KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES**

* Build and maintain simulation infrastructure that supports humanoid locomotion and manipulation
* Implement and validate models of rigid-body dynamics, kinematics, contact, and actuators, including electromechanical effects where relevant.
* Design and run paired hardware and simulation experiments to characterize system behavior and reduce sim-to-real discrepancies.
* Apply system identification methods to estimate and validate model parameters from experimental data.
* Use classical controls and frequency-domain tools to analyze performance, including bandwidth, stability, and response.
* Develop reproducible Python and C++ tooling for experiment automation, data analysis, and model verification.
* Collaborate with the simulation, controls, learning, and hardware teams to find root causes and improve performance on the robot.
* Establish repeatable best practices for running experiments and validating models against hardware.

**SKILLS AND REQUIREMENTS**

**Technical Skills (Must-Have)**

* **Core Expertise:** Deep expertise in at least one of controls, system identification, simulation, robot modeling, or reinforcement learning, with working knowledge across the others.
* **Hardware-to-Simulation:** Experience connecting hardware data to simulation through experimental modeling or system identification.
* **Software Engineering:** Proficiency in Python (NumPy, PyTorch) and/or C++ (Eigen), and experience with at least one modern simulator such as MuJoCo, Isaac Sim/Lab, or Brax.
* **Experimental Design:** Ability to design structured experiments and reason from noisy data to physical explanations.
* **Controls Fundamentals:** Familiarity with classical control concepts and dynamic system response.
* **Code Quality:** A track record of shipping maintainable code with Git in fast-moving R&D environments.

**Good to Have**

* **Humanoid or Legged Robots:** Experience working with humanoid or legged robot hardware.
* **Reinforcement Learning:** Experience training, tuning, or deploying RL policies.
* **Contact & Actuator Modeling:** Background in contact modeling, actuator characterization, or sim-to-real transfer.
* **Lab Instrumentation:** Hands-on experience with lab instrumentation and hardware validation workflows.
* **Infrastructure:** Experience with Docker and GPU or cloud-based development.

**EDUCATION and/or EXPERIENCE**

* A BS with 3-5 years of relevant experience, or an MS with 1-3 years of relevant experience, or a PhD, in Robotics, Computer Science, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
* A track record of validating simulation models against physical hardware.

**PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS**

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
* Must be able to lift 15 pounds at times
* Vision to read printed materials and a computer screen
* Hearing and speech to communicate
