Apptronik is a human-centered robotics company developing AI-powered robots to support humanity in every facet of life. Our flagship humanoid robot, Apollo, is built to collaborate thoughtfully with people, starting with critical industries such as manufacturing and logistics, with future applications in healthcare, the home, and beyond.  
  
We operate at the cutting edge of Applied AI, applying our expertise across the full robotics stack to solve some of society's most important problems. You will join a team dedicated to bringing Apollo to market at scale, tackling the complex challenges like safety, commercialization, and mass production to change the world for the better.

#### **JOB SUMMARY**

We are looking for a Senior Technical Program Manager to drive hardware development for Apollo. You will drive planning and execution across Electrical Engineering, Firmware, Systems, Quality, and Manufacturing, taking custom electronics from concept through board bring-up, validation, and production release.

This is a deeply technical role. You will engage directly in design reviews and root-cause investigations, manage board spins and firmware releases as one integrated plan, and serve as the primary point of contact for program status, technical risk, and external development and manufacturing partners.

#### **ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES or KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES**

* **Cross-Functional Program Leadership:** Program lead for cross-functional engineering teams; proactively drive planning and execution across Electrical Engineering (EE), Firmware (FW), Systems, Quality, and Manufacturing teams.
* **Hardware-Software Co-Design:** Drive end-to-end alignment between EE and FW teams to ensure seamless hardware-software co-design, board bring-up, signal integrity, and system-level debugging.
* **Electronics Execution:** Oversee technical execution and lifecycle deliverables for Apollo's custom electronics, including actuator and motor driver boards, power distribution, embedded compute, microcontrollers, and sensor interfaces.
* **Technical Point of Contact:** Act as the primary technical point of contact for program status, clearly communicating architecture trade-offs, technical risks, schedule dependencies, and mitigation strategies.
* **Roadmaps & Planning:** Develop and execute detailed program roadmaps, including HW board spins, FW sprint milestones, risk management, and resource allocation.
* **Development Lifecycle Deliverables:** Lead engineering deliverables through each hardware build phase (EVT, DVT, PVT), including EE/FW architecture specifications, DFMEA and risk analyses, design verification, and V&V execution against applicable safety and compliance standards.
* **Release & Revision Tracking:** Establish and run the tracking for firmware releases, hardware revision cycles, and sprints using Jira, Confluence, and Agile/Scrum practices, with clear ownership and accountability for every deliverable.
* **Technical Leadership:** Provide technical leadership during design reviews, circuit/firmware root-cause problem solving, and risk assessments using deep engineering fundamentals.
* **Risk & Bottleneck Management:** Proactively identify program bottlenecks, hardware lead-time risks, and firmware resource gaps, driving immediate technical solutions to ensure timely milestone delivery.

**SKILLS AND REQUIREMENTS**

* **Electronics & Firmware Depth** — Strong technical background in electrical engineering, embedded firmware, electronics architecture, and understanding of V&V activities for electromechanical systems.
* **External Partner Management** — Serve as a key point of contact for external consultants and contractors, including development and manufacturing partners and suppliers.
* **Program Tooling** — Proficiency in project management methodologies and tools such as Jira, sprint planning, and task tracking, including creating trackers, dashboards, and reporting.
* **Cross-Team Influence** — Experience and influence in establishing work relationships across multi-disciplinary teams and with partners in different time zones, building trust and demonstrating critical thinking and thoughtful leadership.
* **Standards & Quality Systems** — Knowledge of industry standards, safety requirements, and quality systems relevant to hardware and electronics.

**EDUCATION and/or EXPERIENCE**

* Bachelor's degree in Engineering (e.g., Electrical, Computer, or related field) required
* 7-10+ years of program management or engineering leadership experience spanning hardware concept development to production release
* Preferred: Experience in robotics, autonomous systems, or other complex electromechanical products
* Preferred: Hands-on experience with motor control, power electronics, or high-speed digital design

**PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
* Must be able to lift 15 pounds at times
* Vision to read printed materials and a computer screen
* Hearing and speech to communicate
