**Job summary:**

We are seeking a Senior Manager, Hardware Test to build and lead the team that validates Apptronik’s robots, from actuators and robotic hands through integrated systems, and to own test strategy from design verification through handoff to the production test team. You will hire and develop a fast-growing team of test engineers while staying hands-on as a player-coach, build the reliability program from scratch, and set the direction for fixtures and data infrastructure. You will lead cross-stack root-cause analysis across mechanical, electrical, and firmware, and plan test equipment and lab capacity.

**Responsibilities:**

* Build and lead the team: Hire, develop and manage a growing hardware test team, setting priorities, hiring bar and career paths, and staying hands-on technically as a player-coach.
* Own test strategy from DVT to production: Define how robot hands and actuators are validated and tested, including pass/fail criteria, and hand off production test methods to test ops with the documentation and training to run them at scale.
* Own reliability: Run life, durability and failure-mode testing, build the reliability program from scratch.
* Lead cross-stack root-cause analysis: Diagnose issues across mechanical, electrical and firmware, and turn test data into clear, actionable feedback for design teams.
* Own test equipment and lab capacity: Plan and budget for fixtures, dynamometers and test assets, make build vs buy calls, and keep equipment calibrated, maintained and sized for upcoming builds.
* Own program execution and reporting: Plan test around hardware build timelines, and communicate results, risks and recommendations to engineering and program leadership early.

**Required qualifications:**

* 10+ years in hardware test, validation or reliability engineering, including 3+ years managing engineers, ideally as a player-coach.
* Track record of hiring, developing and retaining a technical team, including growing a team through a period of fast headcount growth.
* Hands-on experience testing and characterizing electromechanical systems (actuators, motors, gearboxes, or robotic hands/grippers), including dynamometer or custom test stand operation.
* Demonstrated ownership of test strategy from design validation through production, including defining pass/fail criteria and handing off test methods to a manufacturing or test ops team.
* Experience building or leading a reliability program, including life testing, failure analysis and failure-mode tracking.
* Strong cross-disciplinary debugging ability across mechanical, electrical and firmware domains, with the judgment to run root-cause analysis through your team.
* Experience planning test equipment, fixtures and lab capacity, including budgets and build vs buy decisions.
* Ability to read electrical schematics, mechanical drawings and component datasheets.
* BS in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Robotics, Computer Science or a related field (MS or PhD preferred).
* Ability to sit at a desk and work on a computer for prolonged periods, lift 15 pounds at times, and read printed materials and screens, with hearing and speech to communicate.
