Apptronik is a human-centered robotics company developing AI-powered robots to support humanity in every facet of life. Our flagship humanoid robot, Apollo, is built to collaborate thoughtfully with people, starting with critical industries such as manufacturing and logistics, with future applications in healthcare, the home, and beyond.  
  
We operate at the cutting edge of Applied AI, applying our expertise across the full robotics stack to solve some of society's most important problems. You will join a team dedicated to bringing Apollo to market at scale, tackling the complex challenges like safety, commercialization, and mass production to change the world for the better.

**JOB SUMMARY**

A humanoid's usefulness is decided by its hands, and this role leads the team that makes ours capable of real work. As the Engineering Manager - Dexterous Manipulation, you will build and lead the team responsible for dexterous manipulation, setting its direction within the company's broader technical strategy and advancing the hand capabilities that will let our robots perform real tasks. You will do this through strong people leadership, sound technical judgment across learning-based control, simulation, and hardware, and close partnership with hardware and product teams.

**ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES or KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES**

* Lead, hire, and grow a team of dexterous manipulation engineers, building a culture of rigor, curiosity, and fast iteration.
* Set the team's roadmap and priorities in line with the company's broader technical strategy, so dexterous capabilities feed directly into the behaviors the company is investing in.
* Advance dexterous manipulation capabilities from research to the robot, with measurable progress on task success for target use cases.
* Partner with the data and infrastructure teams to define how hand-level skills, training data, and learned policies fit together across the stack.
* Guide the team's technical direction by drawing on the expertise of engineers and partner teams, and make clear trade-off decisions across learning-based control, simulation, and teleoperation.
* Work with hardware teams to turn manipulation needs into requirements for future hands, sensing, and actuation.
* Establish practices that carry promising research from simulation to hardware, backed by clear metrics and evaluation.
* Communicate progress, risks, and resource needs clearly to leadership and partner teams.

**SKILLS AND REQUIREMENTS**

* **Dexterous Manipulation:** Strong background in robotic manipulation, ideally with multi-fingered hands, and the technical depth to evaluate approaches and guide decisions.
* **Learning-Based Robotics:** Working knowledge of current research in reinforcement learning, imitation learning, and learned visuomotor policies for robotics, and how they fit into a broader strategy to deliver robot skills.
* **Hardware Deployment:** A track record of taking manipulation capabilities from simulation or research to physical robots.
* **People Leadership:** Experience as a technical leader and mentoring other engineers; prior experience managing engineers is a plus.
* **Execution:** Ability to set a roadmap, break it into deliverable work, and drive it to results across teams.
* **Communication:** Clear communicator who aligns partner teams and represents the team well to leadership.

**EDUCATION and/or EXPERIENCE**

* MS/PhD in Robotics, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent experience).
* 5+ years of relevant experience in robotic manipulation or robot learning (or 3+ years with a PhD), including 2+ years mentoring engineers or leading a technical team.
* A proven track record of deploying dexterous manipulation capabilities on physical hardware.

**PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
* Must be able to lift 15 pounds at times
* Vision to read printed materials and a computer screen
* Hearing and speech to communicate

The salary range for this position is $300,000 - $330,000.
