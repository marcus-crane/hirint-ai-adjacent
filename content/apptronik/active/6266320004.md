Apptronik is a human-centered robotics company developing AI-powered robots to support humanity in every facet of life. Our flagship humanoid robot, Apollo, is built to collaborate thoughtfully with people, starting with critical industries such as manufacturing and logistics, with future applications in healthcare, the home, and beyond.  
  
We operate at the cutting edge of Applied AI, applying our expertise across the full robotics stack to solve some of society's most important problems. You will join a team dedicated to bringing Apollo to market at scale, tackling the complex challenges like safety, commercialization, and mass production to change the world for the better.

### JOB SUMMARY:

Apptronik is seeking a Staff Mechanical Engineer to design the torso of Apollo, our humanoid robot. This is a hands-on, high-impact individual contributor role. You will own the torso's mechanical design from architecture through verification.

The torso is the robot's core. It must protect high-performance compute and high-density batteries from impact, carry the loads that pass between the arms, head and legs, and house sensitive perception hardware, all inside a compact, dynamic and thermally constrained frame. This role requires deep structural and packaging expertise, and the ability to turn visionary industrial design into high-fidelity, functional A-surfaces.

### ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

* **Torso Architecture & Structural Design**
  + Torso Ownership: Own the mechanical design of the torso assembly, including architecture, detailed design, integration and verification.
  + Structural Chassis: Design the primary torso chassis to protect internal electronics from high-energy impact events such as falls. Use advanced materials and topology optimization to balance strength, stiffness and mass.
  + Load Path & Interfaces: Define the structural load paths and mechanical interfaces between the torso and the shoulders, neck and pelvis/waist, so loads from every limb are managed cleanly.
  + Mass & Rigidity Trade-offs: Run trade-off studies on mass distribution, structural rigidity and packaging volume, so the torso supports the robot's overall dynamic performance.
* **High-Density Packaging & Integration**
  + Subsystem Packaging: Lead the tight-space integration of the compute cluster (NVIDIA Jetson/SoMs), battery modules and perception sensors within the torso.
  + Thermal Management: Design heat paths, venting and airflow for compute and batteries while maintaining IP (Ingress Protection) requirements.
  + Serviceability: Design for assembly and field service so modules can be installed, removed and replaced without disturbing the rest of the robot.
* **Interconnect & Cable Management**
  + Harness Routing: Architect the routing strategy for wire harnesses running through and out of the torso, including the high-flex shoulder and neck interfaces.
  + Reliability: Design for multi-million cycle reliability and signal integrity, working closely with the electrical team.
* **Industrial Design Intent & A-Surfaces**
  + ID Partnership: Work directly with Industrial Design to turn design intent into final torso A-surfaces. Make sure mechanical constraints (draft, wall thickness, cooling vents, fasteners and split lines) do not compromise the Apollo design language and brand identity.
* **Verification & Collaboration**
  + Design Verification: Plan and execute the Design Verification Test (DVT) work for the torso, including environmental, vibration, impact and lifecycle testing.
  + Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with controls, electrical, software, perception, thermal and manufacturing engineers to make sure the torso meets system-level requirements.
  + Design Reviews: Prepare for and take part in design reviews, bringing data, analysis and prototypes to support design decisions.

### SKILLS AND REQUIREMENTS

* **Structural Design Expertise:** Proven track record designing load-bearing, impact-resistant structures. Experience with FEA and topology optimization, and with advanced materials such as aluminum, magnesium, composites and high-performance plastics.
* **Packaging Mastery:** Exceptional skills in tight-space integration of electronics, batteries and sensors. Experience with ruggedized electronics packaging or automotive/aerospace interiors is a plus.
* **Surfacing Expertise:** Proven ability to work with Class-A surfaces and complex geometries, with expert-level proficiency in the surfacing tools of SolidWorks, NX, CATIA or similar.
* **Thermal & Environmental Design:** Experience designing for heat dissipation, venting and ingress protection in compact enclosures.
* **Cable & Harness Design:** Experience routing harnesses for reliability through dynamic, high-flex interfaces.
* **Mechanism Experience:** Experience designing multi-axis robotic mechanisms or high-DOF dynamic systems, and the ability to understand how torso structure and interfaces affect them.
* **Collaboration:** A strong collaborator who communicates clearly with industrial design, electrical, controls, software and manufacturing partners.

### EDUCATION and/or EXPERIENCE:

* Bachelor’s degree (or equivalent) in Mechanical Engineering or related field
* 8+ years of experience in development of Structures, Mechanisms, Electronic packaging, or similar, specifically within the robotics, automotive, or aerospace industries
