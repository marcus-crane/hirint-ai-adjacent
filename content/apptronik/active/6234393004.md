Apptronik is a human-centered robotics company developing AI-powered robots to support humanity in every facet of life. Our flagship humanoid robot, Apollo, is built to collaborate thoughtfully with people, starting with critical industries such as manufacturing and logistics, with future applications in healthcare, the home, and beyond.  
  
We operate at the cutting edge of Applied AI, applying our expertise across the full robotics stack to solve some of society's most important problems. You will join a team dedicated to bringing Apollo to market at scale, tackling the complex challenges like safety, commercialization, and mass production to change the world for the better.

### **Role Overview**

Apptronik is building advanced humanoid robots at scale, and manufacturing test is a critical part of ensuring reliability, quality, and production readiness across all robot subsystems.

We are seeking a Manager, Test Operations to be a hands-on technical leader to drive development, maintainability, and scalable hardware and software integrated solutions used for manufacturing and validation testing of robotic systems. This role will work closely with test and validation engineers, systems engineers, firmware developers, manufacturing, and operations teams to implement robust and maintainable test infrastructure supporting products from early NPI through production ramp.

This is a highly hands-on role focused on test automation, systems integration and manufacturing support in a fast-paced robotics environment.

### **Responsibilities**

**Manufacturing Test Development**

* Lead the team that deploys manufacturing test software and hardware for:
  + PCBAs
  + Actuators
  + Cable harnesses
  + Electromechanical subassemblies
  + Full robot system testing
* Ensure the team develops scalable and modular test frameworks used across multiple test stations and product lines
* Focus the team on delivering automated functional tests, hardware validation tools, and diagnostic utilities to the manufacturing floor.
* Establish criteria for APIs and interfaces between test software and hardware systems
* Own the testing process reliability, maintainability, and deployment consistency across manufacturing environments
* Champion flawless integration of:
  + Embedded systems
  + Firmware interfaces
  + Sensors and actuators
  + Motion control systems
  + Data acquisition hardware
* Establish technical direction for hardware bring-up, debug activities, troubleshooting and communication issues in manufacturing environments
* Collaborate with test and validation engineering teams to ensure products are designed with testability and manufacturability in mind
* Drive best practices and process improvements within the test organization
* Ensure test documentation, procedures, and architecture diagrams are maintained
* Implement code reviews and software release processes (CI/CD)

**Manufacturing Operations Support**

* Deliver processes and key metrics that provide actionable data for:

+ Ensuring internal manufacturing operations and contract manufacturers are fully supported for deployment, maintenance, and troubleshooting of manufacturing test stations
+ Supporting rapidly changing production ramps and resolving manufacturing test issues to minimize downtime
+ Proposing manufacturing test requirements and workflows for future products

### **Required Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, Robotics, or related field
* 5+ years of experience in leading complex electromechanical system test development for automated manufacturing systems
* Excellent communication and stakeholder management; able to represent the team to senior leadership and customers.
* Proven technical leadership — leading engineers and owning delivery of complex, cross-disciplinary systems.
* Formal people-management experience — hiring, developing, and directing a team.
* Willingness to travel to manufacturing sites, up to 20%
* Experience leading teams engaged with:

+ Embedded systems
+ Hardware/software integration
+ Serial communication protocols
+ Networked systems

* Familiarity with modern software development practices:

+ Git
+ Code reviews
+ CI/CD workflows
+ Issue tracking systems (Jira or similar)

* Strong troubleshooting and problem-solving skills working with complex electromechanical systems
* Comfortable working in fast-paced and evolving environments

### **Preferred Qualifications**

* Experience in robotics, aerospace, automotive, or similar electromechanical systems
* Experience with communication protocols: ROS/ROS2, EtherCAT, CAN, RS485 …etc
* Experience with manufacturing databases, MES systems, or traceability systems
* Experience developing scalable architectures for test infrastructure
* Familiarity with Linux-based development environments
* Experience with automated data analysis and reporting tools
* Experience supporting contract manufacturers or production environments
* Experience using AI-assisted software development tools and workflows
