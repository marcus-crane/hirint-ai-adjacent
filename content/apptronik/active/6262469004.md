Apptronik is a human-centered robotics company developing AI-powered robots to support humanity in every facet of life. Our flagship humanoid robot, Apollo, is built to collaborate thoughtfully with people, starting with critical industries such as manufacturing and logistics, with future applications in healthcare, the home, and beyond.  
  
We operate at the cutting edge of Applied AI, applying our expertise across the full robotics stack to solve some of society's most important problems. You will join a team dedicated to bringing Apollo to market at scale, tackling the complex challenges like safety, commercialization, and mass production to change the world for the better.

## **JOB SUMMARY**

We are looking for a Staff Technical Program Manager to drive delivery of the software that runs on and around Apollo. You will own cross-team programs from requirements through integration, validation, and release onto real robots, keeping software, firmware, and hardware moving on one plan.

This is a high-autonomy, high-visibility role. You will work side by side with engineering leads on architecture and trade-offs, give leadership a clear, honest view of where programs stand, and help shape how the program management team plans and executes.

## **ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES or KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES**

* **Software Program Delivery:** Own end-to-end delivery of multi-team robot software programs, from requirements and architecture through integration, test, and release.
* **Release Management:** Build and run the software release train, including cadence, cut criteria, quality gates, go/no-go reviews, and patches and updates to robots in the lab and at customer sites.
* **Hardware/Software Integration:** Line up software milestones with hardware build phases, plan bring-up of new actuators, sensors, and compute, and manage dependencies across firmware, electrical, and mechanical teams.
* **Test & Quality Infrastructure:** Partner with infrastructure teams on CI/CD, hardware-in-the-loop testing, simulation, and evaluation so every release ships with measurable quality signals.
* **Orchestration:** Manage the critical path, risks, and cross-team dependencies, and bring decisions to leaders as clear options with trade-offs.
* **Communication:** Define program health metrics and report status, risk, and change to executives.
* **Customer & Field Feedback:** Connect commercial and deployment teams to engineering so customer needs and field issues flow into software priorities.
* **Safety-Relevant Software:** Work with safety and quality leads to build processes for safety-relevant software, including AI/ML components.
* **Leadership & Mentorship:** Mentor other TPMs and improve how the program management team plans and executes.

## **SKILLS AND REQUIREMENTS**

* **Robot Software Delivery** — A track record of shipping software that runs on physical hardware, in robotics, autonomous vehicles, drones, automotive, aerospace, consumer electronics, or embedded/IoT.
* **Software Lifecycle & Release** — Deep fluency in the software development lifecycle, including release management and CI/CD, and in running agile teams inside hardware-gated schedules.
* **Technical Depth** — Enough technical depth to engage in architecture discussions on real-time systems, Linux and embedded software, robotics middleware (such as ROS 2), and compute and latency budgets.
* **HW/SW Integration** — Hands-on experience with HW/SW integration and a working understanding of hardware development cycles and build phases.
* **Robotic Systems Programs** — Direct experience delivering programs on robotic systems, taking a robot's software from prototype bring-up through integration and field testing on real hardware.
* **Robot Software Stack** — A systems-level understanding of a robot's software stack, including controls, behaviors, perception and state estimation, planning, and the real-time data pipelines between them.
* **Sensors, Actuators & Compute** — Experience with how actuators, sensors, and embedded compute shape software schedules, performance, and test plans.
* **Validation & Release Decisions** — A track record of planning validation on robots and test rigs and turning test results into clear release decisions.
* **Executive Communication & Influence** — Strong executive communication and the ability to influence without authority.
* **Program Tooling** — Proficiency with program tools such as Jira, Confluence, or Smartsheet.

## **EDUCATION and/or EXPERIENCE**

* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical or Computer Engineering, Robotics, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent experience, required
* 10+ years of experience in software engineering, technical program management, or engineering program management, including 5+ years as a TPM leading multi-team software programs
* Preferred: Experience on humanoid, legged, or mobile manipulation robots, including taking a robot or autonomous system from prototype to production or first customer deployment
* Preferred: Program leadership in controls and behaviors, perception and state estimation, ML and robot learning (including MLOps), or simulation and evaluation
* Preferred: Experience with robot learning, manipulation or dexterous hands, edge AI inference platforms, or shipping ML components into safety-critical robotic or autonomous systems
* Preferred: Experience with safety-relevant software processes, such as functional safety for robots or autonomous vehicles
* Preferred: Experience with fleet operations, over-the-air updates, deploying robots at customer sites, or cloud infrastructure (AWS or GCP, Kubernetes, Docker)
* Preferred: Prior experience as a software engineer, or building PMO processes and growing TPMs at a fast-scaling hardware company
* Preferred: Master's degree in a technical field
