Apptronik is a human-centered robotics company developing AI-powered robots to support humanity in every facet of life. Our flagship humanoid robot, Apollo, is built to collaborate thoughtfully with people, starting with critical industries such as manufacturing and logistics, with future applications in healthcare, the home, and beyond.  
  
We operate at the cutting edge of Applied AI, applying our expertise across the full robotics stack to solve some of society's most important problems. You will join a team dedicated to bringing Apollo to market at scale, tackling the complex challenges like safety, commercialization, and mass production to change the world for the better.

**JOB SUMMARY**

Design, Prototype, and Assemble Humanoid Robots and associated technologies / auxiliary equipment.

**ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES or KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES**

* CAD/Design and manufacture basic jigs/fixtures for testing
* Utilize manual mill/lathe, CNC machines, and 3D Printers
* Harness schematic creation
* Harness assembly
* PCBA soldering and component modification
* Assemble electromechanical subsystems without full documentation
* Collaborate with R&D Engineers and Test/Validation Engineers to identify and solve complex problems
* Assist with Test designs and Test implementation
* Document issues and/or assembly processes to be utilized by production technicians as needed

**SKILLS AND REQUIREMENTS**

* Proven hands-on skills and electromechanical aptitude
* Proficient in reading and interpreting technical drawings, schematics, and specifications
* Excellent problem-solving skills
* Electrical and/or Mechanical Technician certifications are a plus
* Proficient in using hand tools and torque devices
* Initiative and self-motivation - ability to work with little supervision
* Collaborative nature with the ability to work in a team environment
* Basic computer skills
* Good written communication
* Strong work ethic and commitment to safety
* Willingness to work flexible hours, including evenings and weekends, as needed

**EDUCATION and/or EXPERIENCE**

* Associate’s Degree in Mechanical, Electrical, or similar field
* At least 2 years of experience in electrical and/or mechanical assembly or related field

**PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS**

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
* Must be able to lift 50 pounds at times
* Vision to read printed materials and a computer screen
* Hearing and speech to communicate
