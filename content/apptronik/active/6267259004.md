Apptronik is a human-centered robotics company developing AI-powered robots to support humanity in every facet of life. Our flagship humanoid robot, Apollo, is built to collaborate thoughtfully with people, starting with critical industries such as manufacturing and logistics, with future applications in healthcare, the home, and beyond.  
  
We operate at the cutting edge of Applied AI, applying our expertise across the full robotics stack to solve some of society's most important problems. You will join a team dedicated to bringing Apollo to market at scale, tackling the complex challenges like safety, commercialization, and mass production to change the world for the better.

**JOB SUMMARY**

Apptronik is seeking a Mechanical Engineer – Structural Simulation to support the structural integrity, mass optimization, and dynamic performance of our humanoid robot, Apollo. In this role, you will contribute to simulation efforts that help move designs from concept to validated hardware while reducing the need for extensive physical prototyping.

You will use FEA and related tools to support analysis of physics challenges such as impact dynamics, fatigue in high-DOF joints, and multi-body dynamics. You’ll work closely with senior engineers and cross-functional teams, learning how system-level behaviors connect to the physical capabilities of the robot’s structure.

**ESSENTIAL DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES or KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES**

* Simulation Workflows: Contribute to the development and documentation of simulation workflows and methodologies across the team.
* Early-Stage Analysis: Support the integration of simulation into early design decisions by running studies and sharing results with design engineers.
* Modeling Standards: Follow and help refine team standards for modeling (materials, boundary conditions, safety factors).
* Non-Linear & Dynamic FEA: Assist with non-linear and dynamic simulations (e.g., robot falls, extreme events) under the guidance of senior engineers.
* Fatigue & Life Prediction: Support fatigue life analyses and contribute to the evaluation of life prediction methods.
* Mass Optimization: Contribute to mass optimization efforts by running design studies that balance stiffness, durability, and weight targets.
* Test/Sim Correlation: Participate in test and simulation correlation activities, helping compare results to test data so models become more predictive over time.
* Material Characterization: Assist with material characterization efforts, including data collection and model inputs for advanced materials.
* Collaboration & Learning: Work with senior engineers to grow your technical depth, and participate in technical design reviews.
* Cross-Disciplinary Support: Collaborate with mechanical, controls, and test teams to help identify and resolve physics-related design issues, and contribute simulation inputs to trade studies.

**SKILLS AND REQUIREMENTS**

* Software: Working knowledge of industry-standard simulation tools (e.g., Ansys, Abaqus, SimCenter).
* Physics Fundamentals: Strong grounding in solid mechanics, with exposure to vibration (shock and modal analysis), heat transfer, and multi-body dynamics.
* Humanoid Context (preferred): Exposure to high-speed moving mechanisms or lightweight structures for mobile platforms.

**EDUCATION and/or EXPERIENCE**

* Experience: 4-6 years of experience in structural simulation and/or mechanical design, preferably on electromechanical products (robotics, aerospace, or automotive). Internship, co-op, or graduate research experience will be considered.
* Education: BS or MS in Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, or a related field. Coursework or research in computational mechanics is a plus.

**PHYSICAL REQUIREMENTS**

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
* Must be able to lift 15 pounds at times
* Vision to read printed materials and a computer screen
* Hearing and speech to communicate
