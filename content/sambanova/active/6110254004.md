SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

### **About the role**

SambaNova Systems is looking for a talented and motivated **ASIC** **Network Architect** to help us solve the most challenging and impactful problems in machine learning and artificial intelligence.

### Responsibilities

* Conduct research and development in state of the art networking solutions
* Define and own networking architecture specifications for performant, robust, and efficient communications on the next generation super-pod scale ML hardware
* Define and own the ML networking roadmap, leading a cross-functional effort across software, systems, and silicon
* Work with executive leadership to align network initiatives with product competitiveness requirements
* Track and contribute to industry standards, representing SambaNova

### Required Qualifications

* B.S. Computer Science, Computer Engineering or Electrical Engineering
* 10+ years of industry experience in ML silicon development in GPU, ML accelerator or networking ASIC
* Proven track record of successful full-cycle development and delivery from planning to product in networking for such silicon solutions
* Track record of ownership and definition of various networking and routing protocols
* Deep understanding and hands-on experience defining various networking topologies for large machine clusters.
* Deep understanding of congestion management, QoS, security isolation
* Deep understanding of networking reliability, availability and serviceability
* Capable of abstracting and analyzing network performance from real world application data
* Capable of breaking down networking requirements into silicon, packaging, system SW, RunTime and Compiler SW requirements
* Strong hands-on analytical and debug skills to make progress on challenging problems in an ambiguous and highly dynamic environment
* Strong coding skills with C++, Python, or Rust
* Highly motivated, self-driven, independent, organized and strong collaboration and communication skills
* Cross-functional team leadership experience

### Preferred Qualifications

* M.S. or PHD degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering or Electrical Engineering
* 15+ years of industry experience in ML / supercomputer / public cloud / networking industries
* Good understanding of ASIC development cycles
* Knowledge of machine learning architecture and/or computer architecture
* Experience in building or proficiently using network simulators
* Experience in leading silicon, packaging, system HW, system SW cross-functional teams
* Experience in conducting perf and perf/tco trade-off analysis on different interconnect choices
* Familiarity with off the shelf optical IP and optical switches
* Understanding / hands-on experience in end to end optical systems
* Hands-on experience in working with IP/Switch hardware vendor to enhance / customize solutions
