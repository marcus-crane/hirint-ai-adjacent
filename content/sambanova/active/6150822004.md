SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

### About the team

The Talent Acquisition team works with SambaNova leadership to build the teams behind the Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit (RDU), the chip powering our full-stack AI platform.

### About the role

We are looking for a Senior Technical Recruiter to partner with hardware engineering. You'll own full lifecycle recruiting for Design Verification, ASIC design, physical design, and PCB/systems engineering, working directly with hiring managers to define roles, calibrate pipelines, and close top talent in a competitive market.

This is 6-month contract with the option to extend or covert to a full-time position based on performance.

### Responsibilities

* Partner with hardware hiring managers to define roles, write job descriptions, calibrate pipeline, and design interviews
* Source passive silicon talent through LinkedIn, GitHub, referrals, conferences, and targeted outreach into the semiconductor community
* Own full lifecycle recruiting for hardware roles across Design Verification, ASIC/RTL design, physical design, and PCB/systems engineering
* Keep feedback loops tight across interviewers and stakeholders
* Maintain accurate data in Greenhouse and track the metrics that matter

### Required Experience

* 5+ years of technical recruiting experience focused on semiconductors
* Proven track record filling roles such as Design Verification, ASIC/RTL Design, and Physical Design
* Demonstrated success sourcing and closing passive talent
* Existing network within the silicon engineering community
* Ability to influence senior engineering leadership
* Excellent creative writing skills
* Strong organizational skills
* AI fluency with the ability to enhance productivity and workflows
* Proficiency with Greenhouse
