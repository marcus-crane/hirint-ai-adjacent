## **About the Role**

As a member of the Growth and Digital Marketing team, this role owns the content engine behind our digital channels. Working closely with the product marketing team, you will identify high-value topics, write most of it, and make sure it attracts attention once it’s published. You'll manage the editorial calendar and the content on [sambanova.ai](http://sambanova.ai), and create the assets demand gen depends on, including landing pages, gated reports, nurture copy, and campaign narratives.

This is a hands-on role that requires a strong writer. Working with external resources, you'll use search and performance data to understand what content resonates with target audiences. Success looks like a content program that measurably grows organic traffic, keyword rankings, and pipeline.

## **What You’ll Do**

### **WEB & ORGANIC SEARCH**

* Own content on [sambanova.ai](http://sambanova.ai), including product pages, solution pages, resource hub, and blog, keeping it accurate as the products and messaging evolves.
* Conduct keyword research and build an SEO content roadmap that maps to high search rankings.
* Handle on-page SEO end-to-end: titles, meta descriptions, heading structure, internal linking, schema, and image optimization.
* Partner with the web project manager and design team on the architecture, performance improvements; and test headlines, CTAs, and layouts.
* Audit existing web page content quarterly, and refresh as needed.

### **FULL-FUNNEL CONTENT**

* Plan and manage the editorial calendar across blog, web, and email, coordinating with product marketing, developer relations, social, and comms.
* Write and edit content across formats: technical blog posts, customer case studies, whitepapers, and newsletters.
* Translate engineering and product input into narratives a non-specialist executive can follow and an AI engineer won't dismiss.
* Maintain voice, terminology, and style standards; and edit work from freelancers, agencies, and internal subject-matter experts.
* Repurpose content. Strong assets can translate into a blog series or email program.

### **DEMAND GENERATION & CAMPAIGNS**

* Create campaign content: landing pages, gated assets, ad copy, webinar abstracts, and multi-touch nurture sequences.
* Work with demand gen and marketing ops to map content to funnel stage and buyer segment, and close the gaps that show up in the journey.

## **What We’re Looking For**

* 4-6 years of experience in content marketing, B2B tech preferred, with published work to share.
* Bachelor’s degree
* Working SEO fluency. Keyword research, on-page optimization, content structure for search and LLM answer engines.
* Demand gen literacy. Understanding campaign frameworks and how to optimize for conversion.
* Analytical habits. Leverage GA4 to evaluate content performance.
* Hands-on tooling with HubSpot CMS.
* Project ownership. Ability to prioritize and meet multiple deadlines for multiple stakeholders on time.
* Curiosity about technology - particularly AI/ML. No CS degree required, but able to understand technology to convey benefits to target audiences.

* Basic design or video literacy (Wistia, Canva, light editing) for fast turnarounds.
