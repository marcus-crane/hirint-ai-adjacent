SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

### **About the Role**

We are seeking a hands-on Rack-level Manufacturing Test Engineer to design, develop, implement test strategies and optimize for fully integrated rack systems. Main focus will be on end-to-end testing of compute, power, cooling and networking components testing at the rack level.

You will work cross-functionally with engineering, manufacturing, and suppliers to build robust automated test solutions, reduce test escapes, and support new product introduction (NPI) to production.

### **Responsibilities**

**Test Development and Automation**

* Rack Test Development: Design, build, and optimize automated manufacturing test sequences specifically tailored for integrated system racks (e.g., L10 to L12 integration levels).
* Infrastructure & Provisioning: Develop robust orchestration environments to automate firmware flashing (BIOS/BMC), network switch configuration, and operating system deployment at scale.
* Power & Thermal Validation: Implement rack-level stress testing, including load-balancing validation, thermal throttling assessments, and power shelf/PDU (Power Distribution Unit) margin testing.

**Automated Test Infrastructure**

* Scripting & Automation: Write and maintain test scripts using Python, Bash, or Go to automate system diagnostic tools, network topology validation, and peripheral checks.
* Diagnostic Tools: Integrate system diagnostics and stress-testing frameworks into the manufacturing execution system (MES).
* Data Analysis & Yield Monitoring: Analyze rack-level parametric test data, monitor manufacturing yields, and establish automated pass/fail criteria to catch assembly or component-level escapes.

**Manufacturing Support**

* Provide hands-on support to manufacturing test technicians for troubleshooting and repair
* Train and guide manufacturing teams on proper use of test equipment and fixtures
* Ensure stability and uptime of test systems on the production floor

**New Product Introduction (NPI) and Global Operations**

* New Product Introduction (NPI): Collaborate with Hardware Design and Software Engineering teams during early prototype phases to ensure the product design supports rack-level testability (DFR/DFT).
* Root Cause Analysis: Drive complex triage efforts for failure analysis at the rack system level, identifying whether issues stem from cabling, firmware mismatches, network configurations, or component defects.
* Factory Deployment: Deploy and support test lines at global contract manufacturing (CM) partner sites, creating clear documentation, standard operating procedures (SOPs), and training for factory technicians.

### **Required Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering (BSEE) or related field
* 5–7+ years of experience in system-level or rack-level manufacturing test engineering
* Strong hands-on experience with manufacturing test systems and troubleshooting
* Experience with automated test development and test equipment integration
* Strong problem-solving skills and ability to work cross-functionally

### **Preferred Qualifications**

* Experience with hardware/software integration testing
* Familiarity with test frameworks, scripting, and automation tools
* Experience working with contract manufacturers and global production environments
* Knowledge of quality and reliability methodologies (e.g., root cause analysis, DFx)
* Experience supporting high-volume manufacturing and product ramp
* Experience with data center hardware operations
