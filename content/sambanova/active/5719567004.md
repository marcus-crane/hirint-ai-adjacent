SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

### **About the Team**

The Cloud Platform team runs SambaNova’s production inference service. We handle capacity planning, deployment, monitoring, and incident response across regions in the United States, Asia, Europe, and Latin America.

**About the Role**

As a Principal Cloud Platform Engineer, you will design and build the software that deploys, schedules, and scales model workloads across our shared accelerator hardware, staying hands-on in the code while leading the technical design. You will set uptime and latency targets for our inference endpoints and share in the on-call rotation that keeps them running.

**Responsibilities**

* Shared ownership of the production inference service across regions, covering availability, latency, performance, change management, and capacity planning
* Designing and building platform services, Kubernetes operators, and controllers that deploy, schedule, and scale model workloads
* Designing scheduling for shared accelerator capacity, including quotas, priority, and fairness between customers
* Rolling out upgrades without disrupting running workloads, and defining how the service recovers when a node or worker fails
* Designing platform security, including tenant isolation, workload identity, and secrets management
* Standing up new regions with infrastructure as code in Terraform and Ansible, and building CI/CD pipelines for model and service deployments
* Building monitoring and alerting in Prometheus, Grafana, and Datadog, and defining SLOs and SLIs that drive reliability work
* Finding and eliminating performance bottlenecks, and designing auto-scaling for variable inference loads
* Leading incident response and postmortems as part of a shared primary/secondary on-call rotation
* Forecasting infrastructure needs and working with finance to manage cloud spend
