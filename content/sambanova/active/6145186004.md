The era of pervasive AI has arrived. In this era, organizations will use generative AI to unlock hidden value in their data, accelerate processes, reduce costs, drive efficiency and innovation to fundamentally transform their businesses and operations at scale.

SambaNova Suite™ is the first full-stack, generative AI platform, from chip to model, optimized for enterprise and government organizations. Powered by the intelligent SN40L chip, the SambaNova Suite is a fully integrated platform, delivered on-premises or in the cloud, combined with state-of-the-art open-source models that can be easily and securely fine-tuned using customer data for greater accuracy. Once adapted with customer data, customers retain model ownership in perpetuity, so they can turn generative AI into one of their most valuable assets.

### **About the team**

Our SambaStack inference serving platform is pushing the boundaries of generative AI and large language models. We are a team of passionate innovators tackling some of the world’s most challenging computational problems.

### **About the role**

SambaNova is hiring Principal Software Engineers for the SambaStack platform. We help enterprises and service providers host their own AI inference platforms, powered by our state-of-the-art RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) architecture. Our cloud-agnostic, enterprise-grade inference serving platform enables seamless deployment, management, and scaling of foundation model workloads at production scale.

As a Principal Software Engineer on the SambaStack team, you will:

* Improve performance, scalability, and reliability
* Introduce new features and platform capabilities
* Design and build components for a state-of-the-art inference serving platform
* Drive technical execution across critical infrastructure initiatives

This is a high-impact, high-visibility role at the intersection of:

* AI infrastructure
* Enterprise software
* Developer experience

### **Responsibilities**

Some of your responsibilities will include:

* Ensure stability, scalability, and performance of the AI inference platform
* Collaborate with ML, Product Managers, QA, and DevOps teams
* Develop components using Python, Go, and Rust
* Build and improve testing frameworks and automation
* Design and implement systems for new AI inference challenges
* Drive engineering excellence and software quality across the platform

### **Basic qualifications**

* 8-12 years of software engineering experience
* Experience building backend systems in Python, Go, or Rust
* Strong experience with Unit testing, Integration testing, and End-to-end testing frameworks
* Solid understanding of Kubernetes and Helm Charts
* Strong understanding of distributed systems and experience building scalable, reliable services
* Excellent problem-solving skills and attention to detail
* Ability to collaborate across cross-functional teams

### **Preferred qualifications**

* Experience with Kubernetes ecosystem, including:
* Familiarity with AI inference serving and orchestration

* Ability to design and build cloud-native systems
