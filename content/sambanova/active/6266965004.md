SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

We are building a next-generation AI inference accelerator and the rack-scale system around it, in Japan, on a leading-edge domestic 2nm process. The work is funded through a multi-year national programme to establish sovereign AI infrastructure, and runs from architecture definition through test silicon, full chip tape-out, and deployment of production racks.

We are looking for the engineering leader who will build and run the Japan team, deliver the programme from architecture through silicon and rack deployment, and be SambaNova's senior engineering voice to programme sponsors and partners in Japan. You will work closely with the US HQ engineering team.

## **What you will own**

* End-to-end delivery of the chip and its system, from silicon through system and rack implementation and deployment.
* The Japan engineering organization: hiring plan, team structure, budget, engineering infrastructure and culture.
* Programme execution against the national programme's milestones, stage gates and reporting obligations.
* The working model between the Japan team and US HQ engineering, and the engineering relationships with sponsors, foundry, IP, packaging and system partners.

## **Responsibilities**

### **Programme leadership**

* Drive the integrated schedule, budget and resource plan with the US HQ engineering team, from architecture definition through test chip, full chip tape-out and production rack deployment.
* Run programme reviews, track progress against milestones and stage gates, and manage technical and schedule risks, dependencies and escalations.
* Own programme reporting to the funding agency, including milestone reports, technical reviews and audits.
* Make the trade-off calls when schedule, scope, cost and technical risk conflict.

### **Technical oversight across the chip development flow**

* Drive the architecture, microarchitecture and RTL plans with the Chief Architect and the engineering teams.
* Work with the team on verification strategy and sign-off criteria, physical design closure (timing, power, area, DFT), and tape-out readiness reviews.
* Plan and lead post-silicon bring-up and silicon validation, from the first test chip through production silicon.

### **Team and site leadership**

* Build the Japan silicon engineering team: recruit, hire, onboard, develop and retain architects, RTL designers, and verification, physical design and silicon validation engineers.
* Hire and manage the Chief Architect and functional leads, and give them room to make technical decisions.
* Establish engineering processes, EDA and compute infrastructure, and security and compliance practices for the site.
* Set the division of work between the Japan team and engineering groups elsewhere, and make that division work in practice across time zones.

### **Partner and sponsor management**

* Represent SambaNova engineering to programme sponsors, government stakeholders, the foundry, and IP, EDA, packaging and system partners, in Japanese and in English.
* Manage the engineering side of partner contracts, deliverables and joint development plans.

## **Minimum qualifications**

* 20 or more years in semiconductor engineering, including 10 or more years leading engineering teams and managers.
* Hands-on technical background across the chip development flow: high-level architecture, design and verification, physical design, and silicon validation. You need not be the deepest expert in each area, but you must be able to lead each discipline credibly.
* Led at least two complex SoCs or accelerators from concept through tape-out to working production silicon, as the accountable engineering or programme leader.
* Owned schedules, budgets and resource plans for multi-year, multi-team silicon programmes.
* Built or scaled an engineering team, including hiring senior technical leaders.
* Experience managing teams and partners across multiple sites and time zones.
* Fluent Japanese and professional working English. The role operates in Japanese with partners and sponsors in Japan, and in English with engineering teams outside Japan.

## **Preferred qualifications**

* System-level experience, from package design through server and rack integration and system bring-up.
* Experience taking a product from silicon into manufacturing and volume production, including working with contract manufacturers and the supply chain.
* Background in AI accelerators, GPUs, or high-performance CPUs and SoCs, and in large language model inference systems.
* Experience on a leading-edge node where the process design kit, libraries and interface IP were still maturing during the project.
* Established relationships across the Japanese semiconductor ecosystem: foundry, packaging, IP, EDA and system partners.
* Experience delivering inside a publicly funded research programme (for example, METI/NEDO), with its milestone structure and reporting obligations.
* Advanced degree in electrical engineering, computer engineering or computer science.
