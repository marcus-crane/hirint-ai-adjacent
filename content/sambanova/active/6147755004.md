SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

## About the Role

SambaNova Systems is looking for a Data Center Deployment Engineer to join our field engineering team. In this customer-facing role, you will own the end-to-end deployment of SambaNova's rack-scale AI systems at customer data centers across Japan and the region. You'll serve as the technical authority on-site, ensuring that power, cooling, networking, and physical infrastructure are ready to support our hardware before, during, and after deployment.

This is a hands-on, high-visibility role that sits at the intersection of infrastructure engineering and customer success. You'll work closely with customers, their data center operators, and internal teams to make deployments go smoothly.

## What You'll Do

* Serve as the primary technical point of contact for customers during rack product deployments, covering all aspects of physical site readiness including power, cooling, networking, and data center services.
* Conduct pre-deployment site assessments to identify potential infrastructure issues and work with customers to resolve them before hardware arrives.
* Develop and communicate cooling plans appropriate to the power and heat load of SambaNova rack systems, with a primary focus on front-to-rear air-cooled environments.
* Validate data center power infrastructure for compatibility with SambaNova systems, including 208V and 415V AC environments.
* Partner with data center operators at facilities to help coordinate logistics, cage/rack assignments, cross-connects, and remote hands.
* Validate customer networking infrastructure, including verification of cabling, uplink switch details, and protocols required for AI workloads.
* Document deployment procedures, site configurations, and any issues encountered for internal knowledge-sharing and customer records.
* Communicate deployment status, risks, and findings clearly to both technical and non-technical customer stakeholders.
* Assist with on-going customer support at a rack and system level.
* Provide input on future product design at a rack level.

## What We're Looking For

### Data Center Infrastructure

* General logistics experience for large hardware deployments: coordinating deliveries, staging, and installation in live data center environments.
* Solid understanding of data center AC power at 208V and 415V and work with customer facilities teams.
* Experience planning and validating cooling solutions for high-density compute, with knowledge of front-to-rear airflow design.
* Familiarity with the operational requirements and processes of major colocation providers (Equinix, NTT Global Data Centers, DigitalRealty, or similar).

### Networking

* Working knowledge of data center network topologies, including three-tier and leaf-spine/Clos designs.
* Familiarity with Ethernet networking protocols including BGP, LACP, VRRP, VLAN, VXLAN, and VPN.
* Understanding of RDMA over Converged Ethernet (RoCE) principles and its relevance to high-performance AI workloads.
* Experience with high-speed data center switching platforms; Juniper and Arista experience is a plus.

### Systems

* Extensive hands-on administrative experience with Red Hat Linux, or other rpm-based Linux distributions, in production environments. This includes system configuration, troubleshooting, and performance tuning.
* Proficiency with BMC, service processor, and IPMI-based out-of-band systems management.
* Familiarity with RAS (reliability, availability, and serviceability)  design at a rack level.

### Additional

* Familiarity with AI and machine learning concepts and terminology sufficient to communicate effectively with AI infrastructure teams.
* Fluent Japanese speaker with strong written and verbal English communication skills.
* Excellent presentation and interpersonal skills; able to build trust and credibility with customer executives and technical teams alike.
* Demonstrated knowledge of Shell scripting or Python.
* Strong organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple deployments and competing priorities.

## Nice to Have

* Experience with DC power systems in data center environments.
* Hands-on experience with liquid cooling technologies.
