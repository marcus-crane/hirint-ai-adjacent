SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

SambaNova Systems, a leader in AI computing, is hiring a Senior Manager, Finance to own planning, analytics and operational finance support across the company. You will join a small, high-performance team where FP&A and strategic finance sit together, so the scope is broad and the exposure is real: you will work directly with senior executives, including the CRO and the COO, and your recommendations will inform critical strategic decisions.

You will partner with sales, business development, operations, engineering,  and marketing leaders to deliver planning, analytics and finance support at every level of decision-making. This role suits someone who is passionate about FP&A, thrives in a fast-paced, feedback-driven environment, and wants a company that will invest in their growth.

**What you will do**

* Run an efficient process for the annual and quarterly financial plans, linked to company strategic objectives. Contribute to growth strategy and mid- and long-range planning
* Build financial models, dashboards and process flows that turn data into decisions. Produce the monthly reporting package: financial statements, variance analysis with executive-level commentary.  Continue Improving forecasting accuracy and visibility
* Partner with sales, operations, engineering, business development and marketing leaders, helping prepare their budgets, forecasts and ad-hoc analyses
* Evaluate impact of new products, operating models, contracts, marketing programs and technology investments. Analyze pricing, product margins, cost of revenue and operating expenses to find ways to optimize them.  Compute monthly sales commissions
* Support the accounting close process and work with the rest of the Accounting & Finance teams. Raise reporting standards through continuous process improvement

**What you will bring**

* Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, or a related field; MBA and/or CPA preferred
* 8+ years of progressive FP&A or corporate finance experience at a high-growth technology company, or in investment banking or consulting
* Proven strength in financial and strategic planning: budgeting, reporting, financial modeling, capital budgeting, and working knowledge of corporate accounting
* In-depth financial statement analysis and a solid grasp of corporate finance concepts
* A track record of working with executive management and communicating clearly at every level of the organization including building presentations and analyses from scratch

**Nice to have**

* Hands-on experience with NetSuite, Abacum and Adaptive Planning
* Experience with hardware or semiconductor business models, including system cost and bill of materials, gross margin, inventory and supply chain finance
* Experience calculating sales commissions or supporting public-company readiness
* Working knowledge of SQL or Python and a BI tool (Tableau, Power BI or Looker) to automate reporting and analysis

**Who thrives here**

* Self-motivated, with a strong work ethic and the initiative to head off problems before they surface
* A consultative, influential communicator and a good listener who builds trust with executives and peers
* Highly organized and detail-oriented, able to juggle competing deadlines and make sound judgments under pressure
* A team player who is also high-achieving, positive and comfortable in a fast-paced, fast-changing environment where feedback is frequent
