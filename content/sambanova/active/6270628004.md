SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

SambaNova is looking for senior engineers with strong AI/ML and software fundamentals, an affinity for working with customers, and at least 5 years of industry experience engaging with customers or building production-grade software. Our team helps customers map, optimize, and deploy the latest generative AI models, both frontier open-weight and customer-specific models, on SambaNova’s dataflow hardware. In this role, you will own customer model bring-ups end to end, from understanding the inference serving needs of a customer all the way through the model optimization and full-scale AI deployments. The work is agent-first where you build and guide agentic workflows through mapping, optimization, and debugging. You will gather telemetry to feed our RSI (Recursive Self-Improvement) pipelines and guide how the pipelines are designed based on customer insights and goals. Our engineering teams will look to you for valuable insight in shaping our tooling and shortening the time it takes to enable new models. As a Senior Field Application Engineer, you are the technical link between some of our most important customers and our internal compiler and performance engineering teams. You will also act as a technical lead for the other Field Application Engineers on the team, setting the standards for how we run bring-ups and mentoring early-career engineers through their first customer engagements.

# **What you’ll be doing**

**Customer engagement**

* Be the technical point of contact for customers on the models you enable, from first request to production.
* Handle customers' benchmarking and performance questions, and present results clearly to technical and non-technical audiences.
* Bring structured customer feedback to internal teams to shape our tools and roadmap.

**Hands-on bring-up**

* Bring up customer-requested models end to end: multi-chip mapping, compile, strict numerical validation against the reference, and serving with measured latency and throughput.
* Work agent-first. Direct AI agents through bring-up and debugging. Build workflows for automated oversight, guardrails, and RSI.
* Benchmark models against performance targets, profile them when they fall short, and work with our compiler and performance engineers to overcome those challenges.
* Contribute to our SDK and libraries to speed up support for new model architectures and inference-serving features.
* Serve as a technical lead for other FAEs, reviewing their bring-ups, setting shared practices for agentic workflows, and mentoring early-career engineers.

**Debugging and diagnosis**

* Take ownership of customer-reported issues: reproduce them, trace the root cause across the model, compiler, runtime, and hardware, and guide customers until the fix lands.
* Learn from each investigation and iterate on reusable diagnostic tools so the next issue is found faster. Along the way, you’ll build a deep understanding of AI workloads and SambaNova’s dataflow architecture that they run on.

# **What we need to see**

* A BS, MS, or PhD in CS, Math, ML, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
* 5+ years of industry experience as a software engineer, ML/AI engineer, customer engineer, field application engineer, forward-deployed engineer, or in another role building customer-facing AI/ML or software solutions.
* Strong Python, hands-on PyTorch, and comfort working on Linux.
* Solid software engineering principles: readable, tested, and maintainable code, version control, and code review.
* ML fundamentals: linear algebra, neural network architectures, and enough floating-point numerics to tell a real accuracy bug from expected precision loss.
* A working understanding of transformer inference: KV caching, attention variants, MoE routing, prefill vs. decode, batching, and optimization techniques such as quantization and speculative algorithms.
* Practical experience building software with AI coding tools.
* A methodical approach to debugging, and experience automating repetitive engineering work with scripts or tools.
* Clear communication. You can explain a result to a customer as well as to a compiler engineer.

# **What sets you apart**

* Experience mentoring engineers or leading technical work across a team.
* Experience with inference engines (vLLM / SGLang / TensorRT-LLM), ML compilers (MLIR / XLA / TVM / Triton), or GPU/accelerator kernels.
* Hands-on experience running or debugging workloads on GPUs or other AI accelerators.
* Profiling tools and computer architecture basics, such as memory hierarchy, high-bandwidth memory and interconnects, and accelerator execution models.
* Exposure to distributed systems, such as multi-node serving or collective communication, and the design patterns that keep them reliable.
* Quantization, speculative decoding, Mamba / SSMs, or open-source contributions.
* An understanding of the full model lifecycle, from training checkpoints to production serving.

# **What success looks like at six months**

* Customer models you brought up are in production at target performance.
* Tooling improvements or model support you contributed to our internal and open-source libraries measurably shorten bring-up for the team.
* Customers and the engineering teams treat you as a trusted technical advisor.
