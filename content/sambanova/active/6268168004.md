SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

### **About the Role**

**SambaNova is hiring a DevOps Engineer for SambaNova’s SambaSuite platform.**

We are helping enterprises and service providers build their **AI inference platforms** for end users, powered by our **state-of-the-art RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) hardware architecture**. Our cloud-agnostic, enterprise-grade MLOps platform abstracts infrastructure complexity and enables seamless deployment, management, and scaling of foundation model workloads at production scale.

As a DevOps Engineer, you will take ownership of our CICD pipelines and help build a robust and scalable release infrastructure across our components.

This is a high-impact, high-visibility role working at the intersection of AI infrastructure, enterprise software, and developer experience.

### **Responsibilities**

* Design, build, and maintain **scalable infrastructure and deployment pipelines** for enterprise AI platforms
* Architect and implement robust CI/CD pipelines using tools such as **CircleCI**, **GitHubActions** etc ensuring stability, scalability, and performance
* Optimize CICD workflows, including configuration management and integration with build systems
* Drive SRE practices including **monitoring, observability, alerting, incident response**, and post-mortems
* Deploy and manage **Kubernetes clusters** and maintain **Helm charts** for application deployments
* Design and implement infrastructure solutions using **GCP** services.
* Establish and enforce infrastructure-as-code standards using tools like **Terraform**, etc.
* Collaborate with development teams to optimize build, test, and deployment processes
* Lead capacity planning, disaster recovery and SecOps workflows
* Automate operational workflows and reduce toil through intelligent tooling and process improvements

### **Required Qualifications**

* 5+ years of experience in DevOps, SRE, or Infrastructure Engineering roles.
* Proven track record of designing, building, and maintaining large-scale infrastructure in production environments
* Advanced proficiency in Linux/Unix systems administration, shell scripting, and automation
* Deep experience with modern CI/CD practices and tools such as CircleCI, Jenkins, GitLab CI/CD, or similar platforms
* Extensive experience with large monorepos or build systems such as Bazel
* Strong expertise in containerization (Docker) and orchestration (Kubernetes), including production deployments
* Expert knowledge of infrastructure-as-code tools such as Terraform, Ansible, or CloudFormation
* Extensive experience with cloud platforms (GCP preferred; AWS or Azure experience also valuable)
* Hands-on experience with monitoring and observability tools such as Grafana, Prometheus, OpenSearch, or ElasticSearch
* Experience with incident management practices and tools like OpsGenie, PagerDuty, Jira, or ServiceNow
* Strong understanding of networking protocols, security best practices, and distributed systems architecture
* Excellent problem-solving and troubleshooting skills with the ability to debug complex infrastructure issues under pressure
* Proven ability to lead technical initiatives, drive projects to completion, and mentor junior engineers
* Strong communication and collaboration skills with experience working effectively across cross-functional teams
