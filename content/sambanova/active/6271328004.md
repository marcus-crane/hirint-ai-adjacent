SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

# **RTL Design Engineer Intern**

### **About the Team**

The RTL Design team builds the compute datapath, on-chip network, and memory subsystems inside the RDU. We write microarchitecture specs and RTL, make power, performance, and area trade-offs, run lint, CDC, and LEC checks, and close timing with physical design.

### **About the Role**

As an RTL Design Engineer Intern, you'll design and debug a block within an RDU subsystem in SystemVerilog. You'll help write microarchitecture specs, run quality checks, and see how blocks move through synthesis, tape-out, and silicon bring-up alongside architects, verification, and physical design. A dedicated mentor will guide you throughout the internship.

### **Responsibilities**

* Write and debug SystemVerilog RTL for a block within an RDU subsystem Contribute sections of microarchitecture specifications
* Run lint, CDC, and LEC checks and fix issues
* Write SystemVerilog Assertions to check design intent
* Work with physical design on synthesis and timing closure
* Debug failures found by design verification
* Present your project results to the team at the end of your internship

### **Required Qualifications**

* Currently pursuing a B.S., M.S., or Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or Computer Science
* Coursework or project experience in digital logic design and computer architecture Experience writing RTL in Verilog or SystemVerilog
* Scripting experience in Python or Linux shell

### **Preferred Qualifications**

* Pursuing an M.S. or Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or Computer Science
* Familiarity with synthesis and static timing analysis
* Familiarity with protocols such as AXI, PCIe, Ethernet, HBM, or DDR
* Experience with lint, CDC, or LEC tools
* Exposure to FPGA prototyping or emulation
* Interest in AI workload acceleration
* Experience with Git
* Prior internship or tape-out experience, including class or university tape-outs
