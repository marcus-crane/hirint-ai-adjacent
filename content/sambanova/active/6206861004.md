SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

SambaNova is hiring a Senior SambaCloud Experience & Ecosystem Lead to make SambaCloud a coherent, recognizable, and trusted destination for AI builders and managed customers. You will own the SambaCloud brand experience, user journey, community and partner integrations, and cross-functional operating rhythm while partnering with Product and Engineering on the infrastructure that powers the service.

## About the Role

This role sits at the intersection of Developer Relations, Product, Engineering, Brand, Community, cloud operations, and go-to-market. You will define how SambaCloud shows up—from identity and onboarding to playground workflows, APIs, integrations, sample applications, and high-visibility demos—and turn user and business needs into a prioritized experience roadmap. You will partner with the Product team on underlying platform and infrastructure priorities and ensure developers and managed customers have reliable paths to access and value.

## Responsibilities

* Own the end-to-end SambaCloud experience across brand identity, information architecture, onboarding, playground workflows, APIs, model access, documentation, examples, and key user journeys
* Develop and maintain a prioritized experience roadmap that connects developer, community, partner, managed-customer, brand, and demo requirements
* Partner with Product and Engineering to translate experience goals into infrastructure, platform, security, reliability, and release requirements
* Define and drive integrations that connect SambaCloud to strategic partners, developer tools, community programs, campaigns, events, and managed-customer workflows
* Drive delivery planning across scope, milestones, quality, risks, dependencies, reviews, and escalation paths
* Build a repeatable demo-readiness program covering environments, API access, model availability, sample prompts, applications, data, scripts, observability, fallback plans, and presenter handoff
* Partner with Developer Relations, Product Marketing, Brand, Community, and Partner teams to turn priority use cases into polished, repeatable experiences for launches, events, customers, partners, and executive briefings
* Represent the needs of developers and managed customers in cross-functional planning and ensure that access, onboarding, entitlements, support, and experience gaps are addressed
* Define and monitor success metrics for activation, time to first successful request, engagement, integration adoption, demo reliability, issue resolution, and user satisfaction
* Maintain a clear release calendar and readiness dashboard; communicate status, tradeoffs, risks, and decisions to technical and business stakeholders
* Create lightweight playbooks, runbooks, and documentation that allow teams to deliver consistent experiences and demonstrations without relying on tribal knowledge
* Evaluate build-versus-partner decisions and ensure all delivered work meets SambaNova’s standards for quality, security, maintainability, brand consistency, and user experience
