SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

SambaNova is hiring a Principal Product Marketing Manager to lead product positioning, portfolio strategy, and sales enablement for SambaRack and SambaStack. This role will define how SambaNova presents its purpose-built AI infrastructure to the market, translate product differentiation into clear customer value, and equip the field with the narratives and tools needed to win.

## About the Role

This is a principal-level product marketing role for an experienced strategist and hands-on creator who can move from market insight to a crisp product narrative, and from that narrative to the launches, messaging, presentations, one-pagers, competitive guidance, and training used across the go-to-market organization. You will own the market-facing strategy for SambaRack—the turnkey, rack-level system built around SambaNova RDUs for large-scale AI inference—and SambaStack—the full-stack enterprise AI platform that combines hardware and software for deployments on premises or through dedicated cloud hosting.

You will partner closely with Technical Product Marketing, Account Executives, and Field Marketing to turn technical proof, customer needs, and field insight into differentiated product messaging and scalable go-to-market execution. Technical Product Marketing will contribute deployment-oriented technical content and demonstrations, while you will own product and portfolio positioning, audience and use-case strategy, launch narratives, competitive differentiation, sales enablement, and consistent market execution.
