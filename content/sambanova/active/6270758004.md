SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

SambaNova Systems, a leader in AI computing, is hiring a Senior Financial Analyst to join its FP&A team. You will build the models, reports and analyses that leadership uses to plan, forecast and decide. On a small team where FP&A and strategic finance sit together, you will take on real scope early and see your work reach senior leadership.

You will partner with sales, business development, operations, engineering and marketing leaders to deliver planning, analytics and finance support. This role suits an analyst who is passionate about FP&A, enjoys modeling and detail, thrives in a fast-paced, feedback-driven environment, and wants a company that will invest in their growth.

**What you will do**

* Support the annual and quarterly planning cycles, including headcount, operating expense and department budgets, linked to company strategic objectives. Contribute to long-range planning
* Build and maintain financial models, dashboards and forecasts that turn data into decisions. Produce the monthly reporting package: financial statements and variance analysis against budget, prior periods and forecast, with clear commentary. Help improve forecast accuracy and visibility
* Partner with sales, operations, engineering, business development and marketing leaders, helping prepare their budgets, forecasts and ad-hoc analyses
* Evaluate the financial impact of new products, contracts, pricing and marketing programs, including product margins and cost of revenue. Compute monthly sales commissions
* Support the month-end close with the Accounting & Finance team. Streamline and automate recurring reports and processes

**What you will bring**

* Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, or a related field; MBA, CPA and/or CFA a plus
* 4+ years of FP&A or corporate finance experience at a technology company, or in investment banking, consulting or public accounting
* Strong financial modeling skills, including budgeting, forecasting and variance analysis, and a working understanding of financial statements and basic accounting
* Advanced Excel skills (INDEX/MATCH, pivot tables, formula-driven models), fluency in Google Workspace and Microsoft Office, and experience with an ERP or planning system
* Clear written and verbal communication, including building presentations and analyses from scratch and explaining them to non-finance partners

**Nice to have**

* Hands-on experience with NetSuite, Abacum and Adaptive Planning
* Experience with hardware or semiconductor business models, including product cost and gross margin analysis
* Experience calculating sales commissions, or supporting R&D or G&A budgets and headcount planning
* Working knowledge of SQL or Python and a BI tool (Tableau, Power BI or Looker) to automate reporting and analysis, and comfort using AI tools to speed up modeling

**Who thrives here**

* Self-motivated, with a strong work ethic and the initiative to take ownership and head off problems before they surface
* A clear, consultative communicator and a good listener who builds trust with business partners and peers
* Highly organized and detail-oriented, able to juggle competing deadlines and make sound judgments under pressure
* A team player who is also high-achieving, positive and comfortable in a fast-paced, fast-changing environment where feedback is frequent
