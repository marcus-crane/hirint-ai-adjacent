SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

SambaNova is hiring a Director of Business Development to help manage, lead, and orchestrate deals alongside Sales, Product, and Engineering. You are an enabler and the connective tissue between the teams that win, build, and deliver our business. You work side by side with Sales, Channel, Customer Success, Product, and Engineering. You report to the Chief Corporate Development Officer, and you will have visible, company-level impact from your first week.

This role is about follow-through and momentum. You are a main point of contact for Sales, Channel, Product, and Engineering on the engagements you support. You hunt for new customers and partners and, in some cases, initiate the engagement yourself, in coordination with Sales and Channel. You keep the day-to-day activity moving and carry each engagement all the way to revenue, including the pre-sales work that gets it there. You also take part in deal structures, pricing, and forecasting, so the numbers behind each deal are grounded in what is really happening. You also contribute to press releases and messaging, so what we say publicly reflects the real state of each partnership. Executives open doors and set direction. You make sure the work behind those conversations gets done, with extreme ownership of the outcome and a bias toward speed. When something is stuck, you take it on and clear it, and the teams who lead our customer relationships are always better off for having you involved.

We build partnerships where both sides win. We aim for relationships that last across multiple product generations. How we work together will change over time, from early co development and co-selling to deeper commercial models. You will help shape that progression and make sure each step leaves our customers, our partners, and SambaNova stronger.

Demand from OEMs, ODMs, NeoClouds, hyperscalers, sovereign customers, and enterprises is growing faster than any one team can track. Each engagement touches many groups, including silicon, systems, software, supply chain, sales, customer success, marketing, finance, and legal. You keep them all pointed at the same outcome. You are responsible for a segment or set of strategic customers and partners, which may be organized by customer, segment, or region, and it will change as the business changes. Within it, you set the business development strategy, deciding which accounts to pursue, how to approach them, and which deal structures and commercial models fit. Sales and Channel lead the relationships and close the deals, and you look further out to shape what comes next.

Responsibilities

**Drive engagements from first conversation to revenue**

Hunt for new customers and partners and, in some cases, initiate engagements yourself, coordinating with Sales and Channel so each account is approached once and with one voice

Keep a clear plan for each engagement covering objectives, stakeholders, milestones, dependencies, risks, and next steps

Move each engagement through its stages, from NDA, MOU, and LOI through MSA, orders, delivery, and revenue recognition

Be a trusted point of contact who always knows where an engagement stands and what is needed next

**Orchestrate across Product, Engineering, Sales, Customer Success, and Channel**

Work alongside Product and Engineering to align each deal with the roadmap, reference architectures, and what we can deliver and when

Work hand in hand with Sales and Channel on account plans, forecasts, and customer meetings, so the customer hears one consistent voice from SambaNova Engage Customer Success before the sale, so expectations are set early and post sale support is ready when the customer ships

Bring in supply chain, operations, marketing, finance, and legal at the right time, and keep everyone moving

Translate what customers and partners need into clear priorities and trade-offs for internal teams

**Take extreme ownership and move fast**

Own the outcome: follow up, close loops, and clear blockers without waiting to be asked

Set and run the cadence, action items, and escalations for the engagements you support, and raise problems early with a recommendation attached

Prepare executives for customer and partner meetings, and make sure commitments made in the room are tracked and delivered

**Build partnerships that win on both sides and last**

Work with the Chief Corporate Development Officer, Finance, Legal, and Revenue Operations to structure agreements, pricing, and terms that work for both sides, including forecast and volume commitments, deposits, and delivery schedules Take part in deal structures, pricing, and forecasting, helping shape the structure of each deal, pricing proposals, and volume forecasts with Product, Finance, and Revenue Operations, and keep them current as each deal evolves

Design engagements that grow across product generations and evolve over time, for example from co-development and co-sell toward deeper commercial models Work with marketing, PR, and sales enablement on press releases, messaging, joint announcements, co-sell motions, lighthouse customers, and training

**Report and improve**

Give leadership a clear, current view of status, risks, and expected revenue across your engagements, and keep the forecast, bookings, and pipeline accurate

Capture what you learn from each deal and feed it back into repeatable playbooks, templates, and standard terms

Your First 90 Days

You will start by listening and learning the engagements you will support. Our teams need a partner who understands the business and the technology well enough to ask the right questions.

1. **Days 1 to 30:** Meet the leaders across Sales, Channel, Customer Success, Product, Engineering, Operations, Finance, and Legal. Learn the engagements you will support, and map where each one stands, who is involved, and what it needs next.
2. **Days 31 to 60:** Put a plan, cadence, and forecast in place for each engagement, agreed with Sales and Customer Success. Identify the biggest blockers and open items, and start closing them.
3. **Days 61 to 90:** Run your engagements day to day, with at least one advanced to a defined milestone, and begin identifying new customers and partners to pursue with Sales and Channel. Propose how our focus areas should be organized by customer, segment, or region as the business grows.
