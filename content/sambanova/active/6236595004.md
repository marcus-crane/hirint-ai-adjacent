SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

**About the Team**

The Design Verification team verifies the RDU, SambaNova's chip for AI workloads. We build SystemVerilog and UVM environments for chip-level control and initialization, including sequencing, state transitions, clocking, and recovery, and work with architects, RTL designers, and firmware engineers.

**About the Role**

As a new grad Design Verification Engineer, you'll write testbenches, tests, checkers, and coverage in SystemVerilog and UVM for RDU features. You'll run regressions, debug simulation failures, and work with RTL designers to find and fix bugs before tape-out.

**Responsibilities**

* Write and execute verification plans for chip-level control and initialization features
* Build SystemVerilog and UVM testbench components, including sequences, checkers, assertions, and functional coverage
* Write directed and constrained-random tests to find corner cases
* Debug simulation and regression failures with RTL designers
* Analyze functional and code coverage and close coverage gaps

**Required Qualifications**

* B.S. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or related field
* Coursework or project experience in digital design and computer architecture
* Experience with SystemVerilog or Verilog
* Scripting experience in Python, Perl, TCL, or shell
* Experience with UVM
* Familiarity with constrained-random verification, functional coverage, and assertions
* Familiarity with clock domain crossing concepts

**Preferred Qualifications**

* M.S. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or related field
* Experience using AI tools such as Claude for verification development and debug
* Internship experience in design verification
