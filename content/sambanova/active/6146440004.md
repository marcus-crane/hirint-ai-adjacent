SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

### **About the team**

The team integrates SambaNova's AI accelerators into rack scale systems and gets them deployed in customer datacenters. We handle the racking and stacking of tightly integrated network, power, and compute equipment in both prototype and production environments, and we take rack solutions through prototyping into mass production so they stay manufacturable and serviceable at scale. We work with architects, engineering teams, customer datacenter teams, sales, and marketing, and we manage vendor relationships to line the equipment up with our current and future roadmap.

### **About the role**

SambaNova is a rack scale developer of AI accelerators. We are looking for a Systems Integration Engineer to help scale the next generation of platform and datacenter deployments.

### **Responsibilities**

Some of your responsibilities will include:

* Rack and stack tightly integrated network, power, and compute devices in both prototype and production environments
* Select, configure, and customize high performance rack equipment for deployment at scale across a wide range of customer datacenters
* Work with architects, engineering teams, customer datacenter teams, sales, and marketing to align rack designs with product needs
* Engage with multiple vendors and build the partnerships needed to support our current and future roadmap

* Working knowledge of specification, configuration, and use of common datacenter support equipment such as network switches, power systems, and fiber and copper networking technologies
* Recent experience with typical datacenter working practices and current and upcoming standards, including rack design, wiring practices, rack power options, and cooling technologies
* Recent experience in prototyping and converting high performance integrated rack solutions to mass production while keeping them manufacturable and serviceable

### **Preferred qualifications**
