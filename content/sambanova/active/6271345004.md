SambaNova is a leader in next-generation AI infrastructure, delivering a full-stack inference platform for customers worldwide. At the core of SambaNova's technology is the RDU (Reconfigurable Dataflow Unit) — a chip built on a dataflow architecture rather than the traditional GPU model. Its decode performance is especially strong for agentic workloads like multi-turn agents, code generation, and long-running applications. RDUs are packaged into SambaRack, rack-scale hardware that lets customers deploy state-of-the-art models with better performance, greater energy efficiency, and faster time to value.

# **DFT Engineer Intern**

**About the Team**

The DFT team designs and implements test infrastructure for the RDU, including scan, ATPG, MBIST, and JTAG. We generate production test patterns, close DFT timing with physical design, and support silicon diagnosis and yield analysis.

**About the Role**

As a DFT Engineer Intern, you'll help insert and verify test logic in RDU designs and generate ATPG and MBIST patterns. You'll write scripts to automate DFT flows, debug simulations, and see how patterns are deployed and how silicon is brought up. A dedicated mentor will guide you throughout the internship.

**Responsibilities**

* Support scan insertion, ATPG, and MBIST implementation at block and SoC level
* Write scripts to automate DFT insertion, coverage analysis, and pattern generation
* Run and debug DFT simulations
* Help maintain JTAG and IJTAG test access infrastructure
* Work with STA and physical design teams on DFT timing closure
* Observe pattern deployment to ATE and silicon diagnosis
* Present your project results to the team at the end of your internship

**Required Qualifications**

* Currently pursuing a B.S., M.S., or Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or Computer Science
* Coursework or project experience in digital design, VLSI, or testing of digital circuits
* Experience with Verilog or SystemVerilog
* Scripting experience in TCL, Python, or Perl

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Pursuing an M.S. or Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or Computer Science
* Familiarity with scan, ATPG, MBIST, or JTAG
* Experience with Siemens Tessent or other DFT tools
* Familiarity with static timing analysis
* Experience with simulators and waveform debugging
* Prior internship experience in DFT, design, or silicon test
