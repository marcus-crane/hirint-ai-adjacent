## About Flock

Road accidents kill and injure people, damage vehicles, and cost businesses fortunes. But traditional insurance does nothing to prevent them - it just pays out claims when they happen. At Flock, we’re changing that. Using telematics, AI, and real-time data, we proactively help our fleet customers prevent crashes. We obsessively focus on keeping drivers safe, and we put our money where our mouth is - paying out millions of pounds per year to fleets that get safer over time.

We were acquired by Admiral in May 2026. Now we combine the backing of the UK’s leading motor insurer with startup energy and speed. We’re growing fast with bigger plans than ever, and we’re hugely optimistic that our approach will save many more lives in the decade ahead.

We’re on a mission to make the world quantifiably safer. Join us!

## About the role

You'll sit at the centre of our underwriting and customer operations, working with brokers and customers to keep new business and renewals moving. You'll report to our Senior Customer Operations Manager and work closely with our underwriters and with the Finance, Risk Intelligence and Product teams.

The role suits someone starting a career in insurance who enjoys solving problems and getting the detail right. You'll build a grounding in underwriting and customer service that carries into underwriting, operations or customer success. We expect you to use AI every day, and to build your own AI tools and workflows that take repetitive work off the team.

## What you'll be doing

* Process broker submissions and gather the information underwriters need to make a decision, using AI tools to read documents and spot gaps.
* Answer broker and customer policy queries by phone, live chat and email, with fast and accurate resolutions.
* Support underwriting analysis on new business and renewals with data-driven insight.
* Triage underwriting referrals and allocate them according to underwriting authority levels.
* Work with Underwriting, Finance, Risk Intelligence and Product to investigate and resolve issues.
* Build and improve AI workflows, prompts and self-service routes that remove manual steps from our processes.
* Capture broker and customer feedback, and turn recurring themes into process improvements and automation ideas.
* Explain policy details and underwriting decisions to brokers and customers, and show them how to self-serve.
* Follow internal procedures, regulatory requirements and company policies.

## What you'll need

Essential

* A genuine wish to start a career in insurance, underwriting or financial services.
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to interpret risk data.
* A customer-first approach that keeps underwriting integrity intact.
* Clear communication, including explaining complex points in plain language, and good rapport with brokers and customers.
* Close attention to detail in everything you process and send.
* Comfort using AI tools every day, and the curiosity to pick up new digital tools quickly.
* The ability to work independently and with others, and to manage several priorities at once in a fast-paced setting.

Preferable

* Some knowledge of commercial motor insurance, or an interest in underwriting and risk assessment.
* Experience in customer or broker-facing roles.
