# **Job Overview**

Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

Saronic is seeking a Senior Tax Accountant to support corporate income tax compliance, provision, and audit activities, as well as indirect tax matters, across our global operations. This role works closely with tax leadership, external tax advisors, and internal finance teams to ensure accurate reporting, timely execution, and compliance with all federal, state, and international tax requirements. The ideal candidate combines solid technical knowledge with strong organizational skills and a desire to grow in a fast-paced, growth-oriented environment.

# **Responsibilities**

* Prepare supporting workpapers and assist with the review of U.S. federal and state income tax returns prepared by external advisors
* Prepare the quarterly and annual worldwide income tax provision under ASC 740, including supporting schedules and tax account reconciliations
* Prepare monthly and quarterly indirect tax filings, including sales and use tax returns
* Gather data and coordinate with global finance teams and external providers to support international tax compliance
* Assist with transfer pricing documentation and intercompany calculations
* Execute and document internal controls over income tax accounting and reporting
* Support IRS and state income tax audits, including data collection and preparation of responses
* Identify opportunities for automation, efficiency, and risk reduction in tax workflows
* Support continuous improvement in tax processes and technology to support scalability as the company grows
* Stay current on changes in U.S. corporate tax law and accounting standards affecting the business
* Partner cross-functionally with Accounting, Finance, FP&A, and Operations teams to gather information and support tax-related needs

# **Qualifications**

* 3-5 years of U.S. corporate income tax experience in public accounting and/or industry
* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field; master’s in taxation preferred
* Working knowledge of ASC 740 and corporate income tax concepts
* Experience working with external tax advisors to deliver high-quality and timely work product
* Strong organizational skills, with the ability to multitask and meet tight deadlines
* Strong communication skills, with the ability to explain tax concepts to non-tax stakeholders
* Solid understanding of key U.S. corporate and income tax concepts, including stock compensation, deferred revenue, R&D credit and fixed assets
* Interest in process improvement and automation within tax reporting or compliance functions
* Proficiency with tax compliance software (e.g., OneSource, Corptax, or equivalent)
* Exposure to international tax coordination or global tax provision processes
* Unwavering attention to detail and commitment to accuracy and quality

**Preferred Qualifications:**

· CPA or equivalent certification (or actively pursuing)

· Knowledge of the defense or advanced manufacturing industry
