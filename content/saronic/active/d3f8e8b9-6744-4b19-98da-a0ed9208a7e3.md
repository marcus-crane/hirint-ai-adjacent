Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

Saronic is hiring a Photographer to take ownership of photography across our various locations. We are looking for someone with a strong photo style that can complement our own established look.

You will work across a range of environments, from action and portrait to product and documentarystyle work. On larger productions you will shoot stills alongside the video crew. Travel to our various locations is part of the job.

# **Responsibilities**

• Lead all aspects of photography on solo location shoots, from setup through final delivery.

• Shoot stills alongside video crews on larger productions, managing equipment and supporting the

production as needed.

• Capture high-quality images across a range of environments, including action, portrait, product,

and documentary-style work.

• Set up and operate studio, strobe, and natural-light lighting setups.

• Cull, edit, retouch, and deliver final images on schedule, and keep the image library organized.

• Travel to our various locations.

• Adapt quickly to changing shoot conditions, schedules, and creative briefs.

• Maintain, transport, and care for all camera and production equipment.

# **Qualifications**

• 5+ years of professional photography experience.

• Demonstrated expertise with professional camera systems and lenses, backed by a strong

portfolio.

• Strong understanding of studio, strobe, and natural-light setups.

• Proficiency with Adobe Lightroom and Photoshop (or equivalent) for editing and retouching.

• Experience working on both independent shoots and as part of a larger crew.

• Confident working autonomously and making creative decisions independently.

• Willingness and ability to travel frequently.

• Strong organizational skills and attention to detail in equipment management.

• FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate is a plus, not a requirement.

# **Physical Demands**

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
* Occasional standing and walking within the office
* Manual dexterity to operate a computer keyboard, mouse, and other office equipment
* Visual acuity to read screens, documents, and reports
* Occasional reaching, bending, or stooping to access file drawers, cabinets, or office supplies
* Lifting and carrying items up to 20 pounds occasionally (e.g., office supplies, packages)
