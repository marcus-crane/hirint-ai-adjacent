Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

Saronic is growing fast, and our footprint has to keep pace: new waterfront sites, new countries, and new kinds of facilities that don't fit a standard office or industrial lease. The Expansion team needs someone who can take on whatever the next expansion problem is, figure out what it actually takes, and get it done.

The Expansion Specialist is a generalist operator who will work on strategic, ambiguous, and often one-of-a-kind projects that sit between real estate, operations, finance, legal, and government relations. One month that might be scouting a test site with open-water access and room to operate; the next it might be building the business case for a new regional hub, or working with local and state government on incentive packages.

You will do well here if you are comfortable starting from a blank page, enjoy being in the field as much as in a spreadsheet, and can turn a messy question from leadership into a clear recommendation.

# **Responsibilities**

* **New market entry.** Evaluate where Saronic should go next. Build market assessments covering proximity to customers and shipyards, labor pools, waterfront and port access, incentives, cost, and security considerations, and bring a clear recommendation to leadership.

* **Strategic projects.** Own cross-functional projects from kickoff to close. Examples include build-to-suit and campus expansion studies, temporary or rapid-stand-up facilities for programs on tight timelines, and working with real estate development partners to build employee housing at various sites.

* **Unconventional deal evaluation.** Screen inbound and unsolicited property proposals, including ones with non-standard structures, and coordinate the right diligence with Legal, Finance, and Security before anything moves forward.

* **Incentives and public partnerships.** Work with economic development organizations, port authorities, and state and local officials to identify and negotiate incentives, grants, and site readiness support.

* **Regulatory and site readiness.** Scope what a site needs to be usable: zoning, permitting, environmental review, waterfront and maritime approvals, utilities, and physical and information security requirements for defense work.

* **Capacity and footprint planning.** Partner with Operations, Manufacturing, and People teams to forecast space needs against hiring and production plans, and flag where the footprint will fall short before it does.

* **Leadership support.** Prepare memos, decks, and briefings for executive and offsite discussions, and handle ad hoc strategic requests from the VP of Expansion and Integration

# **Qualifications**

* 3–6 years of experience in corporate real estate, site selection, industrial or waterfront development, economic development, strategy consulting, or business operations at a fast-growing company.

* A track record of running projects end to end with limited structure and many stakeholders.

* Strong financial modeling in Excel or Google Sheets, including lease-versus-buy, NPV, and total cost of occupancy analysis.

* Clear, concise writing. You can take a complicated situation and produce a one-page memo or a short deck with a recommendation at the top.

* Comfort working with brokers, landlords, public officials, outside counsel, and internal executives, and the judgment to know when to escalate.

* Willingness to travel frequently (20-30%), including industrial and waterfront sites. Mostly domestic, some international

* Bachelor's degree in real estate, finance, economics, engineering, urban planning, or a related field, or equivalent experience.

# **Nice to haves**

* Experience with maritime, port, shipyard, or other waterfront properties.

* Familiarity with defense, aerospace, or government contracting environments, including facility security requirements.

* Experience with state and local incentive negotiations or public-private partnerships.

* Exposure to international real estate or market entry (for example, the UK or Australia).

* Background in construction management, development, or permitting.
