Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking a Senior Data Scientist to join the Integrated Business Tools team, which delivers operational value through custom application development, analytics, and digital solutions for our internal operations. This role will help build out our Databricks platform and turn engineering, manufacturing, supply chain, financial, and G&A operations data into insights that drive decisions across the company.

You will build forecasting, statistical, and machine learning models where they add tangible value, and champion self-service analytics and data literacy so teams can answer their own questions. You will also be a trusted partner to leadership, translating complex analysis into clear recommendations for executive audiences.

This role is suited to someone who enjoys both hands-on technical work and teaching others. You should be comfortable working through ambiguous problems and incomplete data, and excited to shape how a fast-growing company uses its data.

# **Responsibilities**

* Help design and build out our Databricks Lakehouse, integrating data from engineering, manufacturing, supply chain, financial, and G&A systems (e.g., PLM, ERP, MES, and planning tools).
* Build reliable pipelines and curated, well-documented datasets that serve as the trusted source for reporting and analysis.
* Develop forecasting, statistical, and machine learning models for problems such as demand and supply planning, inventory, cost, cash flow, and operational performance.
* Perform deep-dive analyses to find drivers, anomalies, and opportunities in engineering, manufacturing, and business data.
* Deploy and monitor models in Databricks so results are reproducible and stay accurate over time.
* Champion self-service analytics by building certified datasets, shared metric definitions, dashboards, and templates that non-technical users can trust.
* Raise data literacy across the company through training sessions, office hours, documentation, and a community of practice.
* Translate complex analyses into clear, decision-focused recommendations for executive and senior leadership audiences through briefings, written summaries, and dashboards.
* Partner with leaders across the business to identify their highest-value questions and turn them into analytics priorities.
* Work with the team's application developers to embed analytics and models in the custom tools we deliver.
* Establish standards for data quality, governance, and access control, including proper handling of export-controlled data.
* Set best practices for analytics code and documentation, and mentor analysts and junior team members.

# **Required Qualifications**

* 6+ years of experience in data science or advanced analytics (4+ with a master's degree), with demonstrated ownership of analyses and models from scoping through delivery.
* 2+ years of hands-on experience with Databricks, including building pipelines, analytics, or models in a lakehouse environment.
* Strong proficiency in Python (including PySpark) and SQL.
* Experience applying time-series forecasting and statistical modeling to real business problems.
* Proven track record of driving analytics adoption among non-technical users through training, self-service tools, or data literacy programs.
* Demonstrated ability to present analysis and recommendations to executive audiences and communicate assumptions and tradeoffs clearly.
* Ability to work through ambiguous problems and incomplete data, prioritize competing requests, and follow through in a fast-paced environment.
* Bachelor's degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Engineering, Economics, Operations Research, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

# **Preferred Qualifications**

* Experience helping stand up or scale a Databricks environment, including features such as Unity Catalog, Delta Lake, MLflow, Databricks SQL, AI/BI dashboards, Genie, or Databricks Apps.
* Databricks certification (e.g., Data Analyst Associate, Data Engineer Associate, or Machine Learning Associate).
* Experience in a high-growth defense, aerospace, maritime, or manufacturing business, including work with ERP, MES, or PLM data.
* Experience with Power BI, Tableau, or a similar BI tool.
* Experience with AWS or Azure cloud environments.
* Experience working with export-controlled or otherwise sensitive data.
* Experience using AI tools to accelerate analysis and development, with sound judgment in validating outputs and handling sensitive information.
