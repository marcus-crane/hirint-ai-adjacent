Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking an experienced Tooling Technician to help manufacture, assemble, and maintain the tooling used in our prototype and production builds. You will be directly involved in building and assembling the fixtures and jigs used throughout the manufacture of our products, and you will provide input on fixture design to improve manufacturability and maintainability. You will also perform periodic, condition-based review and maintenance of tooling driven by production use.

# **Responsibilities**

* Engage with tooling engineers during the design phase to provide input on a tool's function, manufacturability, and usability, and help take designs through to completed, working tooling.
* Manufacture tooling, fixtures, and jigs using manual and computer-controlled machines, hand tools, and precision alignment equipment.
* Determine fabrication and assembly processes for the designed tooling.
* Perform complex tool setup activities, such as alignment and leveling.
* Provide tooling support to the manufacturing organization, including inspection, maintenance, and repair.
* Perform visual and dimensional inspections of tools using the appropriate documentation.
* Troubleshoot and resolve problems with tools to restore or improve function.
* Work effectively under general direction.
* Operate material-handling equipment to move materials and tools as required.
* Work off-shift periodically as needed, based on equipment and tooling availability.

# **Qualifications**

**Required**

* 3+ years of experience reading engineering drawings and working with CAD models.
* 3+ years of experience in tooling and/or advanced manufacturing — including detail part fabrication, assembly, maintenance and repair, and metrology/inspection of manufacturing tools, fixtures, and jigs.
* 3+ years of experience setting up and operating manual and CNC machines, including mills, lathes, waterjets, and laser/plasma cutters.
* Experience in aerospace, automotive, or a similar precision-fabrication environment.
* Strong understanding of GD&T and its practical application in tool fabrication and inspection.

**Preferred**

* Ability to maintain a periodic maintenance program in a full-production environment.
* Ability to set up and operate high-precision metrology equipment for tool setting and inspection.
* Experience creating, reading, and editing G- and M-codes in CNC programs.
* Experience performing MIG or TIG welding on aluminum and steel.
