Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

As a People Business Partner Manager at Saronic, you will lead a team of People Business Partners responsible for delivering strategic People support across the organization. In this role, you will serve as a trusted advisor to senior leaders while coaching and developing your team to drive high-impact People initiatives aligned with business objectives.

You will partner closely with leaders to develop workforce strategies, strengthen organizational effectiveness, support talent development, and guide organizational change in a fast-paced, high-growth environment. While maintaining a strong understanding of all core People functions, your focus will be on building scalable People practices, developing your team, and fostering a high-performing, mission-driven culture.

# **Responsibilities**

* Lead a team of People Business Partners, providing coaching, guidance, and professional development to ensure high-quality partnership across the organization.
* Serve as a strategic advisor to senior leaders, building trusted relationships and aligning People strategies with business priorities.
* Partner with business leaders to understand organizational objectives, workforce challenges, and future talent needs while proactively recommending solutions.
* Guide leaders on organizational design, workforce planning, succession planning, leadership development, and organizational effectiveness.
* Drive employee engagement initiatives by identifying trends, facilitating feedback mechanisms, and partnering with leaders to develop action plans.
* Support retention and talent development strategies that align with Saronic's mission, culture, and long-term growth objectives.
* Oversee performance management processes, including goal setting, performance reviews, coaching, and continuous feedback practices.
* Provide guidance on complex employee relations matters, ensuring fair, consistent, and compliant resolution while coaching leaders through performance and behavioral challenges.
* Ensure compliance with federal, state, and local employment laws, partnering closely with People Operations and Legal as needed.
* Partner with Recruiting, Total Rewards, People Operations, and Learning & Development to deliver integrated People solutions across the employee lifecycle
* Lead and contribute to company-wide People initiatives, including organizational change, policy development, compensation planning, leadership development, and culture initiatives.
* Develop and improve People Team processes, tools, and communications to enhance manager effectiveness and employee experience.

# **Qualifications**

* Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, or a related field.
* 8+ years of progressive People Business Partner experience
* 3+ years of experience leading, mentoring, or managing People Business Partners or People professionals.
* Strong business acumen with the ability to connect People strategies to operational and organizational goals.
* Deep understanding of employee relations, organizational development, performance management, workforce planning, and change management.
* Demonstrated ability to coach, influence, and build trusted partnerships with leaders at all levels of the organization.
* Excellent communication, facilitation, conflict resolution, and interpersonal skills.
* Experience working in a fast-paced, high-growth, or startup environment.
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with experience using People metrics and HRIS systems to influence decisions.

# **Physical Demands**
