Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Summary**

Saronic is seeking a Technical Operations I to support mission operations across vessel operation, systems support, and mechanical maintenance for our autonomous platforms, including the 12 ft UUV, the 24 ft Corsair, and the 52 ft Mirage. This role is for someone with 2–4 years of experience, working under direct supervision while building proficiency through training and qualification progression.

In this role you will stand operational watches, assist with mission planning, pre- and post-mission checks, launch and recovery, and monitor autonomous vessels during live operations, exercises, and customer demonstrations. You will also execute scheduled maintenance on diesel engines, propulsion, electrical, and hull systems using established procedures and checklists. The ideal candidate is hands-on, eager to learn, and disciplined about safety, documentation, and escalating issues to senior staff. This role works a rotational schedule of 60 days on and 60 days off, built around three 60-day deployments per year.

**Responsibilities**

**How you support the mission:**

* Stand operational watches and support vessel operation during live operations, exercises, and customer demonstrations.
* Assist with mission planning and execute pre- and post-mission checks.
* Support launch and recovery of autonomous vessels and monitor them during live operations.
* Execute scheduled maintenance on diesel engines, propulsion, electrical, and hull systems using established procedures and checklists.
* Support troubleshooting of mechanical, electrical, and autonomy/software issues by gathering data and escalating to senior staff.
* Document maintenance actions, faults, and parts usage clearly and completely.
* Follow operational safety requirements and established procedures on the water and in the shop.

**How you support the team:**

* Build proficiency in vessel systems, mission tooling, operational safety, and maintenance documentation through training and qualification progression.
* Take ownership of your personal qualification progress and training requirements.
* Keep tools, spares, equipment, and workspaces organized and mission-ready.
* Seek out additional mission prep or maintenance work when idle rather than waiting to be assigned.
* Escalate uncertain technical issues to a supervisor and seek guidance before acting on unfamiliar systems.

**Role Requirements**

* Completes mission, operational, and maintenance tasks accurately with guidance from supervisors.
* Responds promptly to direction and completes assigned tasks within expected timeframes.
* Takes responsibility for personal qualification progress and training requirements.
* Follows established procedures and escalates uncertain technical issues to a supervisor.
* Documents maintenance actions, faults, and parts usage clearly and completely.
* Keeps tools, spares, equipment, and workspaces organized and mission-ready.
* Recognizes the limits of their own experience and seeks guidance before acting on unfamiliar systems.
* Rotational work schedule of 60 days on, 60 days off, with travel for three 60-day deployments per year, including OCONUS deployments.
* Able to certify as "fit for work" by demonstrating ability to swim and tread water, lift up to 75 lbs., and spend extended time periods on the water.

**Qualifications**

* 2–4 years of experience in vessel operation, marine or mechanical maintenance, or a related field.
* Hands-on aptitude with diesel engines, propulsion, electrical, or hull systems; formal training or certification is a plus.
* Familiarity with unmanned or autonomous systems, or defense technology programs, is a plus.
* Prior military service, maritime academy, or commercial/recreational boating experience is a plus.
* Comfort reading procedures and checklists and keeping accurate maintenance records.
* Willingness to learn quickly and progress through Saronic's training and qualification path.
* ***Active U.S. security clearance is required in advance of hire**. TOP SECRET clearance with SCI eligibility is preferred, but not required in advance of hire.*

**Physical Demands**

* Ability to certify as “fit for work” by demonstrating ability to swim and tread water
* Frequently and repetitively, lift, push and carry up to 75 lbs.
* Will be exposed to marine environments, including sun, wind, rain, cold, and saltwater spray, often for extended periods
* Must be able to operate in daylight, low-light, and night conditions using marine navigation systems and visual cues
* Ability to perform physically demanding work for extended periods of time, up to 12 hours/day
* This role may require to wear company provided PPE ie. Plate Carrier and Helmet no more than 25 lbs
