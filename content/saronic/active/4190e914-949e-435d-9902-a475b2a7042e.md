Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

**Position Overview**

* The Harness Inspector is responsible for inspecting harness assemblies to ensure compliance with IPC/WHMA-A-620 standards and internal quality requirements. This role requires strong attention to detail, the ability to follow procedures, and a solid understanding of inspection techniques. The Harness Inspector will support quality and production by identifying defects, documenting findings, and ensuring all assemblies meet required specifications.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Perform inspections of harness assemblies to IPC/WHMA-A-620 standards
* Identify defects in crimps, terminations, insulation, and routing
* Verify labeling, configuration, and workmanship meet requirements
* Use inspection tools such as magnification and measurement devices
* Document inspection results accurately within company systems
* Follow work instructions, procedures, and quality standards
* Escalate nonconformances to lead inspectors or supervisors
* Support in-process and final inspections as needed
* Maintain a clean and organized inspection area
* Continuously improve knowledge of standards and inspection techniques

**Required Qualifications**

* IPC/WHMA-A-620 Certification (Required or ability to obtain)
* Strong attention to detail
* Ability to read and interpret basic drawings and work instructions
* Basic understanding of harness assembly and inspection processes
* Good documentation and communication skills
* Ability to follow procedures and standards consistently
* Willingness to learn and develop inspection skills
