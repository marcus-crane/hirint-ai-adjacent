Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are looking for a Senior Manager to lead our U.S. Defense Market Strategy team and run the end-to-end strategy development process for our U.S. Defense segment, which includes non-defense U.S. government markets. You will own the analysis, synthesis, and stakeholder coordination that shape where we compete and how we will win; these efforts will directly inform C-suite decision-making. The role involves close collaboration with leaders and subject matter experts across our U.S. Defense Growth, Programs, Government Relations, Finance, Partnerships, Corporate Development, Shipbuilding, and other key functions.

# **Responsibilities**

* **Team Leadership:** Manage, develop, and set priorities for the U.S. Defense Market Strategy team; own quality control from problem framing through final synthesis.

* **Strategy Development:** Drive the annual strategy cycle for U.S. defense and non-defense government markets, including market sizing, segment prioritization, and tradeoffs; provide inputs into other critical planning processes; track strategic initiatives and prepare quarterly business review materials for executives. These efforts involve closely working alongside subject matter experts from the U.S. Defense Growth teams.

* **Competitive Positioning Analysis:** Systematically track, analyze, and synthesize competitor positioning and pricing; produce competitive landscape analyses, battlecards, and tools that equip the sales and capture teams to win.

* **Special Projects Management:** Structure complex, ambiguous problems, collaborate cross-functionally to develop solutions, and drive execution from planning to implementation.

# **Qualifications**

* 5+ years post-undergrad at a top-tier strategy consulting firm, or 2+ years post-MBA at a top-tier strategy consulting firm.

* Majority of consulting tenure spent on corporate/growth strategy and commercial due diligence, with demonstrated ownership of market sizing, competitive positioning analysis, and value creation planning.

* Mastery of Excel market modeling and executive-ready PowerPoint.

* Ability to distill complex analysis into clear recommendations for C-suite audiences and manage stakeholders across technical and non-technical teams.

* Highly organized, proactive, and effective at driving multiple workstreams to completion in a fast-moving environment.

* Bachelor's degree in business, engineering, economics, or a related field; MBA a plus.

* Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. government security clearance.

* Familiarity with defense, aerospace, or government markets preferred.
