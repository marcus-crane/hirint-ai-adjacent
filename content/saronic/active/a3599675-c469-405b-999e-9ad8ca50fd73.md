Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

Saronic is hiring a Lead Videographer to take ownership of filming across our various locations. You will lead all aspects of filming on solo or small-team shoots, and direct camera on larger productions when needed. You thrive in fast-changing environments, are comfortable working independently, and bring a strong technical foundation across camera operation, lighting, and aerial work.

# **Responsibilities**

• Lead all aspects of filming on solo location shoots, from setup through wrap.

• Direct camera on larger productions, leading additional operators and setting the plan with the

producer.

• You set the look of what we shoot: camera, lighting, and framing.

• Operate drones for aerial footage in compliance with FAA Part 107 regulations.

• Hold the camera and lighting standards the rest of the team shoots to.

• Travel to our various locations on a regular basis (including internationally).

• Adapt quickly to changing shoot conditions, schedules, and creative briefs.

• Maintain, transport, and care for all camera and production equipment.

# **Qualifications**

• 6+ years of professional videography experience.

• FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate required.

• Demonstrated expertise with gimbal systems.

• Strong understanding of 3-point lighting and practical lighting setups.

• Experience working on both independent shoots and as part of a larger crew, including leading

camera.

• Confident working autonomously and making creative decisions independently.

• Willingness and ability to travel frequently.

• Strong organizational skills and attention to detail in equipment management.

• Experience in post-production and video editing workflows, color grading, or a background in

marine, industrial, or documentary filmmaking is a plus, not a requirement.

# **Physical Demands**

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
* Occasional standing and walking within the office
* Manual dexterity to operate a computer keyboard, mouse, and other office equipment
* Visual acuity to read screens, documents, and reports
* Occasional reaching, bending, or stooping to access file drawers, cabinets, or office supplies
* Lifting and carrying items up to 20 pounds occasionally (e.g., office supplies, packages)
