Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking a Facilities Coordinator to keep day-to-day work moving across new site openings and ongoing facility and construction projects by tracking schedules, vendors, budgets, purchase orders, and permits to make sure everything lands on time. This role is the connective tissue between Expansion, Real Estate, Facilities, and Construction leads and the outside parties who execute the work (landlords, general contractors, engineers, and vendors) serving as the point person who keeps submittals, RFIs, and open items moving instead of sitting in someone’s inbox.  
  
The ideal candidate is detail-heavy and organized, with many projects running at once, at different stages and in different locations. At Saronic, priorities can shift quickly. The right person stays on top of a high volume of moving pieces, knows what to chase down versus what to flag up, and keeps the bigger picture in view even while deep in schedules and open items lists.

# **Responsibilities**

* You track timelines, milestones, and dependencies for each new site, from lease signature through daily operations.
* You serve as the liaison between Expansion, Real Estate, Construction, and Engineering leads and outside parties on tenant improvements, construction schedules, close-out items, and ongoing landlord obligations.
* You create and track workflows for submittals, RFIs, and work approvals, keeping them prioritized and moving day to day.
* You manage vendor quotes, scopes, purchase orders, and invoices for construction, build-out, and ongoing facility needs, and prepare purchase requisitions while reconciling budgets with team leads.
* You schedule vendor walkthroughs, site surveys, and inspections, and track punch lists, warranty, and maintenance items through to closure.
* You coordinate building systems work with IT, Security, and EHS including power, cooling, fire suppression, and low-voltage cabling.
* You maintain site files (permits, certificates of insurance, drawings, vendor contacts) and keep floor plans, space programs, and headcount plans current for every team that needs them.
* You support move-in planning and day-one readiness, managing storage, receiving, utilities, janitorial, signage, and onboard service vendors.
* You run a weekly open-items list across sites and prepare clear, concise status updates for leadership.
* You keep decisions, owners, and due dates documented so nothing gets lost between meetings, and take notes during cross-team meetings to track progress and blockers.
* You flag risks to team leads before they threaten a go-live date or project milestone.
* You verify schedules and field updates against reality, keeping project trackers accurate as work progresses.

# **Qualifications**

* 5-7+ years of experience in project coordination, facilities, real-estate, construction, or operations support.
* Strong organizational skills, with a track record of juggling many deadlines, vendors, and open items across multiple projects at once.
* Clear written and verbal communication, including concise status updates for leadership.
* Comfortable working directly with vendors, contractors, and landlords until items are closed out.
* Proficiency with spreadsheets, project trackers, and shared documents; experience with purchase order or procurement systems vital as well.
* Familiarity with permitting, fire and life safety, or basic building code concepts is a plus.
* Experience supporting new site openings, build-outs, or tenant improvement projects.
* Familiarity with IT, security, or EHS requirements for office, warehouse, or industrial space is a plus.
* Experience with a project management tool and Gantt-style scheduling is a plus.
* Bachelor’s degree preferred; equivalent practical experience considered.
