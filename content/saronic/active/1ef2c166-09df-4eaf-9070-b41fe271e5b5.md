Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking a detail-oriented and experienced Quality Assurance Engineer to join our Enterprise Technology team. In this role, you will be primarily responsible for ensuring the quality of the integrations that connect Saronic's core business systems: Salesforce, NetSuite and MuleSoft. You will work closely with developers, system administrators and business teams to test data flows end to end, from an opportunity in Salesforce, through MuleSoft APIs, to customers, orders, pricing and inventory in NetSuite, ensuring our systems meet business requirements before they reach production. You will join a growing QA function and partner closely with Product Owners, Business Systems, Engineering, Integration and Security teams. Our environment requires disciplined change management, documentation and traceability.

# **Responsibilities**

* **Integration Testing:** Plan and execute end-to-end tests of MuleSoft integrations between Salesforce, NetSuite and other business systems. Verify data at every hop: source records in Salesforce, message processing and errors in Anypoint Runtime Manager logs, resulting records in NetSuite, and related Slack notifications and approvals. Validate request and response payloads, data mapping, error handling, idempotency and transactional integrity.
* **Salesforce Testing:** Test Salesforce configuration, flows, approvals and custom objects across sandboxes. Build your own test data, such as accounts, opportunities, products and orders, to drive downstream syncs.
* **NetSuite Testing:** Validate customers, sales orders, purchase orders, bills, items, BOMs, pricing and inventory created by integrations. Use SuiteQL and saved searches to confirm data, and test approval workflows and routing.
* **Test Planning:** Partner with product and delivery teams to translate requirements and user stories into testable acceptance criteria, test plans and test cases before development is complete. Execute functional, integration, regression and UAT support cycles, and help maintain regression coverage as systems scale and change velocity increases.
* **Test Automation:** Write and maintain automated tests using Playwright and TypeScript. Use AI-assisted tooling, including Playwright's test agents and Claude, to draft tests, and review every generated test for intent before it merges.
* **Defect Management:** Log defects in Linear with exact steps, expected and actual results, and evidence. Retest fixes and track issues to closure across sandbox environments.
* **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Work with developers, Salesforce and NetSuite administrators, integration partners and business users during live end-to-end and UAT sessions. Post clear, concise status updates in Linear and Slack.
* **Documentation and Reporting:** Maintain test scenarios, evidence and runbooks so any team member can rerun a test. Report quality status and open risks ahead of each release. Support release readiness and post-release validation, and keep traceability from requirements through testing and release, aligned with change management and internal control expectations.

# **Qualifications**

* **Education:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
* **Experience:** Minimum of 3-5 years of experience in software quality engineering, including at least 2 years testing enterprise system integrations.
* **MuleSoft:** Experience testing MuleSoft integrations, including reading Anypoint Runtime Manager logs and tracing a single record across flows and systems.
* **Salesforce:** Hands-on testing in Salesforce: objects, fields, flows, approvals and sandbox test data setup.
* **NetSuite:** Hands-on testing in NetSuite: transactions, item and customer records, approval workflows, saved searches and SuiteQL.
* **Technical Skills:** Comfort with REST APIs, JSON payloads, Postman and SQL. Working automation experience with Playwright, Cypress or Selenium; TypeScript or JavaScript preferred. Experience with test management and defect tracking tools (e.g., Linear, Jira or similar).
* **Problem-Solving:** Excellent analytical skills, with the ability to isolate where in a multi-system flow a failure occurs.
* **Communication:** Strong written communication. You can write a defect that a developer can act on without a follow-up call, and you report observed facts, not guesses.
* **Attention to Detail:** Meticulous attention to detail and a commitment to maintaining high standards of quality and accuracy.
* **Adaptability:** Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic startup environment with changing priorities and deadlines.
