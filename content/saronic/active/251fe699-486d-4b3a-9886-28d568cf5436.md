Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking an IT Infrastructure Architect to set the technical direction and architecture for IT infrastructure at Port Alpha, Saronic’s next-generation shipyard in Brownsville, Texas. You will lead decisions across network, data center, operational technology (OT), systems and supporting physical infrastructure, partner with our construction team, and coordinate trades from design through commissioning so that the site is ready to scale rapidly.

You are a hands-on technical leader who combines deep infrastructure expertise with a strong understanding of construction and design, and who works independently in a fast-moving environment. As Port Alpha grows, you will build the foundation that keeps the site connected, resilient and secure at scale.

# **Responsibilities**

* Lead IT infrastructure decisions and architecture for Port Alpha, including network, data center, OT, systems and supporting physical infrastructure.

* Create strategic infrastructure plans, roadmaps, standards and reference architectures that align with business goals and can be repeated as the site expands.

* Partner with the construction team, architects and engineers on design, and coordinate trades from planning through commissioning to deliver IT scope on schedule.

* Review construction drawings, specifications and submittals, and provide technical guidance, redlines and approvals for IT-related scope.

* Plan and oversee the network build-out, including wired, wireless, fiber pathways, campus connectivity and resilient external connectivity.

* Design and oversee data center and telecom room spaces, including layout, power, cooling, racks, cabling, compute, storage and backup.

* Lead the architecture of OT networks and infrastructure that support production environments, in partnership with OT, engineering and Information Security teams, including IT and OT segmentation and access control.

* Plan for rapid growth through phased build-outs, capacity planning and forecasting, and lifecycle management.

* Define infrastructure requirements with executives, business lines, engineering teams and vendors, and serve as the technical point of contact on infrastructure projects.

* Ensure solutions meet operational, security, disaster recovery and high availability requirements, and support audits and compliance activities, including NIST 800-171, CMMC and ITAR where applicable.

* Evaluate emerging infrastructure technologies that support future growth, and monitor and optimize infrastructure cost and utilization.

* Manage cabling contractors, telecom carriers and equipment suppliers, and track scope, budget and schedule.

* Work with IT teams at headquarters so that Port Alpha follows Saronic standards and transitions smoothly into ongoing operations and support, and provide escalation support for high-impact infrastructure issues.

* Drive standardized, automated deployment of infrastructure, using infrastructure as code where practical.

* Document designs, as-built records and operating procedures, and mentor engineers and IT staff.

# **Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field (or equivalent experience).

* 10+ years of infrastructure experience, including at least 3 years at a senior engineer or architect level.

* Expert-level knowledge in one infrastructure domain, such as network, data center or systems, and solid working knowledge of at least one other.

* Deep expertise in enterprise network architecture, including LAN, WAN, wireless and fiber, and in network security fundamentals.

* Deep experience with OT and industrial network environments, including IT and OT segmentation, industrial protocols and the operational constraints of production systems.

* Experience designing and building data center or server room environments, including power, cooling, racks and structured cabling, with familiarity with server hardware, virtualization, enterprise storage and backup.

* Experience working with construction teams, architects, general contractors and trades from design through commissioning, including reviewing construction drawings.

* Working knowledge of structured cabling and low-voltage infrastructure standards, including TIA-568 and TIA-569.

* Proven success in large-scale infrastructure design, phased build-outs and capacity planning for rapid growth.

* In-depth knowledge of infrastructure security, disaster recovery and high availability, including security frameworks for regulated environments.

* Strong project management and vendor management skills, including scope, budget and schedule.

* Excellent written and verbal communication skills, from executives to construction and trade partners.

* Ability to work independently with minimal supervision, with sound judgment on architectural trade-offs and comfort with ambiguity and aggressive timelines.

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Experience standing up IT infrastructure at new sites, such as shipyards, factories, labs or large campuses.

* Experience in shipyard, manufacturing or other heavy industrial environments.

* Experience with infrastructure as code and automation tools.

* Industry certifications such as CCNP or equivalent, BICSI RCDD, or PMP.

* Experience working in a defense, aerospace or other regulated environment, with an understanding of defense-grade security requirements and frameworks such as NIST 800-171 or CMMC.

* Willingness and eligibility to obtain a U.S. security clearance.
