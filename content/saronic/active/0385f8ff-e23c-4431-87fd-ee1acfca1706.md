Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking a Growth Strategy & Strategic Operations Analyst to help drive operational rigor, clarity, and scalability across our Growth organization. This role builds and maintains the mechanisms behind Growth's strategic decision-making, including gate review processes, internal operating processes, demand and pipeline forecasting, and the sales infrastructure leadership depends on. It also helps uphold the standard for how the organization plans, prioritizes, and executes as it scales.

You will work closely with Growth leadership and partner across Proposals, Contracts, Programs, Marketing, Product, Government Relations, and Finance, keeping workstreams aligned and cross-functional execution on track. You will be the day-to-day steward of our sales pipeline, GTM processes, customer engagement support, and forecasting mechanisms, while contributing to a broad portfolio of business operations work spanning demand planning, cross-functional coordination, and process documentation and improvement.

We're looking for someone who is highly analytical, operationally minded, and energized by ambiguous problems that cut across multiple parts of the business. You should be comfortable going deep in complex data, learning to design processes that scale, and building strong working relationships across teams.

# **Responsibilities**

* **Pipeline Management & Reporting:** Maintain the sales pipeline day to day, including CRM data integrity, stage definitions, close-date discipline, and the reporting and dashboards leadership relies on to track the business. Support opportunity progression through gate mechanisms such as Concept Reviews, preparing materials and tracking follow-ups so the organization holds a consistent bar at each stage.

* **Growth Operations:** Support core Growth operations, and Commercial Price List (CPL) maintenance, keeping the CPL in sync with the tools and processes that depend on it (e.g., NetSuite). Contribute to demand planning by building and running forecast simulations, modeling resource requirements, and tracking capacity against materialized demand, surfacing improvements to the planning process as you go. Together these give leadership a defensible, decision-ready view of the business.

* **Process Design & Documentation:** Help build out the Growth organization's core operating processes by identifying gaps, codifying how work moves across sales, marketing, product, and programs (e.g., the Opportunity Management SOP), and keeping that documentation current as the business scales.

* **Capture Support:** Support cross-functional execution on the company's most consequential pursuits, coordinating across Growth, Programs, Product, Proposals, Contracts, and Leadership to assemble inputs, track action items, and keep proposal strategy moving. Build and maintain the customer-facing collateral that carries those pursuits, including product packets, slick sheets, and engagement-specific materials.

* **Special Projects:** Take on ambiguous problems, structure the analysis, and drive workstreams from framing through implementation in partnership with Growth leadership.

# **Qualifications**

* 2+ years in Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, Strategy & Operations, or a comparable start in consulting, investment banking, or corporate finance.

* Bachelor's degree in business, engineering, economics, or a related field.

* Exposure to, or strong interest in, defense, aerospace, or another regulated, government-adjacent industry.

* Experience contributing to complex, cross-functional workstreams, with clear examples of what you owned and what it delivered.

* Strong command of analytics and communication tools (e.g., Excel, PowerPoint) and working familiarity with CRM and BI platforms (e.g., Salesforce, Tableau), with the ability to master new tooling quickly.

* Clear written and verbal communication with both technical and non-technical audiences, including comfort presenting work to senior stakeholders.

* Comfort operating with incomplete information in a fast-moving environment, with the instinct to ask questions early and flag risks before they compound.

* Highly organized, detail-oriented, and proactive in managing competing priorities across multiple stakeholders.
