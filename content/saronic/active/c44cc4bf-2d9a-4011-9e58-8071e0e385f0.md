Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

Saronic is hiring a Senior Video Producer to own our video projects from first idea to final delivery. You will bring a commercial producer's discipline with schedules and budgets, and a documentary producer's instinct for story. You will work with teams across the organization, so people skills matter as much as production skills.

This role leads a wide range of projects, from polished commercial spots to long-form, story-driven documentary content. You will partner with leaders, subject-matter experts, creative staff, and outside crews to turn their needs into clear, compelling video. You will set the plan, keep stakeholders aligned, and deliver on time and on budget.

# **Responsibilities**

• Manage video projects end to end: concept, scripting, pre-production, shoot, post-production, and delivery.

• Build and manage budgets, schedules, and production plans, and flag risks early.

• Hire, direct, and manage freelance crew, directors, editors, animators, and vendors.

• Shape strong narratives for both short-form commercial pieces and long-form documentary projects.

• Guide interviews, on-location shoots, and the creative direction of each project.

• Partner with editors and post-production teams to protect quality and the story through final cut.

• Work with several departments and teams to understand goals, gather input, and align on messaging.

• Reconcile feedback and approvals from multiple stakeholders into clear direction.

• Set expectations about scope, timelines, and tradeoffs, and keep everyone informed of progress.

• Coordinate production across our various sites.

• Oversee permits, releases, licensing, insurance, and rights clearances, and maintain organized project files, assets, and archives.

• Help establish and improve production workflows and standards.

# **Qualifications**

• 7+ years of professional video production experience, including producing work in both commercial and documentary settings.

• A portfolio that shows range, from short-form branded or advertising work to long-form, story-driven content.

• Proven command of the full production lifecycle: development, pre-production, shooting, post-production, and delivery.

• Strong budgeting, scheduling, and resource-management skills.

• Experience hiring and directing freelance crews and vendors.

• Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a track record of working through many stakeholders and personalities.

• Skill at handling feedback, resolving disagreements diplomatically, and keeping projects moving without losing relationships.

• Working knowledge of cameras, lighting, audio, and modern post-production workflows (e.g., Adobe Premiere, DaVinci Resolve, Final Cut).

• Ability to manage several projects at once and meet deadlines under pressure.

• In-house or agency experience, familiarity with motion graphics, animation, or archival and licensing workflows, or experience with remote and multi-location productions is a plus, not a requirement.
