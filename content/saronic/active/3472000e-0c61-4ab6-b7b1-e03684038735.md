Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# Job Summary:

The Executive Protection Agent will support the security of executive leadership by providing close protection, secure transportation, and residential security. You will collaborate with the Risk Intelligence team to identify risks to executives and their families and implement appropriate mitigations.

Responsibilities:

* Independently support protective operations, travel security planning, and risk mitigation activities.

* Assist with the development, coordination, and execution of travel security plans, including advance work, transportation security, accommodation assessments, and contingency planning.

* Conduct threat monitoring, intelligence gathering, and vulnerability assessments to identify emerging risks and recommend appropriate protective measures.

* Ensure adherence to established security policies, operational procedures, and emergency response protocols while maintaining operational readiness and situational awareness.

Qualifications

* Bachelor’s degree in Criminal Justice or related field (or equivalent experience)

* 2+ years of experience in executive protection, law enforcement, military, or private security

* Experience integrating threat assessments into protective intelligence strategies

* Experience conducting travel security planning and logistics

* Strong decision-making under pressure

* High level of discretion, integrity and professionalism

* Advanced situational awareness and risk mitigation skills

* Effective written and verbal communication
* Must be willing to travel up to 50%

# **Physical Demands**

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer

* Occasional reaching, bending, or stooping to access file drawers, cabinets, or office supplies
