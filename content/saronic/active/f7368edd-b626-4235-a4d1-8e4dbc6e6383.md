Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking a Sr. Skills & Career Architecture Specialist to build and own the skills and qualification architecture for our manufacturing and trades workforce, extending company-wide as the framework matures. You will define what “qualified” means for each trade job family, design the assessments that verify it, build the qualification ladders that show people how to advance, and maintain the qualification data that tells Production whether we have the certified labor to staff a build.

The architecture you build is a training instrument. You will partner with Production and Quality, who own work execution and inspection standards; with our Instructional Designers, who deliver the training that closes the gaps you identify; and with HR and Compensation, who own job architecture, leveling, and pay structure. Success means any leader can answer, on demand, who is qualified to do what work, at what proficiency, at which site — and what it takes to reach the next level.

# **Responsibilities**

* Define and maintain the skills taxonomy for trade and production job families, including proficiency levels and the criteria that distinguish each level.
* Build and own qualification matrices by job family, site, and work scope, mapping required skills and certifications to each role.
* Establish what constitutes a qualification of record — evidence, sign-off authority, and documentation standard — and define recertification intervals, including code-driven requalification.
* Design the practical, hands-on assessment instruments and scoring criteria that verify proficiency against defined standards rather than course completion.
* Enable frontline managers and leads to assess their own teams; build assessor guidance and job aids, and train and calibrate assessors so the same standard produces the same rating at Austin, Franklin, and Port Alpha.
* Translate assessment results into prioritized capability gaps for Learning & Development and coordinate code-required qualification testing with Quality and third-party testing bodies.
* Define the qualification ladder for each trade job family — the skills, certifications, and demonstrated proficiency required to advance at each step — and extend the model to additional job families as the framework matures.
* Partner with HR and Compensation to align the ladder to existing job levels and pay structure; HR and Compensation own job architecture, leveling, and compensation decisions.
* Own the qualification system of record, maintaining accurate and current skills and certification data for the production workforce.
* Monitor certification expirations and give managers and Production leadership advance visibility of upcoming lapses. Define the requalification standard and process; Production owns scheduling and completion.
* Build and maintain reporting on qualified headcount by trade, proficiency level, site, and certification status to support production planning, workforce capacity decisions, and audit readiness.

# **Qualifications**

**Required:**

• 5+ years in workforce capability, technical training, competency framework design, or qualification management within a manufacturing, industrial, construction, or defense environment.

• Demonstrated experience building a skills taxonomy, competency model, or qualification matrix from scratch and putting it into operational use — not solely maintaining an existing one.

• Experience designing practical, hands-on capability assessment instruments and scoring criteria, and enabling non-L&D evaluators — managers, leads, or supervisors — to apply them consistently through train-the-assessor and calibration.

• Working knowledge of trade qualification regimes (welding, machining, NDT, or comparable), including how code and customer requirements drive requalification and currency.

• Strong data discipline, including the ability to build, maintain, and report from structured qualification data.

• Demonstrated ability to earn credibility with skilled trades and production leadership on the floor, and to translate technical qualification concepts for both floor and executive audiences.

• Ability to build structure in a greenfield environment with limited precedent and competing priorities.

• U.S. Person status as defined below.

**Preferred**:

• Experience in shipbuilding, marine fabrication, aerospace, or another regulated, safety-critical manufacturing environment.

• Familiarity with AWS welding qualification standards, particularly aluminum, or comparable code-based qualification systems.

• Experience aligning qualification or progression frameworks with HR job leveling and compensation structures.

• Experience configuring or operating skills and qualification tracking within an LMS, LXP, or skills management platform.

• Experience standing up structured on-the-job training with task-level qualification sign-off, such as TWI Job Instruction.

• Experience with workforce capacity planning, multi-site operations, or greenfield site rampups.
