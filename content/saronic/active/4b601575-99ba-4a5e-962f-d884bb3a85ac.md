Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking an IT Systems Engineer to help build and run the servers, storage, virtualization and cloud infrastructure that support engineering, manufacturing and corporate operations at Saronic. You will own systems and projects with growing independence, improve reliability and security, and use automation and infrastructure as code to make our environments consistent and repeatable.

This role touches all aspects of IT and partners with every area of the organization, including network, operational technology, cybersecurity, enterprise applications and service desk. This role is a good fit for an engineer who has moved beyond day-to-day administration and wants to own systems end to end in a fast-moving defense technology company.

# **Responsibilities**

* Design, deploy, operate and maintain Windows and Linux servers, virtualization platforms, enterprise storage and core infrastructure services across on-premises, edge and hybrid cloud environments.

* Own assigned systems and projects from design through production support, including planning, change management, documentation and handoff.

* Use infrastructure as code and automation, such as Terraform and Ansible (or similar), to build, configure and patch systems consistently, and reduce manual work and configuration drift.

* Support cloud infrastructure in hybrid environments, including provisioning, access, cost awareness and workload migrations.

* Support the infrastructure behind PLM, CAD and other engineering platforms, including servers, storage, licensing, performance and availability, in partnership with engineering and application owners.

* Support systems for operational and manufacturing technology, such as lab, edge and shop-floor compute and storage, in partnership with OT and manufacturing teams.

* Maintain monitoring, alerting, patching, backup and recovery for assigned systems, and take part in disaster recovery testing.

* Respond to incidents and escalations from the Service Desk and other teams, perform root-cause analysis, and contribute to runbooks and post-incident reviews.

* Support controls aligned to NIST 800-171, DFARS, CMMC and ITAR, including system hardening, vulnerability remediation and audit evidence, in partnership with Information Security.

* Partner with Network Operations, Facilities and other teams on new facility build-outs and system deployments at Saronic sites.

* Help mentor associate engineers and technicians, and share knowledge through documentation and working sessions.

* Take part in on-call rotations and after-hours maintenance as needed.

# **Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent experience).

* 4+ years of experience in systems engineering, systems administration or infrastructure engineering.

* Hands-on experience with Windows Server, Linux, virtualization and enterprise storage.

* Experience with backup, recovery, patching and monitoring for production systems.

* Experience with scripting and automation, such as PowerShell, Python or Bash (or similar).

* Working knowledge of infrastructure as code, such as Terraform and Ansible (or similar).

* Experience supporting cloud environments, such as AWS or Azure (or similar).

* Ability to troubleshoot complex systems issues independently, and to work with other teams to resolve them.

* Clear written and verbal communication skills, including documenting systems and explaining technical concepts to non-technical audiences.

* Ability to support on-call and after-hours work, and to travel occasionally to other Saronic sites.

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Industry certifications such as Microsoft, Red Hat, AWS, Azure or ITIL.

* Experience with government cloud environments, such as AWS GovCloud or equivalent FedRAMP-aligned environments.

* Experience supporting PLM, CAD or other engineering design and data management platforms.

* Experience with engineering compute, such as HPC or GPU clusters.

* Experience supporting manufacturing, lab or other operational technology environments.

* Familiarity with NIST 800-171, DFARS, CMMC and ITAR, and handling of controlled unclassified information (CUI).

* Experience working in a defense, aerospace or other regulated environment.

* Willingness and eligibility to obtain a U.S. security clearance.
