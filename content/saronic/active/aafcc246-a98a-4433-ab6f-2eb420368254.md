Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking a Staff Data Engineer to join Internal Business Tools (IBT) which builds custom applications, analytics, and digital solutions for Saronic's internal operations. You will architect and build our enterprise data platform on Databricks.

You will own the medallion architecture across Saronic's business units and critical domains. That data feeds both the internal tools our team builds and the analytics business teams run themselves. You will understand what the data means and how sensitive it is, and design pipelines and access controls around that.

You will also be the key technical consultant to the business-led analyst teams building on the platform. You will be embedded with the business teams you support, working alongside them and their leaders so the platform reflects how they actually work. This role suits a hands-on engineer who enjoys setting technical direction as much as writing code, on a platform that is still taking shape.

# **Responsibilities**

* Architect and own our Databricks lakehouse: medallion layers, catalog layout, environments, and cost.
* Build batch, CDC, and streaming ingestion from ERP, other enterprise systems, and internally built software, with traceable, replayable loads.
* Design conformed data models and shared business definitions that serve our internal tools, analytics, and BI.
* Create reusable pipeline patterns and standards that domain teams adopt as they onboard.
* Own data quality, lineage, and documentation, with data contracts for published datasets. Quarantine bad records rather than silently dropping them.
* Apply classification, retention, and access controls in Unity Catalog, including for export-controlled, CUI, HR, and financial data.
* Consult for business-led analyst teams: set ownership boundaries and onboarding paths, review their work, and help them self-serve safely.
* Embed with business teams and their leaders to learn their workflows and data needs first-hand, and turn what you learn into platform priorities.
* Run the platform like production software: CI/CD, infrastructure as code, dev/test/prod promotion, and incident response.
* Be a technical voice on the shared data platform roadmap and help make documented build-versus-buy decisions.
* Mentor engineers and analysts, and help grow the data engineering function.

# **Qualifications**

**Required**

* 10+ years of data engineering experience, including owning a data platform's architecture in production.
* 3+ years building production pipelines on Databricks, including Delta Lake, Lakeflow Jobs, and a medallion architecture.
* Expert SQL, and strong Python and PySpark.
* Experience with batch, CDC, and streaming ingestion from enterprise systems such as ERP, HRIS, CRM, or MES, and from internally built applications.
* Strong dimensional and cross-domain data modeling skills.
* Experience with Unity Catalog and with applying classification and access controls in a regulated or security-sensitive environment.
* Experience with CI/CD, testing, and monitoring for data pipelines.
* A track record of leading technical direction without direct authority.
* Experience working directly with non-technical stakeholders, ideally embedded with a business team, explaining tradeoffs in plain terms, and earning their trust as a partner rather than a service provider.
* Comfort shaping standards on a platform that is still being built.
* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, Information Systems, or a related field, or equivalent experience.

**Preferred**

* Standing up a Databricks environment from an early stage, including Lakeflow Declarative Pipelines, Databricks SQL, Asset Bundles, and system tables.
* Databricks Data Engineer Professional or Associate certification.
* Enabling multiple business domains on one shared platform.
* Feeding operational applications, not just reporting, from a lakehouse.
* Building a semantic or metrics layer.
* Fivetran, Airbyte, or Lakeflow Connect, and Terraform.
* Defense, aerospace, maritime, or manufacturing experience, including export-controlled data.
* AWS or Azure.
* Using AI tools to speed up engineering, with sound judgment about outputs and

  sensitive data.
