Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Position Overview**

The NPI Production Inspector is responsible for inspecting incoming mechanical components for dimensional conformity as well as vessel sub-assembly system inspection. Ensure components and assemblies meet internal quality standards, engineering requirements, and applicable specifications. This role requires strong attention to detail, the ability to follow procedures, and a solid understanding of inspection techniques. The Production Inspector works closely with various launch teams to identify defects, ensure compliance, and support overall product quality.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Perform in-process and final inspections on assemblies

- Verify components and assemblies meet drawings and specifications

- Check dimensional tolerances using appropriate measurement tools

- Validate torque values and torque striping

- Identify defects and nonconformances with accuracy

- Document inspection results clearly within company systems

- Support composite and basic weld inspection activities as required

- Assist in receiving inspection and system rollout at alternate production locations

- Participate in First Article Build with Design and NPI Quality

- Lead First Article Inspection for prototype builds (occasionally at supplier, if needed)

- Develop skillset in 3D modeling systems

- Mature inspection plans for assigned commodities in preparation for volume production

- Assist in troubleshooting, repair, and retrofit of prototype vessels

- Partner with Manufacturing department in PFMEA development and reviews

**Required Qualifications**

- Working knowledge of IPC/WHMA-A-620 standards preferred

- Strong attention to detail

- Ability to read and interpret drawings and work instructions

- Basic understanding of dimensional tolerances and measurement tools

- Understanding of torque values and torque striping verification

- Exposure to composite inspection preferred

- Basic understanding of welding inspection fundamentals preferred

- Good documentation and communication skills

- Ability to follow procedures and standards consistently
