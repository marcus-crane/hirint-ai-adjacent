Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking a Insider Threat Investigations Analyst to conduct end to end, from initial triage through case closure. Own investigative fact development, evidence correlation, behavioral risk analysis, and defensible report production. Partner with People, Legal, Cybersecurity, and Physical Security. Add dedicated investigative capacity so referrals are worked promptly and thoroughly as the company scales across multiple sites, including Gulf Coast production. Carry investigations independently, exercise sound judgment on personnel-sensitive matters, and write to a standard that holds up under legal review.

# **Responsibilities**

* Conduct insider threat investigations involving data misuse, intellectual property protection concerns, access abuse, fraud indicators, policy violations, and personnel-related security risks. Build investigative plans, timelines, and evidence maps, and manage multiple concurrent investigations through to closure.
* Triage insider threat alerts and referrals, apply threshold criteria, and open investigations when those criteria are met.
* Collect and analyze identity and access logs, data loss prevention events, device and network artifacts, badge and physical access records, People Team inputs, and open-source and internal information. Correlate technical indicators with behavioral indicators and partner with Cybersecurity and digital forensics for deep-dive analysis.
* Preserve evidence and maintain complete, well-organized case files suitable for legal review.
* Coordinate with the People Team and Legal on personnel-sensitive investigative actions. Prepare and deliver concise investigative briefings. Support interview preparation and documentation, and engage with outside counsel or law enforcement when directed.
* Work alongside Risk Intelligence on counterintelligence matters, pairing internal investigative findings with external open-source and public-records collection, and adjudicating both through a single joint intake. Support matters carrying a foreign or external nexus, including undisclosed outside business activity, foreign contact and travel concerns, and attempted targeting or recruitment of Saronic personnel. Escalate to Legal and Security leadership as appropriate.
* Produce clear, objective, and actionable investigative reports and closure summaries that document outcomes, mitigation recommendations, and follow-up requirements. Capture lessons learned and provide case-driven feedback that improves detection quality and reduces false positives.
* Support insider threat program requirements under 32 CFR Part 117, including adverse information reporting and records handling.

# **Qualifications**

**Required**

* Bachelor's degree, or equivalent practical experience, in Criminal Justice, Intelligence Studies, Security Management, Cybersecurity, Psychology, or a related field.
* 4+ years of experience in investigations, insider threat, intelligence analysis, security operations, or a related role.
* Demonstrated success handling investigations with personnel and legal implications.
* Strong analytical judgment, disciplined evidence handling, and clear written reporting.
* Ability to handle confidential information and personnel matters with discretion and professionalism.
* Ability to work independently, prioritize competing matters, and carry a case from intake to closure without day-to-day direction.
* Ability to obtain a TS/SCI clearance and to work in controlled or classified environments as required. U.S. Person status required for access to export-controlled information.

**Preferred**

* Experience in insider threat, personnel security, workplace investigations, or corporate internal investigations.
* Familiarity with UEBA, SIEM, DLP, and case management platforms, and with building a timeline from multiple log sources.
* Experience integrating technical evidence with behavioral and open-source investigative inputs.
* Experience supporting investigations across geographically distributed manufacturing or production sites.
* Working knowledge of NISPOM and the National Insider Threat Policy minimum standards.
* Interview support or interview documentation experience.
* Prior defense, Intelligence Community, or cleared industry experience.
* Active TS/SCI clearance.
