Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking a Staff SCADA Integration Engineer to be the technical authority for

how our plant-floor and field systems connect to the rest of the business. You will own the

architecture, standards, and delivery of SCADA, historian, and data integrations from PLC

to enterprise systems, and you will raise the bar for the engineers around you. This is an

individual-contributor role with significant technical leadership: you set direction, review

others' work, and take the hardest integration and commissioning problems yourself.

# **Responsibilities**

* Define and own the reference architecture for SCADA, including gateway design, redundancy and high availability, security, backup and recovery, and environment promotion (dev, test, prod).
* Design, build, and commission integrations between PLCs/RTUs, SCADA/HMI, historians, and enterprise systems (MES, ERP, databases, cloud data platforms).
* Develop and maintain tag models, UDTs, equipment hierarchies, alarm philosophies, and standardized templates so new sites and lines can be onboarded repeatably.
* Build and support industrial data flows over OPC UA, MQTT/Sparkplug B, Modbus, DNP3, EtherNet/IP, and REST, including historian and SQL data capture and reporting.
* Lead site commissioning, validation, and go-live support, and troubleshoot complex issues across controls, network, and software layers.
* Partner with OT networking, IT, and cybersecurity teams to apply sound segmentation, authentication, and access-control practices (IEC 62443 aligned).
* Set coding, documentation, and review standards; review third-party and vendor integration work for maintainability.
* Mentor engineers, lead small-to-medium projects, and write the design specs, test plans, and runbooks that keep the systems supportable.

# **Qualifications**

**Required**

* 8+ years of hands-on SCADA integration or controls engineering experience, with a track record of owning systems end to end.
* Deep expertise in at least one major SCADA platform [Inductive Automation Ignition, AVEVA System Platform/InTouch, Rockwell FactoryTalk, Siemens WinCC, or similar].
* Strong working knowledge of industrial protocols: OPC UA/DA, Modbus TCP/RTU, and at least one of DNP3, EtherNet/IP, MQTT/Sparkplug B.
* Experience integrating SCADA with historians (e.g., AVEVA/PI, Canary, Ignition Tag Historian) and relational databases, including writing and tuning SQL.
* Scripting or programming proficiency (Python plus one of Java/Jython, C#, JavaScript, or VBScript).
* Experience with PLC/RTU connectivity (Allen-Bradley, Siemens, Schneider, or similar) and industrial networking fundamentals.
* Demonstrated technical leadership: setting standards, mentoring, and communicating clearly with engineers, operations, and IT.
* BS in Electrical, Computer, or Controls Engineering, Computer Science, or equivalent experience.

**Preferred**

* Experience integrating SCADA with MES/OEE and ERP systems.
* Platform certifications (e.g., Ignition Core/Gold).
* Background in IT/OT security and network segmentation, including AD/LDAP integration and certificate-based TLS.
* Familiarity with Linux server environments, containers, and cloud data platforms.
* Experience with UNS / Sparkplug B architectures.
* Experience in manufacturing environments.
