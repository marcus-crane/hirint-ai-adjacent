Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

Saronic is seeking a Program Operations Analyst to support the drumbeat of our Programs organization and help scale autonomous surface vessel (ASV) and shipbuilding programs from award through delivery. You will support Program Managers with execution and delivery in support of urgent and critical customer missions. In addition to direct program support, you will facilitate process, governance, and operations of the programs team including sales pipeline maintenance, briefing material development, and tiger-team project management.

The Program Analyst will serve as a representative of the Programs team across the company, working closely with Growth, Proposals, Contracts, Legal, Engineering, Production, and Mission Services. This role is ideal for a driven, self-starter with 3-5 years of program or project operations experience in defense technology, consulting, or startup business operations. The ideal candidate thrives in a dynamic, fast-paced environment, builds relationships across the company, jumps in to fill gaps, and is eager to take on increasing ownership. You will work closely with program managers, engineers, and external customers to ensure programs are delivered on time, within scope, and in compliance with government and internal standards.

# **Responsibilities**

**Program Portfolio Oversight:**

* Track customer-funded deliverables across multiple programs, including active programs in execution and high-pWin opportunities in the sales pipeline, and keep program leadership informed of status, risks, and resource needs.
* Support the rhythm of the Programs organization by driving timelines, deliverables, resource requirements, and key milestones across the portfolio.
* Proactively look for ways to contribute; jump in to help close gaps and escalate issues before they impact customers or the mission.
* Recommend and implement enhancements to program management processes, tools, and workflows to improve efficiency and accuracy.
* Run internal campaigns to implement enhancements to program management processes, tools, and workflows to improve efficiency and accuracy.

**Pipeline Visibility & Handoffs:**

* Learn and track the sales pipeline, including both qualitative updates and quantitative values (e.g., pWin, contract value, timing), and help translate it into Programs resourcing and execution planning.
* Help plan and coordinate Programs kickoffs for newly awarded contracts, supporting alignment of cross-functional teams on the execution path forward.
* Support proposal development efforts by analyzing schedule and capability inputs for program execution planning, and help drive smooth transitions from award into execution.

**Program Execution:**

* Coordinate internal and external reviews, program kickoff meetings, technical interchange meetings (TIMs), and status updates.
* Develop and maintain program execution documents (e.g., CDRLs, program schedules, Integrated Master Schedule inputs, and performance tracking metrics).
* Support program managers with contractual and technical documentation, including maintaining a baselined schedule, scope, budget, and risk register.

**Cross-Functional Coordination:**

* Meet and build relationships with teams across the company, and develop a deep understanding of how Programs interacts with each team to advance Saronic’s growth and mission.
* Serve as a point of contact between program managers and internal departments, including engineering, production, procurement, mission services, finance, and business development.
* Support tiger teams addressing urgent issues and risks on active programs; help identify problems, draft work plans and COAs, prepare leadership briefings, and track execution with functional leads.
* Help ensure alignment between technical deliverables and contractual requirements and regulations.
* Partner with Engineering, Quality, Software, Mission Operations, and other teams to gather data & evidence, resolve discrepancies, and ensure program alignment.

**Governance, Reporting & Process:**

* Develop, track, and report on program dashboards and metrics for program leadership, internal stakeholders, and customers.
* Help develop and maintain standard processes, SOPs, and tools for program documentation and reporting, and contribute to improving how the Programs team operates.
* Drive governance and process with existing systems like work breakdown structures, financial tracking & reporting mechanisms, and integrated master schedules are critical to supporting program delivery.

**Compliance & Contract Support:**

* Work with the legal and contracts teams to track contractual obligations, government standards, and compliance flow-downs.
* Help ensure program activities align with DoD acquisition processes and internal SOPs.

**Customer Interface & Engagement:**

* Prepare high-quality PowerPoint materials for customer deliverables, briefings, and demos.
* Coordinate customer visits, test events, and logistics for field exercises.
* Support timely, professional communication with program sponsors, B2B partners, and government customers.

**Team & Culture:**

* Contribute to the Programs team and Saronic culture: help teammates, row in the same direction, and keep us moving fast.
* Grow with the Programs organization, taking on increased scope and ownership over time.

# **Qualifications**

**Education:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, International Relations, Public Policy, or a related discipline.

**Experience:**

* 3-5 years of experience in business operations, program or project management, or program support in a high op-tempo environment in the U.S. Government or a startup.
* Familiarity with defense acquisition lifecycles, DoD contracting structures, and customer engagement.

* Experience with Salesforce CRM or similar tools, including pipeline upkeep, testing, and reporting functions.

* Comfort working through ambiguity and solving new problems, with a willingness to learn quickly.
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to interact effectively across internal teams, external partners, and customers.
* Attention to detail with a focus on maintaining high-quality standards in all activities and deliverables.

**Preferred Qualifications:**

* Experience supporting handoffs between business development, capture, contracts, and execution teams.
