Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking a Network Administrator to support the day-to-day operation, security and reliability of our enterprise network. You will administer wired, wireless and perimeter infrastructure across multiple locations, respond to incidents, and carry out network changes and improvements in partnership with our senior network engineers.

You are a hands-on administrator who is equally comfortable in a device console, a monitoring dashboard and a root-cause investigation, who looks for opportunities to automate routine work and brings a customer-service mindset to supporting our employees. As Saronic grows, you will help keep our network available, optimized and secure at scale.

# **Responsibilities**

* Administer and support the security, performance and availability of our switching, routing, wireless and firewall infrastructure using industry-leading tools like Cisco, Palo Alto and Aruba (or similar).

* Manage secure remote access, including site-to-site and client-based VPN connectivity.

* Monitor network health and performance, respond to alerts, and resolve issues before they impact users.

* Troubleshoot network incidents, escalate complex issues to senior engineers, and document root cause and resolution.

* Implement network changes, upgrades and expansions in line with change management practices and with minimal disruption to operations.

* Support network segmentation and access control policies in partnership with Information Security.

* Identify and deploy automation opportunities to reduce manual work and configuration errors.

* Collaborate with cross-functional teams, including Information Security, Systems Engineering and IT Support, to resolve enterprise-level issues.

* Maintain network documentation, including topology diagrams, IP schemas and operating procedures.

* Support compliance requirements and internal and external audits as needed.

# **Qualifications**

* Industry certifications such as Cisco CCNA or CCNP, or Palo Alto PCNSE.

* Experience with network access control and 802.1X implementations.

* Familiarity with zero trust network architecture and SASE or SSE solutions.

* Working knowledge of network automation using scripting tools (e.g., Python, Ansible, PowerShell).

* Exposure to SD-WAN, IP address management and QoS configuration.

* Experience supporting networks in hybrid or cloud environments, such as AWS/AWS GovCloud or Azure.

* Experience working in a defense, aerospace or other regulated environment, with familiarity with frameworks such as NIST 800-171 or CMMC.
* Willingness and eligibility to obtain a U.S. security clearance.

**Physical Demands**

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
* Occasional standing and walking within the office
* Manual dexterity to operate a computer keyboard, mouse, and other office equipment
* Visual acuity to read screens, documents, and reports
* Occasional reaching, bending, or stooping to access file drawers, cabinets, or office supplies
* Lifting and carrying items up to 20 pounds occasionally (e.g., office supplies, packages)
