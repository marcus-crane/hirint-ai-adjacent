Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are looking for a curious, fast-moving 3D Generalist to join our 3D/Visualization team. You will work across the full 3D pipeline: modeling, texturing, lighting, rendering, animation, and real-time, to create the visuals that bring Saronic's vessels, systems, and environments to life. You will be hands-on from day one, shipping work that appears in customer briefings, events, product films, and across the company.

This is an ideal role for an early-career designer who is hungry to grow, loves experimenting with new tools, and wants to be at the center of a fast-changing field. You will learn from experienced designers across Brand, Industrial, 3D/Visualization, Product, and Video, and you will have the freedom to bring in new techniques and help shape how our team works.

# **Responsibilities**

* **3D Modeling and Asset Creation**

  + Model hard-surface vessels, systems, props, and environments from engineering CAD, concept art, and reference, optimizing geometry for both offline and real-time use.
  + Build clean UVs, textures, and PBR materials that work anywhere the asset goes, from hero renders to real-time engines.
  + Build and maintain a library of reusable, well-organized assets that the whole team can pull from.
* **Lighting, Rendering, and Look Development**

  + Light and render photoreal and stylized scenes for stills, animations, and real-time experiences.
  + Develop look and mood that feels cinematic, accurate, and unmistakably Saronic.
  + Optimize scenes for quality and render time, and keep pace with new rendering technologies.
* **Animation and Real-Time**

  + Animate vessels, mechanisms, cameras, and simulated environments (water, weather, FX) to explain how our technology works.
  + Build interactive and real-time scenes in Unreal Engine, or similar, for demos, events, and internal tools.
  + Prototype quickly to test ideas, then refine the best ones into finished work.
* **New Tools and Workflows**

  + Stay current on emerging 3D, real-time, and AI-assisted tools, and bring in the ones that make the team faster or the work better.
  + Experiment with new techniques, test them on real projects, and share what you learn so the whole team levels up.
  + Help build repeatable pipelines, scene templates, and documentation so projects move smoothly from CAD to final output.
* **Collaboration and Delivery**

  + Work closely with Growth, Industrial Design, Engineering, Brand, Motion, and Video to turn ideas and CAD into compelling visuals.
  + Take direction and feedback well, iterate quickly, and deliver on short timelines, including support for events and customer demos.
  + Manage your own files, versions, and deadlines, and communicate progress and blockers early.
  + Handle sensitive and export-controlled information responsibly, following company guidelines on what can be shown and shared.

# **Qualifications**

* 1–3 years of professional experience in 3D (in-house, studio, freelance, or equivalent), or an exceptional portfolio from school or personal projects that shows the same level of skill.
* A strong portfolio showing end-to-end 3D work: modeling, texturing, lighting, rendering, and animation, with attention to craft and realism.
* Proficiency in Cinema 4D or Blender, plus a physically based renderer (Redshift, Octane, Cycles, or similar).
* Experience with texturing and material tools such as Substance 3D Painter/Designer, and familiarity with procedural texturing.
* Working knowledge of a real-time engine, such as Unreal Engine or Unity.
* Solid fundamentals in form, composition, lighting, color, and camera.
* A demonstrated habit of learning new tools quickly, with examples of picking up new software or techniques on your own.
* Strong communication skills and the ability to take feedback and iterate fast.
* Comfort with fast-moving, ambiguous environments and shifting priorities.
* Ability to work on-site and travel occasionally for events and demos.

# **Preferred Qualifications**

* Hands-on experience with AI-assisted and emerging 3D workflows, such as generative image and 3D tools, ComfyUI-style pipelines, and capture techniques like photogrammetry or Gaussian splatting.
* Experience with Houdini or other procedural and simulation workflows (water, weather, FX).
* Experience with Unreal Engine for cinematic, interactive, or virtual production work.
* Compositing and finishing skills in After Effects or Nuke.
* Experience working from CAD and engineering data (for example from SolidWorks or Rhino), including cleanup and optimization for visualization.
* Familiarity with rendering cached simulations (Alembic, VDB, USD) handed off from FX artists.
* Familiarity with OCIO/ACES color management, so color stays consistent between Cinema 4D, Unreal, and compositing.
* Experience with Plasticity or similar CAD-style modeling tools.
* Basic scripting or technical art skills (Python, Blueprints, or Geometry Nodes).
* Interest in or familiarity with defense, aerospace, robotics, or maritime technology.
