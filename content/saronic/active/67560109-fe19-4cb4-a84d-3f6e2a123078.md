Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

**Job Overview**

We are looking for a Senior Financial Analyst to join our FP&A team and help build the financial infrastructure needed to support Saronic as we scale.

This is not a traditional FP&A role. It is a strong fit for someone who sees a manual process, unclear ownership, or a recurring financial problem and immediately starts thinking about how it could work better. You will work across FP&A, Accounting, Finance tooling, and the broader business to improve how Finance plans, closes, structures data, and operates as the company grows.

We are looking for someone who is forward-thinking, comfortable operating in ambiguity, and willing to identify problems, challenge existing processes, and bring recommendations rather than wait for direction.

**Key Responsibilities**

**Finance Process Improvement and Scalability**

* Lead defined cross-functional initiatives to improve financial processes across Finance, Accounting, tooling, and operational teams
* Proactively identify process gaps, unclear ownership, and emerging challenges, and recommend scalable solutions
* Drive improvements across areas such as cost allocation, operational data flows, revenue and COGS processes, and other workflows where ownership or processes are unclear
* Partner with the Finance tooling owner to translate Finance and business needs into automation and tooling improvements
* Support larger Finance transformation initiatives, such as organizational changes, new business units or entities, system implementations, and changes to financial processes or reporting structures

**Financial Data Integrity and Infrastructure**

* Partner with Accounting and FP&A to improve the quality and consistency of financial data used for planning, reporting, and analysis
* Investigate recurring data issues, identify root causes, and implement improvements that reduce manual corrections and cleanup
* Support governance of financial data tagging and help ensure changes are implemented consistently across Finance processes and tools

**Planning, Forecasting, and Close**

* Drive key components of forecasting, annual planning, and long-range planning cycles, including timelines, communications, templates, and review coordination
* Own the FP&A month-end close cadence, coordinating deliverables, reporting, and dependencies across FP&A and with Accounting
* Improve consistency and efficiency across recurring planning, close, and reporting processes
* Identify opportunities to simplify how Finance operates as the business and organizational structure evolve

**What We’re Looking For**

* 5+ years of experience in FP&A, financial operations, business finance, finance transformation, finance consulting, or a related field
* Strong understanding of core financial concepts and FP&A processes
* Demonstrated ability to take an ambiguous or inefficient process, understand it end to end, and drive it toward a practical solution
* Ability to anticipate problems, identify opportunities for improvement, and bring recommendations rather than wait for direction
* Curiosity and willingness to challenge existing processes, ask why they exist, and recommend a better approach when appropriate
* Strong analytical, project management, and communication skills
* Comfort working across Accounting, FP&A, tooling, and operational teams
* Experience building or scaling Finance processes and infrastructure in a high-growth, rapidly changing, or transformation-oriented environment (strongly preferred)
* Experience with ERP, planning, financial reporting, or other Finance tools (a plus)

**Physical Demands**

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
* Occasional standing and walking within the office
* Manual dexterity to operate a computer keyboard, mouse, and other office equipment
* Visual acuity to read screens, documents, and reports
* Occasional reaching, bending, or stooping to access file drawers, cabinets, or office supplies
* Occasional lifting and carrying of items up to 20 pounds (e.g., office supplies, packages)
