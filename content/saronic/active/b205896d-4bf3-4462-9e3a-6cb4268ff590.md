Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking a Principal, Strategic Partnerships - Europe who will play a critical role in building and executing high-impact partnerships across the defense, technology, and maritime sectors. As part of the International Strategic Partnerships function and Corporate Development team, you will collaborate closely with Saronic’s executive leadership and International Growth teams to identify, cultivate, confirm, and lead strategic partnerships that support the growth of our products, our company, and our impact on the maritime domain in Europe. You will serve as a deal-maker and operator who can translate conversations into durable, multi-year partnerships that drive tangible business outcomes.

# **Responsibilities**

* **Run due diligence and market analyses:** Research and evaluate potential strategic partnership candidates, building the fact base to enable rigorous and holistic decision-making

* **Develop and execute European partnership strategies:** Identify and prioritize potential partnership opportunities across the defense and commercial maritime sectors, engaging with senior stakeholders from a broad range of organizations spanning established defense companies, emerging technology providers, key supplier partners, academic organizations, and Government entities

* **Build and maintain deep, trust-based relationships:** Establish and nurture strong working relationships with key decision-makers and change agents with established and prospective partner organizations

* **Balance strategic vision and hands-on execution in complex, fast-paced environments:** Flex seamlessly from identifying new partnership opportunities designed to enable business objectives to structuring agreements to ensuring seamless transitions from deal to delivery

* **Coordinate across the company:** Lead efforts to structure and establish enduring agreements that support European Growth objectives

* **Negotiate partnership agreements:** Lead negotiations on partnership terms, agreements, and contracts, ensuring that all parties' interests are met while advancing company objectives

* **Stay ahead of defense and maritime sector trends:** Monitor and anticipate developments in defense, maritime, robotics, and autonomy – including funding flows, technology advancements, and policy trends – to proactively identify opportunities

# **Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s degree in business, engineering, or a related field. MBA or advanced degree preferred

* 7+ years of experience in a strategic role in a high-velocity environment, preferably with exposure to defense, aerospace, maritime, or robotics industries; 4+ years of experience in strategy consulting or investment banking preferred

* Familiarity with defense technologies, autonomy/robotics, and maritime systems

* Experience working with European government stakeholders

* Demonstrated record of success in forming and maintaining high-value partnerships with public and private sector entities

* Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to present complex ideas in a clear and concise manner

* Skilled in negotiation and navigating complex internal and external stakeholder environments with the highest degree of discretion

* Eligibility for Government security clearance required

* Ability to travel as needed, including internationally (20-30%)
