Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

The Saronic Quality Team is seeking a Quality Engineer to support the implementation, maintenance and continuous improvement of Saronic's Quality Management System (QMS) across multiple facilities. This role will support core QMS processes, including document control, internal and external audits, corrective actions, management reviews and ISO 9001 certification activities. Responsibilities will also include QMS improvement initiatives, certification readiness at new facilities and cross-functional collaboration to ensure consistent and effective processes.

The ideal candidate will have experience with ISO 9001-based quality management systems in a manufacturing environment, along with strong communication, analytical and problem-solving skills. The position requires the ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment. This role will be based primarily in Austin, TX, with some travel to other Saronic facilities.

# **Responsibilities**

* Maintain and improve QMS documentation and electronic document control systems, including SharePoint, ensuring appropriate review, approval, revision control and accessibility.
* Plan, conduct and document internal QMS audits and coordinate external certification and surveillance audits, including preparation, objective evidence collection and closure of findings.
* Support implementation, standardization and continuous improvement of QMS processes across Saronic facilities, including gap assessments and ISO 9001 certification readiness.
* Monitor and support corrective action processes, including root cause analysis, timely resolution and verification of effectiveness.
* Support QMS management reviews and develop performance metrics to monitor process effectiveness, identify trends and track improvement actions.
* Collaborate with process owners and cross-functional teams to evaluate processes, identify gaps and implement improvements to engineering, manufacturing and business processes.
* Develop and maintain QMS training materials and support training activities to promote process awareness and consistent execution.
* Support compliance with ISO 9001, applicable customer requirements and company policies through process monitoring, audits and continuous improvement activities.
* Support integration of risk-based thinking into QMS processes, including identification, assessment and mitigation of quality-related risks.
* Support cross-functional quality initiatives and special projects as needed, including process improvements, problem-solving activities and other emerging quality priorities

# **Required Skills and Qualifications**

* **Education:** Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Quality Management, Manufacturing, Industrial Technology or a related technical discipline or equivalent relevant experience.
* **Experience:** Minimum of 3 years of experience in Quality Engineering, Quality Assurance or QMS-related roles within a manufacturing or technical environment.
* **QMS Knowledge:** Working knowledge of ISO 9001 requirements and experience with QMS processes, document control, internal auditing and corrective action activities.
* **Technical Skills:** Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications, SharePoint and electronic document management systems.
* **Analytical Skills:** Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities, including data analysis, root cause investigation and process improvement.
* **Communication Skills:** Strong written and verbal communication skills, including procedure development, reporting and cross-functional collaboration.
* **Project Management:** Ability to independently manage multiple priorities and projects while meeting established deadlines.
* **Work Environment:** Ability to work effectively in office and manufacturing environments, including conducting on-site audits and process assessments.
* **Travel:** Ability to travel up to 25% of the time.
* **Adaptability:** Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic environment with evolving priorities and business needs.

# **Preferred Qualifications:**

* Experience implementing, maintaining or improving an ISO 9001-certified QMS.
* Experience conducting internal ISO 9001 audits and supporting external certification or surveillance audits.
* ISO 9001 Internal Auditor or Lead Auditor certification, ASQ Certified Quality Engineer (CQE) or Certified Quality Auditor (CQA).
* Experience in aerospace, defense, maritime or other complex manufacturing environments.
* Experience supporting QMS implementation or certification readiness across multiple facilities.
* Familiarity with structured root cause analysis, corrective action methodologies and continuous improvement practices.
