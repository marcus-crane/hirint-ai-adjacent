Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are hiring a Corporate Counsel, Government Contracts to support Saronic's work with the U.S. Navy and the broader defense customer base. You will support the full contract lifecycle for our Naval autonomy programs: shaping capture strategy with the business, drafting and negotiating Other Transaction (OT) agreements, government contracts, subcontracts, software license agreements, and product integration agreements, while ensuring alignment of contractual activities to Saronic’s status as a nontraditional defense contractor.

You will sit in the Legal department in Arlington, VA, close to our customers, and work day-to-day with Growth, Programs, Contracts, Software and Engineering teams. You will also support the Associate General Counsel, Government Contracts on select corporate matters.

# **Responsibilities**

**Government Contracts and Other Transactions**

* Serve as legal advisor for Saronic’s Naval autonomy programs, advising internal stakeholders on legal and contractual matters from opportunity identification through award, performance and closeout.

* Draft and negotiate Other Transaction agreements, including consortium, prototype, and production variants, and advise on statutory authority, milestone and payment structures.

* Advise on nontraditional defense contractor status and consequences of exposure to traditional FAR-based contracting terms, including cost accounting standards, certified cost or pricing data, audit rights and flow downs.

* Support responses to solicitations, commercial solutions openings and white papers, including proposal assumptions, exceptions and terms and conditions.

* Maintain Saronic's government contracts infrastructure: template OT and subcontract terms, clause libraries, data rights playbook and post-award checklists.

**Subcontracts, Teaming and Product Integrations**

* Draft and negotiate subcontracts, teaming agreements, and bailment agreements with industry partners, primes and suppliers.

* Draft and negotiate product integration agreements with commercial parties in support of government and industry sponsored activities, addressing legal and compliance risk, warranty, insurance, and intellectual property considerations.

* Build and maintain flowdown matrices and templates designed to inform business leads as to which terms are mandatory, negotiable or avoidable for each type of agreement.

**Commercial Items, Software Licensing and Data Rights**

* Structure Saronic's offerings to preserve commercial item treatment and commercial terms wherever possible, and advise on commercial item determinations and their limits.

* Negotiate commercial software license agreements with the government and with partners.

* Advise on technical data and computer software rights (DFARS 252.227 series, and negotiated data rights under OTs), including assertion lists, restrictive markings, government purpose rights and specially negotiated licenses.

* Protect Saronic's IP and independently developed technology while meeting government needs for interoperability, sustainment and open architecture.

**Procurement Law, Compliance and Ethics**

* Interface with the Compliance team on issues relating to procurement integrity, organizational conflicts of interest, gifts and entertainment, lobbying and post-government employment restrictions, and the Procurement Integrity Act.

* Support cybersecurity and supply chain requirements (CMMC, NIST SP 800-171, Section 889 and related restrictions), export controls (ITAR and EAR), and facility and personnel clearance coordination with Security.

**Cross-Functional Partnership**

* Be a trusted, embedded partner to Growth, Programs, Contracts, and Partnerships teams and while supporting senior leadership on high-stakes customer commitments.

* Translate legal risk into clear business recommendations, giving answers and options rather than memos.

# **Qualifications**

* Juris Doctor from an accredited law school and active membership in good standing with at least one U.S. state bar.

* 8+ years of government contracts and transactional experience, in-house at a defense or technology company, at a leading government contracts practice, or a combination.

* Hands-on experience drafting and negotiating Other Transaction agreements, and a working understanding of OT authority, consortia and the path from prototype to follow-on production.

* Expert knowledge of procurement law and ethics for government contractors, including the FAR, DFARS, the Procurement Integrity Act, organizational conflicts of interest, and gifts and lobbying rules.

* Familiarity with the nontraditional defense contractor and commercial item regulatory spaces, and how each shapes the terms Saronic can accept.

* Deep experience with commercial item acquisition, software licensing and data rights, including DFARS technical data and computer software provisions and negotiated rights under OTs.

* Experience with subcontracts, teaming agreements, and hardware and software integrations supporting autonomous systems and programs.

* Sound commercial judgment and ability to give clear calls under time pressure with incomplete information.

* Excellent drafting, negotiation and communication skills, with comfort advising executives and working across functions.

**Preferred**

* Experience with Navy or other Department of Defense maritime, unmanned or autonomous systems programs.

* Exposure to export controls (ITAR and EAR), CMMC and classified contracting issues.

* Experience building templates, playbooks and training for a growing contracts organization.
