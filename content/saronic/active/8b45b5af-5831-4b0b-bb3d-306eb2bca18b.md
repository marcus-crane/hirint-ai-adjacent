Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

Saronic is looking for a Machine Shop Helper to support our machinists and fabrication team as we build the autonomous surface vessels defending America's interests at sea. In this role, you'll keep the shop running by prepping raw materials, staging tooling and fixtures, deburring and cleaning finished parts, operating basic equipment (band saws, drill presses, grinders), and keeping work areas organized and safe.

# **Responsibilities**

* Accurately reading tape measures, gauges, and other test equipment
* Stack finished product in accordance with factory specifications
* Assist with loading and unloading of machines
* Securing raw materials
* Hand deburring of fabricated metal components
* Operation of saws and raw material processing equipment
* Cleaning machinery
* Expediting completed parts
* Participating in crew meetings, reducing waste, actively participating in team problem solving processes, and maintaining quality standards
* Applicants must be willing to work alternate shifts, weekends, and daily overtime as required
* Ensure correct bundle/unit counts, and report final counts to supervisor
* Other duties as assigned by supervisor.

# **Qualifications**

* High School Diploma or GED
* Ability to stand continuously and lift up to 50 pounds
* Prior industry experience preferred, but not required
* Ability to read a tape measure with speed and accuracy, and apply concepts of basic math

# **Physical Demands**

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
* Occasional standing and walking within the office
* Manual dexterity to operate a computer keyboard, mouse, and other office equipment
* Visual acuity to read screens, documents, and reports
* Occasional reaching, bending, or stooping to access file drawers, cabinets, or office supplies
* Lifting and carrying items up to 20 pounds occasionally (e.g., office supplies, packages)
