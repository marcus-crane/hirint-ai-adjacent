Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking a Growth Business Systems & Tooling Manager to join the Growth Strategy & Strategic Operations team and own the requirements, processes, and roadmap for the systems and tooling that enable our entire Growth function across U.S., international, and commercial. This role is the business owner of our CRM and how it functions, the dashboards leadership relies on, and oversees the tooling behind fulfillment, demand planning, forecasting, and reporting. It also sets the standard for how Growth's systems are scoped, governed, and scaled as the organization grows.

You will operate as a trusted partner to Growth leadership and as the connective tissue between Growth and our Enterprise Technology team, who serve as the builders of these systems, as well as Proposals, Contracts, Programs, Marketing, Product, Government Relations, and Finance. You will turn stakeholder needs into well-scoped requirements, designing systems, processes, and tools growth will run on.

We're looking for someone who is highly analytical, systems-minded, and energized by ambiguous problems that cut across multiple parts of the business. You should be comfortable understanding how data and systems fit together, mapping processes end to end, defining what needs to be built so that it outlasts you, and building consensus across teams without relying on formal authority.

# **Responsibilities**

* **CRM Ownership:** Own the CRM from a business perspective, including its requirements, functionality, and roadmap, in partnership with Enterprise Technology, who configure and build. Engage directly with Growth stakeholders across U.S., international, and commercial to gather requirements on what they need, then define and prioritize the features, workflows, and automation that meet them and guide them through delivery.

* **Dashboards, Analytics & Reporting:** Define and govern the dashboards that meet Growth's reporting needs, giving leadership a decision-ready view of pipeline, forecast, demand, and performance. Partner with stakeholders to define metrics and specify reporting requirements, and work with Enterprise Technology to keep data sources, logic, and definitions consistent across tools.

* **Fulfillment, Demand Planning & Forecasting Systems:** Own the business requirements for the tooling and systems that support fulfillment, demand planning, and forecasting, and scope additional tools as Growth's needs evolve. Define the needs for forecasting, resource/capacity assessment, and the Commercial Price List (CPL) so they stay connected to the tools and processes that depend on them.

* **Enterprise Technology Partnership:** Engage with the Enterprise Technology team to represent Growth's needs in enterprise system and tools roadmaps, and ensure Growth's systems requirements are fully represented.

* **Systems Strategy & Process Architecture:** Define the roadmap for Growth's systems and tooling, diagnosing gaps and codifying how work moves from an exploratory sales opportunity to execution. Own intake and prioritization of system requests, documentation, training, and adoption.

* **Special Projects:** As the organization scales, take on the most ambiguous systems and tooling problems, including evaluating and recommending new tools. Structure the work, build consensus on the path forward, and drive alignment from framing through implementation alongside Enterprise Technology and business stakeholders.

# **Qualifications**

* 5+ years in Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, Business Systems, business analysis, or a comparable role owning requirements for CRM and go-to-market tooling.

* Bachelor's degree in business, engineering, economics, computer science, information systems, or a related field.

* Experience in defense, aerospace, or another regulated, government-adjacent industry.

* Working knowledge of CRM platforms and how they are configured, with experience translating business needs into clear requirements for administrators and developers.

* Experience defining dashboards and reports in BI platforms.

* Genuine excitement for systems: the curiosity and aptitude to understand how tools are built and to speak credibly with technical folks, and, where helpful, to prototype or mock up ideas (e.g., Claude) to clarify requirements.

* Demonstrated ownership of complex, cross-functional systems implementations from requirements through rollout and adoption, with measurable outcomes.

* Track record of influencing senior stakeholders without formal authority, across both technical and non-technical audiences.

* Comfort operating with incomplete information in a fast-moving environment, with the judgment to know when to gather more and when to drive to a decision.

* Highly organized, detail-oriented, and proactive in managing competing priorities across multiple stakeholders.
