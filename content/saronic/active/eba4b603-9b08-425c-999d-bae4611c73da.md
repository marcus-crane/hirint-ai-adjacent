Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

Saronic is seeking a Program Operations Manager to support the operational rhythm of our Programs organization and help scale autonomous surface vessel (ASV) programs from award through delivery. You will maintain a bird’s-eye view of all customer-funded activity, from contracts in execution to high-pWin opportunities in the sales pipeline, and make sure work moves cleanly from Growth to Proposals, Contracts/Legal, Programs, and ultimately Engineering, Production, and Mission Services. This role is ideal for a self-starter with 5-8 years of program or project operations experience in defense technology, consulting, or startup business operations who thrives in a dynamic, fast-paced environment, builds relationships across the company, and jumps in to fill gaps and put out fires. You will work closely with program managers, engineers, and external customer to ensure programs are delivered on time, within scope, and in compliance with government and internal standards.

# **Responsibilities**

**Program Portfolio Oversight:**

* Maintain a 1000-foot view of all customer-funded activity, including active programs in execution and high-pWin opportunities in the sales pipeline, and keep program leadership informed of status, risks, and resource needs.
* Serve as the operational backbone of the Programs organization, tracking timelines, deliverables, resource requirements, and key milestones across the portfolio.
* Proactively find projects and opportunities to fill your plate; jump in to close gaps and resolve issues before they impact customers or the mission.

**Pipeline Visibility & Handoffs:**

* Understand the sales pipeline, including both qualitative updates and quantitative values (e.g., pWin, contract value, timing), and translate it into Programs resourcing and execution planning.
* Build and run the Programs kickoffs for newly awarded contracts, aligning cross functional teams and the company on the execution path forward.
* Support proposal development and capture by coordinating cost, schedule, and capability inputs for program execution planning, and drive smooth transitions from award into execution.

**Program Execution:**

* Coordinate internal and external reviews, program kickoff meetings, technical interchange meetings (TIMs), and status updates.
* Oversee preparation of program execution documents (e.g., CDRLs, program schedules, Integrated Master Schedule inputs, and performance tracking metrics).
* Support program managers with contractual and technical execution while supporting a baselined schedule, scope, budget, and risk register.

**Cross-Functional Leadership:**

* Meet and build relationships with teams across the company, and develop a deep understanding of how Programs interacts with each team to advance Saronic’s growth and mission.
* Act as the liaison between program leadership and internal departments, including engineering, production, procurement, mission services, finance, and business development.
* Lead tiger teams to solve urgent issues and risks on active programs; have the ability to quickly identify unique technical problems, build work plans and COAs, brief to leadership, and execute with functional leads.
* Ensure alignment between technical deliverables and contractual requirements and regulations.

**Governance, Reporting & Process:**

* Develop, track, and report on program dashboards and metrics for program leadership, internal stakeholders, and customers.
* Establish and maintain standard process, SOPs, and tools for program documentation and reporting, and continuously improve how the Programs team operates.
* Financial literacy and understanding of accounting principals (GAAP); experience building financial reports and data visualizations.
* Support business operation development from zero to one. Systems like work breakdown structures, financial tracking & reporting mechanisms, and integrated master schedules are critical to supporting program delivery.

**Compliance & Contract Support:**

* Partner with the compliance and contracts teams to track contractual obligations, government standards, and flow-downs.
* Ensure program activities align with DoD acquisition processes and internal SOPs.

**Customer Interface & Engagement:**

* Prepare very high-quality PowerPoint materials for customer deliverables, briefings, and demos.
* Comfortable and skilled at briefing the C-Suite/SES/GO/FO level when needed.
* Coordinate customer visits, test events, and logistics for field exercises.
* Facilitate timely, professional communication with program sponsors, B2B partners, and government customers.

**Team & Culture:**

* Serve as a steward of the Programs team and Saronic culture: help teammates, row in the same direction, and keep us moving fast.
* Mentor and support program analysts as the Programs organization grows.

**Education:**

**Experience:**

* Familiarity with defense acquisition lifecycles, DoD contracting structures, and high-level customer engagement.
* Experience supporting program execution in a fast-paced, hardware/software development environment.
