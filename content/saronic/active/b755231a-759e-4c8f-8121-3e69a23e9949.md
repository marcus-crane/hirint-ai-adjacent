Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Role Overview**

A Quality Assurance (QA) Specialist at Saronic is responsible for managing, maintaining, and controlling quality documentation to ensure compliance with internal standards, customer requirements, and applicable regulatory standards. This role plays a critical part in maintaining document integrity, audit readiness, and quality control across the organization. They will employ technical expertise and analytical skill in supporting Saronic’s reputation and operational efficiency.

**Key Responsibilities**

· Maintain and control quality management system (QMS) documentation

· Conduct tests, inspections, and audits to identify defects or process inefficiencies.

· Analyze results of inspections and tests and propose corrective actions to improve production processes and product quality.

· Ensure compliance with applicable standards (e.g., ISO 9001)

· Administer document control systems (e.g., SharePoint, etc)

· Coordinate cross-functional reviews and approvals of procedures, work instructions, and policies

· Track and manage document revisions

· Monitor document retention schedules and ensure proper archiva

· Create training materials and operating manuals to support quality initiatives.

· Work both independently and in groups to achieve quality goals.

**Required Skills and Qualifications**

· Education: High school diploma or GED required; a degree in a relevant field is preferred.

· Certifications: QA certifications are a plus, especially IPC, ASQ, and ISO.

· Experience: Previous experience in quality assurance or a related field.

· Technical Skills: Proficiency with document management systems,  Strong understanding of document control principles, Advanced Microsoft Office skills (Word, Excel, SharePoint)

· Analytical Skills: Strong data collection, analysis, and problem-solving abilities.

· Communication Skills: Effective verbal and written communication for reporting and collaboration.

· Work Environment: Ability to work in a manufacturing and office environment; meet minimal physical work requirements.
