Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

Saronic is seeking a Director of Growth Innovation to lead growth initiatives across the Department of War’s innovation ecosystem. This individual will be responsible for identifying, pursuing, and capturing opportunities that expand Saronic’s presence with the innovation ecosystem in organizations such as Defense Innovation Unit (DIU), the Office of the Under Secretary of War for Research and Engineering (R&E), DARPA, Service-level innovation organizations and accelerators, and for transitioning those efforts into scaled production and programs of record.

The ideal candidate possesses a deep understanding of the defense innovation landscape, rapid acquisition pathways, and autonomous maritime technologies. They will maintain professional relationships across OSW, innovation organizations, the Services, and industry to develop strategic partnerships, shape emerging problem statements and solicitations, and drive opportunities from prototyping and experimentation through production-scale award.

# **Responsibilities**

* Develop and execute the business development strategy for OSW-level innovation offices and Service innovation organizations.

* Build and maintain trusted relationships with key stakeholders across DIU, R&E, DARPA, the Office of Strategic Capital, CDAO, and Service innovation units such as NavalX, AFWERX, SOFWERX, and the Army Applications Laboratory.

* Identify, qualify, and mature new opportunities across autonomous maritime systems, unmanned surface vessels (USVs), maritime ISR, logistics, and force protection, with a focus on prototyping and scaling initiatives.

* Develop and manage a qualified pipeline from Commercial Solutions Openings (CSOs), Broad Agency Announcements (BAAs), and prototype OTAs through follow-on production and transition.

* Shape emerging problem statements, solicitations, and technology priorities through regular engagement with program managers, technical leads, and portfolio directors.

* Lead capture planning, competitive assessments, win strategies, and solution pitches for priority innovation pursuits.

* Partner with Engineering, Product Management, Government Affairs, and Program Management to align innovation customer needs with Saronic’s product roadmap.

* Represent Saronic at defense innovation forums, industry days, experimentation events, demonstrations, and technology showcases.

* Build transition pathways and strategic partnerships by connecting innovation sponsors with Service program offices, operational commands, prime contractors, and end users to move prototypes into programs of record.

* Monitor innovation funding lines, OSW and R&E priorities, congressional budget actions, and emerging capability gaps to inform business strategy.

* Support proposal development, pricing strategy, and OTA and contract negotiations for strategic opportunities.

* Maintain accurate opportunity forecasting, pipeline reporting, and CRM data.

# **Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s degree in Business, Engineering, Political Science, International Relations, or a related field.

* 8+ years of business development, capture, government, or program management experience supporting the Department of War, with significant exposure to defense innovation organizations.

* Demonstrated success winning prototype awards and transitioning them into follow-on production or programs of record.

* Working knowledge of the missions, priorities, and engagement processes of DIU, R&E, DARPA, Service innovation organizations, and the broader defense innovation ecosystem.

* Strong understanding of rapid acquisition pathways, including CSOs, prototype and follow-on production OTAs, BAAs, SBIR/STTR programs, and FAR-based contracting, and the compliance requirements that apply to each.

* Knowledge of autonomous systems, unmanned maritime platforms, defense technology, or related capabilities.

* Excellent executive communication, presentation, negotiation, and relationship management skills.

* Ability to thrive in a fast-paced startup environment with minimal oversight.

* Willingness to travel approximately 40–60%.

# **Preferred Qualifications**

* Direct experience executing defense innovation efforts, whether in government (e.g., program manager, portfolio lead, or innovation officer within the Department of War) or industry.

* Familiarity with the organizational landscape across DIU portfolios, DARPA technical offices, OSW leadership, the Office of Strategic Capital, CDAO, NavalX, AFWERX, SOFWERX, and other defense innovation accelerators, and a demonstrated ability to build professional relationships through appropriate channels

* Experience with autonomous systems, unmanned surface vessels, maritime operations, ISR, C5ISR, or AI-enabled defense technologies.

* Experience leading prototype projects, experimentation campaigns, or technology transition efforts from demonstration to fielding.
