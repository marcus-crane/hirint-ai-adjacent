Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

**Job Overview**  
As a Mission Operations Engineer, you will be hands-on with the **i**ntegration**,** validation, and iterative improvement of our unmanned vessel for deployment. You will be a member of the Platform Acceptance - Effectors element, the internal-facing validation arm of new assets that are getting commissioned into the Saronic fleet. Your work will directly support both project-based deliverables (e.g., capability rollouts and payload integrations) and ongoing programs (e.g., V1 software release). You will help ensure our systems meet rigorous technical and operational standards through mission integration, detailed testing, data analysis, and cross-functional coordination. You are able to meet deadlines and achieve desired outcomes as both an individual contributor and as a part of your team.

## **Responsibilities:**

## **How you support the mission**

* Develop deep understanding of vehicle HW and SW architecture, and how the vehicle operates as an integrated system.
* Lead the integration of mission systems onto the vessel, including sensors, effectors, and payloads, from initial fit checks through functional validation and operational acceptance.
* Lead planning and execution of structured field tests for vessel performance pertaining to autonomous navigation, perception, communications, and sensor integration, among other vessel functionalities.
* Diagnose and perform real-time troubleshooting of autonomy, networking, and onboard hardware or software during test events, including faults at the interface between the vessel and integrated mission equipment.
* Implement solutions for testing, safety of vessel operations, and system robustness to maximize efficiency.
* Support safe handling, integration, and employment of effectors and hazardous payloads in accordance with applicable range, explosive, and DoD safety requirements.
* Own data logging and telemetry monitoring systems; ensure data integrity for post-test analysis.
* Contribute to root cause analysis (RCCA) and issue triage workflows after field anomalies, with reporting to relevant review boards to eliminate repeat issues.
* Maintain autonomous vessels and test environments, including vessel and support craft operations, through the use of telemetry tools and onboard diagnostics to enhance reliability of all equipment and products.
* Ensure testing and integration align with DoD performance, compliance, and safety standards when applicable.

## **How you support the team**

* Lead planning and execution of test cases to validate software updates, autonomy behaviors, and integrated system functionality, including end-to-end mission thread performance from sensor detection through autonomy decision-making to effector or payload employment.
* Interface with autonomy, software, hardware, mechanical, electrical, and systems teams, as well as payload vendors and customers, to translate product requirements into field-validated test objectives.
* Develop and maintain integration documentation, checklists, and configuration baselines for mission equipment.
* Foster a collaborative and positive team environment, promoting open communication, knowledge sharing, and continuous improvement.
* Develop test procedures, checklists, and safety protocols to support repeatable and safe vessel operation and overall mission execution while eliminating process waste.
* Draft and/or influence training materials, including operator guides, integration and handling procedures, and lessons learned from field events, so that operators, test teams, and customers can safely and consistently operate and integrate the vessel and its mission systems.
* Create post-mission reports with detailed logs, data plots, issue summaries, and engineering feedback.

## **Basic Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field
* 1+ years of hands-on engineering experience (design teams and previous internships qualify)

## **Preferred Qualifications**

* Strong problem-solving and troubleshooting skills (HW/SW) and ability to work effectively in challenging field conditions such as off-shore in open water
* Experience and knowledge of best practices in planning and execution of complex test operations involving autonomous systems
* Experience in flight test or comparable test environments for unmanned or autonomous systems, including familiarity with applicable regulatory and airspace requirements (e.g., FAA regulations for unmanned aircraft) where relevant
* Knowledge of networking protocols and communication systems used in autonomous vessels
* Systems engineering experience, ability to understand multi-disciplinary integrated systems
* Experience with sensor integration such as EO/IR, radar, IMU, sonar, and data processing, along with familiarity with effectors and payloads, including kinetic systems
* Experience with integration and testing of multi-disciplinary integrated systems, including bring-up, calibration, and functional verification of mission equipment
* Understanding of mechanical and electrical interfaces, including mounting and structural considerations, power distribution, wiring and harnessing, connectors, grounding, and EMI/EMC considerations, with the ability to read and interpret mechanical drawings, electrical schematics, and interface control documents
* Familiarity with weapons or payload safety practices, including safe/arm procedures and range safety
* Experience developing or contributing to training materials, technical documentation, or operator procedures for complex systems
* Experience with robotics, AI, or machine learning applications
* Familiarity with software development in Rust, Python, or other languages and CLI environment
* Familiarity with maritime operations
* Understanding of DoD requirements and standards for autonomous systems
* Experience working with embedded systems and real-time computing
* Job requires up to 50% travel.
