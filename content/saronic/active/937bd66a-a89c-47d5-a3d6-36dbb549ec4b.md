Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

Saronic Technologies is seeking a Deputy Program Manager (DPM) to support the Program Manager (PM) in leading the execution, integration, fleet readiness and fleet deployment of Saronic’s Autonomous Surface Vessel (ASV) programs through Contractor Owned, Contractor Operated (COCO) and Government Owned, Contractor Operated (GOCO) relationships.

Working alongside the PM, the DPM serves as a bridge between Saronic’s internal stakeholders and its external customers - ensuring what we build and operate aligns with contract requirements. This role combines systems-level understanding with programmatic and fiscal discipline, driving technical performance, risk management, and integration readiness across customer programs.

This role demands direct, face-to-face engagement with customers to elicit and clarify requirements, capture operational context, and translate those needs into actionable plans codified through contract actions. The DPM partners closely with Fleet Integration & Services Specialists, who execute field-level integration and operational support. Together, they ensure that Saronic systems are safe, reliable, and mission-ready. The DPM also serves as the PM’s principal alternate, leading delegated workstreams and acting on the PM’s behalf when required.

# **Responsibilities**

**Program Execution Leadership**

* Support the PM in end-to-end execution of assigned ASV programs, leading day-to-day execution of delegated workstreams.
* Translate customer and program requirements into actionable engineering and operational deliverables.
* Maintain technical baselines across multiple builds or variants, ensuring traceability from design to delivery.
* Identify and promptly escalate risks and issues, and drive mitigation and timely resolution with cross-functional teams.
* Contribute to Engineering Change Boards (ECB) and other technical working groups at Saronic.
* Own the program team’s day-to-day deliverables, organizing tasks and following through on priorities to meet schedule commitments with minimal rework.
* Make timely decisions on routine program matters using established guidelines and documented procedures, escalating complex issues to the PM.

**Integration & Field Readiness**

* Help plan and coordinate integration, testing, and on-water operations in coordination with Product, Engineering, Mission Operations and Production.
* Work closely with the Fleet Integration & Services Specialists to ensure consistent, safe, and well-documented field execution.
* Ensure technical configurations are validated and approved before production and deployment.
* Capture and communicate lessons learned from test events and field operations into future iterations.

**Cross Functional Coordination**

* Partner with Mission Operations and Field Deployed Engineering to deliver mission success to customers deploying Saronic assets.
* Partner with Product teams to align technical execution with product roadmaps and capability development.
* Partner with Growth to expand Saronic’s workload and backlog across multiple domains.
* Coordinate with Engineering to ensure scope, design intent, and technical priorities are aligned with program goals.
* Interface with Production and Quality to ensure build configurations and acceptance criteria meet customer expectations.
* Support cross-company coordination and information dissemination with inputs for customer meetings, reporting, and deliverables.

**Risk & Configuration Management**

* Maintain visibility into technical, operational, and programmatic risks across assigned programs.
* Coordinate risk review meetings and maintain mitigation plans in collaboration with Program, Product, and Engineering leadership.
* Enforce disciplined configuration management for vessels, payloads, and software versions.
* Oversee technical documentation, ensuring completeness, traceability, and readiness for audits or delivery.

**Customer & Stakeholder Engagement**

* Support the PM across all Program Management functions for assigned programs, including (but not limited to) program kickoffs, program reviews, financial tracking, ensuring execution excellence, and seeking opportunities for on-contract growth.
* Serve, alongside the PM, as a Programs point of contact for customer and partner engagements, bridging operational needs with product capability.
* Participate in design reviews, field demos, and technical exchanges with customer stakeholders.
* Deliver concise updates and after-action reports to leadership and customer audiences.
* Translate real-world customer feedback into actionable improvements for Product and Engineering.
