Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

The Supply Planning Manager is responsible for leading the development, execution, and optimization of supply plans that balance demand requirements with manufacturing, procurement, and inventory strategies. This role manages the supply planning team, drives alignment across operations, and ensures material and capacity availability to support production schedules and customer requirements. The Manager of Supply Planning partners closely with demand planning, procurement, production, logistics, and commercial teams to deliver reliable supply performance while optimizing cost, inventory, and service.

# **Responsibilities**

* Lead, coach, and develop a team of supply planners responsible for production and material planning.
* Own the supply planning process, ensuring accurate and achievable supply plans are developed to support demand forecasts and production targets.
* Collaborate with procurement, production, and logistics teams to balance material availability, capacity, and inventory levels.
* Drive cross-functional Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP) and supply reviews, ensuring alignment between demand, supply, and business objectives.
* Monitor performance against plan (service levels, inventory targets, production adherence) and drive corrective actions where needed.
* Identify and mitigate supply chain risks, including supplier constraints, lead time issues, and production bottlenecks.
* Develop and implement tools, systems, and metrics to improve supply planning accuracy, visibility, and efficiency.
* Partner with procurement on supplier collaboration for critical materials and capacity planning.
* Drive continuous improvement initiatives in planning processes, systems, and analytics.
* Provide clear communication and reporting to leadership on supply chain performance, risks, and opportunities.

# **Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Business, Operations, or related field required; Master’s degree or APICS/CPIM certification preferred.
* 7+ years of experience in supply chain, planning, or operations, with at least 2–3 years in a leadership role.
* Strong knowledge of supply planning, production scheduling, inventory management, and ERP/MRP systems.
* Experience leading S&OP/IBP processes in a manufacturing environment.
* Analytical mindset with strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail.
* Proven ability to lead, motivate, and develop high-performing teams.
* Excellent communication and cross-functional collaboration skills.
* Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, dynamic environment balancing both strategic and tactical priorities.
