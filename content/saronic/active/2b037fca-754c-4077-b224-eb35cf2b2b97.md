Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

**Job Overview**

We are seeking a Manufacturing Engineer Intern to work alongside our manufacturing engineering team on impactful, project-based assignments that improve how we build and deliver our autonomous service vessels. You will be involved in real-world manufacturing challenges at the intersection of robotics, defense, and innovation.

**Responsibilities**

* Assist in hands-on projects to improve manufacturing efficiency and processes
* Conduct time studies and analyze production workflows
* Support floor organization and lean manufacturing initiatives
* Design and implement light tooling or fixtures to assist in assembly or fabrication
* Analyze and improve manufacturing data and documentation systems
* Work cross-functionally with engineering, operations, and fabrication teams

**Qualifications**

* Pursuing a degree in Mechanical, Manufacturing, Electrical, Aerospace Engineering, or a related field
* Strong interest in hands-on manufacturing and process improvement
* Previous manufacturing experience is a plus, but not required
* Eagerness to learn, collaborate, and take initiative in a fast-paced environment
* Strong analytical, communication, and problem-solving skills
* This role requires full-time in office presence (40 hours/week) based at our headquarters in Austin, TX, from May through August 2027

**Physical Demands**

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
* Occasional standing and walking within the office
* Manual dexterity to operate a computer keyboard, mouse, and other office equipment
* Visual acuity to read screens, documents, and reports
* Occasional reaching, bending, or stooping to access file drawers, cabinets, or office supplies
* Lifting and carrying items up to 20 pounds occasionally (e.g., office supplies, packages)
