Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking a Material Planning and Indirect Inventory Manager to own the systems and processes that keep the right materials in the right place at the right time. You will join the Integrated Business Tools team within Enterprise Technology, which builds the systems Saronic uses to run its business. Working closely with Supply Chain, Production, Finance, and our internal NetSuite team, you will turn material requirements into reliable, scalable tooling.

In this role, you will own Material Requirements Planning (MRP) end to end and lead the buy vs. build analysis for our next-generation MRP solution. You will also build a new indirect material management platform covering IT hardware, facilities, maintenance, and other indirect use cases, and stand up supplier integrations for receiving, including ASN and EDI. You will lead a team of 1–3 people and play a significant role in scaling the operations behind the future of maritime autonomy.

# **Responsibilities**

* Own MRP end to end, including planning parameters, BOM and lead-time data quality, planning runs, exception management, and alignment with production and procurement schedules.
* Lead the buy vs. build analysis for next-generation MRP: gather requirements, evaluate commercial and custom options, model total cost of ownership, and present a recommendation to executive leadership.
* Drive the selected next-generation MRP path through design, implementation, data migration, and adoption.
* Design and build an indirect material management platform for IT hardware, facilities, maintenance (MRO), and other indirect use cases, covering requests, stocking, issuance, asset tracking, replenishment, and reporting.
* Build supplier integrations for receiving, including Advance Ship Notices (ASN) and EDI transactions (such as 850, 855, 856, and 810), in close partnership with the internal NetSuite team.
* Define and enforce inventory controls, including cycle counts, reorder points, min/max levels, and reconciliation with Finance.
* Establish KPIs and dashboards for material availability, inventory accuracy, shortages, excess and obsolete stock, and supplier on-time delivery.
* Collaborate with Supply Chain, Production, Finance, IT, and Facilities to translate requirements into technical specifications and deliverables.
* Manage, hire, and mentor a team of 1–3 analysts or engineers, setting priorities and growing their skills.
* Identify risks early, make decisive calls in ambiguous situations, and unblock multiple workstreams.
* Create design documentation, estimates, and timelines, and report progress to executive leadership and stakeholders.
* Stay current with emerging MRP, inventory, and supply chain technologies to continuously improve our processes and tools.
* Participate in Agile development methodologies, including sprint planning, daily stand-ups, and retrospectives.

# **Qualifications**

* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related technical field; a secondary degree or substantial experience in Supply Chain Management, Operations, or Industrial Engineering is highly desired
* 7+ years of experience in material planning, inventory management, or supply chain systems, including 2+ years leading people or cross-functional programs
* Deep hands-on experience owning MRP in a manufacturing environment, including BOMs, routings, lead times, safety stock, and planning exceptions
* Proven experience running buy vs. build or software selection processes, including requirements gathering, vendor evaluation, and total cost of ownership analysis
* Experience implementing or administering MRP, ERP, and inventory systems (Palantir Foundry–based MRP preferred; NetSuite a plus)
* Experience with supplier integrations for receiving, including ASN and EDI standards (X12 or EDIFACT), and working with EDI providers or integration platforms
* Experience managing indirect materials, such as IT hardware, MRO, or facilities inventory, including asset tracking and replenishment
* Strong data skills, including SQL or Python and building reports and dashboards
* Solid grounding in inventory controls, cycle counting, and reconciliation with Finance
* Sound judgment balancing operational excellence with practical business constraints
* Clear written communication and a track record of driving adoption across teams
* Experience in defense, aerospace, or hardware manufacturing a plus
* APICS/ASCM certification (CPIM or CSCP) a plus
