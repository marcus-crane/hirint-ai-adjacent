Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# Job Summary

You will be one of the first operators working under Saronic's Medium Unmanned Surface Vessel (MUSV) umbrella, putting a growing family of autonomous surface vessels through their paces, platforms that carry ISR, electronic warfare, and other mission payloads across extended blue-water and contested littoral waters without a crew on board. That family spans platforms built for long-endurance reconnaissance and persistent maritime domain awareness, as well as low-signature, shore-accessible craft that move critical payloads across thousands of nautical miles of contested seas. As a Mission Operations Operator, you’ll translate operational requirements into executable mission plans, lead field operations, and train military personnel to own these platforms long-term. This role sits at the intersection of field operations and product development; your feedback from the water directly shapes how the fleet evolves. The ideal candidate brings deep maritime operational experience, hands-on comfort with autonomous systems, and the ability to communicate clearly in both the field and the briefing room.

# Responsibilities

## Mission Planning & Execution

• Plan and execute end-to-end MUSV missions across open-ocean, littoral, and ship-to-shore profiles in GPS-denied and communications-degraded environments.

• Serve as primary operator during field operations, providing real-time oversight and go/no-go decision authority.

• Operate Saronic’s Echelon™ mission planning interface and Advanced Mission Planner (AMP™) to build and execute complex autonomous behaviors: loiter and avoid, counter-boarding, autodocking, and conditional mission logic.

• Develop and maintain SOPs, mission playbooks, and contingency procedures covering remote scuttle protocols, emergency response, and port coordination.

• Train and qualify operators on the MUSV mission planner for global remote control and direct line-of-sight operations, including pre- and post-operation checks.

• Coordinate waterspace deconfliction, safety boat integration, port authority notifications, and range safety compliance for all autonomous vessel operations.

## Training & Operator Development

• Develop and deliver MUSV training courses across the full curriculum: MUSV Fundamentals, Operations, Mission Manager (Digital Management), Advanced Operator, and Mission Integration/Readiness Exercises.

• Train and qualify military personnel on MUSV-specific operations, including autonomous navigation in congested waterways, station-keeping, payload operations, and GPS-denied operations.

• Build and run train-the-trainer programs that establish long-term organic military capability with the platform.

## Product Development & Stakeholder Engagement

• Serve as the liaison between Mission Operations and Saronic’s Product and Engineering teams, ensuring field realities directly inform design decisions.

• Capture and synthesize operator and warfighter feedback from testing and deployments to refine requirements, roadmaps, and system usability.

• Act as the primary operational interface with government customers including DIU, the service warfighting labs, NIWC-PAC, and DoD program offices.

• Build reporting and visualization tools to communicate mission status, system performance, risks, and forward plans to internal and external stakeholders.

• Represent Mission Operations at PDRs, IDRs, and CDRs as a subject-matter expert.

• Plan and conduct site surveys to assess port infrastructure, environmental conditions, and logistics constraints ahead of field operations.

# Qualifications

• U.S. Coast Guard Merchant Mariner Credential (500-Ton Master, Oceans) required.

• Bachelor's degree in engineering, maritime studies, or a related field, or equivalent operational experience.

• 5+ years of operational experience in the U.S. Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Coast Guard, Merchant Marine, or Special Operations, with expertise in littoral and blue-water operations, maritime domain awareness, or ISR/EW mission support.

• Experience operating unmanned surface vessels (USVs), autonomous systems, or remotely operated maritime platforms is a plus.

• Working knowledge of Stand-in Forces (SiF), Expeditionary Advanced Base Operations (EABO), and distributed maritime operations concepts.

• Proficiency in mission planning software and maritime navigation systems; experience in GPS-denied or communications-degraded environments is highly desirable.

• Demonstrated ability to develop and deliver technical training to military personnel.

• Strong analytical and problem-solving skills; able to make quick, sound decisions under pressure in dynamic field environments.

• Excellent written and verbal communication skills with a proven ability to author SOPs, CONOPs, and mission briefings for both technical and non-technical audiences.

• Ability to possess an active clearance or have the ability to obtain one.

• Up to 75% travel required for field trials, demonstrations, and customer engagements.

# Physical Demands

• Must be able to swim and tread water; ability to certify as fit for duty is required.

• Regularly lift, push, and carry up to 50 lbs.

• Operate in daylight, low-light, and night conditions using marine navigation systems and visual cues.

• Perform physically demanding work (stooping, bending, reaching, squatting, kneeling, crouching, twisting, and crawling) for up to 12 hours a day.

• Climb and maintain balance on ladders, platforms, and scaffolding.

• Stand and walk for up to 10 hours a day over varied and uneven terrain.

• Work in marine environments including sun, wind, rain, cold, and saltwater spray for extended periods.

• Work in hot and humid conditions with adherence to hydration and heat safety practices.

• Detect alarms, verbal instructions, and hazards in high-noise environments.

• Wear and maintain PPE including safety glasses, safety shoes, and hard hat in compliance with site safety requirements.
