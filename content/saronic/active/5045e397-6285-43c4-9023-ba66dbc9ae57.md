Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

Saronic has recently moved into formal DOT operation and is building out its driver qualification program. We are looking for an experienced commercial driver to take on a single, well-defined job: road test certifying our existing Regulated-class driver population so that every Driver Qualification File is complete and audit-ready. This is a dedicated, hands-on role. You will not be managing people or building programs — you will be in the vehicle, evaluating drivers, and signing the certificates that go into their files. The work is time-bound and high-impact: your signature is what makes each driver's qualification file defensible in an FMCSA audit.

# **Responsibilities**

* Keep accurate, legible, audit-ready records and hand off completed documentation promptly.
* Travel to Saronic sites with advance notice to test drivers on location. Current and anticipated locations include Virginia, Gulfport, and California.
* Administer road tests in accordance with 49 CFR 391.31 for Saronic's Regulated driver classification: vehicles rated 10,001 to 26,000 lbs GVWR, and any truck and trailer combination operated within that range.
* Evaluate each driver on the full set of required skills for that equipment: pre-trip inspection; coupling and uncoupling of combination units where the driver may operate one; placing the vehicle in operation; use of vehicle controls and emergency equipment; operating in traffic and passing; turning; braking and slowing by means other than the brakes; and backing and parking.
* Complete and sign the Certificate of Road Test for each driver, issue the driver their copy, and return the original to the Fleet Department for filing in the Driver Qualification File.
* Test each driver against the heaviest vehicle and trailer configuration their role may actually operate within the 10,001 to 26,000 lbs range.
* Where an employee who holds a Class A or B CDL fills a Regulated role, road test them for that 10,001 to 26,000 lbs scope of use only. CDL-level training and license currency remain the responsibility of the license holder and are outside the scope of this role. This role does not conduct CDL licensing road tests or CDL skills testing.
* Coordinate scheduling with the Fleet Department and HR to work through the existing backlog efficiently and minimize downtime in operational roles that require driving.
* Identify drivers who do not pass or who need remediation, communicate findings clearly and professionally, and conduct re-tests

# **Qualifications**

* Valid Class A Commercial Driver's License in good standing. A Class B CDL may be considered depending on the equipment mix you are qualified to evaluate.
* Minimum 2 years of experience holding and actively driving under a CDL-level license.
* Demonstrated experience trailering across multiple trailer sizes and configurations.
* Working knowledge of FMCSA and DOT regulations, with particular familiarity in Part 391 (driver qualification) and a general understanding of Part 396 (inspection and maintenance).
* Competent to evaluate and determine whether a driver has demonstrated the skills required under 49 CFR 391.31(c). This competency is the basis for your authority to sign the certificate, and it is the single most important qualification for this role.
* Motor Vehicle Record that meets the minimum requirements to drive under Saronic's insurance.
* Current DOT medical examiner's certificate.
* No disqualifying offenses under 49 CFR 391.15.
* Ability to travel domestically with advance notice, including occasional overnight travel.
* Ability to work independently with minimal day-to-day supervision and to manage your own testing schedule against a target.
* Prior experience administering road tests, CDL skills testing, or serving as a third-party examiner.
* Driver trainer, driver instructor, or safety department experience.
* Familiarity with Driver Qualification File composition and retention under 49 CFR 391.51.
* Experience with Samsara or a comparable telematics and ELD platform.
* Experience at a carrier that has been through an FMCSA new entrant safety audit or compliance review.
* Experience evaluating drivers who are not career commercial drivers — much of our driver population operates vehicles as part of a broader operational role
