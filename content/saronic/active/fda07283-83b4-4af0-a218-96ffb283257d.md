Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking a Staff DevOps Engineer responsible for designing, developing, and

maintaining the CI/CD pipelines, infrastructure, and observability for our internal

software platforms. You will join the Integrated Business Tools team within Enterprise

Technology, which builds the systems Saronic uses to run its business. Leveraging your

expertise, you will collaborate with cross-functional teams to implement innovative

solutions that drive our internal software platform.

At the Staff level, you will own the DevOps platform end to end: setting its long-term

direction, managing stakeholder requirements, and mentoring engineers on DevOps best

practices. You will work on complex challenges at the intersection of technology and

defense, and play a significant role in the future of maritime operations.

# **Responsibilities**

* Own the DevOps platform end to end, including its long-term direction, reliability, and alignment with multi-year Saronic goals.
* Design, build, and maintain high-quality CI/CD pipelines that automate building, testing, security scanning, and deployment.
* Design and implement scalable infrastructure as code that meets the needs of various internal stakeholders.
* Build and operate logging, telemetry, and monitoring systems, including SLOs, alerting, dashboards, and incident response.
* Collaborate with technical staff, software engineers, and security engineers to translate requirements into technical specifications and deliverables.
* Create and maintain integrations with third-party APIs and services for interoperability with internal and external systems.
* Optimize performance, security, and reliability through thorough testing, code reviews, performance tuning, and observability.
* Identify technical risks early, make decisive calls in ambiguous situations, and unblock multiple workstreams.
* Create technical design documentation, estimates, and timelines, and report progress to executive leadership and stakeholders
* Mentor engineers on DevOps best practices and provide technical guidance across teams.
* Stay current with emerging technologies, best practices, and industry trends to continuously improve our development processes and tools.
* Participate in Agile development methodologies, including sprint planning, daily stand-ups, and retrospectives, to ensure timely delivery of features and enhancements.

# **Qualifications**

* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field, or equivalent practical experience.
* 8+ years of experience in DevOps, site reliability, platform, or infrastructure engineering, including technical leadership at the senior or staff level.
* Deep experience designing and running CI/CD pipelines at scale for multiple teams (GitHub Actions a plus).
* Experience with package and artifact management, including CI/CD pipelines that build, version, and publish packages (such as NPM) and hosting private package repositories (Artifactory a plus).
* Strong infrastructure-as-code skills, including module design and state management (Terraform a plus).
* Experience creating and maintaining separate development, test, and production environments, with consistent configuration and controlled promotion between them.
* Experience with containerization and container orchestration, including building, securing, and deploying container images.
* Hands-on hybrid cloud and on-premise experience across compute, networking, security, storage, and managed services in multi-account environments (AWS and Palantir Foundry a plus).
* Proven experience building logging, telemetry, and monitoring systems, with SLOs, alerting, incident response, and on-call duties.
* Proficiency in at least one scripting or programming language, such as Python, TypeScript, or Bash.
* Solid grounding in security practices, including least privilege, secrets management, and software supply-chain security.
* Sound judgment balancing technical excellence with practical business constraints.
* Clear written communication and a track record of driving adoption across teams
* Experience leading cloud migrations a plus
