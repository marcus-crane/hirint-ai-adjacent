Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking an employment lawyer to help build Saronic’s legal foundations.

The successful candidate will have experience with employment law counseling and litigation in a government, law firm or corporate environment. They will own counseling matters, exercising sound judgment and taking responsibility for outcomes, and will help establish scalable processes as Saronic expands. The ability to transmit legal information and advice succinctly and cogently to a variety of stakeholders is a must.

This role is located in Austin, TX, with moderate travel required.

# **Responsibilities**

* Collaborate with senior employment counsel and cross-functional partners on a broad range of US and international employment matters, including advice-giving pertaining to recruiting and hiring, performance management, employee relations, leaves of absence, accommodations, and separations.

* Primary responsibility for daily counseling with People team stakeholders on employee relations issues. Oversight of People team investigations. Assist the People and Compliance teams with adjudication of potential employee conflicts of interest and other compliance matters.

* Support the workers’ compensation function and own workers’ compensation settlement discussions.

* Support the employment aspects of US and international mergers and acquisitions, including diligence and workforce integration.

* Manage employment disputes, administrative charges, and related matters in collaboration with colleagues and outside counsel, as appropriate.

* Draft and negotiate employment agreements, contractor agreements and employment-related supplier agreements.

* Own the integration of employment-law changes across multiple geographies into existing policies.

* Translate legal developments and lessons from your work into useful training modules across a variety of audiences, including for production and manufacturing personnel.

# **Qualifications**

* 4-6 years of employment law experience (in-house or law firm preferred).

* Juris Doctor and active membership in good standing with at least one U.S. state bar.

* Experience advising clients and stakeholders on employment counseling matters.

* Excellent written and oral communication skills; ability to communicate legal topics to a variety of audiences.

* Sound judgment, low ego, high ownership, and the temperament to thrive in a fast-moving, mission-driven company.

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Prior in-house experience a plus.

* Experience with manufacturing or production environments and with scaling hourly workforces.

# **Physical Demands**
