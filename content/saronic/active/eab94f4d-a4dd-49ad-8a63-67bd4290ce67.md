Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

The Saronic Depot Inventory Control & Logistics Manager is a hands-on individual contributor who serves as a satellite materials and logistics manager, running our depot sites and keeping physical material flow and IMS/WMS execution in lockstep across a high-volume, fast-paced production environment. Strong understanding and application of materials systems, inventory control, and warehouse operations, and the day-to-day escalation point for systems and process issues on the floor.

This role owns materials inventory and logistics at the depot, managing incoming and outgoing shipments, cycle counts, and daily warehouse activity. It standardizes processes through SOPs, supports training and certification for the people who touch materials, and measures performance so every site runs to the same standard. The role partners with the Materials Systems & Process Manager on strategy and standardization and gets results by working with site teams and cross-functional partners.

This is a critical role. With the depot operating away from Saronic’s HQ, the person in this role is the local authority on materials and logistics decisions, must communicate clearly with depot services / production, Procurement, carriers, and leadership, and must understand end-to-end supply chain and logistics operations, from supplier and inbound freight through storage and kitting to outbound delivery.

# **Responsibilities**

* **Warehouse operations and Inventory**

  · Oversee daily depot activity: receiving, put-away, storage, picking, kitting, replenishment, and shipping.

  · Ensure timely, accurate delivery of materials to spares and services depot techs utilizing Saronic’s processes.

  · Communicate priorities, handoffs, and process expectations clearly to warehouse associates, production, and other stakeholders.

  · Act as floor support and first-level escalation point for systems and process issues.

  **Depot site ownership, logistics, and shipping**

  · Serve as the on-site owner for depot sites, owning materials inventory, storage, and day-to-day operations.

  · Manage incoming shipments: schedule and receive inbound freight, verify it against POs and packing lists, record receipts in the IMS/WMS, and move material to stock quickly.

  · Manage outgoing shipments: pick, pack, stage, and ship material to production sites and other depots, with correct documentation and system transactions.

  · Coordinate with carriers, freight providers, and Procurement on delivery schedules and tracking, and resolve delays, damage, and shipment discrepancies.

  · Manage inter-site transfers and keep depot inventory levels aligned with production demand.

  · Maintain shipping and receiving records, and follow export and trade compliance requirements (ITAR/EAR) where applicable.

  **Process and SOP standardization**

  · Develop, deploy, and maintain standardized SOPs for receiving, storage, inventory control, kitting, and replenishment.

  · Drive standardization so every site runs the same core processes, with documented, approved exceptions only.

  · Manage SOP version control and change management so field practice and written procedure never drift apart.

  **Systems alignment (IMS/WMS)**

  · Align physical material flow with IMS/WMS transactions to ensure inventory accuracy and end-to-end traceability.

  · Ensure accurate execution of material transactions and resolve discrepancies.

  · Act as SME for materials-related system configuration, transaction discipline, and process-to-system gaps.

  · Partner with IT, Finance, Manufacturing, Planning, Procurement, and Quality to define requirements and resolve system or data issues.

  · Ensure cradle-to-grave inventory management, from receipt through storage, issue, and consumption, with every material movement and consumption recorded accurately and properly accounted for in the inventory management system (IMS).

  **Inventory accuracy, audits, and corrective action**

  · Plan and run cycle counts at the depot, investigate variances, and reconcile records to maintain high inventory accuracy.

  · Monitor daily and weekly KPIs, including inventory accuracy, pick accuracy, dock-to-stock time, and shortages, and escalate issues as needed.

  · Conduct process audits, lead root cause analysis, and implement corrective action plans (CAPs). Feed recurring findings back into SOPs and training.

  · Report site readiness and performance trends to Supply Chain leadership.

  **Training and certification**

  · Help onboard and train materials and warehouse team members, using role-based curricula, job aids, and hands-on IMS/WMS exercises tied to the SOPs.

  · Support site-level trainers so training scales beyond HQ staff.

  · Maintain training and certification records showing who is qualified to perform which tasks, at which site.

  · Measure training effectiveness through assessment results and movement in inventory accuracy, pick accuracy, dock-to-stock time, and shortages.

  **Pull systems, process improvement, and site readiness**

  · Implement pull systems such as Kanban and min/max replenishment, and drive continuous process improvements.

  · Run readiness assessments and prepare new and expanding sites to operate to enterprise standards from day one.

  **Safety and compliance**

  · Ensure compliance with safety, quality, and regulatory requirements across warehouse operations.
