Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

The Learning & Development Manager, Manufacturing & Trades leads Saronic’s trade academies, with accountability for producing a qualified trades workforce, including welders, fitters, and machinists, at the volume and pace the production ramp requires. You lead a team of trade instructors across Franklin and Port Alpha and own the delivery, quality, and throughput of technical training for the trades workforce. This function is newly established: early work is building the academies — instruction model, instructor standards, cohort structure, and the training environment itself — with delivery capacity and team structure scaling as hiring demand and headcount allow.

This is a people-leadership role. Your value is the output and development of your team rather than your personal instruction: you will set and monitor instruction quality, build instructional capability in your team, remove blockers, allocate instructor capacity across sites and cohorts, and own the stakeholder relationships with production leadership. You are accountable for your team having an adequate training environment — space, equipment, consumables, parts, and fabrication support — which you secure through Production and Facilities rather than control directly. You will partner with L&D leadership, who own the function’s roadmap and prioritization; with Instructional Design, who build the curriculum your instructors deliver; with the skills and qualification function, who define what “qualified” means and the assessment standards that verify it; and with Production, Quality, and EHS, who own work execution, inspection, and safety standards. Success means the trades workforce is qualified and production-ready on the schedule the business committed to.

# **Responsibilities**

* Lead, coach, and develop a team of trade instructors, with individual development plans and clear performance expectations.
* Own performance management for the team, including goal setting, regular feedback, and calibration against the L&D leveling framework.
* Build instructional capability across the team, developing instructors’ ability to teach, assess, and coach — including external credentials where they exist for the trade.
* Build team culture and remove barriers to productivity, anticipating challenges and adjusting team focus as priorities shift.
* Own end-to-end delivery of the welding and fitting & machining academies, including cohort design, scheduling, and graduate throughput against trades hiring demand.
* Leverage external curriculum and training programs where they outperform building in-house, and manage the associated licensing and vendor relationships.
* Set and monitor instruction quality metrics, and drive a culture of continuous improvement against them.
* Ensure instruction reflects Saronic’s technical, safety, and quality standards, the curriculum standards owned by Instructional Design, and applicable welding and machining codes.
* Secure the training bays, equipment, consumables, parts, and fabrication support required for delivery, working through Production and Facilities.
* Serve as the L&D point of contact for production leadership at Franklin and Port Alpha, managing expectations on scope, timeline, and feasibility.
* Convert incoming business requests into scoped, executable work, confirming that training is the right solution before committing instructor capacity.
* Partner with the skills and qualification function so academy output connects to verified qualification rather than course completion.
* Represent trades training in site and production planning forums, and ensure team contributions support broader business objectives.
* Translate trades hiring plans and production schedules into academy demand, and flag where demand exceeds instructor capacity.
* Allocate instructor capacity across sites, cohorts, and competing requests, and escalate trade-offs that cannot be resolved within the team.
* Evaluate completion, qualification pass rates, and instructor performance data to adjust staffing, scheduling, and cohort design.
* Manage the team’s operating budget, including consumables, equipment, and external training spend.

# **Qualifications**

**Required:**

• 7–9 years in technical training, trades instruction, or workforce development, including direct people-leadership experience.

• Demonstrated transition from hands-on instruction or trade practice to people-centered leadership, including experience hiring and developing technical instructors who bring both trade competence and teaching ability.

• Experience building or running a trade academy or technical training program in a production environment.

• Effectiveness across both establishing a function and scaling it, including comfort working hands-on while the team is small.

• Working knowledge of trade qualification and certification regimes, including how code and customer requirements drive requalification and currency.

• Experience owning delivery against a schedule with accountability for throughput and quality, including setting and holding a team to instruction quality standards.

• Sound judgment on instructor capacity allocation and priority setting, with the ability to make timely decisions and escalate complex issues appropriately.

• Demonstrated ability to operate on a production floor and earn credibility with skilled trades and production leadership.

• Strong written and verbal communication, including the ability to translate learning concepts for both executive and floor audiences.

• U.S. Person status as defined below.

**Preferred**

• Experience in shipbuilding, marine fabrication, aerospace, or another regulated, safety-critical manufacturing environment.

• Familiarity with AWS welding qualification standards, particularly aluminum, or comparable code-based qualification systems.

• Experience running an accredited or externally recognized technical training program.

• Experience managing training delivery against a rapid hiring ramp.

• Familiarity with structured on-the-job training methods, such as TWI Job Instruction.

• Experience supporting greenfield site rampups or multi-site operations
