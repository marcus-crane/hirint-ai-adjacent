Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

## Job Summary

Saronic is seeking a Test Site Manager to own the day-to-day operation of our Gulfport, Mississippi test site, enabling safe, efficient R&D testing of our medium and large autonomous surface vessels (USVs) on the Gulf Coast. In this role you will manage site safety, maritime operations, heavy-lift equipment, logistics, procurement, support vessels, IT, and facilities, serving as the single point of accountability that keeps the site running and testing on schedule. You will coordinate across engineering, operations, and corporate functions, act as the site's primary interface to the real estate team, and represent Saronic to local port, marina, and regulatory partners.

This is a full-time, on-site role based in Gulfport, MS. The ideal candidate is highly organized, safety-focused, and able to juggle multiple concurrent priorities in a fast-moving field environment while keeping people, assets, and infrastructure ready to support the test schedule.

## Responsibilities

**How you support the mission:**

· Manage day-to-day R&D testing operations at the Gulfport site, ensuring test teams have the resources, infrastructure, and support needed to execute on schedule.

· Own site safety, enforcing safe practices and protocols across all on-site and on-water testing activities in the Mississippi Sound and Gulf of Mexico.

· Manage site logistics, including launch, recovery, berthing, and movement of medium and large USVs, and the receipt and staging of equipment, assets, and materials in support of the test schedule.

· Manage procurement through NetSuite, ordering the equipment, materials, and consumables required to sustain testing.

· Own heavy-lift and material-handling operations, including cranes, lifts, rigging, and vessel launch and recovery; ensure certified operators, lift plans, and inspections are in place for every evolution.

· Manage charter and support vessels for test operations, including scheduling, coordination, crew readiness, and vendor relationships, and oversee procurement and upkeep of site vehicles and heavy equipment.

· Oversee site IT to keep testing, communications, and data systems operational.

· Plan and manage facilities updates, including pier, berthing, crane and lift, and shop space, to keep the site capable and aligned with testing needs.

· Maintain severe-weather and hurricane preparedness plans, and lead site securing, evacuation, and recovery when storms threaten the Gulf Coast.

· Serve as the site's point of contact for the Port of Gulfport, marina operators, the U.S. Coast Guard, and local authorities on access, permits, and on-water activity.

**How you support the team:**

· Serve as the single point of accountability for site operations, coordinating across engineering, operations, and corporate functions.

· Act as the site's primary interface to the real estate team, communicating facility needs, changes, and space requirements.

· Foster a safe, organized, and collaborative site environment that enables the test team to focus on execution.

· Anticipate and resolve site-level issues before they impact the test schedule.

· Support visiting engineering and customer teams with site access, orientation, and local logistics.

· Keep leadership informed on site status, readiness, and any risks to testing.

## Role Requirements

· Demonstrated maritime operations experience, with a track record of leading field or site operations in a defense, commercial maritime, or autonomous systems environment.

· Exceptional organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple concurrent priorities across engineering, operations, and corporate stakeholders.

· Strong written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to deliver clear site status and risk reporting to leadership.

· Financial acumen with experience managing an operational site budget, vendor contracts, and procurement.

· Working knowledge of maritime operations for medium and large vessels, including pier and berthing operations, crane and lift operations, rigging, vessel launch and recovery, and marine safety practices.

· Ability to build and maintain effective relationships with local vendors, port and marina operators, regulatory agencies, and senior internal stakeholders.

· Ability to obtain and maintain a Secret clearance required; active Secret clearance preferred.

· Based in or willing to relocate to the Gulfport, MS area. Occasional travel (up to 10%) to Saronic headquarters in Austin, TX and other test sites.

· Able to certify as "fit for work" by demonstrating ability to swim and tread water, lift up to 75 lbs., and spend extended time periods on the water.

## Qualifications

· Bachelor's degree in business, engineering, maritime studies, operations management, or a related field, or equivalent military or industry experience.

· Seven or more years of experience in defense operations, maritime operations, military operations, or a related field, with at least three years leading a team or site.

· Experience with autonomous surface vessels, unmanned maritime systems, or defense technology programs is highly desirable.

· Experience with commercial port operations, including terminal, pier, berthing, and heavy-lift activity, is highly desired.

· Familiarity with the Mississippi Gulf Coast maritime community, including the Port of Gulfport, local USCG Sector, and regional vendors, is a plus.

· Prior military service in a maritime, special operations, or operations/logistics leadership billet is a plus.

· Crane operator certification (e.g., NCCCO), rigging/signal person certification, or other maritime credentials are a plus.

· Experience with NetSuite or a similar procurement/ERP system is a plus.

## Physical Demands

· Ability to certify as "fit for work" by demonstrating ability to swim and tread water.

· Frequently and repetitively lift, push, and carry up to 75 lbs.

· Will be exposed to Gulf Coast marine environments, including heat, humidity, sun, wind, rain, cold, and saltwater spray, often for extended periods.

· Must be able to operate in daylight, low-light, and night conditions using marine navigation systems and visual cues.

· Ability to perform physically demanding work for extended periods of time, up to 12 hours per day.

· Ability to carry 20 lbs. up and down stairs and ladders, and to board and move about vessels, piers, and elevated work platforms.
