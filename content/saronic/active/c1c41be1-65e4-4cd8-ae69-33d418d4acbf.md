Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

**Position Overview**

Saronic Technologies is seeking a detail-oriented and experienced **Quality Supervisor** to join our growing production team. This role is responsible for overseeing inspection activities and leading a team of Quality Inspectors to ensure our autonomous surface vessels meet the highest standards of quality, reliability, and performance prior to delivery.

The **Quality Supervisor** will work closely with engineering, production, and supply chain teams to maintain compliance with design specifications, industry standards, and regulatory requirements. The ideal candidate is shift-flexible, highly organized, and possesses strong technical inspection knowledge along with leadership experience in a fast-paced manufacturing environment.

**Key Responsibilities**

**Inspection & Testing**

* Oversee and guide Quality Inspectors in conducting inspections and tests on components, assemblies, and finished vessels to verify compliance with design specifications, industry standards, and regulatory requirements.
* Ensure consistent application of inspection procedures across shifts and production areas.

**Documentation & Reporting**

* Maintain accurate and detailed records of inspection results, nonconformances, and corrective actions.
* Prepare inspection reports and communicate findings to engineering and production teams to support timely resolution.

**Compliance & Quality Standards**

* Ensure team adherence to internal quality policies, procedures, and applicable industry standards, including DoD-related requirements.
* Monitor inspection activities and enforce quality protocols across production operations.

**Problem Solving & Root Cause Analysis**

* Identify defects or deviations from specifications and lead investigations into root causes.
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams to implement corrective and preventive actions.

**Continuous Improvement**

* Participate in quality improvement initiatives and support the development and refinement of quality control procedures and best practices.
* Recommend process improvements that enhance product quality, efficiency, and consistency.

**Cross-Functional Collaboration**

* Partner with engineering, production, and suppliers to address quality issues and support the successful delivery of autonomous surface vessels.

**Leadership**

* Lead and coordinate inspection activities across shifts while setting the standard for quality performance and accountability.
* Ensure alignment with company standards, production goals, and quality expectations.

**Training & Development**

* Assist with onboarding and training new Quality Inspectors on inspection techniques, documentation processes, and quality standards.
* Provide ongoing mentorship and guidance to support team development.

**Qualifications**

* **5+ years** of experience in quality inspection, preferably in aerospace, defense, maritime, or advanced manufacturing environments.
* **1+ years of experience leading or mentoring technical staff.**
* Strong understanding of quality control principles, inspection methodologies, and root cause analysis.
* Proficiency with inspection tools and equipment (e.g., calipers, micrometers, gauges).
* Knowledge of industry standards and regulatory requirements, including **DoD quality expectations**.
* Excellent problem-solving abilities with strong attention to detail.
* Strong communication and interpersonal skills with the ability to work effectively across cross-functional teams.
