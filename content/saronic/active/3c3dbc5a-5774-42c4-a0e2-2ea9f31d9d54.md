Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

Saronic builds autonomous maritime systems at a pace and scale few companies have attempted. Turning strategy into repeatable execution is what makes that possible, and it sits at the heart of this role. We are seeking a Strategic Operations Associate/Manager to work at the intersection of strategy and operations: translating company objectives into plans, then building the processes, models, and cross-functional rhythms that make those plans real.   
   
You will take on high-priority, ambiguous initiatives that don’t belong to a single function. These efforts span the business and require aligning multiple stakeholders around a shared plan and a shared view of the facts.   
   
We’re looking for someone who is highly analytical, operationally minded, and energized by problems that span multiple parts of the organization. You should be comfortable diving into complex data, structuring unclear problems, building the process or model that solves them, and driving the work through to execution.

# **Responsibilities**

* **Strategic Operations**: Take on emerging, cross-functional initiatives and turn company objectives into clear plans, owners, milestones, and operating cadences. Keep Product, Growth, Programs, and Strategy aligned from planning through execution.

* **Cross-Functional Leadership**: Serve as the integrator on initiatives that cut across the business, owning the plan, chasing dependencies, and holding teams accountable to commitments.

* **Process Design & Improvement**: Identify operational gaps and design scalable workflows across the business. Replace one-off effort with repeatable systems that teams can run without you.

* **Planning & Forecasting**: Build the models and planning frameworks that connect demand, resources, and capacity, giving leadership a clear, decision-ready view of the business and the tradeoffs ahead.

* **Special Projects**: Structure complex, ambiguous problems, build alignment across teams, and drive solutions from first analysis to implementation.

# **Qualifications**

* 5+ years post-undergrad at a top-tier strategy consulting firm, or 2+ years post-MBA at a top-tier strategy consulting firm.

* An operator’s instinct for turning a strategy into a plan, a process, and a cadence

* The ability to build credibility and alignment across leadership and functional teams

* Comfort with ambiguity and a bias toward structuring it and moving to action

* Clear, concise communication, in writing and in front of senior stakeholders

# **Physical Demands**
