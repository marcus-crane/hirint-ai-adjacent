Saronic is a fast-growing defense technology startup focused on developing secure, mission-critical systems for our customers. Our operations depend on seamless communication and collaboration—both in person and across secure digital platforms. We’re looking for a skilled **Audio/Visual (A/V) Technician** who thrives in dynamic environments and is eager to support a fast-paced, high-impact mission.

The A/V Technician will play a vital role in maintaining and supporting our organization’s conference room and event technology. This role will provide hands-on assistance for meetings, executive events, and live productions across our campus. The ideal candidate is a proactive problem-solver with experience in A/V operations and a strong commitment to delivering exceptional user experiences.

**Key Responsibilities:**



* Provide immediate, on-site assistance for A/V issues in conference rooms, huddle spaces, and large event areas.
* Support, maintain, and optimize A/V infrastructure, including Logitech, Huddly, and Microsoft Teams Room (MTR) systems.
* Configure and maintain Q-SYS audio DSP and control systems for conferencing and room automation.
* Operate, configure, and maintain video wall and digital signage systems across offices, production floors, and lab environments.
* Manage and organize A/V inventory and logistics across multiple facilities.
* Set up, operate, and dismantle A/V equipment for meetings, events, and live productions — including secure and executive sessions.
* Perform proactive system health checks, diagnostics, and preventive maintenance to ensure consistent performance.
* Troubleshoot complex A/V, network, and integration issues spanning hardware, software, and connectivity layers.
* Provide end-user support, training, and installation assistance for A/V and collaboration systems.
* Manage support requests and incidents through ServiceNow, ensuring timely updates and resolution tracking.
* Collaborate with IT, Facilities, and Security teams to ensure A/V systems are integrated, secure, and compliant.
* Produce and support podcasts, live streams, and internal broadcast events as needed.
* Participate in an on-call rotation for after-hours and weekend event or system support.
* Travel to remote offices, event sites, or production facilities (up to 25%).

**Required Qualifications:**



* Minimum 3 years of experience in the Audio Visual field; IT experience is a plus.
* Hands-on experience with Logitech, Huddly, and Microsoft Teams Room (MTR) systems.
* Experience with Q-SYS audio DSP configuration, control, and troubleshooting.
* Familiarity with video wall and digital signage technologies and content management systems.
* Strong technical troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.
* Excellent communication, organization, and customer service abilities.
* Ability to remain calm and professional in high-pressure situations involving executives or government officials.
* Comfortable lifting and transporting A/V equipment (up to 50 lbs).
* Eligibility to obtain and maintain an active Secret security clearance.

**Preferred Qualifications:**



* Experience supporting A/V systems in production or manufacturing environments, including factory floors, engineering labs, or test facilities.
* Familiarity with BrightSign or similar digital signage management tools.
* Working knowledge of networking fundamentals, PoE, and VLAN configuration for A/V systems.
* Background in podcasting, livestreaming, or video production.
* Experience supporting A/V systems in secure or classified communications environments.
* Proficiency with ServiceNow or similar IT service management platforms.

**Physical Demands**



* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer.
* Occasional standing and walking within the office.
* Manual dexterity to operate a computer keyboard, mouse, and other office equipment.
* Visual acuity to read screens, documents, and reports.
* Occasional reaching, bending, or stooping to access file drawers, cabinets, or office supplies.
* Lifting and carrying items up to 50 pounds occasionally (e.g., office supplies, packages).
