Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are seeking a Growth Strategy & Strategic Operations Manager to own operational rigor, clarity, and scalability across our Growth organization. This role builds and owns the mechanisms behind Growth's strategic decision-making, including gate review processes, internal operating processes, demand and pipeline forecasting, and the sales infrastructure leadership depends on. It also sets the standard for how the organization plans, prioritizes, and executes as it scales.

You will operate as a trusted thought partner to Growth leadership and as the connective tissue across Proposals, Contracts, Programs, Marketing, Product, Government Relations, and Finance, ensuring alignment, accountability, and seamless cross-functional execution. You will own our sales pipeline, GTM processes, customer engagement support, and forecasting mechanisms end to end, while leading a broad portfolio of business operations work spanning demand planning, cross-functional alignment, and organization-wide process design.

We're looking for someone who is highly analytical, operationally minded, and energized by ambiguous problems that cut across multiple parts of the business. You should be comfortable going deep in complex data, architecting processes that outlast you, and building consensus across teams without relying on formal authority.

# **Responsibilities**

* **Pipeline Management & Reporting:** Own the sales pipeline end to end, including CRM data integrity, stage definitions, close-date discipline, and the reporting and dashboards leadership relies on to track the business. Drive opportunity progression through gate mechanisms such as Concept Reviews, holding the organization to a consistent bar at each stage.

* **Growth Operations:** Own core Growth operations end to end and Commercial Price List (CPL) governance, ensuring the CPL stays connected to the tools and processes that depend on it (e.g., NetSuite). Oversee comprehensive demand planning by building forecast simulations, assessing resource requirements, allocating capacity against materialized demand, and driving ongoing improvements to the planning process itself. Together these give leadership a accurate, decision-ready view of the business.

* **Process Architecture:** Own the design of the Growth organization's core operating processes by diagnosing gaps, codifying how work moves across sales, marketing, product, and programs (e.g., the Opportunity Management SOP), and building workflows that hold as the business scales.

* **Capture Support:** Lead cross-functional alignment on the company's most consequential pursuits, partnering across Growth, Programs, Product, Proposals, Contracts, and Leadership to shape opportunities, steer proposal strategy, and support program execution. Own the customer-facing collateral that carries those pursuits, including product packets, slick sheets, and engagement-specific materials.

* **Special Projects:** Take on the organization's most ambiguous problems, structure them, build consensus on the path forward, and drive execution from framing through implementation.

# **Qualifications**

* 5+ years in Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, Strategy & Operations, or a comparable track record in consulting, investment banking, or corporate finance.

* Bachelor's degree in business, engineering, economics, or a related field.

* Experience in defense, aerospace, or another regulated, government-adjacent industry.

* Demonstrated ownership of complex, cross-functional workstreams from framing through implementation, with measurable outcomes.

* Advanced command of analytics and communication tools (e.g., Excel, PowerPoint) and fluency with CRM and BI platforms (e.g., Salesforce, Tableau), with the ability to master new tooling quickly.

* Track record of influencing senior stakeholders without formal authority, across both technical and non-technical audiences.

* Comfort operating with incomplete information in a fast-moving environment, with the judgment to know when to gather more and when to drive to a decision.

* Highly organized, detail-oriented, and proactive in managing competing priorities across multiple stakeholders.
