Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Position Overview**

We are seeking an experienced and talented engineer to lead the electrical technical execution of Saronic’s products. This role requires both high agency and a strong work ethic to lead an electrical engineering team to execute rapid design, build and test of Saronic’s products. Strong engineering fundamentals, organizational, and communication skills are key to success in this role. You’ll work with Saronic’s engineering, production, and product teams to ensure Saronic’s products are high performance, cost effective and producible at high rate to enable Saronic in redefining maritime superiority.

# **Responsibilities**

* Owns the electrical technical execution roadmap and definition of deliverables for the product in alignment with the product responsible engineer

  + Leads electrical technical development of the product both asynchronously & through team meetings (IPT)
  + Contributes to a logbook of key decisions & supporting documentation for reference of leadership and peers
  + Balance electrical technical & schedule risks to enable rapid development
  + Tracks key performance metrics for the electrical elements of the product from the development phase through production
* Overall product electrical technical execution owner

  + Own overall schedule execution of electrical team by resource planning, setting product milestones and identifying blockers
  + Contributes to high level schedule & tracks execution of the key milestones for electrical subsystems, and partners with engineering project managers on detailed schedule building

* Own product cost estimating & execution

  + Sets high level budget for material and labor, partners with finance to reconcile estimated and actual costs through the product lifecycle
  + Approves high-cost purchases for the product owning high stakes schedule risk decisions

# **Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s degree or greater in Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Naval Architecture, Robotics Engineering, or related field

* 2+ years of experience designing, building, or testing complex mechanical, propulsion or electrical systems

* Demonstrated leadership capabilities at Saronic, 2+ years of formal leadership experience in industry preferred

* Strong engineering fundamentals, organizational and communication skills

* Willingness to work long hours and weekends to lead the electrical engineering team to success on a product

* Strong collaboration and communication skills across disciplines (electrical, software, naval architecture, manufacturing)

* Experience with rapid product development going from fast-paced prototyping to full-rate production
