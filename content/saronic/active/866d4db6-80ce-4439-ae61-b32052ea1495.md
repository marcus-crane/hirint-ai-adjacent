Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

The Porter is responsible for the safe and efficient movement of materials between sites, transporting boats and trailers, operating forklifts (including large-capacity up to 30K), and handling deliveries and pickups from vendors. This role supports production and operations by ensuring timely and accurate material handling while maintaining a focus on safety and professionalism.

# **Responsibilities**

* Transport materials between company sites and designated locations.
* Safely tow and maneuver boats and trailers as required.
* Operate forklifts, including heavy-capacity forklifts (30K), to move and position materials.
* Pick up and deliver materials, equipment, and supplies to and from vendors.
* Assist with loading and unloading trucks, trailers, and other transport vehicles.
* Ensure proper handling, securing, and documentation of materials during transport.
* Maintain cleanliness and organization of vehicles, equipment, and work areas.
* Perform basic inspections of vehicles, forklifts, and trailers to ensure safe operation.
* Follow all company policies, safety regulations, and DOT requirements.
* Support additional material handling and logistics tasks as assigned.

# **Qualifications**

* High school diploma or equivalent preferred.

* Valid driver’s license with a clean driving record.
* Experience operating forklifts, including large-capacity (30K) forklifts.
* Ability to tow and maneuver boats and trailers safely.
* Knowledge of safe material handling and transportation practices.
* Strong attention to detail and ability to follow instructions.
* Reliable, punctual, and able to work independently or as part of a team.
* Ability to lift up to 50 lbs. and perform physical tasks as required.

# **Physical Demands:**

* Prolonged periods of sitting at a desk and working on a computer
* Occasional standing and walking within the office
* Manual dexterity to operate a computer keyboard, mouse, and other office equipment
* Visual acuity to read screens, documents, and reports
* Occasional reaching, bending, or stooping to access file drawers, cabinets, or office supplies
* Lifting and carrying items up to 20 pounds occasionally (e.g., office supplies, packages)
