Saronic Technologies is a leader in revolutionizing autonomy at sea, dedicated to developing state-of-the-art solutions that enhance maritime operations through autonomous and intelligent platforms.

# **Job Overview**

We are looking for a Senior Manager to lead strategic initiatives that cut across our U.S. Defense, International Defense, and Commercial segments. You will drive the enterprise-level strategy development process, synthesizing the work of our three Market Strategy teams into a single view of where we should compete and how we will win. You will also be responsible for strategy-side inputs and rationale behind our most consequential processes, including the long-range plan, annual operating plan, quarterly business reviews, equity and debt financing, and major company initiatives. This is a high-influence role at the intersection of strategy and operations; your work will directly inform C-suite decision-making and involve close collaboration with Growth, Programs, Finance, Corporate Development, Partnerships, Shipbuilding, and other key functions.

# **Responsibilities**

* **Company-Level Strategy Development:** Drive the annual strategy cycle for the company overall, synthesizing findings from our three Market Strategy teams. These efforts involve closely working alongside subject matter experts across Growth.

* **Planning Processes:** Own the strategy-side inputs to the long-range plan and annual operating plan, including topline assumptions and scenario development.

* **QBRs and Initiative Tracking:** Run the quarterly business review process, including content development and synthesis of outcomes; track progress against major initiatives across the organization and surface risks and decisions that require executive attention.

* **Financing Support:** Develop the topline inputs, market narrative, and supporting analysis for equity fundraising and debt financing processes; partner with Finance and Corporate Development to translate strategy into investor-ready materials and respond to diligence requests.

* **Major Initiatives:** Provide the market and strategic perspective on the company’s largest cross-functional initiatives such as capital projects, new facility build-outs, and new business lines; collaborate with Finance, Growth, Programs, and other teams to build business cases that enable well-informed decision making.

* **Executive Projects and Team Support:** Take on priority ad hoc requests from the executive team for key questions that do not fit neatly within a single function; lend analytical and project support to Market Strategy teams where capacity or priorities require.

# **Qualifications**

* 5+ years post-undergrad at a top-tier strategy consulting firm, or 2+ years post-MBA at a top-tier strategy consulting firm.

* Majority of consulting tenure spent on corporate/growth strategy and commercial due diligence, with demonstrated ownership of market sizing, competitive positioning analysis, and value creation planning.

* Mastery of Excel market modeling and executive-ready PowerPoint.

* Ability to distill complex analysis into clear recommendations for C-suite audiences and manage stakeholders across technical and non-technical teams.

* Exceptional judgment and ownership; effective at driving multiple high-stakes workstreams to completion with limited direction in a fast-moving environment.

* Bachelor's degree in business, engineering, economics, or a related field; MBA a plus.

* Familiarity with defense, aerospace, advanced manufacturing, energy, maritime, or other industrial markets preferred.
