We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## **About the role**

We're looking for an Experiential Graphic Designer to join our in house creative team. You'll support graphic design elements across our global experiential marketing, from small to mid scale activations through to Summits, as well as general brand graphic design work as needed. Projects span Trade Show event booths, environmental graphics, and a growing number of regional pop ups, with an ambitious growth plan into 2027 across more events and regions.

You'll work closely with the Creative Direction lead for Experiences & Events, translating brand and creative direction into graphics that hold up across very different local and regional contexts, while keeping the high bar for how ElevenLabs shows up in the world. You'll need to think in materiality and space, how graphics sit on different surfaces, how they interact with architecture, lighting, and movement through a room, to create a full experience rather than a flat graphic applied to a wall.

## **Requirements**

* Proven design credentials with a strong conceptual foundation, and experience taking projects from concept through design to delivery
* A spatial eye, with understanding of spatial and architectural principles, and genuine interest in how graphics combine with materiality and physical space to create experience
* Advanced in the Adobe suite (Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, etc)
* A strong interest in AI, and confidence using it as part of your design process
* Ability to advocate for your ideas, connect across departments, and take ownership of planning and timescales to deliver high quality work

## **Your day to day will include**

* Working across global projects, bringing concepts to life and working independently
* Projects ranging from simple tradeshow booth graphics through to environmental graphics and art direction across large experiential projects
* Developing a design language across projects, evolving it across applications, environments, and the broader channel mix
* Contributing to ideation with the wider brand team
* Clearly presenting your thinking to internal teams and partners
* Delegating tasks to junior designers, artworkers, or external partners when working with you on a project
* Creating artwork ready files where required
