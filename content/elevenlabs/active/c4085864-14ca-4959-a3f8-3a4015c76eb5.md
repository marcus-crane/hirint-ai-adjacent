## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## **About the role**

We're hiring a Head of Healthcare to own ElevenLabs' US healthcare business across providers, payers, and life sciences. Health organizations want agents that talk to patients, members, and clinicians and that are safe, fast, and compatible with the systems they already run. You'll own the healthcare business from strategy and pipeline through to the team that delivers the revenue.

We don't use traditional job titles at ElevenLabs. This role is equivalent in scope and seniority to a Vice President of Healthcare, with ownership of the vertical, authority to hire and lead the sales team, and the chance to work closely with ElevenLabs leaders across the company.

Healthcare buyers hold vendors to a high bar on safety, compliance, and reliability, and we're looking for a leader who has met that bar before. You'll build the healthcare business end to end, including the strategy, team, pipeline, and playbook. You'll stay close to the most strategic deals while hiring and developing the leaders who will run segments as the business grows.

## **In this role you will**

* Define and execute the healthcare go-to-market strategy across health systems, payers, and life sciences (pharma and medtech).
* Own the healthcare revenue number, forecast accuracy, pipeline quality, and sales process, working with Revenue Operations on clean data and clear insight.
* Recruit and lead the sales team, starting with Account Executives and adding segment leaders as the business grows. Coach reps on deal strategy and execution, and develop future leaders.
* Define the enterprise ICP, sales methodology, and a repeatable motion for each segment.
* Lead the largest strategic pursuits with your team, and own the executive relationships at our most important health systems, payers, and life sciences companies.
* Build the customer reference base that establishes ElevenLabs as a trusted partner across healthcare.
* Build our approach to BAA, security, and AI governance review (including MLR for pharma), and develop the executive relationships with Compliance, Privacy, and IT security leaders that health organizations require.
* Partner with Product, Engineering, Forward Deployed Engineering, and Customer Success leaders to shape what we build, integrate, and bring to market for health organizations.
* Establish how we land and expand with each health organization, from first deployment through enterprise-wide adoption.
* Define our position against build-vs-buy decisions, platform bundlers, and vertical point solutions.
* Represent ElevenLabs at industry events and in high-stakes negotiations.

## **What we're looking for**

* 10-15+ years of enterprise sales experience, including at least 5 years leading sales teams.
* A track record selling into health systems, payers, or life sciences, with deep fluency in HIPAA, BAAs, AI governance, and long, multi-stakeholder sales cycles.
* A record of earning the trust of senior health system, payer, or life sciences leaders, and of bringing new technology into their organizations.
* A history of closing at least seven-figure enterprise deals.
* Comfort moving between a C-suite briefing and the details of a prospecting list or Proof of Value.

## **Preferred**

* Experience building a sales motion or playbook from the ground up, whether as a founding leader or inside a larger company.
* Fluency with AI products and workflows.
* Interest in voice and conversational AI.
