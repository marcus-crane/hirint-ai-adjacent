## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## About the role

We're looking for a highly motivated Events Lead to own planning and execution of ElevenLabs' flagship events. Your work will deepen ElevenLabs' brand presence, create high-impact moments with customers and prospects, and generate high-quality pipeline in partnership with our GTM teams.

You'll own the full lifecycle of our flagship owned events - summits, experiential activations, marquee sponsorships, and signature brand moments. This role is focused on our flagship event portfolio, distinct from regional field marketing and third-party conference attendance.

## **What you'll do**

* Develop and execute flagship event plans aligned with business objectives across key markets
* Own owned/branded flagship events, and marquee sponsorships (e.g. F1, major industry summits) end to end
* Collaborate cross-functionally with sales, marketing, product, and GTM teams for seamless execution
* Analyze event performance and provide recommendations for future improvements
* Build and maintain relationships with sponsors, partners, agencies, and production vendors across North America

## **Requirements**

* 8+ years events experience, with at least three in tech/enterprise flagship event marketing
* Track record of producing large-scale, high-profile branded events end to end
* Deep knowledge of the tech ecosystem and executive/enterprise audiences
* Experience working with premium event agencies and production partners
* Based in NYC or SF preferred
