## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## About the role

ElevenLabs is a voice AI company, and more and more startup founders and developers are building their products on our platform. We want to meet them in person and give them reasons to keep building with us.

We are looking for a Field Marketing Manager to own our events and field programs for startups and founders in the US. You will run our founder dinner series, a quarterly demo day in SF, and the happy hours, workshops and meetups that bring developers into the product. You will build Founder's Day Summit, a flagship event for about 100 founders, taking it from first idea to the day itself. You will also decide where we show up at other people's events, from AI conference sponsorships to side events and co-hosted programs with VCs, accelerators and partner brands.

This is a hands-on role. You will build guest lists, host the room, and work with ElevenLabs researchers, startup team members, the Grants program and VC partners to make each event worth a founder's evening. About two thirds of your events will be in San Francisco and one third in New York. You will measure what each event produces, whether that is sign-ups, activated accounts or pipeline, and use it to decide what to run next.

The right person knows the startup and AI scene in SF and New York, has a network of founders, VCs and builders, and has clear views on what makes an event worth attending. You will own a budget, juggle several partners at once, and run the events.

## What you will do

* Own a founder exec dinner series of 10 to 20 guests, sourcing founders through outbound and the Grants program and hosting with ElevenLabs researchers, startup team members and VC partners
* Run a quarterly demo day in SF where founders show what they have built on ElevenLabs
* Lead Founder's Day Summit for about 100 founders, taking it from inception to execution
* Plan and run happy hours, mixers, technical workshops and meetups for founders and developers that drive self-serve adoption, with four to five in the first half of the year
* Choose and manage sponsorships at AI conferences and founder events such as HumanX, Slush and YC Startup School
* Plan side events around the big conferences, such as dinners and parties for the founders attending
* Co-host events with VCs, accelerators and partner brands, agreeing the format, guest list and split of work up front
* Work with the Grants program and startup GTM team to build a pipeline of high-quality founders for every event
* Brief ElevenLabs researchers, speakers and hosts before each event, and make sure founders get a clear follow-up afterwards
* Manage event budgets, venues, vendors and logistics across San Francisco and New York, with about two thirds of events in SF
* Track attendance, sign-ups, activated accounts and pipeline for each event, and report what worked and what did not
* Test new event formats and retire the ones that do not earn their cost

## Requirements

* Experience owning founder, developer or startup events end to end at a technology company, with examples you can share
* A track record of events that produced measurable results, such as sign-ups, activated accounts or pipeline
* A network in the SF or New York startup community, including founders, VCs and accelerators
* Experience running small, curated events where the guest list matters as much as the program
* Experience managing event budgets, vendors and sponsorship decisions
* Experience working with cross-functional teams and external partners, such as VCs, accelerators and conference organizers
* Familiarity with the AI and developer tools landscape

## Bonus
