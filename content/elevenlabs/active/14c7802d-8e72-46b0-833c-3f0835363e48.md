## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## About the role

We're hiring a Principal Sales Execution & NA Enablement Lead to help ElevenLabs win and expand more complex, technical enterprise accounts. This is a commercial role: you'll work on live deals with sales leadership, mobilize the field to execute, and turn what works into standards and plays that scale globally.

You'll be the primary Enablement partner to North America Sales leadership and help set the enterprise execution standards our EMEA and APAC teams adapt.

## What you'll do

* **Operationalize the sales execution foundation:** Turn sales methodology, value-selling artifacts, account planning and evaluation standards into live training, certifications, manager inspection and repeatable field workflows.
* **Build and run GTM plays:** Partner with Sales, PMM, RevOps and other GTM teams to turn priority products, use cases, verticals and competitive opportunities into targeted motions spanning account selection, messaging, field activation and measurement.
* **Improve live execution:** Partner with Sales leadership and selected priority account teams to diagnose execution challenges across deal strategy, executive engagement, technical evaluations and expansion, then turn those patterns into scalable improvements.
* **Drive field execution and adoption:** Equip managers to inspect deals against clear standards and remove the friction that slows sellers down.
* **Partner with NA Sales leadership:** Prioritize the execution gaps that matter most for revenue, including developer-led motions and priority verticals.
* **Codify and scale what works:** Turn successful practices into selling frameworks, execution standards and manager operating rhythms that teams across regions can adopt.
* **Work in an AI-native way:** Use AI, including ElevenLabs' own technology, to scale deal inspection, practice and reinforcement.

## What we're looking for

You know firsthand what it takes to win seven-figure enterprise deals, whether your background is in sales leadership, sales effectiveness, field enablement, GTM strategy or sales acceleration. You may not have closed them yourself as an AE, but you've materially influenced how complex enterprise deals are won.

* 10+ years in enterprise sales, sales leadership or GTM execution, ideally in data infrastructure, cybersecurity, cloud, AI or enterprise SaaS
* Strong commercial judgment on complex deals, including account strategy, value selling, competitive positioning and expansion
* A track record of turning growth opportunities into coordinated field action with measurable pipeline or revenue impact
* Experience scaling execution through managers and systems that changed seller behavior
* Familiarity with MEDDICC, Command of the Message or similar, and the technical fluency to engage developers and FDEs credibly
* The ability to influence senior sales leaders and align Sales, PMM, RevOps and FDE without direct authority
* High AI fluency and comfort building where the operating model is still taking shape

**Bonus:** experience in voice AI.
