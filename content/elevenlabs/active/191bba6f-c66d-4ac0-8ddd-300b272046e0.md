Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. ## **About the Role**

We’re looking for a leader to own our legal operations function and continue building it for our next phase of growth. Working with a dedicated automations engineering team, you will own the systems that ensure the Legal Team scales with the company: contract management, vendor relationships (including outside counsel billing and guidelines) and the overall legal tech stack. The goal is simple: help define what a legal organization looks like in the AI native era.

**In this role, you will:**

* Assist in the design and improvement of the operating model for Legal. Identify work that should be handled by the team and areas where we can continue automating.
* Manage our legal technology stack, including contract lifecycle management (Ironclad), matter management, and creating an e-billing systems
* Manage relationships with outside counsel and legal service providers, including budget and performance tracking
* Work with our automation teams to use Eleven Agents and other tools to change the way work is done across the team
* Define metrics and build dashboards that give us a clear view of the team's priorities, workload and performance
* Partner with Finance, IT, FDE and business teams to keep legal processes aligned with how the company operates

**Who you are:**

* You are a strategist and an operator. You are incredibly intellectually curious and enjoy breaking down and rebuilding processes
* Highly organized, detail-oriented, and proactive - you’re comfortable juggling multiple priorities while maintaining accuracy
* Genuinely interested in the future of AI and emerging technologies.
* A strong communicator who enjoys collaborating with cross-functional teams
* Comfortable working independently in a distributed, high-trust environment
* You have previous experience at a high-growth technology or AI company

**What you bring:**

* 7+ years of experience in legal operations with some experience leading the function
* Deep expertise in legal technology, including CLM platforms (Ironclad a major plus), e-billing systems, and matter management tools
* Experience supporting global teams
* Strong analytical skills with experience gathering metrics to storytell and drive key business decisions
* Excellent organizational skills and ability to manage competing deadlines.
