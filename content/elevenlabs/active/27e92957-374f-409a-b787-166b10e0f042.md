## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

# About the role

We’re looking for an experienced partnerships leader to own and scale ElevenLabs’ strategic relationships with cloud hyperscalers and Systems Integrators, the companies whose platforms, field teams and delivery capacity sit at the heart of how enterprises buy and deploy voice and customer experience AI.

You’ll build and manage co-sell and marketplace programmes with AWS, Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud, and build joint GTM and delivery motions with Regional and Global Systems Integrators. The goal: get ElevenLabs deeply embedded in hyperscaler partner networks and SI practices, so that when enterprises modernize their contact centers or build on cloud infrastructure, ElevenLabs is the natural AI voice layer, and partners have both the incentive and the capacity to sell and deliver it.

This is a 0→1 build in a high-leverage category. You’ll design co-sell and SI GTM frameworks, drive pipeline with hyperscaler field teams, structure marketplace and private offer motions, negotiate referral, resell and joint-offering agreements with SIs, and own the revenue and pipeline outcomes that come from these relationships.

# What success looks like (first 6-9 months)

* **Partner-sourced and influenced revenue:** closed-won ARR attributable to hyperscaler and SI relationships
* **Hyperscaler marketplace and co-sell presence:** listings live, co-sell registrations flowing, and active motions running with hyperscaler field teams
* **SI practice activation:** named SI practice leads, certified practitioners and at least one joint offering or solution play in market
* **Partner-sourced pipeline:** qualified opportunities sourced or co-sold through hyperscaler and SI field teams each quarter
* **Field activation:** measurable engagement with hyperscaler sellers and SI client partners and delivery teams, not just BD or alliance leads, translating to warm intros and joint account planning

# Requirements

* 8+ years in technology partnerships, alliances, or GTM roles within SaaS or cloud, with direct experience running hyperscaler partner programmes and SI/GSI partnerships
* Hands-on experience with hyperscaler co-sell programmes (AWS ISV Accelerate and ACE, Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Programme, Google Cloud Partner Advantage or equivalent) and marketplace operations (listings, private offers, commit burndown). You know how field co-sell actually works, not just the theory
* Working knowledge of the SI ecosystem: relationships with practice leads and client partners at GSIs and RSIs, and an understanding of how SIs build practices, attach services to software ARR, and allocate delivery capacity
* A builder mindset: experience creating co-sell and SI motions from 0→1, rather than inheriting a mature programme
* Comfortable with technical, API-driven products; able to work with solutions engineers to scope solution architectures and articulate ElevenLabs’ value to technical partner audiences
* Experience building enablement, certification or reference architectures that activate partner field and delivery teams at scale
* Based in North America
* **Bonus:** background combining 3-4 years in a quota-carrying sales or solutions role with 3-4 years in partnerships or alliances; prior experience at a company that successfully scaled through cloud marketplace and SI channels
