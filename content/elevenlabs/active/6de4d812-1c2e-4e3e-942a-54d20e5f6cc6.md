## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## **About the role**

We’re looking for an experienced, motivated Enterprise Account Executive to drive ElevenLabs’ growth across Media & Entertainment - including film and television studios, streaming platforms, gaming companies, publishers, sports and media organizations, and other global content businesses.

Media and entertainment companies create, localize, and distribute enormous volumes of content across formats, languages, and markets. Voice and audio are central to those experiences, creating a significant opportunity for AI to accelerate production, expand global reach, deepen audience engagement, and unlock new creative possibilities.

Our ideal candidate is passionate about the transformative potential of AI voice technology and eager to act as a strategic partner, helping leading media and entertainment companies reimagine how they create, localize, distribute, and monetize content.

## **In this role, you will**

* Build and manage a growing portfolio of enterprise Media & Entertainment accounts to help ElevenLabs achieve its revenue goals
* Identify opportunities where voice and audio AI can drive growth, creative innovation, automation, or cost efficiency across workflows - including dubbing and localization, voiceover, audiobooks, gaming characters, pre- and post-production, marketing content, accessibility, and personalized audience experiences
* Develop and maintain a deep understanding of the Media & Entertainment and generative AI landscapes, including customer priorities, competitive solutions, emerging technologies, evolving business models, and industry considerations around rights and responsible AI
* Demonstrate expertise around how ElevenLabs’ technology can create value across content production, localization, distribution, interactive experiences, creative workflows, and consumer-facing products
* Develop and execute account strategies across the full buying group - including Content, Production, Localization, Creative, Product, Technology, Marketing, Legal, and Procurement
* Navigate complex Media & Entertainment environments and identify opportunities to integrate ElevenLabs with existing production, localization, content-management, and distribution ecosystems
* Partner closely with Forward Deployed Engineers to design high-impact solutions, manage successful Proofs of Value, and establish a path to scaled production deployment
* Serve as a trusted advisor by educating customers on generative AI and voice while helping shape ElevenLabs’ Media & Entertainment playbook, including our ICP, messaging, use cases, and customer references

## **Requirements**

* 7+ years of quota-carrying enterprise sales experience in SaaS or technology, ideally with exposure to AI, generative AI, LLM-based products, or API-driven platforms
* Experience selling to Media & Entertainment companies - including film and television studios, streaming platforms, gaming companies, publishers, sports and media organizations, or other global content businesses
* An established network and strong relationships across the Media & Entertainment industry
* Proven success closing seven-figure deals and managing complex, multi-stakeholder sales cycles
* Experience selling to both technical buyers, such as Product, Engineering, IT, and Architecture, and business buyers across Content, Production, Localization, Creative, Marketing, and Operations
* Ability to understand a customer’s content strategy, production environment, and requirements for success, then translate ElevenLabs’ capabilities into solutions that deliver measurable creative and business value
* Proficiency navigating enterprise procurement, security, legal, rights-management, and technical validation processes, with the ability to operate autonomously and partner effectively across functions
* Strong executive presence, the ability to build relationships at the C-suite and senior leadership level, and a willingness to travel frequently for onsite customer meetings and industry events
* Comfort operating in an early-stage, high-growth environment where ambiguity is the norm and priorities evolve quickly
* Passion for voice and audio AI and its potential to transform content creation, localization, distribution, and audience experiences across Media & Entertainment
