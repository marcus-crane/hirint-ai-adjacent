## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

**About the role**

As our founding Sales Development Representative (SDR) based in Canada, at ElevenLabs you will build the outbound engine for one of our fastest-moving new markets, working closely with our Canadian Account Executives.

We don't just sell a product or custom-level solutions; we are partnering with enterprises to help them generate new experiences and monetization opportunities at scale with our breakthrough voice technology. In this role you will:

* Build the outbound motion for Canada from scratch — defining the target account list, the messaging that works in this market, and the AI-assisted workflows that power it.
* Run account-based outbound into a concentrated list of Canada's largest enterprises, multithreading across business units rather than working a high-volume lead list.
* Engage prospects through cold calling, email, and social outreach, using AI agents to handle enrichment, sequencing, and signal monitoring so your focus stays on the highest-value interactions.
* Generate pipeline from our in-market presence — Canadian tech events, hackathons, and community moments — turning the room into booked meetings.
* Work closely with the Account Executives and sales leadership to feed back what's landing, refine the playbook, and report on the KPIs that matter: conversion rates, lead quality, and outreach effectiveness.

**Requirements**

* Prior outbound B2B sales experience with a track record of securing new business meetings.
* Comfortable operating and iterating on AI-assisted workflows and automation tools (e.g. Clay, Apollo, Outreach, or similar) — you don't need to build from scratch, but you know how to run and tune a system.
* Comfortable being early. This is the first SDR seat in a market we're standing up from scratch — you'll be building the playbook, not inheriting one.
* Curiosity — asking insightful questions and developing solutions from a foundation of knowledge and insight.
* Self-motivated, thriving as part of a team while possessing the ability to stay driven and proactive independently.
* Excellent verbal and written communication skills in English. Professional French is strongly preferred given the Quebec market.

**Location**

This role is remote-first and can be executed from anywhere in Canada, with the ability to operate in ET required. There is a strong preference for candidates based in Toronto, where the majority of our Canadian team sits and where we're opening an office.
