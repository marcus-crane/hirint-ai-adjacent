## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

**About the role**

As a Sales Development Representative focused on Government and Public Sector accounts in the US, you will play a crucial role in building ElevenLabs’ government outbound strategy while working closely with the Government Lead and Account Executive. We do not just sell a product or custom solutions. We partner with government organizations to help modernize citizen engagement, automate services, and improve internal workflows through breakthrough voice and conversational AI. In this role you will:

* Conduct research to identify target agencies, departments, programs, and relevant stakeholders across federal, state, and local government, leveraging AI tools, agents, and automated workflows to move faster and at greater scale.
* Engage prospects through cold calling, email, social media, events, and other outbound channels to initiate conversations and generate qualified opportunities.
* Develop a strong understanding of government use cases, including citizen support, virtual agents, benefits administration, internal workflows, and public safety non-emergency response.
* Implement personalized outreach campaigns that reflect each agency’s priorities, procurement environment, and potential use cases.
* Use AI agents and automated workflows to support account research, stakeholder mapping, enrichment, sequencing, and signal monitoring, allowing you to focus on the highest-value interactions.
* Monitor relevant government buying signals, including budget cycles, policy initiatives, leadership changes, RFP activity, contract awards, and modernization priorities.
* Collaborate closely with the Government Lead and Account Executive to exchange insights on prospect feedback, objections, procurement considerations, and market trends.
* Continuously refine outreach strategies and the AI workflows powering them based on performance data and feedback from the market.
* Monitor and report key performance indicators related to outbound activity, qualified opportunity generation, conversion rates, lead quality, and pipeline contribution.
* Partner with sales leadership to analyze results, identify emerging opportunities, and optimize the government outbound strategy.

**Requirements**

* 3+ years of quota-carrying enterprise sales experience in SaaS or technology, ideally with exposure to AI, generative AI, LLM-based products, or API-driven platforms.
* Experience selling into government, public sector, or other highly regulated buyers — comfortable navigating RFPs, longer procurement cycles, budget/fiscal-year timing, and multi-layered legal/security review.
* Proven success closing deals and managing complex sales cycles with multiple stakeholders, including security, compliance, and legal reviewers.
* Deep understanding of enterprise (and ideally government) procurement and legal processes, with the ability to accelerate deal velocity where possible.
* Experience selling technical solutions to product, engineering, or IT leaders; ability to translate complex technology into business value for non-technical stakeholders.
* Strong executive presence and ability to build relationships at senior/agency-leadership level.
* Comfort operating in an early-stage, high-growth environment — this is a build-the-playbook role for a segment we're just scaling, not an inherited book of business.
