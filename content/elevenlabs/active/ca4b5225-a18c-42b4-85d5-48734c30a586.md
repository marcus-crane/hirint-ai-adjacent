## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## About the role

ElevenLabs is expanding globally, and some of our largest enterprise opportunities are won through complex procurement processes. As that motion grows, how we choose which opportunities to pursue, how we position ElevenLabs, and how consistently we execute becomes an increasingly important part of our enterprise strategy. We're building the RFP Engine to own this — a new function at the heart of how ElevenLabs competes for its most strategic deals all across the world. It will shape where we compete, how we differentiate, and how we bring sales, product, security, legal, and executive stakeholders together around a winning strategy.

As the founding leader, you'll define how ElevenLabs approaches major enterprise pursuits globally — with meaningful ownership over which opportunities we pursue, leading the strategy behind our most important responses, and building an AI-first engine that gets smarter with every pursuit: the processes, tooling, and intelligence that make the entire organization better at winning. You will lead a small global team on Day One and scale up the team over time.

We think the right leader could come from two paths: a technical pre-sales or solutions engineering background with deep experience driving strategic RFPs, or senior enterprise proposals/bid leadership with genuine technical fluency. Either way, you understand how complex deals are actually won — equally comfortable digging into technical requirements, shaping a differentiated commercial narrative, coordinating senior stakeholders, or presenting in a finalist session — and you have a builder's instinct: when you see work being repeated, you look for a way to systematize or automate it. You're interested not just in winning the pursuit in front of you, but in making ElevenLabs better equipped to win the next hundred.

## **Requirements**

* Substantial experience with strategic enterprise RFPs — whether from solutions engineering / pre-sales, or from leading an enterprise proposals or bid function at a technology company.
* A builder's approach to AI and automation: you use AI fluently in your own work, you've shipped internal tooling or workflows (or partnered closely with engineers to do so), and you treat repeated manual effort as a design flaw.
* An exceptional communicator in every direction — credible in front of enterprise customers and evaluators, persuasive on the page, and able to mobilize executives, SMEs, and cross-functional teams against hard deadlines.
* Strong commercial judgment: you can read how a deal will be evaluated, shape win strategy with sales teams, and say no to weak pursuits with clear rationale.
* The ambition to build and lead a global function, not just run a process.
