## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## About the role

ElevenMusic is our consumer-facing music creation platform, built for music creators and professional musicians alike. We want ElevenMusic to have a voice, a community, and a content presence that matches the ambition of the product.

We are looking for a Content Creator & Strategist to own that presence. You will make music with the platform every day, push it to its limits, and turn what you discover into educational content, tutorials, and social posts that teach creators what's possible. You will own the ElevenMusic social accounts, build and energise the community around the product, and represent ElevenMusic on stage at creator events.

This is a hands-on role at the intersection of music creation, content, and community. The right person is a genuine music maker who lives on social platforms, has strong opinions about how an AI music brand should show up, and is just as comfortable behind a mic or on stage as they are in an editing timeline. Because you'll be using the product harder than almost anyone, you'll also be a critical feedback loop into the engineering team, shaping the product itself.

## What you will do

* Create educational content and tutorial videos around ElevenMusic — recording, editing, and formatting them natively for each platform and publishing them across our socials
* Make music with ElevenMusic constantly, testing the platform to its limits and turning experiments into content that shows creators what's possible
* Provide structured, high-signal product feedback to the engineering team based on deep daily use, helping shape the roadmap
* Own and manage the ElevenMusic social accounts, from content calendar and strategy through to publishing, engagement, and analytics
* Help build and grow a community around ElevenMusic, sparking conversation, surfacing creator feedback, and championing standout work
* Speak at creator events, representing ElevenMusic through talks, demos, and workshops
* Plan and execute content around product launches and feature releases, coordinating with product, engineering, and marketing teams
* Stay ahead of trends in music creation, AI tools, and platform culture to find opportunities for the brand

## Requirements

* Demonstrated experience creating content, with a portfolio of work you can share
* Proven ability to produce educational content and tutorial videos end-to-end: recording, editing, adding text and motion, and formatting natively for platforms like X, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube
* Experience managing social media accounts for a technology, music, or creative tools company, including content calendars, copywriting, and analytics
* Comfortable presenting publicly — speaking at events, running demos or workshops, and being a visible face for the product
* A genuine understanding of the AI and music creation landscape — you use the tools, follow the space, and have informed opinions about where things are headed

## Bonus

* Experience managing social for a music-related product
* Experience running or growing a community channel such as Discord
* Experience with social scheduling and management tools (Sprout Social, Later, Buffer, or similar)
* Experience building a social presence from early stage or leading a rebrand on social

We believe in hiring the best talent in the world to make an impact in the right role at the right time. At ElevenLabs, strong performance creates opportunities for growth. Here are some of the career paths you can expect after excelling in this role:
