## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

About The Role  
We're scaling from 1,000 → 4,000 employees across 50+ countries, and the complexity of our teams, hubs, and workflows is growing quickly. Our People Partners play a critical role in enabling leads, supporting peeps, and protecting performance and culture as we grow. As a People Partner at ElevenLabs, you'll sit at the intersection of leadership coaching, performance, and day-to-day people guidance.

You'll support Engineering and Product leaders across our international teams, getting to know their rhythms, challenges, and talent needs. Much of the role is coaching: helping leads handle feedback, performance, growth, and change well, and surfacing people issues early. You'll work as part of the wider People team, with Employee Relations and People Ops alongside you on more complex cases.

This is a high-impact role for someone who enjoys coaching, can context-switch quickly, builds trust easily, and brings clarity and calm to fast-moving environments.

## **You will**

* Partner with Engineering and Product leaders across our international teams to support team health, performance, and talent density.
* Coach leads and managers through people challenges, including feedback, performance, conflict, growth conversations, and team change.
* Run proactive 1:1 sessions mainly with leads, and with peeps where needed, surfacing issues early.
* Contribute to organisational design work, advising on team structures, spans and layers, and capability gaps.
* Support leaders and teams through change, such as team moves and new ways of working.
* Support key People programs (performance reviews, engagement, recognition, and lead enablement) for Engineering and Product.
* Work with Employee Relations and, where required, Legal Counsel on early-stage ER issues, escalating complex cases.
* Spot patterns across teams and share them with the wider People team to shape new People initiatives.
* Work with People Ops & Compliance teams to support terminations, mobility, and local requirements in each country where needed.

## **Requirements**

* Experience supporting leaders and teams through people topics such as performance, coaching, growth, and team change.
* Strong coaching skills, with the confidence to guide managers through difficult conversations.
* Strong judgment and the ability to handle sensitive issues with discretion, clarity, and fairness.
* Ability to thrive in a high-velocity, ambiguous environment and make decisions with incomplete information.
* Comfortable with both partnership and hands-on execution when needed.
* Comfortable working with leaders across different timezones and cultures; experience supporting globally distributed or multi-jurisdiction teams is a strong plus.
* Experience supporting Engineering or Product teams.
