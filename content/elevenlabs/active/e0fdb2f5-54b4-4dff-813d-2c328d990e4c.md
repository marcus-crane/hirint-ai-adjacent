## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

**Chief of Staff | ElevenLabs**  
ElevenLabs is one of the fastest-growing AI companies in the world, valued at $11B and expanding aggressively in EMEA. We're looking for a Chief of Staff to work directly alongside our French GM, as we build one of the most exciting regional GTM teams in AI.  
  
**About the role**  
  
France has no dedicated revenue or growth operations function. You are it and you are also the GM's closest operating partner. Roughly:  
  
~50% — Build the engine

* **Revenue operating cadence.** Weekly pipeline inspection, monthly business review, QBR. You own that the inspection happens on time, that the data is clean before it reaches the call, that every number is reconstructable from source, and that actions are tracked to closure. Plus territory and capacity models, Salesforce hygiene, and the BigQuery behind everything we report to EMEA.
* **Scope line:** central Growth owns channels, brand, tooling standards and the global demand engine. France owns local signal definitions, target lists, event audiences, and local campaign execution. You work on their rails and make them better — you don't build a parallel stack.  
    
  ~30% — Leverage for the GM
* **Communications surface.** Board and EMEA updates, QBR content, all-hands material, exec briefings, key customer and partner correspondence.
* **Eyes and ears.** Surfacing signal early, closing loops, prepping the GM for the moments that matter, and making decisions stick after the meeting ends — including telling the GM when he's wrong.

~20% — Orphan projects

* Things that don't belong to any function yet: onboarding, enablement, partner attribution rules, an offsite, a public-sector referencing dossier. You take them, ship them, and hand them off.
* Running through all of it: automation before headcount. Every recurring manual process you inherit — list building, reporting packs, account research, QBR prep, handoffs — you replace with an agent, a skill, or a pipeline. The explicit goal is to run a 20-person GTM org on the ops overhead of a five-person one. This is not a side interest of the role. It is how we intend to win the market.  
    
  **Who you are**
* **You are genuinely AI-native, and you can prove it.** You've built agents, skills, or automations that other people now depend on. Be ready to answer precisely: what did you stop doing manually in the last 90 days, and what replaced it? We care about what you've shipped, not what you think about AI.
* **You are a builder in GTM tooling.** Clay or equivalent enrichment waterfalls, sequencing tools, Salesforce, SQL against a warehouse, APIs and webhooks glued together without waiting for engineering.
* **You produce decision-ready analysis yourself.** Modelling of quota, coverage, pipeline and consumption ramp. If your answer to "where did this number come from" is a screenshot, this isn't your role.
* **Enough enterprise GTM fluency to represent the GM credibly from day one.** Direct exposure to 6-12 month, procurement-heavy cycles: MEDDPICC or equivalent, security review, legal redlines, works council and DPA timelines.
* **Native-level French and fully fluent professional English.** Non-negotiable — you'll be in a French buying committee and writing for a global exec audience on the same day.
* **Low ego, high ownership.** You'll build a capacity model in the morning and clean CRM records in the afternoon. No task is beneath you.

**Location**

This role is remote-first, so it can be executed from anywhere in the EMEA region, however the ability to operate in CET and GMT timezones is required. There is a preference for candidates to be based in Paris, with the option to work out of our office.
