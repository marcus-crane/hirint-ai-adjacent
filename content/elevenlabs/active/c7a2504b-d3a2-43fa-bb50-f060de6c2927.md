## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

* **Excellence everywhere:** Everything we do should match the quality of our AI models.

# **The Role**

ElevenLabs is scaling globally at extraordinary speed, growing from 400 to 1,000+ by the end of 2026. We operate across 46+ countries, with a highly distributed workforce spanning researchers, engineers, GTM teams, operators, creatives, and frontier AI specialists. Compensation and total rewards are core to how we attract and retain the best builders in the world.

This environment is inherently complex. Different regulatory frameworks. Different talent markets. Different role archetypes. High volatility in frontier AI compensation benchmarks. That complexity is not a constraint, it’s the challenge.

We are looking for someone to own and evolve our global compensation and total rewards strategy, designing systems that are competitive in frontier AI markets, internally consistent, globally scalable, and built for long-term sustainability.

You will operate as the single point of accountability for compensation architecture across the company, partnering closely with Operations, Legal and Finance.

If you’re energized by solving hard global compensation problems, building clarity across diverse markets, and designing systems that scale globally without slowing the company down, this role is for you.

## Key Responsibilities

### Compensation Strategy & Philosophy

* Own and continuously refine ElevenLabs’ global compensation philosophy.
* Ensure pay, equity, and incentives reinforce attraction, retention, and performance.
* Balance market competitiveness with internal equity and financial discipline.
* Serve as a trusted advisor on complex, high-impact compensation decisions.

### Job Architecture & Leveling

* Design systems that introduce rigor without slowing the company down.
* Evolve our structure intentionally to match our scale: mature enough to support 46+ countries and a diverse set of roles, but not over-engineered or bureaucratic.
* Drive clarity and consistency in job architecture, leveling frameworks, and compensation bands globally.

### Equity & Long-Term Incentives

* Design equity programs that reflect a core ElevenLabs belief: employees should be genuine owners in the company and invested in our long-term ambition.
* Own our equity philosophy, structure, and grant strategy.
* Partner with Finance and Legal on equity design, refresh cycles, and promotion grants.

### Compensation Cycles & Governance

* Assist annual compensation reviews end-to-end.
* Design clear off-cycle governance and decision frameworks.
* Build calibration rigor and tooling that scales globally.
* Introduce AI-enabled workflows where appropriate to improve speed and consistency.
* Act as the final escalation point for leveling and pay consistency decisions.

### Benefits & Total Rewards

* Own global benefits strategy across a distributed workforce.
* Balance standardisation with regional relevance.
* Ensure benefits are competitive in frontier talent markets while remaining operationally scalable.

## What We’re Looking For

* 5+ years in Compensation or Total Rewards, ideally in high-growth technology companies that operate across multiple countries

* Experience designing and evolving job architectures and leveling systems
