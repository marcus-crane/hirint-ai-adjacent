We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

**About the role**

ElevenLabs is looking for a Head of Strategic Pursuits to lead our largest, most complex and most transformational enterprise deals around the world, from first executive conversation through signature and into deployment.

This is a global leadership role reporting directly to the Chief Revenue Officer. Strategic Pursuits is not traditional sales support. It is a specialized deal leadership function that partners with Account Executives to win opportunities that require significantly more executive engagement, creativity, and cross-functional coordination than a standard sales cycle. These are the deals that don't fit the playbook. They are structured in new ways, they involve multiple senior stakeholders on the customer side (and sometimes their boards and investors), and they pull in nearly every function at ElevenLabs to get done.

You will own the pursuit strategy for these opportunities. That means shaping the transformation narrative with the customer's C-suite, building the business case, designing the solution and the commercial model, running the negotiation, and bringing Sales, Product, Engineering, FDE, Finance, Legal, Partners, and our own executives together to move the deal forward and clear what's in the way. The AE continues to own the account and the relationship. Your job is to make sure we win the deal and that it's structured to set us and the customer up for long-term success.

The work doesn't stop at one deal. You will turn what we learn from each strategic pursuit into repeatable motions: playbooks the field can reuse, input into how we price and package, signal to Product on what our largest customers need, and clean handoffs into deployment so what we sold is what gets delivered.

This is not a sales operations, deal desk, or program management role, and it is not a quota-carrying territory role. We are looking for someone who has personally led and structured nine-figure-scale, multi-year enterprise deals, is comfortable going well outside the playbook to get them done, and has the executive presence to sit across from a Fortune 500 CEO. While initially a senior IC role, there is the potential to build out a team under this function.

**In this role you will**

* **Lead strategic deals.** Own the pursuit strategy for our largest and most complex enterprise opportunities, partnering with account teams from qualification through close. Help decide which pursuits warrant this level of investment and where your time goes.
* **Build executive engagement.** Develop C-suite relationships at our most important customers and prospects, shape transformation narratives, and create compelling business cases tied to measurable outcomes. Bring in the CRO, CEO, and other ElevenLabs executives at the right moments with a clear role for each.
* **Orchestrate cross-functional execution.** Bring together Sales, Product, Engineering, FDE, Finance, Legal, Partners, and executive sponsors to accelerate deal progression and remove blockers. Run the pursuit like a program, with a clear plan, owners, and a cadence, without becoming the bottleneck.
* **Structure and win complex transactions.** Drive competitive strategy, solution design, commercial models, negotiations, and creative deal structures (multi-year commitments, partnerships, co-development, platform and marketplace models, and others we haven't invented yet) to secure transformational agreements that are good for the customer and for ElevenLabs.
* **Work across senior stakeholders on both sides.** Navigate complex buying committees, procurement, legal, and in some cases boards and investors, and keep the deal moving when it gets stuck at the top.
* **Partner on global and strategic accounts.** Work closely with regional leaders and account teams on our most important global accounts, bringing a consistent strategy across regions and product lines.
* **Build repeatable winning motions.** Turn strategic deals into scalable playbooks, improve pricing and packaging, influence product priorities with evidence from the largest deals in the market, and ensure successful handoffs into deployment.
* **Raise the bar on deal craft across the org.** Coach AEs and regional leaders on executive selling, business case development, and deal structuring so the whole Revenue organization gets better at winning big.
* **Be the CRO's partner on big bets.** Bring a clear point of view on which deals to pursue, how to structure them, and when to walk away, and represent that view in leadership and executive forums.

**Requirements**

* **15+ years of relevant experience** in enterprise sales, strategic deals, or business development at technology companies, with a track record of leading and closing very large, complex, multi-year transactions.
* **Deep deal structuring experience.** You have personally designed and negotiated creative commercial structures and know how to make the economics work for both sides while protecting the business.
* **Executive presence.** You are credible with C-suite executives at the largest enterprises in the world and can hold the room alongside our CRO and CEO.
* **Comfort outside the playbook.** You thrive in deals that don't follow a standard process, and you know when to break the playbook and when to rely on it.
* **Cross-functional orchestration.** You can rally Product, Engineering, Finance, Legal, and executives around a deal when none of them report to you, and you do it by being prepared, decisive, and easy to work with.
* **Business case and value fluency.** You can build a credible, quantified business case tied to customer outcomes and defend it to a CFO.
* **Strong commercial judgment.** You understand pricing, margin, and long-term account economics, and you know the difference between a big deal and a good deal.
* **Technical fluency.** You understand AI and platform products well enough to shape solution design and hold your own with a customer's CTO.
* **Operator mentality.** You care about what happens after signature, and you make sure what we sold gets deployed successfully.
* **Ability to operate globally.** You are comfortable leading pursuits with teams and customers across AMER, EMEA, APAC, LATAM, and India.
* **Willingness to travel.** Must be willing and able to travel up to 40% of the time, often with customers' and ElevenLabs' executives.

**Bonus points**

* Experience building or leading a strategic pursuits, strategic deals, or global accounts function at a high-growth AI, cloud, or enterprise software company.
* Experience selling AI, voice, conversational AI, or platform/API products to large enterprises.
* Experience structuring partnerships, marketplace, or co-sell agreements alongside direct deals.
* Experience in a company where the product and the market move weekly and deals have to keep up.
