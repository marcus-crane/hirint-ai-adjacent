We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.

## About the role

We’re looking for an IT Support Engineer based out of our Warsaw office. This is a hybrid role - 4 days per week on-site and one day working from home.

### What You'll Do

* **Device lifecycle management**: assist with laptop procurement, setup, returns, and secure wiping
* **Office IT support**: provide on-hand support to our colleagues in our Warsaw office, as well as support with expansions and/or office buildouts
* **AV support**: ensure meeting rooms and AV equipment are running reliably
* **End-user support:** be the first point of contact for the team by logging, prioritising and resolving support tickets in a timely manner, keeping end users informed throughout.
* **Onboarding**: run IT onboarding calls for new joiners
* **New IT projects**: contribute to IT initiatives
* **Automation and tooling**: build automations and Slack bots to streamline repetitive work
* **Documentation**: write and maintain clear internal IT documentation and guides (fixes, processes and playbooks) to continue building out our internal knowledge base

### What you bring

* A friendly, end-user focused attitude. You genuinely enjoy helping people solve problems.
* Strong organisational skills, attention to details and a proactive mindset.
* Solid IT fundamentals (macOS, iOS and Android Operating systems, networking basics, SaaS tooling, AV systems etc.)
* Hands-on experience with an MDM platform (Jamf, Kandji, Fleet or similar)
* Experience with Okta (or similar IdP) and understanding of access management principles, SSO, MFA and user/group management.
* A willingness to learn new tools and technologies quickly
* Clear written and verbal communication skills with a knack for writing helpful documentation.

### Bonus

* Prior experience in a high growth / startup environment
* Experience in a customer or public-facing role
* Experience with Agentic AI tooling
* Familiarity with bash scripting
