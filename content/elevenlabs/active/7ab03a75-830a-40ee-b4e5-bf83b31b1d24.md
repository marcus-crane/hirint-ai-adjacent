## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## About the role

As part of the trust team, you'll collaborate across functions to design and implement systems that streamline trust workflows, enhance decision-making around risk, and support responsible growth. The team operates as strategic advisors to key cross-functional partners — including Product, Operations, Legal, Marketing, and Engineering — to drive accountability, insights, and operational integrity across the business.

In this role, you will be responsible for:

* **End-to-End Process Management** — Build and maintain strategic automations and standardized processes that support trust and safety functions, promoting consistency and adoption across the org.
* **Cross-Functional Collaboration** — Partner with Engineering and Growth teams to drive initiatives that enhance trust infrastructure and operational effectiveness.
* **Leadership Partnership** — Serve as a trusted advisor to leadership, delivering strategic insights, risk reporting, and actionable recommendations.
* **Data-Driven Strategy** — Oversee analytics across trust functions. Identify risk patterns and areas for improvement; build dashboards and insights that inform policy decisions and guide operational improvements.
* **Reporting & Analytics** — Lead the design and management of dashboards, reports, and KPIs that measure trust and safety performance and impact.

## **Who you are**

* Passionate about responsible AI and motivated by building systems that protect users and maintain platform integrity
* Highly motivated with a strong work ethic — you understand the stakes of this moment in AI and are committed to getting it right
* Analytical and first-principles oriented, comfortable navigating ambiguity and complex risk tradeoffs
* Consistently deliver high-quality work quickly and take initiative from day one

## **Requirements**

* Excellent communication skills and ability to translate complex risk data into clear recommendations
* 4+ years in Security, Trust & Safety Operations, or Risk Operations at a high-growth technology company
* Proven track record building end-to-end solutions, from data collection and analysis through to execution
* Experience working cross-functionally with Policy, Legal, or Safety teams
* Highly adaptable with a proactive approach to problem-solving and process improvement
