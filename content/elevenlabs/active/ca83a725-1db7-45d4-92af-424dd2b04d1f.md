## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

About the role

We are seeking an experienced and dynamic leader to build ElevenLabs' public sector business across Colombia, reporting to our LATAM Regional Lead. The Public Sector Partnerships Lead will identify and build relationships with government institutions across Colombia, bridging the gap between government and our GTM team to drive adoption of ElevenLabs' products and grow government into a strong, durable revenue stream for the company. The role also involves building strategic partnerships with policymakers, industry associations, and coalitions, as well as monitoring regulatory developments and supporting policy campaigns and impact initiatives.

In this role, you will:

**Drive government adoption and revenue (primary)**

* Identify government partnership opportunities across Colombia — from healthcare and education to transport, public banks, and other state-owned enterprises — and bridge the gap between government institutions and the GTM team to drive adoption and deployment of ElevenLabs' products.
* Build and maintain a wide network of relationships across Colombian government institutions — spanning ministries, agencies, public banks, and SOEs — not limited to regulatory or policy counterparts.
* Promote government adoption of ElevenLabs products and services in partnership with the GTM team.

**Build strategic partnerships (secondary)**

* Build and maintain strong relationships with policymakers, regulators, industry associations, and civil society groups.
* Represent ElevenLabs at conferences and industry events, advancing the company's public policy goals.

**Monitor and advise on policy (tertiary)**

* Lead ElevenLabs engagement with government bodies and officials across Colombia, with a focus on AI governance, safety, copyright, privacy, transparency, data protection, competition, and/or digital rights.
* Monitor, analyze, and advise on emerging regulations impacting the company.
* Support the development of internal policies by our Legal, Safety, Security, and other teams.
* Provide strategic counsel to company leadership on regional policy trends and their business implications.
* Define and implement policy campaigns and impact initiatives that advance company objectives.

What you bring:

**Primary requirements**

* 10-15 years' experience in public and private sector (in-house, firm) roles, with a track record of building relationships with government that led to commercial or technology-adoption outcomes — not solely policy or regulatory roles.
* A wide, current network of contacts across Colombian government institutions — spanning ministries, agencies, healthcare, education, transport, public banks and other SOEs. Prior government experience is a plus but not required if network breadth is strong; recency of network matters more than tenure, given how quickly LATAM administrations turn over.
* Experience identifying and opening doors for technology partnerships with government institutions is strongly preferred.
* Contacts or experience across other Spanish-speaking LATAM markets is a plus.
* Strong knowledge of the regional AI policy landscape, including general AI governance, safety, copyright, privacy, transparency, data protection, competition, and digital rights.
* Demonstrated success in designing, advocating for, and influencing policy outcomes, as well as coalition building.
* Public policy experience across Colombia.
* Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, including when briefing government officials and other policymakers.
* Fluency in English and Spanish.
