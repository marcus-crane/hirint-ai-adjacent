## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## About the role

As we grow across APAC, we're looking for an exceptional regional comms lead to build and protect our reputation across the continent. You'll raise ElevenLabs' profile, shape how we're understood, and run campaigns that demonstrate our value in major markets across the region - including India, Singapore, Japan, and Australia. You'll also help build the infrastructure for an elite APAC comms operation, acting as the connective tissue between global and regional teams and ensuring everyone is well-equipped to tell our story.

Responsibilities:

* As we grow across APAC, we're looking for an exceptional comms lead to build and protect our reputation across the region.
* Design and run creative, high engagement campaigns across media, social, and events
* Shape the regional narrative: developing our positioning, turning global news into local stories, and creating original local moments
* Spot reputational risks early and act to mitigate them
* Build relationships with journalists, influencers, customers, industry bodies, and event organisers
* Support executives with media interviews and speaking opportunities
* Partner across sales, marketing, policy, legal, and deployment – especially with the GMs and marketing leads who are spearheading our global expansion
* Define the operating model for comms in APAC, and manage agencies as required in specific markets.

## Requirements

We are looking for someone who has:

* Experience managing communications across multiple markets in APAC
* Strong relationships with journalists at top-tier publications, plus a demonstrable track record of pitching stories and handling difficult corporate issues
* A firm grasp of the political, regulatory, economic, and cultural landscape in the region
* A knack for unusually creative comms campaigns, including non-traditional tactics and channels
* Excellent written and verbal communication
* The ability to thrive in a fast-moving environment with nascent operating models
