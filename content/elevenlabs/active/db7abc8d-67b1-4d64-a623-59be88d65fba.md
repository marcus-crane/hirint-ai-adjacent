## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## About the role

You'll be responsible for:

* Developing and articulating our long-term vision for the future of human-AI interaction, and what we’re building for developers and businesses.
* Shaping the narrative around our products (in particular ElevenAgents), connecting our capabilities to emerging needs and trends in the wider world.
* Leading storytelling and communications for top tier product launches, in partnership with product marketing, and running campaigns between launches to demonstrate the value our platform delivers.
* Owning our security, privacy, and safety communications, including managing incident responses as part of a cross-functional team.
* Producing superb written materials that advance our product story – including keynotes, talk tracks, social posts, and press releases – and creating scrappy product demos to show our technology in action.
* Building the profile of our technical and product leadership, developing novel points of view, and supporting them with media, social, and event appearances.
* Nurturing relationships with journalists, Substack writers, podcasters, technical creators, and other influencers who shape developer and enterprise buying decisions.

## Requirements

We are looking for someone who has:

* Run product or technical comms at a fast-paced B2B technology company, or built AI / software products.
* A record of leading significant launches, and running effective product campaigns in between them.
* Exceptional writing across different formats, with a talent for turning complex, technical topics into sticky narratives.
* Experience working with security, safety, and engineering teams, and managing incidents.
* Deep understanding of the independent media landscape and good relationships with some of the most influential commentators.
* A track record of excellence, be that building products, running superb campaigns, or some other entrepreneurial endeavor.

## **Location**

This role is remote-first, so it can be executed from anywhere on the US East Coast, with the ability to operate in GMT-5 to GMT-4 timezones required. If you prefer, you can work from our New York office.
