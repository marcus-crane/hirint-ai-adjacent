## **About the role**

We are looking for a SIP Expert to own the telephony layer that connects ElevenAgents voice agents to the phone network. SIP traffic is moving from early adoption to large enterprise call volumes, so the hard problems are now reliability and latency at scale.

**Your general responsibilities will include:**

* Owning SIP end to end, from design doc to implementation, testing and ongoing operation, for a product used by enterprises at scale.
* Making call flows such as warm transfers dependable, and simplifying how our call services communicate with each other.
* Diagnosing and fixing load-related failures such as resource exhaustion and dropped connections.
* Collaborating with support, infrastructure and other teams to turn customer needs into shipped features.

**Specific responsibilities might include:**

* Making our SIP edge and routing layer production-ready for large enterprise traffic.
* Designing and shipping lightweight, lower-latency call-handling services.
* Supporting carrier and SIP trunk integrations for enterprise customers.

You will join a small team of five, reporting directly to the team lead, with a high degree of ownership and very little bureaucracy.

## **Requirements**

We do not require any formal experience, certifications, or degrees. Instead, we are seeking enthusiastic engineers who can showcase solving impressively hard problems with artifacts such as past projects, designs, or open source contributions.

**We do require:**

* Experience building and operating large-scale SIP/VoIP systems in production, including debugging load-related failures such as port exhaustion, dropped connections and state shared across services
* Hands-on experience running LiveKit (or comparable real-time media infrastructure) at scale, with comfort in WebSockets, WebRTC and RTP
* Strong backend engineering in Go or a comparable systems language, and a record of owning projects from design document through launch and maintenance
* Based in Europe or a nearby time zone, with at least 2 to 3 hours of daily overlap with CET

**Bonus:** Hands-on experience with Kamailio (or OpenSIPS/FreeSWITCH), or carrier and SIP trunk interconnects.  
  
**Location**

This role is remote-first, so it can be executed from anywhere in Europe or nearby time zones, with the ability to operate in GMT to GMT+4 time zones preferred. If you prefer, you can work from our offices in Dublin, London and Warsaw.
