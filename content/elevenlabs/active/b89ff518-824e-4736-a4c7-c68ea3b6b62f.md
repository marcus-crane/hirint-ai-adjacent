## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## **About the Role**

We’re launching dedicated Digital Natives coverage in North America, a new team built to serve companies whose products and business models are built around technology. These are cloud-first, developer-led organizations that build fast, scale fast, and evaluate vendors the way engineers do.

This is a named account role with a focused portfolio of the most strategic digital native companies in the region. You will be full-cycle - driving new business, as well as expansion inside organizations where the buyer is often a CTO, VP of Engineering, or Head of Product, and where adoption frequently starts with a developer and an API key before it becomes an enterprise relationship. Your job is to find that momentum, multi-thread across technical and business stakeholders, and turn it into durable, multi-product partnerships.

These aren’t transactional engagements. You’ll architect solutions that span across our ElevenAgents, ElevenCreative, and ElevenAPI platforms that require cross-functional collaboration to deliver measurable outcomes, and bring field intelligence directly into our product roadmap. You’re not just executing a playbook, you’re writing it.

## **In This Role You Will**

* Own and grow a focused portfolio of named digital-native accounts in North America, driving both net-new business and expansion
* Identify where our technology is already being used or evaluated inside an account and convert developer-led adoption into company-wide commitments
* Develop and execute multi-year account strategies across product lines, teams, and use cases
* Scope and close large ($xM+), complex deals, including multi-product, usage-based, and custom commercial structures
* Build deep, multi-threaded relationships across engineering, product, platform, and executive stakeholders
* Act as a strategic advisor on how AI voice and multi-modal agents fit into customers’ product roadmaps and architecture
* Partner cross-functionally with Solutions Engineering, Forward Deployed Engineering, Customer Success, and Partnerships to deliver and expand value
* Translate patterns from this segment into repeatable frameworks, offerings, and product insights that inform how we expand Digital Natives coverage to other regions and verticals
* Feed learnings from the field into the product roadmap and the broader strategic sales playbook
* Drive creative outbound and high-touch engagement strategies (onsites, technical deep dives, exec briefings, developer and founder events)

## **Requirements**

* 6-10+ years of quota-carrying experience with a focus on strategic or enterprise accounts
* Experience selling to technology companies, high-growth startups, or developer-led organizations, ideally API, infrastructure, or platform products
* Proven track record of closing and expanding seven-figure+ deals within named accounts
* Experience leading complex, multi-threaded sales cycles where engineering and product leaders are the primary decision-makers
* Strong ability to drive land-and-expand motions, including converting self-serve or usage-based adoption into enterprise agreements
* Experience scoping consultative, custom deals that go beyond standard SKUs
* Deep technical fluency, able to engage CTOs and engineering leaders credibly on APIs, integrations, latency, and architecture
* Executive presence with the ability to influence founders and C-suite stakeholders on long-term AI strategy
* Track record of translating customer needs into product feedback and roadmap influence
* Creative, self-driven approach to outbound and pipeline generation
* Comfortable operating in a high-growth, ambiguous environment, building a new coverage model from scratch
* Willingness to travel for customer engagements (onsites, QBRs, executive meetings)
* Strong bias toward action, ownership, and leveraging AI in day-to-day workflows
