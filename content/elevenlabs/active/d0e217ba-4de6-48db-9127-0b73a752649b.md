## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

**About the role**

We’re looking for a dynamic and experienced Event Manager to drive awareness, engagement, and lead generation for ElevenLabs through world-class event planning and execution. This role is critical to our marketing, sales, and partnership efforts, ensuring our presence at industry-leading events and creating memorable experiences that reinforce our brand in the AI space.

As Event Manager, you’ll own the end-to-end execution of events—designing strategies, securing sponsorships, managing logistics, and leading on-site activations. You’ll play a pivotal role in crafting high-impact events that strengthen relationships, spark meaningful conversations, and position ElevenLabs as a thought leader in AI.

**What You'll Do**

* Develop & Execute Event Strategy – Build and manage a portfolio of events, including major industry conferences, exclusive executive gatherings, and local meetups to drive brand awareness and lead generation.
* Maximize Event Impact & ROI – Define event tiers, identify sponsorship opportunities, and tailor event strategies to different audience segments to optimize reach and effectiveness.
* Lead Full Event Lifecycle – Oversee everything from planning and promotion to execution and post-event follow-up, ensuring seamless coordination and maximum engagement.
* Own On-Site Experience – Act as the primary point of contact during events, ensuring smooth operations, handling last-minute challenges, and representing ElevenLabs with confidence and professionalism.
* Collaborate Cross-Functionally – Work closely with marketing, sales, partnerships, and product teams to align event goals with business objectives and amplify impact.
* Analyze & Optimize Performance – Track key event metrics, gather insights, and continuously refine strategies to enhance future event success.

**Who you are**

We're looking for exceptional individuals who combine technical excellence with ethical awareness, who are excited by hard problems and motivated by human impact. You’ll strive with us if you:

* Are **passionate about audio AI** driven by a desire to make content universally accessible and breaking the frontiers of new tech.
* Are a **highly motivated and driven individual** with a strong work ethic. Our team is aware of this critical moment of audio AI evolution and is committed to going the extra mile to lead.
* Are **analytical, efficient, and strive on solving complex challenges** with a first principles mindset.
* Consistently **strive for excellence**, delivering high-quality work quickly and exceeding expectations.
* **Take initiative and work autonomously** from day one, prioritizing learning and contribution while leaving ego aside.

**What you bring**

* 3+ years of experience in event planning, marketing, or a related field, preferably within the tech or AI industry.
* Exceptional communication and organizational skills, with a talent for creating impactful experiences and seamlessly managing cross-functional collaboration.
* A passion for AI and emerging technologies, with an eagerness to represent ElevenLabs in the industry.
* Proven track record of planning and executing events that drive engagement, build relationships, and generate high-quality leads.
* Experience managing event budgets, negotiating contracts, and ensuring cost efficiency.
* Adaptability and resilience in a fast-paced, high-growth environment, juggling multiple projects and priorities with ease.
* Willingness to travel as needed to oversee event planning, execution, and on-site engagement.
