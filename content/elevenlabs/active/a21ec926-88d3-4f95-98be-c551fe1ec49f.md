## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## About the role

ElevenLabs is building the most advanced AI audio platform in the world. Millions of creators, developers, and businesses use our products, and organic search and our website are among the most important sources of new users and customers at the company.

That organic engine was built primarily for our Creative Platform and self-serve users. This role builds the B2B one.

We are looking for a B2B SEO/AEO Lead to own organic growth for the business buyer across everything we sell — our Agents platform, our developer and API products, and our enterprise offering. Agents is the most immediate opportunity and where you would start, but the mandate is the whole B2B surface: the low-volume, high-intent keywords business buyers actually search, the pages and content that capture them & our visibility inside ChatGPT, Gemini and other answer engines.

This is a hands-on, highly autonomous role. You will do your own keyword research, build and improve pages yourself, vibe code the tooling you need, write and direct content, and analyse your own impact.

The right person has done SEO for a B2B software company where the goal was pipeline rather than sessions. You are comfortable justifying a page with modest search volume because you know what those visitors are worth, and you are as comfortable with the AI search surface as with Google. You use AI as your primary means of production, and you have a quality bar high enough that it never shows.

## What you will do

* Own the low-volume, high-intent keyword universe for our B2B products — starting with Agents, then across API and enterprise
* Own AEO and the approach to measuring it effectively
* Build and ship pages yourself and with the help of growth engineers, including programmatic page sets across verticals, use cases and comparison intents
* Turn new product launches, use cases and customer stories into pages, FAQs and content, working directly with our product marketing managers
* Help direct the B2B half of our content calendar with our Growth Content Writer
* Build the reporting that makes B2B organic impact legible, and report it in pipeline

## Requirements

* Experience owning SEO for a B2B software company
* Strong keyword judgment at the bottom of the funnel
* Hands-on and independent: comfortable working with AI tools, vibe coding, improving existing tooling & shipping fast
* Real, current experience improving visibility in AI search
* Comfortable with content: you can write, brief and edit to a high standard

## Bonus

* Programmatic SEO at scale
* Experience marketing to both developers and business buyers
* Experience in voice AI, CX, contact centre, or developer tools categories
* Experience working alongside a B2B sales team and translating buyer conversations into content
* Multi-language and international SEO
* A portfolio of side projects, automations or programmatic sites you have launched
