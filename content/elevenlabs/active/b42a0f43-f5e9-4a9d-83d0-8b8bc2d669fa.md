## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

**About the role**  
We are looking for a passionate and results-driven **General Manager - Saudi Arabia** who will be responsible for developing and executing the overall strategy for the Saudi Arabia. As a key member of the leadership team, you will own the Saudi Arabia strategy & execution, build a world-class culture, and ensure our Middle East business aligns with and contributes to our overall business strategy and goals.

**Responsibilities**  
• **Revenue Growth Strategy & Targets**: Develop and execute a comprehensive revenue growth strategy to meet the company’s goals in the Saudi Arabia market.  
• **Market Analysis**: Conduct in-depth market research to identify growth opportunities and assess the competitive landscape.  
• **Lead Generation**: Devise strategies to generate high-quality leads and convert them into sales.  
• **Sales Pipeline Management**: Manage the sales pipeline to ensure a steady flow of opportunities and strong conversion rates.  
• **Client and Partner Relationships**: Build strong relationships with key clients and stakeholders to promote ongoing business.  
• **Brand Positioning**: Ensure that the ElevenLabs brand is effectively positioned in the Saudi Arabia market to attract and retain clients.  
• **Team Building**: Recruit, train, and manage a high-performing sales and business development team.  
• **Performance Management**: Implement performance metrics and conduct regular reviews to ensure the team meets or exceeds targets.  
• **Process Optimization**: Identify and implement process improvements to enhance sales efficiency and effectiveness.
