## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## **About the role**

ElevenMusic is a consumer music app where music is the core product: a place to listen, create, remix and stream. The team works in its own environment, separate from the main org, and is agent-first with fast CI, so ideas reach users within a day or two.

You’ll own features end to end, from spotting the problem to shipping the product and the backend behind it. Most of what you build is new, in a space where few companies are working with AI and music, so there’s no playbook. You’ll define the problem yourself, test ideas with real users, and work alongside research engineering to turn model capabilities into things people love using. What you ship goes live to a large audience within days.

You’re a builder with strong product taste who understands every layer of the system. You use AI to move fast, but you own and understand everything you ship. You care about music and the people who make it.

**Requirements**

* Have shipped and owned a well-designed consumer product from zero to real users, across frontend and backend
* Understand your systems deeply enough to spot immediately when a PR is wrong, insecure or needlessly complex
* Show excellent product judgement: you know the most valuable thing to build, and what not to build
* Work autonomously with minimal direction, and take responsibility for fixing problems as you find them
* Have a real interest in music, or in the people who make it

**Bonus:** Open-source or GitHub track record, experience building music or audio products, or experience with recommendation systems or embeddings.

**Location**

This role is remote-first, so it can be executed from anywhere in Europe and the US East Coast, with the ability to operate in GMT-5 to GMT+1 timezones required. If you prefer, you can work from our offices in London, New York, Dublin, Amsterdam, and Warsaw.
