## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## How we work

* **High-velocity:** Rapid experimentation, lean autonomous teams, and minimal bureaucracy.
* **Impact not job titles:** We don’t have job titles. Instead, it’s about the impact you have. No task is above or beneath you.

* **Excellence everywhere:** Everything we do should match the quality of our AI models.
* **Global team:** We prioritize your talent, not your location.

**About the role** We're looking for an experienced, motivated Enterprise Account Executive to drive ElevenLabs' growth across Financial Services: the banks, insurers, lenders, and payments companies people trust with their money, their families, and their futures. These institutions win or lose on trust and service at massive scale, under some of the most demanding regulatory standards anywhere. Their customers increasingly expect to talk, not type, whether they're checking a balance, disputing a charge, filing a claim, or asking about a loan.

Our ideal candidate is passionate about the transformative possibilities of AI voice technology and eager to act as a strategic partner, helping Fortune 500 and large-scale financial institutions reimagine how they serve, sell to, and sound to their customers, safely and at scale.

**In this role you will:**

* Build and manage a growing portfolio of new accounts across banking, insurance, lending and payments, and wealth and asset management to help ElevenLabs meet its revenue goals
* Identify opportunities where conversational AI can drive growth, automation, or cost efficiency in both customer-facing and internal agentic workflows. Our public use cases include account inquiries, payment support, fraud triage, loan origination, claims processing, collections, and compliance verification. Others include caller verification, onboarding, policy servicing and renewals, contact center coaching, and brand voice across marketing and localization
* Develop and maintain a deep understanding of the conversational AI landscape, including customer use cases, competitive solutions, build-vs-buy dynamics, and emerging trends in AI governance and model risk
* Demonstrate expertise, or a strong willingness to learn, about how ElevenLabs' voice technology unlocks value across customer support, virtual agents, in-app assistants, and creative content
* Develop and execute account strategies across the whole buying group (CX and Contact Center Operations, Digital and Product, Technology, and Risk, Compliance, Legal, and InfoSec), landing with one high-impact use case and expanding across lines of business, brands, and markets
* Partner closely with Forward Deployed Engineers to solution high-impact use cases, manage a successful Proof of Value with end customers, guide them through model-risk, security, and architecture review, and set them up to scale successfully in production
* Serve as a trusted advisor, educating clients on generative AI and voice, and help shape the Financial Services playbook: ICP, messaging, and references

**Requirements**

* 7+ years of quota-carrying enterprise sales experience in SaaS or technology, ideally with exposure to AI, generative AI, LLM-based products, or API-driven platforms
* Experience selling to financial services institutions (banks, insurers, lenders, fintech and payments) is a strong plus
* Proven success closing seven-figure deals and managing complex, multi-stakeholder sales cycles
* Experience selling to both technical buyers (product and engineering) and business buyers (CX, operations, digital), and to risk and compliance gatekeepers, translating complex technology into business value
* Adept at deeply understanding a customer's business and unique requirements for success, and translating ElevenLabs' capabilities into solutions that deliver against their needs
* Proficient in enterprise procurement, security review, and legal processes, including regulated-industry vendor risk, model risk, and data protection reviews, with the ability to work autonomously and effectively as a cross-functional partner
* Strong executive presence, the ability to build relationships at the C-suite and board level, and willingness to travel frequently and proactively for onsite client visits and conferences
* Comfort in an early-stage, high-growth environment, where ambiguity is the norm and the state of play changes rapidly
* Passion for voice and audio AI and how it can unlock transformative value for customers

This role is remote-first, so it can be executed from anywhere in the United States, however the ability to operate in EST, CST, or PST timezones is required. There is a preference for candidates to be based in New York or San Francisco, with the option to work out of our office.
