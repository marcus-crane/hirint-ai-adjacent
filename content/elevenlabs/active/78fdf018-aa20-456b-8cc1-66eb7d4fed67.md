## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

# About the role

Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) are among the most important hires we make. They embed with our largest customers, build and ship voice and agent solutions in production, and shape what we build next. Demand from Latin America is growing quickly, and we need FDEs on the ground who understand these markets, languages, and customers.

This is a high-impact role for someone who knows the LATAM engineering talent landscape, can source creatively, and can close people who are choosing between several offers, including from global tech companies hiring remotely.

# **You will**

* Own end-to-end recruiting for Forward Deployed Engineers in LATAM: intake, sourcing, screening, interview coordination, closing, and onboarding handoff.
* Partner with Engineering, Deployment, and LATAM Revenue leaders to define role profiles, scorecards, and interview plans, and keep calibration tight as the role evolves.
* Assess technical depth and customer-facing judgment in screens, and give hiring managers clear, honest signal on every candidate.
* Track pipeline health and market insights in Ashby, and turn them into recommendations on compensation positioning, hiring hubs, and conversion.

# Requirements

* Track record of full-cycle recruiting for engineering roles, ideally technical customer-facing roles such as forward deployed, solutions, or sales engineering, with a strong network or sourcing track record in LATAM.
* Strong technical fluency: you can tell a strong builder from a polished résumé, and hold a credible conversation about APIs, real-time systems, and AI/ML products.
* Proven ability to source and close passive, in-demand candidates in competitive markets, including early-career talent and senior specialists.
* Fluent in English and Spanish, with working knowledge of local hiring practices, compensation norms, and employment models across LATAM (for example, Brazil and Mexico).
* Comfortable in a high-velocity, ambiguous environment, balancing strategic partnership with hands-on execution.

**Bonus:**

* Experience hiring for AI or voice companies or working in a fast-scaling company in LATAM.
* Experience using our ATS, Ashby.
