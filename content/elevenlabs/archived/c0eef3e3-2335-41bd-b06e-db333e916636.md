## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## **About the role**

First impressions matter - especially at a company growing as fast as ElevenLabs. As our dedicated onboarding owner, you'll be responsible for the end-to-end new joiner experience globally: from the moment an offer is signed to the point a new hire is fully ramped and connected to the company. You'll partner across People, Talent, Finance, IT, and the broader business to design a globally consistent onboarding journey that reflects who we are - high-velocity, ambitious, and caring. This is a systems-building role: you'll create the infrastructure that ensures every new joiner, in every timezone, hits the ground running.

* Own the global onboarding program end-to-end - pre-boarding communications, first day experience, first-30/60/90 day frameworks and onboarding feedback.
* Partner with People Operations & IT to ensure all operational onboarding steps (system access, equipment, compliance documentation) are completed seamlessly across every region.
* Collaborate with Leads to build team-specific onboarding guides that complement the company-wide program.
* Design and maintain onboarding materials - welcome tasks, wiki documentation, Leads resources - that stay current as the company evolves.
* Track and analyze new joiner feedback to continuously improve time-to-productivity and early engagement metrics.

## **Requirements**

We do not require any formal experience, certifications or degrees. Instead we are seeking individuals who can demonstrate impact in fast-moving environments. We do require:

* Demonstrated experience owning or significantly contributing to onboarding programs at a fast-growing company, ideally with a global or multi-regional workforce.
* Strong operational instincts - you enjoy building processes that scale, not just one-off experiences.
* Highly organised with excellent attention to detail; you can manage multiple simultaneous new joiner cohorts without anything slipping.
* Collaborative and empathetic communicator - you understand that great onboarding is as much about how people feel as what they know.
