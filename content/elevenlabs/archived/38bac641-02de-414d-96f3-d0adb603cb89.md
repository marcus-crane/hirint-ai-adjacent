## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $22B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## **About the role**

Music is becoming a first-class part of ElevenLabs Creative. You’ll own the UI that lets creators make it, working closely with Research to turn new music models into products people use the next day.

The open question is what the best interface for AI music creation looks like. Creators come for video, dubbing or ads, and music is part of that flow. Making a song from scratch, scoring a video and localising a track into other languages each need a different flow. You’ll form a view on the right experience, build it end to end, and make it easy to adopt each new model as it lands.

You’ll take an ambiguous problem, decompose it, and carry it through legal, design, engineering and launch with Growth. You’ll work in a small, high-ownership team across product, research and go-to-market.

**Requirements**

* Strong Frontend experience: you’ve shipped complex, production UIs (React/TypeScript or equivalent) and are comfortable on the backend when the work needs it
* You’ve owned ambiguous, zero-to-one product work from problem definition to launch, without a PM or a prescriptive backlog
* You’ve worked closely with designers (Figma) and have taste and opinions about UX
* You’ve worked across functions such as legal, safety, growth and design to ship, and keep stakeholders updated without prompting
* You weigh trade-offs and make pragmatic decisions with no single right answer, and build so that new models can be swapped in as they land

**Bonus**

* Music, audio or creative-tools experience, or music as a hobby (singing, an instrument, production)
* Experience building media-heavy interfaces such as audio, video or timeline editors
* Experience working with ML models or research teams
