## About ElevenLabs

ElevenLabs is an AI research and product company transforming how we interact with technology.

We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.  
  
We have expanded from voice into three main platforms:

* ElevenAgents enables businesses to deliver seamless and intelligent customer experiences, with the integrations, testing, monitoring, and reliability necessary to deploy voice and chat agents at scale.
* ElevenCreative empowers creators and marketers to generate and edit speech, music, image, and video across 70+ languages.
* ElevenAPI gives developers access to our leading AI audio foundational models.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## **Chief of Staff, Middle East | ElevenLabs**

ElevenLabs is one of the fastest-growing AI companies in the world, valued at $11B and expanding aggressively across Middle East. We're looking for a Chief of Staff to work directly alongside our GM of Middle East.

**About the role**

This is a high-trust, high-impact role for someone who combines sharp strategic thinking with hands-on execution. You'll be the operational backbone and strategic partner to the GM — driving revenue operations, keeping the team accountable to process, coordinating across some of the world's most complex enterprise relationships, and making sure nothing slips as we scale fast across a multi-market region. No job is too small. No task is beneath you. The upside is significant.

## **What you'll do**

* Partner directly with the GM on strategy, revenue operations, and regional planning
* Own forecasting, pipeline hygiene, and capacity planning across the Middle East team
* Support and coordinate on complex deal execution
* Sit in on AE interviews, customer meetings, representing the GM when needed
* Manage complex calendars, cross-timezone coordination and projects
* Keep the team aligned on company processes and following through on commitments
* Identify and solve operational problems before they escalate
* Use AI tools to automate, streamline, and multiply your own impact
* Handle sensitive matters with discretion and act as a trusted partner across the org

## **Who you are**

* Low ego, high ownership, no task beneath you
* Smart, structured, and practical with genuine commercial instincts
* Comfortable in front of customers and equally comfortable in a spreadsheet or Salesforce dashboard
* Passionate about AI and excited to be at the center of a category-defining company
* Based in Singapore, same timezone as the team.
* **Prior experience in consulting**, **investment/private banking**, RevOps or a high-growth startup GTM environment
* **Fluent in English** (written and spoken); proficiency in **Arabic strongly preferred**

## **What you get**

Ground floor opportunity on a regional team being constructed from scratch. Direct access to senior leadership. Real ownership from day one. The chance to help define ElevenLabs' trajectory across the world's fastest-growing AI markets and equity in a company that's just getting started in Middle East
