We launched in January 2023 with the first human-like AI voice model. Today, we serve millions of users and thousands of businesses - from fast-growing startups to large enterprises like Deutsche Telekom and Meta. Our investors are some of the world's most prominent, including Andreessen Horowitz, ICONIQ Growth and Sequoia. We've raised $781M in funding and our last valuation was $11B - multiples of 11, always.

Everything we do is the result of the creativity and commitment of our team - builders doing the best work of their lives. We are researchers, engineers, and operators. IOI medalists and ex-founders. If you want to work hard and create lasting positive impact, we want to hear from you.

## About the role

We're growing fast, and our people processes and systems need to keep pace. We're looking for a People Operations Lead to help build and own the systems, data, and processes that power the employee lifecycle - from the moment someone joins to every touchpoint after.

Further, you'll be the owner of our HRIS: configuration, data integrity, integrations, and reporting. You'll design lean, AI powered and well-documented processes for onboarding, employee changes, and offboarding that scale globally without adding bureaucracy. And you'll build the reporting layer that gives leaders accurate, real-time answers on headcount, cost, and workforce trends.

This is a hands-on, builder role, supported by a small team of People Operations specialists. You'll be evaluating and implementing case management tooling, cleaning up data models, and using AI to automate manual HR work wherever possible.

## Requirements

* Deep hands-on experience building and scaling an AI powered global People Operations team and processes across multiple countries and jurisdictions.
* HRIS and case management tool experience (e.g. Rippling, Workday, BambooHR, HiBob, ServiceNow) including configuration, integrations, and data governance
* Experience in partnering with Deel who acts as our EOR provider
* Strong analytical skills - comfortable turning messy people data into reliable reporting and insight
* Experience identifying and implementing process or tooling improvements, including applying AI to reduce manual People Operations work
* Sound judgment on data privacy, employment compliance, and payroll governance across global jurisdictions
