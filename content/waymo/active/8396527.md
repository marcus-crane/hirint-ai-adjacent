Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

Hardware Engineering is an innovative and collaborative group of electrical, mechanical, reliability, software and vehicle engineers. We design, build, and perfect the products which are the eyes and ears of Waymo's autonomous driving technology, and integrate those products into vehicle platforms. We're seeking curious and talented teammates to keep us moving in the right direction.

Hardware Engineering designs, builds, and integrates the custom self-driving hardware (SDS), sensors, and base-vehicle systems that power the Waymo Driver. As Director, Software Engineering in Hardware, you will lead Software Engineering in this organization—spanning Embedded Software (ESWE), Sensor Software (lidar, radar, camera), Motion Control, Pose/Positioning, and ESWE/HIL Simulation, and the broader Hardware SWE community. You will drive the development, integration & platformization of our hardware-software stack across multiple vehicle platforms (Jaguar I-Pace, Zeekr Ojai, Hyundai Ioniq 5, and future programs), multiple generations of SDS Hardware and Sensors, and for multiple product lines.

This role follows a hybrid work schedule and reports to the Senior Director of Software Engineering.

**You will:**

* Lead, scale, and mentor the Software Engineering organization within Hardware—spanning Embedded Software (ESWE), Sensor Software (lidar, radar, camera), Motion Control, Pose/Positioning, and ESWE/HIL Simulation, and the broader Hardware SWE community.
* Drive the architectural evolution to a platformized hardware-software stack, decoupling base-vehicle interfaces, compute/sensor kits, and product-specific behaviors.
* Own full-lifecycle delivery, maintenance, and production quality of vehicle and sensor software systems—spanning embedded systems, sensor firmware & processing pipelines, base-vehicle integration, motion control, pose, simulation infrastructure, and OTA firmware transitions, including close analysis of field performance and rapid engineering reactions to real-world discoveries.
* Partner across Hardware Systems, Electrical/Mechanical Engineering, Base Vehicle OEMs, and Onboard Software to establish clean interfaces and left-shift validation onto HIL/Car-on-Bench simulation.
* Champion modern software engineering velocity, safety- rigor, and AI enablement, scaling agentic AI workflows across development, testing, field analysis, and triage.
* Represent Hardware Software in executive reviews, and to external partners such as OEMs and Tier1's.

**You have:**

* BS, MS, or PhD in Computer Science, Computer/Electrical Engineering, Robotics, or equivalent practical experience.
* 15+ years of software engineering experience, including 8+ years leading multi-team engineering organizations (managers, TLMs, and senior/staff ICs) building complex hardware-software systems.
* Deep technical grounding across embedded systems, Linux/RTOS, automotive networking (Ethernet, CAN, FlexRay), and modern C++ production software.
* Proven track record of architecting and shipping platformized software abstractions across multiple hardware generations or vehicle programs from bring-up to scale.
* Strong executive presence and cross-functional influence bridging software, electrical/mechanical hardware, systems engineering, and external OEM/Tier-1 partners.
* Proven track record of delivering and maintaining high-reliability, high-stakes production systems through the full lifecycle, including close analysis of field performance, fleet-scale diagnostics, rapid reactions to production discoveries, fault mitigation, and OTA software/firmware transitions.

**We prefer:**

* Advanced degree (MS/PhD) in Computer Science, Electrical/Computer Engineering, or Robotics.
* Prior executive or senior engineering leadership in autonomous vehicles, software-defined EVs/automotive (zonal architectures, body/cabin controls), aerospace, or robotics.
* Hands-on familiarity with vehicle motion control (propulsion, braking, steering), localization/pose estimation, sensor calibration/synchronization, or manufacturing test software.
* Experience scaling Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL), Car-on-Bench (COB), and Software-in-the-Loop (SIL) simulation, CI/CD pipelines, and AI enablement / AI-assisted engineering workflows.
* Track record of transforming vertically integrated stacks into modular platform architectures across highly complex engineering architectures & organizations.
* Experience with autonomous vehicle or robotics sensor technologies—including lidar, radar, camera, and associated sensor firmware, signal/image processing, calibration, time synchronization, and driver stacks.

**Travel:**

Based in Mountain View, CA (Hybrid), with up to ~5% domestic and international travel to OEM partners, manufacturing facilities, and deployment depots.
