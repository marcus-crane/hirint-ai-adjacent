Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

Waymo is rapidly scaling its driving technology to new cities and geographies. A continual focus on operational efficiency, compliance, and safety is critical to this mission. The Driver Refinement & Validation organization builds the technology and tools to rapidly discover, iterate, and mitigate complex vehicle behaviors while scaling performance in a data-driven way.

The PUDOs (Pick-up/Drop-off) team is directly responsible for perfecting how our fully autonomous car executes pullovers—improving key driving scenarios like passenger pick-ups and drop-offs, parking, and deliveries. As we expand, our goal is to build the advanced signals and software necessary to seamlessly scale the Waymo Driver's performance across these critical real-world touchpoints in every new market.

In this role, you will report to a Technical Lead Manager.

**You will:**

* Work closely with Technical Leads  and Technical Lead Managers  in the org to design and implement horizontal solutions to solve behavioral driving across PUDOs (pick up & drop off)
* Build quantitative evaluation techniques of on-road performance to drive data-driven development
* Work closely with partners across Product and the Onboard stack
* Provide mentorship for junior engineers on the team
* Develop foundational frameworks that help advancement of the Waymo Driver's capabilities
* Directly contribute to solving some of the most challenging driving situations in the long tail

**You have:**

* PhD, Masters, or Bachelors degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Robotics
* 8+ years of Software Engineering experience in complex systems
* Evaluation experience, contributing to scalable eval workflows and building metrics for ML models and analyzing large datasets
* High quality API design with an eye towards evaluation and data-driven techniques
* Experience with working and solving design problems that span multiple components
* Ability to collaborate within and across teams
* Two or more years of management experience

**We prefer:**

* AV or Robotics experience
* Experience with Machine Learning models in production systems
* Coding proficiency in C++, Python, and SQL
