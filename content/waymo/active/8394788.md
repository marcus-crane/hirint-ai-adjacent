Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

Software Engineering builds the brains of Waymo's fully autonomous driving technology. Our software allows the Waymo Driver to perceive the world around it, make the right decision for every situation, and deliver people safely to their destinations. We think deeply and solve complex technical challenges in areas like robotics, perception, decision-making and deep learning, while collaborating with hardware and systems engineers. If you’re a software engineer or researcher who’s curious and passionate about Level 4 autonomous driving, we'd like to meet you.

The Waymo Simulator is a massive, load-bearing distributed system that acts as the final gatekeeper for our technology, evaluating the Waymo Driver and proving its road-readiness. As a Senior Engineer on the Simulint team, you will help tackle an infinitely large, ambiguous challenge:  ensuring the simulation environment is indistinguishable to the Waymo Driver from the real world. You’ll architect the validation pipelines, automated integration test layers, and agentic triage loops that protect this investment, so that every simulation produces usable data. This is a front-line systems role where you will have the autonomy to solve problems with no answer sheet, harden code at a company-wide scale, and directly accelerate our path to fully automated, high-velocity releases.

**You will:**

* Architect release reliability: Design, build, and operationalize multi-stage gated release processes that shift left on regression detection and prevent bad simulations from ever reaching production.
* Own the SIT layer: Lead the strategy and execution of the Simulator Integration Test (SIT) framework, catching breaking changes at presubmit and protecting the simulator platform at release time.
* Optimize test suites at scale: Continuously measure and improve precision, recall, execution cost, and latency across massive distributed test suites.
* Pioneer agentic automation: Drive our transition from human-led triage to intelligent, agentic loops by automating failure monitoring, bug disposition, and test gap coverage.
* Partner cross-functionally: Collaborate closely with simulation customers and engineering teams across Waymo to translate ambiguous system needs into structured, dependable delivery roadmaps.
* Harden systems and eliminate toil: Identify systemic reliability bottlenecks, champion code hardening and error handling, and dramatically reduce operational on-call toil.

**You have:**

* BS, MS, or equivalent practical experience in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
* Domain Experience: 5+ years of professional software engineering experience focusing on release engineering, CI/CD pipelines, backend infrastructure, or automated testing of complex software systems.
* Proactive Hardening & Failure Prediction: Demonstrated ability to anticipate and predict failure modes—proactively evaluating "what if this happens?"—and building robust protections and guardrails before issues occur.
* Technical Breadth: Hands-on proficiency in at least one modern backend or infrastructure language (e.g., Python, Java, or C++) with strong debugging and system-hardening capabilities.
* Problem Solving: Track record of taking ambiguous, open-ended reliability challenges and decomposing them into clean, tractable engineering solutions.

**We prefer:**

* Working knowledge of or familiarity with C++ to analyze and debug simulation engine or physics failures.
* Experience with cloud-scale infrastructure, distributed computing, or large-scale data processing pipelines.
* Production Validation & Monitoring: Proven experience designing, maintaining, and scaling automated build, validation, and gated deployment pipelines in enterprise distributed environments, including testing and monitoring systems in production.
* Familiarity or experience leveraging LLMs and agentic workflows to accelerate developer productivity, triage bugs, or automate root-cause analysis.
