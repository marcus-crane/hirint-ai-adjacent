Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

Waymo is an *autonomous driving* technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. The TaaS (Transportation as a Service) team is responsible for building an amazing user experience and business around the Waymo Driver.

The Trip platform team is a part of TaaS, and focuses on:

* Waymo's core trip platform to create and manage trip life-cycles supporting high-growth use cases like ride-hailing and delivery
* Waymo’s product integrations with third-party B2B partners across ride-hailing and delivery to support our monetization and growth goals.

You will:

* Architect and scale Waymo's core trip platform to support multiple high-growth use cases, including ride-hailing and delivery, across both 1st-party and 3rd-party B2B partners' needs, defining technical direction across teams and markets.
* Architect and ensure high reliability and low latency for partner-facing API services across customer-hailing scenarios, including managing trip requests, calculating pickup and ride times, and calculating the price of rides.
* Establish strategic technical partnerships with external partners and cross-functional teams to co-design extensible APIs and jointly build great Waymo experiences on 3rd party platforms.
* Drive technical standards and mentor engineers while leveraging Google technologies, like OnePlatform API, Borg, Spanner, Flume, and custom-built systems, for our specific problems.
* Adopt and develop forward looking AI-native practices for yourself and the team (agentic development loops, agents in production). Develop infrastructure, processes and guardrails to accelerate the impact of AI.

You have:

* BS degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience.
* 8+ years of experience programming in back-end coding languages.
* Demonstrated ability to architect and build large-scale back-end distributed systems and APIs.
* Experience driving technical strategy, partnering on product road-maps and mentoring junior engineers.

We prefer:

* Experience in C++.
* MS in Computer Science, or equivalent practical experience.
* 10+ years of experience programming in back-end coding languages.
* Experience in developing low latency, large-scale, user facing distributed systems.
* Domain knowledge across the transportation, mobility, and/or autonomous vehicle industries.
