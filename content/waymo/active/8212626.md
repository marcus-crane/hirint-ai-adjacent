Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

Driver Refinement and Validation’s (DRV) mission is to build upon the high-quality ML Driver developed by Driver Intelligence, refining its performance to improve autonomous driving in specific, complex real-world contexts and problem areas (e.g., Ride-Hailing contexts). It aims to ensure that the Waymo Driver always computes safe, compliant, comfortable, and useful plans. As a Machine Learning Engineer in DRV, you will bridge the gap between cutting-edge machine learning and real-world vehicle performance, engineering the validation and onboard systems that ensure the autonomous vehicle reliably executes intelligent and predictable maneuvers.

**You will:**

* Own ML features for Scene Understanding (traffic controls, signs, special regions such as RRX and flood zones, lane states) end to end: data → model → eval → onboard launch
* Mine and curate large-scale driving data (real and simulated) to find long-tail failures, build training and eval sets, and design labeling and supervision
* Design, train, and iterate on model tasks and heads in large multi-task models. Improve calibration, robustness, and generalization to new geographies
* Build rigorous evaluation: PRT metrics, slicing taxonomies, side-by-sides, and staging launches, and turn the results into launch decisions
* Integrate models into the onboard stack (C++), combining learned outputs with safe heuristic fallbacks, Mendel-guarded rollouts, and map-contradiction handling
* Triage real driving events, find the root cause anywhere in the stack, and propose surgical or systemic fixes. Work closely with Perception, Reasoning, Mapping, and ML Infrastructure teams

**You have:**

* BS/MS/PhD in CS, EE, Robotics, ML, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
* Hands-on experience shipping ML models to production, including training, offline evaluation, and deployment (L4: 2+ yrs; L5: 4+ yrs, or a PhD plus 2+ yrs)
* Strong data mining / data engineering: building large-scale batch pipelines (Beam/Flume/Spark or similar) and analytical SQL to find, slice, and curate data
* Proficiency in Python with a modern DL framework (JAX, TensorFlow, or PyTorch) and production C++
* Demonstrated cross-stack generalist work: has owned problems that crossed data, model, and systems/serving boundaries
* Rigorous evaluation instincts: metric design, error analysis and slicing, and A/B or staged rollout

**We prefer:**

* AV/Robotics experience, especially perception (detection, classification, tracking)
* Experience with transformer or temporal models (GRU/RNN), multi-task learning, and model calibration/uncertainty
* Labeling-pipeline and supervision design; autolabeling or synthetic data
* TPU/accelerator performance work; exporting models for onboard/edge inference
* Builds internal tools (visualization, triage, experiment tracking) that accelerate the team
