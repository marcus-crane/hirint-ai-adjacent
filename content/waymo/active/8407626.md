Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

**You will:**

Design and integrate end-to-end Foreign Objects and Debris (FOD) solutions from initial problem definition to final onboard driving system implementation; Streamline model development and evaluation workflows to enable rapid adaptation across diverse new tasks and datasets; Establish a sustainable and scalable model stack that supports seamless expansion across multiple vehicle platforms and cities; Leverage unique FOD signals to enhance scene understanding and refine autonomous driving behaviors for safer navigation; Support cross-functional product applications by improving critical metrics related to safety, rider comfort, and operational cost; and Maintain high performance standards on existing FOD applications while simultaneously fulfilling new product requests during rapid scaling.

**You have:**

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, and 3 years of experience in the job offered or in a Software Engineer-related occupation.

Alternatively, will accept a Master’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, and 1 year of experience in the  job offered or in a Software Engineer-related occupation.

Position requires 1 year of experience in the following:

* Machine Learning & Perception
* Systems & Deployment Engineering
* Data Infrastructure & Analytics
* Core Programming
* Transformer-based deep-learning modeling
