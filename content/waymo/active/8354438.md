Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

Our cars may be self-driving, but Waymo’s workplace services and facilities are steered by the GPS team. The GPS team manages a portfolio that includes oversight of executing multiple teams, strategic business planning with regional site leads, and developing programs to support Waymonaut’s environment and productivity. The team is responsible for the delivery of large-scale operations and portfolio-wide programs ensuring and facilitating the successful delivery of a consistent service program in an extremely fast-paced environment with a high degree of ambiguity.

This role follows a hybrid work schedule and reports to Head of Workplace Strategy and Services.

**You will:**

* Partner directly with R&D hardware and robotics teams to understand emerging technical workflows and co-design specialized lab layouts, equipment footprints, and utility specifications.
* Evaluate active bench and space utilization alongside engineering growth road maps to project multi-year lab demand and forecast future capital build outs ahead of capacity limits.
* Collaborate with Capital Project Managers from initial scoping through delivery so finished labs meet original business needs, while designing modular layouts that easily reconfigure as priorities evolve.
* Maintain lab operating standards, safety-by-design clearances, and specialized third-party vendor support to keep active R&D hardware laboratories organized, compliant, and ready day-to-day.
* Run lab compliance frameworks and audit schedules covering electrical hazards, chemical hygiene, and physical safety readiness.
* Direct budgets, SLAs, and daily execution of third-party technical contractors, lab operations vendors, and specialized equipment service partners.

**You have:**

* BA/BS degree in Architecture, Engineering, Lab Planning, Facilities Management, or a related technical discipline, or equivalent practical experience.
* 5+ years of experience programming, laying out, and managing R&D hardware laboratories, prototype shops, or technical engineering spaces.
* Demonstrated track record translating engineering needs into spatial briefs, drafting layout updates in CAD/Revit, and partnering with Capital Project Managers to deliver builds that meet business expectations.
* Proven experience forecasting future lab space needs alongside hands-on ownership of Day-2 lab governance, minor re-configurations, and vendor supervision.
* Experience in vendorized environments, SLA/KPI governance, SOWs, third-party technical contractors, lab OpEx budgets.

**We prefer:**

* Direct experience planning bench-scale R&D hardware, sensor/optics, prototype, or robotics laboratories.
* Strong working knowledge of adaptable "flex lab" systems—such as mobile benching, overhead power/air carriers, ESD controls, and specialty exhaust—to build rapidly reconfigurable spaces.
* Working knowledge of electrical and chemical lab hazard controls or active credentials such as CFM, CSP, or ASP.
* Experience operating in vendorized environments to manage specialized lab service partners, 5S housekeeping protocols, and ongoing lab Moves, Adds, and Changes (MACs).

**Travel Requirements:**

* This role requires travel approximately 50% of the time, primarily between key Waymo markets and Waymo headquarters as part of job responsibilities.
