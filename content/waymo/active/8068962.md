Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

The Chassis team provides the foundational infrastructure and tools that enable the Waymo organization to release software frequently and with confidence. You will lead the technical direction and implementation of a city-scale simulation environment that serves as the centerpiece for Waymo's release validation process. As a Senior Staff Tech Lead Engineer, you will architect solutions for large-scale simulated environments, drive the migration to modern testing frameworks, and ensure cross-functional alignment to expand test coverage to include complex systems. You will mentor a team of engineers to deliver a reliable, repeatable, and realistic testing environment that mirrors production and shifts outage detection left.

*This Hybrid role reports to our Director of Engineering.*

**You will:**

* Help build and evolve mission-critical horizontal infrastructure and tooling that underpins all commercialization systems
* Collaborate with other engineering teams to develop systems and processes as business needs grow and evolve
* Scale our systems to meet the demands of commercial expansion and growing complexity
* Solve hard, real-world problems such as world simulation for regression testing and evaluation of software services managing fleets for autonomous vehicles

**You have:**

* BS degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience
* 6+ years of backend or infrastructure experience
* 5+ years of tech leadership experience
* Proven track record of driving strategic alignment and consensus across complex, cross-functional organizations

**We prefer:**

* Experience with C++ (or a willingness to participate in professional development activities to stay current on industry knowledge)
* Experience working in environments including nontechnical team members
* Experience with operations tooling, developer productivity, or developer tools
* Experience with simulation environments
