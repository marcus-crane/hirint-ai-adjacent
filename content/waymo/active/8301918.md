Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

**You will:**

* Design, develop, and deploy data pipeline, storage, and query infrastructure supporting autonomous driving training and evaluation;
* Write high-quality, testable, and maintainable code to ensure robust software performance;
* Participate in peer code reviews to uphold engineering standards and drive continuous improvement in development practices;
* Enhance existing systems for greater performance, scalability, and reliability across large-scale applications;
* Monitor system health and proactively resolve issues while providing technical support to internal users; and
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams including data scientists, product managers, and engineers to align data solutions with simulation, planning, perception, and commercialization needs.

**You have:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Information Systems or a related field and 5 years of progressive post-baccalaureate experience in the job offered or or in a Software Engineering related occupation. Alternatively, the employer will accept a Master’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Information Systems or a related field and 3 years of experience in the job offered or in a Software Engineering related occupation.
* Position requires 3 years of experience in the following:
  + data structures and algorithms to optimize backend software performance and efficiency;
  + backend systems and infrastructures for large-scale software applications;
  + large-scale distributed systems and data processing frameworks;
  + data processing frameworks to handle high-volume data pipelines;
  + C++, Java, or Python; and
  + SQL queries for relational database management and data retrieval.
