Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

Operating as part of the Deployment & Construction team within Waymo’s Global Program Services (GPS) organization, the Rapid Deploy Program Manager brings a proven track record of accelerating EV infrastructure rollouts and delivering projects on time, on budget, and with meaningful community impact. In this role, you will tackle the core challenge of aligning cross-functional stakeholders around ad-hoc projects with shifting deliverable requirements and timelines. By establishing clarity around business-critical milestones across regional project teams and vendors, you will help drive global deployment for Validation, PitStop, and Market Entry Service Center sites.

This role follows a hybrid work schedule and reports to the Rapid Deploy Lead.        

**You will:**

* **Own the Global Rapid Deployment Pipeline:** Develop and govern the global rapid-deployment master portfolio schedule and milestone guidance across all markets, tracking the real-time stage-gate status of every site and proactively resolving schedule delays.
* **Drive Continuous Program Optimization:** Lead post-launch project retrospectives, synthesize feedback, and implement continuous process improvements to compress future site deployment cycle times.
* **Deliver Executive Status Reporting:** Provide weekly executive status reports for senior leadership and cross-functional directors on the status or rapid deployments.
* **Manage Spare Parts Pipelines:** Manage the supply pipeline of spare parts (e.g., adapters).
* **Manage Engineering Products:** Manage the sourcing for engineered products such as Skids, containers, etc.
* **Quality Control:** Review drawings, submittals, and as-built documents to verify project designs meet operational program guidance.

**You have:**

* **BA/BS Degree:** BA/BS degree in a related field such as Business Administration, Project Management, or Operations Management (or equivalent practical experience).
* **Program Management Experience**: 5+ years in program management, business operations, or delivery coordination.
* **Master Scheduling Experience:** Demonstrated expertise building complex portfolio schedules and managing programmatic tracking systems.
* **Executive Dashboarding & Communication:** Exceptional communication and data visualization skills, with a proven ability to distill complex multi-project timelines and risks into concise executive briefings for senior leadership.
* **Technical Knowledge:** Familiarity with EVSE charging systems, power distribution, microgrids, and utility coordination.
* **Technical Knowledge:** Familiarity with General Construction, Tentant Improvement Fit-Out, and Civil Site improvement processes.

**We prefer:**

* **Active Project/Program Management Credentials:** Active certification in program or project management.
* **EV Infrastructure or Fleet Launch Experience:** Direct program management experience supporting electric vehicle (EV) charging infrastructure, commercial fleet launches, or modular/mobile facility activations.
