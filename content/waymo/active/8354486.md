Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

Our cars may be self-driving, but Waymo’s workplace services and facilities are steered by the GPS team. The GPS team manages a portfolio that includes oversight of executing multiple teams, strategic business planning with regional site leads, and developing programs to support Waymonaut’s environment and productivity. The team is responsible for the delivery of large-scale operations and portfolio-wide programs ensuring and facilitating the successful delivery of a consistent service program in an extremely fast-paced environment with a high degree of ambiguity.

This role follows a hybrid work schedule and reports to Head of Workplace Strategy and Services.

**You will:**

* Define and govern Waymo's global space planning standards, conceptual layouts, and office density ratios while maintaining a centralized view of all office footprints, lease capacities, and spatial database records.
* Evaluate spatial organization, seat vacancy logs, and occupancy metrics to identify underutilized areas and execute layout re-configurations or density adjustments that absorb headcount growth.
* Partner with HR, Real Estate, and Regional Workplace Leads to translate headcount growth forecasts and utilization trends into regional space requirements and site-capacity triggers.
* Lead complex occupancy planning initiatives by translating business needs into clear spatial briefs and partnering with Capital Project Managers so delivered buildouts meet operational expectations.

**You have:**

* BA/BS degree in Architecture, Interior Design, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
* 5–8 years of experience evaluating, planning, and laying out spaces across commercial offices and industrial structures.
* Direct experience measuring physical space utilization, identifying underutilized offices, and drafting strategic layout updates to optimize existing footprints.
* Demonstrated track record translating multi-location workforce growth forecasts into actionable regional workplace and space planning strategies.

**We prefer:**

* Experience owning occupancy and space planning across multiple regions and countries.
* Experience planning dynamic workplaces where attendance patterns vary and assigned seating does not apply uniformly.
* Hands-on proficiency using occupancy analytics, headcount forecasting, and IWMS/CAD platforms (e.g., Serraview, Archibus, AutoCAD) to inform real estate expansion decisions.
* Proven success collaborating with regional workplace leads, external partners, Real Estate, and Capital Project Managers to deliver unified employee experiences from initial brief through move-in.

**Travel Requirements:**

* This role requires travel approximately 25% of the time, primarily between key Waymo markets and Waymo headquarters as part of job responsibilities.
