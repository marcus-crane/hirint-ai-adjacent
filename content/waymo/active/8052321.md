Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

*The **Business Development (BD) and Strategic Partnerships** team at Waymo plays a vital role in planning the autonomous vehicle (AV) pioneer’s commercial future and driving its go-to-market strategy. Operating with an entrepreneurial mindset, this cross-functional team leverages both quantitative and qualitative analysis to identify, negotiate, and execute "first-of-kind" agreements that expand Waymo's commercial deployment globally.  Ultimately, the team acts as a bridge between Waymo’s cutting-edge engineering and the physical world, building the commercial networks required to scale its driverless technology safely and sustainably.*

*In this hybrid role, you will report to a Strategic Partnerships Manager, Lead.*

**You will:**

* Lead end-to-end partner management: Drive partner strategy, RFP selection, contract negotiation, and ongoing partner performance with payment processors, acquirers, networks, and fintech partners
* Collaborate with Product and Engineering to design, scale, and launch Waymo’s global payment infrastructure into new markets and territories
* Lead cross-functional workstreams across Finance, Product, Engineering, Accounting, and Legal to drive sucessful business outcomes
* Structure and negotiate complex, first-of-its-kind commercial international agreements
* Establish deep relationships with C-level executives across key financial institutions and tech partners to ensure long-term alignment and joint innovation

**You have:**

* 8+ years of experience in partner management in payments, fintech, banking, or technology
* Demonstrated track record structuring and negotiating complex commercial deals across the payments ecosystem
* Experience leading workstreams with cross-functional stakeholders in Product, Legal, Engineering, Finance, Accounting, and other functions
* Experience managing C-level relationships with external partners

**We prefer:**

* Deep domain expertise in global payments infrastructure (e.g., tokenization, card network rules, interchange mechanics, local payment methods, or digital wallets)
* Proven track record of building a payments international infrastructure product roadmap to support a high transaction-volume business
* Exceptional communication skills with the ability to influence senior decision-makers internally and externally
* Comfort working in extremely high-paced, ambiguous environments
