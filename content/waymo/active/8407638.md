Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

**You will:**

* Collaborate and coordinate with teams to develop and implement solutions, in order to advance Waymo's self-driving technology and products.
* Duties include: Define and champion the long-term vision and strategic roadmap for problem area within autonomous vehicle development;
* Analyze autonomous vehicle (AV) behavior across diverse data sources, including realworld driving events and simulated driving scenarios;
* Conduct in-depth root cause analysis of identified issues and collaborate with engineering counterparts to develop and propose effective solutions;
* Negotiate and collaborate with product management counterparts in other organizational units to secure support and resources for new product initiatives and cross-functional projects;
* Develop detailed execution milestones and project plans in conjunction with Technical Program Management (TPM) partners. Establish clear objectives and key results (OKRs) and facilitate regular check-ins to ensure progress and alignment with strategic goals; and
* Develop and maintain a comprehensive long-term product roadmap that directly contributes to Waymo's overarching strategic objectives. Effectively communicate the product vision, strategy, priorities, and potential risks to executive stakeholders and broader organizational teams.

**You have:** Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field and 30 months of experience in the job offered or in a Product Management-related occupation. Position requires:

* + 30 months of experience in working on autonomous vehicles, robotics, or products that utilize Machine Learning or AI;
  + 30 months of experience in defining and setting product metrics, and analyzing and visualizing data to drive product decisions;
  + 30 months of experience in managing cross-functional prioritization across multiple high-impact product initiatives;
  + 30 months of experience in developing and communicating comprehensive product vision and roadmap, including market analysis, user needs, and cross-functional input;
  + 30 months of experience in leading cross-team collaboration for scalable product delivery across platforms;
  + 30 months of experience in technical leadership experience driving system architecture and roadmap execution; and
  + 18 months of experience in Perception technologies such as computer vision.
