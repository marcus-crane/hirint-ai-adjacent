Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

As part of Waymo's Legal team, you will work on the exciting legal issues surrounding our transformational autonomous driving technology and help drive our business lines forward from technology ideation through scaled commercial deployment. We partner with our public policy, safety, security and privacy experts to define transportation policy for the autonomous driving world to come, advising on regulatory changes that support and protect our users around the world. We collaborate with our engineering, product, strategy and operations teams to develop and protect our intellectual property portfolio and drive our corporate and commercial transactions. We ensure compliance with an increasingly complex and dynamic range of global regulations. And we anticipate, mitigate, and litigate high-profile and precedent-setting legal matters.

*This role follows a hybrid work schedule and reports to our Head of Federal Policy*

**You will:**

* Under the direction of the Head of Federal Policy and Government Affairs, create and execute federal lobbying strategies that promote Waymo's policy positions, with a focus on House Democrats.
* Build relationships with Members of Congress and their staff, and with other key external stakeholders who are influential on autonomous vehicle policy.
* Convey a compelling vision for the value of autonomous driving, Waymo's leadership role in the industry, and the federal policies needed to support these priorities.
* Develop subject matter expertise in main policy areas, as determined.
* Manage consultants and engage with trade associations and relevant third parties.
* Collaborate with other members of the Waymo Policy team and other internal cross-functional partners.

**You have:**

* Bachelor's degree and 8+ years of relevant federal government affairs or federal government experience, including 4 years of experience lobbying Congress in the private sector
* Firsthand experience with how major initiatives get through the commerce or transportation committees in Congress
* Strong relationships with active House Democratic policymakers and other important left-of-center policy stakeholders
* Demonstrated track record of lobbying success (e.g., legislative wins, productive relationships with top officials, successfully shaping executive branch activity, etc.)
* Experience with building broad support for complex policy positions and overcoming political obstacles
* Ability to travel as needed, approximately 1 or 2 trips a month

**We prefer:**

* Experience working on regulatory issues facing highly-regulated industries.
* Passionate and knowledgeable about emerging technology and related public policy.
* Knowledge of the policy and regulatory environment surrounding autonomous passenger and commercial vehicles.
* Knowledge of artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, privacy, and other public policy issues related to data-driven industries.
* Enthusiasm for understanding and describing complex technical matters and their possible effects for public policy.
