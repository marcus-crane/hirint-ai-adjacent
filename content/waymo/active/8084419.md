Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

The People Operations Program Manager – People Systems & Process Design leads the strategy, architecture, and delivery of our global People technology ecosystem. Operating as the primary translator between People Team and DTS/IT/Engineering, you will lead end-to-end service delivery implementations, and embed AI and intelligent automation across the employee lifecycle.

This is a high-impact player-coach role requiring both strategic vision and hands-on execution. You will define how technology scales our People function—serving as the Program Manager for People Technology and ServiceNow initiatives—while rolling up your sleeves to map complex processes, define technical requirements, and drive adoption.

*This role follows a hybrid work schedule and reports to our Manager of People Operations.*

**You will:**

* Own the long-term People Tech strategy and architecture, identifying capability gaps, evaluating emerging tools, and building business cases that align technology investments with global People and business goals.
* Program Manage for People Technology and ServiceNow initiatives—driving multiple system integrations, and platform upgrades from discovery, UAT, launch, and post-launch optimization.
* Partner with People COEs (Talent Acquisition, People Business Partners, People Experience Partner, People Ops) to elicit business requirements, challenge legacy processes, and translate complex People policies into clear technical specifications, data models, and architectural workflows for DTS (IT).
* Architect and deploy AI-enabled solutions (e.g., virtual agents, automated case triage, intelligent search, generative AI workflows) and cross-platform automations that eliminate manual process debt and elevate the employee experience.
* Guide the evolution of our employee service delivery platform (ServiceNow HRSD), establishing governance, data privacy standards (GDPR/RBAC), and scalable service catalog structures while partnering with IT admins on configuration and release management.
* Drive organizational readiness, stakeholder alignment, and measurable adoption across global teams, tracking ROI through operational efficiency, resolution speed, and employee satisfaction metrics.
* Execute routine administrative configurations in ServiceNow HRSD, including updates to People case templates, assignment rules, and user criteria.

**You have:**

* 10+ years of experience in People Technology, HRIS/HCM Strategy, HR Transformation, or Enterprise Systems Program Management.
* Hands-on experience leading multiple end-to-end HRIS/HCM implementations, migrations, or global module rollouts (e.g., Workday, SuccessFactors, UKG, or comparable enterprise platforms) alongside cross-system integrations.
* Demonstrated success designing and deploying AI-driven HR tools, intelligent workflows, and process automations that meaningfully reduce manual HR workload at scale.
* Exceptional ability to diagnose complex human/operational problems from the People side and translate them into structured technical requirements, API/integration logic, and system architectures that IT teams can execute.
* Strong experience with enterprise service management platforms; hands-on experience with ServiceNow HRSD (including NowAssist, Virtual Agent, or Lifecycle Events) is a strong plus, though deep experience with comparable platforms (e.g., Jira Service Management, Zendesk Enterprise, Salesforce) will be strongly considered.
* Equally comfortable presenting a multi-year technology roadmap to executives and jumping into a process-mapping session, UAT script, or workflow debug to unblock delivery.
* Proven ability to lead cross-functional global programs across IT, Security, Legal, and People teams with strong command of data privacy, role-based access controls, and change management.
