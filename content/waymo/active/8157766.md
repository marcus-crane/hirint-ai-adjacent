You are the critical partner on our real estate team and responsible for securing, negotiating, and executing the lease agreements for our core operational facilities. You ensure our Operations teams across APAC are empowered with world-class, cost-effective hubs that meet their specific needs for scale and productivity.

*In this role you will report to the Director of Real Estate and Transactions.*

**You will:**

* Execute a regional real estate strategy that aligns with Waymo's long-term expansion plans.
* Work with external support to source infrastructure in the assigned territory.
* Oversee the entire lifecycle of complex real estate transactions, from site selection and financial analysis to negotiation and execution, while also acting as an escalation point during the lifecycle of all real  estate transactions, including build-out through the first day of business.
* Act as a real estate advisor to internal stakeholders, including Operations, Product, and Business Development, providing strategic guidance.
* Prepare and present comprehensive real estate strategies, site analyses, and deal summaries.
* Manage relationships and performance of key external partners, including brokers, landlords, developers, and service providers.
* Expect to travel 35% of the time

* 10+ years of experience in commercial real estate, with a specialty in industrial operations.

* 5+ years of demonstrated leadership experience building, managing, and mentoring diverse teams of real estate professionals.
* Experience partnering closely with cross-functional stakeholders, including Operations, Product, Project Management, Legal and Facilities.
