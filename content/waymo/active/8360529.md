Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

Waymo’s cars navigate the world autonomously, but our global security posture is steered by the expertise of professionals like you. Safety is our foundation and is at the heart of everything we do.

As the Global Threat Intelligence Regional Lead for APAC, you will serve as an independent analytical advisor and strategic partner to our Asia-Pacific operations. Operating as our initial solo representative sitting in Singapore, you will translate complex threat landscapes into actionable insights that protect our people, assets, and expanding markets. You will bridge the gap between business goals and security intelligence, providing the critical risk data that enables our teams to scale Waymo’s pioneering technology with confidence. "

Please note that this is a physical security and geopolitical risk intelligence position focused on personnel, asset, and operational safety, rather than an information security or cybersecurity role.

*This role follows a hybrid work schedule and reports to the Global Threat Intelligence Program Manager.*

**You will:**

* Deliver Actionable Intelligence: Author and distribute high-impact tactical and strategic threat intelligence products—such as event threat assessments, predictive outlooks, and situational briefings—focused on the APAC region
* Partner with Regional Stakeholders: Serve as an intelligence advisor to Singapore-based business units and broader APAC operations, proactively addressing localized security needs for high-profile events and regional operations while maintaining clear ownership over analytical priorities
* Conduct Comprehensive Research: Use OSINT methodologies to research and analyze geopolitical trends, regional stability, and local threat environments impacting Waymo's APAC markets
* Execute PIR-Driven Workflows: Process and deliver tactical, strategic, and proactive intelligence products driven by established Priority Intelligence Requirements (PIRs).
* Collaborate on Vendor Capabilities: Leverage and review external vendor intelligence deliverables and feeds to synthesize regional data, ensuring vendor-produced outputs align with Waymo’s analytic standards
* Maintain Intelligence Networks: Cultivate and leverage an impactful regional network of internal counterparts and external peers, including private sector forums, government partners, and information sharing centers

**You have:**

* Bachelor's degree in Intelligence Studies, International Relations, Political Science, Global Security, or equivalent experience
* 5+ Years of Analytical Experience as a dedicated intelligence analyst within a corporate security, government, or specialized risk intelligence environment
* 5+ years of experience demonstrating a strong baseline understanding of the geopolitical, security, and operational threat landscapes specific to APAC markets.
* Experience in drafting concise, high-impact written intelligence products (e.g., threat assessments, risk outlooks, leadership briefings) that directly inform operational decision-making

**We prefer:**

* Experience operating within a corporate threat intelligence team for a multinational technology, autonomous vehicle, or transportation company
* Demonstrated experience acting as a regional lead for an intelligence function, with proven success coordinating workflows across cross-functional partners and external intelligence providers
* Experience integrating advanced OSINT aggregators, scrapers, visualization platforms, or AI applications into data-driven intelligence cycles
* Proven ability to operate as an independent support function, establishing clear boundaries, managing expectations, and providing expert guidance without acting as a direct operational resource
* Advanced Research Capabilities: Exceptional OSINT skills, critical thinking, and synthesis capabilities, with a proven ability to independently evaluate information validity and analyze complex risks
* Stakeholder Engagement Skills: Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with a track record of briefing cross-functional teams and translating threat data for non-security business units
