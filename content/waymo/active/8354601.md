Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

Operating as part of the Deployment & Construction within Waymo’s Global Program Services (GPS) organization, the Rapid Deploy Specialist, AMER West helps accelerate EV infrastructure rollouts and deliver regional sites safely, on time, and on budget. In this role, you will tackle the core challenge of aligning cross-functional partners around ad-hoc projects with evolving requirements and schedules. Driving deployment for Validation, PitStop, and Market Entry Service Center sites across regional teams and vendors, you will build adaptable EVSE delivery processes and de-risk timelines through alternative execution paths, product options, and tiered scopes.

This role follows a hybrid work schedule and reports to the Rapid Deploy Lead        

**You will:**

* **Deliver Regional Rapid-Deploy Sites:** Lead the end-to-end delivery and project management of all rapid-deployment sites within your assigned geographic region, ensuring on-time and safe site activations.
* **Own Permitting:**Manage the tactical submission, review, and approval of permits for generators and temporary power solutions.
* **Track Project Schedules & RAID Logs:** Monitor project schedules, track milestone progress on dedicated trackers, and maintain regional Risks, Assumptions, Issues, and Dependencies (RAID) logs.
* **Manage Budgets & Cost Controls:**Review and manage the rapid-deployment budgets for projects in your region, ensuring that all physical buildouts remain strictly aligned with the approved capital budget.
* **Supervise Vendors & Contractors:**Direct and manage general contractors in your region, holding external teams strictly accountable to schedule commitments, quality standards, and budget controls.
* **Execute Charging Equipment Handover:**Complete and lead the seamless handover of charging hardware to the charging equipment team once the site is fully built and launched.

**You have:**

* **BA/BS Degree:** BA/BS degree in Project Management, Construction Management, Engineering, or a related technical discipline (or equivalent practical experience).
* **Site or Infrastructure Delivery:**5+ years of experience managing and delivering commercial construction or infrastructure projects.
* **Budget & Cost Control Oversight Experience:**Direct experience reviewing and managing project budget, with demonstrated success keeping complex buildouts aligned with approved capital limits and cost baselines.
* **Vendor Oversight Experience:**Direct experience supervising third-party Project Managers (PMs), general contractors, and construction vendors.
* **Communication:**Proven ability to distill multi-project timelines and risks into concise risk briefings for senior leadership.
* **Technical Knowledge:** Direct experience with EVSE charging systems, Power Distribution, Microgrids, Utility Coordination, General Construction, Tentant Improvement Fit-Out, and Civil Site improvement processes.

**We prefer:**

* **Professional Certification:** Certifications or industry designations in Project Management, Engineering, Facilities Services, or National Electrical Code compliance.
* **Diverse Environment Expertise:** Experience operating in mixed-use environments (office, industrial, labs, or manufacturing).
* **Vendor Expertise:** Experience operating within highly vendorized environments, with a focus on establishing scalable and nimble service strategies.
* **Strong Communication Skills:** Exceptional communication and negotiation skills, with the ability to influence cross-functional decisions and communicate real estate and business case strategies to executive-level stakeholders

**Travel:**

* **[50% travel]: This role requires consistent market presence to oversee site build-out across the markets in the assigned region.**
