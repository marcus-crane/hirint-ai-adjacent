Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

**You will:**

* Lead cross-organizational efforts to enhance system efficiency, deployment speed, and maintainability.
* Improve developer experience and system visibility by streamlining infrastructure workflows
* Optimize model serving infrastructure to maximize the perception system's model performance.
* Develop and scale robust infrastructure for large-scale data processing, extraction, and evaluation.
* Support expansive machine learning development, including data mining, debugging, and visualization tools.
* Contribute to systems development by enhancing testing, performance analysis, and deployment pipelines.

**You have:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field and 7 years of progressive post-baccalaureate experience in the job offered or in a Software Engineer-related occupation. Alternatively, will accept a Master’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field and 5 years of experience in the  job offered or in a Software Engineer-related occupation.

  Position requires 5 years of experience in the following:

  + C++ programming;
  + Distributed system design;
  + Large-scale data pipeline development;
  + Distributed data processing frameworks; and
  + Robotics engineering or Machine learning engineering.
