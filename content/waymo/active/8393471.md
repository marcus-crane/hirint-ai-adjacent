Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

In this role, you will lead the Construction & Project Delivery organization within Global Places and Services (GPS). We plan, design, and build the physical infrastructure that enables Waymo's autonomous vehicle fleet to scale globally. We own the process from initial site acquisition due diligence, to design, to full-scale construction to build world-class physical facilities for Waymo's vehicles. Our team also establishes and enforces the global design and delivery standards that every market builds to, ensuring that whenever a site opens anywhere in the world, it meets the exact same uncompromising bar for schedule, cost, quality, and safety.

This role follows a hybrid work schedule and reports to the Senior Director of GPS & Global Security.

**You Will:**

* Lead Physical Infrastructure Development: Own and deliver all aspects of physical infrastructure development across the entire global portfolio, directing the full due diligence, design, and construction lifecycle to deliver world-class facilities and charging hubs for Waymo's vehicles.
* Oversee Capital Budget: Own the global capital expenditure (CapEx) portfolio, establishing financial controls, cash-flow forecasting, and multi-year budget management to ensure all builds are delivered on or under approved financial targets.
* Set the Global Delivery Strategy: Manage and own the overarching strategy for how global projects are scoped, sequenced, resourced, and scheduled, removing operational bottlenecks and optimizing delivery timelines ahead of commercial market launches.
* Own Global Design & Delivery Standards: Oversee the definition and enforcement of Waymo’s global design and delivery standards, ensuring consistent execution across every market while driving strict compliance with schedule, cost, quality, and safety bars.
* Build & Lead a World-Class Delivery Team: Recruit, mentor, and lead a high-performing organization of design resources, pre-construction managers, construction managers, utility SMEs and more, cultivating a culture of operational rigor, excellence, and safety.

**You have:**

* BA/BS degree in a related field such as Construction Management, Engineering, or Architecture (or equivalent practical experience).
* 15+ years of progressive leadership experience managing large-scale capital projects, corporate workplace buildouts, or industrial/fleet infrastructure delivery
* Large-Scale CapEx Portfolio Experience: Proven track record owning multi-million dollar annual capital expenditure programs from site acquisition through physical commissioning.
* Experience in a formal management or team leadership capacity, with a demonstrated ability to mentor team members, strategically allocate resources, and drive alignment.

**We prefer:**

* Experience in high-velocity logistics, automotive, tech-manufacturing, or mixed-use environments (office, industrial, labs, and depots), effectively understanding needs between corporate environments and operational/testing sites.
* Experience operating within highly vendorized environments, with a focus on establishing scalable and nimble service strategies.
* Active registration as a Professional Engineer (PE), Registered Architect (AIA), or certified Project Management Professional (PMP)
* Exceptional communication, storytelling, and negotiation skills, with a demonstrated ability to influence cross-functional decisions and communicate infrastructure status and strategy to executive-level stakeholders.
