Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

Software Engineering builds the brains of Waymo's fully autonomous driving technology. Our software allows the Waymo Driver to perceive the world around it, make the right decision for every situation, and deliver people safely to their destinations. We think deeply and solve complex technical challenges in areas like robotics, perception, decision-making and deep learning, while collaborating with hardware and systems engineers. If you’re a software engineer or researcher who’s curious and passionate about Level 4 autonomous driving, we'd like to meet you.

Waymo interns partner with leaders in the industry on projects that create impact to the company. We believe learning is a two-way street: applying your knowledge while providing you with opportunities to expand your skill-set. Interns are an important part of our culture and our recruiting pipeline. Join us at Waymo for a fun and rewarding internship!

**You will:**

* Design and Prototype Auto-Research Loops: Architect and implement end-to-end autonomous research workflows using Google Antigravity to enable LLM-powered agents to generate, run, and evaluate their own post-training experiments.
* Develop Recursive Self-Improvement (RSI) Workflows: Build and refine agentic feedback mechanisms that allow reinforcement learning (RL) models to iteratively learn from their own simulation rollouts and continuously self-correct.
* Analyze & Triage Agentic Execution: Set up rigorous evaluation and root-cause analysis pipelines to monitor, debug, and improve the decision-making quality of the autonomous research agents.
* Publish & Document Findings: Collaborate with senior researchers to document novel methodology, benchmark performance gains, and prepare high-quality submissions for top-tier academic conferences.

**You have:**

* Currently pursuing a PhD in Computer Science or a closely related field.
* Demonstrated Research Expertise: Proven track record of independent research in machine learning, reinforcement learning (RL), agentic AI, or human-machine interaction, ideally supported by publications in top-tier venues (e.g., NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CHI, CVPR).
* Hands-on Agentic & RSI Development: Deep technical experience developing autonomous agentic workflows, recursive self-improvement (RSI) loops, or large language model (LLM) orchestration frameworks.

**We prefer:**

* Publications in machine learning, AI, reinforcement learning, ML systems, human computer interaction conferences (e.g., NeurIPS, ICLR, ICML, CVPR, CHI, AAAI).
* Hands on experience designing and building self-play architectures, auto research loop or recursive self-improvement (RSI) loops where models autonomously generate, evaluate, and learn from their own code, prompts, or data.
* Practical experiences in advanced reinforcement learning techniques (such as offline RL, RLHF/RLAIF, or curriculum learning) applied directly to complex, high dimensional closed loop simulation environments.
