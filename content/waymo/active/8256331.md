Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

**You will:**

* Develop deep subject matter expertise on the Imagery component of mapping operations, and serve as a go-to point of contact for our vendor teams
* Test and roll out new mapping  tools, features, and workflows in coordination with our Engineering partners
* Identify opportunities to improve efficiency and own those solutions end-to-end
* Create and maintain clear technical documentation

**You have:**

* B.Tech / B.E. in Civil Engineering, Geoinformatics, Computer Science, or a related quantitative field.
* 2+ years managing technical data pipelines or operations, directly responsible for task flow, throughput, and error triage.
* Track record of technical self-sufficiency: Proven experience ramping up on proprietary tools/pipelines and independently diagnosing data anomalies to root causes.
* Policy & SOP authoring: Demonstrated experience translating raw engineering specs into clear, unambiguous operational workflows for large teams.
* Cross-functional bridge: Experience coordinating directly with Software Engineers to resolve blockers while keeping operations unblocked.

**We prefer:**

* MBA / PGDM with a specialization in Operations, Supply Chain, or Systems.
* Domain exposure: Direct experience with geospatial data, HD maps, GIS, LiDAR/camera sensor data, or AV/robotics pipelines.
* Vendor operations: Experience training, auditing, and enforcing strict quality/SLA metrics (e.g., 98%+ quality bars, Meta-QC) across 20+ member vendor pods.
* Hands-on data querying: Proficiency in SQL or Python for extracting pipeline logs, auditing defect rates, and building operational dashboards.

**Travel requirements**:

* Ability to travel to the US as needed (at least once every 2 years)
