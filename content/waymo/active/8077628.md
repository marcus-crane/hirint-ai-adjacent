Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

Waymo’s cars navigate the world autonomously, but our global security posture is steered by the expertise of professionals like you. Safety is our foundation and is at the heart of everything we do. As a core member of the Global Security team, you will manage the technical infrastructure that protects our people, properties, and physical operations. You will be responsible for the seamless integration of our global security ecosystem, ensuring that disparate platforms from access control and video to communication and risk-monitoring systems work in concert.  This position is based onsite in London, United Kingdom and reports directly to the Head of Security Systems and Technology.

*This role follows a hybrid work schedule and reports to the Head of Security Systems and Technology*

**You will:**

* Serve as an EMEA proxy to the Head of Security Technology & Systems through Site Delivery by providing sign-off during milestone reviews, security designs, and final commissioning. Drive strategic regional initiatives and identify continuous process improvements
* Manage system health for a portfolio of EMEA sites with varying operational requirements. Serve as the primary EMEA contact for hardware failures and software outages
* Partner cross-functionally with Security Investigations, Facilities Project Management, and local security integrators to ensure cohesive security technology delivery and seamless security systems operations
* Conduct site security compliance audits and enforce corporate physical security standards across the region to guarantee continuous operational readiness and adherence to regional and global guidelines
* Support Waymo’s Physical Security Investigations program in the EMEA region, serving as the regional security technology Point of Contact

**You have:**

* 7+ years of progressive security technology experience in a corporate, or large organization, environment
* At least 3 years of experience directly managing a diverse security technology portfolio that includes multiple site types (e.g., offices, warehouses, or labs)
* Demonstrated experience supporting onsite security incident response and emergency coordination within a distributed regional model
* Experience managing regional-scale vendor contracts and overseeing integrator performance metrics.
* Strong verbal and written communication skills with the ability to influence stakeholders and navigate change in a fast-paced environment

**We prefer:**

* Bachelor’s degree in a security-related field (e.g., Criminal Justice, Emergency Management, Security Management)
* Experience managing security technology for fleet vehicle depots, high-velocity logistics hubs, or e-commerce fulfillment centers during rapid company growth
* Familiarity with physical security technology systems, including Integrated Intrusion Alarms, Access Control, and Video Management Systems

**Travel Requirements:**

* Up to 35% International Travel.
