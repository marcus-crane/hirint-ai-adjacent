Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

The Signal Integrity/Power Integrity (SIPI) team is a centralized SIPI resource within Waymo. The team is responsible for ensuring that all electrical designs meet functional SI and PI performance metrics before they progress to production. The team covers a variety of designs including silicon package, PCB, connector and cabling systems, which serve as core parts of computing modules and sensor suites in our autonomous driving systems.

Acting as a critical bridge between schematics and layout, the team rigorously evaluates circuit designs, layout and integration constraints, along with hardware prototypes through structured simulation plans, data-driven analysis, and industry-standard validation methodologies.

**You will:**

* Lead SI and PI design across assigned hardware projects, including lidar, camera, compute modules, and custom silicons
* Deliver end-to-end results from pre-layout feasibility study, post-layout simulation to post-fab correlations
* Define SI and PI acceptance criteria and partner with bench validation teams to root-cause and resolve SI and PI related issues
* Review and direct vendor design and simulation workflows where applicable
* Identify design risks and drive cross-functional solutions in collaboration with EE, PCB layout, Firmware and Mechanical teams

**You have:**

* Masters Degree in Electrical Engineering with 3+ years relevant working experience
* Be able to perform SI and PI simulation softwares such as Ansys HFSS, SIwave, PowerSI, ADS, HSPICE. Familiar with working with IBIS AMI models
* Understand PI fundamentals. Be able to perform power-noise-aware SI analysis
* Familiar with lab equipment such as VNA and TDR, demonstrated experience with simulation-to-measurement correlation and solving real-world SIPI problems.
* Excellent communication skills with experience to collaborate with internal and external teams
* Depending on the project, be able to work with overseas team (east Asian time zone)

**We prefer:**

* Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering or relevant field with 3+ years relevant working experience
* Familiar with substrate and PCB design rules, stackups, materials selection and HDI process. Has experience with the package-board co-design process
* Deep understanding in one or more of the following areas: SERDES, LPDDRx, or  >200A  PDN design
* Experience in root-cause analysis and debugging of real-world SI and PI  hardware issues
* Experience with DoE simulation for high-volume production intended designs
