Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

Our Driver may be autonomous, but Waymo's finances are steered by experts like you. The Finance and Accounting group manages all aspects of our finances and serves as trusted advisors for all our strategic and enterprise endeavors. Along with managing compilation of our financial statements, looking after our cash and overseeing compliance, we partner closely with the business in overseeing company wide resource allocation, developing cutting edge compensation initiatives and generating detailed financial analyses to drive the business forward. We also develop advanced capital strategies to support the company's short- and long-term future.

**This role follows a hybrid work schedule and reports to our Treasurer.**  
**You will:**          
•    Build robust, dynamic financial models from scratch to evaluate business scenarios, capital allocation, and strategic initiatives.      
•    Manage core Treasury operations, including cash management, investments, and FX and interest rate hedging strategies.      
•    Evaluate financing structures and capital market opportunities, including debt, fixed income, and asset-backed financing.      
•    Drive process automation across finance workflows by identifying inefficiencies and implementing AI agents or RPA solutions.      
•    Partner cross-functionally with Engineering, Operations, and Business leaders to translate strategic goals into actionable financial plans.      
•    Synthesize complex financial data into concise executive summaries, dashboards, and strategic recommendations for leadership.      
          
**You have:**        
•    5–7 years of relevant finance experience in corporate finance, FP&A, treasury, or investment banking.      
•    Proven ability to design and build financial models from scratch with high attention to detail and analytical rigor.      
•    Direct experience with core Treasury functions, including cash investments, operational treasury, and FX/interest hedging.      
•    Strong appetite to learn or existing experience in debt financing, fixed income markets, and asset-backed financing.      
•    Proven ability to work cross-functionally, navigate ambiguity, and communicate complex concepts clearly to diverse stakeholders.      
•    A growth mindset: curious, flexible in thinking, highly coachable, and eager to think big and challenge the status quo.      
          
**We prefer:**          
•    Prior background in investment banking or capital markets.      
•    CFA (Chartered Financial Analyst) charterholder and/or MBA from a top-tier program.      
•    Demonstrated experience with agentification, RPA, or leveraging modern AI and automation tools in finance.      
•    Direct experience or strong familiarity with the autonomous vehicle (AV), mobility, or technology hardware industries.      
•    Direct experience in asset-backed financing, fixed income instruments, or structured debt transactions.      
•    Demonstrated passion for building scalable infrastructure and automating manual workflows end-to-end.      
          
**Travel requirements:**          
•    Maybe up to 10% to participate in conferences and investor meetings potentially
