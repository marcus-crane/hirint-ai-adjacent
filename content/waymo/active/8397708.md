Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

As a member of the Communications team, you’ll be on the cutting edge of bringing autonomous driving technology to the public, developing strategies that will increase understanding, acceptance, and adoption of this life-saving technology. We work with reporters, editors, bloggers, and other influencers to tell stories about Waymo and the potential of our technology to change how we move and live. We partner with the engineering, business, policy, marketing, and product teams to create messaging for new products and other initiatives, counter misinformation, and shape our reputation.

This role follows a hybrid work schedule and reports to the PR Lead, Product & Technology.

**You will:**

* **Own Waymo’s proactive safety narrative and data storytelling:** Partner closely with Waymo’s Senior Director of Safety Science and research teams to analyze safety performance data, launch regular Safety Impact Data Hub updates, and translate peer-reviewed studies into compelling outbound stories.
* **Build and mobilize third-party credibility:** Cultivate strategic alignment and co-storytelling with external researchers, validators, and partners across academia, public health, insurance, and safety organizations to reinforce trust and support Waymo’s license to operate and expand.
* **Drive policy-aligned safety campaigns and sub-narratives:** Translate core safety and policy narratives for broader audiences—spanning first responder training and partnerships, women’s personal safety, teen rider trust, and other priority initiatives
* **Connect safety to Waymo’s technical foundation:**Collaborate with Technical and Product Comms peers to articulate how Waymo’s AI, simulation, hardware, and engineering rigor are purpose-built with safety at the core.
* **Scale bespoke safety campaigns globally:** Direct PR agency partners, oversee contractor support, and collaborate with country and regional PR leads to bring localized safety campaigns to life at the international, national, and local level as Waymo scales in 2027 and beyond.
* **Partner on policy cycles and spokesperson readiness:** Serve as the primary comms handler for Safety Research leadership—prepping spokespeople, stepping in as an on-the-record spokesperson as needed, and partnering with Policy Comms to weave Waymo’s broader safety approach into ongoing policy education and incident response moments.

**You have:**

* **10+ years of communications experience** (in-house or agency), grounded in public policy PR with strong technology or auto communications roots.
* **Proven executive presence and counsel:** Track record serving as the primary comms handler for senior technical, research, or executive leaders—able to advise, challenge, and earn trust from day one.
* **Deep media, validator, and spokesperson instincts:** Strong experience working across policy, business, tech, and science/safety press; experience partnering with third-party validators; and readiness to go on the record as a spokesperson.
* **Agency, contractor, and global campaign leadership:** Experience directing PR agencies, managing extended/contractor support, and collaborating across regional or international teams to land cohesive campaigns.
* **Solid incident-response judgment:** Familiarity with regulatory and issues communications, with the ability to partner smoothly with Policy Comms and Legal on high-stakes, regulatory-level moments without losing focus on proactive storytelling.

**We prefer:**

* **Fluency with data and peer-reviewed research:** Demonstrated ability to turn complex quantitative studies, white papers, and safety benchmarks into clear, accurate outbound narratives.
* **Experience communicating in regulated, safety-critical, or frontier technology spaces** (e.g., public health, mobility, aerospace, AI, insurance, or energy); automotive experience is not required.
* **Ability to translate data-heavy and policy-heavy narratives** into accessible, human-centered stories for broader mainstream audiences.
* **A knack for data storytelling:** experience launching recurring data hubs, transparency reports, or major research publications.
* **Education:** Bachelor’s degree in Communications, Public Policy, Journalism, Public Health, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience).

**Travel Requirements:**

* 10–15% domestic and international travel (estimated up to 15%) for team collaboration, industry/academic conferences, and media events.
