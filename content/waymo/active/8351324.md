Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

You will join the Pre-Construction team within the Global Places and Services (GPS) organization. We assess whether a candidate site can become a Waymo site, running the technical due diligence, test-fits, cost estimates, and schedules. We assess the zoning, power, structural, and site constraints before Waymo commits capital. Our work is what keeps Waymo from committing to a site that cannot be built on. This role follows a hybrid work schedule and reports to the Pre-Construction Lead.

**You will:**

* **Establish the Global Zoning & Entitlements Framework:** Define, author, and standardize the global framework and structured checklists utilized across all regions to assess site viability consistently.
* **Oversee All Zoning Activities:** Oversee the global research and feasibility analysis of obtaining zoning, entitlement, permitting, and municipal approvals for intended projects.
* **Serve as the Subject Matter Expert:** Act as the company's foremost internal authority on complex land-use laws, local zoning codes, and permits, advising Waymo's C-suite and executive leadership on regulatory site risks.
* **Drive Cross-Functional Alignment:** Partner directly with cross-functional teams (e.g., Real Estate) to identify site risks early, assess the overall feasibility of candidate sites, and align on land-use constraints.
* **Build and Lead the Team:** Recruit, mentor, and develop high-performing regional zoning resources, growing individual capabilities and scaling coverage to match the expanding market pipeline.

**You have:**

* **BA/BS Degree:** BA/BS degree Real Estate, Land Use Law, Civil Engineering, Urban Planning, Construction Management, Architecture, or related field (or equivalent practical experience).
* **Zoning and Entitlements Leadership:** 7-10 years of experience in real estate development or entitlement.
* **Subject Matter Expertise:** Deep technical understanding of complex zoning codes, discretionary land-use processes, environmental review acts, and municipal permitting requirements.
* **Multi-Site Experience:** Experience managing multiple development projects simultaneously across markets and regions.

**We prefer:**

* **High-Tech Industrial, or Fleet Entitlements:** Prior experience securing entitlements, zoning variances, or grid interconnects for service centers, fleet facilities, distribution centers, or charging infrastructure.
* **Diverse Environment Expertise:** Experience in high-velocity logistics, automotive, tech-manufacturing, or mixed-use environments (office, industrial, labs, and depots), with an understanding of the different needs between corporate environments and operational or testing sites.
* **Advanced Education and Professional Certifications**: Juris Doctor (JD) specializing in Land Use Law, Master of Urban and Regional Planning (MURP), or equivalent graduate-level training, or active professional certifications.
