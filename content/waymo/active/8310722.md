Waymo is an autonomous driving technology company with the mission to be the world's most trusted driver. Since its start as the Google Self-Driving Car Project in 2009, Waymo has focused on building the Waymo Driver—The World's Most Experienced Driver™—to improve access to mobility while saving thousands of lives now lost to traffic crashes. The Waymo Driver powers Waymo’s fully autonomous ride-hail service and can also be applied to a range of vehicle platforms and product use cases. The Waymo Driver has provided over ten million rider-only trips, enabled by its experience autonomously driving over 100 million miles on public roads and tens of billions in simulation across 15+ U.S. states.

**You will:**

* Develop C++ algorithms to process and interpret lidar, radar, and camera sensor data
* Design and implement efficient processing pipelines for large-scale streaming sensor data
* Collaborate with Perception and Planning teams to understand and define data needs
* Design and develop scalable APIs to make sensor data available to downstream consumers
* Support the deployment, testing, and validation of machine learning models
* Contribute to the creation and refinement of system evaluation tools and processes

**You have:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Robotics, or a related field of study and 5 years of progressive,  post-baccalaureate experience in the job offered or in a Software Engineering-related occupation.
* Alternatively, will accept a Master’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Robotics, or a related field of study and 3 years of experience in the  job offered or in a Software Engineering-related occupation.
* Position requires 3 years of experience in the following:

* + C++ algorithm development for sensor data processing (lidar, radar, and camera)
  + Pipeline design and processing for large-scale streaming sensor data
  + Scalable API design and development for downstream consumers
  + Machine learning model deployment, testing, and validation
  + Data needs definition for perception and planning teams
