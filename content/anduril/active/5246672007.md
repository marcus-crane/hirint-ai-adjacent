Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### About the Job

As a member of ArsenalOS, you will help build the software that powers Anduril’s manufacturing, supply chain, and production operations. Our applications are used across the factory floor and broader hardware enterprise, where software needs to be intuitive, reliable, and capable of scaling alongside rapidly growing production.  
  
We are looking for a **Staff Software Engineer, Frontend Platform** with deep expertise in frontend engineering to help shape how these applications are built across ArsenalOS. You will define frontend architectures, build reusable platform capabilities, and establish patterns that enable product teams to deliver high quality web, mobile, and progressive web experiences at scale.  
  
The ideal candidate has experience building complex production applications as well as the shared systems behind them, including component libraries, application frameworks, developer tooling, frontend infrastructure, and architectural patterns used across multiple teams. You should be comfortable moving between hands on product development and broader technical leadership, identifying common problems and turning them into solutions that create leverage across the organization.  
  
This role requires strong software engineering fundamentals beyond the frontend, thoughtful technical judgment, and the ability to drive architecture and adoption across organizational boundaries.

### Required Qualifications

* Deep experience building and architecting modern web applications using TypeScript, JavaScript, React, and related technologies.
* Experience defining frontend architecture and scalable patterns across large applications or multiple product teams.
* Experience building reusable frontend infrastructure, shared libraries, component systems, or application frameworks.
* Strong understanding of browser fundamentals, application performance, testing, observability, and reliability.
* Strong software engineering fundamentals beyond the frontend, including APIs, authentication and authorization, data modeling, and distributed systems concepts.
* Experience leading technically ambiguous initiatives from architecture through implementation and adoption.
* Ability to identify common problems across teams and turn them into reusable platform capabilities and paved paths.
* Ability to influence technical direction across teams while remaining hands on in production code.
* US Citizen is required as this position needs to access export controlled data.

### Preferred Qualifications

* Experience building frontend platforms, design systems, application frameworks, or shared developer infrastructure.
* Experience with progressive web applications, mobile applications, or React Native.
* Experience with offline first, resilient, kiosk, industrial, or other operationally critical user experiences.
* Experience with monorepos, shared libraries, package management, and large TypeScript codebases.
* Experience with enterprise or manufacturing software such as MES, WMS, PLM, or ERP.
* Eligibility to obtain and maintain a US Secret/TS clearance is preferred.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
