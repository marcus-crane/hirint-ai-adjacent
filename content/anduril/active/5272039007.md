Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### **ABOUT THE TEAM**

The Strategic Defense team at Anduril protects the United States and its allies from ballistic, hypersonic, and air-breathing threats. Those threats now arrive in seconds, maneuver mid-flight, and come in volleys designed to overwhelm the legacy systems meant to stop them — so we're building a software-defined command and control layer that can see, decide, and act faster than any human operator could alone. Our team spans autonomy, tracking, simulation and wargaming, hardware, systems engineering, and test and evaluation to build an accredited fire control system fielded rapidly and at scale. We are at the forefront of incubating and maturing AI-native offerings for the integrated air and missile defense domain.

### **ABOUT THE JOB**

As an SRE in Strategic Defense you’ll rack and provision hardware, build the automation that turns a blank server into a hardened node in a running cluster, design how software reaches systems that are only sometimes connected, and debug failures from the Linux kernel up through the network to the pod. You’ll work closely with software, data and operations engineers to get systems into the hands of customers, deployed and supported on real infrastructure with real users.

Above all, you bring a “Whatever It Takes” mindset: pragmatic, fast, and focused on the mission, with sound engineering judgment about what to build now and what to build next.

#### **WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Provision bare metal at scale. Run the pipeline that takes servers from rack to production: PXE boot, kickstart, BMC/iDRAC, firmware, STIG hardening, and handoff into Kubernetes.
* Run Kubernetes without a managed control plane. Operate self-managed clusters (RKE2, k3s, Talos) on-prem and at the edge: networking, storage, upgrades and capacity.
* Design for disconnected environments. Decide how software and data reach hundreds of nodes that may be offline, with limited bandwidth and short update windows.
* Move software across network boundaries. Ship and update systems on air-gapped and classified networks, including through cross-domain solutions and data diodes.
* Debug at every layer. Trace failures across Linux, the network and Kubernetes, then lead the root cause analysis and postmortem.
* Automate the toil. Write the Python and Bash tooling the fleet runs on, and manage infrastructure as code.
* Deploy with customers. Travel to sites to install and support systems, often as the only engineer there.

#### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Currently possesses and is able to maintain an active U.S. Top Secret security clearance.
* 3-8+ years of hands-on infrastructure, systems or site reliability engineering.
* You can explain Linux process states, system calls, memory, file systems and inodes, systemd, and permissions/SELinux, and you can find the process behind a symptom from the shell without a cloud console.
* You network using first principles: TCP/UDP, DHCP, DNS, routing, VLANs and firewalls. You can trace a request hop by hop from client to pod and name the tool you’d use at each step.
* You know Kubernetes below the managed layer: how Services, kube-proxy, CNI, DNS, scheduling, requests/limits and storage actually work, and how to debug them with kubectl, not a UI.
* You write working Python and Bash from scratch, without looking things up: parsing logs and text, handling files and processes, adding retries and error handling, and reading and fixing someone else’s script.

#### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience building, deploying or operating systems on air-gapped, classified or security-hardened networks.
* Experience designing distributed systems for the edge or for disconnected, intermittent, and limited (DIL) conditions: mesh networks, store-and-forward, peer distribution, QoS.
* Infrastructure as code and GitOps (Terraform, Ansible, ArgoCD/Flux); experience with Nix is a plus.
* Observability stacks (Prometheus, Grafana, Loki, OpenTelemetry) and turning their signals into decisions about alerts and capacity.
* Platform and supply-chain security: image signing, PKI and certificate authorities, disk encryption (LUKS), FIPS.
* Ability to read, debug and modify software written in Go, Python, Rust or C++.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
