**ABOUT THE JOB**

Anduril’s Air Dominance and Strike (AD&S) team is seeking a Senior Staff Mission Systems Mechanical Engineer to join our team. The AD&S team is responsible for developing end-to-end “mission-ized” autonomous air systems capable of delivering exceptional warfighter outcomes to our customers.

As a Senior Staff Mission Systems Mechanical Engineer, you will be responsible for the definition, design, and validation of the mechanical and thermal solution that holds dense electronic and RF hardware together and keeps it alive in its environment. The right person will have experience packaging electronics that are tightly constrained in size, weight, and heat, and will have correlated structural and thermal analysis against test rather than trusting the analysis alone. You will begin with mission-level outcomes — the platform interface, the environment, the mass and volume you are allowed — and work backward to enclosures, load paths, cooling, materials, and the tolerances that make all of it manufacturable. This will require taking ownership of environmental qualification, including the failure investigations and redesigns that come out of it.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO** 

* Own the mechanical design of dense electronic and RF assemblies: enclosures, chassis, card cages, and the interfaces that attach them to the platform.
* Design the thermal solution — conduction paths, heat spreaders, cold plates, interface materials, and liquid cooling.
* Perform structural analysis for the real environment: random vibration, shock, modal response and thermal cycling.
* Drive designs through environmental qualification to MIL-STD-810 or program equivalents, including failure investigation and redesign.
* Own tolerance analysis and GD&T, and work with machining, casting, additive and sheet-metal suppliers on manufacturability and cost.
* Select materials with attention to CTE matching, stiffness-to-weight, corrosion and galvanic compatibility, and environmental sealing.
* Mentor junior mechanical engineers and raise the standard of analysis on the team.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS** 

* B.S. in Mechanical Engineering or a related field and 8+ years designing electronic or electromechanical hardware. M.S. and 6+ years also considered.
* Proficiency in a major 3D CAD system (NX, SolidWorks, Creo or equivalent) and in formal GD&T.
* Demonstrated thermal and structural analysis ability (FEA and/or CFD), with evidence of correlating analysis to test.
* Experience taking hardware through environmental qualification.
* Direct experience working with suppliers and shops on design for manufacture, tolerances and cost.
* Able to obtain a US Security Clearance

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS** 

* Knowledge of current and next-generation advanced radar systems.
* Experience with missile design & integration.
* Thermal management of high-power-density electronics, including liquid cooling loops.
* Packaging for microwave or millimeter-wave assemblies, where mechanical tolerance drives electrical performance.
* Additive manufacturing for end-use parts, and composite or lightweight structures.
* Airborne, marine or ground-vehicle environments and the standards that come with them.
* An active U.S. Department of Defense Secret clearance, or eligibility to obtain one.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
