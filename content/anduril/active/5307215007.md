**About the Team**

Anduril's Manufacturing team is producing the next generation of defense systems globally at rate. The Manufacturing Engineering team owns production readiness and process engineering across assigned product lines — from electrical hardware build and integration through to full system-level test. Production Sustaining Engineering sits at the intersection of Design Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality, and Supply Chain — keeping fielded and in-production products healthy while driving the process and test improvements that make production better every week.

**About the Job**

You will be the primary electrical product sustaining engineering presence for the Dive-XL Product on the production floor, bridging the gap between product design intent and what actually happens on the manufacturing floor. You own the lifecycle of in-production electrical products from a manufacturing perspective: triaging production issues, driving root-cause investigations, owning corrective actions, and ensuring design fixes land cleanly in the production process. You will also be a SME for the production and design engineering team for in-process test systems — including the Python-based test automation software that runs them — so that every unit ships with verified functionality, performance, and quality.

This is a staff-level individual contributor role embedded within the Global Head of Production organization with a dotted-line relationship to Platform and Payload Engineering Teams. The right person is equal parts electrical engineer and pragmatic software developer: comfortable designing a breakout board in the morning, writing Python instrument-control code after lunch, and leading an 8D investigation on a field return before end of day.

You are expected to drive systemic improvements to product quality, test coverage, and production throughput — not just resolve individual failures — and to develop process, tooling, and test platform solutions that scale across product versions, variants, and manufacturing sites. Equally important is seeing those improvements all the way through: securing buy-in, implementing the change on the floor, and proving with data that it held.

If you thrive in a fast-paced, resource-limited environment where the framework is yours to build, this role is for you.

**What You'll Do**

**Product Sustaining & Production Support**

* Serve as the primary electrical engineering point of contact on the production floor for assigned programs and product lines — the person production and quality teams call first when something electrical is wrong.
* Triage and resolve production failures in real time, distinguishing between product defects, test system faults, process escapes, and test-limit issues to minimize production hold time.
* Provide rapid-response engineering support to Production, Quality, and Test teams for nonconformance resolution, use-as-is / rework / scrap decisions, and repair dispositions, exercising engineering judgment within defined authority boundaries.
* Own root-cause analysis for complex or cross-functional electrical defects using structured problem-solving methodology (8D, A3, 5-Why, Ishikawa). Partner with Design Engineering, Quality, Supply Chain and Production to drive corrective and preventive actions to closure.
* Identify and escalate systemic product design issues surfaced by production and test data to core Design Engineering teams with clear problem statements, data packages, proposed design changes, and recommended corrective actions.
* Participate in Material Review Board (MRB) activities for electrical and test-related discrepancies and drive resolution timelines.
* Capture lessons learned from production and field events and actively disseminate them into NPI programs so the same failure mode is not designed back in.
* Develop and maintain the Electrical Troubleshooting and approved repair manual, along with engineering disposition guides that give Production the autonomy to resolve high-frequency, low-complexity issues without an engineering hold.

**End-of-Line Test System Design & Development**

* Become the Technical SME for the end-of-line production test systems including test fixtures, cable harnesses, interface boards, automation and instrumentation configurations for assigned product lines.
* Define test requirements in collaboration with Design Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Quality, and Production teams to ensure adequate coverage of functional, performance, and safety-critical parameters.
* Select and integrate test instrumentation (power supplies, DMMs, oscilloscopes, signal generators, data acquisition systems, electronic loads) into automated test stations.
* Design custom interface electronics (breakout boards, signal conditioning circuits, relay switching matrices) to enable reliable and repeatable automated testing.
* Develop and validate test procedures that balance thorough fault coverage with production cycle-time requirements.

**Test Software Development (Python)**

* Assist with the development and maintenance of Python-based test automation software for production test stations, including instrument control, data acquisition, test sequencing, and pass/fail determination.
* Build reusable Python libraries and frameworks for instrument communication (VISA/SCPI), test sequence management, result logging, and integration with manufacturing execution systems (MES).
* Implement data collection and analysis pipelines to capture test results, parametric measurements, and yield metrics in structured formats for downstream analysis.
* Develop user-facing test operator interfaces (GUIs) that guide production personnel through test procedures with clear prompts, real-time feedback, and unambiguous pass/fail indications.
* Maintain version-controlled test software repositories with proper branching, code review, and release management practices.

**Continuous Improvement, Process & Tooling**

* Proactively identify production bottlenecks, yield detractors, process constraints, and test-escape trends using data-driven analysis. Lead improvement initiatives end to end — from data, to countermeasure, to implementation on the floor, to verified and sustained result.
* **Identify and drive process- and tool-based solutions that reduce the manual translation of electrical design intent into production execution** — eliminating hand-transcribed test limits, manually maintained wire and connector lists, and one-off station configurations across product variants and sites.
* **Evaluate and improve the key tool and functional interfaces that impact design-to-production efficiency** — PLM workflows, digital work instructions, ECO flow-down, BOM and as-built configuration reconciliation, and the MES-to-test-data handoff. Own the problem statement, the business case, and the implementation plan for changing them.
* **Own the implementation, not just the recommendation.** Secure stakeholder buy-in, sequence the rollout, update the affected work instructions, control plans, test software, and training, and confirm through post-implementation data that the improvement held and did not simply move the problem.
* Own or contribute to PFMEA development for electrical assemblies and test processes; ensure Control Plans are linked, current, and actually reflected in the work instructions and test coverage on the floor.
* Develop scalable, portable test system architectures that can be replicated across multiple manufacturing sites with minimal site-specific customization.
* Develop scalable frameworks for multi-site production: configuration management alignment, site-specific work instruction adaptation, and structured cross-site sharing of lessons learned and proven countermeasures.
* Build repeatable playbooks for standing up electrical build and test of new product variants, reducing ramp time, first-unit defects, and test system bring-up time at each additional site.
* Create comprehensive test system documentation including schematics, BOMs, setup procedures, calibration requirements, and troubleshooting guides.
* Support the stand-up and ramp of test capabilities at new or expanding manufacturing sites, ensuring test engineering knowledge transfers effectively.

**Cross-Functional Collaboration & Metrics**

* Maintain strong working relationships with Quality, Supply Chain, Manufacturing Engineering, Design Engineering, and Program Management to ensure aligned priorities and rapid issue resolution.
* Track and report key production and test metrics: first-pass yield, test cycle time, test escape rate, retest rate, test system uptime/availability, repeat-discrepancy rate, and engineering response time to production holds.
* Measure and report the realized impact of improvement actions — cycle time, yield, touch labor, or scrap reduction — and the percentage of corrective actions verified effective after implementation.
* Collate and drive corrective actions on design-originated production issues, providing data-backed prioritization to core Design Engineering teams.
* Participate in Production Readiness Reviews, design reviews (DFM/DFA/DFT), and engineering change assessments as the sustaining engineering voice.
* Work with design engineers to embed Design for Testability (DFT) and Design for Manufacturing (DFM) principles in product design before production release.
* Flow test data insights, failure mode trends, and process improvement opportunities back to Design Engineering through structured feedback mechanisms.

**Key Competencies & Attributes**

* **Engineering Judgement:** Technical depth combined with production pragmatism — able to make sound engineering decisions under time pressure without over-engineering solutions.
* **Systems Thinking:** Thinks beyond individual issue resolution to identify patterns, root causes, and scalable solutions that prevent recurrence across sites and variants.
* **Bias for Implementation:** Treats an improvement as finished only when it is live on the floor, documented, trained, adopted, and demonstrably holding — not when the analysis is complete or the recommendation is made.
* **Cross-Functional Influence:** Credibility with both design engineers and production operators; able to speak both languages and bridge communication gaps.
* **Autonomy & Initiative:** Comfort with ambiguity and competing priorities in a fast-moving production environment; able to self-direct and prioritize without constant direction.
* **Pragmatic Software Discipline:** Writes maintainable, version-controlled, documented code that another engineer can own — balancing production urgency against the long-term supportability of the test framework.
* **Communication:** Clear, concise technical communication — written dispositions, engineering reports, and presentations to leadership.
