Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

## ABOUT THE JOB

Anduril Ground Maneuver is the business line that brings Anduril’s sensors, effectors, communications, command and control, and Lattice to military ground vehicles. Our work covers wheeled and tracked platforms, from light tactical vehicles to armored vehicles. Our customers range from Army and Marine Corps program offices to the units that operate these vehicles in the field.

We’re hiring a mechanical engineer for a products that will be fielded across many vehicle types. You’ll join while the design is still taking shape, and carry it through design reviews, integration and test, and into production. You’ll own how the system mounts to a vehicle, how it survives the ride and the environment, and how it packages the compute, power and cabling inside a rugged enclosure. You’ll also help define the mechanical interfaces other Anduril teams and outside partners build to. Other products will be built around those interfaces for years, so they need to be right.

You’ll work closely with systems, electrical, software and mission systems engineers. You’ll also work with product teams in other Anduril business lines and with government engineers and operators.

## WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Own the mechanical design of the kit across vehicle classes.
* Design the enclosure, mounts and brackets that attach the kit to wheeled and tracked vehicles, including the rugged packaging around the PCBAs inside, and define the mechanical interface between the base kit and the mission kits.
* Own thermal design and survivability: reject heat across the full temperature and duty-cycle envelope, and design for the shock, vibration and environment of a ground vehicle, with the analysis and test that proves it.
* Own cable routing, harness strain relief and serviceability so the kit can be installed, accessed and repaired in the field.
* Turn product and customer needs into mechanical requirements with traceability and a verification method, and write the mechanical ICDs — mounting patterns, envelopes, keep-outs, connector locations — with an owner and sign-off on both sides.
* Lead the mechanical content of design reviews (SRR, PDR, CDR), and run trade studies on packaging, materials, weight and manufacturability with clear recommendations to the Chief Engineer.
* Work directly with engineers in other Anduril business lines whose products will ride on the kit — understand their needs and hold the line on the mechanical standard.
* Work hands-on in the lab and on vehicles during integration and test.

## REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* B.S. in Mechanical Engineering or a related field
* 4+ years of mechanical engineering experience, including leading mechanical design on a hardware development program through at least PDR
* Experience designing ruggedized electronics enclosures, mounts or structures and integrating them onto military or commercial ground vehicles
* Strong CAD skills (SolidWorks, Creo, NX or similar) and experience owning a design through release to manufacturing
* Working knowledge of thermal management and structural analysis (hand calcs and FEA) for electronics in harsh environments
* Experience designing to shock and vibration requirements, including MIL-STD-810
* Experience writing mechanical ICDs and owning an interface across teams or companies
* Must be able to obtain and hold a U.S. Secret security clearance

## PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Experience on Army ground vehicle programs (Stryker, JLTV, AMPV, Abrams or similar) or with DEVCOM GVSC
* Familiarity with VICTORY, CMOSS or other open-architecture vehicle standards
* Experience designing a product line or platform that ships in multiple variants
* Experience designing enclosures and mechanical packaging for PCBAs, working closely with electrical engineers on board-level fit, mounting and thermal interface
* Experience with thermal/CFD design or cooling of high-power electronics
* Experience across enclosure fabrication methods (sheet metal, machined, cast, additive) and the supply base behind them
* Familiarity with MIL-STD-461 (EMI) and its impact on enclosure design
* Active U.S. Secret security clearance
