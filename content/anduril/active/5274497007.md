### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Maritime team at Anduril builds autonomous undersea and surface vehicles and seabed sensors. Dive-XL, our extra-large autonomous undersea vehicle, is a complex system of sealed subsystems, payloads, mission software and heavy support equipment. As part of this team, you will build the Product Operations function for Dive-XL within a larger deployment and sustainment group, working closely with engineering, product support, reliability, quality, field operations, maintenance and program management.

### ABOUT THE ROLE

As the Product Operations Manager for Dive-XL, you will own how field and test issues on a high-value fleet are tracked from first report to a verified fix. Dive-XL will be operated by Anduril deployed teams and customer crews, and changes to it move through versioned software releases and controlled engineering changes. You will make sure every issue is understood, routed to the product support, reliability, quality or engineering owner, tied to the release or change that fixes it, and confirmed in the field. You will also use the fleet's issue history to show where reliability and cost to fix can improve. Many issues are diagnosed from vehicle logs, mission recordings and telemetry, so this role needs a leader who can dig into the data personally. If you are passionate about cutting-edge technology, advancing national security, and delivering best-in-class operational support, Anduril invites you to join the team.

### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* **Own the Issue Lifecycle.** Run the Dive-XL issue register from first report to closure, making sure each issue is clearly described, prioritized, assigned to the product support, reliability, quality or engineering owner, and tied to the software release or engineering change that resolves it.
* **Diagnose Across the System.** Work with field teams and engineers to isolate faults using vehicle logs, mission recordings, telemetry dashboards and operator diagnostics, across vehicle software, payloads, navigation, power and support equipment.
* **Work Across Teams.** Coordinate across engineering, production, product support, reliability, quality, field operations, maintenance and program management, teams that sit in different organizations and locations, to keep every issue moving and every owner clear.
* **Close the Loop on Fixes.** Work with the product support, reliability and quality engineers who own root cause and corrective action. Track software fixes through release testing, release notes and fleet updates, and hardware fixes through engineering change and retrofit, then verify in the field that each fix worked.
* **Support New Production.** As new Dive-XL production, payloads and vehicle updates come online, set up how build, acceptance and early field issues are captured and fed back to engineering, so new vehicles enter service ready to be supported.
* **Trend Failures and Cost.** Analyze the fleet's issue history to rank failure modes by mission impact, recurrence and cost to fix, and bring the results to engineering and program leadership.
* **Deployed Team and Mission Support.** Manage communication on high-priority incidents and make sure deployed teams and operators get timely guidance, workarounds and release information.
* **Build the Playbook.** Mentor the people who work issues with you, and standardize how issues are reported, how logs and data are captured, and how fixes are verified as the fleet grows.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
