Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### About the Job

Oracle SCM is central to how Anduril plans, procures, builds, moves, and costs the materials behind its products. As the Senior ERP Administrator for Oracle SCM, you will serve as the functional owner and subject matter expert for the capabilities supporting our Design-to-Build and Procure-to-Pay processes. This is an individual-contributor role reporting to the ERP Technical Manager and acting as the bridge between business stakeholders, Enterprise Architecture, Oracle, and our technical delivery teams. Enterprise Architects define what a solution must accomplish and why; you will own how, when, and where that solution is configured, implemented, tested, deployed, and supported in Oracle SCM.

### What You’ll Do

* Own the functional health, support, enhancement delivery, and operational stability of Oracle SCM across Design-to-Build and Procure-to-Pay.
* Serve as the primary Oracle SCM functional point of contact for business stakeholders, Enterprise Architects, Oracle, and technical delivery teams.
* Partner with supply chain, procurement, manufacturing, and operations stakeholders to understand business needs, prioritize enhancements, and improve end-to-end processes.
* Translate business requirements and architectural direction into scalable Oracle SCM functional designs, configuration, and implementation plans.
* Perform hands-on functional configuration and solution enablement across procurement, inventory, manufacturing, planning, cost management, and related SCM capabilities; this role does not include software development responsibilities.
* Own functional delivery from discovery through production deployment, including release planning, production readiness, cutover, and Hypercare.
* Coordinate with OIC Development, Oracle Development, Security, Enterprise Architecture, Finance, and QA partners to deliver integrated ERP solutions with clear dependencies and ownership.
* Lead functional discovery workshops, solution validation, system integration testing, user acceptance testing, regression testing, and business validation.
* Develop end-to-end test scenarios and own functional defect triage, prioritization, and coordination through resolution.
* Support production operations through incident management, root cause analysis, and problem management, while monitoring functional health and operational performance.
* Identify process improvements and new Oracle SCM capabilities that make business operations more effective while keeping solutions practical, scalable, and supportable.
* Maintain functional designs, configuration records, testing evidence, release documentation, and operational procedures for the SCM domain.

### Required Qualifications

* 7+ years of experience in ERP functional administration, implementation, or consulting, including significant hands-on ownership of Oracle Cloud SCM.
* Deep functional expertise configuring Oracle SCM across procurement, inventory management, manufacturing, planning, cost management, or closely related capabilities.
* Demonstrated experience supporting Design-to-Build, Procure-to-Pay, or comparable end-to-end manufacturing and supply chain processes.
* Proven ownership of ERP enhancements across discovery, functional design, configuration, testing, deployment, Hypercare, and production support.
* Experience leading system integration testing, user acceptance testing, regression testing, business validation, and functional defect resolution.
* Strong understanding of how Oracle SCM configuration interacts with integrations, security, data, financial processes, and adjacent enterprise systems.
* Demonstrated ability to partner with business stakeholders and Enterprise Architects, preserve solution intent, and convert ambiguous requirements into executable functional outcomes.
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to influence business leaders, architects, engineers, vendors, and cross-functional partners.
* Degree in Information Systems, Supply Chain Management, Business, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
* U.S. Person status is required as this position needs to access export controlled data.

### Preferred Qualifications

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
