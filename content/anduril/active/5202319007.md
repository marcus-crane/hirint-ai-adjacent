### About the Job

The Enterprise Architecture team improves the systems and internal technologies that enable Anduril to accomplish its mission. Teams across engineering, supply chain, manufacturing, operations, accounting, finance, and growth rely on these capabilities to work effectively as the company scales.  
  
As Technical Program Manager for Enterprise Architecture, you will bring structure, visibility, and disciplined execution to a team whose work spans the enterprise. You will support both architects who work across multiple systems and specialists who go deep on platforms such as PLM, ERP, MES, and WMS. You will give them the planning, tracking, coordination, and communication scaffolding they need to deliver—without introducing process that slows them down. This role requires enough technical fluency to engage credibly across systems, enough business judgment to keep work aligned with company priorities, and the ownership to impose order without waiting to be told how.

### What You’ll Do

* Own the execution framework for Enterprise Architecture initiatives, translating ambiguous work into clear scope, plans, milestones, owners, dependencies, risks, and measurable outcomes.
* Support both cross-system architects and single-system specialists with planning, tracking, and communication tailored to how they work.
* Coordinate product managers, developers, business analysts, subject matter experts, and functional partners whose contributions are required to deliver architecture initiatives.
* Maintain a reliable, current view of the team’s portfolio in a single system of record, prioritizing work against business need and ensuring commitments remain visible.
* Identify project risks and cross-system dependencies early, drive issues to resolution, and escalate decisions with clear context and recommended paths forward.
* Translate technical activity into concise business-facing reporting that communicates delivery health, measurable impact, and prioritization trade-offs to leadership and stakeholders.
* Establish and run lightweight operating cadences for planning, status, risk review, decision-making, and launch readiness without adding unnecessary bureaucracy.
* Facilitate documentation and knowledge transfer so architecture decisions, dependencies, assumptions, and best practices remain accessible and durable.
* Continuously improve how the team plans, tracks, and communicates its work while adapting quickly to changing business priorities.

### Required Qualifications

* 7+ years of experience in technical project management, technical program management, systems delivery, or a closely related role, preferably supporting architecture or adjacent teams.
* Demonstrated experience leading complex, cross-functional technical projects with responsibility for scope, milestones, dependencies, risks, stakeholder alignment, and outcomes.
* Broad technical fluency across enterprise systems such as PLM, ERP, MES, and WMS, with the ability to hold credible conversations about architecture, integrations, data flows, and delivery trade-offs.
* Proven ability to create structure and accountability in fast-moving, ambiguous environments while using lightweight practices that preserve team speed.
* Strong problem-solving and systems-thinking capabilities, with the ability to develop fact-based recommendations using methods such as root-cause analysis and decision trees.
* Strong reporting instincts and excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to translate technical work for business, technical, and executive audiences.
* Proficiency with project management tools and methodologies, including portfolio visibility, dependency tracking, risk management, and status reporting.
* Degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Engineering, Business, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
* Ability to work in-office full time and travel on a limited basis, less than 10%.
* U.S. Person status is required as this position needs to access export-controlled data.

### Preferred Qualifications

* Experience supporting enterprise architects, solutions architects, or platform teams whose portfolios span multiple business domains and technology systems.
* Experience delivering initiatives across two or more enterprise platforms such as PLM, ERP, MES, WMS, CRM, finance, or manufacturing systems.
* Experience building portfolio reporting and operating cadences that connect technical delivery to business impact and leadership prioritization.
* Experience in hyper growth startup-like environments, with demonstrated success balancing speed, ambiguity, and long-term operational health.
* Familiarity with AI-enabled project management, documentation, analysis, or software delivery workflows.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
