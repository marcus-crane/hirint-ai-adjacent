Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### **ABOUT THE TEAM**

The Voyager team in Anduril Industries is an engineering and design division with a vast experience in developing innovative computing and communications solutions for the network edge. The team specializes in integrating enterprise networking capabilities from global IT leaders with purpose-built hardware and software platforms designed to meet market demands for the most stringent of environmental requirements. 

### **ABOUT THE JOB**

The Voyager production team deliver products to third party customers, and to other Anduril business lines. We are looking for a Production Program Manager to join our growing team. In this role, you will drive the operational cadence of our production business - building execution plans, analysing production data, and maintaining the dashboards that keep stakeholders aligned. You will act as a cross-functional liaison, translating complex data into clear, actionable insights and proactively unblocking bottlenecks before they impact delivery. This requires strong analytical skills, advanced Excel proficiency, ERP experience, and a willingness to leverage AI tooling to work more effectively.  

### **WHAT YOU’LL DO**

**Operational Execution & Planning** 

* **Works with the Production Manager to manage the operational cadence:** Manage and execute the "routine rhythms" of the production business, ensuring key meetings, reporting cycles, and deliverables are completed on time.

* Works with cross functional teams (Supply Chain, Quality, NPI, Logistics. Production Engineers) to gather/collate/report production schedule requirements.

* **Create plans that t**ranslate high-level business objectives into concrete, structured execution objectives for the production team.

* **Aggregates and analyses data - g**athers, cleans, and synthesizes complex operational and production data from various sources to provide a clear picture of performance and bottlenecks.

* Builds and maintains spreadsheets, dashboards, and financial/operational models to track KPIs and support decision-making.

**Stakeholder Management & Communication** 

* **Acts as a cross-functional team liaison point – connects and proactively works with** key stakeholders, including manager, DivOps, the 1P team, VP, and the production floor.

* Translates complex data and production updates into concise, actionable insights for stakeholders at all levels of the organization.

* **Drives alignment by f**acilitating communication across teams to resolve dependencies and ensure everyone is aligned on production priorities.

**Problem Solving & AI-Leverage** 

* **Rapid Problem Identification:** Proactively monitor operations to identify bottlenecks, risks, and inefficiencies before they impact production.

* **High-Speed Execution:** Act as a high-bandwidth generalist who can rapidly unblock issues and implement solutions with a strong bias for action.

* **Leverage Modern Tooling (Claude/AI):** Integrate AI assistants (like Claude) into daily workflows to draft communications, synthesize data, and accelerate problem-solving.

 

### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 4–8 years of experience in operations, consulting, program management, or a fast-paced startup environment.

* Experience using Salesforce, Oracle and/or SAP or similar transferable skills

* High level of business acumen

* Knowledge of hardware production environment with high value components and BOM’s

* Highly adaptable; comfortable stepping into ambiguous situations and creating order out of chaos.

* Advanced Excel skills are a must. Experience leveraging LLMs (like Claude) to optimise work output is highly valued.

### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* MSc in Supply Chain, Logistics, or Data Analytics

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
