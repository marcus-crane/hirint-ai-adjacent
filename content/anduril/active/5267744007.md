## ABOUT THE ROLE

We are looking for a Senior Manager to build and lead the Electrical System Test team in Costa Mesa, CA. The team creates the test systems, harnesses, and DUT configurations that let our hardware get through development, qualification, verification, and validation testing, including EMC and power quality campaigns. You will be accountable for that work across every program the team supports.

You will hire, develop, and manage the engineers on the team, set the standards they work to, and decide where they are assigned as program demand shifts. You will own the test-readiness schedule across programs: test hardware, fixtures, and support equipment delivered on time, DUTs configured and ready when the lab is, and risks raised early enough to act on.

You will work alongside the EMC Engineering, avionics, hardware, software, and program management leads, and represent the team in program reviews and with customers. You will stay close enough to the technical work to review test configurations, unblock engineers, and lead root cause investigations when hardware fails in test or in the field.

## WHAT YOU’LL DO

* **Core responsibility:** Lead and grow the Electrical System Test team. Hire, develop, and manage the engineers on the team, build the hiring plan as programs grow, and develop people who can take on more scope.
* Assign engineers across programs based on priority and hardware availability, and rebalance as demand changes.
* Own test readiness across all supported programs — test systems, harnesses, breakout interfaces, fixtures, and support equipment delivered to the EMC and qualification teams on schedule.
* Maintain one integrated view of test schedule, hardware availability, and risk across programs. Escalate early and remove blockers that cause dead time in the lab.
* Set team standards for test system design, harness and breakout definition, DUT configuration, and test setup documentation, and build a reusable library of harnesses, breakout boxes, and fixtures that carries from program to program.
* Partner with software and firmware leads so test modes, stimulation, and telemetry are built in early, and review test configurations before major test events so the DUT is in a known, verified state for EMC and MIL-STD-704 power quality testing.
* Lead root cause investigations for test and field failures in support of vehicle integration and fielded operations.
* Own the team’s headcount plan, labor budget, and test equipment and lab capacity plan, so capacity keeps pace with the program pipeline.
* Support proposals and new starts. Define the test approach, estimate test hardware and labor, and write the test portions of bases of estimate.
* Represent the team in design reviews, test readiness reviews, and program reviews. Report status, risk, and resource needs to program management and engineering leadership.

## REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering or equivalent.
* 12+ years of experience in electrical test, hardware test, or test systems engineering for aerospace, defense, or similarly regulated hardware.
* 5+ years managing engineers, including hiring, performance management, and career development.
* Experience leading test readiness and schedule across multiple programs or product lines at the same time.
* Hands-on background designing test systems and electrical harnesses, and the ability to review schematics, wiring diagrams, and cable harness drawings.
* Working knowledge of qualification and verification testing for avionics or vehicle electrical systems, including how test configuration and DUT state affect results.
* Working knowledge of MIL-STD-461, MIL-STD-464, and MIL-STD-704, and what a test campaign needs from the test team to close those requirements.
* Familiarity with common electrical bus interfaces (CAN, Ethernet, RS-422, RS-232, MIL-STD-1553).
* Track record of root cause investigations driven to corrective action.
* Experience owning a team budget and headcount plan, and estimating test labor and hardware for proposals or program plans.
* Experience working with suppliers and external test labs, including scoping work and holding them to cost and schedule.
* Experience supporting customer or government reviews of test readiness, test results, or test records.
* Clear written and verbal communication with engineers, program managers, and customers.
* Willingness to travel to test facilities and field sites, approximately 15%.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance.

## PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
