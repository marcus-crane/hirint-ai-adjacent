**About The Job**  
Anduril is seeking an Integration & Test Engineer, Electrical Systems to join the Maneuver Dominance team at our Costa Mesa HQ to own integration and test of electrical systems for Omen, an advanced Group 3 VTOL tail-sitting aircraft. This individual will own integration and test activities across electrical systems including avionics, power systems, actuator and motor controls, mission systems, networking, sensors, and harnessing. This is a hands-on, build-and-run role suited to someone who wants to own a test campaign end to end: requirements, tooling, execution, and reporting.  
  
**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Support bring-up, functional checkout, and troubleshooting of electrical hardware across multiple vehicle systems
* Develop and execute right-sized test plans and procedures for electrical component and subsystem integration
* Coordinate with design engineers across disciplines (electrical, GNC, mission systems, flight software) to burn-down design risks and demonstrate system maturity
* Use standard electrical test instrumentation (multimeter, oscilloscope, logic analyzer, spectrum and network analyzer) to characterize hardware and diagnose failures
* Write and maintain test scripts and tooling (Python or similar) to automate data capture, sequencing, and reporting
* Follow and maintain safety protocols for high voltage and stored energy hazards during integration and test activities
* Maintain configuration control records for test hardware, tooling, and firmware/software versions
* Support Factory Acceptance Testing (FAT), ground test, and flight test activities for prototype and production representative systems

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS AND ATTRIBUTES**

* Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Mechatronics, or related engineering discipline
* 1+ years of experience in integration and test, electrical hardware bring-up, or embedded systems/firmware work with hands-on hardware validation
* Demonstrated hands-on proficiency with multimeters, oscilloscopes, and logic analyzers
* Working software/firmware literacy (C, Python, or similar) sufficient to read embedded code, write test scripts, and reason through firmware behavior during hardware debug
* Comfort working across more than one electrical domain (power electronics, controls, communications)
* Fluency in at least one common communication protocol (CAN, Ethernet, or Serial)
* Willingness and demonstrated aptitude to work safely with high voltage systems (400V+) under supervision
* Basic EMI/EMC fluency (conducted/radiated susceptibility or emissions)
* Wiring harness design, sizing, or fabrication experience
* Extremely organized and detail-oriented, with clear verbal and written communication skills
* A sincere commitment to a positive, inclusive, and collaborative culture
* Defense industry background not required, but genuine interest in the mission is
* Eligible to obtain and maintain a security clearance in United States, United Kingdom, or Australia
