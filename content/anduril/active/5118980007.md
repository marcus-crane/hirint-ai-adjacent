Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Software Platform organization is seeking a Software Engineer to join Anduril’s Tactical Networking Team. We build and operate the Layer 3 networking infrastructure that connects Anduril's autonomous systems and operators across denied, degraded, intermittent, and limited (DDIL) network environments. We own the full network lifecycle — secure VPN tunnels over tactical radios and satellite links, PKI and cryptographic identity for every node in the mesh, intelligent QoS traffic orchestration, and real-time network observability and management.  
  
Security is foundational to everything we build. Every link in the Lattice mesh is authenticated and encrypted, backed by a purpose-built PKI that handles certificate issuance, rotation, and revocation across thousands of intermittently connected nodes. We design to meet rigorous government compliance standards while remaining operationally practical for warfighters in the field.

#### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* Design and implement Layer 3 networking for DDIL environments — VPN tunnel management, dynamic routing, and multi-path failover across tactical radios, satellite, and wired transports
* Build radio integration layers that abstract diverse tactical waveforms behind a unified networking interface
* Design QoS scheduling and traffic shaping to ensure mission-critical data flows under severe bandwidth constraints
* Build network management and orchestration systems for real-time topology configuration and adaptation
* Architect PKI infrastructure for the Lattice mesh — certificate authorities, automated issuance/renewal, revocation (CRL/OCSP), and secure node bootstrapping
* Design certificate lifecycle management that handles rotation and re-keying across nodes with intermittent connectivity
* Implement zero-trust identity verification across all mesh communication paths
* Treat network observability as a first-class citizen — build Prometheus metrics, Grafana dashboards, eBPF-based instrumentation, and CLI tooling for topology inspection, link health, and certificate status
* Debug complex networking issues from packet captures on deployed assets to cloud infrastructure

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 3+ years of professional software engineering experience
* Proficiency in a systems programming language (Rust, C, C++, or Go)
* Strong understanding of Layer 3 networking: IP routing, tunneling (WireGuard, IPsec), TCP/UDP, socket programming
* Experience building distributed or networked systems (SDN/SD-WANs, VPNs, routing daemons, network control planes, or similar)
* Understanding of cryptographic fundamentals: symmetric/asymmetric encryption, digital signatures, certificate chains, TLS
* Experience designing or operating PKI systems (certificate authorities, ACME, EST, or custom issuance protocols)
* Familiarity with asynchronous programming models
* Comfort with network debugging tools (tcpdump, Wireshark, iproute2)
* Must be eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Experience with FIPS 140-2/140-3 validated cryptographic modules
* Experience with post-quantum cryptography (PQC) algorithms, migration strategies, and CSNA 2.0 compliance.
* Familiarity with HSMs or TPMs for key protection
* Experience with dynamic routing protocols (OSPF, BGP) or software-defined networking
* Experience integrating with tactical radio systems or military waveforms
* Familiarity with Kubernetes networking (CNI plugins, service mesh, network policies)

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
