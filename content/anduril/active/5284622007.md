Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**ABOUT THE JOB**

As the Head of Talent Acquisition, EMEA, you own the build-out of Anduril's talent acquisition capability across  
the EMEA region. Reporting to the Senior Director of Talent Acquisition and partnering closely with the Head of  
Strategic Planning for Talent Acquisition, you are a key member of the TA Leadership team with a specific  
mandate: geo-specific scaling. You own the in-country, site-specific, and division-specific hiring build-out across  
EMEA, working in close partnership with the Head of Talent Acquisition, APAC and with each Divisional Head of  
TA who is adding headcount into your region.

This is not a divisional leadership seat. You are not embedded in any single business unit's leadership team; you  
are the connective tissue across all of them for one geography. Multiple divisions will be scaling into EMEA at  
once, often into the same countries and talent pools, and your job is to make sure their combined demand lands  
on a coherent, compliant, and well-resourced regional hiring capability, rather than five uncoordinated efforts  
competing for the same candidates.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

**Regional Strategy and Build-Out**  
• Define and execute Anduril's EMEA talent acquisition strategy, directly informed by the combined hiring  
demand of every division scaling into the region.  
• Own EMEA hiring outcomes (time-to-fill, quality, acceptance rates) as the single accountable owner for  
regional delivery.  
• Own EMEA hiring governance, compliance, and workforce planning across the region's countries, each with  
distinct labor law, works council, and candidate market dynamics.  
• Develop EMEA hiring playbooks, and partner with the Head of Talent Acquisition, APAC to share what's  
working across both regions rather than each building in isolation.  
• Partner with the Head of Strategic Planning for Talent Acquisition to evaluate where EMEA needs additional  
in-country or sub-regional leadership as demand grows (e.g., a dedicated UK or Germany lead), and help  
build the case and the structure for it.  
  
**Cross-Divisional Partnership and Prioritization**  
**•** Serve as the senior talent advisor to EMEA-based business leaders and to every Divisional Head of TA adding  
headcount into the region, synthesizing what could otherwise be five disconnected divisional views into one  
coherent regional talent picture.  
• Partner with in-region recruiting resources and EMEA business leaders to ensure the regional model reflects  
on-the-ground reality, not just headquarters assumptions.  
• Act as the tie-breaker when multiple divisions' hiring demand converges on the same country, site, or talent  
pool with more need than the region can serve at once, using an objective, business-impact-driven  
framework.  
• Drive continuous improvement through data and feedback loops, standardizing what should be consistent  
across EMEA while preserving the flexibility each country and division genuinely needs.

**Operational Cohesion with Peer Regions**  
**•** Partner closely with the Head of Strategic Planning for Talent Acquisition, a peer, not a manager, who is  
accountable for cross-regional cohesion, shared playbooks, and consistency of operating rhythm across  
EMEA and APAC.  
• Loop in the Head of Strategic Planning early on build-out choices (tooling, process, governance) so that  
EMEA and APAC remain coherent for divisions scaling into both regions.  
• Resolve cross-regional alignment issues collaboratively, escalating to the Senior Director of Talent  
Acquisition only when peer alignment can't be reached.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**  
**•** 10+ years of progressive TA experience, including demonstrated success building or scaling EMEA hiring  
specifically, not just supporting it from a distance.  
• Deep understanding of EMEA labor markets, employment law, works councils, and compliance  
considerations across the region's key countries.  
• Proven experience designing and executing an end-to-end regional talent acquisition strategy.  
• Demonstrated ability to lead and influence across a matrixed structure, partnering with multiple Divisional  
Heads of TA and business leaders who do not report to you.  
• Strong track record of driving continuous improvement through data-driven decision making, with the ability  
to report regional hiring outcomes credibly to Global TA Leadership.  
• Exceptional communication and influencing skills, with the ability to reconcile competing divisional demand  
for the same regional capacity and drive consensus without formal authority.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
