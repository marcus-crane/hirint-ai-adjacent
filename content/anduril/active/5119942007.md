Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**About the Role**  
  
Anduril’s Maritime team develops, tests, deploys, and sustains the Anduril Maritime Fleet in challenging operational environments worldwide.  We are seeking a Technical Program Manager to lead international deployment execution for a maritime undersea domain awareness program. This role sits at the intersection of engineering, program management, and customer delivery — you will own the end-to-end execution of international demonstrations and system deployments, from planning through on-site execution. You will be the connective tissue between product teams, engineering, operations, business development, and international customers. This is not a role where you manage from a distance — you will be in the details, in the room with the customer, and accountable for delivery.  
  
**What You'll Do**

* Own planning, scheduling, and execution of international system deployments and demonstrations for undersea domain awareness capabilities
* Build and maintain program schedules in Jira, ensuring milestones, dependencies, and critical path items are current and visible to all stakeholders
* Develop and maintain milestone trackers, risk registers, and action item burndowns across multiple concurrent workstreams
* Drive technical alignment across hardware, software, and systems engineering teams to ensure deployment-ready configurations meet customer requirements
* Prepare and deliver technical briefings and program status reviews to international customers, senior leadership, and cross-functional stakeholders
* Coordinate logistics for international hardware shipments including export compliance, temporary import/permanent export documentation, and customs coordination
* Identify and manage risks and dependencies across engineering, supply chain, compliance, and operations — escalate early and with proposed mitigations
* Translate customer requirements into actionable engineering tasking and ensure product teams understand the operational context behind what they're building
* Keep Jira updated as the single source of truth for schedule status — ensure sprint planning, epic tracking, and program milestones reflect reality, not aspirations
* Drive alignment across stakeholders who don't always agree — get decisions made, documented, and executed
* Support contract amendment processes, milestone payment tracking, and deliverable acceptance with the customer
* Document product baselines, configuration states, and deployment readiness criteria in Confluence
* Operate as the single point of accountability for deployment readiness — if something isn't tracking, you own the recovery plan

**You Should Have**

* 5+ years of technical program management experience, preferably in defense, maritime, or aerospace
* Demonstrated ability to manage international customer engagements and deployments in high-stakes environments
* Strong technical foundation — able to go deep on systems architecture, integration challenges, and hardware/software trade-offs without relying on engineering to translate for you
* Experience building and presenting technical briefings to senior military and government stakeholders
* Track record of managing complex, multi-team schedules with interdependent milestones and hard deadlines
* Proficiency in Jira for program scheduling, sprint management, and milestone tracking — you treat the tool as a real-time reflection of program health, not an administrative chore
* Exceptional organizational skills — you are the person who tracks every action item, every dependency, every open risk, and doesn't let things fall through
* Comfort operating in ambiguity and high-pressure situations where the plan is changing and decisions have real consequences
* Experience with export control processes (ITAR/EAR) and international logistics
* Familiarity with DoD acquisition processes, contract structures (FFP, T&M, CPFF), and milestone-based deliverables
* **Ability to travel internationally up to 25%, including extended deployments for demonstrations**

**Nice to Have**

* Experience in undersea warfare, autonomous systems, or maritime ISR programs
* Background in systems engineering or test & evaluation
* Active Secret clearance or ability to obtain one
* Experience working with Indo-Pacific partner nations
* Experience with Confluence for documentation and product baseline management

**You Are**

* The person who walks into a room and knows the status of every open item without checking their notes
* Comfortable telling a room of senior leaders that something is off track and presenting the recovery plan in the same breath
* Energized by complexity, not overwhelmed by it
* Someone who builds trust with customers through competence and follow-through, not slides and promises
* Direct, organized, and allergic to ambiguity that hasn't been resolved

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
