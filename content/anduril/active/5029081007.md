Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

## **ABOUT THE TEAM** Our Radio Frequency & Communications Engineering Services team in Costa Mesa is a small group of skilled professionals dedicated to pushing the boundaries of RF and antenna technology. With a strong focus on innovation and excellence, we collaborate closely to design and optimize cutting-edge RF solutions that meet the demands of internal customers, external clients, and the industry. **ABOUT THE JOB** We are seeking a RF Systems Engineer to join our team in Costa Mesa, CA. This role is focused on RF design, simulation, build, fabrication, test, and field troubleshooting. This position requires you have proven industry experience, with solid references. We are specifically looking for someone that is humble, mission focused, looking to hit the ground running, takes direction well, pays special attention to detail, and works well with others. If you have a passion for helping others solve complex RF problems, this may be the position for you. Your contributions will be essential in optimizing the RF systems that support the warfighter. **WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Architect, design, integrate, test, and optimize communications hardware across a variety of platforms and systems
* Configure, simulate, evaluate, and test RF systems and datalinks
* Develop RF link budgets, co-site analysis and desense reports
* Create RF system interconnect diagrams
* Select and manage radio, RF component, cable, and antenna vendors
* Support other functional engineering team’s development effort with an understanding of communication system impacts and trades regarding RF systems
* Represent the communications sub-systems in vehicle-level cross-disciplinary trade-off studies that define and optimize the design of various vehicles and systems

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Bachelor’s Degree in Electrical Engineering or related field
* 7+ years of industry experience with hands-on RF systems design, build, test, and troubleshooting
* The ability to work collaboratively with other teams to help define product requirements, do trade analysis, develop specifications and conduct system level tests
* Experience using RF test equipment to design, build, and troubleshoot RF systems
* Knowledge of aviation communications systems (radio, antennae, and similar systems) and tactical communication systems
* Knowledge of the fundamentals of Ethernet and similar networks
* Experience in the electrical integration of avionics modules and payloads into C2 systems, UAVs, ground vehicles, and weapons systems
* Understanding of hardware evaluation methodologies, including development of test cases/suites, performance monitoring, test automation, and data analysis
* Experience with practical communication equipment configuration and troubleshooting
* Experience with RF system modeling and simulation
* Knowledge of modern analog and digital electronics and electronic circuits
* Understanding of EMI , co-site, desense, and common system level integration issues
* Proficient with a variety of RF equipment vendors
* Competence with test equipment such as spectrum analyzers, network analyzers, power meters, function generators, and automation of tests
* Knowledgeable in Python
* Must be able to travel 25 to 75% of the time
* Currently possesses and is able to maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Understanding and familiarity with FCC spectrum authorizations
* Understanding and familiarity with MIL-STD-461 & MIL-STD-464

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
