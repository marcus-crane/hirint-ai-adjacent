Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

## ABOUT THE TEAM

The Lethality team designs, builds, tests, and fields mechanical warhead and ordnance systems that integrate directly with Anduril platforms.

Our engineers work across mechanical design, manufacturing, analysis, testing, safety, and vehicle integration to move new concepts from an idea to tested hardware quickly. The team is highly hands-on and built for engineers who want to design new products, solve difficult problems, and see their decisions become real hardware.

## ABOUT THE JOB

The Lethality Team is looking for a **Mechanical Engineer** to help develop the next generation of warhead and ordnance systems.

You will design new mechanical hardware, work directly with the people building it, test your designs, and use the results to make the next iteration better.

*Yes, you may actually get paid to blow things up.*

You do **not** need to arrive as a warhead expert. We are looking for strong mechanical engineers with practical design experience, sound engineering judgment, curiosity, and the ability to learn a highly specialized domain quickly.

## WHAT YOU'LL DO

* **Design new mechanical hardware** from requirements and early concepts through detailed CAD, drawings, fabrication, assembly, and test.
* **Turn engineering ideas into manufacturable products** by making practical decisions around materials, tolerances, interfaces, manufacturing methods, cost, and assembly.
* **Build, test, and improve your designs** by working directly with manufacturing, technicians, analysis, and test teams to identify problems and drive the next iteration.
* **Integrate your hardware into larger systems** by partnering with vehicle, systems, safety, and other engineering teams to resolve interfaces and technical tradeoffs.
* **Grow your technical ownership** by progressing from defined components and workstreams toward increasingly independent subsystem design and decision-making.
  + **IMPACT YOU'LL HAVE**
    - Deliver new lethality hardware that directly supports U.S. and allied warfighters.
    - Take products from early concepts to physical hardware instead of maintaining legacy designs.
    - Develop specialized expertise in warhead and ordnance engineering alongside experienced technical leaders.
    - See the direct impact of your engineering decisions through manufacturing, integration, and live testing.

## REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* BS in Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, or a related technical field.
* 1 year of practical, end-to-end mechanical product design experience.
* Demonstrated experience taking a new mechanical product or design through multiple stages of development, such as concept, CAD, drawings, fabrication, assembly, testing, and iteration.
* Strong mechanical design fundamentals, including materials, tolerances, interfaces, manufacturing processes, and design for manufacturability.
* Professional CAD experience creating mechanical parts, assemblies, and engineering drawings, with the ability to explain design decisions, tradeoffs, and how test or engineering data informed the design.
* Ability to work onsite in Costa Mesa, travel for testing as required, and obtain and maintain a U.S. security clearance.
* In this role, you will be subject to pre-employment and randomized substance screening, as required by the Company.

## PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Experience with **NX, Teamcenter**, or comparable CAD/PLM tools.
* Experience applying **GD&T, tolerance analysis, and design for CNC machining or precision manufacturing**.
* Experience developing hardware in a fast-paced **R&D or new-product-development environment**.
* Experience in **aerospace, defense, space, robotics, automotive, or other complex physical-product industries**.
* Experience supporting **mechanical, environmental, dynamic, or system-level testing** and using results to improve a design.
* Exposure to **ordnance, munitions, energetics, explosives, weapons systems, or other defense hardware**.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
