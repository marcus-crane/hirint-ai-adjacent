### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Corporate Technology team is responsible for building and optimizing the many systems and internal technologies that enable Anduril to accomplish its mission. Anduril’s Supply Chain, Accounting, Sales & Growth, Field Maintenance, Manufacturing, and many more teams rely on the technology built and maintained by us. We call this system-of-systems “CorpOS”. Within CorpOS lies a suite of digital software solutions aimed at solving problems for our growth, finance, and people teams; CorpOS also interfaces heavily with our ArsenalOS solutions that enable Anduril’s Engineering, Production, and Sustainment departments.

### ABOUT THE JOB

Accounts Payable (AP) is where procurement, supply chain, and finance meet, and at a fast-scaling manufacturer it's where process friction shows up first: invoices that don't match, receipts that lag, suppliers who can't get paid on time, and AP teams who spend their days on exceptions instead of oversight. As Product Manager for Accounts Payable, you'll own the roadmap for the internal tools, automations, and Oracle Fusion capabilities that make the procure-to-pay lifecycle fast, compliant, and largely touchless. This is a product role, not a systems administration role:

* You will treat AP, Procurement, and Supply Chain users as customers: understanding their workflows, measuring where time and rework accumulate, and shipping improvements they actually adopt
* Your mandate is to remove friction from invoice intake through payment, using Oracle Fusion Financials, intelligent document capture (IDR/OCR), workflow automation, and custom-built tooling when the ERP alone does not get us there
* You will work shoulder-to-shoulder with AP, Supply Chain, Procurement, Controllership, and Engineering, translating messy real-world requirements into solutions that are auditable, performant, and built to last through the next order of magnitude of growth

#### WHAT YOU'LL DO:

* Own the product vision, roadmap, and backlog for AP systems and internal tooling, balancing ERP configuration, integrations, and custom development against business impact
* Collaborate with AP, Supply Chain, Procurement, and Controllership to drive friction out of workflows and system-supported business processes
* Partner closely with Finance Operations to strategically align on priorities and translate them into well-documented work breakdown structures and functional designs
* Lead delivery end to end, from discovery and requirements through design, build, testing, deployment, and adoption, working with engineering on internal tooling and integrations
* Drive invoice automation: optimize intake, IDR/OCR extraction, coding, 2-way/3-way match, exception handling, approval routing, and payment execution to increase touchless rates and reduce cycle time
* Define and track product and process metrics (touchless invoice rate, exception rate, cycle time, on-time payment, early-pay discount capture, cost per invoice) and use them to prioritize
* Build in controls by design: segregation of duties, approval limits, audit trail, duplicate payment prevention, and compliance with applicable regulatory and audit requirements, including SOX and government contracting obligations
* Establish standards for change management, testing, release management, and documentation so the AP platform stays clean as the team scales
* Manage the Oracle release cycle: assess quarterly updates, run regression testing, and communicate impact to business stakeholders
* Mentor analysts and product owners, and set the bar for how work on finance systems gets done

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:

* 8+ years of experience delivering on Oracle Fusion Financials with depth in Accounts Payable; similar ERP experience (e.g., SAP, NetSuite, Workday) will be considered
* Expertise in Procure-to-Pay functional processes, with in-depth knowledge of how IDR/OCR capabilities can optimize workflows
* Demonstrated experience designing and delivering finance solutions end to end (requirements through deployment and adoption), not just administering existing systems
* Product management fundamentals: writing clear requirements and user stories, backlog prioritization, working with engineers in an Agile environment, and measuring outcomes after release
* Strong grounding in accounting fundamentals: general ledger, AP subledger accounting, accruals, and the mechanics of a monthly close
* Proven track record leading cross-functional projects across Finance, Supply Chain, Procurement, and Engineering teams
* Working knowledge of integration and data concepts (APIs, file-based integrations, SQL or equivalent) sufficient to partner effectively with engineering on internal tooling
* Familiarity with internal control frameworks and audit requirements (SOX, segregation of duties, audit trail)
* A builder's mindset: able to operate in ambiguity, prioritize ruthlessly, and ship pragmatic solutions in a fast-changing environment
* Excellent written and verbal communication, with the ability to influence senior stakeholders and explain technical trade-offs to non-technical partners

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:

* Experience with payment operations: payment runs, treasury and bank integrations, 1099 and tax reporting, and fraud/duplicate payment controls
* Experience standing up systems and processes for a company scaling through rapid headcount, entity, or geographic expansion
* A background that blends technical depth with finance credibility, such as prior roles as a systems analyst, finance transformation lead, or AP/controllership professional turned product owner

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
