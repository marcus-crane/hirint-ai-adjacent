Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**ABOUT THE TEAM**The **Air Dominance and Strike (AD&S) Electrical Engineering Team** is responsible for developing high-reliability avionics, embedded processing, and power systems for Group 5 air vehicles and missile platforms. Working across hardware, software, and mission autonomy, the team delivers flight-critical electronics, PCB assemblies, and FPGA-based processing architectures that enable Anduril's next-generation autonomous air platforms to operate in complex and contested environments. AD&S Electrical Engineers drive end-to-end development, from system architecture and circuit design to verification, integration, and flight test, ensuring scalable, mission-ready solutions that meet the performance, reliability, and survivability demands of the U.S. Military and modern air warfare.

**ABOUT THE JOB**We are looking for an **Lab Asset Manager** to join our rapidly growing team in **Costa Mesa, CA**. In this role, you will be responsible for managing the facilities and assets for our electrical testing, troubleshooting and harnesses integration lab. If you are someone who thrives in supporting rapid avionics development, then this role is for you.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Manage Test Equipment maintenance activities for Anduril facilities in Costa Mesa.
* Equipment data entry in JIRA system/equipment tracking system and manage the database, ensure that it is updated, including but not limited to updating location and user info, calibration date.
* Perform daily electrical test equipment checkout process and activities for requesters.
* Manage the communication of calibration recalls to tool owners/user.
* Procure and source equipment under the direction of lead and engineers as required.
* Ensure proper equipment performance are properly checked and calibrated.
* Manage and track the physical recall of devices requiring calibration.
* Maintain accurate calibration records and assist in audits as required.
* Ensure all calibration activities are performed accurately on time.
* Manage the coordination of calibration activities with equipment user and calibration vendors.
* Engage with vendors and manufacturers to initiate Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) and repairs if test equipment fails calibration or needs repairs.
* Able to work in a fast paced environment with ambiguous targets and adapt work plans based on new outcome.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**  

* 2+ years of experience in calibration, metrology, or a related field.
* Experience managing calibration at a large organization and production environment.
* Strong attention to detail and ability to follow precise instructions.
* Experience working with and managing 3rd party calibration vendors.
* Experience with a variety of measurement instruments, including but not limited to electronic, mechanical, and optical devices with strong preference on electrical test equipment.
* Familiarization with AS9100 and/or other external auditing and compliance agencies and standards.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance
* Ability to lift a minimum of 25 lbs. Unassisted.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience working in a metrology department or Minimum 3 years experience as calibration specialist.
* Experience in a laboratory or technical environment.
* Knowledge of quality management systems and documentation practices.
* Ability to work effectively in a team-oriented environment.
