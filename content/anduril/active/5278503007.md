Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE JOB

As a key member of Anduril's recruiting team, you will be a specialized Contract G&A Recruiter embedded at the front lines of our mission to scale the teams that safeguard our technology and clear the path for global deployment. Responsible for attracting and hiring world-class legal, regulatory, compliance, real estate, and facilities talent, you will play a critical role in our continued rapid growth, bringing a strategic and hands-on approach to building our G&A organization. In this role, you will hold the keys to our highest-priority legal, real estate, and facilities expansions, collaborating directly with Real Estate & Facilities Leadership to deliver on ambitious hiring needs. To achieve this, you will be given the autonomy and tools to execute on our strategy to build and develop Anduril's world-class team. We are looking for someone who can masterfully handle every aspect of the G&A recruiting lifecycle and is excited to be part of a small, impactful team where no task is too big or too small.

* ***This role will be hybrid in Costa Mesa, CA. This is a 6 month contract with the ability to extend up to 18 months. Opportunity to be converted to a full-time employee based on performance, impact, and business need.***

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Conduct in-depth interviews and competency assessments of potential G&A professionals, including real estate managers, lease administrators, facilities leaders, and facilities/maintenance technicians.
* Engage passive G&A talent using LinkedIn Recruiter, Boolean strings, referrals, SOBO campaigns, and specialized legal, real estate, and facilities networks and professional associations (e.g., ACC, CoreNet Global, IFMA).
* Collaborate closely with Real Estate and Facilities leadership to define role requirements and craft compelling value propositions for G&A professionals.
* Drive a diverse talent pipeline into the organization while ensuring an exceptional candidate experience from initial outreach to offer acceptance.
* Represent the company's brand internally and externally, clearly articulating our unique business model, manufacturing scale, and the operational backbone that enables our mission.
* Leverage internal resources and market insights to build strategic outreach campaigns that sell our unique value proposition to top-tier G&A talent.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 5+ years of direct, full-cycle recruiting experience specifically focused on G&A roles, with meaningful exposure to real estate, workplace, and/or facilities hiring.
* Proven experience conducting thorough competency evaluations across G&A functions (e.g., legal credentialing, regulatory and compliance expertise, commercial lease and portfolio management, facilities operations, and vendor management).
* Experience managing strategic relationships with legal, real estate, and facilities leaders to calibrate candidate profiles and provide transparent pipeline reporting.
* Experience advising leadership on market conditions, G&A talent availability, and candidate compensation expectations to improve hiring outcomes.
* Clear communication style to evaluate candidate motivations and cultural fit within a fast-paced, high-impact environment.
* Experience moving sourcing strategies seamlessly between corporate/office-based roles and hands-on, site- and field-based operations.
* Highly autonomous but deeply collaborative, thriving in a dynamic, high-growth, and mission-critical environment.  
  Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export controlled information or facilities.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
