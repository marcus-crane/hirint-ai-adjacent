### ABOUT THE JOB

As Team Lead Stock Controller, you will direct the day-to-day operations of the inventory, warehouse and materials team members, driving operational excellence across all inbound, outbound, kitting, and storage activities. You will take ownership of inventory accuracy by leading periodic cycle counts, conducting efficient year-end stock takes, maintaining real-time ERP records, and preserving complete product traceability. Working in close partnership with Accounting, you will deliver financial analysis, reconcile variances, and streamline reporting, while collaborating with Procurement and Manufacturing to optimise stock levels and incoming schedules. You will champion an organized, secure, and EHS-compliant stockroom, ensuring your team executes all processes with high precision and documentation accuracy.

### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Role responsibilities include but are not limited to:
* Financial Reporting: provides regular financial analysis and reporting and work cross functionally with the Accounting team
* Team: Leads, guides and coordinate the day-to-day operations of the warehouse and materials associates, ensuring proper workload distribution, training, and cross-functional support
* Inventory Accuracy & Auditing: Monitor and maintain stock accuracy across all storage locations. Lead routine cycle counts, reconcile physical stock against ERP records, and investigate inventory variances. Effectively and promptly resolves all stock discrepancies.
* Inbound & Outbound Handling: Oversee goods receipt, verify shipments against purchase orders/packing slips, and manage staging/kitting of parts for production and dispatch.
* Traceability & Quality: Track inventory movement from receipt through production, ensuring serial and lot/batch traceability. Quarantine damaged or non-conforming items per quality standards.
* ERP & Data Maintenance: Ensure real-time, accurate data entry into the ERP/WMS for all inventory transactions, transfers, adjustments, and receipts.
* Stock Optimisation: Monitor minimum/maximum stock thresholds to prevent shortages or excess inventory. Coordinate returns to vendors and scrap/write-off processes for obsolete materials.
* Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with Accounting, Procurement, Manufacturing/Engineering, and Logistics to provide visibility on material availability and incoming stock schedules.
* Warehouse Safety & Organisation: Maintain an effective and efficient standard, organised, and secure stockroom. Adhere to all workplace Health & Safety (EHS) and safe material handling procedures.
* Carry out regular and year end stock takes ensuring a high degree of efficiency and organisation that meets all deadlines.
* Ensure the team maintain a high level of document accuracy

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**
