Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Support Operations team is responsible for keeping Anduril's deployed fleet of autonomous robots and software products available to the people using them. We are the company's incident response and containment function for deployed systems. Our work lands as deficiency reports and general inquiries from customers, field teams, and automated alerting. We take those signals, assess urgency, work the problem down to an affected system, and exercise steps to restore capability. Our work is captured in case records and associated to known populations to support trend definition, and the recurring patterns we surface provide the raw material for Voice of the Customer (VOC) Product Operations focused on engineering out recurrence. This team is a crucial component of customer and mission success, and plays a critical role in how Anduril continues to deliver cutting-edge capability to the United States and its allies.

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for a Support Operations Specialist, Tier 1 to join our rapidly growing team in Santa Ana, CA. In this role, you will be the first point of contact in the Product Operations chain for Anduril's deployed systems, working a single flat queue of reports from across the company's breadth of programs and products. You will own each case from intake through containment or, when applicable, a clean escalation handover: characterizing and triaging the report signal, tracing incident details and telemetry data to an affected system, establishing priority against the governing service level agreement, and containing the issue by executing documented procedures through remote on-system and guided customer actions. The uptime commitments behind these systems are aggressive, with many missions demanding immediate and effective response when a failure occurs. This will require structured troubleshooting at the interface of hardware, software, and networking, working comfort with the Linux command line and SSH, and the ability to explain a technical problem clearly to a non-engineer customer in writing and by phone. If you are someone who is cool under pressure, extremely detail oriented, focused above all on the customer's experience, disciplined enough to execute a documented sequence exactly, and adaptable enough to recognize when the documentation does not match the system in front of you and find the right next step, then this role is for you. If you are interested in a technical career path with a high level of impact, broad exposure to the capabilities Anduril delivers, and insight into how fielded systems actually fail (and what it takes to recover them), Anduril is interested in speaking with you.

#### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* **Own the First Response:** Triage every inbound report, acknowledge receipt to the reporter, and confirm what activity is underway against their case.
* **Set Priority Against the SLA:** Establish case priority against the governing program service level agreement, and keep the clock visible rather than discovering a breach after it happens.
* **Narrow It to a System:** Perform the analysis that takes a case from a customer's description to the affected system or systems, and, when required, go back to the customer for the detail the report is missing.
* **Contain Known Failures:** Execute documented steps in sequence from product documentation to contain known failure modes, both by remote action on cloud-connected systems and by directing a customer through steps on systems we cannot reach ourselves. Interpret and extrapolate from documented steps when they do not capture the exact state of the system, and pivot to additional documentation when required.
* **Keep the Record Straight:** Maintain a detailed record of the case, actions taken, and progress made through closure.
* **Make the Escalation Call:** Decide when Tier 1 capability has been exhausted, or when an incident is too novel to contain or characterize with the documentation and tools available, and hand it over with a record of the work already done to mitigate redundant cycles.
* **Elevate Business Risk:** Interpret program service level agreements and make the risk characterization call to elevate a report that is potentially damaging to the business, separate from the priority the agreement itself produces.
* **Chase Your Escalations:** Monitor and actively pursue updates on cases escalated beyond Tier 1, rather than waiting for Tier 2 or engineering to report back.
* **Improve the Runbook:** Propose runbook revisions for Tier 2 to validate and incorporate, and flag the cases where no documented path existed at all.
* **Hold the Coverage:** Work a 4x10 shift pattern alongside your team to keep continuous coverage on the queue.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
