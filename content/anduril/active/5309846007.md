**ABOUT THE JOB**

Anduril’s Air Dominance and Strike (AD&S) team is seeking a Staff Mission Systems RF Engineer to join our team. The AD&S team is responsible for developing end-to-end “mission-ized” autonomous air systems capable of delivering exceptional warfighter outcomes to our customers.

As a Staff Mission Systems RF Engineer, you will be responsible for the architecture, design, and validation of the RF and microwave hardware between the antenna and the digitizer — low-noise front ends, frequency conversion, filtering, power amplification, and the synthesis and distribution behind them. The right person will have experience carrying RF hardware from analysis through layout, bring-up, and characterization. You will begin with mission-level outcomes — sensitivity, dynamic range, bandwidth, and the environment the hardware has to survive — and work backward to the architecture, the parts, and the suppliers that can meet them. This will require taking ownership of the measurements that prove the design, with calibration and de-embedding done properly, and of the negotiation with mechanical, thermal, and digital teams. If you are someone who enjoys owning hardware that has to work on the bench and not only in simulation, we would like to hear from you.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO** 

* Own RF and microwave hardware design end to end: low-noise amplification, gain stages, frequency conversion, filtering, power amplification, and the synthesis and distribution behind them.
* Build and defend cascade budgets — noise figure, gain, 1 dB compression, IP2/IP3, spurious, phase noise and group delay — and use them to drive architecture and component selection.
* Design millimeter-wave-capable printed structures: microstrip and stripline, transitions and launches, interconnect, and grounding and shielding that survive assembly.
* Select, evaluate and integrate MMICs and front-end components, and work with suppliers on parts, die and packaging.
* Simulate with circuit and EM tools, then prove the design on the bench with vector network analyzer, spectrum analyzer, noise figure, phase noise and power measurements — with calibration and de-embedding done properly.
* Work with mechanical and thermal engineers on packaging, thermal paths and environmental survival, and with digital engineers across the RF/digital boundary.
* Lead bring-up and debug, write the test procedures, and mentor junior RF engineers.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS** 

* B.S. in Electrical Engineering or a related field and 8+ years of RF/microwave hardware design experience. M.S. and 6+ years, or Ph.D. and 3+ years, also considered.
* Demonstrated ownership of RF hardware from specification through layout, bring-up, characterization and production handoff..
* Hands-on competence with RF test equipment, including correct calibration and de-embedding practice.
* Experience with circuit and EM simulation tools (ADS, Microwave Office, HFSS, CST or equivalent).
* Able to obtain a US Security Clearance

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**  

* Knowledge of current and next-generation advanced radar systems.
* Experience with missile design & integration.
* Design experience above 18 GHz.
* Transmit/receive module design for phased arrays, and array-level calibration.
* GaN or GaAs power amplifier design, and thermal management of high-duty-cycle RF.
* Low phase noise synthesizer and reference distribution design.
* Waveguide components and waveguide-to-planar transitions.
* Low-loss laminates, multilayer millimeter-wave stackups, and design for manufacture of RF assemblies.
* An active U.S. Department of Defense Secret clearance, or eligibility to obtain one.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
