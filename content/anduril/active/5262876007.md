### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Hardware Platform Organization is Anduril's central hardware engineering org. We build the shared technologies, tools, and infrastructure that power hardware development across every Anduril division, from actuators and propulsion to batteries, avionics, rocket motors, and test operations. Our teams set engineering standards, run development manufacturing and test sites, and develop core technology products that ship across multiple product lines.

### WHAT YOU'LL DO

**Motor Control Algorithm Development**  
•       Design and implement field-oriented control (FOC), six-step commutation, and direct torque control algorithms for BLDC and PMSM motors  
•       Develop sensorless rotor position estimation techniques (back-EMF observer, sliding-mode observer, high-frequency injection) for applications where Hall sensors or encoders are not viable  
•       Design current, speed, and position control loops with rigorous bandwidth, stability margins, and disturbance rejection specifications  
**Modelling & Simulation (MIL / SIL / HIL)**  
•       Build high-fidelity motor and inverter plant models in MATLAB / Simulink, including magnetic saturation, iron losses, and thermal coupling  
•       Develop controller models and validate performance through Model-in-the-Loop (MIL) simulation against requirements  
•       Execute Software-in-the-Loop (SIL) testing using Embedded Coder auto-generated C code to verify fixed-point scaling, quantisation effects, and code-generation fidelity  
•       Support Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) integration with real-time simulators (dSPACE, Typhoon HIL, or equivalent) for full controller board validation  
•       Integrate Simulink auto-coded algorithm modules with the microcontroller RTOS, peripheral drivers, and application framework  
**Firmware & Embedded Systems**  
•       Implement motor control firmware in C on microcontrollers (e.g., STM32, TI C2000, NXP S32K) with hard real-time constraints  
•       Configure and optimise motor control peripherals: PWM timers, ADC synchronisation, encoder/Hall interfaces, comparator-based overcurrent protection  
•       Architect firmware for deterministic control loop execution — interrupt prioritisation, DMA pipelines, and minimal-jitter scheduling  
•       Implement fault detection, diagnosis, and safe-state management in firmware (overcurrent, overvoltage, overtemperature, sensor loss, stall, phase loss)  
•       Develop communication interfaces (CAN, SPI, UART, RS-485) for command, telemetry, and diagnostics

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

•       Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Controls Engineering, Mechatronics, or equivalent hands-on experience  
•       10+ years of professional experience designing and implementing motor control algorithms (FOC, Trapezoidal, or equivalent) for BLDC or PMSM motors  
•       Strong foundation in classical and modern control theory — PID tuning, frequency-domain analysis, state estimation, observer design  
•       Proficiency in MATLAB / Simulink, PLECS for control design, motor modelling, and auto-code generation (Embedded Coder or equivalent)  
•       Deep experience writing production-quality embedded code on  microcontrollers with hard real-time motor control loops  
•       Perform Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) integration with real-time simulators  
•       Working knowledge of power electronics: three-phase inverter operation, PWM schemes, dead-time compensation, DC bus dynamics, and loss estimation  
•       Hands-on experience with motor control peripherals — ADC triggering, encoder/Hall/resolver interfaces, PWM complementary outputs, hardware overcurrent comparators  
•       Familiarity with communication protocols: CAN, SPI, I²C, UART, RS-485 etc  
•       Strong bench skills — oscilloscope, current probe, frequency response analyser, dynamometer or motor test stand  
•       U.S. Person status is required as this position needs to access export-controlled data (ITAR / EAR)

### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

•       Master's or PhD with thesis work in motor control, power electronics, or electric machine design  
•       Experience with sensorless control techniques at low speed and standstill (HFI, pulsating voltage injection)  
•       Experience with SiC or GaN power device integration and high-switching-frequency inverter design  
•       Experience with real-time simulation platforms (dSPACE, OPAL-RT, Typhoon HIL) for HIL validation  
•       Background in defense, automation, automotive, aerospace,  or robotics motor control applications — actuators, gimbal drives, electric propulsion  
•       Experience with thermal modeling of motors and inverters, I²t protection, and derating algorithms  
•       Active or ability to obtain a U.S. security clearance

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
