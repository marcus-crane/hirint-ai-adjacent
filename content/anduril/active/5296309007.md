Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**ABOUT THE TEAM**

We are seeking a Vulnerability Security Engineer to join Anduril’s vulnerability management team. There are two  
primary responsibilities to this role: To be the technical front door for bug bounty and vulnerability disclosure  
reports and to contribute to offensive security engagements across all of Anduril.  
  
**ABOUT THE JOB**

You will own the intake, technical validation, coordination, and closure of externally reported vulnerabilities. You  
will validate and if needed, work directly with researchers and identify the engineering, product, infrastructure, or  
operations teams affected by each finding, then provide those teams with the evidence and context needed to  
resolve it.  
  
You will also assist senior offensive security engineers throughout offensive security engagements; from scoping  
and target research through execution, reporting, remediation guidance, and retesting. As your technical breadth  
and judgment grow, you will independently own bounded portions of engagements in your demonstrated areas of  
strength.  
  
This role is designed as a development path to Senior Offensive Security Engineer. It is not a vulnerability  
management ticket routing position or a reduced scope web testing role. You will be expected to validate attack  
paths, build practical prototypes and tooling, communicate with technical credibility, and steadily expand into  
unfamiliar target classes.  
  
**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Triage and coordinate bug bounty and vulnerability disclosure reports, including scope, quality, duplication,
* validity, impact, urgency, ownership, and case status.
* Reproduce reported behavior, adapt or complete proofs of concept where appropriate, establish affected components and conditions, and provide a clear technical basis for disposition and severity.
* Identify affected teams across Anduril, communicate the issue and practical attack path, establish accountable owners, and shepherd remediation across organizational boundaries.
* Retest fixes and mitigations implemented from internal offensive security engagements as well as external contributions.
* Monitor relevant CVE publications, vendor advisories, exploit research, and technical writeups; research applicability to Anduril systems and provide affected teams with concise exposure analysis and recommended
* next actions.
* Assist or shadow senior offensive security engineers on security research, engagement execution, and internal tooling prototypes.
* Independently execute bounded engagement work within demonstrated areas of experience and deliver technically sound findings, reproduction steps, and remediation guidance.
* Build and maintain scripts, proofs of concept, validation harnesses, agent assisted workflows, lab infrastructure, and documentation that make vulnerability response and offensive testing more repeatable.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 3+ years of hands on security testing, penetration testing, vulnerability research, or offensive security experience across two or more of the following:
* Web application penetration testing Cloud penetration testing
* Software or thick client penetration testing
* Firmware analysis
* Familiarity through practical projects, lab work, or assisted engagements across two or more of the following:
* Hardware penetration testing
* Red teaming
* Offensive use of agentic LLM systems
* Reverse engineering
* OT/ICS
* Experience reproducing and validating vulnerabilities, and using scripting, development, or LLMs to automate testing and validation workflows.
* Strong professional communication skills, with the ability to coordinate among researchers and engineering teams without losing technical accuracy or ownership.
* Eligibility to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience triaging bug bounty or vulnerability disclosure submissions and driving reported issues through
* remediation and verified closure.
* Familiarity with CVE, CVSS, CWE, coordinated disclosure, and vulnerability case management workflows.
* Participation in scoped red team, hardware, firmware, reverse engineering, OT/ICS, or AI security
* engagements.
* Public or internally reviewable security research, technical walkthroughs, tools, lab projects, or proofs of
* concept.
* Demonstrated ability to learn unfamiliar systems, accept technical feedback, and expand from assisted work into independent engagement ownership.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
