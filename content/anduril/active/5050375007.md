### ABOUT THE TEAM

Anduril’s Manufacturing Engineering team is responsible for rapidly iterating, scaling, and sustaining cutting-edge defense hardware, including static sensor towers, autonomous ground systems, and high-performance flight vehicles. We bridge the gap between design engineering and physical hardware execution. 

As a Manufacturing Engineer on 1st Shift (6:00 am to 3:00 pm), you will serve as the primary mechanical and assembly authority on the factory floor. You will have a dual mandate: partner deeply with R&D during early architecture to drive rigorous Design for Assembly and Manufacturing (DFA/DFM), and architect the physical assembly processes, precision tooling, and integration lines required to scale complex mechanical hardware from prototype to high-rate production.  

This is a heavily mechanical, hands-on role. You will design assembly fixtures in 3D CAD, perform tolerance stack-up analyses, define structural joining and bonding procedures, integrate smart-tooling systems, and establish repeatable, error-proofed mechanical assembly lines.

#### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* Design for Assembly & Manufacturability (DFA/DFM): 
  + Embed directly with Mechanical Design Engineering during early R&D phases to influence product architecture, driving aggressive part reduction, standard fastener consolidation, and modular sub-assembly design.
  + Conduct detailed 1D and 2D worst-case and statistical (RSS) tolerance stack-up analyses to ensure mechanical interfaces, datum structures, dynamic clearances, and pin/hole alignments assemble consistently without force-fitting.
  + Define hardware assembly strategies, joint clamping designs, thermal interface material (TIM) applications, structural bonding, and torque sequencing early in the CAD development cycle.
* Tooling, Jigs, & Assembly Fixture Design: 
  + Own end-to-end mechanical design of production tooling—including precision assembly jigs, alignment nests, press-fit tooling, ergonomic lift/handling equipment, and mechanical verification fixtures using 3D CAD (NX or SolidWorks).
  + Apply finite element analysis (FEA) where necessary to validate fixture rigidity, deflection under clamping loads, and stress concentrations.
  + Produce fully detailed 2D fabrication drawings utilizing ASME Y14.5 Geometric Dimensioning & Tolerancing (GD&T) for internal machine shops or external toolmakers.
* Mechanical Assembly Process Engineering: 
  + Line Architecture & Flow: Design, sequence, and balance complex, multi-stage mechanical assembly lines. Define takt times, line layouts, and ergonomic workstations to transition hardware seamlessly from sub-assembly to final structural integration.
  + Joining & Fastening Technologies: Establish and qualify robust fastening processes, including torque-tension specifications, torque-angle control, bolt pre-load engineering, and press-fit/interference-fit operations (bearings, bushings, dowels).
  + Adhesives, Sealing, & Dispensing: Develop repeatable application methods for chemical assembly processes, including structural bonding (epoxies, urethanes), thread locking, environmental gasketing/RTV sealing, potting, and Thermal Interface Material (TIM) dispensing.
  + Precision Mechanical Alignment & Calibration: Develop standard processes, measurement techniques, and alignment procedures for precision kinematic mechanisms, optical/sensor payloads, gimbals, and dynamic structural joints.
  + Error-Proofing & Quality Integration: Lead Process Failure Mode and Effects Analyses (pFMEAs) for mechanical assembly workflows. Design physical error-proofing (Poka-Yoke) mechanisms, go/no-go verification gauges, and smart-tool integration (DC electric torque tools with digital data logging) to eliminate assembly errors.
  + Work Definition & Operator Enablement: Author visual, step-by-step Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and digital work instructions that clearly define mechanical assembly sequences, critical tool callouts, fastener specs, and quality inspection gates.
* NPI Ramp, Sustainment, & Root Cause Analysis:
  + Lead technical disposition and first-principles root-cause investigations (8D, 5-Whys, Fishbone) on mechanical assembly failures, binding mechanisms, structural interferences, and joint integrity issues.
  + Bridge the gap between engineering intent and production execution by converting Engineering Bills of Materials (eBOMs) into logical, modular Manufacturing Bills of Materials (mBOMs) within PLM (Teamcenter).
  + Author and implement Engineering Change Orders (ECOs/ECRs) for mechanical modifications, hardware deviations, and assembly drawing updates.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* B.S. in Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, or a closely related technical field.
* 3+ years of experience in manufacturing or mechanical design role with heavy exposure to mechanical assembly and hardware integration (aerospace, defense, automotive, dynamic robotics, or precision industrial hardware).
* Proficiency in 3D CAD modeling (Siemens NX strongly preferred; SolidWorks, CATIA, or Creo acceptable) with a strong portfolio of designed and deployed assembly tooling, nests, or fixtures.
* Advanced working knowledge of ASME Y14.5 GD&T—ability to design assembly datum reference frames, analyze tolerances, and inspect mating parts.
* Deep understanding of mechanical assembly techniques: threaded fastener engineering, interference/press fits, structural adhesives/bonding, torque-tension monitoring, precision shimming, and mechanical alignment techniques.
* Demonstrated experience conducting tolerance stack-up analyses and applying DFA/DFM principles to mechanical assemblies.
* Strong mechanical problem-solving ability grounded in first-principles physics (mechanics of materials, kinematics, statics).
* U.S. Person status is required as this position needs to access export-controlled data.

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 5+ years of experience taking complex structural or electro-mechanical assemblies from initial prototyping through full-rate production (NPI lifecycle).
* Experience implementing smart assembly tooling and integrating digital torque/angle tracking into Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES).
* Hands-on machining and fabrication skills (mill, lathe, 3D printing, press brakes) for rapid prototype tooling and shop-floor iteration.
* Experience with mechanical process validation, including Measurement System Analysis (Gage R&R) and Statistical Process Control (Cpk/Ppk).
* Working familiarity with PLM systems (Siemens Teamcenter) and modern ERP/MES platforms.
