### ABOUT THE JOB

The Deployment Handling engineer owns ground handling procedures and equipment for autonomous maritime vehicle movement at deployment sites. You will design handling fixtures, select and specify ground support equipment, develop handling procedures, and ensure vehicles are moved safely between transport, storage, and water. You will own launch and recovery for maritime autonomous vehicles. Reporting to the Deployment Lead, you will design assist with designing L&R systems, define host vessel interfaces, and develop operational procedures that enable safe deployment and retrieval at sea across a range of conditions.  
  
Your spectrum of support will be diverse in nature and require you to leverage your technical expertise as well as leadership skills to set objectives, build cross-functional teams, and rapidly drive to completion. The ability to leverage your intuition and prior experience will be key in making sure the right design, analysis, and test steps are completed to ensure success. The ideal candidate will leverage their experience executing and successfully completing prior highly optimized multi-disciplinary projects.

### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* Assist with design of ground handling fixtures for vehicle movement, lifting, and transport interface
* Select and procure ground support equipment including cranes, dollies, and custom handling tools
* Develop handling procedures, lift plans, and safety protocols for field operations
* Conduct hazard analyses and safety reviews for vehicle handling operations
* Develop personnel training for ground handling tasks
* Support handling operations at deployment sites in the field
* Coordinate with logistics and deployment teams to integrate handling into operational workflows launch and recovery system design and hardware selection
* Develop operational procedures for launch and recovery at sea
* Perform analysis of dynamic loads, sea state limitations, and safety margins
* Interface with vehicle mechanical design on L&R attachment points and interfaces
* Support at-sea testing and validation of L&R systems
* Contribute to training materials for L&R operations
* Travel up to 50% to test sites and operational locations

* Design and specify ground handling fixtures for vehicle movement, lifting, and transport interface
* Coordinate with logistics and deployment teams to integrate handling into operational workflows
