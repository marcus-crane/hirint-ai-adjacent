Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**ABOUT THE TEAM**  
  
The duty of this organization is to enable our Customers' ongoing Mission Readiness. From the moment they say "go" until the contract is fulfilled. We are only successful when the customer's mission is successful.  
  
Mission Readiness is the post-production shared services organization for Anduril. This team supports the Business Lines and Divisions throughout the lifecycle of their product, from initial sustainment concept through delivery, installation, operation, repair and return. These service offerings also include providing technical publications, training, customer support, connectivity services and other value-added services. To the degree the Business elects, we accept the delegated responsibility for upfront and continued delivery of the committed service solutions. This team endeavors to provide the Business Lines, as our internal customers, shared mission services that are more efficient and cost effective than if they chose to provide them directly. By offering mission support capabilities and capacity that are not "core" to the Business, the Business can focus resources on addressing the next big need while we ensure existing commitments are met.  
  
Within Mission Readiness, our Maintenance Repair & Overhaul (MRO) function provides capability to repair durable products to extend their useful life.   
Repair Engineering is the core technical function for Anduril’s component and hardware in-house maintenance capabilities.  
  
**ABOUT THE JOB**  
  
We are looking for a Director of Repair Engineering to build, from the ground up, the technical engineering backbone of Anduril’s repair function. This discipline is nascent at Anduril today. Production engineers handle returns, but the dedicated practice of assessing acceptable wear, developing non-production repair methods, and determining when a product is ‘serviceable but not like new’ does not yet exist as a standalone function. You will create it. Where our Head of MRO is building and running the repair organization itself, this role owns the engineering rigor underneath it: the repair-versus-discard decisions, the maintenance task analyses, the repair procedures and the product support data that determine what gets fixed, how, and at what level. This work requires both engineering discipline and seasoned judgment. Repair is not production in reverse, and the right answer often lives in the gray space between a spec and what the field actually demands. You will partner closely with Reliability Engineering, Configuration Management and the Business Line Chief Engineers to ensure that reusable products achieve their expected useful life. Anduril moves quickly, so you should be comfortable standing up rigorous engineering processes on products that are still evolving, ensuring quality and speed.  
  
**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Build the repair engineering function from scratch. Define the roles, hire the initial team, and organically adopt resident experts from production and product teams who are already doing adjacent work
* Lead development of repair methods, repair routings, standard repairs and acceptance criteria for Anduril products that are returned from the field with remaining useful life. Define what ‘serviceable condition’ looks like when it differs from original production specs, and codify the engineering rationale behind those decisions. Leverage digital tools and emerging technologies to scale repair knowledge capture and technician enablement.
* Enable repair activity to proceed at the pace each business requires to satisfy availability / turn-time commitments. This will include workflows in the repair cells/shops, prioritizing repair development/improvements, anticipating volumes, etc.
* Partner with Business Line Chief Engineers and Systems Engineering to influence product design for maintainability, reliability and testability, so repair costs and turnaround times improve with failure feedback.
* Oversee the quality of repairs observed in the re-deployed assets to improve repair design and inform technician feedback and training.
* Set the technical standards and tailoring guidelines, in collaboration with Chief Engineers of Business Lines, for how Anduril applies DoD sustainment engineering standards (MIL-HDBK-1390, GEIA-STD-0007, SAE JA1011, MIL-STD-1629A) in a way that fits Anduril's speed and product complexity.
* Produce the repair-related data that feeds Anduril's sustainment planning, accounting.
* Partner with Operational Excellence to establish and improve processes, documentation and digital tools that enable function to scale and increase scope quickly.
* Establish routine BER assessment methodology and partner with Finance and Operational Excellence to enable event-based decision-making for scrap or level of repair.
* Serve as the technical escalation point for repair engineering questions across Business Lines, ensuring consistent methodology even as individual product teams move at different paces.
* Determine when to subcontract specific repair scopes and define requirements where utilized.
