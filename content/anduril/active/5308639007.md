## About the Team

The Battlespace Awareness (BA) Radar team develops radar systems that address national security challenges for America and its allies. We own the full product lifecycle, from concepts and architectures through fielding and sustainment. Working across hardware, software, and firmware, in both the RF and digital domains, we turn raw sensor data into detections, measurements, tracks, and useful mission decisions. You will work with an interdisciplinary team to develop new capabilities, test prototypes, and mature those prototypes into products.

## About the Job

We are looking for a Radar Algorithms Engineer who combines practical signal-processing knowledge with strong software engineering skills. You will develop and implement algorithms in C++, accelerate processing on GPUs and other compute platforms, and take new capabilities from prototype through integration and demonstration on real radar systems.

The work spans more than an isolated algorithm or kernel. Some days you may prototype a new processing method; others you may profile a CUDA pipeline, investigate a missed detection in field data, debug an interaction with firmware, or prepare a feature for a laboratory or field demonstration. You will own correctness and performance across those boundaries, working with scientists and hardware engineers to make the complete system behave as intended.

Our software uses C++ and CUDA on embedded Linux, with Python for prototyping and data analysis. Prior radar experience is valuable, but strong numerical-software engineers from communications, embedded systems, robotics, or other sensing domains can also be a good fit. Accelerator experience matters; expert RTL design and a research-focused advanced degree are not prerequisites.

## What You'll Do

* Design, implement, test, and maintain C++ algorithms for real-time signal processing, detection, estimation, tracking, and radar resource management.
* Translate mathematical ideas and Python or MATLAB prototypes into product software, checking numerical behavior against independent reference implementations.
* Develop and optimize accelerated processing pipelines, using CUDA and GPU libraries where appropriate and collaborating with FPGA engineers on hardware-based processing.
* Make CPU/GPU/FPGA partitioning decisions based on latency, throughput, data movement, numerical requirements, and implementation complexity.
* Profile end-to-end processing, identify bottlenecks, and improve performance without sacrificing correctness or diagnosability.
* Integrate algorithm features with calibration, hardware control, scheduling, asynchronous data acquisition, and downstream processing.
* Analyze collected radar data, reproduce failures, and debug problems across algorithms, software, firmware, and hardware interfaces, including timing, calibration, and data-movement issues.
* Define focused experiments and carry features from prototype through bench integration, data replay, and field evaluation, using measured results to guide the next iteration.
* Build automated numerical and performance regression tests using reference comparisons, representative data replay, and target-hardware checks; turn diagnosed field failures into regression cases.
* Participate in code reviews and develop reusable components that support multiple radar products.
* Work with cross-functional partners to define achievable capability goals, communicate trade-offs, and deliver under changing priorities.

## Required Qualifications

* Bachelor's degree in computer science, electrical or computer engineering, applied mathematics, physics, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
* Strong hands-on C++ development skills, including numerical programming, memory management, data structures, maintainable interfaces, and debugging.
* Understanding of the assumptions and numerical behavior behind algorithms you have implemented, with relevant experience in signal processing, estimation, or other numerical applications.
* Hands-on experience implementing, integrating, or debugging accelerated numerical processing using CUDA or another GPU programming environment.
* Ability to reason about memory layout, synchronization, concurrency, and data movement in high-throughput or latency-sensitive systems.
* Experience validating algorithm correctness with reference comparisons and meaningful tests, and measuring performance rather than relying on intuition alone.
* Experience carrying a feature through implementation, integration, and validation, with clear ownership of your technical contribution and a practical approach to changing requirements.
* Ability to develop in Linux, use Git, and collaborate effectively through code review and cross-disciplinary debugging.
* Clear communication and willingness to work closely with hardware in the laboratory and support field testing as needed.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain a U.S. security clearance.
