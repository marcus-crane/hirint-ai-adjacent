Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

The Test and Evaluation team at Anduril plays a crucial role in our mission , ensuring that every product meets the highest standards of performance and reliability. Our team conducts full system level development testing, new production acceptance testing, software qualification testing and much more.   
  
**ABOUT THE JOB**  
In your role as a Test Site Asset Engineer on the Air Defense team, you are responsible for delivering and sustaining a fully mission-capable test site that meets our engineering teams' testing requirements and exceeds their expectations. You fulfill this responsibility through rigorous operational execution, detail orientation, proactive communication, deep understanding of the test mission, and hard-won product expertise. You feel ownership over the health and performance of all assets and technologies at your site, and ensure the system delivers when the team needs it most. Much of this work is hands-on and top-level: occasional hardware swaps, command-line SSH access, restarts, and basic troubleshooting to keep systems online. When an issue falls outside what can be resolved that way, you coordinate with subject matter experts across product lines and teams to diagnose and fix it, or coordinate SMEs and logistics to replace the affected hardware — communicating issues clearly and documenting solutions along the way. You also represent the realities of the test site back to Anduril, communicating feedback and lessons learned to continuously improve our products and our team. Above all, you set the standard for what good looks like at your site, and you rally your Anduril teammates to meet that standard every single day.  
  
**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Own the daily health of your assigned test site — serve as the single point of accountability for keeping all systems fully mission capable, monitoring status on a daily basis and driving any degradation to resolution
* Maintain systems through occasional hardware swaps, command-line SSH access, restarts, and top-level troubleshooting to resolve issues and restore capability with minimal downtime
* When an issue exceeds top-level troubleshooting, coordinate with subject matter experts across product lines and teams to diagnose and fix it, or coordinate SMEs and logistics to replace the affected hardware
* Clearly communicate issues to SMEs, effectively coordinate the path to resolution, and document solutions for future reference
* Work with engineering to define the correct equipment to have at each site to ensure representative testing
* Ensure our test site assets are fully mission capable across all sites — including fixed hardware, mobile hardware, and flight assets
* Work with site infrastructure to ensure assets have reliable power and network connectivity
* Create a sustainment and sparing plan for parts including budget and timing
* Track failure rates and anticipate maintenance windows to build a cost and support model for years to come
* Maintain lab spaces to a high level of organization and professionalism, creating processes for labeling, organization, and stock tracking
* Maintain hazard safety training and processes, fostering an environment and culture to promote safety as the number one priority
* Organize and control inventory of assets and spares
* Travel to test sites and field as needed

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 5+ years of professional experience in an engineering capacity or similar
* Demonstrated problem solving at the interface of hardware, software, and networking in unstructured environments
* Comfort working in a command-line environment (SSH, restarts, basic Linux workflows) to triage and resolve issues
* Working knowledge of IP networking fundamentals — troubleshooting connectivity, IP addressing, and basic network issues
* Strong communication and coordination skills — able to clearly convey technical issues to SMEs, drive cross-team resolution, and document outcomes
* Familiarity with basic AC/DC power (single-phase, three-phase)
* Demonstrated hands-on fabrication and assembly skills
* Impeccable attention to detail and passion for creating and maintaining high-performing, organized spaces
* Familiarity with Jira or other agile development software
* Inventory management experience
* Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, demanding, flexible team environment
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export controlled information or facilities

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience with environmental chamber equipment, vibration qualification testing, and/or battery discharge testing
* Familiarity with electro-mechanical systems such as motors, computers, and power systems
* Familiarity with hydraulics and hydraulic actuators

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
