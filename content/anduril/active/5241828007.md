Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### **ABOUT THE TEAM**

The Manufacturing Production team is responsible for a wide range of activities that are crucial to the manufacturing process, including operating equipment, assembling products, performing quality control checks, and maintaining a safe and efficient work environment.  
  
As a key part of the broader**Manufacturing Learning and Development team**, this role specifically leads the training execution, infrastructure, and delivery for our Supply Chain and Warehouse operations. The MFG Learning and Development team designs, develops, and delivers training programs that enhance technical proficiency, ensure compliance, and support career growth. By joining this team, you will ensure our materials and logistics training infrastructure is tightly aligned with our best practices and established standards for production training.

### **ABOUT THE JOB**

We’re building the future of defense manufacturing — and that starts with how we train, develop, and scale our workforce. As a member of the MFG Learning and Development team, the **Program Manager, Supply Chain & Warehouse Training** will lead the day-to-day execution and delivery of training across our warehousing and materials operations footprint.  
  
In this role, you will lead the hands-on work and inform the broader, long-term training strategy for the business unit. Your first priority will be standing up a standardized onboarding and training curriculum for our core materials roles (including Materials Handlers, Inventory Control, Materials Flow Specialists, and Production Coordinators) and scaling this training across all operating sites. You will start by managing a team of two training specialists, coaching them to deliver high-quality, hands-on, and systems-based learning solutions. Crucially, you will lead the effort to ensure that all supply chain training programs and infrastructure are aligned with the high standards, methodologies, and best practices established by the production training team.

---

### **WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* **Lead and Inform Training Strategy:** Lead the day-to-day execution and delivery of training programs for warehousing and materials operations, while providing critical floor-level insights to inform the broader MFG Training strategy.
* **Align with Production Standards:** Lead the work to ensure all supply chain training infrastructure, delivery methods, qualification standards, and content development align with the established best practices of the broader MFG Training & Development organization.
* **Design & Stand Up Onboarding:** Lead the creation and standardization of a highly effective onboarding program from scratch for Materials Handlers, Inventory Control, Materials Flow Specialists with a focus on scaling the curriculum across all operating sites.
* **Systems Training Execution:** Manage and deliver training programs for core supply chain systems, including Oracle, Forge WMS, and Autotime, while supporting the transition strategy away from legacy systems (Odoo, PackageX).
* **Lead and Develop the Team:** Provide direct leadership, coaching, and daily direction to a team of two training specialists, ensuring excellence in classroom facilitation, floor-level training, and documentation.
* **Track Outcomes & Drive Improvement:** Measure training effectiveness using key operational metrics (e.g., time-to-productivity, picking/packing accuracy, system transaction errors, safety compliance) and use this data to continuously improve programs.
* **Build Strong Relationships:** Partner closely with Supply Chain, Warehouse operations, Industrial Engineering, and Production leadership to understand operational pain points, build trust, and align training solutions to operational needs.

### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 8+ years of experience in Learning & Development, Technical Training, or Program Management.
* **Must have experience working within or directly supporting a supply chain, warehouse, or materials management function within a fast-paced production or manufacturing environment.**
* Proven experience building strong collaborative relationships with operational leaders and translating their business needs into training solutions.
* Experience leading and managing direct reports (this role will have 2 direct reports).
* Demonstrated experience executing, standardizing, and scaling training/onboarding programs across multiple physical locations or sites.
* Superb facilitation and coaching skills—able to engage and train frontline materials handlers, operations managers, and specialized industrial engineers.
* Strong organizational and project management skills, with the ability to lead multiple projects concurrently in a rapidly scaling environment.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance.

#### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Direct experience training staff on ERP and Warehouse Management Systems (WMS), specifically Oracle, Forge WMS, and Autotime.
* Experience supporting system migrations and tool adoption training
* Deep understanding of warehouse safety standards, 5S methodologies, inventory control practices (cycle counting, reconciliation), and materials flow engineering.
* Experience aligning training programs across distinct but dependent business units (e.g., aligning Warehouse Training with Manufacturing/Production standards).
* Lean Qualifications

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
