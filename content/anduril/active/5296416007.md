### ABOUT THE JOB

We are seeking a hands-on Finance Business Intelligence & Analytics Analyst to join our Strategic Financial Operations and Finance Data Systems team. In this role, you will build and deliver financial reporting and analytics for Finance and Accounting across Oracle Fusion and Databricks. You’ll work from requirements gathering through design, development, and iterative delivery of mission-critical reporting solutions. You will also take on special projects that help Finance and Accounting teams solve operational problems with data. You’ll be instrumental in turning complex financial needs into robust, scalable, and automated reporting that supports Anduril’s rapid growth and strategic decision-making. This role sits between Finance and Accounting and our Analytics Engineering team.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Design, build, and deploy financial reporting solutions within Finance and Accounting, partnering with Finance and Accounting stakeholders from requirements through delivery
* Build complex financial reports and datasets, ensuring data integrity, accuracy, and efficiency, and serve as a reporting subject matter expert for Oracle Fusion ERP, Oracle EPM, Databricks, and other platforms
* Build Finance and Accounting reporting, datasets, and dashboards in Databricks (SQL, AI/BI dashboards), working with Analytics Engineering to define requirements for curated finance and supply chain data models
* Reconcile Databricks reporting to Oracle source data (subledger to GL, report to report) so the data warehouse is trusted as a source of truth
* Support Finance master data management (MDM) processes and structures across core financial systems, partnering with Corporate Technology to keep critical financial dimensions for reporting accurate, consistent, and intact
* Integrate disparate financial data sources into a cohesive, reliable, and auditable Finance and Accounting reporting ecosystem, using curated data in the Databricks lakehouse in partnership with Analytics Engineering
* Deliver special projects that solve operational problems and support Finance and Accounting data transformation
* Proactively identify, champion, and deliver opportunities for process automation and reporting enhancements, and apply engineering best practices (version control, auditability, scalability, comprehensive testing) to all deliverables
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams, such as Corporate Technology, to create reporting solutions that scale with the business
* Serve as a technical reporting resource for Finance and Accounting stakeholders: translate complex financial requirements into technical reporting specifications, and explain technical solutions and their implications in business-friendly terms
* Ensure all financial reporting solutions comply with relevant regulations and internal control frameworks by implementing rigorous validation, reconciliation, and audit trails

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 5+ years of extensive, hands-on experience owning and leading finance and accounting systems reporting, with a proven track record of personally designing, building, and implementing reporting solutions from concept to production in a high-growth, technically advanced organization
* Ability to own projects end-to-end with minimal oversight
* Strong hands-on proficiency in reporting design and development within Oracle Fusion ERP (BI Publisher, OTBI), including a deep understanding of its data model
* Mastery of SQL, including Oracle SQL and Spark SQL / Databricks SQL, for complex data modeling, advanced querying, data manipulation, and performance optimization across diverse database environments
* Hands-on experience with a cloud data warehouse or lakehouse (Databricks preferred; Snowflake, Oracle, BigQuery, or Redshift acceptable), including querying large datasets and an understanding of dimensional modeling (facts, dimensions, grain)
* Understanding of finance and accounting principles
* Proven experience in Finance Master Data Management (MDM), including data modeling, data governance, and data quality initiatives, particularly within Oracle ERP (Fusion) and Oracle EPM environments
* Exceptional analytical and problem-solving skills, with a proven ability to break highly ambiguous and complex financial challenges into clear, actionable technical reporting solutions and then personally build and implement them
* A strong aptitude for engineering best practices, such as Git-based version control, modular design, automation, testing, and reconciliation discipline
* Outstanding verbal and written communication skills, with a proven ability to gather requirements from diverse stakeholders, present technical solutions, and influence adoption across the organization
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export controlled information or facilities

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Palantir Foundry experience

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
