Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

The mission of Anduril’s Mission Systems Division is to deliver integrated sensor, electromagnetic warfare, and autonomous systems that give warfighters decisive advantage across contested domains. Mission Systems is scaling fast — winning new business, ramping production, and fielding capability to operators who need it now. The opportunity in front of us is to connect every stage of that lifecycle — growth, deal stack, production, sustainment, and fielding — into a single, coherent data picture that lets leadership move faster and teams execute with full visibility. 

### ABOUT THE JOB

We’re hiring a Business Engineering Operations Lead to build that connective tissue. You’ll design the dashboards, data pipelines, and program management tools that give the division a real-time view of the entire business — and you’ll use that view to surface the strategic insights that drive what we do next. This role reports directly into Division Operations and works hand-in-hand with the Head of Engineering for Mission Systems. If you want to be the person who wires together a scaling defense division and puts the right information in front of the right people at the right time, this is the job. 

**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Design and build end-to-end program management tooling that connects the full Mission Systems lifecycle — growth and capture, deal stack, production, sustainment, and fielding — into a unified, navigable system
* Audit existing data pipelines across the division, identify gaps, redundancies, and disconnects between systems, and drive changes to close them — owning the problem from diagnosis through implementation
* Create the “one-stop shop” analytics platform for Mission Systems — a central hub where leadership can derive strategic initiatives, track key metrics, and extract actionable insights without chasing data across a dozen tools
* Develop dashboards, reports, and visualizations that translate complex cross-functional data into executive-quality narratives, enabling senior leadership to make faster, better-informed decisions on resource allocation, investments, and strategic priorities
* Identify and define processes that are missing or broken across the division — from data governance and reporting cadences to cross-team handoff workflows — and stand them up
* Directly support the Division Operations team and Head of Engineering for Mission Systems on strategic analyses, special projects, and operational problem-solving as a trusted analytical partner
* Evaluate and recommend tooling investments — whether building internal solutions, extending existing platforms, or integrating third-party tools — to ensure the division’s data infrastructure scales with the business
* Tackle special projects as needed — from automating recurring reports to building forecasting models to standing up data quality frameworks to cross-divisional process alignment

 **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* You’re a data-first problem solver who sees the thread connecting a new business opportunity in the pipeline to a production constraint on the floor to a sustainment gap in the field — and you build the tools that make those connections visible to everyone
* You’re highly autonomous — you don’t wait to be handed a requirements doc; you dig into the data, find what’s broken or missing, build the solution, and brief leadership on the impact
* You have strong technical skills in data analytics — you’re fluent in tools like Python, SQL, Tableau, Power BI, or similar platforms and can build everything from quick ad-hoc analyses to production-grade dashboards
* You actively use AI tools like Claude to accelerate your work — whether it’s drafting analysis frameworks, writing code, automating reporting workflows, or pressure-testing your own thinking — and you’re always looking for ways to multiply your output through intelligent tooling
* You’re a translator — you can sit with a business leader, understand the strategic question they’re really asking, turn it into a technical requirement, and then execute the build yourself or drive it to completion with the right team
* You’re comfortable operating across organizational boundaries, pulling data and requirements from engineering, program management, business development, production, and sustainment teams that don’t naturally share information
* You have strong written and verbal communication, with the polish to present strategic insights to senior executives and the directness to drive process adoption across working-level teams
* You’re both high ownership and low ego — you’ll architect a division-wide analytics strategy in the morning and manually clean a messy spreadsheet to unblock a deliverable in the afternoon
* You have 3–5 years of experience in Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, Program Management, or Operations — ideally in a defense, hardware, or high-growth technology environment
* Experience with data pipeline tools, ETL workflows, BI platforms, or internal tooling development is a strong plus
* Must be eligible to hold a US Top Secret clearance

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
