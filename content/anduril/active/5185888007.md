The Test and Evaluations Systems Integrator will be responsible for integrating and testing software and hardware systems to ensure they function together seamlessly. This role requires a strong understanding of both software and hardware components, as well as experience in networking and hardware integration.  
  
The ideal candidate will be comfortable working in computer terminals and have a deep knowledge of Linux system architecture. They will be responsible for assisting in the implementation of test plans, evaluating system performance, and identifying areas for improvement. The T&E Systems Integrator will also be responsible for integrating new hardware and software components into new and existing systems, ensuring compatibility and optimal performance.  
  
**WHAT YOU'LL DO:**

* Identify and resolve software and hardware issues
* Integrate new hardware and software components into new and existing systems
* Evaluate system performance and identify areas for improvement
* Configure and admin IP based LAN & wireless networks
* Configure and admin Ardupilot based flight controllers
* Configure and admin embedded Linux systems
* Collaborating with cross-functional teams to ensure test requirements are met
* Provide technical guidance and support to team members
* Lead Flight Test Operations as an RPIC by ensuring aircraft readiness, creating mission profiles, and adhering to safety protocols

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Proven experience integrating and testing software and hardware systems
* Experience with Linux system admin via command line
* Strong knowledge of networking protocols and technologies
* Experience with hardware integration, including experience with sensors, camera systems, embedded systems, radios, IP network switches, micro controllers, actuators.
* Ability to make wiring harnesses. Including soldering, crimping, fault isolation
* Repair of avionics systems malfunctions
* Familiarity with testing methodologies and tools
* Strong problem-solving skills and ability to troubleshoot issues
* Able to work extended hours as required
* Strong familiarity with Ardupilot based flight controllers
* Obtain and maintain a FAA part 107 certificate
* In this role, you will be subject to pre-employment and randomized substance screening, as required by the Company.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:**

* RC pilot experience in Groups 1 - 3 rotary and fixed wing UAVs
* Good understanding of aircraft/system performance and log data analysis
* Experience with scripting languages
* Familiarity with version control systems
* Familiarity with test automation tools and frameworks

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
