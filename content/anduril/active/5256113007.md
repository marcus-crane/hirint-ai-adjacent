### ABOUT THE JOB

The Environmental, Health & Safety (EHS) Systems & Analytics Specialist is responsible for the quality, integrity, and presentation of EHS and sustainability data across the enterprise. This role owns the metrics, dashboards, and reporting that EHS, business leadership, and the Board use to understand safety and sustainability performance, and makes certain that the data behind those reports is complete, accurate, and traceable to a defined source.  
  
This position also owns the company's safety observation and close call program, and supports the EHS Operations, Environmental, and Sustainability teams in tracking compliance obligations, analyzing audit results, and responding to audit and records requests. Because much of this data is confidential medical or personal information, the Specialist is responsible for the controls that protect employee privacy in everything EHS publishes.  
  
This position does not own recordability determinations or the OSHA 300 logs, which remain with the EHS Operations team. Instead, the Specialist makes certain the underlying data is complete and accurate, and that required reports are completed and filed on time.  
  
The Specialist partners with site EHS teams, the Environmental and Sustainability managers, Legal, and Anduril's data and software teams, and builds breadth across safety, environmental, and sustainability disciplines with support for professional certification.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

Data Management, Quality & Privacy

* Serve as the data steward for EHS and sustainability data, owning the definition and documented source of every metric the function publishes.
* Audit data completeness, classification consistency, and accuracy across sites, and partner with site EHS teams to correct the root causes of poor data entry.
* Define and maintain the standards for how EHS events are recorded and classified, so data is comparable across campuses.
* Own the controls that protect employee privacy in published data, including minimum cell sizes, suppression of small and complementary cells, reporting rates rather than counts, and exclusion of narrative text and personal identifiers from broadly visible reporting.
* Partner with Legal and the data teams on appropriate access, permissions, and handling of confidential medical and personal information.

Metrics, Dashboards & Executive Reporting

* Design, build, and maintain the EHS dashboards used by EHS, site leadership, and business leaders.
* Develop and maintain leading indicator metrics, including reporting participation, corrective action closure and aging, and other measures of management system effectiveness.
