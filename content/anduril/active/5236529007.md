## ABOUT THE TEAM

Anduril's Electronic Warfare (EW) division builds next-generation electronic warfare platforms — hardware-enabled, software-defined systems that are fielded, operational, and scaling fast. The EW team operates at a tempo the defense industry isn't used to: delivering novel system configurations into active operations in days, not months, securing major contract awards in under 24 hours, and earning unanimous fielding recommendations from government evaluators. Production is scaling from hundreds to thousands of units across multiple product variants, and the release and delivery challenges are scaling with it.

### ABOUT THE ROLE

We are looking for a Software Product Manager to own what ships to the operator. EW Mission Applications builds the software between the device and the operator — operator applications, Lattice integrations, shared visualization components, and the internal tooling that the rest of the business line runs on. You will own the roadmap for that surface: which capabilities reach operators, through which channel, and in what order.

EW Mission Applications delivers through multiple channels: each channel has different users, different timelines, and a different definition of done. Teams are organized by product surface rather than by program, so every engineering team supports several programs at once. Every program in the business line wants something from those teams. You decide what gets built, you write down why, and you hold that line when program pressure arrives — which it will, constantly, and for good reasons.

This role is product-focused and technically grounded. You won't write production code day to day, but you need to understand the device SDK boundary, the client architecture, and the integration surface well enough to tell a real blocker from a preference, to write requirements engineering can build against, and to negotiate scope with embedded engineering on the merits.

### 

### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* Own the EW Mission Applications product roadmap: what is committed, what is queued, what we declined and why — published and current enough that program management and engineering leads both plan against it
* Run a single intake path for product requirements from programs, customers, partners, and internal teams, and give every request a scoped, sized answer: accept, defer, or decline, with a reason
* Define operator capabilities before design and engineering start — the problem, the workflow, and the acceptance criteria that QA builds integration coverage against
* Deconflict competing program demands on shared engineering teams using a written, defensible prioritization framework
* Represent EW Mission Applications in the recurring device API review: bring the integration needs, justify the priority, agree the build order with embedded engineering, and track requests through to delivery
* Partner with UI design on operator workflows and with QA on test coverage, owning the requirement behind both
* Turn recurring field issues from our mission software engineering team into roadmap items and test coverage on a standing cadence
* Track delivery commitments against actuals and publish the results, so our estimates improve and our release commitments earn credibility
* Brief delivery status, roadmap changes, and risk to stakeholders at every level — engineers, program leadership, and customers — without flattening technical nuance into a status color
* Travel up to 25% to support integration events, deployments, field visits, and customer engagements

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 5+ years in software product management, technical product ownership, or a closely related role
* Direct ownership of a product roadmap, including the authority and the track record to decline work
* Demonstrated ability to write requirements and acceptance criteria that engineering and test executed against
* Strong written and verbal communication skills — you'll be writing the roadmap, running prioritization calls, and briefing leadership
* Experience owning a software product surface that depended on hardware or firmware built by a team you did not control
* Experience deconflicting priorities across concurrent customers or programs competing for the same engineering capacity
* Ability to read and navigate codebases and API surfaces: you don't need to write production code, but you need to understand what is changing and what it costs
* Comfortable operating with incomplete information and requirements that move
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Top Secret/SCI security clearance
* Willingness to travel up to 25%

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
