## ABOUT THE JOB

Every military capability, whether a robot, a fired effect, or a sensor, has to survive at the edge before it can do anything else. Anduril's Expeditionary Systems (AES) business line exists to make that possible. We build containerized combat systems for U.S. and Allied militaries: expeditionary command centers, AI factories, and containerized effects platforms. Anduril's ruggedized power and common shared architecture. These systems address the growing demand for high-performance compute at the tactical edge, driven by LLM and HPC workloads, while offering a survivable, deployable alternative to fixed data centers that are increasingly vulnerable targets.  
  
Each system ships as a complete, mission-ready package, engineered from proven building blocks and designed to scale to hundreds of units per year. The containerized form factor enables rapid deployment, tactical flexibility, and low-cost proliferation of capabilities and munitions. The result is a single architecture from which a warfighter can command, compute, and deliver effects, the complete physical stack for AI-driven warfare at the edge  
  
We are seeking a highly experienced Electrical Engineer to serve as Technical Lead for the Fire Control Module (FCM), the circuit card assembly that connects our containerized effects platforms to their armament payloads. The FCM is the electrical bridge between platform and weapon: it carries Universal Armament Interface (UAI) communications to the payload over MIL-STD-1553, and it controls the safety-critical power, interlock, and release circuitry used to prepare, arm, and launch munitions. As Technical Lead, you will own the FCM end to end, from architecture and schematic through qualification, production, and fielding, and you will be the technical authority for the module across systems, software, firmware, mechanical, test, and safety engineering, as well as with our payload partners and customers.  
  
Exact candidate title and leveling to be determined based upon technical acumen, professional experience, leadership ability, and assessed capabilities through the interview process.

## WHAT YOU'LL DO

* Serve as technical lead and design owner for the Fire Control Module PCBA, accountable for its architecture, design maturity, performance, schedule, and technical risk from concept through production.
* Define the FCM board-level architecture and detailed design, including the MIL-STD-1553 interface, discrete I/O, payload power switching and distribution, and safety-critical arm, fire, and release circuitry.
* Implement the electrical layer for UAI-compliant communications with armament payloads, partnering with firmware and software teams on store management, initialization, and arming/release sequencing.
* Translate platform and payload Interface Control Documents (ICDs) into electrical requirements, and drive interface compatibility with payload vendors and government partners.
* Design safety-critical circuits with independent hardware inhibits, interlocks, and fault tolerance against inadvertent arming or launch, and support hazard analyses, FMEA/FMECA, and weapon safety review board submissions.
* Own the full electronics design cycle: requirements definition, major component selection, schematic capture, layout constraints and routing rules, bring-up, debug, test, validation, and integration in system.
* Conduct component and architecture trade studies balancing performance, safety, cost, manufacturability, obsolescence, and future scalability.
* Perform signal integrity and power integrity analysis of circuit designs, including MIL-STD-1553 coupling, termination, and stub design.
* Lead qualification of the FCM to environmental, EMI/EMC, and power quality requirements (e.g., MIL-STD-810, MIL-STD-461, MIL-STD-1275), including E3 and HERO considerations for ordnance-interfacing electronics.
* Design for manufacturability and test, define the production test strategy, and own the test fixtures used to scale the FCM into production with our contract manufacturers.
* Lead payload integration, compatibility testing, and live-fire events, along with field testing and operational validation through military exercises and real-world deployments.
* Lead design reviews (SRR/PDR/CDR), own FCM design documentation, and provide technical direction and mentorship to engineers supporting the module.
* Design rugged, high-reliability electronics for our warfighters.
