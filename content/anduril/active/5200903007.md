### ABOUT THE JOB

Anduril designs and fields systems across an unusually broad materials portfolio, and the design decisions behind them depend on mechanical test data that is accurate, repeatable, and delivered on time. This role owns the execution of that testing.

This is your opportunity to be the person who makes the materials lab run — turning a growing set of capital equipment into dependable throughput, taking routine testing off engineers’ plates, and setting the standard for how mechanical testing gets done. You’ll own the bench, not just a task on it.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* **Run Mechanical Testing.** Execute tensile, compression, and other mechanical tests to established methods (e.g., ASTM / ISO) across programs — set up fixturing, operate the load frame, and capture results accurately.
* **Prepare and Manage Specimens.** Machine, measure, label, and track test specimens, maintaining traceability from receipt through reported result.
* **Own Data Quality.** Capture, reduce, and record test data so results are accurate, complete, and analysis-ready for the engineering team.
* **Keep the Lab Running.** Perform routine equipment upkeep, calibration checks, and consumable management to maximize uptime and equipment utilization.
* **Take Testing Off Engineers’ Plates.** Absorb routine test execution so engineering time returns to design, analysis, and program support rather than running the bench.
* **Uphold Lab Safety and Order.** Maintain a safe, clean, well-organized lab and follow all material-handling, PPE, and safety protocols.

#### WHO YOU ARE

* You take pride in doing hands-on work precisely and repeatably. A test run to method and data you can stand behind is its own reward.
* You are meticulous about the details that make data trustworthy — alignment, calibration, labeling, and traceability.
* You have a bias for action and ownership. You keep the queue moving and don’t wait to be asked.
* You communicate clearly with the engineers you support, surfacing anomalies rather than burying them.
* You care about a safe, orderly lab and treat the equipment like it’s your own.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Associate degree in a technical field, or equivalent hands-on experience in a materials, mechanical, or manufacturing test environment
* 3+ years of hands-on experience operating mechanical test equipment (servo-hydraulic or electromechanical load frames)
* Experience with specimen preparation and machining, precision measurement (calipers, micrometers, extensometers), and test fixturing
* Working knowledge of standardized test methods (e.g., ASTM / ISO) and the discipline to follow them exactly
* Careful data-capture and record-keeping habits, with strong attention to traceability
* Comfort with basic shop and hand tools and a strong lab-safety mindset
* Ability to manage a queue of work and shifting priorities independently in a fast-paced environment
* Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. Top Secret security clearance

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Active U.S. Secret or Top Secret security clearance
* Experience with fatigue, fracture, or other advanced / dynamic mechanical testing
* Experience testing advanced or non-standard materials — composites, high-temperature alloys, or additively-manufactured parts
* Familiarity with metallography or specimen sectioning, mounting, and polishing
* Hands-on experience with test-frame software and data-acquisition systems (e.g., Instron, MTS)
* Calibration, gauge R&R, or equipment-maintenance experience
