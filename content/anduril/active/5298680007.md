Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

## Key Responsibilities Technical Execution & Operations

* Deployment Support: Support deployments, customer test events, and field operations by executing technical operations, conducting field maintenance, and providing customer field support under the direction of a senior engineer.
* Launcher & GSE Setup: Help set up, operate, and tear down launcher and ground support equipment — canisters, cabling, power, and handling equipment — across multiple launch configurations.
* Checkout & Load: Run built-in test (BIT), preflight checkout, and mission load / reprogramming on the round using the provided tools and procedures.
* Procedural Adherence: Follow documented procedures and SOPs to complete assigned work accurately and safely, working within strict energetics and range-safety requirements exactly as trained.
* System Maintenance: Perform preventative maintenance and basic troubleshooting on launcher subsystems — hydraulics, onboard power generation, cabling, and connectors.
* Documentation Compliance: Use internal systems (e.g., JIRA, Marcus, Odoo) to complete tasks, close tickets, and maintain accurate records.

## Problem Solving & Troubleshooting

* Routine Issue Resolution: Tackle hardware, software, and networking issues using established methods; seek guidance for unfamiliar situations.
* Methodical Troubleshooting: Apply a structured approach to fault isolation between the round, the cabling, and the ground equipment, and escalate clearly when an issue exceeds your training.
* Root Cause Analysis: Support incident response and root cause analyses for systems in the field.

## Learning & Knowledge Development

* Skill Building: Build expertise through repetition, feedback, and exposure to deployments; actively develop technical knowledge across assigned systems and subsystems.
* Documentation Review: Follow technical documentation, work instructions, and troubleshooting guides to complete tasks and tickets — and redline them when a step doesn't match reality in the field.
* Continuous Learning: Capture lessons learned during deployments in an easily referenceable format, and feed issues and failures back to the senior engineer and to engineering.

## Communication & Collaboration

* Status Communication: Communicate field issue status to internal and customer teams, keeping stakeholders informed.
* Team Collaboration: Work effectively with engineers, technicians, and team leads to support seamless execution.
* Safety Culture: Adhere to safety procedures, guidance, and best practices around energetics and live systems, and encourage teammates to do the same — no improvising around safety.

## Required Qualifications

* 2+ years of experience in technical field operations, hands-on hardware/software support, or similar operational roles — or equivalent military/technical training.
* Strong problem-solving skills in unstructured environments at the interface of hardware, software, and networking.
* Ability to follow documented procedures and technical guidance with accuracy and attention to detail.
* Mechanical aptitude for troubleshooting and maintaining technical equipment, tools, cabling, and connectors.
* Strong communication skills; able to clearly convey status updates and technical issues to teammates and stakeholders.
* Strong willingness to learn complex systems quickly and to take direction in a safety-critical, fast-moving environment.
* Willingness to travel up to 80% domestically and internationally, including to austere field environments.
* Familiarity with safety procedures and risk-assessment practices.
* Willingness to relocate to Costa Mesa, CA or Columbus, OH (Arsenal-1).
* Eligibility to obtain and maintain a U.S. Secret or Top Secret security clearance and support operations involving sensitive materials.
* Ability to support the handling of and working with munitions.
* Ability to meet health and medical requirements in advance of deployments in accordance with DFARS 252.225-7040, DoDI 3020.41, and/or any additional requirements outlined by region or supporting force, as applicable.

## Preferred Qualifications

* Bachelor's or associate degree in Engineering, Technology, or a related technical field — or equivalent hands-on training (military tech school, trade certification, apprenticeship).
* Prior military or field service experience in a technical operations, munitions, ordnance, avionics, or weapons-maintenance field — USAF 2W0X1 / 2W1X1, Navy AO / AT, Army 89B / 89D, USMC 2311 / 2336, or equivalent.
* Ammunition handling, range operations, or any safety-critical hazardous-materials background.
* Experience with work-order management systems (e.g., JIRA, Marcus, Odoo).
* Basic understanding of networking concepts, power systems, hydraulics, or related technical domains.
* Exposure to Linux and the command line, soldering or cable termination, or reading schematics.
* Familiarity with MIL-STD-1553/1760 or munition test equipment (CMBRE/CMBRE+) — a plus, not expected.
* Experience with hand tools, forklifts, or aerial lifts.

## What Success Looks Like

* Reliable Execution: You consistently complete assigned tasks accurately, safely, and on time using established procedures.
* Growing Independence: You demonstrate increasing ability to operate independently on routine tasks with minimal supervision.
* Procedural Compliance: You follow SOPs and documentation standards, contributing to operational discipline and team culture.
* Knowledge Capture: You document lessons learned and redline procedures in ways that are easily referenceable for future deployments.
* Safety First: You maintain zero preventable safety incidents and actively support a culture of safety.
* Team Contribution: You communicate status clearly, collaborate well, and support overall mission success.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
