### ABOUT THE JOB

Anduril is seeking a Senior Structural Analyst to join RMS in Huntsville, AL. As a Senior Structural Analyst, you will own the structural analysis of solid rocket motor hardware: propellant grains, motor cases, nozzles, igniters, and ignition safety devices. You'll operate in a lean, high-ownership culture where you directly shape the product and move fast enough to test new designs in months, not years. The right person combines first-principles depth in stress, dynamics, shock/vibration, and thermal-structural analysis with fluency in the tools of the trade, and wants their analysis to be tied to real hardware and real test data.

* Perform static and dynamic structural analysis across metallics, composites, and viscoelastics (propellant, insulation, liner) using the right tool for the job, from hand calculations and scripts to detailed FEA.
* Build and own FEMs at the global and subsystem level, including motor cases, nozzles, and grain, along with model organization, documentation, and configuration control.
* Analyze coupled and transient environments such as ignition pressurization, shock and vibration, flight and captive-carry loads, and thermal-structural interaction, where the answer isn't a closed-form margin.
* Set and defend structural margins against customer requirements, industry standards, and safety regulations.
* Partner with design and hardware engineers to turn analytical results into better designs, fast.
* Plan and support validation testing: instrumentation and load requirements, Do-Not-Exceed limits, real-time load monitoring, data collection, model-to-test correlation, and dispositioning of inspection findings.
* Document methodology and results in technical reports for internal teams and external stakeholde

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* BS or higher in engineering or applied physics with a structures focus.
* Strong command of statics, dynamics, mechanics of materials, and material behavior.
* Proficiency in commercial FEA (ANSYS, Abaqus, or NASTRAN), including linear and nonlinear static and buckling solution sequences.
* Proficiency in Python or MATLAB (or equivalent) for analytical modeling, data reduction, and automation.
* Experience with CAD tools (NX, SolidWorks).
* Comfort establishing scope, requirements, and methodology for ambiguous, ill-defined problems, and driving them to closure with minimal direction.

### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 5+ years of relevant structural analysis experience, or equivalent education, and demonstrated ownership of hardware outcomes.
* MS or higher in Mechanical Engineering or applied physics with a structures focus.
* Structural analysis experience on solid rocket motors or comparable pressure-vessel / harsh-environment hardware.
* Solid rocket motor propellant grain structural analysis (viscoelastic materials, bore strain, cure shrinkage, thermal cycling).
* Composite and filament-wound case analysis and tooling such as WoundSim, CadWound, or similar.
* Hands-on structural test experience: sensor selection, data acquisition, and post-processing (static, buckling, cyclic).
* Workflow automation tooling.
