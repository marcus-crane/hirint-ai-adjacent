### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for a Production Supervisor to join our rapidly growing team at our Morrisville, North Carolina facility. In this role, you will be responsible for leading front-line production teams through the day-to-day execution of manufacturing operations, ensuring work is performed safely, on schedule, and to precise quality standards. You will oversee shift operations, troubleshoot production issues in real time, and partner with engineering, quality, and EHS teams to mature processes as we scale from development toward full-rate production. This will require hands-on manufacturing leadership experience, a strong working knowledge of production systems and quality processes, and the ability to make sound decisions in a fast-paced, safety-critical environment. If you are someone who thrives on building structure in undefined or evolving environments, holds your team accountable to high standards while developing their skills, and stays calm and decisive under pressure, then this role is for you.  
  
**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Lead, motivate, and manage a number of front-line team members. Provide training, facilitate professional development, and conduct performance reviews.
* Champion an EHS-focused culture by ensuring production processes, equipment, and team members comply with established requirements. Actively work with the EHS team to continuously improve processes and drive accountability to 5S standards.
* Ensure products meet all quality standards and specifications. Implement and utilize quality control processes, participate in improvement and corrective action activities, and drive reduction in variability.
* Work closely with production planning to execute production schedules to meet demand requirements.
* Develop and refine process instructions. Ensure these processes are diligently communicated, trained to, and followed.
* Continuously evaluate and improve production processes to enhance efficiency, reduce waste, and lower costs.
* Coordinate with the maintenance team to ensure all production equipment is optimally utilized.
* Drive urgency and accountability by leveraging tools such as SPC, KPIs, and other performance data analysis. Employ problem solving and data-driven decision-making, and coach the production team to do the same.

## **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 5+ years experience in a production or manufacturing environment, including at least 2 years in a supervisory role
* Strong leadership, organization, and communication skills
* Demonstrated history of solid problem solving skills and decision-making ability
* Ability to work under pressure and meet deadlines
* Proficiency in production management software and Microsoft Office
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance

## **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Manufacturing, Business Management, or a related field
* Exposure to multiple production management platforms including ERPs, MESs, and other data management systems
* Experience working in safety and quality critical environments such as energetics, aerospace, chemicals, etc.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
