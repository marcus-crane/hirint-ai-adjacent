Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

As part of our Anduril Rocket Motor Systems team, you will safely and compliantly operate commercial vehicles to transport dangerous goods (DG), hazardous waste, inert chemicals, parts, tooling, and other mission-essential cargo on and off the facility in McHenry, Mississippi. In addition, you will support local and regional movements while meeting strict regulatory, safety, and security standards in a high-hazard rocket motor production environment.

**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Operate commercial vehicles for local and short-haul dedicated routes originating from the Rocket Motor Systems facility in McHenry, MS
* Safely transport DG and non-DG materials including chemicals, hazardous substances, rocket motor components, live motors, parts, tooling, and other sensitive items on and off site
* Perform thorough pre-trip, post-trip, and en-route vehicle and cargo inspections per DOT, FMCSA, and Anduril standards
* Ensure all shipments are properly packaged, marked, labeled, placarded, and documented in accordance with 49 CFR,  and company requirements
* Load and unload vehicles using proper techniques; secure loads with approved blocking, bracing, and tie-down methods suitable for live motors and hazardous cargo
* When applicable, verify dispatch tickets, shipping papers, & Safety Data Sheets (SDS).
* Comply with all hours-of-service regulations, vehicle weight limits, route restrictions, and hazardous materials transport rules
* Respond appropriately to emergencies, spills, or incidents following company and regulatory emergency response procedures
* Maintain accurate driver logs, delivery records, and required documentation
* Coordinate closely with logistics, shipping/receiving, engineering, and operations teams for timely on/off-site movements
* Perform minor vehicle servicing and maintain cleanliness and serviceability of assigned vehicles
* Complete and maintain required HAZMAT, security awareness, and driver safety training
* Operate forklifts and material handling equipment as needed for loading/unloading
* Strictly adhere to all Anduril safety protocols, ITAR/FAR/DFARS, and classified/sensitive material handling procedures

**Skills & Competencies**

* Strong knowledge of hazardous materials regulations and safe handling practices, especially with energetic materials and rocket motors
* Excellent defensive driving skills and situational awareness
* Ability to work independently with minimal supervision in a secure facility environment
* High attention to detail regarding documentation, cargo security, and safety
* Good physical condition and stamina for loading/unloading operations
* Strong communication and teamwork skills
* Flexibility to support changing schedules and urgent mission-driven requirements

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Valid Commercial Driver’s License (CDL) Class A with Hazardous Materials (H) endorsement
* Minimum 5+ years of verifiable commercial driving experience
* Clean Motor Vehicle Record (MVR) with no major violations
* Ability to pass DOT medical examination and pre-employment drug/alcohol screening
* Minimum 5+ years experience transporting hazardous chemicals, flammable materials, or live ordnance/motors strongly preferred
* Ability to lift and move materials up to 50+ lbs and work outdoors in varying weather conditions
* Must be eligible to obtain and maintain a U.S. Secret clearance

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Current TWIC card holder
* 10+ years of verifiable commercial driving experience
* 10+ experience transporting hazardous chemicals, flammable materials, or live ordnance/motors strongly preferred
* Familiar with RCRA/HAZWASTE procedures

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
