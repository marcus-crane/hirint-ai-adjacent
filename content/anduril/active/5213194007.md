### ABOUT THE JOB

As a **Business Operations Analyst**, you will sit at the intersection of operations, strategy, and data, turning messy, complex operational challenges into structured data to inform high stakes business decisions.  
  
Your primary focus will be producing high-quality, repeatable data analysis and synthesis to enable informed, data-backed decision-making across leadership and cross-functional teams. You will get hands-on with our data architecture—leveraging modern enterprise data platforms and analytical tools to establish reliable sources of truth, build intuitive dashboards, and translate quantitative findings into strategic narratives for executive audiences.

### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* **Build Repeatable Analytics Workflows:** Design, execute, and automate high-quality, repeatable data models and analytical pipelines to track critical operational, business, and financial metrics.
* **Drive Business Decision-Making:** Partner closely with leadership and functional stakeholders to deliver actionable insights, evaluate operational trade-offs, and guide strategic decisions through data synthesis.
* **Data Visualization & Dashboarding:** Develop clear, self-service dashboards and visualization frameworks that equip functional teams with the tools and metrics to monitor and improve their processes.
* **Executive Storytelling:** Translate complex analyses into clean, executive-ready presentations, reports, and narratives, with results presented directly to senior leadership teams.
* **System & Tool Deep-Dives:** Work deeply within our data tools and business infrastructure (including Salesforce, Oracle, Palantir Foundry, Databricks, and other core enterprise systems) to execute scrappy analyses on short timelines and build robust data pipelines over the long term.
* **Drive Operational Excellence:** Proactively identify friction and process bottlenecks across the organization, using quantitative analysis to drive sustainable operational solutions.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* **2–5 years of professional experience** in Business Operations, Business Analytics, Strategy & Operations, Management Consulting, Banking, or a similar analytical operating role.
* **Demonstrated expertise in Business Analytics and Data Visualization:** Proven track record of structuring ambiguous operational problems, manipulating large datasets, and presenting data visually to influence decisions.
* **Enterprise Data Tooling:** Hands-on experience querying and working within modern data and analytics platforms (e.g., Salesforce, Oracle, Palantir Foundry, Databricks, SQL, or equivalent enterprise data environments).
* **High Fluency in Excel & PowerPoint:** Advanced capability in Excel (complex modeling, pivot tables, data manipulation) and PowerPoint (building polished, executive-ready narrative decks).
* **High Ownership & Bias for Action:** You are self-driven, hyper-organized, and detail-oriented; you step into gaps proactively and follow through from insight to execution.
* **Communication & Stakeholder Management:** Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to distill sophisticated quantitative insights clearly to senior leadership and diverse cross-functional teams.
* **Security & Compliance:** Must be a U.S. Person and eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. security clearance due to access to export-controlled information.
* Willing to travel as necessary (up to 25%)

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
