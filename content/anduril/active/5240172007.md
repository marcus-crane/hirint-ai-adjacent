Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for an Industrial Engineer to join our rapidly growing team in Quonset, RI. This role sits within our technical shared services organization (3SI) and carries a dual mandate. First, you operate as a flexible resource for the site: production lines "lease" your time to design and optimize their manufacturing operations as their needs peak and shift — moving across programs and bringing data-driven IE rigor to production line and workstation design, capital equipment, and the metrics that keep a line healthy. Second, you hold a standing factory-macro remit on behalf of 3SI: owning site-level factory layouts, leading cross-functional technical projects, and optimizing material flow across the whole facility rather than any single line. You'll zoom from one line's workstation up to how the entire site moves material and uses its floor space, working shoulder-to-shoulder with Engineering, Supply Chain, Quality, Business Operations, and the Factory Services team to get complex undersea hardware built and shipped to spec.  
  
If you are someone who thrives on bringing order to a fast-scaling production floor — and can hold both the single-line detail and the whole-factory picture at once — this role is for you.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Deliver core Industrial Engineering standards and capabilities to Quonset's production lines — capacity planning, labor standards, time studies, workstation and line layouts, and discrete event simulation (DES)
* Deploy across production lines as a shared resource — partnering with each line's Manufacturing leads to scope the work, deliver it, and hand off cleanly
* Own site-level factory layout and material flow — plan and maintain the master floor layout and optimize how material moves between receiving, storage, lineside, and shared services as programs scale and new capabilities come online
* Specify, justify, and deploy capital equipment and tooling — define requirements, own the business case and capex justification, and support vendor selection, build, install, and qualification through to production readiness.
* Lead cross-functional technical projects on behalf of 3SI — scope, schedule, and drive site infrastructure, equipment, and process-improvement initiatives from definition through execution.
* Own operations health metrics and KPIs and build the tools and reports that communicate line and site health across the org.
* Identify bottlenecks and constraints across layout, part shortages, staffing, and tooling, then drive resolution with Manufacturing, Supply Chain, Engineering, and Quality.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 5+ years past experience in a rate manufacturing environment and production-type role where complex mechanical and/or electrical assemblies were built.
* Experience using real operational data to make informed suggestions and decisions.
* Experience and demonstrated skills with AutoCAD.
* Proven line/facility design: value stream mapping, continuous/one-piece flow, and workstation design
* Experience with Lean Manufacturing principles/implementation.
* Able to read technical documentation (drawings, vendor schematics, CAD) and understand hardware processes across electronics and mechanical structures
* Understanding of the means and methods of how material flows around a facility including but not limited to pull systems + triggers, Kanban and MIN MAX calculations.
* Experience modeling operations using discrete event simulation.
* Working knowledge of an MES and an ERP (Oracle or equivalent).
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance.

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Bachelor's or higher in Industrial, Mechanical, Aerospace, Manufacturing, Electrical, or Computer Engineering.
* Hands-on design of tooling, fixtures, or GSE (ground support equipment) for heavy or large-format hardware.
* Data analytics knowledge and experience including the use of SQL to create complex custom queries, mining and joining tables from production and supply chain databases (experience with Palantir Foundry a plus).
* Experience creating and owning program, project or production schedules.
* Experience with Simio and/or Siemen’s Plant Simulation.
* Experience in a marine, subsea, or other regulated/harsh-environment hardware program

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
