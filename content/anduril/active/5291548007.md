**ABOUT THE ROLE**

We are looking for a Senior Wireless Network Engineer to lead the design, deployment, and ongoing optimization of our enterprise wireless infrastructure. This role owns the full lifecycle of our Wi-Fi environment from RF survey and network design through implementation, performance tuning, and incident response. You will serve as the subject matter expert for all things wireless, collaborating closely with network, security, and facilities teams to deliver reliable, high-performance connectivity across our sites.

**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Lead RF site surveys using Ekahau survey tools to assess coverage, capacity, and interference, and produce detailed heat maps and design documentation.
* Architect and deploy enterprise wireless networks using Aruba and Mist platforms, including access point placement, controller configuration, and SSID design.
* Design and optimize RF environments for high-density deployments, ensuring appropriate channel planning, power settings, and band steering policies.
* Own wireless network performance: establish baselines, monitor KPIs, troubleshoot degraded performance, and drive root cause analysis for recurring issues.
* Manage the full lifecycle of wireless infrastructure including firmware upgrades, certificate management, and hardware refreshes.
* Define and enforce wireless security standards including 802.1X authentication, EAP methods, RADIUS integration, and rogue AP detection.
* Develop and maintain technical documentation including as-built diagrams, standard operating procedures, and runbooks.
* Mentor junior network engineers and serve as an escalation point for complex wireless issues.
* Evaluate emerging wireless technologies and contribute to the long-term network roadmap.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* **10+ years** of experience in enterprise networking with a focused specialty in wireless infrastructure.
* **Deep hands-on experience with Aruba** wireless platforms (Aruba Central, AOS-CX, ArubaOS) and Juniper Mist (Mist AI cloud dashboard, Marvis virtual assistant).
* **Proficiency with Ekahau** site survey and design tools (Ekahau Site Survey / Ekahau AI Pro).
* **Strong RF design fundamentals:** 802.11 standards antenna theory, channel planning, path loss modeling, and interference mitigation.
* **Experience with 802.1X/EAP, RADIUS** - certificate-based authentication.
* **Solid understanding of network fundamentals:** VLANs, switching, routing, QoS, DHCP, and DNS as they relate to wireless deployments.
* **Experience supporting high-density environments** such as conference centers, warehouses, or large open-plan offices.
* **Ability to read and produce** RF heat maps, floor plans, and network diagrams.
* Ability to obtain and maintain a US Secret Clearance

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Relevant certifications: Aruba Certified Mobility Expert (ACMX), Mist AI Wireless (JNCIS-MistAI or equivalent), CWNE, or similar.
* Familiarity with Ekahau Connect and collaborative survey workflows.
* Experience with network automation and scripting (Python, Ansible) for configuration management.
* Exposure to IoT and OT network segmentation in wireless environments.
* Experience in multi-site or global enterprise environments.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
