### **ABOUT THE JOB**

Anduril is seeking a Tooling Engineer to join the Manufacturing Engineering team at Arsenal-1 in Ashville, Ohio. The  
Tooling Engineer is responsible for designing, developing, and maintaining the tooling, fixtures, and jigs required to  
support large scale sub-assembly production. This role works at the intersection of design engineering and  
production execution — translating engineering requirements into physical tooling that enables technicians to build  
flight-critical hardware safely, accurately, and repeatably. The Tooling Engineer partners closely with manufacturing  
engineers, design engineers, and production technicians to ensure the line has the right tools to hit rate while  
maintaining quality and positional accuracy.

### **WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Design and develop assembly tooling, fixtures, jigs, drill guides, holding fixtures, and handling equipment for  
  aircraft sub-assembly operations using 3D CAD (NX)
* Perform tolerance analysis and GD&T stack-ups to ensure tooling meets positional accuracy and  
  interchangeability requirements for airframe structures
* Create detailed tooling drawings, specifications, and bills of material for internal fabrication or vendor  
  procurement
* Support first article tooling prove-out on the production floor, iterating on designs based on technician  
  feedback and build results
* Develop and maintain tooling maintenance schedules, calibration requirements, and inspection procedures to  
  ensure tooling remains within tolerance throughout its service life
* Collaborate with manufacturing engineers to identify tooling needs driven by new product introductions,  
  design changes, or process improvements
* Perform root cause analysis on tooling-related quality issues and implement corrective actions — including  
  rework, redesign, or replacement of worn or underperforming tools
* Work with external tooling vendors to source, quote, and qualify outsourced tooling — including on-site  
  acceptance testing and first article inspection
* Support Design for Manufacturability (DFM) reviews by providing tooling feasibility input and recommending  
  design features that simplify fixturing and reduce assembly complexity
* Develop and document standard tooling design practices, reusable modular fixture concepts, and lessons  
  learned to accelerate future tooling development cycles
* Support production rate ramp by identifying tooling bottlenecks and designing duplicate, multi-station, or  
  quick-change tooling solutions to increase throughput
* Manage tooling inventory, tracking, and storage — ensuring tools are available, identifiable, and in serviceable  
  condition when needed on the line

### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, or a related  
  field
* 5+ years of experience in tooling design and development for aerospace or defense manufacturing — aircraft  
  assembly tooling strongly preferred
* Proficiency in 3D CAD software (NX, SolidWorks, or CATIA) and 2D drafting for tooling drawings
* Strong understanding of GD&T principles and tolerance stack-up analysis as applied to assembly tooling and  
  airframe structures
* Experience with metallic tooling materials (aluminum, steel, Invar) and an understanding of material selection  
  trade-offs for fixture design
* Hands-on experience proving out tooling on a production floor and iterating designs based on build feedback
* Ability to read and interpret aircraft engineering drawings, specifications, and assembly procedures
* Familiarity with aerospace quality standards (AS9100, AWS D17.1) and configuration management practices
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance

### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience with composite tooling (layup mandrels, cure tooling, trim fixtures) in addition to metallic assembly  
  tooling
* Knowledge of laser tracker, CMM, or photogrammetry-based metrology systems for tooling verification and  
  alignment
* Experience designing tooling for automated drilling, fastening, or robotic assembly systems
* Familiarity with FEA for structural and thermal analysis of tooling under load or cure cycle conditions
* Experience with additive manufacturing (3D printing) for prototype tooling, soft tooling, or production aids
* Prior experience in a rate production environment supporting production ramp from low-rate to full-rate
* PLM/PDM experience (Teamcenter, Windchill) for tooling configuration and revision control
* • Hands-on fabrication skills — machining, welding, or sheet metal work — that inform practical tooling design
