Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

Anduril's ArsenalOS team builds the digital infrastructure that powers how Anduril designs, sources, manufactures, delivers, and sustains hardware at scale. We are not a traditional internal IT organization. We build, buy, customize, integrate, and operate the systems that make Anduril's physical operations faster, more reliable, and more scalable.  
  
We are hiring a P2P Systems Specialist to own punchout configuration, supplier punchout onboarding, and touchless PO adoption across Anduril's supply chain. You will manage the full punchout lifecycle — catalog setup, cXML/OCI integration, supplier testing, and go-live , as well as expanded coverage to additional business units. Beyond punchouts, you will identify and address upstream PO data quality issues that drive downstream invoice mismatches and manual AP intervention. This role works closely with our Digital Tools and product teams to make purchasing faster, cleaner, and more automated.  
  
**What You'll Do**

* Diagnose and resolve existing punchout configuration issues that are blocking supplier transactions and onboarding
* Own the end-to-end punchout setup process for new suppliers: catalog configuration, cXML/OCI integration testing, and go-live validation
* Expand punchout coverage to additional business units beyond the current footprint
* Partner with the Digital Tools feature lead to execute against punchout roadmap
* Work with suppliers directly to troubleshoot integration issues, validate catalog data, and ensure successful onboarding
* Document punchout configuration standards and runbooks for sustainment after the initial backlog is cleared
* Identify and address upstream PO data quality issues that break downstream invoice matching, contributing to manual AP work
* Monitor punchout health metrics and supplier adoption rates post go-live

**What Success Looks Like**

* The punchout configuration backlog is cleared within one quarter of start date
* Touchless PO rates move measurably with near 100% reliability
* Suppliers that were blocked on onboarding are live and transacting
* New BU punchout setups have a repeatable, documented process that does not require engineering support
* Upstream PO data quality issues that cause AP invoice mismatches are identified and addressed, reducing the manual intervention rate

**Required Characteristics**

* **Configuration depth:** You have lived in punchout setup. cXML, OCI, catalog configuration, supplier testing, the whole cycle. You can diagnose why a supplier's catalog is not rendering and fix it without escalating.
* **Supplier facing:** You are comfortable working directly with external suppliers, walking them through integration requirements, and troubleshooting issues in real time. You do not need someone else to be the interface.
* **Urgency:** You move quickly, document as you go, and escalate blockers immediately.
* **End-to-end P2P thinking:** You understand that punchout quality affects everything downstream: PO accuracy, invoice matching, AP workload. You think about the full chain, not just your configuration.
* **Sustainment mindset:** You do not just fix things; you build the runbooks and standards so the next person can maintain what you set up.

**Required Qualifications**

* 5+ years of experience with procure-to-pay systems, specifically punchout catalog configuration and supplier punchout onboarding
* Hands-on experience with cXML or OCI punchout protocols
* Working knowledge of Oracle Cloud Procurement or similar ERP purchasing modules
* Experience troubleshooting supplier integration issues in a production environment
* Strong attention to detail and ability to manage a high volume of configuration tasks simultaneously

**Preferred**

* Experience with Oracle Cloud punchout setup specifically
* Background in defense, aerospace, or manufacturing procurement
* Familiarity with EDI and other electronic transaction methods
* Experience measuring and improving touchless PO rates or straight-through processing metrics
