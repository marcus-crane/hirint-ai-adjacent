**ABOUT THE JOB**

We are seeking a Senior IT Security Engineer to architect, build, and own classified infrastructure for Anduril's Space team from the ground up. This is a foundational role — you won't be inheriting an existing environment, you'll be designing and standing up the systems, standards, and processes that the team will rely on as it scales. That means making decisions about architecture, tooling, and automation frameworks, then implementing and hardening them to full compliance. You'll work at the intersection of security, infrastructure, and engineering to ensure our classified environments are built right from day one and remain resilient, compliant, and mission-ready.

**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Design and build classified infrastructure from scratch, including server environments, virtual platforms, networking, and enterprise services — establishing the architecture and hardened baselines the Space team will operate on
* Define and implement STIG compliance strategies across Windows, RHEL, and VMware, using SCAP, SCC, and manual validation; stand up repeatable processes for ongoing compliance scanning and remediation
* Architect and deploy virtual infrastructure (primarily VMware vSphere, and potentially vSAN and Horizon/VDI), making foundational decisions on topology, resource allocation, and scalability
* Build out enterprise services from the ground up, including Active Directory, DNS, DHCP, WSUS/patch repositories, and repository servers
* Develop automation frameworks using Ansible, scripting (PowerShell, Bash, Python), and infrastructure-as-code practices to ensure environments are reproducible, consistent, and minimally dependent on manual intervention
* Establish monitoring, alerting, and incident response processes for system health, performance, and security posture
* Lead vulnerability remediation efforts, driving technical implementation for CAT I/II/III findings and partnering with ISSO/ISSM stakeholders to meet required timelines
* Design and implement backup and disaster recovery strategies, defining RPO/RTO targets and validating them through regular testing
* Guide environments through the ATO process, producing documentation, compliance evidence, and technical demonstrations for security assessments
* Create comprehensive runbooks, architecture documentation, and knowledge transfer materials to enable the team to scale beyond a single point of ownership

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 7+ years administering and hardening Windows Server and Linux (RHEL) in classified or regulated environments
* Demonstrated experience building infrastructure from scratch, not just maintaining existing environments
* Deep knowledge of STIG application and validation using SCAP/SCC tools, with the ability to design compliance programs, not just execute checklists
* Strong VMware vSphere experience, including architecture decisions, provisioning, and patching
* Experience standing up and managing Active Directory, DNS, DHCP, and enterprise patch management in new environments
* Advanced scripting and automation skills (PowerShell, Bash, or Python) with hands-on Ansible experience
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience architecting VMware vSAN and Horizon/VDI environments
* Familiarity with engineering applications such as Siemens NX, Teamcenter, or other CAD/CAM/CAE software
* Track record of leading environments through the full ATO lifecycle
* Experience mentoring junior administrators or leading small infrastructure teams
* Relevant certifications such as CompTIA Security+, RHCSA, RHCE, VCP, or CISSP

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
