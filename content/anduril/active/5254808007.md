By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. ### **ABOUT THE JOB**

We are looking for an Integrated Product Support Analyst to develop the provisioning and sustainment data that will support our program throughout the entire product lifecycle. This role owns that foundation. Reporting to the Logistics Program Manager, you'll help build the program’s provisioning dataset, design the operating model that keeps it current as the aircraft matures, and ensure it flows cleanly into the sustainment team's contract deliverables. It's a foundational role with real ownership — the decisions you make about how this data is structured and maintained will shape how the program is sustained for years. The Logistics Program Manager retains deliverable accountability and the customer relationship; you own the data domain beneath it and co-author the deliverables that depend on it.

### **WHAT YOU'LL DO**

**Own the provisioning and logistics data baseline**. Working with cross-functional partners, establish and maintain the authoritative dataset — CAGE codes, part numbers, BOMs, MTBF and failure-rate data, unit prices, provisioning parts lists — as the trusted source the program builds its deliverables against. You bring the different inputs together into one coherent picture the sustainment team can rely on.

**Build and run the operating model.** Design and operate the process that keeps the dataset current, reconciled, and version-controlled as the design and test results evolve. This is yours to shape from the ground up — a rare chance to define how something works rather than inherit it.

**Partner across the program***.* Much of this role is bringing people together. Working alongside supply chain, reliability and sustaining engineering, procurement, and design, you’ll gather the data the sustainment mission depends on. You'll build the relationships and the rhythm that make that collaboration effective and repeatable, earning cooperation through credibility rather than mandate.

**Co-author the deliverables***.* Develop the provisioning-based CDRLs and their supporting analysis — sparing recommendations, support equipment data, parts lists — preparing the drafts and the well-reasoned rationale the Logistics Program Manager takes to the customer.

**Partner with the whole Integrated Product Support team.** Your data domain underpins the entire sustainment effort — spares list review, support equipment sourcing, and the clean source data that feeds the logistics engineering and technical writing teams who produce the formal LPD products (LORA, MTA) and Technical Orders. You keep that pipeline healthy and moving.

**Set the tooling standard.** Lead with AI-enabled tools to build data solutions quickly, using modern software to work efficiently, all while working fluently through any the government-mandated logistics tools (e.g., Power Log-J) where the contract requires it.

#### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor's degree in logistics, supply chain, engineering, or a related technical field (equivalent military logistics experience may substitute for education).
* 3+ years of integrated product support, provisioning, or sustainment experience in a defense or military environment — enough to operate independently on provisioning fundamentals.
* Working knowledge of DoD Integrated Product Support, with hands-on depth in provisioning and cataloging: CAGE codes, NSN and part number structures, provisioning parts lists, and how reliability data (MTBF, failure rates) drives sparing.
* Experience developing or directly supporting government logistics deliverables against DIDs and CDRLs.
* A collaborative, self-starting approach to cross-functional work — comfortable partnering across engineering, supply chain, and procurement to bring data together.
* Strong Excel fluency plus resourcefulness with AI-enabled tools to build solutions independently.
* Comfortable operating with autonomy in a fast-moving program and energized by early-stage, formative work.
* Able to travel up to 5–10% domestically.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret clearance.
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export-controlled information or facilities.

#### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Hands-on experience with Logistics Product Data (GEIA-STD-0007) and government logistics tools (Power Log-J, SlicWave, Tempo, COMPASS, Relyence, or equivalent).
* Experience owning or maintaining a provisioning baseline through a prototype-to-production transition.
* Ability to read engineering drawings, parts lists, and BOMs and extract logistics data directly from design.
* Working familiarity with how LORA, FMECA, and maintenance task analysis outputs drive provisioning and support equipment decisions.
* Experience with IUID/MIL-STD-130 item identification.
