**ABOUT THE ROLE**  
We are seeking a **Senior EMC Design and Compliance Engineer** to join our Electrical Engineering Design team. In this role, you will collaborate closely with electrical, mechanical, and materials design teams, as well as corporate compliance groups and third-party laboratories. The ideal candidate will review system-level electromagnetic compatibility (EMC) requirements, design robust pre-compliance and compliance test plans, and independently execute and supervise EMC testing — including travel to HQ and third-party labs to oversee test campaigns. Strong consideration will be given to candidates equally well-versed in EMC design able to review circuit schematics, advise on PCB component selection, guide PCB stack-ups/floorplans, and analyze CAD designs for optimized electromagnetic performance.  
 **Key Responsibilities**

* Define product, module and interface-level EMC requirements and develop pre-compliance and compliance test strategies.
* Own and operate the local EMC lab; plan, execute, supervise, and troubleshoot EMC testing (MIL-STD-461 RE102/RS103, MIL-STD-464C field-level requirements) at third-party labs and internal facilities
* Evaluate test data, correlate emission failures with clock/data harmonics or other root causes using measurement and simulation data and implement effective design countermeasures.
* Communicate findings and design trade-offs clearly to internal stakeholders and external suppliers, including negotiating design changes and schedules when EMC issues are found late in development.
* Plus: Review schematics, CAD designs, cable terminations, and PCB/flex layouts for EMC risk, including signal integrity and impedance trade-offs; Provide technical guidance on PCB component selection, floor planning, stack-ups, shielding, and grounding/bonding strategies for housings and fasteners.
* Ability to travel up to 20%.
* Must be eligible to obtain and maintain a clearance.

**Required Qualifications**

* Strong knowledge of MIL-STD-461 and MIL-STD-464 EMC test methods and requirements
* Experience with radiated and conducted emissions/immunity testing, from pre-compliance through full compliance campaigns
* Hands-on experience with EMC test equipment — EMI receivers, signal generators, power amplifiers, LISNs, current probes, and antennas
* Experience managing or maintaining an EMC test lab, including equipment calibration and test capability planning
* Working knowledge of PCB layout, stack-up, and flex circuit design as it relates to EMC and signal integrity
* Familiarity with shielding materials, conductive/non-conductive coatings, and grounding/bonding practices, including galvanic corrosion considerations
* Willingness to travel to third-party labs to supervise and support test campaigns
* Strong root-cause analysis skills and the ability to communicate findings across engineering disciplines
* Simulation (Plus): experience with EM simulation tools such as Ansys HFSS, Altair FEKO, or CST Studio Suite
