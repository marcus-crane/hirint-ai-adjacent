Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

Anduril's ArsenalOS team builds the digital infrastructure that powers how Anduril designs, sources, manufactures, delivers, and sustains hardware at scale. We are not a traditional internal IT organization. We build, buy, customize, integrate, and operate the systems that make Anduril's physical operations faster, more reliable, and more scalable.  
  
We are hiring a Senior ERP Functional Analyst to own the configure-test-sustain cycle for supply chain modules in Oracle Cloud. You will translate business requirements into working Oracle configuration, making sure the system actually does what the business needs it to do. The role covers two critical areas: (1) core SC workstreams including business acquisition setups, ASNs, EDI, Purchase Requisition applications, and compliance, and (2) new Oracle modules coming online (OTM, GTM, PMIS). Success is not Oracle expertise for its own sake. It's understanding the business problem first, then configuring Oracle in a way that actually works for the end users.  
  
  
**What You'll Do**

* Own Oracle configuration, testing, and documentation across procurement, order management, and compliance
* Lead functional setup for new Oracle modules (OTM, GTM, PMIS) including business process mapping, configuration, and UAT coordination
* Execute legal entity and new business acquisition set ups in Oracle
* Configure and test DPAS compliance flows, ASN processing, EDI transactions, and Procurement application changes
* Serve as the functional bridge between product managers (who define requirements) and ERP engineers (who build integrations), translating business needs into Oracle configuration
* Develop and maintain test scripts, configuration documentation, and runbooks for ongoing module sustainment
* Support ERP channel escalations as a subject matter expert on SC module functionality
* Identify configuration gaps and process issues proactively, recommending solutions before they become production incidents

**What Success Looks Like**

* Configuration backlogs across active Oracle modules are systematically triaged, prioritized, and resolved
* Business teams aren’t waiting on functional gaps that configuration support can address
* SWEs are no longer flexing onto configuration and testing work; engineering capacity is fully dedicated to build
* New module go-lives (OTM, GTM, PMIS) have clean configurations, tested integrations, and documented runbooks before launch
* New legal entities and Business Acquisition setups land on time without blocking downstream implications
* ERP support escalations have a knowledgeable functional analyst in the loop, reducing resolution time

**Required Characteristics**

* **Oracle depth:** You can independently configure, test, troubleshoot, and document Oracle Cloud modules. You don’t need an engineer to get through a set up - you’ve done it yourself and understand what’s happening under the hood
* **Process thinker:** You understand procure-to-pay end to end. You know that a configuration choice in purchasing ripples into receiving, invoicing, and AP, and you think about those downstream effects.
* **Bridge builder:** You sit between product and engineering and make both sides more effective. You translate business requirements into configuration and configuration constraints back into product trade-offs.
* **Proactive ownership:** You do not wait for a ticket. You see a gap in configuration, a missing test case, or a process that will break at scale, and you flag it before it becomes a production issue.
* **Documentation discipline:** You leave a trail. Runbooks, test scripts, configuration decisions, and the reasoning behind them. The next person who touches your work can understand what you did and why.

**Required Qualifications**

* 5+ years of Oracle ERP functional analyst experience, with depth in SCM modules (Procurement, Order Management, Inventory, or Purchasing)
* Hands-on experience configuring Oracle Cloud ERP
* Strong understanding of procure-to-pay business processes end to end
* Experience with UAT planning, test script development, and defect triage
* Ability to read and understand technical specifications well enough to bridge product and engineering conversations

**Preferred**

* Experience with Oracle OTM, GTM, or PMIS modules
* Experience supporting legal entity or multi-org setups in Oracle
* Familiarity with DPAS, FAR/DFARS, or other federal procurement compliance requirements
* Experience with EDI transaction sets (850, 855, 856, 810)
