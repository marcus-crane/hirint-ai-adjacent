Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**About the Role**

The Director of Test & Evaluation – Robotics will provide executive and technical leadership across Anduril’s Test Operations, Test Engineering, and Test Automation teams. In this role, you will lead end to end test and evaluation across multi-domain unmanned hardware, driving the integration and validation of Lattice Command & Control (C2) and Collaborative Autonomy (CA) capabilities.   
  
You will accelerate engineering development by scaling automated SIL/HIL frameworks and high-tempo field testing. The ideal candidate brings extensive multi-domain testing experience, deep software/hardware integration understanding, and a proven track record of building safety critical test organizations that accelerate mission ready deployments.

#### What You’ll Do

* Lead both the Flight Test Team and Test Engineering Team, providing technical guidance and mentorship
* Establish and refine flight test methodologies for evaluating mission autonomy capabilities using surrogate vehicles in fast-paced test environments
* Develop long-term capacity planning for flight test capabilities to support future product initiatives and company growth
* Drive the verification and validation (V&V) of Lattice C2 and Collaborative Autonomy behaviors
* Architect and scale automated SIL, HIL, and regression test pipelines, ensuring software changes are thoroughly vetted before deploying to physical hardware in the field.
* Oversee live test execution, test infrastructure, range safety protocols, instrumentation, and real-time telemetry pipelines across test ranges.
* Drive cross-functional collaboration with Product, Engineering, and Program Management to align test strategies with product development roadmaps
* Cultivate a strong organizational foundation focused on safety standards, regulatory adherence, and performance
* Ensure compliance with FAA, FCC, and other regulatory requirements throughout all flight test operations
* Manage resource allocation, budgeting, and scheduling for flight test campaigns across multiple programs
* Create and implement standardized processes, metrics, and KPIs to measure test effectiveness and drive continuous improvement

#### Required Qualifications

* Bachelor's degree in engineering, computer science, robotics, or related technical field
* 10+ years of experience in testing unmanned aerial systems with at least 5 years in a leadership position
* Proven expertise in both software and hardware integration for complex autonomous systems
* Demonstrated success leading flight test operations as a Test Director for mission-critical platforms
* Strong understanding of aviation regulations and compliance requirements for unmanned systems
* Experience managing technical teams with diverse skill sets across operations and engineering
* Exceptional problem-solving abilities for troubleshooting complex hardware and software issues
* Outstanding communication skills for conveying technical concepts to stakeholders at all levels
* Flexibility to adapt to dynamic schedules and willingness to travel to test sites as required
* Current FAA Part 107 certification or ability to obtain one
* In this role, you will be subject to pre-employment and randomized substance screening, as required by the Company.

#### Preferred Qualifications

* Advanced degree in a relevant technical or management field
* Direct experience with multi-agent collaborative autonomy, swarming architectures, or distributed Command & Control (C2) systems.
* Proficiency with modern software tools (Python, C++, Linux, CI/CD pipelines, Git) for automated data parsing and telemetry analytics.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Top Secret SCI security clearance

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
