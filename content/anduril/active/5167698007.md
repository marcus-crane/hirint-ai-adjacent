Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

As a Tool Maker at Anduril, you will be responsible for precision fabrication, repair, and modification of jigs, fixtures, dies, molds, gauges, etc., with a focus on production tooling that supports advanced manufacturing operations for Maritime Operations.

You'll work from 2D Drawings, model-based definition (MBD) data, and GD&T to build tooling to tight tolerancing, while partnering directly with engineers, welders, and machinists to get critical tooling into the hands of production fast. This role may also lead and cross-train less experienced Tool Makers.

### **What You'll Do**

* Lay out, fabricate, assemble, and repair jigs, fixtures, forms, templates, molds, dies, and related tooling per blueprints, MBD models, sketches, and specifications
* Set up and operate manual and/or CNC lathes, mills, surface grinders, brakes, and punch presses to cut, shape, and finish tooling components to specified dimensions
* Work with a range of materials including aluminum, steel, stainless steel, tool steels, and Off-the-Shelf Hardware
* Apply and interpret GD&T (per ASME Y14.5) from engineering drawings and CAD/MBD data
* Use precision measuring and metrology equipment such as micrometers, calipers, laser trackers (LEICA, FARO), etc., to verify dimensional accuracy and troubleshoot out-of-tolerance conditions
* Break down and prepare tool designs for fabrication, assembly, & inspection.
* Setup and fabricate welding/assembly fixture tooling from rough structure; coordinate with welders and other tooling fabricators as needed.
* Safely operate, forklifts, and other shop lifting equipment to move and position tooling and materials
* Diagnose and correct tooling issues in production, recommending design or process improvements and documenting changes
* Maintain tooling documentation, spare parts lists, and job hour tracking
* Lead and cross-train junior Tool Makers; assign and check work as needed
* Maintain a clean, safe work area in compliance with safety, quality, and PPE requirements

### **Required Qualifications**

* 5+ years of experience in a manufacturing environment building or maintaining tooling (jigs, fixtures, dies)
* 5+ years of experience setting up and operating lathes, mills, and surface grinders
* 5+ years of experience with precision inspection equipment (micrometers, calipers, Laser Tracker, etc.)
* Demonstrated ability to read and interpret 2D Drawings, GD&T, and engineering specifications
* Ability to complete tooling builds start-to-finish with little to no assistance, and to write build instructions for other shop personnel
* Strong communication with salaried and hourly personnel
* Ability to lift, push, and pull up to 35 lbs frequently within an 8-hour shift
* Must qualify as a "U.S. Person" under ITAR/Export Control regulations (U.S. Citizen or U.S. Permanent Resident)

### **Preferred Qualifications**

* Associate degree or technical certificate in machining, tool & die, or mechanical technology
* CNC machining experience
* Welding certification and ability to fabricate/repair tooling via FCAW, GMAW, or GTAW
* Electrical, pneumatic, and hydraulic troubleshooting skills
* Crane and forklift operation experience
* Experience leading or cross-training junior fabrication/toolmaking staff
* Ability to work varied shifts, overtime, and weekends as demands require
