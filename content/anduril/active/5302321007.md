Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE JOB

The Business Associate will own the analysis driving our facilities, capital investment, production capacity, and revenue forecasting. Partnering with RevOps, Finance, and Sales, you’ll keep production schedules honest against real demand and surface resource constraints before they become delivery problems. You will also lead the division’s annual planning cycle, support go/no-go product decisions, and deliver the ad hoc insights the General Manager and Head of Operations rely on daily. If you want ambiguous questions, direct access to data, and a short line between your analysis and the final decision, this is the seat for you.

### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* **Own the analytical arm of the business** — This is the team that wakes up every day thinking about future planning. Own analysis related to business, real estate, and production planning for the Intelligence Systems business line.
* **Drive facilities and capital planning** — Manage planning and strategy for facilities (including office space, manufacturing, and testing), secured spaces, and capital investments.
* **Inform go-to-market decisions** — Support the Futures team on GTM decisions, using data to drive go/no-go calls on which products to pursue based on market demand.
* **Partner on revenue forecasting** — Partner with Revenue Operations and Finance on ACV and revenue forecasting. Work with Sales teams and RevOps to monitor pipeline health, identify material changes to forecast, and ensure production schedules are aligned with third-party and first-party product demand.
* **Map staffing to the pipeline** — Understand and communicate the planning and mapping of staffing to new and current projects, with the ability to develop plans to address resource constraints that would impact pipeline growth or international deployments.
* **Lead annual planning** — Drive annual planning for Intelligence Systems as part of the corporate planning cycle, and lead content development and planning to support other business reviews.
* **Support the GM and Head of Operations** — Deliver ad hoc analysis for the General Manager and the Head of Operations.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 5+ years of experience in business analytics, strategic finance, revenue operations, corporate strategy, or a comparable analytical function.
* **Strong analytical toolkit** — Advanced financial and operational modeling, with fluency in excel and AI tools. You will work directly with data rather than waiting on a reporting team.
* **Translates analysis into decisions** — Demonstrated ability to turn ambiguous business questions into structured analysis, and to communicate findings clearly and simply to non-analytical audiences, including executives. Strong written and verbal communication.
* **High ownership and capacity** — Able to manage multiple large workstreams and priorities in parallel, at a rapid pace, and report out on progress regularly.
* **Comfortable with cross-functional ambiguity** — Experience working across Finance, Sales, Operations, and Engineering without formal authority over any of them.
* Ability to work onsite five days a week at either our Reston, VA or Costa Mesa, CA office.
* Possesses and is able to maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance, at minimum.
* Ability to travel up to 25%

### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Bachelor's degree in Business, Economics, Finance, Statistics, or a related quantitative field.
* Experience in defense, aerospace, or another hardware manufacturing environment, particularly with production planning or capacity modeling.
* Experience working with Salesforce products.
* Experience supporting ACV, bookings, or revenue forecasting in partnership with RevOps or FP&A.
* Experience with facilities, real estate, or capital investment planning.
* Experience in the intelligence community or national security mission space.
* Experience working effectively in a matrix organization structure.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
