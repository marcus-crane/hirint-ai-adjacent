Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Legal Operations team is responsible for optimizing the efficiency and effectiveness of the Legal Department at Anduril and works cross-functionally to streamline workflows, automate routine tasks, implement best practices and support regulatory compliance efforts. With the overarching aim of supporting the Company's overall strategic goals, the Legal Operations team acts as a bridge between legal, the Company's business units, and outside counsel.  
  
This role sits within Legal Operations and is dedicated to the Compliance and International Trade Compliance (ITC) teams. You will build and run the operational backbone for these teams and serve as the connection point between them and our Tools & Technology team, which builds AI workflows, automation, and technology for Legal.

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for a Legal Operations Associate (non-lawyer position) to join our rapidly growing team in Orange County, California (preferred) or Washington, DC. In this role, you will build out and own operations for our Compliance and ITC teams: managing their Jira intake and workflows, finding gaps and efficiencies, maintaining auditable records, and acting as the main point of contact for both teams and for Tools & Technology. You will keep day-to-day compliance operations running smoothly so attorneys and compliance professionals can focus on substantive risk.  
  
If you are someone who is scrappy, adaptable, loves to learn, and detail-oriented then this role is for you.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

**Operational point of contact and program support**

* Serve as the main operational point of contact for the Compliance and ITC teams, and for the Tools & Technology team, translating compliance needs into clear requirements, priorities, and timelines
* Triage and provide frontline support for compliance and ITC requests and reports from the Company, following established SOPs and escalating to attorneys as appropriate
* Support the operational aspects of compliance programs, including International Trade Compliance, Investigations, Cybersecurity, and overarching General Compliance
* Support the operational execution of compliance monitoring, auditing, and risk assessment activities
* Support cross-functional projects that require operational support for compliance-related matters, working with teams such as Real Estate, Procurement, Finance, Supply Chain, and Growth/Business Development

**Workflow management**

* Own and manage Jira for the Compliance and ITC teams, including project configuration, queues, request types, fields, automations, dashboards, and reporting
* Refine workflows for triaging potential compliance issues and reports, including routing, SLAs, and escalation paths
* Identify gaps, bottlenecks, and inefficiencies in compliance processes, and propose and implement improvements with measurable outcomes
* Develop, implement, and maintain efficient processes, workflows, and standard operating procedures for compliance initiatives, policies, and procedures
* Develop lightweight workflows to keep the company organized and efficient
* Map and document the end-to-end workflows for filings and licenses, and identify gaps, delays, and manual steps
* Design and implement workflow automation, such as intake, status tracking, reminders, document collection, and renewal and expiration alerts
* Track and report on cycle times, backlog, and bottlenecks for filings and licenses, and recommend efficiency improvements
* Develop and implement data governance protocols for data integrity and reporting; maintain, organize, and clean up compliance data
* Extract, analyze, and visualize data to surface trends, generate reports, and support program management

**Records, training, and audit readiness**

* Maintain accurate and auditable records of compliance activities, training completion, policy acknowledgements, incident investigations, and trade filings and licenses
* Create and maintain repositories for compliance and ITC information, and organize documentation, facilitate data requests, and keep audit trails readily available
* Support the development and delivery of compliance training programs for different employee groups, including scheduling, tracking, and reporting
* Support efforts to monitor and detect potential violations, including preparing data and reports for review
* Prepare and maintain records and documentation, such as contracts, regulatory licenses, letters of intent, waivers, and notices

**Vendor, outside counsel, and budget support**

* Support procurement of compliance-related software and services, and liaise with Indirect Supply Chain to ensure correct and timely payment of outside counsel
* Draft, review and redline routine contracts, e.g., NDAs, SaaS licenses, vendor contracts, and engagement letters
* Coordinate electronic signatures and manage contract records in our Contract Lifecycle Management (CLM) tool

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
