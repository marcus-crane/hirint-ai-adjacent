Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

We are seeking a seasoned Senior Financial Analyst with 3-5 years of experience to join our Workforce Finance team within Corporate FP&A. As Anduril rapidly scales to transform defense technology, this role is critical to ensuring we build a strategically aligned, efficient workforce. The ideal candidate will have a strong background in workforce planning, financial modeling, business partnering, and process improvement—ideally with exposure to hypergrowth companies or defense/aerospace industries. This individual will be instrumental in driving workforce financial strategy, guiding headcount decisions across the enterprise, and optimizing our most important investment—our people.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* **Strategic Workforce Planning & Analysis**

+ Lead building structure for enterprise headcount planning and forecasting in a hypergrowth defense technology environment
+ Develop labor cost models and scenario analysis to support rapid scaling across Engineering, Manufacturing, and Operations
+ Align workforce investments with business strategy, growth objectives, and government contract requirements
+ Support ad-hoc analysis on workforce trends, hiring velocity, and organizational evolution

* **Workforce Financial Management & Reporting**

+ Track cost-per-hire, labor efficiency metrics, and workforce costs aligned to contract vehicles and program budgets

* **Analytics & Insights**

+ Build workforce cost analytics and benchmarking against defense, aerospace, and high-growth tech companies
+ Analyze productivity metrics: revenue per employee, ACV per employee, department ratio analysis
+ Track industry ratios for Departments in SG&A, Manufacturing, and Engineering functions
+ Model workforce composition and efficiency across program types (production, R&D, customer-funded, IRAD)
+ Conduct organizational design analytics and turnover cost analysis

* **Data Systematization & Automation**

+ Systematize workforce data to establish single source of truth for all reporting across a rapidly scaling organization
+ Collaborate with HR, IT, and Finance to ensure data integrity
+ Drive automation and analytics to surface business trends and scaling challenges
+ Implement workforce reporting tools that support corporate and government reporting requirements

* **Planning, Budgeting & Compliance**

+ Lead annual headcount and compensation planning cycles
+ Own merit increase, promotion budgeting, and contingent workforce spend management
+ Model workforce ramp scenarios for new contract wins and production scaling

* **Executive Reporting & Business Partnership**

+ Create workforce financial models, headcount reports, and executive reporting
+ Prepare business case analysis for workforce investments and new program staffing
+ Lead monthly enterprise presentations to business partners and senior leadership
+ Partner with HR, FP&A, Program Management, and business leaders across functions
+ Serve as subject matter expert on workforce financial drivers and defense industry workforce trends

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or related field; MBA/MS or professional certification (CPA, CFA, CMA) is a plus
* 3-5 years of experience in financial analysis, workforce planning, or FP&A; experience in high-growth technology, defense, aerospace, or government contracting preferred
* Strong understanding of workforce planning, human capital analytics, and HR financial metrics
* Experience with government contract cost accounting (FAR/DCAA) and labor allocation principles highly desirable
* Advanced proficiency in Excel, financial modeling, and data analysis tools
* Experience with HRIS systems (Workday, Rippling, Greenhouse, ChartHop) and ERP systems (Oracle EPM, Foundry)
* Proven ability to manage workforce finance in hypergrowth or scaling organizations
* Strong data governance and automation capabilities; ability to establish "single source of truth" reporting
* Exceptional business partnering and communication skills with ability to influence cross-functional stakeholders
* Thrives in ambiguity and comfortable building frameworks from the ground up
* Proven track record managing multiple priorities in fast-paced, deadline-driven environments
* Must be authorized to work in the United States

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience in defense, aerospace, or government services industries
* Familiarity with FAR/CAS compliance and indirect rate structures

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
