Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE JOB

As a Senior Procurement Operations Manager on our Deployed Programs team within Centralized Procurement, you will be the tip of the spear for procurement performance across Anduril's business lines. You will embed directly with division procurement teams, diagnose execution gaps, and drive measurable improvement in supplier delivery, PO management, and buyer effectiveness. This is not a headquarters role that reviews dashboards from a distance. You will be on the ground with buyers, in supplier meetings, and in the weeds of ERP data, solving problems that directly determine whether hardware reaches the production floor on time. Centralized Procurement owns the playbook: the standards, the training, the KPI framework, and the escalation authority that keep procurement operating consistently across every division. The Tiger Team is how that playbook comes to life. You will pilot the model in early divisions, then expand it across the enterprise, shifting from embedded execution to coaching as each business line matures. If you want a high visibility role where your work directly shapes how a fast scaling defense technology company buys, builds, and delivers, this is your mission.

### WHAT YOU’LL DO

**Embed and Drive Execution**  
• Deploy into business line procurement teams during pilot and expansion phases. Pair directly with buyers and supply chain leaders to assess current state, identify gaps, and stand up the standard operating model.  
• Lead weekly OTD drumbeat reviews at the division level. Own the agenda, drive root cause analysis on past due POs and supplier delivery failures, and ensure corrective actions convert into measurable improvement.  
• Coach buyers on core procurement execution: PR to PO conversion within the two day standard, PO acknowledgement follow up, DPAS compliance, and supplier performance management.  
• Conduct hands on ERP and MRP work alongside buyers. Pull on order reports, validate promise dates, flag at risk deliveries, and ensure data hygiene in the procurement system of record.

**Operationalize the Centralized Playbook**  
• Roll out standard procurement processes, templates, and escalation frameworks so every division operates from the same baseline. Adapt the playbook to local program realities without compromising the standard.  
• Stand up and maintain the KPI reporting cadence for assigned divisions. Track and report on the full metrics framework: PR to PO conversion, PO acknowledgement rates, past due PO lines, receiving issue ticket closure, invoice resolution, DPAS PO OTD, and supplier OTD.  
• Execute the escalation and remediation framework. At Tier 1, provide coaching. At Tier 2, deploy as embedded support for 30 day improvement sprints. At Tier 3, support centralized oversight of the affected function until performance is restored.  
• Benchmark procurement performance across divisions. Identify which teams are ahead, document what works, and propagate best practices through the Peer Learning Network.  
  
**Build the Foundation**  
• Deliver buyer training aligned to centralized standards: ACD code compliance, procurement policy and governance, War Chest navigation, and KPI reporting workflows.  
• Support the buildout of automated reporting dashboards and the On Order Report (OOR) so divisions move from manual, weekly data pulls to machine generated, daily visibility.  
• Contribute to the career framework and professional development standards that define what good looks like in a buyer at every level, from IC2 through Senior Buyer.  
• Support hiring decisions as the central quality bar holder. Evaluate procurement candidates, pressure test technical and behavioral competency, and ensure new hires ramp through a consistent onboarding standard.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Bachelor's degree in supply chain management, business administration, industrial engineering, or a related field.
* 5 to 8 years of experience in procurement, supply chain operations, or strategic sourcing, with direct ownership of supplier delivery performance and purchase order management.
* Ability to travel approximately 40 to 60% of the time, variable based on which divisions are in pilot expansion, or steady state.
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export controlled information or facilities.

### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Master's degree in supply chain, operations management, or MBA.
* Experience in defense, aerospace, or hardware manufacturing procurement.
* Deeper experience within:
  + Supplier performance management and development programs
  + DPAS (Defense Priorities and Allocations System) rated order compliance
  + Procurement policy and governance in a regulated environment
  + Standing up procurement operations in new or immature business units
  + Coaching and training junior procurement professionals
* Proficiency in data visualization tools (Tableau, Power BI, Looker) for building procurement dashboards.
* Familiarity with high mix, low volume manufacturing environments where procurement complexity is driven by product diversity rather than volume.
* Background in Lean, Six Sigma, or structured problem solving methodologies applied to procurement processes.
* Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. TS clearance.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
