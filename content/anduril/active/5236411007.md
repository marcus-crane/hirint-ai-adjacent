### ABOUT THE JOB

As the ISSM, you will serve as the senior security authority for the Space team, leading all Risk Management Framework and Assessment & Authorization activities across classification levels. You'll own the authorization portfolio, drive continuous monitoring strategy, and ensure our IT engineering and networking infrastructure operates in a compliant, hardened posture at all times.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Lead all RMF processes for system authorizations (ATO, IATT, IATO) and develop and maintain System Security Plans, Security Assessment Reports, and POA&Ms — tracking remediation within required timeframes
* Manage the continuous monitoring program including vulnerability scans, account audits, access reviews, and risk assessments across all protection levels
* Coordinate security control assessments with validators, SCAs, and Authorizing Officials, and interface with government security organizations (DISA, NSA, DIA, service CIOs) as the primary technical security representative
* Lead incident response for security events requiring investigation, containment, and reporting
* Provide direct leadership and mentorship to ISSOs, establishing performance expectations and ensuring comprehensive security coverage across all systems
* Manage GRC platforms, track compliance evidence, and brief leadership and customers on authorization status, security posture, and risk decisions

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 5+ years of experience in information assurance or cybersecurity, with direct RMF and A&A experience
* DoD 8570/8140 compliance at IAM Level II or higher (e.g., CAP, CASP+, CISM, or CISSP)
* Demonstrated experience managing system authorization packages (SSPs, SARs, POA&Ms) across multiple classification levels
* Experience interfacing with government Authorizing Officials and security control assessors
* Strong understanding of NIST 800-53 security controls and CNSSI 1253
* Currently possesses and is able to maintain an active U.S. Top Secret security clearance

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* CISSP or CISM certification
* Experience with space systems or satellite program security environments
* Familiarity with GRC platforms (eMASS, Xacta, or similar)
* Prior experience leading or mentoring a cybersecurity team
* Background working with DISA STIGs and SCAP-based vulnerability management tools
* Experience briefing senior leadership and government customers on risk decisions

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
