**ABOUT THE JOB**   
Warfighter Systems is seeking a **Staff Technical Program & Manufacturing Engineer** to lead development and direct supplier engagement in building and delivering advanced AR/VR optical systems. This position joins a small but growing team in Taiwan where you will be on the front-line managing Anduril’s strategic supplier engagements, including managing supplier partnerships, developing and executing on program schedules & deliverables, driving manufacturing process improvement, and leading problem solving and cross-functional communication. This is a leadership position, and Anduril is seeking a multi-talented individual who is unafraid to take ownership, span disciplines and ensure that our critical optical systems deliver per plan.   
  
**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Program Management: Lead supplier engagements, manage timelines and deliverables, and align resources to ensure successful production-quality and successful delivery of complex display and optical systems. Balance competing priorities across multiple products and programs in a fast-paced, startup environment.
* Build Management: Lead planning and execution of optical system build events across supplier sites, including configuration matrices, daily build plans, clear-to-build matrices, BOM reviews, unit allocation, and daily build reporting.
* Technical Expertise: Understand the architecture, design, performance targets and manufacturing / test processes to guide design convergence and build management. Support engineering teams in resolving technical issues and making trade-offs to meet product objectives. Ensure compliance with industry standards and government regulations, such as ITAR and RMF.
* Cross-functional Collaboration: Serve as the primary liaison between engineering teams, supplier partners and Anduril leadership. Align cross-functional teams on their goals, dependencies, and deliverables. Drive effective communication and collaboration across distributed teams and locations.
* Risk Management: Identify and mitigate risks proactively, including technical, schedule, and production related risks. Maintain and drive ‘Major Issues Lists’, monitor development dependencies, anticipate bottlenecks, and adjust plans as needed to maintain program maturity, quality and deliverables.
* Stakeholder Management: Manage relationships with key supplier partners, ensuring program alignment with contractual obligations and stakeholder expectations. Prepare and deliver clear program updates, technical briefs, and performance reports for internal and external audiences.
* Process Improvement: Implement and refine processes to enhance program execution and efficiency within a startup environment. Leverage best practices in program management while adapting to the unique challenges of the defense sector.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* BS in Optics, Physics, Materials Science, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or Industrial Engineering
* 8+ years of technical program management and manufacturing process development experience delivering hardware products, or equivalent engineering experience with demonstrated program leadership
* Demonstrated experience taking a module or system from concept through qualification and into mass production
* Direct experience leading contract manufacturers and component suppliers, including on-site build support
* Technical fluency in optics and optical manufacturing sufficient to interrogate supplier data and engineering assumptions independently
* Experience owning hardware program schedules including tooling lead times, long-lead components, and qualification campaigns
* Fluency with NPI processes, phase-gate reviews, BOM and ECO management, and build readiness mechanisms
* Excellent written and verbal communication in both English and Mandarin, including executive-level status reporting and technical documentation
* Supplier-Embedded Presence: Willingness and ability to work primarily on-site at key supplier and contract manufacturing (CM) partner facilities with day-to-day operations based directly out of supplier sites rather than an Anduril office or remote/WFH environment
* Travel: Ability to travel domestically and internationally up to 25-50% across supplier networks and secondary manufacturing sites as needed
* Must Be Clearance Eligible

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* MS in an engineering discipline, or an MBA
* Experience with optical components, ophthalmic or prescription lenses, optical coatings, and optical metrology
* Prior program leadership on a head-worn display, camera module, or other optics-intensive consumer or defense product
* Working knowledge of optical manufacturing processes including injection molding, lamination, die cutting, CNC machining, coating, and active alignment
* Experience with capacity modeling, discrete-event simulation, or manufacturing analytics platforms
* PMP or Lean Six Sigma certification
* Experience standing up a new production line or qualifying a new supplier from scratch

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
