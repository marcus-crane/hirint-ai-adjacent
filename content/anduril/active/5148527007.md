Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### **ABOUT THE TEAM**

Anduril's Lethality team designs, builds, tests, and delivers the warheads and energetic subsystems that enable our autonomous defense systems to accomplish their missions. Our engineers work across the full hardware development lifecycle, translating mission requirements into mechanical designs, validating performance through testing, and partnering with manufacturing teams to bring those designs into production.

Within Lethality, our maritime engineering team develops warhead systems for platforms operating on and beneath the water. These systems must perform reliably in demanding environments, where pressure, corrosion, shock, and long-term storage introduce complex engineering challenges.

We're a small, growing team where engineers have meaningful ownership of the hardware they design and direct visibility into how it performs. You'll work alongside experienced warhead designers, structural analysts, explosives specialists, and manufacturing partners, solving technical challenges that don't always have established solutions.

Our approach is hands-on and iterative. Engineers don't simply deliver drawings and move on. They work across design, fabrication, and testing, using real hardware performance to guide engineering decisions and continuously improve the product.

### **ABOUT THE JOB**

We're looking for a Mechanical Engineer to develop the mechanical components and subsystems of maritime warheads, from initial requirements and detailed design through fabrication, testing, and production readiness.

You'll take ownership of critical mechanical hardware, applying engineering fundamentals to solve structural, environmental, and manufacturing challenges. You'll create detailed designs, evaluate technical trade-offs, work with manufacturing partners, and use test results to improve hardware performance. Depending on the program and your experience, you may own the design of a complete mechanical subsystem or take responsibility for critical components within a larger warhead development effort, working alongside senior engineers on system-level decisions.

We're interested in engineers who have demonstrated ownership of mechanical hardware and know how to turn engineering concepts into physical, tested products. Experience in aerospace, robotics, automotive, marine systems, research, or other hardware-intensive environments can provide a strong foundation.

You'll have the opportunity to develop specialized expertise in maritime engineering, energetic systems, and warhead development while contributing directly to hardware that moves from engineering design into real-world testing.

### **WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* **Own mechanical hardware design.** Develop mechanical components and subsystems from engineering requirements through detailed CAD models, manufacturing drawings, design reviews, and hardware validation.
* **Solve complex mechanical engineering challenges.** Apply structural analysis, materials engineering, tolerance analysis, and engineering judgment to evaluate design trade-offs and improve performance, reliability, and manufacturability.
* **Design for demanding environments.** Develop hardware that can withstand maritime operating conditions, including pressure, corrosion, mechanical shock, and long-term storage requirements.
* **Turn designs into physical hardware.** Work closely with prototype fabrication teams, suppliers, and manufacturing partners to produce, evaluate, and refine mechanical components and assemblies.
* **Validate designs through testing.** Contribute to test planning, instrumentation, hardware preparation, and data analysis. Use test results to investigate failures, validate performance, and inform subsequent design decisions. Testing may include component, system-level, and underwater explosion (UNDEX) evaluations.
* **Develop specialized warhead engineering expertise.** Collaborate with experienced warhead designers and energetic materials specialists to understand system architecture, component interfaces, and how mechanical design decisions influence overall performance and safety.
* **Partner across engineering disciplines.** Work with vehicle integration teams, structural analysts, test engineers, and explosives specialists to translate system requirements into practical mechanical solutions.
* **Support manufacturing and production readiness.** Collaborate with critical subcontractors and manufacturing partners to resolve technical issues, address nonconformances, and improve the repeatability and quality of manufactured hardware.
* **Document and communicate engineering decisions.** Develop specifications, drawings, test procedures, assembly instructions, and technical reports that support the design, manufacture, integration, and validation of hardware.
* **Participate in technical reviews and customer interactions.** Present engineering decisions, explain trade-offs, incorporate feedback, and contribute to technical discussions with internal stakeholders and external partners.
* **Support hands-on testing and integration.** Participate in hardware assembly, test preparation, and evaluation activities in accordance with established safety procedures and applicable qualifications.

### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering, aerospace engineering, ocean engineering, or a related technical discipline, or equivalent relevant education and hands-on engineering experience.
* 5+ years of relevant mechanical engineering experience, including direct ownership of at least one mechanical component, assembly, or subsystem through a complete design, build, and test cycle.
* Demonstrated ownership of at least one mechanical component, assembly, or subsystem through a complete design, build, and test cycle, including direct responsibility for engineering decisions and design deliverables.
* Proficiency with 3D CAD software (such as NX, SolidWorks, or equivalent), including experience creating fabrication-ready models and engineering drawings with appropriate geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T).
* Ability to apply mechanical engineering fundamentals, including structural analysis, material selection, failure mechanisms, and tolerance analysis, to evaluate design trade-offs and resolve technical challenges.
* Hands-on experience contributing to at least one hardware test cycle, including test planning, instrumentation or setup, data analysis, and using results to inform design or validation decisions.
* Experience producing engineering documentation, including design specifications, manufacturing drawings, test procedures, or technical reports.
* Demonstrated ability to independently investigate engineering challenges, evaluate solutions, and advance designs with limited direction and technical review at key milestones.
* Experience collaborating across engineering, manufacturing, and test teams to communicate technical decisions, resolve issues, and incorporate constructive feedback.
* Interest in applying mechanical engineering expertise to maritime defense systems, warhead development, and complex hardware challenges.
* Ability to travel up to 25% during periods of increased testing activity.
* Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. government security clearance at the level required for the position.
