Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**ABOUT THE TEAM**

Anduril's Maritime Division — one of Anduril's fastest-growing divisions — has assembled a diverse team of experts in software, robotics, artificial intelligence, sensor fusion, and data analysis to build the next generation of autonomous maritime capability. Within Maritime, the Heavy Metal business line is standing up a world-class execution concept underpinned by a digital-first production system and adoption of new, advanced manufacturing technologies. Anduril is redefining how industry does shipbuilding and bringing this new approach to a customer base that has historically been under-served  
  
**ABOUT THE JOB**  
We are looking for a Program Operations lead to join the Heavy Metal team. In this role you will be the operational right hand to the Program Manager for a new platform and serve as the connective tissue across Finance (EVMS), Stakeholder Integration, Supply Chain, Workforce Development, and Facilities while also acting as a division-level business generalist who helps the program scale without breaking.  
  
This is a hybrid role by design. Part program-facing: the discipline required to run a large and complex program on cost, on schedule, and against EVMS. Part division operations: strategic initiatives, executive rhythm, planning cycles, and repeatable execution patterns that the program needs as it scales into what will become one of Anduril's largest programs. You will be equally comfortable in a manufacturing bay as in an executive review.  
  
**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Serve as the operational backbone for the product Program Manager, coordinating across the program's core AoRs (Stakeholder Integration, Supply Chain, Workforce Development, Facilities) so that the program can scale and meet its deliverables.
* Support Stakeholder Integration across a demanding external environment (customers, prime partners, government & community stakeholders) and key internal contributors (Heavy Metal’s Manufacturing Technology and Digital teams) — preparing briefing materials, tracking commitments, and acting as a swiss army knife to close loops across a program with an unusually broad set of watchers.
* Work alongside Supply Chain to translate procurement and vendor performance data into program-level risk pictures — surfacing long-lead exposure, sole-source concentrations, and supplier ramp risk before they become schedule slips.
* Partner with Workforce Development on the hiring, training, and skillset scaling plan required to man a program that is growing at an exponential rate — turning a workforce plan into a program-integrated tracking system rather than an HR spreadsheet.
* Support Facilities as a program dependency — connecting facility milestones, capital deployment, and production readiness so that the facility readiness and the product build plan are treated as one integrated schedule, not two.
* Lead strategic initiatives and special projects on behalf of the Program Manager and Heavy Metal leadership — from operating-model design to executive-review preparation to one-off diligence sprints on production, real estate, security, and growth opportunities as the program grows.
* Support the corporate planning cycle for the program — helping build and monitor performance against goals, financial plans, and headcount plans, so we can hit aggressive growth targets while staying honest about where we are.
* Onboard new team members and help stand up the internal operating cadence (all-hands, business reviews, division-wide events) — so that a rapidly growing team ramps fast and inherits a culture of 'doing things differently.'

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 3+ years of experience in some combination of program operations, consulting, corporate strategy, finance, defense/aerospace program management, or general business analysis.
* You love solving complex, ambiguous, cross-functional problems in a people-centric way — and you can hold a program-management lens and a general-business-operations lens at the same time.
* You're both high ownership and low ego — strong outcome orientation, high humility, and comfortable doing the unglamorous work of running a large program.
* You get energy from being a thought partner — translating operational problems into data problems, and then executing on them.
* You are tactical as well as strategic — you do not only sit at a 30,000-foot level; you will spend time in the yard, in the data, and in the details.
* You are a discerning and incredibly fast learner, and you can move between finance, supply chain, workforce, and construction fluently enough to be useful in each.
* Willing and able to be based in Washington, D.C. with regular travel to Costa Mesa, CA and other Anduril sites.
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export-controlled information or facilities.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Typically 5+ years of experience and a Bachelor's degree or equivalent education/work experience. Brings solid, hands-on expertise and can operate independently across a wide range of responsibilities.
* Exposure to Earned Value Management (EVMS) — either as a practitioner, an auditor, or a program leader who has had to make decisions off of it.
* Prior experience inside a shipbuilding, heavy-manufacturing, aerospace, or large defense program — especially one navigating a customer relationship with the U.S. Navy or a prime shipbuilder.
* Experience standing up an operating cadence for a fast-growing team or program — battle rhythms, review structures, planning cycles, tracking systems.
* Comfort working across engineering, manufacturing, supply chain, finance, and workforce functions simultaneously — this role sits at the seam of all of them.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
