**ABOUT THE TEAM**

Anduril's Electromagnetic Warfare (EW) business line builds next-generation products — hardware-enabled, software-defined systems that are fielded, operational, and scaling fast. The EW team operates at a tempo the defense industry isn't used to: delivering novel system configurations into active operations in days, not months, securing major contract awards in under 24 hours, and earning unanimous fielding recommendations from government evaluators. Production is scaling from hundreds to thousands of units across multiple product variants, and the release and delivery challenges are scaling with it.

**ABOUT THE JOB**

We are looking for a Product Manager to join our rapidly growing EW team in sunny Costa Mesa, California. The Product Manager will serve as liaison within the EW business line and complement the Development, Growth, and Manufacturing teams to bridge the gaps between market demand, profitability, and factory operations. If you thrive in ambiguity, have a meticulous attention to detail, an ownership mentality, and a passion to win, this role is for you.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Partner with the Product Architect to own the product vision, roadmap, and commercialization strategy for an EW family of systems
* Work collaboratively with cross-functional teams to define product requirements and ensure alignment between system capabilities and mission objectives in complex operational scenarios
* Champion the New Product Introduction (NPI) process for emerging core products, variants, and accessories
* Responsible for planning, directing, and measuring the seamless execution of the product development process from NPI through manufacturing readiness, full rate production, and into sustainment
* Co-Chair the product Change Control Board (CCB) with Configuration Management for production sustainment
* Leverage inputs from various sources (market, growth, customer, reliability, automation, manufacturing, scalability) and drive product level decisions with high visibility and impact to the business line and customers
* Ensure status and effective communication with key stakeholders across the product development lifecycle

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor’s degree in STEM, or equivalent experience in a complementary field
* 8+ years in product or program management, emphasizing in technical product ownership
* Experience managing NPI transition through full rate production, with the development gates and reviews associated
* Experience supporting design and execution for advanced, combined hardware/software systems
* Experience across both commercial product and defense industries
* Proven ability to decompose and organize technically complex projects in a structured approach
* Aptitude for working with internal and external stakeholders, across a wide range of technical comfort
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export-controlled information or facilities

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Able to obtain and hold a U.S. Secret Clearance / active U.S. Secret or Top-Secret security clearance.
* Experience with global product lines in a wide range of advanced technologies
* Experience with AS9100 quality management systems
* Experience with various MIL-STD testing and associated requirements
* Experience in business case proposals and contract negotiations

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
