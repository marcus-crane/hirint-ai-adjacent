### ABOUT THE JOB

The **Technical Security Application Engineer, Manufacturing**, is a hands-on technical role responsible for the installation, configuration, troubleshooting, and maintenance of physical access control systems (PACS) and video surveillance systems (VMS) across Anduril's sites, with a particular focus on Arsenal-1. This is a specialist role focused on system hardware, application-level configuration, and day-to-day technical problem solving.  
In addition to hands-on system work, you'll support small-to-medium technical security projects, coordinate with vendors and integrators, and communicate progress and issues to stakeholders. You'll work closely with senior team members on larger deployments while owning the reliability and performance of the systems in your area of responsibility. A working knowledge of IP networking is essential, as these systems are network-dependent.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Install, configure, test, and troubleshoot access control hardware (readers, controllers, door hardware, panels, credentials) and video surveillance hardware (cameras, intercoms, emergency intercoms).
* Perform application-level configuration and administration of access control and video management systems.
* Diagnose and resolve system, hardware, and network-related issues, escalating complex problems as needed.
* Support the deployment, commissioning, and upgrade of technical security systems across sites.
* Coordinate with vendors and integrators on installations, service calls, warranty issues, and system upgrades.
* Assist in managing small-to-medium technical security projects, including scheduling, tracking tasks, and reporting status to stakeholders.
* Perform preventative maintenance and health checks to ensure system uptime and reliability.
* Configure and troubleshoot networking components supporting security systems (IP addressing, VLANs, switches, PoE, basic firewall/port configuration- in collaboration with Network Engineering teams).
* Create and maintain technical documentation, as-builts, wiring diagrams, and standard operating procedures.
* Maintain accurate device inventory and hardware/spares stock for supported sites.
* Communicate clearly with internal stakeholders, providing updates on project progress, system issues, and resolutions.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 5+ years of hands-on experience installing, configuring, and troubleshooting physical access control and/or video surveillance systems.
* Extensive experience with Genetec Security Center.
* Certification in Omnicast and Synergis.
* Experience troubleshooting hardware, cabling, and low-voltage systems.
* Ability to read and interpret wiring diagrams, floor plans, and technical documentation.
* Experience working with vendors and integrators on installations and service.
* Strong communication skills with the ability to update stakeholders on technical work and project status.
* Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and a proactive problem-solving mindset.
* Familiarity with low-voltage cabling standards and structured cabling best practices.

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 7+ years of experience in technical security systems, with a strong focus on access control, and intrusion detection.
* Experience supporting technical security systems across multiple or global sites.
* Strong project management skills and extensive experience with large-scale technical security system implementations and upgrades.
* Existing active SECRET clearance.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
