Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

## ABOUT THE JOB

Anduril is seeking a Manufacturing Operations / Production Manager to join our Production team in Sydney. Reporting to the Director of Operations, this role is responsible for planning, coordinating, and controlling manufacturing operations across our Sydney Maritime Production Facility.  
  
This is a senior management role managing a team of 40-50 people across assembly, test, and integration operations for complex robotics and subsea hardware platforms. The role requires both strategic operations management capability and frontline technical leadership, placing you at the forefront of fostering team development and overseeing day-to-day delivery operations. Success in this pivotal position demands consistent achievement across team management, production operations, and financial goals, ensuring the delivery of world-class, mission-critical defence products.

## WHAT YOU'LL DO

**Production Leadership & Execution**

* Lead day-to-day production operations across multiple product lines, ensuring efficient production and delivery of subsea robotics hardware.
* Own the production plan and coordinate with program management, planning, and supply chain to align resources with build schedules and delivery commitments.
* Strategically manage production scheduling by working closely with planning and manufacturing engineering teams to solve for desired production performance outcomes. Tactically direct daily operations to meet target on-time delivery goals.
* Monitor production metrics (units completed, cycle time, rework rates) and drive corrective actions when performance deviates from targets.
* Manage shift schedules, overtime, and workforce allocation to balance workload across the team and meet surge requirements when needed.
* Ensure work orders are correctly executed, clear daily production blockers, and generate daily pass-down and quality reports reporting out across teams.

**Test & Integration Oversight**

* Oversee system-level and subsystem test operations, ensuring test procedures are followed and test equipment is maintained and calibrated.
* Drive yield improvements through collaboration with manufacturing engineering, design engineering, test engineering, and quality teams â including root-cause analysis and corrective actions to reduce retest cycles.

**People Leadership & Development**

* Recruit, onboard, train, and retain a high-performing team of supervisors, coordinators, assembly and test technicians across a team of up to 50 people.
* Set clear performance expectations, conduct regular reviews, and actively coach and mentor team members fostering professional growth, cross-training, and a culture of accountability on the production floor.
* Identify skill gaps and coordinate technical training programs, working to continuously increase the capability and competency of the team, inclusive of system-level troubleshooting.

**Quality & Compliance**

* Ensure all production and test activities comply with applicable quality management system requirements, workmanship standards, and regulatory frameworks.
* Support Material Review Board (MRB) dispositions, nonconformance investigations, and corrective/preventive action (CAPA) processes.
* Lead cross-functional communication with engineering, supply chain, and quality teams to support root cause investigations and corrective actions.
* Champion a culture of built-in quality, emphasising defect prevention over detection.

**Safety & Environment**

* Maintain a safe production environment in compliance with WHS legislation and company policies.
* Conduct regular safety walks, toolbox talks, and incident investigations; drive corrective actions to closure.
* Ensure proper handling, storage, and disposal of hazardous materials and controlled substances used in production.
* Enforce safety procedures and drive continuous improvement in safety performance.

**Continuous Improvement & Process Excellence**

* Identify and lead Lean manufacturing and continuous improvement initiatives (5S, Kaizen, standard work) to increase efficiency and reduce waste.
* Collaborate with Manufacturing Engineering to refine work instructions, tooling, fixtures, and production processes.
* Support new product introduction (NPI) and engineering change order (ECO) implementation on the production floor.
* Leverage data-driven decision-making to establish KPIs and coach the production team to do the same.

**Strategic Communication**

* Effectively communicate priorities, production progress, risks, and resource needs to senior leadership and facilitate cross-functional collaboration with engineering, field operations, maintenance and international sites.
* Conduct transactions and maintain accuracy across enterprise systems (e.g., Oracle, JIRA, MES).
* Support the broader team to ensure inventory accuracy and clear supply chain coordination.

## REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Manufacturing, Operations Management, or a related technical discipline; equivalent combination of education and experience considered.
* 15+ years of progressive experience in manufacturing or production operations within complex assembly environments.
* 5+ years in a direct leadership role managing teams of 20+ people, with demonstrated experience scaling and managing teams of 40-50.
* Proven expertise implementing Lean manufacturing principles, including 5S, Kaizen, and standard work methodologies with a constant desire to achieve continuous improvement.
* Strong background in production scheduling, capacity planning, and shop floor management systems (MES/ERP).
* Demonstrated success in employee development, coaching, and mentorship at scale.
* Strong knowledge of production change management principles (NPI, ECOs)
* Excellent leadership, organisation, and communication skills working across multiple business divisions.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active NV2 Security clearance.

## PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Experience in defence, aerospace, or similarly regulated manufacturing environments.
* Experience in high-risk material handling using overhead cranes, stored energy and robotic systems.
* Familiarity with ISO 9001 and ISO 45001 standards.
* Experience scaling production from low-rate initial production (LRIP) to full-rate production (FRP).
* Experience in a high-growth startup or rapid-scaling manufacturing environment.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
