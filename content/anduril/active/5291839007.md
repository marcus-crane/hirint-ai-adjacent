## About the Team

The Anduril Battlespace Awareness Radar team develops state-of-the-art radar systems, deployed to tackle the most significant security challenges of America and its allies. The Radar team is responsible for all aspects of radar system development, from the ideation stage through initial production. In this role, you will work closely with an interdisciplinary technical team to define test strategies, build automated validation infrastructure, perform testing against representative hardware, and mature prototypes into reliable products.

## About the Job

We are seeking a Radar Integration Lead with training and experience in a relevant engineering field to lead the design of radar integrations into operational concepts of employment. The Radar Integration Lead will be a subject matter expert on Anduril radar products to ensure that customers can take full advantage of their capabilities and that Anduril provides a seamless, efficient user experience to system integrators working with Anduril radar products. Working with Radar Operations Leads, Technical Operations Engineers, product owners, and engineering functional leadership, the Radar Integration Lead will work cross-functionally to ensure effective training, systems engineering, product deployment planning, technical documentation, and integration engineering ultimately leading to mission success for our integration partners.

## What You'll Do

* Serve in a customer-facing capacity, working directly with industry partners and the Department of War to develop and deploy Anduril radar products to meet customer and operational needs. Support the delivery of contract deliverables such as training manuals, pre-deployment site surveys, and technical documentation.
* Manage, unblock, and accelerate cross-team projects spanning multiple functional areas. Instill an efficient execution mindset and workflow across the teams.
* Provide input to product and engineering teams to drive the development of product features and configurations that enhance or improve the employment of Anduril radar products.
* Support end users in understanding and using our capabilities through the development of training content, integration into unit training schedules, and delivery of training.
* Advise customers and other business lines on the appropriate employment of Anduril radar systems.
* Remove obstacles to drive clarity and progress. Identify gaps in communication or schedule, manage issue escalations, support teams balancing competing priorities, and proactively drive results.
* Demonstrate high ownership of all pieces of work; become a trusted partner to Anduril’s engineers.

## REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 10+ years of experience as a radar engineer, systems integration lead or similar role.
* Comfortable working across mechanical, electrical, and software aspects of radar or other sensor integration into complex mission systems.
* Experience employing radar systems in military operations and their integration into larger operational CONOPS and systems.
* Strong familiarity with integrated air defense systems, their architectures, and the role of radars in these systems.
* Experience translating and decomposing technical concepts into operational requirements and performance specifications.
* Strong understanding of radio-frequency (RF) propagation and how radar systems work.
* Experience in project management and product deployment roles.
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Top Secret/SCI security clearance.
* Willing to travel up to 30%.

## PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Client management experience.
* Ability to work within organizations with minimal structure and direction.
* Prior military or government acquisition or contracting experience.
* Experience with tools like Jira and Confluence.
