### **ABOUT THE JOB**

Anduril’s Propulsion Manufacturing Engineering Team is seeking a Propulsion Test Engineer to join our team. The propulsion manufacturing test team is responsible for high scale production testing of jet engines and subcomponents.  
  
As a Propulsion Test Engineer, you will own the end-of-line static-fire test operations.The right person for this role has the ability to both build new clean-sheet static-fire test equipment as well as troubleshoot and improve existing systems. If you are someone who can independently drive projects to completion and own a critical part of engine production then this role is for you.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Develop test stands, instrumentation packages and equipment to support production engine test and integration.
* Act as sole ‘buck-stops-here’ owner for static-fire production testing operations
* Design test procedures and processes for production technicians to operate test facilities
* Develop system requirements and test campaigns for Anduril’s wide range of airbreathing turbine applications.
* Work with engine control systems including FADEC’s, ECU’s, Starter-Generators and related hardware.
* Work with actuators, sensors, connectors and architecture of engine control systems.
* Test, diagnose, and analyze engine data on test stands, on test expeditions, and in flight test.
* Lead data reviews and root cause investigations

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical, Mechatronics Engineering, Aerospace Engineering or similar.
* 5+ years relevant engineering experience in gas turbine, aerospace test engineering, or rocket propulsion roles
* Experiencing designing, building and qualifying test equipment
* Experience with instrumentation types, selection and data acquisition
* Familiarity with test planning, readiness reviews, P&IDs and other test documentation
* Engineering programming ability in Python, Matlab or other scripting language
* Experience with airbreathing or rocket propulsion testing
* Ability to collaborate well with other electrical, mechanical, software, firmware, and test engineers.
* Practical experience with diagnostics monitoring, failure detection, RCCA and data analysis.
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export controlled information or facilities
* Travel time up to 50%
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance

#### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Ability to assist with designing simple mechanical parts (CAD). Knowledge of manufacturing methods (machining, sheet metal, composites, 3D printing, injection molding, etc.) preferred.
* Experience in procuring development parts and working in a fabrication environment
* Familiarity with CAN, XCP, ARINC or other common bus protocols
* Experience with CANape or similar CAN interface tool.
* Experience in the design and implementation of automotive or aviation control electronics.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
