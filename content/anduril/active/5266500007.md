**ABOUT THE TEAM**  
The Air Dominance and Strike Aerostructures Engineering Team is responsible for designing and analyzing advanced structural parts for Group 5 air vehicles and missile platforms. Air Dominance and Strike Structures Engineers own the full development lifecycle, from conceptual to detailed design and analysis of primary and secondary structures, ensuring high-performance, resilient, and scalable mechanical systems that meet the demands of complex, contested environments.  
  
**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Design and release structural repair solutions and repair drawings for fielded and in-production Fury air vehicles, covering both metallic and composite primary and secondary structure
* Serve as the on-the-floor liaison engineer for production and sustainment, resolving build issues and protecting configuration while maintaining schedule
* Partner with MRB analysis engineers to translate non-conformance dispositions into manufacturable repair drawings and work instructions
* Author and release Engineering Change Requests/Orders (ECRs/ECOs) to improve drawings, models, and specifications based on production feedback
* Apply design-for-manufacturing and design-for-repair principles to produce solutions that are cost-effective and easy to execute at rate
* Analyze recurring production defects and test escapes to identify trends, and drive systemic design or process changes to reduce rework, scrap, and cycle time
* Coordinate across Manufacturing Engineering, Quality, Tooling, and M&P to develop and refine repair methods, fixtures, and inspection criteria
* Provide day-to-day technical guidance to manufacturing and quality teams on build intent, repair acceptability, and configuration control

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor's in Aerospace Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or equivalent experience
* 5+ years of experience in aircraft structural design, sustainment, or liaison engineering, including hands-on production floor support
* Proficiency in CAD software (Siemens NX or equivalent) for generating repair and production drawings
* Working knowledge of metallic and composite structural design, behavior, and associated fabrication processes (forming, machining, bonding, assembly)
* Experience creating, reviewing, and interpreting engineering drawings, 3D models, and GD&T
* Strong problem-solving skills and experience leading cross-functional root cause and corrective action efforts
* Excellent communication skills and demonstrated ability to work cross-functionally with technicians, engineers, and leadership
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance
* Ability to travel up to 25% to vendors, production, and test sites as needed to support hardware builds and troubleshoot issues directly

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience with MRB authority, non-conformance systems, and configuration management in a regulated aerospace environment
* Prior liaison or production support engineering role working daily with manufacturing and test operations
* Background in design-for-manufacturability, lean manufacturing, and continuous improvement for complex assemblies
* Experience with fast-paced prototype design and manufacture of uncrewed aircraft
* Hands-on manufacturing experience
* Practical aircraft knowledge (A&P certification, pilot's license, etc.)

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
