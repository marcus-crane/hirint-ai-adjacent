### ABOUT THE TEAM

## The Global Mobility team is responsible for getting Anduril talent where the mission needs it. We partner with leadership and cross-functional teams to help Anduril expand globally and staff new programs.

### ABOUT THE JOB

## As the Global Mobility Program Manager, Tax, you will develop the strategy and operating model for mobility tax at Anduril, spanning international and domestic assignments, cross-border transfers, business travel, and the reporting obligations that follow. In this individual contributor role, you will translate complex tax requirements into clear policies, durable controls, and practical guidance for the business, while supporting mobile employees directly through cost projections, mobility briefings, and the documentation that accompanies a move. Your technical judgment and project management skills will be essential in identifying tax implications early, keeping stakeholders informed, and ensuring compliance as the company expands into new jurisdictions globally.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Develop the global mobility tax framework, principles, governance, and service model in partnership with Corporate Tax teams.
* Build clear policies and decision frameworks for international and domestic business assignments, cross-border transfers, and other mobility scenarios.
* Support the global expansion strategy, ensuring compliance in international jurisdictions.
* Partner with Payroll, Corporate Tax, Finance, Legal, People, and business leaders to identify tax implications early and support sound workforce decisions.
* Support employees across the mobility lifecycle, including cost projections, assignment consultation and documentations, tax briefings, and coordination of tax return preparation with external advisors.
* Serve as the escalation point for employee and manager questions on mobility and assignment questions, providing clear, consistent guidance and a high-quality employee experience.
* Establish reporting, playbooks and decision frameworks.
* Manage external tax advisors and vendor partners, service quality, and delivery against agreed standards, timelines, and outcomes.
* Monitor regulatory and business changes, assess program impact, and continuously strengthen policies and controls.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Minimum of 7+ years of experience in global mobility tax, expatriate tax, or business-traveler compliance.
* Experience in an in-house global mobility tax function, tax advisory firm, or both.
* Strong knowledge of individual income tax, payroll withholding and reporting, social security and totalization, tax equalization and protection, and permanent establishment risk.
* Proven experience building or scaling mobility tax policies, controls, and workflows for a globally operating company.
* Excellent project management skills with the ability to handle multiple priorities and lead cross-functional initiatives.
* Experience managing external tax advisors and implementing mobility tax technology.
* Highly organized, data-driven, and operationally sharp with strong attention to detail and accuracy.
* Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to translate technical tax requirements into clear guidance for employees and business partners.
* Analytical thinker with a proactive mindset; thrives in fast-paced, evolving environments and meets tight deadlines.
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export controlled information or facilities

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Experience supporting a defense, aerospace, or other highly regulated industry, including how export control and clearance requirements affect mobility decisions.
* Working knowledge of equity compensation taxation for mobile employees, equity tax treatments, and cross-border sourcing of RSUs and options.
* Hands-on experience with shadow payroll, hypothetical tax, and tax equalization settlements across multiple jurisdictions.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
