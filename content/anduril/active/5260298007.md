**ABOUT THE JOB**

Anduril’s Air Dominance and Strike (AD&S) team is seeking a Senior Staff Mission Systems Radar Engineer to join our team. The AD&S team is responsible for developing end-to-end “mission-ized” autonomous air systems capable of delivering exceptional warfighter outcomes to our customers.

As a Senior Staff Mission Systems Radar Engineer, you will be responsible for the definition, analysis, and validation of radar sensing performance across the system — from the mission-level detection and tracking outcomes the platform has to deliver, down to the waveforms, apertures, and processing that produce them. The right person will have experience taking a radar design from first-principles budgets through hardware bring-up to measured performance. This will require taking ownership of the models, calibration and test campaigns, as well as managing margin across various systems (i.e. antenna, RF, and software).

**WHAT YOU’LL DO** 

* Own radar system architecture and the performance budgets behind it — detection range, signal-to-noise, probability of detection and false alarm, range and Doppler resolution, angular accuracy.
* Derive subsystem requirements from mission-level needs, flow them down to antenna, transmit/receive, frequency conversion, digitization and signal processing, and hold the design to them through the program.
* Design and analyze waveforms and timing: pulse-Doppler and LFM/FMCW schemes, PRF selection, ambiguity and eclipsing analysis, range-Doppler coupling, and the tradeoffs these force back onto the hardware.
* Specify the detection and estimation chain — pulse compression, coherent and Doppler integration, CFAR, clutter suppression, adaptive beamforming, monopulse or interferometric angle estimation — precisely enough for firmware and software to implement it.
* Define, lead and report calibration and test campaigns, including array calibration and embedded-element pattern characterization.
* Drive closure across antenna, RF, firmware, software and mechanical teams when performance comes up short, and make the call on where to spend margin.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS** 

* B.S. in Electrical Engineering, Physics or a related field and 8+ years of radar engineering experience. M.S. and 6+ years, or Ph.D. and 3+ years, also considered.
* Deep working command of radar fundamentals: the radar range equation, matched filtering, detection and estimation theory, Doppler processing, and clutter and multipath behavior.
* Phased array fundamentals: beamforming and taper design, element and embedded-element patterns, grating lobes and scan loss, and quantization and calibration error budgets.
* Fluency in MATLAB and/or Python for system modeling, algorithm prototyping and reduction of measured data.
* Demonstrated experience carrying a radar design from model through hardware bring-up to measured performance.
* Ability to write and defend requirements, budgets and trade studies.
* Eligible to obtain a US Security Clearance

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS** 

* Knowledge of current and next-generation advanced radar systems.
* Experience with missile design & integration.
* Microwave or millimeter-wave systems (X, Ku, K or Ka band).
* Electronically scanned arrays: calibration methods, mutual coupling, beam pointing error budgets and failed-element effects.
* EM simulation (HFSS, CST, FEKO or equivalent) and a track record of correlating simulation to chamber measurement.
* Moving-platform radar: ground and sea clutter, motion compensation, and adaptive techniques such as STAP.
* Specifying signal processing for implementation in FPGA or RFSoC fabric and working alongside firmware engineers to close it.
* An active U.S. Department of Defense Secret clearance, or eligibility to obtain one.
