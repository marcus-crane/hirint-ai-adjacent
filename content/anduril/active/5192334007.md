Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

## **ABOUT THE TEAM**

The Connected Warfare division builds systems to support global command and control missions. Our Joint portfolio, anchored by the Enterprise Data Mesh (EDM), connects data across organizations, domains, and classification levels so that commanders and analysts can make time-sensitive decisions from a single source of truth.

## **ABOUT THE JOB**

We are looking for a Senior Director, Growth to own the growth strategy and revenue trajectory for the Joint C4I portfolio. EDM is live, scaling, and has real pull from the warfighter. Today, the business is concentrated in a single bill payer (CDAO) and a narrow set of programs. You will expand the network of bill payers for mesh capabilities beyond CDAO into DISA, the Combatant Commands, and DIA; broaden the CDAO relationship to include adjacent programs like AES, C&S, and Voyager; and shape requirements and competitive positioning 12-18 months ahead of solicitation.  
  
This is not a coordination role. You are a player-coach who personally runs pursuits, shapes requirements, and sits across from senior government buyers. You will build the capture campaigns that turn today's early traction into funded, scaled programs of record.

## **WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Own the number: carry the growth plan for the Joint C4I portfolio, with a primary objective of scaling EDM and be accountable for pipeline health, capture progress, and multi-year revenue trajectory
* Expand the bill-payer map by identifying and cultivating new organizations to fund mesh capabilities beyond CDAO, with DISA, the Combatant Commands, and DIA as priority targets. Build relationships, shape demand signals, and position the company before formal solicitation
* Grow the existing CDAO relationship into a portfolio account by adding adjacent programs including AES, C&S, and Voyager
* Personally lead capture campaigns for mid-eight- to low-nine-figure opportunities: shape requirements, develop win strategies, coordinate proposals, and close
* Serve as the senior growth face to Joint and intelligence-community buyers at the O-6/GS-15 level and above, cultivating these relationships directly rather than through intermediaries
* Shape requirements early by engaging requirements owners, writing white papers, and running demonstrations that prove capability before acquisition timelines formalize
* Track incumbent and emerging C4I competitors, including their positioning and contract vehicles, and turn that intelligence into capture strategy and product roadmap input
* Drive a tight loop between customer requirements and the product roadmap with Engineering, Product, and Delivery, so what we sell is what we can build and sustain

## **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 10+ years of experience in business development, capture, or growth leadership within the defense or intelligence sectors
* Has carried a quota or closed deals in the eight to nine figure range, and can describe specific programs you personally shaped and won, not programs you supported or staffed
* Demonstrated record of growing a defense or defense-technology portfolio from early traction to meaningful scale across multiple customers and contract vehicles
* Experience selling software, compute, infrastructure, or communications solutions to C4I buyers in the Federal Government, with an understanding of how data mesh, enterprise data architecture, and edge compute fit into the Joint warfighting stack
* Experience selling directly to DoD end-customers and requirements owners, not through primes as intermediaries
* Deep understanding of the C4I competitive landscape: who the incumbents are, what they sell, where they're entrenched, and where they're vulnerable
* Fluency in DoD acquisition processes, budget cycles, and PPBE, including how Joint programs get funded and where the money moves
* An established, active network within OSD R&E and adjacent Joint organizations, with existing access to CDAO, DISA, DIA, or Combatant Command J2/J6 staffs
* Player-coach mentality: you personally lead high-priority captures while building the team and process around them
* Bias toward speed and non-traditional acquisition, and comfort operating outside the legacy defense BD playbook
* Exceptional written and verbal executive communication, able to brief a COCOM J6 and write a capture strategy the team can execute
* Currently possesses and is able to maintain an active U.S. TS/SCI security clearance

## **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience working directly with CDAO, DISA, DIA, or Combatant Command J2/J6 staffs on data, networking, or enterprise IT programs
* Background in data mesh, data fabric, or enterprise data architecture solutions, with an understanding of the technical landscape and not just the sales motion
* Prior military or government service in Joint C4I, intelligence, or communications roles
* Experience at a high-growth defense technology company or venture-backed defense startup, where you've learned what it means to build while selling
* International defense growth experience, including FMS, DCS, or coalition partner engagement

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
