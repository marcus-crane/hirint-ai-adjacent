### **ABOUT THE TEAM**

Anduril's Global Communications team is responsible for how the company communicates with the world and with its own employees. Our remit spans corporate communications, media relations, editorial and executive communications, internal communications, divisional and product communications, international communications, and community relations.  
We work across the company to tell the story of Anduril's technology, people, and business; establish its reputation with the audiences that influence its success; and help employees understand where the company is headed and their role in getting there.

#### **ABOUT THE JOB**

Anduril is seeking a Senior Director of Corporate Communications to lead the company's corporate communications and enterprise-level internal communications. Reporting to the VP of Global Communications, this person will be responsible for Anduril's corporate narrative, reputation, media relations, financial communications, and company-wide employee communications.  
  
Anduril is growing rapidly, expanding into new markets, investing in significant manufacturing capacity, and taking on increasingly complex programs around the world. We're looking for an experienced communications leader who understands how to build and protect the reputation of a large company, translate complicated business developments into compelling stories, and help employees stay connected to the company's priorities as it scales.  
  
The ideal candidate combines strong business instincts, exceptional judgment, and extensive experience advising senior executives. They should be equally comfortable developing a long-term corporate communications strategy, managing a sensitive media issue, advising on a significant corporate development, or preparing communications for an important company-wide announcement.  
  
We're looking for someone who can set the strategy, build the capabilities needed as the company grows, and personally drive high-priority work. The right person brings a strong point of view, sound judgment, intellectual curiosity, and the ability to operate independently in a fast-moving environment.

#### **WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* **Lead Anduril's corporate communications strategy:** Develop and execute the communications agenda that establishes Anduril's position as a leading defense technology and industrial company. Translate business priorities, performance, investments, and major milestones into compelling narratives for external audiences.
* **Own corporate media relations.** Build and maintain relationships with influential business, financial, technology, and national news organizations. Drive proactive coverage of Anduril's business and strategy, manage high-profile media engagements, and serve as a senior advisor on sensitive inquiries.
* **Lead enterprise-wide internal communications:** Own the strategy and execution of communications that reach employees across Anduril, including company all-hands meetings, major organizational announcements, corporate updates, and internal communications from the C-suite. Ensure employees understand the company's direction, priorities, and major business developments.
* **Serve as a strategic communications advisor to senior leadership:** Partner directly with Anduril's executives on consequential corporate decisions, business developments, organizational changes, and reputational challenges. Provide clear, actionable recommendations grounded in a deep understanding of the business.
* **Lead financial and major corporate communications:** Partner with Finance, Legal, IR and executive leadership on communications related to financial performance, corporate transactions, strategic investments, capital raising, and other significant business milestones. Bring the experience and discipline required to communicate complex financial matters accurately and effectively.
* **Manage corporate issues and crisis communications:** Anticipate emerging reputational risks, develop response strategies, and coordinate across Legal, Government Relations, business leadership, and other stakeholders on sensitive matters. Build the processes and preparedness necessary to respond effectively to high-stakes issues.
* **Develop and strengthen Anduril's corporate reputation:** Identify opportunities to increase understanding of the company's business, leadership, and role in the defense industry. Develop sustained communications efforts that reach beyond individual announcements and establish a clear, credible understanding of Anduril among priority audiences.
* **Build the corporate communications function as Anduril scales.** Establish the strategy, processes, external partnerships, and capabilities needed to support a rapidly growing company. Lead a lean team initially, remain deeply involved in execution, and help shape the function's growth over time.

#### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 10+ years of experience in corporate communications, including significant experience supporting a high-growth technology company, major corporation, or similarly complex organization.
* Demonstrated experience developing and leading corporate communications strategies for large, high-profile organizations, including periods of significant growth or transformation.
* Experience with financial communications, major corporate transactions, and communications involving complex business and financial matters.
* Strong relationships with top-tier business, financial, technology, and national media, with a track record of securing meaningful coverage and managing sensitive media engagements.
* Significant experience advising C-suite executives on corporate strategy, reputation, organizational developments, and high-stakes communications decisions.
* Demonstrated ability to manage complex issues and crisis communications with sound judgment under pressure.
* Exceptional writing, editing, and verbal communication skills, with the ability to translate complicated technical, financial, and business information into clear, compelling communications.
* Ability to operate independently, establish priorities, develop recommendations, and execute effectively with limited resources.

#### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Think like a business leader. You want to understand how the company operates, where it is headed, and the opportunities and challenges that will shape its future.
* Have strong instincts and an independent point of view. You can assess a complicated situation, develop a recommendation, and confidently advise senior leadership.
* Understand that building a corporate reputation requires sustained work, deep relationships, and a compelling story grounded in business performance.
* Are comfortable operating at every level, from advising senior executives on sensitive decisions to writing a media response or developing an all-hands presentation.
* Can bring structure and clarity to ambiguous situations, establish priorities, and move quickly from strategy to execution.
* Have strong editorial judgment and know how to communicate complex subjects simply and precisely.
* Are intellectually curious and willing to learn the details. Anduril's business is technically complex, and credibility comes from understanding the substance.
* Are a builder who enjoys creating capabilities, improving processes, and establishing a strong foundation for a growing team.
* Anticipate opportunities and problems before they arrive and take responsibility for driving the work forward.
* Know when to be aggressive, when to be measured, and when the best communications decision is to say very little.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
