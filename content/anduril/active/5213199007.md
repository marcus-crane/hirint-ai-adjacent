Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**ABOUT THE ROLE**  
  
We are seeking a Head of Production to own the strategic development, operational direction, and execution of production for high-volume compute devices. In this role, you will serve as both a strategic leader and a hands-on operator, responsible for defining and driving the future success of the production portfolio and ensuring consistent, flawless execution across engineering, supply chain, and contract manufacturing. You will own the end-to-end relationship with contract manufacturers (CMs), lead the transition from New Product Introduction (NPI) through to sustained high-rate mass production, and build supply chain resilience — all while ensuring operational excellence, scalability, and adherence to rigorous quality and cost targets.  
  
**WHAT YOU'LL DO**  
  
***Contract Manufacturer Management (High-Volume Compute)***

* **Own CM Relationships & Performance:** Own the relationship, performance, and output of contract manufacturers producing compute devices at high volume. Drive CM communications, onboarding, capacity planning, and new product introduction (NPI) through to mass production ramp.
* **KPI Management:** Establish and manage KPIs across yield, quality, cost, throughput, and on-time delivery; hold CM partners accountable to them. Use data to drive continuous improvement and business impact.
* **Production Ramps:** Lead production ramps and volume transitions across multiple CM sites and geographies, ensuring on-time delivery against aggressive targets.

***Supply Chain Management***

* **Supply Chain Strategy:** Own supply chain strategy for high-volume production: demand/supply planning, component sourcing, inventory management, and logistics.
* **Risk & Resilience:** Build resilience against component shortages, single-source risk, and geopolitical or lead-time disruption.
* **Component Sourcing:** Partner with procurement and engineering to secure allocation of critical and long-lead components at scale.

***Contract Negotiation***

* **Manufacturing Agreements:** Negotiate manufacturing agreements, pricing, capacity commitments, SLAs, and cost-down roadmaps with CMs and suppliers.
* **Commercial Terms:** Structure commercial terms that protect margin, ensure supply, and align partner incentives with volume and quality targets.
* **Cost Reduction:** Drive year-over-year cost reduction through negotiation, design-for-cost, and competitive sourcing.

***Strategic & Operational Leadership***

* **Executive Partnership:** Serve as the production voice to the executive team and board; translate operational status, risk, and trade-offs into clear executive-level recommendations. Act as a key partner to Anduril's SVP of Manufacturing and broader Executive Leadership, contributing to company-wide production strategy and vision.
* **Cross-Functional Partnership:** Partner cross-functionally with Engineering, Product, Procurement, Quality, Program Management, Deployment Operations, and Finance to align production plans with company goals and ensure seamless product maturation and delivery.
* **Talent & Team Building:** Build, lead, and develop a high-performing production/operations organization. Attract, develop, and retain top-tier talent, fostering a culture of ownership and excellence.
* **Process & Systems Excellence:** Establish, mature, and continuously refine manufacturing processes, operational playbooks, and systems to enable efficient scaling and consistent quality.
* **Digital Manufacturing Infrastructure:** Advise on development and oversee implementation of digital technologies — including PLM, ERP, MES, and QMS — that enable production operations to scale efficiently while meeting customer requirements.
* **Compliance & Standards:** Ensure adherence to applicable industry standards (e.g., AS9100D) and all relevant safety, regulatory, and environmental requirements.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 10+ years of progressive leadership in high-volume production of compute or electronics hardware (servers, data center infrastructure, or comparable devices), scaling products from prototype to full-rate production.
* Proven track record managing contract manufacturers / EMS partners at scale, including NPI-to-mass-production ramps. Direct ownership of at least one new product ramp through full-rate production.
* Deep supply chain management expertise — planning, sourcing, risk mitigation, procurement, and warehousing functions essential to high-volume hardware manufacturing.
* Demonstrated contract negotiation skill with CMs and suppliers (pricing, capacity, SLAs, cost-down).
* Strong executive presence with the ability to influence and partner with C-level leadership and the board.
* Experience managing a workforce of 100+ employees across quality, supply chain, manufacturing engineering, operations, and technicians.
* Experience managing a P&L in a production environment, including capital expenditure forecasting and resource allocation.
* Demonstrated strategic thinking and strong problem-solving and analytical skills, with the ability to understand how problems and solutions integrate within the broader organization.
* Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Operations, Supply Chain, or related technical field; advanced degree a plus. Equivalent practical experience with a proven track record considered.
* Must be eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience scaling a new compute product line from launch through high-volume production (on the order of thousands of units annually).
* Global CM/supplier management experience across multiple regions.
* Background blending in-house and outsourced manufacturing models.
* Experience with complex electromechanical systems in a deeply technical industry such as aerospace, defense, consumer electronics, or data center infrastructure.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
