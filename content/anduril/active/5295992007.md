Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

ABOUT THE JOB:  
  
We are on the lookout for an exceptional Senior International Accounting Manager to lead Anduril’s accounting operations across Continental Europe and the Middle East from our Amsterdam office. This role owns the month-end close, statutory reporting, audit deliverables, and accounting operations for the Company’s legal entities throughout the region, and is the senior accounting leader on the ground as that footprint expands.  
  
You will stand up and scale the accounting function while partnering with Legal, Tax, Treasury, Payroll, and Operations as each new jurisdiction comes online. This is a leadership seat: you will hire, develop, and lead the accounting team in Amsterdam, and manage the outsourced providers supporting the countries where we do not yet have our own people.  
  
The ideal candidate has run a multi-country close, is fluent in the difference between a US GAAP reporting package and a local statutory filing, and would rather design the process than inherit it. Join us in our mission to disrupt the defense industry and elevate your career with a team dedicated to making a significant impact on a global scale.  
  
WHAT YOU’LL DO:

* Own the month-end, quarter-end, and year-end close for the Company’s legal entities across Continental Europe and the Middle East, delivering to internal deadlines while ensuring the accuracy of financial data.
* Own statutory reporting across the region, including statutory financial statements, local reporting differences, and adherence to local filing deadlines in each jurisdiction.
* Manage local statutory audits end to end, acting as the primary point of contact for local auditors while coordinating with the HQ team.
* Review journal entries, account reconciliations, and fluctuation analyses; validate foreign currency remeasurement and translation impacts for the region’s entities.
* Own accounts payable operations and the procure-to-pay cycle for the region, from invoice receipt through payment disbursement, including vendor relationship management and payment performance.
* Hire, develop, and lead the regional accounting team in Amsterdam - including accounting managers and specialists - as the Company grows throughout the region, and manage outsourced accounting providers where local work is performed externally.
* Serve as the primary accounting liaison between the US parent company and the
* Own the accounting workstream on new entity and country launches, partnering with Legal on incorporation and registration and with Business Operations on site readiness.
* Ensure adherence to internal controls and accounting policies, and design and document new controls as entities and requirements come online.
* Partner with Tax, Treasury, and Payroll on VAT and local tax filings, transfer pricing support, intercompany settlement, cash forecasting, and payroll approvals across the region.
* Support the configuration and ongoing maintenance of the region’s legal entities within the Company’s ERP, including any local statutory reporting basis.
* Identify and execute opportunities for process improvement, including the adoption of automation and AI tools, so that the function scales faster than its headcount.
* Partner with regional operational and program leaders to support business decision-making.
* Work on ad-hoc projects with accounting and finance teams across the Company, from leases and fixed assets to inventory, intercompany, and tax provision support.

WHO YOU ARE:

* 10+ years of progressive accounting experience, including 5+ years in a supervisory or management role.
* Experience owning the close and statutory deliverables for multiple legal entities in more than one country.
* Experience leading an accounting team across more than one country, including the development of other managers.
* Experience working within a global organization reporting to a US parent; public accounting experience a plus.
* RA, ACA, ACCA, CPA, or equivalent certification preferred.
* Strong understanding of US GAAP and IFRS, with practical experience in local statutory reporting requirements across European jurisdictions; Middle East exposure a plus.
* Experience managing local statutory audits and external accounting or audit providers.
* Advanced Excel skills and proficiency with ERP systems (Oracle Fusion Cloud preferred).
* Excellent leadership, team management, and interpersonal skills, with a track record of hiring and developing accountants.
* Strong organizational and project management skills, with the ability to keep multiple jurisdictions and workstreams on schedule.
* Comfortable building process where little exists today, with a proven ability to manage competing priorities in a high growth organization.
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a mindset for continuous improvement.
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to work effectively with colleagues across time zones.
* Fluency in English; additional European languages a plus.
* Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
