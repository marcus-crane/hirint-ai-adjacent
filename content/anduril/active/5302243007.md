Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for a **Director of Supply Chain, Heavy Metal (VCS)** to join our leadership team in **Baltimore, MD**. In this role, you will be the end-to-end supply chain owner for the Virginia-class submarine supermodule program, architecting and executing the procurement, sourcing, and material flow strategies required to fuel the Baltimore shipyard. You will build and lead a high-performing team across strategic sourcing, subcontracts, procurement, material planning, and supplier quality, while working in lockstep with VCS Program Operations, Shipyard Construction, and Manufacturing Engineering. This role requires deep commercial acumen, command of heavy industrial and defense manufacturing supply bases, fluency in FAR/DFARS and EVMS-driven program controls, and a relentless bias for operational de-risking. If you are an aggressive, mission-focused operator who thrives in the yard as much as the boardroom and wants to fundamentally transform how naval vessels are built for the U.S. Navy, this role is for you.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Architect and execute the overarching supply chain and industrial base strategy for VCS supermodule construction, scaling the operation into a multi-billion-dollar annual production engine.
* Recruit, build, and lead an elite, yard-integrated supply chain organization spanning strategic sourcing, subcontracts management, procurement, and supplier quality engineering.
* Establish and secure resilient supply pipelines for specialized heavy naval commodities, including structural steels, heavy forgings, precision castings, complex piping systems, and submarine-grade electro-mechanical hardware.
* Partner closely with VCS Program Operations to integrate procurement commitments directly into the program master schedule and Earned Value Management System (EVMS) baselines.
* Drive aggressive risk-mitigation initiatives to eliminate single-source dependencies, secure critical long-lead raw materials, and resolve supplier capacity bottlenecks before they impact shipyard throughput.
* Lead the negotiation of high-value subcontracts, master service agreements (MSAs), and strategic partnership frameworks in strict compliance with U.S. Government procurement regulations.
* Collaborate with Heavy Metal Digital and Manufacturing Technology teams to establish real-time, digital material tracking from supplier facilities all the way into supermodule production bays.
* Maintain active, high-level interfaces with key external stakeholders—including the U.S. Navy, prime shipyard partners, and defense industrial base suppliers—to ensure programmatic alignment and transparent execution.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 10+ years of progressive supply chain, subcontracts, or strategic sourcing experience within heavy industrial manufacturing, defense shipbuilding, or aerospace programs.
* 5+ years of direct people-management experience building, scaling, and leading high-performing supply chain teams in demanding, high-growth environments.
* Demonstrated track record of negotiating and managing high-dollar defense subcontracts and complex agreements under FAR/DFARS regulations.
* Extensive experience managing long-lead raw materials, structural hardware, and heavy component manufacturing pipelines.
* Strong practical understanding of Earned Value Management Systems (EVMS) and how supply chain performance impacts schedule and cost variances (SV/CV).
* Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Industrial Engineering, Business Administration, or a related technical discipline (or equivalent practical experience).
* Willing and able to be based full-time on-site in Baltimore, MD, with regular travel to Costa Mesa, CA, and key vendor sites.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance.

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Direct experience supporting U.S. Navy shipbuilding programs (e.g., Virginia-class, Columbia-class, or surface combatants) and familiarity with NAVSEA technical requirements and quality standards.
* Experience standing up greenfield supply chain operations or leading the transition from facility standup into full-rate serial production.
* Familiarity with nuclear/subsea defense supply networks and rigorous quality frameworks (e.g., SUBSAFE-adjacent quality control standards).
* Advanced degree (MS, MBA) or industry credentials (e.g., APICS CSCP, CPIM, CPSM).

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
