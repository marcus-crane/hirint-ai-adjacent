Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are seeking a Telecommunications Infrastructure Analyst with a proven track record in IT infrastructure facility assessments, strategic planning, and structured cabling design standards. This role demands a strong ability to gather and evaluate comprehensive infrastructure requirements, including power, cooling, structured cabling, network capacity, and security. You will leverage your extensive experience to translate technical findings into actionable Statements of Work (SOWs), collaborating effectively with diverse stakeholders and third-party design teams. Your deep understanding of corporate IT standards, building physical networks, security policies, and vendor-agnostic network technologies will be crucial in ensuring reliable, scalable, and secure infrastructure solutions.

**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* You will establish and maintain the critical bridge between the Facilities organization and the external low-voltage design and integration vendors providing essential IT technical consultation to both parties to ensure successful technical outcomes.
* Conduct site visits to assess infrastructure needs, including power, cooling, structured cabling, network capacity, and security. Traveling will be required for this role.
* Develop technical site plans in collaboration with third-party architects, low-voltage design firms, and engineering teams.
* Ensure compliance with corporate IT standards and security policies.
* Gather comprehensive infrastructure requirements and conduct detailed evaluations.
* Translate complex technical details into actionable SOWs.
* Communicate and collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to ensure project success.
* Provide clear, concise, and opinionated guidance, and push back when necessary to ensure project alignment and success.
* Manage changes to project scope, schedule, and costs using appropriate verification techniques.

### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Minimum of 6 years of experience in a Network and/or Systems Infrastructure role.
* Minimum of 4 years of experience in infrastructure analysis, site build, and M&A assessments.
* Proven experience in gathering and evaluating infrastructure requirements.
* Strong ability to translate technical details into actionable SOWs.
* Strong understanding of construction phases and analyzing construction drawings
* Excellent communication and collaboration skills; capable of effectively working with diverse stakeholders.
* Strong understanding of corporate IT standards and security policies.
* Ability to conduct thorough site assessments (power, cooling, structured cabling, network capacity, security).
* Experience working with third-party architects, low-voltage design firms, and engineering teams.
* Extensive experience with vendor-agnostic network switching, wireless, and routing.
* Experience with ticketing systems and managing a ticket queue.
* Passion for technology and a team-focused attitude.
* Ability to learn new technologies quickly.
* Strong documentation skills.
* Strong problem-solving skills.
* Ability to travel to remote sites will often be required.
* Must be able and willing to lift 50 pounds.
* Able to obtain and maintain a US Secret Clearance.

### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Currently holds or can obtain a BICSI RCDD (Registered Communications Distribution Designer) Certification.
* Certification in project management or related fields.
* Experience in large-scale infrastructure projects which can contain multiple phases. This includes expansions, remodels, retrofits, and greenfield sites.
* Experience in designing telecom room layouts and cabling conveyance throughout a building.
* Familiarity with M&A processes and assessments.
* Knowledge of ICD 705 / TEMPEST requirements.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
