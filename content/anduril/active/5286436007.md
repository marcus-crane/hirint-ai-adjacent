Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for an Environmental Health and Safety (EHS) Manager to stand up and lead the EHS function at Arsenal-2, our new shipyard in Baltimore County, Maryland. This is an onsite role responsible for building the EHS management system for a facility that does not yet exist — writing the programs, securing the permits, and setting the standards that thousands of tradespeople will work to for decades.  
  
Arsenal-2 is the largest industrial commitment in Anduril's history, a multi-billion-dollar investment matched by a comparable commitment from the U.S. Navy. At full scale the facility will encompass roughly two million square feet of industrial space, a 1,200-foot graving dock, and approximately $900 million in machinery and production equipment. It will initially manufacture critical components for the Virginia-class submarine program and is designed to progress toward increasingly large and complex structures, as well as future maritime products and programs.  
  
As the senior EHS professional on site, you are the owner of record: you hold the permits, you set the standard, and you are the person regulators, the Navy, and Anduril leadership call when they have a question about how this facility is run. Your scope spans occupational safety, environmental permitting and compliance, industrial hygiene, chemical and regulated waste management, contractor safety, incident management, and emergency response. You will lead a small, senior team and grow it on a schedule tied to construction milestones and production ramp. This role is for someone who wants to build a system, not just manage one.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Own the development and implementation of the site's EHS management system from a blank page, adapting Anduril enterprise programs to 29 CFR 1915 Shipyard Employment, 29 CFR 1926 Construction, and 29 CFR 1910 General Industry
* Own the site environmental permit portfolio end to end — air construction and operating permits, potential-to-emit determinations and Title V applicability, wastewater and stormwater discharge authorizations, and hazardous waste generator status under 40 CFR Parts 260–279
* Lead permit applications directly, developing emissions inventories from equipment lists and SDS-level chemical data, coordinating stack and control-device specifications with engineering, and serving as the primary point of contact to the Maryland Department of the Environment
* Navigate split OSHA jurisdiction across the site — Maryland's state plan (MOSH) covers the construction and general industry scopes, while maritime and shipyard employment remain under federal OSHA — and own both relationships and the boundary between them
* Stand up the high-consequence programs first: confined and enclosed spaces in shipyard employment, hot work and fire safety including Marine Chemist and Competent Person coverage under NFPA 306, energy control, fall protection, cranes and rigging, and work over or adjacent to water
* Establish the industrial hygiene baseline for welding fume and manganese, hexavalent chromium, lead, abrasive blasting media, coatings and solvents, and noise — including the exposure assessment strategy and the medical surveillance program that follows from it
* Define and own the contractor safety standard governing demolition, heavy civil, and marine construction trades on site, including prequalification, orientation, and performance management
* Lead incident and near-miss investigations including root cause analysis, and drive corrective and preventive actions to closure across the site rather than in isolation
* Serve as the site's EHS interface and liaison with federal OSHA, MOSH, MDE, EPA Region 3, the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, the U.S. Coast Guard, Baltimore County, and U.S. Navy program stakeholders
* Meet Navy and prime-contract EHS obligations, including contract flow-down requirements and government safety requirements such as EM 385-1-1 on applicable scopes
* Own regulatory compliance documentation and recordkeeping — OSHA logs, environmental permits, monitoring and sampling records, waste manifests, and reporting deadlines — and keep the site audit-ready at all times
* Advise facility design and equipment selection while changes are still inexpensive: ventilation, means of egress, fire protection, crane envelopes, chemical storage, and waste handling
* Report EHS performance and leading/lagging indicators to site and functional leadership, and build the emergency response structure for a 24/7 industrial waterfront site
* Manage, coach, and develop EHS staff; forecast and justify EHS headcount against construction milestones and production ramp, and lead hiring for the roles you add

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
