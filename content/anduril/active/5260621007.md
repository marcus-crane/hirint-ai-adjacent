**ABOUT THE JOB**

As a Production Maintenance Technician at Arsenal 1, you will be the backbone of our manufacturing reliability. You will be responsible for ensuring maximum uptime, performance, and safety of all production assets. This is a multi-craft role requiring hands-on troubleshooting and repair across advanced automation, industrial robotics, automated conveyance, PLCs, and general mechanical machinery.

We are actively hiring across both **First and Second Shifts** for highly skilled technicians who thrive in fast-paced, high-tech manufacturing environments.

**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* **Equipment Commissioning, Line Setup & Integration**
  + New Line Installation: Assist with mechanical assembly, precision leveling, anchoring, routing, and drop-in placement of new production machinery.
  + Commissioning Support: Partner closely with internal engineers and external OEM/integrators during the start up of new production lines.
  + Baseline Establishment: Help build our maintenance infrastructure from scratch by assisting in creating new standard operating procedures (SOPs), spare parts inventories, and Preventative Maintenance (PM) schedules.
* **Automation, Robotics & Electrical**
  + Troubleshoot, program adjustments, and support industrial robotics, including teach pendant operation, mastering, calibration, and mechanical rebuilds.
  + Diagnose and troubleshoot Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs) to trace I/O, resolve hardware faults, and safely execute program resets.
  + Troubleshoot electrical components up to 480V 3-phase, including servo drives, Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs), sensors, proximity switches, and safety circuits.
  + Interpret electrical schematics, ladder logic, P&IDs, and technical equipment manuals.
* **Conveyance & General Mechanical Machinery**
  + Perform preventive and reactive maintenance on high-speed conveyance systems (belt, roller, chain, overhead), sorting systems, and automated transfer systems.
  + Diagnose, repair, and replace mechanical components including gearboxes, motors, drive assemblies, bearings, shafts, belts, and pulleys.
  + Troubleshoot and rebuild pneumatic and hydraulic systems (cylinders, valves, manifolds, regulators, and compressors).
  + Perform basic machining, fabrication, and welding repairs as needed to support production continuity.
* **Preventative Maintenance & Continuous Improvement**
  + Execute rigorous Preventative Maintenance (PM) schedules using our Computerized Maintenance Management System (CMMS) to document work orders, parts usage, and root-cause solutions.
  + Perform rapid root-cause failure analysis (RCFA) to prevent repeat breakdowns.
  + Actively participate in Total Productive Maintenance (TPM) and 5S initiatives.
  + Adhere strictly to Lockout/Tagout (LOTO), Arc Flash safety (NFPA 70E), OSHA regulations, and company safety standards.
  + Collaborate with cross-functional teams, which requires regular interaction with production and manufacturing stakeholders and frequently working directly on the production floor.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Minimum 3–5+ years of experience as an Industrial Maintenance Technician, Electro-Mechanical Technician, or Mechatronics Technician in a high-volume manufacturing environment.
* Demonstrated proficiency troubleshooting both mechanical and electrical systems.
* Hands-on experience with industrial automation, PLCs, and 6-axis robotics.
* Proven experience repairing complex automated conveyance systems.
* Strong knowledge of NFPA 70E, NEC standards, and safe work practices around 480V systems.
* Proficient in the use of standard hand/power tools, precision measurement equipment (calipers, micrometers, dial indicators), and electrical test equipment (multimeters, megohmmeters).
* Must be able to work the designated shift (1st or 2nd) and be available for occasional overtime or weekend support as production schedules require.
* U.S. citizenship is required pursuant to contractual obligations.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Associate degree or vocational certification in Mechatronics, Industrial Automation, Electrical Engineering Technology, or related discipline.
* Formal certifications in FANUC, KUKA, ABB, or Rockwell Automation/Allen-Bradley systems.
* Experience in high-reliability industries such as aerospace, defense, automotive, or high-speed packaging.
* MIG/TIG welding and manual mill/lathe machining experience.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
