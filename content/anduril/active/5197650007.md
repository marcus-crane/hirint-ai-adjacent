## About the Team

The Hardware Platform Organization is Anduril's central hardware engineering org. We build the shared technologies, tools, and infrastructure that power hardware development across every Anduril division — from actuators and propulsion to batteries, avionics, rocket motors, and test operations. This role sits in our Dublin office supporting the Voyager product line within Connected Warfare. Dublin is a growing hardware site operating at high rate production with frequent engineering change, and you will be the first dedicated configuration management hire there — joining at the point where the function is being stood up from scratch in parallel with a Siemens Teamcenter PLM rollout.

## About the Role

We are looking for a Configuration Management Engineer to own configuration management and change control for the Voyager hardware line. You will define and maintain product baselines, drive engineering change processes, and serve as the focal point for configuration identification, status accounting, and audits in a fast-paced design, engineering, and manufacturing environment.

This is a greenfield build. You will not be inheriting a mature framework — you will be writing the CM plan, defining the change control process, and standing up the forums and tooling that will carry Voyager through high-rate production. You will work alongside systems engineering, program management, and the broader Hardware Platform organisation, and partner closely with the global Configuration Management team on standards and tooling.

## What You'll Do

* Own end-to-end configuration management for the Voyager product line, including configuration identification, change control, status accounting, and audit activities.
* Define and maintain product baselines (requirements baseline, EBOM, MBOM, SBOM) in Teamcenter, migrating from existing Excel and Jabber-based tracking as part of the PLM rollout.
* Manage the engineering change lifecycle from initiation through implementation: document change requests, compile impact analyses, drive reviews through CCB/CRB, and track execution.
* Chair and facilitate configuration control forums — Change Control Boards and Technical Review Boards — setting agendas, running the meetings, and ensuring decisions are recorded, communicated, and closed out.
* Write the CM plan and supporting process documentation for the Dublin site from scratch, aligned with Anduril global standards.
* Lead and coordinate configuration audits (FCA/PCA), providing direction before, during, and after audit events to prevent and resolve issues.
* Partner with Configuration Management Analysts to build and maintain status accounting dashboards that track ECRs, ECNs, and implementation status.
* Provide guidance to engineering teams on Teamcenter workflows for engineering release, part revisions, BOM updates, and documentation modifications.
* Perform periodic audits of as-built records against product definition data to ensure configuration accuracy across the product lifecycle.

## Required Qualifications

* Bachelor's degree in an engineering discipline (Mechanical, Electrical, Aerospace, Manufacturing, or related field), or an equivalent combination of education and experience.
* 5+ years of direct product configuration management, engineering change management, or product data management experience — covering product structure, parts, drawings, and requirements management.
* Experience working in high-complexity, high-volume or high-rate production environments (e.g. aerospace, defence, automotive including EVs, medical devices, robotics, rail, or similar regulated hardware industries).
* Hands-on experience with a PLM and/or product data management toolset for complex hardware configuration (Teamcenter, Windchill, ARAS Innovator, SAP PLM, SolidWorks PDM, or equivalent).
* Familiarity with at least one established configuration or change management standard — for example EIA-649/C, MIL-HDBK-61B, AS9100, ISO 9001, ISO 10007, ISO 13485, or IEEE 828.
* Right to work in Ireland, or EU/UK citizenship. Candidates relocating from the EU/UK will be supported.
* Must be eligible to obtain and maintain Irish, UK, or allied national security clearances as required for the role.

## Preferred Qualifications

* Experience setting up or scaling a CM function in a greenfield, startup, or high-growth hardware environment.
* Hands-on experience with Teamcenter, including Teamcenter Requirements and Teamcenter Quality.
* Experience leading or chairing Change Control Boards, Technical Review Boards, or equivalent formal change governance.
* Experience managing EBOM-to-MBOM reconciliation in a high-rate production environment.
* Familiarity with hardware/software co-release — including embedded firmware baselines, version dependencies, and compatibility matrices.
* Proficiency in technical writing of configuration management processes and procedures.
* Confluence and Jira experience for process documentation and change workflow management.
* Database skills, including SQL queries for configuration data analysis.
* CM2 certification, or equivalent configuration management certification.
