Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### **ABOUT THE ROLE**

The Vice President, Global Material Movement & Warehousing provides strategic leadership and operational oversight for all warehousing and material movement activities across Anduril’s expanding multi-site footprint. This executive will own the end-to-end warehousing strategy, operating model, and organizational design required to support the company’s rapid production scale-up. The VP sets the global warehousing vision, makes resource allocation and capacity tradeoffs across sites, and owns the function’s cost structure and performance outcomes. This role is responsible for driving operational excellence and efficiency in the near term while building the warehousing capabilities necessary to support Anduril’s hyperscale growth and long-term business objectives.

### **KEY RESPONSIBILITIES**

* **Strategic Leadership**: Establish and provide strategic and operational leadership across multiple warehousing sites, ensuring consistency in safety, quality, cost, and delivery performance across geographies
* **Enterprise Strategy & Planning**: Define and execute a multi-year global warehousing and material movement strategy that directly enables Anduril’s production growth targets, translating company-level objectives into a scalable operating model
* **Organizational Leadership**: Build, scale, and lead a high-performing global warehousing organization spanning multiple sites and hundreds of team members; establish the leadership bench, operating cadence, and talent strategy to support rapid expansion
* **Capacity & Capital Strategy**: Drive function-level strategy including facility footprint decisions, capacity planning, capital deployment, automation investments, and workforce sizing aligned to multi-year business forecasts
* **Cost Management**: Own the full warehousing cost structure across all sites; identify and execute cost optimization initiatives while maintaining or improving service levels to production
* **Operational Excellence**: Establish the operational excellence framework and continuous improvement culture across the function, leveraging lean manufacturing, Six Sigma, or similar methodologies to reduce costs, improve accuracy, and increase throughput
* **Technology Vision & Innovation**: Define and own Anduril’s forward-looking technology vision for global material movement and warehousing. Actively scan the frontier of what’s possible — especially at the intersection of AI, robotics, and autonomy — and push Anduril to adopt (or build) best-in-class and novel solutions, not just proven ones. Shape both the hardware side (robotics, automation systems, autonomous material handling) and the software/systems side (AI-enabled planning, decision intelligence, modern WMS architectures)
* **Cross-Functional Collaboration**: Partner closely with Manufacturing, Procurement, Finance, Production Planning, and site leadership to ensure seamless, accurate, and efficient material flow across the enterprise
* **New Site Standup & Expansion**: Lead the warehousing strategy and execution for new site launches, ensuring operational readiness from day one as the company expands its manufacturing footprint
* **Performance Metrics**: Establish and monitor KPIs across safety, inventory accuracy, cycle time, cost per unit, and service level to ensure performance targets are met across all sites
* **Compliance & Safety**: Ensure compliance with all regulatory, environmental, health, and safety requirements; foster a safety-first culture across the organization
* **Executive Leadership**: Serve as a member of the Production senior leadership team, contributing to functional and corporate strategic planning; represent the warehousing organization in executive contexts as appropriate
* **Workforce & Location Strategy**: Lead workforce and location strategy, including labor market analysis, site selection criteria, and workforce sizing to support new facility stand-ups and geographic expansion

### **QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor’s degree in supply chain management, industrial engineering, operations management, business administration, or a related field; MBA or advanced degree preferred
* 15+ years of progressive experience in warehousing, distribution, or material movement operations, with at least 7-10 years in a senior or executive leadership role overseeing organizations of 200+ employees; experience in a high- or hyper-growth environment
* Proven track record of leading large, complex warehousing or fulfillment operations across multiple sites or locations
* Experience standing up greenfield warehouse operations or managing large-scale facility transitions required
* Demonstrated ability to set and scale an operational excellence framework, with deep expertise in continuous improvement methodologies
* Deep expertise in warehouse management systems, automation technologies, and emerging logistics technologies
* Prior experience managing large warehousing or distribution budgets and capital programs
* Direct cost structure ownership in a warehousing or distribution environment with demonstrated ability to manage financial performance at scale
* Executive presence and sophisticated communication skills; proven ability to influence senior leadership, represent the function externally, and shape strategic planning
* Excellent cross-functional collaboration skills with the ability to drive alignment across diverging stakeholder priorities
* Experience in manufacturing, defense, aerospace, or similarly complex production environments a plus
* Ability to travel domestically as needed
* Ability to relocate, if not already local, to be onsite in Costa Mesa, CA
* Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. TS or Secret clearance

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
