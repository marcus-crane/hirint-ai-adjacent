Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**ABOUT THE JOB**  
  
Anduril's Air Dominance & Strike (AD&S) Manufacturing Engineering organization is seeking a Project Manager to drive the planning, tracking, and operational rhythm of our Project Engineering team. This Project Manager will partner with the Project Engineers who execute MDP/PDP stage gate deliverables and cross-functional manufacturing engineering work across every AD&S program. The Project Manager is accountable for the scope, schedule, and budget of the projects the team is responsible for delivering.  
  
This is a high-leverage role with significant exposure and influence on the evolution of the production process. The Project Engineering team touches every program in the AD&S portfolio, and the Project Manager is the person who brings structure, visibility, and discipline to that work. You will own the project management apparatus: detailed scope definitions, schedules, CAPEX tracking, portfolio-level status comms, and the organizational systems that keep the manufacturing engineering function operating cleanly. You will also take on several adjacent operational workstreams critical to the health of the broader ME organization; program staffing and talent acquisition coordination, audits, and the confidential planning artifacts that support team growth.  
  
The ideal candidate is a high-performing individual contributor with a demonstrated ability to bring order to complexity, hold people accountable to commitments without having positional authority over them, and operate with discretion on sensitive personnel and organizational information. We are open to a range of backgrounds namely project management, program operations, engineering operations, consulting, or similar operational leadership roles. You will be an integral part the operational backbone of AD&S Manufacturing Engineering.  
  
**WHAT YOU'LL DO**  
  
Project Management for Manufacturing Engineering Projects

* Own accountability for scope, schedule, and budget across all Project Engineering team projects. The Project Engineers execute, but you are the one tracking commitments, surfacing risk, assisting the Project Engineers with cross-functional collaboration, planning, project management and communication.
* Build and maintain detailed scope definitions for every active project which requires clear, documented, and agreed to by stakeholders before execution begins.
* Build and maintain detailed, up-to-date project schedules in, including dependencies, critical path, and milestone tracking.
* Establish and run the project cadence with weekly status, stakeholder updates, risk reviews ensuring that the Project Engineering team has the visibility and support needed to deliver.
* Partner with Project Engineers to translate program and functional asks into structured project plans with clear deliverables, owners, and dates.

CAPEX & IRAD Tracking and Portfolio Communication

* Own detailed tracking of AD&S Manufacturing Engineering CAPEX with approvals, committed spend, actuals, and remaining balance by project, by quarter, and by fiscal year. Building a strong working relationship with program managers, finance, and divisional operations.
* Maintain the CAPEX and IRAD dashboard as the single source of truth for manufacturing engineering execution status that is ready to produce on demand for leadership reference and reviews.
* Prepare and maintain portfolio-level documentation that records investment justification and DOA approvals.

Hiring & Onboarding Operations Support

* Support hiring operations for the organization in partnership with the broader recruiting function by helping track open roles, coordinate interview logistics, and keep the overall process moving.
* Help coordinate interview panels across the engineering team, supporting consistent coverage and a positive new hire onboarding experience ensuring they have what they need to be successful in their first 45 days.
* Partner with recruiting on pipeline tracking, offer status visibility, and periodic process check-ins.
* Maintain the organization's job description library, ensuring open roles have approved, up-to-date postings.

Operational Infrastructure and Data Hygiene

* Run periodic data hygiene checks across the operational systems the team relies on, ensuring staffing, allocation, and reporting data are accurate and up to date.
* Partner with managers and program leads to confirm that team assignments, charge codes, and headcount data reflect reality.
* Maintain planning artifacts that support team growth and organizational design, working closely with leadership on structure and sequencing.
* Handle sensitive personnel and organizational planning information with complete discretion.

Cross-Functional Partnership

* Build strong working relationships with Program Management, Chief Engineering, Finance, People Operations, and talent aquisition as the Project Manager is the manufacturing engineering function's operational liaison across every one of these groups.
* Drive accountability and commitments across teams and individuals who do not report to you, using influence, follow-through, and credibility rather than positional authority.
* Travel to other Anduril sites as needed to support project reviews and cross-site coordination (~10%, depending on business needs).

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
