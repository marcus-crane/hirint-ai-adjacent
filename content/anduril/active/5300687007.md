### ABOUT THE JOB

Anduril is looking for an Avionics Systems Engineer with experience in high-reliability space electronics to join our team in Costa Mesa, CA. The role spans electronics oversight, processing-architecture definition, manufacturability, power and thermal analysis, test, validation and integration, aligning each against system mission requirements.  
  
The Avionics Systems Engineer is as comfortable in a schematic review as in a fab/assembly process discussion or a radiation test report walkthrough, and partners with mechanical, thermal, software/FW/RTL and procurement teams to drive PCBA designs from concept through bring-up, qualification and delivery of flight hardware.

#### **WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Provide system-level oversight of PCBA design and manufacturability (DFM, SI/PI, IPC) and of fabrication and assembly process controls (reflow profiles, solder-joint reliability, polymerics, outgassing, cleanliness).
* Define processing architecture: generate trade-study criteria and translate functional requirements into compute, thermal and power budgets.
* Stay current on MCUs, processors, FPGA/SoC options and high-speed sensor interfaces (SerDes, SLVS-EC, Ethernet, fiber Ethernet) to inform component selection and the product roadmap.
* Review SW/FW/RTL repositories at the architecture level to understand how software drives board function, extract actionable FDIR responses and identify feature requests.
* Own digital readout and sensor-interface chains; author and maintain interface control documents and functional requirements.
* Interpret thermal, power, structural and mechanical analysis (random vibration, shock, thermal cycling, CTE/solder-joint fatigue) and translate implications into PCBA and mounting/staking decisions.
* Drive component selection and procurement: pedigree, screening, derating, rapid alternate sourcing and the trade-offs between COTS, radiation-tolerant and rad-hard parts.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* BS in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Aerospace, Physics or a related field, or equivalent hands-on experience.
* Typically 5+ years of related space or high-reliability electronics experience.
* Experience participating in module, unit or system-level integration and test campaigns.
* Strong electronics fundamentals: ability to read a schematic, reason about power and thermal budgets and follow component selection.
* Working familiarity with compute architectures: resource estimation, power utilization, thermal load handling and data movement.
* Understanding of component pedigree, screening and derating, the distinctions between COTS, radiation-tolerant and rad-hard parts, and the radiation effects (TID, SEE, displacement damage) that drive those choices.
* Baseline grasp of PCBA fabrication steps (reflow, solder-joint reliability, cleanliness).
* Willingness to get hands-on at the part, process and test level, and to work across disciplines.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. security clearance.

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Experience owning a processing architecture end to end: partitioning algorithms, validating signal-chain performance and carrying designs from schematic through bring-up, HITL, DITL and delivery.
* Deeper knowledge of MCUs, processors and FPGA/SoCs: signal-chain partitioning across heterogeneous compute, latency response and precision timing.
* Experience authoring interface control documents and defining sensor interfaces and digital-readout chains.
* Experience authoring or deeply reviewing embedded flight software, RTL and OS/sensor interaction.
* Ability to derive TID, SEE and displacement-damage estimates from orbital environment analysis; experience with shielding and transport tools and heavy-ion/TID test campaigns.
* Hands-on experience with PCBA layout and simulation for space electronics.
