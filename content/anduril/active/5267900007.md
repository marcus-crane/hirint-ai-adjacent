### ABOUT THE JOB

As a Product Support Engineer at Anduril, you will be a critical individual contributor within our rapidly growing Maritime team. You will drive the development and implementation of comprehensive sustainment strategies for cutting-edge defense products, directly contributing to their operational readiness and longevity. This is an impactful role where you will take ownership of defining critical maintenance plans, shaping technical data packages, optimizing sparing, and ensuring seamless lifecycle management. Your expertise will be vital in translating engineering innovation into reliable, supportable systems for our warfighters, directly enabling Anduril’s mission to transform defense technology. You will own product support analysis and sustainment cost for a fleet of maritime autonomous vehicles. Reporting to the Support System Lead, you will build the life cycle cost model, run the analysis that determines how the fleet gets supported, and turn reliability and maintenance data into the provisioning, manning, and cost picture the program and the customer need. Sustainment is where the majority of a platform's lifetime cost lands, and this role owns understanding it before those costs are locked in by design decisions.  
  
Your spectrum of support will be diverse in nature and require you to leverage your technical expertise as well as leadership skills to set objectives, build cross-functional teams, and rapidly drive to completion. The ability to leverage your intuition and prior experience will be key in making sure the right design, analysis, and test steps are completed to ensure success. The ideal candidate will leverage their experience executing and successfully completing prior highly optimized multi-disciplinary projects.

### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* Develop and maintain comprehensive, ground-up sustainment plans for the specific product(s) you support
* Partner with customers, operations teams, and cross-functional engineering teams to define, implement, and continuously improve effective maintenance and repair strategies
* Ensure the availability of up-to-date maintenance procedures, specialized tools, and accurate technical manuals to support field sustainment operations
* Serve as the primary point of contact for all sustainment-related matters within your product team, proactively identifying and resolving issues
* Collaborate with design and manufacturing engineering teams during new product development phases to integrate best sustainment practices and design-for-supportability concepts early in the process
* Create and maintain a Service Bill of Materials (SBOM) and other critical sustainment artifacts (e.g., provisioning lists, support equipment lists), ensuring they establish a central, up-to-date repository for all sustainment data
* Supply the support terms of the operational availability basis, meaning logistics delay and preventive maintenance time, alongside the reliability lead who owns the reliability allocations.
* Build and maintain the life cycle cost estimate and the operating and support cost model, with sensitivity analysis and affordability reporting, and publish the estimate at program gates.
* Build and own the life cycle cost model for the vehicle fleet and its support system.
* Perform product support analysis covering provisioning, manning, facilities, and support equipment.
* Produce operating and support (O&S) cost estimates and keep them current as the design matures.
* Run cost trade studies on sustainment approaches and feed results into design decisions.
* Develop spares and provisioning plans in partnership with RAM and Maintenance.
* Define the product support strategy and supporting elements of the logistics architecture.
* Produce cost and supportability analysis for customer deliverables and program reviews.
* Establish the data pipeline that converts reliability and maintenance data into cost projections.
