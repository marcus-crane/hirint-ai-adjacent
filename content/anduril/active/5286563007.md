**About the job**  
  
Anduril’s Ground Maneuver business line integrates mission systems — command and control, sensors, and effectors — onto the ground maneuver platforms the Department of War’s close-combat and Special Operations formations fight from. These capabilities move from prototype to fielded system on timelines the sector has not seen before, and they are judged by the Soldiers, Marines, and Special Operators who crew, drive, and fight the platforms they ride on.  
  
As Senior Director, Program Director — Ground Maneuver, you will own the development and deployment of this portfolio in partnership with leadership, engineering, and manufacturing, leading a team of program managers across multiple concurrent programs. You will own cost, schedule, risk, and performance outcomes, and you will be the senior relationship lead for Army, Marine Corps, and Special Operations program offices, test organizations, and the operational units they equip. We are looking for an operator who understands business: someone with the credibility that comes from having served, the judgment to make hard cost, schedule, and commercial trade-offs, and the executive presence to represent Anduril to service leadership, program executive offices, and allied partners. This role lives at the customer interface, and it goes where the equipment goes — including austere, forward, and contested operational environments such as Ukraine, the Middle East, and the Indo-Pacific. You will both lead and execute.  
  
**What You’ll Do**

* In partnership with leadership, engineering, and manufacturing teams, own the development and deployment of Anduril’s ground maneuver mission systems — command and control, sensors, and effectors integrated onto manned and unmanned ground platforms — to meet the operational needs of the Department of War.
* Own the integration of command and control, communications, computing, sensors, and effectors onto ground maneuver platforms, both Anduril-built and government- or partner-furnished — driving interface definitions, SWaP-C budgets, spectrum and EMI deconfliction, and open-architecture compliance so the system fields as one capability rather than a set of subsystems.
* Lead a team of program managers who will develop and adhere to strict schedules, cost constraints, and regulatory requirements, with an emphasis on coordinating between multiple hardware production and platform integration teams, managing supply chains, and ensuring that quality, environmental qualification, and delivery commitments are met.
* Develop strong, trusting relationships with Anduril’s partners and DoW customers, including Army, Marine Corps, and Special Operations program offices (USSOCOM SOF AT&L and its component commands) and the operational units they equip, ensuring we have tight feedback loops that enable us to shape solutions, rapidly iterate, and ultimately field solutions faster.
* Autonomously execute on broad and/or ambiguous requirements from internal engineering, customers, and third-party corporate partners to own successful delivery of outcomes on multiple programs simultaneously.
* Develop and implement lightweight processes, tools, and metrics for program planning, scope management, risk management, and customer communication to enable Anduril to move rapidly.
* Own the program’s path through government test and evaluation — soldier and operator touchpoints, mobility and live-fire trials, SOF user assessments, and environmental qualification — and convert operator feedback into design and production changes on short cycles.
* Go where the equipment goes. Travel to austere, forward, and contested operational environments — including Ukraine, the Middle East, the Indo-Pacific, and allied training areas — to run user assessments and demonstrations, support deployed and forward-stationed units, and carry firsthand operator observations back into design, production, and program strategy.
* Be high ownership on all pieces of work and become a trusted partner and advisor to Anduril’s business leaders and engineers.
* Foster a creative, high-performance culture within the team, and proactively assess team skills versus needs, identifying personnel development and/or recruitment opportunities to strengthen the team’s capabilities.
* Collaborate with finance and business development teams to develop proposal responses describing technical solutions and novel pricing strategies that set Anduril up for long-term financial and operational success.
* Partner with business development and capture teams to grow the Ground Maneuver portfolio — shaping requirements and program strategy with conventional and Special Operations program offices, other USG stakeholders, and the operational units they equip, and identifying the contract vehicles, rapid-acquisition authorities, transition paths, and funding lines that turn prototypes into programs of record.
* Partner with Business Line leadership and adjacent Anduril businesses and support organizations to grow the business with allied and partner nations, with emphasis on the Indo-Pacific and Europe — supporting international demonstrations, coalition exercises such as those run across the Pacific theater, and user assessments, and working with capture, trade compliance, and engineering teams on exportable configurations and FMS/DCS pursuits that extend Anduril’s ground maneuver mission systems to allied formations and their platforms.

* Familiarity with ground platform qualification and safety release processes, including MIL-STD-810 environmental and MIL-STD-461 EMI testing.
* Personal deployment or forward-operating experience in austere or contested environments, with comfort working alongside U.S., allied, and partner-nation formations with minimal support.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
