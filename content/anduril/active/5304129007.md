Anduril is seeking a Staff MRB Engineer to join our Fury Team in Columbus, Ohio. The Staff MRB Engineer will serve as the foremost technical authority for nonconformance disposition and Material Review Board operations, driving enterprise-level strategy for nonconformance management, quality risk mitigation, and production continuity. This role is responsible for establishing the MRB frameworks, delegation-of-authority structures, and engineering standards that scale across programs and facilities. The Staff MRB Engineer will resolve the most complex and consequential disposition decisions, develop the analytical methodologies the MRB organization relies on, and serve as the senior technical liaison between production operations, design engineering, quality, and external customers.  
  
WHAT YOU'LL DO

* Serve as the final technical authority on the most complex and high-risk nonconformance dispositions — including structural, flight-critical, and system-level deviations that impact airworthiness or mission capability.
* Architect and own the MRB process framework: delegation-of-authority structures, disposition criteria, escalation paths, and documentation standards that scale across our Fury program.
* Lead enterprise-level root cause analysis on systemic nonconformance trends, driving corrective actions that eliminate recurring quality escapes across the production system.
* Develop and maintain the engineering analysis methodologies (stress, damage tolerance, functional assessment) that the MRB organization uses to evaluate nonconformances.
* Establish nonconformance metrics, risk models, and reporting frameworks that provide leadership with actionable visibility into quality health and production risk.
* Drive strategic process improvements based on nonconformance trend data, audit findings, and customer feedback — targeting systemic reductions in escape rates and disposition cycle times.
* Represent Anduril's MRB capability to external customers, regulatory bodies, and government quality representatives; serve as the primary technical contact for customer-witnessed MRB activities.
* Collaborate with design engineering leadership to influence design standards and tolerancing practices that reduce nonconformance rates at the source.
* Develop and maintain strategic relationships with vendor design authorities for disposition of complex supplier-designed components and assemblies.
* Mentor and develop the MRB engineering team across all levels; establish competency models, training curricula, and delegation frameworks that scale the organization's disposition capacity.
* Lead quality system audit preparation and response for MRB-related findings; drive closure of corrective actions and ensure continuous compliance with AS9100 and program-specific requirements.
* Create knowledge management systems that capture critical disposition precedents, engineering rationale, and lessons learned for organizational reference.

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 12+ years of experience in engineering roles with significant depth in MRB, nonconformance management, or dispositions engineering within aerospace or defense manufacturing.
* Deep expertise in MRB processes, delegation-of-authority frameworks, and nonconformance disposition for complex aerospace structures and systems.
* Strong command of engineering analysis techniques relevant to disposition (stress analysis, damage tolerance, functional assessment).
* Proven ability to resolve the most complex disposition decisions involving flight-critical or system-level nonconformances.
* Track record of developing MRB process frameworks, standards, or methodologies that have been adopted at an organizational level.
* Excellent communication skills with the ability to present complex technical rationale to executive leadership, customers, and regulatory bodies.
* Deep knowledge of quality management systems (QMS) and standards (AS9100, ISO 9001) as they pertain to nonconformance management.
* Must be able to obtain and hold a U.S. security clearance.

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Previous MRB chairperson or MRB authority designee experience.
* Experience establishing MRB operations for a new production program or facility.
* Background in structural or stress analysis engineering.
* Experience with aircraft production lines at rate, including high-volume disposition environments.
* Familiarity with government quality requirements (DCMA, MIL-STD, FAR/DFAR).
* Experience interfacing with DCMA QARs or customer quality representatives on MRB activities.
* Lean Six Sigma certification (Green Belt or higher).
* Experience developing engineering training programs or competency frameworks.

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
