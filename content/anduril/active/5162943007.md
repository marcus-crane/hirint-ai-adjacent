### ABOUT THE JOB

As an Inventory Specialist, you will join our Mission Readiness team at the Santa Ana, CA office. The Mission Readiness team has a valuable impact on Anduril's business and mission, responsible for supporting our production and business growth through material management and inventory flow. In this role you own the accuracy and integrity of physical and system inventory — running cycle counts, reconciling against the warehouse management system, and driving discrepancies to root cause before they reach the production floor. The right person for this role can demonstrate strong ownership and the ability to solve operational challenges with creative solutions in a fast-paced, resource-limited environment. This person will be flexible to provide support wherever needed to ensure material moves accurately and on schedule under stringent quality standards.   
  
**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Own inventory accuracy across physical and system records at the Santa Ana and/or Costa Mesa locations
* Perform daily, weekly, and scheduled cycle counts, and support wall-to-wall and annual physical inventory counts
* Investigate inventory discrepancies and drive them to root cause using system transactions, documentation, and physical investigation
* Reconcile physical inventory against WMS/ERP records and execute inventory adjustments in accordance with established controls and approval processes
* Execute accurate inventory transactions — bin-to-bin movements, transfers, reservations, and unreservations — and ensure every material movement is supported by the appropriate Transfer Order (TO), work order, or other required documentation
* Trace material movements from receiving through storage, WIP, deployment, and outbound activity
* Maintain accurate bin locations, material master information, and clearly defined inventory locations
* Identify misplaced, unidentified, excess, damaged, or otherwise discrepant material and resolve it with the responsible team
* Support material segregation and control for high-value, sensitive, or deployment-critical inventory
* Support receiving, put-away, picking, staging, shipping, and material movement activities as needed
* Operate forklifts, pallet jacks, and other material handling equipment when properly trained and authorized
* Partner with Materials, Production, Deployment, and Supply Chain to develop inventory control procedures, standard work, and KPI reporting
* Identify system and process gaps that create inventory inaccuracies or operational inefficiencies, and drive corrective action
* Escalate significant inventory risks or trends to leadership  
    
  **REQUIRED QUALIFICATION**
* 2+ years in inventory control, warehouse operations, materials management, or an equivalent role
* Experience performing cycle counts and investigating inventory discrepancies
* Experience working with a Warehouse Management System (WMS), ERP, or equivalent inventory control tooling
* Must be able to work 1st or 2nd shift and overtime as needed
* Valid driver's license
* Ability to be onsite, local to Santa Ana, CA or Costa Mesa, CA  
   

  #### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience working in the defense/aerospace industry
* Familiarity with Odoo, SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, or similar ERP/WMS platforms
* Familiarity with inventory management through lot/serial/batch tracking
* Experience with barcode scanners and warehouse technology
* Experience developing or improving warehouse SOPs and standard work
* Experience working in a high-volume or high-value inventory environment

* Ability to prioritize tasks efficiently and effectively

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
