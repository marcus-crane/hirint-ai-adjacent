Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Manufacturing Production team is responsible for a wide range of activities that are crucial to the manufacturing process including operating equipment, assembling products, performing quality control checks, and maintaining a safe and efficient work environment. The Manufacturing Training and Development team plays a critical role in equipping employees with the skills, knowledge, and resources needed to excel in their roles. They design, develop, and deliver training programs that enhance technical proficiency, ensure compliance, and support career growth within the organization.   
This position offers an opportunity to contribute to a growing training function while enhancing our employee’s training experiences.

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are seeking a Technician Trainer to join the team to develop and deliver training programs for our Manufacturing teams supporting our Cutting Edge Production Lines at Arsenal (Ohio). This role is essential to building a skilled workforce that adheres to safety protocols, quality standards, and operational efficiency. The ideal candidate has a strong background as a technician or technician trainer in a manufacturing environment, and is skilled at translating technical processes into accessible and effective training.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Deliver in-person, on-the-job, and classroom-style training for new hires and technicians **across multiple product lines**

* Support the development and maintenance of training content (including job aides), focusing on essential technical skills, equipment operation, quality practices/processes, and safety procedures
* Conduct and facilitate hands-on, practical training sessions directly on the production floor, guiding technicians through real life scenarios and troubleshooting exercises to reinforce skills
* Enforce the development and standardization of skills matrices and on the job training documentation
* Create and continuously improve training materials, including instructional design guides, standard operating procedures, and other resources tailored to manufacturing
* Collaborate with SME’s, production leadership, stakeholders within manufacturing to inform learning objectives and content requirement, and ensure content aligns with operational goals and compliance and safety standards.
* Coordinate with external vendors and suppliers as needed for specialized training or equipment
* Ensuring all programs follow central Manufacturing Training and Development guidelines
* Support the development of standardized record keeping processes, attendance records, competency assessments and progress reports to track training effectiveness and compliance
* Support learning administration, including scheduling, logistics, setup, and evaluation
* Partner with instructional designers and LMS Professionals to ensure content is up to date and relevant
* Instill a strong culture of safety first and adherence to protocols in all training activities
* Responsible for assigned training areas and act as SME to support those areas

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 5+ years of experience as a technician or technician trainer in a production or manufacturing environment
* **A self-starter who is energetic, driven, and always looking for opportunities for continuous improvement**
* Strong technical knowledge of manufacturing equipment and tooling, standard manufacturing and assembly practices, and safety standards
* Strong communication and facilitation skills; able to coach and explain complex processes clearly
* Proficiency with Microsoft Office and other documentation tools
* Comfortable using or learning LMS and digital tools for training documentation
* Effective organizational skills to manage training schedules, materials, records, and documentation
* Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, rapidly changing work environment
* Driven, motivated, and committed to continuous improvement
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export controlled information or facilities

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Familiarity with adult learning principles or train the trainer certifications
* Training Certifications such as Certified IPC Trainer (to include J-STD-001 CIT, IPC-A-610/620 CIT, IPC-7711/7721 CIT)

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
