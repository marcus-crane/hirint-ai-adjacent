Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

The Advanced Capabilities team at Anduril develops operationally relevant wargaming and training to customers in multiple regions. This position is in a new simulation center in Abu Dhabi to provide training and simulation services to partners and customers in the region.

**ABOUT THE JOB**

We are looking for a **Wargame Designer** to join our growing team in **Abu Dhabi, UAE**. Initial role will be in **London, UK**, while the simulation center is being constructed. In this role, you will:

**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Research, design, and develop operationally relevant wargames within the Air Maneuver Dominance / Advanced Capabilities team, working directly with EDGE and the Emirati Ministry of Defense to translate customer objectives and operational problems into structured, immersive wargames built in Anduril’s own proprietary modeling and simulation tools and Augmented Reality / Virtual Reality software. You will own scenario design end-to-end, including operational context, orders of battle, friendly and adversary forces, objectives, timelines, victory conditions, and the analytic questions each event is meant to answer.
* Run and facilitate in-depth multi-day Wargaming Exercises, serving as the designer and control cell lead for planning, preparation, execution, and recovery. You will drive game play, adjudicate outcomes, manage friendly and opposition moves and injects, and adapt scenarios in real time to keep events challenging, credible, and aligned to customer learning objectives.
* Build and maintain a reusable library of wargame scenarios, models, and content in Anduril M&S and AR/VR software, refining and improving processes across our Software, Hardware, Logistics, Growth, and Engineering teams so that events can be rapidly configured, repeated, and scaled from small demonstrations to large exercises without loss of fidelity.
* Design and implement structured After-Action Reviews and user feedback loops to gather, triage, and prioritize insights, requirements, and challenges surfaced during wargames, exercises, and daily corporate demo use, and turn those findings into concrete improvements to scenarios, adjudication logic, and event delivery.
* Provide input to product and engineering teams to drive the development of features, models, and configurations that enhance or greatly improve Anduril’s wargaming, mission simulation, and Virtual Warfare products while you advocate for the tools and content designers need to build better wargames faster.
* Support end-users in understanding advanced warfare concepts through the development of relentlessly improving wargame content, integration into existing Anduril and customer systems, and delivery of effective events that maximize engagement and learning while minimizing the impact of hardware or software issues on the experience.
* Develop and contribute to an Advanced Capabilities team that includes cross-functional representation from Project and Program Managers, Operations Analysts, Mission Simulation Engineers, Wargaming Engineers, Design/Artists, senior Air Maneuver Dominance team and corporate leadership, other business lines, as well as customers. You will be critical to ensure the team continues to improve, refine, and streamline how wargames are designed, delivered, and learned from across both small-scale demos and large-scale exercises and events.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Proven experience designing, developing, and running wargames, structured analytic exercises, or operational training scenarios across a diverse set of clients, teams, roles, and operational environments while providing excellent customer service to the Air Maneuver Dominance business line (primary) as well as additional business lines (Secondary).
* Demonstrated comfort communicating effectively both internally and externally: on the team, as well as across Anduril’s many other business lines, software platforms, organizations and clients.
* Experience with scenario design, complex problem solving, proactive planning, rehearsals, and troubleshooting to ensure a seamless, transparent customer experience. Successful teammates have demonstrated a level of ‘healthy paranoia’ that borders on OCD-level attention to detail and obsession with ensuring the wargame plausible, repeatable, controllable, and without breaking, before putting customers inside Anduril’s immersive wargaming and VR/AR world.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain associated country background check and security clearance to support increasingly complex and potentially classified exercises.
* Willingness to travel, be based in the UAE, and participate in collaboration with teammates in the UK and USA.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience designing and building wargames or simulations in government or civilian wargaming or simulation software such as AFSIM, SIMDIS, DCS, or comparable constructive, virtual, or gaming-engine platforms.
* Experience and familiarity with military operations, tactics, techniques, procedures, and hierarchies for both friendly and adversarial forces.
* Familiarity with Confluence, Jira, Slack, Quip, MS Office and Zoom as means to organize data and communicate across our teams effectively.
* Experience building trust with clients and teammates quickly and effectively under sometimes stressful conditions.
* Prior military or government experience, particularly in wargaming, operational planning, red teaming, or exercise design.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
