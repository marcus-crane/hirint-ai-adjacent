Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**ABOUT THE JOB**

We are looking for a Technical Project Manager to join our rapidly growing team in Costa Mesa, CA. You will work with the Machine Vision team to coordinate the deployment and sustainment of new tools, processes, equipment, and capabilities across the company. You will be involved in the scoping, delivery, and impact of every machine vision project. That means routine engagement with product lines, partnering with the Machine Vision manager on the breakdown and allocation of assignments, pulling in cross-functional teams across Anduril, and staying with a system after it is installed to both measure its impact and support modifications and continuous improvement efforts. To be effective in this job you must be ambitious and high agency, organized and detail oriented, a quick learner and a concise communicator, fiscally intelligent, and humble.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* **Discovery and Scoping:** Engage with product lines to evaluate their pain points, failure matrices, and current inspection and rework burden. Quantify the baseline (escape rate, inspection hours, scrap and rework cost) so that every deployment has a measurable “why” and a defensible return before engineering time is committed.
* **Requirements and Technical Documentation:** Work closely with machine vision engineers to develop the technical design documentation for a solution, including requirements, acceptance criteria, integration constraints, and the test plan that proves the system performs before it is handed to the floor.
* **Scope and Expectation Management:** Forecast scope creep and ensure the product line understands the capabilities and limits of a solution both now and as their design and process change.
* **Schedule and Delivery Ownership:** Manage delivery dates of machine vision deployments across several concurrent projects. Build schedules that respect lead times on optics and hardware, engineering capacity, and the factory downtime windows available to you. Routinely communicate hurdles, risks, and milestones to both Automation management and the product lines you support.
* **Cross Functional Engagement:** Own the engagement required to deliver machine vision solutions to the factory floor, working across manufacturing and quality engineering, controls, IT and infrastructure, facilities, and the product lines themselves.
* **Deployment and Sustainment:** Drive installation, acceptance testing, and operator training. Stand up the documentation, spares, and escalation paths that keep a system working, and feed recurring failures back into the team’s roadmap.
* **Scale the Function:** Turn individual deployments into a repeatable playbook of intake, prioritization criteria, documentation templates, and deployment metrics, so the team can serve more product lines without adding a coordinator for every project.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **Please always verify communications:**

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
