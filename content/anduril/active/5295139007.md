Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**ABOUT THE JOB**   
  
We are looking for a **Logistics Program Manager** to join our rapidly growing team in **Costa Mesa.** In this role, you will be responsible for owning the Integrated Product Support (IPS) strategy and execution for one of Anduril’s Autonomous Airpower Business Line programs.   
The Logistics Program Manager is the single accountable owner for Integrated Product Support for a program from design through end of life, and the primary logistics interface to the customer’s Product Support team. You will define the support and logistics constructs based on customer and organizational requirements, own customer relationships, manage large, cross-functional efforts, and establish standards, processes, and pathways where none exist. 

This role requires a working knowledge of the 12 IPS elements and demands someone equally fluent in traditional acquisition logistics and modern sustainment practices—comfortable authoring an ILSP and executing a logistics support strategy for a deployment in the same week. 

You will need to balance direct team management and collaborative cross-functional relationships to deliver results directly to internal and government customers.   
   
   
**WHAT YOU’LL DO** 

* **Own the Product Support Strategy:** Define, build, and implement the IPS and Sustainment strategy for your program.

* **Drive Through Ambiguity:** Establish clear planning assumptions amid shifting requirements, navigate policies, and make decisions without waiting for perfect data.

* **CDRL Ownership:** Own development and delivery of the sustainment CDRL suite—ILSP, LORA, Provisioning Technical Documentation, Sparing Analysis, Technical Manuals, Contract Field Service Reports, Transition Support Plan—ensuring regulatory compliance, on-time submittal, and government acceptance.

* **Own Tech Data Development:** Manage the cross-functional team through development, delivery, and maintenance of logistics product data, technical data, technical manuals, and training.

* **Lead Product Support Execution:** Establish and manage execution across areas including spares provisioning, PHS&T operations, repair-and-return pipelines, and Deployment Operations.

* **Cost & Performance:** Build O&S cost estimates and BOEs for proposals. Track program schedule, cost, and performance and manage key Product Support Technical Performance Measurements.

* **Leadership & Customer Interface:** Manage the logistics team and serve as the primary voice to government stakeholders. Manage internal and external meetings and conferences.

* **Process Creation & Hands-On Execution:** Build sustainment SOPs from scratch, proactively solve ambiguous problems, and step in to unblock fielding timelines. Embrace startup life.

#### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor’s degree in engineering, business/operations, or a related field. Equivalent experience may be substituted in lieu of education.

* 7+ years of progressively complex experience in acquisition logistics, product support, or sustainment program management supporting a DoW environment or comparable government programs. Relevant experience includes lifecycle logistics, Integrated Logistics Support (ILS), Integrated Product Support (IPS), or Product Support Manager (PSM) roles.

* Demonstrated ownership of a product support strategy spanning multiple IPS elements on a DoD or comparable government acquisition program.

* Working knowledge of all 12 Integrated Product Support elements: Product Support Management, Design Interface, Sustaining Engineering, Supply Support, Maintenance Planning & Management, Packaging Handling Storage & Transportation (PHS&T), Technical Data / Technical Publications, Support Equipment, Training & Training Support, Manpower & Personnel, Facilities & Infrastructure, and Computer Resources.

* Direct experience authoring and delivering logistics CDRLs against DIDs, and negotiating sustainment scope with a government customer.

* Experience working in or with an Interim Contractor Support (ICS) or Contractor Logistics Support (CLS) construct.

* Experience building and leading a logistics or product support team, including hiring and developing junior logisticians.

* Strong communication, leadership, presentation, and interpersonal skills sufficient to build relationships and influence customers, cross-functional groups, and all levels of leadership.

* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. security clearance.

* Willingness and ability to travel up to 30%.

 

#### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Prior service as an ILS, IPS, or Sustainment Manager, Lifecycle Logistician, or Product Support Manager on a DoW acquisition program of record.

* Experience standing up or managing an Interim Contractor Support (ICS) or Contractor Logistics Support (CLS) construct.

* Familiarity with USAF or other DoW customer sustainment processes.

* Government proposal and business case analysis experience, including BOE development.

* Experience at a startup, a new program start, or an organization scaling rapidly—or as the first or only logistician supporting a program.

* Active U.S. security clearance.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
