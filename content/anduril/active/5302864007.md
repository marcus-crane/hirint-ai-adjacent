**About the Job**  
  
We are seeking a Manufacturing Optimization Specialist to support the development and deployment of the Anduril Production System (APS) to drive continuous improvement and operational excellence across our manufacturing sites in the US. This role is the on-the-ground force multiplier for the APS team: you will work closely with the Manufacturing Optimization Team and site production leadership to build, deploy, and sustain the physical and digital systems that lean manufacturing runs on – metric boards, standard work, visual management, kanban, and document control.  
  
The ideal candidate will have a hands-on background in manufacturing operations and will have spent time working on a shop floor. The candidate should have a working knowledge and application of 5S, visual management, standard work, and metric board tracking, along with the organizational discipline to own a document library, a physical inventory of consumables, and a deployment checklist end-to-end in a fast-paced manufacturing setting. You will be responsible for making sure every APS pilot launches with every artifact in place on day one, and for keeping the team’s standard tools and templates current, controlled, and accessible to every site. This is an excellent opportunity for a detail-driven manufacturing professional ready to make an impact through hands-on execution alongside the people building the product. Skill in building digital tools and electronic databases – dashboards, trackers, and simple apps that replace manual record-keeping – is a plus.  
  
A successful candidate will be expected to work out of Arsenal-1. Travel up to 50% may be required during pilot deployments.  
  
**What You'll Do**

1. Build the physical artifacts of APS. Partner with the Manufacturing Optimization Team to design and construct the monuments, tools, and visual management elements that production teams rely on – metric boards, standard-work binders, cell-level documents, templates, kanban systems, and other floor-ready materials.
2. Own record retention and document control. Partner with Quality to move APS documentation into controlled status, maintain revision history, and ensure the floor is always working to the latest approved standard.
3. Run team supplies and inventory. Order the materials, boards, printers, labels, and consumables the team needs to deploy at each site. Keep a clean, current inventory so the team never shows up to a pilot missing a critical item.
4. Support and lead 5S activities during APS pilot launches. Plan and execute Sort / Set-in-Order / Shine / Standardize / Sustain events on the floor alongside the Manufacturing Operations team, and coach the site team on sustaining the standard.
5. Manage the APS SharePoint library. Serve as the owner of the team's standard documents and templates – keep it organized, keep it clean, enforce naming and versioning, and make sure every site across the company is pulling from the latest and greatest.
6. Evaluate and optimize the APS toolkit. Pressure-test existing templates and tools, gather feedback from the floor, and help the team design and prototype new tools and templates for future APS versions.
