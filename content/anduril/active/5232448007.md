Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. ### ABOUT THE JOB

We are seeking a high-agency Product Sourcing Program Manager to join our Product Sourcing Engineering (PSE) team in Costa Mesa, CA. In this role, you will serve as the analytical engine driving transparency, velocity, and risk mitigation across our end-to-end hardware supply chain. You will partner directly with Sourcing Engineers, Supplier Industrialization Engineers (SIE), Supply & Demand Planners, and Production Leads to translate complex procurement, supplier, and inventory data into actionable intelligence. If you thrive in a fast-paced hardware prototyping and manufacturing environment and want to build the operational visibility that safeguards our critical path, we want to hear from you.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* **Build Operational Intelligence:** Design, build, and maintain production dashboards and automated reporting to track mission-critical supply chain metrics (On-Time Delivery, supplier quality/yield, Purchase Price Variance, lead times, critical path shortages, and inventory health).
* **Analyze & De-Risk the Supply Chain:** Perform root-cause analysis on delivery bottlenecks, supplier capacity constraints, and cost drivers to proactively mitigate risks before they impact hardware build schedules.

+ Utilize exception and action reporting to resolve blockers and eliminate supply chain constraints

* **Bridge Engineering & Procurement:** Partner with Product Sourcing Engineers and Program Managers during New Product Introduction (NPI) to track Bill of Materials (BOM) maturity, tooling readiness, and long-lead component procurement.
* **Drive Strategic Decision-Making:** Synthesize complex data sets across multiple systems into concise, high-impact briefings and executive summaries for operational leadership.
* **Scale Analytics Infrastructure:** Assist in formalizing analytical procedures, establishing standardized reporting protocols, and automating recurring manual workflows across the supply chain organization.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Data Analytics, Computer Science, Industrial Engineering, Finance, Business Administration, or a related quantitative field. Master’s degree is a plus.
* Minimum of 3 years of professional experience in data analytics, supply chain management, or operations analysis with a heavy focus on reporting, data modeling, and business intelligence.
* Demonstrated technical proficiency with:

+ Microsoft Excel: Expertise with advanced formulas, data modeling, macros, Power Query, pivot tables, and dynamic reporting.
+ Microsoft PowerPoint: Proficiency with slide design to effectively communicate data-driven insights, operational risks, and program updates to cross-functional teams and leadership.
+ BI / Data Visualization Tools: Hands-on experience building actionable dashboards in modern BI platforms (e.g., Palantir Foundry, Databricks, Power BI, etc).

* Strong working knowledge of core supply chain and manufacturing principles (ERP/MRP logic, BOM structures, lead-time variances, and inventory turns).
* Excellent communication and presentation skills, with a proven ability to translate complex data into clear, actionable narratives for cross-functional stakeholders.
* Ability to work onsite in Costa Mesa, CA.

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Hands-on experience with Palantir Foundry (building pipelines, ontology integration, or front-end Workshop/Contour dashboards).
* Direct experience extracting, querying, and analyzing data from Oracle ERP / MRP systems.
* Experience in a high-mix, low-mid volume environment or rapid-prototyping aerospace, defense, or high-tech manufacturing environment.
* Work with AI tools and generative models to accelerate the development and automation of internal reporting dashboards and data pipelines.
* Prior exposure to hardware/software product operations, New Product Introduction (NPI), or defense/military hardware lifecycles.
* Proven ability to thrive in a high-tempo, sprint-based engineering culture with the flexibility to adapt to shifting operational priorities and critical production pushes.
* Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. Secret Clearance.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
