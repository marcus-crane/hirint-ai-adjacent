Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years. 

**WHAT YOU’LL DO** 

* Manage the Group 5 Mission Autonomy test organization, overseeing the test and evaluation of the autonomy software stack and its components from inception to decommissioning through progressive test environments (SITL, HITL, Flight).
* Manage project test leads and lead test engineers executing test programs for all Group 5 MA programs. Support these teams' customer / third party engagements and relationship management.
* Plan, schedule, and coordinate the execution of multiple simultaneous test programs with internal and external stakeholders. Coordinate and deconflict test and evaluation resources between test programs. Manage the teams responsible for test infrastructure.
* Manage test planning process, test plan approval, test readiness reviews, test data analysis, test artifact production. Work with project test leads to develop and maintain test mission scenarios, test procedures, test cards/Detailed Methods of Test (DMOTs). Manage production of Anduril and customer-facing test reports, processed data packages, and verified/validated software.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS** 

* Experience managing test teams conducting Developmental Test or Operational Test for multiple programs.
* Operational experience as a Pilot or CSO/NFO of USAF, USN, or USMC 4th or 5th generation fighter/bomber aircraft or RPAs.
* Bachelor's degree in STEM, or equivalent experience in a relevant field.
* Experience planning and directing test events with go/no-go decision authority in developmental, operational, or integrated test environments.
* Familiarity with software development practices, progressive test environments (SITL → HITL → Flight), and systems engineering processes.
* Ability to leverage operational experience and technical knowledge to evaluate autonomous system behavior and make informed decisions in the absence of clear customer requirements.
* Self-starter with high ownership, mission-oriented mentality. Desire to see the product meet the need of the end customer.
* Strong analytical skills, with the ability to interpret test data, diagnose system behaviors, and recommend actionable improvements.
* Organized and detail-oriented, with strong verbal & written communication skills.
* Proven track record of working collaboratively with multidisciplinary teams to achieve project milestones and deliverables.
* Experience coordinating across multiple external organizations (customers, air vehicle providers, range authorities, test support asset providers).
* Willingness to work flexible hours. Travel as required for test events and customer engagements.
* Active U.S. Top Secret security clearance and experience with Special Access Programs.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS** 

* Graduate of a military Test Pilot School (e.g., USNTPS, USAF TPS, Empire Test Pilots' School)
* Direct experience testing autonomous systems, Unmanned Aerial Systems (Group 3+), or manned-unmanned teaming.
* Familiarity with government reference architectures for autonomous systems.
* Experience conducting software verification and validation testing.
* Masters degree in STEM, or equivalent experience in a relevant field.
* Knowledge of current industry and military standards related to unmanned aerial systems and their operation within national airspace.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
