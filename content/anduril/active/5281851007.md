**Position Summary**

The Product Quality Engineer is responsible for ensuring that manufactured products meet all engineering specifications, contractual requirements, and regulatory standards throughout the full product lifecycle — from initial production through field deployment and sustainment. This role places particular emphasis on field operations quality, requiring the engineer to deploy to operational sites, conduct in-field inspections and root cause investigations, and drive corrective actions that bridge the gap between factory and field performance. The successful candidate combines hands-on technical depth with strong communication skills to serve as the quality voice across production, engineering, and field operations teams.

**Key Responsibilities**

**Production Quality**

* Lead and execute product inspection, verification, and acceptance activities throughout the manufacturing process.
* Develop, maintain, and improve inspection plans, quality control procedures, and acceptance criteria aligned with engineering drawings and specifications.
* Conduct first article inspections (FAI), in-process inspections, and final acceptance testing; document results and disposition nonconforming material.
* Analyze production quality data (defect rates, yield, SPC trends) to identify systemic issues and drive continuous improvement.
* Collaborate with manufacturing engineering on process capability studies and design-for-quality initiatives.

**Field Operations Quality**

* Deploy to customer and operational field sites to perform quality assessments, product inspections, and failure investigations on fielded hardware.
* Serve as the on-site quality representative during integration, test, and acceptance events at field locations.
* Lead root cause and corrective action (RCCA) investigations for field failures, including 8D, fishbone, and 5-Why analyses; drive timely closure of corrective actions.
* Establish and maintain field quality feedback loops to ensure lessons learned are incorporated into production processes and design changes.
* Coordinate with field service, logistics, and customer support teams to triage quality escapes and prioritize resolution.

**Quality Systems and Compliance**

* Support and sustain the Quality Management System (QMS) in accordance with AS9100, ISO 9001, or equivalent standards.
* Participate in internal and external audits; own audit findings and drive corrective actions to closure.
* Author and revise quality procedures, work instructions, and inspection records as needed.
* Ensure compliance with applicable government, customer, and regulatory quality requirements.

**Required Qualifications**

* Bachelor's degree in Engineering (Mechanical, Industrial, Aerospace, Manufacturing, or related discipline).
* Minimum 5 years of progressive experience in a product quality, quality engineering, or quality assurance role within a manufacturing or production environment.
* Demonstrated experience conducting quality activities in field or operational environments — not solely factory-based.
* Working knowledge of root cause analysis methodologies (8D, 5-Why, Ishikawa) and statistical quality tools (SPC, Pareto, capability analysis).
* Familiarity with quality management system standards (AS9100, ISO 9001, or equivalent).
* Proficiency in reading and interpreting engineering drawings, GD&T, and technical specifications.
* Strong written and verbal communication skills; ability to produce clear investigation reports and present findings to cross-functional stakeholders.
* Willingness and ability to travel up to 30%, including to remote or austere field sites.

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Experience in defense, aerospace, or other highly regulated industry.
* ASQ Certified Quality Engineer (CQE), Certified Quality Auditor (CQA), or Six Sigma Green/Black Belt.
* Hands-on experience with CMM, optical measurement, or other metrology equipment.
* Prior experience supporting government or military customers in field environments.
* Familiarity with MIL-STD inspection and sampling standards (e.g., MIL-STD-1916, ANSI/ASQ Z1.4).
* Experience with PLM, MES, or ERP-based quality modules (e.g., SAP QM, Windchill, Arena).

**Work Environment and Physical Requirements**

* Combination of office, factory floor, and field site environments. May include exposure to outdoor conditions, confined spaces, or elevated work areas at field locations.
* Ability to stand, walk, and perform hands-on inspections for extended periods. Occasional lifting up to 35 lbs.
* May require the ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. Government security clearance.
