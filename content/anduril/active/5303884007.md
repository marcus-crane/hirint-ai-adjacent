**ABOUT THE ROLE**  
We are looking for an **EMC Systems Engineer** to join our team in Costa Mesa, CA. You will own electromagnetic environmental effects (E3) compliance for one or more programs — from decomposing customer and platform requirements, through planning and executing MIL-STD-461 and MIL-STD-464 campaigns, to delivering the compliance data package that closes them out. This is a systems engineering role. You will build the verification plan and compliance matrix that maps every E3 requirement to a method, an owner, and a milestone, then drive that plan against the program schedule: chamber bookings, test article and support equipment readiness, long-lead fixtures, and gate reviews. You will scope and manage the work with internal and external EMC labs, write and administer the test statements of work, and review the plans, procedures, and reports that come back. Day to day you will work across the EMC engineering, hardware, firmware, mechanical, and program management teams, keeping them aligned to the same requirements, the same test configuration, and the same dates. You will keep the program informed on EMC status and schedule risk, and make sure the compliance evidence is organized and ready when it is needed.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Core responsibility: Own the E3/EMC compliance posture for one or more programs end to end — requirements definition through qualification closure and customer acceptance.
* Decompose platform and customer requirements (MIL-STD-461, MIL-STD-464, DO-160, program ICDs and SOWs) into verifiable component- and system-level requirements, and develop the tailoring rationale that goes with them. This covers the full system-level E3 flow-down, including lightning, precipitation static, RADHAZ (HERO/HERP/HERF), ESD, and intra-system EMC.
* Build and maintain the EMC verification plan and compliance matrix — every requirement mapped to a method (test, analysis, similarity, inspection), an owner, and a milestone
* Align the EMC campaign to the program schedule: chamber availability, test article and support equipment readiness, long-lead fixtures, and gate reviews.
* Write and manage statements of work for external EMC test labs. Evaluate scope, capability, quotes, and schedule, then serve as the technical point of contact through execution.
* Review and approve test plans, procedures, and reports. Assemble and deliver the compliance data package, including supporting analyses, deviations, and waiver requests.
* Keep the EMC engineering, hardware, firmware, mechanical, and program teams working to the same requirements, the same test configuration, and the same dates.
* Act as the program’s point of contact for EMC. Carry status, findings, and schedule impact between the engineering teams and the program managers, systems engineers, and customers who need to act on them.
* Maintain real-time visibility into EMC technical and schedule risk. Escalate early and drive every open finding to closure.
* Build reusable capability — verification plan and compliance matrix templates, test readiness checklists, and lessons learned carried forward to the next program.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Physics, or equivalent.
* 3+ years of EMC/E3 engineering experience on aerospace, defense, or similarly regulated hardware.
* Systems engineering background: decomposing requirements, building and maintaining verification plans and traceability, and carrying a technical scope through the program lifecycle from requirements definition to qualification closure.
* Working knowledge of MIL-STD-461 test methods (CE, CS, RE, RS) and MIL-STD-464 system-level E3 requirements, including how limits are tailored and what evidence closes them.
* Demonstrated ownership of a technical scope spanning multiple teams, with personal accountability for schedule and deliverables.
* Experience authoring or reviewing EMC test plans, procedures, and reports.
* Ability to read and interpret schematics, wiring diagrams, cable harness drawings, and mechanical assembly drawings.
* Must possess the ability to understand new concepts quickly; apply them accurately; organize, schedule, and coordinate work phases; and determine the appropriate approach at the task level or, with assistance, at the project level to provide solutions to a range of complex problems.
* Ability to work independently and as part of a team; able to perform complex tasks in one or more engineering areas.
* Strong organizational skills and the ability to keep a multi-team effort on schedule.
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
* Willingness to travel to test facilities and field sites, approximately 25%.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**
