### **ABOUT THE JOB**

We are seeking a Finance Manager to join our Corporate Finance team. This individual will serve as the connective tissue across Corporate and Division finance teams to produce financial analysis and insights across Anduril’s business. The ideal candidate is detail-oriented, proactive, and highly collaborative, with FP&A experience analyzing enterprise data and translating it into a clear story that guides executive decision-making and optimizes financial performance.  
  
**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Run the Cadence: Execute the financial operating calendar (monthly, quarterly, annually), building and maintaining internal team templates and deliverables, preparing executive presentation materials and driving inputs and alignment from cross-functional partners ahead of key decisions.
* Connect the Dots: Run the mechanics of consolidated budgeting, forecasting, and annual planning, working hand-in-hand with each Division’s finance team to understand key operational drivers and analyze performance against plan.
* Build the Narrative: Translate complex financial data into clear, executive-ready analysis for the CFO and C-Suite, explaining the "why" behind our performance, highlighting key trends and critical context, and framing risks, opportunities and trade-offs so leadership can act on them.
* Build for Scale: Continuously improve our financial consolidation, reporting, and forecasting infrastructure, building robust processes and models that can keep pace with Anduril’s dynamic growth.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 5-9 years of relevant experience in Corporate Finance/FP&A, Investment Banking, Investor Relations, Consulting or Strategy, preferably with exposure to corporate consolidations.
* Strong financial acumen and proficiency in Excel/financial modeling
* Comfortable working across financial systems (e.g., ERP, consolidation, and planning tools), understanding how data flows between them, with an eye for improving the processes and tooling that make analysis faster and more reliable.
* Exceptional written and oral communication skills and the ability to work seamlessly cross-functionally; collaborative by default, comfortable building trust with partners across Finance, Accounting, and the business.
* Intellectually curious - you seek to understand the why behind the numbers and dive into the operations as needed.
* Action oriented with end-to-end process ownership and high-quality work despite tight deadlines.
* Preferred to have DoD and/or Tech industry experience
* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, Statistics, Economics, Engineering, Business, or related field
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export controlled information or facilities

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
