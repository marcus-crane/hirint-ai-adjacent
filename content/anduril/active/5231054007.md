Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Simulation Integrations team owns the integration of Simulation products into other ecosystems. We're the bridge between Anduril's high-fidelity simulation engines and the production services that power Lattice, enabling simulated entities, sensors, and autonomy to participate in the same world as live assets.

### **ABOUT THE ROLE**

We are looking for a Senior Software Engineer with distributed systems experience to design and write the services that combine multiple independent simulators into one world, as well as the tooling that lets users author, reuse, and debug the scenarios they run. This is a new area with a wide surface. You will be one of the first engineers on it, which means you will set the technical direction, make the architectural calls, and be accountable for them. This is a greenfield project with lots of ambiguity and there are many existing standards in this domain that vary in quality. You will own the project end-to-end from design all the way to deployment.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Design and build the services that federate independent simulators into a single simulated world while ensuring coordination.
* Own the wire and networking layer. This includes protocol encoding and decoding, deciding what the network topology and protocols are, and the interoperability work required to make simulators built by different teams and vendors talk to each other.
* Make simulation runs deterministic and reproducible.
* Ensure high performance and low latency, especially when used interactively by humans in the loop.
* Integrate with the rest of the platform. We typically use Go for our services that provision and schedule compute, the gRPC/protobuf APIs that expose it, and the web UI the team owns.
* Set the technical direction for this area, write the design docs, and raise the bar through code review and mentorship as the team grows.
* Partner with Anduril business lines when they onboard onto the platform — work alongside their engineers to get their simulators, models, and test workflows integrated, and stay with them until it works in their hands and not just yours.
* Reduce the cost of that onboarding over time by turning the integration work you do by hand into documentation, reference integrations, and SDKs, so the next team can self-serve.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 7+ years of professional software engineering experience
* Deep, current production experience in modern languages like C++ and/or Golang
* Experience building networked, concurrent, or low latency systems, including multithreading, synchronization, pub/sub, and the failure modes that come with them
* Strong systems fundamentals: memory layout and allocation behavior, cache effects, sockets and UDP/TCP, serialization, and Linux
* Demonstrated ability to profile and optimize a real system, and to reason about latency and throughput with numbers rather than intuition
* Experience owning an ambiguous, cross-cutting project end-to-end and driving it to production
* Experience working directly with the engineering teams who consume what you build — integrating them, unblocking them, and taking their feedback into the next version
* Strong written and verbal communication — you can write a design document that a skeptical reader can evaluate
* Computer Science degree or equivalent industry experience
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* You have a bias for action and taking ownership. You have a track record of fixing a problem when you see one, including problems that are not formally yours.
* Experience with distributed simulation and interoperability standards — DIS (IEEE 1278), HLA/RTI (IEEE 1516), TENA, or equivalent proprietary federation layers
* Experience with simulation engines, physics or flight dynamics models, game engines, or discrete-event and time-stepped simulation frameworks
* Experience with software-in-the-loop or hardware-in-the-loop test systems, robotics simulation, or synthetic environments used for test and training
* Experience making a distributed system deterministic or reproducible, including logical clocks, lockstep execution, or record and replay
* Experience with gRPC/protobuf, or with designing binary protocols and schema evolution
* Experience running services on hardware or containers on Kubernetes, or otherwise operating what you build
* Experience building developer tooling or a platform product with real users, and iterating on their feedback
* An active U.S. Secret or higher security clearance

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
