Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

## ABOUT THE TEAM

Anduril's Air Defense team develops, integrates, and fields autonomous systems that find, track, and defeat threats in the sky while keeping operators in control of engagement decisions. The team brings together Roadrunner, Sentry, Anvil, Pulsar, and Wisp with existing and third-party sensors and effectors through Lattice, creating a software-enabled family of systems for the modern battlespace. As control of the air becomes more contested, these networked capabilities give warfighters the awareness and response options needed to protect forces against rapidly evolving UAS threats.

## ABOUT THE JOB

Anduril is fielding integrated air defense in the Middle East. As the single named technical authority, you own the architecture and technical performance of Anduril's sensors and effectors, the partner and customer-furnished systems we integrate, the networks that connect them, and the sustainment posture that keeps each site running once it goes live thousands of miles from the nearest Anduril lab.  
  
You will work across direct commercial sales, foreign military sales executed jointly with primes and government program offices, and multi-site rollouts already at initial operating capability. Each carries different engineering consequences: localization requirements that reshape the technical baseline, integration scope that is negotiated rather than specified, and milestones that depend on third-party integrations landing on schedule. They draw on different parts of the same product line, and you maintain the technical picture and keep the answers consistent across them.  
  
You will spend real time forward because Middle East deployments aren't run from a desk, and the technical decisions that matter most are made next to the system with the operators who have to trust it.

## WHAT YOU'LL DO

**Technical authority for the region.** Serve as Chief Engineer for Air Defense's Middle East programs. Own system architecture, integration strategy, fielding readiness, and sustainment outcomes across every program in the portfolio. Set the technical baseline, chair design reviews, and make the technical decisions that keep schedules aligned with the engineering. Exercise this authority across engineers who don't report to you.  
  
**Integration across systems you do not own.** Middle East programs integrate coalition hardware. Define interfaces to partner radars and effectors, customer-furnished equipment, legacy command and control being displaced, and third-party systems the customer has already bought. Much of the integration scope arrives underspecified. Negotiate it directly with partner vendors and government program offices, then give Anduril a clear account of what integrates cleanly, what requires an engineering sprint, and what isn't technically ready.  
  
**Foreign sales engineering.** Direct commercial sales and foreign military sales create engineering requirements beyond those of domestic programs, including releasability and export posture, in-country localization and production content, sustainment concepts measured in years rather than task orders, and technical terms negotiated into the contract before design begins. Own the engineering position in those negotiations and write the basis of estimate that supports it.  
  
**Field operations and recovery.** Deploy to the region, work alongside operators and field service teams, and own technical performance in the field. When a fielded site degrades, lead the recovery under time pressure and across a time zone gap, then feed what you learn back into the product to prevent the same failure at the next site.  
  
**Customer technical authority.** Represent Anduril's engineering position to partner nation ministries, program offices, primes, and authorization authorities. Bring their requirements and constraints back to Anduril. Translate customer-stated operational requirements into executable technical plans, and explain engineering constraints in terms a customer can act on.  
  
**Growth.** Shape the technical content of pursuits as the regional pipeline expands faster than the delivered baseline. Write credible estimates and flag any commitment that the engineering can't support.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
