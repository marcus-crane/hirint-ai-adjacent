Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**ABOUT THE TEAM**

The Hardware Platform Organization is Anduril's central hardware engineering org. We build the shared technologies, tools, and infrastructure that power hardware development across every Anduril division, from actuators and propulsion to batteries, avionics, rocket motors, and test operations. Our teams set engineering standards, run development manufacturing and test sites, and develop core technology products that ship across multiple product lines.

**ABOUT THE JOB**  
Anduril is looking for strong technical leaders to spearhead the development of core hardware products for use across the company’s divisions. You’ll own the technical development of a component or subsystem from concept refinement through detailed design, build, qualification, and transition to production. These projects will require you to leverage your technical expertise and leadership skills to set objectives, build a team, and produce real products on a rapid timeline. The ability to leverage your intuition and prior experience in programmatic decision-making will play a key role in making sure the right design, analysis, and test steps are completed to ensure success while carefully balancing technical risk against execution speed in a company that ships.  
  
**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* **Lead Technical Execution:** Own the technical baseline and lead a multi-disciplinary engineering team (mechanical, electrical, controls, thermal, structures, propulsion where applicable, materials, firmware) through detailed design, integration, and qualification of a hardware product.
* **Oversee Prototype Build & Integration:** Coordinate the fabrication and assembly of prototype and development articles, resolving engineering challenges that arise during build.
* **Drive the Test Campaign:** Lead the engineering team in planning and executing a comprehensive qualification program — component-level, subsystem, environmental, HALT/HASS, and system integration test — to validate performance and expand the operating envelope.
* **Manage System Design & Architecture:** Guide the team through critical design trades, subsystem optimization, and interface management with the divisional programs that integrate the product, ensuring a cohesive and high-performance architecture.
* **Ensure Reliability & Safety:** Serve as the technical authority for product reliability and safety; partner with Quality, Reliability & Safety and the divisional integration teams to produce the data and artifacts required to qualify the product for use in fielded systems.
* **Problem Solve & Troubleshoot:** Act as the lead technical problem-solver, driving root cause analysis and implementing effective solutions for issues discovered during integration, test, and field use.
* **Prepare for Production:** Partner with the manufacturing engineering team to ensure the design is production-ready (DFM/DFA) and support a clean transition to rate production.
* **Support Divisional Integration:** Work directly with engineering teams across divisions as they integrate the product into their platforms, including at customer and test sites.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience as a Chief Engineer, Integrated Product Team (IPT) Lead, or senior program or technical leader on a complex hardware product program (component, subsystem, or integrated assembly) in defense, aerospace, or an adjacent high-reliability industry.
* Deep, hands-on experience leading a multi-disciplinary team through at least one major phase of hardware development (detailed design, prototype build, qualification test, or production transition).
* Proven track record of taking a complex hardware product from design into build and successful qualification and fielding.
* Strong technical foundation across multiple core hardware disciplines (e.g., mechanical, electrical, controls, structures, propulsion, materials, systems integration).
* Strong understanding of the “why” behind product and subsystem design decisions.
* Experience on mission-critical DoD or advanced hardware development programs.
* Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical, Electrical, Aerospace, Materials, or a related engineering field.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience taking a hardware product through qualification and into rate production that ships across multiple end systems or customer programs.
* Direct experience leading the prototype build of a precision hardware subsystem (actuators, gimbals, propulsion components, optical/EO payloads, or similar).
* Prior experience with DoD technology development organizations (e.g., DARPA, AFRL, NAVAIR, ONR) on advanced hardware programs.
* Background in a “Skunk Works,” “R&D”, “Phantom Works,” or fast-paced startup environment focused on rapid hardware prototyping.
* Experience with a wide range of manufacturing processes, including precision machining, additive manufacturing, advanced composites, and automated assembly.
* Working knowledge of product qualification standards for defense hardware (e.g., MIL-STD-810, MIL-STD-461, DO-160).
* A “player-coach” leadership style with a willingness to be deeply involved in the day-to-day build.
* Be clearance eligible

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
