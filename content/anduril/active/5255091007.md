Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Air Defense team builds robots that find other robots and knock them out of the sky. At a time when air superiority can no longer be taken for granted, the Air Defense team provides mission critical capabilities to warfighters. From detection to tracking, identification, deterrence, and defeat, our family of networked sensors and effectors enables our customers to rapidly close the kill chain against a broad range of UAS threats. Working across product, engineering, business development, logistics, operations, and mission success, the Air Defense team develops, tests, deploys, and sustains the Anduril Air Defense Family of Systems in challenging operational environments worldwide.  
  
Air Defense Advanced Concepts identifies and develops the technological breakthroughs that propel the next generation of the Air Defense integrated family of systems. That work spans the sensors and effectors we build or integrate; the interfaces and tools used to develop and deploy those integrations; and the software capabilities that make an integrated system greater than the sum of its parts. Advanced Concepts is organized into cross-functional domains made up of systems, software and hardware engineers, product managers, and technical program managers who own their products end to end. We sit alongside the Air Defense growth teams to align product and capture strategy while delivering capabilities to our program teams that are actively supporting the fight. This team is the incubation cell supporting the most dynamic mission in defense technology today.

### ABOUT THE JOB

As the Advanced Concepts Technical Program Manager, you operate as an extension of Advanced Concepts leadership, with a view across IRAD execution, proposals and the operating rhythm of the organization. You'll turn strategy into a plan the organization can execute, knowing that priorities are just as dynamic as the mission we support. You'll own how the function makes and records decisions. You'll write down and enforce the working relationships between Advanced Concepts and the program and functional teams we partner with. Most importantly, you drive action in a team that is execution heavy and operationally aligned.  
  
You must be a technical leader. You need to be able to tell whether an IRAD milestone, an integration plan, or a proposal's technical volume is credible. You need the judgment to fix it without escalating prematurely. You will carry real authority on operating decisions. You will empower those with authority over product and technical decisions, resolve conflict and ambiguity when it arises, and orchestrate a smooth operation through excellent communication and by leading with data.   
  
Within 90 days, you'll own the Advanced Concepts operating cadence and have shipped an integrated execution roadmap across our cross-functional partners. Within six months, every Advanced Concepts submission, from ROM to proposal, will be built on a standard estimating method you put in place, and our IRAD and planning cycles will run on the process you build.

### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* **Operating Rhythm:** Run the Advanced Concepts operating cadence: leadership syncs, IRAD and business reviews, periodic engagements with internal and external engineering partner teams. Own agendas, pre-reads and the decision log, and follow every decision through to closure.
* **IRAD Execution:** Own execution tracking across the Advanced Concepts IRAD portfolio. Hold every line to its approved plan and surface technical and schedule risk early. Run re-plans within approved lines, and bring re-allocation recommendations to the leadership. Lead with data.
* **Codify the Seams:** Maintain the working agreements between Advanced Concepts and program teams, functional engineering teams, business development, operations, and other business lines. Establish SLAs and enforce them. Triage support requests from program teams against agreed terms. Staff the ones that fit, and escalate the ones that don't with a recommendation.
* **Opportunity Assessment and Technical Advocacy:** Evaluate new pursuits and IRAD proposals critically for technical feasibility, strategic alignment and return on investment. Coordinate Advanced Concepts' role in proposals: bid/no-bid inputs, technical volumes and ROMs from the subject matter experts, and tracking impact to existing commitments. Make the case for resourcing.
* **Estimating and Labor Forecasting:** Derive and vet the labor forecasts behind what Advanced Concepts submits, from early ROMs through full proposals, for external bids and internal IRAD alike. Then scale it. Build the estimating method, templates and historical-actuals reference the whole organization uses, so estimates are consistent, traceable to a basis, and better with every proposal we submit.
* **Decision Support:** Prepare leadership for business-line decisions with briefs, option tables and staffed recommendations. Represent Advanced Concepts in forums and commit within delegated authority.
* **Organizational Change:** Lead the function's org design, charter and process changes. That includes rolling out potentially disruptive changes and building communication plans for each level of stakeholder.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

This role is not for everyone. You will live on a constant rollercoaster of "it's so over / we're so back," and you need to be the force that steadies the ship in large swells. We are far more interested in what you have done than where you are coming from.  
  
**Core Attributes:**

* **Technical Credibility:** You have built and fielded real systems. You can hold your own in a design review. You know when a question is yours to answer and when it belongs to the technical authority.
* **First Principles Thinker:** You break complex problems down to their fundamental components and build solutions from what is known to be true, not from how it was done before. You always question the status quo.
* **Build with Longevity in Mind:** The structures you build (cadences, agreements, processes) outlive your personal involvement. You're unafraid to hand off something you've built, even when it's succeeding.
* **Allergic to Ambiguity:** You use questions as tools, and you have a proven track record of sound decisions under uncertainty and with limited information. You can clearly say "No" to internal stakeholders, including senior ones.
* **Master Communicator:** You have elite writing and speaking skills. You can turn a messy situation into a one-page decision brief that a leader can act on in five minutes.
* **Analytical Rigor:** You're a dialectical thinker who can interpret contradictory information. You use data to back up recommendations and update your view as new information arrives.
* **Demonstrated Humility:** No job is too small for you. You get results through people who don't report to you, and you champion the successes of the engineers and product owners you enable.

**Experience:**

* 8+ years of experience, including at least 4 years of hands-on technical work (systems, hardware, software, or test engineering) and at least 3 years leading cross-functional programs or initiatives as a Chief Engineer, Technical Program Manager, Engineering Manager, or equivalent
* Proven experience leading a technical team of peers to field hardware or software products in real operating environments
* Experience operating in a matrix organization and delivering through people outside your reporting line
* Working knowledge of defense R&D funding and contracting: IRAD vs. CRAD, contract types, the proposal lifecycle
* Experience building and defending labor estimates and bases of estimate (BOEs) for engineering work, from ROM through proposal
* Bachelor's degree in an engineering discipline, computer science, or a related technical field, or equivalent hands-on engineering experience
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Top Secret security clearance
* Willing to travel up to 25% of the time

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
