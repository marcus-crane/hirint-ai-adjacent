Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### **ABOUT THE TEAM**

The Strategic Defense business line builds and deploys integrated hardware-software systems that protect the homeland and support global command and control missions. Our programs deliver Anduril’s Lattice-powered capabilities to the nation’s most critical missile defense and battle management architectures, connecting sensors, effectors, and decision-makers across every domain. 

The Mission Operations (MOps) team is responsible for managing the execution, delivery, growth, and success of our customer accounts. Working across product, engineering, business development, and logistics teams, our Mission Operations Engineers own and shape customer relationships by embedding themselves within government programs to develop, plan, and deploy Anduril products in support of their missions. They work side-by-side with customers to understand specific problem sets and then shape Anduril’s offerings alongside engineering teams to fit the mission need. 

### **ABOUT THE JOB**

**Own the deployment lifecycle for Anduril compute infrastructure.** You will lead the planning, deployment, and sustainment of Anduril hardware nodes—including Menace-T, Menace-I, Voyager compute devices, and 3P server configurations—at government sites nationwide. This includes coordinating site surveys, managing installation activities, conducting acceptance testing, and ensuring each deployment meets mission requirements. You will develop and maintain the processes that turn a one-time installation into a repeatable, scalable deployment capability. 

**Serve as the technical bridge between government customers and Anduril engineering.** You are the primary liaison for our government stakeholders, translating their operational needs into actionable engineering requirements and keeping them informed on delivery timelines, integration status, and system performance. You will represent the customer perspective in internal planning sessions and ensure Anduril’s engineering teams have the context they need to build the right solutions. 

**Drive third-party system integration.** Work with partner organizations to integrate sensors, effectors, and other third-party systems into Anduril hardware form factors. You will assess power, cooling, weight, and rack-space requirements; coordinate integration testing; and ensure deployed configurations meet both Anduril and government specifications. 

**Build and formalize fleet management.** Stand up and own a fleet management capability for deployed Anduril nodes. This includes tracking hardware assets from procurement through deployment, maintaining configuration records, monitoring system health in coordination with SRE teams, and building the reporting and dashboards that give program leadership visibility into the state of the fleet. 

**Partner with Software Engineering and SRE teams.** Collaborate closely with software engineers and site reliability engineers to ensure deployed hardware is running the correct software baselines, that updates are applied efficiently, and that operational issues are triaged and resolved quickly. You will participate in incident response and help shape the processes that keep our systems healthy at scale. 

#### **WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Plan and execute site deployments of Anduril compute nodes (Menace-T, Menace-I, Voyager, etc.), coordinating across logistics, engineering, and government site teams
* Serve as the day-to-day technical point of contact for government customers, managing expectations, communicating status, and escalating issues
* Assess and coordinate the physical integration of third-party sensors and effectors into Anduril hardware form factors, including power, cooling, network, and rack-space requirements
* Develop and maintain a fleet management system to track deployed assets, configurations, software baselines, and system health across all program sites
* Partner with SRE teams to monitor deployed node health, coordinate software updates, and support incident response
* Create and deliver technical reports, deployment readiness reviews, and status briefings for government and internal leadership
* Drive continuous improvement of deployment processes, documentation, and playbooks to enable repeatable, scalable site activations
* Support program management activities including schedule tracking, risk identification, and deliverable management
* Contribute to the development of training materials and conduct end-user training at deployed sites

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
