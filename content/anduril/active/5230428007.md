**ABOUT THE JOB**  
We are seeking a **Senior Software Engineer** to bridge the gap between security operations, platform architecture, and product delivery. In this role, you will hold a high-impact, cross-functional position, collaborating directly with our Firmware, Platform, OS, Experience (Client/App), and Cloud teams.  
  
You will not just be pointing out vulnerabilities—you will be an active engineering partner. You will integrate security tooling directly into our CI/CD pipelines, triage findings, write proof-of-concepts, and partner closely with Feature Teams and Product Owners to design, implement, and verify secure fixes across the entire hardware-to-cloud lifecycle.  
  
**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Automate Security in CI/CD: Architect, integrate, and maintain automated security tooling (SAST, DAST, SCA, Secret Scanning, and SBOM generation) within modern CI/CD pipelines.
* Triage & Vulnerability Management: Ingest findings from automated tools, internal testing, and external disclosures. Distinguish signal from noise, evaluate risk, and translate raw alerts into actionable Jira tickets with clear remediation guidance.
* Cross-Team Remediation: Act as the technical security authority for Product Owners, feature engineers, and engineering managers to prioritize and implement secure fixes without sacrificing product velocity.
* Silicon-to-Cloud Threat Modeling: Lead threat modeling sessions and architectural security reviews for components spanning bare-metal firmware, custom Android OS layers, client apps, and cloud backends.
* Incident Response & Research: Participate in product security incident response (PSIRT), conduct root-cause analysis, and occasionally perform vulnerability research, fuzzing, and reverse engineering on internal targets.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 8+ years of software engineering experience with a heavy focus on security, systems engineering, or DevSecOps.
* Demonstrated Track Record: Proven experience integrating security automation into build pipelines and successfully collaborating with software teams to patch vulnerabilities.
* Languages: Proficiency in at least two of the following: C/C++, Rust, Go, Python, or C#.
* DevSecOps & Cloud: Hands-on experience with modern CI/CD tools (GitHub Actions, CircleCI) and cloud environments (AWS or Azure), with knowledge of Terraform and Kubernetes.
* Soft Skills: Exceptional cross-functional leadership, communication, and stakeholder management skills. You know how to talk about risk with Product Managers and technical trade-offs with Principal Engineers.
* Must Be Clearance Eligible

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Proven ability to deliver high-quality results at pace, ideally within a startup or high-growth environment
* Experience integrating Unity-based applications with external hardware or systems (drones, robotics, sensors, wearables, or other edge devices)
* Familiarity with real-time data streaming, message passing, or protocols commonly used for system-to-system communication (e.g., gRPC, Protobuf, MQTT, WebSockets, ROS)
* Experience working with cross-platform or cross-service integrations, including consuming and designing APIs/SDKs across engineering teams
* Android development experience below the application layer
* A strong understanding of how software interacts with hardware, from device drivers and firmware interfaces up through the application layer
* Professional software development lifecycle experience using tools such as version control, CI/CD systems, and automated testing frameworks

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
