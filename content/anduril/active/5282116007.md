Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

Strategic Defense builds the command and control and fire control software for integrated air and missile defense. We work the hard end of the problem: ballistic missiles, hypersonic glide vehicles, cruise missiles, and saturation raids that arrive faster than any human-paced decision cycle can absorb.  
  
The US air and missile defense enterprise was assembled over four decades, one program at a time. Army air defense runs on one battle manager, short range air defense on another, the ballistic missile defense system on a third, and the fleet on a fourth. Each of them was engineered well for the threat it was scoped against. None of them was designed to be extended by anyone other than the prime that built it, which is why adding a sensor or an effector to an existing architecture is still a multi-year integration program rather than a software release.  
  
That is the opening we are working. A software-defined fire control layer, built on Lattice, with interfaces open enough that a new sensor, a new interceptor, or a new effects chain can be brought in at the pace software moves rather than the pace a hardware program moves. The technical case is strong and the early programs are real. What we need now is someone who can turn that into share inside programs of record.

### ABOUT THE JOB

You will own Anduril's business development across US integrated air and missile defense: Army, Navy, Air Force, the Missile Defense Agency, and the Joint programs that cut across them. You will carry the pipeline, the forecast, and a bookings commitment.  
  
The work starts earlier than the RFP. By the time a requirement is written, the architecture it assumes has usually already been chosen, and the useful work happened eighteen months before that in requirements documents, analyses of alternatives, and conversations with program offices and operational units about what is actually limiting them. You will spend a substantial share of your time there, and you will be measured on whether the programs that eventually get written have room in them for what we build.  
  
You will also spend real time arguing internally. Customer requirements that are worth winning but that the roadmap does not yet serve are the most valuable thing you can bring back, and bringing them back usefully means understanding our product well enough to say what would have to change and what it would cost.  
  
Expect to travel up to 65%, concentrated in Huntsville, the National Capital Region, and the test ranges and operational units where this capability actually gets exercised.

#### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* **Own the US IAMD pipeline and the number.** Set the pursuit strategy, maintain the pipeline of record, build the forecast, and own the bookings commitment for the portfolio.
* **Shape programs before they are written.** Engage program offices, requirements sponsors, capability development organizations, and operational units early enough to influence the architecture a future solicitation assumes.
* **Run captures end to end.** Lead competitive assessment, black hat analysis, teaming strategy, price-to-win posture, and proposal strategy across multi-year pursuits, working with Capture and Proposal Operations.
* **Translate customer requirements into the product roadmap.** Bring back what the customer actually needs in a form Product and Engineering can build against, including the parts that are inconvenient for the current baseline.
* **Build the teaming relationships.** Structure partnerships with primes and subsystem suppliers who are, in most cases, also competitors. Know when subcontracting is the right path to a position and when it forecloses one.
* **Work the full range of acquisition pathways.** Other Transaction Agreements, Commercial Solutions Openings, middle tier acquisition, software pathway programs, and traditional programs of record, including when each is the faster route to a fielded position.
* **Represent Anduril.** Serve as a senior technical and commercial voice for the company with general officers, SES-level acquisition leadership, and at the forums where this community gathers.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates.   + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
