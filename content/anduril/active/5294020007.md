### ABOUT THE JOB

Anduril is seeking a skilled and experienced Low Voltage Technician to join our facilities and asset improvement team. The Low Voltage Technician is responsible for the installation, maintenance, testing, troubleshooting, and repair of low-voltage electrical and communications infrastructure throughout company facilities. This position supports the deployment and ongoing operation of structured cabling systems, fiber-optic networks, wireless access points, security systems, and other low-voltage infrastructure supporting industrial, manufacturing, warehouse, and office environments.  
  
This position reports to and works as part of the Asset Improvement Team, providing dedicated low-voltage infrastructure support to Facilities. The technician will work closely with the internal Network Engineering Team to coordinate network infrastructure installations, deployments, upgrades, and troubleshooting activities. This role requires advanced hands-on technical expertise, the ability to troubleshoot complex infrastructure issues, and the ability to independently execute projects while maintaining high standards for workmanship, safety, and documentation.
