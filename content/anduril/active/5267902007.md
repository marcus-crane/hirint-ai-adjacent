### ABOUT THE JOB

You will own requirements, interfaces, and the verification plan for a maritime autonomous vehicle, and run the campaign that takes it from separate subsystem deliveries to a vehicle that works in the water. You will lead the SEIT function and direct the MBSE systems engineer supporting it.  
  
Subsystem teams across structures, energy, propulsion, communications, and vehicle management are designing in parallel against interfaces that do not fully exist yet. This role writes those interfaces down, holds teams to them, and decides what gets tested in what order to retire the most risk per dive. **[HM to confirm before posting: number of subsystem teams, first in-water date, depth rating, how much in-water time the program has.]**

### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* Own the vehicle requirements baseline, from program requirements down to subsystem specifications
* Own interface management: ICDs, interface control, and change control across subsystems
* Define and own the verification and validation plan, including the requirements verification matrix
* Plan and run the integration and test campaign: bench, SITL, HITL, dockside, and at-sea
* Own vehicle technical budgets: mass, volume, power, energy, and buoyancy
* Chair design reviews (SRR, PDR, CDR, TRR) and drive actions to closure
* Lead test readiness and anomaly resolution during at-sea testing
* Direct the MBSE systems engineer and set how the system model is used on the program
* Travel up to 25% to test sites and operational locations

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Bachelor's degree in Systems Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or equivalent
* 8+ years in systems engineering on complex hardware and software vehicles, including at least 2 years leading integration and test
* Experience owning requirements and interface baselines across multiple subsystem teams
* Experience planning and executing verification campaigns on physical hardware
* Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. Secret security clearance

### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Experience with AUV, UUV, submarine, spacecraft, or aircraft integration and test
* Experience with MBSE and SysML, either owning a system model or consuming one
* Experience leading at-sea or remote field test campaigns
* Experience on a program where the requirements baseline did not exist yet
* Experience in defense or maritime robotics
