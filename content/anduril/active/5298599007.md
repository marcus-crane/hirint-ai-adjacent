**Job Description**  
  
  
**ABOUT THE JOB**   
We are looking for a Thermal Mechanical Analyst to join our rapidly growing team in Reston, VA. In this role, you will join early and work with other mechanical engineers to shape the thermal design of mil-rugged electronic enclosures from the first sketches through production. This will require finite element analysis thermal software experience, CAD, and a strong background in electronic systems. If you are someone who has experience with correlating thermal models with test data, then this role is for you.   
  
**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Build high-fidelity thermal models of electronics enclosures, including material properties for each component, thermal interfaces, and boundary conditions based on the customer’s operating environments.
* Predict component and chip junction temperatures, and confirm that each design meets thermal requirements across the full operating range.
* Perform structural analysis, including shock, vibration, and modal analysis, to support the mechanical design.
* Open, modify, and iterate on CAD models independently
* Recommend heat-management solutions, such as conduction paths, heat sinks, materials, and interface choices, during concept and design trades.
* Correlate analysis results with test data, then document findings in reports and present them at design reviews.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering or a closely related field.
* 6 - 7+ years of experience in thermal analysis, with a strong background in electronic systems.
* Proficiency with finite element analysis (FEA) thermal software. No strong preference for specific tools.
* Experience with structural analysis, including shock, vibration, and modal analysis.
* CAD proficiency sufficient to open, edit, and modify models independently.
* Strong communication skills, including presenting technical results to engineers and program stakeholders.
* Currently possesses and is able to maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Master’s degree in mechanical engineering or a related field.
* Experience with thermal design for ruggedized or military electronics and familiarity with environmental test standards.
* Experience correlating thermal models with test data.
* Experience working on a product from concept through production.
