### ABOUT THE JOB

As our Sr. Program Manager, People Operations, you will lead change management initiatives for our critical Workday implementation across the People organization. This is a strategic change leadership role requiring deep expertise in organizational change management, HCM system transformations, and stakeholder engagement. You will serve as the bridge between technical implementation teams and People team members, driving adoption, managing resistance, and ensuring seamless transition through training, communication, and support strategies. Your work will ensure that our People teams are prepared, enabled, and empowered to leverage Workday effectively as Anduril scales.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

### Workday Implementation Change Leadership

* Lead comprehensive change management strategy for the Workday implementation across all People teams, serving as the primary change advocate and liaison between technical implementation teams and business stakeholders throughout the rollout lifecycle
* Own and manage the post-implementation backlog, prioritizing issues, enhancements, and feature requests based on business impact, user feedback, and system stability requirements
* Develop and maintain the hypercare playbook, including incident management protocols, escalation pathways, support resources, and success metrics for the critical stabilization period following go-live
* Partner with technical leads and project managers to understand system configurations, new processes, and functionality changes, translating these into clear change impacts and readiness requirements for People teams
* Serve as the conduit between technical IT teams and People team members, translating complex technical concepts into accessible guidance and articulating business concerns and adoption barriers to technical teams
* Define change management requirements, communication plans, and training strategies that guide implementation decisions and ensure user readiness from day one

### Change Strategy & Stakeholder Management

* Design and execute comprehensive change management plans including stakeholder analysis, impact assessments, readiness evaluations, and resistance management strategies
* Drive change management initiatives related to system upgrades, process changes, and new system implementations across HR, payroll, recruiting, and compensation functions
* Influence and contribute to enterprise-wide enablement plans for all managers and employees, ensuring end-user training, communication strategies, and support resources are comprehensive and aligned with organizational readiness
* Build and maintain strong partnerships with People team leaders to identify change champions, assess readiness gaps, and co-create adoption strategies tailored to each team's needs
* Conduct ongoing pulse checks and feedback sessions to monitor adoption, address concerns, and iterate on change approaches

### Training & Enablement

* Design and deliver comprehensive training programs for People teams on Workday functionality, including end-user training, process documentation, quick reference guides, and job aids
* Partner with cross-functional stakeholders to shape and influence manager and employee enablement strategies, ensuring end-user training addresses real-world use cases and adoption barriers across the organization
* Develop role-based training curricula tailored to different user groups (HR Business Partners, Recruiters, Payroll Specialists, Compensation Analysts, managers, and employees)
* Create and maintain a library of training materials, video tutorials, and self-service resources to support ongoing learning and system updates
* Establish train-the-trainer programs to build internal capability and sustainable support structures

### Post-Launch Continuous Improvement

* Execute the hypercare playbook during the critical post-go-live stabilization period, coordinating rapid response to issues, monitoring system performance, and ensuring adequate support coverage
* Manage and triage the post-implementation backlog, working with technical teams to prioritize bug fixes, process improvements, and enhancement requests based on business criticality and user impact
* Design and implement post-go-live support structures including super-user networks, office hours, and escalation pathways
* Monitor adoption metrics, user satisfaction scores, and system utilization data to identify areas needing additional support
* Gather user feedback and partner with technical teams to prioritize system enhancements and process improvements
* Develop and maintain comprehensive process documentation and standard operating procedures for all Workday processes used by People teams

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
