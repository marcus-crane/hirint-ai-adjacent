## ABOUT THE ROLE

We are looking for a Senior Manager to build and lead the EMC Test Engineering team in Costa Mesa, CA. The team plans, sets up, and executes EMI/EMC qualification testing on our hardware, from individual electronic units through fully integrated air vehicles, and owns the integrity of the data that qualification decisions are built on. You will be accountable for that work across every program the team supports.

You will hire, develop, and manage the EMC test engineers and technicians, set the standards they test to, and decide where they are assigned as program demand shifts. You will run the in-house EMC lab and oversee campaigns at external and government labs, and you will own the test schedule, lab capacity, and equipment plan that keep qualification on track.

You will be the team’s technical authority on MIL-STD-461, MIL-STD-464, MIL-STD-704, and DO-160 — able to defend a tailoring position in front of a customer or government reviewer, and to make the final call on test readiness and data. You will work alongside the EMC Engineering, Electrical System Test, hardware, and program management leads, and stay close enough to the work to step in when a failure needs to be traced to its coupling path.

## WHAT YOU’LL DO

* **Core responsibility:** Lead and grow the EMC Test Engineering team. Hire, develop, and manage EMC test engineers and technicians, build the hiring plan as programs grow, and develop people who can take on more scope.
* Assign engineers and technicians across programs based on priority, chamber availability, and hardware readiness, and rebalance as demand changes.
* Own EMI/EMC qualification test execution across all supported programs, from unit-level testing through integrated air vehicle testing.
* Serve as the team’s technical authority on the standards: what each requirement protects against, how limits are tailored to a platform and mission profile, and what a given result proves. Approve tailoring positions and defend them with the customer and government reviewers.
* Own the flow-down from MIL-STD-464 system-level E3 requirements to the MIL-STD-461 subsystem limits and test configurations that verify them, together with the EMC Systems Engineers.
* Chair test readiness reviews for major test events. Set entry and exit criteria and make the final call on whether an article is ready for the chamber.
* Own test data integrity. Set the team’s standards for measurement setup, corrections, ambient characterization, and margin reporting, and approve the test plans, procedures, and reports that go to the customer.
* Lead failure investigations to the coupling path, work mitigations with EMC Design Engineering, and make sure every fix is proven by re-test.
* Run the in-house EMC lab and manage campaigns at external and government labs — chamber scheduling, instrumentation, statements of work, cost, and schedule.
* Own the team’s headcount plan, labor budget, and lab capacity plan, and estimate test labor, lab time, and external lab cost for proposals and new starts.

## REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Physics, or a related technical field.
* 12+ years of hands-on EMI/EMC test experience on military, aerospace, or avionics hardware.
* 5+ years managing engineers and technicians, including hiring, performance management, and career development.
* Ownership of multiple full MIL-STD-461 qualification campaigns from test readiness review through report delivery.
* Deep command of MIL-STD-461 and MIL-STD-464, including how limits are tailored to a platform and mission profile, and how to defend a tailoring position in front of a customer or government reviewer.
* Working command of MIL-STD-704 power quality and RTCA DO-160.
* Experience running an EMC test lab or test team, including chamber scheduling, instrumentation, and lab operations.
* Experience managing external EMC test labs, including scoping work and holding them to cost and schedule.
* Experience owning a team budget and headcount plan, and estimating test labor and lab cost for proposals or program plans.
* Track record of failure investigations traced to the coupling path and closed with a verified fix.
* Clear written and verbal communication with engineers, program managers, and customers. Test reports are customer deliverables.
* Willingness to travel to test facilities and field sites, approximately 15%.
* Must be able to obtain and maintain a DoD Secret clearance.

## PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Experience building an EMC test team or standing up an EMC lab.
* System- or platform-level E3 test experience on aircraft, UAS, missiles, or munitions.
* E3 specialty depth in HERO, lightning direct and indirect effects, precipitation static, HEMP, or RF hazard assessment.
* Experience in an ISO 17025 / A2LA accredited EMC lab, or leading a lab through accreditation.
* Understanding of filtering, shielding, and grounding practice sufficient to guide fixes with design engineering.
* NARTE EMC Engineer certification.
