**WHAT YOU’LL DO:** 

* Own hardware cradle-to-production: from concept generation and trade studies through CAD design, prototyping, testing, and transition to production.
* Design medium-to-large mechanical assemblies (e.g., foldable or deployable mechanical structures, structural bracketry, doubler plates, and mounting interfaces) and integrate them onto larger vessel or vehicle platforms.
* Define and optimize structural load paths to balance mass, stiffness, and manufacturability.
* Design structural or mechanical integrations in accordance with harsh environments: vibration, thermal, mechanical shock, humidity and corrosion.
* Integrate commercial-off-the-shelf (COTS) electro-hydraulic actuators, hydraulic power units (HPUs), gas springs, and electro-mechanical release mechanisms into structural frameworks, collaborating with Electrical Engineers on power and control interfaces.
* Design fixtures or assembly aids needed to manufacture and align assemblies reliably.
* Hands-on prototyping, diagnosing assembly/fitment challenges, and supporting low-rate initial production (LRIP).
* Lead design reviews, perform hand calculations and structural sizing, coordinate with vendors, and execute hardware test plans.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:** 

* Bachelor's, Master’s, or PhD degree in Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, or a related discipline.
* 2+ years of professional experience in mechanical/structural design (CAD).
* Demonstrated experience designing structural assemblies and plate structures (e.g., aluminum, carbon steel) with an emphasis on constraint, tolerancing, and load paths.
* Experience designing subsystem-level mechanical hardware and structural assemblies (as opposed to consumer electronics, enclosures, or micro-mechanisms).
* Strong understanding of manufacturing processes for large components, including when and why critical machining tolerances are required.
* Demonstrated hands-on fabrication, assembly, and testing skills.
* High competence with engineering first principles (statics, dynamics, mechanics of materials).
* Eligible for U.S. Security Clearance (DoD Secret, Top Secret)
* Up to 15% travel

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:** 

* Experience in collegiate vehicle engineering teams (FSAE, Baja SAE, or similar hands-on design/build competitions).
* Industry background in automotive (chassis, body-in-white), robotics, heavy machinery, or mobile industrial equipment (e.g., masts, booms, lift mechanisms).
* Experience with low-rate initial production (LRIP), resolving manufacturing line bottlenecks, or designing production line tooling.
* Experience integrating actuators, hydraulics, sensors, and basic electrical interfaces.
* Proficiency in Siemens NX and Teamcenter.
