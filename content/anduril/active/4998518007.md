Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

Anduril’s Air Dominance and Strike (AD&S) team is seeking a Senior Mission Systems Radar Engineer to join our team. The AD&S team is responsible for developing end-to-end “mission-ized” autonomous air systems capable of delivering exceptional warfighter outcomes to our customers.

As a Senior Mission Systems Radar Engineer, you will be responsible for the integration, test, and calibration for critical radar systems.  The right person will be comfortable on both sides of that loop: building radar performance models in MATLAB or Python, and standing at the bench, in the chamber, or on the range.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Integrate and bring up radar hardware and software: new units on the bench, installed units on the platform, and the signal chain working end to end.
* Plan and run test campaigns across bench, anechoic chamber and outdoor range — the procedure, the instrumentation and fixturing, the execution, and an honest record of what was actually done.
* Perform array calibration: channel-to-channel amplitude and phase alignment, embedded-element pattern measurement, and beam pointing verification, including how each drifts with temperature and failed elements.
* Reduce and analyze measured data and report how it compares to prediction — pattern cuts, range-Doppler maps, detection statistics, calibration residuals.
* Build and maintain radar performance models — link budgets, detection range, clutter and interference analysis.
* Prototype and tune signal processing in MATLAB or Python: pulse compression, Doppler processing, CFAR and angle estimation.
* Own a defined piece of a subsystem’s integration, test or verification and carry it to completion.
* Work directly with RF, firmware and software engineers during integration, bring-up and debug.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* BS. in Electrical Engineering, Physics or a related field and 3+ years of relevant experience. M.S. and 1+ year also considered.
* Hands-on radar or RF hardware test experience: bringing a unit up, running the measurement, and owning the result.
* Solid grounding in radar fundamentals: the radar range equation, matched filtering, detection theory and Doppler processing.
* Working proficiency in MATLAB and/or Python, including reduction, analysis and plotting of real measured data sets.
* Comfort in a lab with signal generators, spectrum analyzers, oscilloscopes and vector network analyzers.
* Able to obtain a US Security Clearance

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Knowledge of current and next-generation advanced radar systems.
* Experience with missile design & integration.
* Array calibration: channel-to-channel alignment, embedded-element patterns, and beam pointing error budgets.
* Exposure to phased arrays or digital beamforming.
* Microwave or millimeter-wave hardware experience.
* Familiarity with FPGA-based signal processing, fixed-point effects, or HDL.
* Anechoic chamber or outdoor range test experience.
* Automating test data collection and reduction so a campaign produces comparable results run to run.
* An active U.S. Department of Defense Secret clearance, or eligibility to obtain one.
