### **ABOUT THE ROLE**

We are looking for a Systems Administrator to independently support and administer Windows and Linux systems within secure enterprise environments. This role is responsible for day-to-day system operations, advanced end-user support, troubleshooting issues beyond documented Tier 1 procedures, maintaining approved configurations, and owning technical issues through resolution or appropriate escalation.  
  
**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Administer, configure, troubleshoot, and maintain Windows and Linux workstations and systems.
* Independently diagnose and resolve hardware, operating system, application, authentication, and network connectivity issues.
* Perform user, group, permission, service, file-system, and enterprise authentication administration using approved processes.
* Support domain-connected Windows and Linux systems, including troubleshooting domain membership and authentication issues.
* Support workstation provisioning, system deployments, patching, upgrades, approved software installations, and configuration changes.
* Troubleshoot TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, routing/connectivity, and physical network issues and determine when escalation to network engineering is required.
* Use system logs, Event Viewer, command-line tools, PowerShell and/or Bash to investigate and resolve technical issues.
* Support virtualization environments and virtual machines within established architecture and procedures.
* Create, maintain, and improve SOPs, technical documentation, and repeatable administrative processes.
* Evaluate operational and security impact before making changes and follow approved change-management and configuration-control processes.
* Partner with Cybersecurity, Engineers, and other technical teams to resolve complex issues and maintain secure system operations.
* Provide technical guidance and support to Junior Systems Administrators as needed.

#### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* **Active TS/SCI security clearance.**
* **Active CompTIA Security+ certification or higher DoD 8140/8570-aligned certification.**
* Working knowledge of Windows system administration and troubleshooting.
* Working knowledge of Linux administration, command-line tools, permissions, services, and troubleshooting.
* Experience with Active Directory or comparable centralized identity and authentication services.
* Understanding of TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, IP addressing, subnetting, and network troubleshooting.
* Experience independently troubleshooting systems using logs, diagnostic tools, and technical documentation.
* Familiarity with PowerShell, Bash, or similar administrative scripting.
* Ability to work independently within established security requirements, SOPs, and configuration baselines.
* Strong documentation, communication, troubleshooting, and customer-service skills.

#### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience with VMware, Hyper-V, or similar virtualization technologies.
* Experience supporting engineering, manufacturing, or specialized technical applications.
* Experience in DoD, government, classified, or other regulated computing environments.
* Additional Microsoft, Linux, VMware, networking, or security certifications.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
