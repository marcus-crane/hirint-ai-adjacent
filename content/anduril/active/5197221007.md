Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for a Production Program Manager to join our rapidly growing team in Irvine, CA, to lead the end-to-end execution of Sentry Deployments. In this role, you will bridge the gap between production, warehousing, and field operations to scale our global fleet and support high-volume expansion. Additionally, you will establish a robust field-to-factory feedback loop, turning real-world operational lessons into product enhancements and repeatable launch playbooks.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* **Planning, Production, & Top Level (DIP) Operations**

+ Partner with planning, production, engineering, product management, deployment operations, and business development on strategic priorities and delivery commitments.
+ Develop standardized tower packout processes that work for all involved parties and enable effective intake of returning components into MRO processes to increase efficiency and reduce waste at scale
+ Drive DIP strategy and execution, ensuring proper handoff between production, warehousing, Deployment Ops and the CBP Account team.
+ Ensure the process output includes the necessary customer deliverables (700-21 documentation) and establishes proper asset traceability across systems
+ Implement a process for rework and upgrade of assets after storage and before deployment
+ Plan and execute deployment readiness for new XRST variants to accelerate time-to-market and support higher volumes

* **Deployment, Sustainment & Field Support**

+ Support definition and execution of deployment strategy that scales efficiently with XRST, while still supporting other CI and AD products across the fleet/globe.
+ Build internal-production maintenance and repair approaches to efficiently support the fleet of legacy CI Towers
+ Use data and analytics to predict and prevent issues across our deployed systems before they become problems at scale
+ Develop field support approaches for upcoming XRST variants optimized for lower cost at high volumes, strengthening our position for large contracts

* **Field-to-Factory Feedback & Continuous Improvement**

+ Drive initiatives that quickly convert field learnings into product improvements, production innovations, planning enhancements, and better processes across all products
+ Partner with Mission Integration and Sustainment Engineering to implement design-for-manufacturability and design-for-installation improvements based on production learnings (e.g. Productize Tower Move kits + load-out for Mk5 PTU conversion across the fleet)
+ Turn operational lessons from XRST deployments into repeatable playbooks that help future products launch faster and scale more efficiently

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Operations Management, Business or related technical field
* 3+ years of experience in operations program management, business operations, engineering/technical roles, preferably in a complex hardware industry
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export controlled information or facilities

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Strong understanding of the manufacturing environment and the supporting processes that enable production
* Experience launching new products with condensed timelines
* Experience with project management methodologies (Agile, Lean, Six Sigma, etc.) and tools (Jira, Confluence, Gantt Chartsetc.)
* Excellent problem-solving, analytical, and organizational skills
* Strong communication and leadership abilities to influence and align cross functional teams
* Ability to work in a fast-paced, dynamic environment with evolving priorities

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
