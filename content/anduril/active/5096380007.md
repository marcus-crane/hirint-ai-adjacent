Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

Anduril is fielding the next generation of Autonomous Underwater Vehicles (AUVs) to tackle the extremely challenging industry demands of seabed exploration. Anduril has brought to the market a unique, ultra-long-range, full-ocean-depth platform, and a completely refreshed maritime vehicle and flexible manufacturing architecture that scales from "large" to "extra-large" vehicle sizes. Today, Anduril is executing on multi-million-dollar contracts while simultaneously performing Robot-as-a-Service (RaaS) AUV operations.  
  
As our team continues to quickly grow, we are looking for motivated candidates that are inspired to work within an extremely energetic, creative, and innovative team that is passionate about subsea robotics and exploring the open ocean. We work in a highly dynamic environment where collaboration and idea sharing are a way of life.

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for a **Director of Global Supply Chain** to join our rapidly growing team supporting the **Dive-XL Product**. In this role, based in **Costa Mesa, CA**, you will be responsible for building and leading the supply chain function, ensuring material, capacity, and supplier readiness are in place ahead of every rate increase, every new product version, at all global production sites. You will set the multi-year strategy for how the function evolves, spanning org design, process architecture, systems roadmap, and talent, while staying close enough to the work to personally unblock the hardest constraints and represent supply chain credibly to executive leadership. This will require deep expertise in strategic sourcing, production planning, supplier development, and supply chain systems architecture, along with a proven track record of scaling operations across global manufacturing sites. If you are someone who is energized by building a function that becomes a durable competitive advantage, someone who can be in the details on the highest-stakes shortages and escalations while simultaneously developing the leaders and organization that can increasingly handle them without you, then this role is for you.  
  
Reporting to the Global Head of Production, Undersea Dominance, you will operate within a matrix structure with dotted-line responsibilities into corporate Supply Chain leadership. This is not a role where you delegate and observe. You will own the outcome end to end.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

**Further Deepen & Stand Up Undersea Dominance Supply Chain Function**

* Build an integrated, resilient supply chain that supports product development, production ramp,
* sustained manufacturing, customer delivery, supplier quality, inventory performance, and total cost of
* ownership.
* Build the integrated plan for standing up the function: phased by production milestone, with entry and
* exit criteria, named owners, and hard dates. Work backward from production start and each rate step
* to define required readiness in people, process, systems, supplier qualification, and inventory position.
* Develop and establish demand and S&OP process/governance.
* Own the risk register, dependency map, and critical path, along with the governance around them:
* readiness and gate reviews, plus an executive cadence that gives leadership real status without
* generating theater.
* Write the operating model: planning cadences, material call and shortage escalation, receiving and
* inspection flow, kitting and line-feed, cycle counting, change control, and decision rights for each.
* Own the capital, tooling, and infrastructure requests supply chain depends on (storage footprint,
* material handling, receiving and inspection capacity), sequenced against facility construction.
* Partner and collaborate with Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality, Finance, Program Management,
* and Business Development across global suppliers and manufacturing sites.

**Drive Material and Supplier Readiness**

* Drive supplier readiness with Sourcing, Product Sourcing Engineering, and Supplier Quality:
* qualification, capacity reservation, tooling, and dual-source coverage completed ahead of need dates,
* not in parallel with production.
* Own the readiness sequence for manufacturing BOM release, including BOM risk assessment and
* alternate component strategy for constrained and long-lead parts.
* Define and drive the metrics that matter: clear-to-build, material availability, schedule attainment,
* supplier on-time delivery, and inventory accuracy. Hold the team to root-causing misses rather than
* expediting around them.
* Serve as the senior supply chain voice at the site. Own executive relationships across Sourcing,
* Manufacturing, Engineering, Finance, and Deployment Ops, and translate material shortages, capacity
* limits, and supplier risk into clear recommendations for leadership and stakeholders.

**Turn the Startup into a Day-to-Day Operation**

* Convert the standup effort into a durable operating function — replace one-off heroics and manual
* workarounds with standard cadences, documented processes, and clear decision rights that run
* without you in the room.
* Build and scale the team as headcount ramps: hire and develop planners, buyers, and material
* leaders; design the org structure the site grows into; and create a culture that retains strong operators.
* Mature the systems backbone so the function runs on reliable data rather than spreadsheets — stand
* up and stabilize ERP/MRP and planning tooling as volume and product mix grow.
* Institutionalize continuous improvement: establish the metrics, reviews, and root-cause discipline that
* let the function keep pace with each rate increase and every new product family, and shift the team
* from firefighting to steady-state execution.
* Identify and mitigate global supply risks; drive shortage resolution, continuity plans, supplier
* diversification, and production readiness.
* Lead Sourcing Engineering and supplier development, including supplier technical readiness,
* qualification, capacity planning, quality, delivery, and recovery.
* Partner with Design Engineering to integrate supplier capacity, manufacturability, and sourcing
* considerations into new product development.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
