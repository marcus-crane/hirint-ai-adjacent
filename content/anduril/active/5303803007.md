Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Maneuver Dominance team in London builds the Mission System and Autonomy for Anduril's autonomous air platforms. Aircraft built to operate alongside crewed aviation and deliver effects in contested environments.  
  
Hardware is where the mission system meets the airframe. Our sensors, payloads, stores, compute and radios have to share power, space and cooling on a compact aircraft, survive heat, vibration and interference, and still be ready to fly in months, not years. Boards that work first time, and that are easy to build, test and fix, are what keep the whole system moving. That is the problem you would own.

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for a Senior Electrical Engineer to join us in London and own electronic hardware across our mission system and payloads. This is a full-cycle role. You will take customer needs and mission parameters, turn them into measurable requirements, and carry the design through schematic, layout, bring-up, qualification and integration on the aircraft. That includes boards that form part of the Stores Management System, where the interface to the weapon has to be right first time. You will be the voice for electrical design in vehicle-level trade studies, and you will be in the lab and at the test range when the hardware needs debugging.  
  
We are looking for someone who sees the whole system, not just the board: low-ego and high-ownership, mission focused, and able to justify a design decision to engineers and to leadership alike. This is a full-time, on-site role, because the work is hands-on and the team learns from each other at the bench.

### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Own the development of electronic hardware for payloads and subsystems across the Mission System, including sensor interfaces, power management, compute and networking
* Turn customer asks and mission parameters into quantifiable hardware requirements, and create architectures and designs that meet them
* Deliver full-cycle PCB design: requirements capture, schematic design, component selection, layout (completing it or supervising it), bring-up, test, debug, qualification and system integration
* Design mixed-signal boards that combine high-speed digital, precision analogue, RF and high-power circuits, including microcontroller and processor based designs
* Perform power design and electrical loading analysis, and develop power management and distribution for payloads and subsystems
* Design, test and qualify boards for the Stores Management System, including MIL-STD-1553 data bus and MIL-STD-1760 aircraft-to-store interfaces, and safety-critical circuits such as release consent, arming and interlocks
* Develop system interconnect diagrams and define the wire harnesses that connect equipment within a subsystem and onto the air vehicle
* Design for manufacture, test and the operating environment from the start, applying IPC standards and accounting for temperature, vibration and EMI
* Troubleshoot issues that span the electrical and software boundaries, using lab equipment and basic software debugging tools
* Conduct peer-level and cross-discipline design reviews, and represent electrical design at Preliminary Design Reviews (PDR), Critical Design Reviews (CDR) and Test Readiness Reviews (TRR)
* Collaborate closely with mechanical, RF, software, firmware, systems and test engineers to deliver fully functional, mission-critical products
* Establish and refine team processes for PCB design, design for manufacturing (DFM), part creation and library standards
* Support integration, ground and flight test of prototype and production representative hardware, across multiple concurrent projects and customers

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 7+ years of hands-on industry experience designing, testing and troubleshooting complex board-level electronic hardware
* Bachelor's degree in Electrical or Electronic Engineering or a related field, or equivalent practical experience
* Full-cycle PCB design experience, from requirements capture through schematic, layout, bring-up, qualification and sustainment
* Experience designing mixed-signal, multi-layer PCBs, including stack-up, impedance control, grounding, protection and filtering for power and sensor applications
* Experience with power design, power management and electrical loading analysis, and with designing and integrating high-power electronics
* Hands-on experience with microcontrollers and processors, including bring-up, and familiarity with common MCU, CPU and FPGA devices
* Familiarity with common interfaces such as Ethernet, CAN, I2C, SPI, PCIe, USB, RS-232, RS-422 and RS-485
* Fluency with lab test equipment: oscilloscopes, DMMs, logic and spectrum analysers, bench power supplies, electronic loads and current probes, plus experience automating tests
* Experience using Altium Designer (other ECAD tools considered), including component library development and management
* Working knowledge of the relevant IPC standards for design, fabrication and assembly
* Experience with rapid payload or sensor integration into air vehicles, UAVs, ground vehicles or weapons systems
* Outstanding written and verbal communication skills, and the habit of documenting a design as it develops
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active UKSV Security Clearance

### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Experience designing electrical and power systems for UAS or rotorcraft
* Design and test experience against MIL-STD-461, MIL-STD-810 and DO-160, or UK equivalents such as Def Stan 59-411 and Def Stan 00-35

* A strong background in one or more of the following: radios and RF systems; radar or optical sensors (EO/LWIR/MWIR/SWIR); gimbals and electro-mechanical or actuator systems; SIGINT/ELINT/EW/ESM systems; tactical data links and encryption
* Experience designing hardware for MIL-STD-1553 data bus and MIL-STD-1760 aircraft-to-store interfaces, ideally as part of a Stores Management System
* Experience with stores, weapons or launched effects integration, including weapon safety assurance
* Experience developing low-level firmware for bring-up and test
* Experience with power systems, including batteries
* IPC CID or CID+ certification
* Experience taking hardware from prototype to production at a fast-paced aerospace, defence or vehicle company

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
