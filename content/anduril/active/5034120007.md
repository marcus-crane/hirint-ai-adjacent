### ABOUT THE JOB

Rendezvous and proximity operations are where spacecraft operations stop being a checklist and start  
being a craft. You own that craft at Anduril. As a Spacecraft Operations Systems Engineer for RPO, you own how our spacecraft plan, rehearse, and  
fly close to other objects.

You'll do this work well before launch. RPO campaigns are won in AI&T, on the flatsat, and in the  
rehearsals, not just on-console. You'll validate procedures against hardware-in-the-loop testbeds, build  
and run day-in-the-life and contingency sims, and push ops constraints back into the GNC, flight  
software, and ground software designs while those designs can still change.  
This role is directly tied to ongoing programs within Anduril's Space Business Line. We work with mission  
partners and customers to deploy reliable and robust capabilities on operationally-relevant fielding  
timelines to meet complex challenges across the DoD and IC.

### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* Design and own RPO mission plans: relative motion design, approach and hold-point  
  sequencing, delta-V budgets, and the timeline that ties them to comm passes, lighting, and  
  ground contact
* Build passive safety into every approach, so that a missed burn, a dropped pass, or an off-  
  nominal state doesn't put the vehicle on a collision trajectory
* Author the flight rules and procedures that govern close approach: keep-out volume  
  enforcement, go/no-go gates, abort and breakout criteria, custody handoffs, and conjunction  
  screening against both the target and third parties
* Own the mission operations plans and procedures for spacecraft programs across all phases,  
  including LEOP, commissioning, on-orbit checkout, and operations
* Drive the pre-launch campaign from the ops side: write and run day-in-the-life and mission  
  rehearsals, inject the failures, and turn every finding into a procedure change or a design change
* Work with AI&T as the operations voice, validating procedures and command sequences on  
  flatsats and hardware-in-the-loop testbeds before they touch flight hardware
* Feed operational constraints back into GNC, flight software, ground software, and comms  
  design
* Create and analyze the metrics used to debug and monitor vehicle and campaign performance
* Triage anomalies, root cause failures, and coordinate next steps under time pressure
* Train operators and mission crews on Anduril spacecraft and on RPO execution specifically
* Support demanding schedules during ground testing, launch windows, and on-orbit operations
* Work with other teams and government agencies to obtain required licenses (FCC, NOAA) and  
  comply with regulations for operating spacecraft, including proximity operations coordination

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Top Secret security clearance
* Strong engineering background from industry or school, ideally in Aerospace Engineering,  
  Astrodynamics, Computer Science, or another engineering field
* 4+ years of professional spacecraft operations, mission planning, or flight dynamics experience
* Direct RPO experience on a flown or near-flight mission, on either the planning or execution  
  side: relative orbit design, proximity operations trajectory planning, maneuver planning and  
  reconstruction, or close-approach execution on console
* Experience taking a spacecraft through pre-launch operational readiness: AI&T support,  
  procedure validation, mission rehearsals, day-in-the-life testing, and operational readiness  
  reviews
* Ability to quickly understand and navigate complex systems and detailed requirements
* Capable of solving complex technical problems with little oversight
* Clear written communication, including procedures, flight rules, and training material

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
