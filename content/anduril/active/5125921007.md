**THE ROLE**  
Anduril is seeking a Tooling Design Engineer, Structures & Integration to lead the development, procurement, and deployment of critical tooling that enables the rapid production and assembly of our support structures and integrated hardware systems.    
You will own the tooling lifecycle for complex metallic components and assemblies across our product lines (e.g., Roadrunner, Fury, Dive-LD, and Altius). This includes everything from high-precision CNC machining fixtures, weld and bond jigs, to forming/stamping dies and final integration tooling.  
You should be deeply experienced in modern metal fabrication methods, obsessed with geometric accuracy, and thrive in an environment where you are expected to design on Monday, have parts on the machine by Wednesday, and be validating on the shop floor by Friday.  
  
**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* **Design & Own:** Design, model, and detail robust metallic tooling, assembly fixtures, weld fixtures, hydroform/press brake tooling, and inspection fixtures using CAD (NX preferred).
* **DFM / DFA Integration:** Partner directly with Hardware/Structures Design teams during early architecture phases to drive Design for Manufacturing, Assembly, and Tooling (DFM/DFA/DFT).
* **Execution & Validation:** Oversee tool fabrication both in-house and through external suppliers. Lead on-site tool commissioning, First Article Inspections (FAI), and tool qualification (FAT/SAT).
* **Root Cause & Optimization:** Rapidly troubleshoot assembly discrepancies, tool deflections, and part repeatability issues on the production floor using metrology data (CMM, laser trackers, FARO arms).
* **Vendor Management:** Interface with machine shops, tool and die makers, and material vendors to ensure aggressive lead times, cost-efficiency, and strict quality conformance.
* **Process Standardization:** Establish tooling design standards, library components, and maintenance/re-calibration intervals for metallic production lines across multiple product variants.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical, Manufacturing, Aerospace Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.
* 3+ years of hands-on experience designing and deploying production tooling specifically for metallic structures (aluminum, steel, titanium alloys).
* Mastery of 3D CAD modeling, large assembly management, and drafting (Siemens NX preferred; SolidWorks/CATIA accepted).
* Deep understanding of **GD&T per ASME Y14.5**, with direct experience establishing datum schemes and dimensional control strategies.
* Extensive domain knowledge in modern metallic fabrication: CNC multi-axis milling, turning, sheet metal brake/forming/stamping, and thermal joining processes (GTAW, laser welding, friction stir).
* Experience with structural hand calculations and FEA to validate fixture stiffness, deflection under load, and thermal stability.
* Demonstrated ability to operate with extreme autonomy in high-pressure, fast-turnaround hardware production environments.
* Must be eligible to obtain / maintain a US Security Clearance.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience in aerospace, space, or motorsport hardware production (thin-gauge skins, primary structures, pressure vessels).
* Direct experience with large-scale aerostructure assembly jigs and thermal stress relief/heat-treating fixtures.
* Familiarity with metrology software and equipment (PolyWorks, SpatialAnalyzer, FARO Arm, Leica Laser Tracker).
* Hands-on fabrication background (machining, manual TIG welding, sheet metal work).
* Experience scaling production from Low-Rate Initial Production (LRIP) to high-volume manufacturing.
