Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**ABOUT THE ROLE**We're looking for a Technical Program Manager to help drive technical results for key IT projects and programs across automations, artificial intelligence, enterprise systems, infrastructure, and networking. You'll manage execution end to end, from initial scoping and risk identification to stakeholder communication, while building the metrics and reporting that keep leadership informed on program, project, and system health. This role is a good fit for someone with solid technical grounding who wants to grow into a strategic, data-driven program management role within the IT department.  
  
**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* **Ensure Overall Success:** Drive the successful execution of the IT program and projects, ensuring defined requirements are met, stay on schedule, and align with budget.
* **Customer & Stakeholder Management:** Manage relationships with IT customers, uphold service level agreements (SLAs), and monitor request satisfaction. Own project deliverables and provide regular status updates to internal stakeholders.
* **Program Execution:** Lead execution across project tasks and work plans, partnering closely with senior team members, engineering, and operations counterparts. Operate with a high degree of ownership and independent judgment. Serve as a trusted partner to our IT, engineering, and operations teams.
* **Technical Coordination:** Develop a working understanding of IT system and infrastructure performance, including networking and server/cloud environments. Support incident response and after-action reviews. Gather and analyze metrics to identify areas for improvement and track the impact of new initiatives. Maintain a clear picture of system or process health that can be communicated to stakeholders.
* **Project Coordination:** Define project scope and track changes. Identify, document, and monitor project risks. Maintain and update project schedules, action items, and documentation.
* **Stakeholder Engagement:** Act as a point of contact for internal teams, gathering requirements and feedback. Represent the services and technical capabilities of the IT team to business stakeholders.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor's degree in a technical field like Computer Science, Information Systems, OR a degree in a related field with a demonstrated passion for technology and IT operations.
* 4+ years of program and project management experience within a technology environment managing multiple concurrent workstreams.
* 5+ years of experience in a technical role, such as IT support, systems administration, network operations, or other related technical disciplines.
* Demonstrated ability to analyze technical and operational data, build metrics, and use them to drive decisions and track program and project health.
* Experience working directly with technical teams (engineering, infrastructure, networking, etc.) to translate business requirements into actionable project plans.
* Strong analytical and problem-solving abilities.
* Excellent organizational skills with a high detail orientation and commitment to quality.
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills.
* Occasional travel to other company sites may be required, up to 10% of the time.
* Able to obtain and maintain a US Secret Clearance.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Project management certification (e.g., PMP, CompTIA Project+, CAPM).
* Experience with IT Service Management (ITSM) frameworks like ITIL.
* Hands-on experience with project management and collaboration tools (e.g., JIRA, Confluence, Smartsheet).
* Solid understanding of core IT Operations domains: networking, server administration (Linux/Windows), cloud services (AWS, Azure), and cybersecurity.
* Experience working with vendors to manage service delivery or support.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
