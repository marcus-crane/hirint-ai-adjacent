Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Maneuver Dominance team in London builds the Mission System and Autonomy for Anduril's autonomous air platforms. Aircraft built to operate alongside crewed aviation and deliver effects in contested environments.  
  
A mission system is only useful if it does what the mission needs. Our payloads and subsystems are being developed in parallel, across teams and across companies, and every release has to earn its place against the customer's mission. The mission threads, the analysis, and the evidence that each feature makes a difference are what hold that together. That is the problem you would own.

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for a Senior Systems Engineer to join us in London and lead mission engineering across our systems. You will bring pragmatic mission engineering thinking to complement and enhance the overall mission system capability, ensuring end-user needs are met in challenging operational environments. You will be part of a lean, high-ownership culture where you directly shape the product and operate at a velocity to fly new designs within months, not years.

### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Execute a right-sized, Agile Mission Engineering approach as per the Mission Engineering Analysis Plan for development of the Mission System and Payloads at the speed of relevance
* Support design teams in the evolution and progress of Technical Performance Measures (TPMs) and Measures of Effectiveness (MoEs) throughout the design process
* Contribute to System Design Reviews (SDR) and engineering governance structures such as Preliminary Design Reviews (PDR), Critical Design Reviews (CDR), Test Readiness Reviews (TRR), Safety Review Boards (SRB) representing the Mission Engineering discipline
* Own and maintain Mission Threads, operational scenarios and the mission-level decomposition that drives system requirements
* Develop key Mission Engineering artefacts including the Mission Engineering Analysis Plan, Investigation and Analysis Results, and Assurance Recommendations
* Run operational analysis of mission scenarios, using simulation, wargaming and analysis of alternatives where they best answer the question
* Recommend and prioritise development features for each release, justified against customer priorities and the minimum deployable capability, and present the rationale to the customer
* Champion the use of the Test and Evaluation Master Plan (TEMP) so that test events are designed around mission threads and measures of success
* Shape the design of simulation and Software In The Loop (SIL) environments used for mission analysis, and input into surrogate testing
* Create issue board workflows and coordinate ticket triage, action assignment, artefact capture and closure
* Support wholistic Systems Safety and Airworthiness efforts, including identifying hazardous scenarios for the autonomy assurance process
* Support ground and flight test of prototype and production representative Mission Systems, analysing results against mission threads and measures of success

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* BEng/BSc/MEng in Aerospace, Mechanical, Electrical, Computer Science, Robotics, Mechatronics, Operational Research, Mathematics, or a similar engineering field, or equivalent experience
* 7+ years industry Systems Engineering or Operational Analysis experience in the Defence or Aerospace industries, specifically crewed aircraft, rotorcraft, EVTOL, UAS or weapon systems with a focus on Payload Integration, Mission Systems, GNC or Autonomy.
* Demonstrable Mission Engineering or Operational Analysis proficiency, including solicitation of requirements with the customer/end-user, collaboration with design teams
* Experience developing Mission Engineering artefacts, including mission threads, concepts of operation and pragmatic analysis plans
* Experience running scenario-based analysis, simulation or wargaming that has informed design or capability decisions
* Deep understanding of the test and evaluation process as applicable to developmental, acceptance and operational testing
* Experience with crewed or uncrewed developmental autonomous aircraft (Group 5 UAS or larger)
* Outstanding written and verbal communication skills to include presentation of developed artefacts
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active UKSV Security Clearance

### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Military operational experience, for example in aviation, ISTAR or fires
* Experience with open mission system architectures, for example PYRAMID, OMS/UCI, or STANAG 4586, and with modular open systems approaches more generally
* Experience with mission engineering methods, for example the US DoD Mission Engineering Guide, and with constructive simulation tools
* Working knowledge of UK export control and security regimes, including the UK Military List, SPIRE and OGELs, F680, and the handling of classified and OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE material
* Exposure to guidelines, standards and processes such as ARP-4754, ARP-4761, JSP 936 and Def Stan 00-56 desirable
* Experience transitioning technology out of development and into a delivery programme or product line
* Familiarity with reliability engineering, safety analysis, and failure mode analysis

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
