## About the Team

The Battlespace Awareness (BA) Radar team develops radar systems that address national security challenges for America and its allies. We own the full product lifecycle, from concepts and architectures through fielding and sustainment. Working across hardware, software, and firmware, in both the RF and digital domains, we turn raw sensor data into detections, measurements, tracks, and useful mission decisions.

## About the Job

We are looking for a Staff Software Engineer to own substantial radar software subsystems and lead the delivery of algorithm capabilities. You will act as a technical lead and hands-on contributor, connecting radar performance, software architecture, hardware behavior, and mission requirements while coordinating dependencies with adjacent teams.

The work requires moving comfortably between analysis, implementation, and troubleshooting. You might investigate why a radar missed a target in the field, prototype a processing improvement, review a real-time data pipeline, or work with firmware and RF engineers to isolate a calibration or timing problem. You will use technical depth and practical intuition to decide which design, analysis, and test steps are needed, then lead the work through to a demonstrated capability.

This role extends beyond an individual algorithm or software module. Within your area, you will shape designs and engineering practices, lead multi-quarter efforts involving one or two teams, mentor engineers, and develop shared technology where it benefits related radar products. Our software uses C++ and CUDA on embedded Linux, with Python for analysis and prototyping and FPGA-based processing where appropriate. Strong candidates may come from radar software, applied radar/RF research, signal processing, or related sensing systems, provided they can contribute to operational software and own technical delivery.

## What You'll Do

* Own significant radar software subsystems and algorithm capabilities from requirements and architecture through implementation, test, field evaluation, and sustainment.
* Translate mission needs and observed performance limitations into clear technical objectives, acceptance criteria, and multi-quarter delivery plans for connected components, aligned with product priorities.
* Lead data analysis and system troubleshooting for the capabilities you own, tracing relevant interactions with RF hardware, calibration, timing, digital processing, detection, tracking, and external interfaces.
* Prototype algorithms, build analysis tools, and contribute to the implementation, review, and debugging of production software.
* Make architecture and implementation trades for your subsystems across CPUs, GPUs, and FPGAs, balancing numerical behavior, latency, throughput, data movement, reliability, and development effort.
* Work with research scientists, RF and electrical engineers, FPGA and firmware engineers, and software teams to integrate capabilities and resolve system-level limitations.
* Guide experiments, data collections, and laboratory or field demonstrations, using measured results to verify improvements and determine the next iteration.
* Develop shared processing components and interfaces where they improve reuse across related radar products, while preserving product-specific needs and operational compatibility.
* Improve numerical validation, automated regression tests, observability, and reproducible data replay for the systems you own, helping adjacent teams adopt useful practices; turn field failures into durable fixes and useful tests.
* Mentor mid-level and senior engineers, and work with technical leads and program partners to communicate decisions, evidence, risks, and priorities clearly.

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
