Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

# About the role

Anduril Industries builds advanced technology for national security, pairing autonomous systems with a software-defined approach to hardware. Our Battlespace Awareness team develops the radar sensing that lets our systems detect, track, and understand what is happening in contested environments.

As a Staff Software Engineer on the Radar team, you own significant subsystems end to end and lead multi-quarter work across one or two teams. This is a technical-lead and staff-IC role: you stay hands-on in the code while setting architecture, diagnosing hard integrated-system problems, and mentoring the engineers around you. We look for an applied domain authority who knows what the processing should produce and can diagnose why the real system differs, not only a narrowly specialized software architect.

# What you'll do

* Own significant radar subsystems or connected capabilities through requirements, architecture, implementation, integration, and evaluation.
* Lead multi-quarter work spanning one or two teams: decompose ambiguous objectives, coordinate dependencies, and make defensible trade-offs.
* Stay hands-on in the code: inspect, implement, review, and debug relevant software, using Python or MATLAB-style analysis and working effectively in a production C++ stack.
* Diagnose integrated-system problems end to end using data, instrumentation, logs, or replay, distinguishing hardware, calibration, timing, algorithm, and implementation causes, and verifying the fix.
* Reason about architecture, state, interfaces, numerical behavior, concurrency, and CPU/GPU/FPGA constraints for the subsystems you own, drawing on specialists while keeping ownership of integration and the delivered capability.
* Mentor mid-level and senior engineers, align with adjacent technical leads and managers, and communicate progress, risks, and limitations plainly.

# Required qualifications

* Deep expertise in radar, RF sensing, or related physical-sensor DSP and estimation, with a working understanding of neighboring components. You can explain expected behavior, assumptions, limitations, and interactions across a sensing chain.
* Hands-on software and analysis: you have contributed to operational numerical or systems software and can still inspect, implement, review, and debug relevant code. Delegation alone does not establish this.
* End-to-end diagnosis: a concrete example of using data, instrumentation, logs, or replay to isolate an integrated-system problem and verify a fix.
* System and implementation judgment: you can reason about architecture, state, interfaces, numerical behavior, concurrency, and CPU/GPU/FPGA constraints for the subsystems you own.
* Staff-level technical ownership: you have owned significant subsystems or connected capabilities through the full lifecycle and led multi-quarter work involving one or two teams. Org-wide domain ownership is not required.
* Leadership and clear communication: you mentor engineers, align with adjacent leads, handle disagreement constructively, and change decisions when the evidence warrants.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Top Secret SCI security clearance

# Preferred qualifications

* **Radar processing and resource management:** detection, estimation, clutter and noise processing, beamforming, tracking, scheduling, or calibration. Depth in every area is not required.
* **RF and measurement intuition:** antenna behavior, gain and compression, timing, coordinate frames, and uncertainty that connect code to physical performance.
* **Field experimentation:** you have planned a collection or demonstration, analyzed the outcomes, and turned findings into technical work.
* **Accelerated and embedded processing:** CUDA/GPU, FPGA/HLS, embedded Linux, DMA, or hardware interfaces that make implementation trades more concrete.
* **Shared product technology:** you have improved reusable numerical components, interfaces, or tooling across multiple products without unnecessary abstraction.
* **Mentoring senior engineers:** you can guide technically capable colleagues who need deeper domain context, not just supervise junior task execution.
* **Customer and program engagement:**you turn operational needs into technical objectives and explain capability, evidence, and limitations credibly.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
