**ABOUT THE TEAM**

We are looking for a highly skilled **Metrology Specialist** to program, optimize, and lead complex inspections across a wide variety of products that we design and develop. In this role you will write, debug, and optimize inspection programs across contact and non-contact systems, troubleshoot the most difficult measurement problems, and help shape how the lab does its work. As the Metrology lab grows, you will work across a range of equipment — Portable CMM, CNC/fixed CMM, CT scanner, laser tracker, and optical systems from manufacturers such as Hexagon, Zeiss, Keyence, and LK Metrology — supporting incoming quality inspections, internally manufactured components, quality investigations, and root cause analysis, while mentoring other members of the team. 

**WHAT YOU’LL DO** 

* Program and operate metrology equipment across contact and non-contact systems to validate that parts meet requirements.
* Write, debug, and optimize inspection programs to improve accuracy, repeatability, and cycle time.
* Troubleshoot complex measurement issues — determining why an inspection is failing or producing inconsistent results — and resolve process-level problems.
* Design, build, and validate holding fixtures used on the CMMs.
* Work closely with the Quality Engineering team to support problem-solving, root cause analysis, and product design.
* Define best-practice inspection and reporting strategies with cross-functional teams.
* Maintain and improve lab equipment and processes (calibration, cleanliness, best practices), reducing downtime and ensuring data reliability.
* Train and mentor other metrology team members and serve as the go-to resource for difficult measurement problems.
* Help ensure compliance with industry standards (e.g., AS9100, ISO 17025).
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams, which requires regular interaction with production and manufacturing stakeholders and frequently working directly on the production floor.

 

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS** 

* 5+ years of metrology experience, including programming CMMs.
* Hands-on experience with at least three different types of metrology equipment (CNC CMM, portable CMM, laser tracker, large-volume or automated 3D scanners, optical comparator, etc.).
* Proficiency with CMM software; PolyWorks preferred (PC-DMIS, Calypso, Verisurf, MCosmos, or equivalent acceptable with the ability to learn PolyWorks quickly).
* Excellent understanding of GD&T (ASME Y14.5).
* Experience designing and building holding fixtures for CMMs.
* Proficiency with precision measuring equipment (micrometers, height gauges, and other hand tools).
* Strong analytical skills, attention to detail, and excellent communication skills.
* Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. security clearance.
* U.S. citizenship is required pursuant to contractual obligations.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS** 

* Associate’s degree or technical training.
* Experience with advanced or specialized inspection methods, including NDT.
* Experience with CT scanning and automated or large-volume 3D scanning.
* Experience with PolyWorks|DataLoop or similar metrology data management platforms.
* Familiarity with AS9100 or ISO 17025 quality systems.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
