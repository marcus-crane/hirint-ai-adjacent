### ABOUT THE JOB

As a Mid-Level Oracle EPM Analyst, you will serve as a hands-on functional and technical contributor across Oracle EPM Cloud modules including Enterprise Planning and Budgeting (EPBCS), Financial Consolidation and Close (FCCS), Account Reconciliation (ARCS), Enterprise Data Management (EDMCS), Narrative Reporting, and Profitability and Cost Management. Your core focus areas will be Planning and Consolidations processes, with significant responsibilities in dimension and metadata management, financial and management reporting, and data exchange and integration. You will collaborate closely with finance stakeholders, IT partners, and external consultants to support month-end close, annual budgeting, long-range planning, and ad-hoc analysis cycles.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* **Planning (EPBCS)**

+ Design, build, and maintain planning applications including operating expense plans, revenue forecasts, workforce planning, and capital budgets.
+ Develop and optimize business rules (Groovy and calculation scripts) to support allocation logic, driver-based models, and rolling forecasts.
+ Configure data forms, task lists, approval workflows, and Smart View templates to streamline the budgeting and forecasting process for end users.
+ Partner with FP&A business leads to gather requirements and translate them into scalable application design.

* **Consolidations (FCCS)**

+ Support the monthly, quarterly, and annual financial consolidation cycle including intercompany eliminations, currency translation, ownership adjustments, and journal processing.
+ Maintain and troubleshoot consolidation rules, configurable calculations, and equity pickup logic.
+ Collaborate with Corporate Accounting to ensure accurate statutory and management consolidations in line with US GAAP or IFRS requirements.
+ Assist with close-process automation using FCCS task manager and supplemental data collection.

* **Account Reconciliation (ARCS)**

+ Administer reconciliation profiles, matching rules, and transaction-matching configurations to support balance sheet substantiation.
+ Work with accounting teams to onboard new reconciliations and continuously improve automation rates.

* **Dimension & Metadata Management**

+ Serve as a steward of the enterprise dimension library across EPM Cloud applications, ensuring consistency of Entity, Account, Currency, Custom dimensions, and Smart Lists.
+ Manage metadata load files, hierarchies, and attribute mappings; perform impact analysis before structural changes are promoted to production.
+ Leverage Enterprise Data Management Cloud Service (EDMCS) for governance workflows, change requests, viewpoints, and cross-application metadata synchronization.
+ Maintain naming conventions, documentation, and metadata standards in alignment with the broader data governance framework.

* **Data Exchange & Integration**

+ Design and manage data integration jobs using EPM Automate, Data Management (FDMEE / Data Integration), REST APIs, and flat-file interfaces to load actuals, budgets, rates, and reference data.
+ Build and maintain inbound and outbound data mappings, transformation rules, and validation checks between EPM Cloud and source systems (ERP, HCM, data warehouse).
+ Monitor integration health, troubleshoot load failures, and implement exception-handling and reconciliation controls.
+ Automate recurring data workflows using EPM Automate scripts and scheduling tools to reduce manual intervention during close and planning cycles.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 3-5 years of experience in EPM system administration, development, or analysis, within a fast-paced environment.
* Foundational expertise with Planning within the Oracle Cloud EPM suite (PBCS, FCCS, ARCS)
* Ability to operate with limited oversight on assigned tasks and seek guidance on more complex issues or strategies.
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with meticulous attention to detail.
* A solid understanding of core financial concepts (e.g., budgeting, forecasting, financial reporting, general ledger).
* Excellent communication and collaboration skills to work effectively with finance and technical stakeholders.
* Appreciation for and adherence to engineering best practices, such as version control, audit-ability, and scalability.
* Must be authorized to work in the United States.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
