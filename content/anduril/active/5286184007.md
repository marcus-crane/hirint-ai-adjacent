**ABOUT THE JOB**  
  
As the ISSO for a large-scale Army Command & Control (C2) program, you will serve as the hands-on security practitioner responsible for day-to-day compliance execution and vulnerability management across a cloud-native platform. You'll work directly under the ISSM to manage CVE remediation workflows, review third-party vendor security artifacts, maintain authorization documentation in eMASS, and enforce continuous monitoring across multiple environments running on a Kubernetes-based PaaS.  
  
**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Track and coordinate CVE remediation across all program environments using automated container vulnerability scanning, enforcing SLA timelines with owning engineering teams
* Review third-party vendor onboarding security packages: container scan results, SAST reports, CSSP audit logging documents, POA&Ms, SBOMs, STIG checklists, and network/data flow diagrams
* Validate STIG compliance using SCAP, Evaluate-STIG, and review against DISA STIGs for containerized applications and Kubernetes infrastructure
* Manage and audit user access controls through the platform's role-based permission model, enforcing least privilege and RBAC across all environments
* Monitor security events through coordination with the program's Cybersecurity Service Provider (CSSP), which runs 24/7 monitoring; review audit logs, investigate anomalies, and escalate per incident response procedures
* Maintain all security artifacts in eMASS including POA&Ms, risk assessments, authorization letters, and continuous monitoring evidence across multiple eMASS records
* Review platform Change Requests for security impact
* Support RMF activities including security control assessments, SCA evidence collection, and ATO package preparation
* Conduct weekly CVE expiry reviews, compile per-team remediation lists, and coordinate bulk outreach to engineering teams
* Assist with ISA development and review for system interconnections and Cross-Domain Solution paths

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 5+ years of experience in cybersecurity or information assurance with hands-on compliance execution in a DoD environment
* DoD 8570/8140 compliance at IAT Level II or higher (e.g., Security+, CySA+, or CCNA Security)
* Working knowledge of NIST 800-53 security controls, DISA STIGs, and RMF processes
* Experience with vulnerability scanning tools and CVE remediation tracking workflows
* Experience managing user access controls and conducting account/access reviews
* Currently possesses and is able to maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience with container security scanning (Trivy, Grype, or similar) in Kubernetes environments
* Familiarity with cloud PaaS platforms in a DoD context (IL2 through IL6)
* Experience with eMASS for RMF package management and continuous monitoring
* Hands-on experience reviewing third-party vendor security artifacts (SBOMs, SAST/DAST reports, POA&Ms) for onboarding decisions
* Familiarity with Cross-Domain Solutions and multi-enclave environments
* CompTIA CySA+, CEH, or GIAC certifications

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
