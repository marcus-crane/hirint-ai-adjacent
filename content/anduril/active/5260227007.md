**About the Job**

We are looking to hire a **Systems Safety Engineer** to join our rapidly growing team in Broomfield, CO. The Battlespace Awareness team develops tactical sense- and decision-making software that provides advanced capabilities for Anduril's command and control (C2) products. This software must work with various combinations of compute hardware, connected sensor/effector hardware, and supporting software services. It must be effective, robust, and **safe** across various configurations, mission sets, and customer experiences.

Because our C2 products issue tasking to kinetic and non-kinetic effectors, integrate with autonomous platforms, and operate in human-on-the-loop and human-in-the-loop decision cycles, safety is a first-class engineering discipline, not a downstream compliance check. We need a Systems Safety Engineer who can own end-to-end safety assurance: from requirements decomposition and hazard analysis through software performance characterization, validation against government standards, and evidence packages that hold up under customer, regulatory, and legal scrutiny.

**What You'll Do**

* **Own systems safety end-to-end** for C2 software and its interfaces to sensors, effectors, and autonomous platforms — identify and solve problems between the seams and push through to reach desired outcomes
* **Lead safety requirements gathering, decomposition, and traceability** across cross-functional teams, translating mission-, system-, and government-level requirements into subsystem-level functional, performance, and safety specifications. Define success in the face of ambiguity
* **Validate that our systems are safe** — architect and execute performance-characterization campaigns for safety-critical software behaviors (classification, sensor/effector management, threat forecasting, autonomous tasking) using both simulated data and field-testing data
* **Apply industry and government safety standards** rigorously — including **MIL-STD-882E** (DoD System Safety) and adjacent frameworks, where relevant.
* **Conduct and document safety analyses**: Functional Hazard Analysis (FHA), **Fault Tree Analysis (FTA)**, Failure Modes & Effects Analysis (FMEA/FMECA), Preliminary Hazard Analysis (PHA), System Safety Assessment (SSA), and Common Cause Analysis (CCA).
* **Characterize software-driven performance for safety** — quantify false-track rates, misclassification consequences, latency-under-load, degraded-mode behavior, and human-machine teaming failure modes; translate results into risk assessments and mitigation strategies that protect the warfighter *and* limit liability exposure to Anduril.
* **Validate compliance with government requirements** — partner with program offices (Army, MDA, Air Force, Navy) to establish means of compliance, author certification and verification artifacts, and represent Anduril at safety review boards and design reviews (SRR, PDR, CDR, TRR).
* **Develop test plans, acceptance criteria, and analysis tools** to evaluate system safety and performance; use data-driven analysis to verify customer safety requirements are met, and create artifacts to trace safety performance as the system evolves
* **Integrate safety up front** into the development lifecycle — not as a side group, but as a discipline embedded with algorithm engineers, software engineers, and product teams; build lightweight, high-velocity safety processes that match Anduril's product cadence without compromising rigor.
* **Develop and implement modeling and simulation frameworks** to validate safety-critical algorithmic performance, test system behavior under nominal and adversarial scenarios, and provide data-driven insights for continuous safety improvement

**Required Qualifications**

* **5+ years** of systems safety, systems engineering, or safety-critical software engineering experience. Degree in Engineering, Physics, Computer Science, Mathematics, or a related field
* **Demonstrated application of MIL-STD-882** (preferably 882E) across a defense program lifecycle — including hazard tracking, risk assessment codes, and closure of safety-significant items.
* **Hands-on experience performing Fault Tree Analysis (FTA)** and at least two of: FHA, FMEA/FMECA, PHA, SSA, CCA — with artifacts you can walk us through.
* **Requirements engineering fluency** — decomposition, traceability, and verification/validation. Comfortable translating vague customer intent into testable safety requirements
* **Experience validating that safety and performance requirements are actually met** — through analysis, simulation, HITL/SITL, and field testing

* Proficiency with **Python** (and/or MATLAB) for data parsing, modeling & simulation, data analysis, and visualization of safety and performance evidence
* Demonstrated experience collaborating across cross-functional teams and communicating complex technical findings clearly to developers, system leads, and customers
* Comfortable working with ambiguity, evolving priorities, and challenging problems

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
