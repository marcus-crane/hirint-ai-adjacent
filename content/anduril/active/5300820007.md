### ABOUT THE JOB

The **Technical Security Application Engineer, Special Projects**, is a hands-on technical role responsible for the installation, configuration, troubleshooting, and maintenance of physical access control systems (PACS) and video surveillance systems (VMS) across Anduril's more austere sites, with a particular focus on Test Sites, Executive Residences, and temporary spaces. This is a specialist role focused on system hardware, application-level configuration, and day-to-day technical problem solving.  
In addition to hands-on system work, you'll support small-to-medium technical security projects, coordinate with vendors and integrators, and communicate progress and issues to stakeholders. You'll work closely with senior team members on larger deployments while owning the reliability and performance of the systems in your area of responsibility. A working knowledge of IP networking is essential, as these systems are network-dependent.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Design, deploy, and sustain technical security systems for non-traditional environments, including test sites, executive residences, event spaces, and temporary sites, where permanent infrastructure and corporate network connectivity may not exist.
* Conduct site surveys and threat assessments to define detection, assessment, and response requirements for each environment, and translate them into deployable designs, bills of material, and installation packages.
* Design and commission intrusion detection and perimeter systems, including interior and exterior sensors, fence detection, ground-based radar, and video analytics detection zones.
* Work with the TechSec Systems team in prototyping, assessment, and selection of AI-enabled video analytics and alarm monitoring to detect, classify, and verify threats; and tune detection rules to reduce false alarms without degrading detection performance.
* Define alarm verification, escalation, and response workflows in coordination with the Security Operations Center and site security teams.
* Survey, design, and deploy wireless backhaul, including point-to-point and point-to-multipoint links, with link budget analysis, line-of-sight and Fresnel zone validation, and link encryption.
* Integrate cellular and satellite connectivity as primary or failover communication paths for sites without wired infrastructure.
* Develop and maintain standardized rapid-deploy kits, including trailer-mounted and pole-mounted surveillance and detection units, with documented deployment, teardown, and asset recovery procedures.
* Partner with the Executive Protection team to design and support residential security systems that meet protective requirements while respecting the privacy, aesthetics, and daily use of principals and their families.
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams, which requires regular interaction with test site management teams and executive protection stakeholders.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 5+ years of hands-on experience designing, installing, and troubleshooting intrusion detection (including wireless intrusion sensors) and video surveillance systems.
* Experience programming and commissioning commercial intrusion panels and exterior perimeter detection systems.
* Hands-on experience designing and troubleshooting wireless point-to-point or point-to-multipoint networks.
* Experience configuring and tuning video analytics or AI-based detection to manage false alarm rates.
* Ability to read and interpret wiring diagrams, floor plans, and technical documentation.
* Experience working with vendors and integrators on installations and service.
* Strong communication skills with the ability to update stakeholders on technical work and project status.
* Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and a proactive problem-solving mindset.
* Familiarity with low-voltage cabling standards and structured cabling best practices.
* Ability to travel extensively, up to 40%, and work in outdoor, remote, and after-hours conditions.
* U.S. citizenship is required pursuant to contractual obligations.

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 7+ years of experience in technical security systems, with a strong focus on intrusion detection and perimeter security.
* Experience with Genetec Security Center.
* Manufacturer certifications such as Bosch intrusion, Axis, or Hanwha.
* Experience with remote video monitoring or alarm monitoring center operations, including familiarity with ANSI/TMA AVS-01 and UL 827.
* Experience with ground-based radar, fence detection, or thermal imaging systems.
* Experience supporting executive protection, residential security, or major event security operations.
* Experience designing off-grid power systems or deploying mobile surveillance trailers.

* Existing active SECRET clearance.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
