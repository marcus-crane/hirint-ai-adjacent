**ABOUT THE TEAM**

Anduril’s Manufacturing Automation team is seeking a Mechanical Engineer to join our team. The Manufacturing Automation team is responsible for the design, integration, and deployment of automated manufacturing systems across Anduril factories. As a member of this team, you will have the opportunity to help define what production automation looks like for Anduril at a national scale.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Lead the mechanical design of automated manufacturing equipment, from initial concept through commissioning and handover
* Develop system-level designs, simulations, and engineering studies to de-risk concepts and fully define overall equipment requirements
* Perform simulations for robotic systems and equipment, including reach studies and system architecture development
* Design, build, and integrate fixtures and assembly tooling for automation applications
* Design, build, and integrate EOAT for industrial robots / cobots
* Design equipment utilizing mechanical, electrical, pneumatic, and hydraulic power systems
* Design and integrate safety guarding, light curtains, interlocks, and other safety-related mechanical components
* Create detailed 3D models, 2D manufacturing drawings, and BOMs
* Select appropriate mechanical components, including bearings, linear motion systems, actuators, gearboxes, fasteners, etc.
* Perform mechanical analysis, including stress, strain, and thermal analysis
* Oversee prototype fabrication, assembly, and testing of mechanical designs
* Troubleshoot and resolve mechanical issues during design, commissioning, and production rollout
* Collaborate with electrical engineers, controls engineers, and robotics engineers to ensure seamless integration of mechanical systems

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, or similar field
* 4+ years of experience in mechanical design, with a significant focus on automation, machinery, or robotics
* Strong 3D CAD capability in NX
* Proficient 2D CAD capability in AutoCAD
* Basic understanding of mechanical components commonly used in automation, such as linear actuators, rotary actuators, servo motors, VFDs, gears, bearings, pneumatics, etc.
* Experience with fabrication processes including machining, welding, sheet metal fabrication, and additive manufacturing
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export controlled information or facilities

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 6+ years of experience in mechanical design, with a significant focus on automation, machinery, or robotics
* Strong ability to perform mechanical calculations (statics, dynamics, material selection, etc.)
* Experience performing FEA
* Strong 2D CAD capability in AutoCAD
* Experience with motion studies, tolerance stack-ups, and design simulation
* Experience designing and fabricating tooling such as mandrels, jigs, and fixtures

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
