**ABOUT THE JOB**

We are seeking a Product Data Quality co-op for winter 2027! This is a paid, in-person co-op. Product data quality is how we know a 3D model and its annotations (the geometry, tolerances, and manufacturing notes that define a part) are complete, unambiguous, and usable by every team downstream. On Maritime Digital Production, you will build and run automated checks on model-based product definitions, measure where data quality breaks down between engineering, manufacturing, and quality, and help close those gaps. You will work with the engineers who author the models, the developers who build our manufacturing systems, and the technicians who build and inspect our vehicles.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Receive problem statements, guidelines, requirements, and reporting milestones
* Define and automate quality checks on CAD models: valid geometry, complete and machine-readable (semantic) PMI, correct GD&T, and consistent model organization and metadata
* Build a data quality scorecard that reports pass/fail and PMI coverage by part, assembly, and release, so issues are fixed before release
* Trace product definition data (features, PMI, part identifiers) from design into work instructions, inspection plans, and shop floor systems, and document where data is lost or reinterpreted
* Perform trade studies on validation approaches and neutral data formats, and propose improvements for approval
* Test, verify, and demonstrate your solutions with the engineers and technicians who use them

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Must be pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Systems Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
* Must be returning to school at the end of the internship to continue academic studies for at least one quarter or semester.
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export-controlled information or facilities; U.S. clearance eligibility may be required depending on program.
* Ability to complete an in-person 16-24 week internship in Santa Ana, CA, in winter 2027.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Working knowledge of GD&T and engineering drawings (ASME Y14.5 or ISO GPS)
* Experience with a 3D CAD system (such as NX, SolidWorks, CATIA, or Creo)
* Coursework or project experience with model-based definition, PMI authoring, or digital thread concepts
* Familiarity with ASME Y14.41 and Y14.47, MIL-STD-31000, STEP AP242, JT, or QIF
* Scripting experience in any language (such as Python), including CAD APIs or data processing
* Exposure to PLM, MES, or ERP systems (such as Teamcenter)
* Previous experience leading a project and working with data collection, analysis, and driving improvements
* Strong technical communication skills, with the ability to brief technical leadership on conclusions
