Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### **ABOUT THE TEAM**

The Global Security Operations Team safeguards our people, mission-critical technology, and operational footprint through an integrated, intelligence-led security framework. Our remit spans physical security infrastructure, Global Security Operations Centers (GSOC), guard force management, crisis response, counterintelligence, and comprehensive risk management. We operate at the intersection of commercial speed and defense-grade security, enabling rapid innovation and operational freedom across domestic and global environments.

### **ABOUT THE JOB**

As our footprint expands across commercial and defense markets, field deployments, and strategic demonstrations, we are seeking an autonomous, mission-driven Global Event Security Lead. In this role, you will own the physical security lifecycle and threat management for domestic and international events, major defense trade exhibitions, field trials, and off-site customer demonstrations. Your core objective is to design, implement, and lead robust security architectures that safeguard personnel, proprietary hardware, and sensitive information in diverse and dynamic environments.  
This is a senior individual contributor role requiring high autonomy, strong analytical acumen in threat and vulnerability assessments, cross-functional operational leadership, and deep expertise in event security management across domestic and global venues.

#### **WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* **Lead Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Act as the dedicated security business partner to **Events, Marketing, Communications, BD, and Workplace**, embedding early into planning cycles to integrate physical access, badging, and venue security into project timelines.
* **End-to-End Event Security Execution:** Independently design, project-manage, and execute physical security operational plans for major domestic and international defense trade shows (e.g., AUSA, Paris Air Show, DSEI), customer demonstrations, and executive off-sites.
* **Site & Facility Security Assessments (PSVAs):** Conduct formal Physical Security Vulnerability Assessments, threat analyses, and site surveys for event venues, static displays, hotels, transit corridors, and **Anduril satellite offices, test ranges, and regional facilities** globally.
* **Hardware & ITAR Asset Protection:** Partner with **Logistics, Trade Compliance, and Engineering** to design secure display footprints, anti-tamper protocols, overnight monitoring, and physical chain-of-custody measures for proprietary technology, Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), and ITAR/EAR-controlled platforms.
* **On-Site Operational Leadership:** Serve as the lead security operator on the ground during major activations—managing access control points, resolving venue-level issues, and supervising contract guard forces seamlessly in the background.
* **Vendor & Resource Management:** Directly scope, vet, contract, and oversee third-party security guard forces, technical access providers, and specialized event security vendors worldwide.
* **External Stakeholder Liaison:** Establish operational coordination with venue managers, show organizers, local law enforcement, emergency services, and host-nation authorities (including U.S. Embassy Regional Security Officers abroad).
* **Traveler Safety & Intelligence Integration:** Partner with internal Threat Intelligence to assess location-specific risks and deliver actionable, practical security briefs for attending Anduril staff and executives.
* **Scale Security Workflows:** Conduct post-event reviews with internal partners, track vendor performance, and iterate on Anduril’s event security playbooks, checklists, and access standards to support company growth.

#### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* **Experience:** 5+ years of progressive physical security, event security, or site security experience within corporate defense/aerospace/tech, high-end event operations, or relevant law enforcement/government security environments.
* **Independent Execution (IC3):** Demonstrated track record of independently managing end-to-end physical security projects and events from initial site survey through post-event wrap-up with minimal oversight.
* **Cross-Functional Partnership:** Proven ability to build collaborative, productive relationships with non-security business partners (Event Managers, Brand/Marketing, Executive Assistants, Real Estate teams).
* **Physical Security Assessments:** Demonstrated experience conducting structured Physical Security Vulnerability Assessments (PSVAs) for facilities, dynamic venues, and corporate offices.
* **Vendor & Contractor Oversight:** Practical experience scoping, onboarding, and managing contract security guard forces and physical security vendors.
* **Asset & Access Control Fundamentals:** Solid working knowledge of physical access control systems (PACS), perimeter hardening, and the handling/display requirements for proprietary technologies and ITAR/EAR defense articles.
* **Travel Flexibility:** Willingness and ability to travel **30%–50%** (domestically and internationally) aligned with events and site assessment schedules.
* **Clearance Eligibility:** Must be a U.S. Person (U.S. Citizen or Lawful Permanent Resident) due to ITAR requirements, with the ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. Government Security Clearance.

#### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Active **U.S. Secret or Top Secret / SCI** security clearance.
* Industry certifications such as **Physical Security Professional (PSP)**, **Certified Protection Professional (CPP)**, or **Project Management Professional (PMP)**.
* Direct experience securing major defense/aerospace exhibitions (e.g., AUSA, Sea-Air-Space, Paris Air Show, DSEI, Farnborough).
* Formal training in threat modeling, protective intelligence, or physical security assessment methodologies.
* Established networks with domestic public safety agencies and international bodies (e.g., Overseas Security Advisory Council - OSAC / DSAC).

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
