# Deployment Coordination Analyst

The Deployment Coordination Analyst is the organizational subject matter expert responsible for ensuring end-to-end deployer readiness across all mission-critical personnel. You will own the comprehensive predeployment lifecycle, from credentialing and medical clearance to training coordination and logistical support, ensuring seamless compliance with DoD standards, contract requirements, and international travel regulations. This role demands exceptional organizational skills, strategic process improvement thinking, and the ability to build trusted relationships across internal teams, government agencies, and external partners.

## Key Responsibilities

### Deployment Requirements & Compliance Management

* **Company-Wide Coordination:** Serve as the primary SME for all deployment requirements including credentialing, authorizations, security clearances, contract stipulations, regulatory standards, and international travel policies
* **External Stakeholder Management:** Build and maintain relationships with government agencies, military organizations, and external contractors to align administrative strategies and ensure deployment readiness
* **New Hire Integration:** Coordinate deployment readiness briefings for new hires, ensuring they understand requirements and processes before first deployment
* **Compliance Oversight:** Ensure all deployers meet federal, DoD, contract, and international travel requirements with meticulous attention to detail

### Predeployment Readiness Program

* **Program Development:** Develop and manage comprehensive predeployment readiness program ensuring training, clearances, documentation, and medical readiness are consistently met across the organization
* **Medical Readiness Coordination:** Manage medical readiness program, coordinating with vendors, scheduling appointments, and ensuring all deployers have necessary medical documentation and clearances
* **Training & Documentation Oversight:** Oversee critical predeployment processes including required training completion, personnel data collection, and administrative data integration
* **Tracking Systems:** Design, build, and maintain robust tracking systems and databases for deployer status, medical records, training completion, and credential validity

### Deployer Support & Relationship Management

* **Operational Support:** Track, manage, and proactively support deployer needs throughout the deployment lifecycle, building trust to understand and meet requirements
* **Cross-Functional Coordination:** Serve as primary liaison between Mission Operations, Technical Operations, and Deployment Operations teams to ensure seamless deployer lifecycle management
* **Stakeholder Relationships:** Build and maintain trusted relationships with deployers, ensuring their needs are consistently and empathetically met throughout deployment lifecycle
* **Sensitive Information Handling:** Manage highly sensitive and confidential information (medical records, clearances, incident reports) with utmost discretion and integrity

### Strategic Projects & Process Improvement

* **Travel Program Enhancement:** Lead creation and implementation of robust travel program consolidating travel scheduling, compliance measures, and expense management into unified system
* **Technology Integration:** Facilitate data platform usage through workshops and direct support; drive ongoing integrations for scheduling, dashboard visibility, and training programs
* **Incident Management:** Develop and enforce company-wide incident response strategy ensuring prompt stakeholder notification and efficient resolution of deployment-related issues
* **Process Optimization:** Continuously identify opportunities to streamline predeployment processes, reduce administrative burden, and improve deployer experience

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
