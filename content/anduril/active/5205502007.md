### About the Team

Anduril Electrical Test Engineering deploys test equipment and testing capabilities that support the next generation of Autonomous Vehicles (AVs) designed within Anduril. The Electrical Test Team is embedded within Anduril' s Electrical Engineering team to ensure rapid testing and validation response in design and production while supporting Cognizant Design Engineers taking their products from conception, validation, manufacturing to end of life. We are looking for a candidate to lead testing for our new radio products.

### About the Role

The Senior Test Firmware Engineer sits at the intersection of hardware and software validation within Anduril's Electrical Test team. You'll own the test firmware layer — writing C on microcontrollers that exposes internal hardware for automated testing, and building the Python/LabView frameworks on the host side that drive those tests. Your work directly enables the team to validate Anduril's next-generation radio products from prototype through production. You'll partner closely with Cognizant Design Engineers and the manufacturing team to ensure test coverage keeps pace with rapid hardware iteration. This role is ideal for someone who thrives in a hands-on lab environment, is equally comfortable writing bare-metal C and Python automation scripts, and wants to build test infrastructure that scales across multiple product lines.

**What You'll Do**

* Architect and build a unified test environment consisting of target-side test firmware and host-side Python/LabView execution frameworks.
* Develop C-based MCU test firmware that exposes internal hardware peripherals, drivers, and system functions via serial/bus APIs (UART, CAN, SPI, etc.) for external host control.
* Create reusable abstraction layers and drivers for both on-board microcontroller peripherals and benchtop lab equipment (using PyVISA, SCPI, or vendor APIs).
* Quickly debug and troubleshoot issues that span the electrical, firmware, and software boundaries.
* Engage with the manufacturing and electrical engineering test teams on test coverage and features.
* Participate in schematic and test plan reviews and provide expertise on test firmware capability.
* Maintain automated test suites, analyze results, and work with developers to resolve issues.
* Develop test HITL stations for regression testing.
* Support board bring-up and bench validation as needed.
* Support Design Engineer with DFT, provide feedback from test results to improve quality and test coverage.
* Support manufacture test bring-up and yield.
* Provide technical mentorship to engineering teams.

**Required Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s Degree in Computer Science / Computer Engineering / Electrical Engineering or equivalent.
* 5+ Years of Experience with architecture, design and development in Python.
* Experience writing embedded C for microcontrollers specifically for test instrumentation, register-level access, and exposing test hooks/APIs to a host PC.
* Experience developing or working with Hardware-in-the-Loop (HITL) test systems.
* Familiarity with software development lifecycle (SDLC) and Agile methodologies.
* Experience with test management software (e.g., JIRA) and automated testing tools (e.g., Jenkins, CircleCI).
* Strong understanding of software QA methodologies.
* Experience with Linux operating systems and commands.
* Experience with peripherals and developing test and validation strategies.
* Advanced experience with computer hardware communication bus protocols: USB, I2C, I2S, CSI, Ethernet, SPI, PCIe, HDMI, CAN, serial comms, etc
* Collaborate with a cross-functional team to support product development from prototype to production
* Understanding of lab safety protocols when working in all types of lab environment.
* Familiarity with measurement analysis techniques such as Gage RR and Process Capability Analysis.
* Deep understanding of hardware-software integration and system-level testing methodologies.
* Experience with source code revision and control. Experience with GitHub is a plus.
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export controlled information or facilities.
