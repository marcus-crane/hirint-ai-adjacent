Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**Overview**  
Anduril's ArsenalOS team builds the digital infrastructure that powers how Anduril designs, sources, manufactures, delivers, and sustains hardware at scale. We are not a traditional internal IT organization. We build, buy, customize, integrate, and operate the systems that make Anduril's physical operations faster, more reliable, and more scalable.   
  
We are hiring an Oracle Applications Product Manager to own business requirements, prioritization, and implementation for the following Oracle modules: Transportation Management (OTM), Global Trade Management (GTM), Project Management Information Systems (PMIS), as well as the Redwood UX migration. This role ensures each module lands with clear requirements, a sequenced rollout plan, and strong business partner alignment. You will work across Supply Chain, Material Management, Finance, Construction, and Strategic Operations to understand what these teams actually need from the new modules and translate that into a buildable, prioritized roadmap for our development team.  
  
**What You'll Do**

* Own the product roadmap and requirements backlog for OTM, GTM, PMIS, and Redwood migration workstreams
* Partner with business stakeholders across Supply Chain, Finance, Construction, and Logistics to translate operational needs into prioritized requirements
* Work closely with the ERP engineering team to define scope, sequence sprints, and manage trade-offs across concurrent module rollouts
* Define acceptance criteria and coordinate UAT with end users for each module go-live
* Establish success metrics for each module launch and track adoption post go-live
* Communicate rollout status, risks, and decisions to Cross Functional partners and Arsenal OS leadership
* Work with peer product managers owning other core workstreams (Procure to Pay and Design to Build) to address cross-functional dependencies and deliver low-tech-debt capabilities

**What Success Looks Like**

* All three module rollouts land on schedule with requirements that reflect actual business needs
* Business stakeholders have a clear line of sight into what is shipping, when, and what trade-offs were made
* The ERP engineering team has a clean, prioritized backlog and is not context-switching between competing stakeholder requests
* Redwood migration completes by the July deadline without disrupting active SC workflows
* Post go-live, adoption is tracked and issues are caught early, not discovered months later
* Strong sustainment plan, process ownership, and vision of existing modules and future modules and capabilities needed to address the most pressing business problems.

**Required Characteristics**

* **Product over project:** You own outcomes, not Gantt charts. You care whether the module actually improves the business, not just whether it launched on time.
* **ERP fluency:** You understand how Oracle Cloud modules work, how they connect, and where the gaps are. You can have a technical conversation with a developer and a process conversation with a buyer in the same meeting.
* **Cross-functional credibility:** You can align Supply Chain, Finance, Construction, and Engineering without owning any of them. You earn trust by understanding their problems.
* **Execution focus:** You ship value early, iterate quickly, and measure impact in business outcomes.

**Required Qualifications**

* 5+ years of product management or business systems experience in ERP environments, with direct exposure to Oracle Cloud SCM
* Experience managing multiple concurrent system rollouts with competing stakeholder priorities
* Strong understanding of supply chain, manufacturing, or procurement operations
* Excellent written and verbal communication; ability to convey trade-offs to both technical and business audiences
* U.S. Person status is required as this position needs to access export-controlled data

**Preferred**

* Experience with Oracle OTM, GTM, or PMIS specifically
* Background in defense, aerospace, or hardware manufacturing
* Familiarity with Redwood UX migration paths
* Experience working alongside infrastructure and platform engineering teams
