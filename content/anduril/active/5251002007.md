Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Corporate Technical team is responsible for building and optimizing the many systems and internal technologies that enable Anduril to accomplish its mission. Anduril’s supply chain, accounting, sales & growth, field maintenance, manufacturing, and many more teams rely on the technology built and maintained by us. We call this system-of-systems “CorpOS”. Within CorpOS lies a suite of digital software solutions aimed at solving the problems for our growth, finance, people teams that interplays heavily with our ArsenalOS solutions that enable Anduril’s engineering, production and sustainment departments.

### ABOUT THE JOB

The Finance Solutions Analyst is the hands-on engine behind Oracle Fusion Financials at Anduril. As the business scales across entities, geographies, and programs, the finance platform has to keep pace: new legal entities stood up cleanly, master data kept accurate, issues resolved before they reach the close calendar, and the occasional acquisition absorbed without breaking anything. You'll own much of the configuration, data, and support work that makes that possible, and you'll do it with the pace and ownership expected of a team that runs multiple workstreams in parallel.  
  
This is a builder's role with a broad book of work:

* You'll work primarily in Oracle Fusion Financials, with deep exposure to the core Finance modules and Project Financials, rather than across the full ERP footprint
* Your scope spans planned work (international legal entity enablement, master data updates, enhancements) and run work (production support, quarterly release testing), and you'll balance both in a sprint-based delivery model
* You'll work shoulder-to-shoulder with Accounting, Controllership, and the Finance Center of Excellence, and coordinate with the teams that own Supply Chain capabilities where their processes touch Finance

### WHAT YOU'LL DO:

* Own hands-on configuration, maintenance, and enhancement of Oracle Fusion Financials across General Ledger, Accounts Payable, Accounts Receivable, Fixed Assets, Cash Management, Subledger Accounting, and Project Financials
* Lead legal entity enablement for international expansion across core Finance modules: ledger and business unit setup, intercompany design, currency and calendar configuration, tax and bank setup, and security, translating operational, accounting, and legal requirements into application design decisions
* Execute Finance and Projects master data updates, including chart of accounts values and hierarchies, cost centers, project templates, project and task structures, and related reference data, following established governance
* Provide production support for Oracle Fusion Financials: triage and resolve incidents, perform root cause analysis, monitor scheduled processes and interfaces, and support month-end and year-end close
* Support M&A integrations as one workstream within the broader portfolio: entity setup, data conversion, mapping, validation, and Day 1 readiness for acquired businesses
* Own data conversion and loading for entity and master data work: extract source data, map to Oracle templates, validate quality, and leverage AI tools to eliminate manual work and build repeatable processes for future efforts
* Plan and execute testing cycles, including unit, integration, and user acceptance testing, and run regression testing for Oracle quarterly updates, resolving defects with technical teams and Oracle Support
* Manage intake, prioritization, and status of requests in Jira (or equivalent), keeping the backlog clean, estimates realistic, and stakeholders informed
* Partner with Accounting, Controllership, and the Finance CoE to gather requirements, assess impacts, and deliver well-documented, tested solutions rather than one-off workarounds
* Coordinate with Supply Chain system owners only where their processes create dependencies for Finance, escalating cross-team issues early so Finance timelines hold
* Operate in an agile, sprint-based delivery model: own your workstream, hit sprint milestones, surface blockers early, and keep programs moving without waiting for direction
* Maintain configuration documentation, standard operating procedures, and the Finance knowledge base (e.g., Airtable and Quip), capturing design decisions and lessons learned so future efforts start further ahead
* Support audit and compliance activities by maintaining configuration evidence, approval trails, and change records

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
