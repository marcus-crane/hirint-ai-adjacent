Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE JOB

The Head of Electrical Strategic Supply Chain is a Director leadership role responsible for architecting and executing the sourcing strategy across all electrical commodities. This includes semiconductors, custom and standard wire harnesses, connectors/interconnects, PCBAs, passives, and active discrete components.   
  
This role is critical to securing the advanced technologies and components that power our mission-critical hardware. you will navigate complex geopolitical landscapes, tightly regulated defense supply channels, and constrained global commodity markets. You will lead a high-performing team to build a resilient, scalable, and high-velocity supply chain capable of moving rapidly from New Product Introduction (NPI) to high-volume production. Partnering closely with Engineering, Quality, Manufacturing, and Executive Leadership, you will secure supply continuity against global market volatility, drive cost and technology roadmaps, and negotiate key commercial partnerships with global component manufacturers, distributors, and contract manufacturers.

#### **WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* **Enterprise Supply Chain Vision & Strategy:** Develop and execute comprehensive global commodity strategies across all electrical sub-tiers—semiconductors (ICs, SoCs, microcontrollers, FPGAs, power electronics), custom wire harnesses & cable assemblies, connectors, and passive/electromechanical components.
* **Team Leadership & Scale:** Build, mentor, and lead a high-performing team of commodity managers, strategic sourcing leads, and technical sourcing engineers across multiple global sites.
* **Executive Supplier Relationship Management:** Serve as the executive sponsor for Tier 1 suppliers, Contract Manufacturers (CMs), and critical technology partners. Establish long-term strategic alliances, joint development agreements, and robust performance management systems (QBRs, KPIs, scorecards).
* **Cross-Functional Engineering Alignment:** Partner directly with Electrical Engineering, Layout/Component Engineering, and Hardware Architecture from early concept phases to drive "Design for Supply Chain" (DfSC), alternate component qualifications, and standardization across product lines.
* **Commercial & Strategic Negotiations:** Lead high-stakes negotiations for master service agreements (MSAs), development agreements, long-term supply agreements (LTSAs), and complex statement of works (SOWs)—optimizing pricing structures, IP protection, warranty, payment terms, and lead-time commitments.
* **Supply Chain Resilience & Risk Mitigation:** Proactively identify single-source dependencies, geopolitical vulnerabilities, and capacity constraints. Drive proactive risk mitigation plans, dual-sourcing strategies, and strategic buffer inventory models.
* **Cost Architecture & TCO Modeling:** Champion robust "should-cost" modeling, teardown analyses, and TCO evaluations to optimize value from concept through end-of-life.
* **Executive Communication:** Conduct regular operational and strategic reviews with C-suite and executive leadership, serving as the primary executive escalation point for all critical supply chain matters.

#### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Engineering, Operations, Business, or a related technical discipline.
* 10+ years of experience in electrical & electronics strategic sourcing, global commodity management, or supply chain leadership within complex hardware manufacturing environments.
* 5+ years of proven experience building, managing, and scaling high-performing procurement or supply chain teams.
* Demonstrated expertise negotiating enterprise-level agreements and managing diverse commodity portfolios (e.g., complex electronics, custom chips, or contract manufacturing).
* Proven track record of managing supplier lifecycles and negotiating complex multi-million-dollar commercial agreements.
* Deep understanding of New Product Introduction (NPI) workflows and scaling from prototype to high-rate production.
* Ability to travel up to 25%-50% domestically and internationally as needed.
* Ability to relocate, if not already local to be onsite in Costa Mesa.

* Well-versed in TCO analysis and should costing to ensure data-driven analytics tied to component and supplier selection, in a product-based ecosystem

* Proven leadership in driving and influencing other teams to a common goal. You know how to effectively communicate and collaborate cross functionally and with all levels within an organization

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
