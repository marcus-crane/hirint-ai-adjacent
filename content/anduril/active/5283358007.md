# Product Sourcing Engineer (Electronics Focused)

**Maritime & Maneuver Dominance: Heavy Metal - Engineering & Operations**

## **ABOUT THE JOB**

The Product Sourcing Engineer (Electronics Focused) drives the supply network development and cost management of Anduril's electronic hardware across their lifecycles. This role focuses specifically on electronic components, PCBAs, sensors, communication systems, and related electronics critical to our Maritime & Maneuver Dominance platforms. You'll collaborate with suppliers and cross-functional teams, including engineering, quality, and manufacturing, to ensure our autonomous systems have reliable, high-performance electronic solutions.

## **WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Conduct supplier selection for electronic components, PCBAs, semiconductors, sensors, and subsystems, leveraging our approved supplier list and identifying new strategic partners in the electronics sector
* Partner with strategic electronics suppliers to accelerate product development via activities such as:
  + Concept scoping to quickly solidify electrical and electronic requirements
  + Feature trade-off/trade tables for component selection, performance specifications, and cost optimization
  + Should cost/clean sheet models for PCBAs, cable assemblies, and electronic modules
  + Joint design development of custom electronics and subsystems via clear statements of work
  + Simulation of electronic designs and signal integrity analysis
* Drive the development and consolidation of a manufacturing BOM for electronics, working with Quality & Manufacturing to ramp production internally and with our suppliers with high flexibility
* Manage electronics-related CAPEX throughout NPI, including test equipment, programming fixtures, and production tooling
* Conduct value-stream mapping to optimize supply network lead time and flexibility for critical electronic components
* Complete BOM risk assessment for electronics, including alternate component sourcing, second-sourcing strategies, and supply chain resilience planning for semiconductors and long-lead items
* Ensure completion of all component qualifications ahead of production, including:
  + Component validation and testing (environmental, EMI/EMC, reliability)
  + Compliance with military specifications and standards (MIL-STD-810, MIL-STD-461, etc.)
  + Supplier quality audits and capability assessments for electronics manufacturers
* Lead electronics cost engineering activities to converge bottoms-up cost curves to product cost targets, maximizing total cost of ownership (TCO) across the product lifecycle
* Manage ad-hoc cost issues including semiconductor shortages, component obsolescence, tariffs/import-export costs, and cost inflation
* Reduce complexity through commonality optimization, component/supplier rationalization, and standardization of electronic platforms across product lines
* Navigate electronic component lifecycle management, including obsolescence mitigation and technology roadmapping
* Stay current on emerging electronics technologies, semiconductor trends, and regulatory requirements affecting the electronics supply chain

## **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or other technical field
* 3+ years of experience in one or more of the following:
  + Sourcing engineering for electronics, PCBAs, or electronic components
  + Electronic circuit design and development
  + Supplier engagement and development in the electronics sector
  + Design of electronic systems and devices
* Understanding of electronic components including passive components, semiconductors, connectors, sensors, and integrated circuits
* Familiarity with PCBA design, manufacturing processes (SMT, through-hole), and assembly techniques
* Knowledge of electronic testing standards, qualification requirements, and reliability principles
* Experience with value/procurement engineering or TCO-related work at the component & product level
* Ability to travel 10% - 40% of the time to supplier sites, manufacturing facilities, and other Anduril locations
* Ability to relocate, if not already local to be onsite in Costa Mesa, CA

## **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Master's degree or advanced technical degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related field
* 5+ years of experience in electronics sourcing, supplier development, or electronic system design
* Deeper experience within:
  + Design & development of PCBAs, electronic assemblies, or embedded systems
  + Advanced sourcing activities for electronics or semiconductors, including stints as commodity/sourcing managers
  + Electronics supplier development (DFM optimization, process capability, supply chain management)
  + Early supplier engagement for next-generation electronics and emerging technologies
  + Component engineering and lifecycle management
* Experience with military or aerospace electronics applications and related qualification requirements (AS9100, etc.)
* Familiarity with developing high-reliability electronics in a high-mix, low-volume environment
* Knowledge of electronics supply chain dynamics, including semiconductor foundries, distribution channels, and allocation management
* Understanding of advanced electronics technologies (RF/microwave, high-speed digital, power electronics, optoelectronics)
* Experience navigating component obsolescence and technology transitions
* Proficiency with electronic design tools and ability to read schematics and PCB layouts
* Experience with contract manufacturers (CMs) and EMS providers
* Exposure to working in a fast-paced, start-up environment
* Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. Top Secret security clearance

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
