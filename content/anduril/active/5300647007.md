Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

Anduril Expeditionary Systems (AES) is the team at Anduril dedicated to the development and fielding of next-generation hardware, from man-portable systems to autonomous launchers, that modernize the command and control capabilities of U.S. and Allied forces. Getting hardware from prototype to production requires more than great engineering; it requires disciplined execution, tight cross-functional coordination, and people who can hold complexity across fast-moving programs. If you have a track record of bringing novel designs to production and a pioneering spirit, join us. Your work will directly change the balance of defense capabilities on a global scale.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

As a Business Systems Engineer Intern, you will design and build the internal tools, integrations, and dashboards that connect our operational systems and surface actionable information to the teams that need it. You will work directly with Supply Chain, Production, and Engineering stakeholders to understand their workflows and translate those needs into functional solutions.

* Build integrations between internal systems, connecting data across ERP, MES, PLM, JIRA, and other operational platforms to eliminate manual data transfer and reduce information silos.

* Design and develop dashboards that provide real-time visibility into production status, supply chain health, and engineering development progress.
* Build interactive applications that allow users to view, filter, and amend source data directly, reducing dependence on static spreadsheets and manual reporting.
* Generate automated reports that surface key program metrics for Supply Chain, Production, and Engineering audiences.
* Consolidate fragmented dashboards and reporting tools into unified, maintainable solutions.
* Partner with cross-functional stakeholders to identify workflow gaps and translate them into tool requirements.
* Support the transition from legacy Excel-based workflows to scalable, application-driven solutions.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Must be pursuing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in Computer Science, Information Systems, Software Engineering, or a related technical field.
* Must be returning to school at the end of the internship to continue academic studies for at least one quarter or semester.
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export-controlled information or facilities; U.S. clearance eligibility may be required depending on program.
* Ability to complete an in-person 12-week internship in one of our offices in summer 2027.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Proficiency in at least one programming language: Python, JavaScript, or TypeScript preferred.
* Experience building dashboards or data visualizations using tools such as Tableau, Power BI, Grafana, or equivalent.
* Familiarity with REST APIs and system integration concepts.
* Comfort working with structured data - SQL, JSON, CSV - and translating it into meaningful outputs.
* Strong communication skills, able to gather requirements from non-technical stakeholders and explain technical solutions clearly.
* Experience with low-code or no-code application platforms (Retool, AppSmith, Airtable, or equivalent).
* Familiarity with ORACLE, JIRA, Confluence, or similar project tracking tools.
* Exposure to supply chain, manufacturing, or operations environments.
* Experience with data pipeline tools or ETL workflows.
* Prior internship or project experience building internal tools or automations.
* A bias for action: you identify gaps and fill them rather than waiting to be asked.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
