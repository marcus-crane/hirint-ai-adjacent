By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. ### ABOUT THE JOB

Anduril is building the next generation of defense technology, and our Imaging business unit is scaling rapidly across multiple sites. We are looking for a Senior Inventory Manager to stand up and lead the inventory management function within Imaging’s supply chain organization. This is a foundational leadership role: you will own warehouse operations, logistics, trade compliance, and inventory accuracy across all Imaging locations and third-party logistics (3PL) partners. You will build the team, processes, and systems from the ground up to support production ramp and multi-site expansion.This role sits alongside Supply Chain PlanninG and Procurement within the Imaging supply chain organization. You will work closely with material planners, buyers, production, quality, and program teams to ensure the right material is in the right place at the right time.

#### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* Own warehouse operations across all Imaging sites, standardizing receiving through shipping processes, layouts, SOPs, and safety standards.
* Select and manage 3PL providers; define SLAs and hold partners accountable to performance targets.
* Optimize space utilization, racking, and material flow to support production schedules and growth.
* Oversee receiving (dock scheduling, inspection, ERP receipts, discrepancy resolution) and shipping (fulfillment, packaging, carrier scheduling, BOL, POD).
* Establish KPIs for dock-to-stock, shipping accuracy, and on-time shipment.
* Build and manage inbound/outbound logistics networks across sites, warehouses, 3PLs, and suppliers, optimizing cost, speed, and reliability.
* Own ITAR, EAR, and customs compliance for Imaging; serve as liaison to Anduril’s central trade compliance team and maintain classification and duty records.
* Build inventory control frameworks: cycle counting (ABC), physical inventories, and perpetual reconciliation to a 98%+ accuracy target.
* Drive E&O reduction and disposition; implement lot traceability, serialization, and ERP transaction discipline.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 7+ years of progressive experience in inventory management, warehouse operations, or logistics in a manufacturing or defense environment; at least 3 years in a multi-site or multi-warehouse leadership role.
* Demonstrated experience establishing and managing 3PL relationships, including contract negotiation, SLA definition, and performance management.
* Working knowledge of ITAR/EAR export control regulations and import compliance processes; experience managing controlled-item shipments.
* Hands-on experience with ERP systems (SAP, Oracle, or similar) and warehouse management systems (WMS).
* Strong understanding of inventory control methodologies: ABC analysis, cycle counting, safety stock optimization, and E&O management.
* Experience leading and developing teams of 15+ across multiple locations; ability to hire, coach, and set performance standards.
* Bachelor’s degree in Supply Chain Management, Industrial Engineering, Business, or a related field.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Experience in regulated manufacturing environments with serialization and lot traceability requirements.
* APICS CPIM, CSCP, or CLTD certification; Lean Six Sigma Green Belt or higher.
* Experience with optics, electro-optical systems, or precision hardware manufacturing.
* Familiarity with foreign trade zones (FTZ), duty drawback programs, and customs brokerage.
* Experience with rapid-growth or startup environments where you had to build processes and teams from scratch.
* PMP or project management experience for warehouse buildouts and system implementations.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
