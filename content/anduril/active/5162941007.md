### **ABOUT THE JOB**

The Deployments Team orchestrates the global fielding of Anduril’s cutting-edge hardware and software products. Operating at the critical intersection of product innovation and mission execution, we manage forward logistics, complex site installations, comprehensive maintenance & repairs, field inventory management, and reverse logistics. Working in close partnership across Product, Engineering, Growth, Business Development, Technical Operations, and Mission Operations, the team is central to ensuring unparalleled customer and mission success, directly shaping the future of Anduril’s defense solutions.  
  
We are seeking a **Senior Manager, Deployments Trainer & Special Projects** to join our rapidly expanding team in Santa Ana, CA. In this pivotal role, you will be instrumental in defining and executing the strategic vision for deploying Anduril’s people and technology across a global operational footprint within a specific division. You will design and deliver training programs that raise the operational bar across Deployment Operations, building role-specific curricula and Business Line-tailored content that accelerates team readiness at scale. This role demands a sharp eye for operational excellence — you will define and own KPIs, lead cross-functional projects spanning DOps and DSAP, and serve as a subject matter expert on the systems and processes that underpin deployment execution. You will be a critical bridge between senior leadership and frontline teams, conducting deep-dive analyses, surfacing actionable insights, and ensuring that institutional knowledge is captured and disseminated across the organization.  
  
This is a unique opportunity for an operationally driven leader who thrives at the intersection of training, process improvement, and cross-functional collaboration. You will directly shape how teams are developed, how performance is measured, and how operational best practices scale alongside Anduril's expanding product lines and global missions. Your ability to translate complex operational challenges into structured frameworks, training programs, and measurable outcomes will be essential in shaping the future of Deployment Operations at Anduril.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

**Capability Development & Delivery**

* **Design and continuously evolve Deployment Operations training programs** for Deployment Operations Managers, Operations and Logistics Associates, and Logistics Program Management roles, ensuring curricula reflect current operational standards and emerging best practices.
* **Build comprehensive, role-specific training guides and certification pathways** for Operations & Logistics Associates, Senior Operations & Logistics Associates, Deployment Operations Managers, Logistics Deputies, and Logistics Program Managers, enabling consistent performance and professional development across the organization.
* **Develop and deliver Business Line-specific Deployment Operations training curricula** tailored to the unique operational requirements of each line of business, ensuring teams are equipped to execute against program-specific standards and timelines.
* **Serve as an operationally embedded training resource across Business Lines**, accelerating team readiness by reinforcing the operational cadence, standards, and methodologies established by active programs.
* **Travel to regional sites and company locations to deliver hands-on training**, share best practices, and drive consistency in operational execution across the organization.

**Operational Excellence & Cross-Functional Projects**

* **Act as subject matter expert and strategic partner to DSAP on systems design** for platforms such as Oracle OTM and TMS, ensuring system configurations align with operational workflows and scalability requirements.
* **Own and drive cross-functional projects spanning DOps and DSAP teams**, including KPI development, On-Time Delivery Matrix, Forward Deployed inventory reporting, Inventory Adjustments, and other initiatives that strengthen operational visibility and accountability.
* **Define, document, and maintain deployment KPIs and performance metrics**, training stakeholders across the organization on how to interpret data, identify trends, and act on performance insights to drive continuous improvement.
* **Train team members on all relevant dashboards, KPI frameworks, and reporting tools**, building organizational self-sufficiency and ensuring teams can independently leverage data to inform operational decisions.
* **Lead special projects and cross-functional working groups** — such as developing and documenting Salesforce and GOE best practices, IRAD inventory requests, and MX Forecasting — ensuring institutional knowledge is captured, standardized, and disseminated at scale.
* **Conduct deep-dive operational analyses on behalf of Directors and Senior Leadership**, identifying gaps, surfacing inefficiencies, and recommending targeted improvements when leadership bandwidth is constrained.

**Peer Leadership & Influence**

* **Define and execute the strategic KPI roadmap** for Deployment Operations across multiple Business Lines, establishing long-term operational frameworks that scale proactively with Anduril’s growth trajectory and align with company-wide strategic objectives.
* **Foster a collaborative, mission-focused culture** that supports professional growth, employee engagement, and accountability for delivering impact.
* **Champion the development and adoption of new tools, techniques, and methodologies** that streamline logistics, field operations, and installation processes, embracing digital transformation in deployment and sustainment operations.
* **Evaluate deployment processes and performance metrics** rigorously, driving continuous improvement initiatives that reduce costs, eliminate inefficiencies, enhance reliability, and improve data quality and forecast accuracy.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
