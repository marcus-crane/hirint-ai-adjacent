**ABOUT THE TEAM**  
We are looking for a **Senior Manufacturing Engineer (Low Observable Systems)** to support our Fury autonomous aircraft program at our [**Arsenal 1**](https://www.anduril.com/arsenal/arsenal-1) facility in Ashville, OH. Anduril's manufacturing teams are scaling production of low observable (LO) structures and coatings — radar absorbent materials (RAM/RAS), specialty topcoats, and composite assemblies — that give our air and sea systems a survivability edge. We're looking for a Low Observable Manufacturing & MRB Quality Engineer to own both the manufacturing process engineering for LO materials/coatings and the Material Review Board (MRB) disposition process for nonconforming material across LO and composite build lines. This is a hands-on, floor-facing role that sits at the intersection of manufacturing, quality, and engineering.  
  
**What You'll Do**  
- Develop, qualify, and sustain manufacturing processes for low observable treatments, including surface preparation, RAM/RAS application, specialty coatings (e.g., primers, conductive/non-conductive topcoats), and bonded composite structures  
- Serve as a voting/engineering member of the Material Review Board (MRB), leading root cause investigations and dispositioning nonconforming material (use-as-is, rework, repair, scrap) in accordance with AS9100/ISO 9001 and customer/program requirements  
- Author and maintain work instructions, process specifications, travelers, and inspection criteria for LO and coatings processes  
- Drive corrective and preventive action (CAPA) programs to close out nonconformances and prevent recurrence; track MRB trends and feed data back into process control plans  
- Partner with Design, Quality, and Supply Chain to assess manufacturability and producibility of LO features early in the design cycle  
- Support first article inspection (FAI/AS9102), process validation, and statistical process control (SPC) for coating thickness, cure, adhesion, and RCS-relevant surface properties  
- Coordinate with NDI/NDT and quality inspection teams to define acceptance criteria for coating integrity, bond lines, and composite defects  
- Lead or support root cause/corrective action (RCCA) investigations using structured problem-solving (8D, 5-Why, fishbone) for escapes and internal nonconformances  
- Own documentation and configuration control for LO/coatings specs, ensuring compliance with internal material specs and applicable military/industry standards  
- Identify and implement continuous improvement initiatives to reduce scrap, rework, and cycle time on LO production lines  
- Support audits (internal, customer, and regulatory) related to quality systems, MRB records, and LO process compliance  
  
**Required Qualifications**  
  
- Bachelor's degree in Materials Science, Mechanical Engineering, Chemical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, or related technical field, or equivalent practical experience  
- 3+ years of experience in aerospace/defense manufacturing, with direct exposure to low observable materials, coatings, composites, or RAM/RAS processes  
- Demonstrated experience participating in or leading a Material Review Board (MRB) process, including nonconformance disposition and root cause/corrective action  
- Working knowledge of AS9100/ISO 9001 quality management systems and standard quality tools (SPC, FMEA, 8D, FAI/AS9102)  
- Hands-on familiarity with coating application processes (spray, dip, or specialty application), cure cycles, surface prep, and adhesion/bond testing  
- Ability to read and author engineering drawings, work instructions, and process specifications; GD&T literacy  
- Strong root-cause problem-solving skills and comfort working directly on the manufacturing floor  
- Must be a U.S. person (U.S. citizen, lawful permanent resident, or protected individual under 8 U.S.C. 1324b(a)(3)) due to export control (ITAR/EAR) requirements on this program; ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. government security clearance  
  
**Preferred Qualifications**  
  
- Experience with radar cross-section (RCS) testing, RAM/RAS material characterization, or electromagnetic test methods  
- Prior work on classified or SAP (Special Access Program) low observable programs  
- Six Sigma Green/Black Belt certification  
- Experience with composite layup, autoclave/oven cure processing, and bonded structure repair  
- Familiarity with PLM/MRP/MES systems (e.g., Windchill, SAP) for traveler and nonconformance records  
- Active DoD security clearance (Secret or higher)
