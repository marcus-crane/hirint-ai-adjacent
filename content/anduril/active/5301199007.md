Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### **ABOUT THE TEAM**

The Finance Data Systems pillar within the Finance Transformation Office serves as the backbone of financial integrity and operational intelligence at the company. We build the foundational data ecosystems and analytical frameworks that drive critical business outcomes. Our mandate is to transform raw, disparate operational and financial data into a reliable, high-integrity information architecture. By bridging traditional finance rigor with modern analytics, we empower division leadership, hardware engineering, corporate functions, and operational teams to make rapid, data-informed decisions as we scale production and expand strategic initiatives.

### **ABOUT THE JOB**

We are seeking an experienced **Finance Data Strategist** with deep domain expertise in cost accounting, manufacturing, or operations finance to join our team. In this pivotal role, you will act as the strategic bridge connecting division finance teams, operational business stakeholders, and analytics engineers. You will lead high-impact data initiatives centered on complex operational finance—such as unit economics, Bill of Materials (BOM) cost rollups, inventory valuation, and production variances. Rather than writing data pipelines yourself, you will own the functional architecture: defining requirements, untangling complex business logic, establishing financial definitions, and partnering with our analytics engineering team to translate business questions into scalable data products.

#### **WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* **Lead Complex Finance Data Projects:** Drive the end-to-end strategy, roadmap, and delivery of data initiatives focused on product unit economics, BOM costing, inventory tracking, manufacturing variances, and operational performance.
* **Bridge Finance & Engineering:** Serve as the primary translator between division finance stakeholders, manufacturing/supply chain leaders, and analytics engineers. Convert ambiguous business needs into clear, actionable functional specifications and data requirements.
* **Define Financial & Operational Logic:** Establish standardized methodologies for complex finance calculations—such as standard costing, overhead absorption, landed costs, scrap rates, work-in-progress (WIP) tracking, and multi-tier BOM cost rollups.
* **Partner with Analytics Engineering:** Collaborate directly with analytics engineers who design and execute data models (e.g., in Databricks, Palantir Foundry, Snowflake) to ensure data assets reflect functional reality and business context.
* **Ensure Financial Integrity & Validation:** Lead user acceptance testing (UAT) and establish reconciliation frameworks to validate that engineered data products reconcile back to the general ledger, ERP systems, and standard accounting controls.
* **Drive Stakeholder Alignment & Adoption:** Build alignment across hardware engineering, manufacturing, program management, and FP&A. Enable stakeholders to leverage new analytical tools and self-serve reporting to drive product margin improvements.
* **Master Data Governance:** Partner with business systems and master data teams to govern key finance dimensions (item masters, cost centers, routing structures, and chart of accounts mapping) to prevent downstream reporting drift.

#### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 5+ years of progressive experience in Cost Accounting, Operations/Manufacturing Finance, Strategic FP&A, or as a Finance Business Analyst/Product Owner in a hardware, aerospace, automotive, or high-complexity manufacturing environment.
* Strong functional understanding of inventory accounting, multi-level BOM structures, cost variance analysis (PPV, usage, efficiency), standard costing methodologies, and gross margin analytics.
* Proven track record of driving cross-functional data or systems projects from ideation to operational adoption, acting as the product owner or functional lead.
* Demonstrated experience partnering with technical teams (analytics engineers, BI developers, or data engineers) and translating finance terminology into logical data models.
* Familiarity with enterprise ERP systems (e.g., Oracle Fusion Cloud, SAP, NetSuite) and how manufacturing/procurement transactions flow into the general ledger.
* Solid data literacy—ability to navigate complex datasets, interpret relational database concepts, review business logic, and perform intermediate data analysis (e.g., advanced Excel; functional knowledge of SQL or modern BI tools like Tableau/PowerBI is a strong plus).
* Exceptional communication and stakeholder management skills; capable of challenging assumptions, aligning conflicting requirements, and presenting complex financial concepts simply.
* Comfort operating in a fast-paced, hyper-growth environment with high ambiguity and evolving operational workflows.
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export-controlled information or facilities.

#### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Prior experience in defense tech, aerospace, or advanced hardware manufacturing.
* Experience with modern data platforms (e.g., Palantir Foundry, Databricks) from a functional/business architect standpoint.
* CPA, CMA, or background in standard cost accounting implementation in an ERP environment.
* Experience with Product Lifecyle systems (Teamcenter) and Warehouse Management tools.

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
