### ABOUT THE JOB

As a People Compliance Specialist, you will play a critical role in managing, scaling, and operationalizing Anduril’s people compliance programs. Serving as a subject matter expert, you will collaborate closely with cross-functional teams—including People Operations, Employment Legal, and People Business Partners —to ensure compliance across federal, state, and local regulations. In this role, you will drive key compliance workflows, compliance initiatives for our growing hourly and non-exempt workforce, background check adjudications, and mandatory reporting obligations.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Support the maintenance and continuous improvement of People Team policies, procedures, forms, templates, and process documentation, including communication and acknowledgment tracking.
* Oversee HR reporting, audits, documentation management and end-to-end background check and adverse action processes.
* Manage and administer Anduril’s drug and alcohol screening program, ensuring full compliance with federal and state testing requirements (pre-employment, random) and mandatory reporting.
* Serve as the primary subject matter expert and escalation point for compliance-related inquiries from employees, Onboarding, People Operations, and Talent Acquisition.
* Lead routine and ad-hoc internal compliance audits, identifying operational vulnerabilities and implementing proactive corrective actions.
* Lead the preparation, validation, and submission of mandatory compliance reporting (e.g., EEO-1, VETS, and state-specific reporting).
* Design, standardize, and maintain standard operating procedures (SOPs), documentation retention schedules, and workflow materials across all people compliance programs.
* Oversee mandatory employee compliance training programs, ensuring tracking and high completion rates across both exempt and hourly employee populations.
* Partner with external vendors to ensure SLA adherence and operational excellence.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 5+ years of dedicated experience in HR compliance, people operations, or employment legal operations.
* Hands-on experience administering and managing DOT-regulated drug and alcohol testing programs and clearinghouses.
* Strong knowledge of federal, state, and local employment laws and regulations.
* Proven ability to own and drive programs independently, with strong analytical and project management skills.
* High level of discretion and integrity when handling sensitive, confidential employee data.
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to explain complex regulatory requirements to stakeholders at all levels.
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export-controlled information or facilities

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
