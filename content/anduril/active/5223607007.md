Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**ABOUT THE TEAM**  
Our team is building and scaling production of next-generation autonomous air vehicles at Arsenal. We own the full integration line, from subassembly through final acceptance testing, and are ramping from early production into rate. The team works hand-in-hand with Manufacturing Engineering, Quality, and MRB to solve problems on the floor and get units out the door.

**ABOUT THE JOB**  
We are looking for two Integration Specialists to join the Fury production line at Arsenal, one per shift. In this role, you will be the line's first responder when a build error or nonconformance occurs, owning the resolution end to end from assessment through rework and quality buyoff. You will review drawings, create NCRs, redline corrective work instructions into Forge, and hand units back ready to build. Beyond problem resolution, you will drive process improvements by identifying bottlenecks, improving workstation layouts, and translating floor knowledge into standard work. This is not a desk job, you remain a working technician on the line, performing assigned build operations and covering stations alongside your team. If you thrive on solving problems at the point of work, can move between a drawing and a tool without missing a beat, and want to shape how a new production line runs, this role is for you.

**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Perform assigned Fury build operations per work order and standard work, held to the same quality and pace standard as any technician on the line.
* Cover stations during absences, surges, and critical-path builds; maintain qualification on the stations you support.
* Respond to production calls and own the rework/disposition loop end to end — from NCR creation through redlined work instructions, ME approval, corrective work, and final quality buyoff.
* Review engineering drawings and CAD data to confirm requirements and deviations; draft redlines and corrective instructions for ME approval.
* Disposition recurring nonconformances with established fixes; escalate novel or engineering-authority items to MRB / Manufacturing Engineering.
* Monitor build flow across stations, identify bottlenecks and WIP build-up, and recommend sequencing changes to keep the line balanced.
* Develop and maintain standard work documentation; improve workstation layout, tooling placement, and material presentation.
* Execute and support functional and acceptance test procedures for aircraft systems including hydraulics, fuel, and landing gear.
* Onboard and cross-train technicians on station standard work, safety, and quality expectations.
* Share 6S, FOD, and tool-control duties on the line.
* Collaborate with Manufacturing Engineering and Industrial Engineering to translate floor observations into actionable process improvements.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Manufacturing floor experience in aerospace assembly, aircraft integration, or similar large-scale mechanical/electrical build environments.
* Strong blueprint reading and ability to interpret engineering drawings and CAD data.
* Hands-on experience with nonconformance processes, rework execution, and quality inspections.
* Demonstrated ability to troubleshoot build issues on the floor and drive them to resolution.
* Familiarity with quality management standards (IPC-620, IPC-A-610, or equivalent).
* Comfortable working with cranes, hoists, PPE, and in a 6S/FOD-controlled environment.
* Eligible to obtain and maintain a U.S. Secret Clearance

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Aviation or aerospace integration and test experience, particularly on large airframe or vehicle platforms.
* Experience with MES/ERP systems such as Forge or similar work order platforms.
* Familiarity with lean manufacturing methodologies (5S, kaizen) and continuous improvement practices.
* Experience developing standard work, work instructions, or process documentation.
* Demonstrated capability to train or mentor production technicians.
* NX CAD fundamentals

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
