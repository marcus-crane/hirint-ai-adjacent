## Job Description

As a Project Manager on ISR Kits, you will supprt the day-to-day execution of a complex, multi-task-order UAS program. You will serve as the operational link between the Program Manager, the Technical Operations, Deployment Operations, and the Engineering teams responsible for airframe integration, payloads, GCS software, and sustainment. Your primary focus will be the Thunderdome program priorities.  
  
**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Scheduling — Support the integrated master schedule across design, build, test, deployment, and sustainment of mission systems program requirements. Keep internal engineering, external partners (e.g., airframe OEM), and customer milestones synchronized.
* Training — Support delivery of the mission systems operator and maintainer training pipeline across customer cohorts; support the needs of instructors and curriculum materials readiness.
* Logistics — Coordinate with Deployment Operations to plan and execute movement of airframes, GCS, payloads, spares, and support equipment between integration/build and test sites locations. Own tracking and validation of shipping, and customer reception points.
* Documentation — Assist Program Management and Systems Engineering in documenting the connection of requirements, design, as-built configuration, and verification evidence in support of customer deliverables
* O&S Support — Stand up and maintain tracking of consumption and coordination related to operations and support for deployed systems: spares pipeline, field service engineering rotations, depot touch-points, and reliability feedback into engineering.
* Program Management / Engineering / Technical Operations Comms — Serve as the daily link between Program Management and the engineering disciplines (systems, SW, HW, flight test) with the Field Support Representation teams. Assist in translating field realities into engineering priorities and engineering realities into customer-ready messaging.
* Deployment Execution — Support on-site coordination for customer demonstrations, flight test series, and operational deployments. Deploy on the on-the-ground as Anduril Program Management representative when required.
* Risk & Issue Management — Assist in maintaining the program risk register; proactively identify paths to drive closure of top risks; escalate blocking issues with timely communication and options to Program Management.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS AND ATTRIBUTES**

* Hands-on UAS/UAV operator experience (Group 1–3)
* Demonstrated project coordination experience in a technical, operational, or deployed environment
* Comfort operating in ambiguity; programs and contract portfolio is actively evolving and priorities will shift
* Experience maintaining and updating project tracking tools within Excel, Airtable, etc
* Experience building briefs in Power Point communicating technical details and trade-offs
* Outstanding written and verbal communication skills, including presentation of delivered artifacts
* A sincere commitment to a positive, inclusive, and collaborative culture
* U.S. security clearance (Secret or higher); U.S. Person required for program access

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
