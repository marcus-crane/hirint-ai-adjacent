Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### **ABOUT THE TEAM**

The Reliability Engineering team partners across Anduril's engineering, manufacturing, and operations organizations to ensure our autonomous systems survive real-world conditions and deliver consistent performance for our customers. Our mandate spans the complete product lifecycle - from early concept design through production scale-up and fielded operations. We use data, statistical methods, and engineering analysis to forecast how our systems will perform over time and influence design decisions, including part selection, redundancy strategies, and maintenance approaches. We establish tailored reliability frameworks by program and partner with Quality to drive design robustness, failure prevention, and rapid response to issues in the field.

### **ABOUT THE JOB**

Anduril Intelligence Systems (AIS) group is focused on positioning Anduril as a lead provider of specialized engineering and products for Intelligence Community (IC) customers. We work within the IC to understand their requirements, shape their concepts of operation, and deliver exquisite capability across their problem set. We aim to develop and deploy critically needed capabilities that address our customers’ most pressing national security requirements.  
  
We are seeking a Reliability Test Technician to join our team in Santa Ana, CA, supporting reliability test execution, instrumentation, and fixture build-out across our Intelligence Systems products. This role is critical to building a sustainable reliability test capability for the business, and will partner with our Reliability Engineers to support the physical execution of test campaigns - HALT, HASS, environmental, vibration/shock, and more. You will own test setup builds, equipment operation, data capture, and calibration coordination, and be responsible for standing up repeatable, well-documented test processes (SOPs, test plans/procedures, readiness checklists) so that testing can scale predictably as new programs are added. This is a highly hands-on role for someone who enjoys working in a lab/test environment, is comfortable with tools and test equipment, and takes ownership of getting testing completed correctly the first time.

### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* Set up, execute, and monitor reliability tests including HALT, HASS, thermal cycling, vibration, shock, and other reliability tests
* Operate and maintain test equipment (repetitive shock shakers, electrodynamic shakers, IR chambers, ESPEC chambers, vacuum chambers, ingress chambers, DAQ systems, and sensors/instrumentation), and maintain a fixture and equipment inventory to support reuse across programs
* Build, assemble, and validate test setups (i.e. HTOL racks) and fixtures needed for reliability and environmental test campaigns, working from designs provided by partner Engineering teams
* Troubleshoot test setup and equipment issues in real time to keep test campaigns on schedule
* Document test observations, anomalies, and failure data with sufficient detail to support root cause investigations
* Support failure analysis activities through test setup, in-situ monitoring, and physical teardown/inspection of test articles, including use of metrology equipment (CT, X-ray, CMM, Keyence scope, etc.)
* Support Reliability Engineers in executing DOEs, including RF-related test campaigns
* Coordinate test article logistics (receipt, tracking, prep, teardown) across concurrent programs, including for external testing
* Write and maintain test plans, procedures, SOPs, and final test reports; support test readiness reviews (TRRs) with clear documentation
* Create and maintain test readiness checklists to ensure consistent, repeatable test execution across technicians and programs
* Serve as the primary interface with Quality for equipment calibration scheduling, tracking, and upkeep
* Support Reliability Engineers in witnessing tests at outside test houses, capturing data and documentation as needed
* Track qualification test article status and maintain test logs/records to support compliance reporting

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Associate's degree or technical certification in Electronics, Mechanical/Manufacturing Technology, or equivalent, plus 2+ years of relevant hands-on test/lab experience (additional experience may substitute for degree)
* Hands-on experience with environmental test equipment such as thermal chambers, vibration/shaker tables, or HALT/HASS chambers
* Practical mechanical aptitude - comfortable building test setups and fixtures using hand tools, power tools, and basic shop equipment
* Experience with data acquisition systems and common test instrumentation (accelerometers, thermocouples, strain gauges, etc.)
* Ability to read and interpret mechanical and electrical drawings/schematics
* Strong documentation habits - able to write clear test plans, procedures, SOPs, and reports, and accurately maintain test logs
* Ability to work independently in a lab environment, managing multiple concurrent test setups
* Willingness to travel occasionally to support testing at external test labs
* Currently possesses and is able to maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance

### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Familiarity with test standards such as MIL-STD-810, GM/Qualmark HALT specifications, and MIL-STD-461/464
* Direct experience operating HALT/HASS equipment (i.e. Qualmark or similar chambers)
* Experience with metrology and inspection equipment (CT, X-ray, CMM, Keyence scope, or similar)
* Prior experience in a defense, aerospace, or other regulated hardware test environment
* Basic machining or fabrication experience (mill, lathe, 3D printing) for test setup builds
* Familiarity with DOE concepts and structured test planning
* Experience with equipment calibration processes and records
* General knowledge of Linux, PCBA architecture, communication buses (I2C, SPI, UART), and networking concepts (TCP/IP, OSI layers) - not required, can be learned on the job

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
