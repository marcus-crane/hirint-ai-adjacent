**About the Role**  
  
Anduril Industries is building next-generation defense technology to protect the United States and its allies. We are looking for Production Technicians to join our Rocket Motor Systems manufacturing team. In this role, you will perform hands-on final assembly operations on solid rocket motors and their subassemblies, working in a fast-paced production environment where precision, safety, and teamwork are paramount.  
  
You do not need prior experience with solid rocket motors or energetic materials to be successful here — we will train you. What matters most is that you bring a strong safety mindset, a willingness to learn, the technical aptitude to master detailed procedures, and the ability to work collaboratively with your team.  
  
**Core Responsibilities**

* Perform solid rocket motor final assembly operations including mandrel extraction, nozzle installation, and igniter assembly integration per detailed work instructions
* Execute igniter subassembly build-up, including component preparation, epoxy application, and curing processes
* Conduct motor marking, labeling, and serialization in accordance with configuration control requirements
* Perform leak check and pressure testing of completed motor assemblies using calibrated test equipment
* Complete pack-out and preparation of finished motors for storage or shipment, ensuring compliance with packaging and handling specifications
* Maintain accurate and thorough records of all assembly operations, inspections, and test results in the production tracking system
* Participate in daily production stand-ups, communicate status and blockers, and coordinate handoffs between shifts or stations

**Safety & Compliance**

* Solid propellant and other energetic materials are exceptionally dangerous if mishandled — adhere strictly to all safety protocols, standard operating procedures, and explosive safety requirements at all times with zero tolerance for deviation
* Maintain housekeeping and Foreign Object Debris (FOD) control standards in all work areas
* Identify and escalate safety concerns, near-misses, and hazards immediately through established reporting channels
* Use required Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) correctly and consistently
* Support and participate in safety audits, process hazard analyses, and continuous improvement initiatives

**What We're Looking For**  
  
We hire for mindset and aptitude. If you've built things with your hands — whether in a factory, a shop, the military, or your own garage — and you take pride in doing precise, careful work, we want to hear from you.

* Ability to follow detailed written and visual work instructions with precision and consistency
* Moderate to high mechanical aptitude: comfortable working with hand tools, torque wrenches, fixtures, and small components
* Strong attention to detail and quality — you catch your own mistakes before they move downstream
* Team-oriented attitude with clear, direct communication skills

**Preferred**

* Military service or experience in an environment with strict procedural discipline
* Experience with calibrated measurement tools (micrometers, calipers, pin gauges, etc.)

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
