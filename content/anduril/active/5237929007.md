Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**Team and Role Overview:**

Anduril's Expeditionary Systems team designs and delivers containerized command centers, datacenter-strength AI capabilities, launched effects, and sensing systems for US and Allied militaries. These complete, mission-ready solutions provide survivable, intensive compute at the edge and enable rapid, low-cost deployment of proliferated, survivable capabilities across the entire military echelon.

**Our product areas include:**

* Command & Control — deployable, certified command centers for tactical and operational headquarters
* AI Factories — ruggedized, high-density compute platforms that bring datacenter- class AI processing to the tactical edge
* Effects — containerized launcher and weapon system platforms enabling rapid deployment of proliferated, survivable strike capabilities
* Sensing — expeditionary sensor systems in transportable form factors

We're seeking a Systems Engineer to join a lean, high-output team where every engineer has direct impact on fielded products. You'll own systems engineering across the full lifecycle — from concept and architecture through integration, test, and deployment — on products that go to real units in real operational environments.

**Key Responsibilities:**

*Systems Architecture & Design*

* Develop system architectures and lead design trades across mechanical, electrical, software, thermal, and power domains
* Decompose operational needs into system and subsystem requirements with clear traceability, testability, and allocation
* Create and maintain systems engineering artifacts including ICDs, system block diagrams, ConOps, and requirements specifications
* Drive SWaP, transportability, survivability, and human factors trades that shape the product from day one
* Lead and support design reviews (SRR, PDR, CDR) with technical rigor and cross-functional alignment

*Integration, Verification & Validation*

* Own verification and validation planning and execution across analysis, inspection, demonstration, and test
* Support system integration activities, troubleshooting cross-discipline issues as hardware, software, and firmware come together
* Conduct FMEA/FMECA and manage technical risk throughout the development lifecycle
* Support environmental qualification campaigns (thermal, vibration, shock, EMI/EMC, transportation)
* Travel to field exercises, deployments, and customer sites to validate system performance, support operators, and bring feedback back to the design team

*Documentation & Process*

* Develop and maintain Technical Data Packages (TDPs), Systems Engineering Management Plans (SEMPs), and program documentation
* Utilize modern systems engineering tools (Cameo, MBSE/SysML, Jama, DOORS) to model architectures, manage requirements, and maintain traceability
* Build documentation that enables customers to operate, maintain, and sustain Anduril products in the field without on-site support
* Ensure compliance with applicable MIL-STDs and defense standards

**Qualifications:**

*Required:*

* Bachelor's degree in Systems Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Computer Engineering, Physics, or related technical discipline
* 2-6 years of systems engineering experience in a complex, multidisciplinary environment
* Experience developing, documenting, and maturing systems from design through production or fielding
* Ability to work across engineering disciplines — comfortable engaging with mechanical, electrical, software, and manufacturing teams
* Strong requirements development and documentation skills
* Excellent written and verbal communication
* U.S. citizenship required; ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. Secret security clearance

*Preferred:*

* Active Secret or TS/SCI clearance
* Experience with containerized, expeditionary, or ruggedized military systems
* Familiarity with military standards (MIL-STD-461, -810, -1275, -1316, -1760)
* Experience with systems engineering tools: Cameo, MBSE/SysML, DOORS, Jama
* Experience with weapon system integration, launch systems, C2 systems, or high-performance compute platforms
* Exposure to rapid prototyping and hardware delivery on compressed timelines
* Field support or deployment experience with military end users
* Master's degree in Systems Engineering or related field

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
