Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Design & Construction team at Anduril delivers the factories, labs, offices, and secure spaces that enable our teams to design, build, and deploy advanced defense technology. Our projects span high-performance manufacturing facilities, mission-critical secure environments, and collaborative workspaces, giving our team exposure to a uniquely wide variety of technical and operational challenges.  
  
More than just delivering buildings, we are (literally) rebuilding the American defense industrial base. Our work directly accelerates how quickly critical technology moves from concept to production and into the hands of the warfighter.

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for a Manager, East Asset Improvements to join our rapidly growing team in Washington, DC. This position sits within the broader Design & Construction (D&C) team and covers Day 2 and asset improvement projects across Anduril’s East Coast portfolio, with the highest concentration of work in the Boston and Washington, DC metro areas.  
  
This is a player/coach role. You will lead a team of 3–4 Asset Improvement Project Managers while personally managing a handful of projects yourself. You are the single point of contact for all East Coast asset improvement work. You are the escalation point for your team and the final filter on scoping, budgeting, procurement, and execution for every project in the region. Your team runs the day-to-day; you own the outcome.  
  
Once a building is open, the work isn’t done. Programs grow, teams reorganize, new equipment arrives, secure spaces expand, and building systems need upgrades. This team owns that work: reconfigurations, capital improvements, MEP and infrastructure upgrades, lab and production fit-outs, and secure space modifications, often inside occupied, operating facilities. You will lead the team that delivers this work quickly, safely, and predictably, while staying close to the Division Leaders and site teams who depend on it. Whether it is autonomous submarines, robot fighter jets, or AR headsets, we stay close to our end users to ensure we’re fueling their success.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Regional Ownership:
  + Serve as the single point of contact for all East Coast asset improvements for D&C leadership, site leaders, Facilities, and business unit stakeholders.
  + Own the outcome on every project in the region. Review and approve scope, budgets, procurement strategy, and execution plans before work moves forward.
  + Act as the escalation point for your team, helping PMs resolve difficult commercial, technical, and stakeholder issues.
* Hands-On Project Delivery (Player):
  + Personally manage a handful of asset improvement and Day 2 projects, typically the most complex, sensitive, or high-visibility work in the region.
  + Own the full lifecycle on your projects: requirements gathering, scoping, estimating, design team management, procurement, construction, and closeout.
  + Pick up projects in Boston, DC, or other East Coast locations as needed to balance team workload and cover gaps.
* Team Leadership (Coach):
  + Lead, develop and coach a growing team of 3–4 Asset Improvement Project Managers across the East Coast.
  + Assign and balance project portfolios across the team based on complexity, geography, and capacity.
  + Set clear expectations for delivery quality, communication, and documentation; hold regular 1:1s and provide direct, actionable feedback.
  + Lead by example: use your own projects to model the delivery standards, documentation, and stakeholder communication you expect from the team.
* Portfolio and Program Management:
  + Own the East Coast asset improvement pipeline, from intake and scoping through closeout and turnover.
  + Partner with Facilities, Workplace, and business unit leaders to prioritize requests and align the pipeline with operational needs and capital availability.
  + Establish consistent delivery standards, templates, and reporting cadences so every project is run the same way regardless of who manages it.
  + Maintain accurate project data in our project management systems and report portfolio status, risks, and decisions to D&C leadership.
* Delivery Oversight Across the Team:
  + Oversee scope, schedule, budget, and quality across a high volume of concurrent small-to-mid-sized projects.
  + Ensure work in occupied buildings is planned and phased to minimize disruption to engineering, manufacturing, and secure operations.
  + Review design documents, MEP and life-safety scopes, and constructability with a critical eye; ensure compliance with codes, landlord requirements, and Anduril standards.
  + Drive clean closeout and turnover to Facilities Management, including as-builts, O&M documentation, warranties, and asset data.
* Budget and Financial Oversight:
  + Develop and manage the East Coast asset improvement capital plan and individual project budgets.
  + Work closely with Finance/FP&A on forecasting, accruals, and cash flow.
  + Review and approve estimates, change orders, and pay applications; push for cost transparency and value.
  + Help build our historical cost database to inform future project estimates.
* Vendor and Contracting Strategy:
  + Build and maintain a strong regional bench of general contractors, MEP trade partners, architects, and engineers in the Boston and DC markets.
  + Develop contracting approaches suited to high-volume Day 2 work (MSAs, task orders, unit pricing) that reduce procurement time and change order disputes.
  + Evaluate vendor performance and hold partners accountable for safety, quality, schedule, and cost.
* Stakeholder Management:
  + Work cross-functionally with Facilities Management, Security, IT/Network Engineering, EHS, Real Estate, Manufacturing, Legal, and others.
  + Coordinate with landlords and property managers on approvals, lease requirements, and building rules.
  + Communicate project progress, challenges, and solutions clearly to senior leadership.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
