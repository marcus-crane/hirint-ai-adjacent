Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### **About the Team**

Anduril's Supply Chain organization is building the systems infrastructure to scale procurement, planning, and logistics across a rapidly growing defense technology portfolio. Within this organization, the Digital Supply Chain function is responsible for optimizing supply chain operations through implementation, management, and continuous improvement of the digital platforms that power sourcing, inventory, fulfillment, and supplier management. The team ensures that supply chain processes are efficient, scalable, and integrated with the latest industry standards.

### **About the Role**

As a Supply Chain Systems Specialist, your primary responsibility is to respond to reported supply chain system incidents submitted by internal stakeholders across Anduril's global supply chain user base. You will own the intake, evaluation, debug, escalation, communications, and tracking of reported incidents across the platforms that support procurement, purchasing, planning, inventory, and logistics operations. The user base supported by this role includes supply chain analysts, procurement specialists, buyers, planners, and program managers. Support case duration and technical complexity will range widely.

Beyond reactive support, you will be embedded in supply chain operations, working cross-functionally with Procurement, Purchasing, Planning, Program Management, IT, Engineering, Business Systems, and other stakeholders to solve complex systems problems that span multiple teams and don't fall neatly into any single domain. You bring structure to ambiguity, think in systems, and drive solutions where others see only complexity.

### **What You'll Do**

***Triage, Troubleshooting & Incident Resolution***

* Serve as the critical bridge between supply chain operations and digital supply chain systems (ERP, PR apps) identifying and resolving software issues that impact supply chain efficiency.
* Monitor intake of reported software problems across multiple supply chain platforms and triage based on urgency, impact, and scope.
* Conduct root cause analysis by observing actual supply chain processes and how they interact with digital systems to identify disconnects between software functionality and operational needs.
* Escalate to engineering support when required and provide clear operational context to software development teams about supply chain realities and downstream impacts.
* Develop workarounds and best practices that accommodate both system limitations and supply chain requirements, minimizing disruption to ongoing operations.
* Proactively identify potential unreported problems by regularly analyzing system data patterns, monitoring supply chain KPIs, and reviewing process adherence across platforms.

***Cross-Functional Problem Solving & Systems Thinking***

* Step into high-priority problems that span multiple teams and systems, bringing structure to ambiguity and driving resolution without waiting for clear ownership or prescribed solutions.
* Project manage across large, cross-functional groups of stakeholders—leading meetings, managing communications, and driving alignment toward solutions amid challenging deadlines and moving milestones.
* Support system implementations, upgrades, and migrations by leading user acceptance testing, developing training materials, and championing change management across the organization.
* Propose targeted improvements to existing workflows and system configurations that enhance the end-user experience and reduce recurring support needs.

#### **Required Qualifications**

* Bachelors Degree in Supply Chain Management, Industrial Engineering, Information Systems, Business Analytics, or a related field.
* 2+ years of experience in supply chain systems, technical support, ERP/MRP administration, business systems analysis, or a related operations/technology role, with hands-on experience in one or more supply chain platforms (e.g., SAP, Oracle, NetSuite, Coupa, or similar).
* Hands-on debug expertise with integrated enterprise systems, including ERP, MRP, WMS, PLM, or procurement platforms.
* Functional understanding of core supply chain concepts, processes, and terminology (sourcing, procurement, inventory, payment) and how they relate to software tools and data objects.

* History of collaborative engagement with software engineers and cross-functional technical teams.
* Self-driven and proactive. You don't wait on others to solve problems. You step into the gap to offer solutions yourself.
* Comfortable communicating broadly: presenting findings and recommendations to leadership, facilitating discussions across technical and non-technical audiences, and documenting solutions clearly.
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export controlled information or facilities.

#### **Preferred Qualifications**

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
