### ABOUT THE JOB

You'll own the production readiness of space hardware — take maturing and flight-proven designs and drive them to repeatable, higher-rate manufacturing. Intelligently pace design changes with a holistic view of the program and its build schedule.

### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Use first principles as a basis for decisions and continuously look for opportunities to simplify designs, reduce part count, and design for manufacturing, assembly, and test (DFM/DFA/DFT) — improving producibility, repeatability, and reliability at higher production quantities
* Modify and mature existing designs to resolve problems realized in production – fit, tolerance, assembly, and yield issues
* Update designs to reduce build and test cycle times, improve first-pass yield, and remove bottlenecks from assembly, integration, and test (AI&T) operations
* Own bills of material (BOMs) and engineering drawings – release and maintain part and assembly drawings, GD&T, and BOM structure; process engineering changes (ECO/ECN) and manage revisions and effectivity
* Propagate engineering changes through assembly hierarchies — revise parent assembly models, drawings, and BOMs when subcomponent designs change, ensuring next-higher-level documentation stays current and consistent
* Act as the Responsible Engineer for your hardware in production: disposition nonconformances, author red-lines and rework/retrofit instructions
* Partner with manufacturing, supply chain, and test engineering to transition designs from low-rate to higher-rate production and to stand up repeatable work instructions, tooling, and fixtures
* Build and validate prototypes and production trial builds, analyze failures, and feed lessons learned back into the design and the build process
* Travel to test sites, production facilities, and vendors as needed
* Create excellent technical documentation – assembly and work instructions, procedures, test plans and reports, vendor specifications and statements of work, etc.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* BS or higher degree in engineering or a hard science
* CAD skills (any platform) and ability to make high quality engineering drawings that meet common industry standards, including GD&T
* Experience taking hardware designs into manufacturing and production – design for manufacturing/assembly and drawing release. Experience transitioning hardware from prototype or low-rate to higher-rate production
* Working knowledge of BOM structures, revision control, and engineering change orders (ECO/ECN), ideally within a PLM/PDM system
* Demonstrated hands-on fabrication and assembly skills. Experience working with hand tools, mill, lathe, etc.
* Strong understanding of engineering fundamentals: heat transfer, statics, solid mechanics, materials
* Familiarity with tolerance analysis and inspection methods
* Excellent attention to detail
* Highly motivated, self-starting, and able to perform duties autonomously without supervision
* Very organized and willing to establish scope and requirements for ill-defined problems
* Passion for defending the United States and her allies
* Be able to obtain and hold a U.S. Top Secret security clearance

### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 4+ years of mechanical design or production/sustaining engineering experience in a professional setting with space hardware
* Experience in NX/Teamcenter or equivalent CAD/PLM
* Experience with nonconformance disposition and Material Review Board, root cause and corrective action (RCCA), and first article inspection (FAI)
* Experience designing tooling, fixtures, and ground support equipment to improve build and test throughput
* Ability to integrate electronic components (PCBAs, motors, sensors, etc.) into mechanical designs
* Experience developing code in higher-level languages (e.g. Python, MATLAB, C++) for analytical models, data acquisition, and automation
* Experience with statistical process control, tolerance stack-ups, or design of experiments (DOE) to improve yield

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
