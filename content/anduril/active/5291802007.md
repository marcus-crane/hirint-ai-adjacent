Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

Supply Chain Operations sits within Anduril's central Supply Chain organization. It owns how material actually arrives, and the data and systems behind it: what suppliers ship, how it is packaged, labeled, and documented; the EDI and Supplier Portal connections that carry purchase orders, shipments, and invoices; and whether all of it can be received, processed, and put to use without touching a person twice. We make the operational requirements executable at every dock and every connection, across a rapidly growing and diverse supplier network. The work is measured in problems permanently removed, not tickets closed.

#### ABOUT THE JOB

You own the day-to-day health of EDI and the Supplier Portal. When a transaction fails, a trading partner cannot onboard, or an order, shipment, or invoice does not go through, you are responsible for resolving it. You implement and support the processes behind both systems, train suppliers on them, and analyze transaction data to identify what is breaking and why. You own the operational mechanics that make EDI and the Supplier Portal work, partnering with IT and Business Systems when a fix requires a system change.

#### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* Implement and support EDI processes with suppliers and internal stakeholders, including trading partner onboarding, testing and validation of transaction sets, and troubleshooting failed or rejected documents to keep order, shipment and invoice flows moving
* Implement and support Supplier Portal processes, including supplier onboarding and enablement, registration and invoicing workflows, and day-to-day issue resolution as portal adoption scales across the supply base
* Train external suppliers on EDI and Supplier Portal processes, and create, maintain and update the supporting training materials, guides and job aids so suppliers can transact with Anduril accurately and without hand-holding
* Act as a responsive point of contact for external suppliers and internal Supply Chain, Finance and Accounting partners, triaging questions, resolving transaction issues, and documenting recurring problems so they can be addressed at the process level
* Analyze large supply chain data sets to measure transaction health, adoption, exception trends and cycle times, and translate findings into clear recommendations and recurring reporting for Supply Chain leadership
* Build and maintain data maps across supplier-facing systems, ERP and downstream reporting, defining field-level mappings, validation rules and supporting documentation that enable integrations and system implementations
* Support system implementation and enhancement projects alongside IT and Business Systems teams: gathering and documenting requirements, writing and executing test cases, validating data, and supporting training and rollout to internal teams and suppliers
* Create and maintain standard operating procedures, process maps and job aids for EDI and Supplier Portal processes, identifying automation and efficiency opportunities as transaction volume grows

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
