Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

**ABOUT THE TEAM**

Anduril’s Manufacturing Maintenance team ensures maximum equipment uptime, builds world-class preventive and predictive maintenance programs, and supports the commissioning and ramp of new production lines. As we stand up and expand facilities (including Arsenal-1), the team is responsible for the reliability of manufacturing equipment that enables rapid, software-defined production of autonomous systems.

**ABOUT THE JOB**

The Industrial Equipment Maintenance Intern will support the reliable operation of production equipment used to build Anduril’s autonomous systems. This is a hands-on role working alongside experienced maintenance specialists, technicians, and engineers. You will gain practical experience with industrial equipment, preventive maintenance systems, troubleshooting, and the unique challenges of high-rate manufacturing for defense applications.

**WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Assist maintenance specialists with preventive and corrective maintenance on manufacturing equipment (assembly systems, automation/robotics, torque tools, machining/fabrication equipment, conveyors, material handling systems, and facility support equipment).
* Support equipment inspections, basic troubleshooting, and recovery of production equipment to minimize downtime.
* Help execute and document work orders in the CMMS; update equipment histories and contribute to PM schedule accuracy.

* Participate in equipment commissioning, startup, and maintainability reviews for new or expanding production lines.

* Support spare parts identification, basic inventory tasks, and vendor coordination under supervision.

* Observe and contribute to reliability activities, including basic root-cause analysis and continuous improvement ideas.

* Read and interpret mechanical drawings, electrical schematics, and equipment manuals with guidance.

* Maintain a clean, organized work area and strictly follow all safety, quality, and environmental procedures.

* Collaborate with production, manufacturing engineering, and quality teams on equipment-related issues.

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Must be pursuing a bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Industrial/Mechanical Engineering Technology, Manufacturing Engineering, Industrial Maintenance, Mechatronics, or a related field.
* Must be returning to school at the end of the internship to continue academic studies for at least one quarter or semester.
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export-controlled information or facilities; U.S. clearance eligibility may be required depending on program.
* Ability to complete an in-person 12-week internship in one of our offices in summer 2027.

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Strong mechanical aptitude and genuine interest in hands-on equipment work and manufacturing reliability.

* Ability to follow procedures, communicate clearly (verbal and written), and work effectively in a team environment.

* Willing to work a standard manufacturing schedule (typically on-site, full-time during the internship term); occasional overtime or shift flexibility may be needed.

* Prior coursework, projects, or experience with industrial equipment, automation, robotics, PLCs, hydraulics, pneumatics, or manufacturing processes.

* Familiarity with CMMS systems, Lean/Six Sigma concepts, or basic reliability practices.

* Hands-on experience with tools, mechanical assembly, or electromechanical systems.

* Interest in defense technology, autonomous systems, or advanced manufacturing.

* Ability to stand, walk, climb, bend, and work in a manufacturing environment for extended periods.

* Lift and carry up to 40–50 lbs; work around automation, noise, and industrial equipment while wearing required PPE (safety glasses, hearing protection, steel-toe shoes, etc.).

* Comfortable working in typical plant conditions (varying temperatures, dust, etc.).

**WHAT YOU’LL GAIN**

* Direct exposure to hyperscale manufacturing of advanced autonomous defense systems at facilities such as Arsenal-1.
* Hands-on experience with modern industrial equipment, automation, and reliability practices in a fast-moving environment.
* Mentorship from maintenance leaders building programs from the ground up.
* Opportunity to contribute meaningfully to equipment uptime and production scale-up.
* Competitive pay, networking across engineering and operations teams, and potential pathways into full-time roles.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
