Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

The **Launched Effects** team at Anduril is responsible for scaling the manufacturing and delivery of the **ALTIUS** family of systems (including the ALTIUS-600 and ALTIUS-700). As the premier Air-Launched Effects (ALE) platform for the U.S. military and its allies, ALTIUS is redefining modern warfare. These tube-launched, autonomous aerial systems must be built to survive extreme deployment environments, requiring precision manufacturing of advanced composites, complex avionics, and modular payload integrations for ISR, Electronic Warfare, and kinetic strike capabilities.  
  
As part of the Launched Effects manufacturing team, you will bridge the gap between cutting-edge R&D and high-rate production. We are a fast-moving, mission-driven group of manufacturing engineers, quality specialists, and production operations experts who refuse to accept traditional, slow defense manufacturing timelines. We continuously optimize our assembly lines, implement automated testing, design for manufacturing (DFM), and build resilient supply chains to deploy reliable systems to the front lines at unprecedented speed. If you want to work on a team where your production and manufacturing decisions directly impact global security, this is it.

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for a Senior Product Quality Engineer (PQE) in Anduril’s Drone Program to join our rapidly growing team in Columbus, OH. In this role, you will define and execute quality strategies across the full product lifecycle of advanced drone systems — from design validation through production and field performance. You will develop and maintain inspection criteria, test protocols, and acceptance standards to ensure hardware meets rigorous reliability and performance requirements. Working closely with design, manufacturing, supplier quality, and support engineering teams, you will collaborate on root cause corrective action (RCCA) efforts both internally and across the supply chain — partnering with supplier quality engineering to investigate non-conformances and drive corrective actions on incoming materials and components. You will champion process improvements that reduce defects and increase yield, maintain quality documentation including control plans, failure analysis reports, and RCCA records, and support new product introductions by establishing quality gates and first-article inspection processes. This role requires expertise in quality methodologies (FMEA, SPC, 8D), a strong foundation in mechanical systems and blueprint interpretation, and hands-on experience with test and measurement tools. If you thrive in a fast-paced environment, bring strong analytical and communication skills, and have a passion for continuous improvement and product excellence, this role is for you.  
  
*This position is permanently based in **Ashville, OH**, and requires candidates to temporarily relocate for a comprehensive 3-month training program: 1 month in **Santa Ana, CA**, followed by 2 months (approximately) in **Atlanta, GA**. To support your transition, Anduril will fully provide and cover all corporate housing, transportation, and travel accommodations during this training period. Your recruiter will discuss the logistics of this program with you in detail during the hiring process.*

*As a **Senior Product Quality Engineer at Anduril in Ashville, OH**, you will work inside & outside the organization to define, develop, and execute processes that deliver high quality programs. This role focuses on collaborating and driving early engagement with our design teams and partner to meet our quality requirements the first time and to evaluate the product health & performance. The right person for this role has knowledge of product performance, sub-system performance criteria, metrology, and continuous improvement. An ideal candidate for this role has experiences in manufacturing, product development, test/system performance criteria, metrology, and quality planning. If you are someone who loves to build world-class quality processes and missile products, work hands-on with engineering, and be accountable for results of a small team, then this role is for you.*

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Drive product development and production for Altius programs using processes such as drawing and work instruction reviews, quality planning, inspection, acceptance test performance management, end-of-line metrics, verification, validation, and qualification of processes and parts, sub-assemblies, and the vehicle, itself.
* Drive clarity & resolution for production ambiguities associated with product maturity, process stability, and field performance.
* Champion the transition from new product introduction (NPI) to a sustainable & predictable, high rate manufacturing process, supply chain, and deployment support.
* Own the elimination of product risk to improve reliability across the product lifecycle.
* Ensure the execution of product and process requirements. Define and improve such requirements.
* Lead Root Cause and Corrective Action (RCCA), Material Review Board (MRB) activities, and inspection process performance. Review the effectiveness of actions and share lessons learned across the product team.
* Lead product health data analysis and improvement activities. Establish metrics for measuring quality and the factors that predict quality performance.
* Support the continuous improvement of the Quality Management System. Define and improve required documentation and activities.
* Develop an overall quality plan (similar to APQP) for various missile projects. Leverage resources and direct reports to delivery quality through the development cycle. Design, development, and leverage elements of the Anduril QMS to build confidence and containment for missile customers quality expectations. Set expectations for qualification of internal and external manufacturing processes including measurement methods, PPAP/FAI, inspection methods & test protocol & acceptance test plans for missile programs. Lead the transition from engineering qualification to manufacturing variance testing for product quality coverage.
* Lead product health data analysis and improvement activities for the missile projects.
* Matrix lead supplier quality engineers who are supporting the design and development activities with the suppliers for Missile programs.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
