### ABOUT THE JOB

You will lead structural mechanical design for a maritime autonomous vehicle. Reporting to the Structures Lead, you will own the primary structure, performing analysis and design for pressure across the full ocean depth envelope.  
  
The structure has to survive full-depth pressure cycling, marine corrosion, and fatigue over the vehicle's service life, while staying light enough to meet buoyancy and endurance targets. Those three pull against each other, and this role owns the trade.

### WHAT YOU'LL DO

* Own structural mechanical design for the vehicle primary structure
* Perform structural analysis: FEA and hand calculations
* Select materials and protective coatings for marine environments
* Define structural interfaces with propulsion, buoyancy, energy, and payload systems
* Support GD&T definition and interface with manufacturing
* Drive trade studies balancing structural mass, strength, and producibility
* Support prototype builds and structural test campaigns
* Contribute to design reviews (PDR, CDR) and documentation
* Travel up to 25% to test sites and manufacturing facilities

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Structural Engineering, or equivalent
* 5+ years of experience in structural design and analysis for marine, aerospace, or robotic systems
* Proficiency with FEA tools (ANSYS, Abaqus, or equivalent)
* Experience with materials selection for corrosive or high-pressure environments
* Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. Secret security clearance

### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Experience with pressure vessel or submarine structure design
* Working knowledge of composite structures for marine applications
* Experience with fatigue and fracture mechanics analysis
* Familiarity with ASME or DNV structural design codes
* Experience in defense or maritime robotics
