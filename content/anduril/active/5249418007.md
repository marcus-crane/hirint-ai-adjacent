**ABOUT THE JOB**  
We seek a **Senior Software Engineer** with hands-on Android Open Source Project (AOSP) development experience on Qualcomm SoC platforms to contribute to low-level system software and platform integration. The ideal candidate has shipped Wi-Fi and/or Bluetooth solutions on Qualcomm AOSP-based products. Candidates will work on customizing and optimizing Android builds, device drivers, HALs, and middleware layers to support wireless connectivity and coexistence. Familiarity with embedded Linux, kernel modules, and SoC-specific toolchains is essential to ensure seamless hardware-software co-design and performance tuning.  
  
**BASIC** **QUALIFICATIONS**

* 8+ years of hands-on experience with the AOSP build system and platform customization
* Experience developing and debugging Wi-Fi and/or Bluetooth software on Qualcomm AOSP SoCs, ideally through to a shipping product
* Knowledge of Qualcomm SoC architecture and driver development
* Proficiency in C/C++ and embedded Linux programming
* Familiarity with cross-compilation toolchains and kernel module development
* Must be able to travel up to 20%
* Must Be Clearance Eligible

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Experience with Android connectivity frameworks and HALs (wpa\_supplicant, cfg80211/mac80211, Bluetooth stack/BTFM)
* Experience with Wi-Fi/Bluetooth coexistence, RF bring-up, and over-the-air debugging
* Experience with hardware bring-up and board debugging
* Understanding of power management and thermal control on mobile SoCs
* Exposure to security features and trusted execution environments (TEE)

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates.
