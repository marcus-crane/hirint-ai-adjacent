### ABOUT THE TEAM

The **Air Dominance and Strike Aerostructures Engineering Team** is responsible for designing and analyzing advanced structural parts for Group 5 air vehicles and missile platforms. Air Dominance and Strike Structures Engineers own the full development lifecycle, from conceptual to detailed design and analysis of primary and secondary structures ensuring high-performance, resilient, and scalable mechanical systems that meet the demands of complex, contested environments.

### **WHAT YOU’LL DO**

* Develop and define loads and environments for missile products at conceptual, preliminary and detailed design  
  maturities
* Build finite elements models for use in structural dynamic simulations
* Perform random vibration and shock analysis
* Determine steady and transient vehicle responses during critical flight events
* Perform multi-body kinematic simulations for events like launch/deployment, staging, and payload separation
* Perform flutter and aeroservoelastic analysis
* Collaborate closely with adjacent disciplines like Aerodynamics, Flight Engineering, Thermal, and Structural  
  Integrity
* Prepare detailed technical reports and present findings to internal teams, management, and external customers
* Develop dynamic test requirements and overall qualification approach for avionics and secondary structures
* End-to-end ownership of modal testing and environmental vibration/shock testing, define instrumentation,  
  perform data processing, perform model correlation

### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor’s degree in engineering or applied physics with a focus in structures
* 5+ years of experience in a structures, loads, or dynamics related role with high ownership and accountability for  
  hardware outcomes
* Deep understanding of vibration theory, structural dynamics, rigid body dynamics, and finite element methods
* Proficiency in the use of FEMAP and SimCenter Nastran or similar
* Proficiency in Matlab, Python, or similar for developing analytical models, data acquisition and automation
* Self-directed, results oriented, and capable of working in a fast-paced development environment
* Excellent time management, organizational, and communication skills
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance

### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Prior experience performing loads and environments analysis during missile or launch vehicle development
* Prior experience predicting captive carriage environments for stores
* Prior experience with launch vehicle coupled loads analysis (CLA)
* Prior experience with modal testings and post-test model correlation
* Familiarity with MIL-STD-810, SMC-S-016, GFSC-STD-7000 or similar
* Experience working with air vehicles operating in high-speed, extreme flight environments
* Experience with the complexities of coupled aero-thermo-structural loading
* Experience with the coupled dynamics of missiles deployed in cannisters and launch tubes
* Ability to obtain and hold a U.S. Top Secret security clearance
