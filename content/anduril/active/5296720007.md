Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

We are seeking a hands-on and detail-oriented Treasury Manager with 3-6 years of progressive corporate treasury experience to help operationalize and scale our global treasury function. Reporting to the Assistant Treasurer and managing a Treasury Analyst, you will own day-to-day execution across global cash management, liquidity forecasting, banking infrastructure, and treasury systems, while helping to design, document, and remediate the treasury controls required to operate as a public company.

### ABOUT THE JOB

This role is equally operational and build-oriented: you will close gaps in existing processes, stand up repeatable controls and reporting, and partner across Accounting, FP&A, Tax, Legal, and the business to ensure Anduril's capital is secure, visible, and productively deployed.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* **Global Cash & Liquidity Management:**
  + Manage daily global cash positioning across all legal entities, ensuring sufficient liquidity to fund operations, payroll, and capital commitments.
  + Prepare, maintain, and continuously improve short- and long-term global cash flow forecasts, including variance analysis against actuals.
  + Execute cash concentration, pooling, and sweep structures to minimize idle balances and optimize working capital internationally.
  + Support intercompany funding, cross-border payments, and cash repatriation in coordination with Tax, Legal, and Accounting.
* **Treasury Operations & Systems:**
  + Own day-to-day treasury operations, including payment initiation and release, bank portal administration, and user access and entitlement reviews.
  + Coordinate the Treasury Management System (TMS) implementation with the outside consultant engaged for the project, serving as the internal day-to-day owner across requirements gathering, configuration, testing, cutover, and ongoing optimization for cash visibility, forecasting, and payments (e.g., Kyriba, Ripple/GTreasury, FIS).
  + Build and maintain bank connectivity and integrations between banking platforms, the TMS, and the ERP.
  + Document treasury processes end to end and drive automation to replace manual, spreadsheet-dependent workflows.
* **Controls, Compliance & Public Company Readiness**:
  + Design, document, and operate key treasury controls, including segregation of duties, payment approval matrices, and bank account authorization standards.
  + Partner with internal control teams and external auditors to remediate identified gaps and evidence control operating effectiveness.
  + Maintain treasury policies and procedures (cash management, investment, FX, bank account management) and monitor compliance with them.
  + Support readiness workstreams required to operate treasury as a public company, including reporting cadence, audit support, and counterparty risk monitoring.
* **Foreign Exchange (FX) Risk Management:**
  + Support execution of hedging transactions (spot and forward) in accordance with the company's foreign exchange policy.
* **Banking Relationship Management:**
  + Administer the global bank account structure, including account opening and closing, signatory maintenance, and KYC documentation.
  + Track bank fees and analyze account analysis statements to identify cost savings and service rationalization opportunities.
  + Serve as a day-to-day point of contact for banking partners and escalate service issues to resolution.
* **Investment & Debt Support:**
  + Execute and monitor short-term investment activity in line with the investment policy, balancing safety, liquidity, and yield.
  + Support monitoring of debt and credit facilities, including draw and repayment mechanics, covenant tracking, and lender reporting.
  + Assist with diligence and execution support for new financing initiatives.
* **Reporting & Analysis:**
  + Prepare recurring treasury reporting for Finance leadership, including liquidity position, forecast accuracy, counterparty exposure, and key risk metrics.
  + Partner with Accounting on cash reconciliations, month-end close support, and cash flow statement inputs.
  + Perform ad-hoc analysis to support capital allocation and finance decision-making.
* **Team Leadership & Development**:
  + Manage, coach, and develop a Treasury Analyst, setting priorities, reviewing daily deliverables, and providing timely feedback and career development support.
  + Delegate and supervise recurring treasury work, including cash positioning, payment processing, bank administration, and reporting, ensuring accuracy and adherence to controls.
  + Build training materials, desktop procedures, and backup coverage so core treasury functions run without single-person dependency.
  + Set performance objectives, conduct performance reviews, and support hiring as the treasury team scales.
* **Projects & Cross-Functional Partnership**:
  + Lead or support treasury projects such as TMS implementation, bank rationalization, new entity and market setup, and M&A integration, managing external consultants and implementation partners against scope, timeline, and deliverables.
  + Partner with Accounting, FP&A, Tax, Legal, Procurement, and business units to support global operations and scale treasury alongside company growth.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* **Education:** Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business Administration, or a related field. CTP (Certified Treasury Professional) is a strong plus.
* **Experience:** 3-6 years of progressive corporate treasury experience, including hands-on ownership of daily cash management and payment operations.
* I**nternational Exposure:** Working knowledge of global cash pooling, cross-border payments, multi-currency bank structures, and FX transactions.
* **Controls Orientation:** Demonstrated experience designing or operating treasury controls, documenting processes, and supporting internal or external audit requests.
* **Leadership:** Experience managing, mentoring, or directing the work of an analyst or junior team member, including reviewing output and developing their capability.
* **Technical Skills:**
  + Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel (financial modeling, pivot tables, macros, etc.).
  + Experience with a Treasury Management System (e.g., Kyriba, Ripple/GTreasury, FIS) is highly preferred.
  + Familiarity with ERP systems (e.g., Oracle Fusion, SAP, etc.).
  + Experience with bank reporting formats and connectivity (BAI2, MT940, host-to-host, API) is a plus.
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export-controlled information or facilities.

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* **Systems Implementation:** Experience running or supporting a TMS deployment alongside an external implementation partner, including vendor management, requirements definition, user acceptance testing, and go-live cutover.
* **Public Company Readiness:** Experience supporting IPO preparation, SOX implementation, or treasury control remediation at a high-growth company.
* **Analytical & Problem-Solving:** Exceptional analytical, quantitative, and problem-solving skills with meticulous attention to detail.
* **Communication:** Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to articulate complex financial concepts clearly to various stakeholders.
* **Self-Starter:** Proactive, organized, and capable of working independently as well as collaboratively in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
* **Adaptability:** Ability to manage multiple priorities and adapt to changing business needs and market conditions.
* **Languages:** Fluency in English required; proficiency in other languages relevant to our global operations is a plus.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
