Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

Anduril is seeking an experienced, hands-on Environment, Health and Safety (EHS) Manager to support our Australian Operations.

As the EHS Manager, you will work directly with operations, engineering, maintenance, project teams and leadership to maintain a strong safety culture, ensure regulatory compliance, manage risk and provide practical solutions that enable the business to operate safely and efficiently.

Anduril operations present a diverse range of high-risk activities, including heavy manufacturing, lifting operations, offshore testing, electrical work, lithium-ion battery systems, machinery and general industrial activities.

The successful candidate will be equally comfortable on the factory floor, working autonomously, in a management meeting, reviewing technical documentation or leading an incident investigation.

This position reports to the EHS Senior Manager for Australia.

### **What You’ll Do**

**Safety Leadership & Culture**

* Own and lead the site’s EHS strategy, programs, standards and continuous-improvement initiatives
* Serve as the primary EHS interface to site leadership and operational teams
* Develop a strong, proactive safety culture where employees and contractors actively identify and control hazards
* Conduct regular workplace inspections, safety observations, risk reviews and leadership safety engagements
* Coach leaders, supervisors and employees in effective risk management and safe work practices
* Develop and deliver EHS training, inductions, toolbox talks and awareness programs

**High-Risk Operations**

Provide specialist EHS oversight and practical guidance for activities including:

* Lifting operations
* Electrical work, electrical safety, isolation/LOTO
* Lithium-ion batteries, including charging, storage, handling, and emergency response
* Offshore testing and field activities, including mobilization, travel, contractor management and emergency planning
* Heavy manufacturing equipment, machinery guarding, hazardous energy and maintenance activities
* Hot work, working at heights and other high-risk work as applicable
* Contractor and visitor safety management

**Risk Management & Compliance**

* Maintain the site’s risk management framework and ensure critical risks have effective controls
* Lead the development and review of risk assessments
* Monitor compliance with applicable Australian legislation, regulations, codes of practice and relevant standards
* Maintain the site’s EHS management system, documentation and records
* Prepare for and support internal, external and regulatory audits
* Track corrective actions are closed effectively

**Incident Management**

* Lead investigations of incidents, injuries, near misses and environmental events
* Identify root causes and implement corrective and preventive actions
* Manage incident reporting, notifications and follow-up in accordance with legal and company requirements
* Analyse trends and use data to identify opportunities for risk reduction
* Coordinate emergency preparedness, drills and response arrangements

**Environmental & Occupational Health**

* Manage the site’s environmental compliance obligations and programs
* Support waste management, hazardous substances, spill prevention and pollution-control initiatives
* Monitor occupational health risks such as noise, hazardous chemicals, dust, ergonomics and other workplace exposures
* Coordinate occupational hygiene assessments and health surveillance where required
* Promote employee wellbeing while maintaining a strong focus on prevention and risk control

**Contractor & Project Support**

* Establish EHS requirements for contractors and verify contractor compliance
* Review equipment specifications, layouts and processes from an EHS perspective
* Support management of change to ensure risks are identified before changes are implemented
* Provide EHS input into new technology, equipment and process introductions

### **Required Qualifications and Experience**

* Bachelor’s degree in Occupational Safety, Industrial Hygiene, Environmental Science, Safety/Systems Engineering, or a related technical discipline
* Progressive EHS experience in engineering, manufacturing, maritime, defence, aerospace, or other complex technical/industrial environments
* Experience conducting hazard analyses or risk assessments integrated with engineering and program decision-making, not purely after-the-fact compliance work
* ICAM Lead Investigator certification
* Demonstrated ability to influence multidisciplinary teams without direct authority
* Strong communication, facilitation, and relationship-building skills
* Ability to translate technical risk into actionable business and engineering recommendations
* Natural tendency towards challenging the status quo; leans into hard problems instead of walking away

### **Preferred Qualifications and Experience**

* Direct experience in maritime, shipbuilding, or naval engineering/operations environments (vessel construction, shipyard operations, marine systems)
* Experience with ISO 45001, ISO 14001
* Advanced degree in Safety Engineering, Environmental/Occupational Health, Systems Engineering, or a related field

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
