Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Shipbuilding Finance team is responsible for driving the financial strategy behind Anduril's new Shipbuilding business, including Arsenal-2 and Anduril's support of the Virginia-class submarine program. Our role plays a pivotal part in that growth through strategic planning, investment analysis, and decision support for leadership. 

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for a Senior Finance Associate to join our team in Costa Mesa, California. In this role, you will build the business line’s forecast and long-range plan, create the financial reviews presented to the C-suite and board, develop the models behind major investment decisions, and run the analysis that informs key strategic and operational decisions. This will require advanced financial modeling skills along with a strong understanding of how investment and deal decisions flow through the financial statements. If you are someone who thinks like an owner, moves quickly on incomplete information, and holds a high bar for accuracy, then this role is for you. 

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Build and maintain the long-range plan and rolling forecast, linking program cost, schedule, and production inputs to the P&L, cash flow, and capital requirements
* Create C-suite, board, and customer presentations, building the analysis that explain actuals, key drivers, risks, and long-term outlook
* Build and maintain the long-term financial model and business case for Arsenal-2
* Build models for major capital decisions (facility and capex investments, financing, and partnerships) with analysis of returns, payback, cash, and margin
* Build dashboards tracking performance vs. plan, spend, and operating metrics such as labor, throughput, and schedule
* Partner with program finance, supply chain, engineering, and operations to gather program-level detail and roll it up into business-line analysis
* Run shipbuilding finance special projects end to end: scope the question, build the analysis, and write the recommendation

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 3+ years of experience in investment banking, private equity, corporate development, or strategic finance
* Bachelor's degree or higher in Finance, Accounting, Economics, or a related field
* Advanced Excel and financial modeling skills, with the ability to build dynamic models from a blank sheet that another person can audit
* Strong understanding of accounting concepts and how deal and capital decisions flow through the financial statements
* Track record of owning recurring corporate finance deliverables, such as forecasts, executive presentations, and KPI reporting, in addition to transaction work
* Strong written and oral communication skills, with the ability to turn complex analysis into crisp recommendations for senior leadership
* Must be authorized to work in the United States

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Experience in aerospace and defense, heavy manufacturing, or other capital-intensive industries
* Direct operating experience in a high-growth environment
* Familiarity with government contracting (contract types, funding and obligations, EACs)
* Familiarity with Oracle ERP, Oracle EPM, Databricks and Palantir Foundry

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
