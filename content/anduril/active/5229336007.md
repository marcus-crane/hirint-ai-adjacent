**ABOUT THE TEAM**

We are looking for a skilled **Metrology Technician** to perform quality inspections on a wide variety of products that we design and develop. In this role you will operate metrology equipment to measure components and assemblies, working from established procedures and inspection plans to generate accurate, defensible quality data. As the Metrology lab grows, you will work across a range of equipment — Portable CMM, CNC/fixed CMM, CT scanner, laser tracker, and optical systems from manufacturers such as Hexagon, Zeiss, Keyence, and LK Metrology — supporting both Production and R&D through receiving inspections, in-process checks, and root cause investigations. 

**WHAT YOU’LL DO** 

* Operate CMM and other metrology equipment to perform precise measurements of parts to a defined inspection plan.

* Interpret engineering drawings, blueprints, and GD&T (ASME Y14.5) to understand measurement specifications.

* Run existing inspection programs and create or edit simple programs using the appropriate software.

* Perform routine calibration and maintenance checks on lab equipment, and help keep the lab clean and organized.

* Analyze measurement data and produce clear, detailed inspection reports.

* Identify out-of-spec results and equipment issues, and escalate appropriately.

* Document observations and communicate findings clearly to the cross-functional teams requesting support.

* Suggest process improvements to enhance measurement efficiency and accuracy.

* Help ensure compliance with industry standards (e.g., AS9100, ISO 17025).

 

**REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS** 

* Associate’s degree or equivalent technical training.

* 2+ years of experience as a quality inspector or CMM operator.

* Experience with metrology hand tools (calipers, micrometers, bore gages, thread gages, etc.).

* Familiarity with CMM software; PolyWorks preferred (PC-DMIS, Calypso, or equivalent acceptable).

* Ability to read and interpret technical drawings and specifications.

* Strong attention to detail and commitment to accuracy.

* Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. security clearance.

 

**PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS** 

* Experience with 3D scanning or CT scanning.

* Experience with optical measurement systems (OMM or 2D comparator).

* Working knowledge of GD&T (ASME Y14.5).

* Experience with PolyWorks|DataLoop or similar metrology data management platforms.

* Familiarity with AS9100 or ISO 17025 quality systems.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
