We're seeking a Senior Product Designer to join ArsenalOS Design. This is a generalist seat on the design team. You will serve wherever ArsenalOS needs design most. Right now we need an execution-minded designer to serve supply chain and supplier experience. Building the experiences that ensure our customers get the very best, and the external hub for anyone who wants to supply Anduril's manufacturing. You'll produce high-quality work at speed, validating ideas quickly and building momentum wherever the team's priorities point next.

### What You'll Do

As a Senior Product Designer on ArsenalOS Design, you'll work in service of the broader design team. Building up context to swarm wherever ArsenalOS needs design partnership most. Right now that's looks like: Supplier Portal, Non-Conformances, Inspections, and the system for supply chain. Ideal state is connecting information and action to the right person at the right time - bridging technical processes with business workflows. More tactically, you will:

* Design intuitive interfaces that connect technical systems and business functions, improving process efficiency, evaluation, and reporting
* Own information architecture, flows, and a lightweight design PRDs before implementation begins. Components shipped without a designer-blessed architecture break; you are the layer against that
* Judge experience, whether a problem calls for new craft or for reuse. Extending an existing ArsenalOS pattern, borrowing a primitive from elsewhere in the system, in service of one coherent product rather than a patchwork of team-local UIs
* Rapidly iterate and prototype, with strong visual and interaction craft
* Own all aspects of design execution from discovery through implementation and support
* Collaborate directly with cross-functional teams - PMs, engineering, supply chain - to understand their workflows and translate complex processes into intuitive interfaces
* Partner with Design Leads while exercising appropriate autonomy in your daily work, making decisions quickly to maintain momentum
* Balance immediate tactical solutions that address urgent business needs while contributing to long-term design impact and value
* Create scalable components that work across device types, considering the unique environments where our technology operates
* Engage in contextual research by visiting operational environments to gain firsthand understanding of user needs and processes
* Communicate design decisions effectively to both technical and non-technical stakeholders, bringing them along in your process
* Collaborate with other designers across multiple verticals to maintain consistency and share best practices, working in the open to ensure cross-functional awareness

### Who You Are

* **Creative by default.** Your work opens a world of possibilities for what we can build. You garner feedback with team members, walk in your users' shoes, and explore external trends to get your inspiration. You thrive in ideation and refine through iteration, and you lean on your instinct and experience to produce work that scales and provides delight.
* **Visual design expert.** You have a deep understanding of visual design principles, including composition, color theory, typography, and Gestalt principles. You can create visually stunning and functional designs that enhance user experience and brand identity.
* **Systems thinker who knows when not to design something new.** You think beyond the pixels and consider the entire experience holistically. Faced with a new problem, your first instinct is to ask what already exists in the system that solves it. Then to extend or promote that pattern rather than build a one-off. When something genuinely warrants new craft, you can make that call and defend it. You champion the difference between the two to the rest of the team.
* **Storyteller.** You are an effective communicator. You can tell a story through the lens of your users, and your mocks resonate because you understand them well. Your story has a beginning, middle, and end, taking your audience on a journey that engages their problems head on, welcomes feedback, all while showing the savvy of the solution.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
