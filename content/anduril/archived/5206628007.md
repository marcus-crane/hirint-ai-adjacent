By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. ### **ABOUT THE JOB**

Anduril is seeking a Lead Electrical Engineer to join our Maneuver Dominance team. As a Lead Electrical Engineer, you will own the electrical architecture and technical direction of an aircraft development program from early concept through fielded production. This role serves as the primary electrical point of contact across the program, partnering closely with mechanical, software, firmware, and program leadership to ensure a cohesive, reliable system. You'll guide the vehicle through the full development lifecycle - from design and integration to rigorous testing - while setting technical standards and holding final sign-off authority on electrical designs. As a senior technical leader, you'll also mentor and grow a team of engineers, shaping both the platform and the people behind it. This is a unique opportunity to lead the electrical foundation of a next-generation autonomous aircraft, working at the intersection of innovation, speed, and real-world impact.  
  
**WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Own the electrical architecture and technical roadmap for a major subsystem or full vehicle platform, from concept through fielded production
* Lead full cycle PCB design efforts across multiple concurrent boards/subsystems, including requirements definition, schematic design, component selection, layout oversight, bring-up, test, debug, and system integration
* Serve as the technical lead for troubleshooting issues that span electrical, firmware, and software boundaries, guiding junior and senior engineers through complex root-cause analysis
* Drive the architecture and development of payloads and subsystems (sensors, payload interfaces, power management, and networking) across multiple programs or a single high-complexity program
* Define and own system interconnect diagrams and wire harness architecture at the vehicle or subsystem level, ensuring compatibility across all integrated electronic equipment
* Lead cross-functional collaboration with electrical, mechanical, software, firmware, and test engineering teams, acting as the primary electrical point of contact for program leadership
* Operate and lead teams in a fast-paced environment supporting new developments, customer-operated hardware, and rapid field iteration
* Lead and facilitate peer-level and cross-discipline design reviews, holding final technical sign-off authority on electrical designs within your scope
* Own the integration & testing strategy of a rotorcraft system through TRLs 3-8, including risk identification and mitigation planning
* Lead vehicle platform system design & analysis, setting technical direction and standards for the broader engineering team
* Own requirements analysis, verification planning, and compliance strategy at the program level, ensuring alignment with project goals and industry standards
* Mentor and develop Electrical Engineers and Senior Electrical Engineers, providing technical guidance, career development, and design oversight
* Partner with program management to inform scope, schedule, and resourcing decisions based on technical risk and complexity
* Represent the electrical engineering function in customer-facing technical discussions and design reviews as needed

#### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Bachelor's Degree in Electrical Engineering or equivalent
* 8+ years of experience designing, testing, and troubleshooting complex board designs, with demonstrated ownership of full subsystems or platforms
* Proven track record leading electrical design efforts from concept through fielded/production hardware
* Deep knowledge of modern analog and digital electronics and electronic circuits
* Expert-level competence with test equipment such as oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, thermal chambers, current-probes, and automation of tests
* Strong familiarity with standard interfaces such as Ethernet, CAN, I2C, SPI, PCIe, USB, etc.
* Strong familiarity with common MCU, CPU, FPGA devices and technologies
* Familiarity with software development processes and tools such as git, Github, JIRA, etc.
* Advanced proficiency with electronic CAD tools (Altium preferred, Eagle and others acceptable)
* Demonstrated experience in design for signal integrity / power integrity at a system level
* Experience with battery systems and ability to lead alternate battery systems design efforts
* Extensive experience with high reliability systems, including design for redundancy and fault tolerance
* Experience with radio frequency-based systems
* Experience leading rapid payload/sensor integration efforts across multiple teams
* Experience designing and integrating high power electronics at scale
* Experience in fieldable system design, including lessons learned from fielded hardware
* Expert-level ability conducting worst case circuit or uncertainty analysis, and teaching this skill to others
* Demonstrated ability to lead and mentor engineers, with high ownership and a strong bias toward action
* Experience balancing multiple concurrent projects/programs and prioritizing competing technical demands
* Eligible to obtain and maintain an active U.S. Secret security clearance

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
