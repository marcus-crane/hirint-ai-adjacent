Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

### ABOUT THE ROLE

We are seeking a **Principal Technical Program Manager (TPM)** reporting to the Head of Infrastructure Engineering to lead mission-critical, high-visibility engineering initiatives across the company. In this role, you will operate at the intersection of technical depth, executive leadership, and operational execution.   
  
Specifically, you will function in three primary capacities as **Chief of Staff (~15%)** to the department head—driving strategic planning, org-wide operating cadences, executive alignment, and unblocking critical internal engineering efforts. Simultaneously, you will own InfraEng’s **Operational Excellence (OE) charter** **(~25 - 35%)** across Cloud, Platform, Networking, and Datacenter/On-Prem functions, driving rigor throughout the organization to ensure enterprise-grade reliability and QOS paces our audacious expansion and growth. You will also directly quarterback **high-visibility flagship programs (~50-60%)** —ranging from critical reliability and resilience efforts, cross-cutting architectural migrations, and manufacturing enablement.

### **WHAT YOU'LL DO**

#### 1. Chief of Staff Function & Strategic Leadership (~15%)

***Curating the rhythm that produces speed, accuracy, executive sponsorship and trust:***

* Partner directly with the Head of Infrastructure Engineering to translate organizational vision into prioritized technical roadmaps, resource allocations, and cross-team execution plans.
* Execute and continuously tune the department's operational rhythm, including executive business reviews, operational reviews, and C-level briefings spanning complex engineering initiatives.
* Drive cross-functional alignment and unblock dependencies across InfraEng in order to deliver value greater than the sum of individual teams/capabilities.
* Spearhead strategic initiatives, vendor and service provider restructurings, and cost-optimization programs to maximize efficiency without compromising security or deployment velocity.

#### 2. Operational Excellence (OE) & Resilience (~25 - 35%)

***Defining, measuring and continuously improving the processes and technologies that achieve unreasonably high SLOs, SLAs and QoS.***

* Define, launch, scale and measure Operational Excellence (OE) processes and mechanisms across all networking, cloud, platform, and datacenter functions.
* Identify and scope tooling and automation opportunities that continuously lessen human intervention as a success dependency.
* Institute systemic availability frameworks, observability and incident recovery strategies, and automated zonal failure detection and mitigation standards.
* Lead high-leverage operational forums (e.g., Weekly Infrastructure Operations Reviews), engineering rigor inspections, post-incident reports and root-cause remediations.
* Design and implement (organization and/or enterprise-wide) process, standards, common ways of working where none exist today.

#### 3. Flagship Special Programs (~50%)

***Single Threaded Owner (STO) and organizational API for initiatives such as:***

* Directly execute high-stakes, time-critical engineering programs spanning hybrid infrastructure, Factory Systems and Manufacturing networks, sovereign defense enclaves, multi-cloud user enablement.
* Quarterback strategic responses to systemic, enterprise-wide risks, orchestrating cross-functional technical remediations at scale.
* Drive technical readiness and architecture validation for complex customer scenarios and rapid Department of Defense / warfighter scenario builds on modern infrastructure.
* Champion foundational modernization programs, including Infrastructure as Code (IaC) and Software Defined Networking (SDN), least-privilege automation.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* **10+ years** of technical program management, engineering or engineering leadership experience driving complex, high-impact programs across distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, and enterprise platforms.
* Demonstrated experience acting as a **strategic advisor, Principal TPM, or Chief of Staff counterpart** to executive engineering leaders, managing cross-organizational strategy, headcount/resource prioritization, and executive reporting.
* Deep hands-on expertise building and running **Operational Excellence (OE), Site Reliability (SRE), or High Availability (HA)** frameworks across multi-region cloud (AWS/Azure/GCP) and hybrid/datacenter environments.
* Proven track record leading **large-scale incident mitigation, failure testing, and automated resilience initiatives** with aggressive recovery time objectives.
* Broad technical literacy spanning core IT/infrastructure domains: cloud computing architectures, networking (LAN/WAN/routing/SD-WAN), compute/virtualization/Kubernetes, and enterprise security/IAM.
* Exceptional communication skills with the ability to translate deep technical risk and architectural trade-offs into actionable executive-level business decisions.
* Ability to thrive in high-tempo, ambiguous, mission-critical environments with complete autonomy.
* Must be a U.S. Person and be eligible to obtain and maintain a U.S. Government Top Secret security clearance.

### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Direct experience supporting **defense technology, aerospace operations, mission control/constellation reliability, or government contracts**.
* Experience designing and executing enterprise-wide security, IAM/ABAC modernization, or agentic AI/autonomous workflow controls at scale.
* Background in streamlining regulated SDLC/CI-CD workflows, compressing deployment cycles through risk-based engineering automation.
* Active U.S. Secret or Top Secret/SCI clearance.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
