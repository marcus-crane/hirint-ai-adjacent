Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

#### **ABOUT THE TEAM**

The Counter-Intrusion Business Line works on products that enable operators to protect their people and assets from threats on land and sea. We are deployed in every major global operating theater and support a diversity of mission sets across both U.S. Government and allied partners.

#### **ABOUT THE JOB**

As a Product Operations Engineer on the Counter-Intrusion product operations team, you will be the architect behind how we observe, maintain, and evolve our deployed fleet. Where others respond to incidents, you build the systems, queries, and processes that prevent them. You will design and develop uptime reporting, plan and execute software deployments to towers, create and refine monitoring solutions for both new and legacy products, and author the runbooks and SOPs that the broader team relies on daily.  
  
You will own the operational tooling, reporting infrastructure, and deployment processes that keep our Counter-Intrusion technology healthy at scale. You should bring strong technical depth in observability and systems thinking, paired with the judgment to prioritize what to build next based on fleet data and operational patterns. If you are passionate about ground-breaking technology, contributing to the national security mission, and engineering operational excellence from the ground up, Anduril is interested in speaking with you.

#### **WHAT YOU'LL DO**

* Design, develop, and maintain queries and dashboards for uptime reporting across the Counter-Intrusion deployed fleet, translating raw telemetry into actionable fleet health metrics
* Serve as the product operations planner and executor for software changes deployed to towers, owning the end-to-end lifecycle from change validation through rollout and post-deployment verification
* Work with tower software engineering to develop and tailor monitoring solutions for new tower deployments, ensuring observability is built in from day one rather than bolted on after the fact
* Improve and optimize monitoring configurations for legacy products, identifying gaps in coverage and reducing alert noise to surface genuine issues
* Continuously author, update, and refine runbooks, SOPs, and troubleshooting guides, ensuring operational knowledge is codified, accurate, and accessible to the team
* Assess and ratchet up the quality of the fleet's observability and telemetry in partnership with product and engineering teams, driving standards for what good monitoring looks like
* Analyze fleet-wide operational data to identify systemic reliability trends, proactively surfacing issues before they become incidents
* Define and implement process improvements for deployment workflows, incident response procedures, and operational tooling based on data-driven insights
* Develop and maintain automation to reduce manual operational overhead across the fleet

#### **REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* 5+ years of experience in a technical operations or engineering role with hands-on responsibility for production systems
* Prior experience in a product operations, technical support, or site reliability role supporting deployed hardware/software systems, with demonstrated progression in scope and ownership
* Proficiency in developing monitoring queries, dashboards, and alerting configurations using observability platforms such as Datadog, Grafana, or similar tooling
* Experience planning and executing software deployments to distributed systems, including rollout strategy, validation, and rollback procedures
* Scripting and automation skills (e.g., Python, Bash) with a track record of building tooling that improves operational efficiency
* Experience with software development tooling such as Git, and issue tracking tools such as Jira
* Demonstrated ability to author clear, thorough technical documentation including runbooks, SOPs, and troubleshooting guides
* Proven ability to work independently, identify what needs to be built, and deliver it without close direction
* Excellent written, visual, and verbal communication skills
* Active U.S. security clearance required

#### **PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS**

* Prior military or government agency exposure/experience
* Experience building queries and dashboards in Datadog, Grafana (PromQL/LogQL), or equivalent observability platforms.
* Experience with Linux system administration and command-line troubleshooting of electromechanical or networked systems
* Experience supporting autonomous sensor tower systems or similar deployed C-UAS/counter-intrusion technology
* Exposure to Anduril's Lattice OS or similar command-and-control software platforms
* Experience operating in limited risk tolerance production environments with on-call responsibilities
* Willingness to work non-standard hours and weekends when operational needs require

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
