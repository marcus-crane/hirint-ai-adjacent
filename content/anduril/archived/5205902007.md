### ABOUT THE TEAM

The Manufacturing Production team is responsible for wide range of activities that are crucial to the manufacturing process including operating equipment, assembling products, performing quality control checks, and maintaining a safe and efficient work environment.

### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for a Production Test Specialist to join our rapidly growing team in Santa Ana, CA. In this role, you will be responsible for conducting functional and acceptance testing on electronic assemblies, subsystems, and finished products to ensure they meet performance specifications and quality standards. If you are a highly motivated team player who thrives in a fast-paced environment and is a detail-oriented self-starter able to work with minimal oversight, then this role is for you.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* Execute functional, acceptance, and environmental stress screening (ESS) tests on electronic assemblies, subsystems, and finished products per approved procedures
* Set up and operate test stations, fixtures, and automated test equipment (ATE), using instruments such as oscilloscopes, DMMs, spectrum analyzers, power supplies, signal generators, and data acquisition systems
* Analyze test data, identify anomalies, and document pass/fail results per quality and configuration management requirements
* Troubleshoot and isolate failures to the component, board, or subsystem level, coordinating with engineering on root cause analysis and corrective action
* Read and interpret test procedures, schematics, wiring diagrams, and engineering drawings to ensure correct test configurations
* Perform first article inspections and in-process quality checks
* Maintain accurate test logs, defect records, and traceability documentation
* Support development of new test procedures, fixtures, and process improvements, and ensure test equipment is calibrated per schedule

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* 3+ years of experience in production, functional, or acceptance testing of electronic assemblies, PCBAs, or integrated systems
* Proficiency with common test instruments (oscilloscopes, DMMs, signal/function generators, power supplies, spectrum analyzers) and ability to read schematics, wiring diagrams, and test procedures
* Experience documenting test results, generating reports, and maintaining traceability records
* Strong troubleshooting skills, able to isolate failures to the component or subsystem level
* Basic electrical understanding (Ohm's Law, voltage drop, signal integrity, power measurements) and familiarity with common connectors (Molex, Amphenol, TE Connectivity, Hirose)
* Experience with automated test equipment (ATE) or test automation frameworks
* Knowledge of ESD-safe handling practices
* Meticulous attention to detail in executing tests, recording data, and documenting findings
* Able to lift up to 50 lbs. unassisted
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export controlled information or facilities

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Associate degree or higher in electronics, electrical engineering technology, or related field
* Experience with LabVIEW, Python, or other test automation/scripting tools, and version control systems (e.g., Git)
* Familiarity with environmental testing (thermal cycling, vibration, humidity) and ESS procedures
* Working knowledge of IPC-A-610 acceptability standards; hand soldering per J-STD-001 and/or rework experience
* Knowledge of ISO 9001 / AS9100 Quality Management Systems
* Aviation, defense, or aerospace production testing experience
* Experience with data analysis or statistical process control (SPC) for test yield monitoring

#### **ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS**

* Requires the flexibility to work assigned shifts, overtime, weekends, and extended hours during peak periods as needed.
* Able to stand for long periods, climb ladders, bend, grasp, sit, pull, push, stoop, and stretch as needed
* Able to travel 50% of the time with short notice

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
