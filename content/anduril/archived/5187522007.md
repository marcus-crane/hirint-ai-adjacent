Anduril Industries is a defense technology company with a mission to transform U.S. and allied military capabilities with advanced technology. By bringing the expertise, technology, and business model of the 21st century’s most innovative companies to the defense industry, Anduril is changing how military systems are designed, built and sold. Anduril’s family of systems is powered by Lattice OS, an AI-powered operating system that turns thousands of data streams into a realtime, 3D command and control center. As the world enters an era of strategic competition, Anduril is committed to bringing cutting-edge autonomy, AI, computer vision, sensor fusion, and networking technology to the military in months, not years.

We are looking for a Quality Control Manager to lead one of our production quality control teams at our factories in the Costa Mesa area.  This role will manage multiple supervisors with leads and inspectors for the quality control team.  You will partner with heads of production, product quality engineers, manufacturing engineers, and our production teams to ensure quality controls are executed as per expectations. This is not just inspection but leading the development of the controls as well as what happens when controls are exceeded.   As a leader, you will be accountable for labor, processes execution, continuous improvement, and advocating quality behaviors across the various production functions.  You will partner across the organization and help prioritize process and product quality issues that impact the effectiveness of the control process and the quality of Anduril's products.  This role will focus on both product quality issues as well as process issues that impact efficiencies and completeness quality control operations.

#### What you'll do:

* Drive a safety first culture and mentality with your team and partners.  Expedite resolution of any safety issues impacting the environment.
* Manage our quality control resource plan for the sites and processes, including resource planning, recruiting, hiring, onboarding, and performance management.
* Ensure that employees are trained & qualified on work  instructions, procedures, and hands-on functions, documenting training  records.  Must be able to pass internal audits.
* Establish performance management and quality controls / insights using dashboard data, production priorities meetings, and collaboration with data reporting teams.
* Develop and maintain Quality control standards, ensuring consistent application through staff  training.
* Drive resource alignment and inspector capabilities for:  Incoming/Receiving Material  Inspection, In-process inspection, End-of-line Inspection, material  containment, and MRB inspection operations.
* Drive quality process improvement (QPIs) and investment in manufacturing execution system (MES) and quality management systems (QMS) procedures.
* Collaborate with other quality control managers & product quality engineers to ensure consistent execution of the quality controls.
* Collaborate with Quality Engineering in the creation of inspection plans for incoming materials,  parts, and assemblies from suppliers and monitor their compliance
* Utilize Lean Principles  to improve key KPIs/Processes such as On-Time Delivery and Product Quality
* Propose, develop and implement quality assurance policies and procedures and review with the product quality engineers
* Partner with Development Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, and Product Quality Engineering to support the new product introduction and prototype part process.  Work with incomplete inspection requirements and early design phase parts to capture observations and identification of possible key and critical characteristics that may impact the final inspection plan/procedure/part drawing or specification.
* Ensure equipment and facilities are ready and available to meet quality control requirements.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. * **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).

* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
