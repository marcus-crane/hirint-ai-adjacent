### ABOUT THE JOB

The Manufacturing Team is responsible for rapidly iterating and building cutting-edge defense hardware, including static equipment, moving ground equipment, and flight vehicles. In this role; you will be assigned to a wide range of projects across manufacturing engineering, manufacturing operations, and manufacturing development, and you will need to adapt quickly as priorities shift. On any given week you might be standing up a new production line, driving KPI tracking and reporting, running a scrappy analysis to unblock a decision, or helping coordinate a facility move. No two projects look the same, and success depends on how quickly you can get up to speed on unfamiliar problems.  
You should be comfortable with manufacturing processes and be able to translate technical discussions with engineers into clear action items, schedules, and owners from a project management perspective. Deep, hands-on technical manufacturing expertise is not required, but familiarity with how things are built — and the ability to hold your own in a technical conversation — will help you succeed. You’ll support increasing efficiencies throughout our end-to-end processes, with a focus on introducing scale and resiliency into our growing people and tools footprint. If you thrive in fast-paced, technical environments and excel at juggling multiple responsibilities, we want to hear from you.

#### WHAT YOU'LL DO:

**Project Planning and Execution**

* Define project scope, create schedules, and manage all phases from conception to completion across a shifting mix of manufacturing and production projects.
* Develop and manage project costs, and ensure adequate staffing and tools are available.
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams including manufacturing engineering, manufacturing operations, design engineering, industrial engineering, business lines, and facilities — gathering requirements, providing updates, managing expectations, and addressing concerns.
* Lead initiatives across Anduril sites to solve ongoing production issues, set up new processes that will help harmonize production locally and globally
* Translate technical discussions with engineers into concrete action items, owners, and timelines from a project management perspective.

**Program Oversight and Support**

* Support program managers and manufacturing leadership in the preparation and management of critical technical and business forums.
* Provide structure to ambiguity and help drive stakeholders toward decisions on tactical and strategic issues.
* Develop and execute strategies to drive business impact and outcomes, and own large parts of end-to-end manufacturing KPI tracking to enhance transparency and generate actionable reports.

**Data Analysis and Reporting**

* Execute scrappy analyses on short timelines to get answers quickly, while also considering more scalable solutions that will make Anduril successful in the long term.
* Analyze manufacturing data to identify trends, patterns, and areas for improvement in manufacturing processes.
* Facilitate data-driven decision-making by providing timely and accurate manufacturing information.

**Systems and Processes Improvement**

* Develop an understanding of how our manufacturing systems (ERP, MRP, MES, PLM, etc.) function and how to leverage data from them to equip teams with insights and tools for tactical and strategic decisions.
* Analyze best practices across teams and develop ways to standardize and implement them across all teams.
* Assist in the documentation and standardization of business procedures and reporting protocols.
* Collaborate with the digital tools team to evaluate solutions that improve the efficiency of engineers in delivering simulations, capacity plans, and facility layouts.
* Support adoption tracking for new manufacturing standards across a distributed team.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:

* Bachelor’s degree in engineering, data science, business, or a related field.
* 2+ years of experience in a manufacturing environment, with a focus on project or program management, data analysis, or process improvement.
* Familiarity with manufacturing processes and the ability to translate technical discussions into action items, schedules, and owners.
* Comfort adapting across a wide variety of project types and getting up to speed quickly on unfamiliar problems.
* Strong analytical aptitude, with the ability to intuitively think about problems in terms of numbers and data models.
* Strong written and verbal communication abilities, with the capacity to present complex data clearly to diverse audiences.
* A reliable self-starter — highly organized, resourceful, and detail-oriented.
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export-controlled information or facilities.

#### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:

* Hands-on or technical manufacturing experience (helpful but not required).
* Familiarity with MES, ERP, and PLM systems and how they interface with each other and with operations.
* Familiarity with Confluence/JIRA, SharePoint, and Airtable.
* Familiarity with SQL, TypeScript, Power BI, Tableau, Foundry, and Databricks.

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
