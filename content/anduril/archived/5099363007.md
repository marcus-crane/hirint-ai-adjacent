### ABOUT THE JOB

We are looking for a **Director of Space Planning** to join our rapidly growing team in **Costa Mesa**. As Director of Space Planning, you will lead our Occupancy Planning team within the Real Estate & Facilities (RE&F) organization. Reporting to the VP of Global Real Estate and Facilities, you will play a pivotal strategic role in shaping how our dynamic, multi-division organization uses its Southern California real estate to drive mission-critical outcomes. This is a high-visibility leadership position at the intersection of business strategy, workplace enablement, and operational excellence in a complex, growth-oriented environment. 

You will build and lead a high-performing team responsible for turning headcount forecasts, technical requirements, and cross-functional adjacencies into intelligent, executable space allocation and move strategies that support rapid scaling while optimizing utilization and employee experience.

#### WHAT YOU’LL DO

* **Lead Occupancy Demand Forecasting**: Partner closely with leaders across business divisions (each with multiple product lines) and G&A functions to develop and maintain rolling 18-24 month space demand forecasts. Integrate headcount projections for office/desk needs with detailed technical/light industrial space requirements (e.g., labs, integration/test areas, specialized infrastructure). Refresh forecasts quarterly.
* **Drive Adjacency and Portfolio Optimization:** Analyze and incorporate complex adjacency requirements to foster collaboration, innovation, and operational efficiency across divisions. Develop and present multiple space allocation plan options that balance individual and enterprise needs, then facilitate cross-organizational governance processes to secure decisions on building-level assignments. Refresh comprehensive portfolio allocation plans biannually.
* **Transition Planning:** Partner with Facilities Workplace  teams and Design and Construction teams to develop execution of detailed occupancy and construction plans, drive change management and phasing strategies to relocate teams efficiently with minimal disruption to critical defense programs.
* **Team Leadership & Capability Building:** Lead, mentor, and grow a Space Planning team. Implement best-in-class tools, processes, and data-driven methodologies (e.g., stack planning, utilization analytics, scenario modeling) to elevate the function. Own and drive this function across our Southern California real estate portfolio. Own and drive the global planning program methodologies and approach to be used across RE&F.
* **Stakeholder Engagement & Strategic Alignment:** Serve as a trusted advisor to business division leaders, G&A executives, and RE&F peers. Collaborate with Design & Construction, Facilities Operations, and Workplace teams to ensure plans align with broader portfolio strategy, workplace standards, and business priorities.
* **Portfolio-Wide Impact:** Contribute to longer-term portfolio strategy as the company scales toward 10-12 million square feet globally, with heavy emphasis on the Southern California hub.

#### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS

* **Proven Expertise****:** 10 years of progressive experience in space planning, occupancy planning, workplace strategy, or facilities planning, with at least 5 years in a leadership role managing complex, multi-building portfolios (ideally 2M+ sq ft).
* **Tech/Complex Environment Background:** Demonstrated success in fast-growing manufacturing technology or similarly dynamic multi-division organizations. Experience supporting R&D, industrial, and office environments is highly preferred.
* **Strategic & Analytical Acumen:** Strong ability to translate business forecasts and technical requirements into actionable space plans. Proficiency with space management software (e.g., IWMS, CAFM, Stack Planning tools), data analytics, and scenario modeling.
* **Leadership & Influence:** Track record of building high-performing teams and influencing senior executives and cross-functional stakeholders in matrixed environments. Excellent facilitation and communication skills.
* **Change Management:** Experience planning and executing large-scale moves and portfolio transformations in growth settings.
* **Education:** Bachelor’s degree in Architecture, Interior Design, Real Estate, Facilities Management, Business, or related field (Master’s or relevant certifications like COREnet, IFMA, or AIA a plus).
* Must be a U.S. Person due to required access to U.S. export controlled information or facilities

### **Protecting Yourself from Recruitment Scams**

Anduril is committed to maintaining the integrity of our Talent acquisition process and the security of our candidates. We've observed a rise in sophisticated phishing and fraudulent schemes where individuals impersonate Anduril representatives, luring job seekers with false interviews or job offers. These scammers often attempt to extract payment or sensitive personal information.

To ensure your safety and help you navigate your job search with confidence, please keep the following critical points in mind:

* **No Financial Requests:**Anduril will never solicit payment or demand personal financial details (such as banking information, credit card numbers, or social security numbers) at any stage of our hiring process. Our legitimate recruitment is entirely free for candidates.
* **Please always verify communications:**
  + Direct from Anduril: If you receive an email from one of our recruiters, it will *only* come from an `@anduril.com` address.
  + Via Agency Partner: If contacted by a recruiting agency for an Anduril role, their email will clearly identify their agency. If you suspect any suspicious activity, please verify the agency's authenticity by reaching out to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com).
* **Exercise Caution with Unsolicited Outreach:** If you receive any communication that appears suspicious, contains grammatical errors, or makes unusual requests, do not engage. Always confirm the sender's email domain is @anduril.com before providing any personal information or clicking on links.
* **What to Do If You Suspect Fraud:** Should you encounter any questionable or fraudulent outreach claiming to be from Anduril, please report it immediately to [contact@anduril.com](mailto:contact@anduril.com). Your proactive caution is invaluable in protecting your personal information and upholding the security and trustworthiness of our recruitment efforts.
