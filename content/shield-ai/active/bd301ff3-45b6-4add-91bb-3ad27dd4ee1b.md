Shield AI is a venture-backed defense-tech company with the mission of protecting service members and civilians with intelligent systems. Its products include Hivemind autonomy software, V-BAT and X-BAT aircraft, and Aechelon simulation and synthetic reality technologies. With offices and facilities across the U.S., Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, Shield AI’s technology actively supports operations worldwide. For more information, visit [www.shield.ai](https://shield.ai/). Follow Shield AI on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shield-ai), [X](https://x.com/shieldaitech), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/shield_ai), and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Shield_AI).

The Hivemind Division of Shield AI seeks a highly skilled and detail-oriented Autonomy Engineer with Technical Writing Skills to join our team. The ideal candidate will have a background in software engineering and robotics and be able to become a superuser of our Hivemind Enterprise product: a software product that enables scalable design, deployment, evaluation, and iteration of autonomy architectures for a wide range of robotic platforms. This role requires you to be able to work cross-functionally with developers across engineering teams and learn to use the Hivemind SDK product, then draft your knowledge into well-written technical documentation and clearly communicated tutorials capable of being followed by customers with wide differences in skills and level of experience.

### What you'll do:

* Become an expert user of the Hivemind Enterprise product.
* Contribute to the selection of documentation creation platforms and tools.
* Work with developers and our Customer Engagement team to generate technical writing to describe product functionalities.
* Generate diagrams, flow charts, and other visual aids to describe supported workflows.
* Devise a strategy for connecting auto-generated API documentation with hand-written user guides.

### Required qualifications:

* Background in robotics and autonomy.
* Coding proficiency with C++.
* Highly proficient at technical writing (academic papers, technical tutorials, software product documentation, etc.).
* Strong understanding of what excellence in software product documentation looks like.
* Comfortable working in a Linux environment.
* Fast thinker, fast doer, able to generate results rapidly with minimal input in order to unblock iteration.
* Excellent communicator.

### Preferred qualifications:

* Familiar with software engineering practices with regards to software versioning, packaging, deployment.
* Experience within the aviation and/or aerospace industry.
