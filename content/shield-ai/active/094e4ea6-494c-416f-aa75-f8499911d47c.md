### Job Description:

**The Strategic Autonomy Capture Manager, PACOM** will lead Shield AI’s mission autonomy engagement strategy and design capture plans involving PACOM entities. This individual will guide, shape, and inform theater service-component command relationships and influence policy and requirements across Shield AI’s product portfolios.

### What you'll do:

* Develop and implement a comprehensive strategic capture plan spanning Shield AI’s mission autonomy product portfolios, with a focus on growing programs with long-term revenue potential and mission impact.
* Drive teams toward multi-year strategic PACOM autonomy program wins.
* Build and maintain trusted relationships across key PACOM decision makers, including requirements and acquisition officials.
* Anticipate customer needs and influence policy, requirements, and solicitations to positively align with Shield AI portfolios.

### Required qualifications:

* 10+ years experience in strategic capture or policy development in the USG
* Deep knowledge of PACOM theater priorities and decision-making processes, with exposure to operational missions
* Experience with emerging technologies / AI
* Strong leadership, communication, and analytical skills with the ability to cross-collaborate across technical and operational teams
* Active Secret clearance (TS preferred)

### Preferred qualifications:

* Advanced degree in business, engineering, or a related field
* Prior PACOM assignment experience
