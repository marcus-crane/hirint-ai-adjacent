### Job Description:

Are you a curious and motivated engineer looking to help develop state-of-the-art autonomous aircraft? Are you eager to apply your engineering fundamentals to real hardware while learning from experienced teammates? Do you want to work alongside mission-driven and values-focused people? Shield AI may be the place for you.

As a Mechanical Engineer on the Mechanisms team, you will contribute to the design, development, and integration of mechanical systems for Shield AI’s autonomous aircraft. Your work may include a focus on gearbox development and integration, including gears, shafts, bearings, housings, lubrication systems, and related interfaces.

You will complete well-defined engineering tasks with guidance from experienced engineers and collaborate with internal and external partners to design, prototype, test, and release hardware. This role offers the opportunity to build practical experience across the product-development lifecycle while helping the team achieve its performance, schedule, and reliability goals.

### What you'll do:

* Support the design and integration of mechanical mechanisms for autonomous aircraft, potentially including gearboxes and other power-transmission systems
* Create and update 3D CAD models, engineering drawings, bills of materials, and interface documentation
* Complete defined design tasks involving component sizing, material selection, tolerance analysis, and mechanical packaging
* Perform foundational structural, kinematic, and machine-design calculations with guidance from senior engineers
* Assist with preliminary finite element analysis and document assumptions, methods, and results
* Support the selection and integration of gears, bearings, shafts, splines, seals, fasteners, and lubrication components
* Help build, inspect, test, and iterate prototype hardware for functionality, performance, and reliability
* Support pre-production builds, aircraft integration, and ground and flight testing
* Investigate well-defined technical issues, document findings, and contribute to root-cause and corrective-action activities
* Collaborate with mechanical, propulsion, electrical, avionics, flight-controls, manufacturing, and test teams
* Follow established engineering, configuration-management, and product-release processes
* Develop plans for assigned tasks, communicate progress and risks, and incorporate feedback from teammates

### Required qualifications:

* 0-1 years of related experience with a bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, or a related technical field, or equivalent work experience
* Understanding of fundamental mechanical engineering principles, including statics, dynamics, mechanics of materials, materials science, and machine design
* Academic, internship, student-team, research, or project experience using 3D CAD software
* Ability to create or interpret basic engineering drawings
* Familiarity with dimensional tolerancing and GD&T fundamentals
* Ability to perform foundational engineering calculations and clearly document assumptions and results
* Hands-on interest in designing, building, integrating, or testing mechanical hardware
* Strong problem-solving, organization, documentation, and communication skills
* Ability to complete well-defined engineering tasks with guidance from peers and managers
* Ability to collaborate effectively, accept feedback, and work in a fast-paced environment with evolving priorities
* A demonstrated record of working hard, being trustworthy, holding yourself to high standards, and being kind to others
* Ability to obtain an S//SAR-level security clearance is desired

### Preferred qualifications:

* Internship, student-team, research, or project experience involving aircraft, UAVs, robotics, automotive systems, or other mechanical products
* Coursework or project experience related to gearboxes, gearing, bearings, shafts, splines, seals, lubrication, or rotating machinery
* Familiarity with gear geometry, gear-train calculations, bearing selection, or mechanical power transmission
* Experience with NX or a similar production CAD platform
* Exposure to tolerance stack-ups, mechanical fits, and GD&T application
* Exposure to finite element analysis, including static, modal, buckling, or fatigue analysis
* Familiarity with MATLAB, Python, or other engineering analysis tools
* Hands-on machine-shop, mechanic, fabrication, assembly, or inspection experience through professional, academic, or hobby activities
* Exposure to FMEA, design of experiments, root-cause analysis, or reliability testing
* Familiarity with additive manufacturing, machining, casting, forging, or other mechanical manufacturing processes
* Exposure to RTCA DO-160, MIL-STD-810, structural testing, vibration testing, or mechanism testing
* Ability to thrive in startup, R&D, or defense-innovation environments
