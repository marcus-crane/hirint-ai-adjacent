### Job Description:

Shield AI is seeking a highly capable Production Engineering Technician to oversee technician training, continuous improvement initiatives, and production analytics for the V-BAT production line. The role is responsible for developing workforce capability and establishing robust, data‑driven operational systems that enhance safety, quality, delivery performance, cost efficiency, and production scalability.

### What you'll do:

**Training**

* Own the VBAT production training program, including onboarding, role-based training, cross-training, recertification, ongoing skills development, and technician qualification records.
* Develop and deliver effective classroom, workstation, and on-the-job training for assembly, production, and test processes; maintain skills matrices, on the job training assessments, and training readiness metrics.
* Continuously identify skill gaps, align training to current production needs, and assess effectiveness through observations, competency checks, audit results, technician feedback, and production performance.

**Lean Manufacturing and Continuous Improvement**

* Lead continuous-improvement initiatives using lean manufacturing principles—including 5S, visual management, standard work, root-cause analysis, waste reduction, and flow optimization—to improve safety, quality, delivery, and cost.
* Use production, quality, and training data to identify and help resolve bottlenecks, cycle-time variation, recurring defects, rework, material-flow issues, equipment downtime, ergonomic risks, and workforce capability gaps.
* Own the CCAR process and lead the Manufacturing Council, creating a responsive closed-loop system that turns technician feedback and shop-floor opportunities into prioritized, implemented, verified, and sustained improvements.

**Data Analytics**

* Build a data-driven operating model for the VBAT production team by establishing standardized performance metrics, reporting cadences, and visual management routines that enable timely, data-driven decisions.
* Develop and maintain daily, weekly, and monthly dashboards tracking output versus plan, throughput, first-pass yield, defects and rework, cycle time, downtime, labor efficiency, training completion, and cross-training coverage.
* Analyze production capacity, staffing, and improvement impact using takt time, labor content, workstation capacity, skills readiness, and before-and-after performance data to support production-ramp planning and sustain measurable gains.

### Required qualifications:

* Bachelors degree in Industrial or Systems Engineer, or Data Analytics; equivalent technical experience in avionics or aerospace production will be considered.
* 3+ years of experience in a manufacturing or technical support role, preferably within aerospace, defense, UAS, or other complex hardware manufacturing environments.
* Ability to operate effectively in a fast-paced, highly dynamic environment with evolving priorities.
* Excellent analytical, organizational, and communication skills.
* U.S. citizenship and ability to obtain/maintain a security clearance.

### Preferred qualifications:

* Experience with data visualization tools, such as Power BI, Palantir, Tableau, Cognos, and/or Microsoft Access.
* Proficiency in Lean Manufacturing principles, including 5S, visual management, standard work, root‑cause analysis, waste reduction, and flow optimization.
* Ability to translate technician feedback and production challenges into structured problem-solving activities.
* Experience operating, maintaining, and facilitating training programs and performance across large scale operations and organizations.
* Excellent communication and presentation skills, with the ability to train and influence technicians, supervisors, and cross-functional stakeholders.
