Shield AI is a venture-backed defense-tech company with the mission of protecting service members and civilians with intelligent systems. Its products include Hivemind autonomy software, V-BAT and X-BAT aircraft, and Aechelon simulation and synthetic reality technologies. With offices and facilities across the U.S., Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, Shield AI’s technology actively supports operations worldwide. For more information, visit [www.shield.ai](https://shield.ai/). Follow Shield AI on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shield-ai), [X](https://x.com/shieldaitech), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/shield_ai), and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Shield_AI).

The Autonomy Capabilities team develops reusable software that enables autonomous systems to plan, decide, coordinate, and execute missions. Our work includes mission behaviors, motion planning, multi-agent coordination, and contingency handling across unmanned platforms.

As an entry-level engineer, you will build and test well-defined autonomy software with regular guidance from experienced engineers. This is a full-time role focused on learning the codebase and delivering small, reviewable changes rather than owning an algorithm or architecture end to end.

What you'll do:  
\* Implement small, well-defined C++ changes to autonomy capabilities, examples, configuration, or tooling.  
\* Add unit tests and simulation scenarios using established frameworks and expected behaviors.  
\* Analyze simulation outputs and reproduce failures using logs, visualizations, and debug tools with guidance.  
\* Help connect autonomy capabilities to perception products, platform interfaces, and mission software.  
\* Document assumptions, test results, and setup instructions; participate in code and design reviews.  
\* Work with autonomy, perception, integration, and simulation teammates to understand how capabilities are used.

We are seeking Autonomous Software Engineers for full-time roles starting in 2027! This is a paid, in-person, full-time position for students graduating during the 2026-2027 academic year. We are now reviewing applications for positions starting after graduation in 2027.

### Required Qualifications

* Currently pursuing a Bachelor's, Master's, or PhD in Computer Science, Robotics, Electrical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, or a related field, graduating between December 2026 and August 2027.
* Foundational C++ or Python programming demonstrated through coursework, projects, research, internships, or work experience.
* Coursework or project exposure to robotics, controls, planning, AI, simulation, or optimization.
* Familiarity with Linux, Git, debugging, and basic software-testing practices.
* Ability to break down well-defined problems, communicate progress, and use feedback to improve work.
* Ability to obtain a SECRET clearance.

### Preferred Qualifications

* Prior internship or co-op experience writing software.
* Projects involving path planning, controls, state machines, multi-agent systems, or simulation.
* Experience with ROS, pub-sub systems, CMake, containers, CI, or automated tests.
* Experience building simulation, visualization, or data-analysis tools.
