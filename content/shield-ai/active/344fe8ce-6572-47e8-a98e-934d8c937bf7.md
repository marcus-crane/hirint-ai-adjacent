### Job Description:

We are seeking a Staff Mechanical Engineer who can lead complex hardware efforts from concept through deployment. This individual will own mechanical design while integrating electronics, RF systems, and composite structures into cohesive, field-ready platforms. You should be comfortable operating as a technical lead and driving cross-functional execution.

### What you'll do:

* Lead mechanical design and integration for airborne or ruggedized systems, from concept through production.
* Own system-level packaging of electronics, including radios, antennas, PCBAs, and harnessing.
* Design and integrate components into composite and metallic structures.
* Drive cross-functional collaboration across electrical, RF, manufacturing, and test teams.
* Serve as project lead on select programs, managing scope, schedule, and technical execution.
* Perform and guide analyses including structural, thermal, and environmental considerations.
* Create and review CAD models, drawings, and release packages for production.
* Support prototype builds, integration, and troubleshooting in lab and field environments.
* Ensure designs meet reliability, manufacturability, and performance requirements.

### Required qualifications;

* Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering or related field.
* 8+ years of experience in mechanical design and product development.
* Strong experience with CAD (e.g., NX, SolidWorks, Creo).
* Proven ability to take products from concept to production.
* Experience working in cross-functional engineering environments.
* Demonstrated ownership of projects or subsystems with minimal oversight.

### Preferred qualifications:

* Experience integrating electronics into mechanical systems, including:
* Radios, antennas, and RF components.
* PCBAs and embedded electronics.
* Familiarity with wiring and cable systems, including coax, RF cables, and harness design (e.g., RG-series, RMA considerations).
* Experience designing for composite structures and hybrid assemblies (composite + metal).
* Understanding of thermal management for electronics (heat dissipation, airflow, conduction paths).
* Knowledge of grounding, shielding, and EMI/EMC considerations.
* Experience with ground support equipment (GSE) design or test infrastructure.
* Familiarity with environmental requirements (vibration, shock, temperature, ingress).
* Experience working closely with RF or electrical engineering teams on system integration.
* Hands-on build, integration, and troubleshooting experience.

### Leadership expectations:

* Act as a technical lead for projects or subsystems.
* Drive design reviews and decision-making.
* Mentor junior engineers and elevate team capability.
* Balance speed and rigor in a fast-paced development environment.
