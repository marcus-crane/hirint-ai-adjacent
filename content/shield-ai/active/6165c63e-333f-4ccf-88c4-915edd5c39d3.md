Its products include Hivemind autonomy software, V-BAT and X-BAT aircraft, and Aechelon simulation and synthetic reality technologies. With offices and facilities across the U.S., Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, Shield AI’s technology actively supports operations worldwide. Follow Shield AI on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shield-ai), [X](https://x.com/shieldaitech), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/shield_ai), and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Shield_AI).

Shield AI is looking for a detail-oriented, technically strong Senior, Revenue Accounting to join our fast-growing Accounting team. Reporting to the Manager, Revenue Accounting, you will run the day-to-day revenue recognition work across our defense software and hardware programs. You will oversee the full revenue lifecycle—from contract review through month-end close—collaborating cross-functionally with other departments such as Legal, Sales, Program Finance, and Business Development. This is a high-impact, high-visibility role, where you will get hands-on exposure to complex contracts, government contracting, and a revenue function that is still being built.  
   
What you'll do:  
\* Review customer contracts and identify performance obligations, transaction price, variable consideration, and timing of recognition in compliance with ASC 606.  
\* Prepare technical accounting memos and contract summaries for review by the Revenue Manager.  
\* Gather data and run analyses that support SSP studies and allocations as new products and pricing models launch.  
\* Support audit readiness, including preparation of schedules, memos, and responses to internal and external audit inquiries.  
\* Partner with Billing, Sales Ops, and Program Finance to resolve contract, invoicing, and booking issues upstream.  
\* Find and carry out process improvements and automation in the ERP and related revenue tools.  
\* Support special projects, including new product launches, international entity expansion, and systems implementations.  
  
  
Required qualifications:  
\* 3+ years of accounting experience, with significant hands-on revenue recognition work under ASC 606  
\* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field.  
\* Working knowledge of ASC 606, including performance obligations, variable consideration, contract modifications, and over-time vs. point-in-time recognition.  
\* Strong Excel and analytical skills. Comfortable building and maintaining detailed revenue schedules.  
\* Experience with ERP and CRM systems.  
\* Strong written communication skills, including documenting accounting positions and processes clearly.  
\* High attention to detail and a sense of ownership over accuracy and deadlines.  
\* Able to work across departments and juggle multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.  
  
  
Preferred qualifications:  
\* CPA or actively pursuing CPA licensure.  
\* Big 4 or national public accounting experience, ideally with technology or aerospace/defense clients.  
\* Experience in SaaS, hardware, or technology-driven companies. Defense or aerospace experience is a strong plus.  
\* Familiarity with government contract types (FFP, T&M, cost-plus) and regulations (FAR, CAS, DFARS).  
\* Experience with SOX compliance and executing internal controls.  
\* Exposure to multi-entity or multi-currency accounting.  
\* Experience with revenue automation tools or ERP implementations.
