### Job Description:

As a Supplier Quality Specialist, you would be joining a small team that supports PCBA (Printed Circuit Board Assembly) rework for a variety of aircraft electronics. Furthermore, you would work alongside our engineering team in the development of new products such as PCBA, harness, electronic components, and test rack systems.

### What you'll do:

* Own and execute new product introduction (NPI) rework builds across all relevant work centers, delivering qualified processes on aggressive timelines.
* Establish the framework for reliable PCBA rework and repair.
* Lead triage of non-conforming PCBAs, drive root-cause resolution, and implement preventative actions with Supplier Development Engineers.
* Own resolution of production rework issues while safeguarding design integrity, product quality, and delivery timelines.
* Identify, prioritize, and deliver continuous improvement projects that improve rework yield, performance, and time.

### Required qualifications:

* High school diploma or equivalency certificate and 5+ years of professional experience with SMT and/or PCBA rework equipment and processes.
* IPC-A-610, J-STD-001, and/or IPC-7711/7721 (rework/repair) certification.

### Preferred qualifications:

* Associate or bachelor’s degree in engineering or related technical discipline.
* 4+ years of hands-on experience owning SMT rework equipment operation and process control in a high-volume production setting.
* Proven experience implementing Design for Manufacturability (DFM) specifically for complex SMT assemblies.
* Ability to create and deliver technical presentations for rework process readiness, design reviews, and manufacturing readiness reviews.
* Experience leading the transition of SMT rework processes from design/NPI to volume manufacturing as part of cross-functional teams in fast-paced iterative environments.
