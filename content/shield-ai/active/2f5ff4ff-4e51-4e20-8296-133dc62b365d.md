**Job Description**

**This position may be based San Mateo, CA, the surrounding San Francisco region, or Seattle, WA.**

Shield AI is seeking a highly skilled Senior Network Engineer to help advance and secure our mission-critical global infrastructure. You will design, deploy, and maintain resilient network systems that power Shield AI’s enterprise and development environments—spanning data centers, on-premises sites, and public cloud networks.  
You’ll partner with cross-functional teams to innovate, automate, and optimize network performance, availability, and security for operations supporting both classified and unclassified workloads. Limited travel to Shield AI sites may be required.

### What you'll do:

**Network Engineering**  
\* Experience with public cloud networking in Azure and AWS, including secure connectivity models (e.g., ExpressRoute, Transit Gateway, Cloud WAN / vWAN).  
\* Design, architect, and implement routing, switching, WLAN, and firewall infrastructure across data centers and remote offices.  
\* Deploy and manage WAN and VPN connectivity, ensuring secure and reliable access for enterprise and mission environments.  
\* Develop and apply network automation to streamline provisioning, monitoring, and configuration management.  
\* Cloud, Platform, Security, and Infrastructure engineering teams to develop standardized network architectures supporting self-service platforms and engineering automation.  
\* Recommend and implement scalable, automated approaches to operational tasks and vulnerability remediation.  
\* Contribute to Agile delivery cycles and engineering principles while managing assigned network projects and deliverables.  
\* Conduct network capacity planning and provide recommendations for growth and optimization.

**Operations and Support**  
\* Plan and execute network and firewall changes in accordance with change-management and security governance processes.  
\* Continuously monitor, analyze, and improve network performance, resiliency, and security posture.  
\* Provide advanced troubleshooting for network-related hardware, software, and application issues.  
\* Configure and maintain secure connectivity services (e.g., VPN/ZTNA, HTTPS, SFTP).  
\* Create and maintain clear operational and configuration documentation; mentor operational teams on processes and procedures.  
\* Engage vendors and strategic partners for escalated troubleshooting, lifecycle planning, and technology evaluations.  
\* Participate in on-call rotation and scheduled maintenance activities.  
Maintenance  
\* Lead or assist with network upgrades and patching within defined maintenance windows.  
\* Maintain network device software and license compliance.  
\* Ensure security vulnerabilities are identified and remediated in coordination with IT security teams.

### Required Qualifications:

\* 6+ years of experience supporting and administering complex network systems in production environments.  
\* Expertise in network routing, switching, and security architectures.  
\* Proficiency in at least one Network Operating System (e.g., NX-OS, JunOS, Arista EOS).  
\* Proficiency in at least one Network Security OS (e.g., PAN-OS, FortiOS).  
\* Experience with network automation and infrastructure-as-code practices (e.g., Terraform, Ansible, Python).  
\* Strong understanding of firewall management, segmentation, and VPN technologies.  
\* Excellent analytical, organizational, and troubleshooting skills.  
\* Effective written and verbal communication across technical and non-technical audiences.  
\* Proven ability to work independently and deliver in a structured, process-driven environment.  
\* Bachelor’s degree in a technical discipline or 6+ years of relevant experience with a professional certification (e.g., CCNP, JNCIS, VCP-NV, or equivalent).  
\* Experience building and managing scalable hybrid infrastructure connectivity solutions linking on-premises systems with cloud networks.

### Preferred Qualifications:

\* Familiarity with Juniper and Palo Alto network solutions and centralized management platform  (Panorama preferred).  
\* Familiarity with WLAN technologies and wireless authentication (e.g., 802.1x, RADIUS).  
\* Experience with NAC solutions and network access policy enforcement.  
\* Experience with ZTNA / SASE solutions (Prisma, Zscaler, or Cato)  
\* Working knowledge of virtualization or operating system administration (VMware, Windows, or Linux).  
\* Demonstrated project management and leadership skills.  
\* Team-oriented, collaborative mindset with the ability to coach peers and present to non-technical stakeholders.  
\* Solution-driven, adaptable approach in a fast-paced, change-controlled environment.
