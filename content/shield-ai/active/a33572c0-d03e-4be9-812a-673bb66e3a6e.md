### 

We are seeking a motivated Materials Planning Intern to join our Supply Chain organization. In this role, you will have the opportunity to apply your analytical and problem-solving skills to real-world materials planning challenges that directly support the timely production and delivery of Shield AI systems. ### Required qualifications:

* You are an undergraduate, graduate pursuing a degree in Supply Chain, business or related field to graduate in December 2027 or Spring 2028.

### Preferred qualifications:

* Great written and verbal communication.
* Demonstrated history of showing ownership of project outcomes and delivering exceptional results.
* Record of working hard, being a trustworthy teammate, holding yourself and others to high standards, and being kind to others.
