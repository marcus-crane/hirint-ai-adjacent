**Job Description:**

Shield AI is redefining autonomous aviation with cutting-edge AI and GPS/RF-denied navigation technologies. Now, we’re building a next-generation aircraft like no other—one that will push the boundaries of innovation and performance.

We are looking for an Electromechanical Engineer to join our team and support the design and integration of avionics systems for advanced aircraft. In this entry-level role, you will apply engineering fundamentals, work alongside experienced engineers, and contribute to designs from initial development through manufacturing and testing. If you are eager to learn, open to feedback, and passionate about building high-performance aerospace systems, we want to hear from you.

### What you'll do:

* Support the design of electromechanical solutions for avionics packaging, electrical interconnects, thermal management, and aircraft integration
* Create and update 3D models, engineering drawings, bills of materials, and other design documentation
* Assist with structural, thermal, and modal analyses under the guidance of experienced engineers
* Work with electrical engineers to define and document mechanical interfaces for circuit cards, connectors, wiring, and harnesses
* Help investigate well-defined design and manufacturing issues, evaluate solutions, and document results
* Support prototype builds, system integration, environmental testing, failure analysis, and flight-test readiness activities
* Follow, and contribute to, established engineering processes, configuration-control practices, and functional best practices
* Develop plans for assigned tasks, communicate progress and risks, and execute against team priorities
* Build effective working relationships within the team, incorporate feedback, and share relevant knowledge
* Contribute to products that advance Shield AI’s mission while demonstrating the company’s values

### Required qualifications:

* A bachelor’s degree in Mechanical or Aerospace Engineering
* Understanding of fundamental mechanical engineering concepts, including mechanics, materials, heat transfer, and manufacturing processes
* Academic, internship, project, or hands-on experience using CAD software such as NX, SolidWorks, or CATIA
* Ability to create or interpret basic engineering drawings and mechanical layouts
* Familiarity with electromechanical systems, circuit cards, connectors, wiring, and harness integration
* Basic understanding of structural or thermal analysis methods
* Strong problem-solving, documentation, organization, and communication skills
* Ability to complete well-defined engineering tasks with guidance from peers and managers
* Ability to collaborate effectively, accept feedback, and work in a fast-paced development environment with evolving priorities
* Ability to obtain an S//SAR-level security clearance is desired

### Preferred qualifications:

* Internship, co-op, student-team, research, or project experience involving UAVs, aircraft, robotics, electronics packaging, or other electromechanical systems
