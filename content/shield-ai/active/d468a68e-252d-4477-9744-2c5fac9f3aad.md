### 

Shield AI is seeking a Senior Program Finance Analyst to support financial execution, forecasting, and analysis across key programs. This role will partner closely with Program Management, Engineering, Operations, Supply Chain, Business Development, and Finance leadership to provide visibility into program performance and turn program execution data into reliable forecasts and useful financial analysis.  
The ideal candidate has experience in the aerospace and defense industry, specifically within program finance, FP&A, or similar roles; thrives in a fast-paced and evolving environment; and can balance financial rigor with changing business needs. This role combines program finance execution and financial modeling support in approximately equal measure. Execution responsibilities provide the foundation: accurate inputs, dependable deliverables, and timely visibility into changes. Your modeling work will connect that information to broader product lifecycle economics.  
This is a hands-on individual contributor role. The Senior Program Finance Analyst will maintain financial models, analyze actual performance against plan, identify financial risks and opportunities, and support process improvements.  
   
What you'll do:  
Own assigned program finance deliverables  
\* Maintain recurring forecasts and EAC schedules, incorporating validated inputs from Program Management, Engineering, Manufacturing, Supply Chain, and other functional owners.  
\* Coordinate input deadlines, follow up on missing information, and investigate assumptions that appear incomplete or inconsistent.  
\* Analyze actuals against forecasts and budgets; explain cost, timing, and schedule-related changes and prepare clear program reporting.  
\* Investigate variances directly with stakeholders, resolve routine discrepancies, and escalate material risks, assumption changes, or unresolved questions.  
\* Own assigned deliverables through completion, with appropriate review of significant changes and leadership-facing conclusions.  
Connect execution data to lifecycle analysis  
\* Translate program actuals, forecasts, milestones, and operational assumptions into inputs for product lifecycle financial models.  
\* Reconcile program-level information with assumptions across RDT&E, Production, and Sustainment, maintaining traceability to source data.  
\* Help explain differences between an individual program’s performance and broader product economics.  
\* Work with team members to assess whether execution results warrant changes to longer-term assumptions, recognizing that one program may not represent the entire product.  
Build and improve financial analyses  
\* Develop and maintain Excel templates, supporting schedules, and moderately complex analyses, including work that begins with incomplete inputs.  
\* Use transparent formulas, consistent structures, documented assumptions, and checks that make analysis understandable and reviewable.  
\* Conduct scenario and sensitivity analyses with guidance; communicate findings, uncertainties, and implications clearly.  
\* Improve recurring spreadsheets and workflows to reduce manual effort and strengthen reliability.  
\* Build, maintain, and scale financial models used for forecasting, budgeting, and long-term planning

###
