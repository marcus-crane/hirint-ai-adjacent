### Job Description:

The Director, DoN Growth Campaign Leader leads the internal operating rhythm that enables Shield AI’s UAS pursuits with the Department of the Navy, including the U.S. Navy and U.S. Marine Corps. Opportunity-focused Go-To-Market (GTM) leaders retain ownership of their customers, capture strategies, account plans, and individual pursuits. This role serves as the connective tissue across those leaders, integrating information across the DoN portfolio and providing the VP, Mission Solutions with a clear, decision-ready view of campaign health, priorities, dependencies, risks, mitigations, and resource requirements.

The Director connects DoN pursuits with relevant activity across other Services, allied and partner nations, product and engineering, U.S. Government approvals, aircraft availability, ship and at-sea test windows, airworthiness and shipboard-suitability sequencing, internal investment, and industrial strategy. The role identifies shared dependencies and resource conflicts early, helps sequence competing demands, and coordinates the cross-functional support required for GTM leads to execute their captures effectively.

The role maintains the authoritative DoN campaign picture and runs the operating cadence that turns portfolio insight into action. This includes campaign reviews, risk and dependency management, executive dashboards, action tracking, investment recommendations, and escalation of decisions requiring VP or executive attention. The Director ensures that information flows efficiently from the GTM organization to the VP, Mission Solutions and that leadership receives clear recommendations, tradeoffs, and required decisions.

GTM leads remain accountable for customer engagement, account planning, capture strategy, win strategy, price-to-win, proposal content, and pursuit outcomes. The Director enables their success by improving coordination, reducing friction around shared resources, and ensuring that the broader organization is aligned behind the highest-priority Department of the Navy opportunities.

### Scope & authority:

This is a senior, high-visibility seat with authority, not a reporting function:

* Portfolio: Multiple concurrent Navy and Marine Corps pursuits across the X-BAT FoS, representing a multi-billion-dollar addressable opportunity.
* Team: Leadership by influence role, leveraging dedicated capture-operations and analyst support, with the expectation to build the team as the portfolio scales.
* Investment: Develops and co-stewards annual campaign and indirect investment against prioritized campaign returns.
* Governance: holds a standing seat in DoN bid/no-bid and investment-prioritization reviews, and sets the agenda for the executive campaign review.
* Growth path: a pipeline role toward senior Department of the Navy growth or general-management leadership at Shield AI.

### What you'll do:

* Own the DoN view. Build and maintain the integrated DoN campaign picture across all X-BAT FoS DoN pursuits: strategy status, critical path, milestones, dependencies, decision points, and campaign health in one authoritative view.
* Enable GTM leads. Give each GTM lead the templates, playbooks, data, competitive inputs, cost and schedule inputs, and cross-functional access they need to build and execute their own capture plan. Success is measured by their outcomes, not by your visibility.
* Synchronize functions. Coordinate engineering, product, export, legal, contracts, finance, government relations, and business operations against a single prioritized campaign plan so GTM leads are not individually negotiating for the same scarce resources.
* Surface conflicts between campaigns. Identify where two or more campaigns compete for the same aircraft, test window, ship or range availability, engineering effort, approval bandwidth, or indirect dollar, and bring executive leadership a clear tradeoff and a recommendation.
* Drive executive decision quality. Run the operating cadence — campaign reviews, gate reviews, risk registers, action tracking, and executive dashboards — so leadership receives timely, accurate, decision-ready information on bid/no-bid, investment, and prioritization.
* Steward indirect investment. Work with Finance and Business Operations to track campaign investment, indirect spend, and resource planning against campaign priorities and expected return.
* Scale discipline. Capture what worked and what did not across captures, and convert it into repeatable tools, templates, metrics, and playbooks so each new campaign starts further ahead than the last.
* Support a culture of accountability, urgency, collaboration, and continuous improvement.
* Roll up your sleeves. Personally drive the execution of critical customer responses (RFI, RFP, CSO, and other solicitation vehicles) — assembling cross-functional inputs, holding the response schedule, and driving on-time submission, while the GTM lead retains ownership of win strategy, price-to-win, and proposal content.

### Decision rights & interfaces:

* This role is accountable for: the integrated campaign plan and DoN view; the operating cadence and campaign-health reporting; identification and escalation of cross-campaign dependencies, conflicts, and risks; path-to-win roadmap; and recommendations on investment prioritization and sequencing.
* Opportunity GTM leads are accountable for: customer relationships and engagement, capture strategy, win strategy and price-to-win for their pursuit, proposal content, and the bid/no-bid recommendation for their opportunity.
* Government Relations, Export, Legal, and Contracts are accountable for: data-rights, political and legislative engagements and outcomes, disclosure determinations, legal positions, and contracting terms.
* Engineering and Product are accountable for: technical solution definition, configuration decisions, performance commitments, and coordination with relevant DoN technical authorities on airworthiness and shipboard-suitability requirements.
* Program Management is accountable for: execution of customer-funded programs after award.
* External customer engagement by this role is in support of, and at the invitation of, the responsible GTM lead.

### Required qualifications:

* U.S. person as defined by ITAR (22 CFR 120.62), with the ability to obtain and maintain a DoD Top Secret/SCI clearance and Special Access Program (SAP) eligibility.
* 12–20+ years in capture management, program management, strategic operations, business operations, technical program management, or a comparable cross-functional leadership role.
* Demonstrated success leading highly complex initiatives with multiple interdependent workstreams across engineering, business development, operations, finance, and executive leadership.
* Working knowledge of how Department of the Navy programs actually get funded and fielded — ASN(RD&A) and NAVAIR program-office dynamics (particularly PAE(A)) and the unmanned aviation portfolio), Navy and Marine Corps resource sponsorship and requirements staffing (N9/N98) and the Marine Corps aviation requirements process, the PPBE/POM cycle and Navy budget submission rhythm, and rapid-acquisition pathways such as OTAs, CSOs, Middle Tier of Acquisition, SBIR/STRATFI, DIU, ONR, and NavalX, including the transition from prototype to program of record.
* Proven ability to influence without direct authority and to drive accountability across functions and geographies.
* Strong executive communication: leadership briefings, dashboards, metrics, and decision-support materials that lead to decisions rather than discussion.
* Deep operational rigor in integrated schedules, critical path, milestones, action tracking, dependency management, and risk mitigation.
* Comfort operating in ambiguous, fast-changing environments with competing priorities across multiple time zones.
* Working understanding of the dependencies unique to naval aviation — NAVAIR airworthiness and flight-clearance processes, shipboard integration and suitability, environmental and marinization requirements, ship and at-sea test window availability, and DT/OT execution at Patuxent River and associated ranges.
* A service orientation toward the GTM leads this role supports, and the judgment to know when to escalate versus resolve quietly.

### Success in this role:

* GTM leads report that the role makes their campaign easier to run, not heavier to report on.
* DoN path-to-win is in place and running on a predictable, repeatable cadence that the whole organization can plan against.
* Cross-campaign resource and timing conflicts reach leadership as a decision with a recommendation, not as a surprise.
* Executive leadership can see, at any time, an accurate health picture of the DoN X-BAT FoS portfolio and make investment calls with confidence.
* Campaign mechanisms are reused across opportunities, and each new pursuit stands up measurably faster than the previous one.
