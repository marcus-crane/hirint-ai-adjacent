**Job Description:**

At Shield AI, we are revolutionizing defense technology to protect service members and civilians with intelligent, autonomous systems. Our Hardware Test Engineering team plays a critical role in ensuring X-BAT is reliable, resilient, and mission-ready. We develop custom electrical and mechanical test systems that evaluate aircraft hardware under representative and extreme environmental conditions.  
  
As an Electrical Engineer I supporting X-BAT, you will help design, build, and automate systems that validate hardware ranging from power electronics and communications equipment to integrated aircraft subsystems. You will gain hands-on experience with PCB design, Python-based automation, instrumentation, and environmental testing, including HASS, ESS, and HALT. If you enjoy solving multidisciplinary problems, learning quickly, and building dependable aerospace hardware, this role is for you.

### What you'll do:

* Design, build, and validate test systems for critical X-BAT components and subsystems.
* Translate engineering requirements into test plans, procedures, and measurable pass/fail criteria.
* Support testing across power electronics, RF, communications, embedded hardware, and aircraft system integration.
* Design PCBs and interface electronics that connect test equipment to devices under test.
* Build custom test fixtures incorporating relays, PLCs, PCBAs, sensors, instrumentation, and harnessing.
* Develop Python scripts and other automation tools to improve test repeatability, efficiency, and scalability.
* Configure and operate laboratory equipment such as oscilloscopes, power supplies, electronic loads, data-acquisition systems, and network analyzers.
* Analyze test data, identify trends and anomalies, and communicate results through clear technical reports.
* Troubleshoot aircraft hardware and test-system failures using structured root-cause analysis.
* Participate in cross-functional design reviews and advocate for design-for-test, reliability, and maintainability improvements.
* Partner with design, systems, software, manufacturing, and flight-test teams to resolve integration issues.
* Create and maintain schematics, bills of materials, test procedures, calibration records, and system documentation.
* Follow laboratory safety, configuration-control, and equipment-handling practices.
* Work closely with experienced engineers and technicians while progressively taking ownership of increasingly complex projects.
* Adapt and innovate in a fast-moving environment where your work directly affects aircraft performance, product quality, and mission success.
* Perform other duties as assigned.

### Required qualifications:

* Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related discipline; equivalent relevant hands-on experience may be considered. Students completing an applicable undergraduate or graduate degree in 2026 are also encouraged to apply.
* Strong foundation in circuit analysis, analog and digital electronics, and laboratory measurement techniques.
* Working knowledge of mechanical and software concepts used in multidisciplinary aerospace systems.
* Hands-on engineering experience through coursework, internships, employment, research, student organizations, or personal projects.
* Ability to read schematics, use standard electrical test equipment, and methodically troubleshoot hardware.
* Ability to document technical work clearly and communicate effectively with engineers, technicians, and other stakeholders.
* Demonstrated initiative, curiosity, sound judgment, and willingness to take ownership of technical problems.
* A team-first mindset and a record of working hard, acting with integrity, maintaining high standards, and treating others with respect.

### Preferred qualifications:

* Experience with Python or another scripting language used for test automation or data analysis.
* Experience with PCB design tools such as Altium Designer, KiCad, or Eagle.
* Familiarity with hardware interfaces such as UART, SPI, I²C, CAN, Ethernet, or USB.
* Experience with automated test equipment, data-acquisition systems, or hardware-in-the-loop test systems.
* Familiarity with RF equipment, power electronics, embedded systems, or communications hardware.
* Exposure to environmental and reliability testing such as HASS, ESS, HALT, thermal cycling, vibration, or shock testing.
* Familiarity with mechanical CAD tools such as SolidWorks, NX, or CATIA.
* Experience with requirements management, version control, issue tracking, or configuration-controlled engineering workflows.
* Ability to prioritize effectively, solve problems under pressure, and adjust quickly as program needs evolve.
