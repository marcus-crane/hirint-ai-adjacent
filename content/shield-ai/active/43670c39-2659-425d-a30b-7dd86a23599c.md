### Job Description:

We’re hiring a  Strategic Talent Acquisition Partner to help power our next phase of growth across EMEA. This is a high-impact, business-critical role at the heart of Shield’s AI international expansion—supporting new market entries, building and scaling best-in-class  sales and engineering organisations, and partnering with senior leaders to shape our future. This is not a transactional recruiting role. You will operate as a strategic advisor, market expert, and builder—owning how talent enables scale across diverse EMEA markets.

### What you'll do:

Strategic Talent Partnership

* Partner closely with senior and executive-level stakeholders to understand business objectives, growth plans, and capability gaps.
* Translate market entry and growth plan into scalable, data-driven regional talent acquisition strategies.
* Act as a thought partner and advisor on talent trends, market intelligence, and competitive hiring strategies.
* Advise leaders on market feasibility from a talent perspective (availability, cost, timing, and risk).

Execution with Excellence

* Own the end-to-end recruitment lifecycle for all roles including senior, critical, and hard-to-hire roles.
* Partner to design and execute sourcing strategies that attract high-impact engineering and commercial talent across the EMEA region
* Shape local hiring approaches while maintaining a consistent global hiring bar.

Employer Branding & Talent Attraction

* Act as an EMEA ambassador for our employer brand, ensuring we are positioned as an employer of choice in the region
* Partner with Branding, Comms, and Leadership to shape and localise our employer value proposition for different EMEA countries.
* Identify and activate employer branding opportunities (events, content,  
  communities, referrals, social presence).
* Ensure inclusive, consistent, and authentic candidate experience that reinforces our brand and values at every touchpoint.

Driving at Scale

* Use data to forecast hiring needs, track progress, and continuously optimise our recruitment performance.
* Improve TA processes, tools, and ways of partnering to support speed, quality, and scale.
* Be a visible voice for talent across the EMEA leadership community.

### Required qualifications:

* Proven experience as a strategic talent acquisition partner driving full cycle hiring for both engineering and go to market talent within the defence space or Public Sector Saas space.
* Demonstrated success supporting new market entries and scaling teams at pace.
* Strong credibility working with senior leaders and executives as a trusted talent advisor and business partner.
* Deep understanding of EMEA talent dynamics, sourcing strategies, and competitive intelligence.
* Highly consultative, commercially minded, and comfortable operating with ambiguity.
* Ability to balance strategic thinking with hands-on execution.
