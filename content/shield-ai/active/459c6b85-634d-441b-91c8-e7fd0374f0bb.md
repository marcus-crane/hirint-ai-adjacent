### Job Description:

Shield AI is seeking an Incoming Quality Inspector to verify that purchased composite parts and assemblies meet engineering drawings, specifications, and purchase requirements. This role will perform receiving inspections, document results, and help prevent nonconforming material from entering inventory or production.

### What you'll do:

* Inspect incoming composite parts and assemblies against engineering drawings and specifications.
* Check for visible defects such as cracks, exposed fibers, voids, surface damage, and indications of delamination.
* Verify dimensions, hole locations, trim profiles, bondlines, and installed hardware against defined acceptance criteria.
* Use calipers, micrometers, height gauges, inspection fixtures, and other measurement tools.
* Review supplier inspection reports, certificates of conformance, material certifications, and required process records.
* Verify part identification, revision, and lot or serial traceability.
* Document inspection results and identify, segregate, and report nonconforming material.
* Coordinate with Supply Chain and Supplier Quality Engineer resolve discrepancies.
* Follow inspection procedures and ensure measurement equipment is within calibration.

### Required qualifications:

* Two or more years of quality inspection or related manufacturing experience, including experience with composite parts.
* Ability to read engineering drawings, specifications, and dimensional tolerances.
* Familiarity with composite materials, manufacturing processes, and common defects.
* Experience using precision measurement tools and documenting inspection results.
* Strong attention to detail and communication skills.

### Preferred qualifications:

* Experience inspecting aerospace composite structures, bonded assemblies, or sandwich panels.
* Familiarity with carbon fiber, fiberglass, honeycomb core, and bonded inserts.
* Working knowledge of geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T).
* Experience reviewing first article inspection reports and supplier nondestructive inspection records.
* Familiarity with AS9100 quality systems and AS9102 first article requirements.
