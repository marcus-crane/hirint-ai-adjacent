**Job Description:**

Shield AI is redefining autonomous aviation with cutting-edge AI and GPS/RF-denied navigation technologies. Now, we’re building a next-generation aircraft like no other—one that will push the boundaries of innovation and performance.

We are looking for a Staff Electromechanical Engineer to join our team, focused on the design and integration of avionics systems and driving designs from concept to production. If you thrive in fast-paced environments, excel in cross-functional collaboration, and are passionate about building high-performance avionics systems, we want to hear from you.

### What you'll do:

* Design complex electromechanical solutions for avionics packaging, electrical interconnects, thermal management, and aircraft integration
* Perform structural, preliminary thermal, and modal analyses for avionics to ensure survivability in harsh environments
* Ensure avionics meet performance, safety, environmental, and EMI/EMC compliance standards, including DO-160, DO-254, DO-178C, MIL-STD-810, and MIL-STD-461
* Own your design work from concept and design reviews through system validation, failure analysis, and flight-test readiness

### Required qualifications:

* B.S. and/or M.S. in Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, or a related field
* 8+ years of experience in mechanical and electrical equipment design for aerospace, including harness integration and mechanical component design
* Expertise in CAD software such as NX, SolidWorks, CATIA, or similar tools for 3D modeling, drawing creation, and system layout
* Experience collaborating with electrical engineers to define PCB constraints, develop circuit cards, and manage all mechanical interfaces
* Familiarity with the PCB layout process and advanced manufacturing methods
* Familiarity with eCAD software such as Altium, Cadence, or Allegro and harness design software such as Capital or Zuken
* Experience with finite element analysis, thermal analysis, and fatigue or lifecycle testing
* Ability to work in a fast-paced, agile development environment with evolving priorities
* Ability to obtain an S//SAR-level security clearance is desired
