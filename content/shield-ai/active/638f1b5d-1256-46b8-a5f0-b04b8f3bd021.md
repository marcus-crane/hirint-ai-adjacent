With offices and facilities across the U.S., Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, Shield AI’s technology actively supports operations worldwide. ### Job Description:

At Shield AI, we’re redefining the battlespace with autonomy-enabled airpower. As part of our global expansion, we’re looking for a bold and visionary Director – USMC Advanced Systems to lead strategic sales, customer engagement, and capture efforts for our X-BAT and other advanced systems supporting USMC markets. This is a high-impact role at the cutting edge of aerospace and defense innovation. You’ll be on the front lines, driving adoption and revenue for Shield AI’s technologies by engaging directly with top-tier USMC stakeholders.

You’ll bring a rare mix of defense sales expertise, technical credibility, and a deep understanding of USMC missions and acquisition frameworks. From identifying new opportunities to closing complex deals and launching early-stage programs, you’ll own the entire advanced system business development lifecycle for USMC customers.

The Advanced Systems Program Director – Marine Corps will lead Shield AI’s Advanced Systems programmatic capture actions involving Dept of the Navy opportunities with a focus on USMC programs. This individual will guide, shape, and inform X-BAT and other advanced system capture actions as Shield AI’s lead agent for program office interaction and design.

### What you'll do:

* Lead Shield AI’s work with acquisition offices shaping and responding to solicitations.
* Drive teams toward multi-year strategic Dept of Navy (DoN) program wins and close high-value contracts.
* Build and maintain trusted relationships within program offices and manage industry partner relationships.
* Anticipate customer needs and influence policy, requirements, and solicitations to positively align with Shield AI portfolios.

### Required qualifications:

* 10+ years strategic capture / DoN acquisition experience
* Deep knowledge of DoN/USMC acquisition and/or requirements processes
* Experience with emerging technologies
* Strong leadership, communication, and analytical skills with the ability to cross-collaborate across technical and operational teams
* Active Secret clearance (TS preferred)
* DAWIA Program Manager - Advanced certification

### Preferred qualifications:

* Advanced degree in business, engineering, or a related field
* Navy/Marine Corps program capture within previous 5 years
