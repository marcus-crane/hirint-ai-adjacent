### 

Airworthiness and Safety Intern: Support Shield AI’s Airworthiness and Safety team in developing the documentation, analyses, and processes needed to safely test and field V-BAT, X-BAT, and autonomy capabilities.

 Working under the team’s guidance, the intern would:

\* Help prepare airworthiness approval packages, compliance matrices, and supporting evidence for military and civil aviation authorities.  
\* Organize flight-test and operational data supporting safety assessments and certification activities.  
\* Support hazard tracking, safety reporting, incident trend analysis, and corrective-action follow-up.  
\* Maintain regulatory approval trackers and improve documentation, templates, and reporting tools.  
\* Coordinate with engineering, flight operations, and customer support teams to collect and organize technical information.  
\* Support aviation safety audits and mishap investigations

Desired qualifications:   
Pursuing a degree in engineering, aviation safety, or a related field.  
Strong technical writing, analytical, and organizational skills. Interest in unmanned aircraft, aviation, aviation safety, and certification; familiarity with data analysis tools is a plus.
