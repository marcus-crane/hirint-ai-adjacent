Shield AI is a venture-backed defense-tech company with the mission of protecting service members and civilians with intelligent systems. Its products include Hivemind autonomy software, V-BAT and X-BAT aircraft, and Aechelon simulation and synthetic reality technologies. With offices and facilities across the U.S., Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, Shield AI’s technology actively supports operations worldwide. For more information, visit [www.shield.ai](https://shield.ai/). Follow Shield AI on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shield-ai), [X](https://x.com/shieldaitech), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/shield_ai), and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Shield_AI).

We are seeking a Manager, Autonomy Software Integration & Test to lead a team supporting the development, integration, testing, and deployment of autonomous systems in Ukraine.

This is a hands-on leadership role that combines people management with technical execution across software, hardware, aircraft systems, and flight operations. The ideal candidate has strong software and systems-integration experience, develops high-performing engineers, and can lead complex work in fast-paced field environments.

This role will primarily support operations in the Kyiv region, with occasional travel to other locations within Ukraine.

### What You'll Do

* Lead, mentor, and develop a team of Autonomy Software Integration & Test Engineers.
* Set technical priorities, assign resources, manage schedules, and ensure the team delivers against operational requirements.
* Guide the development and integration of autonomy software across fixed-wing, multirotor, and other unmanned systems.
* Provide technical direction for software bring-up, system configuration, troubleshooting, and mission readiness.
* Oversee simulation, SIL, HIL, VIL, ground-test, and live flight-test activities.
* Lead troubleshooting and root-cause analysis across software, onboard compute, networking, radios, sensors, flight controllers, and mission systems.
* Coordinate engineering support for demonstrations and operational test events, including pre-flight preparation and post-flight analysis.
* Partner with autonomy, systems, hardware, flight-test, and operational teams to integrate and validate new capabilities.
* Establish effective test procedures, integration workflows, documentation, and deployment-readiness standards.
* Remain technically engaged and contribute directly to development, integration, and field troubleshooting when needed.

### Required Qualifications

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Robotics, Aerospace Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
* 5+ years of experience developing, integrating, or testing software for robotics, autonomy, aerospace, embedded systems, or a related field.
* Previous experience managing or leading engineering teams.
* Strong software-engineering experience in C++ and Python.
* Experience with Linux-based systems and hands-on hardware/software integration.
* Experience supporting field testing, flight testing, demonstrations, or operational exercises.
* Familiarity with MAVLink, PX4, ArduPilot, QGroundControl, or related unmanned-systems technologies.
* Strong leadership, communication, and problem-solving skills, with the ability to operate independently in dynamic environments.
* Ability to travel within Ukraine as required.

### Preferred Qualifications

* Experience leading teams supporting autonomous aircraft or unmanned systems.
* Experience with SIL, HIL, VIL, simulation, or flight-test environments.
* Familiarity with ROS, DDS, embedded systems, or real-time systems.
* Experience with flight controllers, avionics, RF communications, datalinks, or networking systems.
* Background in defense, robotics, aerospace, or autonomous systems.
* Ukrainian and English language proficiency.
