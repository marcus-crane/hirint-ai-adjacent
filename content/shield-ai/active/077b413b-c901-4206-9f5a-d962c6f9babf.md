Job Description:

### What you'll do:

* Lead the technical development and performance improvement of suppliers supporting welded assemblies, large metallic structures, sheet metal components, and complex structural assemblies.
* Assess supplier capabilities across welding, sheet metal forming, structural fabrication, machining, assembly, tooling, inspection, and special processes.
* Conduct supplier capability assessments, manufacturing readiness reviews, process audits, capacity analyses, and onsite technical evaluations.
* Partner with Strategic Sourcing and Engineering to evaluate and select suppliers capable of meeting technical, quality, cost, schedule, and production-rate requirements.
* Serve as the primary technical interface between suppliers and internal Engineering, Quality, Manufacturing, Supply Chain, and Program teams.
* Lead supplier industrialization activities, including manufacturing planning, tooling and fixture readiness, process validation, capacity planning, qualification, and production ramp-up.
* Review engineering drawings, specifications, GD&T, bills of material, weld symbols, material requirements, special-process requirements, and statements of work.
* Evaluate supplier welding capabilities, including welding equipment, personnel qualifications, weld procedures, process controls, inspection methods, and compliance with applicable aerospace welding standards.
* Review welding procedure specifications, procedure qualification records, welder certifications, and nondestructive inspection requirements.
* Work with suppliers to control weld distortion, residual stress, dimensional variation, heat-affected zones, joint fit-up, and other risks associated with large welded structures.
* Evaluate sheet metal forming processes such as brake forming, roll forming, stretch forming, hydroforming, stamping, and complex contour forming.
* Assess supplier tooling strategies for large structures and sheet metal components, including weld fixtures, assembly jigs, forming tools, trim tools, and inspection fixtures.
* Drive design-for-manufacturability and design-for-assembly discussions to improve joint design, tooling access, weld accessibility, dimensional control, producibility, cost, and lead time.
* Lead technical problem-solving involving weld defects, forming defects, springback, distortion, dimensional nonconformances, tooling issues, material variability, and assembly fit-up.
* Develop and manage supplier technical development plans, production readiness plans, capacity recovery plans, and corrective-action plans.
* Apply structured root-cause and corrective-action methods, including 8D, 5-Why, fishbone analysis, fault-tree analysis, and other appropriate problem-solving tools.
* Review and approve supplier manufacturing documentation, including process flow diagrams, manufacturing plans, weld plans, inspection plans, control plans, PFMEAs, work instructions, and qualification documentation.
* Coordinate First Article Inspection activities in accordance with AS9102 and support the resolution of FAI discrepancies and manufacturing nonconformances.
* Evaluate supplier production capacity using cycle times, yield, equipment availability, skilled labor, tooling, floor space, material flow, and inspection capacity.
* Support suppliers through engineering changes, new product introductions, prototype builds, qualification activities, and production-rate increases.
* Develop measurable improvement plans for suppliers experiencing delivery, quality, capacity, or technical performance issues.
* Lead onsite supplier recovery efforts and communicate progress, technical risks, and corrective actions to program and executive leadership.
* Monitor supplier performance trends to identify recurring issues, systemic risks, and opportunities for continuous improvement.
* Support alternate-source development and risk-mitigation strategies for sole-source, long-lead, and flight-critical structural components.
* Work with suppliers to implement lean manufacturing, process controls, mistake-proofing, yield improvement, cycle-time reduction, and production-flow improvements.
* Ensure suppliers comply with applicable AS9100, AS9102, engineering, configuration-control, special-process, and customer requirements.
* Provide technical guidance and mentorship to less-experienced Supplier Development, Supplier Quality, Manufacturing, and Supply Chain team members.
* Travel regularly to supplier locations to conduct assessments, resolve technical issues, and support production readiness and recovery activities.

### Required qualifications:

* Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, Welding Engineering, Industrial Engineering, or a related technical field.
* 7+ years of experience in supplier development, manufacturing engineering, welding engineering, supplier quality engineering, or a related technical role within aerospace, defense, or another highly regulated manufacturing environment.
* Demonstrated experience developing suppliers and resolving complex manufacturing, quality, capacity, or delivery issues.
* Technical experience with one or more of the following commodity or process areas:
* Large metallic airframe structures
* Welded structural assemblies
* Sheet metal components and assemblies
* Sheet metal forming and fabrication
* Structural frames, supports, mounts, and enclosures
* Complex mechanical and structural assemblies
* Precision-machined structural components
* Tooling, fixtures, and assembly jigs
* Aerospace welding and special processes
* Strong working knowledge of welding processes such as GTAW/TIG, GMAW/MIG, resistance welding, laser welding, or electron-beam welding.
* Knowledge of sheet metal manufacturing processes, including cutting, bending, forming, rolling, trimming, drilling, fastening, and structural assembly.
* Experience evaluating welding procedures, welder qualifications, joint preparation, filler materials, shielding requirements, heat input, distortion control, and weld inspection methods.
* Ability to read and interpret complex engineering drawings, GD&T, weld symbols, bills of material, material specifications, and manufacturing requirements.
* Experience assessing tooling and fixture concepts for welding, sheet metal forming, large-structure assembly, and dimensional inspection.
* Experience conducting manufacturing process audits, supplier capability assessments, capacity analyses, and production readiness reviews.
* Experience with First Article Inspection requirements and AS9102 documentation.
* Working knowledge of AS9100 quality management systems and aerospace supplier qualification requirements.
* Experience applying structured root-cause analysis and corrective-action methodologies, including 8D, 5-Why, and fishbone analysis.
* Knowledge of PFMEA, control plans, process capability, statistical process control, measurement systems, and inspection methods.
* Demonstrated ability to lead cross-functional technical teams and supplier improvement initiatives with limited oversight.
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to make data-driven technical decisions.
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to communicate technical risks and recovery plans to suppliers and senior leadership.
* Ability to manage multiple supplier development projects and priorities in a fast-paced environment.
* Ability to travel to domestic and international supplier facilities as required.
* Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. government security clearance.

* Experience leading supplier recovery efforts involving critical delivery, capacity, manufacturing, or quality issues.
* Experience with lean manufacturing, Six Sigma, value-stream mapping, process capability analysis, and production-flow optimization.
* Familiarity with NADCAP-controlled processes, including welding, heat treatment, nondestructive testing, coatings, and chemical processing.
* Familiarity with FAR, DFARS, ITAR, export compliance, and government contracting requirements.
* Experience working in both prototype and production environments with aggressive schedule and production-rate requirements.
* ASQ Certified Quality Engineer, Certified Welding Inspector, Six Sigma Green Belt or Black Belt, or another relevant professional certification.
