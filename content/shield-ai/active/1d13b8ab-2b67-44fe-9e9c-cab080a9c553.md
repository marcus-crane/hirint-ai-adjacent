### Job Description:

Shield AI is seeking a Senior Anaplan Solutions Architect to set technical direction and lead connected planning for Corporate FP&A. In our lean team, you will be both architect and hands-on builder, independently guiding complex design decisions while coaching FP&A colleagues toward greater self-sufficiency. You will help advance an autonomous, continuously updated forecast grounded in business drivers, robust data, and validated machine-learning inputs, freeing Finance partners to advise the business and giving leaders timely projections.

### What you'll do:

* Architect and build connected models for financial statements, workforce, allocations, annual planning, and long-range scenarios.
* Shape the planning architecture and roadmap with Finance leaders, balancing business value, scalability, and delivery capacity.
* Establish design standards and resolve cross-model dependencies to keep planning solutions intuitive, performant, and maintainable.
* Coach FP&A users and builders to own more of their analysis, scenarios, and model development.
* Integrate trusted enterprise data and predictive outputs into Anaplan, partnering with data and analytics engineers.
* Advance continuously refreshed, driver-based forecasting with clear reconciliation, review, and override controls.
* Provide technical leadership to builders and implementation partners, owning solution quality, acceptance, and knowledge transfer.
* Required qualifications:
* Extensive hands-on Anaplan experience with a record of architecting and delivering complex connected planning solutions.
* Deep understanding of Corporate FP&A and the judgment to translate Finance priorities into architectural decisions.
* Demonstrated ability to independently lead complex delivery while building directly in a lean team.
* Experience with integrations, model performance, reconciliation, and controlled deployment.
* Experience mentoring builders, developing Finance users, and advising senior stakeholders on design and delivery tradeoffs.

### 

* Anaplan Solution Architect certification or Master Anaplanner designation.
* Experience with integrated three-statement planning and driver-based forecasting.
* Experience integrating predictive models or enterprise data platforms with planning applications.
