### Job Description:

Shield AI is seeking a Data Engineer to build the reliable, governed data foundation for Finance's next generation of planning and decision support. Working on our Databricks platform, you will own reusable integrations, semantic-layer infrastructure, and production data services that support analytics and AI-enabled applications. You will combine engineering rigor with AI-assisted development to increase delivery speed, reliability, and cost efficiency.

### What you'll do:

* Build and operate scalable pipelines and integrations connecting enterprise data to Finance products.
* Develop semantic-layer infrastructure and governed data access for analytics, planning, and AI applications.
* Establish end-to-end pipeline observability, data-quality controls, and resilient operations.
* Engineer the services and integrations behind Databricks Genie and other AI-enabled Finance workflows.
* Use AI coding assistants to accelerate development while validating correctness, security, and performance.
* Apply FinOps practices to manage platform spend and optimize cost without compromising service quality.
* Partner with analytics engineers and enterprise teams on architecture, data contracts, and sustainable ownership.

### Required qualifications:

* Hands-on production data engineering experience with SQL and Python or PySpark.
* Experience with cloud data platforms, orchestration, data modeling, and governed serving patterns.
* Ability to design for observability, recoverability, security, and maintainability.
* Proficiency with AI coding assistants and independent validation of generated work.
* Practical experience enabling AI capabilities on enterprise data, ideally in Databricks; adjacent Snowflake Cortex or comparable experience is welcome.
* Strong cross-functional collaboration and production ownership.

### Preferred qualifications:

* Experience with Databricks, Delta Lake, cloud infrastructure, and deployment automation.
* Experience developing AI application backends, model integrations, or LLM-based workflows.
* Experience with FinOps, financial data, or enterprise-system integrations.
