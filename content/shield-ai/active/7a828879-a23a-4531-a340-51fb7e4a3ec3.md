The Senior Flight Ops Technician is a senior, hands-on technical role supporting the development, testing, and operation of unmanned aircraft systems in Ukraine. The role bridges aircraft prototyping, hardware integration, HIL/ground testing, aircraft maintenance, and field flight operations, working closely with hardware, software, autonomy, and flight-test teams.  
  
The technician will build and modify prototype aircraft, integrate avionics and electrical systems, fabricate parts using 3D printing and other rapid-prototyping methods, and troubleshoot mechanical, electrical, communications, and system-level issues. They will also build, maintain, and troubleshoot HIL rigs and related test infrastructure, help establish and maintain a local prototyping and flight-operations lab, and support aircraft readiness and field testing.

This role is best suited for a versatile, self-sufficient senior technician who can move seamlessly between the lab, aircraft, HIL environment, and flight-test site—rapidly solving problems and helping engineering teams get systems ready for test and flight.  
Flight Ops Technician III.

### What You'll Do:

* Build, modify, assemble, maintain, and repair prototype unmanned aircraft and related test systems.
* Install and integrate avionics, sensors, compute hardware, radios, antennas, payloads, wiring, and other experimental hardware.
* Fabricate prototype and replacement components using 3D printing, hand and power tools, and other rapid-prototyping methods.
* Build, modify, and troubleshoot wiring harnesses, connectors, electrical assemblies, and aircraft electrical systems.
* Build, wire, configure, maintain, and troubleshoot HIL rigs, test fixtures, and related engineering test infrastructure.
* Integrate flight computers, avionics, sensors, actuators, communications equipment, and other aircraft hardware into HIL and bench-test environments.
* Diagnose mechanical, electrical, avionics, communications, networking, and system-level issues across aircraft and test equipment.
* Support SIL, HIL, VIL, bench, ground, and live-flight testing, including aircraft functional checks and troubleshooting.
* Perform aircraft inspections, maintenance, repairs, configuration changes, and pre- and post-flight activities.
* Help establish and maintain a local prototyping and flight-operations lab, including tools, test equipment, fabrication equipment, spares, batteries, and support equipment.
* Support flight operations, including aircraft setup, launch and recovery, test-site setup and teardown, and preparation of equipment for field deployments.
* Maintain aircraft, test-system, maintenance, configuration, inventory, and other technical documentation.
* Work closely with hardware, software, autonomy, and flight-test engineers to rapidly diagnose problems, implement modifications, and improve test capabilities.
* Transport personnel and equipment to test sites and travel within Ukraine to support testing, demonstrations, and operations.
* Mentor less-experienced technicians and maintain high standards for workmanship, configuration control, organization, and safety.

### Required Qualifications:

* 5+ years of hands-on experience in UAS, aviation, aerospace, robotics, electronics, manufacturing, test support, or a related technical field.
* Strong mechanical and electrical aptitude with experience building, modifying, maintaining, or repairing complex electromechanical systems.
* Experience integrating hardware into aircraft, robotics, prototypes, test systems, or comparable platforms.
* Hands-on electrical experience, including wiring, connectors, soldering, crimping, cable fabrication, and electrical troubleshooting.
* Experience building, maintaining, or troubleshooting HIL rigs, test stands, test fixtures, or similar hardware/software integration environments.
* Ability to read and work from mechanical drawings, wiring diagrams, schematics, and engineering documentation
* Experience with rapid prototyping, fabrication, and iterative hardware development.
* Ability to systematically troubleshoot mechanical, electrical, communications, and system-level problems.
* Experience using common mechanical and electrical tools and test equipment.
* Experience supporting engineering, ground, flight, or field testing.
* Strong attention to detail, workmanship, safety, and configuration control.
* Ability to work independently and solve technical problems in fast-moving workshop, laboratory, flight-line, and field environments.
* Valid driver's license and ability to transport equipment and personnel to operational sites.
* Ability to travel within Ukraine as required.

### Preferred Qualifications:

\* Experience maintaining or integrating fixed-wing and multirotor UAS.  
\* Experience with autonomous or experimental aircraft and Group 1 or Group 2 UAS.  
\* Experience building or maintaining HIL environments for aircraft, robotics, or autonomous systems.  
\* Experience with 3D printing, CAD, and prototype part fabrication.  
\* Experience integrating avionics, flight computers, sensors, actuators, radios, antennas, or payload systems.  
\* Experience with PX4, ArduPilot, or similar flight-control systems.  
\* Familiarity with Ground Control Stations, telemetry, RF systems, datalinks, and networking.  
\* Familiarity with Linux, command-line tools, scripting, or basic software troubleshooting.  
\* Experience using oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, power supplies, or similar electrical test equipment.  
\* Experience establishing or maintaining an engineering lab, prototype shop, HIL lab, or flight-operations facility.  
\* Background in defense, aerospace, robotics, aircraft maintenance, manufacturing, or flight test.  
\* Experience working in austere or rapidly changing field environments.  
\* Ukrainian and English language proficiency.
