### About the Role

The Product Support Engineering Intern (Sustainment & Data) position at Shield AI focuses on optimizing the reliability, availability, and maintainability (R&M) of autonomous defense systems. You will develop critical sustainment tools, analyze logistics data, and build data pipelines to track fleet health.

### Required Qualifications

* Current student pursuing a degree in Industrial Engineering, Systems Engineering, Data Science, Aerospace, Mechanical Engineering, or a related STEM field.
* Strong proficiency in Python, SQL, R, or data visualization tools (e.g., Tableau, Power BI).
* Solid understanding of probability, statistics, and engineering economics as applied to reliability and logistics.
* Effective communication skills to translate complex data sets into actionable engineering recommendations.
* Ability to commit to an on-site, 10-week full-time internship program.

### Preferred Qualifications

* Prior experience or coursework in reliability engineering, logistics engineering, or predictive maintenance.
* Familiarity with database design, data warehousing, or system architecture concepts.
* Knowledge of aerospace or military sustainment standards (e.g., MIL-HDBK-217, MIL-STD-1629).

### Project Scope & Description

* Develop Sustainment Tools: Create, maintain, and optimize tools, dashboards, and data pipelines/systems to track fleet metrics and maintenance data.
* Logistics & R&M Engineering: Assist with Reliability, Availability, and Maintainability (R&M) analyses, including Failure Mode, Effects, and Criticality Analysis (FMECA).
* Data Integration: Clean and analyze telemetry, maintenance logs, and supply chain data using programming languages and tools like Python, SQL, Palantir Foundry.
* FRACAS Management: Support the Failure Reporting, Analysis, and Corrective Action System (FRACAS) to identify trends in component failures and improve product lifecycle management.
* Cross-Functional Collaboration: Partner with software engineering, logistics, and field operations to automate data collection and improve technical documentation.
