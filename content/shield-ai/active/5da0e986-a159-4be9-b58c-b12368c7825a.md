### Job Description:

The Quality Assurance team is responsible for ensuring the overall quality of software releases. This team is the gatekeeper that ensures only approved and assured software is released to customers and clients.   
The Quality Assurance team works closely with the Development and Hardware teams to achieve this goal.

Software Test Engineers report to the Quality Assurance lead and then to the Director of Engineering.

### What you'll do:

* Complete software engineering and quality assurance tasks across the software development lifecycle (SDLC), including test case development and execution; regression testing; implementing automated tests; verifying product performance goals and measures; ensuring project requirements are met. This includes documentation of all testing activities and approval matrices of software releases.
* Complete tasks to an agreed schedule and recognise any likely delays or failures early and communicate them immediately to the Director of Engineering.
* Collaborate with other Software Engineers and Software Quality Assurance Engineers by participating in ad-hoc discussions to improve software design, testing processes and outcomes, and software quality.
* Test environment preparation, including hardware setup, device flashing, software installation, software execution, integration testing, operational testing on flights in and around Melbourne.

### Required qualifications:

* Tertiary qualifications in Computer Science or Software Engineering, or equivalent overseas qualification (such as NZCE).
* Cross-platform testing experience, including Windows and Linux platforms.
* Proficient in setting up and managing different test environments and operating systems.
* Ability to analyse and solve complex software engineering problems individually, or as part of a team, depending on project requirements.
* Reliable and able to work to project schedules and deadlines.
* Experience working in an Agile software development environment, contributing to software solutions and automation frameworks.
* Strong knowledge of fault tracking systems.

### Preferred qualifications:

* ISTQB accreditation
* Experience with automated testing tools
* Hardware-in-the-Loop (HIL) and Software-in-the-Loop (SIL) test experience
* Experience in testing and validating real-time embedded software systems.
