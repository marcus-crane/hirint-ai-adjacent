### Job Description:

**The Strategic Capture Manager, USG Data Agencies** will lead Shield AI’s engagement strategy and design capture plans involving Government and Military Data/AI agencies. This individual will guide, shape, and inform Data/AI agency relationships and influence AI policy and requirements across Shield AI’s software and simulation portfolios.

### 

* Develop and implement a comprehensive strategic capture plan spanning Shield AI’s software and simulation portfolios, with a focus on Data/AI offices at senior USG and US Military agencies.
* Drive teams toward multi-year strategic program wins with USG Data/AI agencies.
* Build and maintain trusted relationships across key USG / US Military AI decision makers, including offices such as the DoW Chief Data / AI Officer (CDAO), DoW Chief Information Officer, and critical Dept of Homeland Security (DHS) and executive branch agencies.
* Anticipate customer needs and influence policy, requirements, and solicitations to positively align with Shield AI portfolios.

### Required qualifications:

* 10+ years experience in strategic capture or policy development in the USG
* Deep knowledge of USG and US Military data/AI policy process
* Experience with emerging technologies / AI
* Strong leadership, communication, and analytical skills with the ability to cross-collaborate across technical and operational teams
* Active Secret clearance (TS preferred)

### Preferred qualifications:

* Advanced degree in business, engineering, or a related field
* Prior experience in USG AI policy agencies
