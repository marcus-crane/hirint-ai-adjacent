In this role, you’ll gain exposure to multiple disciplines that are essential to delivering world-class aerospace systems, including Program Planning Engineering, Production Planning, Material Logistics Engineering, Quality Engineering, Prototype Build Engineering, and Advanced Manufacturing Engineering.

### Intern Project Scope & Description:

* Work on real projects, meaningful and interesting work, with a mentor to guide you.
* Have a great learning experience with a humble, inspiring team.
* Help solve problems during development of next generation products.
* Support program planning and scheduling activities, helping track milestones, deliverables, and dependencies across engineering teams.
* Assist production planning and logistics engineers with material coordination, inventory tracking, and supply chain optimization.
* Partner with prototype build teams to coordinate build readiness, track work orders, and update configuration documentation.
* Collaborate with quality engineers to collect production data, perform defect analysis, and support corrective actions.
* Participate in advanced manufacturing and process improvement projects, contributing to initiatives that enhance efficiency, repeatability, and product quality.
* Prepare reports, presentations, and metrics to communicate progress and insights to engineering and leadership teams.

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:

* Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Industrial Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Systems Engineering, or a related technical field.
* Strong organizational, analytical, and communication skills.
* Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a fast-paced environment.
* Proficiency with Microsoft Office (Excel, Project, PowerPoint).

### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS:

* You are expected to graduate in December 2027 or Spring 2028.
* Experience or coursework in project management, manufacturing systems, quality engineering, or supply chain logistics.
* Familiarity with data visualization or process modeling tools.
* Demonstrated interest in aerospace, prototyping, or advanced manufacturing technologies.
