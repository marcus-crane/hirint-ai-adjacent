### Job Description:

The Quality Systems Manager is responsible for developing, implementing, maintaining, and continuously improving Shield AI's enterprise Quality Management System (QMS). This role ensures the QMS meets AS9100, applicable AQAP/NATO, customer, contractual, and regulatory requirements while supporting rapid growth across development, production, and fielded products.

This leader will drive the integration of AQAP 2110 requirements into Shield AI's existing QMS, establishing a scalable system that supports international defense customers without creating duplicative quality processes. The role partners closely with Engineering, Manufacturing, Supply Chain, Program Management, Configuration Management, and Field Operations.

### What you'll do:

* Own and continuously improve the enterprise QMS, ensuring compliance with AS9100 and applicable AQAP, customer, contractual, and regulatory requirements.
* Lead the assessment, gap closure, implementation, and certification readiness activities required to support AQAP 2110.
* Develop and maintain a single integrated QMS that supports multiple programs, customers, and applicable international defense requirements.
* Own the internal audit program, including audit planning, auditor qualification, findings, corrective actions, and effectiveness verification.
* Lead preparation for AS9100, AQAP, customer, government, and third-party audits and assessments.
* Own enterprise corrective action and continuous improvement processes, ensuring systemic issues are effectively identified, corrected, and prevented from recurring.
* Establish and maintain processes for document control, quality records, objective evidence, and retention.
* Partner with Engineering and Configuration Management to ensure appropriate configuration control, traceability, and as-built product records.
* Establish processes for identifying and implementing contractual and customer-specific quality requirements, including quality planning and appropriate flow-down to suppliers.
* Support Government Quality Assurance activities and serve as a key interface with customers, government representatives, and authorized quality representatives.
* Establish enterprise quality metrics and provide leadership with visibility into QMS health, compliance risks, audit performance, and systemic quality issues.
* Lead cross-functional initiatives to simplify, standardize, digitize, and improve quality processes.
* Build, develop, and lead the Quality Systems & Compliance team.

### Required qualifications:

* Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality, Operations, or a related technical discipline; equivalent combinations of education and significant relevant experience may be considered.
* 8+ years of progressive experience in Quality, Quality Assurance, Quality Systems, Compliance, or Product Assurance.
* 3+ years of leadership experience in Quality Systems, Quality Assurance, Compliance, or a related function.
* Demonstrated experience developing, implementing, or maintaining an AS9100 Quality Management System.

### Preferred qualifications:

* Direct experience with AQAP 2110 or other NATO Allied Quality Assurance Publications.
* Experience supporting international defense customers, Government Quality Assurance, or government/customer quality representatives.
* Experience conducting QMS gap assessments and implementing compliance roadmaps.
* Experience integrating customer or regulatory requirements into an existing AS9100 QMS.
* Strong understanding of configuration management, traceability, product acceptance, and objective quality evidence.
* ASQ CMQ/OE, CQA, CQE, or equivalent professional certification.
* AS9100 or ISO Lead Auditor certification.
