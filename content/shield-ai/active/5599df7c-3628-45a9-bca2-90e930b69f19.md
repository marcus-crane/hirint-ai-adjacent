With offices and facilities across the U.S., Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, Shield AI’s technology actively supports operations worldwide. ### Job Description:

At Shield AI, we’re redefining the battlespace with autonomy-enabled airpower. As part of our global expansion, we’re looking for a bold and visionary Director – Army Advanced Systems to lead strategic sales, customer engagement, and capture efforts for our X-BAT and other advanced systems supporting U.S. Army markets. This is a high-impact role at the cutting edge of aerospace and defense innovation. You’ll be on the front lines, driving adoption and revenue for Shield AI’s technologies by engaging directly with top-tier Army stakeholders.

You’ll bring a rare mix of defense sales expertise, technical credibility, and a deep understanding of Army missions and acquisition frameworks. From identifying new opportunities to closing complex deals and launching early-stage programs, you’ll own the entire advanced system business development lifecycle for Army customers.

The **Advanced Systems Capture Director – Army** will lead Shield AI’s Advanced Systems engagement strategy and design capture plans involving Army opportunities. This individual will guide, shape, and inform X-BAT and other advanced system capture actions for the US Army.

### What you'll do:

* Develop and implement a comprehensive strategic capture plan for X-BAT for USG land forces.
* Drive teams toward multi-year strategic Army program wins.
* Build and maintain trusted relationships across key Army stakeholders and influencers, focused on Aviation, Fires, and Intel communities.
* Anticipate customer needs and influence policy, requirements, and solicitations to positively align with Shield AI portfolios.

### Required qualifications:

* 10+ years strategic capture / Army policy development experience
* Deep knowledge of Army requirement and/or acquisition processes
* Experience with emerging technologies
* Strong leadership, communication, and analytical skills with the ability to cross-collaborate across technical and operational teams
* Active Secret clearance (TS preferred)

### Preferred qualifications:

* Advanced degree in business, engineering, or a related field
* Army program capture within previous 5 years
