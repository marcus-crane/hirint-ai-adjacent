**Job Description:**

As the Battery system mechanical lead, you will be responsible for all mechanical aspects of battery systems for X-BAT, the Launch and Recovery Vehicle, and ground equipment. This includes battery enclosure design, internal cell packaging and interconnection, qualification, integration, and release to manufacturing.

You will help us build a team of world-class engineers to support all design needs. A vertically integrated battery pack design will enable top performance, ease of integration, reliability, and rapid supply chain procurement. If you enjoy working cross-functionally and solving hard problems, please apply!

### WHAT YOU’LL DO:

+ Define mechanical specifications and execute the battery system external enclosure, thermal management and environmental protection for X-BAT, and the ground Launch and Recovery Vehicle.

+ Drive optimal performance while managing size, weight, DFM, safety, and reliability

+ Hands-on prototyping, testing, qualification, and handoff to production

+ Define assembly drawings, designing for tolerance, assembly, and serviceability

+ Ensure system performance and reliability are met through rigorous system-level shock, vibration, ingress, and thermal verification

+ Investigate root-cause for the mechanical package, environmental, manufacturing, etc.

+ Support design reviews, drive execution of assigned engineering deliverables in support of program objectives

+ Work in a fast-paced environment

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:

+ You have a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering or equivalent

+ Aerospace enclosure design experience on large aircraft, or an equivalent system (rocket, satellite, submarine, automotive, etc.)

+ 5+ years relevant experience

+ Have delivered aerospace systems to market

+ Proficient with mechanical engineering development tools, simulation, and test equipment

+ Strong technical fundamentals and analysis
