### WHAT YOU’LL DO:

* Own design responsibility of a new battery pack, from concept through production
* Drive optimal performance while managing size, weight, manufacturing, and reliability
* Hands-on prototyping, testing, qualification, and handoff to production
* Define requirements, specifications, assembly drawings, 2D drawings
* Root cause failures and production problems
* Drive a cross-functional team to execute the top-level architecture
* Provide mentorship, design review, and program execution leadership
* Work in a fast-paced environment

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:

* Bachelor's Degree in Mechanical Engineering, or equivalent
* 5+ years of experience
* Strong technical fundamentals in mechanics, thermal design, and electronics
* Have designed and delivered a battery product to market before
* Proficiency in CAD modeling
* Experience with various manufacturing processes
