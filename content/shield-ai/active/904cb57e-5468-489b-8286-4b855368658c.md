### Job Description:

Software Engineers are part of the development team responsible for developing and supporting software products for research projects and for commercial release.  Software Engineers report to the Director of Engineering.

### What you'll do:

* Complete software development tasks, including writing new software code, primarily supporting Mission System and Support tools, and fixing bugs, as directed by the Director of Engineering.
* Complete tasks to an agreed schedule, and to recognise any likely delays or failures early and communicate them immediately to the Director of Engineering.
* Participate in ad-hoc discussions with other developers to assist in generating ideas, solving bugs and designing software applications.

### Required qualifications:

* Tertiary qualifications in Computer Science or Software Engineering, or equivalent overseas qualification (such as NZCE).
* Top academic achiever (i.e. minimum Distinction average) or demonstrated relevant previous experience
* Strong analytic/problem solving skills
* Ability to solve complex problems individually, or as part of a team, depending on project requirements.
* Reliable and able to work to a schedule.
* Experienced in designing and implementing new software modules and maintaining/updating existing source code while adhering to company standards.
* Strong C++, JavaScript and ReactJs Development skills
* Strong verbal and written communication skills to communicate complex ideas (eg. to customers or in technical reports.)

### Preferred qualifications:

* Experience with GIS mapping services (e.g. OpenStreetMaps, ArcGIS, or GeoServer) and interactive map libraries (e.g. Leaflet).
* Experience with video processing libraries (e.g. FFmpeg or GStreamer)
* Experience with frontend UI/UX design
