### Job Description:

Shield AI is seeking a YouTube Producer to lead the development, production, publishing, and growth of the company’s YouTube channel. This role combines the craft and editorial judgment of a senior video editor with channel strategy, audience development, and performance ownership.

The YouTube Producer will turn Shield AI’s work in autonomy, aviation, defense, and artificial intelligence into clear, accurate, audience-focused stories. They will manage projects from concept through final edit, packaging, publishing, and analysis while partnering with technical experts, executives, and communications teams.

### What you'll do:

* Own Shield AI’s YouTube programming strategy, editorial calendar, publishing cadence, and channel-growth plan.
* Develop and produce video concepts including explainers, product stories, interviews, flight-test content, event coverage, customer narratives, and short-form video.
* Lead projects from creative brief through scripting, interviews, production planning, editing, review, and delivery.
* Edit high-quality long-form and short-form content, including story structure, pacing, sound, graphics, color, captions, and final delivery.
* Package and publish videos using effective titles, thumbnails, descriptions, chapters, playlists, metadata, and calls to action.

### Required qualifications:

* 7+ years of experience in video production, post-production, digital media, editorial, or content marketing.
* Strong portfolio demonstrating video editing, visual storytelling, interview production, narrative structure, and digital-video judgment.
* Advanced proficiency in Adobe Premiere Pro and working knowledge of After Effects, Photoshop, Audition, DaVinci Resolve, or equivalent tools.
* Demonstrated experience producing and publishing content for YouTube or similar digital-video platforms.
* Strong understanding of YouTube packaging and analytics, including thumbnails, titles, audience retention, click-through rate, watch time, and subscriber growth.
* Ability to independently develop concepts and manage productions from idea through published video.
* Willingness to travel periodically for production, events, customer activities, or field shoots.

### Preferred qualifications:

* Experience managing or growing a YouTube channel for a technology, aerospace, defense, industrial, science, or mission-driven organization.
* Experience with camera operation, lighting, location audio, and directing interviews or on-camera talent.
* Experience managing freelance editors, motion designers, production crews, agencies, or vendors.
* Familiarity with YouTube Shorts and adapting long-form content for short-form distribution.
* Experience working with regulated, security-conscious, or high-growth technology organizations.

### What success looks like:

* A consistent YouTube publishing cadence that supports Shield AI’s brand and business priorities.
* Clear, compelling content that makes Shield AI’s mission, people, products, and technology more understandable to broad audiences.
* Stronger channel performance across watch time, retention, engagement, returning viewers, and subscribers.
* Reliable, scalable production workflows that maintain quality, accuracy, and security.
