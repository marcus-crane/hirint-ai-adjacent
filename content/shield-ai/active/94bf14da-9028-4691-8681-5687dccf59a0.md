### Job Description:

Help power the next generation of aircraft. As a **Staff Power Systems Engineer**, you’ll lead power system development from concept to production, solve complex technical challenges, and mentor a talented engineering team.

### What you'll do:

As a Staff Power Systems Engineer you will:

* Define and execute the vision of the Ranger power system from concept to production.
* Design, develop, and integrate state-of-the-art high-power systems tailored for optimal performance and reliability in harsh environments
* Enable an uninterruptible power system
* Make vs. buy trade studies. Select, test, and integrate modules for use in the aircraft
* Ensure system performance and reliability are met through rigorous verification
* Debug complex problems related to the electrical design, environmental, manufacturing, etc.
* Help build the team and lead multiple engineers dedicated to the power system design
* Drive a cross-functional team to execute the top-level architecture
* Provide mentorship, design review, and program execution leadership
* Work in a fast-paced environment

### Required qualifications:

* Power system design experience on a large vehicle, aircraft, or an equivalent power system (rocket, satellite, submarine, etc.)
* 10+ years relevant experience
* Have delivered power system and power electronic products to market
* Proficient with electrical engineering development tools, simulation, and test equipment
* Strong technical fundamentals and analysis
