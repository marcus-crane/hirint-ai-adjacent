### Job Description:

Help build the technology behind the next generation of autonomous aircraft. As a Lead, Electrical Engineer, you will serve as a player-coach: leading the architecture, design, integration, and delivery of advanced electrical systems while directly managing and developing a small team of electrical engineers.You will remain deeply hands-on technically while setting direction for your team, driving execution across disciplines, and building the leadership experience needed to grow into broader engineering management responsibilities, including eventually leading full avionics organizations.

#### Growth Opportunity

This role is intended for an experienced technical leader who wants to expand their impact through both hands-on engineering leadership and people management. Successful performance can lead to broader responsibility for multiple teams, larger avionics portfolio.

### What you'll do:

* Lead the architecture, design, integration, and production release of electrical systems and subsystems for autonomous aircraft and support equipment.
* Own technical decisions across complex architectures involving FPGAs, power management, analog and digital circuitry, servos, microcontrollers, sensors, radios, and embedded systems.
* Remain hands-on with part selection, schematic capture, simulation, analysis, verification, debugging, design reviews, and production support.
* Lead and develop a team of electrical engineers through technical mentorship, coaching, performance feedback, and effective delegation.
* Establish technical direction, priorities, and development plans for your team in alignment with program and aircraft objectives.
* Partner closely with firmware, software, mechanical, manufacturing, supply chain, test, and systems engineering teams to deliver integrated products.
* Support the design and integration of embedded systems and sensors that enable aircraft autonomy and AI capabilities.
* Define technical requirements, product features, interfaces, and verification strategies.
* Own project execution for your team, including schedules, risks, dependencies, deliverables, and cross-functional communication.
* Lead and participate in architecture, design, integration, and production readiness reviews.
* Help develop automated test solutions and improve hardware development, verification, and production processes.
* Identify and implement improvements that increase engineering efficiency, product quality, aircraft reliability, and team effectiveness.
* Build the technical and organizational foundation required to scale the avionics engineering team over time.

### Required qualifications:

* Bachelor’s degree in electrical engineering or equivalent practical experience.
* 8+ years of industry experience in electrical engineering, including multiple product development cycles.
* Experience leading technically complex electrical or avionics designs from concept through production.
* Strong fundamentals in circuit design, embedded systems, hardware architecture, analysis, verification, and debugging.
* Experience delivering products across EE, ME, firmware, software, manufacturing, and systems engineering disciplines.
* Demonstrated ability to lead, mentor, and develop engineers while maintaining hands-on technical ownership.
* Proven track record of shipping high-quality products, navigating ambiguity, resolving obstacles, and delivering against challenging schedules.
* Strong communication, collaboration, prioritization, and cross-functional leadership skills.
* Demonstrated ownership, initiative, sound technical judgment, and commitment to engineering excellence.
* Ability to obtain a Secret/SAR-level security clearance desired.
