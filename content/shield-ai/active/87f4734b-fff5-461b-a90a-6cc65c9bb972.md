### Job Description:

As a **Staff Flight Test Engineer / Test Director**, you will serve as the **end-to-end Test Director** for the integration, verification, and flight test of **Hivemind Mission Autonomy** on **small Unmanned Aircraft Systems (UAS)**. You will own the **technical direction, execution, and readiness** of test campaigns, applying a **fly–fix–fly methodology** to rapidly mature autonomy capabilities from simulation and lab environments through flight.

In this role, you are the **single technical authority for test execution**—responsible for determining test readiness, directing test conduct, managing risk, and ensuring data-driven decisions throughout development and flight test operations. You will lead cross-functional teams across engineering, safety, and program management, aligning stakeholders around clear objectives, disciplined execution, and continuous learning.

### What you'll do:

* **Act as Test Director** for system-level integration and flight test campaigns of Hivemind Mission Autonomy on small UAS and external platforms
* **Own test readiness decisions**, including entry/exit criteria for lab, simulation, ground, and flight test phases
* **Plan, direct, and execute fly–fix–fly test campaigns**, ensuring rapid iteration while maintaining disciplined configuration control and safety
* **Define and enforce test strategy**, including system-level verification and validation, test phasing, and prioritization of objectives
* **Direct cross-functional test execution**, coordinating across various engineering disciplines and test operators.
* **Determine test infrastructure requirements**, including SIL, HIL, simulation environments, flight test assets, and automated test pipelines
* **Approve and oversee test procedures**, test cards, and execution plans, serving as the final authority during test conduct
* **Define performance metrics and success criteria**, ensuring test data is actionable and traceable to requirements and mission outcomes
* **Lead flight test risk assessments**, hazard analyses, and mitigation planning; enforce safety-of-flight discipline during operations
* **Serve as primary test interface** to internal leadership, customers, and external stakeholders, providing clear reporting on test progress, results, and risks
* **Establish test standards, SOPs, and best practices** aligned with industry and DoD DT&E/OT&E expectations
* **Document test results, anomalies, corrective actions, and lessons learned**, driving continuous improvement across autonomy flight test efforts
* Support travel to test sites, customer events, and integration activities as required
* Perform other duties as assigned

### Required qualifications:

* B.S. degree in a STEM discipline (Aerospace Engineering, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related field)
* 7+ years of experience in **system integration and test**, including SIL, HIL, simulation, and flight test environments
* Demonstrated experience **leading test execution**, including authority over test conduct, readiness, and risk decisions
* Strong background working cross-functionally with systems engineering, software, hardware, safety, and program management teams
* Highly organized and methodical, with the ability to manage complex, fast-paced test campaigns
* Strong written and verbal communication skills, including briefing technical status to leadership and customers
* Demonstrated integrity, accountability, and commitment to high standards while fostering a collaborative, respectful team environment

### Preferred qualifications:

* Working knowledge of **DoD DT&E and OT&E** processes and documentation expectations
* Direct experience with **small UAS, autonomy, or robotic flight test operations**
* Prior role as **Test Director, Test Conductor, or Lead Flight Test Engineer**
* Experience operating within **DoD software and hardware laboratories** or government flight test ranges
* Familiarity with safety-of-flight processes, hazard analysis, and configuration control in flight test environments
