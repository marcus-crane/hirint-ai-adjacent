## Job Description:

We are seeking a Propulsion Performance Engineer to own engine calibration and performance for the reciprocating engines that power the V-BAT platform. This role serves as the technical owner for engine calibration strategy — driving test plans, ECU tuning, and performance analysis from bench through flight test — and is accountable for the torque, power, speed, and fuel efficiency characteristics the engine delivers across its full operating envelope.  
  
You'll work hands-on with engine hardware, electronic fuel injection and engine control systems, and engine test stands, creating and executing engine testing strategies under the direction of senior staff propulsion engineers. Candidates with a background in air-cooled or aviation two-stroke engines are especially encouraged to apply; at minimum, we're looking for someone with deep hands-on experience on reciprocating engines (four-stroke or two-stroke) who can own calibration decisions with minimal oversight.

### What You'll Do In This Role

* Support the engine calibration strategy by defining test plans, calibration methodology, and acceptance criteria across dynamometer, bench, and flight test campaigns.
* Perform ECU configuration and tuning (including fuel mapping, ignition timing, and injector phasing) to optimize engine performance, fuel efficiency, and reliability across the operating envelope.
* Analyze and deliver torque, power, and fuel consumption results, identifying calibration or hardware improvement opportunities and driving corrective action.
* Lead troubleshooting and root-cause investigations for engine testing for the development team and production teams.
* Reduce and interpret test and flight data to validate performance against requirements, closing gaps between design intent and measured behavior.
* Represent engine calibration and performance in design reviews, technical readiness checkpoints, and cross-functional discussions with design, controls, and flight test teams.
* Develop and maintain calibration documentation, procedures, and performance reporting to support program milestones.
* Support refinement of performance models for the propulsion system and the vehicle through targeted test campaigns and data review.

### Required qualifications:

* Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, or a related engineering discipline.
* 5+  years of experience in a mechanical or aerospace engineering role, with a focus on engine performance or calibration.
* Deep hands-on experience with reciprocating (piston) engines.
* Demonstrated experience with electronically controlled fuel injectors and fuel injection systems.
* Proven experience with ECU configuration and engine calibration, including ownership of calibration decisions.
* Strong ability to analyze and interpret engine torque, power, and fuel consumption data to drive engine design, ECU calibration, and vehicle performance decisions.
* U.S. Citizenship or ability to comply with ITAR requirements.

### Preferred qualifications:

* Podium finish in FSAE competition, with at least one first place trophy in any dynamic event.
* Background in air-cooled engines, particularly aviation two-stroke platforms.
* Experience with dynamometer test cells and associated data acquisition systems.
* Deep familiarity with fuel maps, ignition timing strategies, and closed-loop engine control logic.
* Proficiency in Python or MATLAB for data reduction and calibration tooling.
* Experience writing custom data analysis tools to automate engine performance analysis and calibration workflows.
* Experience supporting military propulsion systems, tactical aircraft, or high-performance aerospace platforms.
* M.S. or Ph.D. in Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, or a related engineering discipline.
