### Job Description:

Shield AI is a venture-backed defence technology company building intelligent autonomous systems for aircraft. Our mission is to protect service members and civilians through AI and autonomy.

We’re looking for a Technical Program Manager to join our Port Melbourne team, leading the development, integration, and deployment of the world’s best AI pilots across a range of transformative autonomous systems (UAVs, USVs, UUVs).

This is a high-impact, hands-on role working at the centre of our growing Australian operations. You’ll manage complex engineering programmes from early concept through to field testing and deployment — and work directly with customers, defence stakeholders, and internal teams to make it happen.

### What you'll do:

* Lead and deliver end-to-end technical program management leverage your industry knowledge to shape/define program and organizational objectives
* Oversee the full programme lifecycle – initiation, planning, execution, monitoring, and closure – ensuring exceptional outcomes.
* Fulfil contractual and customer deliverables with precision, timeliness, and clarity.
* Build and maintain strong relationships with internal and external stakeholders, including government, defence, suppliers, and technical teams.
* Operate with sensitivity to cultural, political, and military dynamics across domestic and international partnerships.
* Conduct critical path analysis, manage earned value tracking, and report on programme health metrics.
* Proactively identify and mitigate risks and blockers.
* Lead organised, efficient meetings with clear actions and follow-up.
* Support business development efforts, including EOIs, RFTs, RFIs, proposals, and unsolicited submissions.

### Required qualifications:

* Significant experience as a technical project or programme manager in complex engineering or technology domains.
* Proven track record of delivering complex, cross-functional projects aligned with long-term business strategy.
* Experience managing 0→1 product development cycles.
* Strong commercial understanding of project financials, including earned value management, estimates at completion, and contract data requirements.
* Proficiency in project management tools (e.g. Microsoft Project, Smartsheet, Vena, Salesforce).
* Experience working with or within the Australian Defence Force.
* Outstanding communication, leadership, organisation, and influencing skills.
* A proactive, dependable, and collaborative mindset — you work hard, uphold high standards, and support your team.

### Preferred qualifications:

* Experience with software or autonomy development programmes.
* Familiarity with the ASDEFCON (or equivalent) contracting suite.
* Exposure to multi-national teams, flight test campaigns, or airworthiness certification.
* PMP or Certified Scrum Master (CSM) accreditation.
* Experience working with or managing international defence related projects.
