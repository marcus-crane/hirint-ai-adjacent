### Job Description:

We are seeking a Senior Cybersecurity Engineer to lead cybersecurity activities for an airborne aerospace and defense platform. This role will integrate cybersecurity across avionics, mission systems, aircraft networks, communications, embedded software, and ground-support systems.

### What you'll do:

* Develop the platform’s cybersecurity architecture, requirements, and protection strategy.
* Perform threat modeling, vulnerability assessments, attack-surface analysis, and cyber risk assessments.
* Define protections for secure boot, authentication, access control, encryption, key management, software updates, and data communications.
* Apply defense-in-depth across aircraft systems, networks, radios, data links, and external interfaces.
* Lead DoD Risk Management Framework activities and develop required authorization evidence.
* Evaluate hardware, software, firmware, and supplier designs for cybersecurity risks.
* Develop cybersecurity verification plans and support vulnerability scanning, penetration testing, and cyber-resiliency testing.
* Coordinate risk mitigation and corrective actions with engineering teams, suppliers, customers, and program leadership.
* Ensure cybersecurity solutions support aircraft safety, availability, and mission effectiveness.

### Required qualifications:

* Bachelor’s degree in cybersecurity, computer engineering, electrical engineering, software engineering, systems engineering, or a related field.
* Typically seven or more years of relevant cybersecurity or systems security experience.
* Experience with aircraft, aerospace, defense, or other mission-critical embedded systems.
* Knowledge of avionics, mission systems, platform networks, communications, or data links.
* Experience with threat modeling, secure system architecture, vulnerability analysis, and security testing.
* Familiarity with DoD RMF, NIST security guidance, cryptography, and key management.
* Strong technical leadership, writing, and customer communication skills.
* Ability to obtain and maintain the required security clearance.

### Preferred qualifications:

* Cybersecurity experience on military aircraft, unmanned aircraft, or airborne mission systems.
* Familiarity with NIST SP 800-53, NIST SP 800-160, DO-326A, DO-355, and DO-356A.
* Experience with anti-tamper, program protection, supply-chain risk management, or cross-domain solutions.
* Experience supporting cyber testing, penetration testing, or adversarial assessments.
* Relevant certification such as CISSP, Security+, CEH, or GIAC.
* Active U.S. security clearance.
