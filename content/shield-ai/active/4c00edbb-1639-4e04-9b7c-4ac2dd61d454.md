### Job Description:

We're looking for an Associate Sourcing Specialist to help bring innovative products to life by leading supplier purchasing initiatives and building high-performing supplier partnerships. Working across cross-functional teams, you'll help ensure critical materials and components are available to support development, production, and long-term success in a highly regulated and capacity-constrained environment.

### What you'll do:

* Own the sourcing and procurement of munitions commodities across defense suppliers.
* Identify, qualify, onboard, and manage suppliers capable of meeting cost, quality, delivery, and technical requirements for prototype, development, and production builds.
* Request and evaluate supplier quotations, perform cost analyses, negotiate pricing and lead times, and execute purchase orders and long-term agreements where appropriate.
* Partner cross-functionally with engineering, program management, legal, and finance to align sourcing strategies with program and business objectives.
* Build and manage strategic supplier relationships, including sole-source and limited-source vendors, to ensure continuity of supply.
* Ensure compliance with ITAR, EAR, DFARS, and other applicable U.S. government regulations and export controls.
* Identify and mitigate supply chain risks, including geopolitical, capacity, and regulatory risks specific to munitions.
* Continuously identify opportunities for cost savings, lead time reduction, process improvements, and supplier consolidation.

### Required qualifications:

* Education & Experience: Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain Management, Business, Engineering, Operations, or a related field, or equivalent combination of education and relevant experience, including exposure to defense or munitions programs or Aerospace Programs.
* Defense & Munitions Expertise: 2-5 years of experience with munitions or defense-related commodities in strategic sourcing, purchasing, or supply chain.
* Regulatory Knowledge: Demonstrated expertise in ITAR, EAR, DFARS, and U.S. government contracting requirements, including experience working with export-controlled materials and compliance frameworks.
* Analytical Acumen: Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to manage multiple suppliers and priorities in a fast-paced manufacturing environment.
* Security Clearance (if applicable): Ability to obtain and maintain a U.S. security clearance, depending on program requirements.
