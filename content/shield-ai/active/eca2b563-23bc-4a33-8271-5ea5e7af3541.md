### WHAT YOU’LL DO:

* Own the design of motors and other electric machines for X-BAT and other next gen aircraft
* Design sub-systems and components including wound field motor, permanent magnet motor, and motor mechanical structural components
* Integrate designs with the aircraft structure, engine, power electronics, and thermal system
* Create detailed 3D CAD models and 2D drawings of electro-mechanical designs at a component and motor sub-system level
* Release detailed work instructions and documentation in close collaboration with manufacturing
* Execute hands on testing and full qualification on a representative dyno, engine test stand, and full aircraft
* Perform noise and vibration testing on components for motors and power electronics, including vehicle powertrain and thermal systems

### REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:

* 5+ years of experience in mechanical design of electric motors
* Bachelors degree in mechanical engineering or equivalent
* Strong technical fundamentals with material selection, mechanics, dynamics, thermal, DFM, and tolerancing
* Proficiency with 3D CAD modeling
* Experience with GD&T, stack-up analyses (linear & radial), and determining realistic tolerancing limits based on manufacturing best practices
* Have delivered motor products from design through production
* Hands on experience to verify performance and integrate with power electronics
* Experience testing for noise, vibration, and harshness (NVH)
* Hands-on experience with prototyping, testing, and troubleshooting
* You have a demonstrated record of working hard, being a trustworthy teammate, holding yourself and others to high standards, and being kind to others
