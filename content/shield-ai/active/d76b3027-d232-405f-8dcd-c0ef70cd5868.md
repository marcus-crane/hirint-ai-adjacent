The Tool Control Coordinator is responsible for establishing, implementing, and managing a comprehensive tool control program modeled after commercial and military aviation standards. This role will develop and enforce policies, processes, and accountability systems that ensure all tools used within maintenance operations are tracked, controlled, and compliant with aviation safety and regulatory requirements. The Tool Control Coordinator role will coordinate daily tool room operations, support maintenance and air vehicle operator teams, and oversee the lifecycle of controlled tools and equipment.

### What You'll Do:

+ Develop and implement a robust tool control program, including policies, procedures, tool inventories, shadow boards, tracking systems, and inspection protocols.
+ Lead the daily operations of tool rooms and tool issue points, ensuring full accountability and availability of tools to support maintenance work and field operations.
+ Establish and maintain tool control documentation aligned with regulatory, OEM, and internal quality requirements.
+ Assume the responsibilities of the tool calibration program.

- Conduct regular tool audits, inspections, and discrepancy investigations, ensuring timely resolution and reporting of missing or damaged tools.
- Train technicians on tool control standards, handling procedures, documentation accuracy, and safety expectations.
- Collaborate with production, quality, safety, supply chain, and engineering teams to ensure tools and equipment meet program and aircraft operational requirements.
- Manage tool procurement, calibration scheduling, repair processes, and replacement planning.
- Monitor and report tool control metrics, compliance findings, and program performance to senior leadership.
- Identify and drive continuous improvements to tool control processes, systems, and workflow efficiency.
- Ensure operations comply with OSHA, FAA, DoD, and internal safety/quality standards as applicable.
