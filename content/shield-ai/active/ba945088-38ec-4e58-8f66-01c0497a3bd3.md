The Administrative Assistant provides proactive administrative support to leaders and teams by coordinating schedules, meetings, travel, and administrative activities that support efficient day-to-day business operations. This role serves as a reliable resource for day-to-day administrative needs while ensuring a high level of organization, responsiveness, and service to leaders and stakeholders.

### What You'll Do

* Manage calendars for assigned leaders, coordinating meetings, resolving scheduling conflicts, and maintaining calendar accuracy across multiple time zones.
* Coordinate internal and external meetings, including scheduling, logistics, and stakeholder communications appropriate to the supported leader level.
* Coordinate business travel, prepare itineraries, and process expense reports for supported VP-level leaders in compliance with company policies.
* Provide day-to-day administrative support, including meeting logistics, note-taking, action item tracking, document management, and administrative processes.
* Serve as a primary administrative contact for supported leaders and teams, ensuring timely communication and coordination.
* Coordinate scheduling and logistics for customer, partner, and stakeholder engagements, as appropriate.
* Build effective working relationships across internal stakeholders to support day-to-day business operations and administrative activities.
* Support local leadership activities, executive visits, offsites, and other team events through effective planning and logistical coordination.
* Ensure continuity of support during vacations, leaves of absence, and other coverage periods.
* Participate in the administrative community by sharing knowledge, supporting team initiatives, and contributing to a collaborative, professional work environment.
* Provide on-site office support Monday through Friday, 10 a.m.–7 p.m., including receiving packages, coordinating office maintenance and services, and managing office-related expenses.

### Required Qualifications

* 3+ years of executive, administrative, or office support experience.
* Fluency in English both spoken and written.
* Experience managing calendars, scheduling meetings, and coordinating administrative activities for leaders or teams.
* Experience coordinating travel arrangements and processing expense reports.
* Strong organizational and time management skills with exceptional attention to detail.
* Ability to manage multiple priorities and adapt to changing business needs in a fast-paced environment.
* Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to interact professionally across all levels of the organization.
* High level of professionalism, discretion, and integrity when handling confidential information.
* Proficiency with Microsoft Office, Google Workspace, and collaboration tools such as Microsoft Teams or Slack.
* Ability to work independently, take initiative, and follow through on assigned responsibilities.

### Preferred Qualifications

* Experience supporting VP, Senior Director, or equivalent leadership.
* Fluency in Arabic both spoken and written.
* Experience working in an aerospace, defense, manufacturing, or other fast-paced organization.
* Familiarity with travel and expense management systems such as Concur and enterprise systems such as Costpoint.
* Experience coordinating meetings, offsites, or team events.
* Experience supporting cross-functional teams and collaborating with multiple business partners.
* Associate's or bachelor's degree in Business Administration or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.
