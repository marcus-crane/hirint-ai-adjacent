### Job Description:

Shield AI is seeking a marketing manager to build and execute the audience growth strategy across first-party social channels and third-party digital communities. This role will develop growth models, identify scalable acquisition opportunities, manage paid-growth levers and budget, and partner with our communications, social, field marketing, and internal video teams to expand reach, engagement, and influence among key audiences.

### What you'll do:

**Audience Growth & Analytics**

* Own audience growth analysis across Shield AI’s first-party social channels, including LinkedIn, X, Instagram, and YouTube.
* Analyze third-party audience and community behavior across platforms such as Reddit, YouTube, and relevant industry forums.
* Build audience segmentation frameworks to better understand priority personas, content interests, channel behaviors, and acquisition opportunities.
* Partner with Social and Communications teams to inform organic content strategy with performance insights and audience intelligence.
* Manage paid-growth levers and budget to accelerate audience acquisition, engagement, and channel-level performance.
* Track and report on audience growth, engagement, conversion, and retention metrics across channels.
* Identify high-potential communities, creators, conversations, and distribution opportunities relevant to Shield AI’s mission and products.
* Develop dashboards, reports, and recommendations that translate data into clear growth actions for Marketing and Communications stakeholders.
* Support testing across creative, copy, targeting, channels, and content formats to improve reach and engagement.
* Monitor platform trends, algorithm changes, and audience behavior shifts to inform channel strategy.

### Required qualifications:

* 3+ years of experience in growth marketing, digital marketing analytics, audience development, social analytics, or a related field.
* Experience analyzing performance across social platforms such as LinkedIn, X, Instagram, YouTube, Reddit, or similar communities.
* Familiarity with AI-enabled analytics, content optimization, or marketing workflow tools.
* Strong analytical skills with the ability to translate data into actionable recommendations.
* Experience managing or supporting paid social, paid audience growth, or digital acquisition campaigns.
* Proficiency with analytics, reporting, and dashboarding tools.
* Strong understanding of audience segmentation, channel performance, testing, and growth metrics.
* Excellent communication skills with the ability to present insights clearly to cross-functional stakeholders.
* Strong cross-functional collaborator with the ability to partner effectively with Social, Communications, Creative, and Marketing teams.

### Preferred qualifications:

* Experience in aerospace, defense, national security, advanced technology, or B2B technology.
* Experience with social listening, community intelligence, or creator/influencer analysis tools.
* Experience building executive-ready dashboards or recurring performance reporting systems.
