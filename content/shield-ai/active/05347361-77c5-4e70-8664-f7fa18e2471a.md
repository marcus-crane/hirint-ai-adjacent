Shield AI is a venture-backed defense-tech company with the mission of protecting service members and civilians with intelligent systems. Its products include Hivemind autonomy software, V-BAT and X-BAT aircraft, and Aechelon simulation and synthetic reality technologies. With offices and facilities across the U.S., Europe, the Middle East, and Asia-Pacific, Shield AI’s technology actively supports operations worldwide. For more information, visit [www.shield.ai](https://shield.ai/). Follow Shield AI on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/shield-ai), [X](https://x.com/shieldaitech), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/shield_ai), and [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@Shield_AI).

We’re hiring a Principal Strategic Talent Acquisition Partner to help power our next phase of growth across APAC. This is a high-impact, business-critical role at the heart of Shield AI’s international expansion—supporting new market entries, building and scaling best-in-class software and commercial organisations, and partnering with senior leaders to shape our future in the region.

This is not a transactional recruiting role. You will operate as a strategic advisor, market expert, and builder, owning how talent enables scale across highly diverse APAC markets, cultures, and regulatory environments.

### What You’ll Do

* **Strategic Talent Partnership**
* Partner closely with senior and executive-level stakeholders to understand business objectives, growth plans, and capability gaps across APAC.
* Translate regional market entry and growth plans into scalable, data-driven talent acquisition strategies.
* Act as a thought partner and advisor on regional talent trends, market intelligence, and competitive hiring strategies.
* Advise leaders on market feasibility from a talent perspective, including availability, cost, timelines, and risk across different APAC countries.
* **Execution with Excellence**
* Own the end-to-end recruitment lifecycle for all roles, including senior, critical, and hard-to-hire positions.
* Design and execute sourcing strategies that attract high-impact engineering and commercial talent across APAC.
* Adapt hiring approaches to local market realities while maintaining a consistent global hiring bar.
* **Employer Branding & Talent Attraction**
* Act as an APAC ambassador for Shield AI’s employer brand, positioning us as an employer of choice across the region.
* Partner with Branding, Comms, and Leadership to shape and localise our employer value proposition for different APAC markets.
* Identify and activate employer branding opportunities (events, content, communities, referrals, social presence).
* Ensure an inclusive, consistent, and culturally authentic candidate experience that reinforces our brand and values at every touchpoint.
* **Driving at Scale**
* Use data to forecast hiring needs, track progress, and continuously optimise recruitment performance across the region.
* Improve TA processes, tools, and operating models to support speed, quality, and scale in complex, multi-country environments.
* Be a visible and trusted voice for talent within the APAC leadership community.

### What We’re Looking For

* Proven experience as a strategic talent acquisition partner, driving full-cycle hiring for both engineering and go-to-market talent in high-growth tech environments (aerospace, defence, robotics, or deep tech desirable).
* Demonstrated success supporting new market entries and rapid scaling across the APAC region.
* Strong credibility partnering with senior leaders and executives as a trusted talent advisor and business partner.
* Deep understanding of APAC talent dynamics, including sourcing strategies, cultural considerations, and competitive intelligence across multiple countries.
* Highly consultative, commercially minded, and comfortable operating with ambiguity and complexity.
* Ability to balance strategic thinking with hands-on execution in a fast-moving environment.
