### Job Description:

Job Description: Be the expert on integrating weapons an payloads on our aircraft. Using best practices and MIL-STD-1289 to drive the designs for integrating weapons and stores to a weapons bay and designs for external Pylons

### What you'll do:

* Combine and collaborate with aircraft design teams to perform mechanical weapons integration activities
* Design pylons and fittings
* Work across analysis teams to iterate designs
* Support design reviews and report on accomplishments of the design process
* Support integration and test efforts including ground and flight tests
* Be the go to person on weapons and payloads physical integration to X Bat

### Required qualifications:

* Strong CAD skills (NX preferred)
* B.S. in Mechanical/Aerospace Engineering
* 10+ years in mechanical or mechanism design
* Familiarity with Bomb rack Units/Assemblies (BRA/BRU) and Launcher Assembly Units (LAU)
* Knowledge of Pneumatic power systems for military aircraft uses
* Demonstrated Experience in designing suspension and release mounting fixtures and fittings
* Demonstrated experience in weapon pylon
* Ability to design
* Ability to work independently in fast-paced, cross-functional teams and across functional organizations
* A demonstrated record of working hard, being trustworthy, holding yourself to high standards, and being kind to others
* Ability to obtain a S//SAR level security clearance desired

### Preferred qualifications:

* Experience with UAVs, aircraft, or robotic systems
* Familiarity with electro-mechanical actuators
* Hands-on machine shop or mechanic experience (professional or hobby)
* Ability to thrive in startup, R&D, or defense innovation environments
