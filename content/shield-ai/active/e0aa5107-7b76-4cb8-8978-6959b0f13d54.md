### Job Description:

The Hivemind Enterprise Team is seeking a Senior Application Security Engineer for our Hivemind Enterprise product.

The Hivemind Enterprise product is a software development kit (SDK) that enables scalable design, deployment, evaluation, and iteration of autonomy architectures for a wide range of robotic platforms. Hivemind Enterprise is a highly technical software product providing tools and user interfaces to support multiple user personae, ranging from AI developers to test engineers.

### What you'll do:

* As an application security engineer for Hivemind you will join our cybersecurity engineering team to help define our application security policies, deploy and configure scanning tools within our CI/CD pipelines, review scanning results and work directly with our developers to identify and fix security issues in or code.
* In addition, you will have the opportunity to help solve security issues before they start by working with our architecture team to design a secure-by-default software product, performing threat modeling, and validating designs meet our secure coding standards.

### Required qualifications:

* Bachelor’s degree with approximately 5+ years of relevant experience, or Master’s degree with approximately 4+ years of relevant experience, in cybersecurity, software engineering, or a closely related technical field
* Experience developing or testing cyber secure code
* Strong understanding of modern software engineering practices, including CI/CD, automated testing, configuration management, and software lifecycle processes
* Familiarity with threat modeling
* Experience with software analysis tools, including static analysis, dynamic analysis, dependency mapping, CVE scanning, etc.
* Comfortable working in a Linux development environment
* Strong organizational, communication, and cross-functional collaboration skills
* Basic understanding of key cryptography concepts

### Preferred qualifications:

* Previous experience on an application security team for a large, complex software product
* Experience as a DevSecOps engineer, especially in a GitLab environment
* Experience writing software or testing Python, C/C++, and/or Go
* Red-teaming experience
* Software reverse engineering skills
