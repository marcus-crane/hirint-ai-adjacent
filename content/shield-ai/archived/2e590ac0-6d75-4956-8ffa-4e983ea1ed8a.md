### Job Description:

Help shape the story behind cutting-edge technology with real-world impact. In this role, you'll develop compelling communications that elevate our brand, engage key audiences, and support strategic initiatives across media, executive, and corporate channels. Working closely with cross-functional teams, you'll transform complex ideas into clear, impactful messaging that advances our mission.

### What you'll do:

* Write and edit copy across formats, including press releases, social posts, op-eds, and marketing materials.
* Support external communications, including media outreach and executive communications.
* Coordinate cross-functional stakeholders to move projects from planning through approval and publication.
* Support executive communications, including media briefings, speaking engagements, and message prep.
* Support relationships with external partners, including PR firms and other companies and organizations.
* Collaborate with internal teams to develop and refine messaging for defense, technology, and policy audiences.
* Research and draft thought leadership content, ensuring consistency in voice and tone.
* Monitor news, industry trends, and competitor communications.
* Provide administrative support for communications and broader marketing team.

### Required qualifications:

* 2-4 years of experience in defense technology, corporate communications, or public relations.
* Bachelor's degree in communications, journalism, national security, political science, or related field.
* Experience working across teams and managing stakeholders.
* Ability to manage multiple projects and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
* Familiarity with media relations, executive communications, and messaging.
* Strong writing and editing skills with attention to detail.
* Interest in AI, autonomy, and defense technology.
* Ability to explain complex technical concepts clearly.
* Demonstrated ownership of projects from start to finish, including follow-through on details and deadlines.
* Strong judgment and critical thinking, including the ability to assess messaging and identify risks or gaps.

### Preferred qualifications

* Experience supporting communications for a defense, aerospace, government, or technical organization.
* Understanding of the U.S. defense ecosystem and national security landscape.
* Experience working with PR agencies and external partners.
