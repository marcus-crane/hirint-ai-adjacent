As we continue our rapid global growth, we're looking for a People Experience Operations Partner to join our international People team based in Melbourne. This is an exciting opportunity to play a key role in shaping the employee experience for a growing global workforce while helping scale operations in one of the world's most innovative technology   
  
**About the Role**  
  
As our People Experience Operations Partner, you'll own and deliver key people operations activities across assigned international locations. You'll ensure employees and managers receive a seamless experience throughout the employee lifecycle, while balancing global consistency with local employment requirements.  
  
This role combines hands-on operational excellence with process improvement and strategic partnership.

### What You'll Do

* Deliver exceptional employee experiences across onboarding, employment changes and offboarding processes.
* Coordinate employment documentation, eligibility checks, systems access, payroll setup and benefits administration.
* Partner with managers and employees to provide timely, responsive and empathetic support.
* Maintain accurate employee data, HR systems and personnel records.
* Liaise with payroll providers, benefits partners and employers of record (EORs).
* Support performance review cycles, promotions, talent reviews and development programs.
* Coordinate international mobility activities, relocations, work authorisations and transfers.
* Contribute to compliance, reporting, audits and employment process governance.
* Drive process improvements, automation initiatives and scalable ways of working.
* Support international growth initiatives, including new-country and entity launches.

### What You'll Bring

**Required Experience**

* 5+ years' experience in People Operations, HR Operations, or a related HR function.
* Hands-on experience supporting employee lifecycle processes across multiple countries.
* Strong knowledge of onboarding, offboarding, employment documentation, payroll coordination, leave administration and benefits support.
* Experience maintaining HRIS records, analysing people data and driving operational excellence.
* Ability to work independently, manage competing priorities and collaborate across multiple time zones.
* Excellent communication skills and a strong customer-service mindset.
* Demonstrated success improving processes through automation, systems enhancements or continuous improvement initiatives.

**Preferred**

* Experience with UKG, Workday, Jira, or similar HR technology platforms.
* Experience working with international mobility, EOR providers, new-country expansions or entity transitions.
* Experience in a high-growth technology, engineering, aerospace or defence environment.
* Additional language capabilities.
