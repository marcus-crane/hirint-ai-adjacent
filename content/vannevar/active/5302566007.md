Vannevar builds AI systems for the Department of War's most consequential missions. We have 125 deployments across every branch and combatant command spanning our core platform and five distinct products. An an example of how we work, when major combat operations started with Iran, we fielded a new product supporting 24/7 operations and 9,000+ users in three months.  
  
How we build is our advantage: we deploy forward with the people who own the mission. Our engineers, product team, and CTO deploy forward to the point of friction, including visiting units in Ukraine. We focus on embedding directly with operational units supporting great power competition with China, combat operations with Iran, and counter-narcotics missions.

### About the role

We are looking for a Government Relations Director to work directly with our Senior Vice President for policy and strategy to expand Vannevar’s government relations effort.

As a Government Relations Director, you will develop legislative strategies that result in policies, authorities and long-term program funding that support the company’s strategic objectives. You will lead engagements with key legislative stakeholders on Capitol Hill, highlighting areas where Vannevar is leading in adoption of emerging national security technology. You’ll support our existing Department of Defense deployments and help us expand into new national security missions.

You will work closely with the mission team to understand our relationships with program offices and deployment goals. You’ll analyze budget justification books, congressional testimony and other relevant government documents to understand our customer’s requirements and resources to shape go to market strategies.

**U.S. Persons status with at least a current Top Secret US DoD or IC clearance is required.** **This is a customer-facing role required to be based in Washington D.C.**

### About you

You are passionate about building next generation capabilities for our public servants and have experience in navigating complex legislative processes to drive new technology adoption and program scaling within the Department of Defense and Intelligence Community. You have navigated Capitol Hill’s defense, and intelligence committees as a senior staffer and/or in an external government relations role, preferably with specific expertise in Appropriations. Successful candidates will have direct experience working for congressional Republicans – as staff in a Member’s office, on a committee staff, or in a Republican conference leadership role – and bring established relationships with Republican Members and staff on the Hill, Senate experience is especially desirable. You are motivated to work on a small team where you can have an outsized impact and drive significant growth at a national security startup by marrying up government relations to go-to market strategy through creative approaches, deep understanding of our technologies and their applications to strategic competition with nation states.

### What you’ll do

* **Represent Vannevar on Capitol Hill.** Work closely with our SVY for public policy and deployment leads to understand our strategic vision and maintain awareness of the mission wins we are developing for our US Government partners. Use this knowledge to communicate Vannevar’s technology, mission value and strategic vision to stakeholders on Capitol Hill and within the Department of Defense through presentations tied to their specific concerns and agendas.
* **Own specific legislative objectives.** Own Vannevar’s engagement with legislative stakeholders for multiple company objectives targeted at achieving discrete growth goals, while also folding into the company’s broader strategy.
* **Partner with Mission team.** Support Vannevar’s go-to market strategies through embedding government relations within our deployments from new footholds to category-defining new programs.

### What we’re looking for

* 5+ years navigating Capitol Hill’s defense committees and issues
* Prior experience working for congressional Republicans (e.g., as staff in a Republican Member’s office, on a Republican committee staff, or in a Republican Conference leadership role) is strongly preferred, as is Senate experience.
* Aptitude for working with internal and external stakeholders, from go-to market strategies and legislative agendas
* Proven ability to navigate complex legislative processes to achieve impact
* S. Citizenship status is required as this position will require the ability to access U.S. only data systems and export controlled data.
* Top Secret Clearance, or ability to quickly obtain one is required.
* Must be located in the Washington DC metro area.
