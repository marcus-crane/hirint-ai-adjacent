### About the role

We are looking for an IT Endpoint Engineer to own the technical health and continued development of our corporate endpoint environment across macOS, Windows, and iOS.

You’ll be responsible for the systems and automation that keep our device fleet secure, reliable, compliant, and manageable at scale, including MDM, patching, vulnerability remediation, configuration management, endpoint health monitoring, and device lifecycle management.

You’ll partner closely with Security to translate requirements such as CIS Benchmarks and NIST 800-171 controls into practical technical implementations. Where off-the-shelf management tooling falls short, you’ll build scripts and automation to deploy controls, detect configuration drift, remediate issues, and make endpoint state observable.

This is an engineering-oriented IT role. We’re looking for someone who can go deep on difficult endpoint problems, automate away repetitive administration, and improve the underlying systems rather than relying on manual processes.

### What you’ll do

* Own the management and continued development of our endpoint environment across macOS, Windows, and iOS.
* Partner with Security and IT to translate security and compliance requirements into practical endpoint controls and deployment plans.
* Own endpoint patching and vulnerability management, ensuring remediations occur within established SLAs.
* Develop scripts, automation, and tooling across macOS and Windows to deploy configurations, remediate issues, and reduce manual administration.
* Build recurring health checks and monitoring to identify configuration drift, missing controls, unhealthy devices, and gaps in management coverage.
* Own technical projects from planning through testing and deployment, accounting for dependencies, risk, and user impact.
* Own root-cause analysis and resolution of complex endpoint issues, using recurring problems as inputs into improvements to our tooling and architecture.
* Stay close to user-facing IT issues alongside the broader team so that endpoint engineering priorities reflect real problems across the company.

### What we’re looking for

* 5+ years of experience in endpoint engineering, systems administration, IT infrastructure, or similar technical roles.
* Strong technical knowledge of macOS and Windows administration, scripting, and troubleshooting in an environment with a distributed or remote user base.
* Hands-on experience administering endpoints through enterprise MDM platforms such as Microsoft Intune, including configuration profiles, application deployment, compliance policies, and device lifecycle management.
* Experience managing enterprise patching and vulnerability remediation within applicable remediation timelines.
* Strong scripting and automation skills, with experience using tools such as PowerShell, Bash, Python, or similar technologies to solve endpoint management problems.
* Demonstrated ability to independently investigate complex technical problems using logs, telemetry, command-line tools, scripting, and other diagnostic methods.
* Experience leading technical projects from planning through testing and deployment, with the ability to identify dependencies, risks, and potential user impact.
* Experience translating technical, security, or compliance requirements into practical solutions and determining an appropriate deployment path.
* Ability to work autonomously, make sound technical decisions, and manage competing priorities.
