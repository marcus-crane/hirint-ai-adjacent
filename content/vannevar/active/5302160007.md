### About the role

As an Information Systems Security Officer (ISSO), you will help get our products authorized and keep them authorized. Supporting our ISSM and government ISSMs, you will own day-to-day security and authorization work across multiple systems, spanning cloud (FedRAMP, DoD IL5) and classified (IL6, Secret, TS/SCI) systems. You will partner with engineering, AppSec, and DevOps to keep packages current, keep controls verifiable, and move our systems toward continuous ATO (cATO). This is a hands-on technical role that directly shapes how quickly our software reaches the mission.

**What you'll do**

* Build and maintain ATO and IATT packages (SSPs, FIPS 199 / CNSSI 1253 categorization, CRMs, POA&Ms) under NIST SP 800-37, NIST SP 800-53, FedRAMP, the DoD Cloud Computing SRG, and JSIG.
* Assess security controls across cloud and on-prem classified systems (IAM, logging, encryption, network segmentation) and document inheritance from cloud and hosting providers.
* Complete DISA STIG checklists and run vulnerability scans, then drive remediation with engineering.
* Support cATO and continuous monitoring (NIST SP 800-137): automate evidence collection, review changes for security impact, track POA&Ms, and keep eMASS and Xacta current.
* Respond to customer security questionnaires and coordinate with government customers and third-party assessors.
* Monitor deployed systems for unauthorized changes and suspicious activity, and support incident response, audits, and media sanitization.
* Maintain security policies and procedures aligned to customer and authorization requirements.

### What you should have

* Active TS/SCI clearance
* 4+ years in cybersecurity, including 2+ years as an ISSO, ISSE or in a similar authorization role on DoD, IC, or federal systems
* Hands-on ATO experience under NIST SP 800-37 and 800-53 in cloud and classified environments
* Working knowledge of cloud security (AWS, Azure, or GCP) and on-prem classified systems, plus familiarity with containers and microservices (e.g., Kubernetes) and Git-based change management (e.g., GitHub)
* Experience with eMASS or Xacta, DISA STIGs, and SCAP/SCC or ACAS scans
* DoD 8140 compliant certification (Security+ CE or higher)
* Clear, concise writer who can explain controls to engineers and authorizing officials

### Nice to have

* DoD IL5/IL6, FedRAMP High, or cATO experience
* AWS GovCloud and cloud security tooling such as Wiz, CrowdStrike, or Splunk
* Scripting in Python, Bash, or PowerShell
* CISSP, CCSP, or AWS Certified Security Specialty
