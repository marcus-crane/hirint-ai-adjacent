Vannevar builds AI systems for the Department of War's most consequential missions. We have 125 deployments across every branch and combatant command spanning our core platform and five distinct products. An an example of how we work, when major combat operations started with Iran, we fielded a new product supporting 24/7 operations and 9,000+ users in three months.  
  
How we build is our advantage: we deploy forward with the people who own the mission. Our engineers, product team, and CTO deploy forward to the point of friction, including visiting units in Ukraine. We focus on embedding directly with operational units supporting great power competition with China, combat operations with Iran, and counter-narcotics missions.

### About the role

We're looking for a Senior Accountant to own Vannevar's books and payments. You'll be our only in-house accountant on a small finance and business operations team. That makes you the person the company relies on to get the accounting right, support the broader team with AR and AP, and adapt our systems to continued business evolution and scaling.

This is a high-ownership role. You'll run the close, keep AP and day-to-day accounting moving, support billing and collections, and work with outside advisors on tax, compliance, and insurance. We recently migrated to an AI-native ERP, and we expect you to keep pushing what our tools (and AI) can do for us. As the business grows and changes, you'll take on projects that make our accounting and related activities faster, more automated, and ready for what comes next.

### What you’ll do

**Own the close and the books** — You'll run our monthly, quarterly, and annual closes and make sure our financial statements are right. You catch errors before anyone else does, with the most attention on what matters most to the business (like revenue recognition) and the biggest dollar amounts. We're aiming to close within 7 business days, and you'll help us get there and stay there.

**Be our accounting expert** — When something new comes up (a new contract structure, product cost structure, an unusual transaction), you'll research the right treatment, form a point of view, and apply it. You know when to bring in outside advisors, and you come to those conversations with a perspective, not just questions.

**Keep AP and day-to-day accounting running smoothly** — You'll own our AP queues so nothing slips through the cracks, and move vendors and employees onto automated and self-service workflows (in modern AP, travel, and ERP tools) so routine work takes less of everyone's time.

**Make our finance function better, one project at a time** — Beyond the recurring work, you'll take on projects that change how we operate: automating a manual workflow, putting a new tool or AI capability to work in our close, or designing the accounting and cost tracking for a new line of business before it scales. We'll scope these together; you'll own them end to end.

**Support billing and collections** — You'll support the people responsible for invoicing and collections: helping make sure customers are billed on time, keeping AR current, and flagging overdue balances early. Much of this means reading U.S. Government contracts to understand billing terms and obligations.

**Tax, compliance, insurance, and vendors** — We're a fast-growing company with a deliberately lean internal team, and important work doesn't always fit a neat category. You'll support tax and compliance work with our external tax accountants, help manage our insurance, and own relationships with key vendors, especially for travel.

### What we’re looking for

**Essential**

* CPA, with 5–7+ years of accounting experience
* **Ownership** — You own the outcome and the process behind it, not just the steps. When a process isn't working, you fix it rather than work around it, and when something is at risk, you raise it before anyone has to ask.
* **Accuracy where it matters** — You know which numbers are most important to get right, and you design systems and checks that keep them right without lots of manual effort. Big-picture business sense is a real plus, but it doesn't substitute for getting the details right.
* **Embraces new tools and ways of working** — You pick up new systems quickly, and you're always looking for ways automation and AI can make the work faster and more reliable.
* **Integrity and mission** — You hold yourself to a high standard of integrity, and you care about the mission we serve.

**Big plus**

* Experience in defense / defense tech, or at a software company with federal government sales. Hands-on experience with government systems like Procurement Integrated Enterprise Environment.
* Hands-on experience with a modern ERP, or a track record of learning new systems quickly and adapting them creatively to the business
* Public accounting experience
