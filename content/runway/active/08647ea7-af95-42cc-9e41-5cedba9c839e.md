We are building AI to simulate the world through merging art and science.  
  
We believe that world models are at the frontier of progress in artificial intelligence. Language models alone won’t solve the world’s hardest problems – robotics, disease, scientific discovery. Real progress requires models that experience the world and learn from their mistakes, the same way that humans do. And this kind of trial and error can be massively accelerated when done in simulation, rather than in the real world.  
  
World models offer the most clear path to general-purpose simulation, changing how stories are told, how scientific progress is made and how the next frontiers of humanity are reached.

Our team consists of creative, open minded, caring and ambitious people who are determined to change the world. We aspire to continuously build impossible things and our ability to do so relies on building an incredible team. If you are driven to do the same, we'd love to hear from you.

## **About the role**

We are looking for a Motion Designer to join Runway Labs and help visualize the next applications of our AI research. Working closely with designers and prototype researchers, you’ll explore how emerging capabilities could become new tools, interfaces, and experiences.

This role combines motion design, interaction thinking, and experimentation. You should be able to take an early idea and use movement to explain how it works, what it feels like to use, and why it matters. Your work will help the team explore possibilities, make design decisions, and communicate a vision for future products.

You’ll collaborate with researchers, engineers, designers, and prototype researchers to imagine experiences around AI video, world models, and agentic systems. Through motion studies, interface explorations, and concept demos, you’ll make ideas concrete while helping shape their behavior and visual expression.

We’re looking for someone with strong design foundations, excellent motion craft, and the curiosity to explore experiences that don’t yet have established conventions.

## **What you’ll do**

* Partner with designers and prototype researchers to imagine and visualize new applications of Runway’s AI research.
* Create motion studies and concept demos that show how future interfaces and experiences could work.
* Explore how people interact with AI systems, using motion to express actions, feedback, transitions, and changes in state.
* Translate early research capabilities and open-ended ideas into clear visual scenarios and interaction sequences.
* Work alongside prototype researchers to develop concepts, inform working prototypes, and refine the details of an experience.
* Experiment with multiple directions, using quick studies to test assumptions and help the team decide what to pursue.
* Develop a motion language that gives new experiences a coherent sense of timing, behavior, and character.
* Present explorations clearly, explaining the design choices behind them and the possibilities they reveal.

## **What you’ll need**

* A portfolio demonstrating strong motion craft, visual design, and the ability to communicate how an interface or experience works.
* Strong foundations in timing, typography, composition, and visual hierarchy.
* Experience collaborating with product designers, creative technologists, or prototyping teams to develop ideas.
* The ability to move between rough explorations and detailed motion studies, choosing the right level of fidelity for each stage.
* Fluency in motion design tools and familiarity with interface design and prototyping workflows.
* An understanding of how motion helps people follow an interaction, understand system behavior, and navigate an experience.
* Curiosity about AI video, world models, agentic systems, and the applications they could enable.
* Comfort with ambiguity, an independent approach to exploration, and openness to changing direction as the team learns.

\* Experience with interactive prototyping, creative coding, 3D, or real-time graphics is welcome.
