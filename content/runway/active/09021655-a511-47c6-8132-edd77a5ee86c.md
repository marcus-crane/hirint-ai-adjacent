We are building AI to simulate the world through merging art and science.  
  
We believe that world models are at the frontier of progress in artificial intelligence. Language models alone won’t solve the world’s hardest problems – robotics, disease, scientific discovery. Real progress requires models that experience the world and learn from their mistakes, the same way that humans do. And this kind of trial and error can be massively accelerated when done in simulation, rather than in the real world.  
  
World models offer the most clear path to general-purpose simulation, changing how stories are told, how scientific progress is made and how the next frontiers of humanity are reached.

Our team consists of creative, open minded, caring and ambitious people who are determined to change the world. We aspire to continuously build impossible things and our ability to do so relies on building an incredible team. If you are driven to do the same, we'd love to hear from you.

### About the role

We’re looking for a Senior HR Generalist to own the day-to-day of Runway’s HR function and shape how it scales. You’ll run core programs end to end, advise managers and employees on sensitive situations, unblock teams so they can move faster and build the processes that keep a fast-growing team compliant and well supported. You’ll work directly with the HR Manager as a strategic partner, and you’ll have the autonomy to make decisions, set priorities and fix what’s broken without waiting to be asked.

### What you’ll do

* Own the employee lifecycle from onboarding through offboarding for full-time employees and contractors, and keep improving the process as the team grows
* Own HRIS administration and data integrity in Rippling, including reporting, audits and workflow automation
* Manage employee visa and immigration processes in partnership with outside counsel
* Oversee leave administration and accommodations across jurisdictions, and keep policies and practices compliant with current employment law
* Run key programs end to end with the HR Manager, including performance reviews, open enrollment, engagement surveys and compensation cycles
* Act as the go-to resource for employees and managers on benefits, policies and HR processes
* Identify gaps in HR operations and ship fixes: updated policies, clearer documentation and more efficient tooling
* Pitch in wherever the team needs; no task is too small if it moves things forward

### What you’ll need

* 5+ years of experience in an HR Generalist or people operations role at a high-growth company
* Deep working knowledge of benefits, leave administration, immigration basics and employment law across multiple states
* Experience running company-wide programs such as performance cycles, open enrollment and engagement surveys
* Strong HRIS skills, with Rippling experience a strong plus
* Clear written and verbal communication, and the credibility to advise managers and senior leaders
* Comfort operating independently in a fast-paced, ambiguous environment
* Exceptional organization and attention to detail
* A high ownership mindset and a team-first attitude
