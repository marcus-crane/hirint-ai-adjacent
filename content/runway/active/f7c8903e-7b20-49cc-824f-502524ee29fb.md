We are building AI to simulate the world through merging art and science.  
  
We believe that world models are at the frontier of progress in artificial intelligence. Language models alone won’t solve the world’s hardest problems – robotics, disease, scientific discovery. Real progress requires models that experience the world and learn from their mistakes, the same way that humans do. And this kind of trial and error can be massively accelerated when done in simulation, rather than in the real world.  
  
World models offer the most clear path to general-purpose simulation, changing how stories are told, how scientific progress is made and how the next frontiers of humanity are reached.

Our team consists of creative, open minded, caring and ambitious people who are determined to change the world. We aspire to continuously build impossible things and our ability to do so relies on building an incredible team. If you are driven to do the same, we'd love to hear from you.

## **About the Role**

We're hiring a **Social Media Specialist, Influencer Marketing** to run Runway's paid influencer and affiliate programs. You'll scout and pitch creators (primarily on YouTube), negotiate flat-fee paid briefs and performance-based affiliate deals, and own each relationship end to end, from first outreach through contract and payment. This is not a content production role.

The ideal candidate is highly online, organized, analytical, and action-oriented. You understand how content moves across platforms, can identify emerging trends early, know how to spot relevant creators and influencers and facilitate relationships with them, and can translate social insights into clear recommendations for the marketing and creative team.

## **What You’ll Do**

* Scout and identify creators, primarily on YouTube, relevant to Runway's paid influencer and affiliate programs
* Pitch creators on both programs: flat-fee paid briefs for influencers, and signup/performance-based deals for affiliates
* Own outreach, negotiation, contracts, and paperwork for each creator relationship, end-to-end
* Partner with outside agencies running outreach at scale to keep the creator pipeline full
* Stay immersed in YouTube and the broader AI/creative tools creator landscape, tracking trends and surfacing who's worth pursuing next
* Share what's resonating with creators and audiences with the creative team to help inform organic content direction
* Partner with the growth marketing team on attribution and reporting to track program performance and ROI
* Coordinate with the creative team on briefs given to paid influencers

## **What We’re Looking For**

* At least 2 years of experience in influencer marketing, affiliate marketing, social/creator partnerships, or a related role
* An existing network of creator relationships, ideally with strength on YouTube
* Comfortable negotiating fees and contracts directly with creators
* Strong understanding of how content performs on YouTube specifically, plus a broader read on social and creator trends
* Highly organized, able to manage a high volume of relationships, deals, and paperwork at once
* Polished, confident, low-ego, and able to represent Runway well in creator conversations
* Interest in AI, creative tools, video, design, filmmaking, advertising, and the broader creator economy

## **Nice to Have**

* Experience running affiliate programs specifically, not just influencer marketing
* Experience working with influencers or outreach agencies
* Experience working at a creative, consumer, prosumer, AI, design, or technology company
* Familiarity with Runway and the broader AI creative ecosystem
