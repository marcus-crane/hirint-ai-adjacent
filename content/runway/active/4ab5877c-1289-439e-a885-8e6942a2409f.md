We are building AI to simulate the world through merging art and science.  
  
We believe that world models are at the frontier of progress in artificial intelligence. Language models alone won’t solve the world’s hardest problems – robotics, disease, scientific discovery. Real progress requires models that experience the world and learn from their mistakes, the same way that humans do. And this kind of trial and error can be massively accelerated when done in simulation, rather than in the real world.  
  
World models offer the most clear path to general-purpose simulation, changing how stories are told, how scientific progress is made and how the next frontiers of humanity are reached.

Our team consists of creative, open minded, caring and ambitious people who are determined to change the world. We aspire to continuously build impossible things and our ability to do so relies on building an incredible team. If you are driven to do the same, we'd love to hear from you.

## About the role

\**Open to hiring remote across North America — we also have offices in NYC, London, San Francisco, Paris and Seattle.*

We’re looking for a Creative with a focus on campaigns for Product Marketing. This is not a growth role. It’s a hands-on campaign creative role for someone who makes the work themselves, with AI as their primary medium. You’ll own full-funnel launch campaigns for Runway’s products, features and research: launch films, key visuals, landing pages, lifecycle emails, onboarding flows and everything in between. You’ll report to the Creative Director of Product Marketing.

We’re open to creatives who lead with art direction or with copy. What matters is that you make the work yourself and can move across both. Your work will directly shape how people discover, understand and adopt what Runway builds. That means work with real taste that stops people mid-scroll, makes new research feel tangible, and turns curiosity into first use. You’ll also help build the workflows and automations that let a small team ship at the pace of the product. You’ll partner closely with product marketing, design, research, growth and product teams.

## What you’ll do

* Build full-funnel launch campaigns end to end: the idea, the script, the boards, the generation, the edit, the finish.
* Make launch films, key visuals and campaign systems that hold up next to the best brand work in the world.
* Turn research breakthroughs into work people outside the field can feel, not just read about.
* Extend each campaign across every surface (landing pages, social, email, in-product) without losing the idea.
* Work at the pace of the product: several launches at once, fast timelines, quality never optional.
* Build the machine behind the work. Turn what you make into reusable prompts, workflows and automated pipelines, so every campaign starts further ahead than the last.
* Treat every new model as new creative territory, and show the team what’s now possible before anyone asks.

## What you’ll need

* 4+ years making creative at an agency, studio or brand, with real campaigns shipped for real clients.
* A portfolio that proves taste first. AI-generated or AI-assisted work should be central to it.
* Fluency with image and video models, plus the craft tools to finish (editing, compositing, design).
* Art direction instincts: casting, styling, light, composition, type and pacing.
* A strong lead discipline (art direction, motion/edit or copy) and comfort across the others.
* A builder’s mindset. You’ve chained tools together, automated the repetitive parts, or built workflows others use.
* The range to move from a cinematic launch film to a single perfect frame in the same week.

## Bonus points

* Experience launching creative tools, AI products or research.
* You’ve already built something interesting on Runway.
