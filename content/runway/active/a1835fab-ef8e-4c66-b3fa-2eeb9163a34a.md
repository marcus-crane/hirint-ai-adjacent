We are building AI to simulate the world through merging art and science.  
  
We believe that world models are at the frontier of progress in artificial intelligence. Language models alone won’t solve the world’s hardest problems – robotics, disease, scientific discovery. Real progress requires models that experience the world and learn from their mistakes, the same way that humans do. And this kind of trial and error can be massively accelerated when done in simulation, rather than in the real world.  
  
World models offer the most clear path to general-purpose simulation, changing how stories are told, how scientific progress is made and how the next frontiers of humanity are reached.

Our team consists of creative, open minded, caring and ambitious people who are determined to change the world. We aspire to continuously build impossible things and our ability to do so relies on building an incredible team. If you are driven to do the same, we'd love to hear from you.

### About the Role

We’re looking for a Senior/Staff HR Business Partner to work directly with Runway’s leaders and teams on how they grow, organize and perform. You’ll advise managers and executives on people decisions, lead org design and talent programs, unblock teams so they can move faster and turn what you see across the business into HR priorities.

### What You’ll Do

* Partner with leaders on org design, headcount planning, team structure and leveling
* Coach managers and executives on performance, feedback, career development and difficult conversations
* Own complex employee relations cases from intake through resolution, including investigations, in partnership with Legal where needed
* Work with Compensation and Talent Acquisition on offers, promotions and market adjustments
* Use headcount, attrition, engagement and performance data to spot risks early and recommend action
* Design and roll out programs that fit how Runway’s teams work, from manager training to engagement initiatives
* Step in wherever the team needs it

### What You’ll Need

* 7+ years of experience in HR, including at least four as an HR Business Partner supporting fast growing teams
* Experience at a high-growth company, ideally through periods of rapid headcount growth
* A record of advising senior leaders and earning their trust
* Strong employee relations experience, with sound judgment and discretion
* Working knowledge of employment law, compensation practices and performance management frameworks
* Clear written and verbal communication
* Comfort operating independently in a fast-paced, ambiguous environment
* A high ownership mindset and a team-first attitude
