Skydio is the leading US drone company and the world leader in autonomous flight, the key technology for the future of drones and aerial mobility. The Skydio team combines deep expertise in artificial intelligence, best-in-class hardware and software product development, operational excellence, and customer obsession to empower a broader, more diverse audience of drone users, from [utility inspectors](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/energy-and-utilities) to [first responders](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/public-safety), [soldiers in battlefield scenarios](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/national-security/tactical-isr), and [beyond](https://www.skydio.com/solutions).

**About the Team:**

Recruiting Operations at Skydio aims to bring operational excellence and support to the Recruiting team and cross-functional stakeholders, enabling them to hire efficiently and effectively, while creating a delightful and personal experience for our candidates and hiring teams.

**About the Role:**

We are looking for an energetic and organized Recruiting Coordinator to work onsite at our HQ located in San Mateo, CA. This is a perfect role for a recent graduate or someone looking to launch their career in People Operations. Your primary responsibility will be to serve as the "Face of Skydio" for our candidates—managing their onsite experience, scheduling their interviews, and ensuring they feel welcomed. You will learn the ins and outs of recruiting tools like Ashby and Workday while supporting university recruiting events and intern programs.

**How You’ll Make an Impact:**

* Coordinate and schedule phone, video, and onsite interviews for candidates across various departments
* Greet candidates onsite at our HQ, providing office tours and ensuring a warm, welcoming environment
* Collaborate with the University Recruiting team to help coordinate campus events, career fairs, and intern onboarding
* Maintain accurate records in our Applicant Tracking System (Ashby) to ensure no candidate falls through the cracks
* Assist the broader People team with ad-hoc projects related to employer branding, swag management, and event planning
* Learn to identify scheduling conflicts and proactively solve puzzles to keep the hiring process moving fast

**What Makes You a Good Fit:**

* Ability to be in-person at our San Mateo HQ 4-5 days per week
* 0-1+ years of experience (internships, campus leadership, event planning, student clubs, Resident Advisors (RAs) or customer service experience is a plus!)
* You are hyper-organized with a strong attention to detail—you love a checked-off to-do list
* You have a "customer obsession" mindset and genuinely enjoy making people feel comfortable and valued
* You are tech-savvy and eager to learn new software platforms (Ashby, G-Suite, Workday) quickly
* You are a clear communicator who isn't afraid to ask questions when you hit a roadblock
* You are excited to start your career at a fast-paced, high-tech company building the future of flight
