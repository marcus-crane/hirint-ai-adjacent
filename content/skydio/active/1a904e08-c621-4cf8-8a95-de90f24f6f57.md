Skydio is the leading US drone company and the world leader in autonomous flight, the key technology for the future of drones and aerial mobility. The Skydio team combines deep expertise in artificial intelligence, best-in-class hardware and software product development, operational excellence, and customer obsession to empower a broader, more diverse audience of drone users, from [utility inspectors](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/energy-and-utilities) to [first responders](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/public-safety), [soldiers in battlefield scenarios](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/national-security/tactical-isr), and [beyond](https://www.skydio.com/solutions).

**About the role:**

We build flying robots that give people superpowers, so our customers can solve problems faster, cheaper and more safely. As the Staff Product Manager for Construction, you will spearhead Skydio’s work to revolutionize the way construction companies track and understand their sites. You will own the strategy and execution as we double down on supporting our existing construction customers, while dramatically expanding the scale and scope of what we can do for this industry by developing construction-specific products.

Your work will build on a world-leading autonomous flight technology foundation that is already operating at scale across public safety, energy, transportation, and every branch of the military.

This in an in-office role: 5 days/week at our HQ in San Mateo, CA.

**How you'll make an impact:**

* Define Skydio's strategy for the construction market. Lead market research and discovery to understand construction end users and economic buyers: how they work, how they buy and where Skydio can deliver the most value.
* Own the product roadmap from first customer problem to launch and growth. Work closely with a small, senior engineering team, build prototypes yourself with AI tools, put them in front of customers and iterate quickly.
* Spend real time on job sites with superintendents, construction teams and project executives, and turn what you learn into products they can't work without.
* Shape how we go to market. Work with our GTM teams on positioning, pricing and packaging, and join account reps in the field to close early deals.
* Build partnerships with the platforms construction teams already use, so Skydio data flows into their workflows and solves customer problems end to end.
* Help build a new business line for Skydio, with direct visibility to company leadership.

**What makes you a good fit:**

* You’ve taken new software products from an initial customer problem through launch and growth, and owned decisions about both the product and how to sell it.
* You want to spend time with customers. You ask good questions to understand their problems, how they work, what tools they need to integrate with and what makes a product worth paying for.
* You make hard product calls with imperfect information and give a small team clear priorities.
* You're AI native. You use AI every day to build working prototypes and test ideas. You can get into technical details with engineers and make sound calls about what to build and what to reuse.
* You're scrappy. You get a lot done with a small team and know how to pull in the right people to make things happen.
* You're an excellent communicator. You write clearly, speak plainly and sweat the details.
* Construction or construction technology experience is strongly preferred.
* Experience with drones, robotics, remote sensing, GIS, photogrammetry or reality capture is a plus but not required.
* Obtaining FAA Part 107 certification within the first 60 days of employment is strongly encouraged for all Skydio employees and required for certain positions.
