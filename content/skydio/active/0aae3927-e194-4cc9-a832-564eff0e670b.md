Skydio is the leading US drone company and the world leader in autonomous flight, the key technology for the future of drones and aerial mobility. The Skydio team combines deep expertise in artificial intelligence, best-in-class hardware and software product development, operational excellence, and customer obsession to empower a broader, more diverse audience of drone users, from [utility inspectors](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/energy-and-utilities) to [first responders](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/public-safety), [soldiers in battlefield scenarios](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/national-security/tactical-isr), and [beyond](https://www.skydio.com/solutions).

**About the role:**

We are seeking an enthusiastic and detail-oriented test engineering technician to help support hardware-focused validation testing of intelligent aerial vehicles and ground equipment within a team dedicated to maximizing product reliability for our customers.  
  
**How you'll make an impact:**

* Support test setup, instrumentation, execution, checkpoint and tear down inspections for a variety of performance, reliability and environmental stress tests for our flying and ground based hardware using a broad range of lab equipment
* Execute hands-on maintenance of test lab infrastructure and DUT’s
* Work closely with test engineers to understand the item under test, execution procedures, data requirements, and potential failure modes of each test to ensure the earliest possible detection of issues
* Develop and fabricate custom test fixtures and infrastructure to add capability, improve repeatability, and save time through automation
* Document and escalate test failures quickly; collaborate with reliability, mechanical, electrical, and software engineering teams to perform failure analysis and ensure the product continuously improves through all stages of development
* Optimize execution efficiency with preventative maintenance on equipment and helping to ensure a high standard for safety and organization within the lab

**What makes you a good fit:**

* 3+ years relevant experience
* Acute attention to detail - ability to identify subtle degradations in mechanical feel or system performance that may be precursors to test failures
* Proactive - you anticipate needs, identify areas to improve efficiency and work towards these goals without explicit prompting to support the team
* Curious - you’re driven to understand the nuances of complex systems and are always interested in learning new skills
* Hands-on and fabrication technical skills and experience with wire harnesses, 80/20, light machining and/or woodworking (or a knack and an eagerness to learn)
* Creative problem-solving - you’re able to think outside the box to develop innovative solutions for test challenges, fixture designs, and automation improvements
* Solid computer skills including documentation, web research, and executing linux commands and python scripts
* Excellent written & verbal communication
* Obtaining FAA Part 107 certification within the first 60 days of employment is strongly encouraged for all Skydio employees and required for certain positions.
