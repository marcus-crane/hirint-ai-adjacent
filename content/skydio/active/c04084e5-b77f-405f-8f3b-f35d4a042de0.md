Skydio is the leading US drone company and the world leader in autonomous flight, the key technology for the future of drones and aerial mobility. The Skydio team combines deep expertise in artificial intelligence, best-in-class hardware and software product development, operational excellence, and customer obsession to empower a broader, more diverse audience of drone users, from [utility inspectors](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/energy-and-utilities) to [first responders](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/public-safety), [soldiers in battlefield scenarios](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/national-security/tactical-isr), and [beyond](https://www.skydio.com/solutions).

**Our National Security Vision:**

Our vision for national security customers rests on two tenets: help build the domestic industrial might to deter conflict and win a robotic war of attrition if necessary; and apply the best of our dual-use technology foundation to equip our soldiers with world-leading capability.

**About the Role:**

We are hiring an Industry Marketing leader for our National Security business. This person is accountable within Marketing for making Skydio **known, trusted, and chosen** with the Department of War (DoW), key civilian agencies and our military allies around the world. You will own marketing strategy, messaging, product launches, and go-to-market enablement for the global national security business. This role is based on our headquarters at San Mateo and has high visibility with the executive leadership team

**How you’ll make an impact:**

* Own the national security marketing strategy, plan, and messaging.
* Work closely with Marketing partners to lead an integrated program across brand, product marketing, field, and events.
* Craft the messaging and positioning that translates Skydio’s autonomy, products, and industrial capability into mission value for DoW and other federal customers
* Lead go-to-market for new product launches, ensuring Sales, Communications, Policy, and product and channel partners are fully enabled.
* Equip Sales, Mission Success, and other internal customer champions with sales tools, competitive positioning, and content that articulate Skydio’s differentiation.
* Build content grounded in the different customer vertical’s terminology, while maintaining political and regulatory saviness
* Create product experiences that bring Skydio solutions to life for current and future customers.

**What makes you a good fit:**

* 8-10 years in B2B or B2G product or industry marketing with proven success launching products with measurable pipeline impact.
* Exceptional writer with comfort rolling up your sleeves and building from 0-1.
* Ideally fluent in or willing to learn defense and federal procurement, colors of money and budgeting, and credibility from senior government officials down to frontline drone operators.
* Required military service or significant hands-on experience working with defense organizations. You must be proficient in how military organizations evaluate and procure technology.
* This position may require access to export-controlled technology, technical data, and/or controlled information, including information subject to the International Traffic in Arms Regulations (ITAR), Export Administration Regulations (EAR), and/or U.S. government contract requirements. Candidates must be eligible to access such information without additional U.S. government authorization, unless the company determines that any required authorization can be obtained
