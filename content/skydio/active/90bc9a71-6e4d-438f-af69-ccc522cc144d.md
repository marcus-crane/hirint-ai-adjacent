Skydio is the leading US drone company and the world leader in autonomous flight, the key technology for the future of drones and aerial mobility. The Skydio team combines deep expertise in artificial intelligence, best-in-class hardware and software product development, operational excellence, and customer obsession to empower a broader, more diverse audience of drone users, from [utility inspectors](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/energy-and-utilities) to [first responders](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/public-safety), [soldiers in battlefield scenarios](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/national-security/tactical-isr), and [beyond](https://www.skydio.com/solutions).

## **About the Role**

Skydio is seeking an experienced physical security professional to join our team as Solutions Engineer – Commercial Site Security. Commercial Solutions Engineering is the technical arm of Skydio's commercial go-to-market motion. We sit between the customer and the product: translating industrial and security problems into deployable programs, proving capability in the field, and making sure Skydio's products are integrated and flown at scale across sites that were never designed with drones in mind.

Coverage is tiered by customer size rather than by territory, so the team is measured on technical win rate, trial and proof of concept outcomes, program expansion inside existing accounts, and the requirements we push back into the product organization.

Solutions Engineers are critical members of the Skydio organization and work closely with Account Executives, Engineering, and Product to support deal progression, customer confidence, successful deployments, and the long-term success of commercial sUAS programs. Internally you are, in every sense, the voice of the customer.

This is a remote role. Travel of approximately 50 to 75% is required to support customer engagements, field activities, deployments, team onsites, and company events.

This role reports to Senior Manager, Commercial – Site Security.

## **How You'll Make an Impact**

* Collaborate with enterprise security customers to understand their security operations and develop tailored drone solutions as a trusted expert and an integral member of the account team.
* Deliver Security Command demonstrations tied to customer scenarios while explaining support capabilities, operating limitations, and features accurately.
* Work with the customer to design dock coverage models, autonomous patrol missions, detection zones, and customer-operated, managed-service, or hybrid workflows specific to their requirements.
* Own trial execution from start to finish including baselines, measurable success criteria, scope, dates, while coordinating resources and support to maintain the scorecard.
* Validate supported VMS, intrusion detection, and access-control workflows with enterprise customer IT and integration partners.
* Translate field observations into prioritized feedback for Product and Engineering, connecting reproducible issues and workflow gaps to customer impact.
* Own site assessment quality for accounts. Solutions Engineers are the responsible individual for verifying dock placement prior to deployment along with power and networking requirements.
* Coordinate operating approvals and BVLOS, multi-drone, and other waiver requirements for the airspace and operations your customer falls under.

## **What Makes You a Good Fit**

* 5+ years of relevant experience in commercial security operations, security systems integration, or customer-facing technical roles serving enterprise security teams.
* Practical understanding of perimeter protection, alarm verification, SOC/GSOC workflows, guard operations, and incident response.
* Experience deploying and supporting physical security technology, with the ability to troubleshoot hardware, connectivity, video, and configuration issues.
* Strong understanding of drone technology, sUAS operations, and regulatory compliance.
* Excellent problem solving skills and the ability to think critically in high-pressure situations.
* Exceptional written and verbal communication with operators, technical stakeholders, security executives, including candid conversations about limitations.
* Ability to manage concurrent opportunities within a Sales organization, prioritize technical effort, and coordinate customer and partner commitments.
* Lead by example. Willing to do whatever is needed to get a customer situation resolved.

## **Nice to Have**

* Familiarity with Dock based drone security operations, dock based remote operations, or counter-UAS programs
* Hands-on experience with enterprise VMS platforms such as Fusus, Genetec, or Milestone and alarm or access-control integrations.
* Experience with security analytics, thermal imagery, API’s, or event-drive integrations.
* Familiarity with technical limitations of detection systems and the regulatory limits on counter-drone mitigation.
* Experience deploying security programs across multiple facilities or working with managed security and drone service providers.

## **What Is Required**

* FAA Part 107 Certificate or obtained within 60 days of hire.
* Ability to travel 50% to 75% of the time.
* Ability to lift up to 60 lbs unaided.
