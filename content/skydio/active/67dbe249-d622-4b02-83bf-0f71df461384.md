## **About the role**

We are seeking an enthusiastic and detail-oriented Battery Test Technician to support the validation and reliability testing of lithium-ion battery cells and packs. You will play a critical role in our development cycle, ensuring our energy storage systems meet rigorous performance, safety, and reliability standards for our family of flying robots.

***Please Note:*** This position requires a regular onsite presence (5 days per week) at our headquarters in San Mateo, California.

## **How you'll make an impact:**

* Execute battery testing protocols, including thermal characterization, rate capability, pulse testing, cycle aging, and calendar aging, across a wide range of temperature and environmental conditions.
* Operate and maintain specialized battery test equipment, including battery cyclers, environmental chambers, and data acquisition systems.
* Fabricate custom test fixtures, wiring harnesses, and battery assemblies to support validation efforts.
* Perform high-stakes safety and abuse testing, such as crush, short-circuit, and thermal excursion tests, adhering to strict safety protocols.
* Monitor and document battery performance indicators, including voltage, current, temperature, cell swelling, and capacity retention.
* Collaborate with engineering teams to characterize failure modes, escalate test anomalies, and continuously improve our battery test infrastructure and data collection processes.
* Maintain a high standard for lab organization, safety, and preventative maintenance for all test equipment.

## **What makes you a good fit:**

* Hands-on experience with battery testing, lab instrumentation, and data acquisition systems.
* Familiarity with lithium-ion battery chemistry, safety protocols, and testing standards (e.g., UN38.3, IEC 62133).
* Acute attention to detail—ability to identify subtle anomalies during complex test sequences and provide clear, actionable documentation.
* Proactive mindset: you anticipate needs, identify efficiency improvements, and work toward goals with minimal oversight.
* Experience with fabrication techniques (e.g., wiring, soldering, light machining) and test fixture design.
* Solid computer skills, including the ability to execute Python scripts, manage data, and perform documentation.
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with a collaborative approach to solving complex engineering challenges.
