**About the role:**

The Environmental Health & Safety (EHS) Specialist is a critical member of Skydio's expanding EHS organization. You will support Headquarters, R&D, Field Operations, and deployment activities while helping build the EHS programs, systems, and operating structure these functions need as they scale. Unlike traditional site-based safety roles, this position operates across office and laboratory spaces, engineering and R&D areas, field deployment locations, customer sites, public-safety facilities, rooftops, and other non-traditional work environments. You'll work closely with EHS Leadership, Field Operations, Deployment Engineering, R&D, Facilities, Training, and business leaders to identify hazards, implement controls, support regulatory compliance, investigate incidents, deliver training, and establish scalable EHS processes. A major part of this role is building EHS infrastructure for business areas that have not historically had dedicated EHS support.

**Location:**

This is an office based position at our HQ in San Mateo, CA. Travel is required based on operational need and may include customer deployment locations, public-safety facilities, rooftop installations, remote field locations, other Skydio sites, and incident-investigation, training, and audit locations.

**How you'll make an impact:**

• Support development and implementation of EHS programs across Headquarters, R&D, Field Operations, and deployment activities.

• Partner with EHS Leadership to build new divisional safety programs, procedures, training, inspection systems, risk assessments, and operational controls.

• Conduct field and site safety assessments for deployments, installations, customer locations, and remote operations.

• Perform Job Hazard Analyses (JHAs), pre-task risk assessments, and site-specific risk assessments, and recommend engineering, administrative, PPE, and procedural controls.

• Support safe work practices for rooftop work, work at height, ladder use, electrical activities, batteries, environmental exposure, and public/customer-site operations, including roof access, roof hatches, skylights, roof edges, load limitations, exclusion zones, and competency requirements for elevated work.

• Establish weather-related work restrictions for wind, rain, lightning, ice, extreme heat, and other field conditions.

• Investigate injuries, incidents, near misses, property damage, and significant hazards; identify root causes, establish corrective actions, assign owners, and track actions through closure.

• Develop and deliver EHS training to employees, managers, field teams, engineers, and other affected personnel.

• Support contractor and vendor safety programs, including pre-task risk assessment and contractor qualification.

• Maintain EHS documentation, training records, inspection records, incident records, and corrective-action tracking, and support emergency preparedness and response planning.

• Support EHS audits, inspections, regulatory readiness, PPE management, and continuous-improvement initiatives; collect and analyze EHS performance data to support dashboards, trends, and reporting.

• Maintain a visible, collaborative EHS presence across supported business groups and sites, partnering with leaders and employees to identify unsafe conditions and improve safety culture.

• Participate in new process, equipment, facility, deployment, and change-management reviews, and support EHS Leadership in establishing scalable systems that apply consistently across domestic and, as applicable, international operations.

**What makes you a good fit:**

• 3+ years of progressive EHS specialist-level experience.

• Degree in Environmental Health & Safety, Occupational Safety, Environmental Science, Industrial Hygiene, or a related field preferred; equivalent relevant experience considered.

• Working knowledge of OSHA general-industry requirements, and familiarity with fall protection, ladder safety, PPE, Hazard Communication, electrical safety, LOTO, emergency preparedness, ergonomics, and incident investigation.

• Experience conducting JHAs, risk assessments, inspections, and incident investigations.

• Experience supporting field operations, construction, installations, engineering, R&D, manufacturing, or facilities environments.

• Ability to interpret regulations and translate requirements into practical operational controls.

• Strong written and verbal communication skills, strong organizational skills, and the ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously.

• Ability to work independently while maintaining close partnership with operational teams.

• Comfortable operating in fast-changing environments where programs and processes are still being developed.

• Comfortable working in both traditional office environments and active field environments that may involve weather exposure, ladders, rooftops, powered equipment, batteries, electrical systems, construction-type activities, and interaction with customers or contractors.

• Ability to travel as business needs require, climb ladders and stairs, access rooftops, walk field sites, and respond to EHS incidents and urgent operational needs.

**Preferred Qualifications:**

• OSHA 30-Hour General Industry or Construction, First Aid/CPR/AED certification or Trainer credentials, and Fall Protection Competent Person training

• Board certification (ASP, CSP, CHST, OHST, or similar)

• Experience with ISO 45001/14001 or other management-system frameworks, VelocityEHS or another EHS management system, and developing safety programs from the ground up

• Experience supporting public-safety, drone/UAS, aviation, construction, deployment, telecommunications, or rooftop work
