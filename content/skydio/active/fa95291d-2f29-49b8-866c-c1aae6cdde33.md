Skydio is the leading US drone company and the world leader in autonomous flight, the key technology for the future of drones and aerial mobility. The Skydio team combines deep expertise in artificial intelligence, best-in-class hardware and software product development, operational excellence, and customer obsession to empower a broader, more diverse audience of drone users, from [utility inspectors](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/energy-and-utilities) to [first responders](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/public-safety), [soldiers in battlefield scenarios](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/national-security/tactical-isr), and [beyond](https://www.skydio.com/solutions).

## **About the role**

We are looking for a driven, results-oriented Solutions Engineer to lead technical discovery and implementation with the Department of the Air Force. You will own the technical relationship across Air Force major commands and wings, understand how airmen actually operate, and show how Skydio's autonomous aircraft, docks and software solve real problems on and off the installation. You will be the glue between the customer, our Sales team, and our Product and Engineering teams, turning Air Force requirements into achievable plans to field Skydio technology to specification. This role reports to the Solutions Engineering Manager, National Security.

## **Customers and missions you'll support**

* Security Forces: installation security, base defense, perimeter and flight-line patrol, and automated response with Skydio Dock
* Maintenance and Civil Engineering: aircraft and hangar inspection, airfield and FOD surveys, and infrastructure inspection with 3D scan workflows
* Air Force Global Strike Command: missile field and remote site security
* Air Force Special Operations Command, Air Combat Command, Air Mobility Command and Agile Combat Employment (ACE) units operating from austere locations
* Innovation and acquisition organizations, including AFWERX, AFLCMC and wing-level innovation cells

## **How you'll make an impact**

* Lead technical discovery with Air Force units, identify their measures of success, and run Proofs of Concept and field trials on active installations
* Plan and run demonstrations end to end: set up equipment, fly and troubleshoot the system, and brief its value to airmen, commanders and acquisition staff
* Lead implementation of Skydio Dock and Remote Ops on Air Force installations, working with base communications squadrons, airfield management and Security Forces to get systems integrated, approved and flying
* Navigate the approvals that come with operating on an installation, including airspace coordination, frequency authorization, cybersecurity (RMF/ATO) and network connection requirements
* Guide Sales, Customer Success and Product through Air Force contracting pathways, including SBIR/STTR, AFWERX, OTAs and GSA
* Write the technical content for RFIs, RFPs and white papers
* Act as the voice of the Air Force customer to shape Skydio's product roadmap
* Support go-to-market and business activities as needed

## **What makes you a good fit**

### **Requirements**

* Ability to travel up to 50% of the time, mostly to CONUS installations, with some OCONUS travel
* FAA Part 107 certification, or the ability to obtain it
* Active Secret clearance, or the ability to obtain one

### **Experience and skills**

* Technical knowledge of or experience with DoW and Air Force UAS, sensors and data analysis systems
* Working knowledge of how the Air Force operates installations, including Security Forces, Civil Engineering, maintenance and communications squadrons
* Experience with secure, government-compliant storage, handling and analysis of data, and with the Risk Management Framework and ATO process
* Excellent written communication and presentation skills for audiences from airmen to general officers
* Experience implementing new technology for customers and working with technical teams to deliver on schedule
* A strong background in at least one technical area: UAS, radio systems, software, security, networking, prototyping or electronics
* Demonstrated experience with federal or Air Force acquisition of technology
* 5+ years in solutions engineering, technical sales or a technical or operational role in defense, security, law enforcement or security cooperation, and a technical bachelor's degree or an equivalent mix of education and experience
* This role supports products for our military customers and is subject to a government contract that requires Skydio to limit this position to U.S. citizens. We welcome every qualified candidate who is eligible to work in the United States to apply. Skydio does not currently offer employment-based visa or permanent residency sponsorship for this role.

## **Nice to haves**

* Prior Air Force service, especially in Security Forces, aircraft maintenance, Civil Engineering, communications, AFSOC or as a UAS operator
* Experience with integrated base defense, flight-line security or installation physical security systems
* Experience with AFWERX, SBIR/STTR, Spark cells or other Air Force innovation programs
* Familiarity with counter-UAS and airspace deconfliction on and around military airfields
* Experience operating in contested or degraded environments (EW, GPS-denied, comms-limited)
* Hands-on experience with RF systems, datalinks and mesh networking radios
* Understanding of sensor payloads and tactical data-sharing tools such as TAK
* Background in test and evaluation, acquisition or fielding new systems
* Ability to bridge operators and engineers, from briefing senior leaders to training airmen on the flight line
* Experience leading small teams through demos, field trials or deployments.
