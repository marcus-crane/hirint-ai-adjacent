Skydio is the leading US drone company and the world leader in autonomous flight, the key technology for the future of drones and aerial mobility. The Skydio team combines deep expertise in artificial intelligence, best-in-class hardware and software product development, operational excellence, and customer obsession to empower a broader, more diverse audience of drone users, from [utility inspectors](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/energy-and-utilities) to [first responders](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/public-safety), [soldiers in battlefield scenarios](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/national-security/tactical-isr), and [beyond](https://www.skydio.com/solutions).

## **About the role**

We are looking for a driven, results-oriented Solutions Engineer to lead technical discovery and implementation across the Department of Homeland Security. DHS components secure the border, the coastline, ports of entry, federal facilities and disaster zones, and each one buys and operates differently. You will learn their missions, show how Skydio's autonomous aircraft, docks and remote operations software fit them, and build achievable plans to deploy and sustain Skydio technology to specification. This role reports to the Solutions Engineering Manager, National Security.

## **Customers and missions you'll support**

* U.S. Customs and Border Protection: U.S. Border Patrol, Air and Marine Operations and the Office of Field Operations, for border surveillance, interdiction support and port-of-entry security
* U.S. Coast Guard: maritime domain awareness, search and rescue, port security and cutter-based operations
* ICE Homeland Security Investigations (HSI), Federal Protective Service and the U.S. Secret Service: investigations, federal facility security and protective operations
* Transportation Security Administration: security for airports, surface transportation and pipelines, including perimeter and critical infrastructure protection
* FEMA and CISA: disaster response, damage assessment and critical infrastructure protection
* DHS Science and Technology Directorate and component innovation offices

## **How you'll make an impact**

* Lead technical discovery with DHS components, identify their measures of success, and run proofs of concept (POCs) and field trials in operational environments, from remote border areas to ports and urban federal facilities
* Plan and run demonstrations end to end: set up equipment, fly and troubleshoot the system, and brief its value to agents, officers, program managers and leadership
* Lead implementation of persistent, remotely operated capability with Skydio Dock and Remote Ops, including beyond-visual-line-of-sight (BVLOS) operations, network integration and connection to existing command and surveillance systems
* Navigate DHS IT security and data requirements (DHS 4300A, FedRAMP and component ATOs) and FAA approvals for public aircraft operations
* Help Sales, Customer Success and Product navigate DHS contracting, including component procurement offices, S&T programs such as the Silicon Valley Innovation Program, Other Transaction Authority (OTA) agreements and the GSA Multiple Award Schedule
* Write the technical content for RFIs, RFPs and white papers
* Act as the voice of the DHS customer to shape Skydio's product roadmap
* Support go-to-market activities such as trade shows, customer events and partner enablement

## **What makes you a good fit**

### **Requirements**

* Ability to travel up to 50% of the time, including to remote border and coastal locations
* FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate, or the ability to obtain it
* Active Secret clearance, or the ability to obtain one, and the ability to receive a favorable DHS suitability determination
* 5+ years in solutions engineering, technical sales or a technical or operational role in federal law enforcement, homeland security or defense, and a technical bachelor's degree or an equivalent mix of education and experience
* This role supports products for our government customers and is subject to a government contract that requires Skydio to limit this position to U.S. citizens. Skydio does not currently offer employment-based visa or permanent residency sponsorship for this role.

### **Experience and skills**

* Technical knowledge of or experience with federal law enforcement or homeland security UAS, sensors and data analysis systems
* Understanding of how DHS components are organized, how they operate and how they buy technology
* Experience with secure, government-compliant storage, handling and analysis of data, including Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI), law-enforcement-sensitive data and chain-of-custody requirements
* Excellent written communication and presentation skills for audiences from field agents to senior executives
* Experience implementing new technology for customers and working with technical teams to deliver on schedule
* A strong background in at least one technical area: UAS, radio systems, software, security, networking, prototyping or electronics
* Demonstrated experience with federal acquisition of technology

## **Nice-to-haves**

* Prior service with CBP, USCG, HSI, FPS, the Secret Service or another federal law enforcement agency, especially as a UAS operator, program manager or instructor
* Experience with border technology, such as surveillance towers, ground sensors and common operating pictures
* Maritime operations experience, including shipboard or small-boat UAS operations
* Familiarity with counter-UAS authorities and operations and with airspace deconfliction
* Familiarity with the American Security Drone Act, NDAA compliance and the Blue UAS program
* Experience with BVLOS waivers, Certificates of Waiver or Authorization (COAs) or public aircraft operations
* Experience operating in contested or degraded environments (EW, GPS-denied, comms-limited)
* Hands-on experience with RF systems, datalinks and mesh networking radios
* Understanding of sensor payloads and data-sharing tools such as Team Awareness Kit (TAK)
* Background in test and evaluation, acquisition or fielding new systems
* Ability to bridge operators and engineers, from briefing senior leaders to training end users in the field
* Professional working proficiency in Spanish
