Skydio is the leading US drone company and the world leader in autonomous flight, the key technology for the future of drones and aerial mobility. The Skydio team combines deep expertise in artificial intelligence, best-in-class hardware and software product development, operational excellence, and customer obsession to empower a broader, more diverse audience of drone users, from [utility inspectors](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/energy-and-utilities) to [first responders](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/public-safety), [soldiers in battlefield scenarios](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/national-security/tactical-isr), and [beyond](https://www.skydio.com/solutions).

## **About the role**

We are looking for a driven, results-oriented Solutions Engineer to lead technical discovery and implementation with the U.S. Department of State and with federal government customers in Canada and Latin America. You will support diplomatic security at home and abroad, State-funded partner capacity programs, and direct sales to our partners in Canada and Latin America. You will work across languages, cultures and export control rules, translate partner requirements for our Product and Engineering teams, and build achievable plans to deliver, train and sustain Skydio technology to specification. This role reports to the Solutions Engineering Manager, National Security.

## **Customers and missions you'll support**

* U.S. Department of State: Diplomatic Security Service and overseas facility security, the Bureau of International Narcotics and Law Enforcement Affairs (INL) and its partner capacity programs, and the Bureau of Political-Military Affairs
* Canada: Department of National Defence and the Canadian Armed Forces, the Royal Canadian Mounted Police (RCMP), the Canada Border Services Agency, and procurement through Public Services and Procurement Canada
* Mexico: federal defense, security and law enforcement institutions, including the Secretariat of National Defense (SEDENA), the Secretariat of the Navy (SEMAR) and the National Guard (Guardia Nacional), often through U.S.-funded programs
* Latin America: defense, security and law enforcement institutions in Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Central America, the Caribbean and other partner nations, usually through INL and other U.S.-funded security assistance programs
* U.S. Embassy teams, Offices of Security Cooperation and Narcotics Affairs Sections in Ottawa, Mexico City and other Latin American capitals

## **How you'll make an impact**

* Lead technical discovery with State and partner-nation customers, identify their measures of success, and run proofs of concept (POCs) and field trials in the U.S., Canada and Latin America
* Plan and run demonstrations end to end: set up equipment, fly and troubleshoot the system, and brief its value to operators, officials and embassy staff, in English or Spanish
* Lead implementation and training for partner forces, including train-the-trainer programs and long-term sustainment plans
* Work with Skydio's trade compliance team on export controls (ITAR and EAR), end-use requirements and Canadian Controlled Goods Program requirements, so deals and deliveries stay compliant
* Help Sales, Customer Success and Product navigate State Department procurement, INL-funded programs, Foreign Military Sales (FMS), Direct Commercial Sales (DCS) and Canadian and Latin American government procurement
* Assist with developing technical content for RFIs, RFPs, tenders and white papers, including Spanish, Portuguese and French materials where needed
* Act as the voice of State and Canadian and Latin American partner customers to shape Skydio's product roadmap
* Support go-to-market activities such as trade shows, customer events and partner enablement

## **What makes you a good fit**

### **Requirements**

* Ability to travel up to 50% of the time, with regular international travel to Canada and Latin America
* A valid U.S. passport and the ability to travel internationally on short notice
* FAA Part 107 Remote Pilot Certificate, or the ability to obtain it, and willingness to obtain host-nation pilot credentials such as a Transport Canada advanced operations pilot certificate
* Active Secret clearance, or the ability to obtain one
* Full professional proficiency in Spanish, spoken and written (ILR level 4 or equivalent)
* 5+ years in solutions engineering, technical sales or a technical or operational role in defense, security, law enforcement or security cooperation, and a technical bachelor's degree or an equivalent mix of education and experience
* This role supports products for our government customers and is subject to a government contract that requires Skydio to limit this position to U.S. citizens. Skydio does not currently offer employment-based visa or permanent residency sponsorship for this role.

### **Experience and skills**

* Technical knowledge of or experience with defense, security or law enforcement UAS, sensors and data analysis systems
* Working knowledge of export controls, security assistance or international government sales
* Experience with secure, government-compliant storage, handling and analysis of data, including data sovereignty concerns of foreign partners
* Excellent written communication and presentation skills for audiences from field operators to senior foreign officials
* Experience implementing new technology for customers and working with technical teams to deliver on schedule
* A strong background in at least one technical area: UAS, radio systems, software, security, networking, prototyping or electronics
* Demonstrated experience with U.S. federal or foreign government acquisition of technology

## **Nice-to-haves**

* Prior service with the Department of State (Diplomatic Security, INL or an embassy team), a Security Cooperation Office, or as a military advisor to partner forces
* Experience training or advising Canadian or Latin American defense, security or law enforcement forces
* Experience with FMS, DCS, Building Partner Capacity programs or end-use monitoring
* Professional working proficiency in French or Portuguese
* Experience operating in contested or degraded environments (EW, GPS-denied, comms-limited)
* Familiarity with counter-UAS and airspace deconfliction
* Familiarity with the American Security Drone Act, NDAA compliance and the Blue UAS program
* Hands-on experience with RF systems, datalinks and mesh networking radios
* Understanding of sensor payloads and data-sharing tools such as Team Awareness Kit (TAK)
* Background in test and evaluation, acquisition or fielding new systems
* Ability to bridge operators and engineers, from briefing senior officials to training end users in the field
* Experience leading small teams through demos, field trials or deployments
