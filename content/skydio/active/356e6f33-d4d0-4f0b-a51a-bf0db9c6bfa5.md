## **About the Role**

A Skydio Field Service Coordinator (FSC) is responsible for coordinating field service operations for Skydio X10 Dock products. As part of the Customer Support Operations team, the Field Service Coordinator serves as a point of coordination between customers, Field Service Technicians, third-party service providers, Customer Support, and internal operations teams.

The FSC manages the complete lifecycle of service work orders—from creation through completion—ensuring service appointments are scheduled efficiently, required parts are available, documentation is complete, and repairs are tracked through resolution. This role is collaborative and requires strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment.

## **Impact**

As a Field Service Coordinator, you will be responsible for ensuring field service activities are planned, executed, and documented properly. You will schedule service appointments with customers and third-party service providers, manage work orders throughout their lifecycle, coordinate parts logistics, and monitor repair progress to ensure timely completion.

You'll work closely with Customer Support, Field Service Technicians, Logistics, and other cross-functional teams to resolve issues, maintain accurate records, and ensure customers receive timely updates throughout the service process. Your coordination efforts will directly contribute to improving operational efficiency, reducing equipment downtime, and delivering a best-in-class customer experience.

## **Key Responsibilities**

* Schedule and coordinate field service appointments with customers, Field Service Technicians, and third-party service providers.
* Manage work orders from creation through completion, ensuring timely updates and accurate documentation.
* Monitor and manage the Field Service RMA Jira queue, ensuring repair requests are routed and processed efficiently.
* Create sales orders for replacement parts and coordinate shipment to customers, technicians, and repair locations.
* Track replacement parts and ensure returned components are received, documented, and processed according to company procedures.
* Coordinate preventative maintenance work orders and recurring service activities with customers and third-party vendors.
* Collaborate closely with Customer Support, Logistics, Repair Operations, and other internal teams to ensure seamless service delivery.
* Maintain accurate service documentation within Jira, Salesforce, and other related business systems.
* Communicate service status, scheduling updates, and logistical information to internal stakeholders and customers.
* Identify scheduling conflicts, resource constraints, and process improvements to optimize field service operations.
