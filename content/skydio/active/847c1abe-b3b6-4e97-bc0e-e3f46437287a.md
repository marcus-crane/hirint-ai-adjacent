**About the role:**  
We are looking for an engineer with a focus on electric motors and propulsion systems to help model, architect, optimize, and refine the electric propulsion systems that power our drones.

**How you'll make an impact:**

* Strong mathematical and physical understanding on motor power and loss mechanisms
* Excellent knowledge on finite element method
* Knowledge on motor thermal modeling
* Experience on electric motor loss characterization
* Knowledge on motor control theory

**What makes you a good fit:**

* Being able to model, architect, and optimize a propulsion system, from ESCs to motors to propellers
* Understanding electric motor losses and flux, develop test plans and perform measurements
* Detailed motor design and optimization to optimize performance, efficiency and size
* Explore and design innovative motor technologies
* Create simulation model for thermal model development and system engineering
* Integrate and optimize with motor control team and ESCs
* Familiarity with construction and manufacturing techniques of outrunner PMSMs in the ~500W size class
* End to end understanding of drone propulsion systems, from battery to ESC to motors to propellors
* Obtaining FAA Part 107 certification within the first 60 days of employment is strongly encouraged for all Skydio employees and required for certain positions.
