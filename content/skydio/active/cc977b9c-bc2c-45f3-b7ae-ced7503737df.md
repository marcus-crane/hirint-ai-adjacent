Skydio is the leading US drone company and the world leader in autonomous flight, the key technology for the future of drones and aerial mobility. The Skydio team combines deep expertise in artificial intelligence, best-in-class hardware and software product development, operational excellence, and customer obsession to empower a broader, more diverse audience of drone users, from [utility inspectors](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/energy-and-utilities) to [first responders](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/public-safety), [soldiers in battlefield scenarios](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/national-security/tactical-isr), and [beyond](https://www.skydio.com/solutions).

**About the role:**

We are looking for a highly skilled Sr. Data Analyst with a strong background in systems integrations, workflows, dashboards and development. This role will focus on building, maintaining, and optimizing data flows between NetSuite and other business-critical platforms. As well as creating beautiful dashboards out of that.  
  
**This would be based out of our San Mateo, CA Headquarters and would need to be in the office 2-3 times a week.**

**How you’ll make an impact:**

* Own and manage the Palantir Foundry platform from a Finance perspective.
* Design, develop, and enhance data pipelines on the Palantir Foundry platform.
* Troubleshoot integration issues, ensuring data accuracy and system reliability.
* Document reverse ETL flows, technical specifications, and best practices.
* Define and design ontologies to structure and organize enterprise data.
* Proven ability to build custom applications and interactive dashboards within Palantir Foundry.
* Gain deep understanding of our business processes to drive system improvements and automations in Palantir.

**Desired qualifications:**

* 3+ years of experience with data visualization tools
* 3+ years of experience with Databricks or similar platform
* 3+ years of experience with Pyspark, Typescript
* 2+ years of hands-on experience with the Palantir Foundry platform (Preferred)
* Experience with Building/Maintaining integration pipelines between NS and other business applications
* Experience in data analysis and visualization
* Bachelor's or Master's degree in CS/MIS or other equivalent fields.
* Understanding of Consumer Electronics hardware product lifecycle is preferred but not required.
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with an intellectual curiosity.
* Self-starter and proactive personality with a proven track record of following through and delivering on responsibilities.
