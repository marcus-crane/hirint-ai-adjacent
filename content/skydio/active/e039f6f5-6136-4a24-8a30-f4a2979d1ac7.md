Skydio is the leading US drone company and the world leader in autonomous flight, the key technology for the future of drones and aerial mobility. The Skydio team combines deep expertise in artificial intelligence, best-in-class hardware and software product development, operational excellence, and customer obsession to empower a broader, more diverse audience of drone users, from [utility inspectors](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/energy-and-utilities) to [first responders](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/public-safety), [soldiers in battlefield scenarios](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/national-security/tactical-isr), and [beyond](https://www.skydio.com/solutions).

**About the Team:** You will be an integral member of our cross-functional infrastructure team. Working closely with each team of engineers, you will identify the areas that can be enhanced to lower developer’s cognitive load, and make our engineering workflows and pipelines even more efficient. You’ll write common tools, provide development best practices, and guide with thoughtful leadership as we deliver software for our growing customer base world-wide.

**About the role:** You’ll build the platform that puts AI coding agents into the daily workflow of hundreds of Skydio engineers. You’ll work across our codebase, CI, and internal infrastructure to produce secure tools that increase our development velocity. You’ll work directly with engineers to find where they lose time and build the tools that get that time back.

**How you'll make an impact:**

* Build and extend our agentic coding platform: agent integrations, MCP servers, and sandboxed execution
* Design guardrails such as permissions, secrets handling, and auditability, so agents can be trusted with sensitive code.
* Build the infrastructure that lets teams quickly deploy internal tools and agents
* Embed with teams across firmware, autonomy, cloud, and apps to find workflow bottlenecks and fix them.
* Measure impact through adoption, cycle time, and quality, and use that data to set priorities.
* Drive adoption: be the internal expert on AI development tools and help engineers get real value from them.cost-efficient.

**What makes you a good fit:**

* 5+ years building developer tools, infrastructure, or platform systems.
* Hands-on experience with LLM-based tools and agents: you’ve built with them, not just used them
* Strong grounding in Python/Go/TypeScript/C++, CI/CD, and cloud infrastructure AWS/GCP
* A product mindset toward internal users: you talk to them, ship quickly, and iterate.
* Experience with security-sensitive environments or monorepos
