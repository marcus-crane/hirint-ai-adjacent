The Skydio team combines deep expertise in artificial intelligence, best-in-class hardware and software product development, operational excellence, and customer obsession to empower a broader, more diverse audience of drone users, from [utility inspectors](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/energy-and-utilities) to [first responders](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/public-safety), [soldiers in battlefield scenarios](https://www.skydio.com/solutions/national-security/tactical-isr), and [beyond](https://www.skydio.com/solutions).

**About the role:**   
We are seeking a talented AP Accountant with experience in high-growth environments to own the end-to-end accounts payable function. The ideal candidate will bring strong operational expertise in accounts payable, a process-improvement mindset, and a collaborative approach.

This role will work closely with the Finance and GL teams, as well as cross-functionally with departments such as Hardware, Manufacturing, and Legal, to ensure that our AP processes are efficient, accurate, and scalable.

**Location**: This role will be based out of our San Mateo office and requires 3 days a week in office.

**How You’ll Make an Impact:**

Invoice and Expense Management

* Accurately enter invoices into the accounting system (Coupa) after confirming proper authorization
* Process invoices promptly and in accordance with vendor payment terms
* Review the AP aging report weekly and resolve past-due invoices
* Reconcile assigned vendor statements promptly to maintain account accuracy
* Review and approve employee travel and expense (T&E) reports for payment
* Investigate and resolve discrepancies by coordinating with internal departments and vendors to address missing documentation, invoice issues, or purchase order variances

Month-End Close

* Review account reconciliations for AP-related close items
* Prepare month-end accruals with appropriate supporting documentation
* Complete monthly balance sheet reconciliations accurately and on schedule
* Assist with monthly and quarterly vendor flux and other balance sheet income statement fluxes

Process Improvement and Special Projects

* Identify and implement improvements that streamline workflows and increase the accuracy of accounts payable processes
* Monitor ERP system activity to ensure accurate invoice postings between the GL and accounts payable subledger, including Coupa
* Collaborate with the Business Systems team to troubleshoot and resolve system-related issues

**What Makes You a Good Fit:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Accounting, Finance, or a related field
* At least two years of relevant accounts payable experience
* Experience scaling processes at high-growth companies, preferably hardware startups
* Proficiency with ERP systems and automated accounts payable workflow platforms, such as Coupa, NetSuite, and Ramp
* Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a deadline-driven environment
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills
* Strong interpersonal skills and the ability to work effectively with employees, vendors, and all levels of management
* Demonstrated initiative and the ability to work independently with minimal supervision
* A problem-solving mindset and a strong sense of ownership from start to finish
