## **What to Expect**

At Nuro, safety is our guiding principle and our promise—every mile matters as we drive autonomy forward. As an Autonomous Vehicle Operator, you bring this commitment to life through the care, judgment, and accountability you demonstrate on every drive.

You’ll play a critical role in advancing self-driving technology by operating test vehicles in manual and autonomous modes for up to eight hours a day across a variety of real-world traffic and road conditions. You’ll use Nuro’s software and equipment to collect data and provide detailed feedback that helps our engineering teams build safer, more reliable self-driving technology.

## **About Nuro**

Nuro believes self-driving vehicles are the most immediate and profound opportunity for AI to drive positive change in the physical world. Safer streets, more time for what matters, and easier access to the world around us, that’s why we’re building a universal autonomy platform: self-driving for all roads and all rides.  
  
Founded in 2016, Nuro is a physical AI company developing Level 4 autonomous driving technology for a wide range of vehicles, use cases, and markets. Powered by the Nuro Driver™, our universal autonomy platform enables the global mobility ecosystem to deploy autonomy at scale, from robotaxis and logistics fleets to personal vehicles.  
  
With years of real-world deployment experience and a flexible, partner-led business model, Nuro is working toward a future where millions of autonomous vehicles powered by our technology help make everyday life safer, easier, and more connected.  
  
Nuro has raised over $2B in capital from Uber, NVIDIA, Google, Softbank, Fidelity, T. Rowe Price, and other leading investors.

## **What You'll Do**

* Drive Nuro test vehicles in manual and autonomous modes for up to eight hours a day while following all safety and driving procedures
* Monitor the vehicle’s safety, navigation, and driving systems while staying alert to traffic, pedestrians, bicyclists, weather, and road conditions
* Collect real-world driving data using Nuro’s cutting-edge software and share feedback with engineers
* Complete vehicle checks before and after each shift, report problems, and perform basic upkeep such as washing vehicles and checking tire pressure
* Troubleshoot operational issues and coordinate with Mission Operations (dispatch) to resolve problems safely
* Use laptops, tablets, smartphones, and other tools to complete reports and document your work
* Occasionally speak with members of the public or support ridealongs for company leaders, investors, engineers, and other guests

## **To Be Considered, You'll Need to**

* Always practice safe driving and follow detailed procedures
* Be available five days per week on the fixed shift you select when you apply
* Meet the driving-record requirements below and pass a background check and drug screen
* Stay focused and observant while sitting and driving for long periods
* Communicate clearly in writing and verbally, and be comfortable using modern online collaboration platforms  (e.g., Slack, Google Meet, or similar)
* Stay calm, solve problems, and work independently
* Maintain reliable attendance and arrive on time consistently

## **Working Conditions and Essential Functions**

Due to the nature of the position, this job requires:

* Sitting for 3 to 4 hours at a time for up to 8 hours per day in an enclosed vehicle alone or occasionally with a co-driver
* Bending, squatting, stretching and lifting up to 25 pounds on a regular basis
* Side and up/down movement of the head, neck, back and shoulders necessary to operate a vehicle safely
* Ability to work in a vehicle that may brake, swerve, slow, or stop abruptly
* Holding and grasping with hands and frequent use of foot/feet to operate foot pedals when driving the vehicle

## **Pre-Employment Qualifications**

* Must have a current valid California driver’s license and at least three consecutive years of U.S. driving history
* You have a clean driving record and can pass a criminal background check and drug test
* Must meet and maintain all applicable driving record requirements and qualify for an Autonomous Vehicle Tester (AVT) license where required by state law
