**Who We Are**

Nuro believes self-driving vehicles are the most immediate and profound opportunity for AI to drive positive change in the physical world. Safer streets, more time for what matters, and easier access to the world around us, that’s why we’re building a universal autonomy platform: self-driving for all roads and all rides.  
  
Founded in 2016, Nuro is a physical AI company developing Level 4 autonomous driving technology for a wide range of vehicles, use cases, and markets. Powered by the Nuro Driver™, our universal autonomy platform enables the global mobility ecosystem to deploy autonomy at scale, from robotaxis and logistics fleets to personal vehicles.  
  
With years of real-world deployment experience and a flexible, partner-led business model, Nuro is working toward a future where millions of autonomous vehicles powered by our technology help make everyday life safer, easier, and more connected.  
  
Nuro has raised over $2B in capital from Uber, NVIDIA, Google, Softbank, Fidelity, T. Rowe Price, and other leading investors.

**About the Team**Nuro Driver Core Software Engineering works across onboard software robustness, efficiency, and software architecture and verification. The team has three pillars: A, Nuro Driver Module Robustness; B, Nuro Driver Module Efficiency; and C, Software Architecture and Verification. The application-software scope includes autonomy and supporting onboard modules.

**About the Role**We are looking for a senior C++ software engineer to improve the structure and production behavior of onboard Nuro Driver software. You will work with module owners and infrastructure partners on application-level designs, with particular attention to module boundaries, dependency contracts, testability, and resource constraints. The role is hands-on and does not require sole architectural authority over the stack.

**About the Work**

* Lead a prioritized cross-module workstream and related architecture pilot from problem definition through implementation, rollout, and measurement.
* Design explicit application behavior for dependency failures, stale data, lifecycle transitions, restart, fault containment, and safe degradation with the relevant owners.
* Evaluate module decomposition, interfaces, and dependency structure. Simplify unnecessary distribution and modernize C++ implementations using supported runtime frameworks.
* Assess compute, memory, data-flow, and scheduling impacts of application changes with Middleware/Performance; translate agreed constraints into component-level targets.
* Design components for meaningful tests, deterministic replay, and simulation. Partner with the existing engineers on input capture, configuration, timing, state initialization, regression tests, and fidelity gaps.
* Write architecture proposals with alternatives, ownership, resource impact, test strategy, and migration plans; drive joint review with module owners, Fleet Infra, Onboard Performance, Simulation, Systems, and Safety.
* Review new or materially changed module boundaries and interfaces, provide rigorous code reviews, and drive adoption of agreed engineering patterns.
* Use AI-assisted development tools where appropriate while remaining accountable for design quality, behavioral correctness, testing, and production readiness.

**About You**

* Strong modern C++ and Linux systems fundamentals, including concurrency, memory management, inter-process communication, performance, and failure handling.
* Significant experience developing and maintaining production C++ software in a large codebase.
* Experience independently leading ambiguous systems-software projects from initial design through cross-team adoption and measurable production impact.
* Sound judgment about module decomposition, interface contracts, failure boundaries, testability, and resource partitioning, including when an additional process boundary is justified.
* Strong production debugging, testing, observability, and verification discipline.
* Effective written and verbal communication, with demonstrated ability to build stakeholder alignment and provide high-quality design and code reviews.
* 5+ years of relevant production software-engineering experience, or equivalent demonstrated scope and impact.
* Demonstrated ownership of a substantial component architecture, modernization, or cross-team software project through implementation and adoption.
* Experience diagnosing and resolving complex reliability or performance issues in Linux-based systems.
* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field, or equivalent practical experience.

**Bonus Points** 

* Experience with autonomous vehicles, robotics, embedded systems, real-time systems, or another safety-critical domain.
* Experience with deterministic simulation or replay, fault injection, fuzzing, or behavior-preserving validation.
* Experience profiling and optimizing CPU usage, memory allocation, lock contention, latency, or cache behavior.
* Familiarity with publish/subscribe systems, protobuf contracts, module lifecycle frameworks, and resource-aware decomposition on constrained platforms.
* Experience with Clang tooling, static analysis, sanitizers, Bazel, or C++ library/toolchain development.
