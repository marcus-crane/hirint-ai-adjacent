**Who We Are**

Nuro believes self-driving vehicles are the most immediate and profound opportunity for AI to drive positive change in the physical world. Safer streets, more time for what matters, and easier access to the world around us, that’s why we’re building a universal autonomy platform: self-driving for all roads and all rides.  
  
Founded in 2016, Nuro is a physical AI company developing Level 4 autonomous driving technology for a wide range of vehicles, use cases, and markets. Powered by the Nuro Driver™, our universal autonomy platform enables the global mobility ecosystem to deploy autonomy at scale, from robotaxis and logistics fleets to personal vehicles.  
  
With years of real-world deployment experience and a flexible, partner-led business model, Nuro is working toward a future where millions of autonomous vehicles powered by our technology help make everyday life safer, easier, and more connected.  
  
Nuro has raised over $2B in capital from Uber, NVIDIA, Google, Softbank, Fidelity, T. Rowe Price, and other leading investors.

**About the Role**

Our software organization is growing and we are looking for talented students to join our Technical Program Manager (TPM) team as part of the Pathfinders Internship Program.

As a Pathfinder, you will gain meaningful work experience while applying the knowledge and skills you have gained in the classroom. You will have the opportunity to make connections with interns and employees across the company, and to make a quantifiable impact on one of our teams.

We hire students at the undergraduate and graduate levels for roles lasting 12, 14 or 16 weeks. Longer term internships are available for research positions.

We are looking for a Technical Program Manager (TPM) to help Nuro deliver complex and strategic multi-functional software engineering projects.

**About the Work**

* Act as the internal “glue” by communicating and coordinating across all teams including software and hardware engineering, product, operations, and business to drive multiple complex, cross-functional initiatives related to remote monitoring and operation
* Lead technical reviews, synthesize results and build plans to resolve product issues
* Develop requirements, define timelines/milestones, identify/mitigate risks, allocate resources, track progress, resolve dependencies, unblock progress etc
* Regularly communicate progress to company leadership and program partners

**About You**

* You are pursuing a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in computer science, mechanical, electrical or aerospace engineering, and/or equivalent technical experience/aptitude, graduating August 2027 - December 2027
* You are passionate about becoming a technical program and/or project manager and about gaining experience bringing complex software products to market
* You collaborate well with others and take pride in helping teams succeed
* You are keenly discerning with an ability to prioritize and execute on multiple concurrent tasks.
* You have the DNA to thrive in the ambiguity of a dynamic, start-up environment
* You have a real passion for helping drive the delivery of creative products leveraging AI, robotics and/or machine learning

**Bonus Points** 

* Prior experience in robotics, autonomous systems, or safety-critical applications

 

For this position, the reasonably expected monthly salary is $8,000. Your actual monthly pay will depend on several factors, including your experience, qualifications, education, location, and skills.
