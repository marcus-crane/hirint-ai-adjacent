**Who We Are**

Nuro is a self-driving technology company on a mission to make autonomy accessible to all. Founded in 2016, Nuro is building the world’s most scalable driver, combining cutting-edge AI with automotive-grade hardware. Nuro licenses its core technology, the Nuro Driver™, to support a wide range of applications, from robotaxis and commercial fleets to personally owned vehicles. With technology proven over years of self-driving deployments, Nuro gives the automakers and mobility platforms a clear path to AVs at commercial scale—empowering a safer, richer, and more connected future.

**About the Team**

The Safety Office is part of Nuro’s Public Trust organization and operates independently from our technology development and business operations teams. Our role is to help ensure that Nuro develops and deploys autonomous vehicle technology in a way that protects safety, supports sound decision-making, and earns public trust. We work across the company to define safety expectations, evaluate evidence, guide risk-informed decisions, and strengthen the processes that support safe development and deployment.

**About the Role**

We are looking for a Senior or Staff Safety Management Systems Engineer to design, implement, and continuously improve Nuro’s company-wide Safety Management System (SMS) for autonomous vehicle development and operations. You will build the policies, risk management processes, reporting mechanisms, safety performance measures, and assurance activities that help embed safety into technical, operational, and business decisions.

**About the Work**

* Architect and deploy an SMS (aligned with ISO 45001, ICAO SMS principles, AVSC SMS Information Report, or equivalent aviation/automotive models) that covers the entire lifecycle of the product including the development phase through the operational phase.
* Define safety policy, processes, and performance indicators across all AV programs.
* Establish and maintain a fully functioning end-to-end Risk Register that will be used by leadership to monitor company risk on a real time basis.
* Lead hazard identification, risk assessment, and mitigation planning for AV activities.
* Establish and promote safety culture, including training and awareness programs for all business units.
* Manage safety reporting systems and oversight of incident/near-miss investigations.
* Collaborate with legal and compliance for regulatory engagement and audits.
* Support continuous improvement based on field data, incident trends, and feedback loops.
* Work with the TPM, safety standards, and operational safety teams to ensure cohesive company-wide safety integration.
* Strong fundamentals understanding of quality assurance.

**About You**

* Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in engineering, safety, systems, or a related field, with 5-8+ years of experience in safety management, risk management, or assurance roles in high-risk industries such as automotive, aviation, rail, robotics, or autonomy.
* Proven experience designing, implementing, or operating a cross-functional Safety Management System.
* Strong background in hazard identification, risk assessment, risk registers, safety performance measurement, corrective action, and continuous improvement.
* Working knowledge of safety culture, safety reporting, incident investigation, and organizational learning practices.
* Strong communication, facilitation, and change management skills, with the ability to influence across technical and business teams.
* Familiarity with quality assurance principles and how they support safety management systems.

**Bonus Points**

* Experience with autonomous vehicles, robotics, transportation, aviation, or fleet operations.
* Familiarity with tools such as JIRA, JAMA, Polarion, Confluence, or risk management platforms.
