**About the Team**

The Safety Office is part of Nuro’s Public Trust organization and operates independently from our technology development and business operations teams. Our role is to help ensure that Nuro develops and deploys autonomous vehicle technology in a way that protects safety, supports sound decision-making, and earns public trust. We work across the company to define safety expectations, evaluate evidence, guide risk-informed decisions, and strengthen the processes that support safe development and deployment.

**About the Role**

We are looking for a Senior/Staff Safety Standards Engineer to join our Safety Group. You will be responsible for developing, maintaining, and operationalizing compliance with all relevant AV safety standards and best practices. You will work cross-functionally with engineering, operations, and legal teams to ensure that Nuro is following best practices and emerging industry standards for autonomous vehicle safety.

**About the Work**

* Serve as the subject matter expert for AV safety, AI safety, cybersecurity, quality, and assurance standards, including ISO/PAS 8800, ISO/TS 5083, ISO 26262, ISO 21448/SOTIF, ISO/SAE 21434, UL 4600, ISO 9001, and related frameworks.
* Develop, maintain, and socialize internal processes, templates, and guidelines for safety safety documentation, and ensuring internal processes map to industry standards.
* Manage third-party certifications, conformance assessments, and external audit support.
* Train engineering and V&V teams on current interpretations of standards.
* Work with cross-functional teams to translate requirements into actionable development, validation, and evidence artifacts.
* Ensure traceability from system requirements through to safety case arguments and evidence.
* Advise on updates and improvements to align with evolving standards and regulatory expectations.
* Monitor emerging standards, regulatory expectations, and industry best practices, and recommend improvements to Nuro’s safety processes.

**About You**

* Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in engineering or a related technical field, with 5-8+ years of experience in safety-critical domains.
* Deep working knowledge of ISO 26262, ISO 21448/SOTIF, and adjacent automotive or AV safety standards.
* Experience developing or maintaining safety cases, safety arguments, assurance evidence, or compliance frameworks.
* Demonstrated ability to drive cross-functional compliance in a hardware/software product environment.
* Experience with safety argumentation, safety cases, and V&V integration.
* Strong written communication skills, with the ability to turn complex standards into clear internal guidance.

**Bonus Points**

* Experience with autonomous vehicles, robotics, transportation, aviation, or fleet operations.
