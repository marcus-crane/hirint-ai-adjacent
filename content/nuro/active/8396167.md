**Who We Are**

Nuro believes self-driving vehicles are the most immediate and profound opportunity for AI to drive positive change in the physical world. Safer streets, more time for what matters, and easier access to the world around us, that’s why we’re building a universal autonomy platform: self-driving for all roads and all rides.  
  
Founded in 2016, Nuro is a physical AI company developing Level 4 autonomous driving technology for a wide range of vehicles, use cases, and markets. Powered by the Nuro Driver™, our universal autonomy platform enables the global mobility ecosystem to deploy autonomy at scale, from robotaxis and logistics fleets to personal vehicles.  
  
With years of real-world deployment experience and a flexible, partner-led business model, Nuro is working toward a future where millions of autonomous vehicles powered by our technology help make everyday life safer, easier, and more connected.  
  
Nuro has raised over $2B in capital from Uber, NVIDIA, Google, Softbank, Fidelity, T. Rowe Price, and other leading investors.

**About the Role**

As we scale and deploy driverless vehicles in operations with our partners, we're building a small team of program owners who take on Nuro's most important new challenges. You'll be given an ambiguous problem, build the plan, execute it, and either hand off to a permanent owner or become one. Then you move to the next problem.

Whereas traditional rotational programs may have rigid formats and expose you to smaller challenges, this is an opportunity to take on mission-critical work. You will own outcomes, not recommendations.

**What you'll do**

* Take ownership of a critical challenge with no current owner, from scoping through execution.
* Build structure and a plan where none exists, then ship against it.
* Work directly with engineering leads, product managers, and program owners across the company to get context fast and build fast.
* As needed hand off cleanly to a permanent owner, and move to the next initiative or become the permanent owner yourself.

**What we're looking for**

* Strong technical foundation. CS/Engineering or other technical degree preferred.
  + Consulting experience in technical industries or program leadership at a top company is an equally strong path to success in this role.
* Track record of ramping into unfamiliar domains and topics quickly and producing real output, not just building context and understanding.
* Comfortable owning ambiguous, zero-to-one work without a lot of structure handed to you.
* No AV background required. But we are looking for applicants excited about the space. A willingness to learn it fast matters more than prior exposure.
* Evidence that you can build trust with technical teams quickly, since you'll be the new person in the room more often than not.
* Expertise in running programs, both technical and non-technical.

**What makes someone a fit**

You'd rather own a hard, undefined problem than execute a well-scoped one. You get energy from the first 90 days of something new, not the steady state. You've probably already done this informally, jumping into gaps other people avoided because they were messy.
