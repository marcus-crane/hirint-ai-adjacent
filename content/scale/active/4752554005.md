At Scale, we believe that AI will dramatically improve the world, and our mission is to accelerate the development of AI. As Scale continues to grow its Government Relations & Policy Insights function, we are looking for an AI Policy Lead to support policy development and engagements with governments and third parties in the US and globally.

The successful candidate will combine deep AI policy expertise, experience driving regulatory and policy outcomes, and the ability to connect business objectives to policy wins.

**You Will:**

* Serve as Scale’s in-house AI policy subject-matter expert
* Lead the company’s development of external policy positions and product policies
* Represent Scale in engagements with the US and foreign governments
* Advocate on behalf of Scale’s positions in trade associations, standards development organizations, and other external fora
* Assist in the formation of Scale’s government relations strategy and priorities
* Lead Scale’s response to government rulemakings and requests for comment
* Collaborate with the Scale Policy Lab to advance a policy-relevant research agenda
* Contribute to Scale’s thought leadership on AI policy
* Advise and brief executives on policies impacting artificial intelligence policy
* Build relationships with relevant external stakeholders
* Monitor policy and regulatory changes for potential impact to Scale’s business
* Collaborate effectively with cross-functional stakeholders including Business Units, Communications, Marketing, and others.

**Ideally You’ll Have:**

* At least 8-10 years of tech policy and/or government experience
* Demonstrated expertise in AI Policy, via responsibility for AI within a government agency, AI-related experience in the tech sector, publication of AI-related research, etc.
* A history of working with third parties such as trade associations and think tanks
* Experience engaging government officials and third-party stakeholders, ideally on behalf of a government or private-sector entity
* Excellent written, verbal, and public speaking skills
* A history of working in a fast-paced environment and successfully managing multiple works streams simultaneously
* Ability to work cross-functionally with a high degree of autonomy

**Nice to haves:**

* Experience working in a tech company
* Experience with rulemakings, requests for comment, and other regulatory submissions within government and/or the private sector
* Recognized leadership in an AI-related research area
* Experience working with foreign governments and/or the diplomatic community
