# **Senior Director, Solutions Architecture & Advisory, Enterprise GTM**

## **About Scale AI**

Scale AI is the data foundation for AI, helping organizations build and deploy reliable production AI applications. Our mission is to develop reliable AI systems for the world's most important decisions. The Enterprise business delivers production-grade generative AI applications to large companies across industries, combining generative AI expertise with the infrastructure to run it.

## **The role**

Scale is hiring a senior leader to run two connected functions in Enterprise GTM: Solutions Architecture and Advisory, a newly forming discovery and scoping function that runs before a build begins. Together they decide whether Scale takes on the right problems and then delivers on them. Advisory helps customers choose and scope their highest-value AI use cases, and Solutions Architecture turns those choices into production systems quickly. You will be accountable for that full arc.

This is a senior operator role, reporting to the Global VP of GTM, Enterprise. The Solutions Architecture team is small today and is planned to double or triple next year, so you will hire managers and front-line leaders and shape how both functions work with each other and with the rest of GTM. An Advisory lead runs day-to-day delivery and builds the engagement model, and you will own the function overall: its direction, guardrails, resourcing and results.

You will need real technical depth, credibility with CTOs, Chief Data Officers and Chief AI Officers, and first-hand experience implementing AI inside a company rather than only advising on it from outside. A forward-deployed background is a strong fit.

## **What you will own**

### **Solutions Architecture**

* Lead, hire and grow the Solutions Architect team supporting Enterprise GTM, including managers and front-line leaders, and own the technical layer of the sales process from use case selection through implementation.
* Partner with Account Executives and GTM leadership to drive bookings and lands, as a hands-on presence inside deal cycles.
* Define what Solutions Architecture becomes at Scale: build the playbook, set the direction, and scale the team from there.
* Set the standards for pre-sales solutioning, proofs of concept and technical validation, and make sure every Solutions Architect is technically proficient in generative AI, LLM and agentic systems.
* Own senior technical relationships with CTOs, chief data officers and chief AI officers through the pre-sales process, as a credible technical voice for Scale.
* Work with Product and Engineering to keep the roadmap, delivery capacity and scoping commitments aligned, and carry customer feedback back to the technical teams. Keep the handoff to delivery clean for the Product Managers and Frontier Agent Engineering teams who build and deploy.
* Measure the team on one outcome: reducing implementation risk and getting customers to production impact as quickly as possible.

### **Advisory**

* Advisory is a structured discovery and scoping phase that runs before a build begins. It reduces risk by getting use case selection, feasibility and scope right up front, instead of finding constraints in the middle of a build.
* Own the Advisory function overall: set direction, guardrails and scope, and hold the function accountable for its goals, while the Advisory lead runs day-to-day delivery and engagements.
* Work with the Advisory lead to formalize engagement models, pricing and go/no-go criteria, and keep Advisory focused on discovery and scoping rather than general AI strategy or consulting.
* Define the profile of an Advisory operator: people who have implemented AI inside a company, not pure consultants. Consulting early in a career is fine; execution experience is what matters.
* Run the early engagements hands-on, refining the model before building a team to delegate to.
* Own the Solutions Architect resourcing for every Advisory engagement (data validation, architecture scoping, dependency mapping and feasibility input).
* Act as the escalation point, resourcing owner and decision-maker on scope and priority trade-offs.

### **Across both functions**

* Land better: stronger upfront use case selection, together with credible technical validation, produces larger and more defensible initial contracts.
* Deliver better: discovery that surfaces data and organizational constraints early means fewer scope changes and a faster path to production.
* Expand faster: a shared roadmap and a trusted technical and advisory relationship give customers a reason to bring Scale into new business units.

## **Ideally you'd have:**

* A strong engineering background and 10 or more years working with customers on technical solutions, before or after the sale.
* First-hand experience implementing AI inside a company, not only advising on it.
* Forward-deployed or closely similar experience, leading people who work inside customer environments.
* At least 5 years leading and developing technical teams, including hiring and developing managers, and experience building a team or a new function from a small base.
* Working knowledge of AI/ML and large language models, hands-on exposure to LLM or agent systems in customer deployments, and development experience in Python, Java or a similar language.
* Senior relationships with CTOs, chief data officers or chief AI officers, and the presence to be credible in a room with them.
* Experience working across product, engineering, finance, legal, sales and operations in ambiguous situations, and comfort setting direction and guardrails for a function that others run day to day.
* A self-starter's approach to resolving technical problems with the customer, curiosity about customer needs, empathy, and the ability to move quickly.

## **Nice to haves:**

* Experience scoping or delivering large technical solutions. Contract values in the nine figures are a plus.
* Experience scaling a solutions engineering or similar customer-facing technical team.
* Experience designing and pricing paid discovery or scoping engagements.
* Prior work as a technical go-to-market resource.
* Startup experience.
* Experience with enterprise customers in financial services, insurance, healthcare or other regulated industries.
