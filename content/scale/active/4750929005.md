Every AI model is shaped by its training data. Scale's Quality team helps decide whether that data is good enough to ship to the world's leading AI labs, and in this role you'll drive that decision for a portfolio of our highest-impact GenAI programs.

As a Data Quality & Operations Lead, you'll operate in a GM-style role: you'll set the quality bar, manage your vertical's costs, and figure out why quality moved before anyone asks. You'll lead a large team of coding specialists who measure and improve data quality across these programs, from agentic RL environments to multilingual post-training data. These are frontier-level problems. The programs you support train the models people are talking about.

You'll hold delivery teams accountable across these programs, helping solve nuanced quality issues and leading strategic initiatives that enable reliable, scalable operations across the organization.

**What you'll do**

* **Manage the quality system.** Own day-to-day operations: plan and allocate workforce capacity, produce quality reporting, measure productivity and efficiency, and drive quality control policies.
* **Lead a team of coding specialists.** Oversee hiring, coaching, and performance management for a growing team.
* **Turn unclear customer requirements into clear standards.** Work with delivery teams to interpret and clarify customer requirements, then translate them into measurable signals of data quality.
* **Control the QC budget.** Make tradeoffs between coverage, cost, and risk, with real customers and real money on the line.
* **Get ahead of quality trends.** Identify, root-cause, and surface defect patterns that delivery teams can act on.
* **Build the future of QC.** Our team already uses agentic capabilities; you'll help decide what gets built next and lead the work to keep scaling and advancing quality operations.

**Ideally you'd have**

* A Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, AI/ML, Engineering, Operations, or another STEM field
* 3+ years of experience leading teams and scaling large operational or technical processes
* Hands-on experience in top-tier management consulting, strategy & operations, product management, or program management
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, particularly with SQL and AI development tools, and close attention to detail
* Experience building systems, processes, and teams across multiple customer segments or product lines
* Proven ability to take initiative in cross-functional technical environments
* Deep experience owning and improving operational metrics end to end

**Nice to have**

* Master's degree in Computer Science, AI/ML, Engineering, or Operations
* Experience leading teams in AI/ML services, data annotation, or model evaluation
* Experience in a fast-growing, less-structured startup environment
* Experience building teams from the ground up
