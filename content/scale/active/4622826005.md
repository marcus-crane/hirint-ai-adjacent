Scale AI is hiring a Mission Product Specialist to shape the product strategy behind Thunderforge's ModSim and planning-tool integrations across our strategic partners. This role sits at the intersection of joint military planning and product judgment — you'll define how the tool chain should evolve for planners at PACOM, EUCOM, NORTHCOM, and other commands, and stay close enough to the technical build to keep that strategy grounded in reality.

You're a strong fit if you combine deep Joint Planning Process (JPP) credibility, a strategic eye for product and planner workflows, and enough hands-on ModSim/technical fluency to support the build when needed.

**You will:**  
Own and support product strategy for Thunderforge's ModSim and planning-tool integrations, bringing JPP expertise and planner insight to roadmap and prioritization discussions  
Propose process and product improvements based on planner workflows, feedback, and adoption metrics  
Partner with product teams to help define and improve observability and telemetry for the program  
Represent PACOM, EUCOM, NORTHCOM, and other strategic partner's needs through proactive communication and detail-oriented execution  
Work with product and engineering teams to surface integration and reliability gaps that inform strategy and priorities  
Support hands-on troubleshooting and maintenance of ModSim and data-integration environments  
Help triage and diagnose incidents, contributing to postmortems and status visibility through resolution
