Scale is looking for an experienced leader to own Business Finance across our fastest-growing business units: Enterprise, Public Sector, and Global Public Sector. This role will be instrumental in setting strategy, financial plans, and operating rigor as we accelerate the adoption of AI across many industries. This is a hands-on role for someone who wants to know the business inside out, from the model to the boardroom.

As the company matures, you’ll build best-in-class finance processes. You’ll own the operating plan and forecast for these business units and partner closely with the Executive Team on real-time, high-priority business issues and critical decisions. The ideal candidate has the business acumen and technical skills to back up their recommendations and build strong cross-functional relationships.

**You will:**

* Own Business Finance end to end, including planning, forecasting, and reporting for the Enterprise, Public Sector, and Global Public Sector businesses
* Lead, grow, and develop a high-performing strategic finance team and shape the structure of the team around each business unit as the organization scales
* Be a true partner to BU leaders: get deep into how each business works, own the top-line and bottom-line story, and help leaders make the tradeoffs that drive business outcomes
* Lead the preparation of financial analysis, business insights, and presentation materials for regular financial and business updates to the Executive Team
* Partner with accounting to support month-end close, flux review processes, and deliver high-quality financial reporting
* Own the financial models behind forecasting, budgeting, and cash burn. Set the methodology and track the metrics that keep forecasts accurate
* Design, automate, and optimize flexible finance processes to meet the needs of a dynamic and fast-growing business environment
* Work with cross-functional teams to develop and prioritize strategic initiatives that drive revenue growth, cost optimization, and operational efficiency

**Ideally you'd have:**

* 10+ years of finance experience, including leadership roles in Strategic Finance
* Background in investment banking, private equity, hedge funds, or growth equity strongly preferred
* Experience scaling finance in a high-growth, multi-BU technology company
* Proven ability to streamline processes and solve operational inefficiencies
* Excellent project management skills and fluency with large data sets
* Strong executive presence and interpersonal skills; ability to lead discussions with the Executive Team
* Track record of building and managing finance teams

*This role requires current U.S. citizenship to support Scale’s Public Sector business.*
