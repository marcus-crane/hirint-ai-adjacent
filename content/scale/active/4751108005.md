Scale’s rapidly growing Global Public Sector team is focused on using AI to address critical challenges facing the public sector around the world. Our core work consists of:

* Creating custom AI applications that will impact millions of citizens
* Generating high-quality training data for national LLMs
* Upskilling and advisory services to spread the impact of AI

We are looking for an entrepreneurial and experienced product leader to play a pivotal role in the ideation and development of transformative AI solutions for government and government-backed customers across the Middle East. The ideal candidate has deep experience building AI/ML applications, thinks strategically about how to solve a problem, is an excellent listener, is comfortable getting into the weeds, and has a strong understanding of software engineering principles and practices. You will own large AI projects end to end, working directly with customers and leading a cross-functional team of engineers, MLEs, and operators to deliver solutions that drive meaningful impact for our customers and significant revenue for our business.

**You will:**

* Lead design workshops with clients to define custom AI solutions
* Scope new AI application use cases across government entities, and turn them into a clear roadmap, phasing, and success metrics
* Build MVPs and demos to validate ideas quickly, and take the best of them to production
* Lead cross-functional development with diverse stakeholders (Engineering, MLE, Operations, and Go-to-Market)
* Engage consistently with end users to gather feedback and ensure we are prioritizing effectively
* Scope model evaluation sets and performance requirements, review results, and iterate on the solution
* Stay up to date with the latest research in applied AI and training custom LLMs
* Give regular progress updates to the client and Global Public Sector leadership

**Ideally, you’d have:**

* 4+ years of experience building products, including 1–2+ years building AI-powered products in production
* Strong technical background (STEM degree) and/or experience building technical software products
* Hands-on experience with LLMs and applied AI, and a strong understanding of generative AI in enterprise settings
* Sharp product sense and customer instinct: the conviction to take a position and defend it, and the judgment to change it when the evidence says so
* Comfort with client-facing work and the pace and ambiguity of a forward-deployed team
* Experience with vibe coding tools (e.g., Replit, Lovable, Bolt) and design tools (e.g., Figma, Canva, Miro)
* Exceptional leadership, presentation, and communication skills, with the ability to influence cross-functional teams

**Nice to haves:**

* Proficiency in Arabic, both written and spoken
* Coding experience (Python)
* Prior experience as a founder, founding engineer, or forward-deployed PM
* Experience working with government or GCC customers
