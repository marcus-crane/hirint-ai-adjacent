## **About the team**

Scale's Applied Intelligence Systems team builds the enterprise agent platform (SGP). It is the foundation that lets enterprises build, run, govern and trust AI agents in production, on any major cloud and inside customer environments. Our work spans how agents are built and orchestrated, how they run securely, how they remember and reason over enterprise data, how they're evaluated and observed, and the infrastructure that keeps all of it reliable. Models and frameworks change every week. We build the layer that runs and governs them.

## **Sample projects**

* **Building agents.** The SDK, harness and deploy pipeline that take an agent from commit to a signed, versioned agent in a customer's environment, plus multi-agent orchestration where parent agents delegate to children, pause and resume.
* **Running agents safely.** Isolated sandboxes, identity that proves who an agent acts for, and a gateway that checks permissions and audits every tool call, with credentials agents never hold.
* **Reliability at scale.** Kubernetes fleets, upgrades and disaster recovery across AWS, Azure and GCP, and proving the platform can absorb 10x agent workload.
* **Tooling for every deployment.** A fleet control plane, a trusted image registry, and the CI/CD and release gates that carry every change to customers.
* **Observability.** Shared instrumentation and fleet-wide health views, with sensitive data redacted before it's stored.

## **What you'll do**

* Own and set the technical direction for large areas of the platform, from design through production, on-call and iteration.
* Write the designs that cross team boundaries, and lead the reviews that raise the bar around you.
* Be the named technical owner of a production-critical system, accountable for its reliability, security and roadmap.
* Build across backend and infrastructure, working directly with LLMs and agent frameworks, and learn the next technology as the ecosystem moves.
* Turn what the industry ships each week into something customers can use in days, not quarters.
* Mentor engineers and partner with product, research and field teams.

## **What you bring**

* **8+ years of full-time engineering experience.**
* **Production AI or agent experience.** An LLM or agent sat in the critical path of something you built and ran. You've seen how these systems fail in real use and design around it.
* **Deep Python.** Python is our primary language. We write modern, typed, async Python and build on the latest AI and agent tooling.
* **Cloud experience.** You've built and run production systems on AWS, Azure or GCP, ideally more than one, or in customer-hosted environments.
* **Distributed systems depth.** You have strong opinions on failure modes, idempotency, multi-tenancy and correctness.

## **Strong plus**

* **Hyper-growth startup experience.** You've scaled a product or platform through rapid growth in customers, load and team size, and you know what breaks first. Founders and early engineers are especially welcome.
* **Hands-on Kubernetes.** You've deployed and operated production services on Kubernetes, and you can debug rollouts, scaling and networking beyond a Helm chart.
* **Agent platform building blocks.** Workflow or orchestration engines, sandboxed code execution, identity and permissions, tracing and evals.
* **Languages beyond Python.** We also work in Go and TypeScript.
