As an Account Executive (AE) supporting the Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA), you will bridge the gap between cutting-edge AI research and mission-critical production in the intelligence domain. You will lead the charge in deploying agentic AI, meaning autonomous, reasoning systems, into operational analytic workflows.

We are looking for an executive account leader who understands that winning at DIA is not about a successful six-month prototype. It is about getting into program budgets. Scale already has proven programs across the Department of War (DoW) and in intelligence-adjacent work. Your mandate is to take what works, bring it to your buying centers, and convert it into funded, multi-year DIA business.

This is an individual contributor role with heavy leadership expectations. You are the CEO of your territory: one accountable owner for the customer and the commercial result.

### **You will:**

* **Own the territory and the number.** Carry and exceed an annual bookings target tied to your territory. You own the full lifecycle: identifying opportunity, shaping requirements, converting to a funded award, and expanding. You build and maintain a qualified pipeline of at least 3x your target.
* **Replicate what is proven.** Bring proven Scale offers to new DIA buying centers instead of starting custom pilots. You qualify hard: we pursue work that expands or connects an existing program, or that has a clear funding path.
* **Turn proof into programs.** Secure executive sponsorship and programmed funding (POM, program-office, and production vehicles) so prototypes survive the valley of death and become deployed capability.
* **Tactical execution**. You don’t just delegate. You personally draft white papers and OTA responses, lead complex proposal development, architect deal structures, and navigate the nuances of defense and intelligence acquisition.
* **Renewal, expansion, and growth.** Own the long-term account strategy across DIA. Defend renewals, maintain executive relationships, identify the next use case, and secure follow-on funding that turns a single deployment into an enterprise standard.
* **Partner with delivery.** Delivery owns execution; you own the commercial relationship. You stay close to mission outcomes and customer health, and you surface risk early, working with Engagement Managers, Technical PMs, Engineers, Product, and Customer Success.
* **Direct agentic solutioning.** Work with your Solutions Engineer and analysts to co-design agentic AI workflows. You translate an analyst’s mission problem into a specific technical requirement our engineers can build against.
* **SI and prime orchestration**. With Scale’s Partner Managers, negotiate with Tier 1 systems integrators and primes so Scale is the agentic AI engine inside their larger programs. You are the lead negotiator on subcontracting and teaming agreements.
* **Team selling.** Work with Capture, who shape demand ahead of you, and with peer AEs in adjacent territories. You deconflict before engaging an account a peer owns.

### **Ideally you'd have:**

* **Experience:** 7+ years across intelligence operations, government acquisitions, complex enterprise IT sales, or defense business development. Time in uniform or as a government civilian in the intelligence community counts heavily.
* **Proven track record:** You have closed and implemented multi-year deals and know how to get a line item into the budget.
* **Domain expertise:** Deep understanding of DIA’s modernization priorities and where AI/ML, computer vision, and automated analysis apply to the intelligence mission.
* **Valley of Death navigator:** Demonstrated ability to move technology from “cool demo” to “deployed capability” inside a major program office or intelligence customer.
* **Clearance:** Active TS/SCI required.
* Geography: Washington, DC, with the ability to work on-site or with the customer three days a week.
* **Travel:** Ability to travel at least 30% of the time.

### **Nice to haves:**

* **Strategic network:** Existing relationships across DIA headquarters and mission offices, plus relationships at the major primes and integrators.
* **Education:** BS/BA required; technical degree or MBA preferred.

### **Who You Are**

* **Mission-driven:** You believe whoever wins in AI wins the next century, and you want that to be the United States and its allies.
* **Relentless:** You thrive in the long game of defense procurement. Bureaucracy doesn’t discourage you; you see it as a puzzle to be solved.
* **Adaptive:** You can operate in the high-velocity environment of a fast-growing company while keeping the polish required in front of a senior intelligence leader.
* **The closer-operator:** You have the executive presence to brief a senior DIA leader and the stamina to spend 10 hours in a SCIF on a proposal response.
* **Radically accountable:** You don’t wait for a marketing lead or a BD specialist to hand you a deal. You build your own funnel, navigate the “no’s,” and find the creative path to “yes.”
* **Technically credible:** You aren’t a software engineer, but you can explain the difference between RAG and agentic reasoning to a skeptical technical evaluator.
