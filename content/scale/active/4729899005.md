Scale's Data Analytics team is centralized, embedding data analysts across delivery, operations, finance, and engineering to turn ambiguous problems into clear, actionable insights. As a Senior Data Analyst for Public Sector, you'll build analytics solutions from the ground up - partnering closely with cross-functional teams and leadership to solve complex problems, design dashboards, build tables, and perform ad-hoc analyses that shape how Scale runs and measures its Public Sector business.  
  
We're looking for someone who's relentlessly curious, comfortable owning ambiguity, and skilled at turning cross-functional relationships into sustainable, scalable analytics solutions.  
  
***Active Secret U.S. security clearance required*****You will:**

* Own data quality for your domain - document models, troubleshoot discrepancies, and establish centralized metric definitions
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams to identify, define, and track key metrics to be used in monitoring, planning, and one-off decision-making
* Develop repeatable, scalable analytical solutions, such as dashboards, data models, or standard processes
* Partner with Ops to identify and track efficiency initiatives, measuring their impact on cost, quality, and delivery outcomes over time
* Partner with Finance to analyze cost drivers across programs and help inform pricing strategy
* Be willing to deepdive when needed and dig into the messy details to get to the root of a problem
* Initiate and drive complex projects to completion with minimal guidance

**Ideally you'd have:**

* 6+ years of relevant work experience
* Advanced SQL skills and experience building with data modeling tools (e.g. DBT)
* Experience building dashboards and reports with BI tools (e.g. Tableau, Metabase)
* Strong business acumen - comfortable partnering directly with cross-functional stakeholders
* Excellent communication skills - able to translate technical analysis into clear, actionable narratives
* Comfort with ambiguity in a fast-moving environment and able to make sound trade-offs
* Active Secret U.S. security clearance is required; Top Secret U.S. clearance is preferred

**Nice to haves:**

* Familiarity working within air-gapped/federal environments and navigating their constraints
* Prior experience supporting a Business Operations function, with exposure to process analytics or process mining (e.g. workflow optimization)
