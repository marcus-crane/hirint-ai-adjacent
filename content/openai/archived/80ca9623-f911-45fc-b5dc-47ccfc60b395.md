**About the team**

This team is crucial in building and maintaining OpenAI's brand identity, ensuring a consistent and impactful brand presence both inside the company and to the world. We bridge product development, communications and marketing, weaving cohesive storytelling and brand representation across all touchpoints through our innovative design and strategic communication.

Communicating the advances and benefits of AGI is one of the most important works at OpenAI, and our goal is to do it beautifully, accessibly, and with transparency and authenticity. The addition of a Visual Designer specializing in presentations will strengthen our ability to tell compelling stories and explain complex concepts through visually engaging means

**About the role**

* Develop and refine presentation designs for major OpenAI events, supporting the communication needs of leadership and sales teams.
* Create and maintain a library of design systems and templates for presentations, accessible to all teams across OpenAI.
* Push the boundaries of presentation design by integrating innovative, motion, and interactive elements.
* Enhance our technology's explanation through intelligent, concept-driven, and aesthetically appealing visuals.
* Collaborate closely with other designers and departments, ensuring a cohesive and effective visual communication strategy.
* Act as a key contributor to OpenAI's mission, helping to make complex AI concepts understandable and engaging through visual storytelling.

* Opportunity to work on prominent projects, influencing the representation of a leading AI research and deployment company

**You Might Thrive in This Role if You**

* Have a Bachelor's degree in Design or a related field.
* Have 8+ years of professional experience in visual design, with a focus on presentation design.
* Have a strong portfolio demonstrating expertise in creating engaging and innovative presentations.
* Are proficient in Keynote, Google Slides, and other relevant design software.
* Have experience in developing and managing design systems and templates.
* Have the ability to work collaboratively in a fast-paced and evolving environment

* Work collaboratively, balancing the needs of stakeholders and simplifying complex ideas into compelling concepts.
* Have a deep appreciation for aesthetics, user experience, and the ethical aspects of design in AI.
* Are detail-oriented, and process driven, from concept to final output.
* Can inspire and sustain a culture of excellence, partnering with producers, program managers, and product marketing managers.
* Collaborate with creatives, researchers, product managers, product marketers, and engineers to serve a global audience.

**Nice to Have**

* Skills in additional areas such as web development, motion design, or type design.
* Capability to lead creative projects with a clear vision and innovative approach.
* A passion for continuous learning and improvement in the field of design and visual communication.
* Experience working with data & insights.
* Experience collaborating with or working on a product team.
* Demonstrate creative vision, purpose, and innovation.
* First management experience in leading projects, small internal or external teams
* Sense of humor and passion for continuous learning and improvement.
