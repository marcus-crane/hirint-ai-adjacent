**About the Role**

OpenAI’s in-house Creative Team is looking for a Motion Design Producer to help shape and scale how our brand comes to life through motion design across every touchpoint — from identity systems and campaigns to product, web, and comms work.

You’ll partner closely with our Brand Identity & Brand Design leadership to manage intake, resourcing, timelines, and creative workflows — ensuring design projects move seamlessly from concept to delivery. You’ll serve as the operational backbone of the design practice, building clarity and momentum across a growing cross-functional team. As Motion Design is a shared function in Creative Design, you will also partner closely with other production functional ecosystems to deliver motion design support.

This is a highly collaborative role that requires both strategic oversight and hands-on execution. You’ll be a key connector across Marketing, Product, Comms, and Web — driving alignment, enabling great design, and creating the systems that keep our creative work consistent, efficient, and impactful.

**In this role, you will:**

* Manage Day-to-day Motion Design Operations: Oversee the end-to-end management of design projects — from scoping and prioritization to delivery — ensuring resources, timelines, and dependencies are well-planned and communicated.
* Produce High-Priority Motion Projects: Serve as a hands-on individual contributor (IC) on large, high-priority motion projects, owning production from kickoff through delivery.
* Shape Intake & Resourcing: Partner with creative and marketing, creative, production and ops leads to evaluate new requests, define scope, and allocate design capacity across internal and external partners.
* Build Cross-Functional Alignment: Act as the connective tissue between design and partner teams (Product, Web, Brand, Comms, and GTM), ensuring design work ladders up to broader strategic goals.
* Elevate Systems & Process: Design and implement operational frameworks that enable the design team to scale effectively — from creative reviews and feedback loops to asset tracking and tool optimization.
* Manage External Partners: Oversee relationships with design vendors, freelancers, and production partners; ensure output meets OpenAI’s creative and technical standards.
* Uphold Craft & Consistency: Partner with the Brand Identity and Brand Design team to ensure design work is cohesive, high-quality, and reflective of OpenAI’s evolving visual identity.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have 8+ years of experience in design production, brand operations, or creative program management within an in-house team or agency environment.
* Are fluent in the design process — from concept and iteration through production and delivery — and can anticipate what a creative team needs to succeed. Should be experienced in full identity explorations and production needs.
* Are a natural systems-builder who can bring order to complexity without stifling creativity.
* Excel at stakeholder management and communication — you’re equally comfortable talking to designers, executives, and engineers.
* Are proactive, unflappable, and skilled at driving clarity in fast-moving environments.
* Are fluent in tools like Asana, Airtable, Figma, and Google Workspace.
* Have experience building or scaling global brand systems.
* Have familiarity with motion, 3D, or experiential design production.
* Have a strong understanding of creative operations in a technology-driven or design-led organization.
