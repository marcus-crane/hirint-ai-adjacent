**About the Team**

OpenAI’s Corporate Security team protects our people, places, and operations so that employees can safely pursue our mission to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity. The team partners across the company to enable a secure, resilient, and trusted environment that supports both safety and innovation.

We operate in dynamic, high-visibility environments, including dense urban offices and major events where discretion, credibility, and sound judgment are essential. The Corporate Security team works closely with Real Estate & Workplace, Legal, Communications, HR, IT, and external partners to maintain a proactive and people-first security posture.

**About the Role**

The CorpSec Program Manager plays a critical role in the advancement, coordination, and maturity of OpenAI’s global corporate security operations. This position is responsible for managing and designing scalable, cross-functional programs that protect OpenAI’s people, facilities, and critical infrastructure.

This role requires exceptional program management skills, strong cross-functional collaboration, and the ability to lead multiple complex initiatives simultaneously. Sitting within the Special Projects function, the position owns a defined portfolio across Global Operations, Protection, and Special Projects, including operational governance, protection-related initiatives, project delivery, documentation, and coordination with internal and external partners.

**In This Role, You Will:**

* Lead the development and global implementation of Corporate Security programs, runbooks, SOPs, and decision-making frameworks.
* Oversee and coordinate initiatives across all Corporate Security pillars, ensuring seamless communication, accountability, and documentation.
* Manage multiple high-impact projects simultaneously, ensuring milestones are met and outcomes are aligned with strategic security and business objectives.
* Define and track key performance indicators (KPIs) for security protection programs to ensure accountability and continuous improvement.
* Partner with internal teams, Legal, REW, IT, People, and Communications, to ensure operational readiness, compliance, and integration with organizational priorities.
* Coordinate security vendor and contract-service programs, including documentation, stakeholder alignment, service expectations, and operational follow-through.
* Collaborate with Global Ops and Protection teams to develop and scale programs supporting Corporate Security initiatives and driving consistent planning, governance, and operational readiness across the organization.
* Develop and manage documentation, communications, and training materials that strengthen awareness, preparedness, and operational consistency.
* Identify and drive opportunities to scale and improve operational efficiency through process optimization and technology integration.
* Contribute to the development of a resilient, inclusive, and high-performing security culture by mentoring team members and reinforcing best practices.
* Support Corporate Security through procurement coordination ensuring timely acquisition of goods and services, accurate documentation, and alignment with internal financial policies.

**You Might Thrive in This Role If You Have:**

* 7+ years of relevant experience in security operations, program management, or a related discipline within corporate, government, or high-technology environments.
* Proven experience managing complex, multi-stakeholder programs that span geographies and disciplines.
* Strong understanding of physical security systems, vendor management, and operational governance frameworks.
* Demonstrated success developing and implementing SOPs and operational standards for diverse teams.
* Exceptional organizational skills with the ability to manage competing priorities, timelines, and deliverables with precision and accountability.
* Excellent communication and collaboration skills, capable of influencing and aligning cross-functional partners.
* Experience building and scaling programs through process design, governance, and change management, creating operational structures that improve consistency, visibility, and execution across teams.
* A proactive, solution-oriented mindset with the ability to anticipate challenges, manage ambiguity, and deliver results independently.
* A people-first approach to leadership and a commitment to fostering inclusion, mentorship, and operational excellence.
* A high level of proficiency in Google Workspace.
* Experience and aptitude using OpenAI’s Chat and Codex models.
