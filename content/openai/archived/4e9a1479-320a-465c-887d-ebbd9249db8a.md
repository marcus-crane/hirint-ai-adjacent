**About the Team**

OpenAI didn’t begin as a traditional company. It began as an idea: that artificial intelligence could be developed in a way that benefits everyone. As a creative team, our role is to help make sure the work behind that idea is understood, as it leaves the lab and meets the world.  
This company works at the frontier of intelligence. Like any frontier, it’s unfinished, constantly shifting, and still being explored. Our job is to stay close to that uncertainty and help shape how this story gets told, in a way that feels grounded and human. We do this through campaigns, launches, films, brand systems, and work that doesn’t fit neatly into any of those categories yet. This team is for people who want to help shape something from the beginning.

**About the Role**

We’re looking for a designer to help shape OpenAI.com. This role is responsible for designing exceptional web experiences that bring our brand, mission, and products to life for a global audience. You’ll work closely with engineering, brand design, and marketing to build and evolve OpenAI.com into a world-class destination.

**In this role, you will:**

* Design pages and user flows that help people explore OpenAI’s research, understand our products, and find what they need on OpenAI.com.
* Work from early sketches and wireframes through interactive prototypes and high-fidelity designs, refining the work as you learn what’s clear and where people get stuck.
* Shape the story of a page with writers, marketers, and brand designers, using layout, typography, and interaction to bring the content to life.
* Partner with engineers through launch to refine how each experience looks, feels, and works across screen sizes, with attention to accessibility and performance.
* Build on the web design system and work with brand and engineering to turn recurring design needs into reusable components and patterns.
* Share work in progress, explain your design decisions, and give other designers specific feedback in critique.

**You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* 8+ years of experience designing large-scale, consumer-facing websites or web products.
* A knack for organizing complex information into pages and navigation that people can find their way around.
* Experience working with engineers, marketers, writers, and brand designers to take web projects from concept to launch.
* The ability to connect the purpose of a page to the details of its design, from the overall flow to typography, spacing, and interaction states.
* Curiosity about OpenAI’s work and a willingness to share early ideas, seek feedback, and revise a design.
* Experience designing for modern web technologies and content management systems.
* An eye for when motion or a small interaction can help someone understand what’s changed or what to do next.
* Experience using research, analytics, or A/B tests to make design decisions.
