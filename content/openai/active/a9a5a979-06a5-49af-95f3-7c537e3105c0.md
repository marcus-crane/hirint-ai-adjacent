**About the Team**

The Agent Identity team builds the foundational identity infrastructure that enables AI agents to securely act on behalf of users and organizations. We develop the systems that allow agents to establish their identity, demonstrate whose authority they are operating under, and securely access resources across applications and environments.

Our team owns agent identity and delegation, authentication and authorization, credential lifecycle management, revocation, and integrations with external platforms. We also build Sign in with ChatGPT, enabling OpenAI to serve as an identity provider across a growing ecosystem of applications.

As AI agents become increasingly autonomous, our work is foundational to how users, organizations, and developers establish trust in agentic systems.

**About the Role**

We are looking for experienced software engineers to design and build the identity infrastructure powering the next generation of AI agents across OpenAI’s products, including ChatGPT and Codex.

You will tackle complex, largely unsolved engineering challenges at the intersection of identity, security, distributed systems, and agent infrastructure. You’ll help define how agents authenticate across environments, how users securely delegate authority to agents, and how agents interact with external applications and services.

This is an opportunity to shape foundational technical primitives at OpenAI while helping influence how the broader industry approaches identity in an increasingly agent-driven world.

**In this role, you will:**

* Design and build foundational agent identity primitives, including agent authentication, delegated authorization, and credential lifecycle management.
* Develop secure mechanisms that enable agents to act on behalf of users and organizations across internal and external systems.
* Architect authentication and authorization flows for connectors, third-party applications, and cloud-based agent execution environments.
* Build and evolve Sign in with ChatGPT, enabling secure identity federation and interoperability across the broader application ecosystem.
* Design highly scalable, reliable, and secure distributed systems that support credential issuance, rotation, revocation, and access enforcement.
* Partner closely with engineering, product, security, and external platform teams to solve emerging identity challenges and help shape industry standards.
* Drive technical direction and own complex initiatives from initial architecture through production deployment.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have experience designing, building, and operating large-scale distributed systems or business-critical backend infrastructure.
* Have deep technical experience in identity and access management, workload identity, identity providers, authentication, authorization, or credential lifecycle management.
* Alternatively, have experience building cloud-based AI agents, agent execution environments, browser-based automation, or connector infrastructure, with firsthand exposure to identity and authentication challenges.
* Have familiarity with identity and authorization protocols such as OAuth 2.0, OpenID Connect, or related emerging standards.
* Bring a security-first mindset and proactively consider trust boundaries, failure modes, and potential abuse when designing systems.
* Thrive in ambiguous problem spaces where established technical patterns may not yet exist, and enjoy defining solutions from first principles.
* Have a track record of driving complex technical initiatives, influencing architectural decisions, and collaborating effectively across engineering teams.
* Are excited about defining how identity, trust, and delegated authority will work in a world where AI agents increasingly act on our behalf.
