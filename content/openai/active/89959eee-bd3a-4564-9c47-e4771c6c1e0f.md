**About the Team**

The ChatGPT Business Growth team helps businesses discover ChatGPT, bring their teams on board, and make AI part of their everyday work. We build experiences that help owners, admins, and members get lasting value from ChatGPT, from creating a Business workspace to sharing useful work, building habits, and expanding adoption across a team.

**About the Role**

We’re looking for an experienced, hands-on Product Manager to own a meaningful area of growth for ChatGPT’s self-serve Business offering. You’ll set the strategy, define the roadmap, and work closely with engineering, design, data science, and go-to-market partners to turn customer needs into products that drive measurable growth.

Depending on the charter, your work may focus on helping businesses choose and start with ChatGPT, bringing more teammates into a workspace, deepening adoption and retention, or reaching small businesses through useful workflows and distribution partners. Across these areas, the goal is the same: help more teams get value from AI and build a durable business around that value.

This is an individual contributor role with end-to-end ownership of a defined growth outcome. You’ll combine product judgment, customer insight, and rigorous experimentation to decide what to build, align teams around the opportunity, and take ideas from early tests to scaled experiences.

This role is based in San Francisco, CA Or New York, NYC. We use a hybrid work model of 3 days in the office per week and offer relocation assistance to new employees.

**In this role, you will:**

* Own the product strategy, roadmap, and results for your growth area, translating an ambitious goal into clear priorities and a practical plan.
* Understand the needs of business owners, admins, and members through customer conversations, product data, and close partnership with design and go-to-market teams.
* Build and improve experiences that help businesses get started, invite colleagues, adopt useful workflows, and keep finding value in ChatGPT.
* Identify new growth opportunities, including tailored experiences and distribution paths that help small businesses adopt ChatGPT.
* Define success metrics and design experiments that distinguish incremental growth from activity that would have happened anyway. Use what you learn to scale, iterate, or stop an approach.
* Lead execution with engineering, design, and data science, making thoughtful tradeoffs between speed, customer experience, and long-term impact.
* Align with core product, monetization, marketing, sales, and partnerships teams on shared goals and dependencies. Drive outcomes across surfaces and capabilities that span team boundaries.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have 6+ years of product management experience, including a track record of delivering measurable growth for self-serve SaaS, business, or team-based products.
* Have owned a meaningful growth problem from strategy through launch and iteration, with depth in acquisition, activation, expansion, retention, or product-led distribution.
* Are highly analytical and fluent in experimentation, with the judgment to connect funnel and cohort metrics to real customer value.
* Have strong product sense and empathy for business users, and can turn a complex customer journey into a simple, useful experience.
* Work effectively with technical and nontechnical partners, communicate clearly, and influence decisions without relying on formal authority.
* Are comfortable with ambiguity, move quickly, and stay hands-on as you learn what works and adapt your plans.
* Are motivated by OpenAI’s mission and excited to help businesses use AI in their everyday work.
