**About the Team**

The Applied AI Architect team partners with organizations to turn OpenAI's most capable models into meaningful, real-world impact. We work with customers across industries to identify where AI can create value, design secure and scalable solutions, and help those solutions move from early exploration into sustained production adoption. The team brings together technical strategy, customer partnership, and practical deployment expertise, working closely with Sales, Product, Engineering, Research, and specialist delivery teams.

**About the Role**

As Head of Applied AI Architects for Commercial Sales, you will lead OpenAI’s global Applied AI Architect (AAA) organization for Midmarket and SMB. You will lead through frontline managers and own the technical strategy, coverage model, and customer outcomes across both segments, from discovery and evaluation through production adoption and expansion.

In **Midmarket**, you will align AAAs closely with Sales and focus hands-on technical support on opportunities where it can materially improve the outcome. In **SMB**, you will build a model that reaches more customers through repeatable deployment practices, self-serve technical resources, and partner delivery, with a clear path to specialist support.  
  
**Location:** Austin, TX orSan Francisco, CA

**In this role, you will:**

* Hire, coach, and develop AAA managers across Midmarket and SMB, setting a consistent bar for technical judgment, customer partnership, and execution.
* Partner with Commercial Sales leadership to prioritize accounts, plan territory coverage, and allocate technical capacity. Set clear criteria for dedicated, pooled, and partner-led support.
* Set technical standards for how teams qualify use cases, run evaluations, and guide customers into production across ChatGPT, Codex, and the OpenAI API. Set expectations for technical account plans, including milestones for production readiness and adoption.
* Sponsor critical customer engagements and coordinate with Enablement, Delivery, and partners so customers have a clear owner and next step.
* Track technical coverage, technical wins, deployment risks, time to production, adoption, and contribution to revenue growth. Use those signals to decide where to invest capacity and which practices to scale.
* Bring recurring customer needs and deployment blockers to Product and Engineering.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have led managers and scaled customer-facing technical teams across multiple regions or customer segments.
* Have built coverage models that combine hands-on technical engagement with pooled, self-serve, and partner-led support.
* Build credibility with executives and engineers and coach teams through architecture, security, evaluation, and deployment decisions. Know when to step into a critical technical decision, when to coach through your managers, and when to bring in specialists.
* Have helped customers move AI or complex software into production and can show measurable gains in adoption or business outcomes.
