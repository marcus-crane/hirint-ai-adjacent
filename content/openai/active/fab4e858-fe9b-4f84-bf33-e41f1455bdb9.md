**About the Team**

Ads Marketing Science helps advertisers prove and improve their media performance. We build credible evidence of business impact, turn that evidence into better advertiser decisions, and develop the first- and third-party measurement solutions, insights, and programs that help Sales and clients succeed at scale.

**About the Role**

We’re looking for a Marketing Science Lead to build and lead our approach to advertiser measurement across Asia Pacific. Reporting to Marketing Science leadership, you’ll own the regional strategy and priorities, serve as a senior advisor to advertisers and agencies, and represent APAC’s needs in our global measurement roadmap.

This is a hands-on leadership role with broad ownership. You’ll decide where Marketing Science can have the greatest impact, establish how we support different markets and clients, and carry work from problem definition through implementation, adoption, and demonstrated value. You’ll work closely with regional Sales leadership and global Product, Engineering, Data Science, Partnerships, Legal, and Privacy teams.

Success means more credible evidence of advertiser impact, better media decisions, and regional capabilities that scale beyond individual engagements. You’ll combine strategic judgment and executive influence with the ability to get into the data and build.

**In this role, you will:**

* Own the APAC Marketing Science strategy. Set the regional learning agenda, prioritize markets and advertiser needs, and make clear tradeoffs between strategic client work, measurement development, and scaled support.
* Lead senior advertiser and agency relationships. Work with marketing, analytics, and business leaders to define what success looks like, resolve complex measurement questions, and translate evidence into investment and optimization decisions.
* Build first- and third-party measurement capabilities. Partner with global teams and regional providers across verification, attribution, incrementality, brand lift, sales lift, visitation, and marketing mix modeling (MMM), adapting approaches to local data availability and market needs.
* Set the standard for measurement quality. Guide study design, power analysis, diagnostics, and interpretation. Distinguish attributed outcomes from causal impact, communicate uncertainty clearly, and turn findings into practical improvements and follow-up tests.
* Shape the partner and product roadmap. Identify APAC capability gaps, prioritize partner opportunities with Partnerships, and turn recurring advertiser needs into clear requirements for Product, Engineering, and Data Science.
* Scale Marketing Science support. Build reusable programs, playbooks, insights, tools, agents, and automated workflows that help Sales and clients answer common questions with consistent quality and appropriate privacy protections.
* Build regional measurement credibility. Equip Sales with clear guidance and evidence, develop useful case studies and best practices, and represent OpenAI in senior partner and industry conversations.
* Develop regional capability. Establish ways of working across markets and time zones, mentor colleagues, and help shape future hiring and coverage priorities as the business grows.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Bring 10+ years of relevant experience in advertising measurement, marketing science, or a related field, with a demonstrated record of leading strategy and delivering results across multiple APAC markets.
* Have independently built and led a regional measurement program or substantial cross-functional initiative, including setting priorities, making resource tradeoffs, and taking accountability for adoption and advertiser value.
* Are a trusted advisor to senior advertisers and agencies, with experience turning complex or inconclusive evidence into clear recommendations and influencing consequential business decisions.
* Bring deep expertise in one or two measurement disciplines and strong working fluency across the broader landscape. You can evaluate methodological tradeoffs, identify limitations, and engage specialists effectively.
* Stay close to the technical work. You can work directly with large datasets using SQL and Python or R, validate analytical outputs, and use AI tools to accelerate work while maintaining rigor.
* Understand APAC’s diverse advertiser, agency, and measurement ecosystems, and have worked across markets with different data availability, languages, and privacy requirements.
* Have influenced Product, Engineering, Data Science, or external measurement partners to turn client needs into durable capabilities, and can lead through ambiguity without relying on formal authority.
