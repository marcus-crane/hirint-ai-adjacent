**About the Team**

The Enablement team translates OpenAI’s product innovation into real-world customer impact by equipping customer-facing teams with the knowledge, practice, and frameworks they need to succeed. Within GTM onboarding, we establish a shared foundation during the first 30 days and build deeper role readiness for technical GTM teams as they progress through ramp.

**About the Role**

OpenAI is hiring a GTM Technical Onboarding Program Manager to define and deliver the role-specific onboarding experience for technical customer-facing GTM roles.

Foundational onboarding establishes a common baseline during the first 30 days. This role owns the deeper development that follows, with a particular focus on days 30–90, when new hires need to translate product and deployment knowledge into practical, job-specific capability.

You will partner with technical GTM leaders, Product, and subject-matter experts to turn complex product and deployment concepts into role-specific curricula, hands-on practice, assessments, certifications, and manager reinforcement.

Success means technical GTM new hires build the product fluency, scoping ability, solution judgment, and customer-facing confidence required for their roles. Managers have clear readiness signals, learning experiences stay current as products evolve, and role-specific ramp becomes repeatable across teams and regions.

**In this role, you will:**

* Own the design and delivery of day 30–90 role-specific onboarding for technical GTM roles
* Define learning outcomes and readiness standards in partnership with technical GTM leaders
* Build curricula and learning paths that translate complex product and deployment concepts into job-specific behavior
* Design and facilitate hands-on experiences such as labs, demos, simulations, technical discovery practice, and customer scenarios
* Create assessments, certifications, and manager-ready signals of proficiency and deployment readiness
* Partner with Product, technical subject-matter experts, and GTM leaders to source, validate, and refresh content as products and guidance evolve
* Coach subject-matter experts and facilitators to teach technical content clearly and effectively
* Establish repeatable content-governance and reinforcement systems that scale across roles and regions
* Use learner feedback, performance data, and AI-enabled workflows to improve practice, learner support, content maintenance, and program efficiency

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have held a customer-facing technical role and enjoy helping others build strong product, solution, and customer judgment
* Develop fluency quickly in complex products, technical architectures, integrations, deployment considerations, and customer workflows
* Understand how technical GTM teams scope solutions, navigate technical tradeoffs, and connect product capabilities to business outcomes
* Are energized by turning hard-won technical knowledge into practical guidance, hands-on practice, and repeatable ways of working
* Communicate complex technical concepts clearly to both technical and non-technical audiences
* Operate effectively in a fast-moving environment where products, technical guidance, and customer needs change quickly
* Bring low ego, high ownership, and a genuine passion for helping technical teams become more capable and confident

**Basic qualifications:**

* 5-8 years experience in technical enablement, Solutions Engineering, Solutions Architecture, Technical Success, Implementation, technical consulting, or another customer-facing technical role
* Demonstrated ability to quickly develop fluency in complex products, technical architectures, integrations, and deployment considerations
* Experience working directly with customers on complex, deeply technical SaaS platforms is required
* Strong technical discovery, scoping, and solution judgment, with the ability to connect customer workflows and business needs to technical recommendations
* Ability to explain complex technical concepts clearly and turn them into practical, role-relevant guidance for technical GTM teams
* Experience creating or leading hands-on technical experiences, such as labs, demos, simulations, discovery practice, or customer scenarios
* Strong cross-functional collaboration skills with Product, technical subject-matter experts, and GTM leaders
* Strong program management, facilitation, and written and verbal communication skills
* Willingness to work in-office in San Francisco

**Preferred qualifications:**

* Direct experience in Solutions Engineering or Solutions Architecture is strongly preferred
* Direct experience working with AI/ML products or platforms is preferred
* Experience designing or delivering enablement, onboarding, technical training, or knowledge-sharing programs is a plus
* Familiarity with instructional design principles or adult learning is a plus
* Experience leading subject-matter expert programs or designing technical assessments and certifications
