**About the Team**

Business Marketing helps knowledge workers, developers, and business decision-makers understand and adopt OpenAI’s products, supporting both self-serve growth and our managed sales team. Our creative work makes complex capabilities clear, grounded, and human as they move from the lab into businesses and everyday work.

**About the Role**

We’re looking for an experienced Art Director to develop and direct creative for OpenAI’s business audiences. You’ll turn product capabilities, customer insights, and marketing briefs into visual ideas that can carry a campaign across channels. That might mean developing the visual concept for a launch, storyboarding a film about how a customer uses AI in their work, or building a campaign system that holds together across a landing page, paid social, and a keynote.

This is a hands-on role. You’ll sketch concepts, build key visuals, and set the visual direction for designers, producers, and external partners. You’ll work closely with writers, Business Marketing, Product Marketing, and product teams to make sure the work is accurate, useful to the audience, and unmistakably OpenAI.

To us, creativity is a team sport. Ideas get better when you talk them through and build on each other’s thinking. We value curiosity over certainty, kindness over ego, and the ability to balance craft with decisiveness.

**In this role, you will:**

* Lead visual concept development and art direction for B2B campaigns, product launches, customer stories, and industry events, from brief through final production.
* Partner with writers and marketers to define the idea around a specific audience, what they need to understand, and what we want them to do next.
* Work with product and technical partners to show how capabilities work in practice, using product imagery, demos, diagrams, and motion without overstating what the technology can do.
* Develop campaign visual systems that adapt to different formats and audience needs, from a launch film or event keynote to a product page, paid campaign, or sales presentation.
* Art direct photography, illustration, motion, and video. Make work yourself when the project calls for it, and give clear direction to internal teams and external partners when others are producing it.
* Maintain a high visual bar across touch points and help evolve how the OpenAI brand shows up for business audiences.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have 8+ years of art direction or related creative experience in-house, at an agency, or both, with a portfolio that shows your role in developing concepts and taking them through production.
* Have experience creating work for business or technical audiences, with examples of making a complex product, service, or idea clear and compelling.
* Bring strong visual design fundamentals in typography, composition, imagery, and systems, and can apply them across digital, social, film, and experiential work.
* Can build a strong campaign idea and show how it adapts across formats without losing what makes it distinctive.
* Can explain your creative decisions to marketing and product partners, work through feedback, and make sound tradeoffs when priorities or timelines change.

**Nice to Have**

* Experience with AI, enterprise software, developer tools, or other emerging technologies.
* Experience art directing customer stories, product demos, or keynote and event creative.
* Motion design, video art direction, or on-set experience.
