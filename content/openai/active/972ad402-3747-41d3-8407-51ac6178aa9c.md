**About the Team**

OpenAI’s Scaled Ads team helps mid-market advertisers grow on the platform while protecting user trust. Client Partners own commercial relationships, vendor teams optimise delivery and AI agents take on repeatable work.

**About the Role**

You’ll own a portfolio of mid-size advertisers from their first campaign through renewal and expansion. Your job is to earn the budget owner’s trust and spend your time where a commercial conversation can change the outcome.

**In this role, you will:**

* Qualify new advertisers against their goals, budget and ability to measure results. Agree what a successful first campaign would demonstrate.
* Build relationships with marketing leads and executive sponsors. Use campaign results to make a case for the advertiser’s next investment.
* Review your book with vendor CSMs. They optimise committed spend while you address renewal risks, negotiate budgets and forecast revenue.
* Use agent-prepared account briefs and flag recommendations that miss important customer context.

**You might thrive in this role if:**

* You’ve owned a revenue target across a portfolio of digital advertisers and can describe a renewal or expansion you earned.
* You can explain campaign economics to a budget owner and prioritise accounts when several need attention at once.
* You work well with account managers and know when an advertiser can act independently.
* You have min. 2-4 years experience selling Ad Solutions.
* You speak fluent German.

**What success looks like:**

A portfolio with repeat advertisers, credible expansion plans and a forecast grounded in customer commitments.
