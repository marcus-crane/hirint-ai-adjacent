**About the Team**

The Real Estate & Workplace (REW) team at OpenAI designs and manages spaces, programs, and services that empower our employees and advance our mission. Our team covers a diverse range of functions, including leasing, global operations, analytics, construction, workplace experience, and employee services. We strive to support OpenAI’s mission of researching and deploying safe artificial general intelligence that benefits all of humanity by optimizing the employee experience and maintaining operational excellence and financial discipline through rigorous focus on flexibility, cost effectiveness, intentional design, and scalable, people-centric solutions.

**About the Role**

We are seeking an experienced workplace leader to own the workplace experience for our New York City office. The core of this role is business partnership, employee experience, and workplace programs: understanding the needs of NYC-based teams and turning them into practical services that align with OpenAI’s broader workplace standards.

Alongside your own workplace responsibilities, you will manage a team member focused on hard-services facilities, setting priorities, coaching their development, and overseeing delivery. This role is part of Americas Workplace Operations and works closely with Workplace Experience, Global Food Programs, People, Executive Business Partners, Corporate Security, IT, and Facilities specialists.

This position is based in New York, NY, with the requirement of being in the office **5 days per week**.

**In this role, you will:**

* Own the day-to-day workplace experience in New York City, ensuring the office is welcoming, well run, and responsive to local employee and business needs.
* Build relationships with NYC leaders and the teams you support; anticipate growth, seating, onboarding, and visitor needs, and turn feedback into practical service improvements.
* Manage and develop a facilities-focused team member, setting clear expectations, reviewing priorities and delivery, and maintaining accountability for service quality and follow-through.
* Own local workplace programs and partner with Workplace Experience, Food Programs, and Events to deliver services and initiatives that strengthen culture and connection in NYC.
* Oversee hard-services facilities work through your direct report, reviewing maintenance priorities, vendor performance, and open issues; support escalations and involve Facilities specialists or building engineering as needed.
* Partner with your direct report, NYC building management, and service providers to resolve facilities needs and communicate business impacts, with your direct report leading day-to-day coordination and follow-through.
* Partner with IT, Corporate Security, People, Executive Business Partners, and other REW teams on office readiness, moves, employee needs, and responses to workplace disruptions.
* Monitor local service quality, spending, and recurring issues; use employee feedback and operational data to prioritize improvements and escalate risks.
* Communicate workplace updates clearly and proactively, and maintain accurate NYC-specific guidance on office services, workplace programs, and policies.

**You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* Experience owning workplace operations or employee services in a growing organization, balancing employee experience with consistent daily execution.
* Experience managing and developing a team member while continuing to own workplace programs and stakeholder relationships.
* Working knowledge of hard-services facilities operations and vendor management, with sound judgment to guide a direct report and involve technical specialists.
* Experience partnering with landlords or building management in NYC or a similarly complex urban office environment.
* Strong people skills and the ability to build trust with employees, senior leaders, cross-functional partners, and service providers.
* The ability to move quickly, prioritize competing needs, and communicate clearly when plans or conditions change.
* A hands-on, “no problem too small” approach, paired with the ability to delegate effectively and build repeatable processes.
* A broad understanding of the REW ecosystem, with curiosity and flexibility to work across functions as needed.
