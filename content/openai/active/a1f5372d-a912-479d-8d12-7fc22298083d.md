OpenAI’s largest and most strategic customers are turning AI ambition into deployments that transform how their organizations work. We are seeking a senior leader to build, manage, and scale a high-performing team of Delivery Leads responsible for orchestrating complex, high-impact AI initiatives from scoping and planning through launch, adoption, and measurable customer value.

The Delivery Leads team serves as the central orchestration layer across OpenAI’s most strategic customer engagements, bringing together account teams, technical specialists, Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs), customers, and delivery partners around integrated delivery plans, clear commitments, and shared outcomes. The team drives execution across multiple workstreams, resolves critical dependencies, and ensures successful deployments translate into sustained adoption, consumption, and business impact.

In this role, you will own the team's strategy, operating model, and delivery excellence while hiring, developing, and leading senior customer-facing talent. You will stay close to our most complex customer engagements, providing executive-level guidance, navigating delivery risks, and building the scalable mechanisms, playbooks, and operating rhythms that enable consistent execution as our business grows.

This role is based in New York or San Francisco and follows a hybrid schedule of three days per week in office. Regional travel is expected.

## **In This Role, You Will**

* **Own the strategy and operating model for Delivery Leads**, defining how the team engages across OpenAI's most strategic accounts, establishes delivery accountability, and drives consistent execution from pre-sales scoping through launch and initial adoption.
* **Hire, lead, mentor, and develop a high-performing team of Delivery Leads**, building a team with strong technical fluency, executive presence, commercial judgment, and the ability to lead complex enterprise AI transformation programs.
* **Oversee delivery execution across a portfolio of strategic customers**, ensuring priority AI initiatives have integrated delivery plans, clear milestones, accountable owners, dependencies, resource commitments, and measurable success criteria.
* **Drive delivery excellence and accountability**, establishing mechanisms to monitor delivery health, identify risks early, resolve critical escalations, and ensure teams consistently deliver against customer commitments.
* **Build scalable delivery frameworks, tools, and operating rhythms**, including portfolio reviews, executive reporting, delivery health dashboards, resource planning, and repeatable playbooks that improve predictability and efficiency across engagements.
* **Serve as an executive escalation point and trusted advisor** for our most complex customer deployments, helping senior customer stakeholders navigate competing priorities, organizational dependencies, and critical decisions to accelerate AI transformation.
* **Partner cross-functionally with Sales, Technical Success teams, FDE, Product, and external delivery partners** to ensure seamless coordination, clear ownership, and an integrated customer delivery experience.
* **Shape the evolution of OpenAI's delivery model**, translating field insights into improvements in customer engagement, delivery methodologies, resourcing strategies, product capabilities, and organizational effectiveness.

## **You'll Thrive in This Role If You**

* **8+ years of experience leading complex enterprise technology delivery or transformation programs**, including 4+ years managing technical delivery, professional services, customer engineering, or enterprise transformation teams.
* **Proven track record building and scaling high-performing teams** in fast-growing technology environments, with demonstrated success developing senior talent and establishing scalable operating models.
* **Deep experience overseeing complex, multi-workstream enterprise deployments**, including programs spanning technical implementation, systems integration, organizational change, and multiple internal and external stakeholders.
* **Strong technical fluency**, with the ability to understand enterprise AI architectures, APIs, integrations, security requirements, technical dependencies, and deployment risks. Experience with Generative AI is preferred.
* **Demonstrated ability to establish operational rigor and delivery predictability**, including designing portfolio governance, resource allocation models, escalation frameworks, performance metrics, and executive reporting mechanisms.
* **Proven ability to influence senior executives and drive alignment** across complex global organizations, navigating ambiguity, competing priorities, and cross-functional dependencies to deliver results.
* **Strong commercial acumen**, with an understanding of how successful deployments translate into customer value, product consumption, retention, and expansion.
* **Experience building scalable delivery organizations and engagement models**, balancing the needs of highly customized strategic engagements with repeatable processes, consistent execution, and operational efficiency.
* **Exceptional communication and leadership skills**, with the ability to move seamlessly between executive-level strategic conversations and the technical and operational details required to deliver successful outcomes.
* **Operate with humility, ownership, and a bias for action**, bringing sound judgment, a customer-first mindset, and a willingness to do what it takes to make customers and the team successful.
