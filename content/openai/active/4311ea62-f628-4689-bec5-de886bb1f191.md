**About the Team**

Customer Education helps customers build the practical skills and confidence to use AI and OpenAI products safely and effectively. We bring together customer insight, learning and enablement expertise, product signals, and measurement to help organizations move from access to durable adoption.

Within Customer Education, the Certification team helps individuals demonstrate competence for in-demand AI skills and helps customers and partners validate that their workforce has the skills they need to meet their AI-enabled business goals. Our work centers on the belief that learners should understand where to go to build skills and capabilities, how to demonstrate them, and what to pursue next. Similarly, Enterprise leaders should understand what each credential signifies and how it supports workforce readiness. We do this with transparency for the overall OpenAI credential framework and providing trusted, role-based certifications that verify practical capability.

**About the Role**

As Certification Operations and Policy Program Manager, you will build and run the processes that make OpenAI certifications accessible, fair, and reliable for candidates and the organizations that rely on them.

This is a hands-on individual contributor role on the Certification team, with no direct reports. You will define candidate policies, turn them into practical processes, and own their implementation as the program grows. This is not a general program-management or support-only role: it combines policy development with hands-on operational delivery.

You will work closely with the Certification team, product and technical teams, Legal, Privacy, Security, Accessibility, Support, and delivery vendors to define operational standards and apply policies consistently and fairly. You will connect the partners needed to turn certification requirements into workable candidate processes.

You will start with early certification launches and build reusable playbooks, service standards, and review processes for live and virtual delivery, and establish metrics and reporting for both formats. Initially, you will also triage escalated vendor and candidate cases, resolve routine delivery or credential-access issues, and route substantive appeals through the established independent-review process.

Success means candidates know what to expect, delivery partners know what to do when something goes wrong, and sensitive decisions are documented and independently reviewed.

**In this role, you will:**

* Define candidate-facing policies and communications across the certification journey, including eligibility, registration, exam rules, retakes, exceptions, expiration, and renewal. Partner with the Certification team to translate program requirements into policy and with Legal, product, technical teams, and vendors to build the processes that uphold it.
* Own the accommodations process from request through delivery. Work with Accessibility, Legal, and delivery partners on how requests are reviewed and implemented, and protect sensitive information throughout.
* Own certification records and evidence workflows, including results, credential issuance and corrections, and case records. Put approved access, retention, and disclosure rules into practice with Privacy, Security, and platform teams.
* Own vendor workflows and operational delivery standards, including handoffs, service levels, readiness checks, and escalation paths. Initially serve as the first point of contact for escalated delivery and credential-access cases, coordinating vendor fixes, candidate communications, and corrective actions.
* Partner with Revenue Operations to define and report program metrics and maintain a scorecard.
* Coordinate the response to exam integrity incidents, including suspected cheating, impersonation, or unauthorized sharing of exam content. Work with Security, Legal, and delivery vendors to document evidence, route cases to the appropriate decision-makers, and communicate and implement approved outcomes, such as score invalidation or credential revocation.
* Administer appeals and independent reviews. Set intake and response timelines, assemble the case record, screen for conflicts of interest, and route decisions to qualified reviewers who were not involved in the original decision. Document and communicate outcomes without deciding cases you administer.
* Coordinate Legal guidance on candidate policies, exceptions, and sensitive cases. Translate approved guidance into playbooks and training, and use trends in incidents, appeals, accommodations, and vendor performance to improve delivery.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have owned operations or candidate policy for professional certification, licensure, high-stakes assessment, or a similarly governed program.
* Have taken a certification, licensure, or comparable high-stakes program from launch readiness through live delivery, and can show how you used operational data and candidate feedback to identify recurring problems and improve delivery.
* Have experience working at a technology company in a fast-paced environment, setting priorities and delivering work amid changing requirements and evolving business needs.
* Have defined candidate-facing policies and translated them into clear communications and operational workflows that internal teams and vendors can apply consistently.
* Have built or run an accommodations process, implementing candidate accommodations while protecting sensitive information.
* Have administered incidents, complaints, appeals, or exceptions, documenting decisions and maintaining review by people who were not involved in the original decision.
* Have managed external delivery vendors against service standards and followed through when those standards were not met.
* Have handled sensitive candidate, assessment, or credential records and worked with privacy or security teams on appropriate controls.
* Have translated complex policies into candidate communications and operational guidance, and coordinated implementation across vendors and internal teams without direct reporting authority.
* Have personally written operational playbooks, checked launch readiness, triaged cases, and improved delivery processes.
* Have resolved time-sensitive operational issues within established policy and escalated exceptions to the appropriate decision-makers.
* Have applied accessibility, fairness, and credential-integrity policies consistently in candidate-facing operations.

**It’s a plus if you have experience:**

* Launching or scaling technical certifications across live and virtual delivery.
* Working with exam delivery, identity verification, proctoring, credential issuance, or candidate support systems.
* Supporting accreditation or quality audits for personnel certification programs.
* Building repeatable operating processes across regions, delivery partners, or multiple certification programs.
