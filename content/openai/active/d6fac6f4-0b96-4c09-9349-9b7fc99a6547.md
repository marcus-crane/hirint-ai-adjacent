**About the team**

Customer Education helps enterprises develop the capabilities their workforce needs to use AI effectively, build and deploy AI solutions, and get more value from OpenAI’s products.

**About the role**

We’re looking for a hands-on technical educator who enjoys building with AI and helping others learn to do the same. You’ll create practical education for enterprise developers building with OpenAI’s APIs and working with Codex.

You’ll turn real customer use cases into clear lessons, working examples and hands-on labs, building on our established portfolio of bootcamps, webinars and technical content. The goal is to help builders develop skills they can apply to their own applications and development workflows.

This role combines technical development, learning design and live teaching. You’ll build and test what you teach, work directly with learners and create resources that can be reused across in-person, live virtual and self-paced learning.

Working closely with the Builder Education team, you’ll independently own defined content, lab and delivery workstreams. You’ll partner with technical experts and customer-facing teams to keep learning accurate, useful and current as products evolve.

**In this role, you will:**

* Build practical technical education. Turn customer needs into clear learning objectives, lessons, demonstrations and exercises. Sequence concepts, practice, feedback and assessments so learners can build and apply new skills.
* Create hands-on labs and learning environments. Build working examples and reliable, secure lab infrastructure. Use automation and platform integrations to make setup, access and delivery repeatable as programs grow.
* Teach directly and improve through practice. Deliver in-person bootcamps and live virtual sessions, explain technical concepts clearly and help learners troubleshoot. Use their questions and feedback to improve the experience.
* Own content from concept through launch. Work with technical experts to develop, test and publish content, code examples and labs. Maintain technical accuracy and update materials as products and customer needs change.
* Make learning reusable. Adapt content across bootcamps, webinars and self-paced courses, and create resources that customers, internal teams and partners can use independently.
* Run defined program workstreams. Manage scope, timelines and dependencies, coordinate contributors and support program operations. Build repeatable processes that keep content, environments and delivery ready for learners.
* Use evidence to improve learning. Assess whether builders can apply what they learn, and work with program and analytics partners to understand how programs support product adoption and continued use.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have hands-on technical experience in software development, cloud infrastructure or AI workflows, and have built applications, integrated APIs, used Git or other version control and worked with coding agents. You can share examples of applications, API integrations or AI workflows you personally delivered.
* Can turn complex technical topics into clear learning experiences, with purposeful practice and ways to assess whether learners can apply new skills.
* Have built and maintained hands-on labs or technical learning environments for external customer or developer audiences, with attention to reliability, security and ease of use.
* Are an engaging teacher and clear writer who can explain technical decisions, respond to learner questions and troubleshoot live.
* Stay curious about AI, explore new capabilities firsthand and test the code and workflows you teach. You use learner feedback to improve your work.
* Have independently owned a technical learning program from an ambiguous brief through launch and iteration, making practical scope decisions and contributing across content, teaching and program operations.
* Have experience beyond education-only settings, working with product, engineering or customer teams. Experience in a fast-paced tech environment is preferred.

**Nice to have:**

* Experience with infrastructure automation tools such as Terraform and integrations that support technical labs.
* Experience with learning platforms, LMS/SCORM, certification or assessment design, or learning analytics.
* Relevant technical certifications or experience delivering technical training across live and self-paced formats.
