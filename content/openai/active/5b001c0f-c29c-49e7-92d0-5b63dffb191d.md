# About this Team

The HR Business Partner (HRBP) team at OpenAI partners with leaders on complex organizational and talent decisions where the right path is not always obvious. We stay close enough to the business to understand how it works and focus our time on the decisions that benefit most from our judgment, context, and influence.

We work with urgency, empathy, and sound judgment. We value sincerity, intellectual honesty, and collaboration over ego.

# About this Role

As a Senior HR Business Partner supporting Tech, you’ll partner with leaders in engineering, product, and other technical teams as they build and scale their organizations. You’ll get close enough to the work to understand what’s slowing teams down, then help leaders make decisions about structure, talent, and how teams work together as technical priorities evolve.

This is an individual contributor role reporting to an HRBP Lead. You’ll advise senior leaders on complex decisions and work directly with managers to make those decisions stick.

# In this role, you will:

* **Translate technical priorities into org and talent decisions.** Help leaders decide what capabilities to build, how to structure teams, and where ownership needs to sit.
* **Diagnose what’s getting in the way of team performance.** Use data and input from the people doing the work to separate structural issues from problems with roles, priorities, or how teams work together.
* **Develop technical leaders.** Coach managers through performance, growth, and succession decisions, and help them set clear expectations for both people managers and individual contributors.
* **Lead change as technical priorities shift.** Help leaders think through the impact of reorganizations or changes in ownership and communicate decisions clearly to the teams affected.
* **Build People practices that work for technical teams.** Partner with fellow HRBPs and People specialists to adapt performance, development, and talent processes without adding unnecessary overhead.

# You might thrive in this role if you have:

* 7–10+ years of HR experience, including at least 5 years as an HR Business Partner in a high-growth or complex environment.
* Experience partnering with engineering, product, or other technical organizations, and curiosity about how technical teams make decisions and deliver work.
* A track record of helping technical leaders navigate org design, workforce planning, or talent decisions as teams grow or priorities change.
* Skill in coaching senior leaders and managers, including the ability to challenge a decision constructively and maintain trust.
* Sound judgment under pressure and the ability to act on incomplete information while staying open to new evidence.
* Discretion, fairness, and sound judgment when the stakes are high.
