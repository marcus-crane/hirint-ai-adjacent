**About the Team**

Like every team at OpenAI, the Marketing team contributes to our broader mission of ensuring responsible and widespread adoption of artificial intelligence With that aim in mind, we are responsible for developing and executing strategies that drive awareness, engagement, and usage for OpenAI’s products and platform amongst our core audiences. We take a data-driven approach to understand our customers' needs and challenges, ensuring that their voices are reflected in product development and messaging. We then partner closely with Product, Engineering, Research, Comms, and Design teams to create a cohesive customer experience across all our channels. Our focus extends beyond just promoting product features; we aim to provide valuable insights and resources that help our users make the most out of AI technologies.

**About the Role**

As a Product Marketing Manager for Partner Marketing on the Hardware team, you will help shape the partnership strategy for a new product ecosystem and bring that ecosystem to market with the right partners, programs, and commercial motions. This role calls for a strategic, relationship-oriented marketer who can identify high-potential partners, understand their priorities, and turn shared opportunities into durable, high-impact go-to-market plans.

You will work closely with Product, Design, Engineering, Integrated Marketing, Sales, Comms, Finance, Legal, and partner-facing teams to define how first-, second-, and third-party partners participate in our hardware ecosystem. You will lead the marketing and commercial aspects of partnerships, support joint launches, and ensure partner needs are understood internally while OpenAI’s product, brand, and customer experience standards are represented externally.

This role reports into the VP of Product Marketing, Hardware, and offers a unique opportunity to build the partner marketing foundation for a new category of AI-powered devices.

**In this role, you will:**

* Help craft and implement the partnership strategy for OpenAI’s hardware product ecosystem.
* Identify, prioritize, and evaluate potential partners for participation in first-, second-, and third-party partner programs.
* Build and maintain strong relationships with partners, serving as a trusted counterpart through launches, growth moments, ambiguity, and challenges.
* Understand partner stakeholders, business models, priorities, and constraints, and advocate for those perspectives internally.
* Lead the commercial marketing aspects of partnerships, including positioning, launch strategy, sales enablement, distribution, and joint marketing plans.
* Manage partner execution for joint product launches, campaigns, events, and other go-to-market efforts.
* Partner cross-functionally with Product, Design, Engineering, Legal, Finance, Sales, Comms, and Integrated Marketing to align partner strategy with product and business goals.
* Develop partner narratives, messaging, and materials that clearly communicate the value of OpenAI’s hardware products and ecosystem.
* Monitor partner performance, market feedback, and customer signals to improve future programs and deepen partner impact.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have 10+ years of experience in product marketing, partner marketing, business development marketing, ecosystem marketing, or related go-to-market roles.
* Have experience building or scaling partner programs, ideally in consumer hardware, platforms, devices, developer ecosystems, or emerging technology categories.
* Are highly relationship-oriented and can build trust with external partners while navigating complex internal priorities.
* Can translate partner needs, customer insights, and product strategy into clear positioning, launch plans, and commercial programs.
* Have led successful cross-functional launches or campaigns with multiple internal teams and external partners.
* Bring strong business judgment across marketing, sales, distribution, and commercial partnership models.
* Are an excellent communicator and storyteller, with the ability to influence senior stakeholders internally and externally.
* Are comfortable operating in a fast-moving, ambiguous environment where the strategy, product, and ecosystem are being built in parallel.
