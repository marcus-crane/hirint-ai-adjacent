**About the Team**

OpenAI’s **Global Security Operations Center (GSOC)** protects the safety of our people, workspaces, and global operations. We operate 24/7 in a fast-paced, high-growth environment, providing incident response, threat monitoring, situational awareness, and support to teams across the organization. Our mission is to enable OpenAI employees to do their most important work securely and with confidence, wherever they are in the world.

**About the Role**

We are looking for a **GSOC Operator** to join our Global Security Operations Center team. In this role, you will monitor security systems, identify and respond to incidents, and provide real-time support to employees and partners around the world. You will be responsible for maintaining situational awareness, coordinating emergency communications, and ensuring accurate documentation and escalation across all security events.

This role is based in our San Francisco office. GSOC operates 24/7, and shift coverage—including nights, weekends, and holidays—will be required.

**In this role, you will**

* Monitor and manage access control, video surveillance, alarms, and other security technologies.
* Communicate with internal partners using Slack, Jira, and other collaboration tools.
* Identify, assess, and respond to security alerts and incidents; escalate appropriately.
* Support emergency notifications and mass communication systems during critical situations.
* Provide global travel monitoring and situational awareness to help keep employees safe.
* Maintain detailed logs, reports, and documentation of all security events and responses.
* Follow established procedures and contribute to continuous improvement of playbooks and workflows.
* Deliver excellent service and support to employees, visitors, vendors, and partner teams.
* Participate in routine training, drills, and skill development to maintain readiness.

**You may be a fit for this role if you have**

* 5+ years of experience in GSOC, corporate security, or protective operations, **or** a bachelor’s degree in a relevant field.
* Military, law enforcement, or emergency operations center experience may substitute for traditional GSOC experience.
* Hands-on experience with access control and VMS platforms (e.g., Genetec).
* Proficiency with Slack, Jira, Google Workspace, and incident documentation workflows.
* Familiarity with travel risk monitoring tools (e.g., International SOS) is a plus.
* Experience with emergency mass notification systems and/or AI-enabled security tools is helpful.
* Strong verbal and written communication skills and the ability to stay composed under pressure.
* A customer-focused mindset, with the ability to learn, adapt, and follow protocols with precision.
* A collaborative, resilient, and team-oriented approach.
