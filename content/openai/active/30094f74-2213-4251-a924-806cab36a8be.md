**About the Team**   
We are a small, fast-moving team that builds and scales OpenAI’s B2B partnerships. Our work spans independent software vendors (ISVs), Marketplace, and emerging partner initiatives. We build the programs, infrastructure, and operating models that help partners launch successfully, generate sustained usage and revenue, and grow alongside OpenAI.

**About the Role**   
In this role, you will build the operating foundation the team needs to scale, including processes, data infrastructure, analytics, and partner incentives. You will define how we measure the health of our ecosystem and the performance of individual partners, then use those insights to recommend where we invest and which partner behaviors we incentivize.

This is a hands-on, zero-to-one role. The tools, data, and processes you need will not always exist yet. You will work directly with Partnerships to determine what matters, build the first version, and improve it as the team learns. You know when a spreadsheet is enough and when it is time to build something more durable.

Success means partners launch faster, more partners generate sustained usage and revenue, and the team can scale without a proportional increase in manual work.  
  
**In this role, you will:**

* Establish the operating model for Marketplace and related B2B partner programs, including deal review, partner onboarding, solution reviews, launches, ownership, and escalation paths.
* Build the data infrastructure, reporting, and operating rhythms used to measure partner activation, pipeline, adoption, usage, revenue, and overall ecosystem health.
* Connect and validate data across existing systems, create dashboards and business reviews, investigate what is driving performance, and recommend where the team should invest.
* Shape partner incentives and benefits using partner feedback, performance data, and program economics. Define eligibility, identify the behaviors we want to encourage, and work with Partnerships and Finance to test and scale what works.
* Turn priorities into execution plans with clear owners, milestones, dependencies, and decisions.
* Work across Partnerships, Product, Engineering, Data Science, Sales, Finance, Legal, and Accounting to resolve blockers and carry initiatives from planning through launch and ongoing operation.
* Build repeatable workflows and automation that reduce manual work while improving the partner experience.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have 5–7 years of experience in strategy and operations, business operations, strategic finance, partner programs, or a related role.
* Have built reporting, dashboards, incentive programs, and scalable operating workflows from scratch—and successfully driven their adoption.
* Are comfortable working directly with data and have hands-on fluency with spreadsheets, SQL, BI tools, and CRM data.
* Have led complex, cross-functional initiatives from planning through delivery, including work with Finance, Legal, and Accounting.
* Bring strong business judgment and analytical curiosity, and can move comfortably between detailed execution and broader strategic questions.
* Take ownership of ambiguous problems, build practical first versions, and improve them quickly based on what you learn.
* Communicate clearly, build trust across functions, and create momentum without relying on formal authority.
* Have experience with B2B software, ISVs, marketplaces, or partner ecosystems. This is helpful but not required.
