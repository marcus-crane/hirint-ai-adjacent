## **Global Transportation Program Manager**

### **About the role**

Develop, improve, and scale employee transportation programs and communications across Bay Area and global offices. Translate priorities into practical, data-informed programs, consistent employee experiences, and repeatable launch resources, while coordinating local stakeholders and vendors.  
  
This position is based in San Francisco, with the requirement of being in the office **3 days per week**.

### **In this role, you will**

* Lead employee-facing transportation programs, TDM initiatives, commuter benefits coordination, and multimodal commute engagement across offices.
* Own transportation communications, employee resources, enrollment journeys, feedback channels, and change-management plans in partnership with Internal Communications and local Workplace teams.
* Analyze commute demand, headcount trends, home-location density data where appropriately available, utilization, and employee feedback to propose program improvements.
* Assist Global Transportation Programs, Senior Manager, in global expansion priorities and standardizations. Coordinate transportation assessments and implementation plans for new or expanding offices, working with Real Estate, Workplace Operations, Benefits, Payroll, Legal, and vendors.
* Build dashboards and concise business cases that assess adoption, accessibility, employee experience, cost effectiveness, and service options.
* Manage assigned program vendors or contractors and oversee implementation of approved strategic initiatives; partner with Operations on service changes.

### **What we are looking for**

Experienced transportation, TDM, mobility, or workplace program manager with strong program development, employee communications, analytical judgment, stakeholder management, and hands-on execution skills. Global or multi-site experience is a plus.

### **You might thrive in this role if  you:**

* Have 7+ years of experience delivering effective commuter programs and TDM strategies at a major company
* Have a background in city planning, transportation planning, and/or project management and experience collaborating and making decisions alongside cross functional teams. Masters in transportation planning a plus
* Experience managing communications to a diverse group of users and navigating various levels of feedback
* Excel at at building and leveraging relationships with external vendors, consultants, and internal teams, and can drive accountability
* Bring a collaborative and proactive approach to project management and delivery

Reporting line: Global Transportation Programs, Senior Manager. San Francisco based.
