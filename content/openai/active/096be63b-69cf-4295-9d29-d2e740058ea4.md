**About the Team**

We partner with technical, product and commercial leaders to build the financial foundations for new businesses. Our work spans planning, investment analysis, pricing and operating performance across software and hardware-enabled products.

**About the Role**

We are looking for a hands-on finance professional who enjoys building the operating detail behind new businesses and stepping back to evaluate where value can be created. You will own budgets, forecasts, models and reporting for assigned workstreams, while helping teams assess markets, pricing and product economics. The role suits someone who has worked across different business models, including a physical-product, manufacturing, consumer or supply-chain setting, and can translate business requirements into practical financial processes and systems.

**You will be responsible for**

* Own budgets, forecasts and performance reporting for assigned programs, including headcount, operating expenses, capital investment and cash requirements.

* Build and maintain product- and customer-level models connecting volume, price, revenue, cost of goods sold, gross margin, inventory and working capital.

* Help operationalize new products and commercial structures through SKU and product-catalog setup, billing requirements, and data flows across customer, enterprise-resource-planning, planning and reporting systems.

* Assess new markets and business models using market sizing, customer segmentation, competitive context, adoption assumptions and pricing scenarios.

* Evaluate build, buy and partner options, manufacturing and supply-chain economics, and commercial terms, turning analysis into clear recommendations for business and finance leaders.

* Run operating reviews, reconcile forecasts to actuals, coordinate follow-through with data owners and escalate material risks or unresolved tradeoffs.

**We are looking for someone with**

* Demonstrated hands-on ownership of budgets, forecasts, financial models and recurring operating reviews, including reconciliation and follow-through.

* Experience with both revenue or pricing and COGS or operations, with direct exposure to at least one manufacturing, consumer-product or supply-chain business.

* Practical systems fluency, such as SKU and billing setup, inventory data, or integrations across CRM, ERP, planning and reporting tools. You should be able to map requirements and work effectively with technical and operations partners.

* Evidence of assessing unfamiliar business opportunities and translating market, customer and cost data into pricing or investment recommendations, with clear communication and sound judgment.

* Helpful but not required: consumer pricing, B2B software monetization, robotics, semiconductors or inference. Breadth can come from different roles; we value what you personally owned over employer reputation or senior titles.
