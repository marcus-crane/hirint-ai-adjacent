**About the Team**

Customer Education helps enterprises build an AI-enabled workforce. We help people develop the skills, confidence and judgment to use AI in their roles, build and deploy AI solutions, and lead changes in how work gets done.

**About the Role**

We’re looking for an Enterprise Education GTM Lead to own the positioning and packaging of OpenAI’s education and enablement offering for enterprise customers. You’ll make clear how our programs help organizations build an AI-enabled workforce, from employees applying AI in daily work to teams redesigning workflows and building new solutions.

You’ll define how these offerings serve different roles and stages of AI maturity, making clear who our education programs are for, the outcomes they support and how customers engage. Working with Customer Education, Marketing, and customer-facing teams, you’ll shape the packages, develop messaging and launch content, and equip account teams to bring the offering into enterprise conversations.

**In this role, you will:**

* Own enterprise positioning and messaging for education and enablement that help customers build an AI-enabled workforce.
* Package education and enablement around enterprise goals, workforce needs and stages of AI maturity.
* Lead launches for priority education offerings.
* Create pitch materials that connect our offerings to customers’ workforce and business goals.
* Develop customer stories that show how enterprises build AI capabilities and achieve business results.
* Use customer feedback and performance data to improve positioning, packaging and content.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have brought education, enablement or services offerings to market for enterprise audiences.
* Are a strong writer and editor who makes complex ideas clear for enterprise audiences.
* Have led launches and written content that improved customer understanding or engagement.
* Have managed competing priorities and worked across teams to deliver launches and programs.
* Have used customer research and performance data to improve positioning and go-to-market plans.
* Use AI actively and are curious about how it changes work across an enterprise.

**Nice to have**

* Experience with enterprise AI adoption or workforce transformation programs.
* Experience developing customer stories or thought leadership with enterprise customers and leaders.
