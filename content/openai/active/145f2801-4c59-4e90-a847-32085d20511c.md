## About the Role

As a Data Science Leader, B2B Marketing & Demand Generation, you will partner with our B2B Marketing organization and help accelerate how businesses discover, adopt, and grow with AI.

You will be a strategic partner to our B2B Marketing leadership team, bringing a deep understanding of the B2B landscape and a point of view on how to grow a business. You will help shape our marketing strategy, identify our biggest growth opportunities, and build the data, measurement, and intelligence capabilities needed to turn those opportunities into impact.

The opportunity spans the full customer journey, from building awareness and engaging buying groups to generating demand, partnering with Sales, and helping customers adopt and expand their use of OpenAI. You will build and lead a team of data scientists and work closely with Marketing, Sales, Product, Engineering, and Finance.

We’re looking for people who bring strong B2B marketing expertise, a track record of influencing growth strategy through Data Science, and experience building and developing high-performing teams.

This role is based in San Francisco, CA. We use a hybrid work model of 3 days in the office per week and offer relocation assistance to new employees.

## In this role, you will:

* **Be a strategic partner to B2B Marketing leadership.** Bring insights, recommendations, and a clear point of view to the most important business decisions. Help leaders understand where we are winning, where we have opportunities, and where to focus next.
* **Shape our growth strategy.** Identify the audiences, accounts, industries, and customer journeys where we can have the greatest impact. Connect market opportunity and customer signals to marketing and go-to-market priorities.
* **Build a full-funnel view of the business.** Connect awareness, engagement, demand, pipeline, conversion, adoption, and expansion. Make it easier for teams to understand how customers move through the journey and where we can improve their experience.
* **Measure what matters.** Build a thoughtful approach to measuring brand and demand generation, combining experiments, causal inference, marketing mix modeling, and other methods. Help leaders understand both near-term outcomes and longer-term effects, with clarity on uncertainty and tradeoffs.
* **Develop buying-group and account intelligence.** Bring together customer and account signals to understand who is engaging, where buying groups are developing momentum, and how Marketing and Sales can better reach and serve them.
* **Improve the path from self-serve to enterprise adoption.** Partner across Marketing, Sales, and Product to understand when businesses are ready for a deeper relationship and how to support the transition to broader deployment and sustained usage.
* **Build AI-native intelligence capabilities.** Partner with Engineering and product teams to turn insights into scalable tools, recommendations, and workflows that help marketers and go-to-market teams make better decisions.
* **Build and develop a high-performing team.** Recruit and grow exceptional talent, set a high bar for analytical rigor and business impact, and create an environment where people can do their best work.

## You might thrive in this role if you:

* Have experience leading Data Science, Analytics, or a related team in a B2B business, with a track record of influencing growth strategy and business decisions.
* Have a strong understanding of B2B marketing and go-to-market, including demand generation, account-based marketing, enterprise sales cycles, and complex buying groups.
* Have experience measuring marketing effectiveness using experimentation, causal inference, and other approaches, and the judgment to choose the right method for the question.
* Have experience connecting marketing, sales, product, and customer data to understand the full customer journey.
* Have a track record of building strong partnerships with senior leaders, challenging assumptions constructively, and turning complex analysis into clear recommendations.
* Have experience hiring and developing high-performing teams, including leaders and senior individual contributors.
* Are comfortable operating in a fast-moving environment, moving between strategy and execution, and building capabilities where the path is not yet defined.

**We’d be especially excited if you bring:**

* Experience building predictive models, account intelligence, or recommendation systems that have been adopted by business teams.
* Experience with product-led growth or self-serve-to-sales motions alongside enterprise go-to-market.
* Curiosity about AI and a desire to use it to rethink how marketing and go-to-market teams operate.
