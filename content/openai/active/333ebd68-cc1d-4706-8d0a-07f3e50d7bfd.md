## **About the Team**

OpenAI’s B2B Marketing team helps businesses understand, adopt, and get value from our products. We work across Product Marketing, Partnerships, Sales, Product, Engineering, Communications, and Marketing to connect advances in AI with the problems organizations are trying to solve.

We’re building our partner marketing function as OpenAI’s products and ecosystem evolve. Our work starts with understanding what OpenAI and our partners can build together, where those solutions solve meaningful customer problems, and how to bring them to market.

## **About the Role**

We’re looking for a Partner Marketing Manager to own go-to-market strategy and execution with strategic technology partners and independent software vendors (ISVs). You’ll bring a product marketer’s approach to the work. You’ll get hands-on with OpenAI’s products and partner integrations, learn how shared customers work, and develop a clear point of view on where the combined solution offers differentiated value.

You’ll turn that work into joint positioning, launch plans, demos, customer stories, campaigns, and sales enablement. You’ll work with OpenAI’s Product Marketing teams to extend our core product narratives into partner-specific use cases, and with Partnerships and your counterparts at partner companies to agree on priorities, coordinate execution, and measure results.

This is a hands-on individual contributor role with no people-management responsibility. You’ll help shape how we prioritize partner opportunities and develop repeatable approaches to joint launches, campaigns, and enablement.

This role is based in San Francisco and follows a hybrid work model, with three days per week in the office.

## **In this role, you will**

* **Identify where we can win together.** Use OpenAI and partner products directly. Work with customers, Product, Engineering, and Solutions teams to understand what the integration supports today, where it fits into existing workflows, and what could drive adoption.
* **Own joint positioning and messaging.** Define priority audiences and use cases, articulate what customers can do with the combined solution, and explain why it matters and how it compares with alternatives. Test the story with customers, technical teams, and the field.
* **Build and execute joint go-to-market plans.** Align with partner teams on audiences, goals, investment, and responsibilities. Lead launches and co-marketing programs with Partnerships, Sales, Communications, and Field Marketing.
* **Create the materials that bring the story to market.** Personally develop launch messaging, solution briefs, demo storylines, customer examples, and sales presentations that make complex capabilities concrete for developers, business users, and enterprise buyers.
* **Equip OpenAI and partner teams to sell and support adoption.** Build practical enablement for both organizations, including how to identify the right customers, demonstrate the solution, answer common objections, and help customers get started.
* **Prioritize opportunities across the partner portfolio.** Recommend where to invest based on customer demand, product readiness, differentiation, and commercial potential. Adjust plans as products and partner priorities evolve.
* **Use results to improve the work.** Track customer feedback, product usage, field engagement, pipeline, and revenue where relevant. Share adoption barriers and integration gaps with OpenAI and partner product teams.

## **You might thrive in this role if you have**

* 10+ years of relevant experience in product marketing, partner marketing, product management, or a related role, including ownership of positioning and bringing technical products or solutions to market.
* Experience building go-to-market plans with technology partners, platform companies, or ISVs and working across organizations to turn shared priorities into launches, campaigns, or sales enablement.
* Strong product and technical judgment. You use AI products directly, experiment with new capabilities, and can explain what an integration supports today and where its limitations matter to customers.
* Exceptional writing and storytelling skills, with examples of positioning, messaging, demos, or launch materials you personally developed.
* A record of using customer, market, and competitive insight to influence product priorities, messaging, or go-to-market decisions.
* The ability to influence technical teams, commercial stakeholders, and external partners, make clear tradeoffs, and move work forward when products or priorities are still taking shape.
* A track record of connecting your work to customer adoption, field engagement, pipeline, revenue, or other meaningful business outcomes.
