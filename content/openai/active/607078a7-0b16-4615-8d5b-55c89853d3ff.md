**About the team**

OpenAI’s Compute Strategy & Partnerships team develops the partnerships and transactions that support our long-term ambitions. Our work across industrial compute spans cloud infrastructure, semiconductors, datacenters, energy, and financing—bringing together the technology, capital, and commercial relationships needed to build and deploy AI at scale.

**About the role**

We’re looking for a Business Development professional to help originate, structure, negotiate, and execute complex strategic partnerships. You’ll work at the intersection of business strategy, finance, technology, and law, turning ambitious objectives into executable agreements and durable relationships.

This role offers direct commercial responsibility across the transaction lifecycle: identifying opportunities, developing a business case, shaping deal terms, aligning internal stakeholders, negotiating with counterparties, and supporting implementation. Many of these transactions involve novel structures, competing priorities, and decisions with significant long-term consequences.

We welcome candidates with backgrounds in business development, transactional law, finance, or investing. An experienced attorney interested in applying their deal skills to commercial strategy and execution could be an excellent fit.

**Key Responsibilities**

* **Develop strategic opportunities.** Identify and evaluate partnerships across the compute and infrastructure ecosystem, informed by OpenAI’s priorities and an understanding of counterparties’ capabilities and incentives.
* **Structure and negotiate transactions.** Develop commercial proposals and negotiate arrangements involving capacity, hardware supply, infrastructure development, financing, strategic investments, and joint ventures.
* **Evaluate economics and risk.** Work with finance and technical partners to assess pricing, capital requirements, financing alternatives, delivery commitments, and downside scenarios.
* **Connect strategy to contract terms.** Translate business objectives and technical requirements into practical terms that balance economics, flexibility, accountability, and execution certainty.
* **Drive decisions and execution.** Coordinate engineering, infrastructure, finance, legal, and other stakeholders; resolve open issues; and maintain momentum through diligence, approvals, negotiation, and closing.
* **Build lasting partner relationships.** Understand counterparties’ objectives, navigate difficult negotiations constructively, and develop solutions that support successful execution.
* **Communicate clearly with leadership.** Distill complex issues into concise recommendations that explain the opportunity, key tradeoffs, and decisions required.
* **Support partnerships after signing.** Help manage commercial commitments, address changing circumstances, and translate lessons from individual transactions into better structures and processes.

**Qualifications**

* Eight or more years of relevant experience in business development, corporate development, transactional law, investment banking, investing, or a related field.
* Meaningful experience structuring and negotiating complex transactions. Relevant backgrounds include M&A, structured finance, project finance, restructuring, commercial real estate, and strategic commercial agreements.
* Strong commercial and financial judgment, including the ability to connect contractual provisions to economics, incentives, and practical business outcomes.
* A record of taking ownership of demanding workstreams and advancing transactions involving multiple parties and competing priorities.
* Excellent writing and communication skills, with the ability to explain complex matters clearly to executives, technical colleagues, and external partners.
* Sound judgment about which issues matter most, when to escalate, and how to propose a workable path forward.
* Intellectual curiosity, attention to detail, and the ability to learn unfamiliar technologies and industries quickly.

**Preferred Skills**

* Juris Doctor (JD).
* Experience with capital-intensive businesses, infrastructure, technology, energy, or semiconductor supply chains.
* Experience developing novel transaction structures or negotiating agreements involving multiple interdependent parties.
* Familiarity with financial models and evaluating investment or financing alternatives.
* Prior AI or semiconductor experience is helpful, but strong transaction skills, commercial judgment, and a willingness to learn are central to success in this role.
