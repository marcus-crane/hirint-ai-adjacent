**About the Role**

Help organizations turn AI into meaningful business results.   
  
OpenAI’s **Sales** teams work directly with customers to identify valuable uses of our technology. Our **Partnerships** teams work with technology and services partners to develop joint business plans, bring solutions to market, and help more customers adopt AI.

This is a **talent pool for GTM Sales and Partnerships** professionals across customer segments and industries. Whether you build customer relationships, develop alliances, or grow partner ecosystems, you can apply without choosing a specific team or opening. You do not need experience in both sales and partnerships.

**In this role, you will:**

Depending on the opportunity, your work may include:

* Developing account or partner strategies, building pipeline, and advancing commercial opportunities from discovery through negotiation and execution.
* Building trusted relationships with customers, partners, executives, and technical teams, and translating their priorities into a clear business case for AI.
* Creating joint business plans and coordinating co-selling, partner enablement, and delivery with internal teams and external partners.
* Supporting customer deployment, adoption, and growth, and tracking forecasts, commercial performance, or partner outcomes relevant to your role.
* Bringing customer, partner, and market insights back to Product, Research, and the wider GTM organization.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have experience in Sales, Partnerships, business development, or a related commercial role, with evidence of customer or partner impact.
* Can build trust across organizations, navigate complex relationships, and communicate credibly with executives and technical teams.
* Bring sound commercial judgment, turn strategy into practical plans, and stay close to execution and measurable outcomes.
* Are curious about AI, learn quickly, and take ownership in a fast-changing environment.
