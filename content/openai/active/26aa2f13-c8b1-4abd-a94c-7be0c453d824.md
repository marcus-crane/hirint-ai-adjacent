**About the Team**

The Hardware Marketing team helps people understand what OpenAI’s products can do and how they can fit into everyday life. We work with Product, Design, Creative, Sales, and Operations to bring new products to market and create experiences that let people try them for themselves.

**About the Role**

We’re looking for a Product Marketing Manager to lead hardware product marketing in Japan. You’ll define who we should reach in Japan, how we explain the product’s value, and how we bring it to market. You’ll bring Japanese consumer insight into product and launch planning early, so it shapes decisions before the work reaches translation.

You’ll report to the International Marketing Lead and also serve as the Asia-Pacific (APAC) marketing lead for Hardware. In that regional role, you’ll coordinate planning with country teams, identify needs they share, and bring market-specific decisions to the global team.

**In this role, you will:**

* Get to know customers in Japan. Use research, competitive context, and customer feedback to identify priority audiences, needs, and barriers to adoption. Bring that evidence into product and marketing decisions.
* Own positioning and messaging for Japan. Turn global positioning into clear Japanese messaging, locally relevant use cases, and briefs. Work with Product to keep claims accurate and set clear expectations about what the product can do.
* Lead go-to-market planning for Japan. Work with Creative, Communications, integrated marketing, and partner-facing teams to carry the product story across campaigns, web, social, retail, and product education.
* Get the market ready for launch. Coordinate local materials and dependencies with Product, Operations, and commercial teams. Flag gaps early, agree on owners, and make sure teams have what they need to explain the product to customers.
* Coordinate hardware marketing across APAC. Work with country teams on shared planning, surface readiness risks, and identify where markets can reuse work and where they need a different approach.
* Use what you learn after launch. Set measures for customer understanding and adoption with Analytics. Use results and feedback to improve messaging, education, and future launch plans.

**You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* Led product marketing or go-to-market work for consumer products in Japan, from customer research and positioning through launch.
* Made technical products easy to understand without overstating what they can do. Experience with hardware or connected devices is a plus.
* Written clear customer-facing messaging in Japanese and worked effectively with global teams in English.
* Led work across Product, Creative, Communications, and commercial teams, and can make a clear case for when a global plan should change to meet local needs.
* Worked across markets or time zones and used customer feedback and performance data to improve a launch or marketing plan.
