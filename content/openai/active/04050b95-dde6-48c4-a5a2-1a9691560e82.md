**About the Team**

Customer Education helps enterprises build an AI-enabled workforce. We help people develop the skills, confidence and judgment to use AI in their roles, build and deploy AI solutions, and lead changes in how work gets done.

**About the Role**

We’re looking for an Advanced User Education Lead to help experienced and ambitious enterprise knowledge workers push further with AI. You’ll turn deep knowledge of OpenAI’s products and how to use them into practical learning that helps people expand their capabilities and achieve more in their work.

You’ll own the learning roadmap for this audience and build, test and deliver the programs yourself. You’ll work with product experts and education partners to help users extend what they can achieve with AI.

**In this role, you will:**

* Own a learning roadmap that helps enterprise knowledge workers deepen their AI expertise.
* Build deep product expertise by testing capabilities and finding valuable applications for enterprise knowledge workers.
* Create practical, reusable learning resources that help users stretch their AI skills through experimentation and practice.
* Incorporate the latest OpenAI capabilities into practical learning for enterprise knowledge workers.
* Use learner feedback and usage data to improve learning and assess skills and sustained product use.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have independently built, delivered and improved learning programs that helped people deepen their expertise at work.
* Use AI extensively, experiment with new capabilities and love helping others get more from it.
* Have turned complex concepts into clear explanations and guided practice for different skill levels.
* Are a strong writer and facilitator who can create engaging learning and teach live.
* Have delivered programs across product and customer-facing teams while navigating ambiguity and changing priorities.
* Use learner feedback and usage data to decide what to build, improve or stop.

**Nice to have**

* Experience with enterprise workforce learning or customer education in a fast-moving technology environment.
* Experience developing learning for professional audiences with different goals and levels of expertise.

**What success looks like**

* Learners apply new AI capabilities to more demanding work and achieve better results.
* Learning programs contribute to sustained product use and increased credit consumption.
