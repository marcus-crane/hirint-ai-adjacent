**About the Team**

OpenAI’s mission is to ensure that general-purpose artificial intelligence benefits all of humanity. The Ads team is exploring how AI can create more useful connections between people and businesses while helping businesses grow.

We’re particularly interested in making sophisticated advertising capabilities accessible to small and growing businesses. Many of these businesses lack dedicated marketing teams or deep advertising expertise. We’re building AI-native tools that can help them move from an idea to effective creative and a live campaign with greater clarity, confidence, and speed.

Design plays a foundational role in defining this emerging product area. The team works across consumer experiences, advertiser tools, AI-assisted creation, and the systems needed to launch, understand, and improve campaigns.

**About the Role**

We’re looking for a Product Designer to shape the next generation of advertising experiences at OpenAI. You’ll design across the advertiser journey—from setup and activation to AI-assisted creative, campaign management, measurement, and iteration.

This is not simply a traditional ads-manager or dashboard-design role. The opportunity combines growth design, technically dense tooling, and new AI-native workflows. You’ll rethink how much expertise should be required to create effective advertising and design experiences that feel approachable to a small-business owner without limiting more sophisticated users.

Craft is central to this role. You’ll be expected to make complex tools feel clear, polished, and coherent while moving quickly, testing ideas, and learning from real-world use.

This role is based in our San Francisco HQ. We offer relocation assistance to eligible employees.

**In this role, you will:**

* Lead end-to-end design for advertiser experiences, from discovery and setup through creation, launch, measurement, and iteration.
* Establish a high bar for interaction design, visual craft, information architecture, and end-to-end product quality.
* Design AI-assisted creative workflows that help businesses turn their goals, brand context, and existing assets into effective advertising.
* Simplify complex campaign tools so they remain powerful for experienced advertisers and approachable to people with little advertising expertise.
* Identify and remove friction across advertiser acquisition, onboarding, activation, engagement, and retention.
* Partner closely with product, engineering, research, data science, go-to-market, and design to shape both strategy and execution.
* Use prototypes, research, experimentation, and quantitative evidence to evaluate ideas and improve outcomes.
* Define new interaction patterns for collaboration between advertisers and AI systems.
* Contribute reusable patterns that create coherence across advertiser tooling and related business experiences.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have a portfolio demonstrating exceptional product thinking, interaction design, visual craft, and attention to detail.
* Have designed and shipped sophisticated SaaS, enterprise, platform, or technically dense products.
* Can make complex tools feel intuitive and visually compelling without hiding the details users need.
* Bring strong growth instincts and understand how design can improve acquisition, onboarding, activation, conversion, and retention.
* Can explain the reasoning behind important craft decisions and show how iteration strengthened the final product.
* Are highly generative, move with urgency, and can turn ambiguous opportunities into clear product direction.
* Use AI meaningfully in your own design process to explore, prototype, communicate, or build.
* Balance user needs, business goals, technical constraints, and measurable outcomes with strong design judgment.
* Communicate clearly and build alignment through compelling narratives and prototypes.
* Are excited to invent advertising experiences that feel native to AI rather than reproducing established industry patterns.

Direct experience in advertising, monetization, or growth is helpful but not required. We’re most interested in designers with exceptional craft, strong product judgment, experience simplifying complex workflows, and genuine excitement about helping businesses grow.
