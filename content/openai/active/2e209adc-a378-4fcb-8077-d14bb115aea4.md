**About the Team**

The SMB Ads team helps businesses discover, adopt, and grow with OpenAI’s advertising solutions. As we scale globally, we are building a high-performing sales organization that combines operational excellence, strong customer experiences, and AI-native ways of working.

We partner closely with Sales, Revenue Operations, Finance, Product, Enablement, Program Management, and external BPO partners to drive advertiser acquisition, activation, revenue growth, and retention.

**About the Role**

We are hiring a Vendor Lead to build and scale the outsourced operating model that supports our Ads business in EMEA and globally.

This is a builder role. You will design the governance, performance systems, capacity plans, quality programs, and accountability mechanisms that enable our BPO partners to deliver exceptional commercial outcomes. You will work across internal teams and external partner leadership to improve seller productivity, advertiser growth, forecasting accuracy, and operational quality.

This is a highly cross-functional individual contributor role that sits at the intersection of Ads Sales, SMB, Revenue Operations, Finance, Enablement, Product, Program Management, and external BPO leadership. You will help define how OpenAI operates with external partners and build the systems, processes, and accountability mechanisms that enable the business to scale.

**In this role, you will:**

* Build and scale BPO operating models, governance structures, and performance frameworks for Ads.
* Drive results across revenue, pipeline, advertiser acquisition, activation, retention, seller productivity, and quality.
* Lead business reviews and executive governance forums with BPO partners and internal stakeholders.
* Develop scorecards, forecasts, inspection mechanisms, and escalation processes that surface risks early.
* Improve sales quality through playbooks, QA frameworks, coaching programs, and performance analysis.
* Partner with Sales, Revenue Operations, Product, Enablement, Finance, and Program teams on new products, markets, and sales motions.
* Create workforce and capacity plans aligned to business demand and growth forecasts.
* Determine which customer segments and sales motions should be handled by BPO partners versus internal teams.
* Use AI, automation, and scalable systems to improve execution, visibility, and seller productivity.

**You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* 5+ years of experience in vendor operations, commercial operations, outsourced sales, or a scaled revenue organization.
* Experience in Ads, advertiser growth, performance marketing, or a comparable SMB sales motion.
* Working knowledge of sales funnels, forecasting, advertiser activation, spend growth, retention, and churn.
* Experience improving operating processes, scorecards, quality programs, or capacity plans across vendors or markets.
* Strong analytical, operational, and communication skills, with the ability to turn data into clear actions and work effectively across teams.
* Interest in using AI and automation to improve execution, visibility, and seller productivity.

**Workplace & Location**

* This role is based in Dublin, Ireland. We use a hybrid work model of 3 days in the office per week. Occasional travel will be required.
