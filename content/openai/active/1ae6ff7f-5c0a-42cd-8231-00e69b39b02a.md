# **About the Role**

On OpenAI’s Cyber Partnerships team, you’ll help partners across the Americas build red-team and purple-team services that improve customer defenses. You’ll represent Daybreak in the partner community, showing security teams how to use AI for cyber defense and how to secure AI systems.

You’ll work with practice leaders and sellers at systems integrators, managed security service providers, and specialist firms to turn customer needs into services their practitioners can deliver repeatedly.

# **In this role, you will**

* **Build red-team and purple-team offerings with partners.** Package fixed-scope red-team assessments and recurring purple-team exercises. Define what customers buy, what partners deliver, and where Daybreak supports the work.
* **Be Daybreak’s go-to partner speaker.** Through partner events, live demos, and workshops, show practitioners how to use AI for cyber defense and how to secure the AI systems they deploy.
* **Set delivery standards for partner red- and purple-team engagements.** Help partners scope authorized engagements, agree on success criteria with customers, and verify improvements in detection and response after fixes.
* **Turn partner ideas into pilots and product proposals.** Lead early customer pilots with partners and bring recurring delivery needs and lessons from those pilots to Daybreak Product.
* **Grow paid Daybreak usage through partner services.** Work with partner sellers and OpenAI account teams to help customers move from an initial assessment to recurring purple-team engagements. Partners earn service fees for delivery. Customers generate paid Daybreak usage when they use it to validate fixes and run follow-on exercises.

You’ll own offer design, practitioner enablement, and partner pilots. Partners sell and deliver the services. Partner Engineering owns integrations, technical validation, and deployment support. Cyber Partnerships leads partner strategy and commercial commitments.

# **You might thrive in this role if you have**

* Hands-on experience in authorized security testing, with depth in red teaming and experience working with defenders to improve detection, response, or remediation.
* Experience building or launching repeatable security services, with an understanding of how to price, staff, and deliver them consistently.
* The ability to explain technical security concepts to buyers and practitioners through customer conversations, demos, workshops, or training.
* Experience helping customers adopt a new security or AI product and applying lessons from early deployments to later engagements.
* The ability to build trust with partner leaders and work across sales, engineering, and product teams.

## **Preferred experience**

* Familiarity with AI models and their harnesses, including how the surrounding software supplies tools and context and enforces permissions.

# **What success looks like**

* Partners launch, sell, and repeatedly deliver red-team and purple-team offers.
* Customers continue using Daybreak after the initial engagement, generating paid usage through fix validation and follow-on exercises.
* Customers can verify improvements in detection and response, and lessons from pilots inform Daybreak product decisions.
