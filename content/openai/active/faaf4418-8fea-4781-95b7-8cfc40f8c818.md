**About the Team**

OpenAI's Legal team works on the legal questions that arise from developing and deploying advanced AI. Our Security and Public Sector Legal team helps protect OpenAI's people, technology, and services and supports their safe and responsible use. We partner closely with technical and operational teams to provide clear advice, navigate sensitive matters, and build practical safeguards.

**About the Role**

We are seeking an Associate General Counsel to advise across national security, cybersecurity, and platform abuse investigations and help build the programs and processes that support our work as OpenAI continues to scale. You will combine exceptional legal judgment with substantial in-house experience, turning complex requirements into effective day-to-day operations.

You will own a varied portfolio, lead sensitive matters, and help teams move from individual solutions to consistent, well-run programs. We are looking for someone who enjoys both counseling on difficult questions and doing the detailed work of implementation: defining responsibilities, securing alignment, developing useful processes, and making sure they work in practice.

You will report to the Deputy General Counsel leading Security and Public Sector Legal and work closely with Security, Product Policy, Global Affairs, and colleagues across Legal.

This role is based in San Francisco, CA or Washington, DC. We use a hybrid work model of 3 days in the office per week and offer relocation assistance to new employees.

**You’ll be responsible for:**

* Advising on national security and security issues affecting AI development and deployment, including protecting sensitive technology, responding to incidents, and working with government and industry partners.
* Leading or supporting sensitive security and internal investigations, advising on scope, evidence, investigative methods, and practical remediation in partnership with the other legal and operational leads.
* Providing day-to-day counseling on cybersecurity, physical security, insider risk, threat intelligence, and information sharing.
* Building and scaling legal programs, including intake and triage, repeatable business guidance, playbooks, and measures of effectiveness.
* Counseling senior leaders through ambiguous decisions, preparing concise recommendations and executive updates, and managing outside counsel and other resources effectively.
* Partnering with specialists in privacy, product, employment, investigations, law enforcement response, trade compliance, and public-sector matters, and taking on emerging issues across the team's portfolio.

**We’re looking for someone with:**

* 15+ years of relevant legal experience, including substantial in-house experience at a technology company and responsibility for building or scaling legal, security, investigations, or compliance programs.
* Strong expertise in national security, cybersecurity, or investigations and a demonstrated ability to advise effectively across adjacent areas.
* A record of personally implementing programs, securing adoption, and improving decisions, execution, or risk management as an organization grew.
* Sound judgment under pressure and the ability to give clear, practical advice when the facts or legal framework are incomplete.
* The ability to build trust across functions, resolve differences constructively, and combine independent judgment with shared priorities and reliable execution.
* Clear, concise writing, the ability to learn unfamiliar technical subjects quickly, and comfort moving between strategic counseling and detailed operational work.
* A JD and an active license or qualification to practice law in your jurisdiction.
