## **About the Team**

Our Devices data engineering team provides the data foundations for developing, launching, and improving consumer devices—from manufacturing and research to product experiences and business operations.

We partner with engineering, product, research, data science, and operations, drawing on OpenAI’s broader data engineering expertise and shared infrastructure.

## **About the Role**

We’re looking for a Data Engineering Manager to build and lead this team, owning its strategy across manufacturing, device analytics, trust and safety, research, finance, logistics, customer operations, and marketing.

As an early leader, you will shape the team’s technical direction and culture while delivering against evolving launch and business needs. This role calls for someone comfortable growing a small function, setting priorities amid ambiguity, and achieving results through others.

This role is based in our San Francisco office.

## **In this role, you will:**

* Hire and develop data engineers, coach senior talent, and delegate domain ownership while setting a high bar for technical rigor and accountability.
* Set the roadmap around the most important launch and business needs. Align partners on capacity, sequence investments, and communicate delivery commitments, progress, and tradeoffs.
* Guide instrumentation, ingestion, and shared data models for manufacturing quality and traceability, device performance and usage, research, and safety.
* Define data contracts and measurement needs with engineering, product, research, and data science early in development.
* Build consistent, well-owned data products for finance, inventory, shipments, returns, customer support, and marketing.
* Set standards for data quality, freshness, monitoring, incident response, and recovery. Build privacy, access controls, and governance into systems from the start.
* Choose where to reuse shared platforms and where device or manufacturing needs warrant specialized capabilities, with clear ownership across infrastructure and partner teams.

## **You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have led data engineering teams delivering production systems across multiple domains, with demonstrated strength in hiring, coaching, and performance management.
* Have grown a data engineering function from a small starting point, establishing its scope, stakeholder relationships, and working culture.
* Bring technical depth in data modeling, event instrumentation, batch and streaming pipelines, orchestration, and warehouse or lakehouse architecture, with the judgment to challenge designs and guide senior engineers.
* Have operated data systems where reliability and privacy matter, and learned from incidents and recovery.
* Earn trust across technical and business teams and resolve competing or incomplete requests into shared priorities.
* Balance immediate delivery with durable foundations, and simplify solutions as needs evolve.
* Ideally bring experience with consumer devices, assistants, mobile products, wearables, or other connected products. Manufacturing, supply chain, device telemetry, or privacy-sensitive research experience is valuable; direct hardware experience is not required.

## **What success looks like**

* Device teams across the company move faster and make better decisions because they can rely on trustworthy data foundations provided by this team—from manufacturing and research to product development and customer operations.
* A healthy, growing team owns its domains independently, develops strong technical leaders, and consistently meets its commitments.
