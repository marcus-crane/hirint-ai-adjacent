**About the team**

The People team helps OpenAI build the teams, capabilities, and working practices needed to deliver on our mission. As AI changes what people and organizations can accomplish, we’re exploring how roles, teams, and ways of working should evolve and putting those ideas into practice within OpenAI.

**About the role**

We’re looking for an Organizational Design Lead to help build our organizational design capability and shape how people and AI agents work together.

You’ll partner with leaders and People colleagues to understand business goals, examine how work gets done, and design the roles, structures, decision processes, and workflows that help teams succeed. This includes exploring where AI can expand people’s capabilities, how responsibilities should evolve, and where human judgment and accountability are essential.

This is a hands-on role. You’ll move between understanding organizational challenges, developing new approaches, testing them with teams, and turning what works into practical tools and methods others can use. You’ll bring a thoughtful perspective on organizations and the ability to make change happen.

**In this role, you will:**

* Translate business goals into organizational choices. Help leaders identify the work, capabilities, roles, and team structures needed to achieve their goals.
* Design how people and AI agents work together. Rethink workflows, responsibilities, handoffs, and decision rights as AI capabilities evolve, with clear ownership and human accountability.
* Improve team effectiveness. Understand where unclear boundaries, coordination costs, or decision processes slow teams down, and develop practical ways to address them.
* Test ideas in real working environments. Partner with teams to prototype new operating practices, gather feedback, and refine approaches before applying them more broadly.
* Connect organizational design with technology. Work with People partners, analytics colleagues, and technical teams to turn ideas into useful tools, workflows, and working practices.
* Build capability across People and leadership. Develop simple frameworks, facilitate working sessions, and help others make sound organizational design decisions.
* Measure and share what works. Assess changes through business outcomes, quality, speed, team effectiveness, and employee experience, and translate learning into repeatable practices.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have meaningful experience in organizational design, operating model development, or organizational effectiveness, with examples of changes you helped implement.
* Understand organizations as interconnected systems of people, work, technology, incentives, and decisions.
* Can work credibly with senior leaders while staying close to the practical realities of teams.
* Bring a design mindset: you listen carefully, make ideas tangible, test assumptions, and adapt based on evidence.
* Have hands-on experience with AI tools and a thoughtful perspective on how AI could change roles, collaboration, and organizational structures.
* Combine strategic thinking with execution, moving from an ambiguous question to a useful intervention.
* Communicate clearly, facilitate difficult conversations, and help people navigate change with empathy and sound judgment.
* Are curious, collaborative, and willing to revise your approach as technology and organizational needs evolve.
