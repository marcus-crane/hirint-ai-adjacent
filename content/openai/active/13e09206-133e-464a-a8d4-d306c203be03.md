**About the Team**

OpenAI’s Education team works with K-12 districts, colleges and universities to bring AI into teaching, learning, research, and campus operations. We partner with the people who know those settings best, from faculty and teaching center staff to campus IT teams. Together, we help them choose where AI can be useful, learn how to evaluate its work, and build the expertise to teach others.

**About the Role**

As OpenAI’s **first Enablement Lead dedicated to Education**, you’ll shape how we help colleges and universities build that expertise. This is a hands-on, customer-facing individual contributor role. You’ll design the learning programs, teach the first cohorts yourself, and work with campus partners to lead future cohorts independently.

ChatGPT Edu will be central to your work. You might help faculty redesign an assignment around a specific learning objective, guide student developers through an OpenAI API or Codex lab, or help a campus IT team evaluate an AI-assisted workflow. You’ll work with institutional leaders to decide what success looks like and return after the session to see what people are using, adapting, and teaching their peers.

This role blends deep technical fluency, instructional design, and customer advisory. You will lead live trainings, workshops, and adoption interventions for audiences ranging from students, faculty members, staff, academic leaders and other relevant constituents, helping them understand not just what OpenAI’s products can do, but how to use them effectively in real-world contexts.

Success in this role means accelerating customer confidence and value, increasing product adoption, helping customers progress toward production use, and turning lessons from individual engagements into resources and practices that benefit many customers.

**In this role, you will:**

* Own the learning plan for an institution from onboarding through broader adoption of OpenAI products, including ChatGPT Work, OpenAI APIs, Codex, Daybreak and other platform capabilities.
* Work with Sales and Applied AI Architects to understand the institution’s goals, then identify who needs to learn what and where hands-on support will help.
* Design and deliver high-impact training engagements, including onboarding sessions, advanced capability training sessions, executive briefings, hackathons, and hands-on workshops for audiences ranging from senior leaders to working teams.
* Equip teaching centers, campus IT teams, and faculty or staff champions to teach others. Co-teach a session, give facilitators materials they can adapt, and use their experience leading the next cohort to improve the program.
* Develop and refine reusable training assets, playbooks, and best practices based on patterns observed across customers and regions and scale them through OAI’s partner network.
* Gather customer feedback from training and enablement engagements, synthesize themes across accounts, and relay insights to internal stakeholders to inform product and program improvements.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have 10+ years of experience owning an enterprise enablement or adoption initiative from discovery through results, aligning executive sponsors, business users, and technical teams around a measurable customer outcome.
* Possess exceptional presentation and communication skills, particularly when conveying the value of technical concepts clearly to senior and executive-level audiences.
* Can build, test, and troubleshoot a working example using APIs or developer tools such as Codex. You can walk developers through how it works, explain how to evaluate the results, and help them adapt it to their own use case.
* Have worked with academic stakeholders and understand how decisions get made across a campus. That experience may come from working at an institution or from supporting institutions as a partner.
* Are comfortable teaching live when a demo breaks or a participant challenges an assumption. You listen, troubleshoot openly, and use what you learn to make the next session better.
* Have a humble attitude, an eagerness to help others, and a desire to pick up whatever knowledge you're missing and are personally committed to fostering the safe and ethical evolution of AI.
