## **About the Team**

Presence is OpenAI’s team building AI agents that organizations can trust to handle real conversations and get things done. Our mission is to turn advances in frontier models into dependable systems that can resolve customer issues, support employees, and improve through experience in production.

Imagine calling about an unexpected charge. An agent needs to understand your situation, verify your identity, find the relevant account information, apply the right policy, and resolve the issue or bring in a person. It needs to do all of this naturally, accurately, and quickly, even when the conversation takes an unexpected turn. Presence brings together the models, execution systems, integrations, and controls that make experiences like this possible across voice, chat and email.

One of our most exciting challenges is what happens after an agent goes live. We're building a system for understanding where agents fail and making them better: investigate a production conversation, reproduce the problem in an evaluation, use Codex to develop a fix, test it against the current agent, and measure the result after deployment. Our ambition is to make more of this improvement process agentic over time, while giving people control over what changes reach users.

Read the [launch announcement](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-presence/).

## What we work on

* **Realtime voice:** Natural conversations with fast responses, interruptions, and reliable audio, even under heavy traffic.
* **Agent systems:** Orchestration, reasoning, tool execution, integrations with company systems, and enforcing permissions and policies.
* **Agent quality:** Production debugging, simulations, evaluation infrastructure, regression testing, and measuring whether changes improve real outcomes.
* **Product and developer experience:** Making it easier for teams to build, test, deploy, and operate sophisticated agents using Codex and OpenAI's capabilities.

## **In this role, you will:**

You'll work at the intersection of frontier research, product engineering, and production systems, with a direct feedback loop from people using what you build. We collaborate closely with Research, Forward Deployed Engineers, and customers to understand where models and systems break down and turn those lessons into improvements that can benefit many deployments.

The impact is already tangible. At launch, Presence was resolving 75% of issues coming into OpenAI's English phone support without a human, and its Codex-assisted improvement process helped cut transfers to people by 15 percentage points over ten days. [Introducing OpenAI Presence](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-presence/)

We're looking for people excited by ambitious engineering problems, building products people depend on, and helping define how AI agents are developed and improved in the real world.
