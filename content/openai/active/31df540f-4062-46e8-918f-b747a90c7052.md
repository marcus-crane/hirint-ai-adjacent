**About the Team**

OpenAI’s People team supports a complex, rapidly growing organization of researchers, engineers, and business teams. The Compensation function ensures accuracy, scalability, and operational excellence across all compensation tools, systems, and data flows.

**About the Role**

We’re hiring a Compensation Operations Analyst to build and run the systems, tools, and processes that power compensation at OpenAI. This role is deeply operational and technical: you will configure HRIS modules, manage compensation tools end-to-end, maintain pristine data quality, and ensure compensation cycles run flawlessly. You’ll partner closely with People Systems, Total Rewards, Finance, and vendors to keep our compensation infrastructure accurate, scalable, and audit-ready.

This role is based in San Francisco, CA. We use a hybrid work model of 3 days in the office per week and offer relocation assistance to new employees.

**In this role, you will:**

* Own configuration and day-to-day operations of compensation tools and HRIS compensation modules.
* Manage annual and off-cycle compensation processes, ensuring data integrity, testing, quality assurance, and on-time execution.
* Build and maintain compensation datasets, dashboards, audits, and data flows across systems.
* Lead compensation vendor relationships, including implementation, troubleshooting, and data submissions.
* Diagnose and resolve system issues, identify root causes, and drive process and tooling improvements.
* Partner with People Systems and Engineering to enhance architecture, workflows, and automation.
* Ensure high data accuracy across compensation systems and proactively identify gaps, risks, or anomalies.
* Translate technical or complex compensation system issues into clear guidance for stakeholders.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have 5+ years of experience in compensation operations, HR operations/systems, or total rewards tooling in high-growth or technology environments.
* Have strong HRIS configuration experience, especially with compensation planning modules; Workday and Pave experience is preferred.
* Bring rigor and precision to data auditing, validation, and lifecycle management.
* Can manage and optimize tools at scale while navigating fast-moving environments.
* Communicate effectively across functions and are comfortable working with technical partners.
* Are action-oriented, organized, and reliable under tight deadlines.
* Have a strong ownership mindset and a bias toward simple, scalable systems.
