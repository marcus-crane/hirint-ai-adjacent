### About the Team:

OpenAI’s Hardware organization develops AI-native silicon and system-level solutions for the unique demands of advanced AI workloads. Building on efforts like Jalapeño, the team is developing future generations of AI-native silicon and tightly integrated systems to power the next generation of frontier models. By co-designing chips, systems, tools, and methodologies, the team helps deliver faster, more efficient, and production-ready hardware for OpenAI’s supercomputing platform.

### About the Role:

We’re looking for an Advanced Manufacturing Technology Lead to develop the fabrication and assembly capabilities behind next-generation high-density, high-speed, high-power compute boards. You will own the manufacturing technology roadmap across bare printed circuit board (PCB) fabrication and board assembly, qualifying critical materials, structures, and processes before product designs are locked.

This is a hands-on technical leadership role spanning early experiments, test vehicles, pilot builds, and transfer to new product introduction (NPI) teams. You’ll partner with board design, signal integrity, packaging, and reliability engineers, along with PCB fabricators, assembly partners, and equipment suppliers, to translate manufacturing evidence into practical design rules and robust production processes.

### In this role, you will:

* Own the manufacturing technology roadmap for next-generation compute boards, identifying gaps across bare PCB fabrication and board assembly and developing capabilities ahead of product needs.
* Partner with board design, signal integrity, packaging, and reliability teams to qualify materials, stackups, via structures, and interconnects before layout freeze, translating results into design rules suppliers can build to.
* Prototype and validate assembly, inspection, and rework methods for large, complex ASIC packages, establishing process windows that address warpage and solder-joint integrity.
* Validate defect-detection methods using known-good and known-defect samples, defining inspection coverage, repeatability, and acceptance criteria.
* Lead test-vehicle and pilot builds with PCB fabricators, assembly partners, and equipment suppliers, qualifying processes against yield, capability, reliability, and throughput targets.
* Transfer validated process windows, tooling requirements, inspection methods, and process controls to NPI teams with clear qualification evidence and handoff documentation.
* Serve as a technical lead for advanced PCB fabrication and board assembly, mentoring engineers, guiding stackup and process decisions, and turning cross-functional failure analysis into reusable design rules and controls.

### You might thrive in this role if you:

* Have developed and qualified advanced PCB fabrication and board assembly processes, taking new technologies from experiments and test vehicles through pilot builds and production transfer.
* Bring strong technical depth in PCB materials, stackups, via structures, and interconnects, and understand how fabrication choices affect electrical performance, manufacturability, and reliability.
* Have hands-on experience with assembly of large, complex ASIC packages, including package-to-board interactions, warpage, solder-joint integrity, and process-window development.
* Have developed or qualified inspection and rework methods, using representative samples and failure-analysis evidence to understand detection limits and verify process effectiveness.
* Use structured experiments, measurement-system validation, and statistical process analysis to establish capability and make clear qualification decisions.
* Work effectively with fabricators, assembly partners, and equipment suppliers, balancing technical risk, yield, reliability, and throughput while transferring processes into production.
* Lead through technical judgment and collaboration, with experience mentoring engineers and influencing board design, signal integrity, packaging, and reliability decisions early in development.
