**About the Team**

Customer Education helps enterprises develop the capabilities their workforce needs to use AI effectively, build and deploy AI solutions, and get more value from OpenAI’s products.

**About the Role**

We’re looking for a Customer Education Adoption Lead to own the adoption and growth strategy for OpenAI’s education programs across enterprise audiences. You will build campaigns, develop distribution channels and improve learner journeys to grow enrollment, participation and continued engagement. You’ll combine strong messaging and hands-on execution with experimentation and analysis to understand what drives results.

You will work with Customer Education, Marketing, and account teams to grow participation and engagement in our education programs.

**In this role, you will:**

* Set the quarterly education adoption and growth plan. Prioritize audiences, programs and channels using customer insight, field feedback and performance data.
* Lead campaigns that bring relevant audiences into our programs. Create campaign materials, test approaches and improve learner acquisition, working with Marketing and Marketing Operations on delivery.
* Build learner journeys with Lifecycle and program owners. Help employees get started, complete learning and take relevant next steps through timely, useful communications.
* Build distribution channels and resources that account teams and customers can use to promote learning across their organizations.
* Set and track team goals for enrollment, learner starts, completion and continued engagement. Use experiments to improve conversion and learner progression, partnering with CE Operations, Data Science and RevOps to assess how education contributes to product activation and sustained use.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have owned education, activation, growth, lifecycle or content marketing programs for external enterprise customers, from audience strategy through launch and iteration, with measurable results in signups, activation and continued engagement.
* Have used audience segmentation, channel testing and lifecycle campaigns to grow participation and sustained engagement.
* Are a strong writer who creates clear, useful campaign content for different audiences.
* Have managed competing priorities and kept programs moving as customer needs and business priorities change.
* Use customer research, performance data and experiments to improve results.
* Use AI actively in your work and are curious about how it changes skills and learning.

**Nice to have**

* Experience partnering with account teams to promote customer education or workforce learning programs.
* Experience working in a fast-paced technology company or similarly complex environment.
