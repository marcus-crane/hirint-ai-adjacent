**About the Team:**OpenAI’s mission is to ensure that artificial general intelligence (AGI) benefits all of humanity. Achieving this goal requires more than cutting-edge research—it demands deep, ongoing engagement with the policymakers, communities, and institutions shaping the future of AI.

The Global Affairs team leads this engagement. We cultivate authentic, collaborative relationships with public officials, regulators, multilateral bodies, civil society leaders, and the broader AI policymaking community to ensure that diverse perspectives inform our work. By bringing external insights into OpenAI and sharing our own learnings transparently, we help advance thoughtful regulation, responsible industry standards, and governance frameworks that promote the safe and beneficial development of AI technologies.

In close partnership with our research, product, and policy colleagues, we work across regions to anticipate emerging issues, craft forward-looking strategies, and champion policies that align technological progress with human values. Our mission is to help shape the global conversation on AI and ensure that the benefits of this transformative technology reach people everywhere.

**About the Role**

As **Head of India Policy, Global Affairs** you will lead OpenAI’s public policy strategy, government engagement, and policy partnerships in India. You will help shape an environment that supports safe AI development and deployment, expands access to its benefits, and advances India’s ambitions for innovation and economic opportunity.

You will serve as a senior policy representative to Indian institutions and build partnerships that turn shared priorities into practical results. Working with Global Affairs leaders worldwide and colleagues in India, you will connect national and state priorities with OpenAI’s work on responsible adoption, public services, AI skills, and research.

This role is based in New Delhi, India, and requires travel within India and internationally.

**In this role, you will**

* Set and execute OpenAI’s India policy strategy, translating global priorities into focused national campaigns with clear goals, milestones, and measures of policy progress and public benefit.
* Build durable relationships with central and state governments, Parliament, regulators, industry, and civil society. Engage constructively across political perspectives and bring local insights into OpenAI’s decisions.
* Develop policy positions, consultation responses, and advocacy on AI governance, safety, privacy, copyright, and liability, working closely with Legal and specialist policy teams.
* Anticipate political and regulatory developments and give leadership clear, actionable advice on risks, opportunities, and tradeoffs affecting OpenAI and the people who use our products.
* Design and deliver partnerships with government, universities, nonprofits, and industry that strengthen AI fluency, support research, and improve public services. Advance local-language access and measure sustained use and outcomes for underserved communities.
* Work with commercial, technical, and infrastructure colleagues to address policy and procurement barriers to responsible adoption and support AI infrastructure opportunities aligned with India’s needs.
* Coordinate policy engagement around product launches and sensitive issues, including youth safety, election integrity, and cybersecurity. Partner with central response teams to ensure timely, accurate engagement with public institutions.
* Represent OpenAI in policy forums and support senior executive engagements. Lead cross-functional projects and external advisers with disciplined prioritization, resource management, and follow-through.

**You’ll thrive in this role if you:**

* 10 or more years of relevant experience in public policy, government, technology, or international affairs, including responsibility for senior stakeholder engagement in India.
* Understand India’s political institutions, central and state policymaking, and digital economy, and have a record of building trust with senior public officials and diverse communities.
* Have delivered meaningful policy outcomes and complex partnerships, with evidence of your ability to set strategy, build coalitions, and carry work through implementation.
* Understand AI policy and can quickly develop fluency in technical issues, explain them clearly, and exercise sound judgment about innovation, safety, and public trust.
* Lead through influence across legal, research, product, communications, and commercial teams, and turn competing priorities into clear decisions and coordinated action.
* Communicate and write exceptionally well in English for policy, technical, and public audiences. Professional proficiency in Hindi or another Indian language is an advantage.
* Demonstrate integrity, intellectual curiosity, and a collaborative approach. You are comfortable with ambiguity, open to challenge, and motivated by OpenAI’s mission.
