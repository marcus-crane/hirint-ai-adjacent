## About the Team

The Applied organization brings OpenAI’s most advanced technology to people and organizations around the world through products such as ChatGPT and the APIs that power a growing ecosystem of developer and enterprise applications. Data Engineering builds and operates trustworthy, secure, and reliable data systems that support decisions across OpenAI.

The Trust & Safety Data Engineering team builds privacy-safe foundations that help OpenAI understand and mitigate abuse, launch products safely, and operate accountable safety systems at scale.

## About the Role

We’re looking for a Data Engineering Manager to lead the Trust & Safety Data Engineering team. This role is accountable for the team’s health, talent, priorities, and results, as well as the data strategy for abuse detection, investigations, enforcement, safety measurement, launch readiness, and transparency reporting.

You will partner with senior individual contributors and cross-functional leaders to turn sensitive, fast-moving safety needs into durable, governed data products and ML data capabilities.

## In this role, you will:

* Build and grow a high-performing, inclusive team; hire well, develop senior engineers, give clear feedback, and manage performance effectively.
* Define the data strategy and roadmap with senior technical leaders, establishing clear ownership and delegation boundaries so decisions are made at the right level.
* Lead the delivery of scalable, governed datasets, pipelines, and data products for critical Trust & Safety workflows.
* Enable ML development with high-quality features, labels, training data, evaluation datasets, and production data pipelines.
* Set strong standards for metric definitions, data quality, privacy, security, access controls, documentation, reliability, observability, and incident response.
* Partner across Trust & Safety, Integrity, Investigations, Engineering, Data Science, Policy, Privacy, and Operations to translate ambiguous needs into measurable outcomes.
* Set clear goals, make explicit capacity and priority tradeoffs, and communicate progress, risks, and commitments across technical and operational stakeholders.

## You might thrive in this role if you:

* Have led and scaled data engineering teams, with evidence of strong hiring, senior-talent development, performance management, and sustained delivery through the team.
* Bring strong technical judgment across large-scale data systems—including SQL, Python or Scala, Spark, orchestration, data modeling, streaming, and lakehouse or warehouse architectures—and can challenge designs and guide senior engineers without owning every implementation detail.
* Have experience in trust and safety, integrity, abuse prevention, fraud, investigations, risk, privacy, or another domain where data systems inform consequential decisions.
* Build strong cross-functional partnerships and turn ambiguous needs into clear ownership, durable data contracts, technical roadmaps, and reliable execution.
* Care deeply about privacy, security, governance, data quality, and the operational health of systems used for sensitive workflows.

## What success looks like

* Trust & Safety teams across OpenAI move faster because they can rely on trustworthy, well-owned data foundations provided by this team.
* The team operates with clear goals, healthy execution rhythms, explicit capacity tradeoffs, strong technical and operational standards, and a hiring and development plan that matches the domain’s ambition.
