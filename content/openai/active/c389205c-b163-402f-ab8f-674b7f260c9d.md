**About the Team**

OpenAI’s Industrial Compute team is developing data center infrastructure to support increasingly demanding AI workloads. The team brings together site development, civil and structural engineering, utilities, construction, commissioning, and operations to move campuses from early evaluation through delivery.

**About the Role**

We’re looking for a Geotechnical Engineer to help evaluate, plan, and deliver data center campuses. You’ll translate subsurface conditions into practical recommendations for site selection, campus design, construction, and long-term reliability. The work spans early due diligence through design review and field execution, with a focus on identifying ground-related risks early and helping teams make informed decisions.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Develop, update, and maintain geotechnical and geoenvironmental scopes of work, Owner’s Project Requirements (OPRs), technical specifications, and engineering standards for site due diligence and design phases, ensuring consistency across data center and power infrastructure projects while supporting risk mitigation, cost optimization, and accelerated project deliver
* Plan and review geotechnical investigations, including borings, in-situ testing, laboratory testing, and groundwater monitoring.
* Evaluate subsurface conditions and develop recommendations for foundations, settlement, seismic design, earthwork, subgrades, pavements, retaining systems, slopes, and groundwater management.
* Assess geotechnical risks during site selection and due diligence, including variable ground conditions, liquefaction, expansive or compressible soils, karst, settlement, and constructability constraints.
* Review geotechnical reports, calculations, drawings, specifications, and consultant deliverables for technical quality and consistency with project requirements.
* Partner with civil, structural, architectural, utilities, and construction teams to integrate geotechnical recommendations into campus plans and building designs.
* Support permitting and agency coordination by providing clear technical documentation and responding to review comments.
* Provide construction-phase support, including review of submittals and field conditions, evaluation of differing site conditions, and recommendations for corrective actions.
* Help establish consistent geotechnical standards, investigation scopes, design criteria, and risk documentation across projects and sites.
* Communicate ground-related risks, assumptions, and tradeoffs clearly to technical and nontechnical stakeholders.
* Review construction documents for foundation systems, earthwork, and ground improvement solutions for data center buildings, equipment yards, and power generation plants to ensure compliance with owner requirements, applicable codes, and project-specific geotechnical recommendations.

**Qualifications**

* A degree in civil engineering, geological engineering, geotechnical engineering, or a related discipline.
* Professional experience in geotechnical engineering for large, complex infrastructure, industrial, mission-critical, or campus-scale projects.
* Strong technical judgment across subsurface investigation, foundation recommendations, earthwork, settlement, groundwater, and seismic considerations.
* Experience reviewing consultant work and translating findings into actionable design and construction recommendations.
* Familiarity with geotechnical analysis methods and common engineering tools used for foundation, slope, settlement, and earthwork evaluation.
* The ability to work effectively with consultants, contractors, designers, and project teams while maintaining clear ownership of technical decisions.
* Strong written and verbal communication, including the ability to explain uncertainty and risk.

**Preferred Skills**

* Experience supporting data centers, power generation, advanced manufacturing, or other critical infrastructure.
* Experience with large campus developments, phased construction, and multiple building or utility packages.
* Familiarity with local permitting, building-code, and geotechnical design requirements across different jurisdictions.
* Professional Engineer license, or ability to obtain licensure where required.
* Experience developing repeatable technical standards or reviewing work across a portfolio of sites.
