**About the Team**

OpenAI’s User Operations team shepherds our customers’ adoption of AI and ensures that our customers' product experience is nothing short of exceptional.We are building the very first post-AGI support team.We resolve complex issues, provide technical guidance, and support customers in maximizing value and adoption from deploying our products. We work closely with Sales, Technical Success, Product, Engineering and others, to deliver the best possible experience to our customers at scale. OpenAI's customers represent a range of diverse backgrounds and maturity, from early-stage startups to established global enterprises.

Within Premium Support, Dedicated Support Engineers combine deep technical troubleshooting with an enduring understanding of our most strategic customers’ architectures, critical workloads, and business priorities. Through proactive reliability work, ownership during incidents, and AI-powered support capabilities, we help customers operate successfully as their use of OpenAI grows.

**About the Role**

We are looking for a leader to build and develop a team of Dedicated Support Engineers supporting OpenAI’s most strategic enterprise customers. Reporting to the Head of Dedicated Support Engineering, you will lead a team expected to include approximately 10–15 engineers. Your team will work directly with customers and partner with Product, Engineering, and field teams to resolve complex technical problems and help customers run critical artificial intelligence workloads reliably.

You will combine people leadership with strong technical judgment, guiding challenging investigations and customer escalations while coaching engineers to make sound decisions independently. Beyond resolving incidents, you will help the team develop a deep understanding of customers’ architectures and business priorities, identify recurring risks, and turn those insights into proactive reliability work and product improvements.

You will shape how the team operates as it grows: establishing account ownership, planning capacity and coverage, building shared knowledge, and improving playbooks and handoffs. Working with the function head, you will contribute to reliable, round-the-clock support coverage and broader service improvements, including carefully tested artificial intelligence and automation that increase quality and capacity.

The ideal candidate brings technical depth, a record of developing experienced engineers, and the ability to build effective teams and operating practices in a rapidly changing environment.

This role is based in San Francisco, CA. We use a hybrid work model of 3 days in the office per week and offer relocation assistance to new employees.

**In this role you will:**

* Hire, coach, and develop experienced engineers, with clear expectations for performance, customer ownership, and independent decision-making.
* Plan account coverage and team capacity, balancing customer support with proactive reliability work and preparation for critical events.
* Guide technical investigations and customer escalations, coordinate with Engineering, and verify that the customer’s problem is resolved.
* Use customer issues and service data to improve playbooks, reduce recurring problems, and inform Product and Engineering priorities.
* Build and test artificial intelligence and automation improvements, with quality checks, human oversight, and measurable results.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have 10+ years of relevant technical customer experience, including 5+ years of people leadership in Support Engineering, Technical Account Management, or a comparable team.
* Have directly managed experienced engineers supporting strategic enterprise customers, with evidence of effective coaching, delegation, and performance management.
* Bring technical depth in application programming interfaces, distributed systems, cloud infrastructure, and enterprise integrations, with sound judgment during complex investigations.
* Build trusted customer relationships and work across teams to resolve competing priorities, clarify ownership, and follow through on commitments.
* Have delivered measurable improvements through artificial intelligence, automation, or support processes, balancing efficiency with customer experience and reliability.
