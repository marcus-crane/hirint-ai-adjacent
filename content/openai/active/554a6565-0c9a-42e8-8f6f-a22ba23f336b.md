**About the Role**

We are seeking a Controls Engineer to design and integrate automation systems for on-site power generation serving large AI data center campuses. You will own control architecture and functional requirements across generation packages, balance-of-plant systems, electrical protection, microgrid controls, and data center monitoring. You will work from concept through factory testing, site commissioning, performance validation, and operational improvement.

**Travel:** 50%

**Responsibilities**

* Define control-system architecture, functional design specifications, sequences of operation, cause-and-effect matrices, alarm philosophy, and interface requirements.
* Integrate OEM controls, PLCs, DCS, SCADA, historian, microgrid controllers, protection systems, and data center BMS or EPMS platforms.
* Develop and review I/O lists, network architecture, control narratives, graphics, alarm limits, interlocks, permissives, and fail-safe behavior.
* Coordinate synchronization, load sharing, islanding, black start, load shedding, restoration, and generation-to-data-center operating modes.
* Evaluate cybersecurity, redundancy, time synchronization, communications protocols, data quality, and remote-support requirements.
* Lead or support software reviews, simulator testing, factory acceptance testing, site acceptance testing, commissioning, and tuning.
* Troubleshoot control-system and plant-performance issues and convert findings into corrective actions and reusable standards.
* Partner with electrical, mechanical, CSA, IT, security, operations, OEM, EPC, and data center controls teams.

**Qualifications**

* 8+ years of controls, automation, or instrumentation engineering experience in power generation, industrial plants, utilities, or mission-critical infrastructure.
* Strong experience with PLC, DCS, SCADA, historian, industrial-networking, and control-system integration.
* Knowledge of generation controls, synchronization, load sharing, protective interfaces, process control, and safety interlocks.
* Experience developing functional specifications, sequences, I/O, alarms, graphics, and factory or site test procedures.
* Ability to troubleshoot across software, instrumentation, electrical, mechanical, communications, and operating-process boundaries.
* Understanding of industrial cybersecurity, redundancy, failover, and change-control practices.
* Bachelor's degree in controls, electrical, mechanical, chemical engineering, computer engineering, or a related field.

**Preferred Skills**

* Experience with major turbine or reciprocating-engine control platforms and microgrid controllers.
* Experience with IEC 61850, Modbus, OPC UA, DNP3, EtherNet/IP, or comparable industrial protocols.
* Experience integrating generation controls with BMS, EPMS, DCIM, or utility systems.
* Professional Engineer license, functional-safety training, or industrial-cybersecurity certification.
