**About the role**

Space helps people and AI agents work with files and collaborate. The Space Foundations team builds the shared systems that make those experiences fast, reliable, and consistent. We’re looking for a hands-on engineer who can take a capability like file sharing from the real-time systems that power it through to the interface people use.

**In this role, you will:**

* Design, build, and operate real-time systems that support collaboration across Space.
* Build shared capabilities such as file sharing and make them work consistently across product experiences.
* Identify and fix reliability and latency issues across backend services and frontend experiences.
* Work with partner teams to understand how people and agents use Space and turn those needs into platform improvements.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have built and operated real-time or distributed systems in production.
* Are comfortable moving between infrastructure and frontend code and owning a feature end to end.
* Can explain technical tradeoffs clearly and use product judgment to decide what to build and where to simplify.
* Are curious about how people work and what makes a platform easy for people and agents to use.
