**About the Role**

As a Mechanical Design Engineer on the Robotics team, you’ll design battery packs for robots and help define how those packs integrate into the rest of the system. In this hands-on individual contributor role, you’ll own mechanical designs from early architecture and packaging through prototyping, validation, and production. You’ll work closely with electrical, thermal, systems, and manufacturing engineers to build packs that are safe, reliable, and practical to assemble and service.

**This role is based in San Francisco, CA and requires in-person presence 4 days a week.**

**In this role, you will:**

* Define battery pack architecture and packaging in collaboration with the engineers designing the rest of the robot.
* Design pack enclosures, structural components, mounts, and interfaces for cell or module integration.
* Make design tradeoffs across size, weight, stiffness, thermal performance, safety, assembly, and serviceability.
* Build and evaluate prototypes, using test results and teardown findings to improve the design.
* Release CAD and drawings, and work with suppliers and manufacturing partners to move designs into production.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Enjoy owning mechanical designs and staying hands-on through builds, testing, and debugging.
* Have strong intuition for how battery packs behave under shock, vibration, thermal cycling, and repeated use.
* Can make practical design decisions as requirements evolve and explain the tradeoffs to teammates across disciplines.
* Think about how hardware will be assembled, inspected, and serviced while it’s still on the drawing board.
* Have 4+ years of professional mechanical or hardware design experience, including direct ownership of battery pack hardware or a major pack subsystem for high-volume automotive, mobility, or legged robotics applications.
* Take mechanical designs from concept through prototyping, validation, and production release.
* Are proficient in 3D CAD, engineering drawings, GD&T, and tolerance analysis.
* Have experience designing pack enclosures, structures, or module-to-pack or pack-to-system interfaces. Experience with battery cell design is a plus.
