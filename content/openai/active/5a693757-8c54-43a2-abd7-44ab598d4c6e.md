**About the Team**

OpenAI and its capital and technology partners are building a global network of data centers for demanding AI workloads. The Infrastructure Quality team sets and verifies quality, reliability, and performance requirements for equipment across that network.

We work with manufacturing partners, general contractors, engineers, and operations staff from supplier qualification through commissioning. Together, we verify that equipment arrives ready for installation, startup, and long-term service.

**About the Role**

As a Manufacturing Quality Engineer for Data Center Modular Builds, you will own supplier manufacturing and integration quality for modular systems, including hot aisle containment (HAC), modular cooling units (MCUs), and electrical skids. Working with mechanical and electrical integrators and their suppliers, you will lead supplier qualification, support product and process development, and translate engineering requirements into manufacturing controls, inspections, and acceptance criteria.

You will partner with cross-functional teams to monitor production and integration quality, conduct audits, and resolve risks to integrated products acceptance and delivery. You will also serve as the primary quality contact for site escalations involving manufacturing or integration defects in modular systems and(OFCI) components. Through structured root-cause investigations, corrective action, and closed-loop learning (CLL), you will drive resolution and feed field lessons back into supplier processes to prevent recurrence and improve future builds.

**Key responsibilities**

***Modular Systems Quality and Supplier Development***

* Lead supplier qualification and support product and process development for mechanical and electrical integrators.
* Oversee manufacturing and integration quality for modular builds, including HACs, MCUs, and electrical skids.
* Work with integrators and suppliers to translate engineering requirements and critical-to-quality (CTQ) characteristics into practical manufacturing controls, inspection points, and acceptance criteria.
* Develop and drive supplier improvement plans (SIPs) using the Plan-Do-Check-Act (PDCA) approach to strengthen manufacturing and integration processes.
* Use production data, audit findings, test results, and supplier scorecards to identify systemic or recurring issues, prioritize improvements, and measure sustained results.

***Site Escalations and Corrective Action***

* Serve as the primary quality point of contact for escalated site issues attributable to manufacturing and integration failures, coordinating containment and resolution with site teams and supplier factories.
* Lead root-cause and corrective-action (RCCA) investigations using structured methods such as 8D and 5 Whys. Verify root causes, track corrective and preventive actions to closure, and confirm their effectiveness.
* Apply closed-loop learning (CLL) to incorporate field findings and lessons learned into supplier and integrator manufacturing controls and future builds, preventing recurrence.

***Manufacturing and Integration Monitoring***

* Partner with Quality Program and Manufacturing Operations teams to monitor production and integration progress, quality controls, inspection results, and factory test readiness. Identify emerging risks and coordinate timely action with suppliers.
* Conduct product and process audits during and after manufacturing and integration to verify compliance with engineering requirements, approved procedures, and quality standards. Document findings and track corrective actions to closure.
* Align production checkpoints, inspections, and factory testing requirements with suppliers, engineering, sourcing, supply chain, commissioning, and quality teams. Coordinate deviation resolution and escalate risks to equipment acceptance or delivery.

**Qualifications**

* Bachelor’s degree in electrical, mechanical, or industrial engineering.
* At least 10 years of data center industry experience spanning quality engineering and manufacturing engineering.
* Experience working with integrators and suppliers across multiple tiers to manage quality throughout the product lifecycle, from OFCI component manufacturing through integration and site issue resolution, and drive product and process improvements.
* Ability to define CTQs and acceptance criteria, develop inspection and test checklists, review test results, and maintain auditable quality records.
* Experience monitoring supplier production, conducting product and process audits, managing controlled documents and deviations, and using supplier scorecards to drive improvement.
* Demonstrated experience leading root-cause investigations and corrective actions for manufacturing or field issues using structured methods such as 8D and 5 Whys, and driving continuous improvement through PDCA.
* Ability to coordinate supplier schedules and plan inspections and tests across multiple equipment programs.
* Ability to communicate quality requirements, resolve issues, and coordinate decisions across suppliers, engineering, commissioning, sourcing, supply chain, manufacturing, operations, and construction teams.
* Willingness and ability to travel approximately 40% of the time to supplier manufacturing facilities and data center project sites.

**Preferred Skills**

* Technical knowledge of data center infrastructure, including mechanical, electrical, and controls systems.
* Experience in supplier performance management for mission-critical facilities.
* ASQ Certified Quality Engineer, Quality Manager, or Quality Auditor.
* Experience in industrial or high-volume manufacturing environments.
