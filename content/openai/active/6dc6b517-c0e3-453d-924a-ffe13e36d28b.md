# About this role

We are looking for a Sales Enablement professional to build the commercial capability of OpenAI’s field teams. You will turn regional business priorities into practical learning, coaching and demonstrated improvement in how people sell. Your focus is sustained capability: helping sellers understand customer problems, create value, develop champions and navigate complex buying decisions, rather than simply completing training or communicating launches.

You will work with regional enablement leadership, sales managers and Readiness Strategy & Design to diagnose needs, shape learning, deliver it and reinforce application in real deals. The role combines direct field credibility with hands-on facilitation, coaching and commercial judgment.

This role is based in our **Singapore** office. We use a hybrid work model of 3 days in the office per week and offer relocation assistance to new employees.

# In this role you will

* Diagnose commercial capability gaps. Use deal reviews, customer conversations, pipeline and conversion evidence, and manager feedback to identify where stronger selling skills would improve execution. Distinguish learning needs from product, process, territory or coverage issues and bring those issues to the appropriate owner.
* Deliver practical sales learning. Facilitate workshops, role plays, customer scenarios and deal clinics that build discovery, problem identification, qualification, value articulation, champion development and executive engagement. Make sessions relevant to OpenAI’s executive-led, rapidly changing sales environment rather than importing a playbook unchanged.
* Coach application in live opportunities. Help sellers apply established enterprise sales disciplines to their own accounts and customer conversations. Partner with managers on follow-through and observable next steps while leaving deal ownership, forecast accountability and people management with the field.
* Build manager reinforcement. Give managers practical coaching prompts, observation criteria and follow-up exercises. Help them recognise strong execution and diagnose specific gaps, so capability development continues between formal sessions rather than depending on an enablement event.
* Own assigned local onboarding delivery. Run practical exercises, facilitation and readiness checks within global programme standards and pathways. Coordinate with the regional lead, onboarding team and Readiness Design on schedules, learner needs and assessments; use observed proficiency to identify targeted support.
* Co-create relevant learning with shared quality. Give Readiness Strategy & Design clear commercial briefs and real customer scenarios. Adapt approved examples, language and delivery formats within agreed guardrails; obtain qualified review for new product, technical, pricing or other substantive claims.
* Connect capability building to sales plays and activation. Help teams practise the customer conversations and behaviours a play requires, working with the sales plays and activation owners. Protect committed coaching and reinforcement time and surface the impact when urgent launches would displace it.
* Choose the delivery route that serves the learner. Blend human coaching, workshops and approved in-workflow learning where appropriate. Use agentic delivery only for accepted services with clear content ownership and a human fallback; retain responsibility for the capability gaps that require direct support.
* Assess demonstrated proficiency and commercial application. Combine practical assessments, observed selling, manager feedback and relevant performance evidence. Do not equate attendance with readiness or attribute commercial results to training alone. Identify where content, delivery or a wider business constraint needs to change.
* Close the field and design feedback loop. Return prioritised improvements and reusable examples to Readiness Strategy & Design. Agree owners and review dates, test revised materials with the field and retire duplicate local versions once improvements are incorporated into the shared source.

# You may thrive in this role if you

* Have personally sold to enterprise customers and can bring credible experience of discovery, qualification, stakeholder management and progressing complex opportunities.
* Have delivered dedicated in-house sales enablement in an enterprise technology business, improving field behaviour beyond launch communications or event coordination.
* Bring experience working within an established enterprise sales frameworks and can adapt its disciplines thoughtfully to AI products, consumption and evolving customer needs.
* Can facilitate practical adult learning, coach sellers and equip managers to reinforce skills using real customer situations.
