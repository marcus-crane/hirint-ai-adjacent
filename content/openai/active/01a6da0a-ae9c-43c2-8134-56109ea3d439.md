**About the Role**

We’re seeking a senior lawyer to participate in commercial legal strategy and execution across OpenAI’s fast-growing data center portfolio, with an emphasis on leasing and colocation. In this cross-functional role, you’ll partner closely with infrastructure, procurement, supply chain, partnerships, and finance teams to structure, negotiate, and manage the transactions that support OpenAI’s data center projects.

We’re looking for an experienced infrastructure or real estate transactions lawyer who thrives in ambiguity and wants to help define the commercial playbook for OpenAI’s data center operations and securing, building, and expanding data center capacity. This role reports to the Associate General Counsel for Infrastructure.

We use a hybrid work model of 3-days in the office per week and offer relocation assistance to new employees.

**In this role, you will:**

* Own commercial legal strategy and risk management for OpenAI’s data center projects, from site selection through delivery, operations, and expansion, and financing of those projects.
* Draft, negotiate, and advise on powered-shell and build-to-suit leases and services agreements, work letters, master colocation agreements, and site-specific service orders on behalf of OpenAI as tenant or customer.
* Work with engineering and operations teams to negotiate power and cooling commitments, commissioning and acceptance criteria, service levels, and remedies for delayed delivery or outages.
* Support strategic partnerships involving AI infrastructure and hardware supply chains for data center builds and expansions.
* Develop frameworks for procurement, licensing, and collaboration with the developers, operators, and suppliers supporting OpenAI’s data centers.
* Partner with finance and operations teams to align contract terms with business and compliance requirements, including when rent or service fees begin and how power costs are allocated.
* Collaborate with policy and regulatory colleagues and local counsel on data center site diligence, including zoning, permitting, and utility access.
* Build scalable, efficient contracting processes that support OpenAI’s data center development at speed while managing risk.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Bring 8+ years of legal experience, with a substantial focus on technology transactions and commercial contracting.
* Have a JD and a license or qualification to practice in CA or the state in which you are based, or the right to practice in such location.
* Have hands-on experience independently drafting and negotiating data center leases and colocation agreements, including technical exhibits governing capacity, delivery, and operations.
* Have experience advising on real estate matters for data center projects, including site control, title and survey review, and easements for power, fiber, and access.
* Demonstrate the ability to operate with autonomy and sound judgment in highly technical and fast-moving environments.
* Bring exceptional communication skills and an ability to distill complexity into actionable, business-aligned guidance.
* Have experience advising on cross-border deals.
* Have prior experience with infrastructure supply agreements, joint ventures, or other complex commercial deals supporting data center construction or operations is a strong plus.
