**About the Team**

DotCom builds the web experiences that introduce OpenAI’s research and products to the world. We bring together engineering, design, content, and production to make complex technology clear, useful, and engaging. Our work spans [OpenAI.com](https://openai.com/), product storytelling such as the [ChatGPT overview](https://chatgpt.com/overview/), and interactive experiences such as [OpenAI Progress](https://progress.openai.com/).

For businesses exploring AI, these experiences help connect new capabilities to real needs. We partner with product and marketing teams to show what OpenAI’s products can do and help people discover how to use them. The work combines visual craft with the engineering behind fast, accessible experiences that reach a global audience.

**About the Role**

We’re hiring a software engineer to take dedicated ownership of B2B web experiences on DotCom. You’ll work directly with product teams to understand their capabilities, then partner with design and marketing to bring those capabilities and stories to life on [OpenAI.com](https://openai.com/) through product pages, interactive features, and discovery experiences.

This is a frontend-focused role for an engineer who combines strong UI skills with product judgment and can connect the interface to the systems behind it. You’ll shape ambiguous projects, make thoughtful technical and design tradeoffs, and own delivery from the first prototype through launch and iteration. We welcome frontend and full-stack backgrounds with a strong track record of building for the web.

This role is based on-site in San Francisco or New York City.

**In this role, you will:**

* Build and evolve B2B product pages and interactive web experiences that help business audiences understand OpenAI’s capabilities and discover relevant products and use cases.
* Partner with product engineers to bring capabilities, integrations, and directory-style discovery experiences onto [OpenAI.com](https://openai.com/), connecting user-facing interfaces with the APIs and data they need.
* Develop reusable UI components and patterns with designers, balancing visual detail, accessibility, responsiveness, and maintainability.
* Use analytics, feature flags, and experiments to understand user behavior and improve discovery, engagement, and conversion.
* Improve performance, reliability, and localization support so experiences work well across devices, languages, and periods of high traffic.
* Drive projects across product, design, marketing, and content partners: clarify goals, question assumptions, resolve dependencies, and carry work through launch and ongoing improvement.

**You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* 7+ years of frontend and/or full-stack software development experience, with an emphasis on building polished web products and integrating APIs.
* Strong frontend fundamentals in JavaScript, CSS, and browser behavior, with experience building and evolving reusable component systems.
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to work effectively across product, design, marketing, and engineering teams.
* Deep care for quality, functionality, and the people using your work, with attention to every detail—from visual interactions and accessibility to the last page request.
* Innate curiosity and strong product judgment: you ask critical questions, challenge assumptions, and turn complex ideas into clear, useful experiences.
* Enjoyment of working quickly, with the initiative to navigate ambiguity, unblock collaborators, and own projects from prototype through launch and iteration.
* Experience with B2B products, web growth, experimentation, or conversion optimization is a plus.
* Experience with React or Next.js, or with high-traffic, localized websites, is helpful; strong web fundamentals and the ability to learn matter most.
