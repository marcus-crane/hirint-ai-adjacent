**About the team**

The Global Ad Solutions team helps businesses connect with people in useful, relevant ways through OpenAI’s advertising products. We partner with advertisers and agencies to understand their goals, build effective strategies, and demonstrate the value of our platform, while putting the user experience and trust at the center of everything we do.

We are building an advertising business from the ground up. That means working across Sales, Product, Marketing, Measurement and Operations to solve new problems, shape how we go to market, and help our customers grow alongside us.

**About the role**

We’re looking for a Sales Manager to build, lead and develop a high-performing team of Client Partners and Customer Success Managers serving airlines, hotels, online travel agencies, tourism boards, cruise lines and other travel brands. You’ll own your team’s revenue performance, customer relationships and execution, while helping define the sales practices and culture that will enable our business to scale.

You are a hands-on leader who can coach through a complex customer conversation, build an actionable plan from data, and bring clarity and focus in a fast-changing environment. You balance near-term results with long-term customer partnerships and make thoughtful decisions that protect user trust.

You’ll coach the team to build advertising partnerships that help people discover destinations, compare options and plan trips. You’ll help advertisers connect campaigns to goals such as bookings, destination awareness and loyalty program growth.

**In this role, you will:**

* Hire, coach and develop a diverse team of Client Partners and Customer Success Managers, providing clear expectations, regular feedback and opportunities for growth.
* Own the team’s revenue and business objectives, planning pipeline and account coverage around seasonal booking windows, new routes or properties, and year-round advertiser priorities.
* Establish a consistent operating rhythm for account reviews, forecasting and prioritization, using data to identify risks, opportunities and actions.
* Coach Client Partners and Customer Success Managers on executive relationships, complex negotiations and account plans that connect launch or seasonal campaigns to longer-term customer goals.
* Build senior relationships with key advertisers and agencies, serving as an executive partner and escalation point when needed.
* Partner closely with Agency, Marketing Science, Solutions Engineering and Product to help advertisers measure outcomes across the travel planning journey, from destination discovery to booking.
* Bring customer and market insights back to internal teams, helping shape product priorities, sales strategy and go-to-market execution.
* Develop repeatable tools, processes and best practices that improve how the team works as the business grows.
* Foster a culture of ownership, collaboration, candor and continuous learning, while upholding OpenAI’s commitment to user trust and responsible growth.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have 10+ years of experience in digital advertising, media, marketing solutions or a related client-facing commercial role, including 3+ years leading and developing sales teams.
* Have a track record of building or growing teams and delivering against ambitious revenue goals in a dynamic environment.
* Are an effective coach who can develop people with different strengths, provide direct and constructive feedback, and create an inclusive, high-accountability team culture.
* Have experience advising senior travel advertisers and their agencies on investment decisions shaped by seasonality, booking windows and loyalty goals.
* Understand how travel advertisers evaluate brand and performance campaigns using measures such as destination awareness, bookings and loyalty program sign-ups.
* Use data to prioritize opportunities, inspect a pipeline, improve forecast quality and turn insights into clear action.
* Can work across functions to solve customer problems and translate recurring feedback into scalable improvements.
* Are comfortable building through ambiguity, moving with urgency, and adapting your approach as the business evolves.
* Communicate clearly and credibly with a range of audiences, from individual contributors to executives, and care deeply about earning customer and user trust.
