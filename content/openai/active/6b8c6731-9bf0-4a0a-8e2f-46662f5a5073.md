**About the Team**

Regulatory & Privacy Operations sits within User Safety & Risk Operations (USRO), part of OpenAI’s broader User Operations organization. We build and run the operational programs that protect user rights and support OpenAI’s legal, regulatory & privacy obligations across its products and developer tools.

We partner with Legal, Privacy Engineering, Product, Policy, Governance, Risk & Compliance, Support Operations, and external vendors to resolve sensitive cases and build reliable, scalable operations. Our remit spans privacy, online safety, AI regulation, and intellectual property.

**About the Role**

We are seeking an experienced Privacy Operations Analyst to own and evolve our global Privacy Operations workstream from Dublin. This individual contributor role combines hands-on management of complex privacy matters with accountability for operational health, vendor quality, and program improvement.

You will take end-to-end ownership of how privacy rights requests are received, assessed, routed, and resolved across a tiered operating model. You will partner closely with Privacy Legal, the Data Protection Officer’s office, Privacy Engineering, and operations teams to translate legal guidance into practical workflows and resolve issues that cross organizational boundaries.

Beyond keeping operations healthy, you will set priorities and deliver improvements that reduce avoidable demand, strengthen controls, and enable safe automation. We are looking for someone who independently identifies problems, makes sound decisions with incomplete information, and carries work from diagnosis through implementation and measurable results.

Privacy will be your primary remit. You will also develop sufficient familiarity with adjacent Regulatory Operations workflows to recognize intersecting risks and provide secondary support when priorities require it.

**In This Role, You Will**

* Own global Privacy Operations across intake, identity verification, case handling, escalations, and closure, including access, deletion, export, correction, and opt-out requests, working within guidance agreed with Legal.
* Independently investigate and resolve complex or sensitive privacy cases, including third-party requests, account restrictions, and complaints about personal information in AI outputs. Recognize when legal interpretation or technical investigation is needed and coordinate the right partners through resolution.
* Own operational health across internal and vendor-supported queues. Use demand forecasts, backlog age, response and resolution times, quality, and capacity data to identify emerging risks and act before deadlines or user outcomes deteriorate.
* Drive vendor performance through clear procedures, training, quality calibration, escalation criteria, and corrective actions. Expand delegated handling where appropriate, with the controls and evidence needed to maintain quality.
* Build and deliver a prioritized roadmap for workflow simplification, self-service, tooling, and AI-assisted automation. Define requirements, evaluate accuracy and failure modes, establish human review and escalation safeguards, and measure impact after launch.
* Translate privacy requirements and Legal guidance into standard operating procedures, decision trees, case documentation, and operational controls. Keep guidance current as products, policies, and requirements evolve.
* Lead Privacy Operations readiness for product launches and policy changes. Assess likely demand, new case types, routing, capacity, and dependencies, and drive practical mitigations with cross-functional owners.
* Investigate handling failures and recurring complaints, establish root causes and wider exposure, and deliver corrective actions with clear ownership and evidence of effectiveness.
* Maintain reliable reporting and case records to support audit readiness, control testing, and Legal-led responses to regulatory inquiries. Communicate the risk, impact, recommendation, and decisions needed clearly to partners and leadership.
* Set priorities across immediate casework and longer-term improvements, make explicit trade-offs, and influence partners across teams and time zones to deliver results.

**You Might Thrive in This Role If You**

* Have 8+ years of relevant experience in privacy operations, regulatory operations, legal operations, or a related field, with substantial hands-on ownership of privacy rights workflows in a global technology environment.
* Have a strong working understanding of GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and privacy rights operations, and can translate counsel’s guidance into consistent, usable processes. You understand the challenges of identity verification, sensitive data handling, and complex request fulfillment.
* Have independently owned a complex operational program, set its priorities, and delivered improvements across multiple teams, with evidence of better quality, lower risk, faster resolution, or reduced manual work.
* Have managed vendor-supported operations and tiered escalation models, including service levels, quality assurance, capacity planning, training, and performance remediation.
* Bring an automation-first approach grounded in sound risk judgment. You can identify what should be automated, define how it will be evaluated, and retain appropriate oversight for sensitive decisions.
* Are comfortable using operational data, dashboards, and case analysis to diagnose problems, test assumptions, and make recommendations. You can distinguish a temporary volume spike from a systemic process or control failure.
* Communicate clearly and concisely, including on sensitive cases and operational risks. Your updates explain what matters, what has changed, and what action or decision is needed.
* Exercise strong judgment in ambiguity, act with urgency proportionate to risk, and know when to decide independently and when to escalate. You follow through without relying on detailed direction.
* Build effective relationships with Legal, Engineering, Product, and operations partners, and can influence decisions without formal authority.

**Additional Experience That Would Be Valuable**

* Experience with privacy operations for AI products, including requests involving model outputs or personal information.
* Practical experience with tooling, automation evaluation, or AI-assisted quality assurance.
* Familiarity with adjacent frameworks such as the DSA, OSA, or EU AI Act, and experience supporting audits or regulatory inquiries.
* A relevant privacy qualification, such as CIPP/E or CIPM.
