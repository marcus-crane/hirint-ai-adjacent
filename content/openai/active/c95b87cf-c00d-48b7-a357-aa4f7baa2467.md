**About this Team**

The HR Business Partner (HRBP) team at OpenAI partners with leaders on complex organizational and talent decisions where the right path is not always obvious. We stay close enough to the business to understand how it works and focus our time on the decisions that benefit most from our judgment, context, and influence.

We work with urgency, empathy, and sound judgment. We value sincerity, intellectual honesty, and collaboration over ego.

**About this Role**

As a Senior HR Business Partner supporting Strategy, Finance, and People, you’ll help leaders translate company priorities into decisions about how to organize, where to build capability, and how to grow their teams. These functions often work across OpenAI, so you’ll help leaders clarify who owns decisions, how teams work together, and where responsibilities need to change as the company scales.

This is an individual contributor role reporting to an HRBP Lead. You’ll move between advising leaders on consequential choices and doing the hands-on work to put those decisions into practice.

**In this role, you will:**

* **Translate business plans into talent plans.** Help leaders identify the capabilities they need, where to build or hire, and what to prioritize as resources and company needs shift.
* **Design teams for the work they need to do.** Advise on structure, roles, and decision ownership, especially when responsibilities span multiple functions.
* **Coach leaders through high-stakes decisions.** Give candid, actionable feedback and help managers navigate performance, succession, and team effectiveness with clarity and care.
* **Lead organizational change.** Work with leaders to explain what’s changing and why, anticipate where teams will need support, and follow through after the announcement.
* **Improve how People programs work in practice.** Partner with fellow HRBPs, People specialists, and cross-functional leaders to make performance, engagement, and talent processes useful and scalable.

**You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* 7–10+ years of HR experience, including at least 5 years as an HR Business Partner in a high-growth or complex environment.
* Experience advising leaders in corporate or enabling functions such as Strategy, Finance, or People, and an ability to connect their work to company-wide priorities.
* A track record of turning business and workforce data into clear recommendations on org design, talent, or leadership decisions.
* Skill in coaching leaders candidly and building alignment when teams have different priorities or ownership is unclear.
* Steadiness through change, with the ability to make progress when priorities shift or there’s no established playbook.
* Discretion, fairness, and sound judgment when the stakes are high.
