**About the Team**

The Recruiting Events program powers OpenAI’s top-of-funnel hiring engine through strategic, high-impact experiences that connect exceptional talent with our people, mission, culture, and work. From intimate dinners that build deep connections to conferences that expand our reach, we curate experiences that spark curiosity, showcase our work, and bring “Why OpenAI?” to life.

**About the Role**

We’re hiring a Program Manager to own a portfolio of recruiting events that supports OpenAI’s hiring priorities. Working closely with the Manager of Recruiting Events Strategy and Recruiting leaders, you’ll shape and scale experiences that attract, engage, and nurture priority talent, helping candidates envision a career at OpenAI.

You’ll translate hiring priorities into a thoughtful portfolio of event experiences and determine which events will most effectively engage the talent we need. You’ll help shape each experience’s goals, format, cross-functional participation, and success metrics, then lead initiatives from planning through delivery, follow-up coordination, and measurement. Your portfolio may evolve across business areas as hiring needs change.

You’ll own end-to-end program delivery and outcomes across your recruiting events portfolio, partnering with the Events team on large-scale production and logistics. You should be comfortable moving between strategic thinking and hands-on execution, with strong judgment, attention to detail, and the ability to influence without owning every aspect of delivery.

This role can be based in San Francisco or New York and requires being in the office three days per week. The role also requires extensive travel depending on the event schedule and workload, with occasional evening work as needed.

**In this role, you will:**

* Oversee programming across your recruiting events portfolio. Partner with Recruiting leads and hiring managers to prioritize monthly and quarterly event series based on hiring needs, target audiences, and partnerships. Apply the established program framework and lead initiatives end to end, adjusting plans as priorities evolve.
* Shape program content and event experiences. Work with recruiters to inform event strategy, audience and invitation plans, and speaker participation. Oversee the development of panels, product showcases, and other programming that reflects OpenAI’s recruiting brand and voice and supports each initiative’s objectives.
* Lead cross-functional partnerships. Align priorities, responsibilities, and timelines with Events, Workplace, Design, Communications, Marketing, and Procurement, resolving dependencies and guiding decisions that keep programs moving.
* Own program execution and post-event follow-up. Work closely with Recruiting Events coordinators to keep delivery on track across the portfolio. Ensure attendance and engagement information are captured, coordinate candidate follow-up with Recruiting partners, and establish clear ownership of next steps through program closeout.
* Manage portfolio performance and recommend improvements. Own KPI tracking and post-event reporting, using candidate feedback, hiring trends, pipeline progression, conversion, and hiring outcomes to assess program effectiveness and inform future programming.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Have 6+ years of professional experience, with a background in recruiting programs or event program management.
* Have a track record of independently owning a portfolio of recurring event programs, with clear examples of how you improved execution, scaled programming, and measured outcomes.
* Make sound prioritization decisions and tradeoffs, anticipate risks, and adapt plans as hiring needs evolve.
* Build trusted relationships with Recruiting leads and partners across Global Events, Workplace, and other functions. You communicate clearly, influence decisions, and align teams around shared priorities and responsibilities.
* Bring creative judgment and attention to detail, with the ability to translate hiring needs into relevant audiences, compelling content, and thoughtful event experiences.
* Are a proactive, resourceful problem-solver who can resolve delivery challenges while identifying improvements that help an established program scale.
* Use data and qualitative feedback to evaluate performance, explain what is working, and make practical recommendations that strengthen recruiting outcomes.
* Are comfortable using Slack, Google Workspace, Notion, Airtable, and Jira to manage programs, maintain visibility, and support collaboration.
