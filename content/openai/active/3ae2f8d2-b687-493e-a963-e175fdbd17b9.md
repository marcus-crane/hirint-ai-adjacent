**About the Team**

The Growth team helps people and businesses discover, adopt, and get lasting value from ChatGPT and other OpenAI products. We partner with Brand Marketing, Product, Engineering, Data Science, Finance, and Creative to connect marketing investment with customer and business outcomes. Our brand data infrastructure brings together media, audience, creative, and research data so teams can plan, measure, and improve brand investment across markets.

**About the Role**

We’re looking for a Brand Data Infrastructure Lead to own the data foundations that make brand marketing measurable and scalable. You will define how brand media, campaign, creative, audience, and research data are organized, connected, validated, and made available for decisions. This hands-on role combines brand and media expertise with practical data fluency, turning fragmented platform and partner outputs into dependable datasets and repeatable workflows.

This role sits within Growth Marketing and works closely with Brand Marketing, Media Operations, Growth Platform’s solutions engineering team, Data Engineering, and Data Science. You own Brand’s data roadmap, business definitions, source requirements, data-quality acceptance, and adoption. Engineering partners own production architecture, connector and pipeline implementation, instrumentation, and technical incident resolution. Data Science owns statistical methodology and model development.

Your first priority is to establish consistent campaign and creative taxonomies, dependable media and measurement feeds, clear data-sharing arrangements, and trusted reporting inputs. Build a foundation that supports TV/CTV, online video, social, audio, out-of-home, and other brand channels, with the coverage and history needed for brand tracking, lift studies, incrementally, and marketing mix modeling.

**In this role, you will:**

* Own the Brand data infrastructure roadmap. Translate brand strategy, media plans, and measurement needs into prioritized data capabilities, source integrations, quality improvements, and reporting foundations. Balance business value, global coverage, delivery dependencies, cost, and sustained adoption.
* Define shared data models and taxonomies. Establish consistent campaign, creative, channel, partner, audience, and market definitions, identifiers, reporting grains, and time conventions. Align stakeholders on how datasets connect and how metrics should be interpreted across platforms and markets.
* Set source and integration requirements. Define the fields, granularity, history, coverage, refresh cadence, and reconciliation needed from platforms, agencies, publishers, and measurement vendors. Partner with engineering on data contracts, integration priorities, acceptance criteria, and ownership throughout the data lifecycle.
* Enable Brand channels and partner data. Lead data readiness for TV/CTV, video, social, audio, out-of-home, and emerging channels. Ensure new partners and campaigns provide usable delivery, spend, audience, creative, and geographic data, working with Media Operations and channel owners on setup and reporting requirements.
* Make Brand measurement data ready to use. Coordinate the datasets and metadata required for brand tracking, brand lift, geo experiments, incrementality studies, and marketing mix modeling. Work with Data Science on study requirements and comparability, and connect media and research outputs with relevant product and business outcomes.
* Own vendor data access and governance readiness. Lead the marketing side of partner evaluation, onboarding, and service reviews. Define required data, delivery terms, permitted uses, and responsibilities with Legal, Privacy, Security, and Procurement, then make those requirements usable for engineering, vendors, and marketing teams.
* Establish data quality and dependable operations. Set expectations for completeness, freshness, consistency, and reconciliation. Use source reports and delivered datasets to identify missing feeds, taxonomy gaps, duplication, and reporting discrepancies; assess business impact, coordinate resolution, and validate recovery with the accountable technical or vendor owner.
* Build usable data products and handoffs. Partner with data and analytics teams on curated Brand datasets, reporting inputs, documentation, and self-service access. Maintain definitions, lineage, training, and support procedures so Brand leaders, regional teams, and measurement specialists can use the data consistently with less manual reconciliation.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Bring 8+ years of experience in marketing data, brand or media measurement, marketing technology, analytics operations, or related roles, including hands-on ownership of data capabilities that improved marketing decisions.
* Have practical experience working with brand media and measurement datasets, including spend and delivery, reach and frequency, creative metadata, brand tracking, surveys, or lift studies. You understand differences in coverage, granularity, definitions, and comparability across sources.
* Understand data models, identifiers, taxonomy, data contracts, and quality checks, and how they support brand lift, incrementality, and marketing mix modeling. You can interpret source extracts and reporting outputs, investigate discrepancies, and work effectively with measurement specialists.
* Have helped enable TV/CTV or other Brand channels across platforms or international markets, partnering with agencies, publishers, and measurement vendors on their data delivery and reporting requirements.
* Translate Brand needs into clear data requirements, acceptance criteria, and rollout plans for engineering and analytics partners. You can validate delivered outputs, explain business impact, and make responsibilities and dependencies explicit. Familiarity with SQL-based analysis is valuable; deep coding expertise is not required.
* Bring strong vendor management, cross-functional judgment, and attention to detail. You can navigate data access, privacy, commercial requirements, and competing priorities while building capabilities that marketing teams adopt and use.
