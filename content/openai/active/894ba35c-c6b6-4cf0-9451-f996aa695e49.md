**About the Team**

OpenAI’s mission is to ensure the responsible and widespread adoption of artificial intelligence. In support of that mission, the SMB Marketing team helps small and medium-sized businesses understand and adopt ChatGPT Business and OpenAI’s B2B solutions. We build a deep understanding of business owners and teams, shape relevant product narratives, and drive awareness, adoption, and growth.

We take a data-driven approach to understand SMB audiences, uncover customer needs, and translate AI capabilities into practical business value. We partner closely with Growth, Product Marketing, Sales, Partnerships, Product, Research, Communications, and Design to deliver a cohesive customer experience across discovery, evaluation, purchase, adoption, and expansion.

**About the Role**

We’re looking for a Product Marketing Manager, SMB to bring together SMB insights, market understanding, and product knowledge to shape positioning, inform product priorities, and deliver go-to-market initiatives that drive adoption and measurable business impact.

You’ll work closely with Product, Engineering, Growth, and Partnerships to understand SMB needs, translate complex AI capabilities into clear customer value, and bring new products and features to market. This role is ideal for someone who combines strategic thinking with hands-on execution, independently navigates ambiguity, and brings cross-functional partners together to deliver results.

**In this role, you will:**

* Define the product and marketing strategy for a component of OpenAI’s SMB portfolio in partnership with cross-functional partners
* Develop an actionable understanding of SMB segments, customer needs, buying behavior, and workflows through research, product usage, market analysis, and competitive insight to improve positioning, adoption, and product-market fit.
* Own positioning and messaging for your product area, translating complex capabilities into compelling narratives for different SMB owners and teams across relevant industries and business functions.
* Partner with Product and Engineering to bring customer needs and market insights into roadmap discussions and product decisions.
* Develop and drive go-to-market plans for new products and partner integrations, owning marketing execution from planning through launch and ongoing adoption.
* Create SMB-facing content, including product narratives, workflow demos and webinar content, one-pagers, FAQs, and launch communications.
* Partner with Product Growth, Partnerships, and Data Science to define success metrics, assess launch and adoption performance, and turn findings into improvements.
* Surface launch risks related to performance, safety, policy, and trust, working with the relevant teams to address them in launch plans and customer communications.
* Align cross-functional partners around priorities, responsibilities, and timelines, making thoughtful tradeoffs and resolving blockers within your initiatives.
* Build repeatable approaches to launches, messaging, and enablement, sharing learnings and mentoring peers to improve the team’s storytelling and execution.

**You might thrive in this role if you are/have:**

* Have experience independently developing product and marketing strategies and bringing products to market, particularly in SMB software, platforms, or data and AI.
* Have experience in structured problem solving environments including but not limited to strategy/management consulting, investment banking, corporate strategy and venture capital
* Understand SMB needs and the partner ecosystem, includingand the market landscape, including advertiser goals and the advertising ecosystem, including measurement, targeting and relevance, creative, auctions, and performance tradeoffs.
* Have strong understanding of SMB workflows and software adoption, with the ability to adapt product value to different industries, use cases, and customer needs. Experience with platforms, integrations, plug-ins, technology partnerships, or SMB ecosystem GTM.
* Can turn customer research and market insights into clear positioning, actionable go-to-market plans, and recommendations that inform product priorities.
* Communicate clearly and persuasively, translating technical concepts into differentiated positioning for a range of audiences.
* Use qualitative and quantitative evidence—including customer interviews, surveys, adoption data, and experiments—to make decisions and improve results.
* Bring structured problem solving and strong judgment to ambiguous challenges, independently setting priorities and managing initiatives from strategy through execution.
* Build effective partnerships across Product, Engineering, Growth, Partnerships, and other teams, influencing decisions and driving alignment without formal authority
