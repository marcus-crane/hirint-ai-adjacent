**About the Team**

OpenAI’s Legal team plays a crucial role in advancing our mission by tackling innovative and fundamental legal issues in AI. The team includes professionals from diverse legal fields – technology, AI, infrastructure, privacy, IP, corporate, employment, tax, regulatory, and litigation – who collaborate closely with colleagues across the company.

If you are passionate about being a technology lawyer working on cutting-edge challenges, you’ll thrive here.

**About the Role**

We are seeking an experienced commercial counsel to join our EMEA Commercial Legal team. You will support OpenAI’s business across EMEA, with Germany, Austria and Switzerland (DACH) as the primary market focus. You will work with Go-To-Market, Product Partnerships, Procurement, Finance, Product, Infrastructure, and Engineering.

This is a great opportunity to join OpenAI’s commercial legal team, negotiating high-impact agreements and helping to build scalable frameworks for responsible growth.

This role can be based in Dublin, Ireland or Munich, Germany. We use a hybrid work model of 3 days in the office per week.

**In this role, you will:**

* Lead complex enterprise technology transactions and handle day-to-day contracting across EMEA, with a primary focus on DACH. Structure and negotiate product partnerships, including integrations and licensing, and support procurement transactions across the region.
* Provide practical, business-oriented legal advice to teams including GTM, Product Partnerships, Finance, Procurement, and Operations.
* Identify and address commercial, product, regulatory, privacy, and other legal issues arising from EMEA commercial transactions.
* Develop strategies and processes for handling legal issues in creative and scalable ways that support growth.
* Monitor and advise on regulatory and compliance matters affecting AI and technology.
* Collaborate with colleagues across OpenAI’s global legal team to ensure alignment across jurisdictions.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Hold a German legal qualification and have 10+ years of experience in independently drafting and negotiating complex commercial contracts, including enterprise technology deals in DACH and product or technology partnerships.
* Are comfortable operating in an entrepreneurial environment, and are proactive and independent.
* Communicate with clarity and business judgment, tailoring advice to enable responsible growth. You are professionally fluent in English. German-language skills are strongly preferred, especially with experience negotiating in German.
* Build cross-functional relationships and adapt communication styles to diverse audiences.
* Have a strong sense of ownership, intellectual curiosity, and enthusiasm for technology.
* Can demonstrate sound judgment in ambiguous or fast-changing situations.
