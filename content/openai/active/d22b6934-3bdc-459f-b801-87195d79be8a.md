**About the Role**

Build and operate the backend systems that power voice conversations in ChatGPT. You’ll lead substantial projects and work across teams to make voice interactions fast and reliable at scale.

**In this role, you will:**

* Design, build, and operate backend services for real-time voice interactions, with a focus on latency, availability, and graceful degradation.
* Lead technical design and delivery from architecture through rollout and production operations.
* Partner with client, infrastructure, and model teams to diagnose end-to-end problems and improve how services interact.
* Define reliability goals and improve observability, incident response, and capacity planning.

**You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* Experience designing, shipping, and operating distributed backend systems in production.
* A record of leading complex technical projects across team boundaries and making sound reliability and performance tradeoffs.
* Experience debugging production issues across services. Real-time streaming, audio, or other latency-sensitive systems experience is a plus.
