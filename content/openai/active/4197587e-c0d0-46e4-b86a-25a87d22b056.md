**About the Team**

OpenAI’s GTM Partnerships team builds a strategic, global partner ecosystem to accelerate customer success, enable responsible enterprise AI adoption, and drive durable growth in support of OpenAI’s mission. We work cross-functionally across Sales, Solutions, Product, Engineering, Security, Legal, Finance, Marketing, Operations, and Customer Success to translate strategic partnerships into measurable outcomes for customers.

Global consulting and technology partners help enterprises move from AI strategy to production deployment. This role will lead OpenAI’s partnership with Boston Consulting Group (BCG) across Europe, the Middle East, and Africa (EMEA), bringing together BCG’s industry, consulting, technology, and delivery expertise with OpenAI’s products and capabilities. You will turn the global alliance strategy into a focused EMEA business plan and repeatable field execution that delivers customer and commercial impact.

**About the Role**

The Partner Director, BCG EMEA will lead OpenAI’s partner business with BCG across the region. You will own the EMEA joint business plan, build executive alignment, and activate BCG and OpenAI field teams to generate qualified pipeline, win strategic opportunities, and drive successful production adoption. You will align regional priorities and execution with the global BCG alliance strategy.

This is a strategic and hands-on leadership role. You will connect senior executive engagement with joint account planning, co-selling, solution and delivery alignment, field enablement, governance, and day-to-day execution. Success requires a strong understanding of how a large, matrixed professional-services organization operates, commercial discipline, technical fluency, and the ability to align teams without formal authority.

The ideal candidate has led a high-impact partnership with BCG, built measurable pipeline and customer outcomes across EMEA, and knows how to turn alliance strategy into account-level execution and successful deployments.

**In this role, you will:**

* Own the EMEA partnership with BCG, including regional strategy, executive alignment, joint planning, pipeline growth, customer outcomes, delivery quality, and long-term expansion, in alignment with the global alliance.
* Develop and execute an EMEA joint business plan with clear account and industry priorities, investments, decision rights, milestones, and measures of success.
* Build trusted relationships with BCG’s EMEA leadership, industry and functional leaders, alliance teams, client account leaders, sales organizations, architects, and delivery leaders, while staying aligned with global stakeholders.
* Translate executive priorities into repeatable field execution spanning account mapping, opportunity qualification, co-selling, solution development, technical engagement, delivery handoffs, and escalation.
* Shape joint solutions and industry plays that combine OpenAI products and capabilities with BCG’s consulting, technology, data, security, governance, and delivery strengths.
* Lead joint account planning and strategic customer pursuits with BCG and OpenAI field teams across EMEA, with particular focus on complex and regulated enterprises.
* Create a clear engagement model for OpenAI sellers and technical teams, including when to involve BCG, how opportunities are qualified, who owns each step, and how channel conflict is resolved.
* Partner with Product, Engineering, Solutions, Security, Legal, Finance, and Commercial teams to support complex customer engagements and remove technical, contractual, compliance, and operational blockers.
* Build BCG’s EMEA field and delivery readiness through executive sponsorship, sales enablement, technical training, reusable solution patterns, and disciplined delivery handoffs.
* Establish regional governance across executive business reviews, pipeline inspection, forecasting, milestone tracking, performance reporting, and escalation, connected to the global alliance operating model.
* Set and monitor EMEA partnership KPIs, including qualified pipeline, conversion, production adoption, customer value, partner readiness, delivery quality, and return on OpenAI investment.
* Prioritize OpenAI resources for EMEA opportunities with the greatest strategic value, commercial potential, customer impact, delivery readiness, and repeatability.
* Represent OpenAI in senior partner, customer, and industry engagements with clarity, sound judgment, and strong executive presence.
* Build the regional operating model and identify the team capacity needed to scale the BCG partnership across EMEA.

**You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* Significant experience leading strategic alliances, GSI, or advisory partnerships, ecosystem sales, business development, or enterprise go-to-market programs in AI, cloud, data, SaaS, or enterprise technology.
* Direct experience working with BCG and deep familiarity with its EMEA organization, incentives, decision-making networks, commercial model, industry and functional practices, and enterprise delivery motions.
* A proven record of leading a multi-year partnership or regional alliance business with measurable pipeline, revenue, adoption, deployment, or customer outcomes.
* Experience designing and scaling joint operating models across large, matrixed organizations, including governance, account planning, incentives, staffing, delivery handoffs, and escalation.
* Demonstrated success shaping and advancing complex C-suite enterprise transformation opportunities from strategy through production deployment.
* Strong executive presence and the ability to build credibility with senior BCG and customer leaders while remaining close to operating details.
* Commercial judgment and experience prioritizing investments, qualifying opportunities, managing forecasts, and making clear tradeoffs.
* Technical fluency across enterprise AI, cloud environments, data platforms, security, governance, risk, and modern application architectures.
* Strong cross-functional leadership and the ability to align Sales, Solutions, Product, Engineering, Security, Legal, Finance, Marketing, and Operations without direct authority.
* Experience building partner enablement, joint solutions, industry plays, and scalable field engagement models across EMEA.
* Comfort operating in ambiguity and creating structure, accountability, and momentum in a rapidly evolving market.
* Excellent written and verbal communication, negotiation, and executive presentation skills.
* A builder mindset, high ownership, low ego, and a consistent focus on customer value and responsible AI adoption.

**What success looks like:**

* OpenAI and BCG have a clear EMEA growth strategy, differentiated joint value proposition, executive governance model, and measurable business plan aligned with the global alliance.
* BCG and OpenAI field teams across EMEA know when and how to engage one another, and qualified opportunities progress through a consistent co-sell and delivery motion.
* The EMEA partnership generates qualified pipeline and converts priority opportunities into secure, successful production deployments with demonstrable customer value.
* Joint solution patterns and industry plays become repeatable rather than remaining bespoke.
* OpenAI resources are allocated transparently toward the highest-impact opportunities.
* BCG’s EMEA teams demonstrate growing readiness and accountability across selling, architecture, implementation, and customer success.
* The EMEA partnership earns sustained executive sponsorship and delivers measurable year-over-year growth.
