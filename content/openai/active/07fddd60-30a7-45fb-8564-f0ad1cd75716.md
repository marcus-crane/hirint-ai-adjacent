## About the team

Applied AI Engineering helps customers build, deploy, and run useful AI systems with OpenAI products. We work alongside customer engineers and technical partners, and bring what we learn back to Product and Research.

## About the role

You’ll lead AIE for SMB and Mid-Market customers globally. The opportunity is to make engineering support effective across a broad customer base: choose where direct involvement matters most, help customers reach production, and make successful approaches easier to repeat across regions.

## In this role, you will

* Build and develop a customer-facing engineering team, set clear standards, and keep ownership close to the work.
* Agree coverage and priorities with regional Sales and Applied AI Architecture (AAA) leaders; make explicit choices about dedicated and scaled support.
* Turn recurring deployment problems into reusable implementations, evaluations, tooling, and delivery practices.
* Set a practical bar for model quality, reliability, safety, latency, and cost, and help the team resolve difficult technical decisions.
* Measure sustained production use and customer value, and use the evidence to improve where the organization spends its time.

## You might thrive in this role if you

* Have led customer-facing engineering teams with responsibility for people, technical quality, and production delivery.
* Can make sound decisions about AI applications and explain the trade-offs to engineers, customers, and commercial partners.
* Have helped an organization serve a broad portfolio through repeatable practices while preserving depth where it matters.
* Have made difficult hiring, performance, and resource decisions, and can show how your leadership improved others’ effectiveness.
* Work well across regions and build trust while resolving competing customer and business priorities.
