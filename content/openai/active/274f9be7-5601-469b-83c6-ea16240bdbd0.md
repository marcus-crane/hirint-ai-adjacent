## About the Team

The Growth team drives adoption and revenue for ChatGPT. This role focuses on B2B and small-business growth: helping business owners, functional leaders, and teams discover how ChatGPT can solve their work challenges and deliver value. We partner across product, engineering, product marketing, content, design, and analytics to turn customer insights and disciplined experimentation into sustainable growth.

## About the Role

We are seeking a growth marketer with deep expertise in search engine optimization (SEO) and a strong experimental approach to answer engine optimization (AEO) to grow ChatGPT adoption among small businesses. You will own the strategy and execution for reaching business buyers through organic search and AI-powered answers, connecting discovery to evaluation, workspace creation, and paid adoption.

You will build an organic acquisition program around how small businesses research problems, compare solutions, and choose tools for their teams. Your remit spans technical SEO, search and question research, content strategy, and AEO experimentation. You will partner with SMB Product Marketing and Content Marketing to translate business use cases, industry needs, and customer proof into useful discovery experiences, and with Web Growth to improve the path from organic discovery to conversion.

This role is based in San Francisco, CA or New York, NY. We use a hybrid work model of 3 days in the office per week and offer relocation assistance to new employees.

## In this role, you will:

* Own the B2B / SMB SEO and AEO roadmap, prioritizing opportunities by business audience, use case, industry, buying intent, and potential contribution to customer acquisition and revenue.
* Lead keyword, question, and audience research across the small-business buying journey—from identifying a business problem to comparing solutions and evaluating pricing, team adoption, and business requirements. Translate findings into prioritized content briefs and page opportunities.
* Own the technical SEO roadmap with web and engineering partners, including crawlability, indexing, rendering, site architecture, internal linking, structured data, and migration readiness. Build durable foundations for discovering and understanding our business content.
* Develop and test AEO approaches that help business buyers find accurate, useful information about ChatGPT in AI-powered search and answer experiences. Improve answer-focused content, product information, and supporting evidence; track visibility, citations, and referral quality while distinguishing observed results from hypotheses.
* Partner with SMB Product Marketing and Content Marketing on use-case and industry pages, comparison and evaluation content, practical guides, and customer stories. Bring search insights and clear briefs; maintain accuracy, originality, and usefulness as the program scales.
* Build measurement with Analytics that connects organic discovery to qualified business traffic, workspace creation, activation, paid conversion, and revenue. Segment performance by intent, audience, and market, and use experiments and cohort analysis to assess impact and attribution limits.
* Partner with Web Growth, Design, and Product on landing-page and conversion experiments that help business visitors take the right next step. Align messaging, proof points, calls to action, and onward journeys with buyer intent, then evaluate downstream customer quality.
* Build AI-assisted workflows for research, briefing, content refreshes, technical audits, and reporting, with human review and quality controls. Use programmatic approaches where they create distinct value for business audiences.
* Coordinate execution across engineering, product marketing, content, web, and analytics partners. Establish a regular testing and learning cadence, and partner on localization to expand proven SEO and AEO approaches into priority markets.

## You might thrive in this role if you have:

* 10+ years in SEO or growth marketing, with a track record of growing qualified organic acquisition for B2B software or products serving small businesses. Experience with self-serve or product-led business acquisition is especially relevant.
* Deep technical SEO expertise, including site architecture, crawling and indexing, structured data, migrations, and internal linking, combined with strong judgment about search intent and content quality.
* Hands-on experience testing visibility in AI-powered search and answer experiences. You can design an AEO measurement approach, evaluate citations and referrals, and explain what the evidence does—and does not—show.
* A strong understanding of small-business buyers and how their needs vary by role, company size, and industry. You can turn customer problems, solution comparisons, and buying questions into a focused organic growth strategy.
* Strong collaboration with product marketing, content, engineering, design, web growth, and analytics teams. You can turn insights into clear briefs, prioritize shared roadmaps, and take initiatives through launch and iteration.
* Strong analytical and experimentation skills, including search and web analytics, funnel and cohort analysis, and landing-page testing. You connect traffic and visibility to activation, paid adoption, and revenue rather than optimizing for rankings alone.
* Hands-on experience publishing, refreshing, and measuring content through a CMS, including landing pages, reusable templates, and localized experiences. You are comfortable moving between strategic planning, technical audits, and page-level QA.
* Experience using AI-assisted workflows and programmatic SEO to improve execution while preserving factual accuracy, original value, and a high-quality customer experience.
* A test-and-learn mindset, clear communication, and comfort operating in a fast-changing environment. You balance curiosity about new discovery channels with disciplined prioritization and measurement.
