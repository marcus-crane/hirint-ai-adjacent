**About the Team**

The Hardware Marketing team helps people understand what OpenAI’s hardware products can do and how they can fit into everyday life. We work with Product Marketing, Design, Creative, Sales, and Operations to bring new products to market and create experiences that let people experience the magic of the products themselves.

**About the Role**

We’re looking for a Channel Lead to build OpenAI’s retail experience and channel marketing programs for consumer hardware.

You’ll set the standard for how customers discover, try, and learn about our products. The role calls for creative judgment and hands-on operating experience: you should be as comfortable reviewing a demo on a store floor as you are deciding where to invest next. You’ll own the customer-facing experience and channel marketing strategy, working with Sales and Sales Operations, which own commercial terms, revenue targets, forecasting, and inventory.

**In this role, you will:**

* Set the retail strategy. Define what our own experiences and third-party retail each do for customers. Decide what to pilot, where to invest, and what to scale based on what we learn.
* Build OpenAI-owned experiences. Lead the customer journey from first impression and hands-on demo through purchase and support. Work with Design, Creative, production partners, and Operations on spaces, programming, staffing, and day-to-day execution.
* Lead channel marketing with retail partners. Build joint launch and marketing plans with retailers. Set the standard for fixtures, displays, signage, demos, and online product pages, and give partner teams the tools to execute consistently across stores and markets.
* Get stores ready for launch. Work with Engineering, IT, Commerce, and Operations to make demos reliable and resolve issues before and after launch.
* Build the capability from the ground up. Set priorities and budgets, lead cross-functional teams and agencies, and turn what works into playbooks other teams and markets can use.
* Measure what customers learn and do. Use customer feedback, field observations, engagement, and conversion to improve the experience. Work with Analytics and Sales to understand sales impact and decide which programs to expand.

**You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* Led consumer retail experience or channel marketing programs across both brand-owned environments and major third-party retailers.
* Brought consumer technology products to market and made unfamiliar capabilities easy to understand through hands-on demos, merchandising, and clear product storytelling.
* Built programs that hold up in the field, from associate training and demo readiness to consistent execution across stores or markets.
* Managed budgets and agency partners, and led creative, technical, operational, and commercial teams through complex launches.
* Used customer insight and performance data to make decisions about what to improve, stop, or scale.
* Built a new function or program and enjoy moving between setting the strategy and solving problems on the ground. Experience introducing a new product category or adapting a retail program across markets is a plus.

**Workplace & Location**

This role is based in San Francisco, CA.
