**About the Team**

The Supply Chain Technology team within OpenAI's Enterprise Platform & Technology organization builds the systems and data foundations that support our supply chain and partner operations. We connect enterprise applications, improve operational visibility, and automate workflows across planning, purchasing, manufacturing, inventory, fulfillment, and returns. We work closely with engineering and business teams to build reliable systems that can evolve with OpenAI's growth.

**About the Role**

We are seeking an Integrations Architect to set technical direction for enterprise integrations, with a strong focus on supply chain and external partner ecosystems. You will help evolve a growing integration portfolio into a scalable, maintainable foundation through clear architecture, reusable patterns, and strong engineering practices. You will also help establish shared integration architecture and reusable foundations needed to support Finance and Supply Chain systems.

This is a hands-on technical leadership role. You will translate business processes into integration designs, evaluate existing implementations, challenge assumptions, and guide improvements from design through production. Your influence will extend across application engineers, integration engineers, platform teams, and business stakeholders.

You will own integration architecture and partner with the engineering team responsible for the shared integration platform. Through clear requirements, working implementations, and operational evidence, you will help shape capabilities that improve how integrations are developed, tested, deployed, observed, and recovered.

**In this role, you will:**

* Own integration architecture across enterprise applications, custom services, and external partners, including e-commerce platforms, logistics providers, contract manufacturers, retailers, and distributors.
* Translate supply chain processes into clear system boundaries, transaction flows, data ownership, interface contracts, and operational requirements.
* Partner with Finance and engineering teams to establish reusable integration patterns connecting operational and financial systems, with clear data ownership, reconciliation, and auditability.
* Assess existing integrations and lead a practical modernization roadmap that improves scalability, reliability, maintainability, and cost while sustaining business delivery.
* Establish reference architectures, reusable connectors, API and event standards, data mappings, versioning practices, and documented design decisions. Lead design and code reviews and mentor engineers.
* Select appropriate API, messaging, EDI, orchestration, and batch patterns, balancing platform capabilities with custom development and the needs of each business process.
* Stay close to implementation by building Python services and reference solutions, reviewing code, resolving difficult production issues, and validating designs through tests and measurable behavior.
* Define reliability and recovery patterns, including idempotency, retries, reconciliation, replay, and continuity planning. Establish operational objectives and ownership across system boundaries.
* Enable observability that follows business transactions across systems, connecting technical execution to business outcomes and supporting diagnosis, safe recovery, and ongoing improvement.
* Turn integration delivery experience into prioritized platform requirements and influence shared engineering roadmaps through prototypes, acceptance criteria, and evidence of value.
* Enable effective AI-assisted development and agentic workflows through well-defined interfaces, testable behavior, explicit approvals, and controls over execution and cost. Apply MCP tools, plugins, or comparable approaches where useful.
* Embed security, access controls, segregation of duties, auditability, and change management into integration design. Coordinate partner testing, cutover, and operational readiness.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Bring 10+ years of experience in enterprise integration engineering and architecture, including technical leadership for business-critical systems.
* Understand supply chain processes well enough to independently translate purchasing, receiving, order management, fulfillment, and inventory needs into integrations and explain the operational impact of missing or incorrect transactions.
* Can support integrations across Supply Chain & Finance systems, working with subject matter experts to translate business processes, reconciliation needs, and financial controls into reliable designs.
* Have personally shaped architectures, improved existing implementations, and guided engineers toward reusable, maintainable solutions with measurable results.
* Have deep experience across multiple integration platforms and approaches, including iPaaS and custom services, and can translate their strengths and limitations into useful platform requirements.
* Are a strong Python engineer who can connect design recommendations to production code, automated tests, deployment practices, and troubleshooting.
* Understand API and event design, asynchronous messaging, EDI, data contracts, authentication, transaction integrity, and failure recovery across distributed systems.
* Have built and operated high-volume integrations and can reason about performance, capacity, reliability, observability, and operating cost.
* Are comfortable with cloud infrastructure, containerized services, version control, CI/CD, and automated integration testing.
* Can influence technical direction across teams, explain tradeoffs to business stakeholders, and mentor engineers without relying on reporting authority.
* Work independently, navigate ambiguity, and take responsibility for outcomes from initial design through sustained operation.

**Preferred Qualifications**

* Integrations with Oracle Fusion ERP or SCM and adjacent enterprise applications such as warehouse, transportation, planning, or product lifecycle management systems.
* Shaping shared integration platforms, developer tooling, or self-service capabilities adopted by multiple teams.
* Building MCP servers, plugins, or agentic enterprise workflows, and applying AI coding tools such as Codex with rigorous review and validation.
