## **About the Team**

The B2B Marketing team is responsible for helping businesses understand, adopt, and get value from OpenAI’s products. B2B marketing is a major and growing priority for OpenAI as we scale our work with companies, developers, and institutions around the world.

Within B2B Marketing, Demand Generation builds the integrated, full-funnel engine that connects audience insights, content, field and digital experiences, paid media, lifecycle, and sales follow-through to qualified pipeline. We partner closely with Sales, Partnerships, Product Marketing, Communications, Creative, Web, RevOps, Analytics, and regional teams to create a cohesive customer experience and scale what works.

## **About the Role**

We’re looking for a Senior ABM Strategist to build and lead the operating model for OpenAI’s highest-priority enterprise accounts. You’ll translate commercial priorities into focused 1:1 and 1 account programs, align Marketing and Sales around shared account plans, and orchestrate buying-group engagement across channels. This is a hands-on builder role for someone who can define the strategy, establish the system, and work directly with cross-functional partners to move strategic accounts toward qualified pipeline and revenue.

## **In this role, you will:**

* Own the enterprise ABM strategy, including account selection, tiering, objectives, program architecture, investment decisions, and performance recommendations.
* Partner with Sales, SDR, and RevOps to build shared account plans, map buying groups, identify whitespace, define engagement plays, and establish clear follow-up ownership.
* Design and execute 1:1 and 1 programs across content, paid media, executive engagement, events and webinars, web experiences, outbound coordination, and other relevant channels.
* Use intent, product, firmographic, engagement, and seller signals to determine when and how to activate priority accounts.
* Translate account and buyer insights into personalization strategies, offers, messaging, and content briefs in partnership with Product Marketing, Content, Creative, and channel teams.
* Establish account-level measurement with Analytics and RevOps across buying-group coverage, engagement, opportunity creation, pipeline, velocity, and revenue impact.
* Build reusable ABM playbooks, governance, operating rhythms, and decision frameworks that enable the motion to scale across products, industries, and account cohorts.
* Develop a point of view on how AI can responsibly improve account research, orchestration, personalization, analysis, and execution while maintaining strong human judgment and quality.

## **You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* 10+ years of experience doing B2B enterprise marketing experience, with deep ownership of account-based marketing or enterprise demand generation programs in a complex sales environment.
* A track record of creating qualified pipeline and advancing strategic accounts through 1:1 and 1 motions—not simply generating engagement.
* Strong understanding of buying groups, account planning, intent and engagement signals, sales orchestration, integrated campaigns, and account-level measurement.
* Demonstrated ability to influence and align senior Sales, SDR, RevOps, Marketing, and GTM partners without relying on formal authority.
* Strong analytical judgment, with the ability to distinguish signal from noise, diagnose performance, and translate insights into clear investment and execution decisions.
* Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to make complex strategies concrete for executives and operators.
* Comfort operating in ambiguity, moving from zero to one, and staying close to execution while building scalable systems.
* Genuine curiosity about AI and how it will reshape enterprise work, buying behavior, and modern go-to-market teams.
