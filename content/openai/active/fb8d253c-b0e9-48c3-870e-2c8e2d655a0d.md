**About the team**

OpenAI’s Industrial Compute team builds the data center infrastructure that supports our research and products. Procurement and Sourcing partners with Engineering, Construction, Deployment, Finance, and suppliers to secure the critical equipment needed to bring new compute capacity online.

**About the role**

We’re hiring an Owner-Furnished Equipment (OFE) Demand Planner for OpenAI’s self-build data center portfolio. Long-lead equipment decisions often come before site schedules, designs, and funding are final. This role helps OpenAI make those decisions early enough to keep deployments on schedule.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Own and publish the OFE demand baseline for self-build data centers. Translate the capacity roadmap and reference designs into equipment quantities and delivery windows by site, building, and phase.
* Work backward from equipment need-by dates to set deadlines for design decisions, funding approvals, capacity reservations, and purchase orders.
* Assess supply coverage with Supply Planning using supplier capacity and lead times validated by Sourcing and production and delivery commitments from Manufacturing Operations.
* Lead planning reviews with Engineering, Construction, Deployment, and Finance to resolve changes in scope, quantities, and delivery dates. Escalate decisions that put deployment milestones at risk.
* Model the schedule impact of supply shortages and recommend where to shift deliveries, reallocate equipment, or reserve additional capacity.
* Provide the OFE demand baseline and forecast changes to the broader Compute Supply & Demand Plan.

**Qualifications**

* Experience owning a demand, supply, materials, or integrated business planning process for complex hardware or capital equipment.
* Sound judgment when inputs are incomplete or changing, with the ability to explain assumptions and uncertainty clearly.
* Experience using supplier lead-time and purchasing data in a project-based planning environment.
* Strong analytical skills and the ability to explain trade-offs clearly to technical and executive audiences.
* Experience influencing decisions across teams and with external partners without direct authority.

**Preferred Skills**

* Experience with data centers, critical facilities, industrial construction, or another capital-intensive, multi-site environment.
* Familiarity with owner-furnished equipment or long-lead electrical and mechanical infrastructure.
* Experience improving forecast accuracy, supply coverage reporting, or planning processes using ERP, planning, or analytics tools.
