**About the Team**

OpenAI’s mission is to build safe artificial general intelligence (AGI) which benefits all of humanity. This long-term undertaking brings the world’s best scientists, engineers, and business professionals into one lab together to accomplish this.

The GTM Agentic Workflows team is building OpenAI’s agentic go-to-market operating system. We work with sellers, GTM leaders, and partners in GTM Systems, Data Science, and Product to connect customer intelligence to account strategy, helping teams prioritize opportunities, grow adoption across accounts, and spend time where it matters most.

**About the Role**

As an individual contributor on the GTM Agentic Workflows team, you’ll own the design, rollout, and performance of processes and workflows across the customer lifecycle, from lead routing through deal execution, delivery, and consumption. Orchestration is central to this role. You’ll bring sellers, GTM leaders, and cross-functional partners together around a shared outcome, then connect the work of people, systems, and agentic workflows to deliver it.

**In this role, you will:**

* Prioritize problems with GTM leaders and the field. Look at where sellers repeat work, where deals stall, and which handoffs break down. Use the frequency and impact of those problems to decide what to tackle first.
* Strengthen the foundations of GTM workflows. Set the strategy for enriching person and company data, and partner with GTM Systems on lead routing and how sellers manage accounts and opportunities in Salesforce.
* Orchestrate delivery across teams. Align owners on the outcome, sequence work across functions, and resolve competing priorities. Make clear who decides, what information carries forward, and when the next team can act.
* Design and launch workflows with Product and Enterprise Systems. Define what triggers the workflow, what information an agent needs, and when a person should review or approve an action. Pilot with field teams and partner with Enablement on rollout.
* Own performance after launch. Measure adoption alongside outcomes such as lead response time, accuracy of proposed updates, and time to complete a handoff. Use those results and field feedback to decide what to improve next.

**You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* 8+ years of experience in Sales Operations, Revenue Operations, or related roles at a high-growth technology company
* Strong systems thinking with deep experience designing and scaling sales systems, processes, and workflows across the full sales lifecycle
* Experience taking a multi-step GTM workflow from a field problem through rollout. You can explain what you automated, where people retained judgment, and how you measured whether it worked.
* Strong proficiency in Salesforce and data tools (e.g., SQL, Excel). Strong analytical skills with high attention to detail
* Experience with territory design, account structures, and pipeline management
* A track record of leading through influence. You can point to a cross-functional initiative where you brought teams with competing priorities to a decision and saw it through to delivery.
* Ability to operate in ambiguity, prioritize effectively, and manage multiple high-impact initiatives simultaneously
