**About the Team**

OpenAI’s GTM Partnerships team builds a strategic, global partner ecosystem to accelerate customer success, enable responsible enterprise AI adoption, and drive durable growth in support of OpenAI’s mission. We work cross-functionally across Sales, Solutions, Product, Engineering, Security, Legal, Finance, Marketing, Operations, and Customer Success to translate strategic partnerships into measurable outcomes for customers.

Global consulting and technology partners help enterprises move from AI strategy to production deployment and sustained adoption. This role leads OpenAI’s partnership with BCG across the Americas, combining BCG’s industry, consulting and delivery expertise with OpenAI’s products to create measurable customer and commercial impact.

**About the Role**

We are looking for a leader with experience leading enterprise AI or digital transformations and translating technology into measurable business outcomes. As Partner Director for BCG, you will build the joint business plan, engage senior executives, and bring together BCG and OpenAI teams to develop opportunities and deliver successful AI adoption.

Partnership and alliance experience is important to this role. It may come from consulting, enterprise business development, joint go-to-market programs or transformation delivery, as well as formal alliance leadership. You will combine commercial discipline, technical fluency and an understanding of advisory firms with the ability to align teams without formal authority.

**The ideal candidate connects AI strategy to customer needs, practical use cases, delivery and adoption. You can build trusted partner relationships, create commercial opportunities and guide initiatives from executive sponsorship to measurable results. Experience at BCG or working closely with BCG or similar advisory firms is valuable, particularly on AI or digital transformation; prior ownership of a BCG alliance is not required.**

**In this role you will:**

* Own the BCG partnership’s business outcomes, including executive alignment, joint planning, pipeline growth, successful AI adoption and long-term expansion, aligned with the global partnership strategy.
* Develop and execute a joint business plan with clear account, industry and AI use-case priorities, investments, accountable owners, milestones and measures of success.
* Build trusted relationships with BCG leadership, industry and functional practices, alliance teams, client account leaders, architects and delivery teams, while staying aligned with global stakeholders.
* Translate executive transformation priorities into account plans, qualified opportunities, joint solutions and delivery plans that address business value, technical readiness and organizational adoption.
* Shape AI solutions and industry offerings that combine OpenAI capabilities with BCG’s consulting, technology, data and change-management expertise to improve business workflows and outcomes.
* Lead joint account planning and strategic customer pursuits with BCG and OpenAI field teams, with particular focus on complex and regulated enterprises.
* Create a clear engagement model for OpenAI sellers and technical teams, including when to involve BCG, how opportunities are qualified, who owns each step, and how channel conflict is resolved.
* Partner with Product, Engineering, Solutions, Security, Legal, Finance, and Commercial teams to support complex customer engagements and remove technical, contractual, compliance, and operational blockers.
* Build BCG’s field and delivery readiness through executive sponsorship, sales enablement, AI technical training, reusable solution patterns and clear delivery handoffs.
* Establish governance across executive business reviews, pipeline inspection, forecasting, deployment milestones, adoption and value realization, connected to the global partnership operating model.
* Set and monitor partnership KPIs, including qualified pipeline, conversion, production adoption, measurable customer value, partner readiness, delivery quality and return on investment.
* Prioritize OpenAI resources for opportunities with the greatest customer value, commercial potential, delivery readiness and repeatability.
* Represent OpenAI in senior partner, customer, and industry engagements with clarity, sound judgment, and strong executive presence.
* Build the operating model and identify the team capabilities needed to scale the BCG partnership and successful customer deployments.

**You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* Experience leading enterprise AI or digital transformations, with clear ownership of business outcomes from strategy and use-case selection through implementation and adoption.
* Practical technical fluency in enterprise AI, cloud and data platforms, including the ability to discuss feasibility, evaluation, security, governance and deployment tradeoffs with technical teams.
* Experience working in advisory firms or closely with BCG or similar organizations, with an understanding of how consulting and delivery teams engage enterprise clients.
* Partnership or alliance experience gained through joint commercial initiatives, business development, consulting or transformation delivery, with measurable pipeline, revenue, adoption or customer outcomes.
* Experience aligning teams across large, matrixed organizations, including account planning, governance, delivery handoffs and escalation. Experience building a formal alliance operating model is valuable.
* Demonstrated success engaging C-suite leaders on enterprise transformation, building a business case and sustaining sponsorship through implementation and organizational change.
* Strong executive presence and the ability to build credibility with senior BCG and customer leaders while remaining close to operating details.
* Commercial judgment and experience prioritizing investments, qualifying opportunities, managing forecasts, and making clear tradeoffs.
* Strong cross-functional leadership and the ability to align Sales, Solutions, Product, Engineering, Security, Legal, Finance, Marketing, and Operations without direct authority.
* Experience enabling field or delivery teams, shaping joint solutions or industry offerings, and making successful customer approaches repeatable.
* Comfort operating in ambiguity and creating structure, accountability, and momentum in a rapidly evolving market.
* Excellent written and verbal communication, negotiation, and executive presentation skills.
* A builder mindset, high ownership, low ego, and a consistent focus on customer value and responsible AI adoption.

**What success looks like:**

* OpenAI and BCG have a clear growth strategy, differentiated AI value proposition and measurable business plan aligned with the global partnership.
* BCG and OpenAI field teams know when and how to engage one another, and qualified opportunities progress through a consistent commercial and delivery process.
* Priority AI opportunities progress from business case to secure production deployment, sustained adoption and measurable business impact.
* Joint solution patterns and industry plays become repeatable rather than remaining bespoke.
* OpenAI resources are allocated transparently toward the highest-impact opportunities.
* BCG teams demonstrate growing AI capability and accountability across selling, solution design, implementation and customer success.
* The partnership earns sustained executive sponsorship and delivers measurable year-over-year growth.
