**About the team**

OpenAI’s mission is to ensure that artificial general intelligence benefits all of humanity. This long-term undertaking brings together the world’s best scientists, engineers, and business professionals into one organization.

In pursuit of this mission, our go-to-market (GTM) team is responsible for helping customers learn how to leverage and deploy our highly capable AI products across their business. The team is made of Sales, Solutions, Support, Marketing, and Partnership professionals that work together to create valuable solutions that will help bring AI to as many users as possible.

**About the role**

We’re looking for a Startup Ecosystem Account Director to help more startups across Asia Pacific build and grow with OpenAI. You’ll focus on the broad base of startups beyond our sales team’s 1:1 coverage, creating engagement approaches that help founders and developers discover our products, get started, and deepen their adoption over time.

This role combines growth marketing, field marketing, online events, content, and ecosystem partnerships. You’ll connect these activities into a coherent journey from a startup’s first interaction with OpenAI to sustained product usage and revenue growth.

You’ll join the APAC Startups team and work closely with sales, technical, marketing, developer relations, and partnerships colleagues. This is a hands-on role for someone who is comfortable owning a revenue target and can set strategy, run experiments, and build repeatable programs across diverse markets.

**In this role, you’ll:**

* **Own growth across startups beyond active sales coverage.** Develop and execute a regional strategy to increase revenue by driving engagement, with a particular focus on API and ChatGPT adoption.
* **Build scalable startup journeys.** Create programs that move founders and developers from initial interest to their first working use case, production deployment, and broader adoption.
* **Run field and online programs.** Design founder gatherings, technical workshops, hackathons, webinars, and other formats with clear audiences, follow-up journeys, and measurable outcomes.
* **Collaborate with Marketing to create useful content.** Work with technical experts, marketers, and customers to produce practical guides, demos, startup stories, and campaigns that help teams solve problems and build with OpenAI.
* **Develop lifecycle campaigns.** Use email, digital channels, and product and engagement signals to deliver relevant guidance at key moments in a startup’s journey.
* **Extend our reach through partners.** Collaborate with venture capital firms, accelerators, startup communities, and other ecosystem partners to reach and support founders at scale.
* **Prioritize across APAC.** Segment audiences by market, stage, use case, and adoption maturity, adapting programs to local needs while building formats that can be reused across the region.
* **Connect growth programs to sales.** Establish clear signals and handoffs for startups that would benefit from direct commercial or technical support.
* **Measure and improve performance.** Own a dashboard spanning activation, sustained usage, revenue growth, retention, and program efficiency. Use experiments and cohort analysis to identify what works and where to invest.
* **Own execution.** Manage the program calendar, budget, agencies, and vendors, balancing ambitious goals with disciplined delivery.

**We’re seeking someone with:**

* 6+ years of experience in growth, lifecycle, developer, or field marketing for a technology business, ideally serving startups or developers.
* A track record of turning campaigns and programs into measurable product adoption and revenue growth.
* Experience combining events, content, digital channels, and lifecycle marketing into an integrated growth motion.
* Strong analytical judgment: you can define success, interpret funnel and cohort data, and distinguish activity from business impact.
* Strong writing and storytelling skills, with the ability to make technical topics useful and accessible to founders and builders.
* Experience working across APAC, with an understanding of how startup ecosystems, languages, and routes to market vary across the region.
* The ability to work effectively with sales, technical teams, marketing, and ecosystem partners.
* An ownership mindset and the willingness to move between strategy and hands-on execution.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* Enjoy helping startups turn promising ideas into products customers use.
* Are curious about AI and willing to learn about APIs and developer workflows, with support from technical colleagues.
* Think about the full customer journey, including what happens after an event, content download, or first API call.
* Know when to tailor a program to a local market and when to build a repeatable regional approach.
* Bring structure to ambiguity, test ideas quickly, and use evidence to decide what to scale.
