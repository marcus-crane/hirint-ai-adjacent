**About the Team**

OpenAI Finance is responsible for ensuring the organization is set up for success in pursuit of its mission.

The Treasury team is responsible for protecting liquidity, enabling scale, maintaining strong controls, and helping the company operate with clarity and resilience. We work across finance and operational partners to ensure funds move safely, visibility remains high, and Treasury infrastructure keeps pace with a fast-moving business. As hands-on operators and builders, we combine financial judgment with AI, automation, and data to solve problems faster, strengthen controls, and continuously improve how Treasury operates.

**About the Role**

We are looking for a senior hire to help structure financing for major deals, improve working capital, and lead cash forecasting and liquidity planning. Reporting to the Treasurer, you’ll partner with Compute and teams across OpenAI to design creative financing and payment solutions that support the company’s growth. AI and automation will be core to how you build and scale this capability.

This role can be based in San Francisco, CA, Seattle, WA, or New York, NY. We use a hybrid work model of 3 days in the office per week and offer relocation assistance to new employees.

**In this role, you will:**

* Structure and negotiate trade finance solutions that improve deal economics and manage risk.
* Improve cash conversion by working with Billing, Collections, Accounting, and business teams to bill sooner, collect faster, improve supplier terms, and reduce unnecessary deposits or inventory commitments.
* Free up cash and credit capacity by managing collateral, letters of credit, guarantees, and renewals.
* Build direct cash forecasts by incorporating business-specific receipts and payments, compute commitments, and financing timing.
* Partner with Treasury’s Investment team to determine liquidity buffers, investment decisions, and contingency funding plans.
* Advise on deal structuring Compute, Finance, and Legal, then coordinate execution with Treasury Operations.
* Improve e-commerce payment economics with Product, Payments, and Strategic Finance, balancing cost, acceptance, risk, and settlement timing.
* Recommend billing, collection, prepayment, and credit terms for new business models, balancing cash protection with ease of purchase.
* Use AI and automation to streamline contract review, credit support tracking, cash forecasting, and treasury workflows.

**You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* 10+ years of progressive experience in corporate treasury or banking, including trade finance structuring, working capital management, and cash flow forecasting.
* A strong track record of negotiating and implementing complex trade finance across multiple instruments.
* Experience delivering measurable cash conversion improvements through billing, collections, supplier terms, or inventory.
* Sound judgment on contracts, risk, and approvals, and the ability to explain trade-offs to senior leaders and align business, finance, and legal teams.
* Built direct cash flow forecasts and reconciled them to an indirect forecast or cash flow statement, with a strong grasp of how commercial terms and balance sheet changes affect liquidity.
* Leveraged AI or automation to improve finance workflows end-to-end.
* Experience in infrastructure, hardware, or other capital-intensive businesses, or familiarity with global e-commerce payments (preferred but not required).
