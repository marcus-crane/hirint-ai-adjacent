**About the Role**

We’re looking for an Optical Mechanical Engineer to design, prototype, and validate high-density fiber interconnects for our AI infrastructure. You’ll work across MT ferrule–based connectors and adapters, optical transceiver mechanical designs, tray-level optical assemblies, and fiber shuffle solutions, translating optical and system requirements into reliable, manufacturable hardware.

This is a hands-on role spanning detailed mechanical design, lab testing, and integration. You’ll partner with optical, mechanical, networking, manufacturing, and data center teams to make fiber connections practical to assemble, route, test, and service—from within a tray or rack to the cabling infrastructure connecting racks.

**In this role, you will:**

* Design and test MT ferrule–based fiber connectors and adapters, addressing alignment, tolerances, retention, mating repeatability, and serviceability.
* Develop mechanical designs for optical transceivers and tray-level optical solutions, balancing packaging density, thermal interfaces, assembly access, and fiber management.
* Design and validate high-density fiber shuffle assemblies, including shuffle cassettes, with clear connectivity maps, controlled routing, and accessible test interfaces.
* Define and execute optical-mechanical validation plans, including optical loss measurements, connector inspection, mating cycles, and relevant environmental and mechanical testing.
* Develop fiber routing within and between racks, accounting for bend radius, strain relief, cable management, labeling, installation, and maintenance access.
* Partner with data center and network teams to translate fiber layouts and cabling infrastructure requirements into hardware interfaces and integration plans.
* Work with suppliers and manufacturing partners to build prototypes, resolve failures, improve assembly consistency, and transition validated designs into production.

**You might thrive in this role if you:**

* You have hands-on experience designing and testing MT ferrule–based fiber connectors and adapters.
* You have developed and tested fiber interconnects, including high-density fiber solutions, and can connect mechanical design decisions to optical performance.
* You have experience with the mechanical design and integration of optical transceivers and tray-level optical assemblies.
* You have designed and validated fiber shuffle solutions, such as shuffle cassettes, including connectivity, routing, assembly, and test considerations.
* You understand fiber routing within and between racks, including bend-radius constraints, strain relief, connector access, and serviceability.
* You are familiar with data center fiber layouts and the cabling infrastructure that supports installation, expansion, and operation.
* You can move between detailed design and hands-on debugging, use measurements to identify root causes, and work with engineering and manufacturing partners to implement practical improvements.
