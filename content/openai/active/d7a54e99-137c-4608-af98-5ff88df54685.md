**About the Team**

The Hardware Marketing team helps people understand what OpenAI’s hardware products can do and how they can fit into everyday life. We work with Product Marketing, Design, Creative, Sales, and Operations to bring products to market and create experiences where people can try them for themselves.

**About the Role**

We’re looking for a Visual Merchant to shape how customers discover, understand, and try OpenAI hardware in stores. Reporting to the Channel Lead, you’ll own visual merchandising for OpenAI-owned experiences and third-party retail. You’ll work with Creative, Design, and retail partners to build displays that tell a clear product story and make it easy for customers to get hands-on.

You’ll take the work from concept through installation, setting a standard in our own spaces and adapting it for partner stores. You’ll be as comfortable giving feedback on a prototype as you are walking a store to see what customers notice, touch, and miss.

**In this role, you will:**

* Make the product story visible. Set the order in which customers see products, signage, and demos. Work with Product Marketing and Creative to keep claims accurate and give customers a clear place to start.
* Develop visual merchandising for OpenAI experiences. Work with Design, Creative, and production partners on fixture placement, displays, signage, and hands-on demo stations. Use mockups and site visits to check sightlines, accessibility, and flow.
* Develop the experience for global retail partners. Define what needs to stay consistent across stores and what can change for different footprints, retailer standards, and local markets. Review partner concepts, samples, and layouts before rollout.
* Turn standards into tools store teams can use. Create layout guides, fixture and signage specifications, and setup and reset instructions. Partner with Production and Retail Operations on prototypes, installation, and quality checks so displays are safe and can be maintained day to day.
* Use what you see in stores to improve the work. Watch how customers approach and use displays, gather feedback from store teams, and check whether installations match the brief. Use what you learn to fix issues and update the next rollout.

**You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* Built visual merchandising for physical retail and can show how you took work from concept through installation. Experience with both brand-owned and partner stores is a plus.
* Made a complex product easy to discover and try through strong spatial layout, signage, and demo presentation. Experience with consumer technology or hands-on product demos is a plus.
* Translated creative direction into practical layouts, specifications, and guides that store teams and vendors can use.
* Worked with Creative, Design, Production, Operations, and retail partners to solve on-site issues and maintain a consistent standard across locations.
* Used store observations or customer feedback to improve what people notice and understand, and know how to keep displays accessible and maintainable.
