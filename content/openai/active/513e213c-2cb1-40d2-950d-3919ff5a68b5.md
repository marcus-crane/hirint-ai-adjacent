OpenAI’s Go-to-Market (GTM) organization helps customers adopt and scale our AI products. As the business grows, we’re looking for a strategic operator to lead high-impact GTM programs that improve execution across the organization.

In this role, you’ll work directly with sales leaders to turn their business priorities into programs their teams can run. You’ll partner with Product, Marketing, Product Marketing, Operations, Enablement, Finance, and Communications to take those programs from strategy through execution. You’ll bring structure to ambiguous problems and build repeatable processes that help the business scale.

### **What you’ll do**

* Lead high-priority GTM programs, including product launches, sales plays, and customer initiatives.
* Partner with sales leaders to identify gaps in pipeline and agree on which programs to run. Work with frontline managers to put those programs into practice and review results.
* Build program plans with clear owners and milestones. Keep teams aligned through regular reviews and concise executive updates.
* Use pipeline and productivity data alongside customer and field feedback to inform decisions and improve program outcomes.
* Build repeatable processes that help teams plan, launch, and measure programs across the GTM organization.

### **We’re looking for**

* 8+ years of experience in Sales Programs, Strategy & Operations, Business Operations, Program Management, Management Consulting, Product Marketing, or a related field.
* Experience working directly with enterprise sales leaders as a business partner. You’ve helped a sales leader identify a growth opportunity, built a program their reps used, and measured its impact on pipeline or revenue.
* A track record of leading complex cross-functional initiatives from strategy through execution.
* Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to bring clarity to ambiguous business challenges.
* Clear communication and experience influencing senior leaders across functions.
* The ability to manage competing priorities and take ownership of results in a fast-paced environment.

### **Nice to have**

* Experience in a high-growth SaaS, AI, platform, or enterprise technology company.
* Experience building operating models, planning cadences, launch processes, or executive reporting.
* Familiarity with product launches, customer engagement programs, or product-led growth.
