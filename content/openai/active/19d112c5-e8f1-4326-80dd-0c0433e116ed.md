**About the Role**  
OpenAI is seeking a senior, hands-on commercial leader and builder to establish and scale Deal Strategy across APAC while personally shaping and executing some of our most complex and high-impact commercial opportunities. As AI becomes increasingly important to how organizations operate, the ability to translate sophisticated products and commercial structures into clear, compelling customer narratives is a strategic advantage.

This is a builder role that will begin as a high-impact individual contributor. The successful candidate will establish the APAC Deal Strategy motion hands-on with the potential over time to build and lead a broader regional team.

In this role, you will serve as the APAC Deal Strategy leader and an embedded strategic partner to account teams, with a particular focus on high-value, complex, and first-of-kind deals. You will work directly with sellers and cross-functional partners to personally lead the overall deal strategy, structuring, value narrative, negotiations, and business cases that bring business terms to contractual closure.

You will also translate insights and patterns from individual deals into a regional operating model, practical frameworks, tools, and resources that improve the quality, consistency, and scalability of deal execution across the field.

This role partners closely with Sales, Pricing, Deal Desk, Strategy, Revenue Operations, Finance, Product, and Legal to help OpenAI move quickly, strengthen proposal quality, and navigate complex commercial opportunities effectively.

**In this role, you will:**

* Partner directly with APAC account teams on complex, high-value, and strategic opportunities, from initial deal strategy through customer proposal, negotiation, and deal closure.
* Develop deal strategies that bring together customer priorities, commercial considerations, competitive positioning, and OpenAI’s broader business objectives.
* Create executive- and CFO-ready proposals, ROI analyses, value narratives, and business cases for highly complex and/or first-of-kind deals.
* Support critical customer and executive conversations by presenting recommendations, business cases, and the rationale behind proposed commercial approaches.
* Build and maintain practical ROI frameworks, calculators, proposal templates, and value narratives that account teams can apply across opportunities.
* Contribute to scalable deal-support mechanisms, including negotiation playbooks, deal clinics, office hours, and structured intake processes.
* Partner with Pricing, Deal Desk, Finance, Product, Legal, Revenue Operations, Enablement, and Strategy to ensure deal recommendations reflect current products, commercial guidance, and business priorities.
* Turn lessons from strategic and bespoke deals into reusable resources that improve speed, consistency, and seller productivity.
* Apply AI to improve deal workflows and scalability through tools such as automated deal briefs, knowledge retrieval, guided proposal development, and self-service resources.
* Identify recurring themes across strategic deals and share insights that can inform future commercial approaches, field motions, products, and policies.

**You might thrive in this role if you have:**

* 8+ years of progressively responsible experience in deal strategy, strategic sales, strategic pricing, commercial strategy, or a related function, ideally with regional or multi-market scope.
* Experience partnering directly with sales teams on highly complex, high-value, or first-of-kind commercial opportunities.
* A track record of personally developing deal strategies, customer proposals, ROI models, or business cases that advanced commercial outcomes and created broader organizational impact.
* Strong communication and storytelling skills, with the ability to translate complex products and commercial concepts into clear, compelling customer narratives.
* Financial and analytical rigor, including experience developing ROI analyses and executive- or CFO-ready materials.
* Experience leading through influence across Sales, Finance, Product, Legal, Pricing, and Operations to navigate complex commercial decisions and align senior stakeholders.
* Strong judgment and the ability to bring structure and clarity to ambiguous or highly bespoke commercial situations.
* A hands-on leadership approach: you are comfortable starting as an individual contributor, working directly with account teams and customers in fast-moving deal cycles, and building credibility through execution.
* Demonstrated leadership potential—such as setting direction, mentoring others, building programs or operating rhythms, and mobilizing cross-functional teams—whether or not you have previously held formal people-management responsibility.
* Familiarity with using AI to improve workflows, scale expertise, and increase productivity.
