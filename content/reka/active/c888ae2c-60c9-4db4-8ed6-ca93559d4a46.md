**Claru (a Reka business line) · San Francisco · Full-time**

Text has run out. The physical world — where skill lives — has never been recorded in a form a model can learn from. [Claru](https://claru.ai) is building that layer.

We sell data to frontier labs and robotics companies: zero to seven figures of ARR in under six months, with a team of five. It is a two-sided marketplace: labs arrive with a research problem and a budget, not a specification; contributors and partner businesses capture the work they already do, paid per approved unit. Both sides get built engagement by engagement. That is this job.

We are hiring a Program Lead and are looking for operators who are AI-native: more time with agents than with people, automation before hiring, systems that scale rather than teams that grow. That is how a team this small outruns much bigger companies. You need to be someone who can be dropped into an unfamiliar problem, with a client waiting, and come back with a plan that holds.

## **What you'll do**

* Turn an open-ended research need into a program with a scope, a standard, a timeline and agreed economics — then own the number
* Work directly with frontier labs and robotics teams: turn a research problem into something deliverable, keep sophisticated buyers confident, and own the relationship from first call to delivery
* Partner with our supply team to build the supply side from nothing if needed: recruit, activate and retain the contributors and partner businesses a program needs, in industries and geographies where none exists
* Go at whichever blocker is in front of you: some days a spec or a tooling gap, other days a person, a partner or an incentive
* Act as product manager for the platform team: your field evidence decides what gets built next, and you speak for the contributor and the client in the roadmap
* Go where the work happens; physical operations are won by whoever showed up
* Build leverage as you go — agents, automation, playbooks — so the second program costs a fraction of the first and the tenth runs itself

## **A typical day**

There is no standard one, but a real day might run like this: you start on the programs already live — checking with the team that supply is filming, that yesterday's batches cleared the quality bar, and chasing the sites that have gone quiet. A buyer wants to change the spec mid-program, so you work out what that does to volume, timeline and cost, and get the new version agreed in writing. Then a first call with a lab you have not worked with before: understand what they are trying to train, and be straight about what we can produce and what we cannot.

## **What we're looking for**

* **Early-stage marketplace operating experience — required.** You have had to get a flywheel turning from a standing start, in a new territory or a new vertical, where neither side existed yet and you had to build both. Every engagement here is that problem again
* **AI-native — required.** You spend more time with agents than with people, you automate a process before you staff it, and your answer to more volume is a better system rather than more headcount
* **You have operated, not only advised.** Former founders, high growth early stage startup hires, consulting, or business operations background is a strong plus and exactly the rigour we want
* Structured problem solving under pressure: first principles, then a plan a client and a team can both act on
* Numerate — you build the model, find where the economics break, and argue for the change
* Credible with a research lead and with a shift supervisor alike
* Bonus: exposure to AI labs or training data pipelines
