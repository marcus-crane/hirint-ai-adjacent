**Prototype Development Technician**

San Jose, CA

Figure is an AI Robotics company developing a general purpose humanoid. Our humanoid robot is designed for commercial tasks and the home. We are based in San Jose, CA and require 5 days/week in-office collaboration. It’s time to build.

We are looking for a Prototype Development Technician to take a hands-on role in building early stage mechanical and electromechanical assemblies for humanoid robot development.

Responsibilities:

* Build  mechanical and electromechanical assemblies from CAD, schematics, and verbal guidance—often with limited to no work instructions
* Perform electrical tasks including wiring, soldering, and assembling custom harnesses
* Help execute tests, diagnose issues and implement solutions in partner with engineering teams
* Iterate on designs and incorporate frequent revisions
* Document build processes, findings, and feedback to support design
* Collaborate closely with mechanical, electrical, and software engineers to support development

Requirements:

* 5+ years of experience in mechanical or electromechanical prototyping or assembly
* Strong mechanical and electrical aptitude
* Ability to interpret engineering drawings, CAD models, and wiring diagrams
* Demonstrated ability to diagnose and troubleshoot issues across mechanical and electrical
* Comfortable working in a fast-paced environment with shifting priorities
* Strong communicator

Bonus Qualifications:

* Degree in related technical field
* IPC certification
* Prior experience building robotic systems
* Experience 3D CAD modeling
* Experience with 3D printing, machining, or other in house fabrication

The US hourly range for this full-time position is between $30 - $40/hr.

The pay offered for this position may vary based on several individual factors, including job-related knowledge, skills, and experience.
