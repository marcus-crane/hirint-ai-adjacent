Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are looking for a Materials Handler to support procurement, inventory management, and kitting for our commercial deployment and data operations teams.

**All shifts are currently available.**

**Responsibilities:**

* Warehouse Management: Organize, stock and validate count of equipment using a warehouse management system while ensuring accurate record-keeping.
* Kitting Parts: Compile equipment into various kits based on Bill of Materials. Deliver kits to designated staging areas according to operations schedule and requirements.
* Inventory Support: Receive, store and account for incoming inventory from suppliers and returned from the field. Some procurement support may also be required.
* Documentation & Tracking: Properly document material movement, including check-ins and check-outs, in the inventory system to maintain precise stock levels.
* Cycle Counts & Audits: Conduct regular cycle counts and participate in inventory audits to reconcile discrepancies and maintain accurate stock records.
* Coordination with Teams: Communicate effectively with warehouse, Dispatch, Fleet, and operations teams to address material needs and resolve shortages promptly.

**Requirements:**

* Respectful work attitude with a championship mindset
* Excellent time management skills and a commitment to punctuality
* Previous experience in materials handling, inventory management, or warehouse operations, preferably in a manufacturing environment.
* Proficiency in using inventory management systems and tracking tools such as barcode scanners.
* Strong attention to detail with a methodical approach to documentation and organization.
* Ability to lift and transport materials safely (up to 50 lbs as required).
* Basic understanding of manufacturing processes and production workflows.
* Excellent communication skills and ability to work effectively in a team setting.
