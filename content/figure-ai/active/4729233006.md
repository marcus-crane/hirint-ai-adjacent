Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are looking for a Thermal Analysis Engineer to simulate and analyze our robots thermal performance and design thermal management systems.

**Responsibilities:**

* Build a thermal model of our actuators, battery, sensors, computers, and other key components.
* Simulate kinematic use cases on the thermal model and determine where thermal mitigations are necessary.
* Generate and execute test plans to demonstrate the necessity and effectiveness of your thermal management designs.
* Inspect first articles, build test stands, and continuously support parts on robot development platforms.
* Work cross-functionally with subsystem owners of to integrate the thermal management system into the robot.

**Requirements:**

* 5+ years of experience in mechanical engineering, 2+ years explicit experience in thermal modeling.
* Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree in Mechanical, Robotics, Mechatronics, or similar field.
* Experienced in using 3D CAD software to design parts with complex geometry.
* Experience designing and building prototypes.
* Experienced with simulation and analysis methodology and software (ANSYS, COMSOL, MATLAB, etc).
* Ability to write and maintain clean documentation for your design process.

**Bonus Qualifications:**

* Experience with high volume manufacturing processes.
* Experience working with contract manufacturers.
* Experience creating 3D CAD models, especially in CATIA V6.
* Experience with textiles.

The US base salary range for this full-time position is $150,000 to $220,000 per year.
