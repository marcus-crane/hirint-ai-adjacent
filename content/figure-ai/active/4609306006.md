Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are looking for an engineer to join our Middleware team with a passion for helping other teams move fast and fearless, and who appreciates the impact multiplier of that work.

Our middleware is an in-house design focused on efficiency and low latency/jitter. Delivering over 200,000 messages per second between 190 nodes on each robot, zero-copy and lock-free. We work hard to provide abstractions that make using it a pleasant experience for all teams at the company.

**Responsibilities:**

* Design and build the core framework and infrastructure the robot runs on.
* Work directly with client teams to understand how they use it and where it gets in their way.
* Turn that understanding into better abstractions and tooling.

**Requirements:**

* At least 7 years of experience building production software in C++
* Mastery of Modern C++ (>20).
* Familiarity with Python.
* Experience working on embedded systems and timing sensitive environments.
* Intimate understanding of the interface between the Linux kernel and userspace applications.
* Passion for developing well tested frameworks and infrastructure that just works day in and day out for years to come.
* Motivated by working directly with other teams to understand their needs.

**Bonus Qualifications:**

* Experience building frameworks and infrastructure with large user bases.

The US base salary range for this full-time position is $180,000 to $250,000 per year.
