Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are seeking a proactive, highly organized Manager to oversee our R&D facility for robot use case testing. In this role, you will lead the end-to-end lifecycle of workcell builds - from initial space design to installation and ongoing maintenance - while maintaining an efficient, safe, and collaborative building environment for various teams at Figure including engineers and operations.

**Responsibilities:**

* Facility & Space Operations: Oversee daily operations of the R&D facility, keeping the space organized, functional, and optimized for multiple cross-functional teams working simultaneously.
* Workcell Project Management: Lead R&D workcell builds from design phase to full assembly, commissioning, and preventive maintenance.
* Space Planning: Strategically map out building floor plans to accommodate incoming equipment, temporary builds, and ongoing operational workflows.

**Requirements:**

* 5+ years of experience in space planning, industrial facility management, or hardware project management.
* Experience using CAD software for 2D/3D facility layout, workcell placement, and spatial planning.
* Demonstrated track record of managing technical buildouts or workcell projects from concept to completion.
* Strong written and verbal communication skills with an ability to keep diverse teams aligned.
* Low-ego, team-first collaborative style with a pragmatic, "can-do" approach to daily problem-solving.

**Bonus Qualifications:**

* Electrical Systems Knowledge: Practical understanding of building electrical infrastructure, power load requirements, and drop planning for industrial machinery/workcells.
* Hands-On Fabrication Skills: Practical experience with custom fabrication, mechanical assembly, prototyping, or working with shop tools to build physical test rigs or workcells.

The US base salary range for this full-time position is between $130,000 – $180,000 base salary annually.
