Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are looking for an experienced PCB Layout Designer to help us design critical hardware for our Humanoid.

**Responsibilities**:

* Responsible for the PCB layout of new and existing designs, from initial placement to production.
* Preparation and release of fabrication/assembly files.
* Perform and manage vendor DFx reports.
* Collaborate with Electrical, Mechanical, and Thermal engineers.
* Manage external PCB layout contractors.

**Requirements**:

* 5+ years of industry experience.
* Strong experience with Altium.
* Strong experience designing for power converters (10W to >1kW).
* Strong experience with flex, rigid flex, and HDI designs.
* Strong experience with DFx (high yield designs, designing for ICT/FCT, etc).
* Strong understanding of rule based designs (creepage/clearance, high speed rules, etc).
* Experience designing for EMI/EMC.
* Experience proposing and selecting material and stackups.
* A good grasp of EE fundamentals.

**Bonus Qualifications**:

* Experience with high speed designs (>8 GHz) and RF.
* Experience with simulation software for power electronics/high speed design (i.e. ANSYS, Hyperlynx, Keysight, etc).
* Experience with high voltage applications (>60V) and compliance requirements.
* Experience with exotic PCB technologies (3D mid, IMS, embedded coin/bus-bar, embedded passives, planar transformers, etc).
* Experience with PLM systems.

The US base salary range for this full-time position is between $150,000 to $250,000 per year.
