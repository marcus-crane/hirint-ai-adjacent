Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

**Responsibilities:**

Production quality

* Own quality on assigned in-house assembly lines, such as battery pack assembly and final robot assembly.
* Define control plans, in-process and end-of-line quality checks, and drive them toward automated inspection and data capture by the start of mass production.
* Monitor first-pass yield and defect Paretos, apply SPC where it adds control, and lead the daily quality response on the line.
* Lead manufacturing line audits covering process, specifications, tooling and gauges, data recording, and operator training.

New product introduction

* Act as the quality voice on next-generation robot builds: review process flows with Manufacturing Engineering, and author and maintain PFMEAs and control plans ahead of each build phase.
* Translate DFMEA severity and safety-critical classifications into process controls, and feed design gaps back to the design teams.
* Define quality line-readiness criteria and sign off readiness for prototype builds through start of production.

Field failures and remanufacturing

* Own failure analysis on field returns and remanufactured units: structured failure coding, teardown, and root cause using 8D, 5-Why, and fishbone analysis.
* Close the loop from field data to production and design corrective actions.
* Drive the DOA and return resolution process together with the remanufacturing and commercial teams.

Quality system and team

* Contribute to building out the manufacturing quality management system: internal audit, corrective and preventive action, deviation handling, equipment qualification, and training standards.
* Partner with Supplier Quality on incoming inspection and supplier corrective actions for components used on the lines.
* Mentor quality engineers and technicians, and raise the standard of structured problem solving across Production, Manufacturing, and Quality.

**Requirements:**

* 7+ years of experience in quality engineering in high-volume electromechanical manufacturing (automotive, consumer electronics, robotics, or similar).
* Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical, Electrical, Manufacturing, Mechatronics Engineering, or a related technical field.
* Hands-on experience authoring PFMEAs and control plans and carrying them from NPI into production.
* Structured problem-solving experience with 8D, 5-Why, fishbone analysis, and formal corrective action processes.
* GD&T knowledge and the ability to interpret complex technical drawings.
* Statistical analysis and SPC using tools such as JMP or Minitab, and the ability to build dashboards from inspection and test data.
* Experience with robotic systems, automation, or complex electromechanical assemblies.
* Track record of working on the production floor directly with operators, manufacturing engineering, and design teams.

**Bonus Qualifications:**

* Battery pack, actuator, or drivetrain assembly experience.
* Field failure analysis, warranty, or remanufacturing quality experience.
* Knowledge of ISO 9001 or IATF 16949 standards.
* Six Sigma Green Belt or Black Belt certification.
* Hands-on experience with automated inspection systems (CMM, vision systems).
* Experience building a quality management system from the ground up.
* CATIA V6 experience.
