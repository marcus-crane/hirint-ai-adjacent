Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We're looking for a senior full-stack engineer, leaning backend, to build a new B2B product on the Helix team. You'll ship the APIs, integrations, and web experiences our business customers use every day, from how they communicate with us over WhatsApp to how they're billed and pay.

This is a small team that moves fast. You'll work directly with product and customers, own features from idea to production, and ship weekly. We value pragmatic engineering over elaborate architecture: simple systems that work, ship quickly, and are easy to change.

**What you'll do:**

* Design, build, and ship production backend services and APIs in Python (Go welcome)
* Build product-facing web features in React alongside the backend
* Integrate with external platforms, starting with the WhatsApp Business API, plus messaging, CRM, and partner APIs
* Ship the payment side of the product: checkout and invoicing flows, processor integrations like Stripe, webhooks, and keeping customer billing accurate
* Own your services in production on AWS or GCP, including containers, CI/CD, monitoring, and cost
* Work directly with product and customers to scope, ship, and iterate on 0-to-1 features

**What we're looking for**:

* 5+ years of software engineering experience, including 3+ years building production backend systems
* Senior-level, hands-on IC who still writes and ships code every day
* Strong Python backend experience (Django, FastAPI, Flask, or similar), or Go with a willingness to work in Python
* Real product experience with React or a similar framework (Vue, etc.), leaning backend
* Experience shipping integrations with third-party platforms and APIs in a B2B or SaaS product
* Experience building product features that involve payments, such as integrating Stripe or another processor for checkout, invoicing, or billing
* A track record of fast shipping at a startup or on a small team, with dated launches you can point to
* Hands-on experience running services on AWS or GCP with containers and CI/CD
* Able to work onsite in San Jose 5 days a week

**Bonus qualifications:**

* Deeper B2B billing experience: usage or invoice-based billing, payouts, idempotency, and reconciliation
* Experience with the WhatsApp Business API, Twilio, or similar messaging platforms
* Early engineer at a B2B SaaS startup who helped take a product from zero to paying customers

The US base salary range for this full-time position is $200,000 - $400,000 per year.
