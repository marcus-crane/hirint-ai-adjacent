Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are looking for a Fabricator to work in our manufacturing machine shop who will work closely with manufacturing teams to support tooling and production needs for our robot lines.

**Responsibilities:**

* Fabricate complex parts and assemblies
* Provide feedback and solutions to improve designs and create efficiency
* Assist Engineers and Technicians with their projects
* Provide training to individuals who require use of the equipment in the shop. Including Engineers/Technicians/etc
* Perform preventative maintenance and repair on machines and equipment
* Maintain inventory of supplies and raw materials within the shop
* Maintain an open and safe environment where teams can collaborate
* Document and track workflow

**Requirements:**

* 5+ years of experience in a related industry
* Strong welding and fabrication skills
* Proficient with manual mills and manual lathes
* Ability to tackle problems in which you may not have expert knowledge
* Must be able to work in a fast paced and collaborative environment
* Ability to understand engineering documentation
* Exceptional organizational, interpersonal, and communication skills
* Safety conscious, dependable, and a positive attitude

**Bonus Qualifications:**

* CATIA experience
* CNC
* JIRA

The US base pay range for this full-time position is between $45/hr-$70/hr

The pay offered for this position may vary based on several individual factors, including job-related knowledge, skills, and experience.
