Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We're looking for an Android lead to own Figure's consumer Android app end to end. The app pairs with proprietary hardware and will ingest high volumes of data.

You'll be the founding Android engineer. You set the technical direction for the platform, ship the app, and grow the team behind it.

**Responsibilities:**

* Own the Android app end to end, from architecture to Google Play release.
* Build and ship a consumer-grade client with best-in-class polish and performance.
* Own core app surfaces: onboarding, capture, tasks, earnings, and notifications.
* Set Android architecture, coding standards, and review practices for the engineers who follow.
* Instrument the app for analytics, experimentation, and crash-free releases.
* Work closely with the iOS lead, hardware integration, and backend engineers to ship complete features.
* Drive fast, high-quality release cycles through Google Play, including staged rollouts.

**Requirements:**

* 6+ years building and shipping consumer Android apps at scale.
* Expert in Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.
* Deep experience with CameraX or Camera2, media pipelines, and offline-first sync.
* Experience as the lead or senior-most engineer on a mobile platform.
* Strong product sense, with performance discipline across a fragmented device matrix.
* Comfortable owning a platform end to end on a small, fast-moving team.

**Bonus Qualifications:**

* Experience at top consumer app companies (Snapchat, Instagram, Uber, or similar).
* Bluetooth (BLE) or connected-device integration experience.
* Cross-platform experience (React Native, Flutter, or KMP).

The US base salary range for this full-time position is $200,000 to $400,000 per year.
