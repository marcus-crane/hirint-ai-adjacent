Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are looking for a **Field Logistics & Dispatch Coordinator** to manage the movement of robots and other equipment between HQ/warehouse and active data collection and customer sites. This role is the central point of coordination for all field logistics - working closely with the logistics commercial operations, and data operations teams to ensure every project is fully equipped on schedule. This role requires excellent leadership, problem solving, and communication skills.

**Responsibilities:**

* Receive and process equipment requests from offsite project teams
* Schedule, track, and manage delivery and return of robots and equipment between warehouse and active sites, coordinating carriers, couriers, or personal-vehicle runs as needed.
* Maintain accurate, real-time status of equipment location, quantities, and availability across all active sites.
* Own the process for preparing, inspecting, and documenting equipment before dispatch and upon return, including condition and damage logging.
* Maintain internal vehicle fleet and coordinate/lease supplemental transport as required.
* Serve as the primary logistics point of contact between project teams and the equipment/inventory function, communicating proactively on ETAs, blockers, and status.
* Help build and maintain SOPs/checklists for kitting, dispatch, delivery, and returns as the program scales.
* Coordinate the pulling, inspection, and kitting of equipment against request manifests.
* Support contingency response when things go wrong in the field.

**Requirements:**

* 3+ years experience in logistics, dispatch, fleet operations, asset management, or field equipment coordination.
* Track record of owning physical equipment through a full cycle - dispatch, delivery, recovery, return - not just booking shipping.
* Strong organizational skills and meticulous attention to detail..
* Excellent communication skills - comfortable coordinating across multiple teams and external partners.
* Proficiency with tracking systems, spreadsheets, and project management tools (e.g., Google Sheets, Jira, Confluence, inventory systems).
* Valid driver's license.
* Thrives in a fast-paced, rapidly changing environment; comfortable solving problems in real time without waiting for permission.

The US base salary range for this full-time position is between **$120,000 - $175,000** annually.
