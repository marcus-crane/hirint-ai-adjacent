Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

As a Business Development Manager, you'll help win and close the key commercial deals and execute important business operations. You'll own deals from first conversation through signed contract, then run the operations that make those deals succeed: coordinating internal teams, managing timelines, and making sure what we sell is what we deliver. This is a hands-on role for someone who can sell, execute, and keep a complex deal moving end to end.

**Responsibilities:**

* Source, pitch, negotiate, and close key commercial deals.
* Own the full deal cycle: discovery, proposals, pricing, term sheets, contract negotiation, and signature.
* Run the operations behind each deal: coordinate Engineering, Deployment, Legal, and Finance to hit commitments on time.
* Build trusted relationships with senior decision-makers at partner organizations.

**Requirements:**

* 5+ years in business development, sales, investment banking, or strategic partnerships, with a track record of closing complex, high-value deals.
* Excellent salesmanship.
* Excellent operational skills; able to run cross-functional projects and keep many workstreams on track.
* Excellent written and verbal communication, including with mid-level and executive audiences.
* Highly organized, detail-oriented, and comfortable with ambiguity.
* Thrive in a fast paced environment.

**Bonus Qualifications:**

* Experience in AI or other deep-tech products.
* Experience operating major projects.
* Basic financial modeling: building deal economics, pricing models, and ROI cases in Excel or Google Sheets.

The US base salary range for this full-time position is $300,000 to $400,000 per year.

The pay offered for this position may vary based on several individual factors, including job-related knowledge, skills, and experience.
