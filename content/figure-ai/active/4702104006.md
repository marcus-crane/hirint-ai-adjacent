Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are seeking a motivated **Power Systems Integration Intern for Fall 2026. I**n this role, you will collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams to validate and improve the performance of our humanoid robots. As a Power Systems Integration Intern, you will own the end-to-end integration of power systems to push the boundaries of our robot's capabilities. You will bridge the gap between physical hardware, firmware development, thermals, controls and use cases, ensuring that operation is reliable, safe, and performant.

**Responsibilities:**

* Design and conduct measurements to characterize power bus performance to validate strategies for robust operation.
* Develop methods to reliably expand the performance envelope, in consideration of cross-functional impacts.
* Collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams to integrate and validate software on robotic hardware.
* Perform hands-on debugging of embedded systems using diagnostic tools such as oscilloscopes and multimeters.
* Troubleshoot system-level integration issues, analyze performance logs, and document root causes.
* Assist in maintaining development environments and version control workflows for embedded software releases.

**Requirements:**

* Pursuing a Bachelor’s degree (Senior) or recent graduate in Computer/Electrical Engineering, Power Electronics, Robotics, or a related field.
* Minimum 10-week internship availability; 1–2 terms preferred.
* Strong understanding of Battery design and operation considerations.
* Strong proficiency with the Linux operating system.
* Strong electrical understanding of the system and components

**Bonus Qualifications:**

* Understanding of motor control theory
* Experience with simulation of power systems, optimization of architectures.
* Proficient with Python.

The US base salary range for this full-time position is between $40 - $45/hr.
