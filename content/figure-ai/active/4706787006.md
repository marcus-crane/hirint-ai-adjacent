Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are looking for a **Warehouse Manager** to run our warehouse of robots, spare parts, and equipment supporting customer deployments and field data operations. This role owns everything that happens to equipment inside the four walls - receiving, storage, inspection, kitting, and inventory accuracy -  and hands off completed kits to the Dispatch team for delivery to the field.

**Responsibilities:**

* Own day-to-day operations of the warehouse: layout, storage, labeling, and organization of robots, spare parts, and data collection equipment.
* Receive incoming equipment and shipments; inspect, log condition, and check items into inventory.
* Build and stage kits against project request manifests, and hand off completed kits to the Dispatch Coordinator for delivery to active sites.
* Maintain accurate, real-time inventory records - quantities, condition, and location - for all equipment held in the warehouse.
* Inspect and process returned equipment, documenting damage or missing items and routing gear for repair, restock, or retirement.
* Manage warehouse safety, cleanliness, and compliance, including proper storage and handling of batteries, tools, and sensitive equipment.
* Hire, train, and manage warehouse staff or temp labor as volume grows.
* Partner with the Deployment Logistics Lead and Dispatch Coordinator to forecast equipment needs and flag shortages or bottlenecks before they impact a project.
* Help build and maintain SOPs/checklists for receiving, kitting, inspection, and returns as the program scales.
* Track equipment lifecycle data (usage, wear, repair history) to support replacement and purchasing decisions.

**Requirements:**

* 4+ years experience in warehouse management, inventory control, or equipment operations, ideally in a hardware, robotics, or field-deployed equipment environment.
* Track record of owning physical inventory accuracy at scale - not just receiving, but full lifecycle tracking.
* Strong organizational skills and meticulous attention to detail.
* Excellent communication skills - comfortable coordinating across logistics, dispatch, and project teams.
* Proficiency with inventory/tracking systems, spreadsheets, and project management tools (e.g., Google Sheets, Jira, Confluence, inventory systems).
* Experience managing or leading a small team.
* Comfortable with physical work: lifting equipment, operating warehouse equipment (pallet jacks, forklifts, etc.) as needed.
* Thrives in a fast-paced, rapidly changing environment; comfortable solving problems in real time without waiting for permission.

**Bonus Qualifications:**

* Experience in robotics, autonomous vehicles, or hardware company warehouse operations.
* Familiarity with inventory management systems or ERP tools.
* Forklift certification.
* Experience supporting rapid scaling of a field operations or deployment program.

The US base salary range for this full-time position is between $150,000 - $200,000 annually.
