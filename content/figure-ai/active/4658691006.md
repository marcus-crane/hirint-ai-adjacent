Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are looking for a highly organized and motivated **Recruiting Coordinator** to support our People team and help scale hiring across engineering, operations, and manufacturing. This role is ideal for an early-career professional who is excited to build strong recruiting foundations, drive process excellence, and grow towards a long-term career in talent acquisition.

**Responsibilities:**

* Coordinate complex interview schedules across engineering, operations, and manufacturing teams, ensuring a seamless and timely experience for candidates and interviewers.
* Support hourly and high-volume hiring pipelines by managing candidate flow, scheduling screens, tracking progress, and partnering closely with hiring managers.
* Assist with sourcing efforts by identifying qualified candidates, managing outbound outreach, and maintaining early-stage talent pipelines.
* Support Figure’s intern and university recruiting programs, including scheduling, onboarding logistics, event coordination, and candidate communications.
* Maintain accurate and up-to-date data in our Applicant Tracking System (Greenhouse), ensuring reporting accuracy, compliance, and process consistency.
* Contribute to continuous improvement of recruiting operations by building documentation, templates, and scalable workflows.
* Help deliver an exceptional candidate experience through proactive communication, attention to detail, and strong follow-through

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor’s degree (or equivalent experience) in Business, HR, Communications, Psychology, or a related field.
* 1–3 years of experience in recruiting coordination, HR, operations, hospitality, or other fast-paced environments.
* Clear, concise communication skills and the ability to work well with cross-functional teams.
* Strong organization and follow-through, with the ability to manage multiple priorities.
* Interest in learning recruiting fundamentals, developing sourcing skills, and growing a long-term career in Talent Acquisition.
* Commitment to fostering an inclusive hiring process and positive candidate experience.

**Bonus Qualifications:**

* Experience supporting high-volume hiring or hourly recruiting.
* Exposure to intern or university recruiting programs.
* Familiarity with Greenhouse or other ATS tools.
* Interest in robotics, AI, or manufacturing environments

The US annual salary range for this full-time position is between **$70,000 – $95,000**, depending on experience, skills, and qualifications
