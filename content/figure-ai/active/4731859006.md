Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are seeking a talented and experienced Warehouse Supervisor to join our dynamic team. The Warehouse Supervisor will be responsible for overseeing the safety, productivity, and performance of warehouse personnel within their designated shift. To accomplish this, the job entails creating a daily plan of operation to be disbursed amongst the team, facilitating cross-departmental collaboration to ensure internal and external customer requirements are met, hiring and training personnel, and supporting warehousing functions with a hands-on approach to ensure all work within shift is completed.

**Responsibilities**

* Supervise and direct warehouse staff by driving daily operational workload, delegating tasks amongst material handlers, and ensuring daily output goals are achieved.
* Lead end-to-end staff modeling, including recruiting, interviewing, hiring, onboarding, training, and (if necessary) completing disciplinary actions.
* Hands on support in daily warehouse processes, including receiving, kitting, material handling, staging, shipping, and inventory management.
* Develop reporting models focused on displaying the health of warehouse operations, including necessary countermeasures to reconcile gaps in process.
* Support inventory control initiatives with cycle counting, inventory review, and necessary inventory management.
* Review on-hand inventory for opportunities to optimize storage conditions.
* Track, maintain, and oversee relevant records for inventory movement within the warehouse, including packing slips from vendor and transactional movements within ERP.
* Troubleshoot warehouse issues in daily operations to maintain smooth operations.
* Lead and support the onboarding and continuous training of warehouse personnel within the daily operations to build capabilities in team.
* Communicate with management to identify status, gaps, and escalations for support.
* Monitor shelf life and enforce proper FIFO rotation; manage expiry sensitive parts.
* Uphold material handling procedures, desk instructions, and SOPs to ensure compliance and efficiency.
* Support company-wide metrics and continuous improvement initiatives related to inventory accuracy, flow, and loss reduction.
* Maintain organization and cleanliness in storage and staging areas in accordance with 5S standards.
* Promote a safe working environment by encouraging safe practices within operations.
* Work closely with cross-functional teams (including Logistics/Dispatch, Service, Commercial Ops, Engineering, Supply Chain, Quality, and Facilities) and be the cornerstone of a collaborative process that ensures inventory accuracy and availability on time.
* Take full responsibility for on-hand inventory, including optimizing and expanding processes to support its health.
* Other Relevant Duties as Assigned.

**Requirements**

* Minimum of 5 -7 years’ experience working in a leadership role within Supply Chain, Logistics, Manufacturing, Inventory Management, Distribution, or similar environment.
* Proven experience handling materials and leading teams in dynamic manufacturing or distribution environments with track record of success. Background in robotics manufacturing is a plus.
* Strong ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
* Experience with advanced inventory methodologies such as ABC classification, cycle counting frameworks, and discrepancy root-cause analysis.
* Experience in cycle counting, discrepancy reconciliation, and root cause analysis
* Strong verbal and written communication skills, including presentation skills.
* Strong organizational and analytical/problem solving skills.
* APICS CPIM, CSCP, or CLTD certification is a plus.
* Competent computer experience (Word, Excel, Power Point, SQL, Access, Power BI, Tableau, SAP, Oracle, Odoo, Slack, JIRA, Google Suite, etc.)
* Familiarity with ERP or inventory management systems to record and track movements
* Detail oriented, multi-task, communication skills across entire organization (internal and external) with ability to collaboratively problem solve.
* Knowledge of proper material handling practices and inventory rotation (e.g. FIFO)
* Ability to work in a hands-on role, maintaining clean, orderly storage and staging areas
* Good communication skills to liaise with service, deployment, fleet, engineering, and quality teams
* Dependability, integrity, and a mindset oriented toward continuous improvement
