Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are seeking a talented and experienced Driver to join our dynamic team. The Driver position is an agile position requiring significant experience in warehousing operations, exceptional driving record, in-depth knowledge of transportation management and route optimization, strong interpersonal and communication skills, and excellent time management skills. The Driver will be responsible for material handling and transportation duties in support of various requests that require transport of material.

**Responsibilities**

* Loading, unloading, and transporting material pick-ups and deliveries between locations and/or external sites that may include but are not limited to campus sites, vendors, customers, postal services/freight forwarders, and 3PL providers.
* Operating vehicles within our fleet, including sprinter and cargo vans, box trucks, and freight trucks with GVWR above 10,001 lbs.
* Completion of daily vehicle inspection checklist and pre- and post-trip inspection forms.
* Transportation of finished and hazardous goods as appropriate within compliant standards of local, state, and federal regulations.
* Interacting with internal and external personnel with appropriate and professional conduct.
* Maintaining timeliness for pickup and delivery routes to a set schedule and adhoc material movements as necessary.
* Ensuring that vehicles have adequate fuel and are kept in working condition by monitoring and reporting vehicle issues.
* Complying to internal driving policies as well as local, state, and federal driving regulations (as appropriate).
* Complying with materials management policies and processes, including First In, First Out (FIFO) material rotations, 5S, Safety, Good Documentation and Warehouse Practices, etc.
* Operating powered material handling equipment (e.g. forklifts, electric pallet jacks).
* Communicating via e-mail the status of material movements and transactions to key stakeholders (when appropriate).
* Identify areas or opportunities for operational improvement. Focusing on efficiency and quality, developing proposals for systems and process improvement, gaining consensus from management and driving the implementation of improvements.
* Become proficient on all key tasks performed within the warehouse.
* Ability to complete daily safety walks, lead and participate in daily management meetings, and maintain daily compliance with PPE policies.
* Other duties as assigned, including involvement in standard warehouse operating procedures to support daily needs (including but not limited to receiving, point-of-use replenishment of material, material put away, operating within company ERP system, etc.).

**Requirements**

* Possess high school degree or equivalent.
* A valid driver’s license is required.
* Minimum 3-5 years of material handling/warehousing/driving experience preferred.
* Ability to effectively communicate with peers and internal customers, both verbally and written.
* Experience and/or certification in operating powered material handling equipment and transporting hazardous goods preferred.
* Ability to lift material up to 50 lbs.
* Extensive walking, repetitive physical motion (squatting, bending, lifting, climbing ladders, etc.), and extended periods sitting is required.
* Experience with computer systems (Microsoft Office, Odoo, Google Suite, JIRA, PLM, etc.) preferred.
* Strong verbal and written skills are required to effectively communicate with team members and internal customers.
* Ability to complete and pass annual Motor Vehicle Record is required.
* Proficiency with direction/GPS use.
* A working knowledge of Department of Transportation (DOT) regulations is required.
* A Commercial Driver’s License (CDL) is a plus.
* Prior certification to transport hazardous materials within DOT regulations is a plus.
