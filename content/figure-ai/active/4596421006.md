Figure is an AI robotics company building general-purpose humanoid robots with human-level intelligence. Our robots are engineered to perform a wide range of tasks in commercial and industrial environments. We are based in San Jose, CA and require 5 days/week in-office collaboration. It's time to build.

We are looking for a Mechanical Engineer to own softgoods and plastic parts.

**Responsibilities:**

* Design parts in 3D CAD that satisfy load conditions, industrial design form factor, and durability requirements.
* Work closely with industrial designer to execute on the vision and form of the system
* Own the industrial design robot buck, keep up to date with latest files from our engineers and communicate with suppliers
* Design injection molded plastic parts with Class A surfaces
* Generate accurate 2D drawings using GD&T per ASME Y14.5.
* Inspect first articles, build test stands, and continuously support part on robot development platforms.

**Requirements:**

* 5+ years of experience in mechanical engineering
* Experience working with industrial design departments
* Bachelor’s or Master’s Degree in Mechanical, Biomechanical, Robotics, Mechatronics, or similar field
* Expert in using 3D CAD software to design parts with complex geometry
* Experience with high volume manufacturing processes specifically injection molding and die cutting
* Experience designing and building prototypes
* Ability to write and maintain clean documentation for design processes.

**Bonus Qualifications:**

* Experienced with simulation and analysis methodology (FEA, MATLAB etc.)
* Experience working with contract manufacturers.
* Experience with fabric manufacturing
* CATIA V6 Experience

The US base salary range for this full-time position is between $120,000 - $180,000 annually.
