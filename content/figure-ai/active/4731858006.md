Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are looking for a Materials Handler to support procurement, inventory management, and kitting for our commercial deployment and data operations teams.

We are seeking a talented and experienced Warehouse Lead to join our dynamic team. The Warehouse Lead will be responsible for directing the safety, productivity, and performance of warehouse personnel within the designated shift. To accomplish this, the job entails hands-on leadership of material handlers in inventory management, kitting, cycle counting, and general warehouse operations in direct support of commercial operations.

**Responsibilities**

* Lead material handlers with hands on work supporting daily operational workload and ensuring daily output goals are achieved.
* Hands-on support in daily warehouse processes, including fleet and deployment picking, kitting, material handling, supporting part requests, and general inventory management.
* Support daily cycle counting, inventory review, and necessary inventory management.
* Track, maintain, and oversee relevant records for inventory movement within the warehouse, including transactional movements within ERP, ensuring accuracy.
* Ensures accurate labeling of materials and documentation for inbound, storage, and outbound movement.
* Lead warehousing projects to ensure timely completion and compliance to requirements.
* Troubleshoot warehouse issues in daily operations to maintain smooth operations.
* Support the onboarding and continuous training of warehouse personnel within the daily operations to build capabilities in team.
* Communicate with management and cross-functional teams to identify status, gaps, and escalations for support.
* Uphold material handling procedures, work instructions, and SOPs to ensure compliance and efficiency.
* Promote a safe working environment by encouraging safe practices within operations and maintaining 5S standards.
* Communicate with management and cross-functional teams to identify status, gaps, and escalations for support.
* Other relevant tasks as assigned.

**Requirements**

* Minimum of 3 -5 years’ experience working within Supply Chain, Logistics, Manufacturing, Inventory Management, Distribution, or similar environment.
* Proven experience handling materials and leading teams in dynamic manufacturing or distribution environments with track record of success. Background in robotics manufacturing is a plus.
* Strong ability to manage multiple tasks and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment.
* Experience in cycle counting, discrepancy reconciliation, and root cause analysis
* Strong verbal and written communication skills, including presentation skills.
* Strong organizational and analytical/problem solving skills.
* Competent computer experience (Word, Excel, Power Point, SQL, Access, Power BI, Tableau, SAP, Oracle, Odoo, Slack, etc.)
* Familiarity with ERP or inventory management systems to record and track movements
* Detail oriented, multi-task, communication skills across entire organization (internal and external) with ability to collaboratively problem solve.
* Dependability, integrity, and a mindset oriented toward continuous improvement
