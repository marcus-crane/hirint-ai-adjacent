Figure is an AI Robotics company developing a general purpose humanoid. Our humanoid robot, Figure 03, is designed for commercial tasks and the home. We are based in San Jose, CA and require 5 days/week in-office collaboration. It’s time to build.

We are looking for a Security TPM to join the Security & Privacy team at Figure, focusing on governance and compliance workflows, and development of our overall security program.

### **Responsibilities**

* Support a team of highly talented security and privacy engineers, fostering growth, innovation, and collaboration
* Set the vision for data and access governance, ensuring alignment with Figure’s business objectives
* Develop the security & privacy program at Figure, and work with teams across the company to implement necessary controls
* Ensure that security, privacy and data protection principles are seamlessly integrated into Figure’s products, services, and operations, while aligning with organizational goals and regulatory requirements
* Establish and enforce data governance policies and frameworks to ensure data integrity, security, and compliance
* Review security & privacy requirements for relevant industry standards and support certification efforts, including SOC, NIST, ISO, and more

**Requirements**

* Ability to build and drive programs affecting all parts of a business
* Ability to effectively advocate for and influence strategic initiatives at the company level
* Experience collaborating with internal and external senior stakeholders at the company
* Strong technical grasp of security & privacy fundamentals, including enterprise controls, SDLC, machine learning, cryptography, data protection, network security, and more.
* Experience navigating global privacy compliance programs (GDPR, CCPA, DSA, etc.)
* Experience navigating industry certification, such as SOC-2, NIST, ISO 27001, ISO/TR 22100-4, IEC/TR 63074
* Bachelors in a technical field such as Computer Science or equivalent experience
* 8+ years of experience leading teams and large scale programs

The US base salary range for this full-time position is between $150,000 - $350,000 annually.
