Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are looking for an Accounting Manager, with a focus on Inventory. This role will own the inventory and cost accounting, including building processes and deploying systems from end to end.

**Responsibilities:**

Cost Accounting and Inventory Management

* Track and reconcile inventory across raw materials, subassemblies, WIP, and finished goods.
* Manage data integrity between ERP (NetSuite) and operational systems (MES, PLM, WMS). Resolve discrepancies between quantities, costs, and system records across locations or systems (e.g., NetSuite vs. MES).
* Maintain cost model, including overhead allocation and labor/machine rates.
* Analyze purchase price variances (PPV), material usage variances, and production inefficiencies to support margin analysis and COGS reporting.

Capital Asset and Fixed Asset Accounting

* Maintain fixed asset schedules and manage capitalization policies for PP&E.
* Ensure proper classification between R&D prototypes vs. capitalizable production assets.
* Track depreciation and coordinate asset lifecycle management.

General accounting

* Lead the automation of end-to-end transaction workflows and balance sheet reconciliations.
* Support with growing volume of transactions (accounting syncs, reimbursements, etc)
* Prepare technical accounting memos.

Lease accounting management.

* Technical accounting analysis for new leases
* Manage monthly lease accounting reconciliation and entries.

Tax

* Support external reporting requirements for government grants or incentives.
* Assist with quarterly and annual compliance.

Audit Readiness

* Lead annual external financial audits.

**Requirements:**

* Education: Bachelor’s in Accounting, Finance, or a related field. CPA preferred.
* Experience: 8+ years in accounting, including 5+ years in cost and inventory accounting at a hardware or manufacturing company. Prior experience as a manager at a high-growth company is a plus.
* Technical accounting: Deep expertise of US GAAP for inventory accounting (ASC 330), fixed assets (ASC 360), leases (ASC 842), and of the R&D vs. capitalizable cost distinction.
* Systems: Hands-on NetSuite experience, ideally including inventory and manufacturing modules. Experience with systems integration and reconciling ERP data against MES, PLM, and WMS systems.
* Analytical Skills: Strong attention to detail, organizational skills, and a knack for identifying and resolving discrepancies.
* Process building: Track record of designing and documenting controls and processes from scratch in a fast-scaling environment.
* Communication: Exceptional verbal and written communication skills for successful collaboration with vendors and internal stakeholders.
