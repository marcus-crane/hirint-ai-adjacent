Figure is an AI robotics company developing autonomous general-purpose humanoid robots. The goal of the company is to ship humanoid robots with human level intelligence. Its robots are engineered to perform a variety of tasks in the home and commercial markets. Figure is headquartered in San Jose, CA.

We are looking for a Systems Integration Engineer to lead the bring-up, validation, and triage of our custom hand.

**Responsibilities:**

* Coordinate across mechanical, electrical, firmware, and technician teams to plan and execute first article bring-ups.
* Build and oversee the execution of benchtop validation.
* Plan and execute integration into the full robot stack and subsequent on-robot validation.
* Develop and maintain detailed documentation of integration processes, test plans, and validation results.
* Perpetually respond to on-robot failures, including coordinating failure analysis and driving solutions from conception to working on robot.
* Drive diagnostics needs to speed up root-cause analysis and on-robot health checks.

**Requirements:**

* Relevant experience integrating custom mechatronic systems for real products.
* Depth in one or more of the following areas: electrical, firmware, controls, or mechanical.
* Excellent communication and planning skills.
* Hands-on, highly technical approach to integration.

**Bonus Qualifications:**

* Prior experience working robotic manipulators.
* Prior experience working with humanoids.
* Prior leadership experience.

The US base salary range for this full-time position is $170,000 to $250,000 per year.
