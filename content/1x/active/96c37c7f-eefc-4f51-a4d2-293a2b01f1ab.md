# About 1X

We’re building humanoid robots that work in the home—doing the chores, handling the tasks, and giving people their time back. Simple, but it’s not.

To do this right, we have to solve robotics, AI, and manufacturing at the same time, at scale, in a form factor that has to be safe enough to live with your family. If you’re inspired by this, you’ll thrive here. We’ve been at this since 2014, and we’re at the point where the hardest problems are behind us and the hardest work is in front of us.

NEO is our flagship—a home robot designed to move, learn, and operate in the real world alongside real people. We’re not demoing it—we’re shipping it.

If you’ve spent your career working on problems that matter and want to see them actually reach the world, this is that moment. We’re scaling with intention, and we need people who want to build something that will fundamentally change how humans spend their time.

# About the Team

The People team is responsible for building the organization that will build the future of humanoid robotics. We partner closely with leaders across the company to strengthen leadership, develop high-performing teams, and build the people programs and organizational foundations needed to support rapid growth.

People plays a critical role in 1X's ability to scale. The team works with urgency, sound judgment, and a builder's mindset to ensure our leaders and employees have the structure, support, and clarity needed to do the best work of their careers.

# Your Charter

The People team is responsible for helping 1X scale its workforce, leadership capability, and organizational effectiveness as the company grows from advanced product development into global production. We partner directly with leaders to build high-performing organizations, develop talent, strengthen leadership, and create the people programs that enable the business to scale.

As the HR Business Partner supporting G&A and Marketing, you will serve as the strategic People partner to corporate functions including Finance, Legal, People, Marketing, Workplace, and other General & Administrative teams. You will work alongside leaders to shape organizational design, coach managers, build scalable people programs, and help these organizations grow while maintaining a high-performing culture.
