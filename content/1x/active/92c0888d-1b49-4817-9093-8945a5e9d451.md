To do this right, we have to solve robotics, AI, manufacturing — at the same time, at scale, in a form factor that has to be safe enough to live with your family. # **About the Team**

The Manufacturing Engineering team owns how NEO gets built — from prototype through volume production. We design the processes, fixtures, and standards that turn engineering intent into a manufacturable reality, and we partner across design, quality, NPI, supply chain, and the production floor to make sure what leaves the line is precise, repeatable, and reliable. Factory controls is the connective tissue that makes the line run — the automation, PLCs, and data systems that keep production moving and give us the visibility we need to improve it.

# **Your Charter**

You’ll own factory controls and automation across the San Carlos manufacturing floor. As the Senior Controls Engineer, you spec and build controls systems in-house, and you deploy, commission, and optimize the automated equipment and PLC-driven systems that production runs on. You build out the PLCs and controls integration that bring new equipment online, and you stand up the factory SCADA and historian backbone that turns what happens on the line into information we can share broadly across the organization. You also define the controls standards that the rest of the facility is built to. Test station design lives with our Manufacturing Test team; you partner with them on controls integration. This is where mechanical process and software come together — the systems you stand up are what make the difference between a line that runs and a line we can actually improve.

# **Key Outcomes**

* Spec and build controls systems in-house — select sensors, actuators, PLCs, I/O, drives, and networking components, and bring the resulting systems from concept through build, deployment, and production use
* Deploy, commission, and optimize automated equipment and PLC-driven stations across the San Carlos production areas — getting new equipment online, dialed in, supported through its lifecycle, and continuously improved as production needs evolve
* Build and maintain the PLC programs and controls integration that support incoming equipment — from vendor-supplied automation through in-house builds and third-party integrators
* Stand up and maintain the factory SCADA and historian stack — define what gets captured, how it’s structured, and how that information is shared broadly across the organization
* Define and maintain the controls standards for the facility — PLC code structure, naming conventions, network architecture, HMI patterns, version control, and documentation practices that every piece of equipment on the floor is held to
* Build and support the scripting, services, and integrations that connect factory equipment to our data systems, MES, and ERP
* Own the reliability of deployed controls systems: uptime, troubleshooting, change management, and the standards that keep them maintainable as the floor scales
* Partner with the Manufacturing Test team on controls integration for test stations, and with ME and production on automation strategy, equipment selection, and commissioning plans

# **Key Competencies**

* Fluent in PLC programming and industrial controls architecture — comfortable taking vendor equipment, in-house builds, and third-party integrations from install through commissioning to long-term support
* Strong hands-on instinct for speccing and building controls systems — selects the right sensor, actuator, drive, or PLC for the job and knows why the alternatives don’t fit
* Strong software instincts outside the PLC — scripts, services, and small applications that glue factory systems together and move data where it needs to go
* Systems thinker who understands how PLCs, equipment, sensors, networks, data systems, and MES/ERP fit together, and who designs for the whole picture rather than one box at a time
* Standards-setter — writes the conventions, templates, and documentation that make the next deployment faster and the next engineer’s job easier
* Builder’s mindset: ships working systems, documents them well, and comes back to improve them rather than leaving them to drift
* Comfortable in a Linux environment and on the command line — reads logs, writes scripts, and troubleshoots at the OS level
* Clear communicator who can work across ME, Manufacturing Test, IT, production, and external integrators without losing the thread

# **Minimum Requirements**

* 5+ years of controls or automation engineering experience in a manufacturing or production environment, with a track record of deploying, commissioning, and supporting automated equipment through its lifecycle
* Demonstrated experience speccing and building controls systems in-house, including sensor and component selection, electrical design, panel build, and bring-up
* Strong PLC programming experience, with Siemens preferred (TIA Portal / Step 7); comparable experience on other major platforms (Rockwell, Beckhoff, etc.) considered
* Proficiency in Python and/or C++ for scripting, data acquisition, and system integration; other languages considered where the fundamentals are strong
* Comfort working in Linux environments — command line, scripting, services, basic sysadmin
* Working experience with industrial networking, including Ethernet/IP and EtherCAT; able to design, configure, and troubleshoot at the network level
* Experience designing and deploying SCADA, historian, or factory data logging systems in a production environment
* Experience defining or significantly contributing to controls standards (code structure, naming, network architecture, HMI patterns, documentation) across multiple pieces of equipment
* Strong documentation habits across code, electrical schematics, network diagrams, and operator-facing HMIs
* Clear written and verbal communication, and the ability to partner across ME, Manufacturing Test, IT, production, and external vendors
