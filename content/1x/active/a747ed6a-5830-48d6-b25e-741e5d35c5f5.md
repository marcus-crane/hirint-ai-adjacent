Role Name: Recruiting Coordinator

Department: Talent

Location: San Carlos (onsite, 5 days a week)

## **About 1X**

We're building humanoid robots that work in home - doing the chores, handling the tasks, and giving people their time back. Simple, but it's not.

To do this right, we have to solve robotics, AI, manufacturing - at the same time, at scale, in a form factor that has to be safe enough to live with your family. If you're inspired by this, you'll thrive here. We've been at this since 2014 and we're at the point where the hard problems are behind us and the *hard work* is in front of us.

NEO is our flagship - a general purpose robot designed to move, learn, and operate in the real world alongside real people. We're not demoing it - we're shipping it. We're excited to meet you, if this excites you.

If you've spent your career working on problems that matter and want to see them actually reach the world - this is that moment. We're scaling, we're hiring with intention, and we need people who want to build something that will genuinely change how humans spend their time - safely creating abundance for all.

## **About the Team:**

The Talent team hires the people who build NEO: robotics, AI, manufacturing, and the teams that support them. Recruiting Coordination is the operating layer of that work. Every interview, every candidate touchpoint, and every record in Ashby runs through this team, so when coordination is excellent, 1X hires faster and candidates leave every conversation wanting to work here.

## **Your Charter:**

The mission of this role is to make interview scheduling fast, accurate, and seamless for every candidate in 1X's high-volume hiring. You own the day-to-day scheduling for a set of roles across engineering, hardware and manufacturing, and G&A, from the first phone screen through the onsite. You run the team's coordination playbook, working alongside our Lead Recruiting Coordinators, who own the standard and the process. When something in the process slows you down, you flag it and help fix it. Hiring speed is on 1X's critical path, and every interview booked quickly and correctly moves NEO forward.

## **Key Outcomes:**

* Own interview scheduling for your assigned roles, from phone screens to full onsite panels, booked quickly and without errors, double-bookings, or missing details.
* Deliver a smooth candidate experience at every touchpoint, with timely confirmations, clear interview details, and well-run onsite days at our San Carlos headquarters.
* Resolve reschedules and last-minute conflicts quickly, keeping candidates, interviewers, and recruiters informed so no interview falls through the cracks.

## **Key Competencies:**

* Highly organized and detail-oriented; manages a high volume of interviews across many calendars at once without dropping anything.
* Stays calm and resourceful when a schedule changes at the last minute, and finds a fix before escalating.
* Communicates clearly and promptly with candidates, interviewers, and recruiters, in writing and live.
* Owns every scheduling request through to done, without needing reminders.
* Handles confidential candidate information with accuracy and discretion.

## **Minimum Requirements:**

* 2+ years of recruiting coordination experience, including interview scheduling in a high-volume environment
* Demonstrated ability to schedule multi-interviewer panels and onsite interviews across busy calendars
* Experience with an applicant tracking system as the system of record for hiring

## **Preferred Skills:**

* Experience in Ashby
* Familiarity with interview scheduling tools such as GoodTime, ModernLoop, or built-in ATS scheduling automation
* Experience in coordinating in-person onsite interview days, including candidate arrival and logistics
* Familiarity with hiring for hardware, robotics, manufacturing, or AI teams
* Exposure to improving a scheduling workflow or building candidate communication templates

## **What does a successful 1X Team Member look like:**

Team members at 1X who thrive here are builders. They move fast, own their work completely, and treat time like it's the one thing you can't get back - because it is. They say what they mean, finish what they start, and hold themselves to a standard before anyone has to ask. We push each other to be better, and we do it with honesty and respect.
