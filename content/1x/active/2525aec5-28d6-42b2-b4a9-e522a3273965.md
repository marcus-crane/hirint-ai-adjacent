# **About the Team**

The Actuators Manufacturing team builds the processes and production systems behind the actuators that move NEO. Actuators are what let a humanoid move precisely and safely around people, so the reliability and cost of every unit we build sets the limit on how many robots reach homes. We're writing the production playbook as we go, side by side with design, quality, and supply chain.

# **Your Charter**

The mission of this role is to make 1X actuators producible at scale: reliably, repeatably, and at a cost that lets NEO reach homes. You'll own critical processes from prototype through production, including machining, assembly, inspection, and validation. Many of these problems haven't been solved before. You'll question convention, test ideas quickly, learn from the floor, and turn what works into dependable production systems. The standard is high, the feedback is physical, and no one is above picking up a tool.

# **Key Outcomes**

* Own actuator manufacturing processes from prototype builds through production release, and be accountable for safety, first-pass yield, quality, and throughput in your areas.
* Partner with design engineering as the manufacturing voice in design reviews, giving DFM, DFA, and DFT feedback, checking tolerances against demonstrated process capability, and driving the changes that make actuators producible at volume.
* Build and qualify the equipment, fixtures, tooling, gauging, and automated workstations for actuator assembly, using DOE, MSA/GR&R, SPC, and capability studies to prove each process before release.
* Deliver end-of-line functional test and traceability, so every actuator ships with station-level pass/fail data tied to its full process history.
* Drive down cost, cycle time, and scrap through automation, line balancing, and root-cause corrective action, backed by PFMEAs, control plans, and work instructions that run the floor.

# **Key Competencies**

* Hands-on builder who develops methods at the station alongside technicians and debugs on the floor, not from a desk.
* Rigorous with data, using DOE, SPC, capability studies, yield, and OEE to prove a change worked.
* Structured problem solver who gets to true root cause under time pressure and closes the loop with controls that hold.
* Strong DFM, DFA, and tolerance instincts, with the credibility to change actuator design early.
* Owns outcomes end to end and communicates directly across design, quality, controls, supply chain, and production.
