# About the Team

The Hardware Engineering team designs and delivers the electromechanical systems that allow NEO to move, manipulate, and interact safely with the world. From structural components and precision mechanisms to complete robotic subsystems, the team owns hardware development from concept through production.

Working closely with electrical, controls, firmware, manufacturing, and robotics engineers, you'll help transform ambitious ideas into robust, manufacturable products that operate reliably in real-world environments.

# Your Charter

Design and develop the thermal systems that enable NEO’s hands to operate reliably across demanding operating conditions.

You will develop active and passive thermal-management solutions for highly integrated electromechanical systems, balancing thermal performance with packaging, power, acoustics, reliability, manufacturability, and cost.

You will use first-principles analysis, prototyping and physical testing to understand system behavior, optimize thermal designs, and validate solutions in hardware.

# Key Outcomes

* Design and optimize active and passive thermal-management systems for NEO’s hands, from early concepts through detailed design, prototyping, validation, and production release
* Use first-principles thermal analysis, experimental data, and simulation results to select and size components, evaluate design trade-offs, and optimize system performance
* Design, build, and instrument prototypes and test setups to evaluate thermal concepts and validate performance under representative operating conditions
* Develop thermal architectures and solutions that effectively manage heat across motors, electronics, structures, materials, and the surrounding environment
* Work closely with CFD and simulation engineers to evaluate design concepts and correlate simulation models with physical test data
* Work closely with mechanical, materials, and electrical engineers to integrate thermal solutions within tight packaging, structural, electrical, and manufacturing constraints
