**About the Team:**

The Test & Validation team makes sure NEO can withstand the real-world conditions it will encounter in homes. We build and operate the test infrastructure, fixtures, and validation systems that allow engineering teams to understand how the robot performs, where it fails, and what needs to improve before it reaches customers.

As a Test Fixture Design Engineer, you’ll work at the intersection of mechanical design, test engineering, and hands-on hardware. You’ll design the fixtures that make rigorous, repeatable testing possible - from understanding the device under test and defining load paths to designing, manufacturing, assembling, and iterating the final hardware in the lab.

**Your Charter:**

Design and deliver the mechanical test fixtures that enable safe, accurate, and repeatable validation of NEO and its critical subsystems.

You’ll own fixture development end-to-end, from understanding the test objective and developing the initial concept through CAD, drawings, fabrication, assembly, test-cell installation, and iteration based on real-world test results. Your designs should be robust and easy to manufacture, while minimizing unnecessary complexity, cost, and test-induced artifacts.

**Key Outcomes:**

* Deliver production-ready mechanical test fixtures on schedule that enable Test & Validation to execute critical validation and reliability testing across NEO hardware.
* Develop fixture designs that provide the required strength, stiffness, accuracy, repeatability, and safety without introducing unwanted constraints or artifacts into test results.
* Own complete fixture design packages, including 3D CAD, 2D drawings, materials, tolerances, hardware, interfaces, and assembly requirements.
* Drive successful manufacturing handoff by working directly with internal fabrication teams and external vendors to resolve DFM issues, manage quotes and lead times, and deliver hardware to test schedules.
* Bring fixtures from CAD into the lab through hands-on assembly, fit checks, installation, and test setup, rapidly iterating designs based on test-cell feedback.

**Key Competencies:**

* Strong mechanical designer who can translate complex test requirements, interfaces, loads, and constraints into simple, robust fixture designs.
* Excellent CAD and engineering drawing skills with a strong understanding of tolerancing, materials, mechanical interfaces, and hardware.
* Practical understanding of manufacturing processes and DFM, with the judgment to balance performance, cost, lead time, and ease of assembly.
* Hands-on engineer who enjoys taking designs from CAD through fabrication and into a working test environment.
* Strong problem solver who can work from first principles and collaborate closely with Test, Design, Manufacturing, and external vendors.

**Minimum Requirements:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering or a closely related engineering discipline.
* 2-4 years of experience designing mechanical test fixtures, assembly fixtures, tooling, or similar custom hardware.
* Strong 3D CAD and 2D engineering drawing skills, with experience designing parts and assemblies for machining, fabrication, or rapid prototyping.
* Experience working with internal machine shops or external manufacturing vendors to turn designs into physical hardware.
* Practical understanding of mechanical testing, including loads, load paths, fixturing, interfaces, and safe test setup.

**Preferred Skills:**

* Experience designing fixtures for robotics, electromechanical systems, automotive, aerospace, consumer hardware, or other complex physical products.
* Experience using hand calculations or FEA to evaluate fixture strength, stiffness, deflection, or dynamic behavior.
* Hands-on experience assembling and bringing up test fixtures in a laboratory or engineering environment.
* Experience integrating sensors, actuators, wiring, or instrumentation into mechanical test fixtures.
* Experience with vibration, mechanical actuation, thermal, or environmental testing.
