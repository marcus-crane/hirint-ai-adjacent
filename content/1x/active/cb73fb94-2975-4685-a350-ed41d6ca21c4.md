## About the Team

The Quality Engineering team enables 1X to scale complex humanoid robot hardware with speed, confidence, and strong manufacturing discipline. We partner across Design Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Supply Chain, Software, and Operations to build quality into the product and production system from the beginning.

As we scale NEO, the team owns quality across new product introduction, production, incoming inspection, MRB, and quality systems. Our goal is to solve quality problems at the source while building practical systems that improve product reliability without slowing engineering or production.

## Your Charter

Build and lead a high-performing Quality Engineering organization that establishes quality from product design through production. You will own quality planning, manufacturing controls, production quality, incoming inspection, MRB, and quality system development while driving technical problem-solving across critical robot systems.

Your charter is to translate design intent into robust manufacturing and inspection controls, resolve quality issues at their source, and build a scalable quality organization that supports 1X’s path to production.

## Key Outcomes

* Build and lead the Quality Engineering organization, including Quality Engineers and the Quality Technician Supervisor, with clear ownership across NPI, production quality, incoming inspection, MRB, and quality systems.
* Establish quality planning for new products by translating design requirements into measurable manufacturing controls, inspection strategies, control plans, and acceptance criteria.
* Lead PFMEA and control plan development with Design and Manufacturing Engineering to identify critical risks and establish effective prevention, detection, and reaction controls.
* Drive timely resolution of significant production and incoming quality issues through containment, structured root cause analysis, corrective action, and verification of durable fixes.
* Develop and improve practical Quality Management Systems and MRB processes that establish traceability, disciplined decision-making, and useful quality data without unnecessary bureaucracy.
* Partner with Design, Manufacturing, Software, Supply Chain, and Operations to improve product and process quality across motors, batteries, electronics, structural assemblies, and other critical robot systems.
* Use inspection results, metrology, defect trends, and production feedback to prioritize systemic improvements, communicate quality performance to leadership, and raise the quality bar as production scales.

## Key Competencies

* Technical and hands-on quality engineering leader who can build an organization while remaining close to complex manufacturing and product quality problems.
* Strong expertise in NPI quality planning, PFMEA, control plans, inspection strategies, and manufacturing process controls.
* Proven ability to lead structured problem-solving, containment, root cause analysis, and corrective action across cross-functional engineering and production teams.
* Strong understanding of electromechanical hardware, including motors, batteries, electronics, and structural components, and the quality controls used to manufacture and inspect them.
* First-principles approach to building quality systems that are practical, scalable, and aligned with engineering and production needs.
* Effective cross-functional leader who can influence Design Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Supply Chain, Software, Operations, and senior leadership.
* Strong operational judgment, ownership, and ability to prioritize quality risks in a fast-moving production environment.
