## **About 1X**

We're building humanoid robots that work in home - doing the chores, handling the tasks, and giving people their time back. Simple, but it's not.

To do this right, we have to solve robotics, AI, manufacturing - at the same time, at scale, in a form factor that has to be safe enough to live with your family. If you're inspired by this, you'll thrive here. We've been at this since 2014 and we're at the point where the hard problems are behind us and the *hard work* is in front of us.

NEO is our flagship - a home robot designed to move, learn, and operate in the real world alongside real people. We're not demoing it - we're shipping it. We're excited to meet you, if this excites you.

If you've spent your career working on problems that matter and want to see them actually reach the world - this is that moment. We're scaling, we're hiring with intention, and we need people who want to build something that will genuinely change how humans spend their time - safely creating abundance for all.

## **About the Team**

The People team builds the organization that builds NEO. We work alongside leaders across 1X so teams can hire well, grow their people, and keep moving fast as the company scales. This role is embedded in our software engineering org, where the pace is high and the work is getting the robot to ship.

## **Your Charter**

The mission of this role is to be the embedded HR partner to 1X's software engineering org, so its managers get the coaching and support they need and its senior leader can stay focused on building.

You report to our Director of HR, who stays the main HR contact for the org's senior leader. You own the partnership with everyone at scale. You sit in on team meetings, meet regularly with managers and directors, coach them through hard conversations, and bring what you hear back to HR leadership as clear themes and recommendations. The org is about 100 people today and growing. When the business needs it, you flex to support other groups.

## **Key Outcomes**

* Own a weekly or biweekly rhythm with the org's managers and directors, so people issues come up early and reach HR leadership with a recommendation attached.
* Coach engineering managers through performance conversations, feedback, and difficult situations, so these are handled well by the managers themselves and don't escalate to the org's senior leader.
* Lead employee relations matters and investigations for the org, with outcomes that are fair, well documented, and compliant with California employment law.
* Partner with Talent Acquisition as the org's hiring liaison, so headcount plans, hiring priorities, and onboarding for new managers keep pace as the team grows.
* Deliver performance, promotion, and compensation cycles for the org on time, with managers prepared for calibration and the conversations that follow.

## **Key Competencies**

* Engineering fluency: understands how software teams and engineering managers work, and earns their credibility quickly.
* Manager coaching: helps managers own hard conversations instead of taking those conversations over.
* Org listening: builds trust across a team, hears things early, and turns them into clear themes and next steps.
* Startup execution: gets things done with little process and no big HR support structure, and is comfortable shifting to where the work is.
* Sound judgment and discretion on sensitive employee matters.

## **Minimum Requirements**

* 5+ years of HR experience, including 3+ years as an embedded HR Business Partner to a defined client group.
* Experience partnering with engineering, software, or other technical teams at a technology company.
* Demonstrated ability to coach managers through performance management and to lead employee relations investigations, with working knowledge of California employment law.
* Ability to work onsite in San Carlos, CA.

## **Preferred Skills**

* Experience at a startup or high-growth technology company, especially in robotics, hardware, or AI.
* Experience supporting a team through a period of fast hiring alongside a recruiting team.
* Familiarity with using engagement, attrition, and performance data to spot trends and inform leaders.
* PHR, SPHR, SHRM-CP, or SHRM-SCP certification.
