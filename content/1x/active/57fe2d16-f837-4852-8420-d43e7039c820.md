# **About 1X**

We’re building humanoid robots that work in any space humans exist with the mission to create an abundance of labor. Simple, but it’s not.   
  
To do this right, we have to solve robotics, AI, software, hardware, manufacturing and operations at the same time, at scale, in a form factor that has to be safe. If you’re inspired by this, you’ll thrive here. We’ve been at this since 2014 and we’re at the point where the hard problems are behind us and the *hard work* is in front of us.   
  
NEO is our flagship - a general purpose robot designed to move, learn, and operate in the real world alongside real people. We’re not demoing it, we're shipping it. We’re excited to meet you, if this excites you.   
  
If you’ve spent your career working on problems that matter and want to see them actually reach the world - this is that moment. We’re scaling, we’re hiring with intention, and we need people who want to build something that will genuinely change how humans spend their time safely creating abundance for all.

# **About the Team**

The Manufacturing team turns NEO from a prototype into a product we can build reliably, repeatedly, and at volume. We design and run the assembly, material handling, test, and end-of-line processes at our Hayward facility. How well we do this decides how many robots reach customers, and how good they are when they arrive.

# **Your Charter**

Design, deploy, and scale the automation systems that produce NEO efficiently, reliably, and safely. This role is on the critical path from prototype builds to scaled production. Every gain in cycle time, quality, and uptime on the line means more robots shipped.

# **Key Outcomes**

* Production automation equipment and robotic cells for assembly, joining, fastening, dispensing, testing, and packaging are designed, deployed, and running reliably at Hayward
* Cycle time goes down and quality and safety go up on every process you automate
* Sensors, actuators, PLCs, and vision systems are integrated with the manufacturing, design, and controls teams into systems that hit throughput and reliability targets
* Equipment failures are traced to root cause and fixed so they do not recur
* Production capacity grows through continuous improvement of automation hardware, tooling, and processes
* Every system you own is documented: schematics, layouts, code, operating procedures, and maintenance schedules
* Each new robot design has a scalable automation strategy before it reaches the line

# **Key Competencies**

* Has designed, deployed, and kept robotic cells running in real production, not only in a lab
* Works across mechanical, electrical, and controls well enough to own a system end to end
* Makes PLCs, vision, sensors, and actuators work together as one reliable system
* Traces every failure to its root cause and fixes it so it does not come back
* Designs for throughput and repeatability from the first version, including for products that are still changing
* Documents work so others can run and maintain it without them

# **Minimum Requirements**

* 9+ years designing, deploying, and maintaining production automation equipment and robotic systems
* Strong background in mechanical, electrical, or controls engineering applied to automation hardware
* Proficiency with PLCs, vision systems, sensors, and actuators, and with integrating them into working systems
* Strong troubleshooting skills and hands-on root cause analysis of hardware and system failures
* Experience documenting automation systems: schematics, layouts, operating procedures, and maintenance schedules
* Experience supporting new product introduction with automation strategies

# **Preferred Skills**

* Experience writing work instructions and process sequences for high-reliability, high-throughput production lines
* Track record of process and hardware improvements that measurably increased production capacity
