# **About the Team**

The Robot Interconnect and Tendon Systems team develops the soft goods, harnessing, tendon routing, and compliant mechanical systems that enable reliable motion, durability, and integration across humanoid robotic platforms.

A key focus area for the team is the development of foot systems that must withstand demanding real-world loading, wear, environmental exposure, and continuous robotic operation while remaining lightweight, durable, and manufacturable at scale.

You’ll work closely with mechanical, industrial design, manufacturing, and integration teams to rapidly prototype, test, and refine assemblies that are critical to robot mobility, reliability, and long-term performance.

# **Your Charter**

Develop and validate the foot systems that connect the robot to the world.

You will design, prototype, integrate, and test foot soft goods assemblies, tendon interfaces, harnessing systems, and compliant mechanical structures that enable robust operation in real-world environments. Your work will focus on balancing durability, performance, manufacturability, and rapid iteration while helping drive these systems from early prototypes to production-ready designs.

As a Mechanical Engineer on the Foot Systems team, your work will directly impact robot mobility, reliability, serviceability, and overall product performance.

# **Key Outcomes**

* Design and release foot assemblies, harnesses, tendon routing systems, and compliant structures that meet durability, integration, and manufacturability requirements
* Execute structured testing and validation efforts that identify wear patterns, failure modes, and reliability risks, resulting in measurable design improvements
* Develop and maintain high-quality CAD models, engineering drawings, BOMs, and documentation that support both prototype and production builds
* Partner effectively with suppliers, manufacturing teams, and cross-functional engineering groups to improve assembly quality, repeatability, and production readiness
* Support rapid hardware iteration cycles while maintaining a high standard of engineering rigor and execution quality

# **Key Competencies**

* Strong hands-on engineering capability with experience building, testing, and iterating physical hardware systems
* Solid understanding of soft goods construction methods, textiles, harness systems, sewn assemblies, wearable products, or tendon-driven mechanisms
* Strong mechanical intuition and ability to quickly diagnose integration, wear, durability, and reliability issues
* Ability to thrive in fast-moving environments with evolving technical requirements and aggressive development timelines
* Strong communication and collaboration skills across design, manufacturing, supplier, and engineering teams
