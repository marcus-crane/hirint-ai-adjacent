# About the Team

The Power Systems team develops the electrical architectures and hardware that store, convert, distribute, and manage energy throughout NEO. We design and integrate battery management systems, power distribution electronics, DC-DC converters, chargers, and protection systems that enable safe, reliable, and efficient robot operation.

Our work spans analog and power electronics, high-current distribution, power conversion, battery interfaces, electromagnetic compatibility, sensing, protection, and system-level electrical integration.

We collaborate closely with Firmware, Mechanical Engineering, Systems Engineering, Manufacturing, and Reliability teams to develop power electronics that meet demanding performance, efficiency, thermal, safety, and manufacturing requirements.

As NEO moves from development into scaled production, our team is responsible for delivering robust, manufacturable power systems that operate reliably across real-world conditions.

# Your Charter

**Own the architecture, design, and development of the power electronics that enable NEO to operate safely and reliably.**

As a Staff Electrical Engineer on the Power Systems team, you will provide technical leadership across battery management systems, power distribution, DC-DC conversion, and charging hardware.

You will own complex power electronics hardware from initial architecture and requirements definition through detailed design, simulation, prototype bring-up, validation, production release, and sustaining engineering.

Your work will involve designing and debugging high-performance power electronics, solving complex system-level integration challenges, and ensuring electrical architectures meet efficiency, power density, thermal, reliability, and safety requirements.

You will also lead EMI/EMC investigations and mitigation efforts, establish engineering practices, influence power system architecture, and mentor engineers while remaining deeply hands-on in hardware design and troubleshooting.

# Key Outcomes

* Define and drive power electronics architectures across battery management systems, power distribution, DC-DC converters, and charging systems.
* Own the full hardware development lifecycle, including requirements definition, circuit design, schematic capture, PCB layout review, prototype bring-up, validation, production release, and sustaining improvements.
* Develop robust BMS hardware supporting cell monitoring, current and temperature sensing, cell balancing, diagnostics, and battery protection.
* Design high-current power distribution systems incorporating electronic fuses, load switching, inrush current control, power sequencing, and fault isolation.
* Develop efficient, high-power-density DC-DC converters and charger power stages that meet electrical, thermal, and system-level performance requirements.
* Analyze system interactions involving peak power loads, regenerative energy, bus transients, startup and shutdown sequences, and coordinated protection functions.
* Perform detailed circuit analysis, worst-case analysis, component derating, design reviews, and failure-mode assessments.
* Lead EMI/EMC design, investigation, and debugging across PCB, subsystem, and integrated robot levels.
* Identify and resolve conducted and radiated emissions, common-mode and differential-mode noise, grounding issues, parasitic coupling, and interference through cables and enclosures.
* Develop and validate EMI mitigation strategies involving PCB layout, filtering, shielding, grounding, and component selection.
* Lead hardware bring-up, bench characterization, design verification, pre-compliance testing, and corrective action validation.
* Partner with Firmware, Mechanical Engineering, Systems Engineering, Manufacturing, and Supply Chain teams to resolve complex integration challenges.
* Drive Design for Manufacturability (DFM), Design for Test (DFT), component qualification, production testing, and manufacturing readiness.
* Investigate prototype, production, and field failures, perform root-cause analysis, and implement verified design improvements.
* Establish power electronics design standards, technical best practices, and validation methodologies across the engineering organization.
* Provide technical leadership and mentorship while driving complex cross-functional engineering decisions.
