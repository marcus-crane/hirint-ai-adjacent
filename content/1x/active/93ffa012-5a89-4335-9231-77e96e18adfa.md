**About the Team:**

The Robotics Deployment team is responsible for the health, reliability, and performance of NEO robots operating outside the lab. Working across robotics, hardware, firmware, and software, the team diagnoses field issues, develops fleet monitoring and diagnostic capabilities, and feeds real-world performance data back into product development.

**Your Charter:** Build and scale the diagnostic capabilities, tools, and processes that keep deployed NEO robots healthy and reliable. Own the technical feedback loop between deployment operations and engineering, turning real-world robot performance and field failures into actionable insights, root-cause investigations, and product improvements.

**Key Outcomes:**

* Maintain the health and reliability of the deployed NEO fleet through effective diagnostics, troubleshooting, and issue resolution across hardware and software systems.
* Develop and maintain robust, production-quality diagnostic tools for robot health monitoring, data collection, and fleet observability.
* Translate live, historical, and commanded-motion diagnostic data into actionable insights that accelerate issue identification and resolution.
* Drive root-cause analysis of field failures and coordinate cross-functional resolution with hardware, firmware, and software engineering teams.
* Improve fleet monitoring and diagnostic capabilities to support increasing deployment volume and operational scale.
* Establish clear runbooks, troubleshooting workflows, and deployment processes that improve consistency and reduce time to resolution.

**Key Competencies:**

* Strong software engineering fundamentals with the ability to build reliable, well-tested tools for complex robotic systems.
* Hands-on robotics knowledge across kinematics, controls, sensors, actuators, and system integration.
* Strong debugging and troubleshooting skills across interconnected hardware, software, and embedded systems.
* Ability to interpret robot logs, telemetry, and diagnostic data to identify root causes and drive corrective action.
* Comfortable working cross-functionally with engineering and deployment teams to resolve complex technical issues.
* Builder mentality with the ability to develop scalable tools and processes in a fast-moving environment.

**Minimum Requirements:**

* 4+ years of relevant industry experience with a BS in Robotics, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field; OR 2+ years with an MS; equivalent practical experience also considered.
* Experience writing production-quality software for robotic, embedded, or electromechanical systems.
* Strong fundamentals in robotics, including kinematics, controls, sensors, and actuators.
* Hands-on experience debugging complex hardware and software systems.
* Experience working in Linux environments and with embedded or real-time systems.
* Willingness to travel up to 25% to deployment sites.

**Preferred Skills:**

* Experience deploying or supporting robotic systems at scale, including autonomous vehicles, mobile robots, or humanoids.
* Familiarity with robotics middleware and tooling such as ROS, logging frameworks, and data visualization tools.
* Experience with cloud infrastructure, including data storage, compute, and remote access.
* Experience building fleet monitoring, observability, or large-scale diagnostics systems.
* Exposure to field service, on-site deployment, or customer-facing technical support environments.
