**Role Name:** CNC Machine Park Intern

**Department:** Manufacturing | Hardware

**Location:** Hayward, CA

## **About 1X**

We’re building humanoid robots that work in home — doing the chores, handling the tasks, and giving people their time back. Simple, but it’s not.

To do this right, we have to solve robotics, AI, manufacturing — at the same time, at scale, in a form factor that has to be safe enough to live with your family. If you’re inspired by this, you’ll thrive here. We’ve been at this since 2014 and we’re at the point where the hard problems are behind us and the hard work is in front of us.

NEO is our flagship — a home robot designed to move, learn, and operate in the real world alongside real people. We’re not demoing it — we’re shipping it. We’re excited to meet you, if this excites you.

If you’ve spent your career working on problems that matter and want to see them actually reach the world — this is that moment. We’re scaling, we’re hiring with intention, and we need people who want to build something that will genuinely change how humans spend their time — safely creating abundance for all.

## **About the Team**

Our Manufacturing team is where NEO physically comes to life. The CNC Machine Park is the heartbeat of in-house part production — every bracket, housing, and precision component that makes NEO move starts here. Joining this team means sitting at the intersection of craft and scale, where the parts you help produce go directly into the robots shipping to real homes.

## **Your Charter**

Help us build an abundant supply of physical labor by learning to produce the precision parts that bring safe, intelligent humanoids to life. As a CNC Machine Park Intern, you’ll be embedded in the day-to-day operations of our machining floor — supporting experienced operators and programmers, building the fundamentals of machining and inspection, and growing into a technical contributor on the team manufacturing NEO at scale.

## **Key Outcomes**

* Support consistent machine uptime by assisting with daily setup, part loading, tool handling, and routine maintenance across the CNC machine park.
* Contribute to quality output by learning to read part drawings, interpret basic G-code, and perform first-look inspections on finished parts.
* Maintain a workshop environment that is clean, organized, and safe — held to a standard the whole team can rely on.
* Grow into progressively more technical responsibilities over the course of the internship, with the goal of becoming a strong candidate for a full-time technical role.
* Be a dependable presence on the floor — showing up, following through, and earning trust shift by shift.

## **Key Competencies**

* Hands-on and curious — you learn fastest by doing, asking, and getting your hands on the machine.
* Detail-oriented — you notice when something is off, and you care enough to flag it or fix it.
* Safety-minded — you respect the equipment, the people around you, and the standards that protect both.
* Ownership mindset — you treat the work as yours, even when you’re still learning.
* Communicates clearly and honestly — you ask when you don’t know, and you tell us when something needs attention.

## **Minimum Requirements**

* Genuine interest in manufacturing and a desire to learn CNC operations from the ground up.
* Comfortable working in a hands-on shop environment with machines, tools, and physical materials.
* Reliable and detail-oriented — consistent attendance and consistent effort are non-negotiable.
* Eager to grow into a full-time technical role in a robotics-focused manufacturing setting.

## **Preferred Skills**

* Prior coursework, vocational training, or hobby experience with machining, fabrication, or shop tools.
* Basic familiarity with reading mechanical drawings, GD&T, or G-code.
* Exposure to inspection tools (calipers, micrometers, gauges) in a school, hobby, or work setting.
* Interest in robotics, automation, or advanced manufacturing.

## **What does a successful 1X Team Member look like**

Team members at 1X who thrive here are builders. They move fast, own their work completely, and treat time like it’s the one thing you can’t get back — because it is. They say what they mean, finish what they start, and hold themselves to a standard before anyone has to ask. We push each other to be better, and we do it with honesty and respect.
