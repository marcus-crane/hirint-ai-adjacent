**CNC Machine Park Internship, Manufacturing | Hardware**

**Location: Hayward, CA**

**About 1X**

We build humanoid robots that work alongside people to solve labor shortages and create abundance.

**The Role**  
As a CNC Machine Park Intern, you will support the daily operations of our CNC machine park, gaining hands-on exposure to real-world manufacturing processes. You’ll work closely with experienced operators and programmers while developing foundational skills in machining, inspection, and shop organization. This role offers a clear path for technical growth and long-term opportunities in robotics-focused manufacturing.  
  
**You Will**

* Support daily operations including machine setup, part loading, cleaning, and basic tool handling
* Observe and assist with CNC machining, inspection, and routine maintenance tasks
* Learn to read part drawings and interpret basic G-code
* Maintain a clean, organized, and safe workshop environment
* Take on progressively more technical responsibilities as experience grows

**Must Have**

* Strong interest in manufacturing and a desire to learn CNC operations from the ground up
* Comfortable working with machines, tools, and in a hands-on environment
* Reliable, detail-oriented, and committed to contributing consistently
* Eagerness to grow into a full-time technical role in a robotics-focused manufacturing setting

**Benefits & Compensation**

* Salary Range: $25 per hour
* Health, dental, and vision insurance
* Paid time off and holidays
