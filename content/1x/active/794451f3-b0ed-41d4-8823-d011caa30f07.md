## **About the Team**

The Robot Mechanical Systems team designs, develops, and integrates the mechanical systems that let NEO move, interact, and operate safely in real homes - from precision mechanisms and structural assemblies to tightly integrated electromechanical systems. We work hand in hand with electrical, controls, software, and manufacturing teams to turn new mechanical concepts into production-ready hardware. As NEO scales toward high-volume manufacturing, this team sets the design standards that decide how well the robot performs and how reliably we can build it.

## **Your Charter**

The mission of this role is to own NEO's most critical mechanical subsystems from early architecture through high-volume production. As a Staff Mechanical Engineer, you are a technical leader on the Robot Mechanical Systems team: you set requirements and interfaces, make the system-level trade-offs between performance, weight, packaging, reliability, and cost, and carry your designs through prototyping, validation, and production ramp. You also raise the bar for the engineers around you by leading design reviews, mentoring, and shaping mechanical architecture across the robot. Your work directly decides whether NEO performs reliably and safely, at a cost and volume that gets it into people's homes.

## **Key Outcomes**

* Own the mechanical architecture of critical NEO subsystems, setting clear requirements, interfaces, and specifications that electrical, controls, and software teams can build against.
* Deliver production-ready hardware by carrying subsystems from concept through detailed design, prototyping, validation, design release, and production ramp, partnering with manufacturing and suppliers to design for manufacturing and assembly.
* Drive system-level trade-offs across structure, weight, packaging, actuation, durability, and cost, grounded in first-principles analysis, simulation, and test data.
* Improve reliability by identifying failure modes, leading root-cause investigations, and closing them with design changes that hold up in real homes.
* Lead the team's technical bar: run design reviews, mentor engineers, and establish mechanical design standards that scale with high-volume production.

## **Key Competencies**

* Mechanical Systems Depth: Deep command of precision mechanisms, structural design, and complex electromechanical systems, backed by first-principles analysis and test.
* System-Level Judgment: Weighs every design decision against the full robot architecture and competing product requirements.
* Builder Mindset: Designs, assembles, tests, troubleshoots, and iterates physical hardware hands-on.
* Manufacturing Mindset: Designs for production from day one - tolerances, processes, cost, and scalability.
* Technical Leadership: Leads complex projects independently, influences decisions across disciplines, communicates clearly, and makes the engineers around them better.

## **Minimum Requirements**

* Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, Robotics, or a related engineering discipline.
* 8+ years of experience designing and developing complex mechanical or electromechanical systems, with demonstrated ownership of significant subsystems.
* Proven track record of leading mechanical subsystems from concept through prototyping, validation, and into production alongside manufacturing teams and suppliers.
* Experience designing complex assemblies with actuators, bearings, transmissions, linkages, and sensors in a major 3D CAD package, validated through FEA, GD&T and tolerance stack-ups, first-principles calculations, and physical testing.
* Demonstrated ability to lead technically: running design reviews, resolving complex engineering problems, and mentoring engineers.

## **Preferred Skills**

* Experience developing mechanical systems for humanoid robots, robotic arms, industrial robots, or similar complex electromechanical products.
* Experience designing robotic joints, actuation mechanisms, transmissions, or tendon-driven systems, and packaging motors, gearboxes, sensors, and wiring into compact architectures.
* Experience taking high-volume electromechanical products (consumer robotics, consumer electronics, automotive, or aerospace) from R&D through new product introduction into mass production.
* Familiarity with reliability engineering, including accelerated life testing, fatigue analysis, and failure analysis.
* Experience establishing mechanical design standards and engineering change processes, including product lifecycle management (PLM) systems and configuration management.
