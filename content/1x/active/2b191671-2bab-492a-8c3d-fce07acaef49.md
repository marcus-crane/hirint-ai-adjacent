**About the Team:**

IT supports engineering, operations, and business teams with reliable technology across devices, accounts, networks, meeting spaces, and labs. By keeping employees connected and productive, the team helps 1X focus on building humanoid robots.

**Your Charter:** Keep employees productive through hands-on IT support, secure devices and access, and dependable workplace technology. Improve the employee experience with clear documentation and practical process improvements.

**Key Outcomes:**

* Resolve tier 1 and tier 2 hardware, software, account, and access issues in person, over chat, and through tickets; escalate infrastructure issues with clear diagnostics.
* Maintain secure macOS, Windows, and mobile devices from procurement, imaging, MDM enrollment, and deployment through repair, recovery, and secure wiping. Keep hardware and software inventory, assignments, warranties, and audit records accurate.
* Complete onboarding and offboarding with People and Security: provision accounts, hand off and recover devices, and remove access. Manage identity and SaaS access, SSO/MFA enrollment, groups, licenses, and password resets.
* Keep meeting rooms, AV systems, printers, peripherals, and lab and operations workstations and connected devices ready for use. Troubleshoot Wi-Fi, wired, and VPN connectivity; support events and all-hands meetings.
* Reduce recurring issues through maintained knowledge base articles, runbooks, self-service guides, and proposed automation or process improvements.

**Key Competencies:**

* Communicate clearly with technical and non-technical colleagues.
* Troubleshoot systematically and escalate with clear diagnostics.
* Prioritize and work independently in a fast-paced environment.
* Document solutions and improve the employee experience.
* Maintain device compliance, access hygiene, and information security.

**Minimum Requirements:**

* 2+ years of experience in help desk, desktop support, or a similar IT support role.
* Strong working knowledge of macOS and Windows setup, troubleshooting, and imaging; experience with an MDM platform and a ticketing system.
* Familiarity with identity and productivity platforms such as Okta and Google Workspace.
* Ability to move equipment around office and lab spaces and lift up to [40 lbs; requirement to be confirmed].

**Preferred Skills:**

* CompTIA A+, Network+, or Security+; Apple or Microsoft certifications.  
  Understanding of networking fundamentals, including DHCP, DNS, Wi-Fi, VLANs, and VPN.
* Scripting with Bash, PowerShell, or Python for automation.
* Experience with conference rooms, AV, or asset tools such as Reftab.
* Experience supporting hardware, robotics, manufacturing, or labs.
