## **About the Team**

The Materials & Logistics team is the heartbeat of our San Carlos operation — the people who keep components flowing, production lines stocked, and shipments moving on time. Without us, NEO doesn’t get built. Every robot that walks out of our facility starts with the work this team does, and we take that responsibility seriously.

## **Your Charter**

As a Materials Handler, you are directly responsible for ensuring the physical flow of materials that keeps NEO production on schedule. Manufacturing and engineering depend on you to have the right parts in the right place at the right time — every shift, without exception. This role sits on the critical path between supply and build, and the precision of your work translates directly into our ability to ship robots that change how people live.

## **Key Outcomes**

* Maintain inventory accuracy at or above 99% through disciplined scanning, labeling, and system updates.
* Keep production lines continuously supplied — zero stoppages caused by material delays on your shift.
* Process inbound, outbound, and internal transfers with complete, accurate documentation, every time.
* Own the safety and organization of your work area — every shift ends cleaner and safer than it started.
* Respond to shifting production priorities quickly, escalating risks early so the team can adjust.

## **Key Competencies**

* Bias for action — you see what needs to be done and move on it without waiting to be asked.
* Detail-obsessed — you treat inventory accuracy and documentation as non-negotiable, because downstream teams depend on it.
* Calm in a fast-paced environment — priorities shift, equipment moves, and you stay focused and safe through it.
* Team-first communication — you flag issues early, share context generously, and never let a teammate get blindsided.
* Safety mindset — you understand that one shortcut can hurt someone, and you refuse to take it.

## **Minimum Requirements**

* Prior experience in warehouse, logistics, or materials handling.
* Hands-on experience operating forklifts and pallet jacks — certification is ideal but not a deal breaker if you can pass training quickly.
* Comfortable using scanners, labels, and shipping documentation in a daily workflow.
* Able to perform physical work, including lifting and moving materials throughout a full shift.
* Reliable and punctual — your team is counting on you to be there, on time, every shift.

## **Preferred Skills**

* Experience working in a manufacturing or production environment (electronics, automotive, or robotics a plus).
* Familiarity with WMS or ERP systems (NetSuite, SAP, Oracle, or similar).
* Current forklift certification.
* Experience supporting a fast-scaling operation where processes are still being built.
