## **About the Team**

The Supply Chain team builds and scales the global supplier network behind NEO, taking complex electromechanical systems from concept to mass production alongside Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality, and Operations. Every robot we ship depends on the right parts arriving at the right cost, quality, and volume. Our work directly decides how fast NEO ramps and what it costs to build.

## **Your Charter**

The mission of this role is to build a structural supply base that can ship NEO at volume, at the right cost, without interruption. You'll own the sourcing strategy, commercial relationships, and supply continuity for every structural part on NEO - die cast and machined components, sheet metal, extrusions, injection molded plastics, composites, and the welded, bonded, and fastened assemblies that bring them together.

Structures are NEO's skeleton and a large share of its bill of materials, so how well we source them shapes both product cost and how fast we can scale. You'll work alongside the Motors & Magnets Global Supply Manager and partner with Supplier Development Engineering, who own whether a supplier can build the part while you own the commercial relationship and the commodity strategy, which the Structures buyers execute day to day.

## **Key Outcomes**

* Own the sourcing strategy for each structural commodity - die casting, CNC machining, sheet metal, extrusion, injection molding, and composites - delivering competitive cost, quality, capacity, and technology advantages.
* Secure supply continuity for every structural part through supplier selection, capacity planning, dual sourcing, and risk mitigation, so no NEO build waits on a structural part.
* Drive measurable cost reduction across the structures portfolio through negotiation, should-cost modeling, tooling strategy, and value engineering with design teams.
* Build and maintain a global structural supplier network that scales from new product introduction through high-volume production.
* Ensure structural suppliers consistently meet requirements for quality, delivery, and responsiveness, with clear performance reviews and fast corrective action when they fall short.

## **Key Competencies**

* Deep understanding of structural manufacturing processes - die casting, CNC machining, sheet metal, extrusion, injection molding, and composites - and what drives cost in each.
* Strong commercial negotiator with demonstrated success managing complex supplier agreements, including pricing, tooling ownership, and capacity commitments.
* Ability to evaluate supplier capabilities, manufacturing processes, cost structures, and scalability risks.
* Analytical and data-driven, with experience building should-cost models and multi-year commodity strategies.
* Excellent cross-functional partner who influences engineering, manufacturing, quality, and supplier stakeholders to make cost, design, and supply tradeoffs together.

## **Minimum Requirements**

* Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Supply Chain, Business, Operations, or a related discipline.
* 6+ years of experience in global sourcing, strategic sourcing, commodity management, or procurement.
* Direct experience sourcing structural or mechanical components across at least two of: die casting, CNC machining, sheet metal, extrusion, injection molding, or composites.
* Proven track record developing sourcing strategies, negotiating commercial agreements including tooling, and managing supplier performance.
* Experience supporting new product introduction (NPI), production ramp-ups, and global supplier management.
