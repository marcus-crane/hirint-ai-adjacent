# Facilities Technician

Department: Facilities

Location: San Carlos

Shift: Monday–Friday, Day Shift

## About 1X

We’re building humanoid robots that work in any space humans exist with the mission to create an abundance of labor. Simple, but it’s not.

To do this right, we have to solve robotics, AI, software, hardware, manufacturing and operations at the same time, at scale, in a form factor that has to be safe. If you’re inspired by this, you’ll thrive here. We’ve been at this since 2014 and we’re at the point where the hard problems are behind us and the hard work is in front of us.

NEO is our flagship - a general purpose robot designed to move, learn, and operate in the real world alongside real people. We’re not demoing it, we're shipping it. We’re excited to meet you, if this excites you.

If you’ve spent your career working on problems that matter and want to see them actually reach the world - this is that moment. We’re scaling, we’re hiring with intention, and we need people who want to build something that will genuinely change how humans spend their time safely creating abundance for all.

## About the Team

The Facilities team keeps 1X’s San Carlos site running so the people building NEO never have to think twice about their space. From equipment uptime to safety compliance, we’re the quiet infrastructure that lets engineering, manufacturing, and operations move fast without disruption. As we scale and ship, this team makes sure the physical world underneath the mission never becomes the bottleneck.

## Your Charter

Own the day to day upkeep, safety and functionality of 1X’s San Carlos facility, including equipment, systems and workspace, so every team building and shipping NEO has a safe, reliable and fully operational environment to work in every day.

## Key Outcomes

* Performing routine repairs and preventive maintenance across plumbing, carpentry and basic electrical, including changing ballasts and light fixtures
* Completing minor plumbing, carpentry and electrical work such as fixing leaks, patching drywall or repairing fixtures
* Conducting facility walkthroughs and safety inspections to catch issues before they become bigger problems
* Logging completed work and tracking parts and supplies used
* Coordinating with outside vendors or contractors when a repair falls outside scope
* Keeping the facility clean, organized and running smoothly day to day

## Key Competencies

* Hands on with plumbing, carpentry and basic electrical work, including things like changing ballasts, switches and fixtures
* Takes ownership of the space and doesn’t wait to be told something needs fixing
* Stays calm and moves quickly when something breaks and people are counting on a fast fix
* Knows when to bring in a licensed electrician or specialty contractor rather than going outside their scope
* Detail oriented with a strong safety mindset
* Comfortable working independently and juggling multiple open issues at once

## Minimum Requirements

* 2+ years of experience in facilities maintenance or a related trade, with hands-on plumbing and carpentry work
* Comfortable performing basic electrical tasks safely (e.g., replacing ballasts, light fixtures, switches, outlets)
* High school diploma or equivalent
* Ability to lift up to 50 lbs and perform physical tasks (climbing ladders, standing/walking for extended periods)

## Preferred Skills

* Experience working in a manufacturing, lab, or industrial environment
* Forklift or scissor lift certification
* Experience with a CMMS (computerized maintenance management system) or ticketing tool
* Broader HVAC or mechanical systems exposure beyond core MEP tasks
