## About the Team

The Test & Validation team builds the methods, infrastructure, and data needed to understand how NEO performs in the real world. We work across Design Engineering and Reliability Engineering to validate new designs, accelerate engineering learning, and prove that critical robot systems can perform reliably over time.

## Your Charter

Lead the Test & Validation Engineering team while remaining a hands-on technical leader for the most important robot-level validation challenges. You will turn engineering objectives into robust test strategies, personally develop complete-robot tests, and establish repeatable methods for task/load characterization, durability, instrumentation, and data collection.

## Key Outcomes

* Lead and develop a high-performing Test & Validation Engineering team, setting priorities, providing technical direction, and aligning execution with program needs.
* Deliver robust validation strategies and test methods across R&D, design exploration, design validation, and lifetime reliability programs.
* Personally lead development of complete-robot validation tests, including task and load characterization, robot-level durability testing, instrumentation, controls, and data acquisition.
* Establish consistent standards for test design, execution, data collection, analysis, tracking, and technical reporting across the team.
* Ensure test results are accurate, repeatable, and actionable, giving engineering teams the data needed to make informed design and program decisions.
* Own planning and utilization of critical test equipment and lab assets, including budgeting, procurement, capacity planning, and equipment readiness.
* Provide engineering and company leadership with clear visibility into test execution, risks, roadblocks, results, and program readiness.

## Key Competencies

* Hands-on technical leader who can move between engineering strategy, detailed test development, and team leadership.
* Strong depth in test and validation of complex robotic or electromechanical systems.
* Excellent understanding of instrumentation, data acquisition, mechanical actuation, fixtures, controls, and test equipment.
* Ability to translate ambiguous engineering questions into practical, repeatable test methods that generate useful data.
* Strong technical judgment and ability to prioritize testing against rapidly changing engineering and program needs.
* Effective people leader who can coach engineers, delegate technical work, and maintain a high execution bar.
* Clear communicator who can explain complex technical results, risks, and tradeoffs to both engineers and leadership.

## Minimum Requirements

* Bachelor’s degree in Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Systems Engineering, or a related engineering discipline.
* 5+ years of experience in Test & Validation, Design Validation, Reliability Test, or a related engineering role involving complex hardware.
* Demonstrated experience leading Test and Validation Engineers through complex projects, including technical guidance, prioritization, and cross-functional execution.
* Experience developing and executing tests for robotic systems or complex electromechanical assemblies, including robot-level task/load characterization or durability testing.
* Hands-on experience with test equipment and methods including data acquisition, environmental chambers, vibration systems, HALT, mechanical actuation, and motor or drive-unit dynamometers.
* Strong experience with test planning, data collection, analysis, tracking, and technical reporting.

## Preferred Skills

* Experience leading both rapid R&D/design exploration testing and longer-duration reliability or lifetime validation programs.
* Experience establishing standardized test processes, templates, data practices, or laboratory workflows across an engineering organization.
* Experience developing complete-robot or system-level validation methods.
* Experience managing test equipment budgets, procurement, capacity planning, and utilization.
* Experience working in robotics, automotive, aerospace, consumer electronics, or other complex electromechanical hardware environments.
