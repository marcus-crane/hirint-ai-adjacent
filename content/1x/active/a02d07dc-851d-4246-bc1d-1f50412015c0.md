# **Your Charter**

As a Senior Robotics Controls Engineer, you will develop the real-time software stack that enables our humanoid robots to move, balance, and manipulate the world with precision and reliability. Your work will span low-level motor control through whole-body dynamic control and estimation, making complex, high-DOF hardware accessible through clean and efficient software abstractions. This role is pivotal in bridging hardware and AI layers, and your contributions will be critical as we transition from R&D to large-scale production.

# **Key Outcomes**

* **Core Robotics Capability:** Develop the real-time software stack that enables our humanoid robots to move, balance, and manipulate the world with precision and reliability.
* **Control & Estimation:** Design and deploy fast, efficient state estimation systems and control algorithms for balancing, locomotion, and manipulation.
* **Hardware Abstraction:** Make complex, high-DOF hardware accessible through clean, efficient software abstractions and APIs that allow higher-level AI systems to command the robot.
* **System Calibration:** Successfully perform system identification and calibration of actuators, transmissions, and full-body kinematics.
* **Diagnostics & Reliability:** Build robust software and tools for hardware bring-up, debugging, health monitoring, visualization, and logging.

### 

# **Key Competencies**

* **Domain Expertise:** Strong theoretical and applied understanding of rigid body dynamics, linear algebra, and numerical optimization.
* **Algorithmic Mastery:** Proven ability to implement optimization-based whole-body control, EKF-based state estimation, or model predictive control for legged systems.
* **Software Engineering:** High proficiency in writing clean, production-ready C++ within a Linux development environment.
* **Best Practices:** Strong grasp of modern software development practices, including version control, testing methodologies, and system architecture.
* **Cross-Disciplinary Troubleshooting:** Ability to integrate, evaluate, and debug complex hardware components, bridging the gap between software and physical sensors/actuators.

# **Minimum Requirements**

* 3+ years of professional experience deploying and debugging control or estimation software on high-DOF (Degrees of Freedom) robots.
* Proven track record of evaluating and integrating robotics hardware components (including sensors and actuators) in a professional or advanced academic setting.

# **Preferred Skills**

* Experience with additional languages and frameworks: Java, Unity/C#, Python, real-time Linux, ROS2, or DDS.
* Background in writing embedded C for sensors or actuators.
* Familiarity with the Yocto OS build system.
* Hands-on, independent experience building or tinkering with robotics hardware from scratch.
