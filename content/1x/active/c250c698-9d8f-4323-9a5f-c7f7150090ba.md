About the Team:

The Prototyping team turns ideas into physical reality.

We work closely with Industrial Design and Engineering to rapidly develop, build, and evaluate the physical systems and components that make up NEO. Our work spans digital models, functional prototypes, mockups, fabrication, materials, and production-intent assemblies.

We are looking for a Fabrication Technician to support hands-on work across the Prototyping shop. You will help assemble and finish physical prototypes, including sanding parts, while stepping in where projects need another set of hands.

This is a varied, hands-on role for someone who takes care with physical work and is comfortable moving between projects as the team’s needs change.

Your Charter: Support the build and finishing of prototypes across the Prototyping team. You will help turn parts and designs into physical builds through assembly, sanding, and practical shop support.

Key Outcomes:

* Assemble prototype parts and components with attention to fit and finish.
* Sand and finish parts to support prototype builds and evaluation.
* Provide hands-on support across projects as build priorities change.
* Raise fit, finish, or assembly issues you notice so the team can address them in the next iteration.
* Keep shared work areas and tools organized and ready for the next build.

Key Competencies:

* Craftsmanship: You take care with assembly and finishing work and notice details that affect the physical result.
* Adaptability: You can move between tasks and support different projects as priorities change.
* Collaboration: You work closely with engineers, designers, and other technicians to get physical builds completed and share what you learn from them.

Minimum Requirements:

* Hands-on experience assembling and finishing physical parts or prototypes.
* Ability to perform sanding and other detail-oriented finishing work.
* Comfort working in a shared shop and supporting multiple projects.

Preferred Skills:

* Experience supporting prototype fabrication or model making.
* Experience spotting assembly or finish issues during physical builds and communicating them to a team.
