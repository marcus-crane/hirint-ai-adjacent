## About the Team

The Autonomy & Safety Engineering team is responsible for ensuring 1X robots operate safely, reliably, and predictably in real-world human environments. We work at the intersection of robotics, autonomy, controls, perception, and systems safety to develop the frameworks, tooling, and validation methodologies that enable safe deployment of humanoid robots at scale. This team plays a critical role in defining how embodied AI systems safely interact with people, homes, and dynamic real-world environments.

## Your Charter

We are seeking a deeply technical Principal Safety Engineer to help define what safety means for a new generation of humanoid robots. Humanoids present safety challenges that have never been fully solved: they are powerful, highly dynamic machines designed to operate autonomously, learn, and interact closely with people in complex, unstructured environments. There is no established playbook for solving many of these challenges and you will help write it. This is not a compliance-first functional safety role; standards and established methods are tools, not the destination.

## Key Outcomes

* Identify hazards unique to our robots and their Operational Design Domain, and reason about them from first principles
* Invent new ways to eliminate or mitigate risk across hardware, controls, software, and AI behavior
* Find the risks others miss - the ones that emerge where physical systems and intelligent behavior interact
* Turn that insight into fundamentally safer architectures, controls, behaviors, and validation strategies
* Establish the engineering principles for safe humanoid design at 1X, and for an industry still being invented
