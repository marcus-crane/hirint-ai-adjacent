## **About the Team**

The Behavior team builds the software that enables NEO to behave like an intelligent, autonomous character living alongside people. Drawing inspiration from modern game AI, we develop the decision-making systems, world simulation, and behavior frameworks that allow robots to perceive their surroundings, understand context, prioritize goals, and interact naturally in dynamic environments.

If you've built believable NPCs, conversational AI systems, or complex gameplay engine systems, you'll find many of the same engineering challenges here—except the characters are real.

## **Your Charter**

Build the behavior systems that bring humanoid robots to life. You will design scalable AI architectures that enable robots to reason about their environment, make autonomous decisions, manage competing goals, and perform complex tasks in real-world homes. You’ll need to write performant systems code and manage latency from cloud systems. Your work will bridge the gap between game AI and embodied intelligence, creating robots that behave as naturally and reliably as the best NPCs in modern games.

## **Key Outcomes**

* Design and implement scalable behavior architectures that enable robots to autonomously perform complex, multi-step household tasks leveraging cloud-based world models.
* Build decision-making frameworks using behavior trees, hierarchical state machines, or similar AI architectures.
* Partner with Core, World Model, Apps, and Motion teams to integrate behavior systems into real-world robot operation.
* Improve the realism, robustness, and adaptability of robot behaviors while establishing engineering best practices for large-scale behavior development.

## **Key Competencies**

* Deep expertise building gameplay systems, conversational AI, or NPC behaviors for complex interactive environments.
* Strong understanding of decision-making architectures, planning systems, and autonomous agent behaviors.
* Excellent software engineering skills with experience building scalable, performance-critical systems in C++.
* Strong systems thinking with the ability to simplify complex autonomous behaviors into maintainable software architectures.
* Comfortable leading technical initiatives and collaborating across multidisciplinary engineering teams in a fast-moving environment.

## **Minimum Requirements**

* 6+ years of professional experience (Senior) or 10+ years (Staff) developing gameplay systems, conversational AI, NPC behaviors, or system architecture.
* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Game Development, Robotics, AI, or a related technical field.

## **Preferred Skills**

* Familiarity with robotics, autonomous systems, reinforcement learning, or embodied AI.
