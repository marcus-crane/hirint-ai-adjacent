## About the Team

The Material Sciences team influences what NEO is made of. Every frame, glove, and thermal system starts with a material choice. Those choices set the weight, safety, durability, and cost of every robot we ship.

## Your Charter

The mission of this role is to make every generation of NEO lighter, stronger, safer to touch, and cheaper to build through better materials. You will own material selection, qualification, and supplier strategy across NEO and build the team and lab capability to iterate on materials as fast as we iterate on robots. This sits on 1X's critical path because every gram of weight, every failure mode, and every dollar of material cost multiplies across thousands of robots working alongside real people.

## Key Outcomes

* Own the materials roadmap for NEO's structures, delivering a [X]% reduction in skeleton weight through advanced composites and high-strength alloys without sacrificing stiffness or safety
* Extend the fatigue life of high-cycle joints and load-bearing structures to [X hours / X cycles], and qualify chassis materials that absorb impact so robots survive real-world falls
* Deliver the next generation of NEO's durable skin: elastomer systems that are safe and natural for humans to touch, protect embedded tactile sensors from sweat, dust, and abrasion, and hold up to [X months] of daily gripping without tearing
* Solve thermal and electrical materials challenges inside tightly packaged limbs, qualifying thermal interface materials that keep motors and compute within operating limits and insulation systems that make high-voltage wiring safe at scale
* Reduce materials cost per robot through alternative material selection and design-for-manufacturing, while qualifying second sources for specialized materials so no single vendor can stall production
* Cut the cycle from new material concept to on-robot validation, building the lab and test capability that makes fast iteration routine
* Build and lead a materials science team, and operate as the trusted materials authority for mechanical design, manufacturing, and safety partners

## Key Competencies

* Translate robot-level goals for weight, safety, durability, and cost into material specifications and qualification plans engineers can execute
* Lead and develop materials engineers, setting clear priorities and holding a high standard for test rigor
* Balance material performance against cost and manufacturability, and make that tradeoff clear to design, manufacturing, and finance partners
* Design test and validation methods that produce trustworthy fatigue, impact, and environmental data quickly
* Manage suppliers of specialized materials, from technical qualification through capacity and risk planning
* Communicate materials decisions in plain language to people who are not materials scientists

## Minimum Requirements

* Experience with fatigue, impact, and environmental testing of structural or soft materials
* Demonstrated ability to manage supplier relationships for specialized or hard-to-source materials
* Advanced degree in Materials Science, Mechanical Engineering, or a related field
