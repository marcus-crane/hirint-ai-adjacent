To do this right, we have to solve robotics, AI, manufacturing - at the same time, at scale, in a form factor that has to be safe enough to live with your family. If you’re inspired by this, you’ll thrive here. We’ve been at this since 2014 and we’re at the point where the hard problems are behind us and the *hard work* is in front of us.   
  
NEO is our flagship - a home robot designed to move, learn, and operate in the real world alongside real people. We’re not demoing it - we’re shipping it. We’re excited to meet you, if this excites you.   
  
If you’ve spent your career working on problems that matter and want to see them actually reach the world - this is that moment. We’re scaling, we’re hiring with intention, and we need people who want to build something that will genuinely change how humans spend their time - safely creating abundance for all.

# **About the Team**

We build the Data Collection Equipment our operators use to collect the data behind NEO. When the gear works, every hour of collection counts. When it doesn’t, data stops, so we build it right.

# **Your Charter**

Build the gear that makes data collection possible. NEO learns from real-world data, and that data depends on equipment our operators can trust every shift. You turn engineering designs into durable, repeatable soft goods, fast, so data collection never waits on a build.

# **Key Outcomes**

* Data collection equipment ships on schedule. Operators never stop collecting because gear isn’t ready.
* First-pass build quality stays high. Parts hold tolerance and meet spec on fit, stitch quality, and durability, and rework is tracked and trending down.
* New designs become working prototypes in days, not weeks.
* The embroidery machine and laser cutter stay up. Tension, setup, and maintenance issues get fixed fast, and downtime is logged.
* Every build is documented (patterns, machine settings, steps) so any part can be rebuilt the same way.

# **Key Competencies**

* Expert hands. Precise sewing and embroidery on small, contoured, and stretchy parts.
* Machine troubleshooter. You find out why thread breaks, puckers, or frays, and fix it at the machine.
* Process-minded. You measure, document, and repeat. No eyeballing.
* Built for prototype speed. Designs change week to week, and you keep pace.
* Straight talker who owns the build. You tell engineers when a design won’t build well, bring a better option, and own quality and schedule.

# **Minimum Requirements**

* Hands-on in cut-and-sew, sample making, or soft goods production.
* Proficient on industrial sewing machines from light to heavy-duty (single-needle, overlock/serger), and skilled at hand sewing, on stretch and technical fabrics.
* You cut and mark parts from patterns and templates within tolerance, and build from tech packs or engineering drawings.
* Comfortable on a computer: you follow digital work instructions, log builds, and load machine files. You do shop math and measure precisely with calibrated gauges.

# **Preferred Skills**

* Glove construction: you have sewn, patterned, or built gloves. This sets you apart.
* Experience on an 18-needle single-head embroidery machine, including thread tensioning and routine maintenance.
* Laser cutter operation for fabrics and soft goods.
* Prototype builds with technical materials like nylon, Kevlar, Zylon, Spectra, or PTFE (Teflon), and you know how each one cuts, sews, and frays.
* Hands-on with computerized embroidery machines: setup, hooping, stabilizers, and thread tension.
* Certificate or degree in apparel or textile production.
