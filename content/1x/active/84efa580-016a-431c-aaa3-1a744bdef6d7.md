**About the Team**

The Data Collection Operations team is responsible for generating the high-quality real-world data that trains and improves NEO’s behavior. This team operates at the intersection of robotics, AI, and human interaction — creating the datasets that directly shape how our robots move, respond, and operate safely in home environments. As we scale deployments, this group plays a critical role in accelerating robot learning and operational readiness.

**Your Charter**

As a Senior Operator on the Data Collection team, your charter is to execute high-quality robot operations while raising the standard for precision, consistency, and operational excellence across the team. You will operate humanoid robots in controlled lab and residential-style environments, support AI data generation workflows, and help improve the systems and processes that enable scalable robot learning.

**Key Outcomes**

* Execute robot operation and data collection workflows with high accuracy, consistency, and adherence to SOPs
* Produce high-quality annotated datasets and detailed issue reporting that improve AI model training and robot performance
* Maintain operational readiness of robots, equipment, and data collection environments across assigned shifts
* Identify workflow inefficiencies and contribute measurable improvements to SOPs, tooling, and operational scalability
* Support successful robot sessions in lab and residential environments while maintaining safety, professionalism, and data integrity

**Minimum Requirements**

* 2+ years of experience operating robots, technical systems, or similar hands-on operational equipment
* Proven ability to follow structured procedures with precision and consistency
* Comfortable working in VR environments and performing physically active tasks throughout the workday
* Ability to lift up to 50 lbs and wear operational equipment weighing up to 30 lbs
* Availability to work flexible schedules including swing shifts, weekends, and overtime as needed

**Preferred Skills:**

* Experience supporting robotics, autonomous systems, or AI data collection programs
* Prior experience contributing to SOP development, process optimization, or operational scaling efforts
* Familiarity with data annotation tools, issue tracking systems, or technical reporting workflows
