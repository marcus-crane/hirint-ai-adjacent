**Role Name:** CNC Operator

**Department:** Machining & Prototyping

**Location:** Hayward, CA (on-site)

## **About 1X**

We’re building humanoid robots that work in home - doing the chores, handling the tasks, and giving people their time back. Simple, but it’s not.

To do this right, we have to solve robotics, AI, manufacturing - at the same time, at scale, in a form factor that has to be safe enough to live with your family. If you’re inspired by this, you’ll thrive here. We've been at this since 2014 and we’re at the point where the hard problems are behind us and the hard work is in front of us.

NEO is our flagship - a home robot designed to move, learn, and operate in the real world alongside real people. We’re not demoing it - we’re shipping it. We’re excited to meet you, if this excites you.

If you’ve spent your career working on problems that matter and want to see them actually reach the world - this is that moment. We’re scaling, we’re hiring with intention, and we need people who want to build something that will genuinely change how humans spend their time - safely creating abundance for all.

## **About the Team**

The Machining & Prototyping team turns engineering designs into precise, functional parts. We work closely with engineers to produce prototypes, one-off components, and small batches that support NEO’s development. Our work helps teams test ideas, refine designs, and solve problems quickly. Quality, flexibility, and close collaboration are central to what we do.

## **Your Charter**

Create an abundant supply of physical labor through safe, intelligent humanoids that work alongside people - by producing the precision parts NEO depends on. As a CNC Operator, you are on the critical path between design and a working robot. Every part you machine, inspect, and sign off on is a part that goes into a humanoid that someone will trust in their home. Your craft is what makes our promise of safety and quality real.

## **Key Outcomes**

* Deliver machined parts that consistently meet print specifications and GD&T callouts, with first-pass yield trending upward quarter over quarter.
* Own the quality of every part that leaves your machine - in-process inspections caught at the source, not on the assembly floor.
* Keep machines running safely and productively across your shift, minimizing avoidable downtime through proactive setup checks, tool monitoring, and clean handoffs.
* Partner with programmers and engineers to surface process improvements - fixture, tooling, or program changes that compound into better parts and faster cycles.
* Maintain a clean, organized, and safe work cell that reflects the standard we hold ourselves to: precision in the work, precision in the space.

## **Key Competencies**

* Strong mechanical intuition - you can feel when a setup is off, hear when a tool is wrong, and trust that instinct enough to stop and check.
* Disciplined about quality - calipers, micrometers, and height gauges are second nature, and you treat in-process inspection as part of the job, not an interruption to it.
* Ownership mindset - you finish what you start, communicate clearly when something isn’t right, and don’t pass problems downstream.
* Safety-first instincts - you protect yourself, the people around you, and the equipment, every shift, without being reminded.
* Curious and coachable - you want to understand the why behind the program, the fixture, and the part, and you grow fast when given the chance.

## **Minimum Requirements**

* 1–3 years of experience as a CNC Operator, or comparable hands-on machining shop experience.
* Ability to read engineering drawings and interpret basic GD&T.
* Comfort working with aluminum and stainless steel, and familiarity with G-code at a read-and-verify level.
* Demonstrated experience performing in-process quality checks with calipers, micrometers, and height gauges.
* Team-oriented, safety-minded, and reliable on shift - ideal but not a deal breaker if you’re newer to a production environment, as long as the fundamentals are strong.

## **Preferred Skills**

* Familiarity with Heidenhain controls and the CNC platforms commonly paired with them.
* Hands-on experience with probing routines, tool changes, and fixture setup.
* Interest in growing into CNC programming, process development, or advanced manufacturing engineering over time.
