# About the Team

The Power Systems team develops the electrical hardware and supporting technologies that store, convert, distribute, and manage energy throughout NEO. We design and integrate battery management systems, battery packs, power distribution electronics, DC-DC converters, and charging systems that enable safe, reliable, and efficient robot operation.

Our work spans power electronics, electrical validation, automated testing, manufacturing equipment, industrial controls, software development, and production data systems. We collaborate closely with Electrical Engineering, Firmware, Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality, and Operations to develop reliable and scalable processes for testing and manufacturing complex power systems.

As NEO moves from development into scaled production, our team is responsible for building the automation infrastructure that ensures every power system is tested, validated, traceable, and ready for integration into the robot.

# Your Charter

Own the automation architecture and test systems that enable NEO's power electronics to move from engineering development into reliable, scalable production.

As a Staff Power Electronics Automation Engineer, you will lead the development of automated validation systems, manufacturing equipment, and production test infrastructure for battery management systems, battery packs, power distribution boards, DC-DC converters, and chargers.

You will own automation solutions from requirements definition and system architecture through software development, hardware integration, validation, equipment commissioning, production deployment, and sustaining engineering.

Your work will combine power electronics fundamentals, electrical measurements, software development, industrial controls, instrumentation, and manufacturing process automation to deliver reliable, repeatable, and scalable systems.

At the Staff level, you will establish reusable automation platforms, define engineering standards, influence equipment architecture and investment decisions, and mentor engineers while remaining deeply hands-on in development, integration, debugging, and production support.
