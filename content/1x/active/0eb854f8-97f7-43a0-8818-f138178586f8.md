# **About the Team**

The Hardware Engineering team designs and delivers the electromechanical systems that allow NEO to move, manipulate, and interact safely with the world. From structural components and precision mechanisms to complete robotic subsystems, the team owns hardware development from concept through production.

Working closely with electrical, controls, firmware, manufacturing, and robotics engineers, you'll help transform ambitious ideas into robust, manufacturable products that operate reliably in real-world environments.

# **Your Charter**

Design and deliver lightweight, structurally optimized, highly integrated structures that enable NEO’s hands to perform reliably and be manufactured at scale.

You will design and develop structural components and assemblies that integrate actuators, electronics, mechanisms, sensors, wiring, and thermal hardware within extremely constrained mechanical envelopes.

You will use structural analysis, FEA, optimization, and physical testing to drive design decisions and develop hardware that balances stiffness, strength, mass, packaging, reliability, manufacturability, and cost.

# **Key Outcomes**

* Design and release structural components and assemblies that meet performance, reliability, cost, and manufacturability requirements
* Develop highly integrated structures that package actuators, electronics, mechanisms, sensors, wiring, and thermal hardware within tight space constraints
* Use structural FEA, first-principles analysis, and physical testing to drive design decisions and optimize structures for stiffness, strength, mass, and packaging
* Design and execute structural tests to validate assumptions, correlate analytical and FEA models, characterize failure modes, and drive design iterations
* Drive designs from concept through detailed design, prototyping, validation, and production release
* Design for high-volume manufacturing and drive DFM, DFA, tolerance analysis, and cost optimization throughout development
* Work closely with electrical, thermal, materials, test, and manufacturing teams to define interfaces and develop integrated mechanical solutions
* Work directly with suppliers and manufacturing partners to resolve DFM issues, validate production processes, and support production ramp
* Support hardware through production ramp and continuous improvement, identifying and resolving design, reliability, and manufacturing issues
