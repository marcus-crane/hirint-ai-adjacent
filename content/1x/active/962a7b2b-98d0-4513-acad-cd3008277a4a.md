**About the Team:**

Test Engineering builds and supports the test systems that verify NEO hardware meets functional and quality requirements before shipment. Working across engineering and manufacturing, the team develops and maintains test stations, fixtures, and processes that identify defects, validate performance, and support production at scale.

**Your Charter:**

Execute reliable, repeatable tests on NEO hardware and robotic subassemblies while maintaining test station readiness and supporting manufacturing quality. This role is critical to identifying hardware failures, improving test coverage, and ensuring production test systems consistently verify product performance as 1X scales.

**Key Outcomes:**

* Execute functional, electrical, and electromechanical tests accurately and consistently against documented test plans and engineering requirements.
* Maintain test station readiness by preparing and verifying fixtures, instrumentation, cabling, power supplies, and associated equipment.
* Identify and troubleshoot test station and hardware failures, providing clear technical documentation and escalating issues to engineering when needed.
* Maintain accurate test records, pass/fail results, serial number traceability, and failure reports to support quality investigations and production decisions.
* Support timely failure reproduction, retesting, and verification to minimize production delays and prevent recurring issues.
* Identify opportunities to improve test procedures, station reliability, and execution efficiency as production requirements evolve.

**Key Competencies:**

* Hands-on technical aptitude across mechanical, electrical, and electromechanical systems.
* Strong troubleshooting skills with a systematic approach to identifying and isolating hardware and equipment failures.
* Detail-oriented execution with a commitment to test accuracy, data integrity, and procedural compliance.
* Comfortable working with hand tools, test fixtures, instrumentation, and production or laboratory equipment.
* Clear communicator who can document technical findings and collaborate effectively with engineering and manufacturing teams.
* Takes ownership of assigned work, adapts to changing priorities, and maintains a high standard for safety and execution.

**Minimum Requirements:**

* 2+ years of hands-on experience in an engineering lab, manufacturing test, electronics test, equipment maintenance, or a similar technical environment; relevant experience expectations will vary by level.
* Practical understanding of mechanical, electrical, or electromechanical hardware.
* Experience using hand tools and basic test equipment, such as digital multimeters and power supplies.
* Ability to follow technical procedures, interpret test instructions, and accurately document results.
* Basic computer skills, including working with spreadsheets and digital documentation systems.

**Preferred Skills:**

* Experience supporting functional, end-of-line, reliability, or design validation testing.
* Familiarity with digital multimeters, data acquisition systems, programmable power supplies, sensors, actuators, or motor-driven systems.
* Ability to read electrical schematics, wiring diagrams, or mechanical drawings.
* Experience troubleshooting PCBs, wiring harnesses, robotics, automation equipment, or complex electromechanical products.
* Experience with manufacturing execution systems, test-data software, or root-cause investigations.
