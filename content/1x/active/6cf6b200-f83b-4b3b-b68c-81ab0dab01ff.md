## **About the Team**

The Prototyping team turns ideas into physical reality.

We work closely with Industrial Design and Engineering to rapidly develop, build, and evaluate the physical systems and components that make up NEO. Our work spans digital models, functional prototypes, mockups, fabrication, materials, and production-intent assemblies.

We are looking for an Electrical Engineer to develop and validate electrical prototypes for NEO. You will work with engineers and technicians to turn electrical designs into physical builds, test what works, and resolve what does not before designs move forward.

This is a hands-on role for someone who can move between design, assembly, testing, and debugging, and who cares about how electrical systems are built as much as how they work on paper.

## **Your Charter**

Develop and validate electrical prototypes and harnessing solutions for NEO, working with the Prototyping team to turn designs into reliable, buildable hardware.

You will bridge the gap between electrical design and physical builds, using what you learn from prototypes, testing, and rework to improve the next iteration.

## **Key Outcomes**

* Develop electrical prototypes, wire harnesses, and cable assembly designs that support testing and iteration on NEO.
* Translate schematics and wiring diagrams into clear build documentation, and work with technicians to resolve issues found during assembly.
* Plan and run continuity and fault-isolation testing, identifying opens, shorts, and connection issues before hardware moves downstream.
* Debug issues on prototypes and on the robot, then make practical design or documentation changes based on what you find.
* Improve recurring builds through better documentation, fixturing, and design-for-manufacturability work with the wider team.
* Partner across engineering and fabrication to move from early prototypes toward reliable, repeatable assemblies.

## **Key Competencies**

Electrical design and prototyping: You can turn electrical requirements into schematics, wiring documentation, and testable physical builds.

Diagnostic instinct: You isolate connection and circuit faults methodically and use test results to guide the next design iteration.

Build awareness: You understand how choices in routing, connections, and documentation affect assembly, rework, and reliability.

Collaboration: You work closely with technicians and other engineers, taking feedback from physical builds back into the design.

## **Minimum Requirements**

* 3–7 years of experience in electrical engineering or a related hands-on hardware development role.
* Experience developing and debugging electrical prototypes, harnesses, or cable assemblies.
* Ability to read and create electrical schematics and wiring documentation.
* Experience using measurement tools to test continuity and diagnose electrical faults.
* Experience working with technicians or fabrication partners to build, test, and iterate on hardware.

## **Preferred Skills**

* Experience with on-robot electrical debugging and rework.
* Familiarity with IPC-620 acceptance criteria or IPC-7711 / 7722 rework standards.
* Experience improving harness builds through fixturing, documentation, or design-for-manufacturability work.
* Familiarity with power, signal, shielding, and grounding considerations in physical assemblies.
