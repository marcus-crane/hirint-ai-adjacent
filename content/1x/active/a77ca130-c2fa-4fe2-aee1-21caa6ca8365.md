# **About the Team**

The Softgoods team is a group of makers, engineers, and designers united by a

passion for creating products people love to touch, trust, and use. We bring

together rigid and soft materials—textiles, elastomers, and foams—pairing

traditional techniques with the exotic and unexpected, always with safety, a

soft-touch experience, and a warm, welcoming feel at our core, carrying every

product from first concept through prototyping to full mass production.

Your Charter: This role transforms ambitious ideas into refined, real-world

products. We're seeking someone who takes pride in building things that work

beautifully, fit together precisely, and solve real problems. Operating at the

intersection of engineering, fabrication, and product development, the ideal

candidate is equally comfortable sketching concepts, cutting and bonding

materials, and resolving manufacturing challenges alongside a cross-functional

team. You'll guide softgoods products from early proof-of-concept through

production ramp, applying technical rigor and a strong commitment to craft at

every stage. This is a fully on-site role, as the best work in this field happens

collaboratively—hands on materials, ideas advancing quickly.
