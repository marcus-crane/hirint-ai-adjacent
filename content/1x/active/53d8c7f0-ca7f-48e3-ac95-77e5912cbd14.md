# Your Charter

As a Digital Design Engineer on the Softgoods team, you'll be the senior surface modeler who turns NEO's industrial design intent into production-grade digital geometry. Working primarily in Autodesk Alias (A-Surface / B-Surface / Class-A), you'll build the high-quality surfaces behind every part people see and touch, the soft covers, trims, foams and molded elastomers that make a humanoid robot feel welcoming at home. You'll sit between industrial design and engineering, translating sketches, clay, and scan data into precise, manufacturable surfaces, and carrying that geometry through tooling release and into mass production. Every Class-A surface you create must integrate seamlessly and functionally with NEO, flowing cleanly into adjacent parts while respecting the robot's joints, range of motion, sensors, thermal paths, and serviceability. Your surfaces become the single source of truth that patterns, molds, fixtures, and parts are built from. This is a fully on-site role, as the best work in this field happens collaboratively with surfaces reviewed against physical prototypes, hands on materials, ideas advancing quickly.
