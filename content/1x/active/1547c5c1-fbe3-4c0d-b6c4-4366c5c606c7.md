**About the Team:**

Test Engineering builds the systems that decide whether NEO hardware is ready to ship. We sit at the intersection of R&D, design, engineering and production. Developing the functional and end-of-line test stations that catch defects early, gate quality on the line, and turn production data into engineering action. As NEO scales into high-volume production, our work is what lets 1X grow without compromising quality.

**Your Charter:** Develop and own test systems for NEO's electronics and robotic subassemblies as they move from design release into high-volume production. This is a hands-on role spanning software, electronics, firmware, fixtures, and production operations — you'll own stations from requirements through deployment and long-term support. You won't just run tests; you'll build the systems that decide whether robot hardware ships.

**Key Outcomes:**

* Own functional and end-of-line test stations for robot subassemblies (feet, head, battery, hands, arms, power boards) across their full lifecycle — requirements through deployment, maintenance, and corrective action.
* Ship operator-facing test apps with guided flows, clear pass/fail verdicts, robust error handling, and unit-level traceability.
* Improve station uptime, cycle time, test coverage, data quality, and deployment repeatability as production scales.
* Reduce escaped defects by reproducing and resolving failures across hardware, firmware, software, instruments, fixtures, and process — driving corrective action when stations or products fail.
* Connect results, serial numbers, board IDs, and logs into test-data systems so production data becomes actionable insight.

**Key Competencies:**

* Strong software development in Python and C++ for test applications, instrument automation, and station infrastructure.
* Systems thinking across electronics, firmware, software, robotics hardware, fixtures, and production processes.
* Root-cause debugging grounded in data, logs, measurement, and controlled experimentation.
* Hands-on hardware ability: reading schematics, probing boards, debugging signals, and using bench instrumentation.
* Bias to action: makes decisions under production pressure, executes, and owns the result.

**Minimum Requirements:**

* Background in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Robotics, or equivalent practical experience.
* Proficiency in Python and/or C++, with willingness to work in both.
* Ability to read schematics and debug electronics at the board or subsystem level.
* Exposure to embedded systems, microcontrollers, or protocols such as CAN, SPI, I2C, UART, Ethernet, or EtherCAT.
* Hands-on experience with bench instrumentation (oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, multimeters, programmable power supplies).

**Preferred Skills:**

* Building production test stations, end-of-line testers, hardware-in-the-loop systems, or test infrastructure.
* Robotics, electromechanical systems, motor controllers, sensors, actuators, batteries, embedded Linux, or real-time systems.
* Deploying and maintaining software across multiple production PCs or factory stations.
* Test-data pipelines, traceability, dashboards, WATS, MES, SQL, Splunk, or similar.
* Linux, Docker, Git, CI/CD, Bazel, remote deployment, or automated station management.
* AI-assisted development tools for faster software development, debugging, documentation, or fixture bring-up.
