# About the Team

The Robot Head team develops the mechanical systems that enable NEO to perceive, communicate, and interact naturally with people. The team designs and integrates structural, electromechanical, and sensing systems that house cameras, microphones, antennas, computing hardware, sensors, and other critical technologies within one of the robot's most complex assemblies.

Working closely with Electrical Engineering, Software, Industrial Design, Manufacturing, and Supply Chain teams, we transform innovative concepts into robust, production-ready hardware that balances performance, reliability, aesthetics, and manufacturability.

# Your Charter

Design and deliver the mechanical systems that power NEO's head assembly.

You will own mechanical components and assemblies throughout the entire product lifecycle—from concept development and prototyping through validation, manufacturing, and production ramp. Your work will directly impact the robot's perception, reliability, thermal performance, structural integrity, safety, and overall user experience.

# Key Outcomes

* Design and release mechanical components and assemblies supporting cameras, microphones, sensors, antennas, wire harnesses, PCBAs, and computing modules
* Deliver robust mechanical designs that meet structural, thermal, reliability, manufacturability, and aesthetic requirements
* Develop detailed CAD models, engineering drawings, GD&T documentation, specifications, and assembly instructions
* Support prototype builds, mechanical integration, testing, reliability validation, and failure analysis throughout development
* Improve manufacturability and scalability by partnering with Manufacturing and Supply Chain teams to implement DFM/DFA best practices
* Collaborate across Mechanical, Electrical, Software, Industrial Design, and Systems Engineering teams to optimize the complete robot architecture

# Key Competencies

* Strong mechanical design fundamentals with excellent first-principles engineering skills

* Strong understanding of materials, tolerance analysis, thermal performance, structural stiffness, vibration control, and durability
