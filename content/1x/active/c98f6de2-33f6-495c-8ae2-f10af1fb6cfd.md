# About the Team

The Power Systems team develops the electrical architectures and power electronics that enable NEO to operate safely, reliably, and efficiently. We design and integrate power conversion, distribution, motor drives, battery interfaces, and embedded electrical systems across the robot.

Our work spans high-frequency switching electronics, power integrity, electromagnetic compatibility, sensing, communication interfaces, and system-level electrical integration. We collaborate closely with Electrical Engineering, Firmware, Mechanical Engineering, Controls, and Systems Engineering to solve complex challenges associated with tightly integrated robotic hardware.

As NEO moves from development into scaled production, ensuring electromagnetic compatibility across interconnected electrical systems is critical to product performance, safety, reliability, and manufacturing readiness.

# Your Charter

**Own the EMI/EMC strategy and solve complex electromagnetic interference challenges across NEO's robotic platform.**

As a Staff Electrical Engineer specializing in EMI/EMC, you will serve as the technical authority responsible for investigating, debugging, and resolving complex electromagnetic interference and compatibility issues across NEO's electrical systems.

You will lead system-level EMI/EMC investigations, identify interference sources and coupling mechanisms, develop mitigation strategies, and validate corrective actions through rigorous testing and quantitative measurements.

Working hands-on with electrical hardware, laboratory instrumentation, and fully integrated robotic systems, you will partner with engineering teams to improve grounding, shielding, filtering, PCB layout, cable routing, and system-level electrical integration.

You will establish EMI/EMC engineering standards, influence electrical architecture decisions, mentor engineers, and develop scalable validation methodologies that reduce development risk and improve reliability across the robotic platform.

# Key Outcomes

* Lead EMI/EMC technical strategy, investigation, and resolution across PCB, subsystem, and complete robot levels.
* Identify, characterize, and resolve conducted and radiated electromagnetic interference generated by switching power supplies, DC-DC converters, motor drives, battery management systems, chargers, and power distribution electronics.
* Diagnose complex common-mode and differential-mode noise, ground loops, parasitic coupling, switching transients, and interference affecting sensitive electronic systems.
* Develop and implement effective EMI mitigation strategies involving PCB layout, grounding architecture, shielding, filtering, cable routing, and component selection.
* Establish repeatable EMI/EMC testing, troubleshooting, and validation methodologies that accelerate root-cause identification and corrective action.
* Lead conducted and radiated emissions testing, electromagnetic immunity testing, ESD testing, and other relevant pre-compliance evaluations.
* Validate corrective actions through quantitative measurements, documented test results, and before-and-after performance comparisons.
* Drive system-level electrical integration improvements to prevent interference between power electronics, embedded controllers, sensors, and communication interfaces.
* Lead external compliance testing efforts and resolve EMI/EMC failures identified during formal qualification.
* Partner with Power Systems, Electrical Engineering, Firmware, Controls, Mechanical Engineering, and Systems Engineering teams to address complex cross-functional interference challenges.
* Establish EMI/EMC design guidelines, validation standards, and engineering best practices that reduce late-stage redesigns and improve product readiness.
* Provide technical leadership and mentorship to engineers across multiple hardware teams.
