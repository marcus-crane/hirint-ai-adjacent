## **Sr. Mechanical Engineer, Prototyping**

**Role Name:** Sr. Mechanical Engineer  
**Department:** R&D Prototyping  
**Location:** San Carlos

**About 1X**We’re building humanoid robots that work in home - doing the chores, handling the tasks, and giving people their time back. Simple, but it’s not.

To do this right, we have to solve robotics, AI, manufacturing - at the same time, at scale, in a form factor that has to be safe enough to live with your family. If you’re inspired by this, you’ll thrive here. We’ve been at this since 2014 and we’re at the point where the hard problems are behind us and the hard work is in front of us.

NEO is our flagship - a home robot designed to move, learn, and operate in the real world alongside real people. We’re not demoing it - we’re shipping it. We’re excited to meet you, if this excites you.

If you’ve spent your career working on problems that matter and want to see them actually reach the world - this is that moment. We’re scaling, we’re hiring with intention, and we need people who want to build something that will genuinely change how humans spend their time - safely creating abundance for all.

**About the Team:**This team has the widest range of projects at 1X. Where other teams go deep on one subsystem, we work across all of it: NEO itself, the ecosystem that supports it, the consumer products we sell alongside it, and new robot concepts and frontier ideas. The work spans the full lifecycle, from early-stage concepts and new technologies to products shipping to customers. Your next project may have nothing in common with your last.

If you enjoy variety, ownership, and building things from the ground up very quickly, this is a team where you will have a direct impact.

**Your Charter:** You will own hardware projects end to end, often starting from unclear requirements, with the independence to decide how you solve them. You define the requirements, design and build the hardware, and test it in the field, moving across disciplines as the work demands. You will be trusted with real responsibility from day one and expected to deliver.

**Key Outcomes:**

* Own complex hardware from concept through design, analysis, build and test
* Define requirements for open-ended problems and turn them into working designs
* Deliver tested hardware on aggressive schedules, in weeks, not quarters
* Resolve whatever blocks delivery, including sourcing or fabricating parts yourself when needed
* Lead root-cause investigations of failures and implement corrective actions
* Provide technical input on projects outside your own through design reviews
* Adopt new hardware tools, methods and agentic AI to improve how you design and build

**Key Competencies:**

* **Mechanical intuition:** Strong mechanical fundamentals, with a practical understanding of how parts are made, assembled and used
* **Independence:** Able to work independently from unclear requirements
* **Craft:** Strong mechanical design skill, producing practical, manufacturable hardware quickly
* **Adaptability:** Able to switch between very different projects and work outside a narrow specialty
* **Ownership:** Takes full ownership of results and sees every problem through to resolution
* **Learns and adapts:** Continually learns and adapts to new hardware tools, methods and agentic AI

**Minimum Requirements:**

* Bachelor’s degree in mechanical engineering or a related field, or equivalent practical experience
* 5+ years of experience with mechanical design and hardware product development
* Experience taking products or prototypes from concept through design, fabrication, assembly and test
* Experience managing multiple hardware projects with competing priorities
* Strong knowledge of manufacturing processes and fabrication constraints

**Preferred Skills:**

* Experience designing, building, testing and troubleshooting mechanical and electromechanical products
* Hands-on experience with 3D printing, machining, sheet metal, casting or other fabrication methods
* Experience working with machine shops, contract manufacturers and other fabrication partners
* Experience developing consumer products intended for real-world customer use
* Experience using agentic AI tools and scripting, such as Python, in your engineering work
* Leadership experience on a project team
* Portfolio of prototypes, products or hardware projects you personally designed and built
* Self-motivated, with strong organizational, written and spoken communication

**What does a successful 1X Team Member look like:**

Team members at 1X who thrive here are builders. They move fast, own their work completely, and treat time like it’s the one thing you can’t get back - because it is. They say what they mean, finish what they start, and hold themselves to a standard before anyone has to ask. We push each other to be better, and we do it with honesty and respect.
