# About the Team

The Power Systems team develops the electrical and mechanical architectures that store, convert, distribute, and manage energy throughout NEO. We design and integrate battery packs, chargers, DC-DC converters, power distribution electronics, and supporting mechanical and thermal systems that enable safe, reliable, and efficient robot operation.

Our work spans mechanical design, thermal management, structural engineering, materials selection, electromechanical integration, reliability, and manufacturing readiness. We collaborate closely with Electrical Engineering, Firmware, Systems Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing, and Supply Chain teams to develop compact, lightweight, and robust power systems.

As NEO moves from development into scaled production, our team is responsible for delivering power systems that meet demanding performance, thermal, safety, reliability, and manufacturability requirements.

# Your Charter

**Own the mechanical and thermal architecture of the power systems that enable NEO to operate safely, efficiently, and reliably.**

As a Staff Mechanical & Thermal Engineer on the Power Systems team, you will lead the mechanical design, thermal management, structural validation, and manufacturing readiness of battery packs, chargers, DC-DC converters, and power distribution electronics.

You will own complex electromechanical hardware from initial architecture and requirements definition through detailed design, simulation, prototyping, validation, certification support, production release, and sustaining engineering.

Your work will focus on developing compact, lightweight, structurally robust, and thermally efficient power systems that meet demanding performance, reliability, safety, and manufacturing requirements.

You will remain deeply hands-on with CAD, FEA, CFD, prototype builds, thermal characterization, and mechanical testing while providing technical leadership, establishing engineering practices, and mentoring engineers across multidisciplinary teams.

# Key Outcomes

* Define and drive mechanical and thermal architectures for battery packs, chargers, DC-DC converters, power distribution electronics, and associated assemblies.
* Develop compact, lightweight mechanical designs optimized for structural integrity, thermal performance, electrical integration, serviceability, and manufacturability.
* Own detailed mechanical design, including 3D CAD assemblies, engineering drawings, GD&T, tolerance stack analysis, and production documentation.
* Design and optimize enclosures, mounting structures, brackets, thermal interfaces, seals, mechanical retention features, and supporting components.
* Lead thermal design and analysis of power electronics and battery systems under continuous, peak, and transient operating conditions.
* Develop effective thermal management solutions for battery cells, power MOSFETs, magnetic components, PCBs, and power conversion assemblies.
* Perform CFD and heat-transfer simulations to evaluate conduction, convection, radiation, airflow, and thermal performance.
* Design and optimize heatsinks, thermal interfaces, heat spreaders, cooling structures, and enclosure ventilation.
* Perform structural FEA to evaluate vibration, shock, drop, mechanical loading, thermal expansion, fatigue, and material failure risks.
* Develop and execute mechanical, thermal, environmental, vibration, shock, and durability validation plans.
* Conduct hands-on thermal characterization using thermocouples, thermal cameras, data acquisition systems, and environmental chambers.
* Correlate simulation results with physical testing and refine mechanical and thermal models based on measured performance.
* Partner with Electrical and Firmware teams to develop thermal derating strategies and optimize power system integration.
* Lead root-cause investigations of mechanical and thermal failures and implement verified corrective actions.
* Drive Design for Manufacturability and Assembly (DFM/DFA), supplier collaboration, tooling development, and production readiness.
* Support manufacturing ramp, supplier qualification, failure analysis, and continuous product improvement.
* Establish mechanical and thermal design standards, simulation methodologies, and validation best practices.
* Provide technical leadership and mentorship while driving complex cross-functional engineering decisions.
