## **About the Team**

The Supply Chain team builds and scales the global supplier network behind NEO, taking complex electromechanical systems from concept to mass production alongside Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality, and Operations. Every robot we ship depends on the right parts arriving at the right cost, quality, and volume. Our work directly decides how fast NEO ramps and what it costs to build.

## **Your Charter**

The mission of this role is to make sure NEO never stops for an electrical part. You'll own the sourcing strategy, commercial relationships, and supply continuity for NEO's electrical components - electrical, electronic, and electromechanical parts such as semiconductors and passives, connectors, battery cells, and sensors. These are the parts most exposed to allocation, long lead times, and end-of-life notices, and the ones where a single missing component can halt a build. You'll stay ahead of that market so NEO can ramp on time and at the right cost. You'll work alongside the Structures and Motors & Magnets Global Supply Managers, and partner with Electrical Engineering and Supplier Development Engineering on part selection and supplier capability, including the battery team on cell evaluation and qualification.

## **Key Outcomes**

* Own the sourcing strategy for each electrical commodity - semiconductors and passives, connectors, battery cells, and sensors - delivering competitive cost, quality, capacity, and technology advantages.
* Secure supply continuity for every electrical part through long-term agreements, allocation planning, buffer strategy, and approved alternates, so no NEO build waits on an electrical component.
* Drive measurable cost reduction across the electrical portfolio through negotiation, direct manufacturer relationships, market intelligence, and design-for-sourcing input to engineering.
* Build and maintain a global network of manufacturers and authorized distributors that scales from new product introduction through high-volume production.
* Manage lifecycle and supply risk across the portfolio, including end-of-life notices, product change notices, single-source parts, and counterfeit prevention.

## **Key Competencies**

* Deep understanding of electrical component markets - semiconductors, passives, connectors, battery cells, and sensors - including lead-time cycles, allocation, and pricing dynamics.
* Strong commercial negotiator with demonstrated success managing complex agreements with component manufacturers and authorized distributors.
* Ability to evaluate supplier capabilities, manufacturing processes, cost structures, and scalability risks, including battery cell quality and safety requirements.
* Analytical and data-driven, with experience managing cost models, demand and supply forecasts, and multi-year commodity strategies.
* Excellent cross-functional partner who influences electrical engineering, manufacturing, quality, and supplier stakeholders on part selection, alternates, and supply tradeoffs.

## **Minimum Requirements**

* Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Supply Chain, Business, Operations, or a related discipline.
* 6+ years of experience in global sourcing, strategic sourcing, commodity management, or procurement.
* Direct experience sourcing electrical components across at least two of: semiconductors or passives, connectors, battery cells, or sensors.
* Proven track record developing sourcing strategies, negotiating commercial agreements, and managing supply through allocation or shortage periods.
* Experience supporting new product introduction (NPI), production ramp-ups, and global supplier management.

## **Preferred Skills**

* Experience sourcing lithium-ion battery cells, including familiarity with cell qualification and transport and safety standards such as UN 38.3 and IEC 62133.
* Experience managing component lifecycle risk, including end-of-life planning, last-time buys, and qualifying alternate parts.
* Experience in robotics, automotive, aerospace, consumer electronics, or other high-volume electromechanical industries.
