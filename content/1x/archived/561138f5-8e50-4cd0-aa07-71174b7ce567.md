## **About the Team**

The Prototyping team turns designs into physical parts, models, and prototypes that help 1X move quickly from concept to reality. We work directly with industrial design and engineering to solve complex machining and fabrication challenges and deliver high-quality parts for rapid iteration and product development.

## **Your Charter**

Own the machining of complex prototype components from material preparation through programming, setup, machining, and inspection. You’ll use multi-axis CNC equipment and CAD/CAM tools to produce accurate, high-quality parts that help engineering and design teams rapidly iterate on NEO.

## **Key Outcomes**

* Deliver complex prototype components that meet drawing, dimensional, tolerance, and surface-finish requirements.
* Program, set up, and operate multi-axis mill-turn CNC equipment to efficiently produce prototype parts and complex geometries.
* Translate 3D CAD/CAM data and detailed part drawings into reliable machining strategies and finished components.
* Consistently hold tight tolerances through appropriate tooling, workholding, machining parameters, and in-process inspection.
* Inspect finished components using hand metrology and identify and resolve dimensional or machining issues before delivery.
* Maintain safe, organized, and production-ready machining operations across projects and equipment.
* Communicate clearly with internal engineering and design customers on project status, timelines, technical constraints, and potential impacts to deliverables.

## **Key Competencies**

* Deep hands-on CNC machining expertise with strong setup, programming, and troubleshooting skills.
* Comfortable machining complex 3D parts, including free-form and non-standard geometries.
* Strong understanding of cutting tools, workholding, feeds and speeds, and machining strategy.
* High attention to dimensional accuracy, surface finish, and overall part quality.
* Strong ability to read and interpret engineering drawings, GD&T, and 3D CAD data.
* Self-directed and resourceful, with the judgment to determine the best approach for one-off and rapidly changing prototype work.
* Strong communicator who can work directly with engineers, designers, and other internal customers.

## **Minimum Requirements**

* 5+ years of experience setting up, operating, and programming CNC machines.
* Experience with multi-axis CNC machining, including mill-turn equipment.
* Experience programming in a 3D CAD/CAM environment; Esprit experience is preferred.
* Experience machining complex 3D parts and holding tight tolerances.
* Proficiency with hand metrology including calipers, micrometers, and surface plate inspection.
* High school diploma or equivalent combination of education and applicable experience.

## **Preferred Skills**

* Experience with G-code/M-code and machine-level CNC programming.
* Experience machining a wide range of materials.
* Experience operating horizontal and vertical saws, manual mills, and lathes.
* Strong understanding of GD&T and detailed part drawing requirements.
* Experience in a prototype, model-making, industrial design, robotics, or other high-mix development environment.
* Strong organizational skills and ability to manage multiple prototype projects with changing priorities.
