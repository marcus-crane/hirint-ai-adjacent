**Senior Account Executive, Germany PUBSEC (Berlin, Germany)**

**Why this role?**

As a Senior Account Executive at Cohere you will have ownership of the full sales cycle - from identifying leads to closing deals, with Defense, Federal Civilian and Local Government Agencies in Germany.

In this role you will be focussed on business development activity across all PUBSEC Government Agencies and Departments in Germany.

We’re looking for an approachable and compelling communicator who loves working with public sector customers to uncover their needs and feels comfortable developing tailored value propositions around how Cohere’s platform can help them drive better mission and citizen outcomes. You’ll lay the foundation for Cohere’s growth by owning your territory and collaborating with teammates across Customer Success, Sales Development, Marketing, and Sales Engineering. You’ll be the voice of the field and help our Product and Engineering teams prioritize the Cohere roadmap with customer-centric care. In this highly self-directed role, you will thrive in a quickly evolving environment.

**As a Senior Account Executive, you will**:

* Drive net-new customer acquisition through targeted outbound activity, senior leader engagement, and relationship building in Germany.
* Develop and execute a sales plan for Germany, prioritizing accounts and opportunities through collaboration with Cohere leadership.
* Act as a trusted advisor and thought partner to public sector customers and prospects by understanding their goals and shaping AI solutions that deliver measurable value
* Work closely with channel and regional partners, as well as major system integrators to scale outreach, accelerate adoption, and maximize customer success
* Leverage senior-level relationships with executives and government decision-makers to drive strategic opportunities and long-term partnerships
* Collaborate with Product and Engineering teams on strategic efforts to deliver enterprise and public-sector solutions
* Partner with SDRs and Marketing to drive top-of-funnel activity and overall territory growth
* Own the full sales cycle; including discovery, technical and business validation, proof-of-concept, procurement, and deal close within a team-selling environment

**You may be a good fit if you have:**

* 10+ years of direct public sector sales experience in Germany, with a proven track record of closing $3M+ annual quotas.
* Deep knowledge of PUBSEC procurement and budget cycles, decision-making processes, and how to work with regional partners and resellers.
* Established network in Germany, including federal agencies, prime contractors, and industry associations.
* Experience selling SaaS, AI/ML, or enterprise software solutions to government clients.
* High tolerance for ambiguity, you are both a strategist and a hands-on operator capable of building a market from the ground up
* Strong organizational skills, with the ability to manage multiple internal and external stakeholders across fast-moving deals
* Creativity and strong technical acumen to structure unique, mutually beneficial partnerships
* Excellent communication skills, you listen deeply, understand what matters to senior decision-makers, and confidently present to both technical and non-technical audiences.
* English and German language proficiency essential.
* Must be based in Germany with willingness to travel (25-35%).
