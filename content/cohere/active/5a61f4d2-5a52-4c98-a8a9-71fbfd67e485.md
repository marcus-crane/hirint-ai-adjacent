## **Who are we?**

Cohere is the leading security-first enterprise AI company. We build cutting-edge foundation AI models and end-to-end products that are designed to solve real-world business problems.

We’re training and deploying frontier models for enterprises who are building AI systems. We believe that our work is instrumental to the widespread adoption of AI and we are looking for folks that want to be part of that.

We obsess over what we build. Each one of us is responsible for contributing to increasing the capabilities of our models and the value they drive for our customers. Cohere is a team of researchers, engineers, designers, and more, who are all passionate about their craft.

We are a global technology company headquartered in Toronto with key offices in London, New York City, San Francisco, Montreal, Paris, Berlin and Seoul. Join us!

**About the team**

The Cohere Total Rewards team is entering an exciting growth phase! Our mission is to deliver a reward strategy that attracts, retains, and rewards world-class AI talent, in support of Cohere’s rapid global growth. To help us achieve that goal, we’re hiring an experienced Technical Compensation Business Partner to own compensation design and execution for our Technical organization, and help build the compensation foundations for the wider business too.

**About the Role**

This is a hands-on individual-contributor role for someone who brings deep experience with AI/ML compensation, and who wants to have a foundational impact on a global business.

Reporting into the Head of Total Rewards, you will partner closely with Technical leaders, HR Business Partners, Talent, Finance, and other cross-functional teams, leading market benchmarking, offers, and program design for highly sought-after technical talent - as well as contributing to wider compensation projects.

You’ll thrive in this role if you’re comfortable operating in ambiguity, have strong judgment and the ability to independently execute, and understand how to balance speed with thoughtfulness. This role also requires significant experience with international compensation.

**Key responsibilities**

* Act as the dedicated Compensation Business Partner for the Technical organization - owning compensation benchmarking, competitive intelligence, market analysis, and offer management for Technical roles across all markets;
* Act as a trusted compensation advisor to our HRBPS and to leaders in our Technical org, supporting them with complex, high-stakes decisions
* Design compensation programs, processes, and ways of working that support Cohere’s fast-growing business;
* Support the design and delivery of our cyclical pay reviews, equity incentives, and other major programs, and own your groups’ compensation cycle actions end to end;
* Act as a SME for our compensation technology and tooling;
* Collaborate with the wider Compensation team and other stakeholders (Legal, Finance, HR Ops, Payroll) on a range of other compensation-related work including policy design, M&A, and pay equity and transparency.

**Required Experience & Qualifications**

* 6+ years of compensation experience, including significant experience with compensation for AI/ML talent;
* Demonstrated expertise in compensation strategy, benchmarking, and pay range design, preferably in a high-growth environment;
* Significant experience with compensation in North America, the UK, and Europe;
* Proven ability to bring clarity to ambiguous bodies of work, and own the outcomes;
* Strong working knowledge of international compensation practices and the regulatory landscape (e.g. pay transparency);
* Strong working knowledge of equity compensation;
* Strong analytical skills with the ability to leverage complex data to support rapid decision-making;
* Exceptional written and verbal communication skills and the ability to build trust with senior stakeholders;
* Adept at educating leaders and managers on compensation fundamentals and driving engagement with compensation programs and initiatives;
* Experience deploying and managing Pave or similar compensation platforms.
