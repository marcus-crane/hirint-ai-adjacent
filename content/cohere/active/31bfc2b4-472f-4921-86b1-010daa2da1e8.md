**Role summary:**  
The Managing Editor runs the operational engine of the editorial team. This role turns strategy and drafts into a functioning production pipeline across all content streams (thought leadership, developer content, customer stories, product marketing content, etc.). This person is most responsible for whether things ship on time and at a consistent bar.

**Core responsibilities:**

* In conjunction with Editorial Content Director, own and maintain the master editorial calendar across all content streams, sequencing work so there is no competition for the same limited writing capacity
* Manage the brief-to-publish workflow: assigning work, setting deadlines, tracking status, unblocking bottlenecks
* Own the review/approval routing for each content type: legal and PR for thought leadership, engineering/DevRel sign-off for developer content, customer and legal approval for customer stories, product marketing alignment for product content
* Set and enforce production-level style and quality standards (distinct from the Content Strategist's audience/strategy standards)
* Manage relationships with freelancers, contractors, and cross-functional SME contributors who feed content into the pipeline
* Report to the Head of Editorial Content on throughput, capacity, and delivery risk
* Own and manage the 24-hour review in the blog tracker
* Run competitive and content-gap analysis to identify where the team's limited capacity should go
* Ensures style guide story alignment (with Content Editor contributing)
* Present editorial performance metrics to cross-functional stakeholders in conjunction with Editorial Director
* Partner with Growth Marketing on content positioning alignment
* Work with MarOps on attribution and any potential pipeline content

**Qualifications**

* 8+ years of experience in editorial or content operations
* Experience running multi-stream or multi-format editorial pipelines (not just a single blog)
