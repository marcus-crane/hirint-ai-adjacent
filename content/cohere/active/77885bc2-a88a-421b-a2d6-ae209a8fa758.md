**About the role:**

Cohere is at the forefront of AI innovation, building cutting-edge language models and AI systems. Our Finance team plays a critical role in supporting our rapid growth and ensuring operational excellence. We value technical expertise, collaborative problem-solving, and a commitment to accuracy and compliance. This role offers the opportunity to make a significant impact on our financial infrastructure while working at the intersection of finance and transformative technology. As we scale our business and navigate an evolving financial landscape, you'll play a pivotal role in driving operational excellence and serving as a key partner on accounting implications of major business decisions.

**In this role, you will:**

* Lead the complete month-end close process, ensuring timely and accurate financial reporting across all entities
* Prepare and review the consolidated financial statements, including the statement of cash flows, and support various management reporting requirements
* Own lease accounting, including accounting assessment under ASC 842, recording and reconciliation of lease-related account transactions, financial reporting, and disclosures
* Own equity accounting such as stock-based compensation under ASC 718 and IFRS, as well as all other equity-related balance sheet accounts
* Own deferred commission accounting under ASC 340-40, including capitalization, amortization, and reconciliation of contract acquisition costs, and related disclosures
* Oversee foreign currency accounting, including functional currency assessment, transaction exposure, and translation adjustments
* Assist with recording purchase accounting, including purchase price adjustments, acquisition-related expenses, fair value allocations, and related reconciliations
* Direct general ledger operations, account reconciliations, and financial statement preparation
* Implement and maintain robust accounting policies, procedures, systems, and internal controls
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure financial accuracy and support business growth
* Leverage technology solutions to enhance accounting efficiency and accuracy

**You may be a good fit if you:**

* Have 7+ years of progressive accounting experience, with prior leadership in fast-paced environments
* Possess strong technical accounting skills and deep understanding of GAAP principles, including lease accounting, equity accounting, business combination, and foreign currency
* Have direct experience owning month-end close processes using close management tools such as Numeric
* Demonstrate expertise in purchase accounting, including acquisitions, purchase price adjustments, and related reconciliations
* Experience with leveraging AI tools to build and improve workflows to achieve operational efficiency and accuracy
* Demonstrate excellence in problem-solving and process improvement methodologies
* Show proficiency with financial systems (Netsuite preferred, but not required)
* Exhibit strong communication skills and ability to collaborate across departments
* Thrive in high-growth, dynamic environments with evolving priorities
* Have experience building scalable accounting processes in technology companies
