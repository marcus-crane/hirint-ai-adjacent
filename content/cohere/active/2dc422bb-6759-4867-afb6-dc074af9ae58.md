**About the Role:**  
  
Cohere is at the forefront of AI innovation, building cutting-edge language models and AI systems. Our Finance team plays a critical role in supporting our rapid growth and ensuring operational excellence. We value technical expertise, collaborative problem-solving, and a commitment to accuracy and compliance. This role offers the opportunity to make a significant impact on our financial infrastructure while working at the intersection of finance and transformative technology. As we scale our business and navigate an evolving financial landscape, you'll play a pivotal role in driving operational excellence and serving as a key partner on accounting implications of major business decisions.

**In this role, you will:**

* Own assigned areas, including journal entries and complex balance sheet reconciliations, together with the related internal controls.
* Prepare the consolidated financial statements, including the statement of cash flows, and support various management reporting requirements
* Analyze fluctuation and variances and explain drivers and methodology to accounting leaders and finance partners
* Maintain the chart of accounts, supporting account mapping, entity setup, and system changes
* Record and reconcile intercompany transactions, including recharges, loans, and cost allocations
* Run monthly intercompany matching and settlement, and resolve differences with counterparties across entities
* Support eliminations in consolidation, and foreign currency remeasurement and translation, ensuring NetSuite processes operate correctly
* Serve as the operational lead for close activities in Numeric, NetSuite, and related systems, maintain the close calendar and checklist, and flag risks or delays to the accounting leaders early
* Partner with technology and systems teams to identify, pilot, and deploy automation and AI-enabled solutions across reconciliations, recurring analyses, exception triage, flux commentary, and evidence gathering.
* Implement and maintain robust accounting policies, procedures, systems, and internal controls
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure financial accuracy and support business growth
* Leverage technology solutions to enhance accounting efficiency and accuracy

**You may be a good fit if you:**

* Have 5+ years of progressive accounting experience, with prior leadership in fast-paced environments
* Hands-on experience with month-end close, GL accounting, and account reconciliations, using close management tools such as Numeric
* Working knowledge of US GAAP, with experience preparing financial statements and supporting ad-hoc financial reporting requests
* Experience with multi-entity accounting, including intercompany reconciliation, eliminations, and foreign currency
* Experience with leveraging AI tools to build and improve workflows to achieve operational efficiency and accuracy
* Demonstrate excellence in problem-solving and process improvement methodologies
* Show proficiency with financial systems (Netsuite preferred, but not required)
* Exhibit strong communication skills and ability to collaborate across departments
* Thrive in high-growth, dynamic environments with evolving priorities
* Have experience building scalable accounting processes in technology companies
