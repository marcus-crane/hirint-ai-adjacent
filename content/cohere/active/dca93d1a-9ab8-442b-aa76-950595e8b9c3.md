## **Who are we?**

Cohere is the leading security-first enterprise AI company. We build cutting-edge foundation AI models and end-to-end products that are designed to solve real-world business problems.

We’re training and deploying frontier models for enterprises who are building AI systems. We believe that our work is instrumental to the widespread adoption of AI and we are looking for folks that want to be part of that.

We obsess over what we build. Each one of us is responsible for contributing to increasing the capabilities of our models and the value they drive for our customers. Cohere is a team of researchers, engineers, designers, and more, who are all passionate about their craft.

We are a global technology company headquartered in Toronto with key offices in London, New York City, San Francisco, Montreal, Paris, Berlin and Seoul. Join us!

## **Why this role?**

In this role, you will manage the team that owns the Cohere inference APIs and SDKs, as well as the Cohere Dashboard, where customers can access our models, manage teams, and audit billing. You will collaborate with internal partners to craft a delightful, intuitive, and unified experience across everything the Cohere APIs and Cohere Dashboard have to offer. As a hands-on leader, you will shift seamlessly between roles as mentor, strategist, and technologist to guide the team toward shared success.

Join our team of builders who are shaping the future of large language models!

## **As an Engineering Manager of the Cohere APIs and DevEx team, you will:**

* Manage, mentor, and grow a talented and enthusiastic team of backend and full-stack software engineers
* Participate in model releases and develop new API capabilities
* Work closely with Modelling and Model Serving teams
* Collaborate with UX designers to ensure customers have an easy and delightful time managing teams, API keys, billing and configurations.
* Oversee and contribute to open source projects

## **You may be a good fit if:**

* You have experience managing engineering teams and a proven track record of delivering high-quality software
* You have experience shipping products with a large number of stakeholders
* You’re comfortable with the front and back end of the stack, and have previously worked with Go, Next.js, TypeScript, and React
* You are passionate about recruiting and growing people and teams
* You are passionate about building rapport with your team and you prioritize 1:1s
* You have excellent communication skills and believe that documentation is a great way to get feedback and discuss technical implementations and designs
* You have the ability to take complex needs and goals and develop easy to understand explanations for all audiences
* You’re excited about AI and have the motivation to scale a company
