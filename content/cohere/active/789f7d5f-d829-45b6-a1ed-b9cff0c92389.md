## **Who are we?**

Cohere is the leading security-first enterprise AI company. We build cutting-edge foundation AI models and end-to-end products that are designed to solve real-world business problems.

We’re training and deploying frontier models for enterprises who are building AI systems. We believe that our work is instrumental to the widespread adoption of AI and we are looking for folks that want to be part of that.

We obsess over what we build. Each one of us is responsible for contributing to increasing the capabilities of our models and the value they drive for our customers. Cohere is a team of researchers, engineers, designers, and more, who are all passionate about their craft.

We are a global technology company headquartered in Toronto with key offices in London, New York City, San Francisco, Montreal, Paris, Berlin and Seoul. Join us!

# **About the Role?**

Cohere’s Procurement team buys everything from pens to compute. We’re looking for a Lead, Direct Strategic Sourcing to own the categories closest to how we build: the technology, software, engineering talent and, over time, the compute that power our research and engineering teams.

Reporting to the Head of Procurement, you’ll build this pillar from the ground up. You’ll set category strategy, run our most complex and highest-stakes technology negotiations, and become a trusted commercial partner to engineering, research and infrastructure leaders. You’ll put the structure in place so these buys scale with both speed and control, and as the function grows, you’ll hire and lead a small team of specialists and analysts.

This is an opportunity for a hands-on, technically fluent sourcing leader who thrives in a high-growth environment, is comfortable navigating ambiguity, and wants to shape how a frontier AI company buys the technology it runs on.

# **Responsibilities:**

## **Own the engineering and R&D categories**

* Own end-to-end strategic sourcing for Cohere’s engineering and R&D spend: technology licensing, OEM partnerships, engineering and IT software, developer and infrastructure tooling, and engineering staff augmentation and services.
* Build and execute multi-year category strategies grounded in spend analysis, market intelligence, and demand forecasts built with Engineering, Research and FP&A.
* Over time, extend the remit into compute (GPU capacity, cloud commitments, data center and colocation), partnering with Infrastructure, Finance and Legal on some of the largest commitments in the company.

## **Lead technology licensing and OEM deals**

* Lead sourcing and negotiation of technology licensing and OEM agreements, including inbound licenses, third-party technology embedded in or distributed with our products, and partnerships that support customer deployments.
* Structure commercial and contractual terms (licensing models, IP and use rights, SLAs, liability, data protection, escrow and exit provisions) hand in hand with Legal, Security and Product.

## **Scale software and eng-related services buying**

* Run RFx, benchmarking and negotiations for engineering and IT software, from early-stage tools to enterprise platforms, balancing engineering velocity with cost, risk and vendor consolidation.
* Own the renewal pipeline: anticipate renewals, right-size licenses based on usage data, and drive multi-year and commitment strategies.
* Build Cohere’s engineering staff augmentation program: preferred vendor panel, rate cards, SOW standards and guardrails for contractor classification and co-employment risk across countries.

## **Partner, deliver value and keep us in control**

* Be the go-to commercial partner for Engineering, Product & Research, Infrastructure, IT and Security leaders, brought in at planning stage rather than at signature.
* Partner with Legal, Security/TPRM, FP&A and Accounting so every deal is compliant, budgeted, properly PO’d and correctly accounted for.
* Deliver and report measurable value: savings, cost avoidance, improved terms and risk reduction.
* Maintain audit-ready documentation and controls in line with Cohere’s procurement policy.
* Use Omnea and AI tooling, including Cohere’s own North, to automate intake, spend analysis and negotiation preparation.
* Create the playbooks for your categories and grow the team behind them: hire, coach and lead specialists and analysts as the function scales.

# **You may be a good fit if you have:**

* 8+ years of progressive procurement or strategic sourcing experience, with significant time owning technology categories (compute, software, technology licensing, IT and engineering services), ideally in a high-growth technology company.
* A track record of negotiating complex, high-value, multi-year technology agreements, including enterprise software and technology licensing or OEM deals.
* A deep understanding of software commercial models (SaaS, usage-based, enterprise license agreements, cloud marketplace and commit structures) and the contract terms that matter in them.
* Experience sourcing engineering staff augmentation, contingent labour or IT professional services, including SOW and rate management.
* Technical fluency: you can discuss architecture, usage and roadmap trade-offs with engineers and researchers and earn their trust.
* Strong analytical skills: TCO and should-cost modelling, spend analysis and clear, data-driven recommendations.
* The ability to influence without authority and communicate crisply with senior leaders.
* A builder’s mindset: you’re comfortable creating structure, prioritizing in ambiguity and moving fast without losing control.
* Experience with procurement and P2P tooling (Omnea and NetSuite a plus).
* A bachelor’s degree in business, engineering, computer science, finance or a related field, or equivalent experience.

## **Bonus points if you have:**
