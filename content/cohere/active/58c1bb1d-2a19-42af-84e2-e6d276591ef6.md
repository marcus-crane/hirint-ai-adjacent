We’re training and deploying frontier models for enterprises who are building AI systems. We believe that our work is instrumental to the widespread adoption of AI and we are looking for folks that want to be part of that.

We are a global technology company headquartered in Toronto with key offices in London, New York City, San Francisco, Montreal, Paris, Berlin and Seoul. Join us!

**Role summary:**  
The Content Strategist decides what the team makes and why. This role sets direction across all content streams by defining audiences, prioritizing based on business impact, and building the measurement framework that proves (or disproves) the work is paying off.

**Core responsibilities:**

* Define content strategy, pillars and priorities within each stream, tied to concrete business goals (pipeline generation, developer adoption, retention, brand awareness)
* Build audience segmentation per stream. For example, a prospective customer, a developer evaluating the product, and an existing customer being featured in a case study all need different treatment
* Own the measurement framework and reporting: SEO/traffic for blog and thought leadership, adoption/engagement for developer content, sales-assist and pipeline influence for customer stories, funnel metrics for product content
* Translate strategy into concrete briefs the Managing Editor can schedule and the Content Editor/Staff Writer can execute against
* Present content performance and ROI to the Head of Editorial Content and cross-functional stakeholders
* Drive content calendar development and planning
* Explore new content opportunities and channels
* Present ROI and performance insights to leadership
* Collaborate with Growth Marketing on messaging strategy

**Qualifications**

* 5+ years of experience in content strategy, content marketing, or a closely related discipline
* Experience with SEO, analytics tooling, and attribution modeling
* Track record connecting content output to business outcomes, not just vanity metrics
* B2B or technical industry experience is a strong plus given the developer content component
