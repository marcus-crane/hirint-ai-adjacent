## **Who are we?**

Cohere is the leading security-first enterprise AI company. We build cutting-edge foundation AI models and end-to-end products that are designed to solve real-world business problems.

We’re training and deploying frontier models for enterprises who are building AI systems. We believe that our work is instrumental to the widespread adoption of AI and we are looking for folks that want to be part of that.

We obsess over what we build. Each one of us is responsible for contributing to increasing the capabilities of our models and the value they drive for our customers. Cohere is a team of researchers, engineers, designers, and more, who are all passionate about their craft.

We are a global technology company headquartered in Toronto with key offices in London, New York City, San Francisco, Montreal, Paris, Berlin and Seoul. Join us!

## **About the team**

The Cohere Total Rewards team is entering an exciting growth phase! Our mission is to deliver a reward strategy that attracts, retains, and rewards world-class AI talent, in support of Cohere’s rapid global growth. To help us achieve that goal, we’re hiring a Senior Global Benefits Specialist to own benefits design and delivery for the global business.

## **About the Role**

This is a hands-on individual-contributor role responsible for owning Cohere’s global benefits programs across all countries where we operate. You will partner with the head of Total Rewards to set benefits strategy, and then lead day to day benefits operations working closely with colleagues in the HR Ops and Payroll teams. You’ll also own key external relationships with brokers and vendors, as well as owning our benefits technology.

This is a builder role - in addition to setting strategy, you’ll have the opportunity to audit our existing programs, identify gaps, and define roles and responsibilities when it comes to delivery, You’ll create processes from scratch, and evaluate how we use technology and third parties to be more efficient and elevate the employee experience. And you’ll have the chance to be the face of global benefits at a fast-moving international business.

You’ll thrive in this role if you’re comfortable operating in ambiguity, have strong judgment and the ability to independently execute, and understand how to balance speed with thoughtfulness. This role also requires significant experience with both North America and international benefits.

## **Key Responsibilities**

* Define our global benefits strategy alongside the head of Total Rewards.
* Own our global benefits portfolio of health, retirement, risk and wellbeing programs across our entities and EOR countries - including annual renewals and Open Enrollments.
* Run day to day benefits operations including enrollments, terminations, life events, payroll and HRIS integrations, and EOR coordination.
* Act as the first line of support for employee benefits queries, and create benefits guides, open-enrollment campaigns, and manager resources to support the business.
* Evaluate and deploy technology to support our operations, including ownership of benefits functionality on our Rippling HRIS.
* Own third party relationships, including brokers and carriers, and carry out vendor evaluations and RFPs.
* Ensure we’re meeting compliance requirements in every country - including things like ERISA, ACA, COBRA, and nondiscrimination testing for the US.
* Own the global benefits budget and forecasts with Finance.
* Own benefits set-up for new markets, entity conversions from EOR, and acquisitions
* Support the HR Ops and Payroll teams with leave of absence management

## **Required Experience & Qualifications**

* 6+ years experience managing global benefits programs, including experience building or transforming benefits programs in a high-growth tech environment.
* Highly experienced at managing benefits benchmarking and design, annual renewals, and Open Enrollment cycles.
* Demonstrated experience with vendor and broker selection and management.
* Experience implementing and managing benefits technology - in particular, Rippling.
* Deep expertise in US and Canadian benefits alongside meaningful experience with international benefits - particularly the UK, France, and Germany.
* Proven experience managing benefits compliance for North America, including ACA, HIPAA, COBRA, ERISA, and nondiscrimination testing.
* Experience with retirement plan administration and compliance including 401(k), GRSP, and UK Auto-Enrollment.
* Experience managing benefits through both owned entities and Employer of Record providers.
* Experience creating and managing benefits through transition periods such as new market entries and M&A.
* Experience influencing senior stakeholders and driving cross-functional projects in lean, fast-moving environments.
* Strong analytical skills and a high degree of comfort with data.
* Technology-first mindset.
