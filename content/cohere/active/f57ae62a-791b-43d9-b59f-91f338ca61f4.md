**Why this role?**The legal team at Cohere is dynamic, fast-paced and cross-functional, supporting all areas of the organization with an aim to provide efficient, scalable legal processes and support. This role offers a unique opportunity to shape the future of enterprise AI contract management and work on streamlining our contractual processes to support rapid growth. The Contract Manager will be a key player in scaling Cohere's contract operations, working closely with the Legal team, Sales, Product, and Finance to ensure smooth contract execution and management.

**As Contract Manager, you will:**

* Serve as the primary point of contact for responding to contract-related requests that don't require legal counsel, providing timely and accurate guidance on standard contract terms and processes
* Assist with triage of legal requests and allocate to appropriate counsel where necessary.
* Manage and maintain legal document management systems, ensuring all contracts, amendments, and related documents are properly organized, tracked, and accessible
* Monitor contract compliance and key dates, providing proactive notifications for renewals, expirations, and critical milestones
* Oversee the contract lifecycle from initiation through execution, including drafting standard agreements, managing approvals, and maintaining contract repositories
* Develop and implement efficient contract management processes and workflows to support business growth and reduce operational friction
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams including Sales, Product, and Finance to ensure contract terms align with business objectives and operational requirements
* Support the legal team with contract administration, data entry, and preparation of contractual materials
* Create and maintain contract templates, playbooks, and standard operating procedures to ensure consistency and efficiency
* Analyze contract data to identify trends, risks, and opportunities for process improvement
* Assist with reporting on contracts

**You may be a good fit if:**

* You have a minimum of 3-5 years of experience in contract management, preferably in a fast-paced technology or AI company
* You possess strong organizational skills with exceptional attention to detail and the ability to manage multiple contracts simultaneously
* You have excellent communication skills (both written and oral) and can effectively collaborate with stakeholders at all levels
* You are comfortable working in a dynamic, fast-paced environment with evolving priorities
* You have a practical, business-oriented mindset with the ability to balance legal requirements with business needs
* You are proficient with contract management software, specifically Ironclad, including setting up and managing workflows and templates.
* You are proficient with Microsoft Office Suite and Google productivity tools,
* You have experience with standard contract types including NDAs, master service agreements, and procurement contracts
* Familiarity with AI industry contracts and technology sector agreements will be considered an asset
* Experience in implementing or optimizing contract management processes will be considered an asset
* You are proactive, solutions-focused, and enjoy finding ways to improve operational efficiency
