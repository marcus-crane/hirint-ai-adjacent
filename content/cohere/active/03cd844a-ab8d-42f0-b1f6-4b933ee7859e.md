We’re training and deploying frontier models for enterprises who are building AI systems. We believe that our work is instrumental to the widespread adoption of AI and we are looking for folks that want to be part of that.

We are a global technology company headquartered in Toronto with key offices in London, New York City, San Francisco, Montreal, Paris, Berlin and Seoul. Join us!

**About the role:**

Cohere is at the forefront of AI innovation, building cutting-edge language models and AI systems. Our Finance team plays a critical role in supporting our rapid growth and ensuring operational excellence. We value technical expertise, collaborative problem-solving, and a commitment to accuracy and compliance. This role offers the opportunity to make a significant impact on our financial infrastructure while working at the intersection of finance and transformative technology. As we scale globally, you will play a pivotal role in transforming our Procure-to-Pay (P2P) engine, safeguarding company assets, and partnering cross-functionally with Procurement, Finance, Legal, and Department Leads to drive spend visibility and operational rigor.

**In this role, you will:**

* **Own End-to-End Global AP:** Direct daily multi-entity, multi-currency accounts payable operations globally, ensuring invoices are accurately coded, approved, and paid on schedule.
* **Build SOX-Ready Infrastructure:** Overhaul AP workflows to meet public-company standards, designing and maintaining documented, testable internal controls (including segregation of duties, vendor master file governance, and payment release controls).
* **Drive Automation & AI Adoption:** Lead the implementation and optimization of modern P2P/AP automation platforms, corporate card programs, and AI-assisted invoice processing to maximize touchless rates and eliminate manual reconciliations.
* **Manage Month-End Close & Tax Compliance:** Own the AP close calendar, partnering with GL accounting on accrued liabilities, prepaid amortization schedules, and reconciliations, while ensuring full compliance with 1099s, W-8/W-9s, and Canadian GST/HST/VAT requirements.
* **Partner Cross-Functionally:** Design scalable approval hierarchies and exception workflows for urgent payments, acting as the primary point of escalation for internal stakeholders and strategic vendors.
* **Lead & Develop the Team:** Supervise, coach, and scale a high-performing AP team, establishing clear SLAs and tracking KPIs

**You may be a good fit if you:**

* Have 7+ years of progressive Accounts Payable experience, including 3+ years managing and coaching a team in a fast-paced, high-growth technology environment.
* Possess hands-on experience building or operating SOX-compliant AP controls and supporting external financial statement audits (PCAOB experience is a strong plus).
* Have deep proficiency with NetSuite and have led implementations or integrations of modern P2P/corporate card platforms (e.g., Ramp, Zip, Brex, or similar).
* Bring solid working knowledge of multi-currency transactions and North American tax/regulatory requirements (US 1099s, W-8/W-9, Canadian GST/HST).
* Are an automation-first builder who thrives in ambiguity, spots process bottlenecks early, and leverages modern tools (including AI) to scale operations without linearly scaling headcount.
* Communicate clearly and empathetically with non-finance stakeholders, enforcing policy guardrails while keeping the business moving.
