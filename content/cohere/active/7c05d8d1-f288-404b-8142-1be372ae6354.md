## **Who are we?**

Cohere is the leading security-first enterprise AI company. We build cutting-edge foundation AI models and end-to-end products that are designed to solve real-world business problems.

We’re training and deploying frontier models for enterprises who are building AI systems. We believe that our work is instrumental to the widespread adoption of AI and we are looking for folks that want to be part of that.

We obsess over what we build. Each one of us is responsible for contributing to increasing the capabilities of our models and the value they drive for our customers. Cohere is a team of researchers, engineers, designers, and more, who are all passionate about their craft.

We are a global technology company headquartered in Toronto with key offices in London, New York City, San Francisco, Montreal, Paris, Berlin and Seoul. Join us!

## About the Role

We are hiring Cohere’s first-ever Senior Manager, Talent Development. This role is a blank canvas — there is no established Talent Development function, no inherited catalogue of programs, and no playbook to follow. You will be the first person in this role, with the opportunity to create something from scratch, experiment, and shape how development works across the company.

We are growing rapidly, and in 2027 we want to invest seriously in developing our managers, leaders, and people. This role is where that investment will take shape. There is already strong internal demand for this work, and you will own the Talent Development function from strategy and roadmap through to budget, design, and delivery.

## What You’ll Do

* Build manager development programs across new managers, experienced managers, and emerging directors. Design, launch, and continuously improve these programs; create engaging cohorts and a community where managers learn from one another.
* Drive manager effectiveness. Work with HRBPs to identify management challenges and design practical interventions that improve how managers lead, develop, and support their teams.
* Establish career and competency frameworks across broad role families. Partner with HRBPs and functional leaders to set consistent expectations for growth and progression.
* Strengthen talent development processes. Build approaches to talent reviews, succession planning, and high-potential development that create meaningful opportunities for employees.
* Improve onboarding learning experiences. Partner with HR Ops and functional teams to design engaging learning that helps new employees ramp quickly and become effective.
* Build for scale. Create simple, effective development tools and approaches suited to a fast-growing orginization, including support for integrating new teams as we expand.
* Own delivery and impact. Manage the roadmap and budget, make pragmatic build-versus-buy decisions, work with external partners, and measure the effectiveness of your initiatives.
* Establish the function as a valued capability across the company, with the opportunity to build and lead a small team over time.

## What Success Looks Like

* Launched foundational development programs for managers and leaders with strong engagement and participation.
* Established clear career development and competency frameworks across prioritized role families.
* Created a manager development community that people actively value.
* Demonstrated measurable improvement in manager effectiveness and development outcomes.
* Established a scalable Talent Development function with clear priorities, strong HRBP partnerships, and effective use of internal and external resources.

## Who You Are

* Proven builder with a track record designing, launching, and delivering manager or leadership development programs with demonstrable impact.
* 8+ years of experience, including relevant experience in organizational development, career development, competencies, and talent processes.
* Experience at a fast-growing tech company or scale-up is essential.
* Practically and commercially minded. You focus on solving real problems rather than introducing unnecessary complexity, and you know when to build, buy, or keep things simple.
* Exceptional at engagement and facilitation. You can engage demanding or skeptical audiences and create development experiences people genuinely enjoy and value.
* Strong partner. You build trusted relationships with HRBPs, managers, and leaders, working collaboratively to solve business problems.
* Able to navigate substantial ambiguity independently. You earn alignment without formal authority and exercise strong judgment on complex decisions.
* Ambitious to build something lasting. You are excited by the opportunity to establish a capability that scales with the company and grows your impact over time.
