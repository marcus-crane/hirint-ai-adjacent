## **Who are we?**

Cohere is the leading security-first enterprise AI company. We build cutting-edge foundation AI models and end-to-end products that are designed to solve real-world business problems.

We’re training and deploying frontier models for enterprises who are building AI systems. We believe that our work is instrumental to the widespread adoption of AI and we are looking for folks that want to be part of that.

We obsess over what we build. Each one of us is responsible for contributing to increasing the capabilities of our models and the value they drive for our customers. Cohere is a team of researchers, engineers, designers, and more, who are all passionate about their craft.

We are a global technology company headquartered in Toronto with key offices in London, New York City, San Francisco, Montreal, Paris, Berlin and Seoul. Join us!

## Why This Role

We're seeking an experienced Finance Systems Analyst to join our FP&A team. We need someone with deep expertise in EPM architecture, design, and product management, with specific implementation experience in Pigment. In return, you'll have the unique opportunity to shape Cohere's financial systems, collaborate with leading minds in AI and finance, and be the "technical" voice of Cohere for financial systems. You will get a chance to create extremely high-impact contributions to our fast-growing company, product, and culture.

## Key Responsibilities

### EPM Architecture & Design

* Design, implement, and maintain enterprise-grade EPM solutions using Pigment
* Architect scalable financial models and planning frameworks
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define EPM requirements and specifications
* Develop and maintain technical documentation for EPM systems
* Optimize system performance and ensure data integrity across EPM platforms
* Own ongoing Pigment platform administration, including access permissions, model performance, technical standards and platform reliability

### Product Management & Business partnership

* Own the product lifecycle for Pigment-based financial systems
* Partner with FP&A leaders to translate business requirements and approved planning methodologies into technical specifications and scalable solutions.
* Maintain a prioritized enhancement backlog and technical roadmap, with priorities agreed with finance across Corporate and Tech FP&A
* Manage stakeholder expectations and communicate technical constraints
* Conduct user acceptance testing and drive adoption of new features

### Implementation & Integration

* Lead Pigment implementation projects from requirements to deployment
* Integrate EPM systems with existing financial infrastructure (ERP, data warehouses)
* Configure and customize Pigment to meet specific business needs
* Develop and maintain data pipelines and ETL processes
* Troubleshoot and resolve complex system integration issues

## Required Qualifications

### Technical Skills

* Pigment (similar EPM) implementation experience - Proven track record of designing and implementing solutions for financial planning and analysis
* EPM architecture expertise - Deep understanding of enterprise performance management frameworks and best practices.
* Financial systems integration - Experience integrating EPM tools with ERP systems, data warehouses, and BI platforms
* Data modeling - Strong skills in financial data modeling and dimensional modeling
* SQL proficiency - Advanced SQL skills for data extraction and analysis
* API development - Experience with REST APIs and data integration patterns

### Product Management Skills

* Product lifecycle management - Experience owning products from conception to deployment
* Stakeholder management - Ability to work with finance, operations, and executive stakeholders
* Requirements gathering - Expertise in translating business needs into technical specifications
* Agile methodologies - Experience working in agile development environments

### Soft Skills

* Problem-solving - Ability to diagnose and resolve complex technical issues
* Communication - Excellent written and verbal communication skills
* Collaboration - Strong ability to work across functional teams
* Attention to detail - Meticulous approach to system design and implementation

## Experience Requirements

* 7+ years of experience in finance systems analysis or related roles
* 5+ years of specific EPM implementation experience (Anaplan, Oracle, SAP, Workday, Onestream, etc)
* 3+ years of hands-on Pigment implementation experience
* 3+ years of product management experience in financial systems
* Experience working with enterprise-scale financial data (>$1B revenue)
* Experience in SaaS or technology companies preferred
* Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Finance, or related field; MBA or Master's preferred
