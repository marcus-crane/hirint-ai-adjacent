## **Who are we?**

Cohere is the leading security-first enterprise AI company. We build cutting-edge foundation AI models and end-to-end products that are designed to solve real-world business problems.

We’re training and deploying frontier models for enterprises who are building AI systems. We believe that our work is instrumental to the widespread adoption of AI and we are looking for folks that want to be part of that.

We obsess over what we build. Each one of us is responsible for contributing to increasing the capabilities of our models and the value they drive for our customers. Cohere is a team of researchers, engineers, designers, and more, who are all passionate about their craft.

We are a global technology company headquartered in Toronto with key offices in London, New York City, San Francisco, Montreal, Paris, Berlin and Seoul. Join us!

**About the Role:**

Cohere seeks a high-performing Talent Coordinator, eager to take on a new challenge here in our Talent Operations team at Cohere. You will schedule a high volume of roles as a member of a small but mighty coordination team, who partner closely with key stakeholders to ensure interviews are arranged in a timely manner and that candidates have an excellent experience interviewing at Cohere. If you consider yourself highly organised, collaborative and someone who goes above and beyond to ensure excellence, we’d love to hear from you.

In this role you will be a liaison between teams supporting hiring managers, the Talent Team, the People team, and many more. Our Talent Coordinators are a primary internal and external point of contact and are a core part of our TA function supporting the scaling of Cohere. The team is not a behind the scenes support function, they are the beating heart of the Cohere Talent team.

You will schedule a high volume of interviews diligently and efficiently, manage a world class candidate experience, build strong and strategic relationships, manage multiple calendars and own the interview process with seamless communication and ownership from start to finish.

Our ATS is Ashby and we utilise the Advanced Scheduling functionality!

**Candidates must be based in Germany to be considered for this opportunity.**

**As a Talent Coordinator, you will:**

* **Schedule a large volume of interviews,** ensuring our hiring teams and candidates are supported via seamless communication
* Manage the candidate experience and build strong relationships across the organisation
* Partner closely with a team of Executive Assistants, Hiring Managers and Recruiters
* Support and optimize our interview processes & increase efficiency/automate where possible
* Provide status updates, answer questions, and ensure timely follow up for our recruiters and candidate
* Support on TA Operations projects where required
* Keep our internal documentation up to date, working with detail and organization to ensure scalability and operational efficiency

**This career opportunity may be a good match for you if you have:**

* 2+ years of professional work experience supporting a fast paced recruitment team
* Experience **scheduling a high volume of interviews**,managing multiple calendars, and supporting a large volume of interviews with hiring managers and executive teams
* Attention to detail coupled with the ability manage a high workload at a demanding pace
* A team player "can-do" attitude and a proven track record of partnering with all functions and levels across an enterprise
* Possess very strong time management & communication skills
* Track record of executing on tasks for high-touch recruitment initiatives thoughtfully and efficiently at a fast pace
* Ability to handle confidential and sensitive information with tact and discretion
* Have a genuine passion for providing excellent candidate experiences
