**Who are we?**

Cohere is the leading security-first enterprise AI company. We build cutting-edge foundation AI models and end-to-end products that are designed to solve real-world business problems.

We’re training and deploying frontier models for enterprises who are building AI systems. We believe that our work is instrumental to the widespread adoption of AI and we are looking for folks that want to be part of that.

We obsess over what we build. Each one of us is responsible for contributing to increasing the capabilities of our models and the value they drive for our customers. Cohere is a team of researchers, engineers, designers, and more, who are all passionate about their craft.

We are a global technology company headquartered in Toronto with key offices in London, New York City, San Francisco, Montreal, Paris, Berlin and Seoul. Join us!

**Why this role?**

We are on a mission to build machines that understand the world and make them safely accessible to all. Data quality is foundational to this process. Machines (or Large Language Models to be exact) learn in similar ways to humans - by way of feedback. By labelling, ranking, auditing, and correcting text output, you will improve Large Language Model’s performance for iterations to come, thus having a lasting impact on Cohere’s tech.

This role will have you focus on financial modelling and finance-based questions. You will teach the Cohere model how to perform across a variety of different functions and logic.

**Please Note: This is a part-time contract position. We are looking for candidates who are able to commit to 16 hours per week at a $30/hour contract rate. This role is available within Canada only and is BYOD 💻 - Bring Your Own Device (laptop).**

**As an AI Data Trainer, you will:**

* Evaluate the model’s ability to respond to objective financial queries ( you will not be giving financial advice)
* Write financial prompts along with verified information to evaluate the accuracy of the model’s responses
* Label, proofread, and improve machine-written and human-written financial outputs.
* Follow our style guide, and make recommendations on unique situations that fall outside of its scope.
* Evaluating financial models in excel.

**You may be a good fit if you have:**

* 5+ years of industry experience working in equity research, corporate finance, M&A, private equity, or corporate development
* Financial analysis training and experience preferred
* CFA is a plus
* An education background in finance (Bachelors, Masters or similar)
* Experience interpreting complex financial statements, earning decks, charts and graphs
* Experience with preparing and understanding financial statements, key metrics, business acumen
* Experience with risk analysis processes
* Experience with financial modelling/analysis that informs strategic decision-making
* You are an expert in Excel and/or VBA

**The Candidate Journey:**

**Multiple Choice Finance Test -** A multiple choice test assessing your financial knowledge.  
**Finance/Investment Analysis Written Test -** This online test will assess your financial/investment analysis skillset, through written and more in-depth questions.
