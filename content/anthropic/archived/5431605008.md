## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role:**

Anthropic is seeking a Compensation Programs Specialist to join our People team. In this role, you'll be the operational backbone of our compensation programs — coordinating and executing the processes that keep our pay programs running smoothly and on time. You'll work closely with Compensation Partners, People Partners, Finance, and Recruiting to ensure compensation workflows are delivered accurately and consistently across the organization. This is a great opportunity for someone who is highly organized, detail-oriented, and energized by running reliable operational processes at a high-growth company.

## **Responsibilities:**

* Own the administration of our non-cyclical compensation programs end-to-end, including processing submissions, coordinating approvals, and ensuring timely and accurate processing in partnership with People Partners, People Operations, and Payroll
* Support bi-annual compensation cycles including data audits, cash, equity, and peer group reviews, and survey submissions
* Coordinate with Recruiting Operations and Equity Administrators to ensure equity grants are processed in accordance with our policies
* Maintain and improve compensation resources for the business and team, including our compensation hub, playbooks, compensation tables, and job architecture, ensuring guidance is current and accurate
* Identify and flag process gaps or inconsistencies, and partner with the broader compensation team to improve operational workflows
* Partner with the Compensation Partners team on program design workstreams

## **Minimum Qualifications:**

* Are highly organized and able to manage multiple concurrent workstreams with competing deadlines
* Have exceptional attention to detail and commitment to data accuracy
* Have proficient Excel/Google Sheets skills, including complex formulas, pivot tables, and data modeling
* Communicate clearly and proactively, keeping stakeholders informed and unblocked
* Are a collaborative partner who enjoys working across People, Finance, Legal, and business teams
* Thrive in fast-moving environments and are energized by building reliable, repeatable processes

## **Preferred Qualifications:**

* Have 4 + years of experience in compensation, total rewards, people operations, or a closely related field
* Have experience with HRIS systems (e.g., Workday) or compensation administration tools (e.g., Shareworks/Carta)
* Have familiarity with equity compensation programs and related administration
* Be familiar with compensation data providers (e.g., Radford/Aon, Mercer)
* Strong collaboration and program management skills
* Be interested in Anthropic's mission and the unique compensation challenges that come with building at the frontier of AI

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
