## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About The Role

We are seeking an Incident & Crisis Management Lead to join Anthropic's Global Safety, Intelligence, and Security (GSIS) team. In this role, you will operationalize Anthropic's crisis management program, equipping the company to prevent, prepare for, respond to, and recover from incidents and crises that could impact our people, operations, or mission. The threat landscape is broad, spanning physical, supply chain, operational, reputational, and geopolitical disruption.

While this role does not directly manage technology incidents (such as IT outages, disaster recovery, or engineering issues), you will ensure the right stakeholders are engaged whenever a threat crosses both physical and technical domains.

This role sits at the intersection of emergency management, enterprise resilience, and executive communication. Working closely with the Enterprise Resilience Manager, you will lead incident and crisis exercises and real-world activations, maintain scalable response plans and playbooks, and build the capability of cross-functional teams to perform under pressure. You will be a visible, trusted partner across the organization, known for steady judgment, clear communication, and a relentless focus on continuous improvement.

This is an individual contributor role with the opportunity to shape a function foundational to Anthropic's long-term resilience. As Anthropic scales globally, the stakes and complexity of our crisis management needs grow with it, and this role is central to meeting that challenge.

This role carries an on-call requirement and after-hours availability. The Incident & Crisis Management Lead is expected to respond to threats as they arise, including evenings, weekends, and holidays.

## **Responsibilities**

* Operationalize Anthropic's crisis management program, translating industry frameworks (NIMS/ICS, ISO 22361, ISO 22301) into response plans, playbooks, activation protocols, and governance structures that fit the organization
* Serve as Incident Commander, or provide direct support to the Incident Commander, during activations, leading and coordinating response teams in real time
* Design and facilitate exercises and tabletop simulations of increasing complexity to test plans, build team capability, and surface improvement opportunities across GSIS and cross-functional partners
* Lead after-action reviews following exercises and real-world incidents; track corrective actions to completion and fold lessons learned into future plans and training
* Partner with GSOC, Legal, People, Communications, IT Security, and Facilities to build coordinated crisis response capability across a broad range of threats
* Maintain and regularly validate Crisis Management Team rosters and activation contacts
* Monitor the threat environment for developments relevant to Anthropic's operations and advise leadership on emerging risks
* Support broader enterprise resilience initiatives, including business continuity and disaster recovery
* Advance the integration of AI tools into how we identify, respond to, and recover from threats
* Support the Enterprise Resilience Manager on special projects as needed

## **You May Be a Good Fit If You**

* Have 8+ years of professional experience in emergency management, incident response, or crisis management
* Have led cross-functional response teams as Incident Commander in high-stress, rapidly evolving situations
* Hold a bachelor's degree in Emergency Management, Risk Management, Business Administration, or a related field, or bring equivalent practical experience
* Have designed and facilitated incident and crisis exercises across multiple levels of complexity, building scenarios that are realistic and instructive
* Communicate exceptionally well in writing and in person, including developing and delivering executive briefings during active incidents
* Build trusted partnerships quickly across a wide range of internal stakeholders
* Manage multiple active workstreams under time pressure with minimal oversight
* Thrive in fast-moving, ambiguous environments and bring a continuous improvement mindset to everything you build
* Are available for on-call support and flexible hours, including nights and weekends, during crisis events
* Are open to occasional international travel

## **Strong Candidates May Also Have**

* Experience in the technology sector, particularly supporting crisis management for cloud-native or AI-driven organizations
* Professional certifications such as CEM, CBCP, MBCI, or equivalent

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
