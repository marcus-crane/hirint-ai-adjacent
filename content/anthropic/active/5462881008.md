## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

Anthropic's Safeguards team plays a critical role in ensuring our AI systems are deployed safely, responsibly, and in line with our values.

As an Incident Manager, you will manage Safeguards incidents involving our AI systems from declaration through closure. You will assess severity, bring in the right people from Legal, Communications, Policy, Threat Intelligence, Security, Engineering, and research teams, keep leadership informed, and see follow-up work through. You will also oversee the Safeguards Enforcement on-call queue on a rotating basis, and help build and scale the incident response program, including runbooks, post-incident reviews, and automation of routine work.

***Important context for this role: in this position you may be exposed to and engage with explicit content spanning a range of topics, including those of a violent, sexual, or psychologically disturbing nature. This role also includes after-hours and weekend incident coverage on a rotating basis.***

## **Key responsibilities**

* Serve as incident commander for Safeguards incidents from declaration through closure
* Assess what qualifies as an incident, propose severity, and coordinate responders across Legal, Communications, Policy, Threat Intelligence, Security, Engineering, and research teams
* Keep everyone working from the same facts by tracking input from each team, establishing what is known, what has changed, and what is still open, and sending regular updates that executives and partner teams rely on
* Make sure Legal, Communications, and Public Policy are consulted before decisions that need them, and coordinate any external notifications
* Run post-incident reviews, assign action items to named owners, and track them through closure
* Oversee the on-call queue during your rotation as the first responder for urgent requests and escalations to Safeguards Enforcement
* Triage each request, resolve what you can, route the rest to the owning team, and recognize when a request is the start of an incident
* Take part in after-hours and weekend incident coverage on a rotating basis
* Scope and deliver projects that improve the incident response program, such as on-call automation, incident readiness, and post-incident reviews and metrics
* Draft and maintain runbooks and standard operating procedures
* Work with Safeguards engineers to automate or reroute requests by writing the decision rules and testing the result
* Maintain the incident record and the metrics that show leadership how the program is performing

## **Minimum qualifications**

* Experience running incidents or urgent escalations from start to finish in trust and safety, security, platform integrity, crisis management, or a comparable field, and readiness to lead high-severity incidents with support
* Hands-on experience with incidents where someone was abusing or misusing a product or platform, such as account compromise, fraud, or coordinated malicious activity
* Ability to make clear decisions under time pressure, record your reasoning, and recognize when a decision belongs to Legal, Policy, or leadership
* Ability to write short, accurate updates that an executive can act on and that hold up to legal and external scrutiny
* Experience coordinating people across many functions who do not report to you, securing their commitments, and following through on them
* Experience improving an operational process on your own initiative, and the ability to tell which steps need a person and which can be automated
* Willingness to do hands-on queue work as a regular part of the job, and to treat it as a source of information about what is breaking
* Ability to participate in after-hours and weekend incident coverage on a rotating basis

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
