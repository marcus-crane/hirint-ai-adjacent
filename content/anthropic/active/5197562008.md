## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

We are seeking a Strategic Finance leader to manage the “machines” finance function covering the chips and accelerators that run our models.

In this role, you will be a key partner to our hardware infrastructure teams, providing financial expertise and guidance to drive hardware decisions. You will own the near and long-term forecasts for hardware, support deal teams, and our engineering teams on hardware ROI decisions.

## **Key responsibliities**

* Own the economics of chip and accelerator capacity: cost per chip-hour and TCO across chip generations
* Evaluate capacity offers and hardware commitments with the Compute deal team: pricing across chip types, counterparties, and structures
* Partner with Capacity Planning and Hardware Engineering on the financial analysis influencing machine strategy
* Own the hardware forecast: delivery volumes by chip over time, expenses, and cash flows
* Build and manage the Machines finance team; set the operating cadence with central FP&A and Accounting
* Serve as the accountable owner for machines FP&A into forecast and close cycles
* Coordinate with Accounting, Treasury, and Infra FP&A on accounting treatment and forecast inputs

## **Minimum qualifications**

* Owned hardware, semiconductor, or hyperscaler capacity economics
* Experience managing or mentoring finance talent
* Strong analytical skills and the judgment to turn an ambiguous structure into a clear financial **recommendation**
* Comfort partnering closely with engineering and commercial teams under deal-timeline pressure
* Passion for Anthropic's mission to build safe, transformative AI systems

## **Preferred qualifications**

* 8+ years in strategic finance, FP&A, or capacity/infrastructure finance
* Capacity planning or infrastructure finance experience at a hyperscaler or major chip maker
* Experience standing up a new finance function
* MBA, CFA, or equivalent

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
