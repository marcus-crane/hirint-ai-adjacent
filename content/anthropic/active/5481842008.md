## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

Anthropic's go-to-market organization is growing quickly, and every territory and account assignment has to land correctly for the field to do its best work. The Strategic Planning team sits at the center of that. We work across field Strategy & Operations, Finance, Systems, Data, Incentives and People to help leadership decide where to invest, turn those decisions into a plan, and keep the plan working all year.

This role owns the account side of GTM planning, who covers what: territories, the data enrichment and system requirements behind them, rules of engagement (RoEs), and the account moves that follow during the year.

You'll join a small, high-leverage team and take end-to-end ownership of that scope. That starts with understanding the plan: how coverage, territories and account data fit together, what assumptions sit underneath them, and what happens downstream when one changes. From there you'll plan your area during the cycle, run it through the year as the business moves, and build the tools that make it easier. When something keeps breaking, we expect you to find out why and fix it at the source.

## Key responsibilities

* Build and maintain the territory models and methods behind the plan
* Partner on segmentation definitions and strategy, and turn them into the account rules and criteria that territories are built on
* Carry territories from design through validation with the business, QA and go-live
* Own data enrichment and system requirements for territories and accounts, working with Systems and Data
* Run account moves and RoEs through the year, and turn one-off requests into a clear process with rules, templates and turnaround times
* Spot patterns in what breaks after a plan ships and feed them into the next plan's design
* Build tools and automations with Claude that remove manual work for the Strategy & Operations business partner community

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
