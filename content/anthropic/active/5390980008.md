## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

Anthropic's GTM Programs team turns our most strategic go-to-market priorities into structured, measurable programs that make our field the most successful they can be, thereby ensuring our customers' experience with our products is nothing short of exceptional. The team serves as a core strategic business partner function to the GTM organization, playing a critical role in driving growth and operational excellence, and in accelerating the safe adoption of frontier AI across the world's leading enterprises.

We're looking for a dynamic operator: a senior individual contributor who will serve as a trusted advisor to AMER Sales Leadership, driving strategic initiatives across Anthropic's GTM organization and owning a portfolio of programs end to end. You'll build a deep understanding of our organization and priorities, where we are and where we're going, and turn that vision into plans you execute: data-driven in determining what needs to happen, creative in how you design each program, and outcomes-driven in ensuring it drives the success of the field.

You'll work across a wide variety of teams, including Sales, Strategy, Enablement, Marketing, and Partnerships, helping them operate as one team behind each program.

GTM Programs is a new function, and this is one of its first hires: an opportunity to build the function, not just join it. The timing matters: every organization is deciding its AI strategy right now, and the programs this team builds shape how our field meets that moment. You'll help define what a well-run program looks like at Anthropic, operating with high autonomy.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Serve as a trusted advisor to AMER Sales Leadership: bring data-backed insight and recommendations into their operating reviews, and shape the strategic initiatives that come out of them
* Own GTM programs end to end: translate leadership priorities into structured programs with clear objectives, owners, and milestones, and carry them from concept through launch, operations, and iteration with measured uplift as the outcome you're accountable for
* Define the measurement plan before a program launches: baseline, targets, the metrics that will show uplift, and how results will be attributed to the program
* Instrument every program into the GTM operating cadence, so leadership can inspect program results in the same reviews where they run the business
* Build the dashboards and reporting that make program performance visible at every level of the organization, from global leadership to the individual seller
* Package what's working (industry patterns, use cases, activation motions) into repeatable plays that sellers can run without you in the room
* Partner with Strategy, Enablement, Product Marketing, and Partnerships to build programs once and land them consistently across the field
* Gather structured feedback from sellers and sales managers, and treat every launch as a hypothesis to improve on
* Build the templates, playbooks, and measurement infrastructure that let future programs launch instrumented by default

## **Minimum qualifications**

* Structured program management: plans, milestones, risks, dependencies, and a decision log that keeps stakeholders on one version of the truth
* Have driven cross-functional initiatives to completion across teams without formal authority over any of them
* Communicate clearly with both sellers and senior leadership, and can make complex material simple enough for the field to act on
* Work effectively in fast-moving environments with high ambiguity, and are comfortable building where no process exists yet
* Are excited about Anthropic's mission to develop AI that is safe and beneficial

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
