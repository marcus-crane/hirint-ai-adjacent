## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

Developers and enterprises build on Claude through the Claude Platform. Our models are getting exponentially more intelligent, and our APIs and developer tools need to evolve just as quickly so developers can take advantage of that intelligence safely.

We're hiring Engineering Managers to lead teams that build these APIs and developer tools. We are shipping APIs and infrastructure for building and running agents in production. These APIs ship alongside SDKs, docs, and tooling that developers (and agents!) utilize in order to build high quality integrations. These abstractions are backed by our identity, verification, and trust platform, which lets us scale access to Claude responsibly.

## Key responsibilities

* Lead a team of engineers to ship high quality work quickly, owning planning, prioritization, delivery, and operational health
* Partner with product to set your team's roadmap, and bring a strong point of view on what developers need
* Talk to customers directly and turn what you learn into engineering priorities
* Guide API design and architecture decisions for distributed systems that have to stay reliable as usage grows
* Work closely with Research, Safeguards, Security, and go-to-market teams to bring new model capabilities to developers safely
* Recruit, coach, and grow strong engineers

## Minimum qualifications

* You have experience managing high-performing software engineering teams shipping developer products, including hiring, coaching, and performance management
* You're product-minded and customer-oriented. You care about who uses what your team builds, and you've shaped roadmaps alongside product partners
* You've built and scaled distributed systems in production, and you have the technical depth required to guide architecture and API design
* You've led teams that ship quickly and hold a high quality bar
* You collaborate well across functions and can align teams you don't manage
* You care about Anthropic's mission and about developing AI safely

## Preferred qualifications

* Experience building agent infrastructure or products on top of LLMs
* Experience with identity, verification, access control, or trust and safety systems

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
