## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

We're hiring a policy generalist to join the Strategic Advisory team within Anthropic's Public Policy team. You'll develop and maintain our policy research strategy and lead the research behind it, to strengthen Anthropic's policy influence in Washington and around the world.

You'll lead the research that backs up Anthropic's policy positions: deciding what evidence a position needs, working with Anthropic's research teams on what our own data can show, building partnerships with outside academics, think tanks and economists, and managing our growing body of commissioned research and economic impact work. You'll turn that research into analysis and materials our federal, state and international teams can use. You'll also run the cross-functional processes that take an open policy question to an agreed, evidence-backed position. As Anthropic's international footprint grows, you'll help the team scale its research and support for markets outside the US.

You're likely a strong generalist who likes to build things and see them through, and who gets energy from turning complex research into something clear, organized and useful. You're curious and rigorous about evidence. You ask what would change a skeptic's mind, you can tell a strong study from a weak one, and you know when to bring in a subject matter expert. You have good judgment, you take initiative, and you're comfortable when the path isn't defined yet. You can run several research projects and outside partners at once while keeping track of how they connect.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Run policy development processes from open question to agreed position: frame the question, bring in the right experts from research, legal, communications and regional policy teams, work through disagreements, and get to a decision
* Develop research plans for priority issue areas: what evidence would strengthen or test a position, what Anthropic's own data and research can show, and where an outside partner is the right source
* Work with Anthropic's research teams, including economics research, Societal Impacts and the Frontier Red Team, to turn their findings into material policymakers can use
* Manage commissioned research from scoping through publication, and build partnerships with outside academics, think tanks, economists and consultancies
* Own economic impact reports and the narratives that go with them for markets around the world
* Anticipate emerging policy issues and work with internal experts to develop Anthropic's perspective and supporting materials before external pressure builds
* Scale the team's support for international markets: adapt strategy and materials for different governments, and build templates and processes so each new market is faster than the last
* Draft briefings, talking points and other materials on short timelines, coordinating with policy communications so messaging stays consistent

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
