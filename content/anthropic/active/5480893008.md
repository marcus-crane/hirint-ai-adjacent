## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

As a Strategic Account Executive - Healthcare & Life Sciences at Anthropic, you'll join the foundational team at the forefront of introducing our frontier AI platform to the world's leading healthcare and life sciences organizations. This team is responsible for our largest and most complex customers in the industry. You'll own a small number of these accounts and drive adoption of safe, frontier AI by securing strategic, enterprise-scale deals across pharmaceutical, biotechnology, research, payor, health system, and hospital organizations.

The ideal candidate is energized by building a new category in an established, highly regulated industry and has a passion for developing new market segments across biopharma, healthcare delivery, and insurance. You pinpoint high-potential opportunities inside very large enterprises, run the strategies to win them, and have the focus and seniority to solve the hardest problems first. What you unlock with these customers will help set the path for the rest of the industry.

By driving deployment of Anthropic's emerging products at scale, you'll help healthcare and life sciences enterprises unlock new capabilities while advancing the responsible and ethical development of AI.

## Where you could focus

You don't need experience in both areas. We'll consider you for the role that best matches your background.

* **Life Sciences:** Pharmaceutical, biotechnology, and research organizations. You'll help customers apply AI across R&D, clinical development, and commercial workflows.
* **Healthcare:** Payors, health systems, and hospitals. You'll help customers apply AI across clinical departments, care management, claims processing, and administrative operations.

## Key responsibilities

* Own a small, concentrated book of our largest healthcare and life sciences accounts, carrying and exceeding an individual revenue target and driving the full sales cycle from outbound to close and expansion
* Engage personally with C-level and senior technical stakeholders (CIOs, CTOs, Heads of R&D, Heads of Data/AI) and key clinical, administrative, and payor decision-makers, building business cases and value narratives, and navigating complex procurement, security, regulatory, and legal review through to production deployment and expansion
* Design and execute sales strategies tailored to each customer's procurement cycles, budgeting processes, and priorities, from drug development to value-based care initiatives, translating market landscape and trend analysis into targeted account plans and campaigns
* Identify and prioritize high-potential opportunities across business units, including R&D, clinical development, commercial, clinical departments, care management, claims processing, and administrative offices, turning strategic relationships into committed revenue and differentiating Anthropic's offering for each use case
* Run a disciplined pipeline and forecasting cadence, with accurate deal inspection, clear next steps, and predictable, coachable execution against quota across a high-value, long-cycle book of business and an evolving sales process
* Partner closely with Applied AI, Solutions Architecture, and Product to design solutions, prove value quickly, and translate industry-specific needs and feedback from scientific, clinical, care coordination, commercial, and administrative leaders into product and roadmap input
* Codify winning use cases, proof points, reference stories, playbooks, templates, and best practices, partnering with Marketing and Enablement to scale them across the broader healthcare and life sciences motion
* Orchestrate cross-functional and partner motions with Customer Success, Legal, Partnerships, and cloud partners to deliver a seamless customer experience, and represent Anthropic as a visible, trusted voice with customers and at industry events

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
