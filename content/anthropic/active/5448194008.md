## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

Anthropic is seeking a Program Manager to join our Accounting organization and help it run as one global team. Accounting is scaling at an unprecedented pace across multiple hubs, and the goals, rhythms, programs, and people that make up the team need to stay connected as we grow. In this role, you will own that connective tissue: the annual goal-setting cycle, the leadership team’s operating cadence, org-wide events and internal communications, and the programs that make a distributed team feel like one.

You will partner with Accounting leadership, the People team, Finance Systems, and hub leads. This is a hands-on role with a lot of range: part chief of staff, part program manager, part culture builder.

## **Responsibilities**

### **Goals and operating rhythm**

* Run the annual goal setting process and the reporting that tracks progress against those goals.
* Look at how the org actually operates (meetings, handoffs, planning cycles, decision making) and fix what isn't working.
* Maintain one org-wide planning calendar that shows close, audit, and team milestones alongside company-wide and team events, so leaders can see the pressure points and plan around them.
* Build and maintain a simple set of org health metrics (attrition, engagement scores, hiring pipeline, training completion, survey trends) and report on them regularly.

### **Leadership and org-wide events**

* Run our extended leadership team (XLT) program, including agendas, meetings, follow-ups, and action items.
* Own planning and agenda for the annual offsite and run the quarterly team all-hands.
* Write the internal updates: a regular team newsletter, plus talking points when leadership needs to explain a change to the whole org.

### **People and culture**

* Coordinate projects across the org that support employee experience programs.
* Partner with the People team to roll out training and development programs for Accounting.
* Review team survey results, identify the themes, and build action plans to address the feedback. Follow through and report back to the team.
* Run hub programs, including visit and rotation programs, local hub events, and meeting norms that work across time zones, so we feel like one global team.
* Run recognition and team rituals: wins, milestones, anniversaries, and peer recognition, especially across hubs.

### **Team operations**

* Support contractor management, including onboarding, tracking, and coordination with the right partners.
* Keep the team's knowledge base current: wiki, org charts, role descriptions, and key process docs.
* Keep the team calendar accurate so everyone knows what's coming.

## **Preferred qualifications**

* 7+ years in program management, operations, chief of staff, or strategy roles. Experience inside a finance or accounting org is a plus, but not required.
* A track record of running recurring programs end to end and getting people to follow through without formal authority.
* Good judgment about what matters and what doesn't. You can handle a lot of moving pieces without dropping them.
* Clear, direct writing. You can brief a senior leader in a few sentences.
* Comfort with confidential information and sensitive people topics.
* Experience supporting distributed or global teams.
* You're organized, but you also know when a process is getting in the way and should be simplified.

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
