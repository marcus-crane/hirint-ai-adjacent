## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

The Anthropic Residency is our paid, fixed-term research program, with placements lasting between six weeks and six months. We are looking for people who have led ambitious work in a variety of technical fields and want to transition to full-time work focused on making sure AI systems are safe and beneficial for all of humanity. If you are a **professor in a quantitative discipline, a technical founder or a staff-level engineer** and want to transition to work on transformative AI, we want to hear from you. Background in machine learning is not required.

## **What to expect**

* A concrete, scoped project matched to your background, with direct mentorship from Anthropic researchers
* Access to Claude models and GPU compute
* Connection to the broader AI safety and security research community

Residents will work on empirical research questions in collaboration with Anthropic's research teams. Areas we are particularly interested in include:

AI safety & alignment

* Scalable Oversight: Developing techniques to keep highly capable models helpful and honest, even as they surpass human-level intelligence in various domains.
* Adversarial Robustness and AI Control: Creating methods to ensure advanced AI systems remain safe and harmless in unfamiliar or adversarial scenarios.
* Model Organisms: Creating [model organisms of misalignment](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/ChDH335ckdvpxXaXX/model-organisms-of-misalignment-the-case-for-a-new-pillar-of-1) to improve our empirical understanding of how alignment failures might arise.
* Model Internals and Interpretability: Advancing our understanding of the internal workings of large language models to enable more targeted interventions and safety measures.
* Safeguards Research: Developing robust defenses against adversarial attacks, comprehensive evaluation frameworks for model safety, and automated systems to detect and mitigate potential risks before deployment.
* AI Welfare: Improving our understanding of potential AI welfare and developing related evaluations and mitigations.

AI security & frontier red-teaming

* Stress-testing frontier models to understand the full extent of their current capabilities in fields such as cybersecurity, robotics or cryptography.

ML systems & reinforcement learning

* Improving how frontier models are trained and building the infrastructure behind that research, from model architecture, optimizers and training data to accelerator simulators, hardware backends and RL environment pipelines.

For a fuller picture of the questions we care about, see our [Recommendations for Technical AI Safety Research Directions](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2025/recommended-directions/) and recent publications from our [Alignment](https://alignment.anthropic.com/), [Interpretability](https://transformer-circuits.pub/) and [Frontier Red](https://red.anthropic.com/) teams.

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
