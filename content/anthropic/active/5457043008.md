## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

The vision for Claude Marketplace is to create an ecosystem flywheel, connecting builders and buyers. We're hiring a Product Manager to own the Marketplace as a product, for the partners who sell on it and the customers who buy through it. You'll work closely with the Marketplace team, which owns the partner and customer relationships as well as the commercial motion, and build on systems owned by other product and engineering teams. You'll have a lot of room to define and build the experience fitting for an AI native world.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Own the Marketplace product end to end for partners and customers, from onboarding and listing through purchase and billing
* Design the commercial workflows behind the Marketplace from first principles, so they scale and meet partners' and customers' finance, audit and compliance requirements
* Shape the business strategy and commercial incentives with Business Development, Finance and Legal, and build them into the product
* Be the product voice partners hear from, and turn signals from partners, customers and the field into a roadmap
* Define the Marketplace's success metrics and how they're measured

## **Minimum qualifications**

* Have owned a product end to end, including the operational workflows behind it, and were the person accountable for its outcomes
* Have built with or shipped on top of large language models, and have used them to make a product experience better
* Have led cross-functional work where most of the teams you depended on didn't report to you, and earned their trust quickly
* Can present to executives at partner companies and represent Anthropic well in front of them
* Write clearly enough that an engineer, an executive and an external partner can each see the tradeoff you're making
* Care about Anthropic's mission, and specifically about building an ecosystem where the companies that build on Claude grow along with it

## **Preferred qualifications**

* 8+ years of product management experience
* Played an active role in a marketplace, app store or partner ecosystem, on either the platform or the partner side
* Taken a product from zero to one, where the users, the product and the economics all had to be worked out at once
* Go-to-market instincts: how co-sell motions, partner programs and platform economics work in enterprise software
* Experience/familiarity with high growth AI native companies, including their business models and growth vectors

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
