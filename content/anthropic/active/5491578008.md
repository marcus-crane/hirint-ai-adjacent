## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

Anthropic is looking for a Head of Campaign Operations to build the tools, processes, and insights for what our enterprise campaigns need to plan, deploy, and drive impact. This role spans our audience campaigns, as well as the channel teams with whom we work to deliver campaigns: paid media, social media, webinars, email, web, content, and more. You'll own how work comes in, gets planned, gets built, launches, and gets measured.

This is a player-coach role. You'll lead a small team of full-time employees and contractors, and you'll build alongside them: the intake, templates, calendars, tools, reporting, and ways of working that let campaign and content leads spend their time on strategy and delivery. Ultimately, you are responsible for building an efficient and automated campaign supply chain that can grow from today's programs to a global operation across regions, languages, and time zones.

The ideal candidate holds an equal mix of campaign strategy, operational expertise, and analytical capability.

You'll report to the Head of Enterprise Integrated Campaigns, and partner closely with the Head of Campaigns and the Head of Content Marketing. Additionally, you'll work closely with Product and Solutions Marketing, Customer Marketing, Field Marketing, Analytics, Marketing Operations, Sales, and our agencies. We want to run as one connected, mostly automated system, with Claude doing as much of the work as it can. You'll design it.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Build the operating model for campaigns, channels, and content: how we plan, build, launch, measure, and improve work across every audience at global scale
* Build and operationalize the intake for campaign, content, and channel requests, with clear criteria, SLAs, and status, ensuring a positive experience for internal stakeholders
* Create the planning tools campaigns rely on, including briefs, plan templates, launch checklists, campaign kits, and the calendar
* Set the operating rhythm: kickoffs, cross-functional campaign syncs, launch readiness, results reviews, and leadership readouts
* Keep a single source of truth for what's planned, in flight, and live, and flag overlaps, gaps, and risks early
* Lead, coach, and develop a small team of full-time employees and contractors, including webinar and email program managers, and set the standard for how those programs support campaigns
* Partner with Analytics and Marketing Operations on tagging, dashboards, channel health, and database health, so results can be inspected weekly and budget can move to what works
* Track budgets, agency deliverables, and spend against plan
* Manage agency relationships and partner with them on operations, including budget management
* Manage various large cross-functional projects
* Use Claude and automation to remove manual work from intake, production, trackers, and reporting, and evaluate and roll out new campaign tools
* Design for global scale from the start: set what is standard worldwide and what regions adapt, and build the regional intake, localization, handoffs, and reporting to support it

## **Minimum qualifcations**

* Have led and developed people, ideally a mix of full-time employees, contractors, and agencies, and stayed hands-on while doing it
* Have built campaign operating models from scratch, with intake, SLAs, calendars, reporting, and reviews that teams kept using
* Have grown campaign operations from an early stage to global scale, and have run them day to day across multiple regions, languages, and time zones
* Understand how integrated campaigns work end to end, and how paid media, web, email, webinars, events, and content fit together
* Are performance minded, with a strong grasp of campaign measurement and the ability to build and interpret reporting when the data is imperfect

* Start with AI and automation, and can show what you've built

* Keep process as light as it can be, and like the unglamorous parts of making things work

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
