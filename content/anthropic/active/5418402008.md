## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the Role**

Anthropic is looking for an empathetic engineering manager to lead a team building internal tools that enable and increase productivity for our employees, especially tools for security, authorization and automation. You'll partner closely with internal users to understand needs and set direction for efficient solutions, often augmenting the tools with Claude for a greater experience and impact. On the Business Technology team, we are passionate about delighting and enabling our users through thoughtful and easy-to-use everyday solutions. At Anthropic, we're building towards an ambitious mission of ensuring AI systems are safe and beneficial to humanity. Our team plays a crucial role in this mission by enabling our teams to work efficiently and securely at scale.

## **Responsibilities**

* Lead and grow a team of engineers building full-stack internal tools for business-critical use cases
* Partner with different teams to deeply understand their workflows and identify opportunities for improved productivity through engineering
* Set technical direction and prioritize roadmap initiatives that drive the most impact for internal users
* Foster a culture of rapid iteration, user feedback, and continuous improvement within the team
* Champion best practices in API design, security, and user experience across the team
* Monitor system reliability, build for operational excellence, and own incident response processes
* Hire, develop, and retain top engineering talent

## **You may be a good fit if you**

* Have 7+ years of experience in software engineering, with 2+ years in an engineering management role
* Have a track record of building and shipping production full-stack software with a focus on usability
* Have experience leading teams building and maintaining scalable internal applications and platforms
* Demonstrate strong technical judgment across modern web development (React, TypeScript, Node.js, Python, Go) and cloud technologies (AWS/GCP/Azure)
* Are skilled at translating ambiguous user needs into clear technical direction and priorities
* Communicate with empathy and strive to build strong relationships with stakeholders and direct reports alike

## **Strong candidates may also**

* Have experience building engineering roadmaps and aligning them with broader business objectives
* Bring a track record of developing engineers through coaching, mentoring, and structured feedback
* Have experience balancing trade-offs across security, privacy, reliability, and user experience at scale
* Have experience with Identity and Access Management tools and security best practices
* Excel at cross-functional collaboration and building alignment across teams with differing priorities
* Have experience anticipating changing business needs and translating them into adaptive technical strategies

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
