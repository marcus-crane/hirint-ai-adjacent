## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

We're looking for a Research Engineer to help make Claude more capable, reliable and useful on complex, real-world work. You'll work across research and engineering, from early ideas and experiments through to improvements in our models.

This is an early-stage team working on open-ended problems. Priorities will change as we learn, and you'll have a lot of ownership over what you work on. We're looking for someone who's excited about that, and who enjoys working closely with both research and product.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Design and run experiments to improve model capabilities
* Build reinforcement learning environments and evaluations
* Develop the tools and infrastructure needed to support this research
* Collaborate with teams across research, engineering and product
* Help shape the direction of a new area of research

## **Minimum qualifications**

* Strong software engineering skills, including proficiency in Python
* Experience training, fine-tuning or evaluating machine learning models
* A track record of owning ambiguous technical projects end to end
* Strong communication skills and a collaborative working style
* Care about the societal impacts and safety of your work

## **Preferred qualifications**

* Experience with reinforcement learning, particularly in long-horizon or sparse-reward settings
* Experience building reinforcement learning environments, simulation systems or large-scale ML infrastructure
* Experience building evaluations or benchmarks for agentic systems
* Experience working closely with product teams to drive model improvements

## **Representative projects**

* Build a simulation where decisions made early on have consequences much later, and use it to train a model

* Design an evaluation for work that takes a long time and has no single right answer
* Investigate where a model's independence breaks down on long projects, and run experiments to improve it
* Develop a way for people to oversee a large amount of work without reviewing every step

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
