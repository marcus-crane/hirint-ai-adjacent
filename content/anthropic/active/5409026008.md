## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

We're looking for a People Specialist to join our People Team.  You'll support People Partners and cross functional teams on the work that helps our managers do their jobs well: performance cases, org changes, manager resources, and the operational processes underneath all of it.

Some weeks you'll be deep in a sensitive performance case coaching a manager. Other weeks you're cleaning up a process, rebuilding a tracker, or helping prep a leader for a hard conversation. We need someone who takes both kinds of work seriously and holds the same quality bar for each.

A lot of the work is sensitive. You'll be close to performance management, org restructures, and situations that touch ER or Employment Legal. Good judgment about what to share, with whom, and in what form is paramount.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Manage performance casework: building documentation, tracking timelines, prepping talking points, and keeping details from falling through the cracks while owning the process end to end
* Own and improve operational processes that managers and People Partners depend on, including outside business activity requests and reorg processing
* Build, maintain, and improve manager resources and documentation so they're actually useful and actually current
* Partner with Leadership Design on manager enablement programs like the performance cycle: logistics, content, delivery, and the unglamorous operational work that makes programs run
* Pull and compile reports and data for sensitive personnel matters
* Maintain tracking systems for casework and manager enablement initiatives; make them better than you found them

## **Minimum qualifications**

* You've supported complex or sensitive casework before and can work with partial information, messy handoffs, and tight timelines
* You're detail-oriented: you catch things, close loops, and remember where to find crucial information
* Strong written communication. You'll be drafting things leaders use in hard conversations, and you're not precious when they get edited (you used Claude as a guide but not as a final editor)
* Good judgment on need-to-know. You think about what to share, with whom, and what goes in writing versus what stays verbal
* You'll push back when something's off. You have a point of view and you're comfortable raising concerns with people more senior than you
* Comfortable with range. You don't check out on the tedious work or treat it as beneath you

## **Preferred qualifications**

* 5+ years in an HR- role: people operations, HR coordination, HRBP, or similar
* Experience at a high-growth tech company where process was being built
* Direct experience supporting HRBPs, Managers and Employees through challenging and sensitive matters
* Working knowledge of HR compliance requirements
* Interest in AI safety
* Claude power user in your current role

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
