## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

Claude Frontier Academy trains engineers at our customers and partners to build with Claude in production. We are scaling quickly, with multiple cohorts running in parallel across cities. This is the role that keeps all of it on schedule.

As Program Manager, you'll own the cohort schedule and the seats, and you'll be the single point of coordination across our delivery partners, forward deployed engineers, the Academy team, and the account and partner managers who nominate participants. Vendors and delivery partners run the rooms. You'll make sure the right people are in the right seats, at the right time, with everything they need. You'll report to the Academy's operating lead.

## Key responsibilities

* Own the schedule and seats: run batching, the waitlist, confirmations, and backfills across parallel cohorts; leadership allocates seats, and you execute, including placing Anthropic's own newly hired forward deployed engineers and technical deployment leads
* Be the counterpart to our delivery and venue partners: sign off on dates, room sizes, and venue holds, and book and backfill Anthropic presenters
* Be the first stop for the field: answer account and partner managers, move them to self-serve, and support them when results are contested; deal with participating organizations directly only by exception
* Run readiness and close-out: enforce the IT readiness gate before each cohort, then run badge rosters, residency additions, extensions, and data requests
* Run the program rhythm: action tracking, steering group inputs, the Academy's internal site, KPI reporting, and following legal, privacy, and IT items through to closure
* Own data hygiene: keep the program's documents, attendee data, and dashboards compliant and organized, and bring everyone to one right place for program information and communications
* Pass on what you learn: bring feedback from each run to the Academy and platform teams
* Build the playbook: templates, trackers, and automations, built with Claude, that make each run take less effort than the last

## Minimum qualifications

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
