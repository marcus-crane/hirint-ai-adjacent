## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

As an Applied AI Architect on the Technology Partnerships team at Anthropic, you will be the technical lead for strategic ISV and technology partners building unique product integrations with Claude. These are software companies whose platforms are systems of record for their customers, and who want those customers to be able to work with their data and workflows directly inside Claude.

This role goes deep with each partner. You will own the technical side of product partnerships from first scoping through launch and beyond. You will help partners design how their platform shows up in Claude (MCP servers, connectors, plugins, and skills), hold the quality bar through evaluations, and act as the bridge between the partner's product and engineering teams and Anthropic's Product, Engineering, and Research teams. You will be a trusted technical advisor to partner engineering leaders and executives, and you will turn what you learn into patterns and playbooks that make every future integration faster and better.

## Responsibilities

* **Technical ownership of strategic integrations:** Serve as the technical lead for strategic ISV and technology partners building product integrations with Claude. Own the technical plan from use-case definition and scoping through build, launch, and post-launch iteration.
* **Integration architecture:** Advise partners on how to expose their platform to Claude, including MCP server design, tool and API surface design for large platforms, authentication and admin onboarding, permissions, and plugin and skill structure. Be opinionated about the patterns that lead to the best experience for joint customers.
* **Evaluations and quality:** Design and run evaluations of partner integrations against the use cases that matter most to joint customers. Turn results into concrete, prioritized feedback for partner engineering teams and hold a high quality bar before launch.
* **Hands-on prototyping:** Build prototypes, demos, and reference skills that show partners the art of the possible, accelerate their build, and contribute to product improvements.
* **Launch readiness:** Partner with Product, Partnerships, Marketing, and GTM teams to get integrations launch-ready on partner-driven timelines such as customer conferences and product events, and support partners and early customers through launch.
* **Product and research feedback loop:** Act as the connective tissue between partners and Anthropic's Product, Engineering, and Research teams. Surface integration gaps and platform requirements, help shape the roadmap for how Claude works with third-party software, and bring early capabilities to partners.
* **Executive technical relationships:** Build trusted relationships with partner CTOs, product leaders, and engineering leads. Explain technical tradeoffs and Anthropic's point of view clearly to technical and executive audiences alike.
* **Scale what works:** Codify reference architectures, integration best practices, evaluation approaches, self-serve guidance, and playbooks so each new technology partnership starts from a stronger foundation, and help enable account teams and the broader partner ecosystem.
* **Joint customer success:** Support account teams on strategic joint customers adopting partner integrations, and serve as an escalation point for complex technical issues.

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
