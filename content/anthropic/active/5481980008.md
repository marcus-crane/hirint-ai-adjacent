## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

Thousands of rare genetic diseases have a known cause but no approved treatment. Advances in gene editing and oligonucleotide medicines have made it possible to design therapies for very small populations, sometimes for a single patient, but developing them remains slow and costly. Anthropic’s Life Sciences team is exploring how frontier AI, combined with deep drug development expertise and strong external partnerships, can help change that.

We’re hiring a Technical Program Manager to bring structure and momentum to this work. You’ll coordinate across programs that support the development of individualized genetic medicines, advance research into new therapeutic approaches, and help patient organizations, non-profits, and researchers use Claude in their own rare disease work. You’ll partner closely with scientists, engineers, clinicians, and external collaborators to keep complex efforts moving toward shared milestones.

Much of this work remains in early stages, so this is a foundational role on a small team. You’ll help define how the team operates, build the plans and processes that allow it to grow, and work with our researchers and engineers to find where Claude can be most useful.

If you’ve run complex programs in drug development or clinical research and want to help advance treatments for rare disease, we’d love to hear from you.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Build and own an integrated program plan, including milestones, dependencies, risks, budgets, and key decisions
* Coordinate with clinical, manufacturing, and research partners on programs that span preclinical development, manufacturing, regulatory review, and clinical care
* Run the operating cadence with external partners, tracking progress and raising risks early
* Work with Legal, Finance, and Partnerships to turn agreements into clear operating plans and track commitments
* Coordinate research across internal scientists, academic collaborators, companies, and contract research organizations, helping to scope projects, sequence work, and support timely decisions
* Help ensure that data is shared responsibly and in a usable form, with appropriate consent, privacy protections, and data-sharing agreements in place
* Lead programs that support non-profits, patient organizations, and academic and independent researchers, from onboarding through ongoing support and feedback
* Help connect genetic diagnosis efforts with therapeutic research and development
* Share insights from partners and programs with Anthropic’s research and product teams to help improve Claude for scientific and medical research
* Write clear status updates, risk assessments, and decision memos, and build processes that scale with the team

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
