Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

We are hiring a Construction Sourcing Manager to develop and execute procurement strategies for the materials, components, and parts needed to build Anthropic's data centers. In this role, you will own end-to-end sourcing for construction materials such as wire and cable, lugs and connectors, conduit and cable tray, breakers and fuses, grounding, pipe, valves, fittings, and structural supports. You will partner closely with engineering, construction, demand planning, general contractors, and trade contractors to make sure the right material is on site when crews need it, establish direct manufacturer and distributor relationships, and negotiate commercial agreements. This role complements our major equipment sourcing leads: they source the equipment, and you source what is needed to install, connect, and energize it.

## Key responsibilities

* Own sourcing for electrical construction materials: medium- and low-voltage cable, building wire, control and instrumentation cable, lugs, connectors, terminations and splice kits, conduit and fittings, cable tray, busway, breakers, fuses, panelboards, grounding, and enclosures.
* Own sourcing for mechanical and structural construction materials: pipe, valves, fittings, flanges, gaskets, hoses, insulation, sensors, strut, hangers, anchors, and fasteners.
* Negotiate Master Purchase Agreements, national pricing agreements, and blanket orders with manufacturers and distributors, including index-based pricing for copper, aluminum, and steel, capacity reservations, and volume rebates.
* Run competitive RFQs, build should-cost models and unit-price benchmarks, and audit contractor material pricing, markups, and procurement schedules.
* Decide with construction and design partners which materials to buy direct versus through contractors, and aggregate demand across sites to secure better price and supply.
* Implement frameworks for ensuring data center partners, general contractors, and trade contractors buy against our negotiated terms, approved manufacturer lists, and technical specifications.
* Work with engineering to standardize parts, qualify second sources, and approve alternates that meet code, listing (e.g., NEC, UL), and design requirements.
* Develop material inventory and logistics strategy including distributor stocking programs, buffer stock, cable cutting and reel management, kitting, staging, and just-in-time site delivery.
* Proactively monitor supply chain volatility (e.g., commodity prices, tariffs, and lead times for medium-voltage cable, breakers, and valves), expedite shortages, and execute contingency plans to avoid project delays.
* Translate drawings, bills of materials, quantity takeoffs, and construction schedules into material forecasts and procurement pipelines, and update them as designs change.
* Partner with Finance on material budgets, commodity exposure, inventory investment, and savings tracking.
* Establish efficient workflows for damaged, defective, short-shipped, and surplus material, including returns and redeployment across sites.
* Negotiate warranty, delivery, and spare parts terms, and track supplier performance on on-time delivery, fill rate, quality, and price.

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
