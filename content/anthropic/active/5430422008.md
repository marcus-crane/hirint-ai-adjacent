## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

We are looking for an experienced EU Policy Analyst to build relationships with external stakeholders and develop policy positions within the EU ecosystem in order to drive progress in AI policy and safety. Working as part of the International Policy team, you will help develop policy engagement with the EU institutions, think tanks, academia and other relevant stakeholders. You will collaborate with policy colleagues on a wide variety of projects, from analyzing policy proposals and mapping the policy landscape to drafting submissions for public comment, planning events, and building strategic campaigns that amplify Anthropic's policy presence. This role will be crucial in advancing and supporting Anthropic's mission within the EU, shaping AI governance to balance safety, innovation, and European competitiveness.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Support policy engagement with the EU Commission, Parliament, relevant agencies and public sector partners on issues related to government policy and regulatory implications for frontier labs
* Engage key policy stakeholders (e.g., via briefings, consultations, demos, roundtables), in coordination with the wider EMEA policy team
* Analyze policy proposals and map the EU policy landscape to inform Anthropic's positions
* Draft submissions for public comment and consultations
* Plan events and build strategic campaigns that amplify Anthropic's policy presence
* Cultivate relationships with key policy and political partners across the EU AI policy ecosystem
* Partner with the EMEA GTM team to support commercial efforts as appropriate

## **Minimum qualifications**

* In-depth knowledge of the EU's digital regulatory landscape, including deep knowledge of EU AI and data policy
* Professional experience working on EU digital policy
* Ability to translate complex technical and regulatory topics into accessible, relevant information that drives understanding of the most crucial debates around AI and its impact on society
* A track record of effective and creative advocacy with EU governments and regulatory authorities
* Experience in event planning and stakeholder management, with strong organizational skills
* Strong written and verbal communication, with the ability to identify core priorities within the EU that effectively represent Anthropic's wider values and goals

## **Preferred qualifications**

* At least 5 years of professional experience working on EU digital policy
* Experience within the EU institutions or the Brussels policy ecosystem
* Experience drafting and executing complex policy campaigns that move the needle on legislative action
* A clear interest in how EU AI and data policy can translate into wider global standards on AI safety and security

This role is based in Brussels and sits on the EMEA Policy Team. It requires travel within EMEA (once or twice per quarter) and occasionally to the United States.

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
