## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the Role

Claude has had commercial impact, but there’s significantly more that frontier AI can do outside of general use cases. Some of the largest opportunities: a step change in health, education, and economic opportunity for the people and institutions the technology would otherwise reach last, sit outside general-purpose use cases. As the Product Manager for Beneficial Deployments within Anthropic Labs, that's your job. You will own two things: the 0-to-1 moonshot bets that could provide a step change in abundance, and the programs that get Claude into the hands of the organizations already working on these problems.

Anthropic Labs runs ambitious product experiments. The team does 0-to-1 product development, taking our most advanced research and turning it into new product categories like Claude Code and MCP. Beneficial Deployments is Anthropic's effort to make sure the benefits of frontier AI reach the people and institutions the market would otherwise reach last: nonprofits, public agencies, and other mission-driven organizations.

We are looking for someone who has founded something, worked inside a civic institution or beneficial organization, and shipped inside a large technology company, with the ability to identify and ultimately define entirely new product categories enabled by AI.

## Responsibilities

* Identify where emerging capabilities could change outcomes for underserved populations
* Work with researchers to understand what's emerging and what it means for users
* Build prototypes yourself to validate ideas before committing resources
* Lead 0-to-1 product development from research to internal prototypes to shipped products
* Define product strategy for experimental initiatives that push beyond our current offerings
* Creatively build MVPs and prototypes to validate product-market fit with the lowest cost possible
* Lead vision, strategy, roadmap, and execution of frontier technologies that leverage the latest AI capabilities to solve real-world problems

* Design and run the programs through which beneficial organizations across all pillars get access to Claude
* Own the path from access to success. Find out what stops these organizations from deploying Claude and remove it

## Minimum Requirements

* Have shipped products at a large technology company and know how to work with engineering, legal, policy, and go-to-market teams at scale
* Possess a deep technical background and have shipped technical products with engineering teams
* Have a track record of products or programs that reached a large number of users or organizations
* Can navigate and execute amidst ambiguity, flex into new domains based on the problem at hand, and find simple solutions
* Think creatively about the risks and benefits of new technologies, beyond existing checklists and playbooks
* Stay hands-on with emerging research and prototype with AI tools like Claude Code

## Preferred Requirements

* Have 5+ years in product management or equivalent operating roles, with experience launching new products and scaling existing ones
* Have worked inside a civic institution, public agency, nonprofit, or other beneficial organization
* Have founded a company or organization, or were one of the first people building one

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
