## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

Enterprises and developers reach Claude through the clouds they already run on: AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft. The cloud partner marketing team works with all three partners to put Claude in front of the people who build with it and the leaders who buy it.

As Program Manager, Cloud Partners, you'll own the programs and campaigns that run across those partnerships: technical webinars, hands-on developer workshops, and digital campaigns. You'll set the program strategy and calendar, decide which formats we invest in, and build each one so it can run on every cloud and in every region. You'll report to the leader of cloud partner marketing and work alongside the marketing leads for each cloud. They own the partner relationships and joint plans, and you'll own the scalable programs that deliver against them.

This is a hands-on individual contributor role with wide scope. You'll design new programs and run them yourself, then direct agencies, regional teams, and partners to scale what works. You'll be measured on the pipeline your programs generate and influence, and on how deeply they reach target accounts and technical audiences.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Own the strategy and calendar for cross-cloud programs, planned with the marketing lead for each cloud and tied to target accounts and pipeline goals
* Lead the technical webinar programs we run with cloud partners, from topic and speaker strategy through promotion, regional and on-demand versions, and follow-up
* Build a repeatable developer workshop model and scale it across cities and regions with cloud partners, systems integrators, and Anthropic's technical teams
* Lead digital campaigns, including audience strategy, creative briefs, budget pacing, and optimization
* Own the budget for your programs and recommend how market development funds (MDF) are invested across them
* Direct agencies and vendors end to end, including scoping, briefs, quality, and spend
* Set the goals for each program before it launches, report on pipeline and account reach, and recommend what to scale, change, or stop
* Build the follow-up motion so program leads turn into opportunities and pipeline
* Work directly with program and marketing counterparts at cloud partners, balancing their goals with Anthropic's priorities
* Turn what works into playbooks and self-serve tools that regional and partner teams can run, using Claude to speed up repeatable work

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
