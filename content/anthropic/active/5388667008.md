## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

This is a partner marketing role that demands product marketing craft. You'll be the single-threaded owner of how every Claude launch lands on partner marketing surfaces. We reach many of our most important customers through our partners. When we ship a new model or capability, it doesn't just launch on first-party surfaces; it launches across our growing partner ecosystem, each with their own product pages, blogs, seller enablement, and go-to-market motions. Our product release cadence has accelerated, and we're looking for someone to own that translation end-to-end.

Model and capabilities launches are the core of the job and the majority of the work. The rest is solution launches — the joint and co-built solutions that land on partner surfaces alongside our models. The two halves reinforce each other. Model launches create the moment; solution launches translate what’s possible for the sellers and customers on the other side.

You'll be the first dedicated owner of launch readiness across our partner surfaces, consolidating work currently spread across several people and creating a center of excellence for how we do this work. This role centralizes launch coordination for our partners — briefing partner teams under confidentiality, shaping how our positioning shows up on their surfaces, and orchestrating execution cross functionally. You'll be the person who knows exactly what's available on which surface, how we're messaging it, and how it all fits together. You will use this knowledge to ensure our launches are positioned for the highest impact and amplification across our partners.

The job is roughly half launch execution and half building. On the execution side, you'll run the launch playbook. On the building side, you'll define what we haven't had the bandwidth to create yet. You'll set the standard for how Anthropic shows up on partner surfaces and hold the rest of the company to it, without anyone involved reporting to you.

Anthropic reaches a substantial share of its customer base through partner surfaces, and the ecosystem is still growing. How Claude shows up on the platforms where enterprises actually buy AI is largely unwritten. This is the role that writes it.

## Key responsibilities

* Own partner launch readiness end-to-end, serving as the directly responsible individual for every model, feature, and solution launch across the partner ecosystem — from the first briefing through launch-day go-live and reach reporting
* Define the operating model for partner launches: set the standard for what readiness means, decide what's durable enough to share and when, and own the trade-offs when timelines and partner needs conflict
* Equip partner sellers and solution architects to position Claude for a customer workload without escalating to us — segment-specific enablement, technical content, and the materials their field actually reaches for
* Translate Anthropic positioning for partner surfaces, taking source messaging from Product Marketing and creating partner-specific versions that account for what's available where, what each partner needs to hear, and how the narrative lands on their platform
* Own the go-to-market motion for joint and co-built solutions on partner surfaces — position what a partner and Anthropic have built together, make the value legible to that partner's sellers and customers, and land it across the channels where their buyers look
* Work with Partnerships, Product, and partner-side teams to shape solutions into launchable moments, and make the call on which are worth a launch at all
* Run launch execution – Manage cross-functional review cycles, support partner launch preparedness, compile post-launch reporting
* Build post-launch momentum through partner webinars, technical content pipelines, and campaigns that keep partners selling after launch-day
* Represent the partner perspective internally — surface Anthropic-direct launches that will affect partner relationships, flag partner-experience issues in product decisions, and make sure partner considerations arrive early rather than as a fire drill
* Turn informal knowledge into documented, repeatable process as our release cadence and partner portfolio grow, and build the function so it scales beyond any one person

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
