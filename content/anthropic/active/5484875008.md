## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

As a Strategic Account Executive for quantitative firms at Anthropic, you'll join the foundational team introducing our frontier AI platform to the world's leading quantitative hedge funds, systematic asset managers, market makers, and proprietary trading firms. You'll drive adoption of safe, frontier AI by securing strategic, enterprise-scale deals with firms whose researchers, technologists, and traders are among the most technical users in financial services. You'll draw on your experience selling to quantitative firms to grow revenue while becoming a trusted advisor to your customers, helping them embed AI into quantitative research, software engineering, data, risk, and operations workflows, and uncovering its full range of capabilities. Working closely with Product and Marketing, you'll continuously sharpen our value proposition, sales motion, and market positioning for quantitative firms.

This role suits someone energised by building a new category with customers who test every claim, often build their own technology, and set a high bar for performance, security, and protection of their intellectual property. You'll identify high-potential accounts and run the strategies to win and grow them. By driving deployment of Anthropic's emerging products in quantitative firms, you'll help these organisations unlock new capabilities while advancing the responsible development of AI.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Own a book of strategic, high-value accounts across quantitative hedge funds, systematic asset managers, market makers, and proprietary trading firms, carrying and exceeding an individual revenue target
* Engage personally with C-level, research, and senior technical stakeholders (CIOs, CTOs, CISOs, Heads of Quantitative Research, Heads of Engineering, Heads of Data/AI), building business cases and value narratives
* Lead accounts through technical evaluation and procurement, security, data governance, regulatory, and legal review, through to production deployment and expansion
* Identify and prioritize high-potential opportunities within your accounts, building and executing account plans that turn strategic relationships into committed revenue
* Expand adoption from engineering teams to quantitative researchers, data scientists, portfolio managers, traders, risk, and operations, including in firms where teams operate independently and keep their work separate
* Run a disciplined pipeline and forecasting cadence, with accurate deal inspection, clear next steps, and predictable execution across a high-value, usage-driven book of business
* Partner closely with Applied AI, Solutions Architecture, and Product to design solutions, support rigorous customer evaluations, prove value quickly, and translate the needs of quantitative firms (research, engineering, data, risk, operations) into product and roadmap input
* Codify winning use cases, proof points, and reference stories from your deals while respecting customer confidentiality, partnering with Marketing and Enablement to help scale them across the broader financial services motion
* Orchestrate cross-functional and partner motions with Customer Success, Legal, Partnerships, and cloud partners to deliver a seamless customer experience, and represent Anthropic as a visible, trusted voice with customers and at industry events

## **Minimum qualifications**

* Enterprise sales experience with quantitative hedge funds, systematic asset managers, market makers, proprietary trading firms, or closely related buy-side or capital markets organizations
* A track record of carrying and exceeding quota in enterprise sales of technical, complex products (API-first platforms, cloud infrastructure, data/ML platforms, developer tools, or enterprise SaaS)
* Experience winning and growing strategic enterprise accounts, including building relationships with senior executives and technical decision-makers
* Operational rigor across complex, multi-stakeholder sales cycles, including balancing velocity with deal quality and forecasting accurately in fast-changing environments
* Ability to hold credible technical conversations with engineers, quantitative researchers, and technology leaders, and experience working effectively with solutions architects or applied AI teams
* Experience guiding customers through security, data governance, and legal review of technology purchases
* Clear written and verbal communication with audiences ranging from hands-on technical users to senior executives
* A genuine interest in deploying AI responsibly and motivation to advance Anthropic's mission of building safe, beneficial AI

## **Preferred qualifications**

* Experience bringing generative AI or LLM-based products, or other emerging platform technologies, to quantitative investment or trading firms
* Working knowledge of how quantitative firms operate, such as the research lifecycle, backtesting, market and alternative data, execution, and risk, and how needs differ between market makers, systematic managers, and multi-strategy funds
* Familiarity with the technology environments common in quantitative firms, such as Python, C++, kdb+/q, large-scale data platforms, and high-performance computing

* Experience selling with and through cloud and ecosystem partners such as AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft Azure, and global systems integrators
* Familiarity with consumption- or usage-based commercial models and value-based pricing conversations

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
