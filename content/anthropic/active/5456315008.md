## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

Anthropic is building data center capacity across many sites at once, and for every site we own or lease we have committed to invest significantly in the host community, on terms we can stand behind publicly.

We're looking for someone to own our community benefits program end to end, across an international portfolio of many sites, so that every commitment is tracked, funded, paid on schedule and publicly reported. You'll negotiate the community-benefit terms with developers and other counterparties, from the term sheet through the lease, then manage those developers after signature, run the payouts and publish each site's yearly reports.

You'll work closely with our data center deal team, Finance, Legal and Policy. Our Community Engagement Managers own on-the-ground community relationships and community benefits agreements with local governments; this role owns the program end to end, including the deal terms behind it.

## **Responsibilities**

**Program management**

* Own the commitments ledger: every community commitment, budget and payout at every site, and who holds the money
* Track payment schedules and rent recovery against each site's construction milestones, with Finance
* Manage developers after lease signature: make sure they actually hold and pay out community funds at our direction, send their data on time, and troubleshoot issues when they arise
* Run payouts: check who receives the money, run select programs, such as the electricity credit, through an outside partner, and publish who got what each year
* Own each site's yearly environmental and jobs reports: get the data from developers and utilities, have an outside firm check it, and publish it
* Track what is committed, signed, funded and paid, and where a deal is stuck
* Keep the terms we sign consistent with our public commitments
* Build and hire the team that runs this function on an ongoing basis

**Deals**

* Negotiate the community-benefit terms in developer leases and capacity agreements: who funds the community commitment, whether it sits on the developer's books or is recovered through rent, and when payments are made during construction
* Work the term sheets with developers and other counterparties, and partner with our data center deal team so these terms are in every new deal and expansion from the start
* Time each site's community commitments to its schedule: as early as the day after lease execution, and no later than substantial completion
* Build and maintain standard form templates for the community commitments and leases, so each new deal starts from an agreed position

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
