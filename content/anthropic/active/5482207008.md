## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

We are seeking a Head of PMO to build the system that lets Anthropic's Real Estate & Workplace organization operate at a much larger scale. As a member of the organization's leadership team, you will build and manage the team that runs the program management office across all four of our functions: Food & Beverage, Facilities, Portfolio Strategy & Transactions, and Construction Delivery. You have three mandates: make our processes work as one system, find and control cost across the real estate lifecycle, and give leadership the data to make large financial decisions with confidence.

You will build dashboards that show how the business is operating. Function leaders own their decisions and results. You own the system that makes those results visible, comparable, and repeatable across offices and specialized facilities in North America, Europe, and APAC. This is a senior role with year-long, organization-wide scope: you will define the problem, set the plan, and be in the room when our biggest operating decisions are made.

## **Key responsibilities**

**Process & Operating Model**

* Set the annual vision and roadmap for the PMO, and bring it in house from our external project management partner without disrupting active work
* Take over the implementation of our new project management platform from our external partner, and drive it to full rollout and adoption
* Own the stage gates and handoffs that carry work from initial request through opening day, across Portfolio Strategy & Transactions, Construction Delivery, Facilities, and F&B, with standard templates, clear owners, and deadlines
* Standardize vendor agreements and benchmarks across regions, with Construction Delivery, Legal, and function leaders owning contract positions

**Cost & Capital Discipline**

* Build capex variance controls with Construction Delivery, tracking budget burn, contingency, and change orders so overruns are caught early
* Benchmark facilities and food service spend across regions and flag outliers to function leaders, who own any renegotiation
* Partner with Finance, along with function leaders, on capital plans and operating budgets, tracking commitments, forecasts, and variances across the organization
* Quantify the savings and avoided costs the PMO delivers, and report them to leadership

**Business Intelligence & Planning**

* Build the leadership dashboards, tracking data such as desks against headcount, leasing deals against plan, time from need to space, on-time delivery, and cost per square foot against budget
* Turn that data into insights and recommendations that inform future real estate roadmaps, then lead operating improvements across functions and measure whether they worked
* Run the organization's operating rhythm and executive reporting to Finance, Chief People Officer, and other senior leaders, including the recurring Real Estate Forum
* Find where Claude and automation can replace manual tracking, reporting, and document work, and put those changes into daily use

**Team & Cross-Functional Leadership**

* Determine what team the PMO needs at our scale, then hire, manage, and develop it
* Hold external partners to the roles, standards, and decision authority Anthropic has defined
* Adjust the PMO's approach quickly as company priorities shift, and bring the organization along

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
