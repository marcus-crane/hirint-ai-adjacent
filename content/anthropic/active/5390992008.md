## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the Role**

Anthropic's GTM Programs team turns our most strategic go-to-market priorities into structured, measurable programs. The team is a core strategic business partner to the GTM organization, driving growth and operational excellence and accelerating the safe adoption of frontier AI across the world's leading enterprises. As Head of GTM Programs, EMEA, you will bring that function to Europe, the Middle East and Africa (EMEA), partnering directly with executive sales leaders, frontline managers, and sellers to understand their needs, then design, launch, and measure the programs that meet them.

This is a high-visibility leadership role that combines quantitative rigor with hands-on program execution. You'll work across a diverse group of cross-functional partners, including sales, marketing, enablement, and strategy, to build a connected system of programs that drives measurable pipeline and revenue impact across the region, then adapt those programs as the business evolves. EMEA spans many countries, languages, and buying cultures, so you will also decide what runs once for the region, what is localized, and what is left to country teams.

GTM Programs is a new function, and this is an opportunity to build it, not just join it. Every organization is deciding its AI strategy right now, and the programs this team builds shape how our field meets that moment. You'll help define what a well-run program looks like at Anthropic, with high autonomy.

## **Key Responsibilities**

* Serve as a trusted advisor to EMEA Sales Leadership: bring data-backed insight and recommendations into their operating reviews, including program and resourcing decisions, and shape the strategic initiatives that come out of them.
* Analyze the health of the EMEA pipeline across volume, conversion, velocity, and efficiency, identify breakdowns in the funnel such as lead quality, routing, or segmentation, and partner with Revenue Operations to implement solutions.
* Shape a consolidated demand generation plan and calendar across EMEA's inbound, outbound, and partner channels, aligning to regional and global sales priorities to meet pipeline generation targets.
* Own GTM programs end to end in close partnership with the field: translate leadership priorities into structured programs with clear objectives, owners, and milestones, and carry them from concept through launch, operations, and iteration, with measured uplift as the outcome you're accountable for.
* Partner closely with Marketing, Enablement, and Sales Strategy to standardize playbooks and build a unified, end-to-end approach to pipeline generation and progression rather than disconnected initiatives.
* Decide what to run once for EMEA, what to localize by sub-region or language, and what to leave to country teams, including outreach and data-protection rules that differ by market.
* Introduce AI-driven insights into lead prioritization, forecasting, and opportunity management, partnering with analytics teams to surface predictive signals such as conversion likelihood and deal risk.
* Define the measurement plan before a program launches: baseline, targets, uplift metrics, and how results will be attributed to the program.
* Instrument every program into the GTM operating cadence, so leadership can inspect results in the same reviews where they run the business.
* Package what's working (industry patterns, use cases, activation motions) into repeatable plays that sellers can run without you in the room, and lead program adoption and communication as the sales organization scales.
* Build and lead the EMEA GTM Programs team, developing people across countries and time zones.

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
