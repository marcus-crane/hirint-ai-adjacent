## About the role

As a Reseller Partner Manager for Japan, you will be responsible for recruiting, managing, and scaling our reseller and distributor partners to accelerate the adoption of Claude across the Japanese enterprise market. You will help partners add our products to their portfolios, build the commercial motions that make reselling Claude attractive, and drive joint go-to-market execution with them.

You will own quarterly revenue targets delivered through the reseller channel, both from new customer acquisition and from expansion within existing accounts. The position involves direct collaboration with partners and internal teams to remove obstacles and implement growth strategies. You will design partner recruitment, enablement, and go-to-market plans, using a test-and-learn approach in ambiguous situations. Strong business creation skills are needed to identify joint value propositions and develop co-selling models. While revenue contribution is the key metric, building a repeatable and scalable reseller program is what drives consistent growth.

This role is critical to expanding our reach in Japan beyond direct sales, enabling us to serve Japanese enterprises at scale through partners they already trust. You will be instrumental in building our reseller channel from the ground up in one of the world's most sophisticated technology markets.

## Responsibilities

**Reseller Partner Management**

* Identify, recruit, and onboard reseller and distributor partners that fit our Japan go-to-market strategy
* Develop and execute joint business plans with key resellers, including revenue targets, pipeline commitments, and enablement milestones
* Build and maintain executive and sales-leadership relationships within partner organizations, navigating complex organizational structures and decision-making processes
* Own the full partner lifecycle from initial engagement through long-term strategic alignment, including regular business reviews and QBRs
* Negotiate and structure reseller and distribution agreements, including commercial terms, margin structures, and deal registration

**Revenue Generation and Pipeline**

* Drive new customer acquisition and upsell revenue through reseller partners and achieve quarterly channel revenue targets
* Work with partner sales teams to identify, qualify, and close enterprise opportunities in key verticals (manufacturing, financial services, retail, telecommunications, life sciences)
* Support joint sales activities including customer workshops, seminars, proof-of-concepts, and account planning
* Track partner-sourced pipeline, forecast channel revenue, and report on partner performance metrics with detailed accuracy
* Manage channel conflict and maintain healthy dynamics between direct sales and reseller partners, including clear rules of engagement and deal registration processes

**Enablement**

* Lead reseller enablement programs covering product knowledge, sales methodology, positioning, and objection handling
* Develop and maintain Japanese-language sales tools, pricing guidance, and partner-facing documentation
* Run partner-facing activities such as training sessions, webinars, email campaigns, and joint events
* Coordinate with global product, pricing, and partner teams to ensure Japanese reseller requirements and customer feedback are represented in program design and roadmap discussions

**Go-to-Market**

* Design and execute joint marketing and demand generation campaigns with reseller partners
* Develop customer success stories and case studies that resonate with Japanese enterprise buyers
* Partner with internal teams to localize global channel programs and GTM strategies for the Japanese market
* Represent the company at partner events, customer engagements, and industry conferences (CEATEC, Japan IT Week, etc.)
* Identify and remove bottlenecks proactively by working with internal teams including Marketing, Sales, Legal, and Finance
* Foster alignment across departments to ensure successful partner execution and performance

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
