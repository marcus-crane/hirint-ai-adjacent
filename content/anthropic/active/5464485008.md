## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

The Claude Partner Network is how consulting firms, systems integrators, and other services partners build practices around Claude. When a company chooses a partner, it wants to know which firms have done this work well, in its industry and for its use case. Specializations are how the program will give it: credentials showing that a partner has demonstrated expertise in a given domain area.

As Partner Specializations Lead, you'll own this program end to end: the requirements a firm has to meet, the evidence review that decides who qualifies, the benefits that make a specialization worth earning, and the operating rhythm that keeps it credible year after year. Your job is to take an early framework into a structure that scales: opening it to thousands of partners, running evaluation cycles, and launching new specializations with cross-functional input.

This role suits someone who likes owning a standard. You'll work with partner managers, operations, enablement, marketing, and industry teams who each hold a piece of the program, and with senior leaders at partner firms who care a great deal about the outcome. A specialization only means something if it's earned, so you'll need to be rigorous with evidence, consistent in your decisions, and firm and clear when the answer is no.

## Key responsibilities

* Own each specialization's requirements, evidence standards, and benefits, and keep them current as our products and the partner network change
* Run each evaluation cycle from invitation to decision: collect partner evidence, reconcile it against internal data, lead the review, and document every decision
* Make and defend qualification decisions, including borderline cases, and apply the standard consistently across firms of very different sizes
* Communicate outcomes to partner executives and their Anthropic account teams, including clear guidance for firms that did not qualify
* Open the program to the wider partner network, and recommend which specializations come next based on customer demand, partner readiness, and Anthropic's priorities
* Define the evidence standard for new specializations with Applied AI, Product, and industry go-to-market teams
* Coordinate the teams that deliver the program's benefits (marketing, the partner directory, field enablement, technical support) so that earning a specialization leads to something real
* Work with Partner Enablement so that partner learning paths and certifications support each specialization
* Build the operating rhythm: renewal, remediation periods, an appeals path, and the tracking and reporting that show whether the program is working
* Design the review process to scale, using Claude and automation so that routine steps don't depend on a person
* Write and maintain partner-facing guidance (program guide sections, FAQs, application materials), clear enough for a partner to know where it stands without asking us, and internal guidance for field teams
* Use program data to recommend where each requirement's bar should sit and when it should move, and bring those recommendations to partner leadership

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
