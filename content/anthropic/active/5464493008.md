## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

**About the role**  
As part of our growing Data Science and Analytics team, you will play an instrumental role in our company’s mission of building safe and beneficial artificial intelligence by driving data-informed decision making across our organization. You bring experience from high-bar teams and are eager to help shape the norms and best practices of a growing data science team as Anthropic continues to scale. In this unique company, technology, and moment in history, your work will be critical to informing our strategy as we deploy safe, frontier AI at scale to the world.

You’ll be the senior data science leader embedded in our Core Apps product team and a strategic thought partner to its product leads. You’ll help set product direction for Claude Code and other new bets across Core Apps: bringing a point of view on where to invest, how to grow our Apps business, and how to deliver more value for our users. As a tech lead manager, you’ll also manage a small team of Product Data Scientists, setting their agenda and raising the bar for how data shapes product decisions.

**Responsibilities:**

* Act as a strategic thought partner to Core Apps product leads, helping shape product strategy, roadmap priorities, and the major bets the team makes
* Set the data science agenda for Core Apps, deciding where the Product Data Science team can have the most impact and what it should deprioritize
* Manage and develop a small team of Product Data Scientists, coaching them to move from answering questions to shaping decisions
* Frame the most important and ambiguous questions facing the business, size the opportunities, and make clear recommendations, including when they challenge the current direction
* Define how Core Apps measures success, establishing the core metrics and measurement frameworks that align teams and leadership on what matters
* Set the standard for experimentation and causal inference across Core Apps, and personally lead the highest-stakes analyses
* Turn complex analyses into clear narratives and recommendations that drive decisions with senior leadership and cross-functional partners
* Establish foundational data practices and shape the analytics infrastructure Core Apps needs to support rapid iteration and decision-making as our products grow

**You may be a good fit if you have:**

* Have extensive experience in data science, including leading analytics for a product area at significant scale  
  Have 3+ years managing data science teams, including setting team direction and developing senior data scientists  
  Have been a trusted partner to senior product and business leaders, with clear examples of changing a roadmap or high-stakes decision  
  Bring strong product sense and business judgment, including the ability to frame the right question and take a position with incomplete information  
  Have deep expertise in experimental design, causal inference, and statistical modeling at high scale, with enough fluency in Python and SQL to stay hands-on and guide others’ technical work  
  Communicate clearly in writing and in presentations, including to executive audiences  
  Have a bias for action and thrive in ambiguous, fast-moving environments where you must create clarity and drive progress  
  Care about the company’s mission of building helpful, honest, and harmless AI
* Strong candidates may also have:  
  Experience with AI/ML products, large language models, or developer tools in the AI/ML ecosystem

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
