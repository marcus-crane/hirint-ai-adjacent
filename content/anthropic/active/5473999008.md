## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

Anthropic's cloud partners are some of the biggest routes for Claude into enterprises across Europe, the Middle East and Africa. Each hyper scaler plans its marketing globally, and requires a dedicated regional counterpart who knows the EMEA market and can turn global programmes into activity that lands with customers regionally.

As Partner Marketing Manager, Cloud Partners, EMEA, you'll be that counterpart. You'll own the EMEA end of our AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft relationships. You'll work closely with our global cloud partner marketing team and with EMEA partnerships, sales and marketing, to ensure that our joint GTM investments land with the customers and industries that matter most in EMEA. Your job is to make sure every major global cloud moment has a clear EMEA plan behind it, that regional co-marketing funding goes to regional business priorities, and that our cloud partners in the region always know how best to work with Anthropic.

This is a chance to help build and shape EMEA partner marketing from the ground up, including how the function works.

## What you'll do

* Be the named regional marketing counterpart for AWS, Google Cloud, and Microsoft in EMEA, leading regional planning and directing investments with each partner
* Create the EMEA strategy for each major global cloud moment (re:Invent, Google Cloud Next, Ignite and similar)
* Build and run a short menu of repeatable regional formats per cloud, such as executive dinners, developer workshops and industry briefings, with a set number of slots each half
* Decide with partners and EMEA sales leaders where co-marketing funding goes, based on the regional segment plan and our focus accounts
* Lead the marketing side of topics where EMEA buyers set the agenda, such as data residency and sovereign cloud capability
* Work with partner sales teams on account-based campaigns for named enterprise accounts
* Track what each programme delivers in pipeline, influenced revenue and customer adoption, and report it back to partners and to Anthropic leadership
* Turn what works into repeatable playbooks other regions and partners can reuse

## You may be a good fit if you

* Care profoundly about AI being adopted safely and responsibly
* Have 10 years experience in B2B marketing, with hands-on experience marketing with or for AWS, Google Cloud or Microsoft
* Have run joint campaigns and events with a hyperscaler, and know how their partner funding, marketplace and co-sell programmes work
* Understand deeply the EMEA enterprise market, including how buying differs between EMEA regions
* Can hold a relationship with senior partner marketers and sales leaders, and say no to a request when it doesn't fit the plan
* Plan ahead, keep track of a lot of moving parts across time zones, and are comfortable building process where none exists yet
* Measure your work and change course when the numbers say so

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
