## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

We're hiring our first dedicated international tax professional in Asia-Pacific, based in Singapore. You'll be a key member of Anthropic's tax team, reporting to our Head of International Tax Operations / Business Advisory and working closely with tax colleagues in Dublin and our San Francisco headquarters.

As APAC Tax Lead, you'll own direct tax compliance and advisory across our Asia-Pacific entities and operations (Japan, Korea, India, Singapore, Australia, Taiwan) and help build tax infrastructure to support Anthropic's rapid international expansion.

## Key responsibilities

* Lead APAC direct tax compliance, working with external advisors (KPMG, PwC, Deloitte) to ensure timely and accurate filings across all jurisdictions
* Support transfer pricing documentation and intercompany transactions for APAC entities, including cost-plus arrangements and intercompany service agreements
* Manage withholding tax positions and treaty relief on cross-border flows, including customer receipts and vendor payments
* Advise on permanent establishment risks, entity structuring, and operational tax considerations as Anthropic expands APAC commercial and infrastructure activities
* Support the tax side of regional infrastructure expansion, including data center, colocation, and subsea cable arrangements and new entity formations
* Partner with indirect tax personnel on VAT/GST and consumption tax matters to align on tax strategy and overall compliance requirements
* Partner with Finance, Legal, and People teams on payroll taxes, equity compensation, and employee mobility matters
* Build relationships with local tax authorities and manage any inquiries or audits
* Contribute to global tax projects including public company readiness, SOX compliance, and tax technology implementations
* Working with indirect tax team on advisory and compliance and audit assistance

## Minimum qualifications

* Strong technical knowledge of corporate tax in one or more major APAC jurisdictions and cross-border tax issues across the region
* Deep experience with transfer pricing documentation, intercompany agreements, and withholding tax and treaty analysis
* CPA, CA, CTA, or equivalent professional qualification
* Excellent communication skills and ability to work across time zones with US and European colleagues
* Self-starter who thrives in ambiguity and can build processes from the ground up

## Preferred qualifications

* 12+ years of international tax experience, ideally with a mix of Big 4/advisory and in-house roles
* Exposure to technology or high-growth company tax matters
* Experience with the tax aspects of data center or infrastructure buildouts
* Knowledge of Pillar Two/global minimum tax rules and digital services taxes
* Experience with Indian direct tax matters, including permanent establishment and withholding tax issues
* Experience supporting IPO readiness or public company tax compliance
* Regional language skills
* Experience with systems buildout or automation

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
