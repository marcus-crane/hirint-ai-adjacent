## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

Join Anthropic as an EMEA-based Customer Success Manager supporting our Beneficial Deployments team's work with nonprofits. You'll be a strategic partner and trusted advisor to mission-driven organizations across EMEA, spanning a range of impact areas. Your focus is driving activation, adoption, and value of Claude across Claude for Enterprise, Claude Code, and the API. That means helping these organizations evolve how they work internally, and guiding them as they evolve how they serve their missions and constituents with AI.

You'll work as a pod with your Account Executive and Applied AI counterpart. Within the pod, you'll decide what level of engagement each customer most needs and either personally deliver it or connect the customer to resources that serve their needs. CS-led engagements range from high-touch 1:1 partnerships to one-to-many cohort engagements, with the latitude to design other approaches where they serve customers better.

As one of our early Customer Success Managers in EMEA, you'll be a pioneer: helping to build regional processes, establish new customer relationships, and represent Anthropic's brand in a high-growth market. You'll operate with significant autonomy while staying closely aligned with global teams, which calls for someone who thrives in ambiguous environments and works effectively across time zones.

The insights you gather from these partnerships will directly inform our research priorities, product development, and strategies for beneficial AI deployment. As the voice of the customer, you'll help shape how we build and deliver AI systems that amplify social good.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Work as a pod with your Account Executive and Applied AI counterpart, building shared territory and success plans
* Drive activation and adoption across your portfolio, using your judgment to match the engagement model to what each customer most needs
* Use your knowledge of each customer and of regional nuances to plan usage proactively, understanding current and future consumption and adoption and how they create realized value for the customer
* Build trusting relationships with nonprofit leaders, program teams, and IT staff to understand their mission, goals, and capacity
* Guide customers in evolving how they work internally, with change management suited to mission-driven cultures, including Train the Trainer programs, Center of Excellence development, and enablement that respects capacity constraints
* Help customers evolve how they serve their missions and constituents with AI, identifying high-impact use cases across program delivery, research, fundraising, and operations
* Become an expert in Anthropic's products across the API, Claude for Enterprise, and Claude Code, guiding customers to the right solutions and keeping them current on what's new
* Document and quantify value realized through social impact outcomes, program effectiveness, cost savings, and mission amplification, and share it in regular impact reviews that build the case for continued investment
* Build the repeatable playbooks, enablement resources, and regional processes that scale across the EMEA portfolio, and bring customer insights back to Product and Research

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
