## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

Anthropic is looking for an experienced G&A Recruiter to help build the business functions that keep our company running smoothly. You'll partner directly with hiring managers across our General & Administrative teams, owning searches that range from high-volume pipelines to highly strategic, hard-to-fill roles — and you'll be expected to context-switch between them without dropping quality.

This is a role for someone who thrives on ownership across a diverse portfolio. You'll manage a high req load across multiple hiring managers, use data and systems to prioritize and surface issues early, and build repeatable processes that scale. We're also looking for someone who actively leverages AI and automation to increase throughput while protecting a great candidate experience.

Beyond execution, you'll bring genuine recruiting expertise to your stakeholders: calibrating profiles, sharing market insight, improving processes, and pushing back constructively when patterns aren't working. As Anthropic grows quickly, this role will be key to scaling the teams that support our mission.

## Key responsibilities

* Own full-cycle recruiting across G&A functions, adapting candidate profiles, comp benchmarks, and assessment criteria to each segment and level
* Context-switch between high-volume hiring and strategic, senior searches while maintaining consistent quality and candidate experience
* Manage a high req load across multiple hiring managers without becoming a bottleneck — using data and systems to prioritize, surface issues early, and keep pipelines healthy
* Build repeatable, scalable processes, and leverage AI and automation to increase throughput across your portfolio
* Integrate into existing hiring rhythms while bringing recruiting expertise — calibration, market insight, and process improvement — and pushing back constructively when patterns aren't working
* Own outcomes across a diverse portfolio, holding yourself accountable for hiring results rather than activity
* Protect Anthropic's culture by deeply understanding our mission and screening for alignment alongside capability

## Minimum qualifications

* Have demonstrated success recruiting across G&A role types and segments (e.g., Finance, Legal, People, Operations) and understand how profiles, comp, and assessment criteria differ across levels
* Can show examples of moving fluidly between high-volume pipelines and strategic searches without sacrificing quality or candidate experience
* Have managed heavy req loads across multiple hiring managers, and use data to prioritize, spot bottlenecks, and surface issues before they become problems
* Build repeatable processes and are actively experimenting with AI and automation tools to increase your throughput
* Have strong stakeholder management skills — you integrate into existing hiring rhythms, add value beyond execution, and push back constructively when something isn't working
* Bring an ownership mentality across a diverse portfolio, with a continuous improvement mindset
* Are deeply curious about Anthropic's mission and passionate about AI's potential to positively impact the world

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
