## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

Anthropic’s EMEA partner ecosystem of AI-native boutiques and regional Systems Integrators is increasingly how our Commercial and Digital Native Business (DNB) customers get Claude into production and grow their use of it. As a Partner Sales Manager (PSM) you will be the connection between that ecosystem and our EMEA sales organisation: you’ll own the working relationship between Commercial and DNB sales leaders and Account Executives on one side and our bench of specialist partners on the other, working alongside the Partner Account Managers who own the individual partner relationships.

Your objective is customer value. You’ll engage the right specialised boutique or regional partner on the right opportunity, and hold that partner accountable for the outcomes they own — Claude enablement, deployment, time-to-value and return on investment for the customer. Partner-sourced and partner-influenced revenue matter and you’ll carry targets for them, but in this role they follow from getting customers live and successful on Claude rather than the other way round. If you have built your career in customer success, partner success or channel roles and enjoy being the person who makes Sales and partners operate as one team, this role is designed for you. We are hiring two PSMs, one based in Dublin and one in London, reporting into GTM Partnerships EMEA.

## Key responsibilities

* Act as the primary link between Commercial and DNB sales leaders and AEs and the EMEA boutique and regional SI partner ecosystem, matching the right specialist partners to the right opportunity
* Drive partner-attach on Commercial and DNB opportunities, working deal-by-deal and AE-by-AE to identify where a boutique or regional SI can accelerate a sale, an activation or a deployment
* Hold engaged partners accountable for the outcomes they own — Claude enablement, deployment, time-to-value and ROI — tracking progress and escalating early where delivery is at risk
* Deliver partner-sourced and partner-influenced revenue against defined targets
* Stay closely coordinated with Partner Account Managers on partner capacity, capability and fit, and build direct relationships with partner leadership and delivery contacts so you always know who is strong at what across the bench
* Represent Sales’ voice into the partner ecosystem and the ecosystem’s voice back into Sales, surfacing blockers, wins and patterns to both sides
* Track and report partner-attach and partner-driven customer outcomes to EMEA Sales and Partnerships leadership
* Help shape how the EMEA boutique and regional SI ecosystem engages with Commercial and DNB sales motions as the programme scales

## **Minimum qualifications**

* Experience in customer success, partner success or channel/partner sales at a B2B software, cloud or AI company
* Track record of operating between Sales and partners or delivery teams — brokering, matching and following through to a customer outcome you did not directly control
* A clear, specific view of what good looks like for customer enablement, deployment, time-to-value and ROI, and demonstrated confidence holding a third party accountable for delivering it
* Strong relationship-building across distinct stakeholder groups: sales leaders and AEs, partner or alliance managers, and partner-side leadership and delivery teams
* Working proficiency with CRM and pipeline tooling (for example Salesforce) and with tracking and reporting adoption, deployment or usage metrics

* Genuine interest in Anthropic’s mission and the safe, responsible development of AI

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
