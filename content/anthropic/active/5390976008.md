## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

Anthropic is hiring a Head of GTM Programs to build a foundational function from the ground up. Reporting to the Global Head of Revenue Operations, you'll own the content, playbooks, and cross-functional coordination that help our go-to-market organization — sales, marketing, partnerships, and customer success — execute consistently and well as we scale across regions and segments.

This is a zero-to-one role. There's no existing playbook to inherit: you'll define what "GTM programs" means at Anthropic, build the initial roadmap, and bring the coordination that lets a fast-growing global GTM organization move faster and more consistently. You'll spend your first months embedded with GTM teams and their leaders, learning where the largest opportunities are and where the right content or coordination would make the biggest difference. Then you'll build it.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Build the global GTM programs function from scratch, working with GTM and revenue operations leadership to define priorities and a roadmap for your first year
* Design and maintain a scalable set of GTM content and playbooks that teams actually use, and keep them current as products, messaging, and the market evolve
* Establish a single, well-organized source of truth for GTM content, with clear ownership and version control across global teams
* Drive alignment across sales, marketing, partnerships, and customer success so that programs work end-to-end rather than optimizing for one function at another's expense
* Use feedback and performance data from across GTM to continuously identify gaps and refine programs and content
* Define success metrics for new programs — adoption, time-to-value, and productivity impact — and report on them regularly
* Partner with regional GTM leaders so that global programs reflect the realities of different markets and segments

## **Minimum qualifications**

* Experience building or scaling go-to-market programs, content, or productivity initiatives within a B2B organization
* A track record of designing and launching playbooks or content that go-to-market teams adopted and used
* Experience partnering directly with multiple go-to-market functions — such as sales, marketing, partnerships, or customer success — and with revenue operations to identify and solve productivity gaps
* Experience leading cross-functional programs end-to-end with a high degree of autonomy
* Experience writing clear, concise go-to-market content or playbooks for use by others

## **Preferred qualifications**

* Experience building a GTM programs, sales programs, or enablement function from scratch, ideally during a period of rapid company growth
* Experience supporting a global or multi-region go-to-market organization
* Background in AI, machine learning, or other emerging technology sectors
* Experience using data to evaluate program effectiveness and to influence go-to-market and revenue operations leadership

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
