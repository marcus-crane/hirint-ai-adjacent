## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

We're hiring a Customer Marketing, Startups Lead to own our strategy for how Anthropic shows up in market with the startups building on Claude, from early-stage founders to our largest AI-native customers. You'll figure out how we can help startups grow, and find creative ways to amplify their stories to inspire more companies to do the same.

This is a role for someone who has led marketing at a startup and knows what founders need. You'll work closely with our Startups account teams to decide where co-marketing will create real value for both the startup and Anthropic. For the startups we prioritize together, you'll work directly with founders and their teams as a marketing partner on channels and the joint messages we take to market. Many of these companies don't have big marketing teams yet, so you'll also advise them on storytelling and growth.

This role is deeply cross-functional. You'll work with Startups Sales leadership, Startup Partnerships, Segment Marketing, Field Marketing, Partner Marketing, Product Marketing, Brand, Comms, and Legal. You'll set direction with senior leaders here and at our customers, and lead a team of startups customer marketers while staying hands-on in the work yourself.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Own startups co-marketing, including deciding which startups and moments are worth investing in
* Be a marketing partner to founders and CEOs on channels and joint messaging, and advise companies without built-out marketing teams on storytelling and growth
* Partner with Startups Sales leadership and embed in the deal flow so you can see deals coming and plan how co-marketing can support them
* Work with account teams on the market development funds (MDF) in startup contracts, and own how those funds are deployed
* Build programs that bring co-marketing to hundreds of startups, beyond our top accounts
* Set the bar for startup storytelling and find creative ways to amplify those stories, from case studies and founder films to content on how startups use Claude
* Find opportunities to bring startups into Anthropic's launches and campaigns
* Work with Startup Partnerships and Segment Marketing on the Claude Startups program and our relationships with VCs and accelerators
* Build a feedback loop that shares what founders care about with Anthropic's leadership and GTM teams
* Lead and develop a team of startups customer marketers

## **Minimum qualifications**

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
