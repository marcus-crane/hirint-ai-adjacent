## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

As a Web Strategy Lead at Anthropic, you will build and own the strategy and roadmap for claude.com and anthropic.com, turning our websites into our strongest acquisition, conversion, and storytelling channels. You are our first web strategy lead, which means you'll shape the vision without an existing playbook: growing awareness, preference, and adoption of Claude across prosumer and enterprise audiences on claude.com, and building trust in Anthropic's research and mission on anthropic.com.

In this role, you'll own the portfolio strategy for our marketing web surfaces, the roadmap and prioritization process that decides what we build, the experimentation program that makes our sites measurably better, and the analytics foundation that makes growth decisions fast. You'll partner closely with Web Design, Growth Product, Data Science, and Engineering day to day, and you'll prioritize and align inbound requests from Product Marketing, Enterprise Marketing, Sales, Research, Policy, Comms, SEO and paid channel owners, and regional marketing and localization teams.

## Responsibilities

* Own the long-term vision and strategy for claude.com and anthropic.com, defining how our marketing surfaces work together as a portfolio to drive awareness, education, and growth
* Own the web roadmap, prioritizing requests from partner teams against that strategy, deciding what we build and what we don't, and holding each surface accountable to its audience and goals
* Lead the web experimentation program on claude.com, finding the highest-leverage opportunities and partnering with Web Design and Growth Product to test, learn, and ship with increasing speed and rigor
* Build our web analytics foundation with Data Science, ensuring we have the instrumentation, dashboards, and funnel reporting needed to make growth decisions quickly
* Lead our international and localization strategy for the web, partnering with regional marketing and localization teams to ensure our surfaces perform in every market we serve
* Partner with Growth Product, Data Science, and Enterprise Marketing to build the tooling, instrumentation, and funnel infrastructure that make web experimentation fast and measurable
* Partner with SEO and paid channel owners on on-page requirements and landing page experiments
* Manage inbound web requests from Product Marketing, Enterprise Marketing, Sales, Research, Policy, Comms, and other stakeholders, prioritizing against growth impact rather than request volume
* Develop data storytelling for the web, turning what we're testing and learning into a clear narrative of business impact
* Present strategy, results, and learnings to marketing and executive leadership

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
