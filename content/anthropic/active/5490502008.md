## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

Anthropic's Life Sciences team is building a world-class research group focused on making fundamental biological discoveries. The team combines cutting-edge AI with hands-on biological research, positioning Anthropic at the forefront of AI-accelerated scientific discovery.

This role offers a unique opportunity to shape how AI transforms biological research. We are looking for an experienced leader to take responsibility for how our research gets executed, across every program and field we work in.

AI is making it much faster to generate scientific ideas, and testing them is now the harder problem. As our Life Sciences Operator for research, you'll be a key partner to our research teams, who work across many fields of biology. You'll build the relationships, the team, and the systems that turn their ideas into results quickly and to a high scientific standard.

You'll help build our approach to operationalizing science. The ideal candidate is self-directed and has built a career in organizations that hold a high bar for scientific and operational rigor.

## Key responsibilities

* Turn research ideas into well-scoped experimental plans with clear milestones and criteria for success
* Select and manage the external partners best placed to run each project, and coordinate work that spans more than one
* Build a team of internal and external experts with the breadth the science requires
* Manage a large portfolio of concurrent projects and see each one through with speed and ownership
* Keep research leadership informed on progress, turnaround time, and outcomes
* Orchestrate research agendas with Claude and our internal agent frameworks
* Build insights into where AI-driven research planning works and where it falls short, and share them with the model-improvement and product teams as evaluations, datasets, and failure cases

## Minimum qualifications

* Experience at a contract research organization (CRO), biotech, or pharmaceutical company, accountable for delivering experimental programs for other scientists from scoping to final data
* Established record of scoping, placing, and managing outsourced experimental work with several CROs at once, from either the sponsor side or the CRO side
* Ability to take an idea from outside your own field, find the right expert, and judge whether a plan is executable and sensibly scoped
* Experience running many projects in parallel, and the ability to show how you tracked, prioritized, and reported on them
* Use of AI tools as a default part of how you work, and interest in finding out how far that can go
* Comfort navigating ambiguity and developing solutions in rapidly evolving research environments
* Results orientation, with a bias towards flexibility and impact

## Preferred qualifications

* A PhD in a biological science, bioengineering, or a related field
* Existing working relationships with CROs across several experimental areas, and a network you can call on to find new ones
* Experience as a study director or project lead at a CRO, or leading external research at a biotech or pharmaceutical company
* Hands-on breadth across more than one experimental area
* Experience scaling an operational function through a period of rapid growth

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
