## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

We are looking for a hands-on Legal Specialist (Law Enforcement) to join our Litigation, Regulatory & Policy team at Anthropic. In this role, you will be a key player in building out our legal function and running how Anthropic responds to law enforcement and government requests for user data, while navigating the intricate regulatory landscape of the AI industry. We expect this role to flex beyond the law enforcement queue into legal operations to support the broader litigation, regulatory, and policy team. The successful candidate will be a strong team player, able to build positive relationships with colleagues and work collaboratively toward shared goals.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Own the end-to-end response to law enforcement and government requests for user data, from intake and validation through production and closeout, including subpoenas, court orders, search warrants, preservation letters, and emergency disclosure requests from domestic and foreign authorities
* Prepare and execute data productions: scoping, data retrieval, coordination with counsel, and eventual production
* Handle foreign and cross-border process, including MLATs and letters rogatory, and manage escalations across jurisdictions
* Handle civil third-party subpoenas and preservation demands
* Maintain expertise in where user data lives across Anthropic's product surfaces and operate the internal data retrieval tooling
* Run and improve the law enforcement response process and tooling
* Partner with Trust and Safety, Security, Privacy, and Engineering on requests that touch user accounts, investigations, or safety escalations
* Coordinate with outside counsel, alongside in-house counsel, on objections, challenges, and narrowing
* Produce recurring reporting on request volume, deadlines, and queue health, and support Anthropic's periodic transparency reporting on government requests
* Monitor legal developments in government access to user data and advise the team on their operational impact
* Support litigation and regulatory matters and stretch beyond core responsibilities as needed to support the legal team's objectives

## **Minimum qualifications**

* Experience in a legal operations, business/operations manager, or paralegal role in an in-house legal department, or similar experience
* Direct, front-line experience handling law enforcement or government requests for user data, including subpoenas, court orders, warrants, preservation letters, and emergency disclosure requests
* Working knowledge of the legal framework for law enforcement access to user data, including the Stored Communications Act and the mechanics of foreign and cross-border requests such as MLATs
* Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to work effectively with colleagues at all levels of the organization
* Demonstrated ability to exercise good judgment and maintain the highest standards of discretion and confidentiality when handling sensitive legal matters, including under time pressure
* Strong project management skills, including the ability to prioritize tasks, meet deadlines, and manage multiple projects simultaneously
* Experience owning an intake, queue, or docketing process end to end,  including deciding what gets escalated and maintaining rigorous tracking
* Comfort operating and improving the tooling a legal process runs on (trackers, spreadsheets, forms, aliases, and simple automations), with a willingness to make changes directly rather than route every change to someone else
* A proactive, solutions-oriented approach to identifying, recommending, and implementing process improvements within the legal department

## **Preferred qualifications**

* 5+ years of experience in legal operations, business/operations management, or a paralegal role in an in-house legal department
* 2+ years of experience at a high-growth technology company, with exposure to the legal challenges and opportunities presented by emerging technologies
* Experience building or scaling a law enforcement response process
* Experience managing legal intake process within a growing company
* Demonstrated ability to think critically and creatively, adapting to new challenges and finding practical solutions to complex legal issues
* Familiarity with legal project management software such as Asana, Trello, or Jira, and a willingness to learn and adopt new tools to streamline legal processes

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
