## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

Anthropic’s Life Sciences team is working to make Claude a useful partner across biology and drug development, so that promising science can reach patients sooner. Genetic medicines are one area where we see real potential, particularly for diseases with few or no treatment options.

Chemistry, manufacturing, and controls (CMC) often sets the pace in this field. We’re hiring a CMC & Manufacturing Lead to bring deep technical expertise to our team. You’ll guide CMC strategy across a range of genetic medicine technologies, work closely with external partners, and help our researchers and engineers make Claude useful for real CMC work.

Much of today’s CMC practice was built for products made at scale for large populations. You’ll help work out what better approaches could look like for smaller ones, including platform processes, faster testing, and ways to carry knowledge from one product to the next.

If you have deep experience manufacturing genetic medicines and want to explore how AI can help accelerate the way they’re developed, we’d love to hear from you.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Guide CMC and manufacturing strategy for genetic medicines, from early development through clinical supply
* Serve as a CMC expert to external partners, reviewing process, analytical, and supply plans and resolving technical issues together
* Provide technical oversight of externally conducted process development, formulation, analytical development, fill-finish, stability, and release testing, with phase-appropriate specifications and control strategies
* Collaborate with scientists across disciplines to turn early research into robust, GMP-ready processes
* Oversee quality for externally manufactured material, including audits, quality agreements, and batch disposition
* Evaluate novel manufacturing, analytical, and delivery technologies, and make clear recommendations
* Develop approaches that reduce the time and cost of reaching clinical material without compromising product quality or patient safety
* Work with Anthropic researchers and engineers to identify where Claude can speed up CMC work, contribute to evaluations and tools, and test results against real-world problems
* Identify CMC and supply chain risks early, and communicate status, tradeoffs, and decisions clearly

## **Minimum qualifications**

* Biotechnology or pharmaceutical industry experience leading CMC for gene therapy, gene editing, or nucleic acid products, including taking a product from preclinical development into the clinic
* Deep expertise in several of the following, and working knowledge of the others: lipid nanoparticles (LNP), mRNA, guide RNA (gRNA), adeno-associated virus (AAV), and antisense oligonucleotides (ASO), including how they are manufactured, characterized, and released
* Deep understanding of GMP manufacturing and regulatory CMC requirements for genetic medicines, including writing or reviewing CMC sections of INDs or equivalent filings
* A strong track record of selecting and managing CDMOs, CROs, and other external partners, including technology transfer and quality oversight
* Demonstrated creativity in manufacturing novel products, shown by process, analytical, or supply solutions you developed where no established precedent existed
* Ability to evaluate novel technologies rigorously from data and turn that evaluation into a clear recommendation
* Clear written and verbal communication, including explaining CMC tradeoffs to people outside the field
* Curiosity about how AI can change drug development, and excitement about Anthropic’s mission to build safe and beneficial AI

## **Preferred qualifications**

* 10+ years in the biotechnology or pharmaceutical industry, with a focus on CMC and gene therapy product development
* An advanced degree in chemical engineering, biochemistry, pharmaceutical sciences, molecular biology, or a related field
* Experience with rare disease or small-population programs, or with platform-based manufacturing and regulatory approaches
* Direct experience leading CMC discussions with the FDA and other health authorities, such as the EMA and MHRA
* Hands-on experience with GMP quality activities, such as vendor audits, quality agreements, deviation review, and batch disposition
* Experience building a CMC function or working in a fully outsourced model
* Experience with small-batch or rapid-turnaround GMP manufacturing and accelerated release testing
* Experience with technical due diligence on external technologies or manufacturing partners
* Hands-on experience using LLM tools like Claude in your day-to-day work

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
