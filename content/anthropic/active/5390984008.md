## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

We're looking for a BDR Enablement Lead to own the curriculum and craft development of Anthropic's business development organization.

Our BDRs are the front door to the pipeline and, for many of them, this is the first serious sales role of their career. What they learn here shapes how they sell for the next decade. This role owns that formation: the onboarding curriculum, sales foundations, the ongoing skill development, and the pipeline generation playbooks that turn effort into meetings.

It is also a role with an unusual opportunity. Anthropic's BDRs should be the most AI-native prospecting team in the industry. You will build the workflows that make that true — using plugins, account research skills, and agentic tooling to change what a BDR can accomplish in a day. Doing this well requires someone who is genuinely excited about the craft of prospecting and about rebuilding it around what AI now makes possible.

## Key responsibilities

* Own the BDR onboarding curriculum, taking new hires from day one to first meeting booked as quickly as we responsibly can
* Enable BDRs on AI-native prospecting workflows, including plugins, account research skills, and agentic tooling that meaningfully change BDR productivity
* Develop and maintain pipeline generation playbooks across segments, personas, and motions
* Run ongoing skill development on sales fundamentals: cold calling, written outreach, objection handling, discovery, and qualification
* Certify BDRs on our products, our qualification criteria, and our messaging
* Partner with BDR leadership to identify where skill gaps are constraining pipeline, and build focused interventions
* Build the coaching and call-review infrastructure that gives BDRs specific, frequent feedback
* Measure enablement impact on ramp time, activity quality, meeting conversion, and pipeline contribution
* Create a clear development path from BDR into the next role, in partnership with sales leadership

## **Minimum qualifications**

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
