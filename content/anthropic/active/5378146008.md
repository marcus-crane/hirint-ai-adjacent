## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the Role**

As a Strategy & Operations team member on the Product Partnerships team at Anthropic, you'll own the analytical foundation behind how Anthropic thinks about the value of data and product partnerships. You'll build the valuation frameworks that inform where we invest, and serve as the analytical partner to deal leads through negotiations.

Your ability to bring analytical rigor to open-ended questions, and to think broadly about the technology and content ecosystem, will serve you well in this role. This is a great fit for someone who thrives in ambiguity, can seamlessly pivot between high-level strategy and detailed analysis, and gets energy from solving problems that don't have an established playbook.

## **Responsibilities**

### **Strategic Analysis and Valuation**

* Own the valuation frameworks used across data and product partnerships, and develop new approaches as partnerships evolve
* Build the valuation models, partnership evaluation frameworks, and ROI analyses behind partnership initiatives
* Maintain a benchmark library of comparable transactions and historical pricing, and conduct industry and competitive research to inform strategic decision-making
* Serve as the internal expert on partnership spend, historical transactions, and forward planning
* Take the team's most complex challenges from ambiguity to clarity, and prepare the analyses and recommendations to address them

### **Deal Support and Negotiation Strategy**

* Review inbound proposals and pricing from partners, and provide deal leads with a defensible analytical view
* Partner with deal leads to develop negotiation strategies grounded in data, and support partner managers in navigating novel partnership structures

### **Operational Excellence**

* Design scalable systems and processes that enable the team to grow impact efficiently, leveraging Claude to build them
* Build dashboards and reporting infrastructure to track portfolio health, deal pipeline, spend, usage, and key success metrics
* Optimize the partnership lifecycle: sourcing, inbound tracking, contracts, cross-functional approvals, delivery, and feedback loops
* Serve as the liaison between Partnerships, Research, Product and Finance, and work closely with xfn teams to address operational challenges

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
