## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

# **About the role**

We're looking for a product marketer to join the Claude Platform Product Marketing team. You'll drive go-to-market for launches across the platform, from new API features and developer tools to the capabilities that ship with new models.

In this role, you'll partner with Product to bring new capabilities to market, write the positioning and messaging that explain what each one does and who it's for, and give Sales the narratives and materials they need to win technical deals. If you've run launches for developer platforms, API products, or technical infrastructure and want to launch new capabilities every week, this role is for you.

# **Responsibilities**

* Lead end-to-end go-to-market planning and execution for new platform products, features, and capabilities
* Develop compelling product messaging, positioning, and value propositions aligned with technical audience needs
* Drive product launches that generate market momentum, from flagship releases to incremental feature updates
* Design and implement developer marketing campaigns to drive API adoption and platform usage
* Support sales enablement initiatives that equip our teams to win: battlecards, pitch materials, customer stories, and objection handling
* Partner with Product to shape roadmap priorities based on competitive intel, customer research, and field feedback
* Work closely with Developer Relations, Comms, Creative, Marketing, and Sales to effectively bring new products to market

# **Minimum requirements**

* Have a proven track record of developing and executing go-to-market strategies for API products or developer tools, including successful product launches
* Have a strong technical background with the ability to understand and communicate complex AI features to both technical and non-technical audiences
* Excel at cross-functional collaboration, particularly in aligning Product, Sales, and Marketing strategies
* Possess strong storytelling skills with a demonstrated ability to create diverse marketing content, including blogs, presentations, sales collateral, website copy, and customer case studies
* Have exceptional writing skills with a demonstrated ability to adapt tone, style, and complexity for diverse audiences (executives, technical users, end customers) and formats (long-form content, product briefs, email campaigns, social media)

# **Preferred requirements**

* Have 10+ years of product marketing experience and at least 5 years focused on developer platforms, APIs, or technical products
* Experience in highly competitive markets requiring sharp differentiation
* A background in marketing AI/ML products or developer tools to enterprise buyers
* Experience working closely with Sales teams on strategic, technical deals

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
