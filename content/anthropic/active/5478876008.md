## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

Anthropic's products are growing fast in reach and complexity. Every new model, product, and channel brings problems no one predicted. We want to catch each issue as early as possible, get customers unblocked fast, and fix the root cause. The best experience is one where a customer never has to ask for help.

We are building a Support Technology engineering team to solve what stands between a customer and Claude, whether they want to use it but can't or are unhappy with their experience. That covers individual users, businesses, and customers who reach Claude through partners.

Our goal is a better product experience for everyone who uses Claude. We make it easy for customers to get help and talk to us, through Claude itself and via human specialists with the tools to solve their problems. We also determine why they needed help and fix the cause in the product.

Billing is a good example. When a charge is confusing, a payment fails, or a plan doesn't unlock, a customer should be able to reach us and get it resolved in one conversation. Then we want to learn why it happened and change the product, whether that's the subscription flow, the payment error, or the invoice, so the next customer never has to ask.

As one of the team's early engineers, you will set the technical direction and build the core systems behind this work. We expect Claude to handle more of each case over time, from diagnosing an issue to resolving it to flagging the product fix behind it. Success means customers run into fewer problems, get them truly solved when they do, and are happy with the help they receive, whether it comes from Claude or a person.

This is a hands-on role with high leverage. You will write a lot of code and lead across teams, since many fixes belong to other product teams. You will partner with our Product Manager, the Product Support team, and product engineering teams such as Billing Platform.

## Key responsibilities

* Improve the product experience at the root: Build agents that spot where customers keep getting stuck and find the root cause, then ship the fix or drive it with the owning team until customers stop running into the problem
* Make our support agents reliable first responders: Build the tools, APIs, and safe, audited workflows our Claude-powered support agents use for common fixes, with evals, guardrails, and clean handoffs to people, and measure whether customers' problems actually get solved
* Build the platform foundations: Create a reliable support platform that scales with our growth, including the experience and tools for customers and specialists, and the data that shows what customers are running into and whether our changes help
* Support enterprise and partner channels: Extend support to business customers, admins, and partner-sold customers, including cases that start outside our own products
* Raise the bar and build with Claude: Set standards for reliability, observability, testing, and rollouts, mentor engineers, and use Claude heavily in your own work

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
