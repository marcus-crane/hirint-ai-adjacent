## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

Reporting into Partner Operations & Systems within Partner Experience, this role is the operations generalist behind the partner program — an ecosystem growing quickly, with volume growing faster and an operational layer still being designed.

The role has two anchors: intake — owning the front door for new process requests and building the intake process itself — and flexibility, serving as the generalist allocated to the most pressing operational issue at the time. You'll serve as the central hub for partner experience, working cross-functionally with Finance, RevOps, Sales, Enablement, Product, and Legal to ensure seamless execution of partnership strategy.

## Key responsibilities

* Own intake for new process requests: triage, scope, and prioritize needs across the partner organization and decide what gets built
* Design new partner operations processes from zero to one: stand up the workflow, documentation, and requirements, and iterate with data
* Build new AI automations with Claude: create and run Claude-powered automations and AI workflows across partner operations, taking them from prototype to production and improving them with use
* Take on special operations projects: join small cross-functional teams on priority initiatives
* Run the process and data side of registrations, referrals, incentives, applications, and onboarding, keeping the data decision-grade
* Encode program policy in the systems: work with program owners so rules are enforced by systems, and own data hygiene for the partner record of truth
* Enable partners and internal teams: build the guidance partners, Partner Managers, and Account Executives need for new initiatives
* Collaborate across go-to-market: partner with Sales, Finance, Enablement, Marketing, and Revenue Operations to stay aligned with the business
* Measure what matters: establish service levels and report on volume, cycle time, and the metrics that show what to fix next

## Minimum qualifications

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
