## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

Anthropic is seeking a Privacy Counsel to join our Legal team. You'll counsel on complex privacy issues and build the processes and programs that address privacy and data protection in a global context. This is a unique opportunity to join a dynamic and growing team, where you will have the chance to shape the company's privacy and data protection strategy and compliance program.

You will work at the intersection of novel legal questions, fast-moving product development, and a high-profile regulatory environment, partnering cross-functionally with Legal, Integrity & Compliance, Safeguards, Security, Policy, Research and Product.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Counsel product, research and engineering teams across the model and product lifecycles, applying laws written before frontier AI with clarity and fresh thinking
* Translate complex technical architecture into clear, actionable risk advice
* Deliver integrated guidance on safety, security and data protection
* Lead privacy on Anthropic's most strategic deals and partnerships, opening creative paths for novel technologies
* Negotiate privacy and data protection terms in enterprise, platform and partnership commercial agreements, including DPAs, BAAs, data sharing agreements and cross-border transfer terms
* Lead privacy diligence on mergers and acquisitions, and support corporate governance
* Collaborate closely with counsel and privacy colleagues on joint analysis, bringing competing views together into a single, well-reasoned position

## **Minimum qualifications**

* JD from an accredited law school and active membership in at least one U.S. state bar
* Hands-on experience advising a technology business (in-house or at a leading law firm) on privacy and data protection
* Experience drafting and negotiating data protection agreements, such as DPAs, BAAs and data sharing agreements
* Working knowledge of key global privacy laws, including those of the Americas, EEA and APAC
* Ability to read and engage with technical documentation, such as system designs and architecture diagrams
* Excellent legal writing, including clear, concise advice

## **Preferred qualifications**

* At least 8 years of privacy and data protection legal experience
* Deep familiarity with software and model development and architecture
* Proven ability to build privacy processes, playbooks and templates that scale with a fast-growing business
* Direct engagement with privacy regulators, including providing briefings and responding to inquiries
* Privacy and data protection work on mergers, acquisitions, and similar transactions
* Background advising safety, security or trust and safety teams, including on privacy incidents and notification decisions

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
