## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

Beneficial Deployments ensures AI reaches and benefits the communities that need it most. We partner with nonprofits, foundations, and mission-driven organizations to deploy Claude in education, global health, economic mobility, and life sciences — focusing on raising the floor for those who need it most.

We're looking for a Research Engineer to make Claude better at the real-world work that matters most to Beneficial Deployments. You'll identify where our models fall short, build the environments and reward signals used to train them, run experiments, and measure whether new capabilities generalize beyond the training data. You'll also work closely with the Beneficial Deployments team, helping shape its roadmap based on where AI capabilities are heading.

## Key responsibilities

* Identify capability gaps in our models and turn them into a research agenda
* Own the end-to-end process of creating RL environments, from task sourcing and reward design through training, using both real-world and synthetic data
* Collaborate with domain experts to design data pipelines
* Develop and improve QA frameworks to catch reward hacking and ensure env quality
* Run generalization experiments to measure how data strategy changes improve model capabilities
* Partner with other RL research teams and product teams to align on capability goals

## Minimum qualifications

* Have experience with fine-tuning large language models for specific domains or real-world use cases
* Have experience with reinforcement learning, reward design, or training data curation for LLMs
* Have a track record of setting your own direction and delivering on ambiguous, high-stakes work with little oversight, such as by running your own team, research program, or company
* Can turn deep technical understanding into clear strategic recommendations that influence senior, non-technical leaders
* Think rigorously about how quickly AI capabilities are advancing and are ready to plan around the possibility that transformative AI arrives soon
* Find value in reading through datasets to understand them and spot issues
* Are excited about a role that combines hands-on applied research with strategy and influence

## Preferred qualifications

* Have experience designing evals or benchmarks for LLMs
* Have domain expertise in healthcare, global health, or philanthropy
* Have experience with multilingual models or low-resource languages
* Have worked with NGOs, ministries of health, funders, or other partners in low- and middle-income countries

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
