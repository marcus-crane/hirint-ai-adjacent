## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

As a member of Platform Developer Relations at Anthropic, you'll shape how developers discover, onboard, build, and scale applications and agents on the Claude Platform. You'll build with our APIs, teach developers how to get the most out of them, and bring what you learn back to the teams building our products.

This role sits within our platform engineering organization. You'll work across content, community, and product influence, and partner closely with product managers, engineers, communications, and marketing. Your audience ranges from independent developers working on side projects to platform teams at large enterprises.

The relationships you build between developers and our internal teams will help us advance our mission of creating reliable, interpretable AI that follows human intent.

## Key responsibilities

* Create high-quality technical content, including demos, sample apps, tutorials, videos, and integration guides, that shows developers how to build effectively on the Claude Platform
* Tailor content to individual developers and enterprise builders, choosing formats and channels that work for each
* Contribute directly to the developer experience of the Claude Platform, including our docs, code samples, cookbooks, and open source skills like the Claude API skill
* Make our docs, SDKs, and samples work well for both developers and the coding agents they build with, and keep them accurate as our APIs change
* Partner with product and engineering on launches from early feedback through release day, with docs, samples, and migration guidance ready when we ship
* Translate developer feedback into clear recommendations, and get involved early enough to influence API and product decisions
* Find pain points in our APIs, SDKs, docs, and agent tooling, and work with engineering to get them fixed, including shipping directly yourself when it makes sense
* Be a voice developers trust, and engage directly and thoughtfully with their questions and criticism
* Build and grow developer communities online and in person through events, workshops, and the places developers already gather
* Partner with communications, marketing, and GTM teams on launches, campaigns, and events, and advocate for messaging that is useful and credible to developers
* Collaborate with research to translate new AI capabilities into accessible educational resources
* Prioritize your work based on data, developer feedback, and business needs, and measure its impact

## Minimum qualifications

* You have significant experience in developer relations, developer experience, or developer-facing product or engineering roles, with a track record of measurably improving how developers adopt or use a platform
* You have hands-on technical abilities: you've recently built with LLM APIs, can write and explain your own demos, and know what makes a code sample reliable and easy to adapt
* You have informed opinions about AI platforms and developer tooling, including what works well and what needs to improve
* You've created technical content that developers use, and you can explain complex ideas to audiences ranging from individual developers to enterprise technical leaders
* You've used developer feedback to influence product or engineering decisions, including ones that were difficult to advocate for
* You collaborate well across engineering, product, communications, and marketing, can disagree constructively, and take ownership of communicating a decision once it's made
* You're passionate about AI's potential and committed to developing it responsibly, with a genuine interest in AI safety

## Preferred qualifications

* Experience growing a developer community and building trust with skeptical audiences
* Experience supporting both individual developers and enterprise platform teams
* Experience using Claude or other AI tools in your own developer relations or engineering work

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
