## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## About the role

The IT Operations team keeps Anthropic running. We make sure every employee can do their best work without friction. We're hiring an IT Operations Engineer to develop our global asset management and hardware lifecycle program across our offices in San Francisco, New York, Seattle, London, Dublin and Sydney. You will bring program expertise to how we track hardware as inventory through its whole life: procurement and receiving, the inventory of record, assignment, refresh, collection and recovery, e-waste and secure disposal, and reseller relationships, iterating and scaling practices as we mature. The same person runs day-to-day operations for our corporate mobile program in addition to expertise in management of laptop and workstation lifecycle operations.

You'll partner with several teams within the IT, Security and business operations organizations: IT Hardware Ops runs procurement, fleet logistics, onboarding and offboarding, the storage room, and physical intake. The Endpoint pod owns MDM, device policy and enrollment. Finance owns the budget and vendor contracts. Hardware requests that come through the IT Support queue land with the IT Support team, and you'll help define how they are handled.

## Responsibilities

**Strategy and Tooling**

* Develop the global asset management strategy: the lifecycle model, the data we keep on every device, and how each office executes against it
* Select, implement and administer the asset management tool, define its data model and integrations, and partner with IT Engineering where the work needs platform-level access
* Contribute to and develop the processes on-site engineers in each office execute against, including the storage room process
* Support the evaluation and onboarding of a hardware lifecycle services vendor and, once in place, run the day-to-day relationship

**Inventory and Assignment**

* Establish the inventory of record across systems and keep it accurate, from receiving through disposal: every device, its state, its location, and who has it
* Run audits of who-has-what and reconcile physical counts against the system of record
* Manage demo, loaner, and travel device inventories, ensuring devices are accurately tracked through complex logistical deployments
* Maintain up-to-date inventory counts and make strategic purchasing decisions in support new hire onboarding and peripherals stock levels

**Procurement and Refresh**

* Support hardware purchasing through our reseller and vendor relationships, working from the refresh calendar
* Build out the refresh calendar and advance it across offices
* Reconcile purchase orders, deliveries and invoices with Accounting and vendors so that what was ordered, received, and billed line up

**Collection, E-waste and Disposal**

* Develop device collection and recovery workflows: offboarding returns, refresh swaps, remote-employee returns and the BPO fleet, including the shipping and tracking that goes with them
* Build the e-waste program: certified recycling and secure disposal partners in each region, data destruction records, and the chain of custody from collection to certificate
* Handle equipment under legal hold or other chain-of-custody requirements

**BPO and Contingent Fleet**

* Provision, track and recover the device fleet for our BPO partners, and be the day-to-day hardware contact for those vendors

**Corporate Mobile**

* Run day-to-day operations for the corporate mobile program and write its runbook
* Manage deployment, break-fix, and carrier relationships associated with a mobile device fleet

**Automation**

* Identify repetitive work throughout the operation and automate it with the tools at hand (scripts, systems integrations / connectors, AI tools)

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
