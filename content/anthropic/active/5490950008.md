## **About Anthropic**

Anthropic’s mission is to create reliable, interpretable, and steerable AI systems. We want AI to be safe and beneficial for our users and for society as a whole. Our team is a quickly growing group of committed researchers, engineers, policy experts, and business leaders working together to build beneficial AI systems.

## **About the role**

As Commercial Counsel, GTM Strategic Alliances at Anthropic, you'll lead legal strategy for some of our largest and most complex customer relationships. You'll serve as the legal partner on critical GTM engagements, architecting bespoke commercial structures. You'll negotiate directly with customers’ and partners’ senior executives and legal teams. Many of these customers also work with Anthropic in other ways, so you’ll look beyond the deal in front of you. You will help deliver on deals that will define how leading companies adopt and deploy frontier AI.

## **Key responsibilities**

* Run end to end legal strategy and execution for Anthropic's most strategic GTM relationships, including those with transformational deployment opportunities
* Architect novel commercial frameworks for custom deployment models, enterprise specific AI implementations, and bespoke solution structures that don’t fit standard contract templates, including AI specific terms and guardrails
* Lead complex negotiations involving senior executives, procurement teams, legal departments, security organizations, compliance functions, and technical stakeholders
* Design playbooks and deal frameworks that enable rapid customization for specific customer or partner requirements while maintaining appropriate risk controls
* Navigate highly competitive, high stakes scenarios where customers or partners have significant bargaining power
* Collaborate with product, finance, and ops colleagues
* Coordinate with other strategic commercial workstreams (e.g., compute) to ensure deals align with other initiatives and commitments
* Navigate cross border complexity for multinational customers and partners, including data localization requirements and stakeholder management across jurisdictions

## **Minimum qualifications**

* Hold a law degree (JD, LLB, or equivalent) and meet the local licensing requirements to work as in-house counsel where you'll be based
* Experience leading Fortune 500 and Global 2000 technology deals
* Demonstrated track record of closing high value enterprise technology deals involving bespoke commercials
* Executive presence and ability to negotiate effectively with senior legal and business leaders
* Expertise in navigating complex enterprise decision making processes involving technical validation, security assessments, AI governance reviews, procurement negotiations, and executive approvals
* Superior judgment in balancing competitive urgency with thoroughness on mission critical deals
* Outstanding communication skills with the ability to translate complex technical and legal concepts into compelling business value propositions
* Genuine intellectual curiosity about AI technology and commitment to supporting responsible enterprise AI deployment
* Comfort with ambiguity and an entrepreneurial mindset. You thrive in environments where you're inventing new approaches rather than applying established templates

## **Preferred qualifications**

* At least 8 years of relevant legal experience, with substantial focus on complex enterprise technology transactions
* Direct experience supporting enterprise sales or partnerships, strategic account, or pursuit teams at high growth B2B technology companies
* Experience with complex SaaS, infrastructure, or platform deals requiring custom deployments, services, or collaborative components
* Familiarity with AI/ML deployment considerations at enterprise scale, including data residency, model and safety reviews, security requirements, regulatory compliance (including emerging AI regulations such as the EU AI Act), and responsible AI frameworks
* Track record of winning competitive, must win deals against established technology vendors
* Experience working with customers who are also suppliers, distributors, investors, or competitors.
* Prior involvement in deals requiring complex commercial structures with committed spend, outcome based pricing, upside sharing arrangements, or multi year transformative partnerships

## **How we're different**

We believe that the highest-impact AI research will be big science. At Anthropic we work as a single cohesive team on just a few large-scale research efforts. And we value impact — advancing our long-term goals of steerable, trustworthy AI — rather than work on smaller and more specific puzzles. We view AI research as an empirical science, which has as much in common with physics and biology as with traditional efforts in computer science. We're an extremely collaborative group, and we host frequent research discussions to ensure that we are pursuing the highest-impact work at any given time. As such, we greatly value communication skills.

The easiest way to understand our research directions is to read our recent research. This research continues many of the directions our team worked on prior to Anthropic, including: GPT-3, Circuit-Based Interpretability, Multimodal Neurons, Scaling Laws, AI & Compute, Concrete Problems in AI Safety, and Learning from Human Preferences.
