# **About the Role**

The Director of Editorial role at Suno is a leadership position that will help establish the framework and POV of editorial at Suno. We are growing a generational company

The ideal candidate is someone who…

* Lives and breathes a diverse range of music
* Attends live shows regularly
* Has deep relationships across labels, artists, managers, and the broader music ecosystem
* Is A trusted taste-maker whose recommendations people seek out — friends, colleagues, and industry contacts alike
* Is fluent in how music moves online: platforms, subcultures, meme-to-mainstream pipelines, and how virality and quality intersect
* Is comfortable being both a strategist and a doer — equally at ease setting editorial vision and personally selecting tracks for a playlist

Listen to the song we made about it: <https://suno.com/s/UR6HQyggM89GakwE>

# **What You’ll Do**

* Set the editorial vision for how music is discovered and celebrated within Suno, including in-app playlist curation, artist spotlights, and genre, style and mood taxonomy
* Oversee the development and maintenance of Suno's curation frameworks — ensuring the platform's musical taxonomy reflects both the breadth of Suno's community and the evolving landscape of AI-generated music
* Champion artist spotlights and features as a meaningful touchpoint for community recognition and platform storytelling
* Ensure editorial decisions are informed by data, community feedback, and cultural context, balancing algorithmic signals with human taste
* Help build and lead the editorial team, including hiring, mentoring, and setting the standards for how curators evaluate and select music
* Work collaboratively with and earn the trust of the current members of the team, rather than disrupting the ecosystem on Day 1
* Partner with Product and Engineering to define how editorial signals (human curation) and algorithmic recommendation systems work together, rather than in competition
* Develop a POV on how music made with Suno should be evaluated editorially — what "quality" and "originality" mean in this context, and how that differs from (or resembles) traditional A&R judgment
* Manage editorial balance between music made with Suno and music licensed for Suno
* Drive editorial not only from the lens of discovery but also engagement/remixing
* Represent Suno's editorial voice externally — in artist and label conversations, press, and partnerships — as a credible authority on music discovery
* Define and track success metrics for editorial programs (engagement, retention, artist satisfaction) and report on program impact to leadership

# **What You’ll Need**

* 10+ years of experience in A&R, editorial, or a related field within the music industry
* A genuine point of view on music curation, discovery, and what makes an editorial program feel authentic and culturally resonant
* Strong operational instincts with experience building and improving processes across marketing or editorial workflows
* AI fluency, or at the very least curiosity and fair review of music made with AI-assistance
* Familiarity with metadata and taxonomy systems
* Excellent communicator: able to represent Suno credibly with senior artist teams and external partners, and communicate clearly and directly internally
* Comfortable with ambiguity and energized by the pace and possibility of a high-growth environment
* Passion for music and sincere belief in technology's potential to expand creative access and expression
* A roll-up-your-sleeves mentality as we grow rapidly and are building a generational company
* A passion for music, culture, and creativity, with a finger on the pulse of industry trends.
* Ability to work cross-functionally across multiple teams (marketing, brand, creative, growth, engineering) that are growing rapidly

### **Preferred**

* Experience at a streaming, publication, or AI company
* Familiarity with music taxonomy, metadata, or editorial curation systems
* Experience working within or alongside product and engineering teams
