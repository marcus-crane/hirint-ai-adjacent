## **About the Role**

As one of the first hires on Suno's accounting team you'll be a true generalist, the person who can pick up whatever the close, the auditors, or the business throws your way and run with it. Reporting to the Director of Accounting, you'll own meaningful pieces of the monthly close, help carry the company through its first-year audit and toward IPO readiness, and bring a measured, meaningful approach to AI that unlocks the efficiency expected of a best-in-class team. We're looking for a doer who wants to build: someone who'll set up a process, document it, operate it, and then automate it. If you want broad exposure and the chance to shape how a fast-growing AI company does its accounting, this is the seat.

Listen to the song we made about the role: <https://suno.com/s/sFjswHv402TX3Yeo>

## **What You'll Do**

* Own significant areas of the monthly close, reconciliations, accruals, and journal entries, and help shorten and tighten the cycle.
* Build and document repeatable processes across the accounting function, balancing startup flexibility with the rigor the business needs.
* Support the company's first-year financial statement audit and help build toward the rigor required for IPO readiness.
* Research technical accounting questions and support audit and tax requests with well-organized workpapers and analysis.
* Put AI and automation to work across the accounting function, thoughtfully and with the right guardrails, to unlock the efficiency expected of a best-in-class team.
* Strengthen a growing accounting team's foundation across process and systems so we can keep making creative fulfillment a daily reality for everyone.

## **What You'll Need**

**Must-Haves**

* 5-7 years of accounting experience, including a mix of Big 4 public accounting and high-growth industry.
* Strong US GAAP knowledge and a track record of owning core close responsibilities end to end.
* Experience supporting external financial statement audits, first-year audit experience is ideal.
* A generalist's range, comfortable moving between technical accounting, operational accounting, and systems work.
* A demonstrated, measured use of AI and automation to make accounting faster and more accurate.
* Builder's energy and the judgment to know when to apply rigor and when to stay flexible in a startup.

**Nice-to-Haves**

* CPA.
* Consumer tech or other high-volume revenue experience.
* Exposure to music royalties, equity, indirect tax, or payroll accounting.
* IPO readiness or SOX experience.
