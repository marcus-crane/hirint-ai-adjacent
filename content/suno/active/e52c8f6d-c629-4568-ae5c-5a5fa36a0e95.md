# **About Suno**

We're building the world's first creative entertainment platform, where the entire world can feel the joy and fulfillment of making music. Music is for everyone: Our users include everyone from grandmothers creating songs for their loved ones, to Grammy winners using Suno Studio, our power tool, to make the most popular hits in the world.

Building the future of entertainment requires ambition. The pace is fast, the problems are hard, and the work demands ownership and intensity. For the right people, it’s incredibly rewarding: a chance to shape a new medium, work with a small team that cares deeply about quality, make music, drink too much coffee, and build something that millions of people use to express themselves in ways that were never before possible.

Suno is the fastest growing consumer entertainment company and the leader in AI music. We are backed by leading investors including Bond Capital, Menlo Ventures, Lightspeed Venture Partners, IVP, Forerunner, Union Square Ventures, Alkeon, Quiet, Matrix Partners, Schroders Capital and, NVentures (venture arm of NVIDIA).

# **About the Role**

We’re hiring an Engineering Manager to lead Suno’s Revenue Growth engineering team: a cross-platform group of backend, web, iOS, and Android engineers who discover and deliver Suno’s highest-leverage commercial opportunities. The team owns pricing and packaging, paywalls and offers, subscriber retention, experimentation infrastructure, and revenue-driving UX improvements, partnering closely with product, data science and the commerce platform team. This is a high-autonomy role that combines product, technical, and people leadership: you’ll stay close enough to the code to review decisions and multiply technical ownership across your team.

Listen to the Suno version of this role! <https://suno.com/s/6u29t3orv1a3kSTv>

# **What You’ll Do**

* Lead and grow a team of backend, web, iOS, and Android engineers building Suno’s revenue product roadmap
* Set the revenue roadmap with product, data science, and other engineering teams, balancing fast experiments against bigger bets
* Create the engineering plan for the next phase of the team: hiring, execution milestones, and operating cadence
* Invest in the experimentation infrastructure and instrumentation the team runs on, so it can test more ideas and act on the results with confidence
* Stay technically close enough to the work to understand system constraints and provide guidance on architecture
* Test and spread AI-enabled engineering workflows, and assess their impact on the team

# **What You’ll Need**

**Must-Haves**

* Experience managing engineers (2+ years), following deep experience as a full-stack engineer
* Product and commercial judgment: comfortable shaping ambiguous roadmaps and connecting user behavior to business outcomes
* Experience running data-driven experimentation: forming hypotheses, choosing metrics, and discerning the limits of what data can teach us
* Hiring and org-building ability: identifying, sourcing, assessing, and retaining talent
* Cross-functional leadership: understanding partner goals, debating ideas constructively, communicating effectively, and building trust
* Genuine enthusiasm for Suno’s mission and the future of music

**Nice-to-Haves**

* Experience in consumer subscriptions, growth, fintech, payments, pricing & packaging
* Billing infrastructure experience with systems such as Stripe and RevenueCat
