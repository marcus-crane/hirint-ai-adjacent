# About the Role

Suno's song creation agent is becoming something other teams build on. As a Senior AI Platform Engineer, you'll build the APIs, SDKs and shared tooling, including eval infrastructure, tracing and model routing, that let engineers across Suno ship agent features faster. You'll work closely with the agent team, partner teams and Infra, and your customers are other engineers, so adoption is a measure of success. You'll help shape the platform as it grows.  
**Listen to the song we made about it:** [**https://suno.com/s/bxgQXsn06Vz3KKEn**](https://suno.com/s/bxgQXsn06Vz3KKEn)

# What You'll Do

* Design and build the platform APIs and SDKs that other teams use to build agent features.
* Develop shared eval infrastructure, including the eval runner, results storage and dashboards, and help the team decide what to build and what to buy.
* Implement end-to-end tracing and observability, so any request can be followed across services from prompt to audio.
* Manage model provider routing, fallback, rate limits and cost controls.
* Partner with engineering teams across Suno to onboard them onto the platform with docs and hands-on support, and with Infra on the reliability and scale of the agent runtime.
* Help more people turn ideas into music by making it faster and safer for teams across Suno to ship agent features.

# What You'll Need

## Must-Haves

* 4–6 years building and scaling backend or platform systems in production, in any language.
* Experience on a team that supports other developer teams, including building a platform, API or SDK that other teams adopted.
* Experience with production LLM or agent systems, for external or internal users.
* Depth in distributed systems and observability, including tracing across services.
* Comfort working with internal customers: scoping with partner teams, earning adoption and handling ambiguity.

## Nice-to-Haves

* Experience building an eval or tracing product.
* Experience running LLM gateway or model routing infrastructure.
* Experience writing SDKs that other teams use.
* Experience with Python backends.
