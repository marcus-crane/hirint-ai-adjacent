# **About the Role**

We're making our first CRO hire to own conversion at Suno, across web and the app stores. You'll sit on the Growth team at the point where our marketing spend turns into revenue: the landing pages, app store listings, funnels, and copy people hit after they click an ad, land from search, or find us in the App Store.

This is a performance job. You'll live in the numbers – conversion rate, install rate, CAC, revenue per visitor – and tie every test you run back to what it did for the business. You'll own the strategy and the copy, build pages and listings from our existing design system, and ship experiments at a pace that surprises people. You'll understand our ideal customer better than anyone in the building – who they are, what brings them to Suno, and what makes them convert — and shape every page, listing, and test around that.

You'll partner with User Acquisition, Lifecycle, Product, Engineering, and Data Science, and you'll work within our existing design system rather than in Figma yourself — grab a template, own the copy and the test, and ship. Conversion is yours to run. This is a senior IC role with real ownership and no team to manage. We want a hacker: someone scrappy and relentless who finds the fast path, ships more tests than anyone thinks is possible, and cares about learning fast and at scale more than about building the perfect system.

# **What You'll Do**

* Understand our ideal customer more deeply than anyone else here, and turn that into pages, listings, copy, and tests that actually speak to them
* Ship a high volume of landing pages across paid, SEO, lifecycle, partnerships, and new launches, assembled from our existing design system and tailored to the channel and audience each serves
* Own app store optimization: the listing copy, screenshots, and store creative that turn store visits into installs, and the handoff into onboarding
* Localize and adapt pages and store listings for our international markets, so conversion holds up across languages and regions
* Write persuasive, on-brand copy and build pages from our existing design components, then test your way to better versions fast
* Run experiments at real volume: A/B and multivariate tests shipped quickly, using AI and whatever scrappy tooling gets you more shots on goal than the team could hit by hand
* Dig into user behavior with heatmaps, session recordings, surveys, and analytics to find where people drop off and why
* Hunt down the highest-leverage opportunities across the funnel by combining performance data, user research, and business context
* Own the conversion numbers end to end across web and app: conversion rate, install rate, CAC, and revenue per visitor, paid and organic
* Partner with Revenue Product on paywall and upsell copy, keeping it clear and true to what people are getting
* Push conversion testing to a volume and speed Suno hasn't seen, and pull the rest of the company along with what you learn

# 

# **Qualifications**

* You're a hacker. You move fast and ship a lot, and you've run a high volume of tests without a big team behind you
* 5-8 years in performance marketing, growth marketing, or CRO. You've owned conversion and funnel testing at scale, on the web and in the app stores (including ASO)
* You can write. You've written landing page copy that actually converted, and you know the customer well enough to know what to say to them
* You can read an experiment and know what it's telling you. We use Statsig internally, so you're comfortable pulling results, judging significance, and deciding what to do next
* You know the landing page toolkit. You've built and tested pages in tools like Optimizely, VWO, or Unbounce, and watched behavior in Clarity or Hotjar
* You set your own KPIs and run your own analysis. You can take user data and research and turn it into a test plan that ties back to the business
* You use AI to get more done than one person normally could
* You can tell a real win from a cheap trick, and you won't ship dark patterns. You work well with product and engineering and get changes live without slowing everyone down
