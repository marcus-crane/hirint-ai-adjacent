# **About Suno**

We're building the world's first creative entertainment platform, where the entire world can feel the joy and fulfillment of making music. Music is for everyone: Our users include everyone from grandmothers creating songs for their loved ones, to Grammy winners using Suno Studio, our power tool, to make the most popular hits in the world.

Building the future of entertainment requires ambition. The pace is fast, the problems are hard, and the work demands ownership and intensity. For the right people, it’s incredibly rewarding: a chance to shape a new medium, work with a small team that cares deeply about quality, make music, drink too much coffee, and build something that millions of people use to express themselves in ways that were never before possible.

Suno is the fastest growing consumer entertainment company and the leader in AI music. We are backed by leading investors including Bond Capital, Menlo Ventures, Lightspeed Venture Partners, IVP, Forerunner, Union Square Ventures, Alkeon, Quiet, Matrix Partners, Schroders Capital and, NVentures (venture arm of NVIDIA).

**About the Role**

We're hiring a Product Manager to help build out Suno's revenue: the ladder from our premium offerings for professional creatives through team and business products. You'll define the offerings, run pilots and betas, decide what to build next, and take each rung of the ladder from first customers to repeatable revenue.

You'll report to the Head of Revenue Product. You'll partner closely with our Engineering, Design, Data Science, Legal, and Commercial teams on the foundations that business customers care about most. This is a high-ownership IC role building a new revenue line, with room to grow as the function scales. It calls for strong technical judgment, fluency in what business security and compliance demand, and the craft to turn complex requirements into product experiences that feel simple.

Listen to the song we made about it: <https://suno.com/s/ORD24mcjDdTjUs6W>

**What You’ll Do**

* Own the product roadmap for our business products for professional creatives, teams, and large companies
* Build the collaboration, workspace, and admin capabilities that make Suno work for organizations, plus the security and privacy foundations that unlock adoption by larger customers
* Design pilots and betas, engage directly with customers and translate what you learn into sharp build / kill / sequence decisions
* Drive business readiness in partnership with Engineering, Security, and Legal: compliance requirements (e.g. SOC 2, GDPR) and the secure, scalable foundations business customers require
* Develop the integrations and connection points that enables Suno to plug into business workflows and systems
* Partner with our commercial and business development teams on the sales motion: land-and-expand strategy, partner feedback loops, and the product gaps that block deals
* Define and drive revenue KPIs for new business lines: new business revenue, partner activation and usage growth, and expansion within accounts
* Communicate results, tradeoffs, and recommendations clearly to the team and to leadership

**Qualifications**

*Must-Have*

* You have 5+ years of product management experience, including taking a new B2B, prosumer, or platform product from zero to revenue. You've owned the full arc: definition, validation, first customers, iteration
* You understand teams and business monetization mechanics: usage-based pricing, seat and tier packaging, and how self-serve and sales-assisted motions fit together
* You've built for organizations, not just individuals: you understand what teams and businesses require (permissions, admin, security, compliance) and have shipped product that met that bar, or can show you know what it takes
* You thrive in ambiguity: you have hands-on experience driving consensus and action in spaces where the strategy is still forming, and you know when to decide versus when to learn more
* You are data-driven, leaning on both customer conversations and quantitative signal to prioritize, and honest about what early data can and can't tell you
* You're a team player with a growth mindset, collaborating effectively across product, engineering, and sales, and influencing without authority. You've partnered closely with sales teams and engaged directly with business customers
* You balance revenue with customer trust. Business customers are betting their products, brands, and unreleased work on us; you know what that requires

*Nice-to-Have*

* You've shipped collaboration, workspace, or admin/governance features for teams or businesses
* You've shipped a developer or platform product, with strong intuition for designing clear, flexible platform primitives, and you understand developer experience, documentation, and partner integration lifecycles
* Familiarity with the music industry, creative tools, or content rights and licensing
* Background in computer science, engineering, or a quantitative field
* You genuinely care about music and about Suno's product. You'll be defining what Suno is worth to the businesses that build on it, and that requires loving what we make
