# **About Suno**

We're building the world's first creative entertainment platform, where the entire world can feel the joy and fulfillment of making music. Music is for everyone: Our users include everyone from grandmothers creating songs for their loved ones, to Grammy winners using Suno Studio, our power tool, to make the most popular hits in the world.

Building the future of entertainment requires ambition. The pace is fast, the problems are hard, and the work demands ownership and intensity. For the right people, it’s incredibly rewarding: a chance to shape a new medium, work with a small team that cares deeply about quality, make music, drink too much coffee, and build something that millions of people use to express themselves in ways that were never before possible.

Suno is the fastest growing consumer entertainment company and the leader in AI music. We are backed by leading investors including Bond Capital, Menlo Ventures, Lightspeed Venture Partners, IVP, Forerunner, Union Square Ventures, Alkeon, Quiet, Matrix Partners, Schroders Capital and, NVentures (venture arm of NVIDIA).

Founded in 2023 by Harvard alumni with a shared passion for music, Suno has quickly grown to **250 employees** while empowering more than 100 million people to create original songs. In 2026, we raised a **$400 million Series D** at a $5.4 billion valuation to continue building innovative products that amplify creativity and imagination.

## **About the Role**

At the core of Suno’s user experience is inspiring people to create their next favorite song. Personalization makes this possible, by helping people discover music they love and ideas that spark their own creativity. We are seeking a Product Manager to lead recommendations across Suno. You will define the strategy and roadmap and partner closely with Machine Learning Engineers and Data Scientists to build our recommendations system from the ground up. This is a rare opportunity to shape personalization for music creation: defining how a product learns someone’s taste and uses that understanding to expand their creative possibilities. This role is ideal for a technical Product Manager with deep experience in and a passion for recommendations. You are hands-on with data, comfortable working through technical details, and excited to build experiences that make Suno more valuable with every visit.

Listen to the Suno version of this role! <https://suno.com/song/d7e92b5b-4936-4c7f-ac0a-6d814672526c>

## **What You'll Do**

* **Lead personalization across Suno** — Own the recommendations strategy and roadmap across all product surfaces and problems. Partner with UX product teams to make their features more personalized.
* **Shape recommendations strategy for creation** — Partner with Machine Learning Engineers and Data Scientists to design approaches and experiments that inspire users to create. Lead product thinking in that partnership, grounding technical decisions in user needs and behavior.
* **Go deep with data** — Build a detailed understanding of our users and product surfaces. Own experiments that move success metrics.

## **Who You Are**

* 6+ years of product management experience, with a track record of improving consumer experiences through personalization
* An effective thought partner to technical teams, fluent in recommendation systems and their evaluation
* Hands-on with data analysis, with experience defining success metrics and designing rigorous experiments
* Sound product judgment that connects user needs, algorithm development, and business impact
* Excited to build from scratch and bring clarity to ambiguous problems
* A clear communicator who aligns teams around a shared vision
* Passionate about personalization and curious about how people’s tastes shape what they listen to and create
