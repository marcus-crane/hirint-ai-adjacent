# **About Suno**

We're building the world's first creative entertainment platform, where the entire world can feel the joy and fulfillment of making music. Music is for everyone: Our users include everyone from grandmothers creating songs for their loved ones, to Grammy winners using Suno Studio, our power tool, to make the most popular hits in the world.

Building the future of entertainment requires ambition. The pace is fast, the problems are hard, and the work demands ownership and intensity. For the right people, it’s incredibly rewarding: a chance to shape a new medium, work with a small team that cares deeply about quality, make music, drink too much coffee, and build something that millions of people use to express themselves in ways that were never before possible.

Suno is the fastest growing consumer entertainment company and the leader in AI music. We are backed by leading investors including Bond Capital, Menlo Ventures, Lightspeed Venture Partners, IVP, Forerunner, Union Square Ventures, Alkeon, Quiet, Matrix Partners, Schroders Capital and, NVentures (venture arm of NVIDIA).

Founded in 2023 by Harvard alumni with a shared passion for music, Suno has quickly grown to **250 employees** while empowering more than 100 million people to create original songs. In 2026, we raised a **$400 million Series D** at a $5.4 billion valuation to continue building innovative products that amplify creativity and imagination.

# **About the Role**

Suno has made it possible for anyone to turn an idea into music. Now we’re exploring how anyone can bring their music to life through video.

As the Product Manager for Video, you will take a new product from its earliest stages through launch and growth. You will shape intuitive creation experiences across web and mobile, work with the broader product team to make videos a compelling part of Suno, and help define a business model that can support creators as the product scales.

We’re looking for an experienced PM with strong product and design judgment, firsthand experience creating videos, and excitement about what generative video makes possible. You should care as much about the people making videos as you do about the technology behind them—and be eager to build tools that help more people express what they hear and imagine.

Listen to the song we made about it: <https://suno.com/s/Y0WfEP7qALvaZanH>

# **What You’ll Do**

* **Define the video creation experience** — Understand how people want to bring their music to life, then turn those insights into a clear product vision and roadmap.
* **Build from zero to one** — Lead the product from early prototypes through launch, making thoughtful choices about creative controls, generative video capabilities, and the path from a song to a finished video.
* **Make video part of Suno** — Partner with other product teams to design how creators share their videos and how audiences discover and engage with them across web and mobile.
* **Shape a sustainable business** — Explore how video creates value for creators and Suno, and help develop a business model that can grow with the product.
* **Launch and grow the product** — Define what success looks like, learn from what creators make and where they get stuck, and improve the experience as adoption grows.

# **Who You Are**

* A product manager who loves making creative tools more powerful and accessible
* 6+ years of product management experience, including launching and growing a consumer product or creative tool
* Firsthand experience creating videos and a strong understanding of video creation tools and workflows
* Excited by generative video and thoughtful about where AI can help creators without taking away their creative control
* Able to turn an ambiguous opportunity into a focused product strategy, make tradeoffs, and bring a team along
* Adept at using user feedback and data insights to understand what’s working and decide what to build next
* Strong analytical thinking and the ability to connect a valuable creator experience to a sustainable business.
* A clear, proactive partner to Engineering, Design, and other teams in a fast-moving environment
