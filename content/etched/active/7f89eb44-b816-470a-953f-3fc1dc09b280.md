**Job Summary**  
We're hiring a **General Ledger Accountant, Close** to own critical elements of our month-end, quarter-end, and year-end close and support scalable close process. This is a hands-on role focused on the accuracy and integrity of the general ledger, ensuring financial results are complete, timely, and properly supported.

You'll be close to the details - owning core close deliverables - while also helping design systems and workflows that scale as the company grows. This role is well-suited for someone who enjoys the rigor of technical close work, understands how transactional activity flows through to the financial statements, and wants real ownership over a critical financial function.

As a GL Accountant, you'll manage day-to-day ledger activities while helping build the controls and processes that scale with us. You'll work cross-functionally with AP, AR, Inventory, and Payroll teams to support accurate, reliable financial reporting.

**Key Responsibilities**

**Close Ownership:** Own and lead core components of the monthly, quarterly, and annual close process, ensuring accuracy, completeness, and timeliness of financial results and strong internal controls over the general ledger:

* Maintain and manage the close calendar, coordinating tasks and deadlines across accounting and cross-functional teams
* Prepare, post and/or review journal entries, including accruals, prepaids, amortization schedules, fixed assets/depreciation, leases, and intercompany transactions
* Perform balance sheet account reconciliations for assigned accounts and resolve reconciling items timely
* Prepare flux analysis for balance sheet and P&L accounts, investigating and explaining period-over-period variances
* Ensure subledgers (AP, AR, payroll, fixed assets) tie out to the general ledger each period
* Review trial balance for unusual or unsupported entries prior to close finalization

**Internal Control:** Support close-related internal control processes by ensuring proper documentation, approvals, transaction accuracy, and record retention in accordance with company policies:

* Maintain organized close documentation (workpapers, reconciliations, journal entry support) to support internal reviews and audits
* Ensure journal entries have proper approval, supporting documentation, and adherence to segregation-of-duties policies
* Maintain up-to-date process documentation and desktop procedures for close workflows
* Support periodic internal control reviews, walkthroughs, and testing
* Support external audit and internal audit requests, including preparation of PBC (prepared-by-client) items

**Systems & Scaling:** Support the continued scaling of close operations by maintaining system accuracy, improving process efficiency, and ensuring GL infrastructure can support company growth across entities and jurisdictions:

* Maintain accurate configuration of chart of accounts, entities, segments, and classes within the ERP
* Support integration between the ERP, subledgers, and other connected financial systems
* Support system setup for new entities, currencies, and jurisdictions as the company expands
* Standardize close processes and checklists to reduce manual intervention and close timeline
* Identify and implement automation opportunities within the close cycle

**Cross-Functional Reporting:** Support accurate financial statements and management reporting in accordance with US GAAP, including technical accounting for non-routine or complex transactions, ensuring accurate data flows into the GL through close partnership with AP, AR, Inventory, and Payroll:

* Own preparing monthly and quarterly financial statement packages
* Support operationalizing technical accounting memos on non-routine or complex transactions
* Own multi-entity consolidations and intercompany eliminations
* Support operationalizing revenue recognition, lease accounting, and other technical accounting areas as applicable
* Partner with AP, AR, Inventory, and Payroll teams to ensure accurate, reconciled handoffs into the GL and resolve cross-departmental discrepancies
* Support ad hoc data requests, schedules, and analysis for Finance leadership and external reporting
