**Job Summary**

We are seeking a highly motivated **Manufacturing Test Engineer** to join our team. In this role, you will bridge the gap between hardware, software, and production, developing test code and test procedures, and ensuring seamless test deployment and production line readiness for our advanced AI systems. You will collaborate closely with cross-functional teams, including ASIC design and software engineering, to build and optimize test processes to drive high quality, with best possible throughput.

This role will leverage the Silicon and Platform Validation teams with the goal of producing common source code between Silicon Test and Production Test. This role will perform as a bridge between the Development Lab and Manufacturing. Tasks will include working with existing test suites and modifying code to function on an actual system in the manufacturing environment. Developments from Scratch will include:

* Architecture of the Production Test-Flow and Automation
* Production User-Interface
* Automated Data-Logging and building a long-term Database and Data Processing Tools

This position will include assisting in the initial bring up and debug of first article modules and servers.

**Representative projects**

* **System-Level Testing Development:** Work closely with the ASIC design and software teams to design and implement test stations for system-level testing and full server burn-in testing at the production line.
* **Diagnostic Test Creation:** Collaborate with the software team to develop diagnostic tests necessary for comprehensive product functional testing.
* **Test Plan Development:** Create detailed test plans to ensure production line flow is efficient, thorough, and aligned with product requirements.
* **ODM Collaboration:** Partner with production engineers at Original Design Manufacturers (ODMs) to help architect and design end-to-end production line flow, including flow, test coverage, and throughput models to meet capacity planning needs.
* **Optimization for Ramp Conditions:** Optimize test time, yield, and coverage to achieve ideal ramp conditions, enabling efficient scaling to meet production demands.
* **Continuous Improvement:** Analyze test data to identify bottlenecks and inefficiencies and implement improvements to boost production line performance.
* **Capacity Planning:** Develop and refine throughput models to ensure production meets current and future demand with optimal resource utilization.

**Representative projects**

* Develop test programs for AI Modules and Servers with focus on standard libraries and new methodology.
* Develop, implement, and manage module-level, system-level, and solution-level hardware test to ensure our products exceed customer expectations for performance and quality
* Design the test and environments for each level of test from board to integrated solution, including equipment selection, test code development, debug and results analysis
* Work with the silicon and platform teams to optimize testing for cost and time, while ensuring that adequate guard bands are developed and implemented for the range of customer environments
* Assist in developing quality and reliability standards for all suppliers working with Etched. Ensure that all components and systems meet or exceed the required benchmarks.
