### Job Summary

Etched is building AI systems that accelerate its own development. As a Research Engineer on the RL Frontiers team, you will train our proprietary frontier models to automate kernel development, ASIC engineering, production pipelines, and 3-D hardware operating systems.

You will use our proprietary simulators and environments to create custom data mixes and post-train models to SOTA performance on our hardest engineering tasks.

### Key Responsibilities

* Own the post-training stack completely end to end.
* Build RL environments with ASIC, kernel, and supercomputing engineers, rewarded by simulation, formal tools, tests, and hardware measurements.
* Create custom data mixes that unlock frontier performance on relevant tasks.
* Create benchmarks that drive real productivity in the models and avoid reward hacking

### You may be a good fit if you

* Have hands-on post-training experience, including SFT and RL (e.g., PPO, GRPO), and have shipped models people used.
* Have taken a model from bad to good on a task it wasn’t built for, ideally with scarce data, for example as an early post-training hire at an applied AI company.
* Are a strong PyTorch and distributed-training engineer who reads the rollouts and checks the numbers before trusting a result.
* Understand the systems side of RL well enough to make the loop faster and cheaper, not just better.
* Are entrepreneurial and make good calls under compute and data constraints. Former founders are a plus.

### Strong candidates may also have experience with

* RL or post-training frameworks (e.g., verl, OpenRLHF, TRL, prime-rl) or inference engines (vLLM, SGLang).
* Agentic, multi-turn RL with verifiable rewards.
* Distillation and training small, specialized models for production.
* ASIC design or verification, accelerator kernels, or large-scale compute infrastructure.

### How we’re different

Etched believes in the Bitter Lesson. We are the first inference-focused frontier AI system, betting early on transformer and transformer-like architectures and on increasing model sizes. Our addressable market is the entirety of inference, unlike many of our competitors.

We are a fully in-person team in San Jose (Santana Row), and greatly value engineering skills. We do not have boundaries between engineering and research, and we expect all of our technical staff to contribute to both and work across disciplines as needed.

### About Etched

Etched is building hardware for frontier intelligence. We co-design chips, racks, software, and manufacturing to deliver best-in-class throughput and latency across both prefill and decode workloads. Our first products are heavily focused on inference. Backed by hundreds of millions from top-tier investors and staffed by leading engineers, Etched is redefining the infrastructure layer for the fastest growing industry in history.
