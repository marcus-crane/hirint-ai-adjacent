## **About Etched**

Etched is building hardware for frontier intelligence. We co-design chips, racks, software, and manufacturing to deliver best-in-class throughput and latency across both prefill and decode workloads. Our first products are heavily focused on **inference**. Backed by hundreds of millions from top-tier investors and staffed by leading engineers, Etched is redefining the infrastructure layer for the fastest growing industry in history.

## **Job Summary**

Etched is looking for a Production Intern to support contract manufacturer (CM) coordination, engineering builds, procurement, and day-to-day production operations.

This is a hands-on support role with exposure to how hardware moves from engineering development into production. You’ll own defined tasks with guidance from the team and build experience across manufacturing operations, supply chain, and program coordination.

## **Key Responsibilities**

* CM coordination: Prepare for CM meetings, capture decisions and action items, follow up with owners, and track schedules, deliverables, and open issues.
* Engineering builds: Maintain build plans and readiness checklists. Track materials, documentation, dependencies, and open actions before and during builds, and flag gaps that could affect execution.
* Procurement: Organize purchase requests, collect supplier quotes, and track POs, lead times, and delivery dates. Follow up with suppliers and internal teams on missing information, delays, or discrepancies.
* Materials and logistics: Track part availability, shortages, shipments, and receipts so the team has a clear view of what is available and what is needed for upcoming builds.
* Documentation and reporting: Keep trackers, meeting notes, build records, and status updates current. Summarize progress, open questions, and blockers so owners can make decisions and take action.
* Process improvements: Improve templates, checklists, and recurring workflows to make coordination easier and reduce manual work.

## **You may be a good fit if you have**

* Strong organization, attention to detail, and follow-through across multiple tasks.
* Clear written and verbal communication, including the ability to ask questions and flag problems early.
* Comfort using spreadsheets to organize information, track status, and identify missing or inconsistent data.
* Curiosity about hardware, manufacturing, procurement, and how engineering teams bring products into production.
* Willingness to learn unfamiliar processes and adapt as priorities change.
* Helpful experience
* Exposure to manufacturing, supply chain, procurement, hardware projects, or project coordination through coursework, student teams, internships, or other hands-on work. Familiarity with bills of materials, purchase orders, inventory tracking, or manufacturing partners is a plus.
* Responsibilities can be matched to the intern’s experience, with guidance and review from the team.
