**Job Summary**

We are seeking an experienced ECAD Librarian to manage and maintain our schematic symbol and PCB footprint libraries, ensuring our design teams work from accurate, consistent, and manufacturable component data.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Develop, maintain, and organize the ECAD library of Allegro schematic symbols and PCB footprints, ensuring alignment with design team requirements and project specifications
* Create and validate complex schematic symbols and PCB footprints in Allegro, ensuring compliance with IPC standards and company guidelines
* Build and maintain complete component metadata, including part numbering, attributes, and datasheet linkage
* Collaborate with the manufacturing team to adjust symbols and footprints as needed, ensuring compliance with design for manufacturability (DFM) requirements and alignment with JEDEC standards and industry guidelines
* Partner with electrical engineers and layout designers to resolve library issues quickly and prevent errors from propagating into fabrication and assembly
* Establish and enforce best practices for library version control, part approvals, and data integrity
* Collaborate with component suppliers to obtain accurate technical data and ensure compatibility with design requirements
* Perform detailed quality checks on symbols and footprints to guarantee design accuracy and reliability

**You may be a good fit if you have (Must-have qualifications)**

* Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering or a related field, or equivalent professional experience
* 5+ years of experience in ECAD library management, including creating Allegro schematic symbols and PCB footprints
* Expertise in Allegro PCB Editor and related tools for symbol and footprint creation
* Good understanding of Part Life Cycle, EOL parts, and RoHS/EU compliance
* Strong understanding of IPC standards for PCB footprint creation, JEDEC package standards, and package dimensional tolerances
* Experience working directly with manufacturing teams to adapt designs for optimal manufacturability and yield
* Experience with version control systems for ECAD libraries, such as Git or PLM software
* Excellent attention to detail and ability to manage complex libraries with a focus on accuracy and efficiency
* Strong communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work cross-functionally with electrical and manufacturing teams
* Experience with scripting languages (e.g., SKILL for Allegro) to automate library management tasks
* Knowledge of BOM management and integration with ECAD libraries
