**Job Summary**

The Electricals Materials Handler owns the lifecycle of every electrical component that goes onto an Etched module: from receiving, through storage, inspection and kitting, to consumption on our in-house SMT line and at our contract manufacturers (CMs). You'll sit on the Inventory team and be the source of truth for board electricals: what we have, where it is, what condition it is in, and what is about to run out.

This hire will run clean consignment with CMs.Success looks like: Etched-owned material at CM sites is counted, reconciled and traceable, so builds don't stall on a missing reel and variances get closed.Board materials are accounted fully: moisture-sensitive parts are handled correctly, kits match the released BOM with no shortages or substitutions, and scrap and write-offs trend toward zero. The data you produce lets Procurement buy against real on-hand, consumed and in-transit numbers instead of estimates.

**Key Responsibilities**

* **Storage and handling:** Receive, label, measure quantity, inspect and put away board electricals, connectors and boards, with full traceability. Run ESD-safe handling and moisture-sensitivity (MSD) control: dry cabinets, bake-out, exposure-time tracking.
* **Consignment with CMs:** Own the record of Etched-owned material held at contract manufacturers: shipments out, consumption, returns and scrap. Run cycle counts and periodic reconciliations against CM reports; investigate and close every variance.
* **Incoming quality control:** Inspect incoming lots: part marking, quantity, packaging, reel and tray condition, date codes. Quarantine and disposition non-conforming material; raise supplier issues through Procurement. Keep first-article and inspection records for every new part number and supplier.
* **Kitting:** Pull and kit components to the released BOM and revision for prototype builds, bring-up boards, in-house SMT runs and CM production. Flag shortages, substitutions and revision mismatches before the kit ships, not after the line stops. Pack and document kits for line-side staging and CM handoff, including MSD status and special-handling notes.
* **Reporting to Procurement:** Publish a weekly view of on-hand, allocated, in-transit and at-CM quantities by part number. Flag parts approaching reorder point, excess or obsolete stock, and lots nearing MSD or shelf-life limits. Feed actual consumption and scrap data back so forecasts and PO quantities match reality.
* **Processes:** Write the SOPs for receiving, consignment transfers, RMA and return-to-stock so the process does not depend on who is on shift. Contribute to the ESD, 5S and safety programs shared with the SMT line.
* **Automation:** Help our agentic manufacturing team pull inventory and consumption data into automated reporting and reorder signals.

* Able to read a BOM, a PCB assembly drawing and a component datasheet, and spot when a delivered part does not match them.
* Hands-on experience with an ERP or inventory system (NetSuite, SAP, Oracle, or similar) for receiving, transfers and cycle counts.
* Comfortable in spreadsheets: lookups, pivots
* Experience working with contract manufacturers on consigned or customer-furnished material.
* Organized under pressure: prototype builds change daily and the kit still has to be right.

* Passionate about automating parts of the job with agentic AI models, and willing to learn about this on the job.
* Ownership mentality: accountable to the outcome, and do whatever it takes to get to the goal.
