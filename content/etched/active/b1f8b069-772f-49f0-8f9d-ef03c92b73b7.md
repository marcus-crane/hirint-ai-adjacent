**Job Summary**

As an Inventory Manager at Etched, you will own the end-to-end strategy, control, and execution of our contract manufacturer (CM) and 3PL inventory operations. In this role, you will lead excess and obsolescence (E&O) mitigation, champion inventory audit and reconciliation processes across global sites, and build robust dashboards to deliver high-precision visibility for leadership and Finance. Operating at the intersection of supply chain operations and financial stewardship, you will play a critical role in optimizing capital efficiency and ensuring operational rigour as Etched scales frontier AI hardware.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Inventory Analysis and E&O Mitigation

  + Review slow-moving and aged inventory on a regular basis to identify E&O risks, and drive timely disposition decisions in partnership with CMs and internal stakeholders.
  + Analyze CM demand waterfall reports and lead deep-dive root cause analysis of E&O drivers, such as forecast changes, MOQ/lead time constraints, EOL, and engineering changes.
  + Partner with CMs, suppliers, internal teams, and spot market channels to mitigate E&O exposure through supply reallocation across CMs, reuse on new programs, rework, vendor returns or cancellations, open-market resale, or scrap as a last resort.
* Inventory Audit and Control

  + Coordinate with CMs and 3PL warehouses to fulfill consigned inventory requirements, and maintain complete, auditable transaction records for all inventory movements to ensure full traceability and support reconciliation.
  + Conduct on-site audits and physical counts of consigned and excess inventory held at CM sites and 3PL warehouses to ensure inventory accuracy and proper control.
  + Lead periodic three-way reconciliation among NetSuite, 3PL warehouse records, and CM inventory reports, and investigate and resolve any discrepancies in a timely manner.
* Reporting and Financial Partnership

  + Build and maintain inventory dashboards and KPIs, including inventory turns, aging, E&O value, and inventory accuracy.
  + Deliver regular inventory health reports to management with actionable recommendations to reduce E&O exposure.
  + Partner with Finance on inventory valuation, E&O reserves, write-offs, and quarterly/annual audits.
* CM Build Validation and Cost Accounting Support

  + Review and validate CM as-built reports for accuracy, including component consumption, build quantities, and lot/serial traceability, against BOMs and work orders.
  + Reconcile CM build and consumption data to NetSuite (work orders, assembly builds, component issues) and resolve variances before month-end close.
  + Validate CM invoices and charges (components, MVA/labor, and E&O claims) against agreed pricing and actual builds.
  + Serve as the primary liaison between Cost Accounting and the CMs regarding BOM and cost discrepancies, scrap/yield losses.

**You may be a good fit if you have (Must-have qualifications)**

* 5+ years of experience in supply chain, inventory management, or material program management within electronics manufacturing or big tech.
* Proven track record of managing contract manufacturers (CMs) and 3PL warehouses, including conducting physical audits, 3-way reconciliations, and consigned inventory controls.
* Strong analytical capability to perform E&O root-cause analysis, drive inventory disposition strategies, and build inventory health dashboards/KPIs.

**Strong candidates may also have experience with (Nice-to-have qualifications)**

* Experience working with ERP systems such as NetSuite, SAP, or Kinaxis for inventory transaction tracking and financial reconciliation.
* Background partnering with Finance and Cost Accounting on inventory valuation, E&O reserves, and audit readiness.
* Direct experience with server rack hardware, complex electronics, or fast-paced NPI-to-production hardware environments.
