## **About Etched**

Etched is building hardware for frontier intelligence. We co-design chips, racks, software, and manufacturing to deliver best-in-class throughput and latency across both prefill and decode workloads. Our first products are heavily focused on **inference**. Backed by hundreds of millions from top-tier investors and staffed by leading engineers, Etched is redefining the infrastructure layer for the fastest growing industry in history.

## **Job Summary**

Etched builds custom chips and complete racks for AI inference. As deployments grow across racks and data centers, the network has to deliver the bandwidth, latency and reliability those systems depend on. Our Supercomputing team builds the software and infrastructure that make that possible.

We’re hiring networking engineers to help design, bring up and operate the networks connecting our inference systems. The work spans RDMA fabrics, network provisioning, configuration management and telemetry, with ownership from initial deployment through production. When performance drops or a link fails, this team builds the tools to understand what happened and get the system back to full capacity.

This is a hands-on role for someone who has worked with real data center networks and writes software to make them easier to deploy, operate and debug. You’ll work directly with the engineers building our hardware, firmware and system software, helping establish how Etched networks scale as we bring more systems online.

## **Key Responsibilities**

* Design and bring up data center networks connecting inference nodes within and across racks, including RDMA fabrics.
* Build software to automate network provisioning, configuration, validation and recovery across deployments.
* Develop safe processes for network changes, with testing, staged rollouts and rollback.
* Build telemetry and diagnostics that expose network health, congestion and failures, and help engineers trace issues across hosts, NICs and switches.
* Investigate bandwidth, latency and reliability issues under inference workloads, working across network configuration, Linux, drivers and firmware.
* Develop automated qualification, burn-in and failure tests for NICs, switches and network configurations before deployment.
* Partner with Node Systems, Fleet Software and deployment teams to integrate networking into system bring-up and ongoing fleet operations.
* Work with hardware vendors to resolve issues and qualify new equipment and firmware.

## **You May Be a Good Fit If You Have**

* Experience building and operating production data center networks, with direct responsibility for deployment, troubleshooting and reliability.
* Hands-on experience with RDMA networking, including RoCE, and an understanding of how fabric configuration and congestion affect performance.
* Strong networking fundamentals, including Ethernet, IP routing, switching and network security.
* Experience writing production software or automation for network provisioning, configuration management, monitoring or remediation.
* Proficiency in a language such as Python, Go, C++ or Rust.
* Strong Linux fundamentals and the ability to debug across hosts, NICs and switches using logs, counters, packet captures and other diagnostics.
* Experience owning technical work from design through deployment and supporting it when things fail in production.
* Comfort working across software, hardware and operations teams.

## **Strong Candidates May Also Have**

* Experience with WAN or backbone networks, including routing, traffic engineering and connectivity between data centers.
* Experience operating CDN, cloud or other large-scale network infrastructure.
* Experience with GPU, TPU or other accelerator clusters.
* Familiarity with switch operating systems and network management interfaces such as SONiC, gNMI or OpenConfig.
* Experience tuning congestion control and diagnosing packet loss, queueing and uneven traffic distribution in RDMA fabrics.
* Experience building network telemetry pipelines and using them to detect failures or performance regressions.
* Experience with network failure testing, failover and recovery at scale.

## **Representative Projects**

* Automate the provisioning and validation of a new rack’s network so it can enter service with a tested configuration.
* Diagnose an RDMA performance regression by correlating host, NIC and switch telemetry under load.
* Build a network rollout workflow that detects unhealthy changes and safely restores the previous configuration.
* Develop tests that show how an inference cluster behaves when links, switches or nodes fail.
