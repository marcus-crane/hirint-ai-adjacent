### **About the Role**

As a Partner Solutions Architect working with the Model Partnerships team at Together AI, you will work with leading open-source model labs to bring their models to market on Together. You’ll support partners through new model releases, from technical bring-up to launch, and drive direct sales to partners and co-sell motions that turn those models into customer adoption and revenue. You’ll be the technical expert on each partner’s models: how they’re brought up and optimized, and how they’re applied in practice. You’ll teach our field teams how to position and sell them, and serve as an overlay resource for customers adopting them. This is an exciting opportunity for a deeply technical professional who is passionate about open-source AI and partner success to make a significant impact in a fast-paced, innovative environment.

### **Responsibilities**

* Be the technical lead for partner launches (for co-sell and first party inference API sales to partners). Partner with the Partnerships team to deliver day-zero and day-one model availability. Own technical bring-up with the lab and our Engineering and Research teams, including validating architectures, inference configurations, performance, quality, and launch readiness.
* Be the technical point of contact for labs including during POC phases through production. Build trusted relationships with partner research and engineering teams. Work through pre-release access, technical requirements, and launch issues as they come up.
* Become an expert on partner models. Build deep, hands-on knowledge of each model, including its strengths, limitations, ideal use cases, and best practices. Assist our GTM in how to position and leverage with end customers.
* Serve as an overlay for partner models. Support customer engagements and escalations involving partner models where deeper model expertise is needed, from demos and POCs to production troubleshooting.
* Feed back to Product and Partnerships. Share technical insights from partners and customers with Product, Engineering, Research, and the Partnerships team.
* Create launch and adoption content. Build technical content for internal and external use, such as launch blogs, cookbooks, reference implementations, and demos, working with Partner Marketing.

### **Qualifications**

* 5+ years of experience in a customer-facing technical role with at least 2 years in a pre-sales function. Experience working with partners directly is a nice-to-have.
* Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to explain complex technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.
* Ability to consult with new and existing customers to map business needs to technical solutions
* Strong technical background, with knowledge of AI and GPU technologies and their integration into high-performance computing (HPC) environments.
* Strong understanding of inference systems in the context of open source LLMs including common serving engines (vLLM, SGLang, Tensor-RT) , testing frameworks (benchmarking), optimization techniques, and production system design.
* Proficiency in Python and skilled at using agents for diagnosing, testing, and building
* Familiarity with common infrastructure orchestration systems (K8s, SLURM) and  Monitoring solutions (Grafana)
* Strong sense of ownership and willingness to learn new skills to ensure both team and customer success.
* Ability to operate in dynamic environments, adept at managing multiple projects, and comfortable with frequent context switching and prioritization.
* Nice-to-have: Proficiency in Mandarin Chinese (spoken and written)

### **About Together AI**

Together AI, the AI Native Cloud, is purpose-built for AI engineers. AI application developers get high-performance inference that scales reliably, fine-tuning and reinforcement learning for creating frontier-level specialized models, and pre-training at massive scale for fully custom intelligence, all around a marketplace of leading open models that teams can run, adapt, and own. Trusted by Cursor, Decagon, ElevenLabs, Salesforce, and Zoom, Together serves 400+ trillion tokens a month.
