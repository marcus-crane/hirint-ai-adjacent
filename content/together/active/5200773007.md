### **About the Role**

As an IT Engineer, you will be working across identity, devices, SaaS platforms, and supporting end users. You should be organized, collaborative, and eager to learn while working within established systems and processes.

### **Responsibilities**

* Provide reliable, hands-on IT support for our in-office and remote employees by efficiently resolving Help Desk tickets while delivering excellent customer service.
* Support Together’s MDM fleet comprising macOS and some Linux devices.
* Provide in-office support by troubleshooting basic network issues and our A/V systems.
* Help manage and optimize Okta and our core SaaS tooling by administering and improving integrations and role-based access controls.
* Support daily procurement and asset management operations by coordinating hardware purchasing and tracking while maintaining accurate asset inventory and device assignments throughout their lifecycle (purchase to decommission).
* Help maintain clear, organized, and accessible IT documentation by creating easy-to-understand technical documentation for both technical and non-technical audiences.
* Contribute to IT initiatives by supporting project execution, managing priorities, and ensuring clear communication with stakeholders through regular updates.
* Help maintain a secure environment by supporting basic security hygiene to include access reviews and basic identity security tasks.
* Support onboarding and offboarding of Together employees, interns, and contractors.
* Support the IT team by creating reliable automations that reduce manual work and improve operational efficiency.
* Collaborates well with a team, but also able to work autonomously when needed: working in tandem with other locations and having consistency in practices, documentation, asset mgmt.,etc.

### **Requirements**

* Practical experience administering Okta, including users, groups, and basic app integrations (Terraform a plus).
* Strong knowledge of Google Workspace.
* Hands-on MDM experience with macOS, ideally with FleetDM.
* Ability to support macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android devices.
* Experience participating in procurement and asset management processes.
* Clear, concise technical writing skills for documentation and knowledge base content.
* Excellent communication and customer service abilities.
* Basic understanding of security principles and best practices.
* Experience with low‑code automation tools (Serval, Okta Workflows, Gumloop).
* Scripting familiarity with common languages (Bash, Python).
* Understanding of API integrations and webhooks and how to use them effectively.
* Experience with AWS provisioning and Identity Center

### **Preferred Qualifications**

* Exposure to security and compliance frameworks (SOC 2, ISO 27001, NIST, CIS, FedRAMP).
* Experience with AI tools, agents, MCPs, automations, and prompting.
* Familiarity with Notion and Linear for documentation and project management.
* HRIS integration experience with systems like HiBob and Workday.
