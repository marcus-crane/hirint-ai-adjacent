### **About The Role**

Together AI is scaling its go-to-market team fast, and we need a Head of Sales Enablement to make every seller, solutions architect, and customer-facing engineer audible-ready, from their first week through every launch.

Our products are deeply technical: inference, post-training, GPU infrastructure, and the research behind them. Many of the people we hire will come from adjacent domains, not just AI infrastructure. Your job is to close that gap quickly and keep the field sharp as the platform evolves.

This is not a role for someone who manages a program from a distance. You'll get in the trenches with the field: join customer calls, hear objections first-hand, and turn what works into training the whole team can use. You'll own field readiness end to end and be accountable for results.

### **Responsibilities**

* Define what "ready" means for each role (AEs, SDRs, SAs, and FDEs), and how we measure it and own it, starting with time-to-ramp.
* Build role-based onboarding that takes new hires from day one to customer-ready, including defining learning objectives, milestones, spearhead content build out, and build assessments and role-playing.
* Build a technical foundations track for hires from outside AI infrastructure, with TMEs and subject-matter experts as content sources.
* Run ongoing learning: targeted sessions, refreshers, deal reviews, and win/loss learnings that keep the field sharp. Define the formats that the field teams want and gets hands on in building it.
* Own the readiness plan for each launch, covering positioning, pricing, technical depth, support, and escalation paths, and serve as a readiness gate that the field has everything they need to be successful.
* Take product and positioning content from PMM and adapt it for real customer conversations. Test it in the field before teaching it.
* Embed deeply with the sales and SA team to understand what enablement and content is needed to adapt to changing market conditions and drive execution.

### **Requirements**

* 7+ years in sales enablement, sales, solutions engineering, or product marketing, including building an enablement function from scratch while a sales team scaled quickly to 100+ people (must-have).
* Experience enabling sellers of highly technical infrastructure products with complex sales cycles, such as cloud, data, developer platforms, or AI/ML
* Deep, hands-on work with sellers or customers. If you haven't carried a quota, you've spent real time on customer calls and are comfortable running a customer conversation yourself.
* Strong grounding in value-based messaging; Command of the Message or a similar framework is a plus.
* A track record of building curriculum for sellers from adjacent domains, teaching technical concepts from first principles.
* A sharp communicator who can give the pitch, explain differentiators, and walk through the competitive landscape on the spot, and handle rapid-fire questions from the field.
* A player/coach who builds content and runs sessions, not just a program manager.
* Curious about how the technology works, and quick to learn it.
