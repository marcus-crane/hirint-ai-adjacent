### **About the role**

As a Forward Deployed Engineer on our Applied AI Inference team, you’ll be the hands-on technical owner for a broad set of fast-growing customers building products on high-quality open models at scale. You’ll work across several customers and get into the details of how each customer selects and applies models in their workload, from evals and prompts to agents and harness integrations.

You’ll go deep technically. You’ll learn each customer’s architecture and use cases well enough to guide their move from closed to open models. That means choosing the right models, benchmarking quality and performance, tuning prompts and pipelines, and integrating models into their systems and workflows. You’ll write code most days, alongside customers and in our own tools.

You’ll own customer outcomes from post-POC to production. Once a Solutions Architect wins the proof of concept, you take the account into production and help it scale. You drive adoption, remove blockers and proactively make sure the customer gets the value they were promised.

You’ll bring a product mindset. You’ll turn what you learn on each deployment into reusable playbooks, tooling, reference architectures and product feedback so each new deployment is faster and needs less manual work than the last.

You’ll work across teams constantly. You’ll work alongside Solutions Architects, Sales, Engineering, Product, and Research.

As one of the early members on this team, you’ll also help define how the function runs, including its playbooks, tooling and hiring. Your work will directly affect customer retention, platform adoption and company growth.

### **Responsibilities**

* **Opinionated Onboarding:** Plan the path to production with the SA and customer, and make sure the right deployment strategy, model choice, ramp plan and monitoring are in place before the workload goes live.
* **Model Selection & Migration:** Help customers move from closed to open models. Evaluate options on the customer’s own data, pick the right model, and handle upgrades and deprecations without regressions.
* **Agent Design:** Build and debug agents and multi-step workflows that hold up in production, including tool calling, planning and retries.
* **Harness & System Integrations:** Get Together working inside the tools customers already use, like coding-agent harnesses, agent frameworks, OpenAI-compatible SDKs and data pipelines. Fix what breaks between them: tool-call formats, streaming, context limits and rate limits.
* **Evals & Prompts:** Build evals that measure what each customer cares about, and use them to tune prompts and catch regressions.
* **Customer Touchpoints:** Proactively monitor your accounts, keep a frequent pulse of how things are going, work closely alongside customers to ensure adoption and success.
* **Automation & Tooling:** Write automation, monitoring, scripts and internal tools that turn repeatable work into systems, for customers and for our own team.
* **Product Feedback Loop:** Bring what you see in the field to Engineering and Product, contribute product enhancements and documentation, and turn recurring patterns into playbooks the whole team can reuse.

### Requirements

* **Experience:** 4+ years in a technical, customer-facing engineering role such as forward deployed engineering, solutions engineering, applied AI engineering or ML infrastructure, ideally at a fast-growing company. You can own these accounts from day one.
* **Agent Design:** You’ve built agents with tool use and run them in production, and you can tell which harness or framework fits a given workload.
* **Model Selection:** You know today’s open models well and have compared open and closed models on a real workload. You can explain your pick in terms of quality, latency and cost.
* **Automation & Coding:** Strong Python. You’ve built tools and automation that other people use, and you lean on AI tools to get your own work done faster.
* **Applied AI & Integrations:** You’ve shipped LLM features into existing products, ideally coding or agentic use cases, and debugged what broke between the model, the SDK and the app.
* **Prompt Engineering:** You’ve written and evaluated prompts for production LLM applications.
* **Ownership & Judgment:** You can juggle many accounts, turn vague goals into clear plans, and know when to go deep, when to automate and when to bring in a specialist.
* **Communication:** You can explain technical tradeoffs clearly to engineers and business stakeholders.Builder Mindset: You’re excited to help build a new team from the ground up.
