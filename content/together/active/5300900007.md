### **About the Role**

We're seeking an exceptional accounting leader to join as Senior Director, Assistant Controller, and help scale our core accounting operations as we grow. Reporting to the VP, Corporate Controller, this role leads a team responsible for close and financial reporting, procure-to-pay, payroll, equity administration, treasury, infrastructure accounting, and international accounting. The ideal candidate is a hands-on leader who will both build and manage a high-performing team and stay closely engaged in the details, establishing the processes, automation, and controls needed to support the company's rapid growth as a private company.

### **Responsibilities**

Close & Financial Reporting 

* Lead the monthly, quarterly, and annual close process across all entities, driving toward a faster, more disciplined close
* Maintain the integrity of the general ledger, with oversight of complex or judgmental reconciliations
* Deliver accurate, timely internal and external reporting, including recurring investor reporting deliverables
* Serve as the primary partner to external auditors on interim and annual audits

Treasury & Procure-to-Pay

* Oversee the procure-to-pay process end-to-end and payment execution across a rapidly scaling vendor base
* Maintain treasury operations across entities and currencies, including cash positioning, banking relationships, and wire approval authority
* Strengthen internal controls over disbursements and approval hierarchies as transaction volume and complexity grow
* Partner with FP&A on cash forecasting and liquidity planning, including funding needs tied to large infrastructure commitments
* Shape procurement and treasury system requirements to maximize reporting and automation capabilities, improving visibility and efficiency

Payroll & Equity 

* Oversee payroll processing and ensure accurate, timely payroll accounting and tax compliance across all entities and jurisdictions
* Oversee equity administration, including option and RSU grants, exercises, cap table reconciliation, and support for liquidity and financing events
* Coordinate with Human Resources and Legal on equity-related matters, including new hire grants, board approvals, and 409A coordination
* Maintain compliance with payroll tax and equity-related reporting requirements
* Evaluate system scalability and support  replacements as needed

Infrastructure Accounting

* Oversee accounting for infrastructure-related transactions, including GPU and data center capital expenditures, fixed asset capitalization policy, useful life determinations, and impairment monitoring as chip generations turn over
* Collaborate with Technical Accounting on the accounting treatment for new or complex infrastructure arrangements, including lease vs. service classification

International Operations & Accounting

* Direct accounting and financial reporting for the Company's international entities, including local audits where required
* Align with local providers and Tax on statutory filings, local GAAP/IFRS reporting, and transfer pricing documentation
* Monitor the Company's growing international footprint across customers, vendors, and employees, and advise on the formation of new entities to minimize risk, in collaboration with Tax and Legal

People & Process

* Build, develop, and mentor a high-performing accounting with clear ownership as the function scales
* Lead process improvement and automation across the accounting function
* Support ad hoc projects, including system implementations and strategic diligence

### **Requirements**

* Bachelor's degree in Accounting, Finance, or related field required; CPA preferred
* 15+ years of progressive accounting experience, including leading multi-level teams; mix of Big 4 and industry experience strongly preferred
* Hands-on experience across general ledger, close, reporting, procure-to-pay, payroll, equity administration, treasury, infrastructure, and international accounting
* Experience operating in a high-growth or fast-scaling private company environment, including supporting investor and board reporting
* Experience with infrastructure, hardware, data center, or other capital-intensive businesses strongly preferred
* Strong systems experience (ERP, payroll, and equity platforms, e.g., NetSuite, Carta, Ramp, Floqast, Zip, Coupa, Paylocity, ADP,); proven track record driving process automation
* Clear communicator who partners effectively across functions
