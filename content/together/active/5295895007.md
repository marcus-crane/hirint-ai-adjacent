### **About the role**

We're hiring a Senior Digital Marketing Manager to lead and scale our paid media programs at Together AI. As part of our Demand Generation and Growth Marketing team, you'll shape how we invest across digital channels to reach AI engineers, create demand, and drive qualified acquisition, pipeline, and revenue.

You'll own our digital channel strategy, budget allocation, and performance optimization, working directly in advertising platforms and with agency partners to execute and optimize campaigns. You'll work closely with Demand Gen, Sales, Product Marketing, and Analytics teams to understand how paid investments influence the broader customer journey, experiment with new approaches, and scale what works.

### **Responsibilities**

* Own paid media strategy and performance across paid search, paid social, retargeting, display, and emerging channels, balancing demand creation, acquisition, and pipeline goals.
* Lead campaign execution and optimization with agency support, bringing hands-on expertise in Google Ads and paid social to evaluate search strategy, account structure, targeting, bidding, creative, and performance opportunities.
* Own media investment planning, including forecasting, budget allocation, pacing, and performance targets across channels and campaigns.
* Measure paid media's impact on acquisition efficiency, pipeline, and broader inbound performance beyond direct attribution. Partner with RevOps and Analytics to identify tracking, attribution, and reporting gaps.
* Drive ongoing experimentation and optimization across audiences, messaging, creative, bidding, and channel mix, using performance insights to diagnose trends, improve results, and guide investment decisions.
* Partner with ABM, Growth, and Field Marketing teams to bring integrated campaigns and events to life through paid media.
* Evaluate new channels, tools, and AI-powered workflows to improve campaign development, creative testing, optimization, and analysis.

### **Requirements**

* 6+ years of experience in B2B paid media, digital marketing, or performance marketing, preferably at a high-growth SaaS, AI-native, or digital-first technology company.
* Deep, hands-on expertise in Google Ads, including search strategy, account structure, keyword targeting, bidding, optimization, and troubleshooting.
* Strong experience executing and optimizing paid social campaigns, particularly on LinkedIn, with familiarity across additional channels such as Reddit, Meta, YouTube, or programmatic advertising.
* Proven experience managing significant paid media budgets, forecasting performance, making investment tradeoffs, and improving acquisition efficiency.
* Strong analytical skills and experience measuring paid media effectiveness across the funnel, with an understanding of attribution, incrementality, and how paid investments contribute to broader business outcomes.
* Experience working with advertising platforms, GA4, and BI tools to interpret performance data, identify measurement gaps, and translate insights into actionable recommendations.
