### **About The Role**

Together AI is the AI native cloud, which has been built bottoms up to cater to the demand for the new generation of AI applications and agents. The company has seen tremendous growth with 10X growth year on year and has over a million developers.

As we continue to scale the company, we continue to see strong community engagement - with developers looking at Together AI not just as an inference platform but also as a go-to place for all things related to open source AI.This newly created role will lead the charter for community engagement, being the advocate for our technical audience and making sure they get what they need to successful, whether they are current users or prospective customers. They will own our X, LinkedIn and Discord channels, and set the strategy for community engagement and growth. They will work across technical teams internally to develop content and other materials to deliver on that strategy.

### **Responsibilities**

* Be the heartbeat of the Together AI technical community - understanding the developers and researchers who follow us and enabling them to be successful
* Operate our community handles across X, Linkedin and Discord
* Own content strategy for these handles, delivering engaging content that the community cares about, across learning content, user content and more
* Collaborate across marketing, developer relations and solution architect teams to deliver on the content strategy
* Experiment and test new ideas to see what resonates with the community - including new content types, competitions and swag
* While the role involved mainly online engagement (80%), there is an aspect of translating their deep understanding to deliver highly engaging in-person meet ups and events (20%) in collaboration with Events/ Dev-rel teams

### **Requirements**

* 3+ years of developer marketing and community engagement experience
* Deep understanding of the AI developer audience
* Keenly aware of social spaces that developers operate in, and ideally, they themselves  operate in those spaces on X
* Naturally inclined towards social presence, always keeping a pulse on what’s happening in the developer world
* Strong ownership mindset and ability to build something from ground up
* Data-driven and ideas person - always thinking of new experiments and content
* Ideally a developer themselves with an engineering background

### **About Together AI**

Together AI, the AI Native Cloud, is purpose-built for AI engineers. AI application developers get high-performance inference that scales reliably, reinforcement learning to create specialized models, and pre-training for fully custom intelligence, all around a marketplace of leading open models that teams can run, adapt, and own. Trusted by Cursor, Decagon, ElevenLabs, Salesforce, and Zoom, Together serves over 400 trillion tokens a month.
