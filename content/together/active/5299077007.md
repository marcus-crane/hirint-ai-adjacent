### **About the Role**

Together AI is building the AI Acceleration Cloud, an end-to-end platform for the generative AI lifecycle that integrates fast, reliable inference and model-shaping services with cutting-edge AI cloud infrastructure.

We’re hiring a Senior University Recruiter to help scale our Early Talent Program across our Engineering and Research functions. This is a builder role as much as a recruiting role. You’ll partner with the University Recruiting Lead to grow our recruiting strategy and attract top talent by building campus partnerships, strengthening our brand, running events and delivering a top-notch candidate experience.

### **Responsibilities**

* Own full-cycle recruiting for internship and new grad roles across our Engineering and Research teams, delivering a fast, high-quality candidate experience
* Map and execute campus recruiting strategies that grow our pipelines and build Together AI’s campus brand
* Build partnerships with universities, student organizations and hackathons
* Partner with hiring managers to shape candidate profiles, interview loops and process improvements
* Support ad hoc programs and projects for the University Recruiting team as we continue to scale

### **Requirements**

* 4+ years of full-cycle recruiting experience, with at least 2–3 years focused on university or early-career hiring
* A track record of building or scaling a university recruiting program
* Comfort with ambiguity and complexity, since you’ll be building from the ground up
* Strong cross-functional collaboration skills and a track record of influencing organizational best practices
* Willingness to travel to conferences and campus events to represent Together AI

### **Preferred Qualifications**

* Experience running campus events, hackathons or technical programs
* Experience recruiting interns and new grads (software, infrastructure or research) at a startup or high-growth tech company
* Comfort using AI and automation tools to speed up sourcing, scheduling and candidate communication

### **About Together AI**

Together AI, the AI Native Cloud, is purpose-built for AI engineers. AI application developers get high-performance inference that scales reliably, fine-tuning and reinforcement learning for creating frontier-level specialized models, and pre-training at massive scale for fully custom intelligence, all around a marketplace of leading open models that teams can run, adapt, and own. Trusted by Cursor, Decagon, ElevenLabs, Salesforce, and Zoom, Together serves 400+ trillion tokens a month.
