### **About The Role**

We’re hiring a Growth Marketing Intern to join our Demand Gen & Growth Marketing team. You’ll help drive growth for our PLG business through experimentation and campaign execution, reaching AI-native and digital-native companies running inference at scale. You’ll run experiments from hypothesis to results, use the data to decide what to try next, and help bring campaigns to market across channels.

### **Responsibilities**

* Develop hypotheses, then design and launch experiments across email, landing pages, messaging and offers that improve adoption and retention and drive upsell.
* Support campaign execution across paid media, social, and webinars to grow qualified signups and revenue.
* Create campaign content and collateral, from webinar promotion and email copy to ad and social assets, using AI tools to produce faster at scale.
* Track and report on how campaigns drive signups, usage and product revenue, auditing setup and tracking to keep data clean and spot where to optimize.
* Lead one capstone project from start to finish and present your results and recommendations to marketing leadership.

### **Qualifications**

* Currently pursuing a bachelor’s degree in business, economics or a related field, or an MBA, and returning to school after the internship.
* Data-driven and analytical, with the ability to read data, spot trends in user behavior, and propose ways to improve acquisition and conversion.
* Curious about AI and eager to build workflows and tools that make marketing faster and smarter.
* Strong written and verbal communicator who can turn complex technical concepts into clear, compelling value propositions.
* Passionate about growing a career in marketing, enjoys pairing creativity with data, and is eager to gain hands-on experience across a range of marketing channels.
* Nice to have: exposure to A/B testing, marketing and analytics tools (HubSpot, GA4, ad platforms), SQL, or building with APIs.

### **About Together AI**

Together AI, the AI Native Cloud, is purpose-built for AI engineers. AI application developers get high-performance inference that scales reliably, reinforcement learning to create specialized models, and pre-training for fully custom intelligence, all around a marketplace of leading open models that teams can run, adapt, and own. Trusted by Cursor, Decagon, ElevenLabs, Salesforce, and Zoom, Together serves over 400 trillion tokens a month.

### **Internship Program Details:**

Our internship program spans over 12 weeks where you’ll have the opportunity to work with industry-leading engineers building a cloud from the ground up and possibly contribute to influential open source projects. Our internship dates are May 17th to August 6th or June 14th to September 3rd.
