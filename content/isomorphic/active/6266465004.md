### **About Iso**

Isomorphic Labs (IsoLabs) was launched in 2021 to advance human health by building on and beyond the Nobel-winning AlphaFold system. Since then, our interdisciplinary team of drug discovery experts and machine learning specialists has built powerful new predictive and generative AI models that accelerate scientific discovery at digital speed.

Our name comes from the belief that there is an underlying symmetry between biology and information science. By harnessing AI’s powerful capabilities, we can use it to model complex biological phenomena to help design novel molecules, anticipate how drugs will perform and develop innovative medicines to treat and cure some of the world’s most devastating diseases.

We have built a world-leading drug design engine comprising AI models that are capable of working across multiple therapeutic areas and drug modalities. We are continually innovating on model architecture and developing cutting-edge capabilities to advance rational drug design.

Every day, and with each new breakthrough, we’re getting closer to the promise of digital biology, and achieving our ambitious mission to one day solve all disease with the help of AI.

### **Your impact**

We are looking for a Technical Program Manager (TPM) for Data who is passionate about accelerating drug discovery through the strategic delivery of large-scale, high-quality scientific datasets.

At IsoLabs, you will be an essential member of the Data team, bridging the gap between cutting-edge AI research, Drug Discovery & Medical Research (DDMR), Data Engineering, and an extensive global network of scientific partners and Contract Research Organizations (CROs).

In this role, you will take ownership of the full program lifecycle for critical data generation and acquisition initiatives. You will design delivery frameworks, manage complex scientific and technical dependencies, and optimize the operational speed to deliver transformative datasets.

### **What you will do**

* Drive the delivery of high-impact data acquisition and experimental data generation programs, ensuring work converges on our core model-training and research milestones.
* Partner closely with AI researchers, drug discovery scientists, data curators, data engineers, and legal/commercial teams to define clear program outcomes, governance structures, and delivery roadmaps.
* Proactively map dependencies, identify critical path bottlenecks, and work alongside engineering and scientific stakeholders to triage technical issues across data schemas, transfer protocols, and automated quality control pipelines.
* Serve as the primary technical program interface for external CROs and data generation partners; track operational cadence, monitor experimental assay deliverables against Statements of Work (SOWs), and lead technical reviews.
* Oversee program-level financial commitments, monitor milestone-based delivery against purchase orders (POs), and maintain multi-quarter forward forecasts to support strategic planning.
* Track and communicate program performance, data velocity, and dataset utilization to ensure our expanding Data Estate delivers measurable improvements in predictive and generative AI model performance. Champion continuous improvement across our data delivery machinery.

### **Skills and qualifications**

### **Essential:**

* Proven experience as a TPM, or Scientific Delivery Manager leading complex programs in a biotech, pharmaceutical, or an AI/ML tech environment.
* Strong understanding of how different datasets can accelerate drug discovery and AI model development.
* Familiarity with experimental data types used in drug discovery e.g. structure, bioactivity, ADMET, genomics, clinical data.
* Demonstrated ability to break down ambiguous, multi-faceted scientific and technical problems into structured, actionable workstreams with clear ownership and measurable KPIs.
* Exceptional communication, relationship-building, and consensus-building skills across multidisciplinary teams - from software engineers and ML researchers to drug designers, commercial leaders, and external executives.
* BSc, MSc, or PhD degree in Life Sciences, Biology, Chemistry, Informatics, or a related discipline
* Solid grasp of how data flows through modern engineering infrastructure into ML pipelines, including understanding data schemas and data quality principles.
* Advanced proficiency with modern project management and collaboration platforms (e.g., Jira, Confluence, Linear, or equivalent)

### **Nice to have:**

* Experience in health data management and the robust governance of human derived data.
* Experience managing external scientific partnerships, multi-partner research consortia, or CRO delivery networks (e.g., managing SOWs, deliverables, and SLAs).
* Working knowledge of machine learning concepts (supervised learning, active learning, data scaling laws) and how data quality/diversity affects model performance.
* Familiarity with FAIR data principles, biomedical ontologies, and electronic lab notebook (ELN) data capture
* Hands-on data manipulation skills (e.g., basic SQL, Python, or data visualization dashboards) to track delivery metrics and analyze program progress.
* Experience with REST APIs/data exchange standards, ETL pipelines.
* Experience managing program budgets, contract stages, and vendor procurement workflows
