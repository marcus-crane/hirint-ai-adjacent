### **About Iso**

Isomorphic Labs (IsoLabs) was launched in 2021 to advance human health by building on and beyond the Nobel-winning AlphaFold system. Since then, our interdisciplinary team of drug discovery experts and machine learning specialists has built powerful new predictive and generative AI models that accelerate scientific discovery at digital speed.

Our name comes from the belief that there is an underlying symmetry between biology and information science. By harnessing AI’s powerful capabilities, we can use it to model complex biological phenomena to help design novel molecules, anticipate how drugs will perform and develop innovative medicines to treat and cure some of the world’s most devastating diseases.

We have built a world-leading drug design engine comprising AI models that are capable of working across multiple therapeutic areas and drug modalities. We are continually innovating on model architecture and developing cutting-edge capabilities to advance rational drug design.

Every day, and with each new breakthrough, we’re getting closer to the promise of digital biology, and achieving our ambitious mission to one day solve all disease with the help of AI.

### **Your impact**

As a Candidate Experience Coordinator, you are the engine of our Talent Acquisition (TA) function. You won’t just support recruitment; you will architect, own, and lead the candidate journey through interdisciplinary collaboration.

Overseeing complex global scheduling, you will own the holistic end-to-end candidate experience— running an efficient and exceptional interview process from the first touchpoint to the final stages. You will drive data integrity to influence hiring decisions, whilst also serving as a process engineer who identifies bottlenecks and implements smoother workflows. This position is designed as a launchpad for your career, giving you interdisciplinary visibility and significant project ownership across the talent acquisition lifecycle.

### **What you will do**

### **1. Own the Candidate Experience Architecture**

* **Curate the Journey:** Act as a primary, consistent point of contact for candidates, ensuring every interaction is timely, seamless, and supportive. You will seamlessly balance high volume scheduling with candidate care.
* **Host the Onsite Experience:** Act as the host and central lead for our onsite (or virtual) interview days, ensuring candidates feel welcomed, prepared, and impressed.
* **Humanise Communication:** Partner across the team to design candidate communications. You will ensure our templates are authentic and aligned with our values, including managing early-stage rejections with empathy and professionalism.
* **Scheduling Excellence:** Orchestrate complex, interdisciplinary interview loops across multiple time zones with speed and efficiency, proactively managing any last-minute changes with candidates, interviewers, and hiring managers.

### **2. Drive Operational Excellence & Data Integrity**

* **Be a "Data Guardian”:** Champion the quality and consistency of our data within our ATS (Greenhouse). You will manage job set up, build interview processes and ensure  candidate statuses are meticulously maintained to enable accurate reporting.

**Onboarding Operations: Oversee critical onboarding and compliance operations, such as right-to-work checks, pobu (integrating people data with relevant google systems), and background checks. Upskill and educate the talent team on criticality of accurate data and Greenhouse (ATS) best practices in both hiring and onboarding processes.**

* **Feedback Loops:** Partner with the Principal Talent Lead to analyse candidate survey data. You will turn these insights into meaningful actions, blending data-driven and experiential perspectives to continuously elevate our standards.
* **Identify & Eradicate Bottlenecks:** Proactively flag recurring scheduling challenges or process delays. You won’t just follow the process; you will help evolve it by suggesting improvements and piloting new workflows.

### **3. Enablement & Strategic Partnership**

* **Scaling processes:** Utilising AI, you will assist in documenting core processes for TA, creating clear and consistent guides for everything from complex scheduling to offer approval workflows. Additionally, you will ensure effective training for any new coordinators and wider TA members.
* **Champion Interdisciplinary Partnership:** You will have opportunities for domain alignment with specific verticals, allowing you to build deeper context on the roles you support and partner closely with Hiring Managers, thereby leveraging diverse skills and perspectives across the business.

### **Skills and qualifications**

### **Essential:**

* **Exceptional Organisation:** Ability to prioritise multiple functions and tasks while maintaining the highest attention to detail in a fast-paced environment.
* **Experience Centric:** A strong focus on providing the best possible candidate experience, with the empathy to manage relationships through the entire recruitment lifecycle.
* **Tech & Data Savvy:** Experience using online calendar/meeting applications (G-Suite preferred) and a data-driven approach with a strong desire to advocate for clean data.
