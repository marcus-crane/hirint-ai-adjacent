Isomorphic Labs is applying frontier AI to help unlock deeper scientific insights, faster breakthroughs, and life-changing medicines with an ambition to solve all disease.

The future is coming. A future enabled and enriched by the incredible power of machine learning. A future in which diseases are curtailed or cured starting with better and faster drug discovery.

Come and be part of an interdisciplinary team driving groundbreaking innovation and play a meaningful role in contributing towards us achieving our ambitious goals, while being a part of an inspiring and collaborative culture.

The world we want tomorrow is the one we’re building today. It starts with the culture at this company. It starts with you.

### **About Iso**

Isomorphic Labs (IsoLabs) was launched in 2021 to advance human health by building on and beyond the Nobel-winning AlphaFold system. Since then, our interdisciplinary team of drug discovery experts and machine learning specialists has built powerful new predictive and generative AI models that accelerate scientific discovery at digital speed.

Our name comes from the belief that there is an underlying symmetry between biology and information science. By harnessing AI’s powerful capabilities, we can use it to model complex biological phenomena to help design novel molecules, anticipate how drugs will perform and develop innovative medicines to treat and cure some of the world’s most devastating diseases.

We have built a world-leading drug design engine comprising AI models that are capable of working across multiple therapeutic areas and drug modalities. We are continually innovating on model architecture and developing cutting-edge capabilities to advance rational drug design.

Every day, and with each new breakthrough, we’re getting closer to the promise of digital biology, and achieving our ambitious mission to one day solve all disease with the help of AI.

### **Your impact**

If you want to solve diseases that have eluded humanity for a century by treating software and autonomous agents as the primary discovery medium, this is where it happens. Bring your technical vision, software systems intuition, and strategic product leadership to bear on accelerating rational drug discovery. At Isomorphic Labs, our competitive edge relies on translating frontier machine learning breakthroughs into biological and clinical milestones at unprecedented pace.

Operating with the mindset of a forward-deployed platform lead with deep ownership, you will own the core horizontal intelligence substrate of the company - spanning our compute infrastructure, grounded scientific data engines, and autonomous agent runtimes.

Bridging hands-on software systems with overarching business strategy, you will eliminate bottlenecks and turn bespoke research workflows into an industrial, high-throughput discovery engine. Crucially, you build with a dual mandate: ensuring our infrastructure accelerates both biological researchers working in flow-state and autonomous software agents conducting automated research at scale.

You will work in a high-performing Product team and collaborate closely with key stakeholders across Engineering, AI, and scientific disciplines.

There is some degree of flexibility around the level of seniority in these positions; this will depend on your skills and experience.

### **What you will do**

* Drive unified platform strategy: Own the product direction for our core technical and researcher platforms, aligning platform capabilities directly with systemic bottlenecks and research milestones.
* Engineer for dual citizenship (humans + agents): Build tools that give human researchers flow-state ergonomics and enable autonomous agents to execute workflows reliably.
* Accelerate research velocity: Continuously compress end-to-end iteration loops, measuring success by cycle times reduction rather than feature volume.
* Enable autonomous experimentation: Guide platforms toward higher autonomy, supporting closed-loop autoresearch and emerging agentic workflows.
* Foster a hardened core with decentralized edge building: Provide reliable primitives so scientists can prototype at the edge, while strategically hardening shared capabilities into core infrastructure.
* Co-lead with Engineering: Establish a single plan and joint objectives with Engineering leads, executing through focused joint delivery.
* Track platform ROI and impact: Measure core leverage metrics, including research velocity, compute efficiency, agent usability, and scientific throughput.
