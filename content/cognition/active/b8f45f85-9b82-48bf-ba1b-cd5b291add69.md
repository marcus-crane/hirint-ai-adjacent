## **We are an applied AI lab building end-to-end software agents.**

We're the makers of Devin, the first AI software engineer.

Our team is extremely talent-dense. Among our founding team, we have world-class competitive programmers, former founders, and leaders from companies at the cutting edge of AI including Scale AI, Palantir, Cursor, Waymo, Tesla, Lunchclub, Modal, Google DeepMind, and Nuro.

Building Devin is just the first step—our hardest challenges still lie ahead. If you’re excited to solve some of the world’s biggest problems and build AI that can reason on real-world tasks, apply to join us.

# About the Role

Cognition works with engineering leaders who are figuring out how to introduce AI into some of the largest and most complex organizations in the world.

They’re making decisions about how thousands of engineers will work, which systems to modernize, and how much more their organizations can accomplish with AI. Many are confronting problems that few of their peers have solved before.

We want to bring these people together.

We’re looking for someone to build Cognition’s customer community and advocacy programs, creating opportunities for engineering leaders to learn from one another, build relationships, and help shape the future of software engineering.

You’ll develop programs ranging from executive advisory groups and intimate gatherings to a broader community of customer champions. We want to create experiences that customers genuinely value, rather than programs that exist primarily to ask them for favors.

Done well, these relationships will also help Cognition grow, creating opportunities for customer referrals, events, advocacy, and deeper engagement with existing accounts.

# What you’ll do

* **Build our customer community.** Design and launch programs that bring together engineering leaders and practitioners across Cognition’s customer base.
* **Create our customer advisory board.** Recruit members, develop valuable programming, and make sure their feedback reaches Cognition’s product and leadership teams.
* **Develop a champions program.** Build a community of engaged customers, with meaningful opportunities for participation and recognition.
* **Bring customers together.** Organize executive roundtables, events, and other opportunities for customers to share what they’re learning.
* **Build relationships.** Develop lasting connections with our most engaged customer executives and engineers.
* **Create opportunities for advocacy.** Work with interested customers on speaking opportunities, campaigns, referrals, and other ways to share their experiences.
* **Improve the programs.** Gather feedback from customers, understand what they value, and use it to make the community stronger.

# Who we’re looking for

* **An exceptional relationship builder.** You earn people’s trust, make thoughtful introductions, and build relationships that last.
* **A strong program designer.** You know what makes a conversation, gathering, or community worth someone’s time.
* **Intellectually curious.** You’re interested in software engineering, business, and the problems senior technology leaders are trying to solve.
* **Credible with senior leaders.** You can have substantive conversations with executives and build relationships based on mutual respect.
* **Creative and resourceful.** You have original ideas for bringing people together and giving them something valuable.
* **An excellent operator.** You can turn ambitious ideas into programs that run smoothly and give people a reason to return.

You might come from community, customer marketing, customer success, executive programs, or events.

We’re looking for someone who has built communities or relationships that people genuinely valued, rather than simply managed a calendar of activities.
