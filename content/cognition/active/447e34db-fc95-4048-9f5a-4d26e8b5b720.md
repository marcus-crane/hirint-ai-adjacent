## **We are an applied AI lab building end-to-end software agents.**

We're the makers of Devin, the first AI software engineer.

Our team is extremely talent-dense. Among our founding team, we have world-class competitive programmers, former founders, and leaders from companies at the cutting edge of AI including Scale AI, Palantir, Cursor, Waymo, Tesla, Lunchclub, Modal, Google DeepMind, and Nuro.

Building Devin is just the first step—our hardest challenges still lie ahead. If you’re excited to solve some of the world’s biggest problems and build AI that can reason on real-world tasks, apply to join us.

## Role Mission

Developers research before they buy. Some of that still happens on Google. More of it now happens by asking ChatGPT, Claude or a coding agent, and on YouTube, Reddit, GitHub and X. The AI coding category is new enough that the answers in all of those places are still open.

You will own how people and agents find Devin and Cognition. That covers answer engine optimization (AEO) for the best AI assistants and agents, organic search on every platform where developers look, technical SEO, content strategy and programmatic pages. You will work directly with the web engineering team to ship, and you will be measured on how often we show up, the organic traffic that follows and the signups it produces.

## What You'll Accomplish

* **Set the roadmap:** Decide which questions, topics and platforms matter most across [devin.ai](http://devin.ai) and [cognition.ai](http://cognition.ai), prioritized by what drives signups.
* **Win in AI answers:** Learn how the leading assistants and agents choose and cite sources, and make Cognition the answer they give for AI coding questions.
* **Show up on every platform:** Google, YouTube, Reddit, GitHub, X and wherever else developers search, with content made for each one.
* **Fix the technical foundation:** Site structure, performance, crawlability and content that both crawlers and agents can read, shipped with web engineering.
* **Build programmatic pages:** Pages that answer real questions and convert, not just rank.
* **Report clearly:** AI-answer visibility, rankings, organic traffic and signups, tracked and explained.

## Exceptional Candidates Have Demonstrated

* **Discoverability results:** You've made a product easier to find, in search, in AI answers or on another platform, and can show the before and after.
* **Technical ability:** You can read a site crawl, write a clear spec, and work well with engineers.
* **Writing and editing:** You can write or edit content developers trust.
* **Real experiments in AI search:** You've tested what changes how AI assistants and agents answer, and you can tell what's proven from what's a guess.
* **Hands-on preference:** You like doing the work yourself.

We care more about demonstrated capability than years of experience.

## Resources & Environment

* Two domains with real authority and a lot of untapped demand
* Direct access to the web engineering and content teams
* A small team with very little process
