## **We are an applied AI lab building end-to-end software agents.**

We're the makers of Devin, the first AI software engineer.

Our team is extremely talent-dense. Among our founding team, we have world-class competitive programmers, former founders, and leaders from companies at the cutting edge of AI including Scale AI, Palantir, Cursor, Waymo, Tesla, Lunchclub, Modal, Google DeepMind, and Nuro.

Building Devin is just the first step—our hardest challenges still lie ahead. If you’re excited to solve some of the world’s biggest problems and build AI that can reason on real-world tasks, apply to join us.

# About the Role

Cognition works with some of the world’s largest enterprises and fastest-growing startups on ambitious applications of AI software engineering. From modernizing decades-old systems to changing how thousands of engineers build software, our customers are doing extraordinary work with Devin—and have compelling stories and results to show for it.

You’ll build the operating engine behind one of Cognition’s most important go-to-market initiatives: creating the most ambitious customer-led marketing operation in AI, spanning everything from major brand campaigns and documentary films to a steady stream of customer proof that helps Sales win deals.

Cognition believes in investing heavily in AI, internal tools, and repeatable systems so a small team can operate like a much larger one. You’ll help set priorities, build internal tools with Devin, and develop the processes that let our customer marketers and creative teams produce great work consistently—and get it into the hands of the marketers and sellers who need it.

You’ll also directly own the customer reference program, develop our measurement framework, and help find exceptional external creative partners. You’ll work closely with marketing leadership, Sales, forward-deployed engineers, and creative teams, with substantial ownership over how the operation is designed and run.

You should be an exceptional BizOps generalist who wants to apply their skills to building an AI-native marketing operation.

# What you’ll do

* **Shape priorities.** Work with marketing leadership, Sales, and FDEs to identify the most important customer stories, industries, programs, and proof gaps, and help determine where the team should focus its resources.
* **Design and run the operating model.** Own how customer marketing work is prioritized, planned, coordinated, approved, and delivered. Establish clear processes and responsibilities so the team can execute reliably at scale.
* **Productize the operation.** Use Devin to build internal tools and automate workflows across story discovery, intake, production coordination, approvals, asset management, and distribution.
* **Build scalable programs.** Develop repeatable formats, templates, and processes that support both ambitious creative productions and a steady stream of high-quality customer proof.
* **Build our amplification programs.** Partner with brand, demand generation, field marketing, and Sales to establish repeatable ways of launching customer stories and adapting them for paid media, social, web, events, and other channels.
* **Productize customer proof for Sales.** Build tools that let account executives quickly find relevant customer results, quotes, clips, and case studies, and establish feedback loops to identify gaps.
* **Own the customer reference program.** Build and manage the program end to end, including recruiting customer references, matching them to prospective buyers, managing requests, and coordinating with Sales and account teams.
* **Build our measurement framework.** Define how we measure customer marketing’s contribution to brand awareness, engagement, pipeline, deal acceleration, and expansion. Use quantitative results and qualitative feedback to evaluate programs and inform priorities.
* **Recruit exceptional creative partners.** Help find and evaluate production companies, filmmakers, writers, designers, and other external collaborators as we expand our creative capabilities.
* **Continuously improve the operation.** Identify bottlenecks, eliminate unnecessary manual work, and find ways for a small team to take on increasingly ambitious projects.

# Who we’re looking for

* **An outstanding cross-functional operator.** You’re exceptional at stakeholder alignment, organizing complex initiatives, clarifying responsibilities, and driving execution across teams.
* **Commercially minded.** You’ve worked closely with Marketing or Sales in a hypergrowth environment and understand how GTM organizations operate.
* **A systems thinker.** You recognize opportunities to turn recurring problems into repeatable processes, tools, and programs.
* **Extremely AI-native.** You use AI extensively, independently build useful software and automations, and enjoy experimenting with tools to improve how teams work.
* **Highly analytical.** You can structure ambiguous problems, work with data, develop useful metrics, and use quantitative and qualitative information to make decisions.
* **High agency.** You take ownership of difficult problems, find practical solutions, and see them through to completion.

Ideally, you’ve spent at least two years in consulting, private equity, or another analytically rigorous environment, followed by two or more years in BizOps, Strategy & Operations, GTM Operations, or a similar role at a hypergrowth technology company.
