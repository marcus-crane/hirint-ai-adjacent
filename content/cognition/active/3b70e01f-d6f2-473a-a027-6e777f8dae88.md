## **We are an applied AI lab building end-to-end software agents.**

We're the makers of Devin, the first AI software engineer.

Our team is extremely talent-dense. Among our founding team, we have world-class competitive programmers, former founders, and leaders from companies at the cutting edge of AI including Scale AI, Palantir, Cursor, Waymo, Tesla, Lunchclub, Modal, Google DeepMind, and Nuro.

Building Devin is just the first step—our hardest challenges still lie ahead. If you’re excited to solve some of the world’s biggest problems and build AI that can reason on real-world tasks, apply to join us.

**About the role**

We're hiring a Head of Tax to build Cognition's tax function as our first in-house tax hire. Reporting to the Controller, you'll own all direct and indirect tax across the company, bring outsourced work in-house, and build processes and controls ready for a public company. You'll be part of a lean, AI-first finance team that aims to redefine how the function operates.

**In this role you will:**

* Build the tax function, including the calendar, processes, controls, and advisor relationships, and use AI tools like Devin to automate as much of the work as possible
* Own the quarterly and annual ASC 740 provision and support the annual audit
* Own federal, state, and international income tax compliance, and manage notices and audits
* Own indirect tax (sales and use, VAT/GST) and associated tracking
* Lead international tax and transfer pricing, including entity structure and intercompany arrangements
* Lead tax on M&A and equity compensation, including diligence, integration, Section 382, and equity withholding

**What we're looking for:**

* You've worked at a Big 4 firm and then in-house at a high-growth tech company, with 5+ years in tax.
* You've owned an ASC 740 provision end-to-end through an audit.
* You're credible across federal and state, international and transfer pricing, M&A, equity comp, and indirect tax, and you know when to bring in advisors.
* You've been an early or first tax hire, or built a tax function from scratch, and you like being hands-on.
* You use AI tools heavily in your work and can show how. Experience at an AI company is a plus.
* You're based in San Francisco and willing to work from the office 5 days per week.
