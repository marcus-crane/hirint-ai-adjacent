## **We are an applied AI lab building end-to-end software agents.**

We're the makers of Devin, the first AI software engineer.

Our team is extremely talent-dense. Among our founding team, we have world-class competitive programmers, former founders, and leaders from companies at the cutting edge of AI including Scale AI, Palantir, Cursor, Waymo, Tesla, Lunchclub, Modal, Google DeepMind, and Nuro.

Building Devin is just the first step—our hardest challenges still lie ahead. If you’re excited to solve some of the world’s biggest problems and build AI that can reason on real-world tasks, apply to join us.

# About the Role

Software runs much of modern life, and advances in AI are opening up new possibilities for how it gets built and what it can accomplish.

We believe AI software engineering will be one of the most consequential technological shifts of our time. We want to explore what that means for the future of software, businesses, and society.

We’re looking for an exceptional writer and editor to help shape Cognition’s thinking and publishing on the future of software engineering.

You’ll work with founders, engineers, researchers, and customer leaders on essays, original research, interviews, and other editorial projects. You'll identify important questions, develop compelling ideas, and turn complex technical and business subjects into clear, insightful writing.

We want to publish deeply researched essays, original analysis, and reporting that bring fresh perspectives to the technological changes shaping our world.

# What you’ll do

* **Shape the editorial agenda.** Identify important questions and arguments about AI, software engineering, and their effects on the world.
* **Write and edit exceptional work.** Develop essays, interviews, and original features with founders, engineers, researchers, and customers.
* **Develop original research.** Explore subjects such as engineering productivity, software investment, and AI adoption in large organizations.
* **Work with experts.** Interview technical and business leaders, challenge their assumptions, and turn complex ideas into clear, compelling writing.
* **Find important customer stories.** Work with customer marketers to uncover stories that reveal something meaningful about how AI is changing an industry.
* **Set an exceptional editorial standard.** Care about everything from the strength of an argument to the precision of an individual sentence.
* **Bring ideas to a wider audience.** Work with the rest of marketing to develop campaigns, video, events, and other formats around our strongest editorial work.

# Who we’re looking for

* **An exceptional writer and editor.** You can make complicated ideas clear without flattening them, and have published work that demonstrates original thinking and command of language.
* **Deep intellectual curiosity.** You enjoy learning difficult subjects, pursuing unfamiliar questions, and understanding how things work.
* **Excellent editorial taste.** You know what makes a piece worth publishing, what’s missing from an argument, and when an idea needs more work.
* **Comfort with technical subjects.** You can understand engineering and economic concepts deeply enough to ask good questions and explain them accurately.
* **Skill working with experts.** You can draw interesting ideas out of busy technical people and help them express those ideas without losing their voice.
* **Independent judgment.** You have your own ideas about what Cognition should publish and can take a piece from an initial question to a finished argument.

You might come from journalism, magazine editing, research, publishing, or independent writing. We care much more about the quality of your thinking and published work than whether you’ve worked in marketing before.

Please share examples of your best writing and editing work.
