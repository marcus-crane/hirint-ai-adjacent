## **We are an applied AI lab building end-to-end software agents.**

We're the makers of Devin, the first AI software engineer.

Our team is extremely talent-dense. Among our founding team, we have world-class competitive programmers, former founders, and leaders from companies at the cutting edge of AI including Scale AI, Palantir, Cursor, Waymo, Tesla, Lunchclub, Modal, Google DeepMind, and Nuro.

Building Devin is just the first step—our hardest challenges still lie ahead. If you’re excited to solve some of the world’s biggest problems and build AI that can reason on real-world tasks, apply to join us.

# About the Role

In one of the most competitive markets in technology, where products and marketing claims are easier to produce than ever, we believe real customer stories and results are what cut through the noise. Cognition has extraordinary stories to tell: our customers span the world's largest enterprises and fastest-growing startups, and our product and forward-deployed engineering teams work closely with them to deliver real engineering value.

Our ambition is to put customers at the center of Cognition's entire go-to-market. We want customer stories to power everything from major brand campaigns, documentary films, and industry thought leadership to events, demand generation, and sales proof.

We're looking for an exceptional marketer to make that happen for a portfolio of industries. You'll work closely with Sales and our forward-deployed engineers to understand our business priorities, build relationships with customer executives, identify compelling stories, and develop marketing programs that help us win new customers and grow existing accounts in those industries.

We're looking for someone with excellent business judgment, strong editorial instincts, and deep intellectual curiosity. You'll need to understand our customers' businesses, recognize which stories matter to their peers, and develop programs that support Cognition's most important commercial priorities.

You'll have considerable freedom to decide which stories to pursue, what to create, and how to bring that work to market. You might come from leading marketing at a fast-growing startup, or from customer marketing, product marketing, integrated campaigns or a similar role.

# What you’ll do

* **Own your industries.** Develop customer marketing strategies for a portfolio of industries and accounts, aligned with Cognition’s business priorities.
* **Partner deeply with Sales and FDEs.** Understand where we’re seeing the most important customer results, what matters to buyers, and where customer marketing can help us win.
* **Build executive relationships.** Earn the trust of CIOs, engineering leaders, and communications teams, and get them excited about ambitious joint marketing projects.
* **Find exceptional stories.** Interview customers, understand the technical and business results they’ve achieved, and identify the most compelling angles.
* **Develop the full portfolio of customer marketing.** Lead projects ranging from written and filmed stories, executive interviews, events, joint communications, and targeted sales proof to major brand films and campaigns.
* **Bring stories to market.** Work with brand, demand generation, product marketing, field marketing, and Sales to reach the right buyers through the right channel and get the most from each stories.
* **Build feedback loops.** Work with Sales and FDEs to understand which stories are resonating, what buyers still need to see, and how to improve your programs.
* **Help shape our industry narratives.** Show what AI software engineering makes possible in the industries you cover, using real customer experiences and results.

# Who we’re looking for

* **Excellent business judgment.** You understand how enterprise companies make technology decisions, how sales teams win, and how to prioritize marketing work against commercial opportunities.
* **A strong editorial eye.** You know how to find a compelling story in a complicated technical project, and can distinguish an interesting result from a genuinely important one.
* **Deep intellectual curiosity.** You love learning how companies and industries work. You’re willing to go deep on unfamiliar subjects and ask questions until you understand them.
* **A great partner to Sales.** You earn the confidence of account executives and sales leaders because you understand their business and produce work that helps them.
* **Credibility with executives.** You can build trust with CIOs, engineering leaders, and communications teams, and persuade them to participate in ambitious projects.
* **Strategic and hands-on.** You can decide what matters, develop a plan, and personally do the work required to execute it.
* **High standards.** You care about the quality of the thinking, the story, and the final creative work.
