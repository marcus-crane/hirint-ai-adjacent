**About the role**

We're hiring a Strategic Finance Lead to cover go-to-market at Cognition. You'll be Finance's partner to Marketing, Sales, and GTM Operations, owning GTM budgets, sales and marketing efficiency metrics, and self-serve forecasting. You'll also represent Finance on deal desk. You'll be part of a lean, AI-first finance team that aims to redefine how the function operates.

**In this role you will:**

* Own marketing budgeting and forecasting, and track spend and ROI by channel and program
* Own the sales budget, including headcount cost, T&E, programs, and tools, and report on performance against plan
* Define and track sales efficiency metrics like cost to book, CAC, and payback
* Partner with GTM Operations on bookings and consumption forecasting, capacity and hiring plans, and sales productivity
* Own self-serve revenue forecasting, along with fraud prevention and tracking
* Represent Finance on deal desk, reviewing discounts, non-standard terms, and deal structures

**What we're looking for:**

* You've worked in investment banking or investing (private equity, hedge fund) and then in strategic finance at a high-growth tech company. GTM finance experience is a plus, but strong general strategic finance experience works too.
* You have strong financial modeling skills
* You understand both enterprise sales and usage-based business models.
* You're comfortable working with sales and marketing leaders, including pushing back on their assumptions.
* You use AI tools heavily in your work and can show how. Experience at an AI company is a plus.
* You're based in San Francisco and willing to work from the office 5 days per week.
