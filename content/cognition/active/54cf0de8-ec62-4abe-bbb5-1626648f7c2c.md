## **About the Role**

As Merch Manager at Cognition, you will own every piece of merch that represents our brand outside the company. That covers event swag, customer and prospect gifts, custom orders for our GTM teams, our merch store, and of course, our infamous Devin plushies. Your work will turn our brand into something tangible and help our sales and marketing teams build relationships.

You will own the merch program end to end, from request and approval through production, fulfillment and delivery. Every item should reflect our brand at the highest level of quality and arrive where it needs to be, on time.

This role is execution-heavy. We are looking for someone who loves logistics, stays calm under a high volume of requests, communicates clearly with every stakeholder, and works closely with vendors. We have plenty of creative voices. We need someone who can turn ideas into products that ship.

## **In this role, you will:**

* Own all external merch, including event swag, customer and prospect gifting, custom team orders, our merch store and plushies
* Take in and prioritize a high volume of custom requests from Sales, Marketing and Events (often 10+ a day), manage approvals, and make sure every order is fulfilled and delivered
* Serve as the primary point of contact for merch vendors and production partners, running regular order reviews, negotiating pricing and timelines, and holding partners to a high quality bar
* Partner closely with Brand Design to ensure all merch and collateral adheres to Cognition's brand guidelines, securing design approval on every piece before it goes out
* Build a single, scalable intake process that replaces scattered requests across Slack
* Forecast demand and inventory across events, campaigns and regions
* Partner with Revenue Marketing to set budget guardrails that decide when teams can order directly and when they need approval, based on event size and expected ROI
* Create repeatable processes, playbooks and documentation that scale alongside Cognition's growth

## **Requirements for the role:**

* 4+ years of experience running merch, promotional products, swag or gifting programs, at a high-growth company or at an agency or production partner with high standards
* Proven ability to manage many concurrent orders and stakeholders end to end without dropping details
* Experience managing suppliers, production timelines and domestic and international fulfillment
* Strong organizational rigor, attention to detail, and a high personal quality bar
* Clear, proactive communication with internal requesters and external partners
* Comfort with budgets, forecasting and basic data analysis
* A good eye for quality, plus the judgment to work within an established brand and creative direction

## **You might excel if you…**

* Have founded, owned or run a merch, promotional products or production agency
* Have launched or operated a branded merch store
* Thrive in a fast-moving startup where you build the process as you go
* Think like an owner of the merch program and its business impact, not just an executor of orders
