**Overview of Opportunity**

Two Six is looking for an Operations Analyst to join our team. Operations Analysts are expected to provide expert advice on employing our Sentr system to support analysis and assessments for our customers. This position will be onsite in Doral, FL.

**What you will do:**

* Integrate Sentr into the planning and execution of operations, from tactical to strategic
* Use Sentr to answer information requirements within the online information environment and train the customer to do the same;
* Work side by side with the customer, using Sentr to understand and visualize data to gain a better understanding of the information environment and support assessments;
* Ability to take holistic and qualitative information requirements and create responsive analytic visualizations that provide insight to difficult problems;
* Integrate Sentr derived and other available data in support of customer assessments requirements;
* Act as a liaison between Two Six and our customers;
* Promote Sentr and Two Six in a variety of locations, and to a variety of customer types;
* Periodic travel CONUS/OCONUS

**What you will need:**

* Minimum of 5 years of experience as an Data Analyst, Information Operations planner, Psychological Operations planner, or Intelligence Analyst
* Minimum of 3 years of experience with data analysis tools such as Sentr, Pulse, Kibana, R, Gephi or similar analytical or open source information tools

**Nice to haves:**

* Prior experience working in a military environment
* Experience with AdTech
* Ability to visualize data in a way that tells a story and helps analysts, planners, and leaders make decisions
* Ability to independently deliver support and training to customers in a self-driven work environment
* The ability to analyze data for trends and patterns and articulate the impact on planned operations
* Professional fluency in Spanish

**Security Clearance:**

* Active TS Clearance with SCI Eligibility
