Through unrivaled collaboration and unwavering trust, we push the boundaries of what’s possible to empower our team and support our customers in building a safer global future.

**Overview of Opportunity**

Two Six Technologies is growing and we are looking to add a **Senior Program Manager** in the DC Metro Area. In this role, you will provide programmatic leadership to strong technical teams to mature DARPA developed technologies and deliver operational capabilities to the Department of War (DoW) Service Cyber Components. The ideal individual will have to understand technical project execution management, the ability to quickly build strong relationships with relevant government stakeholders at DARPA and DoW stakeholders, ability to lead and support interactions with collaborative projects within the company and customer.

Come join our team where we solve complex challenges in support of critical missions for our national security customers and we enable information operations that address urgent human security challenges faced by the world’s vulnerable populations.

**What you will be doing:**

* Provide programmatic leadership across a $10m+ projects to operationalize DARPA R&D technologies and integrate into US Service Cyber Component missions.
* Drive and shape with DARPA and command stakeholders for next generation capabilities.
* Provide programmatic leadership as prime contractor over multiple sub-contractor teams
* Be present to meet with internal and external stakeholder to foster and enhance relationships with stakeholders
* Travel outside of DMV up to 10% time

**What you will need:**

* A leader who actively protects program focus, removes administrative burdens on Program Leads, and balances strict accountability with empathetic team advocacy.
* Bachelor’s Degree or higher in related technical field
* 5+ years experience directly engaging with DoW organizations
* 10+ years leading execution of technical projects
* Strong track record of managing complex government accounts, navigating sensitive mission environments, and converting operational excellence into organic contract growth.
* Active Top Secret/Sensitive Compartmented Information (TS/SCI) clearance with the ability to be read on to Special Access Programs (SAP).
