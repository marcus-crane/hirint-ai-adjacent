At Two Six Technologies, we build, deploy, and implement innovative products that solve the world’s most complex challenges today. Through unrivaled collaboration and unwavering trust, we push the boundaries of what’s possible to empower our team and support our customers in building a safer global future.

**Overview of Opportunity**

Join our **Trusted Electronics team** for a hands-on internship focused on developing innovative solutions for embedded devices in security applications. You’ll work alongside experienced engineers and researchers to bring **proof-of-concept technologies** to real-world solutions. We are seeking several interns for this 12-week internship program, which will be conducted **onsite** in Arlington, VA.

**Responsibilities:**

* Support reverse engineering and vulnerability research efforts across software, hardware, and networked systems.
* Assist in identifying, analyzing, and documenting potential security vulnerabilities.
* Conduct technical research related to network exploitation, RF technologies, and cybersecurity.
* Collaborate with engineers and researchers to develop tools, troubleshoot technical challenges, and document findings.

**Minimum Qualifications:**

* Actively pursuing a degree in **Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering**, or a related field
* Familiarity with at least one of the following:

+ **Modern development languages** such as **Python, C, C++, or Embedded C**
+ **Software-hardware interfaces** and embedded system development

* Strong communication skills and a willingness to contribute to a **collaborative team environment**

**Preferred Qualifications:**

* Academic or hands-on experience with reverse engineering and/or vulnerability research.
* Familiarity with Digital Signal Processing (DSP) and Software Defined Radio (SDR) technologies
* Exposure to radio frequency (RF) technologies, wireless communications, or signal analysis.
* Experience with reverse engineering tools such as Ghidra, Binary Ninja or, IDA Pro, or participation in cybersecurity research projects or CTF competitions.

**Clearance/Citizenship:**

* Due to the nature of our work US Citizenship is required.
