**Overview of Opportunity**

Two Six Technologies is actively seeking a **Lead Embedded Software Engineer** to join our **Telecommunications Research Team**. The team is composed of intellectual individuals, passionate about low-level embedded software development, cellular/wireless telecommunications protocols, vulnerability analysis, and Trusted Execution Environments. The team is growing and looking for someone with a C/C++ development and Agile engineering background who has an understanding on how to architect, develop, and transition proofs-of-concept into modular, fieldable operational capabilities to support national security missions. If you are actively using C, C++, modern operating system internals, and Agile practices to drive technical execution and mentor growing teams, the team wants to talk to you!

**What you will do**

* Direct the technical planning, architectural design, and hands-on C and C++ execution of low-level software tasks for complex embedded communications and security research programs.
* Introduce, tailor, and champion Agile processes (such as sprint planning, backlog refinement, iterative delivery, and continuous integration) across the team to improve development velocity and maintain project schedules.
* Transition research-grade software, proofs-of-concept, and vulnerability analysis findings into modular, scalable, and fieldable operational capabilities for critical national security customers.
* Bridge the hardware/software boundary by developing low-level interfaces, APIs, and automated CI/CD testing pipelines (GitLab CI) to support solution evaluations, range deployments, and hardware-in-the-loop testing.
* Provide technical direction, guidance, and mentorship to engineering team members while leading customer demonstrations and technical exchanges with key external stakeholders.

**What you will need (basic qualifications)**

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical domain; in place of a degree, comparable professional experience or a strong portfolio of relevant technical projects and coursework will be considered.
* Minimum of 5 years of hands-on software engineering experience utilizing C and C++.
* Demonstrated track record of leading technical task prioritization, schedule management, and software delivery within Agile/Scrum development frameworks.
* Minimum of 2 years of deep technical experience working with operating system internals (e.g., privilege levels, binary memory layouts, virtual memory management) or Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs) such as ARM TrustZone.
* Proven experience designing, implementing, and debugging low-level embedded software, hardware/software interfaces, or communications protocols.
* Active Top Secret Clearance with the willingness and ability to obtain and maintain a TS/SCI clearance with Polygraph, and availability to work on-site in Arlington, VA.

**Nice to have (preferred)**

* Practical experience serving in an Agile technical leadership role (e.g., Scrum Master or Tech Lead) coaching multi-disciplinary teams through process adoption and agile workflow integration.
* Hands-on firmware and device driver development experience across Linux and/or embedded real-time operating systems (RTOS).
* Technical familiarity with wireless and cellular telecommunications stacks, RF/SDR systems, or protocol reverse engineering.
* Experience contributing technical solutions and content to proposals, white papers, or customer technical exchanges.
* Active Top Secret/SCI clearance with Polygraph.

**Security Clearance:** Active Top Secret Clearance is required, with the ability to obtain and maintain a TS/SCI security clearance.
