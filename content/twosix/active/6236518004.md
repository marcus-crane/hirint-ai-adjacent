**Overview of Opportunity**

Two Six Technologies is actively seeking a **Systems Software Engineering Intern** to join our **Digital Advantage Mission Ops team.** We are seeking several interns for this 12-week internship program, which will be conducted **onsite** in Arlington, VA.

**Key Responsibilities**

* Implement C and Python systems programming tasks for low-level networking, hardware-facing code, and Linux kernel integration.
* Execute Linux internals work, including kernel module development, driver configuration, device enumeration, and system debugging below userspace.
* Assist with PCIe system architecture alongside high-performance networking offloads and out-of-band management interfaces.
* Proactively communicate project status to team leads and present technical work with an end-of-internship presentation

**Minimum Qualifications**

* Currently pursuing a degree in **Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mathematics, Statistics,** or a related field.
* 1+ year of professional or academic C and Python software development, Linux kernel familiarity and Knowledge of Networking fundamentals
* Familiarity with git and different Linux software development tools
* US Citizenship is required due to the nature of our work.
* Ability to work onsite at our Arlington, VA office.

**Nice to have**.

* Experience in systems programming in C and Python (low-level networking and hardware-facing code)
* Knowledge of Linux internals (kernel modules, drivers, device enumeration, debugging below userspace)
* Familiarity of PCIe system architecture (topology, addressing, peer-to-peer, virtualization, and passthrough).
* Understanding of high-performance networking (kernel-bypass packet processing, hardware offload, line-rate data paths) and out-of-band management (BMC and REST-based interfaces with automation) concepts
