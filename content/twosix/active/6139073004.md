**Overview of Opportunity**

**Two Six Technologies is looking for a Technical Product Engineering Lead to join our team in Arlington, Virginia.** This person will serve as the technical team lead for one of our hardware security products. They will own the end-to-end technical vision and design decisions that span hardware, FPGA, embedded firmware, and software. In addition to setting technical direction, you will be a hands-on team member working alongside other passionate, motivated engineers across hardware, FPGA, and software disciplines. You will help rapidly transition new prototype products to larger-scale production in service of important national security missions.

**Responsibilities Include:**

* Serve as the technical team lead, owning the end-to-end system architecture and technical roadmap across hardware, FPGA, embedded firmware, and software. Lead the engineering team through key architectural and design tradeoff decisions across hardware/FPGA/embedded/software boundaries, ensuring a cohesive, secure system design.
* Act as the primary technical point of contact and decision-maker for the product.
* Lead the planning and execution of a group of tasks associated with one or more technical projects or functions, managing task prioritization and schedule management.
* Provide technical guidance, direction, and mentorship to engineering team members.
* Mature proof-of-concept R&D technology to operational fieldable solutions.
* Develop testing strategies and lead the implementation of unit, integration, and hardware-in-loop test regimens to ensure high reliability and resilience in delivered products.
* Lead the effort to minimize maintenance overhead of a growing product suite by applying scalable and modular design methodologies.
* Engage in technical exchanges with transition partners and key stakeholders.

**Minimum Qualifications:**

* Demonstrated experience serving as a technical lead, architect, or similar role leading engineering work across one or more disciplines (e.g., hardware, FPGA, and software)
* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer/Electrical Engineering, or related technical domain
* Minimum of 7 years of experience with two or more of the following modern programming languages: Python, C , C++, Embedded C, Rust, Go
* Strong experience with Embedded Linux, including Yocto-based build systems
* Linux kernel development experience (drivers, subsystems, or kernel-space debugging)
* Experience working alongside FPGA and hardware teams
* Experience with testing, CI/CD pipelines, and virtual machine test ranges
* Experience deploying secure systems (e.g., secure boot, disk encryption, key management)
* Familiarity applying classical cryptographic algorithms (RSA, ECDSA, AES, SHA).
* Strong leadership and project management skills, with the ability to mentor junior engineers and drive technical execution.
* Ability to work on-site when required for hardware work in Arlington, VA

**Nice to haves:**

* Experience developing and/or customizing bootloaders such as WolfBoot, implementing secure firmware update using digital signatures
* Hands-on experience with PCIe (device drivers, enumeration, or hardware bring-up)
* Experience shipping and iterating on production software in customers' hands
* Graduate degree in Computer Science, Computer/Electrical Engineering, or related Scientific Domain
* Familiarity with validation and/or certification frameworks such as FIPS 140-3, NIAP, CSfC, or others
* Familiarity with post-quantum cryptographic algorithms and their trade-offs in their applications

**Security Clearance:**

* Eligibility to obtain and maintain a DoD clearance up to Top Secret level
