At Two Six Technologies, we build, deploy, and implement innovative products that solve the world’s most complex challenges today. Through unrivaled collaboration and unwavering trust, we push the boundaries of what’s possible to empower our team and support our customers in building a safer global future.

Two Six Technologies is seeking a highly motivated and entrepreneurial cleared Senior Product Manager to join a new, dynamic Internal Research and Development (IR&D) team focused on scaling Helix, our Agentic AI orchestration product. In this role, you'll be at the forefront of a major company initiative, collaborating with engineering, design, data science, and capture to drive competitive advantage from building a reusable agentic orchestration layer targeted at specific mission applications.

About the Team  
Agentic AI is poised to revolutionize how information, intelligence, and cyber professionals operate. It promises to deliver the flexibility and rigor they need while providing intuitive, user-centric workflows that empower faster, more confident decision-making. We're making a significant investment in this technology because we believe that:

• Software should make more decisions on behalf of the user.  
• Trust in AI is built through frequent high-value user-centric experiences.  
• A blend of commercial software and government services can accelerate AI adoption.  
Our team is dedicated to creating an industry-leading agentic technology for critical applications like collection management, intelligence analysis, decision support, and cyber and information operations. If you're passionate about shaping the future of AI in high-stakes environments, we want you on our team.  
This is a fully remote position with flexibility to manage your day around collaboration with your team. DC location is preferred but not required. Some travel will be required to interface with our customers and Two Six employees collaborating on cleared programs.

What you will do:  
• Work with customers and stakeholders to develop a product vision for the agentic AI orchestrator  
• Conduct market research, solicit customer feedback, and test new ideas to build our backlog and roadmap  
• Collaborate in a cross functional team to build agentic AI functionality  
• Write clear and well-bounded users stories that keep the team focused on delivering slices of user value every sprint  
• Engage stakeholders in the innovation process and keep them informed of your work  
• Advocate for solving customer problems by building functionality into the product line  
• Collaborate with other PMs to build value across product lines  
• Advocate for a user-centered culture and drive change in that direction  
• Be open and flexible to jump in where help is needed

What you will need (basic qualifications):  
• Experience collaborating with tech leads, designers, and domain experts to build intuitive user experiences  
• Experience with data scientists to build AI into the pipeline is required; experience with LangChain, LangGraph, LangFlow, MCP, A2A, MCPApps, and RAG is a key differentiator  
• Experience working with leadership, customers, and internal stakeholders to build products that solve hard problems  
• Strong familiarity with the role of a Product Manager and PM tools for discovery and delivery, including creating rapid prototypes to quickly test new product ideas  
• Experience working in scrum/kanban on cross functional teams  
• Team mentality to do what’s best for the product  
  
Nice to haves:  
• Experience creating products for government users, particularly in environments with limitations on technology access and ability to interface with users  
• Experience applying systems thinking to platform technologies, including modular and configuration-driven software development  
• Experience with advanced analytics, natural language processing, large language models and generative AI  
• Interest in supporting US government missions to counter foreign adversaries with excellence in intelligence analysis, and electronic and cyber warfare

Clearance Requirement:  
Minimum TS clearance required; TS/SCI preferred.
