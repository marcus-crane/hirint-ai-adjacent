At Two Six Technologies, we build, deploy, and implement innovative products that solve the world’s most complex challenges today. Through unrivaled collaboration and unwavering trust, we push the boundaries of what’s possible to empower our team and support our customers in building a safer global future.

### **Overview of Opportunity**

Two Six Technologies is seeking a high-impact **Senior Program Manager** to lead mission-critical programs delivering advanced cyber, information advantage, and analytic capabilities and technologies to national security customers for our Digital Advantage Capability.

This role offers significant autonomy to direct multi-million-dollar programs, lead elite technical teams, and cultivate strategic customer relationships. Working in an agile, high-growth environment, you will bridge the gap between advanced research and operational deployment, delivery GOTS/COTS technologies to new and existing customers, optimize internal contract execution, and serve as a trusted interface between government leadership and internal engineering teams. This role is hybrid, working up 2-3 days a week onsite at Two Six office.

### **Key Responsibilities**

**Customer Engagement & Relationship Management**

* Interface directly and frequently with government stakeholders, mission owners, technical directors, and contracting officers, leading with a strong customer-centric focus.
* Translate complex customer goals, desires, and evolving requirements into clear, actionable tasking and execution plans for engineering teams.
* Build and sustain strong, trusted relationships both externally with client stakeholders and internally across Two Six business units and functional teams.
* Maintain a regular presence at TST and customer facilities to participate in strategic meetings, ensure alignment, and foster collaborative partnerships.

**Program Execution, Financials & Business Process Optimization**

* Provide programmatic leadership across multiple high-visibility projects, directing program cost, schedule, scope, and technical performance.
* Accelerate and optimize internal business processes, including the management of large government contract vehicles with multiple stakeholders, complex deliverables, and rigorous project financial planning.
* Oversee programmatic and contractual deliverables to ensure high-quality, on-time execution in accordance with contract terms.
* Lead comprehensive risk management efforts to adapt swiftly to changing mission demands and project dependencies.

**Team Leadership & Capability Delivery**

* Provide clear direction and leadership to cross-functional programmatic, technical, and analytic teams.
* Source, hire, and structure highly specialized, domain-oriented technical teams.
* Structure work streams, streamline project workflows, and enforce rigorous quality assurance across technical outputs.
* Deliver executive-level operational, financial, and technical updates to internal executive leadership and government stakeholders.
* Navigate ambiguity with initiative, establishing clear frameworks and path-to-execution in a fast-paced environment.

### **Basic Qualifications**

* **Experience:** 7+ years of dedicated Program Management experience supporting National Security and defense missions.
* **Domain Expertise:** Proven track record managing complex cybersecurity or advanced technical programs in classified environments.
* **Business & Financial Acumen:** Demonstrated experience managing large government contract vehicles, multi-stakeholder execution, project financial planning, and contractual deliverable management.
* **Customer Focus:** Proven ability to interface directly with customers, build strategic relationships, and translate high-level customer requirements into technical tasking.
* **Education:** Bachelor’s degree or higher from an accredited university.

**Security Clearance**:  Active Top Secret (TS) clearance with SCI eligibility.
