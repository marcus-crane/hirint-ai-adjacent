At Two Six Technologies, we build, deploy, and implement innovative products that solve the world’s most complex challenges today. Through unrivaled collaboration and unwavering trust, we push the boundaries of what’s possible to empower our team and support our customers in building a safer global future.

Are you a Product Manager passionate about transforming complex information into clear insights for policymakers and military operators? Join Two Six Technologies and lead Opportunity Identification, an agentic AI powered product experience in our Sentra Suite for Information Advantage. The Opportunity Identification team empowers information professionals to rapidly pinpoint mission-critical content and shape the outcome to accelerate action.

You'll own the product vision and strategy for a best-in-class user experience, collaborating closely with clients, end-users, and a talented team of engineers, data scientists, and analysts specializing in data collection, enrichment pipelines, agentic AI, generative AI, LLMs, and mobile. If you're driven to make a significant impact by bringing government and military officials from question to answer to action with unparalleled speed and clarity, we want to hear from you.  
This is a fully remote position with flexibility to manage your day around collaboration with your team. DC location is preferred but not required. Some travel (10-20%) will be required to interface with our customers and Two Six employees collaborating on cleared programs.

What you will do:

• Work with customers and stakeholders to develop a product vision for Opportunity Identification  
• Conduct market research, solicit customer feedback, and test new ideas to build our backlog and roadmap  
• Collaborate in a cross functional team to build Opportunity Identification functionality  
• Write clear and well-bounded users stories that keep the team focused on delivering slices of user value every sprint  
• Balance cutting-edge NLP capabilities with platform infrastructure costs  
• Engage stakeholders in the innovation process and keep them informed of your work  
• Advocate for solving customer problems by building functionality into the product line  
• Collaborate with other PMs to build value across product lines  
• Advocate for a user-centered culture and drive change in that direction  
• Be open and flexible to jump in where help is needed

What you will need (basic qualifications):

• Experience collaborating with tech leads, designers, and domain experts to build intuitive user experiences  
• Experience with data scientists to build AI into the pipeline is a key differentiator  
• Experience working with leadership, customers, and internal stakeholders to build products that solve hard problems  
• Strong initiative and the ability to create momentum in a rapidly changing market  
• Strong familiarity with the role of a Product Manager and PM tools for discovery and delivery, including creating rapid prototypes to quickly test new product ideas  
• Experience working in scrum/kanban on cross functional teams  
• Team mentality to do what’s best for the product  
• Experience with data collection, ingestion, pipeline processing, and/or signal hardening

Nice to haves:

• Experience creating products for government users, particularly in environments with limitations on technology access  
• Experience with advanced analytics, natural language processing, large language models and generative / agentic AI  
• Interest in supporting US government missions to counter foreign adversary and non-state actor propaganda and disinformation

Clearance Requirement:  
• Active TS or TS/SCI Clearance is a bonus, but those without a clearance are encouraged to apply.
