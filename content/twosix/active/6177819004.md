**Overview of Opportunity**

Join the Communication Systems division at Two Six Technologies in Arlington, Virginia, where we push the boundaries of software and firmware reverse engineering to uncover vulnerabilities in wireless and embedded systems. As part of our elite team of security researchers, you’ll work alongside CNO developers and hardware engineers, conducting cutting-edge vulnerability research and binary analysis on complex telecommunications hardware and protocols.

Our government customers rely on us to deliver mission-critical security solutions, and we’re looking for a **Senior Software Reverse Engineer** who excels at disassembling embedded targets, bypassing protections, and developing custom tools to manipulate binaries. If you are driven by low-level software analysis, wireless protocol research, and solving high-impact national security challenges, we want you on our team.

**What You’ll Do:**

* Reverse engineer software, firmware, and protocol implementations in wireless and embedded telecommunication systems.
* Analyze complex software protections, binary obfuscation, and communication stack implementations to expose underlying security flaws.
* Develop proof-of-concept exploits and verification tools following DevOps best practices for maintainable codebases.
* Design, build, and maintain custom tooling to accelerate the reverse engineering and disassembly of targeted binaries.
* Work in tight collaboration with CNO developers, vulnerability researchers, and hardware engineers in a fast-paced, multi-disciplinary team.
* Tackle open-ended technical challenges that require creative, first-principles problem-solving.

**What You’ll Need (Basic Qualifications):**

* Bachelor’s (or higher) degree in Computer Science, Computer/Electrical Engineering, or a related field (or equivalent practical experience).
* Extensive experience developing, debugging, and scripting in C/C++ and Python within Linux command-line environments.
* Deep experience with disassemblers and decompilers such as IDA Pro, Binary Ninja, or Ghidra.
* Demonstrated expertise in one or more of the following:

* Firmware analysis across architectures (ARM, MIPS, PowerPC, RTOS).
* System emulation and firmware rehosting via tools like QEMU.
* Fuzzing, crash analysis, and exploit development.
* Binary obfuscation, anti-debugging, and anti-analysis bypass techniques.
* Strong knowledge of fundamental network and transport protocols (TCP/IP, UDP, HTTP).

**Nice to Have (Preferred Qualifications):**

* Experience conducting targeted software reverse engineering on wireless, and embedded telecommunication targets.
* Proven track record defeating modern platform mitigations (ASLR, DEP, Stack Canaries).
* Understanding of cellular protocol standards (4G LTE, 5G) and short-range communications (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee).
* Knowledge of industrial or low-bandwidth serial communications (RS485, RS232, CAN).
* Practical experience writing custom plugins or scripts for Ghidra or the Binary Ninja API.
* Experience developing embedded software in C/C++ for RTOS or embedded Linux environments.
* Background in cryptographic implementations, secure boot mechanisms, or side-channel analysis.

**Clearance Requirement:**

* Active U.S. Security Clearance at the **Top Secret** level with the ability to obtain and maintain **TS/SCI**
