At Two Six Technologies, we build, deploy, and implement innovative products that solve the world’s most complex challenges today. Through unrivaled collaboration and unwavering trust, we push the boundaries of what’s possible to empower our team and support our customers in building a safer global future.

**Overview of Opportunity**

Two Six Technologies is actively seeking a **Senior Full Stack Developer** to join our research team in **Arlington, Virginia**. The team is composed of intellectual individuals, passionate about developing reverse engineering tooling, system architecture, and complex software implementation. The team is growing and looking for someone with a full-stack software engineering background who has an understanding of how to implement scalable, end-to-end software capabilities and collaborate across test automation and engineering leads. If you are actively using modern frontend frameworks, Python, and cloud-based architectures, the team wants to talk to you!

**What you will do**

* Design, develop, integrate, and deploy end-to-end full-stack applications and reverse engineering tooling for critical national security research programs.
* Partner closely with Lead Full Stack Developers, Hardware Test Automation Engineers, and Principal Investigators to implement assigned features and complex technical tasks in a 100% in-office lab environment in Arlington, VA.
* Build scalable frontend interfaces, backend services, APIs, and containerized deployment workflows while proactively communicating progress and technical blockers to team leads.
* Operate under minimal supervision in a fast-paced environment with shifting requirements, applying sound technical judgment to solve complex engineering challenges.
* Provide technical guidance and support to junior and staff teammates as needed to help deliver high-quality, mission-ready software capabilities.

**What you will need (basic qualifications)**

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field and **5+ years of experience** in full-stack software development or a similar software engineering role (extensive experience in Software Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field may substitute for degree requirements).
* Proven proficiency developing full-stack applications across the stack using **Python** and **JavaScript**, with hands-on experience working in Linux-based environments.
* Solid understanding of the software development lifecycle, including version control (**Git**), containerization and orchestration (**Docker, Kubernetes**), and CI/CD automation tools (**Jenkins, GitLab CI**).
* Demonstrated ability to execute complex technical tasks with minimal supervision, proactively communicate status, and provide technical guidance and support to teammates.
* Must possess an active **Top Secret (TS)** clearance with **SCI eligibility** and be willing to work 100% in-person in a lab environment in Arlington, VA.

**Nice to have (preferred)**

* Experience designing, deploying, or integrating scalable applications within **AWS**, **OpenStack**, or other cloud-based architectures.
* Strong professional experience with modern frontend frameworks, specifically **Vue.js**.
* Familiarity with Infrastructure-as-Code practices and tools such as **Ansible**, **Terraform**, or **OpenTofu** to optimize service delivery.
* Prior experience supporting DoD or national security R&D projects, or collaborating with hardware/embedded test automation frameworks.
* Experience contributing to technical documentation, system specifications, or proposal content.

**Security Clearance**

* Must possess an active **Top Secret (TS)** clearance with **SCI eligibility**.
