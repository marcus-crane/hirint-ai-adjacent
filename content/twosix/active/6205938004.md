**Overview of Opportunity**

Two Six Technologies is seeking a Language Enabled Analyst to support a US Government customer. This position involves leveraging linguistic and cultural understanding to develop effective messaging strategies across a variety of social media platforms, engaging with individuals in those global locations, studying data from various social media platforms and other online sources, assessing content validity, and writing reports detailing these assessments.  The work will be hybrid out of Arlington, Virginia.

**What you will do**

* Assess content in target language or dialect for potential intelligence value
* Engage with individuals in target language on behalf of the program
* Develop and implement messaging strategies for propagating messaging content in target language information environment(s)
* Write long-form analytical reports in English that summarize findings to be disseminated to multiple stakeholders
* Analyze a wide variety of OSINT information

**What you will need**

* Native proficiency in Arabic and French
* Demonstrated advanced professional writing skills in the English language
* Minimum of 4 years writing grammatically and culturally appropriate content in target language without peer review
* Minimum of 4 years in an intensive academic or professional background in target region political and/or security issues
* At least one year of experience supporting a government customer
* High level familiarity with traditional and social media in target language, including prevalent trends, key personalities, political dynamics, and current events
* Demonstrated use of target language in either an IC mission set or in an IO/OSINT mission set.
* Experience and demonstrated proficiency using a range of technologies, including databases and open source tools

**Nice to haves**

* Native proficiency in Farsi, Pashto, Dari, or Urdu
* HUMINT/Targeting experience or experience in cybersecurity
* Background in data science and/or programming languages

**Security Clearance:**

* Active TS/SCI clearance required
