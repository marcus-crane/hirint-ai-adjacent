At Two Six Technologies, we build, deploy, and implement innovative products that solve the world’s most complex challenges today. Through unrivaled collaboration and unwavering trust, we push the boundaries of what’s possible to empower our team and support our customers in building a safer global future.

We are looking for a high-energy and resourceful **Office Coordinator** to join our team in Herndon, VA. You will act as the ‘face’ of our company and ensure visitors receive a heartwarming welcome. You will coordinate front desk activities, deliveries, administrative support, meeting room reservations and assistance, and on-site event coordination.

Our ideal candidate has a solid customer service approach and is able to provide professional and friendly service to employees and guests.

This is an on-site position in our new **Herndon, VA o**ffice.

**What you will do:**

* Welcome guests as they arrive at the office and direct visitors to the appropriate person and office
* Maintain office security by following safety procedures and controlling access via the reception desk (monitor logbook, issue visitor badges)
* Ensure reception area is tidy and presentable, with all necessary stationery and material (e.g. pens, forms and brochures)
* Executive and Administrative support as needed
* Receive, sort and distribute daily mail/deliveries
* Monitor stock and order office supplies and consumable inventory
* Assist with on-site event coordination

**What you will need:**

* 2+ years of front desk/reception, office management or administrative experience
* Ability to work on site 5 days per week during core business hours
* Hands on experience with office machines (e.g. fax machines and printers)
* Excellent communication skills (oral and written)
* Great organizational and multitasking abilities
* High school Diploma / GED
* Experience using Google suite and/or MS Office

**What we would like:**

* Experience as an office manager
* Experience working with vendors to order office supplies
* Event coordination experience
* Previous experience working for government contractor
* Associates Degree or above

**Security Clearance:**

* You must be willing and eligible to receive a security clearance

The projected pay range listed for this position is a general guideline and not a guarantee of rate.
