**Overview of Opportunity**

Two Six Technologies is seeking a highly skilled **Technical Program Manager** to lead the execution of classified government programs in Herndon, Virginia. This role provides the opportunity to manage complex technical projects supporting national security, working alongside top engineers to deliver cutting-edge solutions. The ideal candidate will have strong technical and program management expertise, ensuring successful project execution, stakeholder engagement, and mission impact.

**What you will do:**

* Oversee the execution of classified technical programs, ensuring alignment with mission objectives and government requirements.
* Manage project timelines, program staffing, deliverables, and resources while maintaining high-quality outcomes.
* Collaborate with the technology SMEs  to address technical program needs
* Serve as a key liaison between engineering teams and government clients, translating technical concepts into actionable strategies.
* Develop and implement processes, tools, and performance metrics to optimize program execution.
* Present technical and programmatic briefings to senior government officials.
* Support business growth by identifying opportunities to enhance capabilities within the classified domain.
* Participate in and lead proposal generation and delivery.

**What you will need (Basic Qualifications):**

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
* Minimum 5 years of experience managing technical programs or client engagements.
* Strong ability to communicate complex technical concepts to senior government stakeholders.
* Experience collaborating with engineers and cross-functional teams in a fast-paced environment
* Experience managing program budget, resources, and schedules
* Experience documenting processes and ensuring compliance with government standards
* A proven track record of capturing Government R&D contracts/programs
* Familiarity with DoD/IC procurement and contracting processes

**Nice to have (Preferred):**

* Background in embedded systems, mobile systems, telecommunications, or critical infrastructure.
* Knowledge of cybersecurity principles, secure architecture, or penetration testing.
* Background in reverse engineering, vulnerability research, exploit development, or CNO tool development
* Experience leading teams in a rapid prototype environment demonstrating scalable architectures
* Past performance as a Principal Investigator on fast-paced, innovation-driven, advanced technology R&D projects for Government clients
