**Overview of Opportunity**

As a **Senior Systems Engineer** focused on Datacenter Infrastructure, you will design, implement, and maintain server-room and datacenter environments supporting unclassified and classified operations. You will work with servers, networking equipment, rack power, and cooling infrastructure across unclassified server rooms, SCIFs, and/or SAPFs. In addition to deployment and troubleshooting, you will own long-term infrastructure upkeep, including license management, hardware and software upgrades, system updates, configuration documentation, equipment labeling, and cable management.

This is a hands-on role requiring coordination with facilities, security, engineering, and operations stakeholders.  This is a fully onsite role based in our Two Six Technologies office in Herndon, Virginia.

**Responsibilities:**

* Design, plan, implement, and maintain datacenter and server-room infrastructure, including server, network, rack power, and cooling systems
* Plan infrastructure capacity and equipment layouts, including rack space, power, cooling, and network connectivity, in coordination with the facilities team
* Install, configure, test, troubleshoot, and maintain servers, network equipment, and associated rack infrastructure
* Coordinate infrastructure changes and maintenance activities with facilities, security, engineering, and operations stakeholders
* Manage infrastructure licenses, hardware and software upgrades, system updates, and lifecycle replacements
* Maintain accurate infrastructure documentation, including configurations, layouts, changes, and maintenance records
* Maintain equipment labeling and cable management standards, and update records when equipment or connectivity changes
* Follow applicable change-control, safety, physical-security, and facility-access procedures
* Identify infrastructure risks, capacity constraints, and reliability issues; recommend and implement improvements

**Qualifications:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, Engineering, or a related field and 5+ years of overall technical experience OR comparable education and experience will be considered
* 2+ years of experience in a data center, server room, or comparable critical infrastructure environment
* Experience planning datacenter or server-room infrastructure, including power, cooling, and equipment layout requirements
* Prior experience supporting infrastructure in a SCIF and/or SAPF
* Hands-on experience installing, configuring, maintaining, or troubleshooting servers and networking equipment
* Experience maintaining infrastructure documentation, equipment inventories, and configuration records
* Active Top Secret/Sensitive Compartmented Information (TS/SCI) clearance

**Preferred Skills:**

* CCNA certification
* Advanced networking knowledge, including routing, switching, network design, and troubleshooting
* Experience with server, network, rack power, or cooling technologies from relevant vendors
* Familiarity with infrastructure monitoring, asset and license management, and change-control processes

**Security Clearance Requirements:**

* Active Top Secret/Sensitive Compartmented Information (TS/SCI) clearance
