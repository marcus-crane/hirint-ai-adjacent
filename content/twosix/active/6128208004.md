**Overview of Opportunity:**  
  
Two Six Technologies is seeking an experienced and motivated **Lead Data Engineer** to work within a cross-functional development team focused on advancing a state of the art in mission tracking technology enhanced by artificial intelligence and machine learning.  The work will be located in our Arlington, Virginia office

**Responsibilities:**

* Partner across teams to design and support the implementation of a seamless, decentralized, and secure data sharing framework that ensures consistent data exchange across different platform instances.
* Work closely with backend and database teams to align storage strategies across relational, indexing, and specialized time-series engines, balancing fast retrieval with long-term persistence.
* Maintain and align complex, multi-domain data models utilized across dozens of microservices, ensuring data integrity, strict contract management, and system-wide consistency.
* Collaborate with infrastructure and platform teams to adapt and scale data systems as computational needs evolve, ensuring seamless performance across both bare-metal and cloud environments.
* Collaborate on the design and implementation of resilient data patterns (such as CQRS and secure store-and-forward pipelines) to guarantee data security, integrity, and strict access controls in a distributed microservices environment.

**Requirements:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or related field or comparable experience
* **Languages:** 6+ years of professional software development experience, with deep expertise in **Python** and/or **Go (Golang)** within highly distributed environments.
* **Databases:** 4+ years of hands-on experience designing and optimizing relational schemas in **PostgreSQL**, alongside experience utilizing **Elasticsearch** for high-performance indexing and retrieval.
* **Streaming & Caching:** 3+ years of experience implementing and scaling event-driven architectures with distributed message brokers like **Kafka**, and leveraging **Redis** (or similar key-value stores) for high-speed caching.
* Eligibility to obtain and maintain a TS security clearance.

**Nice to have:**

* Exposure to DevSecOps and Agile environments and processes
* Proficient understanding of code versioning tools, such as Git
* A self-driven work ethic and a willingness to jump in and solve hard problems
* Adept at simplifying and communicating complex ideas
* Experience with Kubernetes, AWS, Docker, a plus
* DoD 8570 Level II or Level III Certification, for example, Comptia Security + or CISSP

**Security Clearance:**

* Eligibility to obtain a government clearance if required/needed.
