Through unrivaled collaboration and unwavering trust, we push the boundaries of what’s possible to empower our team and support our customers in building a safer global future.

**Overview of Opportunity**

Join our **Security Operations team**, where we focus on **protecting corporate equities and managing operational risk** through **physical, personnel, and industrial security**expertise. We are seeking one intern for this 12-week internship program, which will be conducted **onsite** in Arlington, VA. This is **NOT** a **cybersecurity** role.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Analyze existing security workflows to create comprehensive process maps that identify key stakeholders, decision points, and potential bottlenecks.
* Utilize Microsoft tools and web-based mapping applications to create comprehensive visual representations of security workflows.
* Develop and maintain continuity references and "how-to" guides for critical security operations to ensure seamless transitions and operational resilience.
* Work closely with members of the security team to gather information and validate the accuracy of process maps and continuity reference materials.
* Assist in updating essential security documentation to ensure alignment with current government regulations and internal policies.
* Identify areas where documentation and/or processes is/are lacking or outdated and propose improvements to mitigate operational risk.
* Support the Information Security team in servicing customer requests and audits.
* Collaborate with the security team on various security projects.
* Present an end-of-internship project that highlights your contributions.

**Minimum Qualifications:**

* Currently pursuing a degree in **Security Management, Risk Management, Criminal Justice, Homeland Security, Intelligence Studies,** or a related field.
* Rising Juniors or Seniors are strongly preferred.
* U.S. Citizenship is required due to the nature of our work.
* Ability to work onsite at our Arlington, VA office 5 days a week.

**Skills & Experience (Preferred Exposure to One or More of the Following):**

* Advanced experience using Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, and PowerPoint) and Visio to develop and format complex documentation, process maps, and presentations.
* Exceptional technical writing skills and the ability to synthesize complex information from various sources into clear, concise, and professional documents.
* Comfort navigating and utilizing various web-based platforms for research, documentation management, and operational support.
* Strong attention to detail and proofreading and editing skills to ensure all security documentation are accurate and professional.
* Excellent customer service attitude and willingness to support security challenges in a fast-paced environment.

**Clearance Requirements:**

* Must have eligibility to obtain a U.S. security clearance.
