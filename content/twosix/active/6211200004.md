## **About the Role**

We are seeking an experienced Senior Platform Software Engineer (AI Specialist)  to join our core Platform Team. Our platform functions as a curated, self-service product designed to accelerate delivery and reduce cognitive load for our Stream-Aligned feature teams.

In this role, you will be the primary architect and technical owner of our internal AI orchestration ecosystem. You will not build isolated features; instead, you will design, deploy, and maintain the underlying infrastructure, developer tools, and shared services that enable full-stack feature developers to seamlessly integrate agentic AI capability into their codebases.

This is a full-time, hybrid position that requires work at our Arlington office three days per week. Applicants must be U.S. citizens and have the ability to obtain and maintain up to a Top Secret/SCI security clearance.

## **What You Will Do**

* **Own AI Infrastructure & Orchestration:** Establish, scale, and maintain a robust internal orchestrator layer to manage LLM interactions, tool execution loops, context windows, and token allocation efficiently.
* **Build Self-Service Tools & Libraries:** Design and package shared utilities, client libraries, and standardized abstractions (such as managed agentic skills and database migration patterns) so feature teams can independently leverage AI tools.
* **Optimize for Fast Flow:** Partner with Stream-Aligned developers to evaluate, select, and optimize prompt engineering guardrails, embedding stores, and testing patterns, ensuring automated pipelines protect flow and speed up deployment frequency.
* **Drive Platform Architecture:** Ensure platform components are hosted independently, version-controlled, and seamlessly importable, allowing for safe rollbacks and clean operational boundaries within a monorepo structure.
* **Collaborate and Upskill:** Participate in internal guilds and cross-team workshops, acting as a technical consultant to ensure architectural decisions across the UI, API, and data model layers remain fluid and stable.

## **What We Are Looking For**

* **AI Orchestration Focus:** Proven engineering experience building and deploying AI agent frameworks, orchestrators, or middleware. Strong familiarity with agentic orchestration frameworks such as LangGraph/LangChain and/or PydanticAI, along with proficiency in advanced prompt management, token optimization, and managing external skill libraries.
* **Full-Stack Application Engineering:** Comfortable working across multiple layers of a modern web application stack—including frontend components, API service layers, and relational databases—to ensure friction-free platform adoption.
* **Infrastructure as Code & Automation:** Proficient with automated delivery tooling, container orchestration, and packaging systems to build “secure by default” execution environments.
* **Agile Experience:** Proven experience operating effectively within an Agile software development environment, paired with a strong understanding of continuous delivery metrics (such as Lead Time and Deployment Frequency) and a commitment to creating predictable automated testing guardrails over relying on gatekeeping steps.
* **Autonomous & Collaborative:** Comfortable taking ownership of technical initiatives, prioritizing work independently, and communicating proactively across team boundaries without requiring micromanagement.
* **Clearance:**Must be US Citizens and have the eligibility to obtain and maintain up to Top Secret/SCI security clearance.

## **Why This Role is Unique**

You will not be stuck building isolated features or manual workarounds. You will have full organizational support to shift architectural foundations left, building a reliable infrastructure ecosystem that transforms our developer experience into a high-trust, fast-flowing environment.
