Through unrivaled collaboration and unwavering trust, we push the boundaries of what’s possible to empower our team and support our customers in building a safer global future.

**Overview of Opportunity**

Two Six Technologies is seeking a Software Engineering Summer Intern to research and develop next-generation technologies for mobile communications and networks. We are seeking candidates that have an interest in research as well as a commitment to developing solutions for the real world. You will join a small, supportive team that values cooperation and creative spirit.We are seeking several interns for this 12-week internship program, which will be conducted onsite in Arlington, VA.

**Key Responsibilities:**

* Actively participate in research programs focused on advancing technology in the areas of resilient communications, next-generation networking, security, and privacy in mobile platforms
* Become intimately familiar with all aspects of what goes on inside of today's smartphones, including the hardware, kernel, drivers, firmware, framework, and apps
* Design and develop custom software solutions on mobile phones and other resource-constrained platforms, e.g., single-board computers

**Minimum Qualifications:**

* Pursuing a degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, or a related field
* 1 year or more of professional or academic software development experience in the C++ or Java programming languages
* Familiarity with standard Linux software engineering tools, e.g. git, gcc, make
* Eligible and able to obtain and maintain a government clearance

**Nice to have:**

* Experience with Android app development using Java or Kotlin
* Experience building or modifying Android or other mobile operating systems
* Experience with network protocol development
* Experience with the Rust programming language

**Clearance Requirements:**

* US Citizen with the ability to obtain and maintain required security clearance up to Top Secret
