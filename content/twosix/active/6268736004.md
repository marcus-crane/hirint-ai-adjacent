**Overview of Opportunity**

Two Six Technologies is actively seeking a **Lead Hardware Test Automation Engineer** to join our research team in Arlington, Virginia. The team is composed of intelligent individuals, passionate about hardware-in-the-loop automation, embedded systems, and delivering resilient, mission-critical cyberspace capabilities. The team is growing and looking for someone with a background in low-level embedded software, Python development, and test automation who can architect end-to-end hardware/software test platforms, support multiple customers, and independently drive successful outcomes at test-and-evaluation events. If you are actively using Python to interface directly with embedded systems and hardware debuggers, the team wants to talk to you!

**What you will do**

* Lead the execution of automated hardware-in-the-loop (HIL), integration, functional, and performance test platforms across four concurrent customer programs.
* Develop low-level Python test harnesses, full-stack tooling integrations, and high-performance test workflows to interface directly with embedded targets, firmware, and RTOS environments.
* Interface with customers as necessary at on-site test events, representing Two Six Technologies through live capability demonstrations, technical troubleshooting, and schedule coordination.
* Operate autonomously in a dynamic environment with shifting priorities, proactively identifying and communicating technical blockers or schedule risks to internal and external stakeholders.
* Provide technical direction, code/design guidance, and mentorship to junior and mid-level engineers as the testing team expands.

**What you will need (basic qualifications)**

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field, paired with **7+ years of experience** (or **5+ years** with demonstrated Lead-level technical ownership) in hardware/embedded test automation, embedded software development, or full-stack software engineering.
* Active **Top Secret** clearance with the ability and willingness to obtain and maintain a **TS/SCI** clearance.
* Proven hands-on proficiency developing automated test frameworks and low-level hardware interface frameworks in **Python** to interface with embedded systems and Linux environments.
* Demonstrable experience testing, debugging, and validating embedded hardware/software systems using common communication protocols (**SPI, I2C, UART, USB, JTAG**) and lab instrumentation (oscilloscopes, logic analyzers).
* Proven track record of owning technical deliverables across evolving schedules, representing engineering teams at external customer test events, and mentoring peers.

**Nice to have (preferred)**

* Hands-on experience performing reverse engineering, binary/firmware analysis, or vulnerability research on embedded systems and hardware architectures preferred.
* Proficiency in **C, C++, Embedded C,** or hardware description languages (**VHDL/Verilog**), alongside full-stack web/API technologies (JavaScript, SQL, Docker, virtualized test environments) for test orchestration and reporting.
* Experience designing automated hardware/software CI pipelines using **GitLab CI, Jenkins, or Ansible**, paired with issue-tracking and artifact management tools (JIRA, Artifactory/Nexus).
* Experience validating and verifying **Xilinx/AMD FPGA System-on-Chip (SoC)** designs and Real-Time Operating Systems (**RTOS**).
* Prior experience interfacing with government or defense customers during on-site integration and test events across multiple concurrent programs preferred.

**Security Clearance:** Active Top Secret Clearance is required, with the ability to obtain and maintain a TS/SCI security clearance.
