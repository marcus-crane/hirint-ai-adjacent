Two Six Technologies is seeking an experienced **Lead** **Front End Engineer** to work on multi-domain platforms serving critical national security functions using cutting-edge modern frameworks. Join our team to solve hard problems in large-scale data investigation and analysis, real-time collaboration, task and resource planning and coordination, and high-dimensional data summarization and visualization allowing lay persons to easily answer complex questions.

Work will be located in either our Arlington, VA or Austin, TX offices.

**What will you do:**

* Define and implement UI framework pieces composed of reusable components to support rapid development of new features
* Work closely with design and other leads to implement detailed designs
* Be comfortable developing full sections of functionality given broad requirements, including identifying and handling of edge cases and error states
* Provide technical direction and mentoring to developers, including one-on-one time to educate and ensure success

**What you need to perform successfully:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or related field and 7+ years practical experience in software engineering. In lieu of degree, comparable experience will be considered.
* 5+ years of experience with component-based frameworks, e.g. Vue, Ember, React, and/or Angular
* 4+ years of experience developing responsive web applications
* 2+ years of experience as tech lead for an agile development team
* Deep understanding of JavaScript, HTML, CSS
* Experience developing with AI coding tools
* Experience with build tools (e.g. Webpack, Vite)
* Experience working with UI/UX Designers and Product Managers to meet customer requirements
* Experience using Git and Jira (or similar tooling) in a development workflow

**Nice If you Have:**

* 2+ years of managerial experience
* 2+ years of experience in API development
* Proficiency with TypeScript
* Experience with Python and/or Java
* Experience with AWS / DevOps infrastructure
* Experience creating features allowing for simultaneous editing of content
* Familiarity with SQL and databases in general

**Clearance:**

* Eligibility and willingness to obtain and maintain a government security clearance up to TS/SCI
