**Overview of Opportunity**

Two Six Technologies is seeking an **Junior Software Engineer** to join our team of dedicated developers and researchers. You’ll work alongside top-tier engineers to build, test, and maintain software solutions for unique embedded systems and national security applications. This position is ideal for recent graduates or early-career engineers who are eager to learn, grow their technical skills in low-level programming and reverse engineering, and make a tangible impact on national security.  
The work will be in our Ellicott City, Maryland office.  
**What you will do:**

* Assist in writing, testing, and debugging clean, maintainable code for low-level software components and embedded systems.
* Support senior engineers in reverse engineering tasks, vulnerability research, and analyzing system behaviors.
* Deconstruct technical systems under mentorship to understand core functionality and security implications.
* Participate in code reviews, technical discussions, and collaborative problem-solving within a small-team environment.
* Document code, technical findings, and software workflows clearly to support ongoing team initiatives.
* Continuously learn and adapt to new technologies, programming languages, and embedded platforms.

**What you will need:**

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Computer/Electrical Engineering, Mathematics, or a related technical discipline (or equivalent foundational experience).
* Foundational experience writing code in C/C++ or Python through coursework, internships, or personal projects.
* Core understanding of computer architecture, operating systems, and basic software engineering principles.
* Familiarity with Linux command-line environments.
* Basic understanding of software security fundamentals and common vulnerability concepts.
* Strong problem-solving skills, curiosity, and an eagerness to learn complex systems under guidance.

**Nice to have (Preferred):**

* Exposure to reverse engineering tools (e.g., Ghidra, IDA Pro, or debuggers) through coursework or CTF competitions.
* Basic familiarity with embedded systems, RTOS, or hardware interfaces.
* Introductory knowledge of cryptography concepts or AI-enabled technologies.
