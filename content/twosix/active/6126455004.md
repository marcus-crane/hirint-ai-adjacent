**Overview of Opportunity**

Two Six Technologies is seeking a Senior RF Systems Engineer to develop software for DSP/SDR systems. Engineers will perform architecture design, software development, testing, analysis, and debugging to support R&D programs in Herndon, Virginia. This role offers a hybrid work. The engineer will have the autonomy to define their toolchains, guide lab build-outs, and select the SDR platforms and software required for the work. The primary focus of this role is RF signal generation, protocol analysis, and signal reverse engineering.

**What you will do (Responsibilities):**

* Reverse engineer COTS devices to map reception logic, with the objective of generating downlink messages to the device via simulated signals.
* Reverse engineer complex RF protocols, focusing on isolating, analyzing, and decoding Pseudo-Random Noise (PRN) codes.
* Synthesize and generate custom RF waveforms based on reverse-engineered signal parameters.
* Detect, identify, and decode captured RF waveforms and complex communication signals.
* Perform RF signal reception and transmission using various baseline modulation schemes (e.g., FSK, PSK, QAM).
* Implement custom SDR solutions using existing COTS platforms and software in a Linux environment.
* Solve technical challenges in communications, signal collection, data processing, and electronic warfare domains.
* Execute experiments and validate generated signals in laboratory facilities using SDR platforms and RF signal test equipment.
* Provide subject matter expertise in signal generation, RF reverse engineering, and Digital Signal Processing (DSP).
* Collaborate with cross-functional engineering teams in a rapid prototyping environment.

**What you will need (Basic Qualifications):**

* Active DoD Top Secret clearance with SCI eligibility required
* Degree in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mathematics, Physics, Computer Science, or equivalent practical, military, or intelligence community experience.
* Minimum 5 years of Digital Signal Processing (DSP) and Software Defined Radio (SDR) development experience.
* Extensive experience in RF reverse engineering, including deriving transmission parameters and regenerating target signals.
* Proven ability to generate and synthesize complex RF signals and waveforms.
* Experience in SIGINT, commercial telecom, cellular, or general communications systems development and testing.
* Ability to architect and implement digital signal processing and SDR solutions, with an emphasis on software and algorithmic implementations.

**Nice if you have (Preferred):**

* Familiarity with the signal structures, modulations, and protocols of space-based systems (e.g., GPS L1/L2, GNSS, SATCOM).
* Experience working with space-dominant modulations like QBPSK and BOC (Binary Offset Carrier).
* Experience with Ghidra and firmware reverse engineering.
* Deep understanding of digital communication concepts, including Forward Error Correction (FEC), Viterbi and Reed-Solomon decoding, interleaving, and framing analysis.
* Experience with satellite simulation software (e.g., AGI STK) or practical knowledge of handling space-related signal phenomena, such as extreme Doppler shift mitigation.

**Security Clearance:**

* Active DoD Top Secret clearance with SCI eligibility required
