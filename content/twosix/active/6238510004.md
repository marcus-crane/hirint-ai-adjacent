At Two Six Technologies, we build, deploy, and implement innovative products that solve the world’s most complex challenges today. Through unrivaled collaboration and unwavering trust, we push the boundaries of what’s possible to empower our team and support our customers in building a safer global future.

Our Security Team is growing and we are looking for a **Physical Security Specialist** for our Herndon, VA Headquarters.  You will support the physical security program by assisting with facility security, access control, visitor management, security procedures, escorting, and compliance activities. This position provides hands-on training and development in physical security operations while helping maintain a safe and secure work environment.

This is an on-site position working at our **Herndon, VA** office.

**What you will do:**

* Assist with monitoring and administering physical access control systems, badges, and employee access permissions
* Support visitor registration, verification, escort procedures, and visitor logs
* Assist with employee badge issuance, replacement, collection, and deactivation
* Help conduct routine security inspections and facility walkthroughs
* Support the maintenance of physical security documentation, logs, and records
* Assist with monitoring security alarms, cameras, and other security systems, as authorized
* Report suspicious activity, security incidents, and potential vulnerabilities to the appropriate security personnel
* Help ensure compliance with company security policies, procedures, and applicable government requirements
* Assist with security awareness activities and employee communications
* Support audits, inspections, and corrective action tracking related to physical security
* Learn emergency response procedures, incident reporting, and facility security protocols

**What you will need:**

* 1+ year of experience in security, customer service or an administrative support role
* Interest in physical security, security operations, or facilities management
* Strong attention to detail and organizational skills
* Effective written and verbal communication skills
* Ability to handle sensitive information responsibly and maintain confidentiality
* Willingness to learn security systems, policies, and procedures
* Ability to follow established instructions and escalate concerns appropriately
* Basic computer skills, including Microsoft Office applications

**What we would like:**

* Familiarity with access control systems, visitor management systems, or security procedures
* Experience working in a regulated or security-conscious environment

**Security clearance needed:**

* Eligibility and willingness to obtain a Top Secret government clearance
