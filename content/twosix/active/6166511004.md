At Two Six Technologies, we build, deploy, and implement innovative products that solve the world’s most complex challenges today. Through unrivaled collaboration and unwavering trust, we push the boundaries of what’s possible to empower our team and support our customers in building a safer global future.

Two Six Technologies is actively seeking a **Principal Cybersecurity Researcher** to join our team in Arlington, Virginia. The team is composed of intelligent individuals, passionate about reverse engineering, vulnerability research, and low-level programming. The team is growing and looking for someone with an advanced technical background that has an understanding on how to solve complex challenges and inspire creativity to address a wide range of difficult problems. If you are actively using reverse engineering and vulnerability research tools and frameworks, the team wants to talk to you!

**What you will do:**

* Lead the technical planning and execution of projects in reverse engineering and vulnerability research, including task coordination, deliverables, and alignment with customer objectives.
* Solve complex technical challenges requiring creativity and resourcefulness while providing technical guidance, mentorship, and training to project and functional staff.
* Author detailed technical documentation, proposal sections, and white papers to communicate research findings and technical solutions to internal teams, stakeholders, and external audiences.
* Provide direction and develop differentiated technical approaches to ensure customer satisfaction and support ongoing business development opportunities.

**What you will need (basic qualifications):**

* Demonstrated expertise in advanced cybersecurity research concepts typically acquired in a similar technical role.
* Expert-level proficiency in two or more of the following: low-level programming (C/C++, Assembly) and the use of industry-standard disassemblers and decompilers, binary re-hosting frameworks to enable dynamic analysis, and building custom greybox/blackbox fuzzing harnesses.
* Proven experience in vulnerability research, including attack surface triage, identifying vulnerabilities, and developing proof-of-concept exploits to demonstrate security impact.
* Extensive experience in technical mentorship and training, with a history of providing thorough solutions to complex problems and guiding less experienced team members.
* Experience leading complex technical projects in reverse engineering/vulnerability research or R&D for National Security customers.
* Experience delivering high‑quality technical documentation, such as vulnerability research and findings, industry white papers, and competitive proposals, that distills complex cybersecurity concepts for diverse audiences.
* Experience in a client-facing technical role, demonstrating strong communication and stakeholder management skills.

**Nice to have (preferred):**

* Master’s or PhD in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical specialty area.
* Experience leading technical sections for multi-million-dollar proposals or contributing white papers to support business capture efforts.
* Experience with embedded systems reverse engineering (e.g., ARM/MIPS architectures, RTOS, bare-metal environments) or cryptographic design principles.
* Experience with custom fuzzing harness development, binary re-hosting, or accelerated RE/VR tooling.
* Recognized participation in the external technical community (e.g., presentations, panels, or professional associations).

**Security Clearance:**

* Active Top Secret clearance and willingness to obtain and maintain an SCI clearance.
