### **ABOUT THE ROLE:**

In this role, you will apply your tax expertise to help train Grok to reason accurately through complex tax issues. You will evaluate sophisticated scenarios across individual, partnership, or corporate taxation and guide how the model interprets and applies statutory authority, Treasury Regulations, IRS guidance, and judicial precedent. This includes resolving ambiguous fact patterns, weighing alternative positions, and clearly articulating defensible tax treatments. Your contributions will directly influence the accuracy, consistency, and interpretive quality of Grok's tax conclusions.

### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Use proprietary software to provide labels, annotations, and inputs on tax-related projects.
* Choose problems in your area of tax expertise and provide rigorous solutions, explanations, and critiques of model responses.
* Deliver high-quality curated data that reflects accurate, well-supported application of tax law.
* Collaborate with technical staff to refine annotation guidelines and improve tools used for tax data.
* Interpret and follow evolving instructions while maintaining precision and adaptability.

### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 3+ years of professional experience in individual, partnership, or corporate taxation, gained through public accounting, national or regional tax practices, specialized advisory firms, or comparable industry roles.

### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* CPA license in good standing.
* Advanced experience in one or more of the following areas:
  + Corporate tax (e.g., mergers and acquisitions, international tax concepts)
  + Partnership tax (e.g., Subchapter K, allocations, basis, restructurings)
  + Individual tax (e.g., high-net-worth individuals, flow-through entities, planning considerations)
* Familiarity with financial reporting for income taxes (ASC 740) and the interaction between tax and financial reporting.
* Strong written communication in English, including experience drafting or reviewing technical tax memoranda, tax planning analyses, or tax controversy positions.
* Proficiency with authoritative tax sources and professional tax research platforms.
* Teaching, training, or mentoring experience in taxation topics, such as individual, partnership, or corporate tax, or tax research.
* Contributions to professional thought leadership, including publications, presentations, firm guidance, or externally published tax commentary.
