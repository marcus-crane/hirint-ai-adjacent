#### **SALES DIRECTOR, BRAZIL**

X is hiring a Sales Director to lead its advertising business in Brazil, one of Latin America's largest advertising markets. You will set the strategy for the Brazil team, own the country revenue number, and serve as the voice of X and its SI solutions, including Grok and Grok Bot, to agencies, brands, industry bodies, and the media. You will lead the Brazil sales team, work with cross-functional (XFN) partners, and serve on the Canada and Latin America (CanLATAM) leadership team.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Owns the Brazil advertising revenue target and the sales and partnerships strategy, and delivers accurate weekly, quarterly, and annual forecasts with Sales Leads, Sales Operations, and Revenue Finance.
* Leads and grows the Brazil sales team, sets a clear vision aligned with global strategy, and builds a high-performance, SI-first culture within the first 6–12 months.
* Builds an SI-led selling approach, using Grok, Grok Bot, and other X SI tools to sharpen account planning, pitches, forecasting, pipeline management, and reporting, and measures the impact on productivity and revenue.
* Builds senior relationships with agency CEOs and top-account CMOs to grow partnerships and advertising investment on X.
* Represents X as Country Managing Director with industry bodies such as IAB Brasil and MMA, the media, and Public Policy stakeholders, and leads a coordinated response to platform challenges in line with company guidelines.
* Serves as the escalation point for Sales, Sales Operations, and Revenue Finance, and removes blockers that affect revenue.

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 12+ years of experience in digital media or advertising sales.
* 7+ years of experience managing sales teams.
* Professional fluency in Portuguese and English.

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience in a senior sales leadership role in digital advertising, media, or technology.
* Track record of building and executing sales strategies that grew revenue.
* Experience leading cross-functional teams.
* Hands-on experience with SI tools, highly preferred Grok and Grok Bot, and with bringing them into a sales organization.
* Experience presenting to C-suite executives, agencies, industry bodies, and the media on SI in advertising.
* Network of brand, agency, and C-suite leaders in the Brazilian advertising market.
* Experience with accurate revenue forecasting and sales operations.
* Experience handling high-stakes escalations.
* Experience working with media, industry bodies, and public policy stakeholders.

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Must be based in, or willing to relocate to, São Paulo, Brazil.
