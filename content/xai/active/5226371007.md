### ABOUT THE ROLE:

We are seeking a Mechanical Commissioning Engineer to execute the buildout, startup, and handover of mechanical systems for SpaceXAI's high-performance data centers. This is a hands-on role where you will commission, test, troubleshoot, and turn over critical cooling, air handling, and fluid distribution systems that support next-generation AI supercomputing infrastructure.

You will work closely with construction, engineering, operations, EPC contractors, and OEM vendors to deliver safe, reliable, and high-quality mechanical systems. This is a full-time, onsite role with travel to SpaceXAI data center construction sites across the United States.

### RESPONSIBILITIES:

* Execute all mechanical commissioning activities for HVAC systems, chillers, cooling towers, CRAC/CRAH units, pumps, heat exchangers, piping systems, hydronic loops, and associated controls in hyperscale data center environments.
* Execute functional performance testing, airflow/water flow verification, system balancing, integrated system testing, and system turnover to operations.
* Troubleshoot and resolve complex mechanical, controls, and process issues during pre-commissioning, commissioning, and startup phases.
* Develop and review detailed commissioning test procedures, scripts, checklists, and documentation in alignment with project specifications, industry standards, and SpaceXAI requirements.
* Coordinate with EPC contractors, OEM vendors, design engineers, and SpaceXAI stakeholders for system energization, startup sequences, and integrated system validation.
* Champion safety by enforcing strict adherence to LOTO, NFPA, OSHA, and SpaceXAI safety protocols; participate in daily safety briefings and job hazard analyses.
* Maintain comprehensive as-built documentation, test records, issue logs, and final commissioning reports.
* Support and deliver training programs for operations and maintenance teams on commissioned systems.
* Participate in constructability reviews, design feedback sessions, and lessons-learned meetings to continuously improve installation quality and future project execution.
* Support commissioning schedules and punch-list resolution to meet aggressive project timelines.

### BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

* Bachelor’s degree in STEM degree or trade school certification in Mechanical Engineering Technology, HVAC, or a related field
* 1+ years of hands-on mechanical experience in HVAC, commissioning, testing, construction, or a related field (data centers, power plants, semiconductor facilities, commercial buildings, or other industrial environments).

### PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:

* Expertise in mechanical cooling and air-handling systems, including chillers, cooling towers, CRAC/CRAH units, precision air handling, chilled water plants, and/or high-density liquid cooling.
* Working knowledge of commissioning test equipment (manometers, anemometers, ultrasonic flow meters, pressure/temperature calibrators, power analyzers, etc.), or demonstrated aptitude to learn it in the field.
* Familiarity with relevant codes and standards (ASHRAE, SMACNA, NFPA, ASME, IMC, LEED, Uptime Institute).
* Ability to read, interpret, and redline mechanical drawings, P&IDs, control schematics, and sequence of operations.
* Experience coordinating with multi-discipline teams, contractors, and vendors in construction environments.
* Ability to work safely in industrial construction environments and follow LOTO, NFPA, OSHA, and SpaceXAI safety protocols.
* Commissioning (Cx) experience, including functional performance testing, airflow/water flow verification, system balancing, integrated system testing, or system turnover.
* Data center cooling systems and prior work on hyperscale or mission-critical projects (100+ MW scale a plus).
* NEBB, AABC, or equivalent Certification in Testing, Adjusting, and Balancing (TAB).
* Hands-on experience with BMS/BAS controls troubleshooting and integration (BACnet, Modbus, LON, etc.).
* Knowledge of refrigerant systems, direct liquid cooling, and advanced heat rejection technologies.
* Current OSHA 30 certification (or equivalent).
* Proficiency with commissioning management platforms (CxPro, Procore, PlanGrid, or similar).
* Technical degree or trade school certification in Mechanical Engineering Technology, HVAC, or a related field.
* Experience implementing Integrated Systems Testing (IST) and Owner’s Project Requirements (OPR) verification.

### ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:

* Willingness to travel extensively and work extended hours, nights, and weekends during critical commissioning and startup windows.
* Ability to travel extensively to SpaceXAI construction sites and remain onsite for extended periods during peak commissioning phases.
* Physical capability to work in industrial environments, climb ladders/stairs, lift moderate loads (up to 50 lbs), and wear full PPE for long durations.
* Valid driver’s license and ability to meet SpaceXAI's driver eligibility requirements.
