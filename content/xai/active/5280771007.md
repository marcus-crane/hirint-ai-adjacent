**CONSTRUCTION SUPERINTENDENT, FLUID/MECHANICAL**

Most construction jobs manage someone else’s crew on someone else’s schedule, then end when the project does. This one does not.

SpaceX builds its own launch sites, factories, test stands, and AI superclusters — and it plans to keep building. The rockets, the chips, and the missions only matter if the ground under them is real. Construction is how that ground gets built, in-house and to a standard the company can actually use. The work does not dry up between jobs. There is a next site, and one after that. You lead the crew that builds them.

You own a self-performing team. Foremen run their people day to day. You run the foremen. You are in the dirt, walking the work, setting the bar, and clearing whatever is in the way. When someone is ready for more, you say so. When the standard slips, you deal with it.

Scope includes, but not limited to industrial HVAC, pipefitting, pipe welding, chilled water, cooling towers, chillers, condensers, CRAC and CRAH units, and setting that heavy mechanical equipment. It also includes all plumbing: domestic water, sanitary, drain-waste-vent, and floor drains. It includes all fire protection: sprinkler fitting, suppression, and pre-action. This is the work that moves heat, water, and fire protection through the building.

The team goes where the next site is — a pad, a factory, a supercluster, whatever has to exist before the rest of the company can move. Right now that is Memphis, TN / Southaven, MS. Then it is the next one. Per diem comes with the assignment.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Directly oversee foremen and, through them, a large self-perform fluid/mechanical crew installing industrial HVAC, pipe, chilled water, cooling towers, chillers, condensers, CRAC and CRAH units, plumbing, and fire protection, including pipe welding and setting heavy mechanical equipment
* Set clear expectations for safety, quality, schedule, and conduct, and hold foremen and crews accountable when those expectations are missed
* Remove roadblocks so crews can keep working: materials, access, sequencing, coordination with other trades, and decisions
* Recommend promotions, role changes, and corrective action based on performance
* Plan and run the night-shift assignment, including extended hours, as part of a 24/7 operation
* Keep fluid and mechanical installations on SpaceXSI’s quality and reliability standards, including code compliance and system integrity required for data center cooling and fire protection
* Track daily progress and maintain logs, red-line drawings, and as-built documentation for mechanical, plumbing, and fire-protection systems
* Build and hold an aggressive construction schedule, on time and within budget
* Champion SpaceXSI’s safety program and a zero-incident culture around pressure piping, hot work, rigging and setting heavy mechanical equipment, and work at heights
* Resolve on-site issues — material delays, technical conflicts, sequencing, and trade coordination — with attention to system integrity and code compliance
* Inspect work for compliance with local regulations, mechanical, plumbing, and fire-protection codes, safety protocols, and SpaceXSI quality standards
* Report progress, fluid/mechanical construction metrics, and risks to senior leadership
* Relocate with the mobile team as assignments change, starting in Memphis, TN / Southaven, MS

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* High school diploma or equivalency certificate
* 3+ years leading fluid/mechanical construction teams, including supervision of foremen
* 3+ years of direct hands-on experience in fluid/mechanical installations, including industrial HVAC, pipefitting, chilled water, plumbing, or fire protection

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience leading large self-perform crews on industrial or mission-critical mechanical, piping, or fire-protection projects
* Direct hands-on experience on industrial processes or heavy mechanical projects
* Postsecondary diploma or degree in construction management, mechanical engineering, or a related field
* Track record on data centers, hyperscale facilities, or similar mission-critical mechanical construction
* Familiarity with Memphis-area building codes, IMC, IPC, NFPA 13, and industrial permitting
* Experience with Procore, AutoCAD, Bluebeam, MS Project, or similar tools on complex infrastructure work
* Strong grasp of mechanical critical path, sequencing, and coordination with electrical, structural, and civil trades through commissioning
* Understanding of data center cooling and fire-protection requirements, including chilled water, cooling towers, CRAC and CRAH units, and systems built for 24/7 operation
* History of leading diverse crews to a deadline without giving up collaboration or safety
* Working knowledge of lockout/tagout, hot work, pressure testing, and a record of building a safety culture on regulated sites
* Strong problem-solving and conflict resolution on technically complex mechanical, plumbing, and fire-protection work
* Proficiency with Microsoft Office (Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Teams, Project)
