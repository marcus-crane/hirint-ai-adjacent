### **ABOUT THE ROLE:**

The Power Generation team is seeking an Electrical Technician to operate and maintain plant electrical systems at SpaceX’s power generation facility. You will work hands-on across medium-voltage generation, distribution, and balance-of-plant electrical equipment up to 13.5 kV and 34.8 kV. This is an operations and maintenance role — you will switch, troubleshoot, repair, and keep electrical systems online in a high-reliability, fast-paced plant environment.

### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Perform operations and maintenance on plant electrical systems, including generators, transformers, switchgear, motor control centers, motors, VFDs, UPS units, automatic transfer switches, and emergency/black-start equipment, at voltages up to 13.5 kV and 34.8 kV.
* Execute switching, isolation, LOTO, racking of breakers, and restoration of medium-voltage equipment in accordance with plant procedures and NFPA 70E.
* Troubleshoot electrical faults on power distribution, protection, grounding, lighting, and auxiliary systems using one-line diagrams, schematics, and electrical test equipment.
* Perform preventive and predictive maintenance, inspections, and repairs to improve reliability and reduce unplanned downtime.
* Support plant startups, shutdowns, outages, and commissioning activities alongside operators, controls technicians, and engineers.
* Install, terminate, and maintain plant electrical equipment, cable, and control wiring as required for operations and maintenance work.
* Respond to electrical alarms, trips, and equipment failures; support root-cause investigations and implement corrective actions.
* Coordinate with operations, engineering, and utility personnel on switching, clearances, and electrical work that affects plant generation.
* Maintain accurate work orders, switching records, and as-found/as-left documentation.
* Follow all safety, LOTO, arc-flash, and PPE requirements while working in energized and hazardous plant environments.

### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* High school diploma or equivalency certificate
* 3+ years of experience as an electrical technician, industrial electrician, or similar role maintaining industrial or power generation electrical systems.

### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience operating and maintaining medium-voltage electrical systems (4.16 kV to 34.8 kV), including switchgear, transformers, and breakers.
* Experience in a power plant, generating station, or other high-reliability industrial facility.
* Ability to read and interpret single-line diagrams, electrical schematics, and equipment O&M manuals.
* Experience with LOTO, switching procedures, and NFPA 70E / arc-flash safety practices.
* Experience troubleshooting motors, VFDs, MCCs, UPS systems, and plant auxiliary electrical equipment.
* Familiarity with electrical test equipment (multimeters, insulation testers, infrared, and similar).
* Journeyman electrician license or equivalent formal electrical training.
* Experience using a CMMS or work-order system to plan and close maintenance work.
* Ability to work independently and as part of a multi-discipline operations and maintenance team.
* Strong written and verbal communication skills.

### **ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Willingness to perform hands-on work in all environments (heat, cold, rain), in tight quarters or at heights.
* Ability to work extended hours, nights, weekends, and rotating or on-call schedules to support plant operations.
* Ability to remain focused in hazardous environments while wearing appropriate PPE.
* Ability to lift up to 25 lbs. unassisted.
* Lifting, standing, climbing, bending, grasping, sitting, pulling, pushing, stooping, stretching, and carrying are generally required to perform the functions of this position.
* Must be able to work on-site at the power generation facility, with potential travel to other SpaceX sites.
