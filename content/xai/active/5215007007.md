### ABOUT THE ROLE:

As a Shipping Specialist supporting SpaceXAI data center operations, you will be responsible for the expedient, accurate movement of high-value materials and equipment (servers, GPUs, networking gear, power systems, cooling infrastructure, and other critical components) needed to support construction, commissioning, and ongoing operations. You will play a crucial role in ensuring efficient inbound and outbound logistics while maintaining product integrity, inventory accuracy, and compliance in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.

### RESPONSIBILITIES:

* Process inbound and outbound material through ERP/WMS systems, partnering with procurement, construction, facilities, and operations teams to ensure quality, quantity, and timely delivery of material to the floor or vendors
* Properly package, label, and manifest parcel, LTL, and large freight shipments (including high-value/sensitive equipment) to protect product integrity throughout the transportation cycle
* Manifest consignments and schedule movements with carriers (FedEx, UPS, DHL, 3PL, expedited courier, etc.) based on delivery demand, ensuring correct documentation and configuration
* Coordinate with carriers and freight forwarders for pick-up and delivery; prepare shipping labels, packing slips, commercial invoices, packing lists, and other required documents
* Verify accuracy of shipping information (addresses, quantities, delivery instructions) and inspect/package products for shipment
* Determine the most cost-effective and efficient shipping methods; track and monitor shipments to ensure on-time delivery, resolve delays/issues, and update tracking systems
* Perform cycle counts and maintain inventory accuracy in shipping-owned incoming/outgoing areas
* Lead problem-solving for transportation issues (routes, delays, customs, rush orders) and maintain system integrity for all shipments
* Operate forklifts and material handling equipment per safety procedures to load/unload carriers
* Evaluate hazardous or sensitive materials and apply appropriate packaging/transport methods based on SDS and specifications
* Coordinate with internal material handlers/drivers to expedite material and avoid cutoffs
* Maintain strong communication and customer relationships with internal requestors and destination teams; provide timely shipment updates
* Collaborate with the team to identify process improvements and recommend initiatives

### BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

* High school diploma or equivalency certificate
* 3+ years of shipping, logistics, warehousing, or materials management experience

### PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:

* Bachelor’s degree
* Experience in freight, expedited shipping, or high-volume manufacturing/technology/data center environments
* Experience with international shipping, export regulations, and customs documentation (where applicable)
* Strong knowledge of shipping procedures, regulations, and documentation
* Familiarity with shipping software (UPS WorldShip, FedEx Ship Manager, MyDHL, or similar) and ERP/WMS systems
* Excellent attention to detail, organizational skills, and ability to multitask/prioritize in a fast-paced environment
* Strong communication and problem-solving skills; ability to work independently and collaboratively
* Forklift or material handling equipment certification
* Hazmat awareness or relevant certification and knowledge of transport guidelines
* Strong computer skills (Microsoft Excel, etc.)

### ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:

* Must be open to working all required shift hours, including nights, weekends, overtime, and holidays as needed to support 24/7 operations
* Comfortable working in warehouse/data center environments with varying temperatures and noise
