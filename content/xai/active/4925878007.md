### **ABOUT THE ROLE:**

In this role, you will help enhance Grok by providing high-quality data annotations, labels, and inputs focused on applied mathematics. You will draw on your expertise in areas such as numerical analysis and scientific computing, ordinary and partial differential equations, probability and applied statistics, computational linear algebra, and mathematical physics or mathematical biology to evaluate model outputs, identify modeling errors or numerical issues, and produce clear, rigorous solutions and explanations. This role requires strong mathematical writing skills, attention to precision, and the ability to maintain logical consistency throughout complex mathematical arguments. Your work will help train Grok to distinguish flawed or inadequately justified approaches from solutions that are mathematically sound, accurately modeled, and practically applicable.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Evaluate model outputs, independently verify mathematical solutions, identify subtle errors in reasoning, modelling, or numerical computations, and produce clear, rigorous, and technically sound solutions and explanations.
* Use proprietary software applications to annotate and label data for designated projects.
* Support the delivery of high-quality, accurate, and well-curated datasets that meet project standards.
* Collaborate with technical teams to develop and train new tasks, contributing to the successful development and implementation of innovative technologies and initiatives.
* Work closely with technical staff to improve the design, functionality, and efficiency of annotation tools and workflows.
* Solve problems across applied mathematics domains aligned with your expertise, including probability, statistics, numerical analysis, optimization, operations research, dynamical systems, data modeling, and related fields. Develop detailed solutions and critically evaluate AI-generated responses.
* Interpret, analyze, and execute tasks accurately in accordance with established guidelines and project requirements.

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Meet one of the following criteria:

+ A master’s degree or PhD in mathematics, with a specialization in an applied mathematics subfield; or
+ A medal in the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) or a comparable high-level mathematics competition.

* English proficiency at CEFR C1 level or higher.

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Peer-reviewed publications in applied mathematics in reputable scientific journals.
* Previous experience in AI tutoring, AI model evaluation, or related applications of artificial intelligence.
* Teaching experience, particularly at the college or university level.
* Strong research skills, including the ability to navigate diverse information resources, academic databases, and technical literature.
* Excellent communication, interpersonal, analytical, and organizational skills.
* Ability to exercise sound independent judgment when working with limited information.
* A strong interest in technological advancement, innovation, and emerging technologies.
