### **ABOUT THE ROLE:**

In this role, you will help train and refine Grok's ability to interpret and generate video content with precision and technical depth. You will draw on your background in editing, motion graphics, and visual effects to evaluate and annotate video data that reflects how strong work is made. Your expertise will directly shape Grok's video capabilities, and your work will reach people around the world. This is a weekend-focused role with set working hours on Saturday and Sunday.

### RESPONSIBILITIES:

* Use proprietary software to provide labels, annotations, and inputs on projects involving video and multimedia elements.
* Critically analyze and clearly articulate what makes a video sequence work or fail at a technical level.
* Deliver high-quality curated data that captures the nuances of motion, timing, transitions, and visual effects.

### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 1+ years of professional experience (including freelance or contract work) in video editing, motion graphics, VFX, or post-production.
* Portfolio of video work, such as edited shorts, motion graphics, VFX breakdowns, or compositing reels (personal website, YouTube, Vimeo, X, ArtStation, or equivalent).

### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Strong skills in video editing, pacing, color grading, and narrative flow.
* Hands-on proficiency with tools such as Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, After Effects, Nuke, or similar.
* Experience with compositing, rotoscoping, motion tracking, or 3D integration (matchmoving, CG compositing).
* Familiarity with AI video generation tools (Grok Imagine, Runway, Kling, Sora, Veo, or similar).
* Scripting or automation experience in post-production (Python, plugins, or similar).
* Clear written and verbal communication in English, including the ability to explain technical and creative decisions.
