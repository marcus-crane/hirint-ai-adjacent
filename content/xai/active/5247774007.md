### ABOUT THE ROLE:

We are seeking a highly organized Growth Recruiting Coordinator to join SpaceXAI’s Human Data team and help scale hiring across our Human Data roles. You will support full-cycle recruiting through candidate communications, process coordination, and accurate pipeline management in a fast-paced environment. Ideal candidates are precise operators who communicate clearly, stay ahead of details, and take pride in delivering a strong candidate experience while helping the team move quickly.

### RESPONSIBILITIES:

* Partner with recruiters and hiring stakeholders to keep priority searches moving from outreach through close.
* Own candidate communications at key stages of the process, providing clear information on role details, next steps, and outstanding questions.
* Coordinate hiring workflows, approvals, and follow-ups so candidates and internal partners stay aligned.
* Create and maintain requisitions in the ATS, keeping stages, data, and process setup accurate for tracking and reporting.
* Monitor inbound candidate and stakeholder inquiries, routing complex cases as needed.
* Help improve recruiting operations by spotting process gaps and keeping documentation and workflows current

### BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

* Bachelor’s degree
* Experience in recruiting coordination, talent operations, or recruiting support in a fast-paced environment.

### PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:

* Strong written and verbal communication skills, with experience handling candidate correspondence and supporting a strong candidate experience from outreach through close.
* Experience coordinating across recruiters, hiring managers, and other internal partners.
* Experience using Greenhouse or another ATS to manage requisitions and workflows, keep pipeline data accurate, and support tracking and reporting.
* Experience supporting high-volume hiring in a startup or similarly fast-moving environment, with accuracy, follow-through, and tight attention to detail.
* Ability to operate independently in an ambiguous, fast-paced setting, with a proactive and adaptable mindset.
