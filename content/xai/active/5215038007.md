**ABOUT THE ROLE:**

As a Construction Electrician, you will install and terminate new electrical infrastructure: conduit, cable tray, underground duct bank, cable, wire, and 480 V building and feeder work. This is a build job, not an operating-plant job — pull, land, and energize new equipment from drawings, then turn it over. You will follow lockout/tagout and grounding procedures and support first energization of new 480 V systems. This position is based in Southaven, MS.

This is an hourly position with overtime. Expect 80% or more travel on a rotation, with per diem.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Install, terminate, and land new campus electrical equipment, including breakers, transformers, switches, buswork, control systems, and batteries at 480 V.
* Install conduit, cable tray, cable, wire, and terminations per issued-for-construction drawings; perform underground duct bank, trench, and cable-pull work.
* Perform installation quality checks and electrical testing (voltmeters, ohmmeters, ammeters, insulation megger) and assist with acceptance and commissioning tests on new gear.
* Read and interpret construction drawings, one-lines, schematics, wiring diagrams, and work orders.
* Follow lockout/tagout (LOTO), grounding, clearance procedures, NFPA 70E, and PPE requirements on an active construction site.
* Uncrate, set, and dress equipment; climb structures; operate hand and power tools, pulls, blowers, pumps, and portable generators.
* Support construction energization sequences, punch-list closeout, and turnover to operations with engineers and vendors.
* Document work performed, test results, and as-built markups.
* Operate company vehicles and specialized equipment; inspect and store personal protective equipment (PPE).
* Perform similar or less skilled incidental duties as assigned.

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* High school diploma or equivalency certificate.
* Current state journeyman electrician license (Tennessee or Mississippi preferred, or equivalent state journeyman license with the ability to obtain TN or MS).
* 5+ years of industrial or data center electrical construction installing conduit, tray, duct bank, cable, and 480 V terminations.
* Ability to read construction drawings, one-lines, manuals, and work packages.
* Proficiency with common electrical test tools (multimeter, megger, ammeter) and construction hand and power tools.
* Working knowledge of NFPA 70E and lockout/tagout (LOTO).
* Ability to lift 50+ lbs, work at heights and in confined spaces, and work outdoors in all weather.
* Hourly position with overtime; willingness to work nights, weekends, and extended shifts during construction and energization.
* Ability and willingness to travel 80% or more on a construction rotation; per diem and/or housing provided while on site.

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Tennessee or Mississippi journeyman electrician license.
* Industrial, manufacturing, or data center construction experience building raceway and cable plant.
* Experience on large-scale, multi-site construction projects that required a travel rotation.
* OSHA 30 or equivalent construction safety training in addition to NFPA 70E.
* Knowledge of print reading for drawings, manuals, and one-lines.
