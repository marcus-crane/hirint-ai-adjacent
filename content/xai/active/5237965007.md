### ABOUT THE ROLE:

As a Commercial Counsel at SpaceXAI, you'll drive our commercial contracting efforts by negotiating high-value enterprise agreements and commercial contracts - primarily enterprise customer agreements for AI products and services but also for strategic partnerships, hyperscaler agreements, other technology deals, vendor agreements, and anything else in the universe! Working closely with Sales, Business Development, Finance and Product teams, you’ll tackle complex agreements, analyze and mitigate risks, ensure compliance, and streamline processes in a fast-paced environment. You’ll tackle frontier problems no other role will match, advancing SpaceXAI’s mission to advance AI-driven human scientific discovery. This role is full-time on-site and based in Palo Alto, Los Angeles, Austin, or New York.

### RESPONSIBILITIES:

* Expertly draft, review, and negotiate complex technology agreements.
* Collaborate closely with technical, product, sales, finance, and legal team members to establish commercial legal strategies that align with business objectives.
* Design legal structures and operations that make it seamless to sell xAI’s incredible products while appropriately mitigating risks.
* Be a Swiss Army knife: pitch in with precision for anything needed in the legal department, even if it is unfamiliar.
* Legal AI ambassador: daily use of our products to accelerate learning and optimize productivity.

### BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

* 8+ years of legal experience, ideally a mix of top-tier law firm and inhouse technology company experience.
* Extensive transaction negotiation experience. Intellectual property and technology transactions experience a strong plus.
* Ability to balance risks to tackle legal issues with a practical, commercial-driven approach.
* Creative solutioning mindset. Ability to problem solve with sound judgment when navigating complex and ambiguous situations. You find workable paths, not just problems.
* Genuine curiosity and passion for AI application in everyday life, a love for being continually challenged,
* Strong sense of ownership and getting things done.
* Tenacity, adaptability, and the stamina to work hard in a fast-paced environment. Lots of gas in the tank.Lots of gas in the tank!
* JD and license to practice law in California or New York, or otherwise eligible to register as in-house counsel.
* Commitment to being in-office 5 days/week.
