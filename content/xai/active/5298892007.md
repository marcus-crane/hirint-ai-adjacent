#### **AI TUTOR, CHEMISTRY - TEMPORARY**

In this role, you will help enhance Grok by providing high-quality data annotations, labels, and inputs focused on chemistry. You will draw on your expertise in areas such as organic, inorganic, physical, and analytical chemistry, biochemistry, computational chemistry, and materials chemistry to select challenging problems, evaluate model outputs, identify errors in chemical reasoning, and produce clear, rigorous solutions and explanations. Your work will help train Grok to distinguish flawed or unsupported reasoning from solutions that are chemically sound, logically rigorous, and scientifically accurate.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Develop and evaluate high-quality scientific training and evaluation datasets using proprietary AI development platforms, applying advanced chemistry expertise to assess model reasoning, factual accuracy, and scientific problem-solving capabilities.
* Play a pivotal role in supporting and contributing to the training of new tasks, working closely with the technical staff to ensure the successful development and implementation of cutting-edge initiatives/technologies.
* Collaborate with the technical staff to enhance the design of efficient annotation tools.
* Design, solve, and critically evaluate complex chemistry problems spanning advanced graduate-level concepts and emerging research areas, identifying limitations in AI model reasoning and developing scientifically rigorous evaluations to advance model capabilities.
* Choose problems from fields across chemistry that align with your expertise in specialized subdomains, including, but not limited to, organic chemistry, physical chemistry, analytical chemistry, inorganic chemistry, computational chemistry, polymer chemistry, nuclear chemistry, or medicinal chemistry, where you can confidently provide detailed solutions and evaluate model responses.
* Regularly interpret, analyze, and execute tasks based on given instructions.
* Operate in a fast-paced environment with rapidly changing priorities.

#### **POSITION DETAILS:**

This is a **temporary** position with SpaceXSI, for the length of time as stated, and is subject to change with minimal notice as business needs dictate.

* LENGTH OF POSITION: 6 months (extensions or conversion to a permanent role may be available based on business needs).
* LOCATION: Remote (United States, United Kingdom, Canada, India, and Australia); for US-based candidates, please note that we are unable to hire in Wyoming and Illinois at this time.

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* PhD in a chemistry-related field; OR a master's degree with 3+ years of experience in one or more of the following areas: research/laboratory, policy/regulatory, strategy and management (in STEM), editorial/scientific writing, scientific communications, scientific instrumentation.
* English proficiency at CEFR C1 level or higher.

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Expertise in chemistry-related subdomains including, but not limited to organic chemistry, physical chemistry, analytical chemistry, inorganic chemistry, organometallic chemistry, biochemistry, nuclear chemistry, computational/theoretical chemistry, polymer/materials chemistry, medicinal chemistry, catalysis, surface/colloid chemistry, or drug discovery.
* Strong ability to navigate various information resources, databases, and online resources.
* Strong passion for and commitment to technological advancements and innovation.
* Advanced expertise in chemistry, demonstrated by multiple publications, including at least one first-author paper, in reputable peer-reviewed journals.
* Relevant industry experience in chemistry-related fields such as drug development, consulting, biotechnology, energy (esp. oil/gas), materials/polymer science, chemical manufacturing/regulation, or computational chemistry.
* Prior data annotation or teaching experience (as a professor, teacher, or tutor).
* Patents or other clear evidence of technological innovation.
* Experience in academic, technical or professional writing, or journalism.
