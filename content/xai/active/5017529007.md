#### **AI TUTOR, WRITING - TEMPORARY**

In this role, you will evaluate, refine, and create expert-level writing across a variety of genres and formats to advance Grok's writing capabilities. Drawing on your expertise in a specific writing specialty, from fiction and poetry to technical, academic, and marketing writing, you will help Grok produce work with greater clarity, craft, and attention to audience. Your expertise will directly shape how Grok writes, and your work will reach people around the world.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Use proprietary software to label, annotate, and refine AI-generated text for clarity, structure, engagement, and domain-specific accuracy
* Apply your writing specialty (e.g., creative fiction, game writing, technical writing) to deliver high-quality curated data that improves Grok's writing, both generally and for specific audiences and use cases
* Collaborate with technical staff and other specialists to refine annotation guidelines, tools, and workflows
* Support other writing and non-writing projects as priorities shift

#### **POSITION DETAILS:**

This is a **temporary** position with SpaceXSI, for the length of time as stated, and is subject to change with minimal notice as business needs dictate

* LENGTH OF POSITION: 6 months (extensions or conversion to a permanent role may be available based on business needs)
* LOCATION: Remote (United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia); for US-based candidates, please note that we are unable to hire in Wyoming and Illinois at this time

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Native or CEFR C2 proficiency in English
* 5+ years of relevant professional writing experience in at least one specialty (creative fiction, game writing, technical writing, nonfiction, journalistic writing, copywriting, web writing, grant writing, academic writing, or poetry), or equivalent demonstrated impact through an externally verifiable portfolio, published work, professional credits, or measurable results.

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Strong, publicly verifiable track record (e.g., published books or substantial short-form work, notable game credits, high-impact technical documentation, bylines in reputable outlets, successful grant awards, peer-reviewed publications, or recognized poetry collections)
* Exceptional grammar, spelling, and editorial skills, with experience using reference materials and style guides
* Ability to teach or critique writing in a collaborative environment on both technical and non-technical levels
* Familiarity with AI tools or prompt engineering
* Comfort with Google Workspace, Slack, Notion, and other remote work and collaboration tools
* Specialty-specific strengths (examples):
  + Creative fiction: Novel deals, substantial sales, awards (Hugo/Nebula or comparable), or critical recognition
  + Game writing: Published credits in notable games with strong narrative design
  + Technical writing: High-impact documentation with measurable outcomes
  + Journalistic/copy/web: Bylines or campaigns with quantifiable results
  + Academic/grant: Peer-reviewed work, citations, or successful funding track record
  + Poetry: Publications in reputable journals or collections demonstrating formal range
