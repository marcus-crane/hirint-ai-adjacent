**ABOUT THE ROLE:**

In this role, you will evaluate, refine, and create expert-level writing across a variety of genres and formats to advance Grok's writing capabilities. Drawing on your expertise in a specific writing specialty, from fiction and poetry to technical, academic, and marketing writing, you will help Grok produce work with greater clarity, craft, and attention to audience. Your expertise will directly shape how Grok writes, and your work will reach people around the world.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Use proprietary software to label, annotate, and refine AI-generated text for clarity, structure, engagement, and domain-specific accuracy.
* Apply your writing specialty (e.g., creative fiction, game writing, technical writing) to deliver high-quality curated data that improves Grok's writing, both generally and for specific audiences and use cases.
* Collaborate with technical staff and other specialists to refine annotation guidelines, tools, and workflows.
* Support other writing and non-writing projects as priorities shift.

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Native or CEFR C2 proficiency in English.
* Meets the qualifications for at least one writing specialty listed below. Applicants will be asked to provide verifiable proof of specialty-specific qualifications.
* **Creative Fiction Writing** (at least two of the following):
  + Verified novel publishing deals with major houses (e.g., Big Five).
  + Novel sales of more than 50,000 units (excluding free promotions).
  + 10 or more short stories in major outlets (e.g., The New Yorker, Clarkesworld).
  + Major award recognition (e.g., Hugo or Nebula finalist, or comparable).
  + Critical acclaim (e.g., starred reviews in Kirkus or Publishers Weekly, or features in Library Journal or the New York Times Book Review).
* **Game Writing** (all of the following):
  + At least 5 years of professional game writing experience.
  + Public portfolio showcasing branching narratives, character development, dialogue trees, and measurable player impact (e.g., reviews or engagement metrics).
  + Published credits in notable games, demonstrating expertise in narrative design, lore creation, and integration with gameplay mechanics.
* **Technical Writing** (all of the following):
  + At least 5 years of technical writing experience, focusing on user manuals, API documentation, software guides, or technical reports for tech, engineering, or scientific fields.
  + Public portfolio of high-impact examples (e.g., documentation that reduced support tickets by 20% or more, improved user adoption, or enhanced compliance rates).
  + Certified Professional Technical Communicator (CPTC) certification or equivalent.
  + Bachelor's degree or higher in Technical Communication, English, Computer Science, or a related field.
* **Nonfiction Writing** (all of the following):
  + At least 5 years of nonfiction writing experience (memoirs, biographies, historical accounts), with 3 or more published books showing commercial or critical success.
  + Public portfolio of samples or manuscripts demonstrating structure, prose, and nonfiction standards.
  + Awards, funding success, or membership in organizations such as BIO or ASJA.
* **Journalistic Writing** (all of the following):
  + At least 5 years of professional journalism experience, reporting for reputable outlets.
  + Experience in investigative journalism, feature writing, or specialized beats.
  + Public portfolio of published work with bylines in major outlets (e.g., NYT, BBC), with awards, citations, or engagement metrics.
  + Bachelor's degree or higher in Journalism, Communications, English, or a related field.
* **Copywriting** (all of the following):
  + At least 5 years of copywriting or marketing writing experience, with high-impact campaigns for brands, agencies, or in-house teams.
  + Experience across ad copy, email sequences, landing pages, and social media.
  + Experience as a Content Strategist, Copy Chief, or Marketing Writer.
  + Public portfolio with quantified impact (e.g., conversion increases of 30% or more, or engagement or revenue growth).
  + Bachelor's degree or higher in Marketing, Communications, English, or a related field.
* **Web Writing** (all of the following):
  + At least 5 years of professional experience in web content creation, digital marketing, or related roles.
  + SEO-optimized writing with demonstrated increases in organic traffic and engagement.
  + Experience managing full content lifecycles (research, writing, editing, and publishing).
  + Public portfolio of high-impact work (e.g., viral articles, conversion-focused landing pages, or B2B whitepapers with quantifiable growth).
  + Certifications in digital marketing tools (e.g., Google Analytics, SEMrush).
  + Bachelor's degree or higher in Journalism, Communications, English, Marketing, or a related field.
* **Grant Writing** (all of the following):
  + At least 5 years of grant writing experience, with more than $1M secured across multiple grants and a win rate of 50% or higher.
  + Experience researching funding opportunities and interpreting grant guidelines from a variety of funders.
  + Samples of narratives, budgets, and evaluation plans that show persuasive writing, logical structure, and alignment with funder priorities.
  + Bachelor's degree or higher in Communications, Public Policy, Nonprofit Management, or a related field.
* **Academic Writing** (all of the following):
  + At least 5 years of academic writing experience, including peer-reviewed publications, theses, dissertations, or scholarly books.
  + Articles in top-tier journals, books with reputable presses, more than 500 citations on Google Scholar, or an h-index of 10 or higher.
  + Public portfolio of samples (abstracts, full papers, or proposals) demonstrating rigorous argumentation, citation styles (APA, MLA, Chicago), and original contributions.
  + PhD in a relevant field.
* **Poetry Writing** (all of the following):
  + Advanced degree in creative writing, literature, poetry, or a related field.
  + Publications in reputable literary journals, anthologies, or poetry collections that demonstrate mastery of a wide range of traditional forms, from haiku to sonnet to villanelle.
  + History of awards, fellowships, or residencies from recognized organizations.
  + One or more of the following: published collections with major presses, poems in outlets such as Poetry Magazine, awards such as the National Book Award for Poetry, or fellowships from the NEA or Guggenheim.
  + Note: If your publication record is primarily free verse, please do not apply. This specialty requires a deep understanding of classical forms and poetic techniques.
