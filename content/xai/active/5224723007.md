### ABOUT THE ROLE:

The fire protection technician provides design support and subject matter expertise in the following areas: fire protection, life safety, building and fire codes, fire hazard and risk assessment of materials and processes, fire and smoke spread, fire detection, fire detection, alarm and sprinkler system design, industrial fire hazards, fire investigation support, and other fire and life safety issues. 

### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Support engineering design, analysis, calculations and specifications for facility fire and life safety systems
* Verify and sustain building adherence to current fire codes and the operational integrity of fire life safety systems
* Develop AM&M (Alternate Means & Methods) to local AHJ (Authority Having Jurisdiction) to help with performance-based design criteria to meet intent of code
* Manage the commissioning phase of fire life safety projects, from initial planning and risk assessments through to post-commissioning monitoring and adjustments
* Conduct routine inspections, audits, and testing of fire life safety systems (e.g., alarms, sprinklers, extinguishers) to identify and resolve deficiencies proactively
* Collaborate with facilities teams, contractors, and external authorities (e.g., fire marshals) to coordinate system installations, upgrades, and certifications
* Prepare and maintain detailed documentation, reports, and records of inspections, compliance activities, and corrective actions to support regulatory audits

### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* High school diploma or equivalency certificate
* 2+ years of experience in the design and construction of fire protection systems and/or fire protection consulting

### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Working knowledge of Building & Fire Codes and NFPA Codes & Standards
* Degree in fire protection engineering
* Professional Engineer License (P.E., passed the Principles of Engineering exam)
* NICET certification in relevant fire protection programs
* Ability to lead project design teams
* Knowledgeable in OSHA compliance
* Fire & Smoke software modeling capabilities
* Construction Administration experience
* Proficient in Autodesk Revit

### **ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Willing to work extended hours as needed
* Willing to travel for specific project needs
