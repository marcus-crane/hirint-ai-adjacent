SpaceXSI is hiring a Fleet Maintenance Supervisor for our AI supercompute facilities in Memphis. You will own fleet maintenance for automotive assets, heavy transit vehicles, trailers, UTVs, and industrial equipment. You will run the work-order schedule, cover emergency breakdowns, and occasionally do the work alongside your technicians. 

This role requires 24/7 on-call coverage for critical breakdowns, and may include nights, weekends, and long hours with limited notice.   
   
**RESPONSIBILITIES:** 

* Lead technicians through daily repairs, preventative maintenance, and continuous improvement projects, to increase uptime and ensure maximum asset availability across our Memphis facilities
* Set the weekly plan, assign work, and triage requests from internal customers by priority and available resources.
* Source third-party vendors for parts acquisition, documentation, training, and on-site repairs as needed
* Be the subject matter expert for all things related to the fleet
* Provide developmental leadership to technicians, facilitating training and advancement of skills
* Track team utilization and the maintenance KPIs used to improve uptime
* Facilitate all Title and Registration needs for the fleet
* Oversee the lifecycle of all fleet assets from acquisition through disposition
* Perform other related duties, as assigned, to ensure smooth operation of the fleet maintenance team

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:** 

* High school diploma or equivalent.
* Functional knowledge of mechanical, electrical, hydraulic and pneumatic systems
* 4+ years maintaining or repairing vehicles, fleet equipment, or industrial equipment.
* 2+ years leading people, including assigning work and running maintenance jobs to completion.

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:** 

* Bachelor’s degree in an engineering discipline or completion of a related trade school
* ASE/OEM certifications
* Proficiency in Microsoft applications such as Excel, Outlook, Word, and PowerPoint
* Experience in a CMMS or other asset-management system
* Experience managing a maintenance budget, third-party vendors, and service contracts
* Ability to work independently with little direction
* Ability to meet deadlines in a fast-paced, ever-changing priorities environment
* Ability to anticipate needs and develop innovative solutions
* Ability to effectively communicate (verbal and written) with engineering, production, build, and support teams

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:** 

* Ability to lift 50 lbs. unassisted, lifting, standing, climbing, bending, grasping, sitting, pulling, pushing, stooping, stretching, and carrying are generally required to perform the functions of this position
* Must be willing to work at elevated heights and in tight spaces
* Must be willing to work long hours, nights, and weekends with limited notice to meet the business needs
* Provide 24/7 on-call support for all critical activities and emergency situations
* Willingness to travel to support the business
