**CONSTRUCTION SUPERINTENDENT, STRUCTURAL**  
  
Most construction jobs manage someone else’s crew on someone else’s schedule, then end when the project does. This one does not.  
  
SpaceX builds its own launch sites, factories, test stands, and AI superclusters — and it plans to keep building. The rockets, the chips, and the missions only matter if the ground under them is real. Construction is how that ground gets built, in-house and to a standard the company can actually use. The work does not dry up between jobs. There is a next site, and one after that. You lead the crew that builds them.You own a self-performing team. Foremen run their people day to day. You run the foremen. You are in the dirt, walking the work, setting the bar, and clearing whatever is in the way. When someone is ready for more, you say so. When the standard slips, you deal with it.  
  
Scope includes, but not limited to structural steel, ironwork, metal deck, framing, elevated slabs, flooring systems, roofing, carpentry, and general construction of the building itself: the metal skeleton, the floors, the roof, and the data halls. Crane and rigging work is in scope when it is raising the structure. Welding is in scope when it is steel structure.  
  
The team goes where the next site is — a pad, a factory, a supercluster, whatever has to exist before the rest of the company can move. Right now that is Memphis, TN / Southaven, MS. Then it is the next one. Per diem comes with the assignment.  
  
**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Directly oversee foremen and, through them, a large self-perform structural crew installing structural steel, ironwork, metal deck, framing, elevated slabs, flooring systems, roofing, and the general structure of the building, including crane and rigging work that raises the structure and welding of steel structure
* Set clear expectations for safety, quality, schedule, and conduct, and hold foremen and crews accountable when those expectations are missed
* Remove roadblocks so crews can keep working: materials, access, sequencing, coordination with other trades, and decisions
* Recommend promotions, role changes, and corrective action based on performance
* Plan and run the night-shift assignment, including extended hours, as part of a 24/7 operation
* Keep structural installations on SpaceXSI’s quality and reliability standards, including code compliance and structural integrity required for data center operation
* Track daily progress and maintain logs, red-line drawings, and as-built documentation for the building structure
* Build and hold an aggressive construction schedule, on time and within budget
* Champion SpaceXSI’s safety program and a zero-incident culture around steel erection, crane and rigging work, working at heights, welding, and heavy structural work
* Resolve on-site issues — material delays, technical conflicts, sequencing, and trade coordination — with attention to structural integrity and code compliance
* Inspect work for compliance with local regulations, building codes, safety protocols, and SpaceXSI quality standards
* Report progress, structural construction metrics, and risks to senior leadership
* Relocate with the mobile team as assignments change, starting in Memphis, TN / Southaven, MS

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* High school diploma or equivalency certificate
* 3+ years leading structural construction teams, including supervision of foremen
* 3+ years of direct hands-on experience in structural installations, including structural steel, ironwork, metal deck, framing, elevated slabs, flooring systems, roofing, or general building construction

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience leading large self-perform crews on industrial or mission-critical structural projects
* Direct hands-on experience on industrial processes or heavy structural projects
* Postsecondary diploma or degree in construction management, structural or civil engineering, or a related field
* Track record on data centers, hyperscale facilities, or similar mission-critical structural construction
* Familiarity with Memphis-area building codes, IBC, AISC, OSHA steel erection standards, and industrial permitting
* Experience with Procore, AutoCAD, Bluebeam, MS Project, or similar tools on complex infrastructure work
* Strong grasp of structural critical path, sequencing, and coordination with electrical, mechanical, and civil trades through commissioning
* Understanding of data center structural requirements, including steel frame, elevated slabs, flooring systems, roofing, and load paths built for heavy equipment and 24/7 operation
* History of leading diverse crews to a deadline without giving up collaboration or safety
* Working knowledge of fall protection, crane and rigging, and structural welding safety, and a record of building a safety culture on regulated sites
* Strong problem-solving and conflict resolution on technically complex structural work
* Proficiency with Microsoft Office (Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Teams, Project)
