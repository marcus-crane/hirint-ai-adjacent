### **ABOUT THE ROLE:**

Starlink, our revolutionary satellite constellation, is delivering low-latency broadband internet worldwide. As a Telecommunications Counsel, you will help the business understand and navigate applicable laws and regulations in Europe and Africa.

### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Advise the Starlink team on the laws, regulations, and policies that impact our business in Europe and parts of Africa, particularly laws relating to telecommunications and internet services
* Analyze legal and regulatory risks and communicate them to the business and technical teams to enable sound compliance decisions and to support telecommunications and other business licensing in Europe and Africa
* Monitor legal and policy developments related to the Starlink business
* Interface with governmental agencies periodically concerning regulatory, consumer protection and law enforcement matters

### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 5+ years of professional experience with regulatory compliance matters in the telecommunications industry related to broadband and/or satellite services
* Juris Doctor, Bachelor of Laws or equivalent degree from an accredited institution
* Active license to practice law in at least one jurisdiction

### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Fluency in French and/or Portuguese
* Knowledge of telecommunications business and regulatory issues outside of the U.S.
* Prior experience managing legal issues for a high-growth multinational company

### **ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* This position is based in London, England and requires being onsite - remote and hybrid work will not be considered
* Must be available to work extended hours and weekends as needed
