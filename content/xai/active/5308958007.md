#### **PRINCIPAL SOFTWARE ENGINEER, X CHAT**

We’re building X Chat into a secure, real-time collaboration platform (channels, threads, DMs, presence, search, and integrations) used by hundreds of millions of people. A core requirement is end-to-end encryption with multi-device support, forward secrecy, and strong performance at scale.

You’ll own the messaging systems that deliver low-latency encrypted chat while integrating calling and live media, and you’ll ship privacy-preserving AI features without compromising E2EE.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Lead implementation of core chat features (channels, threads, DMs, presence, search, notifications, media/file sharing)
* Implement and operationalize end-to-end encryption for messaging (key exchange, multi-device support, performance)
* Own delivery of backend services and cross-platform clients (iOS, Android, web)
* Integrate calling and live media experiences from the Voice & Media stack into chat
* Ship privacy-preserving AI features (summaries, translation, etc.) that respect E2EE
* Drive reliability, latency, and operational metrics for assigned workstreams

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 6+ years building distributed systems or large-scale product features
* Experience with real-time messaging or encrypted communication systems

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Hands-on E2EE messaging implementation
* Experience shipping features in Slack-like or similar collaboration products
* Strong backend (Rust/Go/C++) and/or mobile client skills
* Prior technical leadership of workstreams or small teams
