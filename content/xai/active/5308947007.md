#### **LEAD SOFTWARE ENGINEER, X CHAT**

We’re building X Chat into a secure, real-time collaboration platform (channels, threads, DMs, presence, search, and integrations) used by hundreds of millions of people. A core requirement is end-to-end encryption with multi-device support, forward secrecy, and strong performance at scale.

You’ll own the messaging systems that deliver low-latency encrypted chat while integrating calling and live media, and you’ll ship privacy-preserving AI features without compromising E2EE.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Architect X Chat as a real-time collaboration system (channels, threads, DMs, presence, search, notifications, integrations)
* Own end-to-end encryption design and implementation (key management, forward secrecy, multi-device, sealed sender)
* Set technical direction for messaging delivery, storage, client sync, and protocol trade-offs between security, latency, and scale
* Drive integration points with Voice & Media systems for calls and live media inside chat
* Define privacy-preserving AI feature boundaries (summarization, translation, etc.) within E2EE constraints
* Mentor engineers and own critical system designs and reviews

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 8+ years building large-scale distributed systems
* Production experience designing or operating end-to-end encrypted messaging systems

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Deep knowledge of Signal Protocol, MLS, or equivalent
* Experience with Slack-scale or Discord-scale collaboration systems
* Strong background in real-time pub/sub, fan-out, and multi-client sync
* Track record of setting architecture and leading multi-team technical initiatives
