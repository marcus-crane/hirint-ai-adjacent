### ABOUT THE ROLE:

SpaceXSI operates multi-campus, hyperscale GPU infrastructure where cluster availability directly determines training throughput, customer handover commitments, and capital efficiency of the fleet. The Manager of Supercomputer Site Reliability Engineering will be responsible for both compute and infrastructure performance, and will be responsible for significantly increasing the SRE team size. On the compute side, focus will be put on continuous site monitoring, cross-discipline incident command, deep hardware root-cause analysis (RCA), and emphasis on software systems that orchestrate repair velocity. Additional focus will be on physical infrastructure reliability as well. Understanding system margins and resiliency will be key.

Site Reliability engineering manager owns >99% compute uptime outcome, drives unplanned facilities compute losses to 0, ensures efficient allocation of > 3GW site power, upgrade robust fault-free megapack failover systems capable of supporting all halls for >4 hours (160 MWhr per hall), drives robust hardware inventory systems and repair velocity to eliminate hardware chokepoints.
