### ABOUT THE ROLE:

Join the High Power Engineering team supporting SpaceXAI to design, build, operate, and maintain electrical infrastructure for AI training campuses. As a Design Engineer on the Data Center team, you will work with a small on-site team to scale high-capacity power systems on a schedule set by compute demand. The position is based in Memphis, TN, with potential travel to other sites.

### RESPONSIBILITIES:

* Produce and maintain single-line diagrams, three-lines, panel schedules, cable schedules, conduit and raceway layouts, grounding plans, and equipment elevations.
* Perform load calculations, voltage drop, cable ampacity and derating, conduit fill, and pull-tension analysis including long underground runs.
* Size MV/LV transformers, switchgear, busway, and feeders; specify impedance, BIL, temperature rise, and short-time ratings.
* Write equipment specifications and technical bid evaluations for ring-main units, transformers, switchgear, busway, and PDUs.
* Supply model inputs to protection and power-system studies: nameplate data, impedances, cable constants, and as-designed configurations.
* Resolve field RFIs and issue design changes during construction.
* Maintain organized design and asset documentation for Data Center electrical packages.
* Ensure compliance with NFPA 70, 70B, 70E, NESC, and IEEE standards, prioritizing safety and engineering excellence.

### BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

* Bachelor's degree in electrical engineering, computer engineering, physics, or another engineering discipline.
* 3+ years of electrical design experience in data center, industrial, or utility facilities.

### PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:

* Experience producing issued-for-construction drawing sets in AutoCAD, AutoCAD Electrical, or Revit.
* Working knowledge of NEC Articles 110, 215, 240, 250, 310, and 450.
* Medium-voltage cable system design experience.
* Experience with power systems analysis software and tools: ETAP, SKM, EasyPower, Matlab, PSCAD, etc.
* Revit or BIM coordination experience.
* Engineer in Training certification, or on track for a Professional Engineer license.
* Experience on a repeated, multi-phase build rather than one-off projects.
* Demonstrated initiative, creative problem-solving, and strong analytical/engineering fundamentals.

### **ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS**

* Perform hands-on work in all environments (heat, cold, rain), in tight quarters or at heights.
* Work extended hours and weekends as needed to support construction, energization, and operations.
* Remain focused in hazardous environments while wearing appropriate PPE.
* Lift up to 25 lbs. unassisted.
* Provide 24/7 on-call support for emergencies.
* Willing to work on-site in Memphis, TN. This is not a remote or hybrid role.
* Travel up to 25% for factory witness testing, vendor and OEM engagement, and supporting other sites.
* Valid driver's license.
