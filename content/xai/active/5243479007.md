### ABOUT THE ROLE:

SpaceXAI serves our community of users and customers by working tirelessly to preserve free expression and choice, create limitless interactivity, and create a marketplace that enables the economic success of all its participants.

At SpaceXAI, we now have a substantial opportunity to change how marketers interact with our rapidly growing user base. X is creating a world-class team of media professionals, and seeking an experienced Client Account Manager to grow revenue with advertisers and their agencies.

### RESPONSIBILITIES:

As a Client Account Manager, you will become a true X expert and use that to simplify the platform and ship strong campaigns for priority clients from Greater China and the wider APAC region.

* Educate and consult on X advertising products, best practices, and campaign design
* Execute, optimize, and analyze programs using internal tools and dashboards
* Monitor revenue and pace client campaigns against targets
* Partner with the Client Partner on retention, pitch support, and performance reporting
* Resolve campaign issues quickly and improve the process behind them
* Feed product and tools teams so the ads platform can scale
* Maintain the book and a high-quality client and agency experience
* Mentor new team members as the organization grows

### BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

* Fluency in English and Mandarin (written and spoken) for daily work with Greater China clients, agencies, and internal stakeholders
* 3+ years managing digital marketing programs for advertisers or agencies
* Strong grasp of the digital marketplace and competitive landscape
* Proven ability to read performance and change the levers that move results
* Able to hold large client relationships, work through conflict, and get to a decision
* Clear communication and presentation skills; attention to detail
* Preference for small, collaborative teams with limited supervision
* Willingness to travel across APAC
* BA/BS or equivalent experience
