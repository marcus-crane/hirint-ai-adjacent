### ABOUT THE ROLE:

As a backend engineer, you’ll design and build scalable, high-performance systems that power subscriptions infrastructure, new monetized products, user growth, and creator monetization. You’ll work on mission-critical infrastructure that processes millions of transactions, optimizes user acquisition and retention, and enables creators to earn a living through their content. This is a unique opportunity to make a large-scale revenue impact while creating products that users and businesses love.

### RESPONSIBILITIES:

* Design and build large scale search infrastructure (real-time indexing, serving, and data pipelines) that handles massive write and query loads with strict latency and reliability requirements
* Go deep into search internals (indexing, query execution, and relevance) and help shape the technical direction of the core search infrastructure
* Operate production services end to end, including monitoring, debugging, incident response, and performance optimization
* Lead and drive incident responses and recovery with your peers. Review and contribute to incident postmortems and hold a high bar for the same

### BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

* 5+ years developing large-scale infrastructure, distributed systems  or experience with storage or hardware architecture.
* Proficient in high performance Rust, C++  or JVM-based languages
* Hands-on experience with search and indexing technologies (Elasticsearch, Lucene, or similar) and an understanding of how they work and why
* Experience with operating system internals, filesystems, networking, or database storage engines
* Obsessed performance, reliability, scalability and quality
* Ability to take full ownership of problems and learn whatever is needed to solve them
* Comfortable moving quickly in a fast-paced environment with shifting priorities and tight deadlines

### PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:

* Contributions to open-source search or infrastructure projects
