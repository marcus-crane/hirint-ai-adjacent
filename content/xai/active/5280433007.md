**INSTRUMENTATION & CONTROLS TECHNICIAN (POWER GENERATION)**

As an Instrumentation & Controls Technician, you will keep the controls and field instruments reliable on SpaceXAI’s on-site power plant. The plant supplies power for high-density AI supercomputing facilities in the Memphis and Southaven area, and it has to stay online 24/7.

You will calibrate, troubleshoot, and repair the instruments and control systems on gas turbines and generators, balance-of-plant, fuel gas and process/fluid systems, water, compressed and instrument air, HVAC, and cooling. The turbine fleet is several different manufacturers, and the control platforms are not the same. You are expected to be proficient on every type on site, not specialized in one OEM. Your schedule flexes, including nights, weekends, holidays, and callouts, so the plant always has I&C coverage. This is a full-time, on-site role in the Memphis region.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Calibrate, test, troubleshoot, and repair field instruments and final elements, including transmitters, switches, analyzers, thermocouples, RTDs, control valves, and actuators
* Troubleshoot turbine, generator, and balance-of-plant control systems across every manufacturer on site, including PLCs, turbine controls, and the plant control system
* Find and fix trips, alarms, and bad instrument readings with the shift team and return equipment to service
* Run loop checks and functional checks during start-up, shutdown, commissioning, and outages
* Read and work from P&IDs, loop sheets, wiring diagrams, cause-and-effect charts, and control logic
* Complete calibrations and repairs to procedure, and record as-found/as-left results and work-order closeout in the CMMS
* Identify repeat instrument and controls failures and correct the cause, not only the symptom
* Use AI tools to troubleshoot faster and to simplify calibration records, procedures, and shift handoffs
* Follow SpaceXSI safety procedures, including LOTO, NFPA 70E / arc-flash, confined space, and hot work. Stop work when conditions are unsafe
* Flex shifts and work hours, including nights, weekends, holidays, and callouts, to maintain 24/7 I&C coverage

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* High school diploma or equivalency certificate.
* 3+ years of hands-on instrumentation and controls maintenance in a power plant, generating station, or other high-reliability industrial facility.

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience with gas turbine or generator controls from more than one manufacturer
* Experience with PLCs, turbine control systems, and a plant DCS or equivalent control system
* Experience calibrating and troubleshooting 4–20 mA loops, HART devices, control valves, and instrument air
* Experience with fuel-gas, water, or balance-of-plant process instruments
* Ability to read P&IDs, loop sheets, electrical schematics, and control logic
* Experience with LOTO and NFPA 70E / arc-flash practices when working in control panels
* Experience using a CMMS, including calibration records and work-order closeout
* Experience supporting commissioning, outages, or trip investigations
* Associate’s degree or technical certificate in instrumentation, controls, or a related field

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Willingness to perform hands-on work in all environments (heat, cold, rain), in tight quarters or at heights
* Remain focused while working in hazardous environments, while wearing appropriate PPE
* Must be able to lift up to 25 lbs unassisted
* Lifting, standing, climbing, bending, grasping, sitting, pulling, pushing, stooping, stretching, and carrying are generally required to perform the functions of this position
* Must be able to work on-site at the power generation facility, with potential travel to other SpaceXSI sites
* Valid driver’s license
