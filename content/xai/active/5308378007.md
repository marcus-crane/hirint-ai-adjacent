#### **GLOBAL SUPERVISOR, LAW ENFORCEMENT RESPONSE TEAM (LERT)**

The teams within Safety work to safeguard the user’s voice in balance with manageable risk through policy enforcement that promotes trust in our platform. Areas of focus include privacy, government and law enforcement requests, and safety advocacy. Our global Law Enforcement Response Team operates across regions, including the US, EMEA and APAC, collaborating to handle time-sensitive requests while upholding legal standards and user rights.

As the Global Supervisor of the Law Enforcement Response Team, you will lead a distributed, global team that processes sensitive legal requests from intake through follow-up. As their direct supervisor, you will own the team’s performance, quality and coverage across regions, automate manual processes so the team can scale, and ensure compliance with evolving regulations while protecting user privacy.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Set goals and priorities for the global team, and lead complex, cross-regional projects that improve organizational performance.
* Own SLAs and coverage across regions, including staffing models, handovers between regions and on-call rotations for emergency requests.
* Own headcount and staffing plans, and hire, develop and retain a high-performing, distributed team.
* Set global QA standards and drive accuracy and consistency through quality reviews, calibration and performance data.
* Identify and automate manual workflows. Partner with Engineering, Product, Programs and Data Science on case management tooling and AI automation, and measure the impact on volume, turnaround time and quality.
* Analyze trends and team metrics to identify process improvements and inform operational and policy decisions.
* Serve as the escalation point for Legal on sensitive and high-profile requests, policy questions and emerging risks.
* Manage relationships with law enforcement and government agencies, and represent X externally, including in witness testimony and engagements with regulators and auditors.
* Track changes to regulations, policies and procedures, and prepare the team before they take effect.
* Keep Safety Leadership, Legal and other internal stakeholders informed on operations and risks, and keep the team informed of company changes.
* All other duties and responsibilities as required by the business needs.

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Bachelor’s degree.
* 5+ years of people management experience, including leading global, distributed teams and mentoring and retaining diverse talent.
* 8+ years of experience in law enforcement response, legal process or trust and safety operations, including user information requests and high-volume case management.

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Advanced degree (JD, MA/MS).
* 8+ years in Safety or Legal Operations at a technology or social media company.
* An active X user with a deep understanding of the platform’s role as a global public square and its Safety challenges.
* Proven record of scaling operations through workflow optimization, tooling and automation.
* Familiarity with global regulations, privacy laws and the intersection of legal and policy issues on social media.
* Strong data analysis skills using tools such as Google Sheets, Excel or Tableau, and a track record of data-driven decisions.
* Strategic thinking and business acumen, with excellent judgment and attention to detail.
* Decisive and calm under pressure, with the ability to prioritize, delegate and navigate ambiguity.
* Excellent written and verbal communication, including leading difficult conversations, securing buy-in and implementing change.
* Fluent in written and spoken English.
* Passion for user privacy and freedom of expression.

#### **ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* This role will be 5 days/week onsite at our Austin, Texas office.
* Availability to be on call, including weekends and holidays.
* This role involves exposure to sensitive or graphic content, including but not limited to exploitation, violent threats and other graphic material.
* Travel as needed to collaborate with global teams.
