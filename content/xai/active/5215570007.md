**OSP ENGINEER**

This role will support the design, documentation, and coordination of outside plant (OSP) fiber optic infrastructure. Focuses on producing accurate construction drawings, route maps, and as-built records using AutoCAD and GIS tools.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Prepare and revise OSP fiber design drawings, conduit layouts, splice diagrams, and permitting packages in AutoCAD
* Create and maintain GIS-based route maps, asset inventories, and spatial data for fiber pathways, vaults, and handholes
* Assist with field surveys, redline markups, and conversion of field data into final drawings and GIS layers
* Support BOM development, quantity take-offs, and basic loss-budget calculations
* Coordinate with senior engineers, construction teams, and utilities on design changes and as-built updates
* Ensure drawings and GIS data comply with company standards, TIA/BICSI guidelines, and local permitting requirements

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Associate’s degree in engineering, geospatial technology, or scientific discipline

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience with GIS software (ArcGIS, QGIS, or equivalent) for mapping and spatial analysis
* Working knowledge of AutoCAD (preferably Civil 3D or similar) for utility/telecom drawings
* Basic understanding of fiber optic OSP concepts (conduit systems, aerial/buried placement, splicing, and terminations)
* Strong attention to detail and ability to produce clear, accurate technical drawings
* Prior internship or project experience in telecom, utility, or civil infrastructure
* Familiarity with fiber strand counts, duct banks, and standard OSP construction practices
* Experience with Google Earth, KML/KMZ files, or CAD-to-GIS data exchange

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Willingness to perform occasional field work and site visits
