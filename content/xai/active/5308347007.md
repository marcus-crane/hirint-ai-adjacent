#### **EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT (EMEA/MENA SALES)**

This is a new role at the heart of X's UK & Europe Ads business, based in London. You will provide full executive support to the Managing Director, UK & Europe Ads, keeping the MD, the office, and the leadership team running smoothly so they can focus on clients and growth, while also providing executive support to the Head of MENA Sales based in the X Dubai office.

In this role, you will partner closely with the UK leadership team, MENA leadership, Europe & Africa colleagues, Marketing, Content Partnerships, global teams, recruiters, and external agencies, clients, and industry bodies. You will keep priorities moving with balanced urgency, resourcefulness, collaboration, and a client-partner mindset.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Own calendars and inboxes for the Managing Director, UK & Europe Ads and the Head of MENA Sales, resolving clashes, triaging invitations, and handling looped-in emails and Slack threads to keep executive time protected and prioritised
* Coordinate leadership, all-hands, offsite, and 1:1 meetings, owning agendas, notes, and follow-ups so meetings run prepared with no dropped actions
* Plan the annual travel schedule, book flights, hotels, and itineraries (BCD Travel, TripSource), and manage Ramp expenses on time and within policy
* Coordinate scheduling and event logistics with agencies, industry bodies, clients, and sports partners, serving as a polished, responsive point of contact
* Organise team offsites, all-hands, client hospitality, and the Christmas party, and coordinate interview scheduling with recruiters and hiring panels
* Maintain the leadership project tracker, master index, org charts, and team overview docs, following up with action owners and flagging risks early

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 5+ years of exec support at senior C-Suite level

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Executive support experience in a fast-paced commercial, media, or advertising environment
* Strong organisation, attention to detail, and sound judgement to make decisions and keep priorities moving
* High discretion when handling confidential information
* Confident, warm communication style with internal and external stakeholders at all levels
* Advanced Google Workspace and Slack skills
* Experience with Ramp, TripSource, or BCD Travel
* Resourceful, collaborative working style with balanced urgency and a client-partner mindset

#### **ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Must be able to work full time, in office, in London, UK
