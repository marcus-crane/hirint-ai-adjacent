#### **TECHNICAL PROGRAM MANAGER, SAFETY COMPLIANCE**

SpaceXSI's Safety team protects free expression while ensuring our users can safely and successfully engage with our platform through robust policy enforcement. As we increasingly leverage AI-based automation, we innovate and experiment at a fast pace, striving to be a learning organization with a growth mindset.

As a Technical Program Manager in Safety Compliance, you will own the end-to-end program that ensures our platform complies with major content moderation and online safety regulations. The role centers on translating complex legal requirements into scalable technical systems, operational processes, and cross-functional execution. In addition, you will partner closely with Legal, Policy, Product, Engineering, Safety Operations, and external regulators/stakeholders to build, maintain, and continuously improve the programs that keep us compliant while supporting X’s mission of free speech.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Break down obligations under various regulations  into clear technical requirements, product specs, and operational workflows.
* Drive cross-functional programs from scoping through launch and ongoing operation. This includes content moderation tooling, appeal/complaint systems, transparency reporting pipelines, risk assessment processes, data logging for auditability, and related infrastructure.
* Partner with Engineering and Product to ensure platforms can generate auditable systems and process designs, including moderation decisions, statements of reasons, user appeals and out-of-court dispute settlement readiness, trusted flaggers, and meet record-keeping and reporting needs.
* Own or co-own the technical side of transparency reports, user empowerment features (e.g., recommender controls where relevant), ad transparency, and related data access or researcher data-sharing readiness.
* Align Legal/Policy, Product, Engineering, Ops, and Analytics. Assist in creating clear roadmaps, manage dependencies, surface risks and trade-offs early, and maintain executive visibility through crisp status reporting.
* Monitor regulatory developments, guidance from relevant regulatory bodies and translate changes into program updates. Support audits, information requests, and incident response related to compliance obligations.
* Define KPIs for compliance health (e.g., notice handling SLAs, appeal volumes and outcomes, statement-of-reasons completeness, risk assessment coverage) and drive improvements in tooling and processes.

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 6+ years of technical program management experience in Trust & Safety, Integrity, Content Moderation, Privacy, Security, or Regulatory Compliance programs at a consumer platform or similar scale.
* Experience with system design concepts, data flows, APIs, logging/auditability requirements, and the realities of large-scale content moderation systems (automated + human review).

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Proven ability to translate complex legal/regulatory requirements into actionable technical requirements, product changes, and operational processes.
* Experience coordinating large cross-functional initiatives involving Product, Engineering, Legal, Policy, and Operations teams.
* Direct experience with the EU Digital Services Act, UK Online Safety Act, or similar content regulation frameworks.
* Familiarity with related regimes (e.g., GDPR implications for moderation data, CSAM reporting obligations, age assurance technologies, or other regional online safety laws).
* Experience building or operating notice-and-action systems, appeal workflows, transparency reporting, risk assessment programs, or audit-ready logging systems.
* Background in high-scale Trust & Safety operations or product infrastructure.
* Relevant certifications (e.g., CIPP/E, project management credentials) are a plus but not required.
* Excellent communication skills—able to explain technical trade-offs to non-technical stakeholders and regulatory nuances to engineering teams.
* Track record of delivering complex programs on time in ambiguous, high-stakes environments.
