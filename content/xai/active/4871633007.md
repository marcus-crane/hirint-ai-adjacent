### ABOUT THE ROLE:

The IT Systems Team at 𝕏 manages a dynamic and evolving virtual infrastructure that provides secure, high-performance, and reliable corporate resources for business-critical users and applications. This role focuses on building, deploying, and managing **on-premises Microsoft Windows Server infrastructure**, including Active Directory, VMware Horizon Windows VDIs, and Windows VMs running SQL Server, while collaborating with other teams to deliver scalable, reliable solutions.

### RESPONSIBILITIES:

* Build, manage, and support complex application stacks on **Windows Server**
* Administer **on-premises Active Directory** (domain controllers, Group Policy, identity, and access)
* Deploy, configure, and support **VMware vSphere** technologies, including vCenter and ESXi
* Deploy, configure, and support **VMware Horizon** Windows VDI environments
* Manage Windows VMs hosting **Microsoft SQL Server**, including patching, performance, and availability
* Provide Windows Server troubleshooting and hands-on technical support
* Manage and support scalable storage solutions, preferably **NetApp**, in support of Windows workloads
* Automate Windows infrastructure using **PowerShell** and related Microsoft tooling
* Work with engineering teams and third-party vendors to implement scalable solutions
* Ensure compliance by collaborating with internal audit and compliance teams
* Develop and maintain standard operating procedures
* Participate in a 24/7 on-call support rotation
* Install, configure, test, and maintain Windows operating systems and system management tools
* Proactively ensure high levels of system availability and reliability
* Monitor and optimize Windows application and infrastructure performance, identify bottlenecks, and partner with developers on fixes
* Write and maintain scripts to improve system efficiency and automation

### BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

You are a highly skilled **IT Systems Engineer** with a deep understanding of infrastructure management and automation. You thrive in a collaborative and fast-paced environment, leveraging your expertise to enhance system performance and reliability. The ideal candidate will also have:

* Deep understanding of Microsoft infrastructure management and automation
* 3 to 5 years of experience in the following:
  + - Systems engineering for corporate Microsoft infrastructure
    - Managing large-scale **Windows Server** environments
    - **On-premises Active Directory** administration
    - Virtualization at scale with **VMware vSphere** (vCenter, ESXi)
    - **VMware Horizon** Windows VDI administration
    - Supporting **SQL Server** on Windows VMs
    - Managing scalable storage solutions, preferably **NetApp**
    - Developing and maintaining performance metrics for systems
* Strong understanding of basic networking concepts as they apply to Windows and VMware environments
* Proficiency with **PowerShell** for automation and configuration management
* Ability to translate business needs into technical solutions
* Outstanding analytical and problem-solving skills
* Strong written and verbal communication skills
* Experience writing technical design documents and maintaining documentation
* Familiarity with working in a source-controlled environment
* Experience with VMware data center virtualization and VMware Horizon

### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience with Windows Server clustering, failover, and high availability
* Familiarity with Microsoft configuration and deployment tooling (e.g. SCCM/MECM, Intune, or equivalent)
* Knowledge of storage best practices for SQL Server and VDI workloads on NetApp
