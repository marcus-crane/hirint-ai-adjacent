**BRAND STRATEGIST, MEXICO CITY**

X is hiring a Brand Strategist on a contract basis in Mexico City to create audience-centric ideas, strategies, and creative recommendations for brands in Mexico and Spanish-speaking Latin America. You will join an ambitious team that turns real-time conversations into brand moments that move culture and business results. Rooted in insights, powered by SpaceXSI, and driven by creativity, the team builds custom experiences that start on X and shape the story everywhere else, including campaigns that bring X's super intelligence (SI) solutions, such as Grok and Grok Bot, to life for advertisers.

The team works across 12 cities worldwide, including Mexico City, and brings local cultural insight to campaigns that run across markets. You will work with design, engineering, research, content, and partner teams to take campaigns from idea to launch, and pitch to brand and agency leaders in Spanish and English.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Pitches and produces insights-driven, measurable, on-brand creative campaigns for top brands and agencies in Mexico and Spanish-speaking Latin America that deliver on client key performance indicators (KPIs) and create memorable experiences for people on X.
* Uses knowledge of the platform and Mexican culture to brainstorm, develop, and deliver original recommendations for how brands can show up on X.
* Leads projects end to end, using insights on platform behavior among Mexican and Spanish-speaking audiences, and works with technologists to pitch fully custom solutions.
* Uses Grok, Grok Bot, and other X SI tools to sharpen research, insights, ideation, and pitch development, and brings SI-powered creative solutions to clients.
* Works closely with the Mexico sales team and cross-functional (XFN) partners, partners with the Canada and Latin America (CanLATAM) team, and pitches directly to brand and agency leaders in Spanish and English.
* Identifies opportunities for brand growth and innovation and stays ahead of market trends in Mexico and across Latin America.

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 5+ years of experience in brand strategy or a creative role at an advertising agency, on the client side, or at a technology company or platform.
* Portfolio of brand strategy or creative work.
* Fluency in Spanish and professional fluency in English.

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience across agency, client-side, and platform roles.
* Knowledge of the Mexican advertising, agency, and brand landscape.
* Understanding of Mexican culture and the conversation happening on X in Mexico.
* Knowledge of X and the social media landscape as both a user and a marketer, including X's promoted products and advertising model.
* Track record of creative strategies and ideas that drove brand awareness and user engagement.
* Experience building insights from quantitative data and qualitative understanding of people.
* Experience leading end-to-end projects and managing clients, accounts, and cross-functional teams.
* Hands-on experience with SI tools, highly preferred Grok and Grok Bot.
* Experience delivering presentations and pitches to brand and agency leaders.
* Strong written and verbal communication, time management, and project management skills.
* Experience working as a self-starter in a fast-paced team environment.
* Design skills.

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* This is a contract position.
* Must be based in, or willing to relocate to, Mexico City, Mexico.
