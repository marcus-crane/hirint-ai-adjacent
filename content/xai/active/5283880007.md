#### CONSTRUCTION MANAGER, FIBER

Most construction managers inherit a schedule someone else built and a set of contractors someone else hired. This one does not.

SpaceX builds its own launch sites, factories, test stands, and AI superclusters — and it plans to keep building. The rockets, the chips, and the missions only matter if the ground under them is real. Construction is how that ground gets built, to a standard the company can actually use. The work does not dry up between jobs. There is a next site, and one after that.

You are the senior construction lead for the fiber scope. You own the site relationships, the vendors, and the outcome. You report to central construction leadership, and that line can shift as the project evolves. You are on the job, walking the work, and clearing what is in the way.

Scope includes, but not limited to fiber optic backbone and horizontal cabling, pathways, splicing, testing, certification, and handover documentation for the data center.

Right now the job is the data center in Memphis, Tennessee. Later work can move to other U.S. construction sites.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Act as the senior construction representative for the Memphis data center fiber scope, managing relationships with contractors, partners, and internal stakeholders
* Oversee fiber execution from pre-construction and site development through testing, certification, and handover
* Coordinate multiple vendors so field and technical teams stay aligned on fiber backbone, horizontal cabling, pathways, splicing, and testing
* Identify and mitigate risks to quality, safety, system integrity, and operational readiness
* Drive progress on the fiber scope and resolve roadblocks so the schedule holds
* Align the fiber schedule with SpaceXSI's infrastructure goals through cross-functional collaboration
* Partner with teams on commissioning and close issues as they come up
* Deliver regular updates with clear KPIs and dashboards for internal and external stakeholders
* Hold the work to the technical and quality standards in the base design
* Perform quantity take-offs and field verifications to track progress and support reporting
* Manage change orders, cut unnecessary changes, and control cost
* Lead site safety around fiber installation, splicing, testing, and work at heights
* Support cross-functional partners as the project requires

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 5+ years of experience managing data center or construction projects

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience managing large-scale data center or mission-critical construction projects
* Certification in fiber optics and/or cable installation (FOA, BICSI, or equivalent)
* Advanced expertise in fiber splicing, OTDR testing, and certification procedures
* Deep technical knowledge of hyperscale data center networking, structured cabling, and fiber infrastructure
* Experience in AI/HPC data centers or other mission-critical facilities
* Highly proficient using a light meter, OTDR (Optical Time Domain Reflectometer), OPMs (optical power meter), fiber certifier, loopback, and optical probes
* Knowledge of different fiber types, proper cleaning/validation techniques, switches, and port identifications
* Proficiency with computers, fiber management software, and electronic testing systems
* Excellent problem-solving, communication, and organizational skills
* Deep expertise in construction management, commissioning, and site operations
* Proven ability to lead on-site with authority and build trust with vendors, partners, and technical teams
* Experience managing large programs with multiple vendors and dynamic priorities
* Strong communication skills across disciplines, from field updates to technical escalations
* Thrives in solving complex, real-time problems with adaptability and composure
* Previous experience in data center construction or highly technical facilities
* Familiarity with local Memphis building codes and regulations
* Strong problem-solving skills and ability to handle complex construction challenges
