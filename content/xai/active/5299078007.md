#### **CONTENT COMPLIANCE ANALYST**

We are looking for a legal analyst to join the SpaceXSI Legal team and help X and SpaceXSI to support our regulatory obligations across our products and jurisdictions. Typical examples include transparency reporting, regulator requests for information, risk assessments and audits.

You will work with Legal, Compliance, Product, Safety and Engineering teams to identify, source and validate information and data in order to produce accurate, well-documented outputs that Legal and regulatory stakeholders can rely on. The role offers a real opportunity for impact in an agile, fast-paced environment.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Curate and extract metrics and associated data required under, and as part of, regulatory obligations, such as transparency reports, regulator requests for information, risk assessments and audits.
* Translate legal and regulatory requirements into clear data definitions, and work with Legal, Compliance, Product, Safety, Trust & Safety and Engineering teams to source, validate and document the data needed.
* Build and maintain repeatable queries, pipelines and dashboards, so that recurring work does not depend on one-off requests.
* Own data quality, reconciliation, lineage and documentation, so that outputs are consistent and supported by a clear audit trail.
* Produce clear, accurate outputs for legal and regulatory use, and explain methodology and limitations to non-technical audiences.
* Handle sensitive and personal data in line with internal policies and data protection requirements, and improve tooling and processes, including better use of our artificial intelligence (AI) products.

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 3+ years of experience in data analysis, business intelligence, analytics engineering or a quantitative role.
* Experience writing Structured Query Language (SQL) queries against large datasets.
* Experience using Python for data processing or automation.

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience with cloud data warehouses and business intelligence (BI) tools (for example BigQuery, Snowflake, Databricks, Looker or Tableau), including query optimization and cost-aware querying of very large datasets.
* Ability to translate ambiguous legal or regulatory requests into precise, defensible data specifications - pinning down definitions, counting units, time windows, and scope before writing queries.
* A high degree of accuracy and attention to detail, with a methodical approach to validation and documentation.
* Ability to explain data and its limitations clearly to legal and other non-technical audiences.
* Strong relationship-building and collaboration skills across legal, engineering, and safety teams, including good instincts for when to escalate blockers and when to give stakeholders room.
* Ability to manage multiple concurrent requests with firm external deadlines.
* Sound judgement, integrity and discretion when handling confidential, privileged, and user level data.Bachelor's degree or equivalent experience.
* Experience at a technology, social media or AI platform.
* Experience supporting regulatory or transparency reporting, audits or regulator requests.
* Experience with privacy-aware data handling, such as data minimisation and access controls.
* Familiarity with online safety, platform, data protection or AI regulation.
