### **ABOUT THE ROLE:**

We are seeking a Manager of Operation & Maintenance to lead the people who keep SpaceXSI’s on-site power plant running. You will oversee supervisors, leads, and technicians supporting operations and maintenance of gas turbine generation, balance-of-plant, process and fluid systems, and plant electrical systems. This plant supplies power for high-density AI supercomputing facilities in the Memphis and Southaven area, and it has to stay online.

This is a hands-on leadership role. You will set the operating and maintenance standard, run the organization through trips, outages, and day-to-day work, and build supervisors and technicians who can execute safely without waiting on you for every decision. You will work with engineering, construction, operations leadership, OEMs, and contractors as the plant grows. This is a full-time, onsite role based in the Memphis region.

### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Own operation and maintenance of the power plant, including gas turbines and generators, balance-of-plant, fuel gas and process/fluid systems, water, compressed/instrument air, plant HVAC and cooling, and electrical systems through, including protection, e-houses, blackstart, and switching.
* Lead supervisors, leads, and technicians across plant, process, and electrical crafts as one O&M organization.
* Set daily and weekly priorities so planned maintenance, emergent work, alarms, trips, and outages are balanced without losing generation.
* Own the maintenance program: work planning, scheduling, procedures, CMMS discipline, spare parts readiness, and as-found/as-left documentation.
* Direct start-up, shutdown, switching, isolation, LOTO, and restoration so mechanical, process, and electrical work is coordinated and safe.
* Enforce SpaceXSI safety standards, including LOTO, NFPA 70E / arc-flash, confined space, and hot work. Stop work when conditions are unsafe and hold the organization to that standard.
* Lead outage planning and execution with engineering, OEMs, and contractors, and support commissioning and turnover of new generation and BOP systems into operations.
* Own O&M vendor and contractor performance, cost, and schedule. Work with buyers and accounts payable so accounts and contracts stay in order.
* Drive reliability: bad-actor elimination, root-cause investigations, and standards that raise uptime as capacity grows.
* Coach and develop supervisors and leads. Set expectations, manage performance, and scale the team with the plant.
* Communicate plant status, risks, team performance, and resource needs to site leadership.

### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* High school diploma or equivalency certificate.
* 6+ years of experience in power plant or other high-reliability industrial operations and maintenance.
* 2+ years directly managing supervisors, leads, or a multi-discipline technician organization.

### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Bachelor’s degree in a technical, engineering, or related field, or equivalent experience.
* Experience operating or maintaining gas turbine generation and balance-of-plant, including fuel gas, water, compressed air, and plant electrical systems.
* Experience leading a mixed-discipline O&M team (plant, process, and electrical) in a generating station or similar facility.
* Working knowledge of P&IDs, single-line diagrams, electrical schematics, switching, LOTO, and NFPA 70E / arc-flash practices.
* Experience running a CMMS-based maintenance program, including planning, scheduling, and outage execution.
* Experience managing O&M budgets, OEM service agreements, and third-party contractors.
* Track record of reliability improvement, root-cause investigations, and high-uptime operation of mission-critical plant equipment.
* Ability to develop supervisors and leads while staying close enough to the work to catch problems early.
* Strong written and verbal communication with technicians, engineers, contractors, and site leadership.

### **ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Willingness to be in the plant and to perform hands-on work in all environments (heat, cold, rain), in tight quarters or at heights.
* Ability to work extended hours, nights, weekends, and on-call coverage to support plant operations.
* Remain focused while working in hazardous environments, while wearing appropriate PPE.
* Must be able to lift up to 25 lbs unassisted.
* Lifting, standing, climbing, bending, grasping, sitting, pulling, pushing, stooping, stretching, and carrying are generally required to perform the functions of this position.
* Must be able to work on-site at the power generation facility, with potential travel to other SpaceXSI sites.
