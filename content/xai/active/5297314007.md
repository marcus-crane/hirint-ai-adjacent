#### **SR. SOFTWARE ENGINEER, SAFETY**

We are seeking a Senior Full Stack Software Engineer to support our Safety teams. You will play a critical role in designing, developing, deploying, and maintaining cutting-edge tools and applications that enable trust and safety across SpaceXSI's organizations and products, including brand safety as well as legal and operations. You'll have the unique opportunity to work across the stack and across SpaceXSI, collaborating with cross-functional teams and all of our products to deliver scalable, mission-critical solutions that enhance operational efficiency, provide critical insights, and meet the unique challenges of protecting users, advertisers, and the platform at global scale.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Own the architecture of safety systems end to end, from design and development through deployment and ongoing operation.
* Design, build, develop, and deploy mission-critical tooling for content moderation and enforcement, abuse and risk detection, brand safety controls, legal request processing and compliance, reviewer workflows, and ops case management.
* Monitor and optimize applications for security compliance.
* Build and maintain scalable back-end systems using languages like Java and Scala.
* Integrate with and maintain secure, reliable databases (e.g., PostgreSQL or MySQL).
* Collaborate with trust and safety, brand safety, legal, and operations partners to understand their needs, drive cross-org alignment, and translate those needs into actionable technical solutions.
* Develop APIs and middleware to connect systems and facilitate seamless data exchange, including data pipelines and insights that support safety operations.
* Ensure high performance, reliability, and scalability in all applications, and provide technical leadership in ambiguous, high-scale environments.
* Mentor engineers and set engineering standards for the Safety organization.
* Write clean, maintainable, and well-documented code.

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* A bachelor's or master's degree in computer science or software engineering.
* 5+ years of experience as a full stack developer, with experience in both front-end and back-end technologies.

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Proficiency in JavaScript/TypeScript and one or more front-end frameworks (React, Angular, or Vue.js).
* Strong experience with back-end frameworks.
* Expertise in designing and working with RESTful APIs and/or GraphQL.
* Hands-on experience with SQL databases.
* Familiarity with cloud platforms (AWS, Azure, or GCP) and containerization tools like Docker or Kubernetes.
* Experience owning system architecture end to end, providing technical leadership, mentoring other engineers, and setting engineering standards.
* Strong problem-solving skills and a commitment to delivering quality solutions, including in ambiguous, high-scale environments.
* Excellent communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across teams and drive alignment with trust and safety, brand safety, legal, and operations partners.
* A security-first mindset, with a commitment to protecting the privacy of our users' data.
* A demonstrated ability to succeed in fast-paced, dynamic environments.
