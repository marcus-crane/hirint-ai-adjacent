**SOFTWARE ENGINEER, MEDIA**

We’re looking for exceptional media engineers who want to join us on a new project to deeply integrate SpaceXSI's advanced AI infrastructure into a platform used by around 600 million users every month. We’re bringing SpaceXSI's technology stack and using it to transform the video product experience - video playback, live streaming, Spaces, audio/video calls, and more. This is your chance to contribute in a major way while leveraging all of the powerful AI tools and talented colleagues at SpaceXSI.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Build the next generation of large-scale video services
* Contribute to and rebuild core media processing and distribution pipelines in high-performance languages (Rust, C++, or Go)
* Ensure end-to-end media quality and performance at scale across a rich suite of products and user platforms

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 5+ years of experience in building media, video, or streaming systems
* Experience with high-performance C++ or Go

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* In-depth knowledge of WebRTC, LL-HLS, or video transcoding pipelines
* Experience building and running scalable and resilient distributed systems
* Experience with one or more of the following languages: Go, C++, Rust, Java, or Scala
* Experience with Kubernetes, FoundationDB, ValKey, Envoy, or S3
* Familiarity with formats such as H.264, H.265, AV1, MP4, CMAF, VMAF, RTP, RTMP, LL-HLS, HDR, and DRM
* Obsessed with media quality, performance, and product experience

**COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS:**

$180,000 - $440,000 USD

Base salary is just one part of our total rewards package at SpaceXSI, which also includes equity, comprehensive medical, vision, and dental coverage, access to a 401(k) retirement plan, short & long-term disability insurance, life insurance, and various other discounts and perks.
