### SENIOR SOFTWARE ENGINEER, MEDIA

We’re looking for exceptional media engineers to join us on a new project: deeply integrating SpaceXSI's advanced AI infrastructure into a platform used by around 600 million users every month. We’re bringing SpaceXSI's technology stack to transform the video product experience - video playback, live streaming, Spaces, audio/video calls, and more.

A major focus of this work is **real-time communication**: building audio and video calling that rivals the best conferencing products in the world (think Google Meet and Zoom), from crisp one-on-one calls to large multi-party conversations, and making it smarter with AI. You’ll own systems where every millisecond of latency and every dropped frame is felt by users, and you’ll ship them at a scale few teams ever reach.

This is your chance to contribute in a major way while leveraging all of the powerful AI tools and talented colleagues at SpaceXSI.

### RESPONSIBILITIES

* Build and operate real-time audio/video calling infrastructure (1:1 and multi-party) using WebRTC, RTP/RTCP, SRTP, ICE/STUN/TURN
* Design and scale SFU/MCU media servers for global concurrent sessions
* Implement congestion control, adaptive bitrate, simulcast/SVC, jitter buffers, FEC, NACK/RTX, and fast reconnection for low-latency media under lossy networks
* Work with codecs (Opus, H.264, VP8/VP9, AV1) and hardware acceleration; optimize for quality, latency, bandwidth, and battery
* Deliver screen share, group calls, recording, and cross-platform switching (iOS, Android, web)
* Integrate AI features: real-time transcription/captions, live translation, noise suppression, echo cancellation, video enhancement
* Rewrite core media pipelines in Rust/C++
* Instrument and drive call quality metrics (latency, jitter, packet loss, freeze rate, MOS)

### BASIC QUALIFICATIONS

* 5+ years building high-performance media processing pipelines

### PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS

* Strong Rust or C++
* Production experience with WebRTC internals, SFU/MCU scaling, and real-time media at scale
* Hands-on with codecs, hardware acceleration, and resilience under packet loss/jitter
* Experience integrating ML models into real-time audio/video pipelines
