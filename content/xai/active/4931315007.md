**AI TUTOR, LEGAL & COMPLIANCE - TEMPORARY**

In this role, you will support the training of Grok by providing high-quality annotations and data inputs based on legal expertise. Your work involves legal research, analysis, and applying the law to real-world scenarios across a range of topics. You will help curate data that enables Grok to better capture accurate legal language, reasoning, and decision-making.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Use proprietary software to provide accurate labels, annotations, and inputs on legal materials for AI model training
* Curate high-quality data across a range of legal topics and scenarios, including legal research, analysis, writing, and the application of law to facts
* Analyze complex legal problems aligned with your expertise to strengthen model performance and accuracy
* Collaborate with technical staff to refine annotation guidelines and improve tools used for legal data
* Interpret and follow evolving instructions while maintaining precision and adaptability

**POSITION DETAILS:**

This is a **temporary** position with SpaceXSI, for the length of time as stated, and is subject to change with minimal notice as business needs dictate

* LENGTH OF POSITION: 6 months (extensions or conversion to a permanent role may be available based on business needs)
* LOCATION: Remote (United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia); for US-based candidates, please note that we are unable to hire in Wyoming and Illinois at this time

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Juris Doctor (J.D.) degree
* Active bar admission in good standing
* 2+ years of experience practicing law

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Primary practice area in one of the following: family law, criminal law, health law, employment and labor law, immigration law, civil rights law, antitrust and consumer protection law, entertainment law, environmental law
* Strong legal analysis and writing skills, including the ability to draft clear, concise, and well-reasoned documents
* Excellent reading comprehension and the ability to exercise autonomous judgment with limited or incomplete information
* Experience mentoring, training, or supervising others in a legal setting
* Familiarity with AI tools, data annotation workflows, or structured data projects

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* This role may be offered as a full-time or part-time position depending on role/business needs and candidate availability; full-time positions are preferred and require working standard business hours in your local time zone
* We are unable to provide visa sponsorship
* Must be willing to travel to a SpaceXSI site
