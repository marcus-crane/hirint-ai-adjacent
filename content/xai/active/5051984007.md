#### **SOFTWARE ENGINEER, GROK PRODUCT**

The Grok Main Product team is responsible for Grok's core agentic product experience across web and mobile, as well as everything from building infrastructure and search capabilities to making Grok and Bot excel at shopping. Our focus is delivering the most capable agents – fast, intelligent, and maximally useful – that amplify human productivity across every domain. In this role, you will design and deliver highly scalable product or agentic features that leverage frontier model capabilities. No prior AI experience is required – just sharp thinking, relentless execution, and passion for AI that redefines what's possible.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Design and build the systems that make Grok useful for everything from quick questions to useful work
* Architect high-performance systems for personalized, reliable interactions at global scale
* Build and optimize data pipelines using real-time user signals to drive product improvements
* Own end-to-end features from ideation to deployment and monitoring

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 2+ years of professional software engineering experience building and shipping user-facing products

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Proactive self-starter who turns bold ideas into high-impact realities with focused execution
* Skilled in scalable architectures, Kubernetes, observability, and production tuning for performance/reliability
* Track record shipping consumer-facing products to millions, emphasizing user delight and feedback
* Passionate about AI's transformative potential and committed to building indispensable tools users rely on daily

#### **ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Onsite role: must be able to work in person at our office locations in Palo Alto, San Francisco, or New York
