#### **AI TUTOR, MEDICINE - TEMPORARY**

In this role, you will help enhance Grok by creating and annotating high-quality data across the field of medicine. You will draw on your clinical expertise to evaluate model outputs, identify errors in clinical reasoning, and capture how clinicians think, communicate with patients, and navigate complex medical decisions. Your work will help train Grok to distinguish unsafe or clinically flawed responses from guidance that is evidence-based, diagnostically precise, and consistent with the standard of care. This work spans text, voice, and video, including recording audio and participating in video sessions.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Use proprietary software to create, label, and annotate data on defined medical projects.
* Produce high-quality data efficiently, with exceptional attention to accuracy.
* Help design and launch new medical data tasks, working closely with colleagues and technical staff to bring cutting-edge initiatives to life.
* Partner with engineers to improve the design of fast, efficient annotation tools.
* Help put trustworthy medical information within reach of patients and users worldwide.
* Improve patient outcomes by solving real healthcare problems that train Grok to meet clinical standards across decision-making, drug discovery, diagnostic imaging, and procedures.
* Create and solve complex medical problems within your area of expertise, such as diagnostic reasoning, treatment planning, care coordination, or image interpretation, writing detailed reference answers and critically evaluating model responses.
* Interpret, analyze, and carry out tasks based on evolving instructions.

#### **POSITION DETAILS:**

This is a **temporary** position with SpaceXSI, for the length of time as stated, and is subject to change with minimal notice as business needs dictate.

* LENGTH OF POSITION: 6 months (extensions or conversion to a permanent role may be available based on business needs).
* LOCATION: Remote (United States, United Kingdom, Canada, India, and Australia); for US-based candidates, please note that we are unable to hire in Wyoming and Illinois at this time.

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* MD, DO, MBBS, MBChB, DNP, PharmD, PsyD, DDS, DMD, DPT, DPM, OTD, or DVM degree, or an international equivalent of one of those degrees; OR a master’s degree or Ph.D. in nursing, physician assistant studies, clinical psychology, counseling psychology, public health, epidemiology, biostatistics, pharmacology, or biomedical sciences.
* 2+ years of employment experience in a clinical, research, or health-related role after the qualifying degree was conferred (completion of a residency or other postgraduate training is not required; residency, fellowship, and internship positions count toward the experience requirement).
* English proficiency at CEFR C1 level or higher.

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Skill at navigating medical literature, databases, and online resources.
* Excellent communication, interpersonal, analytical, and organizational skills.
* Sound independent judgment when information is limited, and the ability to explain your reasoning clearly to others.
* A genuine passion for innovation and for using technology to improve patient care.
* Multiple publications, or a first-author publication, in a reputable journal or outlet.
* Prior data annotation or teaching experience (as a professor, teacher, or tutor).
* Experience in technical writing, medical writing, journalism, or another professional writing setting.
* Post-graduate clinical experience or equivalent real world experience.

#### **ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**
