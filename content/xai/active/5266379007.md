**SITE RELIABILITY ENGINEER**

As a Site Reliability Engineer focused on campus reliability, you will design what the campus watches and trusts, technically command cross-discipline SEVs, and build the guardrails that make the next incident smaller. You are the connective tissue across compute, network, storage, power, and cooling on one of the largest AI training campuses in the world. This role demands calm incident leadership, fleet-scale observability judgment, and the ability to drive reliability work across software and facility boundaries. We are looking for candidates from power plants, nuclear power plants, data centers, or people who currently work or have worked in facilities like SpaceXSI with power, compute, cooling, and related plant systems.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Own monitoring architecture and signal quality across GPU and host telemetry (DCGM, Prometheus/Grafana) and facility telemetry (BMS, EPMS, DCIM, SCADA, CDU / direct liquid cooling): what we alert on, suppress, and trust. Consume NOC noise-disposition feedback to drive suppression and redesign. Treat alert noise as a design failure, not an operator failure
* Provide SEV command support: technical incident leadership, bridge coordination with the NOC, paging and escalation (e.g., PagerDuty), and timeline and severity hygiene
* Run blameless postmortems and drive corrective actions to closed, not filed
* Gate high-risk change: fleet-scale firmware/BIOS/BMC rollouts (Redfish, canaries, rollback) and SOP/MOP/EOP-governed work on power and cooling systems through a disciplined change management process
* Lead cross-functional reliability projects spanning compute, network (InfiniBand/RoCE), storage, and facility signal boundaries, including grey failures that cross those boundaries (e.g., NVLink flapping, thermal throttling, power sequencing)
* Partner with Hardware Failure Analysis on systemic failure classes: turn fleet data (MTBF/AFR, repeat-RMA trends) into monitoring, stop-rules, and vendor RCA inputs
* Build and maintain playbooks, run game days, and keep cross-discipline dependency maps current. Own runbook quality jointly with the NOC (SRE designs; NOC operates and corrects)
* Define error budgets and availability objectives at campus and service boundaries as adopted by the business
* Participate in on-call rotations and incident response for SEV-class events in the Memphis / Southaven data center campus
* Demonstrate monitoring and observability design at fleet or campus scale, including alert hygiene, suppression, and signal quality
* Treats alert noise as a design failure, not an operator failure
* Improve mean time to detect (MTTD) and mean time to resolve (MTTR) for severity-class events, and ensure blameless postmortems with closed action items.
* Increase the alert actionable ratio and reduce recurrence of incident classes.
* Expand monitoring coverage of critical dependencies across compute, network, storage, power, and cooling.
* Minimize GPU fleet availability lost to infrastructure events.

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Bachelor's degree in systems engineering, computer science, electrical engineering, or STEM discipline
* 5+ years of experience in site reliability, systems engineering, plant operations, or large-scale production operations
* Experience with at least one of: Prometheus/Grafana, NVIDIA DCGM, or industrial telemetry and controls (SCADA, BMS/BAS, EPMS, DCIM, PLCs, Modbus/BACnet)
* Experience working across at least two of: compute, network, storage, power, and cooling / facilities telemetry
* Experience writing and operating playbooks, runbooks, or SOP/MOP/EOPs with a 24/7 operations, control room, or NOC partner
* Experience with scripting (Python, Bash) for automation and analysis
