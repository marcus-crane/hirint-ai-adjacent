### ABOUT THE ROLE:

We are seeking a strategic and experienced Lead of Yield Management at SpaceXAI to oversee yield strategy and optimization for the X advertising platform. This role is responsible for driving the vision, frameworks, and execution that maximize advertising inventory utilization and long-term revenue performance at scale.

### RESPONSIBILITIES:

* Lead the creation and continuous refinement of yield management frameworks and predictive models to optimize SpaceXAI’s advertising inventory utilization and long-term revenue performance.
* Define, monitor, and report on core yield KPIs, including ad capacity, fill rate, ad load, served impressions, revenue, and CPM performance across all major placements (Home Timeline, Profiles, Search, Replies, etc.).
* Analyze and map advertising inventory supply against key audience and user dimensions, including age, gender, geography, OS, and other relevant segments to identify optimization opportunities.
* Optimize inventory allocation across direct sales, self-serve, DSPs, and all advertising products to balance supply, control pricing dynamics, and maximize advertiser campaign outcomes.
* Partner with Ads Engineering to design and implement scalable processes for real-time inventory optimization, including ad load management, CPM floor strategies, and other critical controls.
* Collaborate on sales strategy development, providing data-driven insights on inventory health, pricing guidance, and revenue growth opportunities.

### BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

* 15+ years of progressive experience in yield management, revenue optimization, advertising operations, or monetization strategy, preferably in large-scale social media, digital advertising, or programmatic platforms.
* Proven track record of building and scaling yield frameworks that have driven significant revenue impact.
* Strong analytical and quantitative skills with deep experience in inventory forecasting, pricing strategies, and KPI optimization.
* Excellent cross-functional collaboration skills, particularly with engineering, sales, and product teams

### PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:

* Thrives in a fast-paced, dynamic environment and can manage multiple priorities with tight deadlines.
* Collaborative mindset with a proactive approach to problem-solving and cross-functional partnership.
