#### **SR. MODEL ENGINEER, POST-TRAINING & RL**

You will work on the most critical post-training and reinforcement learning challenges at any given time — including reward modeling, preference optimization (RLHF/DPO), and RL for improving reasoning, truthfulness, and real-world capabilities.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Design and run rigorous experiments that turn research ideas into measurable gains in model capability
* Improve model recipes through data quality, deduplication, and mixture experiments
* Define what makes RL data and rewards good, including grader design and preventing reward hacking
* Advance training science, from architecture and scaling experiments to post-training recipes for coding and computer-use agents
* Build evals and targeted tasks that expose model weaknesses and guide where the team invests next
* Partner with engineers to take promising results from small experiments to frontier-scale training runs

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Experience writing production-quality code in Python, PyTorch, JAX, Rust, or C++
* Experience training large models (e.g., pre-training, post-training, fine-tuning, or RL training of neural networks with 1B+ parameters)

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* You believe truth-seeking AI is the most important and challenging problem
* You are obsessed about building incredibly useful models through post-training and RL techniques
* You are a power user of AI models and eager to push the boundaries of what’s possible with reinforcement learning and alignment methods
* If you previously worked on post-training, RLHF, or trained models used by millions of people it’s a big plus, but relevant experience is not required
* You take pride in your work and thrive in meritocratic environments
