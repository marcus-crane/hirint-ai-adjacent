**DATA VISUALIZATION ENGINEER**

This Data Visualization Engineer is a versatile, hands-on builder who owns how X turns data into decisions. They will administer our current BI platform, build dashboards quickly, and partner with the business to shape a modern, unified reporting and analytics solution.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Administer our BI platform (currently Tableau Server) as its sole administrator: upgrades, patches, maintenance, license management, SSO, and data drivers
* Support 12 department sites and their Site Admins, plus about 700 users, including owning the tbl-site-admins and tableau Slack channels
* Handle all BI requests through Slack and Jira (about 12 a week): access, ownership changes, data source issues, troubleshooting, and fixing bugs
* Communicate proactively with Site Admins, users, and developers about downtime, upgrades, and widespread issues
* Quickly design and build dashboards and data products that turn complex data into clear, decision-ready insight
* Maintain an inventory of existing reports and dashboards and working with business owners to decide what to migrate, rebuild, or retire
* Help stakeholders pick the right solution for each use case (dashboard, custom application, Grok team bot, and so on) and leading the migration to it
* Set standards for data access, row-level security, and visualization quality

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Bachelor’s degree in a STEM or engineering discipline
* 1+ years of experience working in engineering or operations/process development (internships may qualify)

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience with SQL, including complex joins, window functions, and performance tuning
* Experience in data visualization and dashboard design: chart selection, UX, and storytelling with data
* Row-level security and data access controls
* Rapid dashboard development in one or more BI tools (Tableau, Looker, Power BI, Superset, or similar)
* Platform administration fundamentals: SSO/SAML, APIs, and monitoring
* Comfort using AI tools to speed up analysis, development, and migrations
* Data modeling and warehouse concepts (star schemas, semantic layers) on platforms such as BigQuery, Snowflake, or Databricks
* Python or JavaScript for data prep, automation, or custom visuals
