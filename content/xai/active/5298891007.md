#### **AI TUTOR, BIOLOGY - TEMPORARY**

In this role, you will help enhance Grok by providing high-quality data annotations and inputs tailored to biological contexts. You will draw on your expertise in areas such as molecular and cell biology, genomics, structural biology, and protein science to evaluate model outputs and create training data. Your work will help train Grok to reason correctly from experimental evidence, distinguish well-supported findings from weak ones, and communicate uncertainty honestly.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Utilize proprietary software to provide accurate input and labels for biology projects
* Support and ensure the delivery of high-quality curated data for AI model training
* Collaborate with technical staff to support the training of new AI tasks and contribute to the development of innovative technologies
* Assist in designing and improving efficient annotation tools tailored for biological data
* Select and analyze complex problems in biology, biotechnology and life sciences aligned with your expertise to enhance AI model performance
* Interpret, analyze, and execute tasks based on evolving instructions, maintaining precision and adaptability

#### **POSITION DETAILS:**

This is a **temporary** position with SpaceXSI, for the length of time as stated, and is subject to change with minimal notice as business needs dictate

* LENGTH OF POSITION: 6 months (extensions or conversion to a permanent role may be available based on business needs)
* LOCATION: Remote (United States, United Kingdom, Canada, India, and Australia); for US-based candidates, please note that we are unable to hire in Wyoming and Illinois at this time

* Master’s degree or PhD in a biology field
* English proficiency at CEFR C1 level or higher
