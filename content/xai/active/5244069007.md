### **ABOUT THE ROLE:**

We are seeking exceptional Electrical Engineers to join our growing team in Memphis and help design, build, and optimize on-site power generation for SpaceXAI's high-density AI supercomputing facilities. This role owns plant electrical systems for gas turbine generation — from generator, excitation, and synchronization through 34.5 kV collect/export, protection, e-houses, blackstart, and switching.

You will collaborate across electrical, mechanical, power systems, and construction teams to deliver reliable, massive-scale generation. This is a full-time, onsite role based in the Memphis region.

### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Own plant electrical systems for on-site gas turbine generation supporting AI superclusters, including generators, excitation, synchronization, auxiliaries, and balance-of-plant electrical
* Design and engineer 34.5 kV collect and export systems, protection and control, e-houses, blackstart capability, and related medium-voltage infrastructure
* Serve as switching authority for plant electrical systems, including switching procedures, isolation, clearance, and energization
* Develop single-line diagrams, protection & control schematics, relay settings, and interconnection designs
* Perform detailed power system studies (load flow, short circuit, arc flash, transient stability, harmonics) using ETAP, PSS/E, or equivalent tools
* Integrate generation with plant systems, 34.5 kV collect/export, and downstream data center loads
* Ensure compliance with relevant standards (NERC, ERCOT, IEEE, NEC, NFPA, UL) and support permitting and utility coordination
* Support equipment specification, vendor selection, factory testing, site commissioning, and energization
* Conduct failure mode analysis (FMEA), root cause investigations, and reliability modeling to achieve extreme uptime targets
* Collaborate with cross-functional teams on new facility builds, capacity expansions, and continuous improvement of power standard

### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Bachelor's degree in electrical engineering, physics or electrical engineering technology

### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience with gas turbine generators, excitation, synchronization, islanded operation, and blackstart
* Experience owning plant electrical systems, including 34.5 kV collect/export, protection, e-houses, and switching authority
* Background with data centers, hyperscale facilities, or large on-site power infrastructure (>50 MW)
* Knowledge of ERCOT, NERC reliability standards, and utility interconnection processes
* Experience supporting construction, commissioning, and operations of power systems
* Track record of innovation or high-impact delivery in power infrastructure projects

* Remain focused while working in hazardous environments, while wearing appropriate PPE
