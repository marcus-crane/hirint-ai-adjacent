### ABOUT THE ROLE:

As an Inventory Specialist supporting SpaceXAI data center operations, you will be responsible for the accurate receipt, storage, issuance, kitting, and control of high-value materials and equipment (servers, GPUs, networking gear, power and cooling components, spare parts, and other critical infrastructure). You will maintain inventory accuracy, support just-in-time material flow to construction and operations teams, and help ensure the site can scale rapidly while meeting safety and quality standards.

### RESPONSIBILITIES:

* Receive, store, and issue raw material, parts, details, and hardware to support data center construction, commissioning, and operations
* Store parts and materials in designated locations (bins, racks, VLMs, or staging areas), accurately identifying parts and locations
* Maintain strict material handling practices in accordance with procedures, especially for high-value or sensitive equipment
* Perform picking and kitting functions to assemble necessary kits of parts, material, and hardware; verify all items are available and accounted for
* Verify all material received for conformity with accompanying paperwork, checking for damage, correct part numbers, quantities, and revisions
* Issue material against approved paperwork, including affixing verification and maintaining lot/serial traceability as required
* Perform cycle counts and maintain high inventory accuracy
* Abide by all procedures, work instructions, and standard operating documents
* Manage schedule priorities and independently execute work to meet site deliverables
* Perform good housekeeping in inventory and staging areas
* Support material flow, including staging and just-in-time delivery to internal customers

### BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

* High school diploma or equivalency certificate
* 1+ years of inventory control experience

### PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:

* 3+ years of inventory control experience in a warehouse or distribution environment
* Experience and understanding of inventory, kitting, and material handling processes
* Experience operating a forklift and other inventory/material handling equipment
* Excellent computer skills, including Microsoft Office (especially Excel)
* Experience in aerospace, automotive, semiconductor, electronics, technology, or data center environments
* Ability to read and interpret drawings, procedures, and parts lists
* ERP/WMS system experience
* Bachelor’s degree in logistics or supply chain management (preferred)

### ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:

* Ability to lift up to 25–50 lbs. unassisted; frequent lifting, standing, climbing, bending, grasping, sitting, pulling, pushing, stooping, stretching, and carrying required
* Must be available for one of the following 12.5-hour shifts: 6:00 AM – 6:30 PM or 6:00 PM – 6:30 AM
* Work schedule follows a rotating pattern of 4 days on / 3 days off, then 3 days on / 4 days off (and repeating)
* Must be open to working all required shift hours, including nights, weekends, overtime, and holidays as needed to support 24/7 operations
* Comfortable working in warehouse/data center environments with varying temperatures and noise
