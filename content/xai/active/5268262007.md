**SUPERVISOR, SAFETY OPERATIONS (CHILD SAFETY)**

SpaceXSI’s Safety team protects free expression while ensuring our users can safely and successfully engage with our platform through robust policy enforcement. Child Safety is our highest-stakes area: the team identifies, investigates, and removes child safety related abuse across X, Grok and X Money products, including AI-generated content, and makes the legally required reports to NCMEC. As we increasingly leverage AI-based automation, we innovate and experiment at a fast pace, striving to be a learning organization with a growth mindset.

As Supervisor, Child Safety, you will lead the Austin-based Child Safety analyst team across shifts. You will be hands-on with escalations, improve the team quality and defensibility of every high-risk decision, and coordinate with Safety leadership, Legal, and law enforcement response teams on high-profile cases. You will lead the team’s shift from scaled manual review to analysts focused on “Automation First” appeals, model evaluations and audits, investigations, and escalations.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Lead the Austin-based Child Safety team, a team of analysts (including senior analysts) covering X, Grok and X Money, with consistent standards across all shifts and strict adherence to SLAs
* Act as the escalation layer for Child Safety: be hands-on with complex, urgent, and high-profile cases, including high profile accounts, internal escalations from law enforcement response and legal teams, and SpacexSI products incident escalations
* Own NCMEC reporting decisions for the team. Ensure every enforcement decision is accurate, well-documented, and meets legal obligations, Bring thoughtful, well-reasoned recommendations to leadership on escalations, balancing the protection of minors with SpaceXSI’s free speech principles
* Run the team’s response to viral incidents and emerging abuse trends: track results, and escalate patterns quickly
* Take an automation-first approach. Partner with Program Leads to calibrate classifiers and turn findings into concrete improvements
* Lead the team’s automation evaluations, audits, and labeling ensuring high-quality curated data
* Use querying languages (e.g., SQL or Python) and LLM-powered agents to analyze operational data, identify trends, and drive data-informed improvements to policies and processes. Build that habit across the team
* Own your dashboards and KPIs: know hour to hour how the backlog and escalation queues are doing, and keep a baseline to spot and investigate anomalies
* Keep shift handovers, operational process documentation, and team performance trackers  current so every analyst works from the same playbook
* Provide coaching, mentorship, and professional development to foster critical thinking, efficiency, growth, retention, and wellbeing in a team regularly exposed to highly sensitive content
* Run strong feedback loops as a proactive manager: give constructive feedback early and often, out of a desire to improve, and take it in stride
* Build the team into child safety subject matter experts with a defensibility mindset, so every decision can be explained and defended
* Own team performance end to end: set clear expectations, measure them, reward strong performers, and act quickly and fairly on underperformance
* Partner with internal stakeholders (e.g., HR, workforce management, Legal, Policy, other Safety teams) to align on Child Safety objectives and coverage
* All other duties and responsibilities as required by business need

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 5+ years of experience in online child safety, Trust & Safety operations, criminal investigations, or another safety field
* 2+ years of experience leading or supervising a team

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Proven expertise in identifying and investigating CSAM, CSE and non-CSE related child safety areas, including grooming behaviors and risks in AI-generated content, with strong knowledge of legal obligations (such as NCMEC reporting) and industry standards for protecting minors
* Skilled in crisis and escalation management, with the ability to make sound, defensible decisions under pressure and navigate ambiguity in time-sensitive, high-impact situations
* Able to apply SpaceXSI safety policies with judgment, weighing the protection of minors against free expression in the global public square
* Proficient in data analysis with hands-on experience using LLMs, SQL, or Python to extract insights, identify emerging abuse vectors, and optimize operational performance
* Passionate about AI and automation, with an interest in leveraging technology to improve Child Safety enforcement and reduce manual workloads
* A strong communicator who can articulate business needs, write clear escalation summaries, and build relationships with Legal, Policy, Engineering, and leadership
* An active X - including Grok - user with a deep understanding of the platform’s role as a global public square and its Safety challenges
* A lifelong learner who reflects on their gaps, strives to fill them, and is curious about how to improve
* A team player who places the interests of the team first and balances mission accomplishment with compassion for their people
* Experience managing Child Safety or Trust & Safety teams at a social media or generative AI company, leveraging AI or other automation tools
* Expertise in leading red-teaming and adversarial testing of Large Language Models to proactively identify novel abuse vectors, jailbreaks, and safety failure modes, with a proven ability to translate findings into concrete improvements for enforcement systems, team processes, and platform robustness
* Experience providing human oversight and insights for scaled agentic AI operations, with abilities in prompting and evaluating LLM agents
