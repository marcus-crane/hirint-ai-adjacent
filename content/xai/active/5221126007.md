This organization is for individuals who appreciate challenging themselves and thrive on curiosity. We operate with a flat organizational structure. ### ABOUT THE ROLE:

We are hiring an SMB Client Account Manager to own a portfolio of small- and medium-sized advertisers and their agencies. You will act as a trusted advisor: uncovering business goals, recommending X advertising products and best practices, executing and optimizing campaigns, and driving measurable growth. This role blends hands-on campaign management with strategic relationship-building and a bias toward action in a fast-moving environment.

Ideal candidates are proactive, analytically minded, and comfortable working with limited supervision while collaborating across sales, product, and finance teams.

### RESPONSIBILITIES:

* Own a portfolio of SMB advertisers: understand growth drivers, identify opportunities and risks, and build plans that help clients meet or exceed their goals.
* Consult clients and agencies on X advertising products, campaign setup, targeting, creative, and measurement; educate them on best practices so they can succeed independently and with our support.
* Execute, optimize, and analyze campaigns using internal tools and dashboards; turn performance data into clear recommendations and adjustments.
* Conduct outreach and regular business reviews to strengthen relationships, surface new opportunities, and improve results.
* Identify key stakeholders at each account and help shape their digital marketing plans around X.
* Resolve campaign and operational issues quickly; flag process gaps and feed insights back to product and tools teams.
* Support contract, renewal, billing, and collections processes in partnership with Sales Finance.
* Collaborate with the broader sales team to align on priorities, share learnings, and deliver a consistent client experience.
* Mentor newer teammates as the team scales.

### BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

* Bachelor’s degree or equivalent practical experience.
* 2–3+ years in digital advertising, sales, marketing, media account management, or consulting (agency or client-side).
* Proven ability to build and grow client relationships, manage multiple accounts, and navigate competing priorities.
* Experience translating marketing objectives into campaigns, then optimizing them using performance data.
* Strong written and verbal communication and presentation skills; comfort speaking with business owners and marketing leads.
* Demonstrated ownership, attention to detail, and ability to work independently in a small, high-pace team.
* Solid understanding of the digital advertising landscape and competitive platforms.

### PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:

* Direct experience managing SMB or mid-market advertisers and hitting growth or revenue targets.
* Familiarity with social/platform advertising products (X Ads a plus) and how they fit into a broader media mix.
* Ability to use data and questioning to uncover insights, handle objections, and build compelling recommendations.
* Experience mentoring or onboarding others in a growth-stage environment.
* Passion for X’s mission around free speech and open public conversation, plus comfort in a dynamic, high-ownership culture.
