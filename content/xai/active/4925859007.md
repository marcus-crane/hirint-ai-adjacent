**AI TUTOR, MATH - TEMPORARY**

In this role, you will help enhance Grok by providing high-quality data annotations, labels, and inputs focused on pure mathematics. You will draw on your expertise in areas such as abstract algebra, real and complex analysis, topology, graph theory, and other pure mathematics subfields, to evaluate model outputs, identify mathematical errors or gaps in reasoning, and produce clear, rigorous solutions and explanations. Your work will help train Grok to distinguish flawed or inadequately justified approaches from solutions that are mathematically sound, accurately modeled, and practically applicable.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Use proprietary software applications to annotate and label data for designated projects
* Support the delivery of high-quality, accurate, and well-curated datasets that meet project standards
* Collaborate with technical teams to develop and train new tasks, contributing to the successful development and implementation of innovative technologies and initiatives
* Work closely with technical staff to improve the design, functionality, and efficiency of annotation tools and workflows
* Solve problems across pure mathematics subfields aligned with your expertise, including abstract algebra, real and complex analysis, topology, graph theory, and related areas. Develop detailed solutions and critically evaluate AI-generated responses
* Interpret, analyze, and execute tasks accurately in accordance with established guidelines and project requirements

**POSITION DETAILS:**

This is a **temporary** position with SpaceXSI, for the length of time as stated, and is subject to change with minimal notice as business needs dictate

* LENGTH OF POSITION: 6 months (extensions or conversion to a permanent role may be available based on business needs)
* LOCATION: Remote (United States, United Kingdom, Canada, and Australia); for US-based candidates, please note that we are unable to hire in Wyoming and Illinois at this time

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Master's degree or Ph.D in mathematics, with a specialization in pure mathematics subfield; OR a medal in the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) or a high-level mathematics competition
* English proficiency at CEFR C1 level or higher

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Peer-reviewed publications in pure mathematics in reputable scientific journals
* Previous experience in AI tutoring, AI model evaluation, or related applications of artificial intelligence
* Teaching experience, particularly at the college or university level
* Strong research skills, including the ability to navigate diverse information resources, academic databases, and technical literature
* Excellent written and verbal communication, analytical, interpersonal, and organizational skills
* Strong reading comprehension and critical-thinking skills, with the ability to exercise sound independent judgment when working with limited information
* A strong interest in technological advancement, innovation, and emerging technologies
