#### **SR. MOBILE ANDROID ENGINEER**

We're seeking an exceptional Android engineer to join a small, high-impact team dedicated to creating the world's most valuable AI application, judged by the impact it delivers to users. You'll shape cutting-edge mobile experiences, blending technical mastery with a keen product sense to deliver brilliance that resonates with users. You will work on the most critical product or technical challenge at any given time.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Craft delightful, intuitive user experiences that redefine how people interact with the app
* Obsess over every pixel, animation, and interaction, ensuring the experience feels magical and seamless
* Build blazing-fast, highly performant systems where every millisecond counts
* Drive technical and product decisions, collaborating with designers, backend, iOS, and web engineers
* Own the quality of the products you build - including running a/b tests, finding issues, and proactively making improvements
* Be proactive in finding ways to improve the app - from user feedback and from your own daily experience on the app

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Experience with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Coroutines and Flow

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Very strong product sense – you spot problems in a live product and turn them into solutions that move the business forward
* A proven track record of delivering projects in production apps
* Deep expertise in the Android stack and tooling (Android Studio, Gradle, etc.)
* Experience beyond Android, such as iOS, web or backend development
* Understands analytics and data, and uses metrics to measure impact and guide product decisions
* Solid understanding of the Android platform: its strengths, limitations and trade-offs
* Experience working on large-scale production apps with millions of daily active users
* Strong ownership. You take problems from idea to release and see them through
* Works autonomously and knows when to pull others in
* Clear communicator who can explain trade-offs and decisions to engineers, designers and product partners
* High craft bar – builds intuitive, delightful user experiences and optimizes for performance, stability and reliability
