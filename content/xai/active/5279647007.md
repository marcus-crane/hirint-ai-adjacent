#### **SR. COMMERCIAL COUNSEL, PUBLIC SECTOR**

You'll lead negotiation of SpaceXSI's largest and most complex commercial agreements: high-value enterprise customer agreements for our AI products and services, strategic partnerships, and hyperscaler, technology and vendor agreements. You'll be a trusted advisor to leaders in Sales, Business Development, Finance and Product, set the team's negotiating positions, and mentor more junior counsel.

#### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Lead negotiation of high-value, strategic and novel agreements, including enterprise, partnership, hyperscaler and technology deals.
* Advise business leaders on deal structure and risk, and own decisions on non-standard terms.
* Set and refine the team's negotiating positions, playbooks and escalation paths.
* Mentor more junior counsel and review their work.
* Build legal structures and processes that speed up sales while managing risk.
* Pitch in wherever Legal needs you, even on unfamiliar ground.
* Use our AI products every day to learn faster and work smarter.

#### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 8+ years of legal experience.
* JD and a license to practice law in California or New York, or eligibility to register as in-house counsel.
* Experience leading negotiations on commercial transactions.

#### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience at both a top-tier law firm and an in-house technology company.
* Experience negotiating AI and SaaS transactions.
* Experience advising senior business leaders and mentoring other lawyers.
* A track record of practical, commercial judgment and finding workable paths forward on deals.
* Interest in AI and its everyday applications.

#### **ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Must be able to work in the office 5 days a week.
