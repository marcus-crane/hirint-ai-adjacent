This organization is for individuals who appreciate challenging themselves and thrive on curiosity. We operate with a flat organizational structure. ### **ABOUT THE ROLE:**

We’re seeking an experienced Construction Manager to lead mission-critical build efforts for on-site power generation. As a key on-site leader, you’ll bring credibility, expertise, and steady leadership to fast-paced, high-impact plant projects. You’ll own site relationships, collaborate with engineering, operations, and construction teams, and report to construction leadership.

If you thrive on hands-on leadership, complex industrial and electrical construction, and delivering gas turbine generation, balance-of-plant, and medium-voltage infrastructure at scale, join us to help build the power plant that supports SpaceXAI’s high-density AI supercomputing facilities.

This is a full-time, onsite role based in Southaven, MS with relocation required.

### **RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Act as the senior construction representative for the Memphis / Southaven power generation plant, managing relationships with EPC contractors, OEMs, trade partners, and internal stakeholders.
* Oversee end-to-end project execution from pre-construction and site development through mechanical completion, commissioning, energization, and turnover to operations.
* Coordinate multiple vendors across civil, mechanical, process, and electrical scopes — including gas turbine packages, generators, fuel gas and BOP systems, e-houses, protection, and 34.5 kV collect/export.
* Proactively identify and mitigate risks to schedule, quality, safety, and operational readiness for first fire, synchronization, and commercial operation.
* Drive progress on generation and BOP construction, resolving field roadblocks so turbine, electrical, and process work stay sequenced and on track.
* Align construction schedules with SpaceXAI’s generation and infrastructure goals through cross-functional collaboration with engineering, operations, and central construction.
* Partner with engineering and operations to guide commissioning, start-up, and punchlist close-out; resolve issues that block energization or generation.
* Deliver regular updates using well-designed KPIs and dashboards for internal and external stakeholders.
* Ensure the project meets technical and quality standards in the base design, including plant electrical, mechanical, and process systems.
* Perform quantity take-offs and field verifications to monitor progress and support reporting.
* Manage change orders, identifying solutions to minimize unnecessary changes and control costs.
* Lead site safety initiatives, including LOTO, NFPA 70E / arc-flash, hot work, and a consistent construction safety culture.
* Adapt to evolving plant needs, supporting cross-functional partners as required, regardless of original job scope.

### **BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 5+ years of experience managing large-scale power generation, industrial plant, or other mission-critical construction projects.
* Deep expertise in construction management, commissioning, and site operations.
* Proven ability to lead on-site with authority and build trust with vendors, OEMs, partners, and technical teams.
* Experience managing large programs with multiple vendors and dynamic priorities.
* Strong communication skills across disciplines, from field updates to technical escalations.
* Thrives in solving complex, real-time problems with adaptability and composure.
* Be able to travel as needed.

### **PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Direct experience constructing or expanding gas turbine, combined-cycle, or similar generating plants.
* Experience with medium-voltage electrical construction (switchgear, transformers, e-houses, collect/export, protection) and mechanical / process BOP (fuel gas, water, compressed air).
* Experience with OEM turbine package installation, interconnection, and commissioning support.
* Previous experience on power plants serving data centers, hyperscale, or other high-reliability industrial loads.
* Familiarity with local Memphis / Mississippi building codes, utility interconnection, and plant permitting.
* Strong problem-solving skills and ability to handle complex construction challenges in an operating or near-operating plant.

* Comfortable working in an environment requiring exposure to noise.
* Available to work evenings and weekends, as the schedule varies depending on site operational needs; flexibility is required.
