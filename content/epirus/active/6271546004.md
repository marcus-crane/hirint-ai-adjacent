**About Epirus:** 

Epirus is a high-growth technology company developing solid-state, software-defined directed energy systems that enable unprecedented counter-electronics effects and power management solutions to optimize power efficiency in defense and commercial applications. With a constant emphasis on innovation, we are redefining the future of power to bring tomorrow’s capabilities to life, today. And that’s just the beginning. 

**Job Summary:** 

The **Senior Technical Writer & Curriculum Architect** at the P4 level provides advanced expertise in developing complex training systems, documentation frameworks, and user‑centered instructional materials for Epirus products. This role leads major training development initiatives, establishes documentation standards, and drives UX‑aligned workflows that improve operator comprehension and system usability. 

The P4 operates with high autonomy, partnering across engineering, UX, program management, and product teams to influence design decisions, guide content strategy, and ensure training artifacts support operational readiness. While not a people manager, this role provides mentorship, leads cross‑functional content reviews, and contributes to long‑term training ecosystem planning. 

**Responsibilities:** 

* Lead the development of advanced, multi‑layered training materials such as operator systems guides, interactive learning modules, maintenance curricula, and performance‑based training scenarios.

* Architect curriculum frameworks and documentation structures that scale across systems, product lines, and updates, ensuring consistency and long‑term maintainability.

* Provide high‑level UI/UX input by analyzing operator workflows, identifying usability gaps, and recommending improvements that shape interface clarity and behavior.

* Translate complex engineering, systems behavior, and program requirements into cohesive instructional strategies, visualizations, and technical materials.

* Oversee the implementation of structured content management workflows (GitHub, Git, Markdown) and guide best practices for versioning and documentation quality.

* Lead collaboration efforts with UI/UX designers, engineers, and SME teams to align terminology, user flows, and design decisions with training and usability objectives.

* Direct user research activities such as operator observation sessions, usability studies, and workflow analysis to inform training improvements and interface design.

* Ensure all training content conforms to advanced instructional design principles, technical accuracy standards, and compliance requirements.

* Facilitate and often lead cross‑functional review sessions for documentation, training concepts, UI elements, and system usability, providing authoritative guidance.

* Mentor junior and mid‑level writers, providing feedback on writing, curriculum design, content structure, and documentation workflows.

* Lead training and certification events, set certification standards, coordinate cross‑functional resources, and serve as the primary evaluator for operator proficiency.
