**About Epirus:** 

Epirus is a high-growth technology company developing solid-state, software-defined directed energy systems that enable unprecedented counter-electronics effects and power management solutions to optimize power efficiency in defense and commercial applications. With a constant emphasis on innovation, we are redefining the future of power to bring tomorrow’s capabilities to life, today. And that’s just the beginning. 

**Job Summary:** 

The Technical Writer & Curriculum Design Developer a P3 position creates clear, accurate, and user-focused training content that supports safe and effective operation of Epirus systems. This role develops operator guides, training modules, multimedia content, and instructional materials while also contributing user-focused insights that support interface clarity and training workflow design. 

The position works closely with engineers, UX contributors, and program teams to transform complex technical information into intuitive learning materials and operational workflows. The focus is on producing high‑quality training content, ensuring documentation accuracy, enhancing operator usability, and supporting UI considerations that improve end‑user understanding.  

**Responsibilities:** 

* Develop, write, and maintain technical training content, including operator guides, multimedia learning modules, maintenance procedures, and scenario-based exercises.

* Design and structure curriculum materials that align with system behavior, engineering updates, and program requirements.

* Support UI/UX input by gathering user insights, reviewing operator workflows, and contributing recommendations to improve interface clarity and usability.

* Translate technical engineering data into structured learning materials, graphics, diagrams, and labels that aid user understanding.

* Use GitHub, Git, Markdown, or structured content workflows to manage, track, and version technical documentation.

* Collaborate with UI/UX designers, engineers, and subject matter experts to align terminology, task flows, and interface elements with training objectives.

* Conduct user feedback sessions, basic usability testing, and workflow assessments to improve training and interface comprehension.

* Ensure all training content adheres to style guidelines, instructional design principles, and compliance requirements.

* Participate in cross-functional reviews related to documentation, training modules, UI concepts, and system usability.

* Independently conduct training and certification events, deliver instruction, assess operator competency, and recommend improvements to training and certification criteria.
