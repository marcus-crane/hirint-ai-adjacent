## **About Epirus**

Epirus is a high-growth technology company developing software-defined high-power microwave (HPM) systems with devastating counter-electronics effects on modern drone threats. The Epirus Leonidas HPM family of products, empowered by innovations in AI and microelectronics, is human safe and cost-effective. Distinguishing itself from lasers, Leonidas excels against drone swarms while also bending the cost curve back in DoD's favor - taking drone defeat costs from millions of dollars per drone to pennies on the dollar. 

### Job Summary

This is an exciting role in a growing company. The ideal candidate for the position is someone who has experience designing, verifying, and integrating state-of-the-art FPGA systems and embedded processing solutions for RF applications. In this role, you will work closely with a talented, multi-disciplinary engineering team to design and manufacture novel RF systems for a variety of platforms in a rapid development environment.

### Responsibilities

* Design and verification of RTL modules for FPGA applications
* Initial test and bring-up of custom FPGA circuit cards, including on-board peripherals such as memory, Ethernet, and sensors
* Automation of test environment (up to and including hardware-in-the-loop testing), lab equipment, and measurement devices
* Capturing design tradeoffs, state machine flow, system address maps, and block diagrams

### Basic Qualifications

* BS degree in Computer or Electrical engineering
* 2+ years of technical experience
* Familiarity implementing FPGA and microprocessor designs on embedded hardware
* Experience working with latest generation state of the art Xilinx/Intel Parts and associated tool sets (Vivado, Quartus)
* Understanding of common digital communication protocols (SPI, I2C, UART)
* Good written and oral communication skills
* Collaborative; capable of working across all levels of the organization
* Organized; comfortable working in a fast-paced, ever-changing environment
* US Citizen and/or Permanent Resident (future US Security Clearance will be required)

### Preferred Skills and Experience

* MS degree in Computer or Electrical engineering
* 3+ years of experience
* Experience implementing complex DSP algorithms on FPGAs
* Proficiency with design automation, scripting languages (TCL, Python, Perl, Matlab)
* Knowledge of advanced firmware verification testbench development (UVM, System Verilog)
* Skilled with advanced high-speed Verilog/VHDL designs including high-speed memory interfaces (DDR4, HBM) and high-speed SerDes IO and protocols (100GE, JESD, Interlaken)
* Understanding of digital and mixed signal PCB design including signal integrity analysis, FPGA sizing estimation, and IO selection
* Aerospace/Defense experience and/or military experience
* Current/Active Secret Security Clearance
