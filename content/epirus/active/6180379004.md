**Lead, Production**

**About Epirus**: Epirus is a high-growth technology company that develops solid-state, human-safe directed energy systems that enable unprecedented counter-electronics effects and power management solutions to enhance power efficiency in defense and commercial electronics. With a constant emphasis on innovation, we are redefining modern power systems to increase the velocity of change for our customers and bring tomorrow's capabilities to life, today. 

 

**Job objective:** This is an exciting role in a growing company. The ideal candidate for the position is someone from a hands-on manufacturing background has experience in electro-mechanical assembly, structural integration, and production supervision. This role will lead a group of manufacturing technicians within Epirus. This person will be responsible for day-to-day employee and work management, strategic training initiatives, verification of operating work instructions, and validation of builds to meet customer deliveries. This individual will also serve as a technical working lead, with 30% of the role also spent contributing to hardware build. 

 

 

**Duties:** 

* Manage a team of assembly technicians through daily coaching, annual reviews, and career development

* Set and achieve daily/weekly/monthly team objectives based on department priorities communicate these effectively to the team

* Organize daily work scopes by assigning clear priorities. Create operating procedures for standard work on the floor.

* Ensure safe use of manufacturing equipment and collaborate with calibration techs for equipment or tooling calibration

* Train employees on new skills and maintain a training matrix to track technician progression and qualify work

* Collaborate with the continuous improvement engineering team to implement new equipment and efficiency improvements

* Work in a fast-paced agile environment and adapt to evolving design and production challenges

* Interface with technicians, procurement, engineering, and leadership daily

* Drive accountability, respect, commitment, and speed with discipline to achieve Epirus’s manufacturing goals

 

**Required Qualifications:** 

* 2+ years experience in a supervisory role (or equivalent experience)

* High school diploma; degree in technical or engineering field

* Knowledge of Environmental, Health, and Safety elements, especially related to RF testing

* Self-motivated with a results-driven approach

* Good written and oral communication skills

* Collaborative and people-oriented; capable of working across all levels of the organization

* Advanced skills in MS Office at a minimum

* US Citizen and/or Permanent Resident (future security clearance may be required)

 

**Desired Qualifications:** 

* Aerospace/Defense experience and/or military experience

* Use of MES, ERP systems (iON and Oracle preferred)

* Knowledge of Production Systems and Lean Manufacturing elements

* Geometric dimensioning and tolerances

* Understanding of how MRP works in product development and production environments

* Experience with ISO9001/AS9100 quality management systems elements

* Sets good examples with attendance, positive attitude, and task engagement
