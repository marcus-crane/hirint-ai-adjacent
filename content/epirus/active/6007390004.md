**About Epirus** 

Epirus is a high-growth technology company dedicated to overcoming the asymmetric challenges inherent to the future of national security. Epirus' flagship product, Leonidas, is a software-defined system built using intelligent power management techniques which allow power-hungry systems to do more with less.  

 

**Job Summary:**    
Join Epirus as a **Supplier Quality Engineer** and play a pivotal role in shaping the reliability and performance of leading-edge technologies. In this highly collaborative role, you will drive supplier quality excellence across a dynamic, fast‑scaling supply base. You’ll partner closely with engineering, manufacturing, and supply chain teams to ensure our suppliers deliver the precision, innovation, and consistency that define Epirus products. 

This is an opportunity to directly influence product quality, accelerate supplier readiness, and embed quality into every stage of the product lifecycle while working in an environment where creative problem‑solving and continuous improvement are part of the culture. 

 

**Responsibilities:**  

* Build and lead scalable supplier quality programs, including audits, assessments, readiness reviews, and qualification activities, to support the company’s growth.

* Proactively identify and mitigate supplier risks to improve product quality and reliability from development through production.

* Assess and qualify new and existing suppliers to ensure alignment with Epirus’ technology roadmap and quality standards.

* Lead root-cause investigations and corrective action efforts that deliver measurable improvements in supplier performance.

* Monitor supplier performance, establish key performance indicators (KPIs), and champion continuous improvement initiatives.

* Collaborate cross-functionally to resolve quality issues, manage nonconforming material, and establish clear technical requirements.

* Support first article inspection (FAI), process validation, and the qualification of new suppliers and components.

* Provide technical guidance and coaching to strengthen supplier capabilities and manufacturing processes.

* Develop inspection strategies and measurement systems that improve defect-detection capabilities internally and across the supply base.

* Represent Supplier Quality throughout product development to ensure supplier readiness and the early integration of design-for-manufacturability (DFM) principles.

 

**Basic Qualifications:** 

* Bachelor’s degree in engineering or a related technical field.

* 1+ years of experience in supplier quality, manufacturing quality, supplier development, or manufacturing engineering.

* Hands-on experience with machined parts, sheet metal, PCBAs, cable assemblies, or electromechanical systems.

* Strong analytical, problem-solving, and root-cause analysis skills.

 

**Preferred Skills and Experience:** 

  

* 2+ years of experience in supplier quality, manufacturing quality, supplier development, or manufacturing engineering.

* ASQ Certified Quality Engineer (CQE) or a comparable certification.

* Knowledge of AS9100, AS9102, ISO 9001, and applicable IPC standards.

* Experience working with enterprise resource planning (ERP) and manufacturing execution systems (MES).

* Experience in aerospace, defense, automotive, medical device, or similarly regulated industries.

* Familiarity with process failure mode and effects analysis (PFMEA), control plans, and measurement system capability analysis.

* Working knowledge of geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T) and the ability to interpret engineering drawings.

* Proficiency with inspection tools and outputs, including calipers, micrometers, height gauges, and coordinate measuring machine (CMM) reports.

* Experience using data-analysis tools to identify trends, monitor performance, and define meaningful metrics.

* Excellent communication and stakeholder-management skills, with the ability to influence suppliers in a fast-paced environment.

 

 

Additional Requirements: 

* Ability to travel up to 40% as needed to supplier and manufacturing sites.
