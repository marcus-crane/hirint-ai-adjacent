**About Epirus** 

Epirus is a high-growth technology company developing solid-state, software-defined directed energy systems that enable unprecedented counter-electronics effects and power management solutions to optimize power efficiency in defense and commercial applications. With a constant emphasis on innovation, we are redefining the future of power to bring tomorrow’s capabilities to life, today. And that’s just the beginning. 

**Job Summary:** In this role, you will design electromechanical assemblies, mechanisms, and large structures – following designs through their full lifecycle. Successful candidates use their creativity and engineering knowledge to develop innovative mechanical solutions as we push the boundaries of high-power microwave technology. You will have a hands-on role in designing, building, and testing mechanical designs ranging from small electronics packages to large actuated systems. 

**Responsibilities:** 

* Design and build RF-phased array structures and electronic packages as a key member of a cross-functional product design team

* Perform thermal and structural analyses to validate designs and ensure robust, reliable designs.

* Design cable harnesses for large electronics assemblies and vehicle-mounted systems

* Create CAD models and detailed manufacturing drawings, including tolerance analysis and GD&T in compliance with ASME Y14.5

* Collaborate with the operations team to transition designs into production, driving quality and manufacturability

* Develop and execute environmental testing to validate product performance in real world conditions

**Basic Qualifications:** 

* Undergraduate degree in Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, or equivalent

* 2+ years of relevant experience including hands-on design and build experience

* Demonstrated ability to design complex mechanical assemblies in a highly collaborative interdisciplinary team environment

* Strong written and oral communication skills

* Proficiency in mechanical design packages such as SolidWorks or equivalent

**Preferred Skills and Experience:** 

* Demonstrated comfort with hands-on build and test of mechanical systems

* Demonstrated experience in applying geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T)

* Experience designing harnesses or cables for complex electronic systems

* Ability to understand and contribute to electrical schematics, diagrams and interconnects

* Collaborative; capable of working across all levels of the organization and in multidisciplinary design teams

* Comfortable working in a fast-paced, ever-changing environment
