**About Epirus** 

Epirus is a high-growth technology company developing software-defined high-power microwave (HPM) systems with devastating counter-electronics effects on modern drone threats. The Epirus Leonidas HPM family of products, empowered by innovations in AI and microelectronics, is human safe and cost-effective. Distinguishing itself from lasers, Leonidas excels against drone swarms while also bending the cost curve back in DoD's favor - taking drone defeat costs from millions of dollars per drone to pennies on the dollar. 

 

**Job Summary:**    
We are seeking a Senior Product Quality Engineer to join our innovative, fast-growing defense technology company. This role focuses on our Small and Medium System product lines and requires an agile, collaborative, hands-on professional with experience in assembly, integration, and test of complex electromechanical systems. 

You will serve as a key quality partner throughout the product lifecycle, working closely with engineering, manufacturing, supply chain, and program management teams to deliver robust, reliable, and mission-ready products that meet the demanding requirements of defense applications. 

 

**Responsibilities:**  

  

* Own quality planning and execution across the product lifecycle, from design through production and field deployment, using DFMEA, PFMEA, and control plans.

* Develop and implement quality strategies for inspection, assembly, integration, and test of small and medium complex systems.

* Own quality gates and approval processes to ensure readiness at each phase transition.

* Champion Design for Quality (DfQ) across engineering teams and apply lessons learned across products and programs.

* Define and monitor quality and reliability KPIs, analyze performance trends, and drive measurable product and process improvements.

* Partner with engineering to define critical-to-quality (CTQ) characteristics and key product parameters and establish variation monitoring plans.

* Drive failure reporting, analysis, and corrective action system (FRACAS) execution through timely closure.

* Lead nonconformance management, root cause analysis, and corrective actions to prevent recurrence of assembly and integration issues.

* Own field quality issues from failure investigation and stakeholder communication through corrective-action implementation and closure.

* Support product qualification, first article inspection (FAI), and verification and validation efforts in partnership with test engineering.

* Build a culture of proactive quality ownership and continuous improvement.

 

 

 

**Basic Qualifications:** 

 

* Bachelor's degree in Engineering or related technical discipline.

* 3+ years of experience in quality, manufacturing, or engineering for complex mechanical or electromechanical systems.

* Experience leading cross-functional teams to resolve quality issues, prevent recurrence, and deliver sustained improvements.

* Demonstrated experience owning and executing root cause and failure analyses through successful corrective action implementation and closure using structured problem-solving methodologies.

* Demonstrated experience owning and executing New Product Introduction (NPI) from development through successful transition to production for complex products.

 

**Preferred Skills and Experience:** 

  

* Master’s degree in Engineering or a related technical discipline.

* 5+ years of quality, manufacturing, or engineering experience with complex mechanical or electromechanical systems.

* ASQ Certified Quality Engineer (CQE), Certified Quality Auditor (CQA), or Six Sigma Green Belt or Black Belt certification.

* Working knowledge of electronics, complex assemblies, system integration, and test.

* Experience developing and implementing APQP processes across the product lifecycle.

* Proficiency with data analysis and visualization tools, including Excel, Tableau, Power BI, or similar, to track KPIs and drive decisions.

* Experience using ERP, MES, and QMS platforms to manage quality and production data.

* Working knowledge of quality management system standards, including ISO 9001, AS9100, or equivalent.

* Hands-on experience applying APQP tools, including DFMEA, PFMEA, and control plans, to mitigate product and process risk.

* Strong communication and influencing skills to align stakeholders and drive improvements in fast-paced environments.

 

Additional Requirements: 

* Ability to travel up to 10% as needed.
