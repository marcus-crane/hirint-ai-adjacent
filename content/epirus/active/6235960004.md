**About Epirus** 

Epirus is a high-growth technology company dedicated to overcoming the asymmetric challenges inherent to the future of national security. Epirus' flagship product, Leonidas, is a software-defined system built using intelligent power management techniques which allow power-hungry systems to do more with less.  

  

**Job Summary:**We are looking for an experienced structural analysis engineer to innovate on the next generation of HPM products. In this role, you will work closely with a talented engineering team to design & analyze cutting edge electronic and mechanical assemblies to survive in harsh environments. Ideal candidates will have experience following their designs / analyses through its complete lifecycle from conceptualization through test and verification. 

 

**Responsibilities:** 

* Design / analyze structures for mounting RF phased arrays and other electronic packages

* Perform structural finite element analysis (FEA) on complex mechanical structures and electronic assemblies for a range of solution and load types, including static, modal, and structural dynamics (vibration and shock).

* Build finite element models and perform hand calculations to verify structural integrity across Epirus’s product lines.

* Provide mentoring and serve as mechanical design / analyst lead on product design teams

* Collaborate with a cross-functional team to develop requirements, design, and build electromechanical systems that survive harsh environments encountered during operation and transport

 

**Basic Qualifications:** 

* BS degree in Mechanical, Aerospace, Structural or similar engineering field.

* 8+ years of experience in structural/stress design & analysis of metallic structures.

* Expert level comfort with Finite Element Analysis and structural hand calculations.

* Experience with structural analysis and pre/post processing software packages such as Nastran, FEMAP, Ansys, Patran or equivalent.

* Experience analyzing systems under static loading, shock, and vibrational environments including random vibration.

* Experience with CAD and practical application of GD&T.

* Experience with structural test and qualification of mechanical systems.

 

**Preferred Skills and Experience:** 

* Experience performing stress analysis of electronic systems such as circuit card assemblies.

* Experience designing / analyzing actuated mechanical systems including azimuth and elevation pointing mechanisms.

* Good written and oral communication skills

* Collaborative; capable of working across all levels of the organization

* Organized; comfortable working in a fast-paced, ever-changing environment

* Familiarity with MIL-STD-810 and associated harsh environmental considerations
