**About Epirus** 

Epirus is a high-growth technology company developing software-defined high-power microwave (HPM) systems with devastating counter-electronics effects on modern drone threats. The Epirus Leonidas HPM family of products, empowered by innovations in AI and microelectronics, is human safe and cost-effective. Distinguishing itself from lasers, Leonidas excels against drone swarms while also bending the cost curve back in DoD's favor - taking drone defeat costs from millions of dollars per drone to pennies on the dollar. 

 

**Job Summary:**    
We are seeking a Principal Product Quality Engineer to join our innovative, fast-growing defense technology company. This role focuses on our Large System product lines and requires an agile, collaborative, hands-on leader with deep experience in assembly, integration, and test of complex electromechanical systems. 

As the technical authority for product quality, you will define, develop, and execute quality processes across the entire product lifecycle. You'll partner closely with engineering, manufacturing, supply chain, and program management teams to deliver robust, reliable, and mission-ready products that meet the demanding requirements of defense applications. 

 

**Responsibilities:**  

* Own product quality strategy and execution from concept through production and field deployment to improve assembly, integration, and test performance

* Establish quality gates and approval criteria that ensure phase readiness and mitigate execution risk

* Lead advanced quality deployment (DFMEA, PFMEA, and control plans) to reduce risk and accelerate production readiness

* Architect inspection and test strategies with clear acceptance criteria for complex, large-scale systems

* Partner with engineering leaders to define and key product parameters that protect performance and manufacturability

* Support development of reliability strategy and validation plans that demonstrate mission-ready performance

* Lead FRACAS governance to accelerate failure resolution, verify corrective actions, and prevent recurrence

* Direct nonconformance, root cause, and corrective action initiatives that improve assembly and integration yield

* Drive enterprise quality initiatives and metrics that achieve product quality and reliability objectives

* Serve as the product quality authority for customers, leadership reviews, and regulatory audits

* Mobilize cross-functional teams to resolve high-visibility quality issues and deliver sustained improvement

* Mentor engineers and institutionalize proactive quality ownership across the organization

 

 

**Basic Qualifications:** 

 

* Bachelor's degree in Engineering or related technical discipline

* 6+ years of progressive experience in quality, manufacturing, or engineering for complex mechanical or electromechanical systems

* Proven success leading enterprise-wide resolution of critical quality issues and delivering measurable gains in quality and reliability

* Proven leadership in NPI and scaling complex products from development to production

* Expertise in root cause and failure analysis, reliability testing, and structured problem-solving

 

**Preferred Skills and Experience:** 

  

* Master's degree in Engineering or related discipline.

* 8+ years of progressive experience in quality, manufacturing, or engineering for complex mechanical or electromechanical systems

* ASQ CQE, Certified Quality Auditor, or Six Sigma Green/Black Belt certification with demonstrated enterprise impact

* Strong experience working in electronics, complex assemblies, integration, and test

* Expertise applying reliability modeling and statistical analysis to guide risk-based decisions

* Leadership implementing and optimizing ERP, MES, and QMS platforms to scale quality execution

* Expertise governing ISO 9001, AS9100, or comparable systems to ensure compliance and audit readiness

* Strong experience deploying APQP tools, including DFMEA, PFMEA, and control plans, to reduce lifecycle risk

* Executive-level communication and influence that align stakeholders and drive enterprise quality outcomes

 

 

Additional Requirements: 

* Ability to travel up to 10% as needed.
