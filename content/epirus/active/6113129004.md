## **About Epirus**

Epirus is a high-growth technology company dedicated to overcoming the asymmetric challenges inherent to the future of national security. Epirus' flagship product, Leonidas, is a software-defined system built using intelligent power management techniques which allow power-hungry systems to do more with less.  

 

### Job Summary:

This is an exciting role an innovative, high-growth defense technology company. We are seeking a motivated **Associate Test Engineer, Electromagnetic Laboratory**to join test engineering team at our multi-client testing facility. In this role, you will develop and execute RF, Antenna Over-the-Air (OTA), and Electromagnetic Compatibility (EMC) testing, helping clients troubleshoot failures in real time. The ideal candidate blends deep electromagnetic testing technical expertise with excellent project management and client-facing communication. 

## Responsibilities:

* Develop and execute complex RF, Antenna OTA, and EMC measurements and tests to client statements of work and aerospace/defense standards.
* Develop automation scripts (Python, MATLAB) to optimize test time and streamline data collection.
* Provide active engineering support to clients to assist in troubleshooting failures and analyze data to produce compliance-ready test reports and certificates.
* Interface regularly with the larger test engineering organization in Torrance, CA to further refine test methodology and measurement techniques.
* Support the calibration and preventative maintenance schedules for all laboratory assets.

## Basic Qualifications:

* B.S. or M.S. degree in Electrical Engineering or equivalent.
* 1-3 years of experience (with B.S.) or 0-1 years of experience (with M.S.) in Test Engineering.
* Familiar with electrical test and measurement equipment such as: Multimeters, Oscilloscopes, Function Generators, Power Supplies.
* Theoretical understanding of RF, Antenna OTA, and EMC test and measurement principles.
* Understanding of aerospace and defense testing standards including MIL-STD-461 and DO-160.
* Experience developing automated test stands for various types of hardware.
* Strong written and oral communication skills.
* Organized; comfortable working in a fast-paced, ever-changing environment.
* US Citizen or Permanent Resident (future security clearance may be required).

## Preferred Skills and Experience:

* Experience with RF, Antenna OTA, and EMC Test.
* Highly proficient in using RF test and measurement equipment such as Vector Network Analyzers (VNA), Spectrum Analyzers, High-Speed Oscilloscopes, Waveform & Signal Generators, Power Meters, AutoCal and SOLT Calibration.
* Experience with client-facing project management, particularly communicating changes to schedule and technical scope.
* Experience working in a multi-client or commercial test house environment.
* Deep knowledge of other defense and commercial testing standards including MIL-STD-810, CISPR, IEC 61000, and ISO (automotive EMC).
* Comfortable with object-orientated programming using Python.
