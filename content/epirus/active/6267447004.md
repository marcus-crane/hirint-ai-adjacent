**About Epirus** 

Epirus is a high-growth technology company developing solid-state, software-defined directed energy systems that enable unprecedented counter-electronics effects and power management solutions to optimize power efficiency in defense and commercial applications. With a constant emphasis on innovation, we are redefining the future of power to bring tomorrow’s capabilities to life, today. And that’s just the beginning. 

**Job Summary**

Epirus is seeking a strategic, execution-driven Senior Director of Field Operations & Sustainment (FOS) to lead our global customer-facing deployment, support, and validation operations. As a pioneer in software-defined, high-power microwave (HPM) national security solutions—including our flagship Leonidas platform—we are scaling rapidly to support U.S. Department of Defense (DoD), Department of Homeland Security (DHS), and international allied customers. 

High visibility role, reporting into Chief Operating Officer (COO), this role oversees the end-to-end post-manufacturing lifecycle, serving as Epirus’s Deployment Coordinator while leading our five core operational pillars: Field System Readiness & Deployment, Field Service & Technical Operations, Systems Training & Documentation, Global Delivery & Parts Logistics, and Customer Demonstration & Drone Flight Test Operations. You will own full budget development and P&L management for the FOS organization, aligning operational capacity, workforce phasing, and lifecycle supportability with planned sales pipelines and business opportunities. 

**Responsibilities**

Organizational Leadership, Capacity Planning & Financial Ownership: 

* Organization & Workforce Management: Set operating priorities, build leadership depth, and phase hiring and qualifications against concurrent program needs.

* Budget & Lifecycle Cost Ownership: Develop and manage the full departmental budget based on planned sales and business opportunities. Use field, depot, and support case data to assess reliability, maintainability, spares demand, warranty exposure, and total cost of ownership—feeding findings back into repair, replacement, and redesign decisions.

* Demand & Capacity Planning: Balance resources and assets against customer demands and the need to provide live fire demonstrations and exercise operations. Advance site evaluation and planned to ensure successful execution.

* Contract Execution & Operational Planning: Support the development of Concepts of Operations (CONOPs) for contracted systems. Represent FOS in weekly contract execution meetings, track readiness and fielding actions, and raise risks that could impact contract milestones.
* Cross-Functional Alignment: Partner tightly with Engineering, Program Management (PMO), Sales, Business Development, Marketing, Manufacturing, and Supply Chain to ensure seamless execution from production through field sustainment.

Core Functional Areas 

* *Field System Readiness & Deployment:*

* Serve as Epirus’s Deployment Coordinator, owning companywide deployment schedules, personnel and equipment movements, rotations, and redeployment across functional teams.

* Oversee post-manufacturing system readiness testing and lead pre-deployment site surveys and reviews with customers prior to shipment to validate site readiness and infrastructure requirements.

* *Field Service & Technical Operations:*

* The Field service and technical operations teams are composed of field service technicians and technical operations engineers support all customer repairs, system testing, and on-call customer support, 24/7. They also will manage all service depots and remanufacturing process for component level service.

* FSR Management & Scheduling: Manage Field Service Representatives (FSRs) technicians, supporting, scheduling, deployment rotations, customer coverage, and training across CONUS and OCONUS operations.

* Field Technical Operations Engineering: Manage technical operations engineers to work across the full life cycle of the produce, influencing designs supporting serviceability. Lead field integration, verification, and issue resolution with Engineering, feeding field defects and corrective actions directly into ERAD and release planning.  Implement system upgrades and warranty management.

* 24/7 Product Support Desk: Establish and manage the 24/7 Product Support Desk operating model, governing case intake, severity-based triage, Engineering escalation, repair, verified restoration, and knowledge capture while enforcing strict response and turnaround expectations.

* Depot Repair & Remanufacturing: Lead system fielding, contracted support coverage, and regional service depots. Build depot repair, refurbishment, and component-level repair capabilities, sizing spares and regional warehouse capacity to customer demand and determining optimal utilization of customer-site repair, fly-in support, or in-country depots.

* *Systems Training & Technical Documentation:*

* Govern technical manuals, maintenance guides, courseware, revision control, instructor qualification, training delivery, and training/certification records.

* Build and scale structured customer training programs to certify domestic and allied personnel to operate and maintain Epirus systems, aligning training updates with product releases and deployment schedules.

* *Global Delivery & Parts Logistics:*

* Oversee all company logistics across CONUS and OCONUS, managing global shipments of primary assets, support equipment, and spare parts.

* Ensure strict adherence to customs regulations, international trade compliance, ITAR/EAR export controls, and defense shipping protocols.

* *Customer Demonstration & Drone Flight Test Operations:*

* Oversee UAS Operations, managing a dedicated team of drone pilots and operators who own, maintain, and operate Epirus’s internal fleet of drones.
* Partner with Engineering, Program Management, Business Development, and Marketing to plan and conduct UAS flights for testing, live-fire counter-UAS demonstrations, and customer drone-kill validations, including range coordination and test-asset readiness.

**Basic Qualifications**

* Undergraduate degree in Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering, or equivalent.

* 2+ years of relevant experience including hands-on design and build

**Preferred Skills and Experience**

* Demonstrated ability to design complex mechanical assemblies in a highly collaborative interdisciplinary team.

* Strong written and oral communication skills

* Proficiency in mechanical design packages such as SolidWorks or equivalent

* Demonstrated comfort with hands-on build and test of mechanical systems.

* Demonstrated experience in applying geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T).

* Experience designing harnesses or cables for complex electronic systems

* Ability to understand and contribute to electrical schematics, diagrams and interconnects

* Collaborative; capable of working across all levels of the organization and in multidisciplinary design teams.

* Comfortable working in a fast-paced, ever-changing environment
