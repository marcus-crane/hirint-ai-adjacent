## **About Epirus**

Epirus is a high-growth technology company dedicated to overcoming the asymmetric challenges inherent to the future of national security. Epirus' flagship product, Leonidas, is a software-defined system built using intelligent power management techniques which allow power-hungry systems to do more with less.  

### Job Summary

We are seeking a Senior Manager of Manufacturing to lead a high-performance production organization responsible for delivering world-class hardware at scale. This role is ideal for a leader who thrives in fast-paced, engineering-intense environments and who brings a strong bias toward action, continuous improvement, and rigorous operational discipline. You will own day-to-day factory operations, drive throughput and yield, develop manufacturing talent, and partner cross-functionally to ensure we build products with unmatched quality, speed, and reliability. You will operate at the intersection of hardware, automation, quality, and operational excellence. 

## Responsibilities

**Operational Leadership** 

* Lead all manufacturing operations including production, assembly, testing, material flow, and daily execution.
* Translate company milestones into executable manufacturing plans with clear priorities and accountability.
* Drive throughput, yields, schedule adherence, and cost targets through disciplined execution.
* Build a culture of urgency, ownership, and problem-solving consistent with high-growth engineering organizations.

**Production Management** 

* Optimize production lines for takt time, labor efficiency, and throughput.
* Implement scalable processes that reduce variability and enable repeatable, high-quality builds.
* Monitor KPIs and lead daily/weekly operational reviews to ensure performance to plan.
* Serve as the escalation point for production issues and drive rapid resolution of blockers.

**Team Leadership & Development** 

* Build, mentor, and develop a high-performing manufacturing team across supervisors, leads, and operators.
* Implement robust training, certification, and cross-training programs to maximize capability and flexibility.
* Cultivate a safety-first culture with a deep commitment to continuous improvement.

**Manufacturing Engineering & Process Excellence** 

* Partner with ME, Quality, Supply Chain, and Design Engineering to scale up new products and processes for production.
* Lead factory readiness for new product introduction (NPI), including line layout, tooling, equipment, and staffing.
* Drive systematic root cause analysis and corrective actions (DMAIC, 8D, A3).
* Champion process documentation and standardization through high-quality work instructions and manufacturing procedures.

**Cross-Functional Collaboration** 

* Work closely with Supply Chain on material readiness, constraints, and supplier quality issues.
* Partner with Quality to maintain world-class standards and drive improving FPY and DPPM.
* Collaborate with Engineering to ensure manufacturability (DFM) and rapid design iteration.

## Basic Qualifications

* Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Manufacturing, Operations, or equivalent; advanced degree preferred.
* 6+ years of progressively responsible experience in hardware manufacturing, with 4+ years leading teams.
* Proven success scaling production in fast-paced, engineering-driven environments.
* Strong background in lean manufacturing, 5S, Kaizen, and structured problem-solving.
* Experience with work order systems, MES, ERP, and real-time production reporting tools.
* Demonstrated ability to lead complex cross-functional initiatives with urgency and precision.

## Preferred Qualifications

* Experience in aerospace, defense, EVs, robotics, or high-complexity electromechanical hardware.
* Background in automation, robotics integration, or advanced manufacturing technologies.
* Prior experience improving manufacturability through tooling, fixtures, or process optimization.
* Strong data skills: ability to interpret KPIs and use data to guide decisions.
* Ability to scale teams from early-stage prototype operations to full-rate production.

**Additional Requirements**

* Ability to travel up to 10%, including potential international travel.
* Flexibility to adapt to a fast-moving and ever-changing environment.
