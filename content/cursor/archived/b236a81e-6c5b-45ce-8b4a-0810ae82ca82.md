**SOFTWARE ENGINEER, STORAGE**

As a Software Engineer on the Storage team at SpaceXSI, you'll own the data layer that underpins every product surface: the databases, caches, and the strategy for how teams provision, query, and scale their data stores. Millions of developers depend on Cursor every day, and the future of our storage architecture is one of the highest-leverage problems at the company: get it right, and every team ships faster, every product surface gets more reliable, and SpaceXSI can scale to meet explosive demand. You'll design and execute the path to a robust, multi-database topology built for that growth.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Design the next-generation data architecture: evolving our storage layer into a partitioned, resilient topology that keeps pace with SpaceXSI's rapid growth
* Build query attribution and guardrails: instrumenting every database query by service, catching bad patterns before they hit production, and making it impossible to ship problematic queries without review
* Define the "when to use what" strategy for data stores: creating clear guidance and golden pathways so every team picks the right engine for their workload without second-guessing
* Own cache infrastructure end-to-end: reliability, capacity planning, and patterns that let product teams move fast without worrying about cache correctness

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Experience with relational databases at scale (e.g., Postgres, MySQL, or similar OLTP systems). Experience building and scaling databases or data infrastructure. Understanding of database performance, reliability, and caching.

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience tackling database sharding, migration, or decomposition problems in production environments
* Understand the tradeoffs between different storage engines and can help teams make the right choices for their workloads
* Care about operational excellence: backups, monitoring, query performance, and capacity planning are things you think about proactively
* Strong software engineering fundamentals and enjoying building systems that other engineers depend on

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* If there appears to be a fit, we'll reach out to schedule 2-3 short technicals. After, we'll schedule an onsite in our office, where you'll work on a small project, discuss ideas, and meet the team
