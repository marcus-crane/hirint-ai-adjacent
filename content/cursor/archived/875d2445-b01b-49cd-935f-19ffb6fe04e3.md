**ENGINEERING MANAGER, AGENT & PRODUCT SECURITY**

Cursor's agents work inside the codebases of some of the most security-conscious engineering organizations in the world, holding credentials, reading untrusted content, running tools, and shipping changes. Today they act on authority delegated per task. Where we're headed, they'll work more like coworkers: autonomous over long horizons, holding standing responsibilities, initiating work, and collaborating with people and other agents. The security models that the future needs, identity and accountability for autonomous workers, standing authority rather than per-task permission, agent-to-agent trust, oversight that scales with always-on autonomy, mostly don't exist yet.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Chart the path from supervised agents to trusted autonomous ones: define the security primitives that enable agent autonomy, and lead the team that builds them
* Engineer the isolation that makes Cursor safe to run inside customers' most valuable codebases: tenancy, workspace, secret, and data protections designed for real adversaries
* Build the authority infrastructure autonomous agents need: identity, scoped permissions, policy enforcement, and secure tool execution, so an agent can hold real responsibility with verifiable limits and full accountability
* Build customer-facing security controls that are understandable, usable, and strong enough for Cursor's most security-sensitive customers, with crisp success metrics for security outcomes
* Hire, coach, and grow a high-performing security engineering team while staying technically close to the work: write and review code where useful, and keep execution fast and technically grounded

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Experience leading engineering teams that ship production systems with high security, reliability, or platform stakes

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Wants a problem space where much of the answer isn't written down yet
* Experience leading engineering teams shipping production systems with high security, reliability, or platform stakes, and you want a problem space where much of the answer isn't written down yet
* Strong product and systems judgment: you can reason from user workflows to architecture, threat models, enforcement points, and operational tradeoffs, and you see security as something that enables product capability, rather than only restricting it
* Understand modern application security, authorization, tenancy, identity, secrets, data isolation, and secure-by-default platform design
* Good instincts about AI agents: how they use tools, where authority should come from, how prompt injection and exfiltration show up in practice, and how to build mitigations that hold up against creative adversaries
* Comfortable operating in ambiguity: you can set strategy where precedent is thin, commit to a direction, and stay close enough to the implementation to know when to change it
* Able to partner well across product, infrastructure, ML, and customer-facing teams

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Onsite role based in New York or Palo Alto
