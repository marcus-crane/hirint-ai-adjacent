**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Architect, implement, and deploy projects end-to-end to build enterprise-grade features that help large organizations adopt and scale with Cursor

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Software engineering experience building products. Experience building and shipping software features end-to-end.

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* An entrepreneurial spirit and a love of creating outsized business impact
* Want to be at the frontier of AI transformation with the best companies in the world
* Passionate about building great products that blend excellent engineering with a taste for models and design
* A propensity for creative ideas and a knack for making powerful tools without compromising their ease-of-use

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**
