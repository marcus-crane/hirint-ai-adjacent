**SOFTWARE ENGINEER, MODEL ROUTING & INFERENCE**

As a Software Engineer on the Model Routing & Inference team at SpaceXSI, you'll build the inference platform that powers every AI interaction in the product. This team owns the full inference path: making Cursor's AI faster, more reliable, and more cost-effective at a scale few teams in the world get to operate at. Every agent session, every tab completion, and every chat message flows through your stack.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Build and evolve our inference gateway, a single abstraction over every provider's API semantics, so model onboarding becomes a config change
* Design intelligent cross-provider failover so no single provider outage causes user-visible degradation
* Design routing backpressure and admission control so traffic spikes don't cascade into providers

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Experience building high-throughput, low-latency distributed systems (e.g., inference serving, traffic routing, or real-time data pipelines)
* Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering, math, or scientific discipline; OR 2+ years of professional experience building software in lieu of a degree

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Comfortable reasoning about cost/performance tradeoffs at scale (GPU utilization, provider economics, capacity planning)
* Strong software engineering fundamentals and enjoying shipping production systems that handle millions of requests

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* If there appears to be a fit, we'll reach out to schedule 2-3 short technicals. After, we'll schedule an onsite in our office, where you'll work on a small project, discuss ideas, and meet the team
