**EXECUTIVE ASSISTANT - JAPAN**

We're hiring an Executive Assistant to support the Japan President. This is a high-impact role focused on enabling clarity, efficiency, and execution across a fast-moving go-to-market organization. The ideal candidate is highly organized, an excellent business writer, and confident enough to actively manage competing priorities across complex calendars and workflows.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Calendar & meeting management: Own complex scheduling across multiple senior leaders and stakeholders; prioritize ruthlessly so time aligns with business impact; push back when needed to protect focus and optimize leadership bandwidth
* Travel & expenses: Coordinate domestic and international travel logistics; manage expenses through Ramp with accuracy and timeliness
* Business writing: Draft, edit, and refine internal communications, presentations, and leadership updates; help leaders articulate strategy, summarize meetings, and drive follow-ups; bring structure and clarity to ambiguous inputs and fast-moving discussions
* GTM operating rhythm: Help keep key meetings, planning cycles, and cross-functional touchpoints on track
* Recruiting support: Partner closely with Recruiting to ensure interview days run smoothly and efficiently
* Offsites & events: Support offsite planning for the GTM teams, including venue sourcing, hotel contract negotiations, and travel coordination; oversee event catering and restaurant reservations, managing budgets and vendor relationships to deliver seamless team-building experiences

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 6+ years of experience in an Executive Assistant or similar role
* Fluency in both Japanese and English

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience supporting senior leaders in a fast-paced, tech-forward environment
* Experience supporting go-to-market (Sales, CS, RevOps, or Field Engineering) teams
* Exceptional business writing skills
* Judgment and prioritization: knowing what matters and acting accordingly, with the assertiveness and confidence to push back to improve outcomes
* High level of organization and attention to detail
* Slack-native, AI-forward working style: comfort in fast, async environments and use of modern tools (e.g., ChatGPT, Notion AI) to move faster and produce higher-quality work
* Growth mindset with a willingness to continuously improve systems and processes

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Hybrid role (Japan)
