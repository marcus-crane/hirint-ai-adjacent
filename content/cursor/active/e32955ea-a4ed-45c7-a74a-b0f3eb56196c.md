**REGIONAL DIRECTOR, GLOBAL GOVERNMENT SALES**

You'll build and lead the team that brings SpaceXSI to governments in your region. You'll hire the Account Executives, set territory and account strategy, and put in place the operating rhythm and playbooks that make government selling repeatable. You'll also stay hands-on and personally lead the largest pursuits in the region with your team. You'll represent government customers' needs to Product, Security and Policy, and own the regional relationship with Starlink and other SpaceX teams so that joint pursuits run as one coordinated effort.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* **Build the team.** Hire, develop and coach Global Government Account Executives, and set a high bar for who joins.
* **Own regional pipeline and bookings.** Set the regional sales strategy and own team pipeline generation and forecast accuracy, through regular forecast, pipeline and deal reviews.
* **Lead the largest pursuits.** Work directly with senior government officials on the region's biggest deals, and coach your team through live sales cycles.
* **Make government selling repeatable.** Build playbooks for tenders, procurement frameworks, security and sovereignty reviews, partner teaming and post-signature governance, and use them to speed up how fast new Account Executives ramp.
* **Lead the SpaceX partnership and partner ecosystem in the region.** Set joint account plans with Starlink and other SpaceX teams, and develop the systems integrators and local partners the region relies on.
* **Win early reference customers in new countries,** and bring field insight into positioning, deployment models and pricing.

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 3+ years leading a team, including hiring and developing sellers
* Experience owning a team's pipeline generation and forecast against plan
* Experience personally closing complex public-sector deals and selling to or advising senior government officials (agency head, deputy minister or equivalent)

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Hands-on leadership: you stay close to live deals and turn sellers into consistent performers
* Playbooks and operating rhythm you've built and run
* Fluency in public-sector procurement, contracting and compliance in more than one country
* Enough technical fluency to hold credible AI conversations with technical and senior government audiences
* Joint pursuits run across a complex, multi-company organization
* Experience selling AI, cloud, software or data platforms to the public sector
* Experience building a regional sales team or market entry from scratch
* Relationships across foreign affairs ministries, regional bodies, or defense and security communities
* Experience selling integrated hardware, connectivity and software solutions to governments
* Professional fluency in Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic or French

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Must be willing to travel approximately 30% of the time, mostly internationally.
