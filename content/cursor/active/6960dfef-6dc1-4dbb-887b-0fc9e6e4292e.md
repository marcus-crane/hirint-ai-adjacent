**FORWARD DEPLOYED STRATEGIST**

We're looking for a Forward Deployed Strategist to own our most strategic customer engagements—from initial workshop through delivery. Working in a pod with 1–2 FDEs, you'll partner with executive sponsors to identify high-value use cases, scope tightly focused projects, and stay accountable for outcomes. You'll remain hands-on at the configuration layer while translating technical builds into measurable business results and driving adoption—all while flywheeling valuable feedback into our product offerings.  
This role requires technical credibility in agentic coding and LLMs, executive presence to earn boardroom trust, and the judgment to keep engagements scoped tight, delivered fast, and worth repeating.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Partner with executive sponsors to translate ambiguous business problems into concrete, time-boxed projects with clear ROI, validating technical feasibility through discovery and architecture reviews.
* Own the configuration layer of the technical build—workflows, integrations, and platform tooling—partnering with FDEs on deeper production work while monitoring feasibility and adjusting scope as needed.
* Serve as the primary executive point of contact, selling the vision and plan to C-suite stakeholders and translating between business outcomes and technical implementation.
* Identify expansion opportunities during delivery and escalate product gaps and customer pain to Product and Engineering to shape the roadmap.
* Build reusable playbooks that strengthen the broader FDE organization over time.

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 6+ years in Solutions Architecture, Deployment Strategy, technical consulting, or Engagement Manager roles owning scoping and delivery with technical teams on AI-production workflows.
* Strong understanding of agentic coding and LLMs in production: how they behave, where they fail, and what resilient workflows look like.
* Shipped AI-native workflows past the prototype stage and can distinguish real production constraints from nice-to-haves.

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Hands-on comfort with SpaceXSI workflows, integrations, and platform config; able to read Python/JS-TS to partner effectively with FDEs.
* Domain depth in FSI, insurance, telecom, high-tech, healthcare/life sciences, or semiconductor.
* Experience building out agentic systems and model training.
* Ex-founder experience is a strong plus
