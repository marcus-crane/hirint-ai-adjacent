**LIFECYCLE GROWTH MANAGER**

Lifecycle Growth's job is to get more people using Grok, Grok Bot, X, SpaceXSI and the API every day, and to turn that use into revenue. We own everything after signup: getting people to value, converting them to paid, growing their teams and keeping them coming back. We do it with lifecycle marketing, in-product messaging and a steady stream of experiments, and much of the work runs on agents we build ourselves.

This isn't a traditional marketing role. You'll do the work of a product manager, a product marketer and a marketing engineer, often in the same day. You'll own lifecycle growth for one or more of our products. You'll figure out where the biggest opportunity is, design the program, build it and show whether it actually moved the number.

The job spans consumer and business, self-serve and sales-led, so you'll need to think as clearly about a solo user's first week as about a team's path to an enterprise deal. You'll go deep on the systems underneath, like who's eligible, what triggers a message, how events and attributes are modeled, and how data moves from the warehouse into our messaging tools. When the best fix lives in the product or the data, you'll make the case and either build it yourself or work with engineering to ship it.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Strategy and roadmap: Own lifecycle end to end across our suite of products: Grok, Grok Build, Grok Bot, X and the API, from activation and conversion to paid through team expansion, retention and winback.
* Data and audiences: Use SQL and product data to size opportunities, build the audiences and triggers behind each program, and prove what actually drove revenue.
* Journeys: Architect multi-step, cross-channel programs across email, push and in-app, with event-based triggers, branching and suppression logic, and personalization drawn from product usage, plan and billing state, and company and role enrichment.
* Systems: Drive and continuously improve the data model lifecycle runs on, including identity, events, attributes and eligibility, and partner with engineering on the warehouse syncs, APIs and webhooks that keep it accurate in real time.
* Measurement: Run every program as an experiment with a proper holdout, measure incremental lift, and make judgment calls when a result is inconclusive.
* Portfolio: Manage lifecycle as a portfolio of programs, putting more behind what works and retiring what doesn't.
* In-product: Own in-product messaging and experiments, from onboarding and upgrade prompts to post-purchase plan tours, and partner with product and engineering to improve our in-app comms systems.
* Tooling: Use code and agents to build the internal tools, templates and automated QA the team runs on, so each new program ships faster than the last.

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 8+ years in Lifecycle, Growth PMM or Product Growth at a high-growth product company.
* Hands-on experience running lifecycle programs in a major lifecycle platform, such as Braze, Customer.io or Iterable.

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience running lifecycle for both consumers and businesses, self-serve and sales-led, with strong messaging craft. Experience running lifecycle at scale, across hundreds of millions of users and multiple products.
* Real technical depth. You write SQL, you know how events, identity and attributes fit together, and you're comfortable with APIs, webhooks and warehouse syncs.
* A history of building out multi-channel lifecycle systems with engineering across email, push and in-product messaging, not just running campaigns on top of them.
* Programs you can point to that moved revenue, paid users or retention, backed by holdouts and judged on incrementality and lifetime value rather than opens and clicks.
* Fluency with AI agents and code, using them to do far more than one person typically could.
