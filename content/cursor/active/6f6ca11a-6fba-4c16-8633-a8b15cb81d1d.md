**STRATEGIC ACCOUNT EXECUTIVE, HEALTHCARE**

As a Strategic Account Executive, Healthcare at SpaceXSI, you'll drive revenue growth across a named territory of large healthcare accounts on our Public Sector team. You'll work closely with engineering organizations - from CTOs to individual development teams - to understand how they build software, demonstrate how SpaceXSI transforms developer productivity, and land and expand SpaceXSI across their organizations. This role requires deep technical fluency, healthcare proficiency, the ability to navigate complex healthcare payor and provider buying processes, and a genuine passion for AI-powered developer tools. You'll be a foundational member of our enterprise sales motion.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Own a named book of healthcare accounts; drive new account acquisition, expansion revenue, and long-term strategic growth; lead complex, multi-threaded sales cycles across Engineering, Product, Security, Procurement, Legal, and mission-critical stakeholders
* Become a trusted product expert; guide Healthcare prospects through trials, technical evaluations, security reviews, and large-scale rollouts
* Build executive relationships and cultivate day-to-day champions across health systems, integrators, and technical teams; quantify value with clear ROI cases tied to developer productivity, AI adoption, mission outcomes, and secure software delivery
* Be the voice of the Healthcare customer and influence the roadmap with actionable feedback on product, compliance, deployment, and procurement needs
* Partner closely with Field Engineering and our AI Deployment team to deliver outcomes from proof of concept through production deployment and expansion
* Navigate Healthcare procurement processes, contract vehicles, partners, and compliance requirements to accelerate adoption

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 5+ years of closing experience in enterprise or Healthcare technology sales

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Ideally selling developer tools, technical SaaS, infrastructure, cybersecurity, or emerging technologies
* A consistent track record of landing new logos, expanding strategic accounts, and exceeding quota through self-sourced pipeline generation
* A true hunter, you proactively build pipeline from scratch through outbound prospecting, account mapping, partner leverage, creative sourcing, and relentless follow-up
* Comfortable navigating complex Healthcare sales cycles and selling to technical and mission-oriented stakeholders, from engineers and program leaders to CISOs, CIOs, and senior executives. A top-down and bottom-up fluency
* An analytical approach to understanding customer needs combined with creative, tactical follow-through to advance opportunities
* An excellent communicator who can build trust across all levels of an organization
* Passionate about Healthcare and how AI can ultimately improve patient care

**COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS:**

The expected annual base salary range for this role is $175,000 - $200,000. This role is also eligible for sales commissions or bonuses, with an annual on-target earnings (OTE) range of $350,000 - $400,000. OTE includes base salary plus target incentive compensation, assuming 100% achievement of applicable performance targets. Actual incentive earnings depend on performance and the terms of the applicable plan.

Your actual level and base salary will be determined on a case-by-case basis and may vary based on the following considerations: job-related knowledge and skills, education, and experience.

Base salary is just one part of our total rewards package at SpaceXSI, which also includes equity, comprehensive medical, vision, and dental coverage, access to a 401(k) retirement plan, short & long-term disability insurance, life insurance, and various other discounts and perks.
