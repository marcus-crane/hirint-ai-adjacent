**About the role**

As RVP, Geo Enterprise Sales at Cursor, you'll build and lead the organization responsible for revenue growth across a major geographic enterprise segment. You'll hire and develop frontline sales leaders, set territory and account strategy at scale, and personally engage in the most strategic, executive-level relationships in the region. You'll partner with engineering leaders — from CTOs to VPs of Engineering — to show how Cursor transforms developer productivity, and drive a land-and-expand motion across sophisticated software companies.

This is a second-line leadership role: you'll scale through managers who lead high-performing Account Executives, while staying close enough to the business to shape strategy, unblock deals, and set the bar for excellence. It requires deep technical fluency, command of complex enterprise buying processes, and a genuine passion for AI-powered developer tools. You'll be a foundational leader in our enterprise sales organization.
