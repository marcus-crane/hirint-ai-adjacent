**FIELD ENGINEER, PUBLIC SECTOR**

We're hiring Field Engineers to partner with prospective and strategic customers across Public Sector. You'll be the technical face of SpaceXSI for the federal civilian and defense agencies modernizing how software gets written: helping developers, engineering leaders, and program teams evaluate our platform, running proofs of concept, and making sure they see the full value of agentic coding in their real workflows. You'll work hand-in-hand with Sales, Product, and Engineering as a deal partner, not a demo resource.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Partner with AEs to own technical discovery, demos, POCs, and technical validation for new deals and expansion
* Build trusted relationships with engineering, security, product, and IT stakeholders across agencies
* Work in mission-critical and high-security environments, including developer workflows, AI tooling, and complex or constrained setups
* Help customers connect our platform to outcomes that matter, including developer productivity, secure software delivery, and mission velocity
* Deliver technical presentations, whiteboards, and workshops for technical and executive audiences
* Act as the customer's technical advocate with Product and Engineering, and partner with post-sales on clean handoffs and onboarding

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Experience in Field Engineering, Solutions Architecture, Pre-Sales, or Consulting for enterprise software
* Experience supporting federal, defense, or highly regulated customers

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Hands-on software development experience and a working understanding of the SDLC
* Daily use of AI coding tools and experience with constrained or air-gapped environments
* Experience presenting technical concepts to CTOs, CISOs, and program leaders
* Experience co-owning complex, multi-threaded deals with AEs
* Familiarity with Public Sector buying motions, security expectations such as FedRAMP and DoD IL levels, and partner ecosystems
* Active security clearance

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Must be willing to travel to customer sites as needed

**COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS:**

The expected annual base salary range for this role is $164,500 - $224,000. This role is also eligible for sales commissions or bonuses, with an annual on-target earnings (OTE) range of $235,000 - $320,000. OTE includes base salary plus target incentive compensation, assuming 100% achievement of applicable performance targets. Actual incentive earnings depend on performance and the terms of the applicable plan.

Your actual level and base salary will be determined on a case-by-case basis and may vary based on the following considerations: job-related knowledge and skills, education, and experience.

Base salary is just one part of our total rewards package at SpaceXSI, which also includes equity, comprehensive medical, vision, and dental coverage, access to a 401(k) retirement plan, short & long-term disability insurance, life insurance, and various other discounts and perks.
