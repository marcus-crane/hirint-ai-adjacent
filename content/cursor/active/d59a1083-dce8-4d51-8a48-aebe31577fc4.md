**COMMUNITY OPERATIONS LEAD**

In this role, you'll build the infrastructure and manage the daily operations behind SpaceXSI's global community programs. You'll architect internal tools and automate workflows across our community initiatives, using the latest development tools to help the team work more efficiently. This role will be pivotal in giving our community team the backbone it needs to scale significantly in 2027.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Design, build, and maintain internal tooling and infrastructure that makes our global community programs run more effectively, including our Ambassador programs, student programs, and net-new programs
* Identify operational gaps and engineer solutions: automate manual processes, integrate external platforms, and build custom workflows from scratch
* Own the operational experience behind programs, including application reviews, onboarding, member support, reimbursements, swag logistics, vendor coordination, and event operations
* Work with the Revenue Operations team to build the data infrastructure and dashboards behind community reporting, connecting program data to company-wide tools to track ROI and conversions
* Serve as the main operational point of contact for ambassadors and internal partners, speeding up resolution of outstanding issues and questions

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Experience building databases, workflows, and automations
* Experience managing vendors, budgets, and event operations globally

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Operations systems designed to scale for the next 10x, not just today
* Familiarity with the realities of global operations: time zones, currencies, international shipping, and local regulations
* Bias toward action: shipping an 80% solution today rather than waiting for perfect, and making an impact on day one
* Care for community and an understanding of the importance of building strong relationships with our champions
* Comfort with ambiguity and energy for greenfield problems as an early hire on a rapidly growing team
* Fluency with Cursor, Databricks, Luma, Slack, Notion, Salesforce, CRM, and Clay

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Onsite role: must be able to work in person at the San Francisco office
