## **About the role**

You’ll be a Strategic Account Executive selling Cursor into consumer-facing enterprises where engineering velocity impacts revenue and customer experience (ecommerce, marketplaces, hospitality platforms, media/streaming, entertainment brands). You’ll win by proving real-world impact and scaling adoption across large product engineering orgs.

## **What you’ll do**

* Own a vertical portfolio and net-new strategy across retail/hospitality/entertainment
* Build relationships across Product Engineering, DevEx, Security, and Procurement
* Tie Cursor to outcomes like faster launches, fewer defects, and reduced operational toil
* Run hands-on POVs with real repos and production workflows
* Navigate enterprise security/compliance reviews and commercial negotiation
* Drive adoption plans (enablement, champions, rollout sequencing) and expansions
* Share customer learnings to sharpen Cursor’s positioning and rollout playbooks

## **You may be a fit if**

* You have 7+ years enterprise closing experience; consumer/internet background is a plus
* You’re comfortable selling to technical buyers and quantifying business impact
* You have strong outbound + account planning chops; you create pipeline consistently
* You're a true hunter - you proactively build pipeline from scratch through outbound prospecting, creative sourcing, and relentless follow-up, rather than relying on inbound demand
* You are a high ownership IC who moves fast and communicates crisply
