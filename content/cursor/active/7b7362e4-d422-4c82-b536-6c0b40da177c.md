**ACCOUNT EXECUTIVE, COMMERCIAL - ANZ**

As a Commercial Account Executive at SpaceXSI, you'll drive revenue growth by building strategic relationships with customers and helping them realize the full potential of Grok Build for their development teams. You'll cover the full spectrum, from high-velocity, fast-growing startups to more complex Mid-Market deals, adapting your playbook to each.

You'll work closely with engineering organizations to understand their development workflows, demonstrate how Grok Build transforms developer productivity, and guide them through evaluation, implementation, and expansion. This role combines technical fluency with sales expertise to help companies reimagine how software gets built with AI.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Own the full sales cycle across ANZ, from first outreach to close, working directly with engineering leaders, CTOs, developer platform teams, and procurement stakeholders; build and manage a healthy pipeline through outbound prospecting, inbound follow-up, partner referrals, creative sourcing, and your own network
* Meet and exceed quarterly and annual revenue targets; develop executive relationships and day-to-day champions within each account, becoming a trusted product expert for customers and prospects
* Guide prospects through trials, technical evaluations, security reviews, procurement processes, and broader rollouts; quantify value with clear ROI cases tied to developer productivity, AI adoption, and software delivery velocity
* Design and execute innovative sales strategies for the ANZ market; analyze market trends and translate high-level plans into targeted account activities and campaigns
* Navigate complex buying committees across engineering, IT, security, legal, finance, procurement, and executive stakeholders; be the voice of ANZ customers internally, sharing actionable feedback that influences product roadmap, pricing, packaging, and go-to-market strategy
* Help establish and refine SpaceXSI's enterprise motion in ANZ, including local market learnings, repeatable playbooks, and early customer references

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 3+ years of closing experience in tech sales

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Ideally selling developer tools, technical SaaS, or emerging technologies
* A consistent track record of landing new logos and exceeding quota and managing a high velocity of deals
* A true hunter - you proactively build pipeline from scratch through outbound prospecting, creative sourcing, and relentless follow-up, rather than relying on inbound demand
* Comfortable navigating complex sales cycles and selling to technical stakeholders, from ICs to CTOs
* An analytical approach to understanding customer needs combined with creative, tactical follow-through to advance opportunities
* A knack for bringing order to chaos and a genuine "roll up your sleeves" mentality
* An excellent communicator who can present confidently and build trust across all levels of an organization
* Passionate about AI and excited by the idea that transforming how software gets written is one of the most important problems to work on right now
* A self-starter who thrives in an early-stage, fast-moving environment and can help build process, playbooks, and regional learnings as you go
