SpaceX was founded under the belief that a future where humanity is out exploring the stars is fundamentally more exciting than one where we are not. Today, SpaceX is actively developing the technologies to make this possible, with the ultimate goal of making life multiplanetary and expanding consciousness to the stars.  
  
SpaceXSI is building Super Intelligence to help humanity understand the universe. Our teams are building the full Super Intelligence stack: from our Colossus supercomputers, to Grok models, to our suite of applications, including Grok Bot, Grok Build, Grok Imagine, Grok Voice, and X. We're creating a powerful flywheel where more compute trains better models, better models power more useful products, and what we learn from people using those products shapes what we build next.

You'll be one of our first Strategic Account Executives in Latin America. You'll own a named book of the region's largest enterprises across industries and work with their engineering organizations, from CTOs to individual teams. You'll show how Cursor and SpaceXAI's models change developer productivity. You'll land new accounts, expand them across teams and countries, and help write the playbook for how we sell in the region.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Own a named book of strategic accounts. Drive new logos and expansion, and run multi-threaded cycles across Engineering, Security, Procurement, Legal and executive stakeholders.
* Build your own pipeline. Find new business through outbound, account mapping, partner referrals and your own network.
* Lead technical evaluations. With Field Engineering, run trials and proofs of value in customers' real codebases.
* Prove the value. Build ROI cases tied to developer productivity, delivery speed and quality.
* Handle enterprise requirements. Work through security reviews, data protection rules (Brazil's LGPD and Mexico's privacy law), legal terms and procurement.
* Stay accountable after signature. Partner with our AI Deployment team so customers reach production and expand, and be the customer's voice to Product, Marketing and leadership.

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Achieved 100% or more of quota in at least two of the last three full years
* Closed at least one self-sourced complex enterprise deal in the last three years as owner of record, which went through procurement and a security or legal review and involved five or more stakeholders
* Professional proficiency in English, plus Portuguese (São Paulo) or Spanish (Mexico City)

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience selling developer tools, technical SaaS, cloud, data or AI to technical buyers
* A strong grasp of how modern engineering teams build and ship software
* A hunter's habits: account mapping, creative sourcing and persistent follow-up
* Comfort with both top-down executive selling and bottom-up developer adoption
* Deals you closed through your own network inside LATAM's largest companies
* Working proficiency in both Portuguese and Spanish
* Experience with regional systems integrators or cloud marketplaces

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Must be willing to travel up to 25% of the time, within LATAM.
