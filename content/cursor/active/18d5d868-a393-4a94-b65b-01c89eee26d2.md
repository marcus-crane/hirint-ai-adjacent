**AI DEPLOYMENT MANAGER - JAPAN**

As an AI Deployment Manager (ADM) at SpaceXSI, you'll own the post-sale journey for a portfolio of enterprise customers, driving adoption, retention, and expansion. You'll help organizations successfully roll out Grok, Grok Build, Grok Bot, and Grok APIs and evolve how they work with AI across knowledge work and coding. You'll operate at the center of each account, partnering with developers, admins, and executives while working closely with Sales, Field Engineering, Product, and Support. You'll shape rollout strategy and influence SpaceXSI's roadmap priorities through customer insight.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Manage a book of business with our top enterprise customers, owning the long-term success of each account
* Engage CTO / VP / SVP Engineering stakeholders to demonstrate ROI and guide AI-driven organizational change
* Lead enterprise rollouts end-to-end: onboarding, enablement, internal champions, and scaled adoption
* Turn high-impact use cases into durable, repeatable workflows across teams
* Monitor account health and usage data to drive renewals and expansion with AEs
* Represent the customer internally, synthesizing feedback and influencing product priorities

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Fluent Japanese and business-level English

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience working at the intersection of business and technology and thriving in customer-facing roles
* Able to translate customer needs into clear technical plans and measurable outcomes
* Understand modern engineering and knowledge-work orgs, the SDLC, developer and knowledge-worker workflows, and tool adoption
* Communicate comfortably with multiple functions around an enterprise including developers and senior executives
* Analytical and operational, using data to prioritize and drive action
* Already a power user of Grok, Grok Build, Grok Bot, or Grok APIs (or modern AI tools for knowledge work and coding) with strong opinions about what great looks like (big plus)
