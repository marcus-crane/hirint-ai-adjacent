**CREATOR CAMPAIGN MANAGER**

We're looking for a Creator Campaign Manager to join the SpaceXSI creator marketing team on-site in San Francisco. Our team leads creator and talent partnerships across the SpaceX product suite, running scaled programs across platforms, social channels, and content formats. This role owns creator campaigns end to end, from strategy and sourcing through negotiation, briefing, execution, and payment.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Manage multiple creator campaigns simultaneously, keeping deliverables, budgets, and timelines on track; develop campaigns across platforms and content formats based on each product's goals and audience
* Source and evaluate creators based on audience, content quality, brand fit, and campaign objectives; lead negotiations on rates, deliverables, usage rights, and partnership terms
* Manage contracting, approvals, invoicing, and creator payouts in partnership with legal, procurement, and finance
* Translate complex product capabilities into clear, compelling creator briefs; review content and provide actionable feedback that maintains a high creative bar
* Manage agency partners, holding them accountable for execution, quality, and results; build and maintain a high volume of direct creator relationships
* Track campaign performance and apply learnings to future partnerships; support creator events and activations as needed

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 3+ years of influencer marketing experience at a brand-facing agency or in-house on a brand's influencer marketing team
* Strong command of the fundamentals: sourcing, negotiation, contracting, briefing, project management, and payments

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience executing scaled programs with a high volume of concurrent partnerships
* At least two years managing external partners, either overseeing agencies in-house or managing multiple brand accounts at an agency
* Strong negotiation skills and confidence navigating creator and talent relationships
* A high bar for creative taste and the ability to give clear, constructive feedback
* Experience across multiple social platforms preferred, particularly Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and LinkedIn

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* This is an onsite role. Must be able to work in person at the San Francisco office
