**SOLUTIONS STRATEGY & OPERATIONS MANAGER**

We're hiring a Solutions Strategy & Operations Manager to help build the operating system for our Customer Success and Solutions teams, a role critical to building our business with rigor and pace and scaling customer value fast. You'll begin as a generalist supporting our Solutions Architect, Field Engineering, and Forward Deployed Engineering functions, with the path to own a specific business unit end to end as we grow and scale. There are early foundations in place, but much of the long-term system still needs to be built, from the key metrics that matter to the cadences that drive decisions and the playbooks that scale. You'll be a strategic thought partner and hands-on operator, equally comfortable thinking big-picture with senior leadership and getting into the weeds to build the systems, models, and processes that let the org run on real data.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Metrics & performance: Establish key metrics and compensation models; evaluate performance of the business, drivers, and trends, and guide targeted action plans
* Planning models: Build, refine, and optimize headcount and capacity models to ensure we make the right investments; build segmentation and coverage models to deliver the right service model that ultimately leads to revenue growth
* Customer frameworks: Build and operationalize frameworks such as health signals, customer maturity models, and corresponding playbooks that drive usage and growth
* Dashboards & cadence: Build and operationalize dashboards that surface the right KPIs and insights at the right time to drive action across all levels of the organization; build an end-to-end operating cadence for the leadership team to support fast, informed decision-making
* Leadership partnership: Serve as a trusted advisor to the leadership team, thinking big picture while leaning in to get things done iteratively
* Cross-functional & global execution: Partner closely with RevOps, Go to Market, Finance, and more to bring concepts to life rapidly and iterate together; incorporate a global mindset, taking local nuances and input into account so global plans stick regionally

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Experience in Sales or Customer Strategy and Operations

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience with pre-sales (e.g. sales engineering), direct sales, and technical services teams
* Builder mindset: comfort building from the ground up and flexing between launch-and-iterate and going deep
* Analytical and modeling skills, with an operations eye for foundations such as capacity modeling, staffing, planning, and process
* Stakeholder and project management skills: operating across levels and audiences, outlining what needs to happen, and keeping the team informed and engaged
* Consulting, finance, and/or data science background, or customer-facing experience
* Experience in Business Operations, Sales or Services strategy & ops, or company strategy such as localization and pricing
* Experience with consumption-based pricing and with customer frameworks such as health scores and maturity models, with the open-mindedness to evolve them to meet our business needs

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Onsite role: must be able to work in person at the San Francisco, New York, or Austin office
