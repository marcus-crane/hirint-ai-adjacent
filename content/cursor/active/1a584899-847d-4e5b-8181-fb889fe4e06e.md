**About the role**

In this role, you’ll build the infrastructure and manage the daily operations behind SpaceXSI’s global community programs. You’ll architect internal tools and automate workflows across our community initiatives, using the latest development tools to help the team work more efficiently. This role will be pivotal in giving our community team the backbone it needs to scale significantly in 2027.

**What you’ll do**

* Design, build, and maintain internal tooling and infrastructure that makes our global community programs run more effectively. This includes our Ambassador programs, student programs, and net-new programs.
* Identify operational gaps and engineer solutions, automating manual processes, integrating external platforms, and building custom workflows from scratch.
* Own the operational experience behind programs from application reviews, onboarding, member support, reimbursements, swag logistics, vendor coordination, and event operations.
* Work with the Revenue Operations team to build the data infrastructure and dashboards behind community reporting, connecting program data to company-wide tools to track ROI and conversions.
* Serve as the main operational point of contact for ambassadors and internal partners to increase time towards resolution on outstanding issues and questions.

**You may be a fit if**

* Deep operations experience in building databases, workflows, and automations that scale, and you design them for the next 10x, not just today.
* Experience managing vendors, budgets, and event operations globally, including the realities of time zones, currencies, international shipping, and local regulations.
* Strong bias toward action and would rather ship an 80% solution today than wait for perfect. We need someone that can make an impact on day one.
* You care deeply about community and understand the importance of building strong relationships with our champions. Ultimately, your work will free up our team to spend more time connecting with our community.
* Thrive in ambiguity and are energized by greenfield problems. You’ll be an early hire on a rapidly growing team.
* Fluency with the following tools: Cursor, Databricks, Luma, Slack, Notion, Salesforce, CRM and Clay.
