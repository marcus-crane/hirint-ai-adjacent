**GTM APPLICATIONS ENGINEER**

SpaceXSI's go-to-market org is growing fast, and the software that runs it has to keep up. GTM Apps sits inside Revenue Operations and builds the internal products our Sales, Customer Success, Field Engineering, and RevOps teams use every day for account intelligence, forecasting, pricing, and more. We also enable other teams to build apps for themselves via enablement and maintaining shared infrastructure. As a GTM Applications Engineer, you'll own product surfaces end-to-end by talking to users, shipping full-stack applications, keeping production apps healthy, and raising the bar on reliability and UX.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Design, build, and ship full-stack features for GTM Apps (TypeScript / Next.js and our shared internal stack), from idea through production
* Enable our world-class sales team to exceed their ambitious goals with world-class software; own a product surface and drive its roadmap (e.g. account intelligence, pricing, forecasting, our internal data API, etc)
* Partner closely with RevOps, Data Engineering, and field teams to turn messy operational processes into durable software
* Improve the reliability, performance, and user experience for critical software our GTM teams depend on for daily work
* Build and maintain shared infrastructure other GTM builders can use (e.g. APIs, UI libraries, authentication frameworks, etc)
* Triage and fix issues in our app suite and use that feedback as an input signal for your roadmap; use AI tooling aggressively in your own workflow and, where it helps users, in the products you ship

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering, math, or scientific discipline; OR 2+ years of professional experience building software in lieu of a degree
* Experience in full stack development

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Programming experience in Python, C#.NET, Go, Scala, Java, or similar object-oriented language
* 1+ years of rigorous experience building single page web applications
* Experience with version control, continuous integration, and continuous delivery concepts
* Expertise in designing software systems
* Deep understanding of testing, continuous integration, build, deployment & monitoring
* Expertise in profiling and improving application performance
* Experience with build systems (Make, Bazel/Pants/Buck, Gradle) and package management tools (pip, npm)
* Experience with UNIX-like operating systems and tools like Terraform, Ansible, Puppet for managing them
* Experience working with in-stream data processing using Apache Kafka, RabbitMQ, ActiveMQ, or similar technologies
* Production experience with Docker and Kubernetes
* Front-end experience in Angular, React, or similar JavaScript framework
* Database experience with PostgreSQL, SQL Server, or similar database technologies

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Onsite role: must be able to work in person at the San Francisco or New York office
