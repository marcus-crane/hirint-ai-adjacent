**Regional Director, AI Deployment, Federal**

As Regional Director, AI Deployment, Federal at SpaceXSI, you'll lead and develop a team of AI Deployment Managers supporting our most strategic federal customers across civilian agencies, the Department of Defense, the Intelligence Community, and the systems integrators who serve them. This role exists to scale adoption quality and consistency as federal demand accelerates, turning SpaceXSI's momentum into repeatable, mission-focused deployment outcomes within the security, compliance, and procurement realities of government work. You'll own the post-sale experience for the federal segment, working closely with Federal Sales, Field Engineering, and our Security and Compliance teams to make sure agencies achieve measurable engineering and mission value from SpaceXSI.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Hire, lead, and develop a team of AI Deployment Managers focused on federal customers, including onboarding, coaching, performance management, and ongoing skill development
* Own post-sale account planning across federal agencies and integrator partners, with accountability for account health, usage, risk mitigation, renewals, and expansion pipeline
* Act as the escalation point for complex deployments and high-stakes federal programs, including rollouts in secure, restricted, or air-gapped environments
* Guide best practices for rollout strategy, prompt design, context configuration, and internal enablement, adapted to federal security, authorization, and data-handling requirements
* Partner closely with Federal Sales, Field Engineering, and systems integrators to deliver a seamless post-sale experience and support expansion across agencies and contract vehicles
* Work with Security, Compliance, Product, and Engineering to advocate for federal customer needs, including authorization and accreditation requirements, and to influence roadmap priorities

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 4+ years of experience leading customer-facing technical teams in TAM, Solutions, Deployment, or similar roles
* Experience owning post-sale retention or renewal outcomes for enterprise software or AI platform customers in the federal or public sector

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience managing ADM, TAM, or CSM teams that support federal civilian, DoD, or Intelligence Community customers
* Working knowledge of federal security and compliance frameworks such as FedRAMP, IL4/IL5, and ATO processes
* Experience working with federal systems integrators and navigating government contract vehicles
* An active U.S. security clearance, or eligibility to obtain one
* Familiarity with software development lifecycles and enterprise AI: AI coding tools, AI agents, and LLM APIs
* Experience presenting adoption and mission-impact results to agency CIOs, CTOs, program executives, and technical leaders
* A high-agency, scrappy operator who thrives in fast-moving, ambiguous environments
* Care deeply about AI and believe that transforming how software is written is one of the most important problems to work on right now

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Must be willing to travel to customer sites as needed
