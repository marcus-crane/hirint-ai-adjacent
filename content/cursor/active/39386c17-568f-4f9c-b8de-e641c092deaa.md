**SOFTWARE ENGINEER, CLIENT INFRASTRUCTURE**

As an Engineer on the Client Infrastructure team at SpaceXSI, you'll design and build the systems that make our desktop experience performant and stable for millions of developers. You'll work across the core architecture of the Cursor client, from build and distribution systems to performance instrumentation and shared abstractions that other teams rely on. The work is deeply technical and central to the company's success. Developers expect near-perfect performance and reliability from their trusted tools; your work will make that possible.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Build core client infrastructure: shared abstractions, build systems, and observability foundations
* Improve reliability and performance: eliminating memory leaks, crashes, and frame-time inconsistencies
* Design instrumentation and telemetry systems for profiling, structured logging, and performance measurement
* Partner with product teams to unlock faster release velocity, shipping updates to users daily
* Evaluate Electron vs. native approaches for critical code paths, guiding architectural tradeoffs
* Build internal tooling that enables other engineers to ship safely and quickly on the client platform

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Experience building or contributing to high-performance desktop applications (Electron-based or native)
* Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering, math, or scientific discipline; OR 2+ years of professional experience building software in lieu of a degree

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Deep experience in client infrastructure, build systems, performance, distribution, or observability
* Experience building or contributing to high-performance desktop applications, whether Electron-based or native
* Care deeply about performance, reliability, and developer experience, and have an eye for elegant systems design
* Thrive in environments that are fast-moving, collaborative, and product-focused
* Excited to push the limits of what's possible in AI-driven developer tools

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* If there appears to be a fit, we'll reach out to schedule 2-3 short technicals. After, we'll schedule an onsite in our office, where you'll work on a small project, discuss ideas, and meet the team
