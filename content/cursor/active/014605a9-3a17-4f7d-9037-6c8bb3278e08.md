**FIELD ENGINEER**

We're hiring Field Engineers to partner with prospective and strategic enterprise customers. You'll be the technical lead in the field for SpaceXSI, helping customers evaluate Grok Bot, Grok Build and Grok APIs, running proofs of concept, and making sure they see real value from AI in their engineering workflows. You'll work hand-in-hand with Sales, Product and Engineering to scope solutions, demo capabilities and advise technical stakeholders.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Independently drive the technical sale across Grok Bot, Grok Build and Grok APIs, from discovery through demos and proofs of concept, in partnership with Account Executives
* Translate customer requirements into solution designs and rollout plans, including prompt strategy and context configuration
* Deliver technical presentations, whiteboard sessions and hands-on workshops for developers and engineering leadership
* Bring customer feedback to Product and Engineering to influence the roadmap
* Hand off cleanly to post-sales teams and support expansion with technical validation
* Contribute technical sales assets and examples to the company's shared library

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Experience in Field Engineering, Solutions Architecture, Pre-Sales, or Consulting for enterprise software

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Led technical evaluations or POCs for developer tools or AI products sold to engineering teams
* Hands-on coding in at least one modern language
* Presented to engineering leadership (Director, VP, CTO)
* Working knowledge of CI/CD and enterprise rollout needs like SSO and security reviews

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

Must be willing to travel up to 50%
