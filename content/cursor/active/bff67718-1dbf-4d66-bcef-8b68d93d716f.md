**ACCOUNT EXECUTIVE - COMMERCIAL (SINGAPORE)**

As a Commercial Account Executive at SpaceXSI, you'll drive revenue growth by building strategic relationships with customers and helping them realize the full potential of SpaceXSI for their development teams. You'll cover the full spectrum, from high velocity fast-growing startups to more complex Mid-Market deals, adapting your playbook to each. You'll work closely with engineering organizations to understand their development workflows, demonstrate how SpaceXSI transforms developer productivity, and guide them through evaluation, implementation, and expansion. This role combines technical fluency with sales expertise to help companies reimagine how software gets built with AI.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Win new business and drive revenue for SpaceXSI. Find your way to the right people at prospective customers, educate them about AI-powered development, and help them succeed. You'll own the full sales cycle, from first call to close
* Become a trusted product expert; guide prospects through trials, evaluations, and rollouts; build and manage a healthy high velocity pipeline, meeting and exceeding quarterly targets
* Lead multi-threaded sales cycles across Engineering, Product, Security, Procurement, and Legal
* Develop executive relationships and day-to-day champions within each account; quantify value with clear ROI cases tied to developer productivity and AI adoption
* Design and execute innovative sales strategies; analyze market trends and translate high-level plans into targeted activities and campaigns
* Be the voice of the customer and influence the roadmap with actionable feedback; continuously refine the sales methodology by incorporating learnings into playbooks, templates, and best practices

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 3+ years of closing experience in tech sales

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Ideally selling developer tools, technical SaaS, or emerging technologies
* Comfortable navigating complex sales cycles and selling to technical stakeholders, from ICs to CTOs
* An analytical approach to understanding customer needs combined with creative, tactical follow-through to advance opportunities
* A knack for bringing order to chaos and a genuine "roll up your sleeves" mentality
* An excellent communicator who can present confidently and build trust across all levels of an organization
* Passionate about AI and excited by the idea that transforming how software gets written is one of the most important problems to work on right now
