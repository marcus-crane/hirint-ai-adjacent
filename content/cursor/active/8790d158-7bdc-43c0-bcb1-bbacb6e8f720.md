**STRATEGIC ACCOUNT EXECUTIVE, FEDERAL SALES - AUSTRALIA**

As a Strategic Account Executive, Federal Sales at SpaceXSI, you'll drive revenue growth across a named territory of large federal accounts on our Public Sector team. You'll work closely with engineering organizations - from CTOs to individual development teams - to understand how they build software, demonstrate how Grok transforms developer productivity, and land and expand Grok across their organizations.

This role requires deep technical fluency, the ability to navigate complex enterprise and government buying processes, and a genuine passion for AI-powered developer tools. You'll be a foundational member of our enterprise sales motion, based in either Sydney or Canberra.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Own a named book of federal accounts, winning new agencies and growing revenue in existing ones in your first 12 months.
* Lead complex, multi-threaded sales cycles across Engineering, Product, Security, Procurement, Legal and mission stakeholders.
* Guide agencies through trials, technical evaluations, security reviews and large-scale rollouts, together with Field Engineering and our AI Deployment team.
* Build ROI cases tied to developer productivity, AI adoption, mission outcomes and secure software delivery.
* Work through federal procurement processes, contract vehicles, partners and compliance requirements to speed up adoption.
* Share customer feedback on product, compliance, deployment and procurement needs to shape our roadmap.

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 10+ years of closing experience in quota-carrying enterprise or federal technology sales.
* A bachelor's degree in business, engineering, computer science, or a related field.
* Based in, or able to work from, Sydney or Canberra.

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience selling developer tools, technical SaaS, infrastructure, cybersecurity or emerging technologies.
* A track record of landing new logos and expanding strategic accounts through self-sourced pipeline, including outbound prospecting, account mapping and partner-led sourcing.
* Experience selling to technical and mission-focused buyers, such as engineers, program leaders, CISOs, CIOs and senior executives.
* Knowledge of Australian federal buying motions, procurement processes, security requirements and partner ecosystems.
* Experience with AI or AI-powered developer tools.
