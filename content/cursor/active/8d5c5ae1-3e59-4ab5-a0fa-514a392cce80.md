**About the position**

Strategic alliances across hyperscalers, data platforms, and AI ecosystems are a major growth lever for SpaceXAI. We are seeking a Strategic Alliances GTM Lead to own the sales-facing GTM operating system across AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Snowflake, Databricks, and emerging strategic AI platforms.

This is not a single-partner alliance management role. You will work alongside the Global Cloud Alliance Leaders, Enterprise Sales, GTM Technical, Product, and Partner Marketing to turn alliance strategy, integrations, joint solutions, and partner relationships into measurable commercial execution.

You will own how we co-sell, enable sellers, map accounts, create new routes to market, forecast pipeline, activate joint solutions, and scale successful plays across the partner ecosystem. You will work directly with Enterprise Sales to determine where partners can help sellers enter accounts faster, navigate organizations more effectively, reach the right buyers, expand opportunities, and accelerate revenue.

The Global Cloud Alliance Leaders own the partner: strategy, executive relationships, Product alignment, integrations, joint business planning, and partner investment. You activate that portfolio across the field.

This is scoped as an IC at hire, with a path to team lead as the Strategic Tech Alliances org scales.

We believe collaboration thrives in person, so while this role can be remote, we expect periodic travel to our offices — cozy spaces in North Beach, San Francisco and Manhattan, New York, complete with well-stocked libraries — along with the regular travel this role typically involves.

* Develop and execute the joint GTM strategy across AWS, Microsoft Azure, Google Cloud, Snowflake, Databricks, and emerging strategic AI platform partners
* Serve as the primary GTM counterpart to the Global Cloud Alliance Leaders, translating partner strategy, integrations, joint solutions, programs, and priorities into executable field motions
* Own the sales-facing GTM operating system across joint business plans, new routes to market, co-sell execution, seller enablement, account mapping, pipeline discipline, forecasting, and QBRs
* Work directly with Enterprise Sales to attach the right partner motion to priority accounts, active opportunities, and territory plans
* Build partner-sourced and partner-influenced pipeline through partner sellers, specialists, technical teams, executive relationships, and ecosystem signals
* Build account-mapping motions that identify customer overlap, existing workloads, cloud adoption, AI and data environments, partner relationships, and whitespace
* Identify and unlock new routes to market through co-sell, Marketplace, model and AI platforms, joint solutions, cloud commitments, and ecosystem partnerships
* Translate new integrations and joint solutions into customer use cases, target accounts, seller positioning, enablement programs, and opportunity-generation plays
* Design and execute segment-, industry-, workload-, and solution-specific sales plays for SpaceXAI and partner field teams
* Partner with Global Cloud Alliance Leaders and Partner Marketing on campaigns, executive programs, events, and account-based initiatives tied to GTM priorities and pipeline
* Own pipeline forecasting and track progress against shared commercial and revenue objectives
* Participate in forecast calls, pipeline reviews, and executive QBRs as the accountable GTM owner for strategic alliances
* Identify successful partner motions and systematically scale them across sellers, accounts, industries, workloads, and regions
