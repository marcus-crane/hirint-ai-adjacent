**PRODUCT EDUCATION ENGINEER**

SpaceXSI ships fast: new models, new agentic capabilities, and new workflows every week. The gap between what our product can do and what developers know how to build with it grows with every release. As a Product Education Engineer, you'll close that gap through hands-on labs, workshops, and tutorials, using our product as part of your own development process.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Build our technical learning library, including code walkthroughs, tutorials, labs, workshops, reference implementations, and instructor-led training
* Lead large-format workshops, technical deep dives, and hands-on enablement sessions that help engineering organizations adopt AI coding tools and drive measurable outcomes
* Partner with Product, Engineering, DevRel, and Go-to-Market ahead of major launches so new capabilities ship with clear educational framing, practical examples, and adoption paths on day one
* Get Field Engineers, AI Deployment Managers, and Solutions Architects up to speed quickly on new capabilities
* Design learning experiences for both developers and non-technical users (e.g. engineering leaders evaluating rollout, PMs pairing with agents)
* Measure what works, tracking adoption and fluency gains rather than completion rates, and share insights with Product and Engineering on where developers get stuck

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 5+ years of experience in Field Engineering, Solutions Architecture, Consulting, or software engineering at a developer tools or platform company
* Experience delivering technical training, workshops, or enablement sessions to engineering audiences

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Hands-on experience across the software development lifecycle, with an understanding of how modern engineering organizations build software
* Daily use of AI coding tools, with customized personal development workflows
* Experience building demo environments, sample repos, content-freshness automation, or self-service enablement tooling
* Experience designing technical curriculum or learning programs for both developer and non-technical audiences
* Experience presenting complex technical concepts to engineering leaders and executives to support decisions on tool adoption
* Experience with interactive or AI-native learning formats such as in-product learning, hands-on labs, or live teaching
