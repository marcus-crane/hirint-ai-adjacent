**SOFTWARE ENGINEER, RESEARCH TOOLING**

As a Software Engineer on the Research Tools team, you'll build the infrastructure and applications that enable our researchers to iterate quickly, run complex experiments, and extract insights. This is a full-stack product-engineering role: you'll work directly with researchers and engineers to understand their workflows, identify bottlenecks, and rapidly ship solutions that multiply their productivity. Whether you're creating platforms for experiment orchestration, developing novel visualization tools for model evaluation, or building abstractions for understanding model behavior at scale, your work will directly accelerate our mission of building frontier AI models. No prior ML or research experience is required.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Build and maintain the full-stack applications and infrastructure researchers rely on daily, covering experiment setup, feedback collection, and results analysis
* Own complete product areas from user needs through design, implementation, and iteration, setting standards for how to build research applications
* Create reusable platforms and tools that speed up new research applications, then prototype, ship, and iterate quickly based on real-world usage
* Partner with research teams to turn their workflows and pain points into intuitive interfaces and abstractions that make complex research tasks fast and approachable

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* Bachelor's degree in computer science, engineering, math, or scientific discipline; OR 2+ years of professional experience building software in lieu of a degree

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Experience building tools, platforms, or infrastructure for technical users (engineers, researchers, data scientists, analysts, etc.)
* Experience shipping full-stack products and owning systems from user interface to storage or services, using technologies such as TypeScript and React alongside Node, Python, or Go
* Experience building dense, data-facing tools such as transcript viewers, diffing systems, review queues, observability products, or operational dashboards, with strong opinions about how structured data should be rendered
* Product instincts: identifying the right problems to solve and moving from concept to working prototype with ease
* Ability to move quickly under ambiguity, collaborate closely with researchers and domain experts, and take open-ended problems from rough need to reliable product
* Ability to jump into new problems, quickly develop domain expertise, and independently drive impactful projects from conception to delivery

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Onsite role: must be able to work in person at the Palo Alto office
