SpaceX was founded under the belief that a future where humanity is out exploring the stars is fundamentally more exciting than one where we are not. Today, SpaceX is actively developing the technologies to make this possible, with the ultimate goal of making life multiplanetary and expanding consciousness to the stars.  
  
SpaceXSI is building Super Intelligence to help humanity understand the universe. Our teams are building the full Super Intelligence stack: from our Colossus supercomputers, to Grok models, to our suite of applications, including Grok Bot, Grok Build, Grok Imagine, Grok Voice, and X. We're creating a powerful flywheel where more compute trains better models, better models power more useful products, and what we learn from people using those products shapes what we build next.

Our Forward Deployed Engineers (FDEs) embed with customer engineering teams to build production-grade AI coding workflows and leave behind engineering practices that customers can sustain. As Regional Director, you will lead the team serving banks, insurers, asset managers, and other financial institutions globally, owning the quality of engagements from technical discovery through delivery and handoff. Success means production workflows that customers adopt, measurable progress against agreed outcomes, and customer engineers able to operate and sustain the work after handoff.

## RESPONSIBILITIES:

* Build and lead a team of Forward Deployed Engineers and Strategists, reporting to the Global Head of FDE. Own hiring, coaching, performance management, staffing, and career development, and hold the team to a clear bar for engineering quality and delivery.
* Own technical scoping: work backwards from customer outcomes, run discovery and architecture reviews, and turn ambiguous problems into time-boxed engagements with defined scope, acceptance criteria, milestones, risks, and change control.
* Validate integration, infrastructure, and data constraints early, including retention, entitlements, PII, and other sensitive customer-data requirements, and hold the production engineering bar across engagements: code and architecture review, secure integration, evaluation of model behavior and workflow quality, observability, and failure handling.
* Own delivery through deployment and operational handoff, making sure customer teams have the documentation, operating practices, and ownership to sustain the work, and partner with Sales and Legal so proposals, statements of work, and commitments match technical feasibility and team capacity.
* Serve as technical owner and executive sponsor on the highest-priority engagements, guiding customers through procurement, third-party risk, security, compliance, and model-risk reviews, and working across engineering, business, and risk stakeholders to resolve tradeoffs.
* Run a detailed feedback loop with Product and Engineering, turning recurring constraints and successful implementations into product feedback, reusable delivery patterns, and reference architectures, and enable GSI and consulting partners to the same standards as the in-house team.

## BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

* Experience directly managing engineers who build and ship production software, including hiring, coaching, performance management, and setting technical standards.
* Professional software development experience in Python, JavaScript, or TypeScript, with the judgment to review code, assess architectures, and diagnose integration and production issues.
* Experience building, deploying, and operating AI-powered workflows in production, including evaluating model behavior, debugging failures, and establishing appropriate observability and security controls.

## PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:

* A track record of owning technical engagements from discovery and scoping through production delivery, customer adoption, and operational handoff.
* Experience delivering inside regulated enterprises and serving as the technical owner through security, compliance, and vendor-risk review, with the ability to distinguish customer requirements from product capabilities and identify when specialist judgment is needed.
* Experience delivering software and AI coding workflows in financial services, working with engineering, security, risk, and business stakeholders.
* Familiarity with MSA and SOW mechanics, multi-quarter engagement cycles, and prerequisite legal and procurement reviews, partnering with Sales and Legal to establish feasible delivery commitments.
* Experience translating applicable data-handling and model-risk requirements into engineering constraints, documentation, evaluation, and operational practices.
* Experience presenting technical scope, delivery tradeoffs, and customer outcomes to executive and engineering audiences.
* Experience enabling GSI or consulting partners to deliver technical engagements and building reusable approaches across customer teams.

## ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:

* Traveling to customer sites is required for this role.
