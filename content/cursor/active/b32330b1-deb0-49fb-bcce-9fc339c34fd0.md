**STRATEGIC ACCOUNT EXECUTIVE, ENTERPRISE - SOUTH KOREA**

As a Strategic Account Executive for South Korea, you'll be one of the foundational members of SpaceXSI's enterprise sales team in the region. You'll help large engineering organizations discover, adopt, and expand their use of SpaceXSI, owning strategic accounts across APJ and working directly with engineering leaders, CTOs, developer platform teams, and procurement stakeholders. This is a rare opportunity to bring a product that engineers already love into some of Korea's most sophisticated technology, financial services, enterprise, and digital-native organizations and to help shape how software gets built in the AI era.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Own a named book of strategic enterprise accounts across Korea, driving new logo acquisition and expansion revenue; run a disciplined, full-cycle sales process from prospecting and discovery through evaluation, negotiation, and close
* Build and execute account strategies that connect the Grok platform's capabilities to the technical and business priorities of engineering leaders, CTOs, and developer platform teams
* Create pipeline through outbound prospecting, executive mapping, inbound follow-up, partner referrals, and your own network; partner closely with Sales Engineering, Product, and Customer Success to deliver high-quality evaluations and ensure customers realize value quickly
* Navigate complex enterprise buying processes, including security reviews, legal negotiations, procurement, and multi-stakeholder approval cycles
* Serve as the voice of Korea's enterprise customers internally, sharing market feedback and deal learnings with Product, Marketing, Customer Success, and GTM leadership
* Help establish SpaceXSI's enterprise presence in Korea by building repeatable motions, local market insights, and early customer references; consistently meet and exceed quarterly and annual revenue targets

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 10+ years of enterprise B2B sales experience
* Fluent Korean

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Ideally selling developer tools, infrastructure, cloud, security, AI, or other technical SaaS products to engineering-led organizations
* Experience selling into strategic or large enterprise accounts in Korea
* Comfortable navigating complex buying committees across engineering, IT, security, legal, procurement, finance, and executive stakeholders
* A true hunter: you proactively build pipeline from scratch through outbound prospecting, creative sourcing, executive engagement, and persistent follow-up
* Strong command of pipeline management, account planning, and forecasting across a strategic book of business
* Energized by technical products and can hold credible conversations with senior engineers, platform leaders, and CTOs about how Grok fits into modern development workflows
* A self-starter who thrives in an early-stage, resource-constrained environment and can build process, playbooks, and regional learnings as you go
* Care deeply about AI and believe transforming how software is written is one of the most important problems to work on right now
