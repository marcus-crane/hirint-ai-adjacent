**REGIONAL DIRECTOR, STRATEGIC ENTERPRISE**

Build and lead a team of Strategic Enterprise Account Executives driving revenue growth across new and existing business at SpaceXSI. As an early sales leader, you’ll coach complex enterprise deals and build the playbooks and processes that scale our sales organization. You’ll partner with GTM, Product, and Engineering to bring Cursor to the world’s most sophisticated software teams and help transform how software is built.

**RESPONSIBILITIES:**

* Hire, develop, and coach a team of Strategic Enterprise Account Executives to meet and exceed revenue targets across new and existing accounts.
* Build and execute the regional sales strategy, owning pipeline generation, territory performance, forecast accuracy, and CRM hygiene.
* Support complex enterprise opportunities directly, coaching AEs through live deals and engaging engineering leaders, CTOs, and executive stakeholders.
* Partner with Enablement to build training and onboarding programs that accelerate AE ramp time.
* Work with Product and Field Engineering to refine enterprise positioning and demonstrations, and translate customer feedback into product and go-to-market recommendations.
* Establish repeatable sales playbooks, processes, and operating practices that support the growth of SpaceXSI’s enterprise sales organization.

**BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:**

* 10+ years of professional sales experience.
* 3+ years of experience directly managing Enterprise Account Executives.

**PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:**

* Track record of leading high-performing Enterprise AE teams
* Experience selling developer tools, technical SaaS, AI, or emerging technologies to technical stakeholders, from ICs to CTOs
* Based in the the Bay Area or the Pacific Northwest and have an established network across the enterprise technology market
* A track record of building teams from the ground up and developing AEs into top performers
* A hands-on leader who stays close to deals. You coach through live opportunities, not just pipeline reviews
* Strong operational discipline: you know how to run a forecast, inspect a pipeline, and build repeatable sales processes
* Technically fluent enough to hold credible conversations with senior engineers and CTOs
* Thrive in an early-stage environment and are excited to build the systems and culture of a sales organization, not just manage one
* Help define what great enterprise sales looks like at SpaceXSI, working closely with GTM, Product, and Engineering to land and expand across the world's most sophisticated software teams

**ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:**

* Must be based in the San Francisco Bay Area or Pacific Northwest.

**COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS:**

Base salary is just one part of our total rewards package at SpaceXSI, which also includes equity, comprehensive medical, vision, and dental coverage, access to a 401(k) retirement plan, short & long-term disability insurance, life insurance, and various other discounts and perks.
