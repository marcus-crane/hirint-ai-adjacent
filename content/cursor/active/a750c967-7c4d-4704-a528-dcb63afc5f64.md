**About the Role**  
  
We're hiring Field Engineers to partner with prospective and strategic customers across **Public Sector**. You'll be the technical face of Cursor for the federal civilian and defense agencies modernizing how software gets written for the mission: helping developers, engineering leaders, and program teams evaluate Cursor, running proofs of concept, and making sure they see the full value of agentic coding in their real workflows.

You'll work hand-in-hand with Sales, Product, and Engineering as a deal partner, not a demo resource. You'll scope solutions, demonstrate capabilities, and act as a trusted advisor across the stakeholders that matter in PubSec: engineering leaders, architects, developers, security, procurement, and program leads. This is a highly customer-facing role where deep technical fluency, commercial instinct, and clear communication drive adoption and influence product direction.  
  
**What you'll do**

* Partner with AEs to own technical discovery, demos, POCs, and technical validation for new deals and expansion
* Build trusted relationships across engineering, security, product and IT stakeholders
* Go deep in mission-critical and high-security environments: developer workflows, AI tooling, complex / constrained setups
* Help customers connect Cursor to outcomes that matter: developer productivity, secure software delivery, and mission velocity
* Deliver technical presentations, whiteboards, and workshops for technical and executive audiences
* Act as the customer's technical advocate internally with Product and Engineering
* Partner with post-sales to hand off cleanly and set up onboarding for success

**You may be a fit if**

* You have strong communication skills and executive presence, equally comfortable developing alongside engineers or presenting to CTOs, CISOs, and program leaders
* You understand the SDLC and can connect technical detail to mission and operational impact
* You have hands-on technical fluency (not full-time eng): workflows, AI tooling, complex / constrained environments
* You have deal-partner strategic instincts: co-own with AEs and navigate complex, multi-threaded stakeholder maps
* You have experience in Field Engineering, Solutions Architecture, Pre-Sales, or Consulting, ideally in federal, defense, or highly regulated tech
* You're excited by Public Sector and comfortable learning agency buying motions, security expectations, and partner ecosystems fast
* You hold or can obtain a federal security clearance (active clearance is a plus)
