# The Opportunity

We are looking for a Japan-based Sales Development Representative to join our Marketing team and help create qualified pipeline across Japan and selected Asia-Pacific markets. Japan is the primary market for this role. Regional activity will focus on clearly prioritized campaigns, accounts, events, and partner initiatives where ownership and follow-up expectations are defined.

As an early point of contact for prospective customers, you will combine thoughtful research, responsive follow-up, and relevant outreach to start credible business conversations. You will work closely with Sales and Marketing to understand target accounts, engage the right stakeholders, qualify needs, and hand off well-developed opportunities. This role is well suited to someone who enjoys building relationships, learning complex technology, and working in a collaborative international environment.

# What You Will Do

* Research target organizations, sectors, and stakeholders to support focused prospecting and account-based outreach.
* Engage prospective customers through phone, email, professional networks, digital channels, events, and other appropriate methods.
* Respond promptly to inbound inquiries and leads generated through marketing campaigns, content, webinars, events, and partners.
* Conduct initial discovery to understand business or mission needs, current priorities, relevant stakeholders, timing, and appropriate next steps.
* Qualify opportunities and schedule productive discovery meetings or demonstrations for Account Executives and other customer-facing colleagues.
* Partner with Sales and Marketing on account plans, messaging, campaigns, event outreach, and post-event follow-up.
* Nurture prospects who are not yet ready for a Sales conversation and maintain relevant, professional engagement over time.
* Maintain complete and accurate records of contacts, activities, lead sources, qualification findings, and next steps in Salesforce.
* Support selected APAC campaigns and partner-led activities when the market approach, language path, Sales owner, and follow-up plan are clear.
* Share market observations and prospect feedback to help improve messaging, campaigns, and go-to-market execution.

# What You Bring

* Professional fluency in Japanese and business-level English, with strong written and verbal communication skills in both languages.
* Experience in sales development, business development, inside sales, demand generation, account development, or another B2B customer-facing role.
* A record of creating qualified meetings, opportunities, or pipeline through inbound follow-up, outbound prospecting, or both.
* Ability to research organizations, identify relevant stakeholders, and tailor communication to different audiences.
* Strong listening and questioning skills, with the judgment to distinguish genuine customer needs from early interest.
* Comfort learning and discussing technology-driven solutions and connecting capabilities to customer priorities and outcomes.
* Experience using Salesforce or a comparable CRM, with strong attention to activity records, lead status, source attribution, and next actions.
* Self-direction, persistence, organization, and the ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-moving environment.
* A collaborative working style and the ability to coordinate effectively with colleagues in Japan and across global teams.
* Authorization to work in Japan.

# Preferred Experience

* Experience in enterprise software, SaaS, data, analytics, cybersecurity, risk intelligence, or a related technology field.
* Experience engaging Japanese government, public-sector, regulated-industry, or large-enterprise organizations.
* Familiarity with account-based marketing or account-based sales development.
* Experience supporting events, webinars, partner campaigns, or multi-channel prospecting programs.
* Interest in OSINT, identity intelligence, risk, AI, multilingual data, or mission-driven technology.

# What Success Looks Like

Success in this role means creating credible engagement that develops into qualified Sales conversations and pipeline. You will be known for timely follow-up, sound qualification, relevant communication, dependable collaboration, and accurate CRM records. You will balance consistent activity with quality and judgment, ensuring that prospects receive a professional first experience with Babel Street and that Sales receives useful context for each handoff.
