**Position Overview**

The Mission Intelligence Analyst serves as the bridge between customer mission requirements, intelligence analysis, and technology-enabled operational outcomes. 

This individual works directly alongside customer personnel to understand intelligence gaps, define priority intelligence requirements, investigate complex questions, and identify opportunities to improve how intelligence is collected, analyzed, and disseminated. The analyst leverages the company's intelligence products, data sources, and analytical capabilities to help customers uncover relevant information, identify relationships, assess threats and risks, and make better-informed decisions. 

Unlike a traditional intelligence analyst who may work primarily within an established analytical organization, the Mission Intelligence Analyst operates in a highly collaborative, customer-embedded environment. They must understand not only what the customer needs to know, but also how the customer uses intelligence to make decisions and execute its mission. 

The successful candidate combines strong analytical tradecraft, curiosity, sound judgment, and communication skills with an ability to use modern intelligence technology to improve analytical speed, depth, and effectiveness. 

**Key Responsibilities**

* Mission understanding and intelligence requirements
* Intelligence research and analysis
* Entity resolution and relationship analysis
* Customer engagement and intelligence communication
* Mission impact and continuous improvement

**Required Qualifications**

* 3–5 years of professional experience in intelligence analysis, investigative research, national security, law enforcement intelligence, threat analysis, or a closely related field.
* Demonstrated ability to research complex questions, synthesize information from multiple sources, and develop defensible analytical judgments.
* Familiarity with structured analytical techniques, source evaluation, corroboration, confidence assessment, and communicating uncertainty.
* Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to produce clear analytical products and brief senior stakeholders.
* Experience working with intelligence databases, investigative platforms, research tools, or other information-intensive analytical systems.
* Ability to identify intelligence gaps, develop focused research questions, and work systematically toward actionable findings.
* Strong attention to detail, critical thinking, intellectual curiosity, and professional judgment.
* Ability to engage directly with customers, build relationships, and adapt analytical approaches to different mission environments.
* Ability to work independently, manage multiple priorities, and operate effectively when requirements are ambiguous or evolving.
* Willingness to travel and work at customer locations as required.
