Babel Street is the trusted technology partner for the world’s most advanced identity intelligence and risk operations. We deliver advanced AI and data analytics solutions providing unmatched, analysis-ready data regardless of language, proactive risk identification, 360-degree insights, high-speed automation, and seamless integration into existing systems. Babel Street empowers government and commercial organizations to transform high-stakes identity and risk operations into a strategic advantage.  The actionable insights we deliver safeguard lives and protect critical assets around the world.  Babel Street is headquartered in Reston, Virginia, with regional offices in Boston, MA and Cleveland, OH, and international offices in Australia, Canada, Israel, Japan, and the U.K. For more information, visit [www.babelstreet.com](http://www.babelstreet.com/).

**About the Role** 

We are seeking a Senior Backend Software Engineer to build and evolve the data systems that power Babel Street’s enterprise applications. 

In this hands-on role, you will design, build, and operate backend services that ingest, process, index, store, and serve large volumes of data. You will collaborate with other engineers to plan feature enhancements, make architectural decisions, and deliver reliable, scalable solutions from design through production. 

You will also use AI-assisted and agentic development tools to improve engineering productivity and help strengthen our practices for incorporating these tools into everyday development. 

This position is ideally near our Somerville, MA office but remote candidates will be considered. Eastern or Central time zones are preferred. 

**Why This Role Matters** 

You will help Babel Street transform vast, fast-moving streams of data into intelligence that customers use to make critical decisions. The backend systems you build will power this transformation by ingesting, processing, indexing, and serving data at the speed and scale those decisions demand. 

You will help shape how Babel Street applies agentic development across the software lifecycle. You will work with engineers across the organization to make coding agents effective, secure, and reliable. 

**Key Responsibilities** 

* **Backend Engineering:** Design, implement, test, and maintain backend services and data-processing pipelines using C# and .NET.

* **Data Processing and Search:** Build and optimize systems for ingesting, indexing, storing, querying, and serving large datasets using Elasticsearch and SQL Server.

* **Performance and Scalability:** Identify bottlenecks and improve service performance, query efficiency, reliability, and scalability.

* **Production Ownership:** Own services throughout their lifecycle, from technical design and implementation through deployment and ongoing operation.

* **On-Call Support:** Participate in an on-call rotation supporting systems that operate 24/7. Use metrics, logs, and alerts to investigate incidents, resolve issues, and implement improvements that reduce recurrence.

* **Technical Collaboration:** Contribute to architectural discussions, lead design and code reviews, and mentor other engineers.

* **Development Practices:** Evaluate and recommend improvements to our technology, tooling, and engineering workflows.

* **AI-Assisted Development:** Use coding agents and AI-assisted tools for implementation, testing, and refactoring while maintaining accountability for code quality, security, and correctness.

* **Security and Compliance:** Develop and maintain software in accordance with FedRAMP security and compliance requirements.

**Required Qualifications** 

* 10+ years of professional software development experience building and supporting enterprise applications.
* Strong proficiency in C# and .NET, including designing, building, and troubleshooting production backend services.
* Hands-on experience designing, operating, and optimizing Lucene-based search platforms, preferably Elasticsearch, including index design, mappings, query tuning, and cluster maintenance.
* Strong SQL Server and T-SQL skills, including relational schema design, stored procedures, indexing, and query performance tuning.
* Experience building, maintaining, and scaling data-intensive backend services.
* Experience deploying and supporting production workloads on AWS or Google Cloud Platform.
* Experience troubleshooting production systems using metrics, logs, and alerting.
* Ability to work independently, collaborate effectively on a distributed team, and communicate technical decisions clearly.
* Experience delivering software in an agile development environment.

**Preferred Qualifications** 

* Hands-on experience implementing and maintaining FedRAMP security controls for production software and cloud infrastructure.
* Hands-on experience deploying, operating, and scaling backend services on Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
* Experience using AI coding assistants or agentic development tools for implementation, testing, and refactoring.
* Experience with distributed systems and high-volume data-processing pipelines.
* Experience mentoring engineers and leading technical improvements.
