**Position Overview**

The Mission Engineer is a customer-facing software engineer responsible for turning challenging real-world operational problems into working software solutions. This role operates at the intersection of software engineering, data integration, artificial intelligence, and customer mission execution. 

Unlike traditional software engineers who work primarily within a centralized development organization, the Mission Engineer works directly with customers, operators, analysts, and technical stakeholders to understand how work gets done, identify opportunities for improvement, and deliver technology that solves high-value problems. 

The engineer will own solutions from discovery and technical design through development, deployment, and operational adoption. They will work closely with Mission Analysts, product managers, platform engineers, and customer teams to adapt the company's software capabilities to real-world requirements. 

The ideal candidate is a technically strong, pragmatic engineer who enjoys ambiguity, moves quickly from problem to prototype, and takes ownership of delivering solutions that work in production. 

**Key Responsibilities**

* Customer-embedded problem solving
* AI-enabled solution development
* Software development and solution delivery
* Deployment and operational ownership
* Cross-functional collaboration

**Required Qualifications**

Candidates should demonstrate the following: 

* 3–5 years of relevant experience in professional software engineering, application development, data engineering, or a similar technical role.
* Proficiency in at least one modern programming language, such as Python, Java, TypeScript, JavaScript, Go, or C#.
* Experience designing, developing, debugging, and maintaining software applications.
* Working knowledge of REST APIs, JSON, data structures, databases, and software integration patterns.
* Experience with Git, code reviews, automated testing, and standard software development practices.
* Ability to translate ambiguous customer or business requirements into practical technical designs and working software.
* Strong troubleshooting, analytical thinking, and problem-solving skills.
* Excellent communication skills and the ability to collaborate directly with customers and explain technical concepts to diverse audiences.
* Ability to manage competing priorities, work independently, and deliver results in a dynamic environment.
* Willingness to work directly with customers, including on-site engagements and travel as required.
