**Position Summary:** 

We are seeking an experienced Engineering Manager to lead our Secure Access team. The platform operates primarily on-premise, with one component residing in the cloud. The ideal candidate is a hands-on leader with engineering leadership experience who can work across infrastructure and application development while balancing reliability for existing customers with the delivery of new features. 

**What you will do:** 

In this role, you will lead a team of Software and Platform Engineers responsible for the Secure Access application throughout its lifecycle, including its integration with our on-premises and cloud environments. You will set the technical direction, manage priorities and delivery, and mentor engineers across both disciplines. 

**Required Skills:** 

* Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or a related field, or equivalent practical experience

* At least three years of direct engineering management experience

* At least five years of hands-on experience in platform, infrastructure, or software engineering

* Experience managing virtualized on-premises infrastructure, particularly VMware vSphere/ESXi

* Experience with VDI or remote-access infrastructure

* Experience deploying and operating workloads on a major cloud platform, preferably GCP

* Working knowledge of Linux administration, containers, and CI/CD pipelines

* Ability to break down a product vision into workable engineering tasks

* Ability to communicate clearly and concisely to nontechnical stakeholders

 

**Nice to Have:** 

* Experience with networking technologies, including VLANs, routing, firewalls, VPNs, load balancing, and DNS

* Experience extending or migrating on-premises workloads to GCP

* Experience with automation tools, such as Terraform and Ansible

* Experience working in regulated or security-sensitive environments, including FedRAMP, CMMC, or SOC 2

* Knowledge of identity and access management protocols, including SSO, OIDC, and SAML
