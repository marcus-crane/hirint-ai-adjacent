**A World-Changing Company**

Palantir builds the world’s leading software for data-driven decisions and operations. By bringing the right data to the people who need it, our platforms empower our partners to develop lifesaving drugs, forecast supply chain disruptions, locate missing children, and more.

### **The Role**

We’re looking for a Supply Chain Security Analyst who can think broadly about how risk enters an organization through the third parties it depends on and how that risk is identified, reduced, and monitored over time.

Palantir relies on an extensive supply chain of suppliers, vendors, and partners to fulfill our mission, and that supply chain may introduce substantive information security risks to our ability to operate globally. You will conduct deeply technical risk analysis of these third parties, drawing on penetration tests, audit reports, architecture diagrams, questionnaires, and direct engagement with vendor security teams. Where risks are identified, you will propose and implement mitigative controls, then revisit them as vendor posture shifts, adversary tactics evolve, and industry incidents expose new risk.

This is not a questionnaire-checking role; the adversaries we face are sophisticated and increasingly reach us through our suppliers. We need someone who understands both the detail and the broader system: what a vendor’s evidence actually proves, who the vendor really is and who ultimately owns them, how an AI capability added to a product we already use changes the risk we already accepted, and how to turn a finding into a control that holds up in practice.

### Core Responsibilities

* Assess, manage, and report on overall information security risk to Palantir from our supply chain, including where that risk concentrates across the vendor portfolio and how it shifts over time
* Perform in-depth security assessments of third parties, incorporating numerous data points (e.g. penetration tests, SOC 2 and ISO 27001 reports, questionnaires, architecture diagrams, interviews) to reach a comprehensive risk-based conclusion about the security maturity of the third party
* Assess AI vendors and AI features added to products already in use, covering training and retention commitments, downstream model providers, guardrails, human oversight, and how inputs and outputs are handled
* Identify, propose, and implement mitigative controls to address supply chain risks or security deficiencies, and provide guidance to Palantir employees and prospective suppliers on Palantir security requirements and remediation
* Build cross-functional partnerships with Legal, Procurement, Threat Intelligence, and Physical Security to find opportunities to strengthen supplier security across the business
* Automate and streamline supply chain security workflows to cut manual effort, increase consistency, and keep those systems reliable as they scale
* Maintain awareness of emerging information security risks and trends and their impact on our overall supply chain

### What We're Looking For

* A deep understanding of information security controls, including data protection, access controls, policies and standards, proactive security, reactive security, application security, and related measures
* Experience performing third-party or supply chain risk assessments or evaluations
* Experience with information security and risk management frameworks, standards, and certifications (e.g. ISO 27001, NIST 800-53, NIST 800-171, NIST 800-161, CMMC, SOC 2, NIST AI RMF)
* The ability to read security evidence critically: to tell a meaningfully scoped audit report from a thin one, to see what a penetration test left out of scope, and to distinguish what a control is designed to do from what it actually does
* Familiarity with how adversaries reach organizations through their suppliers and with the controls used to prevent, detect, and contain such attacks
* The ability to engage vendors directly, hold your position under commercial pressure, and negotiate security outcomes rather than accept the first answer
* The ability to develop automation and build scalable workflows that increase efficiency and reduce supply chain security risk

### What We Value

* Strong technical decomposition and analytical skills, with the ability to identify supply chain risks based on industry security breaches and incidents
* The ability to own projects, make progress through ambiguity, and balance competing priorities while working well with colleagues
* Exceptional written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to explain technical risk to non-technical stakeholders and work with partner teams to drive remediation
* A strong desire to own impactful security outcomes in a fast-paced, dynamic working environment
* Willingness and eligibility to obtain a U.S. security clearance preferred

### What We Require

* 3+ years of hands-on experience in information security, third-party or supply chain risk, security assurance, security engineering, or a closely related field
* Demonstrated experience performing third-party or supply chain security assessments, with the ability and interest to work across multiple vendor and technology domains
* A strong sense of ownership, sound judgment, and integrity
