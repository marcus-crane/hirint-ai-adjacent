## **UK — 12-month Fixed-Term Contract**

### **About the Role**

Wayve is pioneering Embodied AI for autonomous driving. We are looking for a Talent Acquisition Associate to join our London team on a 12-month fixed-term contract and support the continued growth of our real-world testing and operations.

In this role, you’ll manage hiring across Fleet Operations in the UK, Europe, and beyond, covering high-volume, specialist, and manager-level roles. Partnering closely with hiring managers and senior leaders, you’ll translate business needs into effective recruitment strategies and help build the teams needed to scale Wayve’s global operations safely and effectively.

### **Key Responsibilities**

* **Hiring Delivery:** Independently manage end-to-end recruitment across high-volume frontline, technical, specialist, and manager-level Fleet Operations roles, from intake and market mapping through assessment, offer, and close.
* **Sourcing:** Build representative candidate pipelines through direct sourcing and a range of attraction channels, developing scalable approaches for Vehicle Safety Operator and other high-volume hiring.
* **Stakeholder Partnership:** Build trusted relationships with hiring managers and Directors, providing consultative guidance on role definition, assessment, market conditions, candidate quality, and closing strategies.
* **Candidate and Hiring Experience:** Deliver an inclusive, transparent, and well-managed candidate experience while supporting hiring teams with structured interviewing, timely feedback, and consistent decision-making.
* **Data and Market Insights:** Use recruitment data and market intelligence to monitor progress, identify risks and bottlenecks, improve funnel performance, and shape realistic hiring strategies.
* **Collaboration and Improvement:** Partner across Talent Acquisition, People, Finance, and Fleet Operations on workforce planning, employer branding, and improvements to hiring processes.

### **About You**

To set you up for success as a Talent Acquisition Associate at Wayve, we are looking for the following skills and experience:

#### **Essential**

* Demonstrated success independently delivering full-cycle recruitment within a fast-paced or scaling organisation.
* Experience recruiting across a broad range of roles, including high-volume or frontline hiring alongside specialist, professional, or manager-level positions.
* The confidence and judgement to advise senior stakeholders, constructively challenge assumptions, and translate business needs into effective hiring strategies.
* Strong direct-sourcing skills and experience using multiple attraction channels to build candidate pipelines.
* A hands-on, adaptable, and highly organised approach, with the ability to balance competing priorities while maintaining strong attention to detail.
* Confidence using applicant tracking systems, recruitment data, and market insights to inform decisions and communicate progress effectively.

#### **Desirable**

* Experience recruiting within fleet operations, automotive, logistics, manufacturing, field operations, mobility, customer operations, or another high-volume operational environment.
* Experience supporting hiring across multiple countries or working with globally distributed stakeholders.
* Familiarity with Ashby or a similar applicant tracking system.
* Experience designing or improving volume-hiring campaigns, recruitment events, or structured assessment processes.
* Experience working in a startup or scale-up environment where priorities and processes continue to evolve.

This is a full-time, 12-month fixed-term contract based in our King's Cross office in London. At Wayve, we want the best of all worlds, so we operate a hybrid working policy that combines a minimum of two days each week in the office with time spent working from home.
