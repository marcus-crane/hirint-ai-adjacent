## **The role**

As a Software Engineer on the Platform Integration Team, you will play a key role in the hands-on software bring-up of new vehicle programs, helping turn them into stable, reliable robot systems. Alongside integration activities, you’ll design and deliver improvements to vehicle-facing software components and frameworks that make future integrations faster, more predictable, and less bespoke. This is a highly technical engineering role within our Robot Software group, where your work directly enables safe, scalable deployment across multiple vehicle programs

**Key responsibilities:**

* Contribute to the software bring-up of new vehicle programs, supporting bench, HIL, and on-vehicle integration activities, including hands-on work at international test locations.
* Design, develop, and maintain vehicle-facing software components and shared frameworks that improve reliability, abstraction, and reuse across vehicle programs.
* Improve the stability of early-stage vehicles by diagnosing integration issues, implementing fixes where appropriate, and driving issues through to resolution with relevant teams.
* Identify and deliver architectural improvements that reduce bespoke changes and make future vehicle integrations faster and more predictable.
* Build and maintain tooling, diagnostics, and metrics to monitor robot software performance and enable continuous reliability improvements.
* Produce clear documentation and runbooks to support knowledge transfer and long-term maintainability.
* Collaborate closely with hardware, safety, controls, calibration, and reliability teams to ensure smooth and scalable vehicle software integration.

## **About you**

In order to set you up for success as a Software Engineer on the Platform Integration Team at Wayve, we’re looking for the following skills and experience:

**Essential**

* Strong software development experience in C++, Python, and/or Rust, with a track record of delivering production-quality systems.
* Experience developing and debugging software on Linux-based robotic or automotive platforms, including multi-process or distributed systems.
* Experience with ROS2 or similar robotics middleware, including node development, message interfaces, and system integration.
* Hands-on experience integrating software with vehicle or embedded hardware, including debugging using logs, telemetry, and on-target testing.
* Practical experience working with vehicle sensors, including topics such as calibration, synchronisation, and data validation.
* Experience working with automotive-grade high-performance compute platforms (e.g., NVIDIA DRIVE, Qualcomm Snapdragon Ride, or similar embedded SoCs).
* Strong systems-level debugging skills, with the ability to diagnose and resolve complex integration issues across software and hardware boundaries.
* Familiarity with vehicle communication systems (e.g. CAN, Automotive Ethernet) and associated debugging tools.

**Desirable**

* Experience with **drive-by-wire systems** or other safety-critical control interfaces.
* Familiarity with state-of-the-art automotive bus analyzing tools (Vector CANoe/ CANalyzer/ Kvaser)
* Experience building or evolving **shared frameworks, libraries, or internal platforms** used by multiple teams.
* Experience improving system architecture to reduce technical debt and increase reuse across projects.
* Experience with **hardware-in-the-loop (HIL)** or simulation environments for integration and validation.
* Exposure to **functional safety concepts** or development within safety-critical systems.
* Experience writing clear **technical documentation**, integration guides, or operational runbooks.
* Experience contributing to observability, logging, or system metrics for reliability and performance monitoring.
