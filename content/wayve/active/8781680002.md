## The role

As the Lead Technical Program Manager for Quality & Compliance at Wayve, you'll build and lead the technical program management function that rolls out company-wide quality and compliance initiatives - spanning quality (including ASPICE), security, data privacy, and operational safety. This role reaches across the whole of Wayve: you'll turn complex, often heavyweight requirements into pragmatic, low-overhead processes that raise the bar without slowing the company down.

You'll lead flagship initiatives yourself while supporting and coaching a small, high-impact TPM team. Your impact is measured in how effectively Wayve meets its quality and compliance obligations - and how lightly those obligations sit on the organisation. A successful TPM leader is a force multiplier - helping teams move faster, more effectively, and with purpose.

### Key Responsibilities

* **Lead the function:** Build, coach, and grow a small TPM team; hire to fill gaps and raise the bar.
* **Roll out company-wide compliance initiatives:** Drive programs across quality (including ASPICE), security, data privacy, and operational safety - from framework to embedded practice.
* **Trusted partner across Wayve:** A leader in the org, you partner with engineering, product, safety, security, privacy / legal, and operations leaders to land outcomes and hold the bar - influencing without authority at every level.
* **Make process simple and low-overhead:** Design pragmatic, lightweight processes that meet the bar with minimal friction; negotiate must-haves vs nice-to-haves and find creative ways to meet long-standing requirements.
* **Establish scalable practices:** Governance, KPIs and dashboards, audit readiness, escalations and operational reviews.
* **Align cross-functionally:** Work across every part of Wayve - and external auditors / partners where relevant; manage dependencies, risk, and trade-offs.
* **Connect to outcomes and represent Quality & Compliance:** Tie delivery to compliance status, audit readiness, risk reduction, and organisational efficiency, and represent quality & compliance in company-level reviews.

## About you

In order to set you up for success as a Lead Technical Program Manager, Quality & Compliance at Wayve, we're looking for the following skills and experience.

**Essential**

* **8+ years in quality / compliance / process programs:** Deep, hands-on experience delivering quality, compliance, or process programs as a technical program manager - ideally spanning standards such as ASPICE, ISO, security, privacy, or safety - including people and process leadership. You've built or scaled a program function and you get things done with ownership and a bias for action.
* **People leadership:** You build, coach, and grow high-performing teams, and raise the bar on the craft.
* **Pragmatic and creative:** You negotiate must-haves vs nice-to-haves, and find creative ways to meet requirements that have long been done a certain way - keeping process simple and low-overhead rather than adding bureaucracy.
* **Exceptional communicator & influencer:** This role crosses all of Wayve; you communicate with unusual clarity and influence without authority at every level, bringing people along.
* **Very good at process:** You design simple, low-overhead processes that raise quality and keep the organisation efficient.
* **Stays neutral and adapts under pressure:** Effective in ambiguous, fast-moving environments; you flex your style and bring structure without slowing delivery.
* **Systems thinking:** You reason about how the parts of a complex organisation and its processes fit together, and how a change in one area ripples into others.
* **Product-minded:** You focus on outcomes and the people your programs serve - prioritising by impact and defining what good looks like, not just tracking activity.
* **Raises the bar without the drag:** You strengthen quality and compliance while keeping the organisation efficient and fast-moving.
* **Strategic & business impact:** You link quality and compliance investment to measurable outcomes - audit readiness, risk reduction, and efficiency.
* **Data-informed:** You steer with the right metrics and evidence, and set direction with clarity.
* **Growth mindset:** Open to feedback and always looking to improve.

**Desirable**

* Working knowledge of automotive / safety-critical or security / privacy standards - e.g. Automotive SPICE (ASPICE), ISO 26262, SOTIF (ISO 21448), ISO 27001, or GDPR / data privacy.
* Background in autonomous vehicles, robotics, or another regulated / safety-critical domain.
* An engineering or computer science degree, or experience working as an engineer.
