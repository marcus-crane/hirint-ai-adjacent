## **The Role**

As a DevOps / Cloud Infrastructure & CI/CD Engineer, you will help build, operate, and improve the cloud platforms, build systems, and continuous integration and delivery infrastructure that support Wayve’s software development lifecycle.  
Working as part of a team, you’ll focus on creating reliable, scalable, secure, and efficient environments for software development, testing, deployment, and release. This is a hands-on role with opportunities to deepen your expertise in cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, build systems, developer tooling, automation, observability, and platform engineering.

## **Key Responsibilities**

* Build, operate, and improve cloud infrastructure, CI/CD pipelines, and build systems supporting development, testing, and production.
* Develop and maintain Infrastructure-as-Code, automation, and developer tooling to enable reliable, repeatable delivery.
* Operate and scale CI/CD services, including build agents, runners, artifact storage, and containerised workloads.
* Implement observability, monitor system health, and troubleshoot infrastructure, pipeline, and deployment issues.
* Work with engineering teams to ensure platforms meet application needs and support scalable software delivery.
* Contribute to incident response, root cause analysis, and continuous improvement of infrastructure and operational practices.

### **About you**

**Essential**

* Strong troubleshooting, problem-solving, and communication skills.
* 5 years of experience in DevOps, cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, build engineering, developer infrastructure, or related engineering roles.
* Experience with **one or more** of the following:
  + At least one cloud platform, such as Azure, AWS, or GCP.
  + Infrastructure-as-Code tools, such as Terraform and Ansible.
  + CI/CD platforms such as GitHub Actions, GitLab CI, Jenkins, Buildkite, Azure DevOps, or similar.
  + Build tooling and concepts including dependency management, caching, incremental builds, and reproducible builds.
  + Networking, cloud architecture, system reliability, and secure access controls.Experience with Linux, Git, and shell scripting.

**Desirable**

* *Experience with build systems such as Bazel, Buildkite, or similar.*
* *Experience operating CI/CD infrastructure at scale, including distributed execution, caching, and artifact management.*
* *Experience with Docker, Kubernetes, and container-based build or deployment environments.*
* *Familiarity with monitoring and observability tools such as Prometheus, Grafana, and ELK.*
* *Understanding of software supply-chain security, secure build practices, and cloud security best practices.*
* *E**xperience supporting large, multi-language codebases or distributed systems.*
* *Experience improving cloud cost efficiency and operational resilience.*

This is a full-time role based in our office in Tokyo, Japan.  At Wayve we want the best of all worlds so we operate a hybrid working policy that combines time together in our offices and workshops to fuel innovation, culture, relationships and learning, and time spent working from home.
