# **The role**

As a Software Engineer on our Fleet Management team, you'll build the tools and services that let Wayve's engineers and operators manage a global fleet of autonomous vehicles and data-collection devices. The work spans frontend, backend, APIs, cloud services, and data — covering vehicle health tracking, software and configuration management, and location-based services.

You'll work alongside more senior engineers, contributing to technical design and taking ownership of features and services end-to-end.

---

**Key responsibilities**

* Build and maintain full-stack features across Fleet Management services.
* Contribute across frontend, backend, API, database, and cloud service implementation.
* Contribute to technical design discussions and help deliver scalable, maintainable solutions.
* Write well-tested, maintainable, and performant code.
* Review peers' code to support quality, consistency, and shared learning.
* Build and support systems that help Fleet Operations and Engineering monitor vehicle health, configuration, software status, and location.

---

**About you**

**Essential skills**

* Professional software engineering experience building full-stack web applications or services.
* Hands-on experience across frontend and backend development, with enough breadth to contribute across the stack.
* Experience designing or working with APIs, databases, schemas, and backend services.
* The ability to deliver clean, tested, maintainable code.
* Familiarity with cloud services, distributed systems, or microservices.

**Desirable skills**

* Experience with React, TypeScript, MUI, Rust, Go, Node.js, Kubernetes, Bazel, or Azure.
* Experience working on fleet management, robotics, autonomy, drone, vehicle, or operational tooling products.
* Experience with scalable backend systems, monitoring, reliability, or high-volume data workflows.

This is a full-time role based in our office Sunnyvale. At Wayve we want the best of all worlds so we operate a hybrid working policy that combines time together in our offices and workshops to fuel innovation, culture, relationships and learning, and time spent working from home.
