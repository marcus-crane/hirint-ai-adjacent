## **The role**

Portal is the cloud platform that connects Wayve to the outside world: handling data exchange with partners, surfacing AI insights, and enabling the commercial deployment of Wayve's AI driver on vehicles across the globe.

We're looking for a **Senior Software Engineer, Data** to help build Wayve's **Data Transfer Hub**: a mission-critical piece of infrastructure that will scale to transfer **petabytes of sensor data per day** from partners covering vehicles around the world.

This is a pivotal project at Wayve. You'll work as part of a small, high-autonomy team building a globally distributed hub-and-spoke data exchange system, working with partners like Uber, Stellantis and Nissan. Your work will help make Wayve's AI model training possible at scale.

**Key responsibilities:**

* Build and operate **large-scale data transfer pipelines for video, LiDAR and sensor data** from global partners at up to PB/day scale
* Contribute to the technical design of the Data Transfer Hub, making pragmatic trade-offs across UX, careliability, throughput, cost, partner constraints, observability and operational support
* Build **parallelised, distributed data transfer pipelines** using Flyte for workflow orchestration, Kafka/event-driven patterns for lifecycle tracing, and Azure for storage and transfer infrastructure
* Help implement and evolve a globally distributed **hub-and-spoke data transfer model** to retrieve and share sensor data at scale with partners
* Write infrastructure-as-code in **Terraform** and pipeline logic primarily in **Python**
* Build reliable operational foundations, including retry strategies, backfills, data integrity checks, lifecycle/state tracking, dashboards and alerting
* Work on **cloud-to-cloud and cross-cloud networking** solutions, with Azure as the primary cloud platform
* Once established, participate in an out-of-hours on-call and support rotation.
* Collaborate with product, commercial and partner-facing teams to understand constraints and deliver solutions that solve real customer and partner problems
* Work in a fast-changing environment where requirements and partner deliverable dates evolve, while maintaining high engineering standards

## **About you**

In order to set you up for success as a Senior Software Engineer at Wayve, we’re looking for the following skills and experience.

**Essential**

* Proven experience building and operating large-scale data transfer, ingestion or data processing pipelines, ideally at multi-TB or PB scale
* Strong software engineering skills
* Experience delivering & maintaining production systems
* Experience with workflow orchestration for parallelised jobs; Flyte preferred, but Airflow, Prefect or similar tools are also relevant
* Experience building reliable, observable data systems, including retry strategies, backfills, data integrity checks, lifecycle/state tracking and operational alerting
* Strong cloud platform skills, with Azure preferred; experience with AWS or GCP is a plus
* Proficiency in Python and Terraform
* Experience with blob/object storage, large-scale file movement, networking or cloud-to-cloud data transfer
* A product mindset: you think about the customer and build solutions that fix real problems, not just implement requirements
* Comfort working in a fast-changing environment with evolving requirements
* A strong sense of ownership: you take responsibility for outcomes, communicate clearly, and help the team move quickly

**Desirable**

* Experience working with external data partners or customer-facing technical integrations
* Background in autonomous vehicles, robotics, geospatial data, video processing or other data-intensive domains
* Experience with Kafka or other event-driven systems for transfer observability and lifecycle tracking
* Experience building dashboards to communicate transfer job status
* Multi-cloud experience across Azure, AWS and/or GCP
* Experience improving cost efficiency, throughput or reliability of large-scale cloud data systems
