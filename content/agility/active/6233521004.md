Agility’s commercially deployed humanoids operate alongside teams in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers—tackling physically demanding and repetitive tasks while enabling workers to focus on higher-value work. With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential.

**About The Role**

The Manager, Mechanical Engineering will both manage mechanical engineers and will be a technical leader who works closely with cross functional teams to drive the successful execution of the robot design.

**About The Work**

* Lead and inspire a talented team of 4 - 8 mechanical engineers to bring products from concept all the way to production.
* Provide coaching, mentorship and guidance, helping the team enhance their skills, improve the quality of their work, and grow professionally.
* Work cross functionally to anticipate and remove roadblocks.
* Be a technical leader for complex robotics systems.
* Develop right-sized process to help the team work more effectively and to produce high quality, reliable designs.
* Regularly communicate project progress, key milestones, and any challenges that arise to senior management.
* Reduce complexity by pushing for simple and elegant designs.
* Drive data-driven decisions and ensure the cross functional team understands the “why”.

**About You**

* Demonstrated extreme ownership, thrive in ambiguity, fast paced environment
* 8+ years of mechanical design experience with 2+ years direct management experience
* B.S. or M.S. in Mechanical Engineering (or equivalent, minimum requirement)
* Proven experience designing and building complex systems, ideally with a background in robotics or autonomous systems
* Demonstrated experience mechanical design, CAD and PDM
* Skilled in conflict resolution and team development
* Ability to be on-site at Agility’s design facility as needed (minimum 4 days per month and as needed to support design activities, reviews, prototype testing, etc.)
* Authorization to work in the USA

 

**Bonus Points**

* Robotics experience and leadership in a robotics organization
* Advanced degree in engineering or science or robotics
* Jira/Confluence experience
* PLM and PDM experience
* Creo experience
