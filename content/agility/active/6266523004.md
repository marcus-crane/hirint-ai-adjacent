Agility’s commercially deployed humanoids operate alongside teams in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers—tackling physically demanding and repetitive tasks while enabling workers to focus on higher-value work. With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential.

**About the role**

Agility is scaling from one site to a network of three: Fremont, CA; Salem, OR; and Pittsburgh, PA. We need an EHS leader to build one consistent safety program across all of them. You’ll set the standard for how we keep people safe around advanced robotics, high-energy equipment, and fast-changing lab and production environments. You’ll also be the person teams across engineering, manufacturing, facilities, and operations trust to help them move fast safely. This role can be based near any of our three sites. Expect to travel between Fremont, Salem, and Pittsburgh 50–70% of the time, with travel heaviest during site launches and expansions.

**About the work**

* Build and lead a unified EHS program, including policies, procedures, training, and metrics, applied consistently across Fremont, Salem, and Pittsburgh as we expand.
* Set safety standards for robotics development and production environments, including robot work cells, R&D and test labs, gantry systems, and human-robot interaction spaces.
* Own electrical and energy safety programs, including NFPA 70E, lockout/tagout, battery and charger safety, and energy storage hazards.
* Lead site inspections, risk assessments, and safety reviews for new equipment, new spaces, and facility buildouts.
* Investigate incidents and near misses, analyze trends, and drive corrective actions to closure.
* Maintain regulatory compliance and recordkeeping (OSHA, EPA, state programs) and own required reporting.
* Partner across engineering, manufacturing, facilities, People Ops, and leadership. Meet teams where they work and build a safety culture people actually buy into.
* Develop and deliver safety training for a workforce that ranges from production technicians to research engineers.

**About you**

* 8+ years in EHS, including 3+ years leading a program across multiple sites, ideally in robotics, advanced manufacturing, hardware, or high-tech lab environments.
* Deep working knowledge of OSHA and EPA requirements.
* Hands-on experience with NFPA codes (70E, 70/NEC, 855 a plus) and electrical, battery, and lithium-ion safety.
* Familiarity with industrial robot safety standards (ANSI/RIA R15.06, ISO 10218) and A3 (formerly RIA) guidance is a strong plus.
* Experience with gantries, cranes, and material handling equipment safety.
* You thrive in fast-paced environments where equipment, layouts, and priorities change often, and you can keep standards high without slowing the work down.
* You build relationships easily and work comfortably across departments, seniority levels, and very different working styles. At Agility, how you work with people matters as much as what you know.
* You’re comfortable with frequent travel and with being the consistent EHS presence at every site.
* Bachelor’s degree in Occupational Safety, Environmental Health, Engineering, or a related field. CSP, CIH, or ASP certification preferred.
* DOT Hazmat compliance experience is a plus.
