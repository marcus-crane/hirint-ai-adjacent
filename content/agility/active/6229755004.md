With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential.

## **About the Role**

We are seeking a hands-on **Manufacturing Test Equipment Technician** to maintain, repair, calibrate, and improve the test stations and equipment that support our manufacturing operations.

This role is ideal for a technically curious, mechanically inclined problem solver who enjoys building, troubleshooting, and keeping equipment operating at a high level of reliability. You will play a critical role in ensuring that manufacturing test equipment remains functional, calibrated, organized, and ready to support production.

In addition to hands-on maintenance and repair, you will help bring structure to equipment documentation, spare parts, inventory, Bills of Materials (BOMs), and maintenance processes. You will work closely with Manufacturing, Test Engineering, Production, and Quality to minimize equipment downtime and continuously improve the reliability and maintainability of our test infrastructure.

## **About the Work**

### **Test Equipment Maintenance & Repair**

* Perform preventive, routine, and corrective maintenance on manufacturing test stations and related equipment
* Diagnose and repair mechanical, electrical, and basic software or system issues affecting test equipment
* Troubleshoot equipment failures and respond to issues quickly to minimize production downtime
* Perform equipment calibration in accordance with established procedures, schedules, and quality requirements.  Setup outside calibration for production test equipment.
* Document equipment failures, repairs, maintenance activities, and calibration results accurately and consistently
* Identify recurring equipment issues and recommend improvements to increase reliability and reduce downtime
* Daily triage manufacturing test issues, resolve, and get test engineering support to help resolve and keep robot manufacturing test cells up.

### **Test Equipment Build & Assembly**

* Assemble, build, modify, and troubleshoot test fixtures, test stations, cables, harnesses, and related equipment
* Build and solder custom cables and harness assemblies used in manufacturing test systems
* Support the installation, setup, and configuration of new test equipment and production test stations
* Assist with equipment upgrades, modifications, and continuous improvement initiatives

### **Parts, Inventory & Documentation**

* Maintain an organized inventory of spare parts, consumables, tools, fixtures, and equipment components
* Track replacement parts and identify components that are approaching end-of-life or require replenishment
* Coordinate the ordering and procurement of replacement parts and equipment components
* Help manage relationships and communication with equipment and component vendors
* Assist with Bill of Materials (BOM) management, including creating BOMs for new test fixtures, maintaining replacement material lists, and managing part ordering processes
* Maintain clear and accurate maintenance procedures, work instructions, equipment records, and documentation
* Read, follow, and help improve standardized work instructions for equipment maintenance and repair

### **Systems & Cross-Functional Support**

* Demonstrate general command-line comfort (e.g., terminal, PowerShell) to configure, troubleshoot, and maintain test equipment and associated computer systems
* Support the configuration and basic administration of computers and systems used in manufacturing test environments
* Communicate equipment status, downtime, repair progress, and technical issues clearly to Manufacturing, Engineering, Production, and Quality teams
* Help establish and improve preventive maintenance, calibration, spare parts, and equipment management processes
* Escalate complex technical issues to the appropriate engineering or technical teams when necessary

## **About You**

* Strong mechanical and technical aptitude with a hands-on, builder mindset
* Experience maintaining and repairing equipment in a manufacturing, production, laboratory, or test environment
* Strong troubleshooting skills across mechanical, electrical, and basic computer or software systems
* Experience performing preventive and corrective maintenance
* Experience performing or supporting equipment calibration
* Ability to assemble, modify, troubleshoot, and repair test fixtures and equipment
* Soldering experience and the ability to build and repair cables and harnesses
* Ability to read and interpret technical documentation, drawings, wiring diagrams, and work instructions
* Experience creating, maintaining, or working from Bills of Materials (BOMs)
* Experience managing spare parts, consumables, tools, and equipment inventory
* Comfort using command-line interfaces for basic system configuration and troubleshooting
* Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
* Ability to clearly document technical work and communicate effectively across teams
* Self-motivated and able to prioritize work in a fast-paced manufacturing environment

## **Why Join Us**

This is an opportunity to play a critical, hands-on role in keeping manufacturing test infrastructure running.

Your work will directly impact equipment uptime, production throughput, product quality, and the ability of the manufacturing organization to scale. You will have the opportunity to not only repair and maintain equipment, but also help build the processes, documentation, spare parts systems, and maintenance infrastructure that support a growing manufacturing operation.

If you enjoy solving technical problems, building things, improving equipment, and seeing the direct impact of your work on production every day, this role offers the opportunity to make a meaningful difference.
