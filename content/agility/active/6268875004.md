## **About the Role**

As a **Cost Engineer**, you will be responsible for developing technical cost models that enable Agility Robotics to make informed sourcing, design, and manufacturing decisions. You will leverage engineering knowledge, manufacturing expertise, and should-cost methodologies to estimate the expected cost of components, assemblies, and manufacturing processes throughout the product lifecycle.

In this role, you will partner closely with Global Supply Managers (GSMs), Engineering, Supplier Industrialization Engineers (SIEs), Manufacturing, Procurement, and Cost Accounting to establish cost baselines, identify cost reduction opportunities, and support strategic sourcing initiatives. While Cost Accounting will establish financial cost targets and track actual product costs, you will own the technical analysis that determines what products and processes should cost based on design, materials, manufacturing methods, labor, tooling, and market conditions.

This role requires a strong combination of engineering, manufacturing, and commercial understanding with the ability to translate complex technical designs into data-driven cost models that improve sourcing decisions and drive long-term product competitiveness.

**Success in this role is measured by the accuracy and effectiveness of should-cost models, the identification of value engineering opportunities, and the organization's ability to make better sourcing and design decisions through technical cost transparency.**

## **About the Work**

* Develop should-cost models for mechanical, electrical, electromechanical, and purchased assemblies using engineering drawings, specifications, bills of materials (BOMs), and manufacturing process knowledge.
* Analyze component costs by evaluating materials, manufacturing processes, labor content, tooling, cycle times, yield assumptions, and overhead factors.
* Partner with Global Supply Managers (GSMs) to support supplier negotiations by providing data-driven cost models and technical cost analysis.
* Collaborate with Engineering during product development to identify cost reduction opportunities through design optimization, material selection, and manufacturing process improvements.
* Support Supplier Industrialization Engineers (SIEs) by evaluating manufacturing processes, tooling strategies, and production methods to validate cost assumptions.
* Work with Cost Accounting to align technical should-cost models with product cost targets and financial reporting.
* Benchmark supplier quotations against internally developed should-cost models and identify significant cost gaps.
* Develop cost models for new manufacturing processes including machining, sheet metal fabrication, injection molding, die casting, printed circuit board assemblies (PCBAs), cable harnesses, and electromechanical assemblies.
* Evaluate the financial impact of engineering changes, supplier changes, and manufacturing process improvements.
* Support request for quotation (RFQ) activities by providing independent cost estimates prior to supplier engagement.
* Maintain libraries of manufacturing process assumptions, labor rates, material costs, and cost modeling standards.
* Develop standardized methodologies, templates, and best practices for technical cost modeling across the organization.
* Support value engineering and cost reduction workshops with cross-functional teams.
* Present cost analyses and recommendations to Engineering, Supply Chain, Finance, and executive leadership.

## **About You**

* Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical discipline.
* 5+ years of experience in cost engineering, value engineering, manufacturing engineering, industrial engineering, strategic sourcing, or product cost management within hardware, robotics, automotive, aerospace, or advanced manufacturing environments.
* Experience developing should-cost models for machined, fabricated, molded, cast, electronic, or electromechanical components and assemblies.
* Strong understanding of manufacturing processes including machining, sheet metal fabrication, injection molding, die casting, electronics manufacturing services (EMS), printed circuit board assembly (PCBA), cable harness manufacturing, and final assembly operations.
* Experience interpreting engineering drawings, geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T), bills of materials (BOMs), and manufacturing process documentation.
* Knowledge of cost modeling methodologies including labor modeling, cycle time analysis, tooling amortization, material utilization, overhead allocation, and manufacturing economics.
* Experience supporting supplier negotiations through technical cost analysis and should-cost benchmarking.
* Strong analytical and quantitative skills with advanced proficiency in Microsoft Excel and cost modeling tools.
* Experience working with enterprise resource planning (ERP), product lifecycle management (PLM), and engineering documentation systems.
* Excellent communication skills with the ability to explain complex technical cost analyses to engineering, supply chain, finance, and executive stakeholders.
* Ability to travel domestically and internationally as required (up to 25%).
