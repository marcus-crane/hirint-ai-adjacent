Agility’s commercially deployed humanoids operate alongside teams in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers—tackling physically demanding and repetitive tasks while enabling workers to focus on higher-value work. With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential.

**About the Role**

The Facilities Maintenance Technician ensures safe, reliable, and well-maintained facilities by performing skilled and semi-skilled tasks in plumbing, carpentry, electrical, painting, and general building maintenance. This role emphasizes preventive, predictive and reactive maintenance, excellent customer service, problem-solving, and teamwork.

**About the Work**

* Perform general maintenance, repairs, and preventive maintenance on building systems, equipment, and facilities.
* Inspect buildings and grounds to ensure timely repairs and maintenance.
* Read and interpret blueprints, shop drawings, OEM and maintenance manuals.
* Create, complete and document work orders, inspections, and maintenance records.
* Trade-specific tasks include:

+ Plumbing: Repair/replace bathroom and kitchen fixtures, clean drains, renew washers, gaskets, and valves.
+ Carpentry: Patch drywall, repair doors/locks, replace ceiling tiles, and perform general construction work.
+ Painting: Prepare surfaces, match colors, and perform touch-ups.
+ Mechanical Systems: Replace filters, service equipment, and support mechanical upkeep.

* Assemble, move, and install furniture, fixtures, stands or equipment as needed.
* Maintain tools, equipment, and workspace in a safe, clean, operational condition.
* Respond to facility-related emergencies and participate in safety drills.
* Operate basic power tools (e.g., drills, saws, grinders, pneumatic/hydraulic tools, presses).
* Other duties as assigned.

**About You**

* 3-5 years experience in general building maintenance and basic trades (plumbing, carpentry, painting, etc.).
* Valid driver’s license with an acceptable driving record.
* Ability to safely operate hand and power tools.
* Strong technical aptitude with the ability to read technical drawings and follow maintenance instructions.
* Basic computer skills (Internet, Email, Word); experience with Jira and Confluence is a plus.
* Excellent soft skills, including:

+ Communication**:** Clear and professional interaction with colleagues and vendors.
+ Teamwork: Collaborative approach and respect for team dynamics.
+ Adaptability: Ability to adjust to changing priorities and work under pressure.
+ Problem-Solving: Proactive in diagnosing and resolving maintenance issues.
+ Attention to Detail: Consistently delivering high-quality, thorough work.

**Physical and Environmental Requirements**This role requires the ability to complete the following physical activities in warehouse-like environments:

* Move yourself in different positions to accomplish tasks while stooping and holding up to 25 lbs.
* Remain in a stationary position frequently, often squatting, standing or sitting for prolonged periods.
* Adjust or move objects up to 50 lbs. in all directions.
* Able to work in various positions/environments including overhead reaching, kneeling, crawling, twisting, and stretching on dusty concrete floors, rubber mats, and steel.
* Routinely required to talk and hear; use hands to touch, feel, handle, push/pull, and move objects; see details of objects that are less than a few feet away; and distinguish between colors, shades, and brightness.
* Exposure to dust, chemicals, electrical/mechanical hazards, and inclement weather.
* Work in areas with poor lighting, ventilation, noise or potentially wet.
* Requires lifting, climbing ladders, driving mobile equipment and physical activity.
* Must carry a cell phone for emergency response.
* PPE required, including but not limited to safety-toe shoes, gloves, hearing protection, and safety glasses.

The physical demands described here are representative of those that must be met by an employee to successfully perform the essential functions of this position. Reasonable accommodation may be made to enable individuals with disabilities to perform the functions.
