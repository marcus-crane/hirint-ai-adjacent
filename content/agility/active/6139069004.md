Agility’s commercially deployed humanoids operate alongside teams in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers—tackling physically demanding and repetitive tasks while enabling workers to focus on higher-value work. With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential.

### **About the Role**

We're looking for a **Senior Technical Recruiter** to help us attract and hire exceptional engineering talent across our software and hardware organizations. In this role, you'll partner closely with hiring managers and the People team to deliver a high-quality recruiting experience while managing full-cycle hiring for technical positions. This is an excellent opportunity for a recruiter who enjoys building relationships, sourcing top talent, and contributing to a fast-paced, mission-driven organization.

### **About the Work**

* Manage full-cycle recruiting for software and hardware engineering roles, from intake through offer acceptance.
* Partner with hiring managers to understand hiring needs and develop effective recruiting strategies.
* Source and engage high-quality technical talent through LinkedIn and other sourcing channels.
* Coordinate interviews and ensure a seamless candidate experience throughout the hiring process.
* Maintain accurate candidate records and recruiting activity within the applicant tracking system.
* Partner with recruiters, coordinators, and cross-functional stakeholders to drive hiring priorities.
* Contribute to recruiting process improvements, documentation, and team initiatives that improve efficiency and scalability.

### **About You**

* 5+ years of recruiting experience ideally with 2+ years of in house experience.
* Experience recruiting for technical roles, preferably within software and/or hardware engineering.
* Strong sourcing skills using LinkedIn Recruiter and other talent platforms.
* Excellent organizational skills with the ability to manage multiple searches simultaneously.
* Strong written and verbal communication skills.
* Ability to build relationships with candidates, hiring managers, and cross-functional partners.
* Experience working with applicant tracking systems and maintaining accurate recruiting data.
* BA/BS degree or equivalent professional experience.
* **Authorization to work in the USA.**

### **Bonus Points**

* Experience recruiting within robotics, AI, autonomous systems, or other deep technology industries.
* Experience supporting high-growth engineering organizations.
* Passion for delivering an exceptional candidate experience.
* Curious, proactive, and excited to continuously improve recruiting processes.
* Commitment to fostering an inclusive and equitable hiring experience.
