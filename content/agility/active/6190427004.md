Agility’s commercially deployed humanoids operate alongside teams in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers—tackling physically demanding and repetitive tasks while enabling workers to focus on higher-value work. With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential.

## **About The Role**

We are seeking a Staff Network Engineer to lead the design and evolution of our edge-to-cloud connectivity. In this role, you will help design highly available, low-latency communication systems that bridge on-premise robot deployments with our cloud-native control plane. You will serve as a technical authority for our network systems architecture, ensuring our scaling fleet is supported by robust, enterprise-grade networking foundations.

## **Key Responsibilities**

* **5G wireless technology:** Architect next-generation connectivity solutions utilizing 5G and private LTE networks, integrating containerized network functions to optimize edge performance.
* **Systems Architecture:** Design and implement scalable, multi-tenant network architectures that support global robot deployments, focusing on security, performance, and fleet-wide observability.
* **Network Performance Engineering:** Engineer solutions for high-throughput, low-latency telemetry and control traffic, utilizing kernel-level tuning and modern packet processing frameworks.
* **Hybrid Connectivity Design:** Define standards for secure, high-performance tunneling between distributed edge devices and centralized cloud infrastructure.
* **Technical Leadership:** Partner with cross-functional leadership to align network infrastructure with long-term business and product goals.

## **Required Qualifications**

* 8+ years of experience in network architecture, systems engineering, or a staff-level networking role in a complex, distributed environment.
* Expertise in Telco/5G wireless networking, including experience with containerized network functions and kernel-level performance optimization.
* Mastery of the Linux network stack and core foundational networking protocols (BGP, OSPF, TCP/IP).
* Demonstrated ability to design and implement complex SDN and virtualization solutions at scale.
* Wired & Wireless Packet Analysis: Expert in conducting deep-dive PCAP and 802.11 Wi-Fi frame analysis using Wireshark and tcpdump to resolve complex wired/wireless network failures, roaming issues, and latency bottlenecks (RCA).
* Wi-Fi Standards Knowledge: Working knowledge of IEEE 802.11 standards (802.11a/b/g/n/ac/ax/be / Wi-Fi 5/6/6E/7) and standard-specific channel/band behaviors across 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, and 6 GHz frequencies.
* Travel required 25% to 50% US (primarily) and Canada

## **Preferred Experience**

* Deep proficiency with AWS cloud networking (VPC design, Transit Gateway, Direct Connect, and IoT Core).
* Familiarity with Linux kernel eBPF/XDP for high-performance packet processing and observability.
* RF Spectrum Analysis: Familiarity with spectrum analysis tools to identify RF interference, noise floor anomalies, and channel contention.

This is a fully remote role with the option to work hybrid if a commutable distance from our Salem, OR, Pittsburgh, PA, or Fremont, CA offices.
