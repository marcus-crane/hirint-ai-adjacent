## **About the Role**

The Associate General Counsel, Corporate will play a key role in managing the company’s corporate legal matters, governance framework, and strategic initiatives. Reporting to the General Counsel, this individual will support the company’s transition to public-company standards while providing legal guidance on corporate transactions, securities compliance, and day-to-day business operations.

The ideal candidate combines strong corporate and securities law expertise with the ability to operate pragmatically in a fast-paced, high-growth environment.

## **About the Work**

### **Corporate Governance**

* Support Board of Directors and committee governance, including preparation of Board materials and resolutions
* Maintain corporate records and oversee entity management across domestic and international subsidiaries
* Assist with implementation of public company governance practices and policies
* Advise leadership on corporate governance matters and fiduciary responsibilities

### **Securities & Public Company Compliance**

* Support development of disclosure controls and procedures
* Assist with preparation and review of public company filings, including Forms 10-K, 10-Q, and 8-K
* Coordinate with external counsel on securities law compliance and regulatory matters
* Monitor developments in securities regulations and advise internal stakeholders accordingly

### **Strategic Transactions**

* Support corporate development initiatives, including acquisitions, investments, and strategic partnerships
* Assist in negotiation and documentation of transaction agreements
* Coordinate due diligence processes and post-transaction integration from a legal perspective

### **Commercial & Operational Support**

* Provide legal guidance on a range of commercial agreements, including customer, partner, and vendor contracts
* Partner with internal teams to identify and manage legal risk across the business including with respect to employment related matters.
* Develop and improve contract templates, policies, and internal processes

### **Compliance & Risk Management**

* Support development of internal compliance policies and training programs
* Partner with cross-functional teams to ensure adherence to regulatory requirements
* Assist with enterprise risk management initiatives and internal investigations when necessary

### **External Counsel Management**

* Coordinate with outside counsel on corporate, securities, and transactional matters
* Manage legal budgets and ensure efficient use of external advisors

## **About You**

* Juris Doctor (J.D.) from an accredited law school
* Licensed to practice law in at least one U.S. jurisdiction
* 8–12+ years of relevant legal experience
* Corporate and securities law experience at a top law firm strongly preferred
* In-house experience at a technology company preferred
* Experience supporting public companies or IPO readiness highly desirable
* Strong understanding of corporate governance, securities regulations, and commercial transactions

**Leadership Profile**

* Practical, business-oriented legal advisor
* Strong communicator capable of translating legal complexity into clear business guidance
* Collaborative partner across Finance, Product, and Operations teams
* Highly organized with strong judgment and attention to detail
* Able to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced growth environment
