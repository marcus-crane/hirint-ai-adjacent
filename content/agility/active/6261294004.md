Agility’s commercially deployed humanoids operate alongside teams in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers—tackling physically demanding and repetitive tasks while enabling workers to focus on higher-value work. With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential.

### **About the Role**

As the Sr. Manager, Global Supply Management (GSM), you will lead Agility Robotics' Global Supply Manager team across mechanical, electrical, electromechanical, and ecosystem device commodities, along with the Cost Engineering function. You will own the sourcing strategy, supplier award decisions, and commercial performance for these categories, ensuring cost, quality, and delivery are aligned to program and production needs.

You will partner closely with the Manager, Direct Procurement and the Manager, Supplier Industrialization Engineering to ensure sourcing strategy, procurement execution, and supplier industrialization are tightly coordinated, and with Engineering, Manufacturing, and Finance leadership to align commodity strategy with product roadmaps and cost targets.

This role requires a strategic sourcing leader who can build and scale a GSM organization, develop and coach commodity leads, and drive should-cost discipline across the portfolio.

Success in this role is measured by supplier award quality, commodity cost performance, supply continuity, and the development of a high-performing GSM team.

### About the Work

* Lead, mentor, and develop the Global Supply Manager team across mechanical, electrical, electromechanical, and ecosystem device commodities.
* Own the overall GSM sourcing strategy and ensure alignment with product roadmaps, program milestones, and cost targets.
* Review and approve supplier selection, award decisions, and major commercial negotiations led by GSMs.
* Own the Cost Engineering function, ensuring should-cost models are developed, maintained, and used to inform sourcing and negotiation decisions.
* Drive commodity strategy reviews, quarterly business reviews (QBRs), and supplier scorecards across the GSM portfolio.
* Partner with Engineering on design-for-manufacturing (DFM), approved vendor list (AVL) / supplier plan of record (SPOR) decisions, and engineering change order (ECO) impacts.
* Partner with the Manager, Direct Procurement and Manager, Supplier Industrialization Engineering to ensure sourcing, execution, and industrialization activities are coordinated end to end.
* Establish and track GSM-level KPIs including cost savings, supplier delivery performance, and supply risk.
* Serve as an escalation point for high-impact supplier and commodity issues.
* Support headcount planning, hiring, and career development across the GSM organization as it scales.
* Represent commodity strategy and supplier risk to Supply Chain and executive leadership.

### About You

* Bachelor's degree in Engineering, Supply Chain Management, or a related field; advanced degree (MBA or MS) preferred.
* 10+ years of progressive procurement or supply chain experience, including 5+ years managing or leading Global Supply Managers or commodity teams within hardware, robotics, automotive, aerospace, or advanced manufacturing environments.
* Demonstrated experience across multiple commodity types (mechanical, electrical, electromechanical, or ecosystem devices).
* Strong understanding of should-cost methodology, cost modeling, and supplier cost benchmarking.
* Proven track record building and scaling sourcing teams, including hiring, coaching, and performance management.
* Strong commercial negotiation and supplier relationship management skills.
* Experience partnering with Engineering on DFM, AVL/SPOR, and ECO processes.
* Excellent executive communication and cross-functional leadership skills.
* Ability to travel domestically and internationally as required (up to 35%).
