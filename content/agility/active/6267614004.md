Agility’s commercially deployed humanoids operate alongside teams in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers—tackling physically demanding and repetitive tasks while enabling workers to focus on higher-value work. With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential.

**About the Role**

We're looking for a versatile, detail-oriented Brand Designer to join our small, collaborative creative team. As an industry leader in robotics, our brand shows up everywhere — from trade show floors and factory environments to pitch decks, product launches, and social feeds. You'll play a key role in shaping how the world sees us.

This role is best suited for someone who thrives on variety and takes ownership of their work. Our team is lean, and that means every designer works across a wide range of projects: one week you're designing signage and wayfinding for facilities, the next you're working on a trade show booth, building assets for a product launch, or updating key pages on the website. We’re looking for an experienced individual contributor who solves complex design challenges and knows how to turn brand strategy into high-impact creative execution.

**What You'll Do**

* Design and produce brand assets across a wide range of formats: event environments and booth graphics, internal/external facilities experiences, video graphics, sales and marketing collateral, presentations, social media content, email and web assets, and more
* Partner cross-functionally with marketing, product, events, and facilities leadership to translate strategic goals into on-brand, high-quality design solutions
* Independently manage multiple complex projects simultaneously, prioritizing effectively in a fast-paced environment with shifting deadlines
* Facilitate the production and print/fabrication process for physical assets (event environments, signage, environmental graphics), managing vendor relationships and quality control
* Contribute fresh ideas to elevate the brand's visual language while staying true to established systems
* Translate complex robotics and AI concepts into clear, engaging visual stories for enterprise, investor and general audiences
* Serve as a brand guardian — adhering to quality standards, and providing design QA and direction for partner teams
* Guide motion, illustration, or photography direction as projects require
* Help build and refine brand asset libraries and design systems

**What You Bring**

* 4-6 years of professional design experience across multiple disciplines, with exposure to both digital and physical/environmental design
* A strong portfolio demonstrating range — show us collateral, social, web, and larger-format or event work if you have it
* Proficiency in Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign) and Figma
* Solid understanding of typography, layout, color, and systems-based design thinking
* Proven ability to operate within, adapt, and evolve complex brand systems and visual identities
* Experience designing for large-format print, signage, or event environments is a strong plus
* Strong project management skills with a track record of driving cross-functional initiatives independently from concept to execution
* A collaborative, low-ego attitude — this is a small team where everyone pitches in, and flexibility matters more than title
* Interest in or experience with robotics, hardware, or technical/industrial brands is a bonus

**Why This Role**

* High-impact, high-visibility work at a company shaping the future of robotics
* Broad creative ownership — you won't just execute, you’ll bring your own ideas and see projects through from concept to final delivery
* A tight-knit, collaborative team where your contributions are immediately visible
* Hands-on work across the full spectrum of brand design, from digital and social to print and environmental
