Agility’s commercially deployed humanoids operate alongside teams in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers—tackling physically demanding and repetitive tasks while enabling workers to focus on higher-value work. With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential.

### **About the Role**

As the **Manager, Supplier Industrialization**, you will lead Agility Robotics' Supplier Industrialization Engineering (SIE) function, responsible for developing supplier manufacturing capability, process readiness, and production throughput to support scalable, reliable production. Based in the USA and managing global teams, you will also act as an individual contributor (IC) for suppliers in the NA region, while serving as the technical leader bridging Engineering, GSMs, and suppliers on manufacturing scalability.

You will partner closely with Global Supply Managers (GSMs), Buyers, Engineering, Manufacturing, and Supplier Quality Engineering (SQE) to prioritize industrialization work, align on shared checkpoints like PPAP readiness and process-change validation, and ensure suppliers are prepared to scale alongside production needs.

Success in this role is measured by supplier process readiness, capacity ramp performance, and the effectiveness of the regional SIE team.

### **About the Work**

* Lead, manage, and prioritize the work of the regional Supplier Industrialization Engineer (SIE) team across Asia, EU, and NA, while directly acting as an individual contributor for suppliers in the NA region.
* Own the supplier industrialization roadmap — process readiness, capacity ramp, and tooling/automation approval — aligned with GSM commodity strategy and program milestones.
* Provide technical capability input to GSMs during supplier evaluation and selection.
* Lead process readiness and run-at-rate assessments, line balancing, and capacity/throughput validation ahead of production ramp.
* Drive supplier development initiatives focused on process capability, yield, cycle time, and manufacturing efficiency (OEE).
* Collaborate with Engineering on design-for-manufacturing (DFM) and design-for-assembly (DFA) reviews.
* Support suppliers through NPI phases (prototype, EVT, DVT), partnering with Sourcing/Procurement on program milestones.
* Partner with Supplier Quality Engineering (SQE) on shared checkpoints such as PPAP readiness and process-change notifications.
* Establish supplier throughput and readiness metrics (e.g., OEE, capacity ramp) to track industrialization progress.
* Manage contract SIE resources, including staffing, onboarding, and performance oversight across regions.
* Serve as an escalation point for supplier process and capacity risk.

### **About You**

* Bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering, Industrial Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related technical field.
* 8+ years of experience in supplier development, industrialization, or manufacturing engineering within a hardware environment, including 2+ years managing or leading a team.
* Strong understanding of manufacturing processes across machining, sheet metal, injection molding, die casting, electronics manufacturing, or electromechanical assembly.
* Six Sigma/Lean experience (DMAIC, Kaizen, VSM) and comfort working with process capability data (Cpk/Ppk, OEE).
* Experience managing distributed or contract technical resources across multiple regions.
* Familiarity with ERP/PLM/MES systems.
* Strong leadership, communication, negotiation, and cross-functional influence skills — particularly the ability to drive change at supplier sites without direct authority.
* Ability to travel domestically and internationally as required (up to 40%).
