Agility’s commercially deployed humanoids operate alongside teams in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers—tackling physically demanding and repetitive tasks while enabling workers to focus on higher-value work. With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential.

## **Overview**

Agility Robotics is defining the future of humanoid robotics by designing and building intelligent, commercially deployable systems that integrate advanced AI, robotics, and real-world automation at global scale. As the company accelerates commercialization and category leadership, we are seeking a highly strategic and execution-oriented **Senior Director, Business Development** to identify, develop, and advance high-impact opportunities that accelerate commercial growth and expand Agility’s market presence.

Reporting directly to the President and partnering closely with Commercial, Product, Operations, and senior leadership, this role will be responsible for building strategic relationships and developing new business opportunities that strengthen Agility’s position in the humanoid robotics market.

This is a highly visible role for a commercial leader who can move fluidly between strategy and execution—identifying opportunities, developing relationships with senior external stakeholders, structuring partnerships, and turning emerging opportunities into scalable business outcomes.

The ideal candidate combines strong commercial judgment with strategic rigor, technical curiosity, and the ability to navigate complex, long-term business development cycles within emerging technology markets.

# **The Mission**

Build and advance the partnerships, customer opportunities, and commercial pathways that establish Agility Robotics as the global leader in humanoid robotics.

This leader will identify and develop opportunities that directly support:

* Commercial growth and customer adoption
* Strategic partnerships
* New markets and vertical expansion
* Ecosystem development
* Long-term customer and enterprise value

# **Core Responsibilities**

## **Business Development & Commercial Growth**

* Develop and execute business development strategies that accelerate commercial adoption of Agility’s humanoid robotics platform.
* Identify, develop, and advance high-value business opportunities across priority customers, industries, and markets.
* Build relationships with senior executives and decision-makers across strategic customers, partners, and ecosystem participants.
* Develop opportunities from initial market identification and relationship development through commercial structuring and execution.
* Partner with Commercial leadership to build and maintain a strong pipeline of strategic opportunities aligned with company growth priorities.
* Develop business cases, commercial models, and strategic recommendations for new opportunities.
* Serve as a senior external representative of Agility with customers, partners, and industry stakeholders.

## **Strategic Partnerships & Ecosystem Development**

* Identify and develop strategic partnerships that accelerate customer adoption, expand market access, and strengthen Agility’s commercial position.
* Build relationships across technology, industrial, systems integration, and customer ecosystems that can enable broader deployment of humanoid robotics.
* Develop partnership strategies and frameworks that create meaningful value for Agility and its partners.
* Lead the evaluation, structuring, negotiation, and development of strategic commercial partnerships.
* Partner cross-functionally to ensure strategic partnerships have clear business objectives, ownership, execution plans, and success metrics.
* Build and maintain a strong external network across robotics, AI, automation, logistics, manufacturing, and adjacent industries.

## **Market & Vertical Expansion**

* Identify and develop new commercial pathways across priority industries, customer segments, and geographies.
* Partner with Product, Commercial, and Operations leadership to evaluate and prioritize high-value deployment opportunities for humanoid robotics.
* Develop go-to-market approaches for emerging verticals and use cases based on customer needs, market readiness, and Agility’s product roadmap.
* Support business development across priority verticals including logistics, manufacturing, automotive, retail, defense, and other emerging applications.
* Identify new channels, ecosystem relationships, and partnership models that can accelerate Agility’s ability to reach and serve customers at scale.
* Develop market and customer insights that inform commercial priorities and future product opportunities.

## **Strategic Opportunity Development**

* Lead complex, cross-functional business development initiatives from initial concept through execution.
* Develop compelling value propositions and business cases for strategic customers and partners.
* Structure commercial frameworks that balance near-term customer adoption with Agility’s long-term platform and business objectives.
* Partner closely with Product and Engineering to ensure customer and partner opportunities align with product capabilities and roadmap priorities.
* Partner with Finance and Legal on commercial structures, negotiations, economics, and agreements.
* Provide leadership with clear recommendations on significant commercial and partnership opportunities.

## **Leadership & Organizational Impact**

* Collaborate closely with the Chief Business Officer and senior leadership to identify and prioritize the company’s highest-impact business development opportunities.
* Establish scalable business development processes, frameworks, and operating rhythms as Agility’s commercial organization grows.
* Create visibility into the business development pipeline, strategic partnerships, key opportunities, risks, and next steps.
* Foster strong cross-functional collaboration across Commercial, Product, Engineering, Operations, Finance, Legal, and Marketing.
* Bring analytical rigor, commercial creativity, and executional discipline to complex business development opportunities.
* Mentor and develop business development talent as the function scales.

# **Qualifications**

* 12–15+ years of experience in business development, strategic partnerships, commercial strategy, enterprise sales, or related leadership roles within high-growth technology or industrial sectors.
* Demonstrated success developing and closing complex strategic partnerships and commercial opportunities with large enterprise customers.
* Experience building new markets, customer segments, partnerships, or commercial channels from an early stage.
* Strong commercial and financial acumen with experience developing business cases, partnership structures, and complex commercial agreements.
* Proven ability to build trusted relationships with senior executives and external partners.
* Demonstrated success leading complex, cross-functional initiatives with significant executive visibility.
* Strong negotiation, communication, and relationship-building skills.
* Ability to translate technical capabilities into compelling commercial value propositions for customers and partners.
* Ability to operate effectively in fast-moving, ambiguous environments with evolving priorities.
* Experience in robotics, AI, automation, advanced manufacturing, logistics, mobility, or deep technology sectors strongly preferred.
* MBA, engineering, or similarly rigorous business or technical background preferred.
