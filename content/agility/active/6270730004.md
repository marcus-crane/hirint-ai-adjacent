Agility’s commercially deployed humanoids operate alongside teams in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers—tackling physically demanding and repetitive tasks while enabling workers to focus on higher-value work. With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential.

**About the Team:**

Our mechanical engineering team is agile and has grown rapidly, and we are looking for a talented engineering leader to join us remotely in the San Francisco Bay Area, or on-site at our Salem facility. We are responsible for delivering advanced and reliable designs that meet a complex set of requirements to our customers, including software developers, applications engineers, manufacturing engineers and ultimately to fleet deployment managers. We work as a group to develop new technologies, engineer new products, and refine/maintain existing designs. The team is composed of engineers from many backgrounds and levels of experience. 

**About The Role:**

We are currently designing the next generation of Digit for commercial scale. Highly reliable and robust designs are critical to successful deployment of our robots in large quantities. Digit works in demanding environments, performing highly repetitive and heavily loaded tasks. The Senior Mechanical Engineer will work closely with other mechanical engineers and cross functional teams to develop the mechanical structure of the robot.  This role will own multiple mechanical and electro-mechanical parts, subassemblies  and will be responsible for the design, prototyping, root cause analysis and DFx for those parts.

**About the work:**

* Own the 3D and 2D design of mechanical parts, electromechanical components and subassemblies, including prototyping of parts and subsystems.
* Collaborate with multidisciplinary teams (electrical, industrial design, software, test, and manufacturing) to define requirements and develop design concepts.
* Drive cross functional solutions to highly complex design challenges
* Perform engineering analyses to validate design decisions, including supporting structural and thermal analyses.
* Create and maintain high quality design documentation.
* Lead cross-functional design reviews.
* Support root cause investigations and implement improvements to enhance Digit robustness
* Own delivery of significant portions of the robot and robot peripherals.
* Track progress and document design work using Jira and Confluence.
* Drive the development of specifications, requirements, test methods and protocols through formal verification to ensure functionality, safety and efficacy of design.
* Mentor and coach more junior engineers.
* Travel requirement (25% travel to Salem)

**About you:**

* 4-6 years of mechanical design experience
* B.S. or M.S. in Mechanical Engineering (or equivalent, minimum requirement)
* Demonstrated experience in mechanical design, electronics packaging,sensors
* Highly driven self starter that shows ownership and accountability in their work.
* 5+ years of 3D CAD experience.
* 5+ years of mechanical design experience.
* B.S. or M.S. in Mechanical Engineering (or equivalent, minimum requirement)
* Proven experience designing and building mechanisms and assemblies, ideally with a background in robotics
* Demonstrated experience in mechanical analysis including modeling tolerance stacks, static and dynamic analysis, FEA and thermal analysis
* Ability to be on-site at Agility’s design facility as needed (3 days a week in the Bay Area and as needed to support design activities, reviews, prototype testing, etc.)

**Preferred Skills and Experience:**

* Robotics experience design experience
* Proven experience designing and building mechanisms and assemblies, preferably in robotics
* Advanced degree in engineering or science or robotics
* Jira/Confluence experience
* PLM and PDM experience
* Creo experience
