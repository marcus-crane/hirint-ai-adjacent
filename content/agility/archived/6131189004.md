Agility’s commercially deployed humanoids operate alongside teams in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers—tackling physically demanding and repetitive tasks while enabling workers to focus on higher-value work. With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential.

### **About the Role**

We are seeking an experienced **Senior Manufacturing Engineer, Electrical** to lead sustaining and continuous improvement initiatives in our robotics manufacturing facility. In this role, you will collaborate cross-functionally to optimize production processes, troubleshoot assembly and design issues on electro-mechanical systems and PCBAs, update electrical designs and implement the engineering changes to drive continuous improvement.

### **About the Work**

* Act as the primary Subject Matter Expert (SME) within the Manufacturing Engineering Sustaining team for all electrical components within the Robot, including high-density PCBAs, power distribution units, cable assemblies/harnesses, sensors, and battery systems.
* Diagnose and resolve (debug) manufacturing, field, and design issues with focus on electronic assemblies and components, supporting root cause analysis (RCA) and lead corrective/preventive actions (CAPA) for nonconformances.
* Collaborate with System and Test Engineering teams to understand, utilize and improve automated scripts and diagnostic tools (Python, C/C++) for flashing firmware, commissioning and testing electronic assemblies.
* Execute electrical design updates using Altium to improve manufacturability, resolve production and design issues, working cross-functionally with design, test, quality, and supply chain teams.
* Perform SME-level technical review on all electrical design changes affecting the production robot, evaluating for DFM/DFA/DFT, technical issues, and standard consistency prior to release.
* Drive the validation and implementation of electrical Engineering Change Orders (ECRs & ECOs) by creating test protocols, performing the tests, reporting out the results, and implementing the validated change on the production line.
* Author and maintain up-to-date technical documentation, including specifications, drawings, BOMs, work instructions, process flow diagrams, PFMEAs, control plans, and tool/fixture/equipment specifications to support production and service operations.
* Disposition of nonconforming material delivered by supplier or produced by production.
* Collaborate with suppliers and contract manufacturers to validate design changes, material substitutions, and implement supplier testing.
* Analyze production data to identify bottlenecks, drive improvements in capacity, quality, cost, and safety, and implement lean manufacturing solutions.
* Provide technical leadership and mentorship to junior engineers and technicians, fostering a culture of continuous improvement and knowledge sharing.

**About You**

* Bachelor’s degree in Electrical Engineering, Manufacturing Engineering or a closely related technical field.
* 6+ years of relevant experience in a commercial, product-driven manufacturing environment supporting a production line (robotics, automotive, aerospace, medical, or similar preferred).
* Hands-on experience with electro-mechanical systems, mechanisms, actuators, PCBAs, and sensors.
* Design experience with Altium with proficiency reading, interpreting and updating electrical schematics, PCB layouts, and cabling diagrams.
* Engineering Change Order (ECO) experience within Product Lifecycle Management (PLM) system, Windchill experience a plus.
* Proficiency in Python and Linux, and/or C languages is a plus.
* Experience with ERP and MES software is a plus.
* Experience developing and implementing manufacturing equipment in production environments (DQ/IQ/OQ/PQ) a plus.
* Proven ability to mentor and lead engineers and technicians effectively.
* Excellent communication and interpersonal skills for cross-functional collaboration.
* Ability to drive initiatives independently and within teams.
