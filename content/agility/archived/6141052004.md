Agility’s commercially deployed humanoids operate alongside teams in warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and distribution centers—tackling physically demanding and repetitive tasks while enabling workers to focus on higher-value work. With industry-leading safety standards and years of proven deployment data, we're pioneering a new era of automation that enhances human potential.

**About the Role**

The Recruiting Coordinator (RC) works closely with a team of recruiters in a fast-paced Robotics start-up. The RC is an integral partner in managing recruiting activities and building candidate relationships, often providing candidates with their first impression of Agility Robotics. You will be an organizational guru, work effectively with internal and external clients and candidates, possess great communication skills, have the ability to shift gears at a moment’s notice, and enjoy the challenges of helping to build a great organization of talented individuals. This is an amazing opportunity for someone looking to broaden their experience and work in a cutting-edge robotics company.  This is a **6-month contract** with the potential for extension.

**About the Work**

* Provide an exceptional candidate experience
* Scheduling interviews and phone screens working directly with candidates and hiring managers, in some cases arranging travel for visiting candidates
* Coordinating the pre- and post-interview debrief meetings and providing debrief materials
* Identifying opportunities for improving the candidate experience and scheduling efficiency
* Updating candidate records in Greenhouse (our ATS)
* Assisting in the coordination of other recruiting activities as needed

**About You**

* 2+ years of recruiting coordinator or equivalent experience at a tech company
* Experience with Greenhouse applicant tracking system
* Self-starter with an insatiable curiosity
* Superb customer focus, attention to detail, and organization skills
* Strong oral and written communication skills
* Ability to work in a fast-paced, quickly changing environment
* Ability to prioritize and manage multiple priorities simultaneously
* Ability to schedule candidate interview travel
* Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, agile environment
* The calm in a storm with a great sense of humor
