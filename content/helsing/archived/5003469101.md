## **Who we are**

Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. 

As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. 

We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications.

## **The role**

As Facility Coordinator, you will keep Helsing's German offices running. This is a hands-on role: you will carry out repairs, assemble furniture, set up rooms and fix the small problems that would otherwise sit in a queue for weeks waiting on an external tradesperson. Alongside the practical work, you will coordinate contractors, manage facilities budgets and work with landlords' building managers on larger items such as kitchen installations and fit-outs. You will support office moves, new office openings and projects such as signage standardisation, working closely with the People, Workplace, IT and Health & Safety teams. The role is based in Munich, with regular travel to Berlin and our other German sites.

## **The day-to-day**

* Carry out general repairs, maintenance and handyman work across our German offices, resolving everyday issues in days rather than the weeks it takes to source external trades
* Deliver office setups and moves end to end, from furniture assembly and room configuration to the opening of new sites, keeping disruption to teams minimal
* Coordinate external contractors and landlords' building managers on larger works such as kitchen installations and fit-outs, assessing the quality of completed work before sign-off
* Own the facilities budget for the German sites, tracking project costs, reviewing quotes and contracts, and finding savings by bringing suitable work in-house
* Partner with the People, Workplace, IT and Health & Safety teams on compliance, site standards and projects such as signage standardisation across offices

## **You should apply if you**

* Have maintained offices or similar sites hands-on, covering repairs, furniture assembly, room setups and office moves
* Bring practical skill in at least one trade, such as electrical, plumbing or carpentry, and can judge the quality of work delivered by outside tradespeople
* Have coordinated external contractors and suppliers, and managed quotes, contracts and budgets
* Organise competing requests across several sites and keep colleagues informed on what will be done and when
* Communicate comfortably in German and English
