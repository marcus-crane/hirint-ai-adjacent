## **Who we are**

Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. 

As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. 

We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications. 

## **The role**

Payroll at Helsing is not a back-office function. It is a foundation of trust.

AsPayroll Lead, you will own Helsing's payroll function across Germany and our growing international footprint. This is a hands-on leadership role: you will remain close to the monthly payroll cycles while designing the operating model, controls and team structure that allow payroll to scale with Helsing.

You will take end-to-end responsibility for how payroll operates across our entities and regions, with particular depth in Germany and the wider DACH region. You will set service standards, controls and escalation paths, and hold our external providers accountable for delivering against them.

You will guide our payroll specialists, sharing work fairly across cycles and building the knowledge and cross-cover that ensures no entity depends on one person.

As we add entities and enter new countries, you will decide how payroll should operate there — from provider and system choices through to controls and the first live payroll. Every colleague at Helsing depends on this being right, on time, every cycle.

## **The day-to-day**

* Own payroll delivery across multiple entities, entities and pay frequencies, remaining hands-on in monthly payroll where needed and ensuring each cycle is delivered accurately and on time
* Design and continuously improve a scalable payroll operating model for Germany and our international entities, covering workflows, intake channels, escalation paths, service levels, approvals, reconciliations and audit-ready evidence
* Guide and develop our payroll specialists, setting clear standards, sharing workload fairly and building cross-cover
* Partner closely with Finance on payroll accounting, reconciliations and month-end, quarter-end and year-end close
* Own the service and commercial relationship with external payroll providers, setting expectations, running performance reviews and selecting new partners as we enter additional countries
* Safeguard statutory compliance across jurisdictions, with particular expertise in German wage tax, social security and relevant labour law, and lead payroll accounting and social security audits
* Lead payroll setup when Helsing enters new countries or establishes new entities, from defining the operating approach and selecting providers through testing and the first successful live cycle
* Reduce manual work and operational risk through better processes, HRIS/payroll integrations and automation

## **You should apply if you**

* Have owned or played a leading role in multi-entity, multi-country payroll in a growing international company, including hands-on responsibility for payroll cycles
* Have selected, managed and challenged external payroll providers and know how to establish clear accountability between internal teams and outsourced partners
* Have guided payroll specialists or a payroll team, balancing workload, quality and cross-cover through periods of growth and change; formal people management experience is valuable but not essential
* Have built or significantly reshaped payroll processes, controls, systems or operating models and can demonstrate what improved as a result
* Have deep knowledge of German payroll compliance and keep that knowledge current, including wage tax, social security and relevant labour law
* Can operate at both strategic and operational levels — comfortable designing how payroll should work while also getting into the detail of a monthly payroll cycle when required
* Communicate fluently in German and English, in writing and in person, with colleagues, providers and local authorities

*Note: We operate in an industry where women, as well as other minority groups, are systematically under-represented. We encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet all the listed qualifications; ability and impact cannot be summarised in a few bullet points.*
