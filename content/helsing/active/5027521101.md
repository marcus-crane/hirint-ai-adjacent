## **The role**

As a Systems Engineer, C2 & Fire Control, you will take Helsing’s command and control (C2) node for counter-unmanned aircraft systems (C-UAS) from working prototype to a qualified system in the field. The node fuses sensor tracks, assesses threats, assigns weapons (TEWA) and controls effectors. You will deliver its functional safety case and qualification path, planning approval work alongside development so the system can be fielded on schedule. Within a software and autonomy team, you will translate defence standards for weapon release and fire control into architecture, including how intercept commands are issued, protected against failure and aborted. You will run system-level tests and support field handover, helping ensure the C2 node can be qualified, fielded and trusted.

## The day-to-day

* Develop the C2 node’s functional safety case, run hazard analyses and turn findings into safety requirements, mechanisms and verification evidence
* Maintain the approvals map, covering applicable norms, required evidence, accountable owners, status and lead times, and plan qualification of hardware and software with test centres and authorities
* Translate weapon-release and fire-control standards into architecture decisions for command integrity, redundancy, abort and safe-state paths, including the safety case for autonomy at each kill-chain step and human approval of engagements
* Define and execute system-level verification campaigns in test beds, at test ranges and during field trials, using root-cause analysis to close findings
* Set safety and interface evidence requirements for sensor and effector partners, review their qualification data, and prepare acceptance tests, operational documentation, training input and user handover
