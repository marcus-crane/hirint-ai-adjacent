## **The role**

Helsing operates on-premises high-performance computing (HPC) infrastructure that supports electromagnetics, computational fluid dynamics, and multi-physics simulation. As an HPC Systems Administrator based in Munich, you will take ownership of this critical environment, ensuring that our team of simulation engineers remains unblocked, productive, and equipped to solve complex problems. You will play a vital role in maintaining rigorous technical standards, optimising compute resources, and scaling our infrastructure to support continuous, large-scale modelling. The role is based on-site in Munich with regular travel to our Tussenhausen site.

## **The day-to-day**

* Own day-to-day administration of our RHEL 9 cluster: compute nodes, NFS storage, InfiniBand fabric, the Altair service VM, and licence servers
* Keep the environment highly available through proactive monitoring, patching, kernel and firmware lifecycle management, and incident response
* Administer Altair PBS Professional, including queues, hooks, and resource definitions
* Publish and maintain simulation applications in Altair Access, including app definitions, remote sessions, and admin roles
* Build and maintain stateless compute nodes with Warewulf 4 (node images, overlays, and profiles)
* Manage identity and access through Red Hat IdM / FreeIPA, and onboard users with clear documentation so engineers can self-serve
* Maintain the security posture required for cleared work: OpenSCAP scanning and fixing findings, audit logging, privileged access control, and producing evidence for compliance reviews
* Automate operational toil with Bash, Python, and Ansible, and hold vendors to account on support cases and RMAs

## **You should apply if you**

* Several years of production RHEL administration (ideally RHEL 9) in an HPC or large shared compute environment, including SELinux, systemd, NFS server tuning, and XFS
* Hands-on Altair PBS Professional administration (queues, hooks, resource definitions); deep experience with a comparable scheduler will also be considered
* Red Hat IdM / FreeIPA administration, including central sudo rules, password policies, Kerberos, SSSD troubleshooting, and LDAP integration with other services
* InfiniBand operations, including subnet manager administration and basic fabric troubleshooting
* Licence server management for Altair licensing and FlexNet/FlexLM
* Scripting and automation with Bash, Python, and Ansible
* Eligibility for SÜ2 clearance and a valid German work permit
* Professional proficiency in English; German is an advantage
