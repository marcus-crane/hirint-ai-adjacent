As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously.

## **The role**

We are looking for an experienced and passionate firmware engineer with a hands-on and problem-solving mindset to join us at our office in Barcelona. You will work on solving real challenges in a fast-paced environment, working closely with a multidisciplinary team of electrical, controls, and higher-level software engineers to build reliable, observable, and debuggable robotics systems.  
  
You will operate at the boundary between hardware and software, with a strong electrical engineering mindset, helping to architect the future of robotics through best-in-class, modern software engineering practices.  
  
This is an individual contributor role. You will be the main owner of the firmware across our robotics platforms — from concept through implementation, validation, and long-term maintenance. It is not a management position, and we're looking for someone who thrives working autonomously and taking end-to-end technical ownership, while collaborating closely with the wider hardware and software teams.

## **Day-to-day**

* Design, implement, and maintain embedded firmware for real-time systems: control, sensing, and system monitoring
* Own the full firmware lifecycle: architecture, implementation, testing, deployment, and maintenance
* Debug complex system-level issues where the root cause may span software, firmware, hardware, or component behavior
* Use hardware debugging tools such as oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, and DMMs to diagnose timing, signaling, and electrical issues
* Act as the technical bridge between hardware and software, collaborating closely with EEs, controls, motion planning, and systems engineers
* Advise leadership on firmware-related technical decisions and strategy
* Drive technical decision making and implementation through hands-on and technically deep work

**Ownership Areas**  
Control systems, including power Stages and Motor control, embedded communications, system health monitoring/logging, and hardware bring-up.  
  
**Tech Stack**

* MCUs: TI c28x MCUs
* Languages: C / C++
* Supporting Systems: Linux host OS, NodeJS / TypeScript, Docker
* Tooling: GitHub Actions, CI/CD
* Protocols:  CANOpenFD, BISSC, SSI, UART, I2C.

## **You should apply if you**

* Have ≥ 5 years of experience developing embedded firmware for real-time systems
* Have a BSc in Electronics, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or equivalent (MSc preferred)
* Are proficient in C/C++ for embedded targets, with a strong grasp of MCU internals, peripherals, and real-time constraints
* Are comfortable using hardware debugging tools (oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, DMMs) and reading schematics
* Are able to work fully independently, owning firmware from concept to validation
* Are proficient in both English & Spanish
