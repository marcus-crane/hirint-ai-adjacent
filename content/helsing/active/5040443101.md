## **Who we are**

Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. 

As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. 

We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications.

## **The role**

As Senior Systems Engineer for Warhead Simulation and Analysis, you will help establish Helsing’s Lethality Centre of Excellence. You will develop the simulation and analysis methods used to assess warhead systems for drones, missiles and other platforms, supporting work from early concept through qualification and production. You will connect modelling and test evidence to system-level requirements, working with engineering teams, test partners and suppliers. The role is based in Munich, with a hybrid working model. On-site work is required for classified tasks.

## The day-to-day

* Develop and assess warhead system concepts for drone, missile and other platform applications, translating system requirements into analysis plans and technical evidence
* Run and validate hydrocode simulations using tools such as Ansys Autodyn, LS-DYNA or IMPETUS, and develop lethality and vulnerability assessment models
* Plan and evaluate warhead test programmes with test ranges and partners, including arena, penetration, static detonation and flash X-ray testing
* Support qualification and production by assessing test and simulation results, working with suppliers on manufacturing constraints and identifying process improvements
* Contribute to safety, insensitive munitions and security requirements, including the secure simulation environment and relevant standards such as STANAG 4439 and STANAG 4170

## **You should apply if you**

* Apply systems engineering methods to complex defence systems, connecting requirements, analysis and qualification evidence
