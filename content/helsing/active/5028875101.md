**Who we are**

Helsing develops artificial intelligence enabled capabilities to protect and defend democracies. We are growing our US operations, cultivating an ambitious and committed team of mission driven professionals to apply their skills to solve challenging problems.

**The role**

As a Systems Engineer, you will contribute to the development of a next generation loitering munition system for the U.S. Army. Working as part of a multidisciplinary engineering team, you will support the development, integration and verification of complex defense systems, helping translate customer requirements into practical engineering solutions.

You will work alongside Senior and Lead Systems Engineers, supporting activities across the systems engineering lifecycle, including requirements development, system documentation, interface management, integration and test.

Your work will span the All Up Round (AUR), Fire Control System (FCS), and their integration into the Infantry Brigade Combat Team (IBCT) force structure. You will collaborate with hardware, software, mechanical, electrical and safety engineering teams to ensure individual components and subsystems work together as part of a fully integrated system.

This is an opportunity for an engineer with a strong technical foundation and an interest in complex defense systems to take ownership of meaningful engineering deliverables, develop their systems engineering expertise and contribute directly to the delivery of operational military capability.

**The day-to-day**

* Support the development, documentation and management of system and subsystem requirements.
* Assist with translating customer and stakeholder needs into clear, measurable and verifiable engineering requirements.
* Maintain requirements traceability, ensuring requirements are appropriately allocated to subsystems and linked to verification activities.
* Develop and maintain systems engineering documentation, including specifications, interface documentation, engineering reports and verification records.
* Support system architecture development and interface definition across hardware, software and mechanical subsystems.
* Work with multidisciplinary engineering teams to support system integration and resolve technical issues.
* Assist with integration, verification and validation activities to demonstrate system compliance against defined requirements.
* Support the preparation and execution of system level testing, including laboratory integration and hardware-in-the-loop activities.
* Analyse test results, document findings and support the investigation and resolution of engineering issues.
* Participate in technical design reviews, including System Requirements Review (SRR), Preliminary Design Review (PDR) and Critical Design Review (CDR).
* Support configuration management, technical baseline documentation and engineering change processes.
* Work with Senior and Lead Systems Engineers to identify technical risks, dependencies and potential design improvements.
* Support the development of engineering solutions that meet system safety, reliability, maintainability and performance requirements.

**You should apply if you**

* Hold a university degree in systems engineering, aerospace engineering, mechanical engineering, electrical engineering, computer science or a related technical discipline.
* Have approximately 3–5 years of engineering experience, ideally within systems engineering, defense, aerospace, advanced manufacturing or complex hardware development.
* Have a foundational understanding of systems engineering principles and the systems development lifecycle.
* Have experience or exposure to requirements development, technical documentation, system specifications or engineering change management.
* Understand the importance of requirements traceability, interface management and configuration control.
* Have an interest in system integration, verification and validation, and the testing of complex engineering systems.
* Are comfortable working across multidisciplinary engineering teams and communicating technical information clearly.
* Demonstrate strong analytical and problem solving skills, with the ability to investigate technical issues and work towards practical solutions.
* Are organised, detail oriented and able to manage multiple engineering tasks.
* Are motivated to take ownership of engineering deliverables and develop your technical capabilities within a growing engineering organisation.
* Are a U.S. citizen and able to obtain and maintain a Secret security clearance.
* Are willing to travel to government test facilities and vendor or partner sites as required.

**Nice to have**

* Previous experience supporting defense, aerospace, military platform, munition or unmanned aircraft development programmes.
* Familiarity with requirements management and systems engineering tools.
* Exposure to Model Based Systems Engineering (MBSE), SysML or Cameo MagicDraw.
* Familiarity with MIL-HDBK-516C, MIL-STD-882E, SAE ARP-4754A, ARP-4761, DO-178C or DO-254.
* Exposure to system integration, hardware in the loop testing or verification and validation campaigns.
* Understanding of system safety, reliability and airworthiness principles.
* Familiarity with Army tactical networks, ATAK, UVC standards or Common Control concepts for uncrewed systems.
* Exposure to configuration management and technical baseline control.
* Able to obtain and hold a U.S. Top Secret security clearance.
