**Mechanical CAD Designer**

**Full time | Washington, DC**

**Who we are**

Helsing develops artificial intelligence enabled capabilities to protect and defend democracies. We are growing our US operations, cultivating an ambitious and committed team of mission driven professionals to apply their skills to solve challenging problems.

**The role**

As a Mechanical CAD Designer, you will support the multi disciplinary design, development & production of a next generation loitering munition system being developed for the US Army, producing and maintaining the CAD models, assemblies and technical drawings that support the development and manufacture of our products.

Working closely with mechanical, electrical and systems engineers, you will translate engineering concepts, sketches, specifications and design inputs into accurate, detailed CAD models and drawings. You will help maintain the vehicle's master CAD assembly, update components as designs evolve, and ensure technical documentation is accurate, consistent and ready for engineering review and manufacturing.

You will play an important role in supporting the wider engineering team, helping ensure parts, assemblies and mounting solutions are properly modelled, documented and incorporated into the overall vehicle design. From updating existing drawings to producing new component models, you will help turn engineering designs into clearly defined documentation that supports the development, build and assembly of our products.

This is a hybrid role and can be based from either of our locations - Washington, DC or Martinsburg WV.

**The day to day**

* Create, update and maintain 3D CAD models, component designs, assemblies and technical drawings based on engineering inputs, sketches, specifications and existing designs.
* Support the maintenance of the vehicle's master CAD assembly, incorporating design updates, component changes and new parts as the product evolves.
* Produce detailed 2D engineering drawings and 3D models for components, assemblies, mounting brackets, fixtures and other mechanical parts.
* Prepare technical drawings with dimensions, tolerances, material specifications, annotations and manufacturing notes to support fabrication, assembly and inspection.
* Apply geometric dimensioning and tolerancing (GD&T) in accordance with ASME Y14.5 where required.
* Support the creation and maintenance of model based definition (MBD) models, incorporating dimensions, tolerances and relevant technical information into CAD models.
* Update and maintain engineering documentation, drawing packages, part information and engineering bills of materials (BOMs), ensuring accuracy and consistency across revisions.
* Incorporate feedback and design changes from engineers, ensuring CAD models and drawings accurately reflect approved engineering requirements.
* Assist with checking assemblies for fit, alignment, clearances and potential interference between components.
* Prepare CAD models and drawings for manufacturing, working with engineers to ensure documentation is clear, complete and suitable for production.
* Support the development of components across a range of manufacturing methods, including sheet metal, machining, additive manufacturing and composite fabrication.
* Work within established CAD standards, templates, naming conventions, revision controls and engineering release processes.
* Collaborate with mechanical, electrical, systems and manufacturing engineers to support design development, documentation and build activities.

**You should apply if you**

* Have approximately 5 years or more of experience in mechanical drafting, CAD design or mechanical engineering design support.
* Have experience producing detailed mechanical CAD models, assemblies and technical drawings.
* Are comfortable working from engineering sketches, specifications, mark ups, existing models and design instructions to produce accurate CAD documentation.
* Have experience working with large assemblies and updating components, subassemblies and drawings as designs evolve.
* Have experience using CATIA, ideally CATIA V5, 3DEXPERIENCE or SolidWorks.
* Have a working knowledge of GD&T and engineering drawing standards, ideally ASME Y14.5.
* Understand mechanical design principles, manufacturing processes, materials, dimensions, tolerances and fit.
* Have experience preparing drawings and CAD documentation for manufacturing, fabrication, machining or assembly.
* Are organized and detail oriented, with the ability to manage drawing updates, revisions and documentation accurately.
* Communicate effectively and work collaboratively with engineers and other technical stakeholders.
* Have an associate's degree, technical diploma, CAD/drafting certification or equivalent practical experience.
* Are a U.S. citizen and able to obtain and maintain a Secret security clearance.

Note: We encourage you to apply even if you don't meet all the listed qualifications; ability and impact cannot be summarized in a few bullet points.
