## **Who we are**

Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. 

As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. 

We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications.

## **The role**

As our Integrated Product Support (IPS) Manager, you will coordinate support planning and technical documentation across the product lifecycle, drawing on your background as a technical documentation specialist. You will oversee the development and delivery of accurate, usable documentation, with a particular focus on S1000D and relevant military standards. Working with engineering, development and customer teams, you will turn technical information into support products that meet operational needs and client requirements. You will also coordinate documentation projects and external providers, helping ensure that IPS deliverables are consistent, compliant and ready for use.

## **The day-to-day**

* Plan and coordinate IPS deliverables across the product lifecycle, aligning documentation with product, operational and client needs

* Oversee the creation, review and maintenance of S1000D technical publications, including operating manuals, maintenance guides and parts catalogues

* Guide the collection and interpretation of information from drawings, specifications, schematics and engineering datasets to maintain accurate documentation

* Coordinate with engineering, development teams and external providers to meet military standards, client requirements and agreed quality criteria

* Manage digital preparation and delivery of documentation, using tools such as Adobe FrameMaker and Microsoft Office to support consistent publications and data transfer

## **You should apply if you**

* Have coordinated technical documentation or IPS deliverables, from planning and review through to delivery
* Apply S1000D and understand related ASD specifications such as S2000M or S3000L
* Interpret complex technical drawings, schematics, specifications and datasets, and translate them into clear documentation
* Use Microsoft Office and technical publishing tools such as Adobe FrameMaker, Photoshop or Illustrator
* Communicate clearly in both German and English with technical teams, clients and service providers
* Experience in the defence industry is a plus
