## **The role**

You will sit at the centre of the Optics Cluster in our Test Department, with a scope that crosses cluster boundaries. A sensing subsystem is not only an optics problem. It sits where optics, electronics, embedded software, and mechanical integration meet. 

Integration decides whether sensor data can be trusted. An EO camera that is perfectly focused but misaligned with the LiDAR produces unsafe data. A LWIR sensor that passes standalone characterisation can still fail beside a hot avionics bay. The airframe tells a different story from the bench. 

You will therefore do more than execute tests. You will act as the sensing system architect for test, and you will: 

* Turn sensing subsystem requirements into structured test cases and test equipment concepts.

* Specify and commission the optical, electrical, and mechanical ground support equipment (OGSE, EGSE, and MGSE) that each modality needs.

* Serve as the department's technical authority on sensor synchronisation, calibration, and multimodal data quality.

## The day-to-day

* **Prototype testing****:** Characterise new sensors and integrated sensing subsystems during development. Turn requirements, such as performance at a given speed, temperature or altitude, into test plans. Design, build, and run the lab setups that show whether the sensor meets them.
* **Integration support:** Help decide how cameras and other sensors fit each new platform, including mounting angles, focus, field of view, and protective window.
* **Production support****:**Define and run the acceptance test procedures that every production unit must pass before shipment.
* **Supplier evaluation****:**Assess new sensor suppliers and components against technical requirements before we commit to procurement.
* **In-service root cause analysis:**Investigate sensing failures in products already flying, in the field or from reports. Reproduce the fault, fix it, and give structured findings to the design and manufacturing teams.
