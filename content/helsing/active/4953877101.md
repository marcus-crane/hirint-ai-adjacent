## **Who we are**

Helsing develops artificial intelligence-enabled capabilities to protect and defend democracies. We are growing our US operations, cultivating an ambitious and committed team of mission-driven professionals to apply their skills to solve challenging problems.

## **The role**

We are seeking an experienced **Facility Security Officer (FSO)** to spearhead security operations at our brand-new manufacturing facility in West Virginia, and to help write the next chapter of our company's growth story. 

This is more than a job; it's a rare opportunity to leave your mark in a brand new role. As a dual-scope leader, you'll blend strategic, corporate-level personnel security and National Industrial Security Program (NISP) compliance work supporting our home office with hands-on physical security management at the plant floor. 

## **The day-to-day**

* Personnel Security & NISPOM Compliance (Corporate Support) 

  + Ensure adherence to the NISPOM (32 CFR Part 117) and serve as the primary liaison with DCSA field offices, government security representatives, and other federal agencies.
  + Manage the end-to-end clearance process (eAPP/DISS) and maintain cleared employee records, briefings, debriefings, and periodic reinvestigations.
  + Develop and deliver comprehensive security training programs, including initial indoctrination, annual refreshers, and specialized briefings (foreign travel, insider threat, etc.).
  + Prepare for, facilitate, and support formal Defense Counterintelligence and Security Agency (DCSA) security reviews, audits, and assessments.
  + Manage and enforce strict compliance procedures for the proper handling, storage, tracking, and destruction of classified, export-controlled, and proprietary information.

   

  Physical Security & Facility Operations (Plant-Level) 

  + Guard & Visitor Management: Manage the contract guard force (scheduling, post orders, compliance) and enforce visitor control procedures, including badging, escort requirements, and access logs.
  + Physical Systems Maintenance: Maintain and monitor plant physical security measures (access control, CCTV, alarms, perimeter security) in accordance with corporate standards.
  + Audits & Asset Protection: Conduct regular security inspections, audits, and vulnerability assessments to enforce security requirements for restricted areas, asset protection, and proprietary information.
  + Incident & Emergency Response: Serve as the primary point of contact for facility-level security incidents, investigations, emergency response coordination, and crisis management.
  + Cross-Functional Collaboration: Coordinate directly with facility management, Human Resources, and safety teams on integrated security planning, policy alignment, and risk mitigation.

## **You should apply if you**

* Bachelor's degree in Security Management, Criminal Justice, Business Administration, or related field (or equivalent experience)

* Experience managing contract or in-house security guard forces

* Proficiency with DISS, NBIS, eAPP, and NISS

* Strong understanding of access control systems, visitor management, and physical security best practices

* Excellent organizational, communication, and interpersonal skills
