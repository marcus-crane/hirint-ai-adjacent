## **Who we are**

Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. 

As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. 

We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications.

## **The role**

As Programme Procurement & Partnership Manager in Munich, you will own supplier and partner relationships within the CA-1 programme. You will secure timely deliveries and shape the move from a prototype-focused supplier selection to a resilient, sovereign and high-performing supply base ready for series production. You will also set up in-service support with suppliers and shape supplier engagements for new product variants, including export versions. Working closely with programme and functional teams, you will turn procurement needs into clear plans and secure the supply that lets Helsing deliver defence capabilities to its customers.

## **The day-to-day**

* Manage supplier and partner relationships across the CA-1 programme, and secure delivery of supplier and partner commitments against agreed milestones
* Transform a prototype-focused supplier selection into a resilient, sovereign and performant supply base for series production
* Establish in-service support with suppliers, including performance-based logistics arrangements with clear service levels
* Support the development of new product variants, including export versions and additional capabilities, by shaping supplier engagements
* Capture procurement needs, align them with programme and functional stakeholders, and track deliveries against scope, cost and time

## **You should apply if you**

* Negotiate supplier agreements that balance commercial, technical and delivery requirements
* Manage supplier engagements from plan to delivery, tracking scope, cost and time with clear reporting
* Apply export control rules to supplier engagements and product variants
* Handle security requirements in supply chains, including the protection of sensitive information
* Have worked on Defence Aerospace programmes and understand their procurement and assurance demands
