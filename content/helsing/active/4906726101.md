# Field Engineer

## Who we are

Helsing develops artificial intelligence-enabled capabilities to protect and defend democracies. We are growing our US operations, cultivating an ambitious and committed team of mission-driven professionals to apply their skills to solve challenging problems.

## The role

As a Field Engineer, you will be responsible for deploying, integrating, testing and troubleshooting Helsing's hardware and software products in real world environments. Working across our engineering, product and field teams, you will help ensure our systems perform reliably under operational conditions.

This is a hands on role for someone who enjoys working with complex technology, solving practical problems and getting products out of the lab and into the field. You will support test campaigns, work directly with equipment and end users, and provide feedback to our engineering teams to help improve product performance, reliability and usability.

You will play an important part in connecting product development with real world application, helping Helsing deliver robust capabilities to the people who depend on them.

## The day-to-day

* Assemble, configure, deploy and test combined hardware and software products, both in the lab and in the field, capturing data and providing structured feedback to Helsing's mechanical, electrical, software and AI engineering teams.
* Prepare, transport, mobilise and demobilise equipment at test locations, including ports, offshore military test ranges and remote environments, supporting testing in real world conditions at sea.
* Install and configure AI software on edge computing devices using Helsing's in-house deployment tools, ensuring systems are correctly set up for field conditions.
* Execute test plans and procedures, documenting results, identifying issues and supporting the investigation and resolution of technical problems.
* Troubleshoot hardware, electrical and software issues in the field, implementing practical fixes where appropriate and escalating more complex problems to the relevant engineering teams.
* Support the development and improvement of test procedures, safety documentation and standard operating procedures, helping ensure field activities are conducted safely and consistently.
* Maintain field equipment, test hardware, tools and inventory, ensuring everything is prepared and available ahead of deployments and test events.
* Work alongside end users during demonstrations, trials and operational testing, providing technical support and helping them understand how to use Helsing's products.
* Capture feedback from field activities and communicate findings clearly to internal engineering and product teams, helping inform future product improvements.
* Support the preparation and delivery of training activities and provide ongoing technical assistance to end users.
* Travel to testing locations across the US.
* Work closely with Helsing's UK and European Field Engineering teams, sharing knowledge and adopting consistent practices across the wider organisation.

## You should apply if you

* Have strong technical aptitude and enjoy understanding how complex hardware and software systems work, particularly when diagnosing why something isn't working as expected.
* Have experience assembling, deploying, testing or troubleshooting robots, aircraft, watercraft or other autonomous or electromechanical systems.
* Are comfortable with software deployment and debugging tools, including command line interfaces, and can work across hardware and software environments.
* Have practical experience diagnosing and resolving hardware and electrical issues, carrying out field repairs or implementing temporary fixes to keep systems operational.
* Can follow technical documentation, test procedures and safety requirements, while also recognising when something needs further investigation or escalation.
* Communicate clearly in writing and speech, producing concise technical documentation and explaining issues effectively to both engineering and non engineering audiences.
* Are comfortable working independently, taking responsibility for assigned tasks and adapting when priorities or conditions change.
* Are happy working in practical, hands on environments, including remote test locations and outdoor conditions.
* Are comfortable travelling to test sites and spending periods of up to two weeks away when required.
* Work well as part of a multidisciplinary team and enjoy collaborating with engineers, technicians, product teams and end users.

Note: We encourage you to apply even if you don't meet all the listed qualifications; ability and impact cannot be summarised in a few bullet points.
