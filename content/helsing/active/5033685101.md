As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously.

## **The role**

Helsing is building a finance function that keeps pace with the company. As Senior Manager, IFRS & Consolidation, you will own group consolidation and technical accounting across a multi-entity, multi-currency group. You will bring consolidation in-house in LucaNet, build a monthly IFRS close and be the go-to person for IFRS questions across Finance, our auditor and deal teams. This is a hands-on role: you write the position paper and then book it. You will work closely with Accounting, FP&A, Tax and Legal on acquisitions, financing, new IFRS standards and investor-grade reporting, and coordinate delivery across the wider accounting team.

## **The day-to-day**

* Run the monthly IFRS group consolidation in Excel (later in LucaNet), including chart of accounts mapping, intercompany eliminations, FX translation and non-controlling interests
* Own technical accounting positions mainly on IFRS 15, 16, 2, 3 and 10, and answer auditor queries on time
* Build a fast, repeatable close by automating intercompany reconciliation, designing consolidation controls and working towards a three-day close
* Account for acquisitions end to end, from the purchase price allocation to consolidating new entities
* Define IFRS 8 segments and KPI and APM definitions with FP&A, and shape the new P&L structure under IFRS 18

## **You should apply if you**

* Have built deep technical IFRS expertise in audit or IFRS accounting advisory at a Big 4 firm
* Have designed a group consolidation process and run it end to end, both in spreadsheets and in dedicated consolidation systems (e.g. LucaNet, SAP Group Reporting, OneStream, Tagetik or Hyperion)
* Have worked through complex group topics: acquisitions and purchase price allocation (IFRS 3, IFRS 10), revenue over time (IFRS 15), leases (IFRS 16) and share-based payments (IFRS 2)
* Write the position paper and then book it, and build the processes, templates and documentation that make the work repeatable under audit deadlines
* Explain IFRS clearly to non-accountants, challenge advisers constructively and work in English every day
