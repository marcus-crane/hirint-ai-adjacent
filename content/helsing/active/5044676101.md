## **Who we are**

Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. 

As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. 

We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications.

## **The role**

## 

As Senior Programme Manager – Air Defence, you will shape and align Helsing's strategic partnerships and customer programmes in air defence. You will work directly with industry partners, procurement agencies and Armed Forces. You will set the business strategy, vision and roadmap for current and future air defence programmes. You will start by understanding customer demand in depth, then use that insight to develop new opportunities for collaboration with Armed Forces and the defence industry. You will own your programmes from first engagement to delivery, and you will make decisions within a clear mandate.

## 

You will join a partnerships and programmes team that brings together many fields of expertise and backgrounds. Together, the team shapes how industry partners and customers think about software and AI in defence, national security and intelligence. You will learn from leading experts and build lasting relationships with industry and customers.

## 

## **The day-to-day**

## 

* Define and deliver the market strategy, vision and roadmap for current and future air defence programmes, with milestones agreed with Domain Leadership each quarter
* Anticipate market trends and customer needs, and turn them into a qualified pipeline of new opportunities with Armed Forces and industry partners
* Own complex defence programmes end to end, from early-stage engagement to high-quality delivery on agreed scope, schedule and budget
* Coordinate programme delivery with government customers, industry partners and internal teams, resolving blockers before they affect milestones
* Run strategic campaigns and manage every activity needed to meet Helsing's strategic goals, tracking progress against defined success measures
* Report to Air Defence Domain Leadership on programme status, risks and pipeline development

## 

## **You should apply if you**

## 

* Have managed complex programmes in the defence industry, government or related professional services, from early engagement through to delivery
* Understand air defence, military or intelligence-community needs well enough to translate customer requirements into programme plans
* Build and sustain productive relationships with Armed Forces, procurement agencies and industry partners
* Work openly with colleagues and stakeholders, and solve problems together
* Take ownership of outcomes and act on your own initiative to move programmes forward
* Are ready to complete the measures required for security clearance under the relevant national laws
