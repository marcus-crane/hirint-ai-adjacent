## **The role**

As a (Junior) Customs Manager at Helsing, you will help shape and build our customs function from the ground up. You will manage our import and export operations, ensure full compliance with all customs regulations, and develop processes and guidelines. In this role, you will work closely with Export Control, Supply Chain, Procurement, Logistics, and Finance.

## **The day-to-day**

* Manage and monitor all import and export operations via external customs brokers and freight forwarders, including verifying customs declarations and documents for accuracy.
* Select, instruct, and manage the performance of customs brokers, and conduct regular quality checks.
* Prepare for, facilitate, and follow up on external audits and inquiries from customs authorities, acting as their primary point of contact.
* Continuously monitor the requirements for Authorized Economic Operator (AEO) status, keep internal controls and documentation up to date, and support the application for or maintenance of the status.
* Build a management system for supplier declarations and preferential origin from the ground up, including processes, system support, requesting and verifying declarations, and supplier communication.
* Develop and implement internal customs guidelines, work instructions, and process descriptions, for example regarding tariff classification, customs valuation, origin, and customs procedures.
* Train internal stakeholders and advise other departments on customs-related matters.
* Identify opportunities for optimization, such as reducing customs costs, simplifying procedures, and driving digitalization.
* Build and functionally lead a customs team in the future as the department grows

## **You should apply if you**

* Completed vocational training in freight forwarding and logistics (*Speditionskaufmann/-frau*) or a degree in Business Administration or Logistics, each combined with an additional qualification in customs (e.g., Certified Customs Specialist, Certificate in Customs Management)
* 3–5 years of professional experience in customs, ideally within an industrial company, at a customs broker, or in freight forwarding
* Sound knowledge of the Union Customs Code (UCC), tariff classification, customs valuation, origin of goods and preferences, as well as customs procedures
* Experience with AEO, customs audits, and supplier declarations
* Strong assertiveness and confidence in representing positions toward internal stakeholders, service providers, and authorities
* Ability to quickly adapt and thrive in new, dynamic environments
* Structured, independent, and solution-oriented working style paired with a hands-on mentality
* Keen interest in taking on leadership responsibilities in the future
* Excellent written and spoken German and English skills
