## **The role**

As an electro-optical sensing expert, you will shape how we assess, process and use satellite imagery. You will build image-processing and quality-assessment pipelines, advise AI research engineers on sensor behaviour and physical limits, and manage technical work with data providers and satellite operators. You will also contribute to mission and constellation planning, weighing payload, coverage and processing trade-offs. Your work will help turn multi-modal satellite imagery into reliable, actionable intelligence for decision-makers and operators. AI expertise is not required.

## **The day-to-day**

* Build and maintain optical image-processing pipelines for radiometric calibration, denoising, destriping, artefact handling, band co-registration, pan-sharpening, super-resolution, orthorectification, mosaicking and map generation
* Define automated image-quality checks for signal-to-noise ratio, modulation transfer function, geolocation accuracy, cloud and haze, and determine whether scenes are fit for purpose
* Manage acquired datasets from tasking and ordering through ingestion, cataloguing, metadata, cloud storage and scalable batch processing
* Assess data providers and satellite operators through technical reviews and pilot acquisitions, examine specifications and support procurement discussions
* Support mission and constellation planning by assessing ground sample distance, swath, revisit, payload selection, coverage, downlink and on-board versus ground processing, and prepare data for AI teams

## **You should apply if you**

* Apply knowledge of optical or infrared remote sensing, including optics, detectors, modulation transfer function, signal-to-noise ratio, radiometry, spectral response and noise sources
* Have implemented and validated classical image-processing methods, including calibration, restoration, co-registration, pan-sharpening, orthorectification and mosaicking, and can explain their trade-offs
* Use geospatial tools and formats such as GDAL, rasterio, OpenCV, QGIS, GeoTIFF or COG, RPCs, digital elevation models and map projections
* Build automated, reproducible processing pipelines in Python, C++ or Rust, including in cloud environments
* Apply space systems engineering knowledge to mission and constellation design, and hold a relevant degree with industry or mission experience
