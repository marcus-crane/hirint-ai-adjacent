## **Who we are**

Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. 

As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. 

We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications.

## **The role**

As Team Assistant in Munich, you will support Helsing's leadership team as it works with government, armed forces and industry partners. Reporting to the Executive Assistant, you will organise high-stakes meetings, manage complex diaries across time zones and handle correspondence with senior officials with care and discretion. You will plan domestic and international travel, keep expenses accurate and make sure information moves smoothly between departments. Your work creates the conditions for colleagues to respond quickly to partners and deliver on commitments to Europe's defence. This is a hands-on role in a fast-growing company, where careful administration has visible impact on how Helsing is experienced by the people we serve.

## **The day-to-day**

* Act as the first point of contact for the leadership team, correspondence with internal departments, external stakeholders and service providers, and filtering and prioritising incoming requests
* Manage complex diaries for several senior colleagues, scheduling internal and external meetings and resolving calendar conflicts before they escalate
* Own end-to-end planning of domestic and international business travel, including itineraries, visas and logistics, so that colleagues arrive prepared for government and industry engagements

* Process travel expense reports and general team expenses accurately and on time, keeping spend transparent and within policy

* Organise internal and external meetings / events with senior officials across business and government, alongside internal C-level executives

## **You should apply if you**

* Have supported senior stakeholders in an administrative, team assistant, office management, workplace coordination, event management or hospitality role
* Organise competing priorities calmly and keep structure in a fast-paced, high-stakes environment
* Communicate with clarity in writing and in person, and have engaged effectively with senior officials in both the private and public sectors
* Exercise sound professional judgement, reliability and complete discretion when handling confidential information
* Work fluently in German and English, and are comfortable with MS Office, especially Outlook

*Note: We operate in an industry where women, as well as other minority groups, are systematically* *under-represented. We encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet all the listed qualifications; ability and impact cannot be summarised in a few bullet points.*
