## **Who we are**

Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. 

As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. 

We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications.

## **The role**

Helsing is growing across several European countries, and our approach to hiring, organising, rewarding and supporting our people must grow with us. As Senior Legal Counsel, Employment Law, you will advise on employment matters across the group, working closely with People, Talent Acquisition, Finance and senior leadership. You will guide matters from initial advice through to resolution, while building the legal frameworks, templates and processes that help a scaling company meet its obligations and support its people.

## **The day-to-day**

* Advise People, Talent Acquisition and managers across the employee lifecycle, from hiring and contracts to performance, restructuring and termination
* Draft and maintain employment contracts, policies, handbooks and templates across our entities, including executive and senior-hire agreements
* Resolve employment disputes and litigation, negotiate settlements, and select and manage external counsel where needed
* Support cross-border employment, compensation, M&A and new entity set-ups, while addressing defence-sector requirements, employee data protection and changes in law through practical guidance and training

## **You should apply if you**

* Qualify as a lawyer in Germany, with both state examinations, and bring substantial expertise in employment law gained in private practice, in-house or both
* Apply deep knowledge of German individual and collective employment law
* Have handled employment disputes and negotiated pragmatic settlements
* Explain legal issues clearly to non-lawyers, give commercially grounded advice and take responsibility for the advice you provide
* Communicate fluently in written and spoken German and English

*Note: We operate in an industry where women, as well as other minority groups, are systematically* *under-represented. We encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet all the listed qualifications; ability and impact cannot be summarised in a few bullet points.*
