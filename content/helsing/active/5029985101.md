## **Who we are**

Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. 

As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. 

We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications.

## **The role**

You will join the Solutions Engineering Team in the Land domain and take end-to-end ownership of verification and validation (V&V) for complex, AI-enabled mission and battlefield management capabilities for modern land platforms, guiding our systems from development through to formal qualification and operational fielding. You will define what needs to be tested, steer testing across internal engineering teams and external facilities, investigate anomalies hands-on in the lab and in the field, and deliver audit-ready documentation that secures final customer acceptance.

## **The day-to-day**

* Agree system qualification requirements and Means of Compliance (MoC) with customers; plan and maintain test plans, verification procedures, and traceable compliance matrices

* Steer end-to-end verification across multidisciplinary internal teams (hardware, software, mechanics) and external accredited test facilities to prove compliance

* Participate in hands-on testing in the lab and on proving grounds; travel to field test campaigns to monitor progress, investigate anomalies, and validate real-world results

* Document defects, deviations, and risks clearly; work closely with development teams across hardware and software to investigate root causes and drive issues to resolution

* Establish, streamline, and continuously improve V&V processes and traceability toolchains in an agile, fast-evolving development environment

* Author qualification reports and compliance documentation; defend test findings, recommendations, and qualification evidence convincingly before customers

* Report V&V status and technical risks regularly to the Chief Engineering and Programme Management teams, keeping all stakeholders aligned on key milestones

## **You should apply if you**

* Have solid professional experience in Verification & Validation (V&V), systems engineering, or system integration testing for complex technical systems in a regulated, safety-critical, or high-reliability industry (e.g., automotive, aerospace, defence, rail, or medical technology)

* Ideally have hands-on experience with formal qualification or certification campaigns, e.g. for defence products or aircraft

* Have navigated final qualification and acceptance reviews, building constructive working relationships with authorities and/or safety assessors

* Bring organizational rigor and the ability to establish clarity and structured processes in complex, dynamic project environments

* Combine confidence and diplomatic tact when challenging assumptions, pushing back on deviations, and maintaining standards with experienced development teams

* Are able to understand the interplay and interfaces across hardware, software, and mechanical engineering

* Enjoy working with physical systems, software and test setups, analyzing data, and getting to the bottom of technical issues

* Are willing to travel to field trials and test campaigns and support intensive integration milestones

**Note: We operate in an industry where women, as well as other minority groups, are systematically* *under-represented. We encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet all the listed qualifications; ability and impact cannot be summarised in a few bullet points.**

## **Nice to Have**
