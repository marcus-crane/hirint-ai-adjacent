## **The role**

As an Specialist Export Control / ITAR, you are the central point of contact for all questions regarding export control, customs, and sanctions. You bridge regulatory compliance with operational speed, ensuring technology, software, hardware, and technical data cross borders legally without unnecessarily slowing down Engineering, Procurement, Sales, or Logistics.

Acting with high agency, you take full ownership of daily operations—from licensing and screening to the customs interface—collaborating closely with HR, Legal, Supply Chain, and our international entities (especially in the US). You drive automation and scalable licensing architectures to enable our growth, designing the critical processes relied upon by our Executive Export Control Officer (*Ausfuhrverantwortlicher*) while formal liability remains at the management level.

## **The day-to-day**

* **US Export Control (ITAR / EAR)**

  + Classify hardware, software, and technology under USML (ITAR) and ECCN (EAR)
  + Assess re-export and transfer requirements (*de minimis*, Foreign Direct Product Rule, ITAR "see-through")
  + Apply for and manage TAAs, MLAs, DSP-5s, and BIS licenses alongside US teams and external counsel
  + Oversee deemed exports, cross-border data transfers, and compliance with US licence provisos
* **EU & German Export Control**
  + Classify goods under the German Export List (AL) and the EU Dual-Use Regulation
  + Prepare and submit BAFA licence applications (individual, global, general) and support *KrWaffKontrG* filings
  + Manage sanctions screenings, embargo reviews, and end-user verifications (EUCs)
* **Customs & Logistics**
  + Handle import/export customs matters, including tariff classifications, preferential origin, and ATLAS
  + Collaborate with forwarders and Procurement to ensure shipments strictly follow licence requirements
  + Support customs simplifications and AEO certification (optional)
* **Internal Compliance Program (ICP)**
  + Develop and document the ICP, embedding automated controls into ERP, PLM, and HR systems
  + Conduct internal audits, lead investigations, and prepare voluntary disclosures where necessary
  + Deliver company-wide trainings, advise on bids/partnerships, and liaise with authorities (BAFA, Customs, DDTC, BIS)
