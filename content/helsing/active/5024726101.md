## **The role**

As a Systems Engineer, Interceptors, you will shape the system architecture and requirements for AD-1 Inferno, Helsing’s jet-powered interceptor within the Contra air defence system. Designed to counter fast drone and cruise missile threats at scale, AD-1 operates at high subsonic speeds and can loiter, re-engage and operate in swarms. You will translate stakeholder needs and the threat environment into a coherent system concept, then develop the requirements, architecture and evidence needed to support testing, certification and production. You will also make sure that safety, security and through-life considerations are built into the system from the outset.

## **The day-to-day**

* Elicit needs from internal and external stakeholders, assess the threat environment and maintain a coherent concept of operations for the weapon system
* Develop and maintain the configured JAMA requirements baseline across the weapon system, subsystems and equipment, ensuring requirements are unambiguous, verifiable, linked and justified
* Conduct trade studies and analysis to decompose requirements and allocate performance parameters, while developing logical and functional architectures using model-based systems engineering (MBSE)
* Own interface control documents and define the verification and validation strategy, including design verification plans that set out how compliance will be demonstrated
* Coordinate certification and safety activities, including compliance matrices, proving evidence, final compliance documentation and the weapon system safety case, while capturing maintainability, testability, training, software upgradeability and growth margins

## **You should apply if you**

* Apply systems engineering methods, including trade studies, requirements decomposition and MBSE architecture development
