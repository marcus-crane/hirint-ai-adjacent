## **Who we are**

Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. 

As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. 

We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications.

## **The role**

Contra puts machine-learned and reinforcement-learning algorithms into an air defence system’s control loop, from edge compute and firmware to actuators. As System Architect, you will define the integrated architecture and functional breakdown that enable engineering teams to build and assure the system. You will set out how learned components are bounded, monitored and supported by fallback paths, and develop the safety argument for their use. You will also represent Helsing in standards and working groups, contributing to the emerging rules for safe AI in safety-critical and defence systems.

## The day-to-day

* Define the system architecture across software, electronics and hardware, and translate it into a functional breakdown that teams can build against
* Specify how machine-learning and reinforcement-learning components are bounded, monitored and assured, including runtime assurance, safety envelopes and fallback paths
* Develop the safety argument for learned components, covering the operating domain, triggering conditions, data and verification evidence
* Translate the architecture into requirements and interfaces for embedded, firmware, autonomy and hardware teams, working with safety and qualification engineers to address current and emerging standards
* Represent Helsing in NATO, national and cross-industry working groups, contributing to standards for AI in safety-critical and defence systems

## **You should apply if you**

* Have architected safety-critical cyber-physical or embedded systems across hardware, software and electronics
* Have experience with model based systems engineering approaches (MBSE)
* Have helped define a standard, guideline or recognised methodology for the safety of machine learning, autonomy or automated driving, for example as a working-group member, editor, convenor or author
* Can translate a top-level architecture into a functional breakdown and explain the trade-offs
* Can work across engineering teams, with customers and in committees to build agreement and progress technical decisions
* Speak English fluently and are eligible to obtain German security clearance; German language skills are an advantage

**Note: We operate in an industry where women, as well as other minority groups, are systematically* *under-represented. We encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet all the listed qualifications; ability and impact cannot be summarised in a few bullet points.**
