## **Who we are**

Helsing develops artificial intelligence-enabled capabilities to protect and defend democracies. We are growing our US operations, cultivating an ambitious and committed team of mission-driven professionals to apply their skills to solve challenging problems.

## **The role**

We are seeking an experiencedSenior Manufacturing Engineer to help stand up our brand-new manufacturing facility in Martinsburg, West Virginia, and to help write the next chapter of our company's growth story. 

This is more than a job; it's a rare opportunity to build a factory from the ground up. As a founding member of our manufacturing engineering team, you'll own the design and implementation of process flows, tooling, and equipment that will bring our production line to life — working hands-on from concept through startup and steady-state production.

## **The day-to-day**

**Process & Production Line Development** 

* Design, build, and continuously improve manufacturing process flows for new and existing product lines.

* Develop and implement standard work stations, work instructions, and standardized processes to ensure repeatable, high-quality production.

* Concept, plan, and design custom tooling, jigs, and fixtures to support efficient and ergonomic manufacturing operations.

* Design and build custom test stands and fixtures to support product validation and end-of-line testing.

* Lead the procurement of capital equipment, tooling, and fixtures, from specification and vendor selection through installation and qualification.

**Factory Standup & Production Support** 

* Manage the timeline and budget for manufacturing engineering workstreams as part of the broader factory standup effort.

* Support the startup, ramp, and stabilization of production lines, including troubleshooting equipment, tooling, and process issues in real time.

* Set up and configure MES (Manufacturing Execution System) processes and work instructions to support production tracking, traceability, and quality control.

* Partner with Supply Chain to ensure tooling, equipment, and materials are procured and delivered on schedule to support production timelines.

* Collaborate with Quality to build in process controls, inspection points, and validation steps that meet product and regulatory requirements.

* Work closely with Production to ensure process designs translate into safe, efficient, and scalable operations on the floor.

**Cross-Functional Collaboration** 

* Serve as a key technical point of contact between Manufacturing Engineering, Supply Chain, Quality, and Production teams throughout the factory standup.

* Support continuous improvement initiatives (Lean, Six Sigma, or similar methodologies) as the factory matures from startup to full-rate production.

* Document processes, tooling designs, and lessons learned to build institutional knowledge as the site scales.
