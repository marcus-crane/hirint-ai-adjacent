## **The role**

As Systems Engineer for our Armament Control System (ACS), you take end-to-end technical responsibility for a safety-critical system: hardware, software, testing, qualification and safety. You will shape the concept for armament control across the aircraft, the ground station and the data links that connect them, then translate that concept into specifications suppliers can build against. You will be the technical counterpart in statement-of-work and contract negotiations, and you will help engineers earlier in their careers grow into systems thinkers. The work sits at the point where architecture, certification and partner management meet, and nothing reaches an aircraft until the evidence shows it is safe.

## **The day-to-day**

* Take technical responsibility for the full ACS lifecycle, from concept and architecture through hardware and software development, integration and test, to qualification and safety release
* Define the system concept and architecture for on-aircraft and ground-station armament control, including tactical data links and ACS software, against standards such as MIL-STD-1760, ARP4754A, DO-178C, DO-254 and MIL-STD-882
* Write and maintain ACS requirements and supplier specifications in tools such as DOORS or Polarion, so that every requirement is traceable to a verification method and a test result
* Act as the technical point of contact in supplier selection, statement-of-work definition and contractual negotiations, holding partners to agreed scope, schedule and performance
* Coach engineers earlier in their careers through design reviews, safety reasoning and certification practice, raising the quality bar across the team

## **You should apply if you**

* Have taken a safety-critical aerospace or defence system through to qualification, and can explain the trade-offs you made along the way
* Design architectures that span hardware, software and interfaces, with working knowledge of embedded systems and tactical data links
* Write precise requirements and supplier specifications, and work fluently in requirements management tools such as DOORS or Polarion
* Apply airworthiness and safety standards, for example ARP4754A, DO-178C, DO-254, MIL-STD-882 or MIL-STD-1760, in everyday engineering decisions
* Communicate technical reasoning clearly in written and spoken English and German, to customers as well as engineering colleagues
* Hold, or are willing and able to obtain, an extended German security clearance (erweiterte Sicherheitsüberprüfung, Ü2 or Ü3)
