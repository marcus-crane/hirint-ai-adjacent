## **Who we are**

Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards.

As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously.

We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits and its ethical implications.

## **The role**

As an IT Support Engineer in Munich, you will be the first point of contact for the people who work in those environments, from internal teams to external customers. You will own the help desk, resolve first- and second-line tickets and take on more complex third-line issues as you grow, all within strict service levels for response and resolution. Working alongside our Site Reliability Engineers, you will automate the repetitive parts of support with scripting and infrastructure as code. Your work keeps secure systems available, so the engineers who build them can focus on the mission.

## **The day-to-day**

* Own the help desk queue, resolving first- and second-line tickets within agreed response and resolution times
* Manage the full user and device lifecycle: onboarding, offboarding, MDM enrolment, account provisioning, and access reviews.
* Troubleshoot complex issues with Site Reliability Engineers and take on third-line support as your knowledge grows
* Automate recurring support tasks with Python, PowerShell or infrastructure as code, and measure the time it saves
* Flag risks early and keep internal users and external customers informed until their issue is closed

## **You should apply if you**

* Have experience in an IT support role, ideally including L2/L3 responsibilities in a fast-moving environment
* Have hands-on experience supporting macOS, Windows, and iOS, and a solid grasp of identity, endpoint, and SaaS troubleshooting.
* Enjoy solving technical problems, are eager to learn new technologies, and have a genuine passion for helping others succeed.
* Can prioritise and manage multiple competing tasks calmly in a dynamic environment.
* Bring a proactive mindset. You spot and resolve potential issues before they become outages or tickets.
* Bring a customer-first attitude with strong communication skills and a focus on delivering an excellent user experience.
* Communicate fluently in German and English.

*Note: We operate in an industry where women, as well as other minority groups, are systematically under-represented. We encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet all the listed qualifications; ability and impact cannot be summarised in a few bullet points.*

## **Nice to have**
