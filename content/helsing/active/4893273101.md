## **Who we are**

Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards.

As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously.

We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits and its ethical implications.

## **The role**

As an IT Engineer in Munich, you make sure the people who use our infrastructure can work, from our own engineers to external customers. You own the help desk, find and fix the root cause of system problems, and automate repetitive work. You join a team of Site Reliability Engineers who aim to fix each problem once.

## **The day-to-day**

* Manage the full user and device lifecycle: onboarding, off-boarding, MDM enrollment, account provisioning and access reviews
* Own the help desk queue, resolving first- and second-line tickets within agreed response and resolution times
* Diagnose and fix complex hardware, operating system and connectivity issues, such as VPN connections that fail or clients that will not start
* Build and maintain VMs, client images and the pipelines that build them
* Automate routine work with scripts and infrastructure as code, and keep documentation, runbooks and inventories up to date

## **You should apply if you**

* Have hands-on experience supporting macOS, Windows, Linux and iOS, and troubleshooting identity, endpoint and SaaS issues
* Understand networking fundamentals such as TCP/IP, DNS and HTTP, and use them to troubleshoot connectivity and VPN issues
* Automate your work with scripts in Bash, Python or PowerShell, and with infrastructure as code tools such as Ansible or Terraform
* Have built VMs or images, or worked with CI/CD pipelines
* Bring a customer-first attitude with strong communication skills and a focus on delivering an excellent user experience

* Communicate fluently in German and English

*Note: We operate in an industry where women, as well as other minority groups, are systematically under-represented. We encourage you to apply even if you don’t meet all the listed qualifications; ability and impact cannot be summarised in a few bullet points.*

## **Nice to have**

* Experience setting up networks, including VLANs, VPNs, routers, switches and firewalls
* Certifications such as CCNA or RHCSA
