**Who we are**

Helsing develops artificial intelligence enabled capabilities to protect and defend democracies. We are growing our US operations, cultivating an ambitious and committed team of mission driven professionals to apply their skills to solve challenging problems.

**The role**

We are looking for a detail-oriented and data-driven People Operations Generalist to join our People team. In this role, you will be the operational backbone of the employee experience—ensuring new hires have a seamless welcome, our people systems run with high accuracy, and our compensation programs remain competitive, equitable, and well-administered.  

This is an ideal role for someone who loves building scalable processes, working with HR data and systems, and balancing analytical work with high-touch employee support. 

**The day-to-day**

* Own the end-to-end onboarding process, from offer acceptance through the first 90 days, ensuring an engaging, organized, and welcoming experience for all new hires.

* Facilitate new hire orientation sessions and coordinate cross-functional onboarding schedules with IT, Finance, and hiring managers.

* Manage pre-boarding compliance, including background checks, I-9 verifications, and new hire paperwork.

* Continuously gather feedback through onboarding surveys and iterate on the program to improve time-to-productivity and new hire satisfaction.

* Serve as the primary administrator for our HRIS ensuring 100% data integrity across all employee records.

* Process employee lifecycle changes accurately and on time, including new hires, promotions, transfers, manager changes, and terminations.

* Conduct regular audits of HRIS data, troubleshoot system issues, and manage integrations with downstream tools (payroll, benefits, ATS, IT provisioning).

* Build and maintain standard and ad-hoc People reports and dashboards (e.g., headcount, turnover, onboarding metrics) to support leadership decision-making.

* Support the administration of recurring compensation cycles, including merit increases, bonus payouts, and equity refreshes.

* Act as a trusted first point of contact for employee inquiries regarding HR policies, benefits, payroll questions, and company procedures.

* Maintain and update internal People documentation, employee handbooks, and knowledge base pages.

* Ensure compliance with federal, state, and local employment laws and regulations.

**You should apply if you**

* 2–4+ years of experience in People Operations, HR Operations, or as an HR Generalist, preferably in a fast-paced or high-growth environment.

* Hands-on experience administering modern HRIS platforms ([e.g., Rippling, Workday, BambooHR, Hibob]) and managing employee data workflows.

* Working knowledge of compensation principles, including benchmarking, salary bands, variable pay, and merit cycles.

* Strong proficiency in Excel/Google Sheets (e.g., VLOOKUP/XLOOKUP, pivot tables, basic formulas) for auditing data and modeling compensation.

* Demonstrated ability to take a manual or fragmented process (like onboarding) and make it scalable, automated, and employee-friendly.

* Exceptional judgment and ability to handle highly confidential compensation and personnel information with discretion.
* Warm, clear, and proactive communicator—equally comfortable presenting to a group of new hires or explaining a compensation policy to a manager.
