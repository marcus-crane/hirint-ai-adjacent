## **The role**

As Cost Controller, Public Contracts, you will oversee pricing and cost calculations for public contracts, ensuring they comply with German public procurement pricing law, including VO PR Nr. 30/53 and LSP. You will prepare for and support government audits, review contract calculations and offers, and contribute to price negotiations with public customers. Working with colleagues across R&D, project management and finance, you will provide guidance on pricing requirements, support development project controlling and the capitalisation of development costs under IAS 38, and improve pricing processes and tools across our sites. This is a full-time, permanent role with a hybrid working arrangement.

## **The day-to-day**

* Calculate and monitor overhead rates in line with VO PR Nr. 30/53, and prepare core documents for period calculations
* Prepare for and support fundamental, planned-cost and individual contract audits, covering market prices and the different cost-based price types
* Review and correct offers, calculations and contracts against VO PR Nr. 30/53, LSP and internal costing guidelines
* Support pre- and post-contract price negotiations, and advise R&D, project managers and management on public pricing requirements
* Contribute to capacity and direct-cost planning, development project controlling and IAS 38 cost capitalisation, while improving pricing processes and tools across sites

## **You should apply if you**

* Have a grounding in business administration, industrial engineering or a related field, and practical controlling experience in manufacturing or audit
* Apply working knowledge of German public pricing law, particularly VO PR Nr. 30/53 and LSP
* Have prepared and reviewed calculations, offers, contracts and audit-ready overhead rates
* Use SAP FI, CO and PS to support controlling and pricing work
* Communicate clearly in fluent German and very good written and spoken English, including with auditors and customers
