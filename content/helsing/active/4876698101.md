## **The role**

Payroll at Helsing is not a back-office function — it is a foundation of trust. As a Payroll Specialist, you will prepare and deliver accurate, compliant payroll data across our international entities, with a particular focus on the DACH region. Payroll is processed in partnership with an external service provider; your role is to ensure that everything upstream of that process is precise, complete and delivered on time. Working closely with HR and Finance, you will contribute to a payroll infrastructure that scales as we grow. The work is detailed, consequential and sits at the intersection of people, finance and operations.

## **The day-to-day**

* Prepare and consolidate payroll data across multiple entities and regions, ensuring all inputs delivered to our external service provider are accurate, complete and submitted on time

* Support the resolution of payroll queries, escalating complex matters where appropriate and communicating outcomes clearly to affected colleagues

* Work with external payroll and service providers to maintain data integrity and uphold agreed service standards

* Monitor and maintain compliance with local statutory obligations — including wage tax, social security and labour law — with a particular focus on Germany and the wider DACH region

* Contribute to month-end, quarter-end and year-end close processes, collaborating with Finance and Accounting on reconciliations and audit readiness

* Identify opportunities to reduce manual effort, increase accuracy and strengthen payroll processes through better use of existing tools and HRIS integrations

## **You should apply if you**

* Have worked in a payroll operations role, handling multi-entity or multi-jurisdiction payroll — ideally including experience working alongside external payroll processors or service providers

* Have solid working knowledge of payroll compliance and statutory requirements in Germany, including wage tax, social security and relevant labour law

* Communicate fluently in both German and English — written and spoken — and are comfortable engaging with colleagues, service providers and local authorities

* Bring the attention to detail and consistency that means colleagues can trust payroll data to be accurate and on time

* Have worked with HRIS or payroll platforms and are comfortable learning new systems quickly

* Are motivated by Helsing's mission to protect democratic societies, and ready to engage with the responsibility that comes with it

*Note: We operate in an industry where women, as well as other minority groups, are systematically under-represented. We encourage you to apply even if you don't meet all the listed qualifications; ability and impact cannot be summarised in a few bullet points.*
