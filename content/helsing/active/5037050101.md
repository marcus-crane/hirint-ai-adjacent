## **The role**

Evaluating modern combat systems and training tactical AI requires more than isolated component models or a fast software backbone — it requires architecting how operational reality is represented and connected inside a synthetic world. Beyond our core simulation platform, we are building the domain and kill-chain layer of our synthetic environment: specifically, how sensors, effectors, command and control (C2) nodes and communication networks interact as coherent, end-to-end kill chains across Red and Blue forces. To support everything from cloud-scale operational analysis (OA) and AI training to high-fidelity system verification and validation (V&V), our synthetic environment must be fundamentally modular — allowing us to scale scenarios and swap model fidelities without breaking the kill chain.

As a Simulation Engineer on our Simulation team, you will serve as the domain architect for this kill-chain layer. You will translate complex military operations into an abstracted, modular simulation architecture — defining the kill-chain interface control documents (ICDs), multi-fidelity model boundaries and domain integration patterns that allow heterogeneous models to plug into a single, scalable synthetic environment. Working closely with core software engineers, AI researchers and military domain specialists, you will ensure our synthetic worlds are architecturally clean, scalable across fidelity tiers and operationally credible.

---

## **The day-to-day**

* Architect the end-to-end kill-chain structure of our synthetic environment, defining how sensing, C2 and battle management, communications and datalinks, and effectors interact across Red and Blue forces
* Design and maintain the interface control documents (ICDs), data contracts and functional boundaries between kill-chain components so models remain strictly decoupled and interchangeable
* Design multi-fidelity kill-chain contracts that allow any sub-model to be swapped seamlessly across granularity tiers — from fast lookup tables and functional power-and-effect models to physics and signal-level representations — without altering surrounding kill-chain logic
* Translate real-world military doctrines, operational concepts and multi-domain engagements into clean, abstracted simulation architectures that scale from single-engagement V&V to large-scale constructive OA campaigns
* Deconstruct, modularise and integrate existing constructive simulation frameworks and partner domain models (e.g. legacy C++ sensor, effector and behaviour models) into our modular kill-chain architecture
* Collaborate with core simulation backbone engineers to ensure domain-level kill-chain designs map cleanly onto the underlying runtime, time-management and execution infrastructure
* Define V&V and consistency criteria across fidelity tiers, ensuring that lower-fidelity abstractions remain calibrated and credible against higher-fidelity reference models
* Connect military operational stakeholders, partner engineering teams and internal AI and platform teams to align all aspects of the synthetic environment domain architecture
