## **Who we are**

Helsing is a defence AI company. Our mission is to protect our democracies. We aim to achieve technological leadership, so that open societies can continue to make sovereign decisions and control their ethical standards. 

As democracies, we believe we have a special responsibility to be thoughtful about the development and deployment of powerful technologies like AI. We take this responsibility seriously. 

We are an ambitious and committed team of engineers, AI specialists and customer-facing programme managers. We are looking for mission-driven people to join our European teams – and apply their skills to solve the most complex and impactful problems. We embrace an open and transparent culture that welcomes healthy debates on the use of technology in defence, its benefits, and its ethical implications.

## **The role**

The compute platform is the foundation for everything the aircraft senses, decides and does. As Senior Systems Engineer for the mission system compute platform, you will shape a high-performance, software- and AI-defined platform that turns our autonomy software into combat capability. You will set the requirements and design concepts for heterogeneous compute across CPUs, GPUs and FPGAs, within tight size, weight and power (SWaP) and thermal limits. You will ensure that the mission-critical applications our software teams build run reliably in flight and that the platform connects cleanly to other mission equipment. You will then work with suppliers to mature promising technology until it is ready to fly.

## The day-to-day

* Set out the engineering roadmap for compute technology, integrated modular avionics (IMA) platforms and storage, so the programme knows which technologies it can commit to at each aircraft build standard
* Write the high-level requirements and design concepts for a mission-critical compute platform that combines CPUs, GPUs and FPGAs, so our software teams can add AI capability without redesigning the hardware
* Work with airframe, power and cooling colleagues to fit the platform within the aircraft's SWaP and thermal budget, so our software sets the limits of capability rather than our hardware
* Design the interfaces to other mission equipment into the architecture from the outset, so integration happens on the rig rather than during flight test
* Plan and run maturation campaigns that bring future compute platforms to a technology readiness level (TRL) suitable for embodiment on the aircraft
* Build partnerships with key suppliers that give the programme early access to the hardware it needs

## **You should apply if you**

* Have specified and delivered airborne or embedded compute platforms into a flight or production programme
* Understand integrated modular avionics and heterogeneous compute, and how CPU, GPU and FPGA workloads behave together on constrained, mission-critical hardware
* Make evidenced trade-offs between performance, reliability, SWaP and thermal budgets, and record them in requirements and architecture that others can build from
* Assess emerging compute technology and mature it through rigs and trials, working with suppliers as partners
* Explain technical trade-offs clearly to engineers, programme colleagues and certification authorities
* Hold, or are eligible to hold, the security clearance required for your location
