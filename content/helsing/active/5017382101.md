## **Who we are**

Helsing develops artificial intelligence-enabled capabilities to protect and defend democracies. We are growing our US operations, cultivating an ambitious and committed team of mission-driven professionals to apply their skills to solve challenging problems.

## **The role**

We are looking for a hands-on, detail-orientedFacilities Managerto take ownership of our Martinsburg site. You will be the backbone of our day-to-day manufacturing operations — ensuring the building runs smoothly, safely, and efficiently, while creating a productive environment for our team and visitors. 

This is a broad and varied role that blends practical, hands-on maintenance with coordination, vendor management, and office administration. If you take pride in keeping things running seamlessly and enjoy being the go-to person who makes the workplace work, we'd love to hear from you.

## **The day-to-day**

* Plan, schedule, and oversee broader maintenance activities, coordinating external contractors and vendors to ensure work is completed to a high standard, on time, and within budget.

* Own the visitor experience end-to-end — managing access, sign-in procedures, security protocols, and ensuring guests are greeted professionally and in line with site requirements.

* Partner with People/HR teams to ensure new team members are set up for success, including workspace preparation, access, equipment, and a smooth first-day experience.

* Oversee general office operations, including supplies, equipment, space planning, and the overall day-to-day functioning of the workplace.

* Help maintain a safe, compliant working environment in line with relevant regulations and company standards.
